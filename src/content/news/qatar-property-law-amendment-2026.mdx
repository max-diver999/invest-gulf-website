## What did the 2026 amendment change in practice?

The law builds on Qatar's long-standing model: foreigners may own in **approved developments**, not across the whole emirate. The 2026 package reportedly clarifies eligible areas, refines residency benefits linked to property investment, and may widen qualifying unit types in master-planned districts. Official gazette text and Ministry of Justice guidance should govern your SPA, not marketing decks from brokers.

| Lusail City | Designated districts | Mixed-use towers, waterfront units |

## Which zones allow non-Qatari buyers today?

Freehold-style rights for foreigners concentrate in **The Pearl** and **Lusail**, with limited pockets elsewhere. Before transfer, confirm: (1) the unit sits inside the registered foreign ownership map, (2) the developer holds valid approvals, and (3) your nationality faces no bilateral restriction. Boundary shifts in amendments make a fresh legal opinion essential even if you toured the same tower in 2024.

## How do residency thresholds interact with property?

Qatar has offered residency routes tied to real estate investment for qualifying buyers who meet minimum values and keep compliant ownership. Exact **QAR thresholds**, family inclusion, and renewal rules change with implementing regulations. Do not assume UAE Golden Visa math (AED 2M) transfers to Doha. Ask counsel for the current Ministry of Interior / investment promotion guidance at signing.

## Why Pearl and Lusail remain the focus

**The Pearl** is a mature luxury island with marina retail, hotels, and established resale liquidity relative to the wider Qatar market. **Lusail** is newer, tied to World Cup legacy infrastructure, metro connectivity, and ongoing commercial build-out. The amendment likely aligns ownership incentives with finishing Lusail residential phases and keeping Pearl competitive with regional luxury hubs.

## What due diligence should run before a QAR transfer?

1. Zone certificate and developer escrow status for off-plan stock.
2. Share of common parts, service charge budget, and sinking fund health.
3. Financing: Qatari bank LTV rules for non-residents if you need leverage.
4. Residency application timeline if that is your primary goal.
5. Repatriation of sale proceeds and tax treatment in your home country.

Pause if the seller cannot produce Ministry of Justice registration workflow or if the unit is marketed as "ownership" outside a designated zone.

## What is the near-term outlook for Pearl and Lusail pricing?

Luxury Gulf markets compete on exclusivity and finished infrastructure. Pearl benefits from proven amenity base; Lusail needs continued office and retail absorption to support tenant demand. Regulatory clarity from the 2026 amendment may reduce buyer hesitation, but supply in Lusail still requires patient hold periods.
