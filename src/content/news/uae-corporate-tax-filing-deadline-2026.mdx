The Federal Tax Authority (FTA) confirmed **September 30, 2026**, as the filing deadline for UAE corporate tax returns covering financial years that ended **March 31, 2026**. Property-holding companies, real estate SPVs, and development entities registered in the UAE must submit returns and settle liabilities under the **9%** federal regime that took effect from June 2023.

## Which property entities must file by September 30, 2026?

Any UAE tax-resident company that earned **taxable income** above the small-business threshold during the March 2025 to March 2026 year should prepare a return. Common property-related profiles:

| Entity profile | Typical filing trigger | Personal ownership contrast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onshore property SPV | Rental or sale gains in company books | Individual landlords: no corporate tax on personal rent |
| Mainland holding company | Portfolio of Dubai or Abu Dhabi units | Same assets held personally: different rules |
| Development LLC | Trading stock or development profits | Verify project-specific registrations |
| Management or brokerage LLC | Fee income plus property income | Mixed activities need split accounting |

Individual investors who hold deeds in their own names and report no UAE corporate entity generally **do not** file corporate tax on personal rental income. The split matters for anyone who bought through a [UAE company structure](/guides/buy-property-through-uae-company/). Full context sits in [Dubai property taxes explained](/guides/dubai-property-taxes-explained/).

## What tax rate applies to property company profits?

Corporate tax applies **9%** on taxable profit above **AED 375,000** after allowable deductions. The first AED 375,000 of profit is taxed at **0%**, which lowers the effective rate for smaller portfolios.

| Profit band (illustrative) | Mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Up to AED 375,000 | 0% on that slice |
| Above AED 375,000 | 9% on excess |
| Qualifying small business | Relief may apply if turnover under AED 3 million and conditions met |

Deductible items often include property management fees, maintenance, financing costs where allowed, and depreciation per FTA guidance. Capital gains on disposals booked in the company follow the same return. Engage an FTA-registered tax advisor before you rely on a spreadsheet from your property manager.

## What documents should finance teams assemble?

Plan four to six weeks of prep before the portal deadline:

1. **Audited or statutory financial statements** (per entity size rules).
2. **Trial balance** tied to each property asset and loan.
3. **Lease register** with rent, free periods, and service charge recoveries.
4. **Fixed asset schedule** including acquisition dates and improvement capex.
5. **Intercompany agreements** if rent flows through group SPVs.
6. **Transfer pricing documentation** where related parties charge management fees.

Missing lease schedules is the most common delay Invest Gulf sees when owners first move Dubai rentals into an SPV. Align numbers with [rental income tax in the UAE](/guides/rental-income-tax-uae/) guidance for personal versus corporate treatment.

## What penalties apply if you miss the deadline?

Late registration, late filing, and late payment each carry FTA administrative penalties. Property companies that only recently migrated from zero corporate tax should confirm their **TRN** is active and that prior provisional returns, if any, were accepted.

Payment is generally due with filing unless FTA grants instalment arrangements. Larger groups should watch whether **advance tax** rules apply based on prior-year liability.

## How does corporate tax change property investment structures?

The September 30 deadline forces clarity on a question many buyers deferred: **Is the SPV still cheaper than personal ownership after 9% tax and compliance cost?**

| Strategy | Corporate tax angle |
| --- | --- |
| Personal freehold in Dubai | No corporate tax on rent; still no personal income tax |
| Single UAE SPV per property | Compliance cost fixed; tax on net profit |
| Offshore parent with UAE branch | Branch filing mandatory; treaty relief case-by-case |
| Family holding company | Estate planning benefit; still file if taxable |

Remote workers and founders splitting time between the UAE and home countries should cross-check personal exposure in [remote work Dubai tax implications](/guides/remote-work-dubai-tax-implications/) and [double tax treaties](/guides/double-tax-treaty-uae/).

## How does the UAE compare regionally on property-related corporate tax?

Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Kuwait use different combinations of zakat, corporate tax, and sector exemptions. The UAE's **9%** headline rate remains competitive for Gulf property platforms that serve international capital, but it ended the era of "zero everywhere" planning.

Treat regional comparisons as structural, not as a reason to delay FTA registration. Non-compliance blocks banking, visa sponsorship renewals tied to company activity, and future property transactions that require tax clearance certificates.

## What should owners do before the next financial year?

After the March 31, 2026 year closes, calendar these steps:

1. Map which entities actually earned UAE-sourced property income.
2. Separate **personal** deeds from **corporate** books before year-end adjustments.
3. Budget advisor fees and audit cost in the SPV P and L.
4. Model whether restructuring (merging SPVs, migrating to personal ownership) makes sense **before** the next year starts.

Rules evolve; verify current FTA publications and Ministerial decisions before you reorganise.
