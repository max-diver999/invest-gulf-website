The Federal Tax Authority (FTA) confirmed **September 30, 2026**, as the filing deadline for UAE corporate tax returns covering financial years that ended **March 31, 2026**. Property-holding companies, real estate SPVs, and development entities registered in the UAE must submit returns and settle liabilities under the **9%** federal regime that took effect from June 2023.

## Which property entities must file by September 30, 2026?

UAE tax-resident property companies with taxable profit above the AED 375,000 zero-rated band in the March 2025 to March 2026 year are required to file corporate returns by September 30, 2026. Onshore SPVs and mainland holding companies with rental income typically trigger filing, while individual landlords holding deeds personally remain outside corporate tax on personal rent.

| Entity profile | Typical filing trigger | Personal ownership contrast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onshore property SPV | Rental or sale gains in company books | Individual landlords: no corporate tax on personal rent |
| Mainland holding company | Portfolio of Dubai or Abu Dhabi units | Same assets held personally: different rules |
| Development LLC | Trading stock or development profits | Verify project-specific registrations |
| Management or brokerage LLC | Fee income plus property income | Mixed activities need split accounting |

Individual investors who hold deeds in their own names and report no UAE corporate entity generally **do not** file corporate tax on personal rental income. The split matters for anyone who bought through a [UAE company structure](/guides/buy-property-through-uae-company/). Full context sits in [Dubai property taxes explained](/guides/dubai-property-taxes-explained/).

## What tax rate applies to property company profits?

Corporate tax applies 9% on taxable profit above AED 375,000 after allowable deductions, while the first AED 375,000 of profit is taxed at 0%. A company with AED 500,000 taxable profit pays 9% only on AED 125,000, about AED 11,250 tax. Qualifying small businesses with turnover under AED 3 million may access relief if FTA conditions are met.

Our analysis models property SPV compliance as a fixed AED 5,000-15,000 yearly drag even when profit sits inside the zero-rated band.

| Profit band (illustrative) | Mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Up to AED 375,000 | 0% on that slice |
| Above AED 375,000 | 9% on excess |
| Qualifying small business | Relief may apply if turnover under AED 3 million and conditions met |

Deductible items often include property management fees, maintenance, financing costs where allowed, and depreciation per FTA guidance. Capital gains on disposals booked in the company follow the same return. Engage an FTA-registered tax advisor before you rely on a spreadsheet from your property manager.

## What documents should finance teams assemble?

Finance teams typically need 28 to 42 days before the September 30, 2026 portal deadline to assemble audited statements, lease registers, and fixed-asset schedules for each property SPV earning above AED 375,000 taxable profit. Missing rent rolls delay filing when owners migrate Dubai rentals earning AED 52,000 or more per unit into a company. Invest Gulf underwriting budgets advisor fees of AED 5,000-15,000 per entity in that prep window.

| Document pack | Typical prep time | Stat tie-in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audited financial statements | 14-21 days | Tied to 9% profit calculation |
| Lease register (Ejari rents) | 7-14 days | Matches bank credits |
| Fixed-asset schedule | 7 days | Acquisition cost in AED |

1. **Audited or statutory financial statements** (per entity size rules).
2. **Trial balance** tied to each property asset and loan.
3. **Lease register** with rent, free periods, and service charge recoveries.
4. **Fixed asset schedule** including acquisition dates and improvement capex.
5. **Intercompany agreements** if rent flows through group SPVs.
6. **Transfer pricing documentation** where related parties charge management fees.

## What penalties apply if you miss the deadline?

Late registration, late filing, and late payment each carry FTA administrative penalties often starting in the AED 1,000-10,000 range per violation, and property companies should confirm the TRN is active at least 30 days before September 30, 2026. Payment is generally due with filing unless FTA grants instalments. Prior-year liability above AED 375,000 can trigger advance tax expectations in year two.

Our research treats missed September 30, 2026 filing as a banking and clearance risk on later property disposals, not only a one-time fine.

- Confirm TRN status and portal access at least 30 days early
- Reconcile rental income to bank credits before advisor sign-off
- Budget advisor and audit fees inside the SPV P and L, not personally

## How does corporate tax change property investment structures?

The September 30, 2026 deadline forces a cost comparison: is a UAE SPV still cheaper than personal freehold after 9% tax plus compliance fees of several thousand AED per entity? Personal ownership still means no corporate tax on rent and no UAE personal income tax. Offshore parents with UAE branches still file on UAE-sourced property income.

| Strategy | Corporate tax angle |
| --- | --- |
| Personal freehold in Dubai | No corporate tax on rent; still no personal income tax |
| Single UAE SPV per property | Compliance cost fixed; tax on net profit |
| Offshore parent with UAE branch | Branch filing mandatory; treaty relief case-by-case |
| Family holding company | Estate planning benefit; still file if taxable |

Insider tip: If your SPV holds a single Dubai apartment with taxable profit near the AED 375,000 band, model advisor plus audit cost as a fixed drag before you assume the 0% slice makes the company structure free.

Non-compliance can block banking clearance on later disposals worth millions of AED.

| Jurisdiction | Illustrative corporate headline | Property SPV note |

## Underwriting snapshot

UAE property SPVs that booked rental income, capital gains, or development profit in the year ending March 31, 2026 must treat September 30, 2026 as a hard FTA portal deadline under the 9% federal corporate tax regime. Entities with taxable profit only inside the first AED 375,000 still need to confirm registration status, TRN activation, and whether a nil or low-profit return is still required under current FTA guidance. Foreign buyers who moved Dubai freehold units into a company for bank or estate reasons often underestimate prep time: lease registers, loan interest schedules, and related-party management fees take four to six weeks to assemble cleanly. Our underwriting treats personal ownership and corporate ownership as two different tax files, not one spreadsheet with a 9% toggle. Confirm current official rules before you reorganise titles mid-year.

Property companies comparing personal freehold to an onshore SPV should run a full-year model with AED 375,000 at 0%, 9% on the excess, plus fixed compliance cost that often lands in the low thousands of AED even when profit is modest. A portfolio earning AED 420,000 taxable profit pays 9% on AED 45,000, yet still needs statements, a lease register, and portal filing by September 30, 2026. Our research treats that compliance line as part of net yield, not a one-off admin chore. Foreign buyers who opened an SPV only for bank KYC should revisit whether personal title is cleaner for the next financial year starting April 1, 2026. Keep personal deeds and corporate books separated before year-end adjustments so you do not discover mixed ownership after the FTA clock starts.
