Dubai off-plan sales picked up steam in Q2 2026, with Oqood registrations, formal paperwork for off-plan purchases, jumping 28% quarter on quarter. The numbers show developers are still launching and buyers still want pre-construction exposure when payment plans and escrow rules stay clear.

## What drove the 28% rise in off-plan Oqood registrations?

Dubai Land Department data shows off-plan Oqood volume up **28% in Q2 2026 versus Q1 2026**. Oqood is the interim registration for an off-plan SPA: it links the buyer to the unit, triggers escrow discipline, and sets the base for the **4% DLD registration fee** on the contract value.

The jump fits the broader pattern where **off-plan has held a 60-70% share** of Dubai deal flow since 2025. Q2 did not reverse January 2026's strong quarterly start; it extended it with more SPAs hitting the register after sales events and post-handover payment milestones.

| Registration component | Typical buyer share | Typical seller share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DLD registration (4%) | 2% (negotiated) | 2% (negotiated) |
| Admin and trustee lines | ~AED 500 | ~AED 500 |
| Oqood certificate | Included in DLD process | Included in DLD process |

Before you wire a booking fee, confirm whether the SPA assigns the full **4%** to the buyer or splits it. That line item changes all-in cost by tens of thousands of dirhams on premium units.

## How do payment plans keep off-plan registrations active?

Payment plan structures continue attracting buyers to off-plan purchases. Many developers offer staged construction payments, with a large balance due only at completion. That approach reduces immediate capital requirements compared with ready property purchases.

For location choice, see [the best off-plan areas in Dubai for 2026](/guides/best-off-plan-areas-dubai-2026/) for infrastructure and handover risk by community.

International buyer participation remained robust, with Golden Visa-eligible properties (AED 2 million+) representing substantial portions of registered sales. The UAE's tax advantages and residency benefits continue attracting global investors seeking Gulf region exposure.

## Which developers and communities show up in the register?

Major master developers still dominate registered off-plan volume: **Emaar**, **Dubai Properties**, **Sobha**, and **Damac** appear across high-volume communities. Smaller launches in **MBR City**, **Dubai Hills**, and **JVC** add incremental registrations when payment plans are competitive.

Developer reputation influences registration activity. Established developers with consistent delivery records command premium pricing and faster sales absorption, while newer developers compete on value positioning and longer post-handover payment terms.

For yield and delivery track records, see [best Dubai developers for rental yield](/guides/best-dubai-developers-rental-yield/).

## What buyer protections sit behind each Oqood registration?

Off-plan buyers should treat Oqood as one step in a chain, not the finish line. Escrow account details, RERA project registration, and SPA refund clauses matter as much as the registration count.

Practical checks before SPA signature:

1. Escrow bank and account match RERA project registration.
2. Oqood or interim registration timeline is written in the SPA.
3. Service charge estimates for the finished product (where published).
4. Cooling-off rights under current RERA off-plan rules.

See [escrow and Oqood explained](/guides/escrow-oqood-dubai-explained/) and [Dubai cooling-off period for off-plan](/guides/dubai-cooling-off-period-off-plan/).

Infrastructure development, including new metro lines and Expo City legacy projects, creates appreciation potential for properties in previously underserved areas. Buyers registering off-plan purchases effectively position for infrastructure completion cycles, but only if handover dates and escrow funding stay on track.

## What does Q2 registration strength imply for H2 2026?

A **28%** quarterly rise in Oqood registrations signals sustained developer sales pipelines, not a one-month spike. If handovers stay on schedule and mortgage availability holds for ready stock, registered off-plan volume often stays elevated into the second half of the year.

Investors should still model **4% DLD**, trustee fees, and post-handover service charges before comparing off-plan headline prices with ready units. Registration volume tells you about **sales momentum**; net yield tells you whether the unit works after costs.

Mortgage registration on completed off-plan transfers adds separate DLD lines. See [DLD mortgage registration fees](/guides/dld-mortgage-registration-fees/) when you plan to finance at handover.
