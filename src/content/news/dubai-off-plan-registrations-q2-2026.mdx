## Understanding Oqood Registrations

Oqood certificates represent the legal documentation of off-plan property purchases in Dubai. When buyers and developers execute Sale Purchase Agreements (SPAs), these contracts must be registered with Dubai Land Department within specific timeframes, triggering the 4% registration fee.

This registration system provides crucial buyer protection by creating official records of purchase agreements and ensuring developer compliance with DLD regulations. The Oqood certificate serves as interim documentation until final title deed transfer at project completion. For detailed explanation of [escrow and Oqood protections](/guides/escrow-oqood-dubai-explained/), buyers should understand the full framework.

The 4% fee structure typically splits between buyer and seller as negotiated in purchase contracts, though market convention varies by developer and project positioning.

## Q2 2026 Performance Drivers

Several factors contributed to Q2's 28% registration increase. Major developers launched significant projects during the quarter, while established developments released additional phases to meet sustained demand.

Payment plan structures continue attracting buyers to off-plan purchases. Many developers offer 1% monthly payments during construction, with 50-70% of the purchase price due only at completion. This approach reduces immediate capital requirements compared to ready property purchases. ## Buyer Protection Framework

DLD's Oqood registration system provides multiple buyer safeguards beyond simple contract documentation. Registered developers must maintain escrow accounts for buyer payments, ensuring funds protection until project milestones.

The registration process also creates enforcement mechanisms if developers fail to meet delivery commitments. Buyers with registered Oqood certificates have formal recourse through DLD dispute resolution processes. This protection framework distinguishes Dubai's off-plan market from many international markets where pre-construction purchases carry higher completion risks.

## Market Confidence Indicators

The 28% quarterly increase reflects several market confidence factors. Dubai's strong delivery track record gives buyers comfort in pre-construction commitments, while the emirate's continued economic growth supports long-term property appreciation expectations.

Infrastructure development, including new metro lines and Expo City expansion, creates appreciation potential for properties in previously underserved areas. Buyers registering off-plan purchases effectively position for this infrastructure benefit realization.

Developer reputation also influences registration activity. Established developers with consistent delivery records command premium pricing and faster sales absorption, while newer developers compete on value positioning.
