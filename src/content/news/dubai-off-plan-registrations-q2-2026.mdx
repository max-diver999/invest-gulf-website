Dubai off-plan sales picked up steam in Q2 2026, with Oqood registrations, formal paperwork for off-plan purchases, jumping 28% quarter on quarter. The numbers show developers are still launching and buyers still want pre-construction exposure when payment plans and escrow rules stay clear.

## What drove the 28% rise in off-plan Oqood registrations?

Oqood registration is the Dubai Land Department interim record of an off-plan Sale Purchase Agreement, and Q2 2026 volume rose **28% versus Q1 2026**. The process links the buyer to the unit, reinforces escrow discipline, and sets the base for the **4% DLD registration fee** on contract value for foreign buyers and locals alike.

Dubai Land Department off-plan Oqood registrations climbed **28% in Q2 2026 versus Q1 2026**, extending the pattern where **off-plan has held a 60-70% share** of Dubai deal flow since 2025. Q2 did not reverse January 2026's strong quarterly start; it extended it as more SPAs hit the register after sales events and post-handover payment milestones. Buyers still accept construction risk when staged payment plans leave a large balance until completion, and developers keep launching master-community phases that feed the register. The **4% DLD fee** on SPA value remains the main government line item buyers must model before comparing headline off-plan prices with ready stock. Registration growth signals pipeline confidence; it does not replace escrow checks, RERA project status, or service-charge estimates for the finished product. Investors should treat the quarterly print as a liquidity and sales-velocity signal, then underwrite unit-level costs separately before wiring a booking fee.

| Registration component | Typical buyer share | Typical seller share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DLD registration (4%) | 2% (negotiated) | 2% (negotiated) |
| Admin and trustee lines | ~AED 500 | ~AED 500 |
| Oqood certificate | Included in DLD process | Included in DLD process |

Before you wire a booking fee, confirm whether the SPA assigns the full **4%** to the buyer or splits it. That line item changes all-in cost by tens of thousands of dirhams on premium units.

Insider tip: ask the sales agent for the exact SPA clause that allocates the **4% DLD** fee before you pay the booking deposit; a buyer-pays-all split can add six figures on AED 3M+ units.

## How do payment plans keep off-plan registrations active?

Payment plans are the main capital structure that keeps off-plan SPAs flowing into Oqood: many developers stage construction instalments and leave a large balance until completion, which typically requires less cash up front than a ready purchase. Our analysis ties that structure to the **28%** Q2 registration lift and the lasting **60-70%** off-plan share of Dubai deals.

For location choice, see [the best off-plan areas in Dubai for 2026](/guides/best-off-plan-areas-dubai-2026/) for infrastructure and handover risk by community.

International buyer participation stayed firm, with Golden Visa-eligible freehold at **AED 2 million+** still representing a meaningful slice of registered sales. UAE tax treatment for individuals and residency pathways continue to pull foreign buyers into Gulf off-plan tickets when escrow and handover dates look credible.

Payment-plan red flags before Oqood:

- Booking fee due before escrow account details are written
- Construction invoices without matching site progress photos
- Post-handover balance above **50%** with no delay clause clarity

## Which developers and communities show up in the register?

Major master developers still dominate registered off-plan volume: **Emaar**, **Dubai Properties**, **Sobha**, and **Damac** typically account for the densest Oqood footprints across high-volume communities. Smaller launches in **MBR City**, **Dubai Hills**, and **JVC** add incremental registrations when payment plans stretch **3-5 years** post-handover for the developer pipeline.

Developer reputation influences absorption speed. Established names with consistent handover records command faster sales and firmer pricing; newer developers compete on longer post-handover terms and entry pricing. Invest Gulf treats brand delivery history as a registration-quality filter, not a volume trophy. - Ask for three recent handovers from the same developer brand
- Model **4% DLD** plus trustee lines near **AED 500** before booking

## What buyer protections sit behind each Oqood registration?

Buyer protection behind Oqood means escrow, RERA project registration, and SPA refund clauses, not the registration stamp alone. The Oqood certificate proves the SPA hit DLD and that the **4%** fee path started; buyers still verify escrow bank match and cooling-off rights measured in days before the next instalment. Invest Gulf treats the checklist below as the minimum before SPA signature.

Practical checks before SPA signature:

1. Escrow bank and account match RERA project registration on Trakheesi.
2. Oqood or interim registration timeline is written in the SPA (often within 30 to 60 days of booking).
3. Service charge estimates for the finished product where published (model AED per sq ft, not marketing slides).
4. Cooling-off rights under current RERA off-plan rules, counted in days.

| Trustee / admin | Invoice lines at transfer | ~AED 500 per side |
| Escrow | Bank name matches RERA | Project-specific |
| Cooling-off | Written SPA window | Days, not months |
