# Oman launches investment residency for OMR 250K property

Oman opened a property-linked investment residency track under Royal Decree 32/2026, aimed at international buyers who commit capital inside approved Investment and Technology Center (ITC) zones. The headline threshold is **OMR 250,000** in qualifying property, maintained for as long as the visa remains active.

## What investment amount and property rules apply?

The program ties residency to **OMR 250,000** minimum property value inside ITC boundaries, not generic Muscat villas outside the zone map. Authorities expect the asset to stay registered in the investor's name (or approved structure) for the life of the permit. Secondary market res below the threshold or outside mapped zones typically breaks eligibility until replaced.

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum property | OMR 250,000 in approved ITC zones |
| Maintenance | Hold qualifying investment for visa period |
| Physical presence | At least 30 days per year in Oman |
| Initial visa term | 10 years, renewable subject to compliance |

Verify the current property register and zone list with Oman's investment authority before wiring funds; decree text can narrow eligible project types.

## Which ITC zones qualify in 2026?

Published coverage includes **Duqm Special Economic Zone**, **Salalah Free Zone**, **Sohar Port and Free Zone**, and **Knowledge Oasis Muscat**. Each zone has different stock: Duqm targets industrial and logistics-led growth, Salalah and Sohar port-linked free zones, Knowledge Oasis Muscat technology and office-led product. Title must sit inside the approved boundary on official maps, not merely marketed as "investment friendly."

## What residency benefits does the visa include?

The investor visa covers the main applicant and immediate family on standard dependency rules. Benefits described in official materials include long-stay residency, property ownership rights in designated areas, permissions to establish or partner in Omani businesses subject to sector rules, and a **potential** permanent residency pathway after **20 years** of continuous compliant investment. That long horizon matters for buyers comparing shorter GCC golden visa cycles.

## How does Oman compare with UAE and Saudi property residency?

Oman's OMR 250,000 entry sits above UAE's commonly cited **AED 2 million** Golden Visa property route in dirham terms but below Saudi Premium Residency property thresholds near **SAR 4 million**. Oman offers a **10-year** initial stamp versus the UAE's typical 5-year renewable golden visa rhythm, while the UAE still wins on liquidity, resale depth, and international banking rails.

| Program | Property hurdle (indicative) | Initial term |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oman ITC investor | OMR 250K | 10 years |
| UAE Golden Visa (property) | AED 2M (verify ICA) | 5 years renewable |
| Saudi Premium Residency | SAR 4M property track | Per current premium rules |

## What economic impact does Oman expect?

Government projections cited in launch materials point to roughly **5,000** residency applications within three years and about **OMR 1.25 billion** in inbound property-linked investment if uptake matches forecasts. Capital is meant to accelerate infrastructure inside ITC zones and support Omani employment targets. Treat projections as policy intent, not guaranteed market absorption.

## How does the application process usually run?

Expect a sequential file: property identification inside an ITC zone, purchase completion with clean title, immigration file linking the deed to the investor residency category, medical and security checks, and visa issuance. Timelines depend on zone developer readiness and whether the unit is off-plan versus completed. Use Omani legal counsel licensed for foreign investment files; do not rely on unlicensed cross-border agents reusing UAE Form B templates.

## What market response followed the decree?

Early 2026 commentary focused on whether Duqm and secondary cities can supply enough completed, deed-ready stock at the OMR 250K band without thin liquidity. Muscat prime remains psychologically central for buyers even when legal qualification sits in a free zone hours away. Monitor transaction volume in ITC registrars before assuming resale depth matches Dubai or Abu Dhabi.
