Oman launched its Investment Residency Program via Royal Decree 32/2026, targeting international property investors. The program needs OMR 250,000 minimum investment in designated Investment and Technology Centers (ITC zones).

<TldrBlock text={"Oman launched a new investor residency program requiring OMR 250,000 (USD 650,000) property investment in designated ITC zones. The 10-year renewable visa targets international investors seeking GCC region access with lower entry requirements than traditional Gulf programs."} />

## Investment Requirements and Zones

The program covers four primary Investment and Technology Centers: Duqm Special Economic Zone, Salalah Free Zone, Sohar Port and Free Zone, and Knowledge Oasis Muscat. Property investments must be within these designated boundaries to qualify for residency benefits.

The OMR 250,000 threshold translates to approximately USD 650,000 at current exchange rates, positioning Oman competitively within regional investment visa markets. Investors must maintain this investment level throughout their residency period to remain eligible for renewal.

| ITC Zone | Focus Sector | Investment Minimum | Annual Stay Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duqm SEZ | Industrial/Logistics | OMR 250,000 | 30 days |
| Salalah Free Zone | Trade/Tourism | OMR 250,000 | 30 days |
| Sohar Port & Free Zone | Manufacturing/Port | OMR 250,000 | 30 days |
| Knowledge Oasis Muscat | Technology/Services | OMR 250,000 | 30 days |

## Residency Benefits and Duration

The investment residency visa provides 10-year initial validity with renewable terms upon maintained investment compliance. Holders receive full property ownership rights within designated zones, business establishment permissions, and sponsorship rights for immediate family members including spouses and unmarried children under 25.

The program includes a potential pathway to permanent residency after 20 years of continuous investment and compliance. This long-term structure appeals to investors seeking stable GCC region access without the higher capital requirements of traditional Gulf investment programs. ## Competitive Regional Positioning

Oman's OMR 250,000 requirement positions the program below UAE's AED 2 million Golden Visa threshold while offering comparable regional benefits. The 10-year initial validity exceeds UAE's 5-year terms, though UAE provides broader geographic investment flexibility and established international business infrastructure.

Saudi Arabia's Premium Residency requires SAR 4 million (USD 1.07 million) property investment, making Oman's threshold significantly more accessible. Qatar's investor residency maintains higher capital requirements, creating a market opportunity for Oman to attract mid-tier international investors. ## Economic Impact and Development Goals

The program aligns with Oman Vision 2040's economic diversification objectives, targeting FDI attraction in non-oil sectors. ITC zones benefit from tax incentives, streamlined business registration, and infrastructure development priority, enhancing investment appeal beyond residency benefits.

Government estimates project 5,000 investment residency applications within the first three years, generating approximately OMR 1.25 billion in foreign direct investment. This capital influx supports infrastructure development in designated zones while creating employment opportunities for Omani nationals. Investors considering this program should understand [Oman ITC visa living requirements](/guides/oman-itc-visa-living/) and ongoing ownership obligations.

## Application Process and Timeline

Applications process through the Investment Promotion and Export Development Authority (Ithraa) with estimated 90-day approval timelines for complete submissions. Required documentation includes proof of funds, property purchase agreements within designated zones, clean criminal record certificates, and medical fitness reports.

The streamlined digital application system accepts submissions in Arabic and English, facilitating international investor participation. Legal due diligence services are available through approved service providers within each ITC zone to ensure compliance with local property ownership regulations. Prospective applicants should review [Dubai versus Muscat property investment comparison](/compare/dubai-vs-muscat-property-investment/) before initiating the application process.

## Market Response and Future Outlook

Regional property developers have announced new projects targeting investment residency participants, particularly in Duqm's mixed-use developments and Muscat's knowledge economy sectors. Early market response indicates strong interest from European and South Asian investors seeking GCC region access at accessible investment levels.

The program's success will largely depend on infrastructure development within ITC zones and the government's ability to maintain competitive advantages versus regional alternatives. Ongoing developments in visa-free travel agreements and business facilitation measures will enhance the program's long-term appeal for international investors.
