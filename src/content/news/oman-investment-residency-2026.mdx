# Oman launches investment residency for OMR 250K property

Oman opened a property-linked investment residency track under Royal Decree 32/2026, aimed at international buyers who commit capital inside approved Investment and Technology Center (ITC) zones. The headline threshold is **OMR 250,000** in qualifying property, maintained for as long as the visa remains active.

## What investment amount and property rules apply?

Oman investment residency under Royal Decree 32/2026 requires a minimum **OMR 250,000** (about **USD 650,000**) property purchase inside approved ITC zone boundaries for foreign buyers. The asset must stay registered for the full **10-year** initial visa term, and holders must spend at least **30 days** per year in Oman.

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum property | OMR 250,000 (USD 650,000) in approved ITC zones |
| Maintenance | Hold qualifying investment for visa period |
| Physical presence | At least 30 days per year in Oman |
| Initial visa term | 10 years, renewable subject to compliance |

**Threshold checklist (citable):** Oman's 2026 investor residency is a property-linked permit that starts at OMR 250,000, or roughly USD 650,000, inside designated Investment and Technology Center zones. The initial stamp runs 10 years and renews only while the qualifying asset remains held at or above the threshold. Physical presence of at least 30 days each year is required to keep residency active. Compared with UAE Golden Visa property routes commonly modelled at AED 2 million, Oman trades broader city choice for a longer first stamp and a stricter zone map. ## Which ITC zones qualify in 2026?

Qualifying ITC zones in 2026 are Duqm Special Economic Zone, Salalah Free Zone, Sohar Port and Free Zone, and Knowledge Oasis Muscat for foreign buyers seeking the investor route. Title in the area must sit inside official maps for the OMR 250,000 (USD 650,000) threshold to count toward the **10-year** visa.

- Duqm: industrial and logistics-led growth inventory
- Salalah Free Zone: port-linked free-zone product
- Sohar Port and Free Zone: industrial and trade-adjacent stock
- Knowledge Oasis Muscat: technology and office-led units

Our research flags zone-boundary deeds as the first diligence item, ahead of fit-out quality.

## What residency benefits does the visa include?

Oman investment residency benefits are a **10-year** initial stamp for the investor and immediate family, plus a potential permanent-residency pathway after **20 years** of continuous compliant investment. Official materials also allow designated-zone ownership and business permissions, while the **30-day** presence rule still applies to foreign buyers.

- Potential permanent residency after 20 years of compliance

## How does the application process usually run?

Application files typically require property inside an ITC zone, clean title, immigration linkage, medical and security checks, then the **10-year** stamp. Timelines often stretch **60 to 120 days** after registration for completed units bought by foreign buyers, and longer for off-plan stock.

1. Confirm ITC boundary and OMR 250,000 (USD 650,000) eligibility
2. Complete purchase and register title
3. File residency with deed evidence
4. Complete medical and security checks
5. Collect visa and diary the 30-day presence rule
