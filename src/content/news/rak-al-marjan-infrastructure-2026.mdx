Ras Al Khaimah completed major infrastructure upgrades on Al Marjan Island ahead of Wynn Resort's targeted Q3 2027 opening. The roughly **AED 800 million** program expanded transport, utilities, and digital connectivity as the island pivots toward premium tourism.

<TldrBlock text={"Ras Al Khaimah finished Al Marjan Island infrastructure upgrades including a new 4-lane bridge, expanded utilities for 15,000 residents, fiber rollout, and transport links ahead of Wynn Resort's Q3 2027 opening, supporting stronger island accessibility and residential demand."} />

## What infrastructure components were upgraded?

The program focused on mainland connectivity and utility headroom for resort-scale visitation.

| Component | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Bridge | New 4-lane link to RAK mainland |
| Utilities | Capacity scaled for ~15,000 residents |
| Waste | Upgraded collection and processing |
| Connectivity | Fiber optic backbone |
| Transport | Bus links and water taxi terminals toward Wynn |

Reliable power, water, and telecom reduce execution risk for residential handovers tied to the same island grid. Buyers in [Nikki Beach and peer projects](/projects/rak-marjan-beach-residences/) should confirm building-specific connection dates vs island-wide completion.

## When does Wynn Al Marjan open and what does it add?

Wynn Resort Al Marjan Island is scheduled for **Q3 2027**, marketed as a **$2 billion** integrated resort with about **1,000** hotel keys, gaming and entertainment venues, and multiple F&B concepts.

The resort anchors tourism marketing for RAK and catalyzes water taxi, aviation, and luxury retail investments around the island.

Employment estimates of **~4,000 roles** across operations support workforce housing and services demand. Context for residents: [RAK cost of living](/guides/rak-cost-of-living-detailed/) and [commute to Dubai](/guides/rak-commute-to-dubai/).

## How are property markets responding?

Developers and brokers cite **15 to 25%** value uplift potential from accessibility and Wynn-driven tourism, with some completed stock reporting **~12%** appreciation after infrastructure announcements. Treat marketing ranges as indicative; verify transacted prices on RAK registry data and live listings.

Infrastructure completion does not remove off-plan timing risk on individual towers. Pair island-wide news with project-specific escrow and handover letters.

## What transport and connectivity changed?

| Route | Indicative travel time |
| --- | --- |
| Dubai (Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road) | About 45 minutes |
| Abu Dhabi (E311) | About 90 minutes |
| RAK International Airport | About 15 minutes |

Enhanced bus services and planned water taxis aim to reduce car dependence for hospitality staff and visitors. Commute planning still matters for Dubai-employed owners; see [living on Al Marjan Island](/guides/living-al-marjan-island/).

## What commercial and retail phases follow?

Retail, F&B, and services clusters typically lag bridge and utility works but precede full resort stabilization. Mixed-use phases target tourism footfall and Wynn employee housing needs.

Investors weighing entry should read the broader [Al Marjan Island property investment](/areas/al-marjan-island-property-investment/) pillar alongside this infrastructure milestone.

## How does Al Marjan compare regionally?

Versus Dubai and Abu Dhabi, Al Marjan offers lower entry on beachfront product and greenfield upside, with trade-offs on school depth and corporate job density. RAK positions the island as a gaming and luxury tourism hub rather than a second Dubai CBD.

## What is the investment outlook after 2026 upgrades?

Near term: monitor Wynn construction photos, utility load tests, and RAK Tourism announcements. Medium term: residential supply waves may compete for the same tenant pool; underwrite net yield with RAK **~4%** transfer costs on resale (not Dubai DLD) and island service charges.

Future phases may add workforce housing and mid-rise product aimed at hospitality staff, diversifying away from pure luxury beachfront.

## Environmental and sustainability features

RAK authorities highlighted waste upgrades and utility efficiency as part of the island program. Buyers should still request building-level green certifications and district cooling charges where applicable.
