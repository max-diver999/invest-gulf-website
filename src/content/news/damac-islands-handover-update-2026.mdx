DAMAC Properties pushed back handover for its Islands project by 8 months, from Q4 2026 to Q2 2027. All 2,400 villas across six artificial islands in Dubailand face delays tied to infrastructure and marine construction. Full project context sits in our [DAMAC Islands overview](/projects/damac-islands/).

## What is the revised handover timeline?

DAMAC shifted practical completion and handover to Q2 2027, an eight-month slip from the prior Q4 2026 target. The revision applies across villa typologies on the six-island master plan, not a single phase.

| Project component | Original timeline | Revised timeline | Delay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infrastructure | Q2 2026 | Q4 2026 | 6 months |
| Villa construction | Q3 2026 | Q1 2027 | 6 months |
| Amenities completion | Q4 2026 | Q2 2027 | 8 months |
| Project handover | Q4 2026 | Q2 2027 | 8 months |

Buyers should re-read SPA milestone language and escrow statements to see how construction-linked payments align with the new schedule.

## What caused the delay?

DAMAC cited coordinated utilities, road networks, and marine facilities across man-made islands. Dubai Municipality approvals and specialized marine works added sequencing risk that a land-based villa community would not face.

The company describes Islands as an isolated schedule change within its portfolio. Cavalli Tower, DAMAC Bay, and AYKON City remain on previously communicated timelines unless DAMAC issues separate notices.

## What compensation is DAMAC offering?

Affected investors receive a 12-month payment plan extension, waiver of late fees on delayed instalments, and 2% annual compensation on amounts already paid during the extension window.

DAMAC also offers priority access to alternative inventory in completed towers and upgrade options within Islands where available. Compensation aligns with themes in [Dubai cooling-off period regulations](/guides/dubai-cooling-off-period-off-plan/) for off-plan buyers.

Document every developer letter in your file. Escrow account statements should match payment extensions before you accept revised milestones.

## Can buyers cancel and get refunds?

UAE off-plan rules preserve cooling-off and refund pathways when material delivery dates shift. DAMAC stated refund processing within 45 days for cancellation requests submitted before 31 July 2026 without penalty charges, subject to contract terms.

Written notice through investor relations with original purchase documents triggers the process. Compare exit math against holding through compensation in [off-plan vs ready property](/guides/off-plan-vs-ready-property-dubai/) before you decide.

## How does the delay affect investment returns?

An eight-month slip pushes first rental income and resale liquidity into 2027. The 2% annual compensation on paid balances partially offsets opportunity cost, and extended plans reduce near-term cash calls.

Luxury villa markets in Dubai still show strong tenant demand where product completes on time, but delayed handover removes that cash flow from models. Stress-test yield assumptions with vacancy and service charges using the [best Dubai developers rental yield guide](/guides/best-dubai-developers-rental-yield/).

## What alternatives exist for impatient capital?

Investors seeking near-term keys can explore completed DAMAC stock or other [best off-plan areas in Dubai](/guides/best-off-plan-areas-dubai-2026/) with verified handover dates. Islands buyers staying in the project should confirm escrow balance and remaining construction percentage with the developer.

Dubai off-plan volume remains healthy when developers disclose delays early and publish compensation. Islands is a reminder that marine master plans carry schedule risk beyond standard villa communities.
