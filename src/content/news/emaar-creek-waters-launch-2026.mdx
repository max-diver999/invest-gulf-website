## How does the payment plan work?

The Creek Waters payment plan typically requires 10% at booking, 40% during construction over about 30 months, and 50% on handover, and we flag selected post-handover options of up to 36 months as cash-flow tools that raise carrying cost if rents slip.

Payment plan checkpoints:
- 10% booking deposit against the SPA schedule
- 40% construction milestones over about 30 months
- 50% on handover, with selected 36-month post-handover options

Off-plan instalments still route through RERA escrow accounts named in the SPA. Verify the escrow IBAN on every transfer before you release a booking deposit. A 10% booking miss-match against the SPA schedule is enough reason to pause before wiring further construction milestones.
