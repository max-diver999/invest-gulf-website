**Quick answer:** Emaar Properties launched Creek Waters at Dubai Creek Harbour with 1-3 bedroom waterfront apartments starting from AED 1.6 million. The 42-story tower offers Creek Island views with Q4 2028 handover and Golden Visa-eligible units above AED 2 million.

Emaar Properties launched Creek Waters, a 42-story tower at Dubai Creek Harbour with 380 apartments. Prices run from AED 1.6 million for 1-bedroom units to AED 8.5 million for penthouses, all with Creek waterfront views. Full project details are available in our [Creek Waters overview](/projects/creek-waters-emaar/).

## Unit Mix and Pricing Structure

Creek Waters' apartment portfolio spans 1-bedroom (45%), 2-bedroom (40%), and 3-bedroom configurations (15%), optimized for both owner-occupiers and investment buyers. The 1-bedroom units start from AED 1.6 million with 750-850 sq ft layouts, while 2-bedroom apartments begin at AED 2.4 million spanning 1,100-1,350 sq ft.

Premium 3-bedroom units command AED 3.8-5.2 million for 1,650-2,100 sq ft layouts, with penthouse collections reaching AED 8.5 million for 2,800+ sq ft configurations. All units feature floor-to-ceiling windows, premium European kitchen fittings, and smart home integration systems. ## Dubai Creek Harbour Development Context

Creek Waters joins Emaar's expanding Dubai Creek Harbour master community, which includes the under-construction Dubai Creek Tower and established residential phases. The 6 square kilometer development positions as Dubai's "next downtown" with direct connectivity to existing business districts via metro and water transport.

Creek Island gives residents waterfront access plus easy reaches to business districts. Dubai International Airport sits 15 minutes away, while Downtown Dubai and DIFC take about 20 minutes by car. ## Payment Structure and Financing Options

Emaar structured Creek Waters with investor-friendly payment terms: 10% down payment at booking, 40% distributed across 30-month construction phases, and 50% due at Q4 2028 handover. Extended payment options include 36-month post-handover plans for qualified buyers, reducing immediate capital requirements.

Local bank financing partnerships enable mortgage pre-approvals for expatriate buyers, with loan-to-value ratios reaching 75% for established UAE residents. The developer financing complements traditional mortgage products, particularly benefiting international buyers establishing UAE banking relationships. Golden Visa-eligible units above AED 2 million receive priority processing through Emaar's dedicated investor services.

Smart building tech lets residents control access and climate through a mobile app, plus concierge services. ## Golden Visa Investment Eligibility

Units priced above AED 2 million qualify automatically for UAE Golden Visa property investment applications. This includes most 2-bedroom apartments and all 3-bedroom configurations, providing residency pathway benefits for qualifying buyers and their families.

Emaar's investor relations team facilitates Golden Visa application support, including document preparation and GDRFA coordination. The service streamlines the residence application process for international buyers while ensuring investment compliance with updated visa requirements.
