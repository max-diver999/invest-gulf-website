Emaar Properties launched Creek Waters, a 42-story tower at Dubai Creek Harbour with about 380 apartments. Prices run from AED 1.6 million for 1-bedroom units to AED 8.5 million for penthouses, all with Creek waterfront views. Full project details sit in our [Creek Waters overview](/projects/creek-waters-emaar/).

## What did Emaar announce at Creek Waters launch?

The launch adds a mid-rise luxury stack on Creek Island within the wider Dubai Creek Harbour master plan. Emaar positioned the tower for end-users and long-hold investors who want Creek views without Downtown pricing. Sales opened with a construction-linked payment plan and post-handover instalment options on select units.

| Launch fact | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Developer | Emaar Properties |
| Location | Creek Island, Dubai Creek Harbour |
| Height / units | 42 floors, ~380 apartments |
| Handover target | Q4 2028 |
| Entry price | From AED 1.6M (1-bed) |

## What is the unit mix and pricing?

1-bedroom layouts start at AED 1.6 million for roughly 750 to 850 sq ft. Two-bedroom units begin near AED 2.4 million; three-bedroom stock from AED 3.8 million up to about AED 5.2 million on larger layouts. Penthouse collections start around AED 6.8 million for 2,800+ sq ft configurations.

| 3-bedroom | AED 3.8M | 1,650-2,100 sq ft | Yes |

Floor-to-ceiling glazing, European kitchen packages, and smart-home pre-wiring are marketed across the stack. ## How does the payment plan work?

Emaar marketed a 10% booking deposit, 40% during construction over about 30 months, and 50% on handover. Qualified buyers can access extended post-handover plans up to 36 months on selected inventory, which lowers upfront capital but increases total carrying cost if rents slip.

Off-plan instalments still route through RERA escrow accounts named in the SPA. Verify the escrow IBAN on every transfer before you release a booking deposit.

## What amenities and Creek Harbour context apply?

Podium amenities include an infinity pool with Creek views, a fitness center, residents' lounge, children's play areas, and landscaped gardens with promenade access. Creek Harbour itself is a long-dated Emaar master community with marina, retail phases, and transport links toward Dubai International Airport (roughly 15 minutes off-peak by car) and Downtown (about 20 minutes).

Infrastructure elsewhere in the district (Creek Marina phases, transport hubs, sanctuary buffers) continues in parallel. Launch pricing should be read against completion risk and service-charge budgets, not launch brochure gross yield alone.

## What does the launch mean for investors?

Broker summaries on completed Creek Harbour stock often cite 6% to 7% net yields after service charges on mid units, but each tower differs. Golden Visa eligibility applies at AED 2M registered property value on qualifying 2-bed and 3-bed lines; verify current ICA rules before you size a visa strategy.

Market reception in the first launch window typically skews toward end-users and regional buyers already familiar with Emaar escrow mechanics. Assignment rules follow Emaar's standard off-plan policy once minimum paid thresholds and NOC fees clear.
