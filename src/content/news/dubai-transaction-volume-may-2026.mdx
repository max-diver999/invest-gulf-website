**Quick answer:** Dubai property transactions totalled AED 25.6 billion in May 2026, with off-plan developments capturing 64% of the volume. This performance maintains Dubai's strong momentum following January 2026's record quarterly start of AED 107.9 billion.

Dubai Land Department (DLD) released May 2026 transaction data showing continued strength in the emirate's property market. The AED 25.6 billion monthly volume represents a 12% increase from April's performance and reinforces the strong trajectory established in 2025's record 205,000 annual transactions.

## Off-Plan Dominance Continues

Off-plan properties commanded 64% of May's transaction volume, equivalent to approximately AED 16.4 billion. This maintains the 60-70% off-plan share that characterized Dubai's market throughout 2025, reflecting sustained developer confidence and buyer appetite for pre-construction investments.

The off-plan preference aligns with Dubai's development pipeline, where major master communities continue launching phases. Projects in Mohammed Bin Rashid City, Dubai Hills Estate, and Dubailand consistently attract significant investor interest during their sales launches. Investors considering these opportunities should understand the [cooling-off period regulations](/guides/dubai-cooling-off-period-off-plan/) that protect buyer interests.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Ready properties | 9.2 | 36% |
| **Total** | **25.6** | **100%** |

## Market Momentum Since January Record

May's performance builds on January 2026's record quarterly start of AED 107.9 billion, establishing Dubai as a sustained high-volume market. The emirate's transaction consistency reflects several structural factors: UAE Golden Visa accessibility for property investors, zero capital gains tax for individuals, and rental yields averaging 6-8% in established areas.

Developer sales strategies have adapted to this volume environment. Major developers now structure payment plans extending 3-5 years post-handover, reducing initial capital requirements and broadening buyer accessibility. This approach particularly benefits international investors managing currency exposures across multiple markets. For detailed analysis of [Dubai's best-performing developers](/guides/best-dubai-developers-rental-yield/), investors should consider track records and delivery timelines.

## International Investment Patterns

Transaction data indicates continued international diversification. Russian and Chinese buyers, who drove significant volumes during 2022-2024, now represent a more balanced proportion alongside increased participation from European and North American investors.

The AED 2 million Golden Visa threshold makes Dubai property an attractive residency-by-investment option compared to traditional European programs. Portugal's recent program modifications and increasing UK investor migration contribute to Dubai's international buyer diversification. Prospective buyers should understand [Dubai's property tax structure](/guides/dubai-property-taxes-explained/) before finalizing investment decisions.

## Developer Pipeline Impact

Major developers maintain strong sales momentum across their portfolios. Emaar's Downtown Dubai and Dubai Hills Estate continue generating consistent volumes, while newer master communities like Dubai Creek Harbour attract investors seeking long-term capital appreciation. For those evaluating location options, our guide to the [best areas to buy property in Dubai](/guides/best-areas-buy-property-dubai/) provides comprehensive area analysis.

The 64% off-plan share reflects buyer confidence in Dubai's delivery track record. Unlike markets where off-plan carries significant completion risk, Dubai's established developer ecosystem and DLD escrow requirements provide investor protection throughout construction periods. Investors should review the [best off-plan areas in Dubai for 2026](/guides/best-off-plan-areas-dubai-2026/) to identify optimal opportunities.

## Looking Forward

Market observers expect transaction volumes to remain robust through 2026's second half. Several factors support this outlook: continued Golden Visa demand, Dubai's status as a regional business hub, and upcoming infrastructure including new metro extensions and Expo City development phases.

The consistent 60-70% off-plan share suggests Dubai's development pipeline aligns well with investor preferences. As ready property inventory in prime areas remains limited, off-plan continues offering access to sought-after locations at pre-appreciation pricing.
