## Why do Al Ghadeer yields hold above the Abu Dhabi average?

Al Ghadeer gross yields typically hold near 8 to 8.5% while Abu Dhabi overall runs about 6 to 7%, because AED 600 to 900 per sq ft entry and 20-minute DIP commutes protect the rent-to-price ratio, and Invest Gulf underwrites the area against JVC compression.

Abu Dhabi's premium communities like Saadiyat Island and Al Maryah often generate only 5 to 6% on capital-appreciation plays. Al Ghadeer's outperformance comes from four structural dynamics:

1. Dubai-Abu Dhabi arbitrage tenants priced out of Dubai rents near DIP, Discovery Gardens, and Al Furjan
2. Service charges near AED 10 to 15 per sq ft versus JVC bands near AED 14 to 20
3. Contained master-plan supply versus JVC's ongoing unit additions
4. Lower per-sqft base that kept rent growth aligned with price growth through 2024 to 2026

## What is the community profile of Al Ghadeer?

Al Ghadeer is typically an Aldar-led master community of mid-rise apartments and townhouses with freehold rights for foreign nationals under Law No. 13 of 2019, and we treat Aldar's roughly 92% on-time delivery record as a construction-confidence signal.

Development character:

- Mid-rise blocks of 4 to 8 floors and townhouse clusters
- Walking paths, cycling tracks, parks, and play areas
- Retail podium with supermarket, cafes, and pharmacy
- Community school that reduces school-run friction for families
- Mosque and community centre

Unit mix:

| Type | Typical size |
|---|---|
| 1BR apartments | ~600-850 sq ft |
| 2BR apartments | ~1,000-1,400 sq ft |
| 3BR apartments | ~1,400-1,800 sq ft |
| Townhouses | ~1,500-2,200 sq ft |

Tenant pool mixes mid-income professionals crossing the Emirates border, families seeking space versus Dubai rents, and remote workers using community infrastructure. Indian, Pakistani, and Filipino expatriates anchor much of the rental base, similar to JVC and Discovery Gardens.

## What infrastructure and amenities does Al Ghadeer offer?

Infrastructure typically includes about 15 hectares of central park, a 5.2 km jogging track, Carrefour Market near 6,000 sq ft, a community school with 720 student capacity, and 24/7 gated security, and Invest Gulf links those amenities to tenant retention across 12 months of leases at 8 to 8.5% gross yields.

### Central park and recreation

- 5.2 km jogging track with rest stations
- Three children's playgrounds by age band
- Multi-sport courts and outdoor fitness stations
- BBQ pavilions and a fenced dog park

### Retail, school, and transport

- Carrefour Market, pharmacy, clinic, laundry, and family dining
- Community school KG to Grade 8 on British curriculum for about 720 students
- E11 access in about 3 minutes; public bus Route 090 about 45 minutes to Abu Dhabi centre
- Planned UAE Rail corridor studies referencing Al Ghadeer after 2030
- Service charges often near AED 10 to 15 per sq ft, below many JVC bands

## How do acquisition costs compare with Dubai?

Acquisition costs typically favour Al Ghadeer because Abu Dhabi's 2% DMT transfer fee is half Dubai's 4% DLD fee, saving about AED 16,000 on an AED 800,000 purchase versus JVC, and we count that saving as Year-1 net improvement.

| Cost item | Al Ghadeer (Abu Dhabi) | JVC (Dubai) |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase price | AED 800,000 | AED 800,000 |
| Transfer fee | AED 16,000 (2% DMT) | AED 32,000 (4% DLD) |
| Broker commission | AED 16,000-20,000 (2% + VAT) | AED 16,000-20,000 |
| Registration / admin | AED 1,000-3,000 | AED 4,000 + VAT |
| Legal review | AED 5,000-10,000 | AED 5,000-15,000 |
| **Total extra cost** | **~AED 38,000-49,000** | **~AED 57,000-71,000** |

## What risks and limitations should buyers accept?

Liquidity risk typically means Al Ghadeer sells slower than Dubai: average time-on-market runs 60 to 120 days versus 30 to 60 days for well-priced JVC stock, and we also flag car dependence and thinner mortgage depth.

Risk checklist:

- Smaller buyer pool than Dubai's 205,000+ transactions in 2024
- No metro; commute depends on car ownership
- Lower international brand recognition than Marina or Downtown
- Secondary mortgage and refinance flexibility thinner than Dubai

## Who should invest in Al Ghadeer?

Al Ghadeer typically suits yield-focused UAE-resident investors who want 8 to 8.5% gross, 2% DMT transfer costs, and Dubai-border commute tenants, and we steer pure appreciation or STR-first buyers toward other communities.

**Good fit:**

- Net-income investors over headline capital-growth plays
- UAE-resident buyers who already understand local ops
- Portfolio diversifiers adding Abu Dhabi to Dubai-heavy holdings
- Family end-users wanting community space at lower rents

**Less suitable:**

- Saadiyat-style appreciation hunters
- Remote first-time internationals needing fast resale
- Short-term rental specialists
- Buyers who must exit inside 60 days

## What due diligence should Al Ghadeer buyers complete?

Due diligence typically covers freehold confirmation on the title or SPA, DMT encumbrance search, 12 months of service-charge history, rental comps from active managers, and building occupancy evidence, and Invest Gulf refuses deals without SC actuals.

Checklist groups:

- Legal: freehold status under Law No. 13 of 2019, clear title, management agreement, completion certificates
- Financial: SC history near AED 10 to 15 per sq ft, void periods, mortgage pre-approval, 2% DMT and broker costs
- Physical: finishes, AC, parking, park and retail condition, gate and CCTV function
- Market: comps from the past 90 days, Al Reef and Gate competition, developer track record
