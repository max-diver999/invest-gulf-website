## What does The Valley cost and yield in 2026?

The Valley typically prices three-bedroom townhouses at AED 1.6M to 2.2M and four-bedroom villas at AED 2.2M to 2.8M, with gross yields of 5.0 to 6.5% as Ejari matures. Annual rent for foreign landlords often sits near AED 90,000 to 125,000 on 3BR stock.

The Valley by Emaar is a freehold family community where foreign buyers typically enter three-bedroom townhouses near AED 1.6M to 2.2M and four-bedroom villas near AED 2.2M to 2.8M. Indicative annual rents of AED 90,000 to 125,000 on townhouses and AED 115,000 to 155,000 on villas support gross yields of about 5.0 to 6.5% when Ejari comps exist, while net yields after community charges around AED 16 to 22 per sq ft plus void often land at 3.0 to 5.0%. Invest Gulf underwriting still treats year-one occupancy as thin versus Mudon, so models start with 6 to 8% vacancy before tightening. Ready Nara phases usually clear income tests faster than off-plan Rivana unless the discount exceeds roughly 12 to 20% after service-charge carry. Buyers should confirm DLD freehold title, phase service-charge filings, and Golden Visa registered value only on units that clear AED 2M.
