The Valley is Emaar's entry villa play on Nara, Alana, and Rivana streets: Emaar master-plan branding at roughly AED 1.6M to 2.2M for three-bedroom townhouses, with four-bedroom villas stretching toward AED 2.8M.

## What does The Valley cost and yield in 2026?

Three-bedroom townhouses at AED 1.6M to 2.2M often rent for AED 90,000 to 125,000 per year; four-bedroom villas at AED 2.2M to 2.8M can reach AED 115,000 to 155,000. Gross yields cluster at 5.0 to 6.5% as Ejari data matures; net yields after Emaar community charges (about AED 16 to 22 per sq ft) and void often land at 3.0 to 5.0%.

| Product | Entry (AED) | Indicative annual rent | Gross yield |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3BR townhouse | 1.6M to 2.2M | 90K to 125K | 5.0 to 6.5% |
| 4BR villa | 2.2M to 2.8M | 115K to 155K | 5.0 to 6.0% |
| Ready Nara (established) | mid 1.6M to 2.0M | use Mudon minus 5 to 8% | 5.5 to 6.5% |

## Which Valley phase should yield investors prefer?

Ready Nara phases with handover history beat off-plan Rivana for income-focused buyers unless the off-plan discount exceeds about 20% after you include service charge during construction and delayed Ejari. See [off-plan vs ready property in Dubai](/guides/off-plan-vs-ready-property-dubai/).

| Phase style | Income profile | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ready Nara / Alana | Faster Ejari, snagging known | Lower discount vs off-plan |
| Off-plan Rivana | Entry price upside | SC without rent, handover slip |
| Villa vs townhouse | Villas: higher ticket, lower occupancy | Townhouses: tighter yield, easier fill |

## How should you underwrite net yield with thin Ejari data?

Use 6 to 8% vacancy in year one, then tighten toward 5% only after you collect three building-specific leases. Stack Emaar community charges, garden or pool upkeep on villas, and 25 to 35% management if you are non-resident.

| Cost line | Conservative assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Community charge | AED 16 to 22/sq ft (confirm filing) |
| Vacancy year one | 6 to 8% |
| Management | 5 to 8% of rent if local PM |
| Net yield | 3 to 5% after all lines |

Rent setting for 3BR family stock often sits near AED 10,000 to 15,000 per month; cross-check [Dubai rent prices by area](/guides/dubai-rent-prices-by-area/) and discount when phase occupancy is under 70%.

## Why does the Emaar badge matter here?

Emaar freehold with DLD title deed is standard; the brand pulls family tenants who would not consider no-name fringe stock. That support shows up as tenant quality and eventual liquidity, not as apartment-style gross yields. Read [Dubai developers guide](/guides/dubai-developers-guide/) for how master-plan operators price margin into launch.

## What tenant and school realities shape void?

Premium schools on-plan still rely on bus routes, not Marina-style walkability. Family tenants ask about commute to DIFC and school timing before they sign. Budget AED 25K to 40K first-year landscaping and pool commissioning on villa stock; tenants expect move-in-ready outdoor space.

## What off-plan and payment-plan risks apply?

Off-plan discount must exceed the loaded cost of delay plus service charge without rent; a practical rule is at least 12% below a ready Mudon comparable. Review [off-plan payment plans Dubai](/guides/off-plan-payment-plans-dubai/) and read assignment clauses before flip plans.

## What should you diligence before you commit?

Long commute to DIFC and Marina, incomplete retail until the community mall delivers, and competition from Town Square and Dubai South on family rent remain core risks. Run [due diligence on Dubai property](/guides/due-diligence-dubai-property/) with snagging reports from owner groups in peer communities.

## Who should buy The Valley?

Patient family landlords with a 5+ year horizon who accept year-one yield compression for Emaar master-plan credibility. Not suited to 12-month traders or buyers who need deep Ejari on day one without a price discount.

## Does The Valley work for Golden Visa planning?

Freehold townhouses and villas from about AED 1.6M upward can support portfolio building, but confirm registered value and current Golden Visa rules on your exact unit (confirm current official rules). Larger four-bedroom villas are more likely to clear AED 2M registered value than entry townhouses.

## What hold period and resale liquidity should you expect?

The Valley rewards hold-through-maturity investors, not quick flips. Expect 5 to 8% longer days-on-market versus established Arabian Ranches stock until Ejari depth catches up. Underwrite one extra year of service charge and finance cost before first Ejari on off-plan purchases.

## Practical FAQ for Valley investors

**Ready vs off-plan:** Income investors should favour handed-over Nara unless off-plan math clears a 20%+ discount.

**Community retail:** Tenants may drive to Town Square until Valley retail phases fully open; factor that into rent on new phases.

**Handover:** DP-era phases improved post-2022, but snagging before first tenant remains normal.

Track **Dubai 2040** southwest corridor plans, but treat marketing timelines as optimistic by 2 to 4 years versus lived community maturity.

## What handover and snagging should you budget for?

Emaar handover quality on recent phases improved versus early DP-era fringe stock, but plan professional snagging before the first family tenant moves in. Warranty claims need documentation within developer windows (confirm current official rules). Budget one month of service charge plus finance carry if Ejari slips a quarter.

## How to underwrite The Valley against Mudon and Town Square

Price The Valley against ready Mudon and Town Square stock of the same bed count, not against Arabian Ranches brochure averages. Ask for recent Ejari comps in handed-over Valley phases and apply a vacancy buffer until retail and school bus routes mature. If the off-plan discount versus a ready Mudon comparable is under about 12%, income investors usually wait for a clearer handover window.

Model service charges at Emaar community bands already discussed in this page, plus one extra year of carry before first rent on newest phases. Confirm DLD transfer math and assignment clauses only for the unit you are buying. Treat Golden Visa eligibility as a separate ICA check on registered value (confirm current official rules).

For family end-users, drive the school run and Marina or DIFC commute at peak once before you sign. For landlords, prefer Nara or other handed-over phases unless the payment plan and discount clear a conservative stress test. Keep the [Dubai South property area page](/areas/dubai-south-property-investment/) and [off-plan payment plans Dubai](/guides/off-plan-payment-plans-dubai/) open while you compare southwest fringe options.

## Practical next steps before an offer

| Step | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Pull 3 recent Ejari comps in-community | Thin data means you need local proof, not marketing yields |
| Walk snagging on ready stock | Fringe villas still show finish variance at handover |
| Check community retail opening status | Tenant retention depends on daily amenities, not only parks |
| Confirm bus routes to target schools | Family rent softens if every school run is 40+ minutes |
| Read SPA payment and delay clauses | Off-plan upside dies if delay costs eat the discount |

If your thesis is Emaar brand at the lowest villa entry, The Valley can fit a 5+ year hold. If your thesis is fast liquidity or day-one deep Ejari, established Mudon or Villanova stock usually wins today. Request a shortlist that mixes one Valley unit with one ready peer so the price gap is explicit before deposit.

## One more Valley check before deposit

Ask the broker for the exact community service charge estimate on your phase and compare it with three ready Mudon listings of the same bed count. If Valley rent comps are still thin, underwrite at the low end of the yield band on this page and extend your hold assumption by one year.
