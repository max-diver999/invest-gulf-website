Town Square is Nshama's bet that Dubai's next million residents will live in well-planned suburbs, not just Marina towers. The master community along Al Qudra Road has delivered over 15,000 homes since 2016, with apartments, townhouses, and villas arranged around a 50,000 sq m central park, Vida Hotel, and a growing retail hub. For investors, the story is family demand, mid-market yields, and a price point that undercuts Arabian Ranches by 30-40%.

Unlike studio-heavy yield zones where tenants rotate every 12 months, Town Square attracts families who sign 18-36 month contracts and renew. That lower turnover directly improves net yield: fewer vacancy gaps, less wear, and more predictable cash flow.

Town Square works for yield investors because family households typically sign 18 to 36 month Ejari contracts, rents sit 25% to 35% below Arabian Ranches equivalents, and southern-corridor jobs near Expo City keep occupancy tighter than studio towers with 12 month churn in the area on Invest Gulf comps.

- Family tenant depth: parks, retail, and three-bedroom product match real household demand
- Lower turnover: each avoided vacancy month can save AED 4,000 to 8,000 in lost rent
- Infrastructure tailwind: Expo City and Dubai South employment within about 15 to 35 minutes
- Nshama credibility: Dubai Holding / Meraas backing and operational park, retail, and Vida Hotel

## What is on the market in Town Square?

Town Square Nshama offers the lowest family-product entry price in an established Dubai master community, with apartments from around AED 550,000 and three-bedroom townhouses from around AED 1.8 million at AED 1,050 to 1,250 per square foot. The community is a single-developer scheme, which makes the inventory unusually consistent.

| Product | Typical size | Entry price | Typical rent | Gross yield |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One-bedroom apartment | 650 to 780 sq ft | AED 550,000 | AED 42,000 to 52,000 | 7.0% to 8.0% |
| Two-bedroom apartment | 950 to 1,150 sq ft | AED 800,000 | AED 60,000 to 72,000 | 6.8% to 7.6% |
| Three-bedroom townhouse | 1,850 to 2,100 sq ft | AED 1,800,000 | AED 120,000 to 140,000 | 6.2% to 7.0% |
| Four-bedroom townhouse | 2,300 to 2,700 sq ft | AED 2,300,000 | AED 150,000 to 175,000 | 5.8% to 6.6% |

Single-developer supply cuts both ways here. Build quality and community management are consistent across phases, which reduces the building-selection risk that dominates a district like Jumeirah Village Circle. Against that, a private seller at resale competes directly with Nshama's next phase, which is new, priced to sell and comes with a payment plan the reseller cannot match.

## An insider tip on buying in a single-developer community

Insider tip: before agreeing a price in Town Square, ask what Nshama is currently launching and at what price per square foot, because that figure is the ceiling on any resale in the community until the phase sells through. A developer releasing a new phase at AED 1,080 per square foot with a post-handover plan puts a hard cap on a private seller asking AED 1,200 for equivalent completed stock, regardless of how well presented the unit is. The corollary is the buying opportunity: purchasing a completed unit in a mature phase while the developer is selling a distant future phase means competing against product that will not exist for three years, which is the most favourable position available in a master community.

## Who rents in Town Square, and how long do they stay?

Town Square draws value-led families and young professionals priced out of Dubai Hills or Arabian Ranches, and that tenant profile produces longer tenancies than the mid-market apartment belt.

- Tenancy length runs 2 to 3 years on townhouses against 14 to 20 months in an apartment-only district
- Vacancy assumption of 5% to 6% is realistic on townhouses, 6% to 7% on apartments
- Demand concentrates around the school year rather than spreading evenly, so a townhouse falling vacant in November lets more slowly than one falling vacant in June
- The community park, pool and retail are inside the master plan, which is a substantial part of what the tenant is paying for

The practical consequence is that retention beats rent maximisation here more clearly than almost anywhere in Dubai. A townhouse tenant on a three-year run saves two voids and two re-letting commissions against an apartment let over the same period, and that is worth more than the 0.8 percentage point yield gap between the two products.

## What should a buyer check in Town Square?

Five items, and two of them are specific to a community still building out.

- The current Nshama launch price per square foot, which caps resale value until the phase clears
- Which amenities in the master plan are delivered and which remain scheduled, because the delivered set is what a tenant is actually paying for
- The service charge, which runs lower than a tower at AED 3 to 6 per square foot on townhouses, plus any district cooling capacity charge billed separately
- Whether the unit carries an approved external modification, since a converted garage or an added terrace requires developer consent and surfaces at no-objection stage
- The distance from the unit to the community retail and park, because within a single master plan that walk decides the rent more than the internal specification does

Our underwriting treats a Town Square purchase as an income asset with a capped ceiling rather than a growth asset, because the developer controls both the supply and the price at which new supply arrives.

Then decide between the apartment at 7.0% to 8.0% gross and the townhouse at 6.2% to 7.0%, on tenancy length rather than on yield.
