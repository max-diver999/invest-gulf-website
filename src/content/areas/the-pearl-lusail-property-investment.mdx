## What is The Pearl 2026 investment snapshot?

The Pearl is Qatar's deepest foreign-freehold resale pool, with one-beds commonly around QAR 800,000 to QAR 1.2 million and mid-market gross yields near 5.5% to 7% after you verify MOJ registration on the exact building. Invest Gulf plans 3 to 5 year holds, not Dubai-speed flips.

Insider tip: Verify the specific tower on the Ministry of Justice foreign ownership register before any deposit, because island branding alone does not prove building-level freehold eligibility.

The Pearl-Qatar underwriting for foreign buyers should start from one-beds near QAR 800,000 to QAR 1.2 million and two-beds near QAR 1.1 million to QAR 1.8 million, with mid-market gross yields of 5.5% to 7% before service charges often QAR 12 to 18 per sq ft. ## What is the Pearl purchase process for foreign buyers?

Purchase process on The Pearl typically runs MOJ register check, independent legal review, SPA and deposit, Ministry of Justice transfer over about 4 to 8 weeks, then utilities and tenancy registration, with total acquisition costs often 2% to 4%. Invest Gulf refuses deposits before register confirmation.

1. MOJ foreign ownership register check (before SPA)
2. Independent Qatari legal review
3. SPA and deposit
4. Ministry of Justice transfer registration (4 to 8 weeks)
5. MOI residency application if that is the goal
6. Kahramaa utilities and tenancy registration

## What red flags should pause a Pearl purchase?

Red flags are deal-stoppers that typically include buildings missing from the MOJ foreign ownership register, studios below QAR 730,000 marketed for residency, gross 7% quoted as guaranteed net, and Dubai-speed resale assumptions of under 90 days. Invest Gulf walks away when register proof is delayed.

- Studio below residency threshold marketed as visa-ready
- Older Viva Bahriya stock without building inspection
- Off-plan phase without registration timeline
