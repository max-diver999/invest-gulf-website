Bluewaters Island is Dubai's answer to the question: what happens when you build a beachfront community with a fixed unit count and no expansion land? Meraas delivered approximately 700 apartments across low-rise buildings on a man-made island connected to JBR by bridge. Caesars Palace hotel, Caesars Resort, Bluewaters Walk retail, and the Ain Dubai wheel anchor the island's lifestyle ecosystem. For investors, the thesis is scarcity, not yield.

No new residential towers are planned on the island. Every apartment that exists today is the total supply. In a Dubai market that launches 50,000+ new units annually, that finiteness is unusual and supports capital preservation in ways that master-planned communities cannot replicate.

Bluewaters Island typically delivers 4.5% to 6.0% gross yield on long-term Ejari lets and about 3.5% to 4.8% net after service charges of AED 20 to 28 per sq ft, with one-bedroom entry from AED 1.8 million to AED 3.2 million in Q1 2026. Invest Gulf underwriting for the area starts from finite Meraas supply of roughly 700 apartments and zero Phase 2 residential expansion, not from Dubai-wide launch volume above 50,000 units a year. A worked one-bedroom near AED 2.4 million with AED 130,000 Ejari rent can print about 5.42% gross and 3.79% net after AED 20,400 service charges, management, and a 5% void. Rent premiums of 15% to 25% over equivalent JBR stock support income, while UK or European landlords facing 20% to 45% domestic rental tax still compare after-tax outcomes carefully.

Bluewaters STR typically needs a DET holiday-home permit at AED 1,520 per year, OA building permission, and professional management fees of 15% to 20% of revenue before net income is real. Our analysts model peak winter occupancy at 80% to 90% and summer at 55% to 65%, with nightly rates of AED 450 to 750 for a one-bedroom versus AED 350 to 550 on comparable JBR stock. Gross STR revenue on a well-managed one-bedroom can reach AED 150,000 to 190,000 annually, yet net often lands near AED 85,000 to 115,000 or 3.5% to 4.8% on a AED 2.4 million purchase after Tourism Dirham, 7% municipality fee, and turnover costs. Confirm Ain Dubai operating status since 2024 and Mollak service-charge history before treating tourist footfall as permanent.

**Insider tip:** Before paying a sea-view premium of 20% to 35%, pull closed Ejari comps for the same building tier; inner-island stock at a 5% to 15% discount can match net yield when service charges sit near AED 20 rather than AED 28 per sq ft.

## Who rents long-term and who books short stays?

Bluewaters long-let tenants typically include Caesars hospitality executives and Marina-corridor professionals on 12 to 18 month leases, while STR guests book 7 to 14 night European beach trips and Gulf weekend stays. We separate the two income models before SPA. Keep OA schedules in writing before funds leave the account.

- Long-let: 12-18 months average
- STR peak: 80-90% October to March
- STR summer: 55-65% occupancy

Service charges of AED 20 to 28 per sq ft reflect hotel-grade commons. See [How to Evaluate Dubai Developer](/guides/how-to-evaluate-dubai-developer/).

## How does Bluewaters compare with JBR for investors?

Bluewaters typically trades at a 30% to 50% price premium over JBR for newer 2018 to 2021 stock and island exclusivity, while JBR wins on entry price, yield percentage, and transaction volume. Invest Gulf routes trophy capital here and beach-income yield to JBR. Verify title pathway in writing before any reservation fee.

| Factor | Bluewaters | JBR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supply | ~700 units, fixed | Deep, six clusters |
| Gross yield | 4.5-6.0% | Slightly higher on non-premium |
| STR nightly | AED 450-750 peak 1BR | AED 350-550 peer |
| Liquidity | Trophy buyer pool | Higher volume |

## Which investor profiles fit Bluewaters in 2026?

Bluewaters typically fits trophy collectors, lifestyle relocators using Golden Visa pathways from AED 2 million registered value, and income-preservation landlords from high-tax home markets accepting 3.5% to 4.8% net. We steer yield maximisers to JVC or Discovery Gardens instead of the area. Model net yield after fees before treating brochure rent as fact.

1. Trophy collector: scarcity over yield
2. Lifestyle investor: personal use plus rent
3. Income preservation: after-tax rental focus
4. STR operator: only with OA and DET clearance

## What red flags should pause a Bluewaters purchase?

Pause a Bluewaters purchase when Mollak history is missing, OA bans STR that marketing still models, Ain Dubai footfall is assumed without a 2024-onward status check, or the seller prices a 20% to 35% sea-view premium without Ejari comps. Our underwriting treats each as a hard stop within a 14 day review.

- Missing service-charge statements
- STR modelled without OA permission
- Trophy liquidity ignored on exit timing
- DET rule changes not stress-tested
