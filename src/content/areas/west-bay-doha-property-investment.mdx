West Bay is Doha's central business district: ministries, embassies, QatarEnergy and major bank HQs, and luxury hotel towers. For investors it is a **corporate housing** play, not a yield-maximisation zone like The Pearl. Foreign freehold is **building-by-building** under Ministry of Justice designation, with far fewer qualifying units than Pearl or Lusail.

## Is West Bay a yield or stability investment?

West Bay is typically a stability investment with gross yields of 4.5% to 6% on MOJ-qualifying CBD towers, versus about 5.5% to 7% on Pearl stock, plus 85% to 95% occupancy on 18 to 24 month corporate leases. We underwrite 70% to 80% renewal rates tied to housing allowances.

## Who rents in West Bay towers?

West Bay tower tenants typically split across government and diplomatic demand near 35%, energy majors near 25%, financial services near 20%, and advisory or legal near 15%, with peak leasing from September to December. We consistently model quieter June to August months as void risk, not as permanent vacancy.

| Segment | Share (indicative) | Lease behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Government / diplomatic | ~35% | Long, allowance-backed |
| Energy majors | ~25% | Corporate leases |
| Financial services | ~20% | 18-24 months |
| Advisory / legal | ~15% | Project-linked |
| Hospitality management | ~5% | Shorter |

## How do foreigners verify MOJ freehold?

Foreign freehold verification in West Bay typically requires a Ministry of Justice building extract, unit-level eligibility, court transfer procedure, and annual registration maintenance before any reservation deposit. Invest Gulf never relies on agent zone maps because Qatar grants ownership in named buildings for foreign buyers planning 5 to 7 year holds at 4.5% to 6% gross.

1. Confirm building on MOJ freehold register
2. Confirm **unit-level** eligibility
3. Review transfer procedure through Qatar courts
4. Budget annual registration maintenance
5. Map property value to MOI residency rules if that is your goal

Examples often cited (verify status): select diplomatic-quarter towers, mixed-use complexes, branded hotel residences, Lagoon villa projects.

## What risks are specific to West Bay?

West Bay-specific risks typically include thin freehold inventory, energy-budget cuts to corporate housing, foreign-ownership policy shifts, office oversupply near towers, and yield compression from CBD premiums that can erase a 0.3% gross edge. Invest Gulf mitigates with landmark or fully occupied mixed-use towers and 5 to 7 year holds.

- Thin freehold inventory limits exit pool
- Economic diversification away from energy affects corporate housing budgets
- Policy shifts on foreign ownership
- Office oversupply can drag prestige of adjacent residential towers
- Yield compression from CBD premium pricing

## How does freehold verification differ from marketing?

Freehold verification typically differs from marketing because MOJ extracts must confirm unit eligibility before deposit, while leasehold or usufruct stock fails standard foreign-freehold resale and residency paths investors expect. We treat brochure "West Bay freehold" language as a lead, never as title proof.

Request the MOJ extract before reservation on tickets from QAR 900,000 upward. Building eligibility is the gate; zone storytelling is not across a 5 to 7 year hold.

- MOJ building extract before deposit
- Unit-level eligibility confirmed
- Residency value vs about QAR 730,000 checked

Empty commercial floors can signal weaker prestige for residential stacks even when CBD views photograph well.

## How do diplomatic leases differ from standard corporate tenancies?

Diplomatic leases typically differ because embassy and consulate contracts can include furniture, utilities, and posting-linked break clauses that make headline rent look lower than net landlord cash after concessions over 12 to 24 months. Our team underwrites net deposit to landlord, not brochure rent, on 4.5% to 6% gross models.

Yields may look lower on paper when utilities are bundled. Clarify break clauses before assuming multi-year stickiness.

## What insurance and OA reserves should landlords hold?

West Bay landlords should typically hold tower OA sinking funds for facade, lift, and chiller plant, plus landlord insurance of about QAR 1,500 to 3,000 per year on a QAR 1.5 million flat. We ask for the last two AGM minutes before purchase.

Contents and loss-of-rent cover sit outside OA budgets. Weak AGM transparency is a red flag even on landmark towers.

## How does West Bay perform through energy cycles?

West Bay typically held 85%+ occupancy through 2020 to 2022 energy stress because diplomatic and government demand partially offset corporate housing cuts, yet we still model 1 month of void in stress cases for the area. Allowances compress before they disappear when oil and gas budgets tighten on 18 to 24 month leases.

Diversify tenant sector exposure across government, energy, and financial services rather than relying on a single employer tower.

## What residency-motivated buyers should verify in West Bay?

Residency-motivated buyers should typically verify MOJ freehold and registered title value against the MOI threshold, commonly about QAR 730,000, because premium CBD views can inflate SPA price without raising the valuation MOI accepts. We match SPA price to valuation, not to brochure fit-out claims on 4.5% to 6% gross underwrites.

Confirm current official rules before assuming any tower clears residency across a 5 to 7 year hold. Building eligibility remains the first gate for foreign buyers, ahead of view premiums that do not raise registered value by 10% to 20%.

Metro connectivity typically favours West Bay towers within a 5 to 10 minute walk of Red Line or Lusail Tram stops for corporate tenants without company cars, while taxi-dependent CBD marketing often trades at a discount. We price true walk-to-work stock above postcard skyline views for foreign buyers at 4.5% to 6% gross.

Stations matter more than brochure radius maps across a 5 to 7 year hold. Test the walk at peak hours before SPA on QAR 900,000 to 2.2 million tickets.

- Prefer 5 to 10 minutes walk to metro
- Recheck 4.5% to 6% gross after SC
- Avoid taxi-only towers for corporate lets

## What property management fees apply in West Bay?

West Bay property management typically costs 5% to 8% of collected rent plus QAR 500 to 1,500 lease renewal fees, with corporate tenants sometimes requesting FATCA/CRS banking details and USD invoicing. We prefer PM firms with embassy client lists when renewal rates are the priority.

Clarify who pays VAT-equivalent charges on commercial-style leases. Generic Doha operators often underperform on diplomatic renewals.

## How should residency investors hold West Bay stock?

Residency investors should typically prioritise registered value and MOJ freehold over view or fit-out, while yield-first buyers should compare Pearl towers at 5.5% to 7% gross before paying West Bay CBD premiums. We see hybrid Lagoon-plus-Pearl strategies work, but they double service charge and PM fees of 5% to 8%.

If MOI permit is primary, do not overpay for Burj-style views that do not raise registered value. If yield is primary, West Bay needs a non-financial reason to win the sheet.

## What language and lease clauses trip foreign buyers?

Foreign buyers typically trip on English leases with Arabic governing-law references, ambiguous utilities clauses, missing parking bay numbers, and furniture inclusion gaps on contracts above about QAR 1 million. We have counsel review QAR 1 million+ purchases even when agents supply template SPAs on 12 to 24 month corporate forms.

Clarify break clauses, chiller charges, internet, and domestic helper room power before keys are exchanged. Ambiguous "all utilities included" language often excludes items tenants expect.

## How do you benchmark West Bay against The Pearl on the same call sheet?

West Bay versus Pearl benchmarking typically means one spreadsheet with purchase price, annual rent, service charge, management percent, void weeks, and MOJ freehold yes/no for matched bedroom counts. We expect West Bay to win on corporate lease length and Pearl to win on gross yield and freehold choice; if Pearl wins both, West Bay needs a CBD-walk or employer mandate reason, because a 0.3% gross edge can vanish after service-charge step-ups.
