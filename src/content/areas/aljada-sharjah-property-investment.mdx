## Is Aljada a strong Sharjah buy in 2026?

Aljada is Sharjah's flagship Arada masterplan: freehold for foreigners, 7% to 9% gross yields on apartments in 2026 listings, and prices often 30% to 40% below comparable Dubai masterplans. Trade-off is thinner resale liquidity and tenants who commute to Dubai, so Invest Gulf underwrites net yield and void for the area, not brochure gross only.

Insider tip: Prefer Naseej one-bedrooms with documented parking and E311 access over headline studio yields above 10% that lack two years of service-charge statements.

| Segment | Entry (planning) | Gross yield | Tenant profile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Studio | AED 400K to 550K | 8.5% to 9.5% | Single commuter |
| 1BR | AED 550K to 750K | 7.5% to 9% | Couple / young professional |
| 2BR / townhouse | AED 750K to 3M+ | 7% to 8% | Family / longer lease |
