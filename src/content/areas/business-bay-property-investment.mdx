Business Bay offers central Dubai addresses at a discount to Downtown, with one-bedrooms from around AED 1.15 million and studios from around AED 850,000 at AED 1,500 to 2,100 per square foot. ## An insider tip on the Business Bay tower spread

Insider tip: Business Bay has the widest building-quality dispersion of any central Dubai district, and two towers at the same AED 1,700 per square foot can differ by a full percentage point of net yield. The driver is the service charge, which ranges from AED 15 to AED 22 across the district, and on a 850 square foot one-bedroom that AED 5,950 gap is 7% of gross rent. The second variable is the owners association arrears position, which in a district with this many separate developers varies more than in a single-developer community. Ask for two years of approved budgets and the arrears rate before comparing two units, because a tower where a material share of owners are behind is a tower where the maintenance budget is not being spent and the reserve is not being built.

## Who rents in Business Bay, and how does that compare with Downtown?

Business Bay demand is corporate and young-executive led, drawn by proximity to Downtown and DIFC at a rent 20% to 30% below the Downtown equivalent. That produces a tenant pool that is large, price-aware and moderately mobile.

- Tenancy length runs 12 to 24 months, similar to Marina and shorter than a family district
- Re-letting takes three to six weeks for a well-priced unit, slower than Marina and faster than the outer belt
- A vacancy allowance of 6% is realistic, rising to 7% in towers set back from the canal
- Short-let is permitted in a portion of the towers and the demand is business-travel weighted, which flattens the seasonal trough compared with Marina or Palm

The comparison with Downtown is the whole point of the district. A tenant paying AED 90,000 in Business Bay would pay AED 115,000 to 120,000 for a comparable one-bedroom in Downtown, and the commute difference is a few minutes. For a landlord that translates into 1.7 percentage points more gross yield for an address that most tenants treat as functionally equivalent.

## What should a Business Bay buyer check?

Six items, and the first three account for most of the difference between a good and a poor purchase here.

- The service charge across two years of approved budgets, since the AED 15 to 22 spread is worth a full percentage point of net yield
- The owners association arrears position, which varies widely in a district with many separate developers
- Whether the unit is canal-facing, set back, or overlooking a plot that can still be built on
- The chiller arrangement and any separately billed capacity charge payable during a void
- Short-let permission, set by the association rather than by the permit
- The handover pipeline within the district, because Business Bay still has plots in development and a block of completions competes for the same tenant pool

Our underwriting treats the canal frontage as a durable premium and any other view in this district as a temporary one, because the district is not built out and the plots between towers are developable.

## Business Bay against Downtown and Dubai Marina

The three are the standard central shortlist and they are not substitutes.

A buyer optimising income within a central address should take Business Bay and choose the tower carefully. A buyer optimising resale speed should take Marina. A buyer optimising address should take Downtown and accept the yield.

## A worked Business Bay purchase

This worked example takes a one-bedroom in Business Bay at AED 1.15 million, 850 square feet, in a tower charging AED 18 per square foot, bought for cash and let on a twelve-month contract. The purpose is to show what the tower choice does to the return in a district with this much building dispersion.

Acquisition. The transfer fee at 4% is AED 46,000. Agency commission at 2% plus VAT is AED 24,150. The trustee fee is about AED 4,200 and the developer no-objection certificate around AED 2,500. Closing costs total roughly AED 76,850, a premium of 6.7%, taking the all-in figure to AED 1,226,850.

Income. A one-bedroom of that size in a mid-tier Business Bay tower lets in the AED 88,000 range, which is 7.7% gross on the purchase price and 7.2% on the all-in figure.

Deductions. The service charge at AED 18 per square foot is AED 15,300. Management at 6% of rent is AED 5,280. A vacancy allowance at 6% is AED 5,280. A maintenance provision at 0.5% of value is AED 5,750. Total deductions are AED 31,610.

Net rent is about AED 56,390, which is 4.9% on the purchase price and 4.6% on the all-in figure. Now run the same unit in a tower charging AED 22 rather than AED 18: the charge rises to AED 18,700 and net rent falls to AED 52,990, or 4.6% on the purchase price. That AED 3,400 difference is the whole argument for reading the approved budget before comparing two units on price.

For a buyer who wants a central address with a working yield, Business Bay is the most defensible answer in Dubai and the reasons are arithmetic rather than promotional. ## What are the risks specific to Business Bay?

Three, and the first is the one that separates a good purchase from a poor one in this district.

Tower dispersion. Business Bay has the widest building-quality range of any central Dubai district, spanning stock from 2010 to 2026 built by many separate developers. Service charges run AED 15 to 22, owners association competence varies, and two units at the same price per square foot can differ by a full percentage point of net yield. The information is in the approved budgets and the arrears rate, and it is available before an offer.

Remaining supply. Unlike Downtown, the district is not built out. Plots between existing towers are developable, which means any view other than the canal is temporary and a block of completions can compete for the same tenant pool. The pipeline is public through off-plan registration and worth checking for the eight quarters after a planned purchase.

Plant age at the older end. Towers handed over between 2010 and 2014 are entering their first major chiller, lift and pump replacement cycle. Whether that arrives as a routine expense or as a special levy depends on the reserve fund, which is the document to ask for before the price is agreed rather than after.

## Who should not buy in Business Bay?

Two buyer scenarios, and both are avoidable with one document.

The buyer who selects on price per square foot alone. At AED 1,500 to 2,100 the district looks uniform and it is not: the AED 15 to 22 service charge spread means two units at the same price can return 4.6% and 4.9% net on identical rent. Reading the approved budget before the offer resolves it in an afternoon.

The buyer paying a premium for a non-canal view. Business Bay still has developable plots between its towers, so any outlook that is not across the water can be built out. The canal frontage premium of 10% to 20% is durable because the waterway is permanent; a premium paid for a sightline across a vacant plot is not.
