## How does Seef compare to Juffair for yields in 2026?

Seef towers typically target finance and banking tenants with gross yields near 5.5% to 7% on newer stock, while Juffair offers lower entry tickets and gross yields near 6% to 7.5% on older mid-market blocks. Invest Gulf picks Seef for Golden Residence aggregation and tenant quality, and Juffair when foreign buyers need higher yield first on a tighter cash budget.

## Does Manama property count toward Golden Residence?

Golden Residence typically requires a qualifying investment on eligible assets, commonly cited near the BHD 200,000 planning band, and is not automatic on every Seef or Juffair purchase. Invest Gulf insists on NPRA and LMRA confirmation for the exact unit before SPA, because aggregation across two units may be required when single tickets sit below threshold for 12 months of planning.

Checklist before residency assumptions:
- NPRA asset letter on the exact unit
- LMRA freehold class confirmed
- Aggregation math if ticket is below planning band
