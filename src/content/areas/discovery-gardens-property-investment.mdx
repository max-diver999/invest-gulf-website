Discovery Gardens is one of Dubai's most misunderstood yield communities. Investors who dismiss it as "old stock near the airport" miss the arithmetic: gross rental returns of 7.5-8.8% on studios and one-bedrooms, service charges among the lowest in the city, and direct access to the Metro Green Line at Ibn Battuta, a commute link that JVC, Sports City, and Dubai South cannot match at the same price point.

Nakheel developed Discovery Gardens in themed garden clusters between 2005 and 2012. The community predates the yield-focused investor wave of 2020-2026, which means prices stayed anchored to mid-market fundamentals rather than off-plan speculation. That maturity is both the opportunity and the risk.

## What are Discovery Gardens price and yield numbers in 2026?

Discovery Gardens studios typically range from AED 380,000 to AED 580,000 and one-bedrooms from AED 550,000 to AED 820,000 in Q1 2026, with gross yields of 7.5-8.8% when rent aligns with price. Metro clusters often cost 10-15% more than outer zones, which Invest Gulf underwriting tracks for foreign buyers below AED 700,000.

## Why does Discovery Gardens yield well?

Discovery Gardens gross yields typically reach 7.5-8.8% because entry prices sit 35-50% below Dubai Marina per sq ft while mid-income rents stay competitive and service charges average AED 11-16 per sq ft yearly. Metro access at Ibn Battuta adds roughly AED 3,000-8,000 per year versus car-only peers near AED 550,000 tickets.

Invest Gulf analysis ranks that charge gap as worth 0.5-1.0 percentage point of net yield versus towers at AED 20-28 per sq ft.

| Yield driver | Discovery Gardens | Premium tower peer |
|---|---|---|
| Service charge | AED 11-16 psf | AED 20-28 psf |
| Metro rent premium | AED 3,000-8,000/year | Often none |
| Typical 1BR entry | AED 550,000-820,000 | AED 1.2M+ Marina |

Discovery Gardens houses healthcare workers, retail staff from Ibn Battuta Mall, hospitality professionals commuting to JBR and Marina, and airport-adjacent logistics workers who need apartments below AED 65,000 per year in rent.

## How does net yield math work on a sample unit?

Net yield on a worked sample means subtracting AED 9,360 service charges, 6% management, 7% vacancy, and maintenance from AED 52,000 Ejari rent on a AED 620,000 one-bedroom, printing about 8.4% gross and roughly 5.3% net. That cash return per dirham often beats Marina units above AED 1.2 million.

| Item | Annual figure |
|---|---|
| Gross rent (Ejari transacted, Q1 2026) | AED 52,000 |
| Gross yield | 8.39% |
| Service charges (AED 13 psf × 720 sq ft) | AED 9,360 |
| Property management (6% of rent) | AED 3,120 |
| Ejari registration + admin | AED 400 |
| Vacancy allowance (7%) | AED 3,640 |
| Maintenance provision | AED 2,500 |
| Total costs | AED 19,020 |
| Net income | **AED 32,980** |
| **Net yield** | **5.32%** |

That 5.32% net yield on a sub-AED 650,000 asset compares to roughly 4.0-4.8% net in Dubai Marina on a similar-sized unit costing AED 1.2M or more. For a deeper breakdown, see the [Gross vs Net Yield Dubai](/guides/gross-vs-net-yield-dubai/) guide.

Insider tip: Pull the JOPD service charge statement for the exact tower before you underwrite AED 11-16 per sq ft; a AED 5 psf gap on 720 sq ft is AED 3,600 a year, about 7% of a AED 52,000 rent.

## Who rents in Discovery Gardens?

Discovery Gardens tenants are typically healthcare, retail, hospitality, and logistics workers on AED 8,000-18,000 monthly salaries who need Metro-linked rent below about AED 65,000 per year. Average tenancy runs 12-24 months with frequent renewals. Long Ejari contracts drive yield, not holiday-home nights.

| Tenant segment | Typical monthly salary | Annual rent band |
|---|---|---|
| Retail and F&B (Ibn Battuta corridor) | AED 8,000-12,000 | AED 42,000-55,000 |
| Hospitality (Marina/JBR commute) | AED 10,000-16,000 | AED 48,000-62,000 |
| Healthcare (Jebel Ali corridor) | AED 12,000-18,000 | AED 52,000-65,000 |

Leasing data show lower turnover when rent stays inside the RERA index band for the building cluster.

- Retail and F&B workers at Ibn Battuta Mall and the surrounding retail corridor
- Hospitality staff from JBR, Marina, and Palm Jumeirah who want Metro access without Marina rents
- Healthcare workers at Mediclinic and NMC facilities in the wider Jebel Ali corridor
- Airport and logistics professionals working Dubai World Central and the Jebel Ali Free Zone
- Young professional couples where one or both commute via Metro to JLT or DIFC

Lower turnover directly improves net yield: fewer vacancy gaps, fewer DEWA re-connections, fewer agency renewal fees. Organic rent increases exist but are capped by the RERA Rental Index formula.

## Should you buy ready stock or off-plan near Discovery Gardens?

Ready stock in an established Discovery Gardens cluster usually wins for immediate 7.5-8.8% gross yield, while off-plan nearby only works if the discount versus ready exceeds about 15% and delivery is verified. Construction voids of 24-36 months erase cash flow at AED 52,000 rent targets. SPA charge estimates often understate costs by 20-30%.

| Path | Typical gross yield | Cash-flow start |
|---|---|---|
| Ready 1BR (AED 550K-820K) | 7.5-8.8% | Immediate Ejari rent |
| Off-plan 1BR (AED 500K-750K) | Projected only | 24-36 months void |

Our underwriting defaults to ready stock unless the off-plan discount clears 15% after full fee stack.

**Off-plan property** in the wider Jebel Ali corridor is sometimes priced at AED 500K-750K for 1BR units with payment plans. New supply near an established community can compress rents in the handover year. For pure yield investors, ready property is the cleaner choice. ## What service charges should you model by cluster?

Older Mediterranean clusters typically cost AED 10-13 per sq ft yearly, Metro-adjacent towers AED 12-15, and renovated premium clusters AED 14-16 before you trust a community average of AED 11-16. On 720 sq ft, a AED 5 psf gap equals AED 3,600 yearly, about 7% of AED 52,000 rent.

Our underwriting never uses community averages without a tower-specific JOPD line item for chiller and sinking fund.

| Cluster type | Service charge range | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Older Mediterranean clusters | AED 10-13 per sq ft | Basic lobby, parking, landscaping |
| Central clusters near Metro | AED 12-15 per sq ft | Pool, gym, chiller in some towers |
| Renovated premium clusters | AED 14-16 per sq ft | Upgraded amenities, newer fit-out |

Buildings with unpaid owner arrears defer maintenance, which shows up as tenant complaints, higher vacancy, and eventual special assessments.

## What red flags should pause a Discovery Gardens purchase?

Red flags that should pause a Discovery Gardens purchase include owners association arrears, undisclosed chiller charges, deferred lift or HVAC replacement on 2007-2012 stock, and yield sheets that ignore 7% vacancy. Most towers are entering MEP replacement cycles in some clusters. Always verify the cluster JOPD statement and sinking fund before SPA.

Invest Gulf due diligence treats any special assessment rumour above AED 50,000 per unit as a hard stop until the OA publishes a written plan.

For a full due diligence framework, see [Due Diligence Dubai Property](/guides/due-diligence-dubai-property/).

- Special assessment rumours without a written OA plan
- Holiday-home claims in buildings without DET permits

## How does Discovery Gardens compare to JVC and International City?

Area comparison typically shows Discovery Gardens leading on Metro access and net yield after AED 11-16 service charges, while JVC leads on newer stock and resale liquidity and International City trades cheaper with thinner exits. Gross yields near 7.5-8.8% can look similar while building age diverges. Match the community to a 5-10 year hold horizon.

| Community | Service charge band | Metro Green Line |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery Gardens | AED 11-16 psf | Ibn Battuta (walkable) |
| JVC | AED 12-18 psf | No direct Metro |
| International City | AED 8-12 psf | No direct Metro |

Our analysis prices Discovery Gardens Metro premium at AED 3,000-8,000 yearly rent versus JVC at similar gross percentages.

Discovery Gardens wins on yield, Metro access, and entry cost. It loses on building age, prestige address, STR potential, and capital appreciation versus waterfront communities. Against JVC: Metro and lower charges here; newer product and deeper liquidity there. Against International City: higher price but better maintenance and resale.

Discovery Gardens fits yield-first landlords with a 5-10 year hold who accept moderate capital appreciation on tickets from AED 380,000 studios upward, not STR operators or trophy-address buyers. Net yield of 5.6-6.9% competes in Dubai mid-market when the cluster is clean. Skip the area if you need beach branding above AED 900,000 master-community pricing.

| Buyer type | Fit score | Typical ticket |

| Buy-to-let yield landlord | Strong | AED 380K-820K |
| STR or holiday-home operator | Weak | N/A |

Shortlist Discovery Gardens when the brief caps purchase price below AED 700,000 and prioritises Ejari cash flow over waterfront prestige.
