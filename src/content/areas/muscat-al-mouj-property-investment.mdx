## What does the Al Mouj market look like in 2026?

Al Mouj is typically Oman's deepest foreign-freehold ITC market, with 10 years of marina operations and apartment gross near 4-6% against 1.5-2.5% net. Entry bands run OMR 80K-180K. Our underwriting plans 180-360 day resale windows, not Dubai flips.

Market checklist for the area:

## How is Al Mouj split by phase and pocket?

Al Mouj typically splits into Marina Promenade apartments, golf villas, retail walk-ups, lagoon stock, outer villas, and newer towers with thinner rental history. Foreign buyers should match pocket to tenant type before a marina label. Our methodology maps OA files, 4-6% gross comps, and three years of SC accounts before SPA.

| Pocket | Character | Typical unit | Rental maturity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marina Promenade | Waterfront towers, cafes, yacht views | 1-3BR apartments | High |
| Golf precinct | Fairway villas and townhouses | 3-5BR villas | High |
| Central retail spine | Carrefour, clinics, services | Walk-up from multiple phases | High |
| Lagoon / inner marina | Quieter water views | Premium 2-4BR | Medium-high |
| Outer villa phases | More space, more driving | 4-5BR+ | Medium |
| Newer tower launches | Modern MEP, thinner rental history | 1-3BR | Rising |

Marina Promenade is the default social address: school buses at lobbies, weekend marina walks, and the strongest short-term assignee demand. Golf precinct trades walkability for space and higher summer AC load.

## What yield should Gulf buyers underwrite?

Gross yield is typically only a screening number in the area: a marina two-bedroom near OMR 115K renting OMR 550-750/month can show 5-6% gross, yet charges, void, and 5-8% management often pull net to 2-3%. Our underwriting rebuilds any 5% net broker quote from OA invoices before SPA.

| Scenario | Gross (indicative) | Main drag | Net (planning) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marina 2BR ~OMR 115K | 5-6% | Service charge + void | ~2.5-3% |
| Golf villa ~OMR 280K | 4-4.5% | SC + larger AC bill | ~2-2.7% |
| Central 1BR ~OMR 85K | 5.5-6% | SC + turnover | ~3-3.5% |

Premium ITC net under **2%** is normal. If a broker quotes Dubai-style **5% net**, rebuild the model with OA invoices and a realistic void allowance.

## What infrastructure supports tenancy?

Al Mouj tenancy typically rests on marina, golf, retail, clinics, and PDO energy assignees rather than tourism alone. Weak spots include thin on-site schooling and summer AC costs that push turnover when rents overshoot by 5-10%. Our clients in the area model 12-month corporate leases before any STR assumption.

Tenancy checklist for foreign buyers:

- Prefer 12-month PDO and energy assignee leases over tourism hope
- Read OA short-stay rules before modelling STR income
- Budget school runs of 15-25 minutes toward Qurum or Madinat Qaboos

Tourism footfall helps F&B on the promenade but does not replace long-term corporate tenancy for most investors.

Red flags that should pause an Al Mouj purchase for foreign buyers typically include treating any ITC deed as automatic ROP residency, marketing 6% gross as net, new phases with no rental history, missing SC schedules, or Dubai-like liquidity promises inside 90 days. Our checklists also require title on the register plus independent Omani counsel.

Pause checklist for foreign buyers:

- No automatic ROP residency on any ITC deed alone
- No 6% gross sold as net without OA invoices
- No Dubai liquidity promises under 90 days without comps

Also confirm the unit sits inside the ITC freehold zone you expect before deposit.

Service charges typically decide net yield in the area more than a single vacant month: request OA audited accounts, reserve funds, and facade or MEP capex plans over 24 months. Escalation of 10-15% year-on-year destroys net fast. Our underwriting treats refused SC history as a walk-away.

| Last 3 years SC history | Spots step-ups and arrears |
| Reserve fund | Facade and pump replacements |

Legal steps on an Al Mouj ITC purchase typically require Omani counsel for SPA review, ITC freehold on the title register, and mapped lease registration if foreign buyers rent the unit out. UAE escrow language does not transfer. Our checklists keep developer counsel off the buyer's side and budget 2-4 weeks for bank and PRO clearance.

Legal checklist for ITC buyers:
