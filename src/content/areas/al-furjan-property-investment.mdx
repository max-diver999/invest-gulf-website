## Is Al Furjan a solid Nakheel buy in 2026?

Al Furjan is a Nakheel freehold community where one-bedroom apartments typically enter at AED 700,000 to 950,000 with 7.5 to 8.5% gross yields, and three-bedroom townhouses near AED 1.8 million to 2.5 million with 6.0 to 7.5% gross before SPA deposit.

| Product | Typical entry (2026) | Gross yield band |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-bedroom apartment | AED 700K-950K | 7.5-8.5% |
| Two-bedroom apartment | AED 1.0M-1.4M | 7.0-8.0% |
| Three-bedroom townhouse | AED 1.8M-2.5M | 6.0-7.5% |

## How does metro access change rents and resale?

Metro adjacency at Al Furjan typically shortens Marina and Media City commutes to about 15 to 25 minutes and supports a 5 to 8% rent premium versus pre-metro west Dubai asks, yet walking distance to Ibn Battuta or Route 2020 stations still decides resale liquidity.

| Effect | Investor impact |
|---|---|
| Commute to MOE / Marina | 15-25 min |
| Tenant rent tolerance | +5-8% vs pre-metro |
| Resale narrative | Infrastructure story |

Do not overpay solely on metro branding. Invest Gulf checklists measure station walk time from the exact cluster, not the community name on the brochure.

## How do Nakheel phases differ for buyers?

Nakheel phases in Al Furjan typically split western clusters with mature retail and eastern phases still adding amenities, so Ejari-backed rents can diverge by 10 to 15% between buildings even at similar list prices.

| Phase pattern | Retail maturity | Rent verification |
|---|---|---|
| Western clusters | Mature shops | Stronger Ejari depth |
| Eastern phases | Still adding | Amenity timeline risk |

Western phases mix apartments and townhouses with established shops; eastern phases need amenity timeline checks. Pull cluster rents via [Dubai rent prices by area](/guides/dubai-rent-prices-by-area/) before comparing list prices. Our research rejects phase blends that hide unfinished retail.

## Who rents in Al Furjan?

Al Furjan tenants typically include Jebel Ali Free Zone and Dubai South professionals on 12 to 18 month apartment leases, plus families seeking gardens below Arabian Ranches tickets on 24 to 36 month townhouse Ejari.

| Tenant type | Typical lease | Product |
|---|---|---|
| JAFZ / Dubai South pros | 12-18 months | 1-2BR apartments |
| Families with gardens | 24-36 months | Townhouses |

Outstanding-tier schools are usually a bus ride away, which shapes marketing around parking, layout, and commute rather than walk-to-campus prestige. Invest Gulf buyer scenarios favour clusters with allocated parking when underwriting commuter demand.

## What should you verify on Nakheel off-plan launches?

Nakheel off-plan payment plans at Al Furjan typically run 30/70 or 40/60 construction-linked, so buyers should verify escrow registration, refuse personal-account wires, and model handover delay up to 12 months before underwriting day-one rent.

- Verify escrow on the regulator portal before any transfer
- Model up to 12 months handover delay in the rent start date
- Refuse wires to personal accounts under any payment plan label

Off-plan suits investors who can absorb delay risk; ready stock suits cash-flow buyers who need Ejari within weeks. Our checklists treat missing escrow proof as a hard stop.

## What are the main Al Furjan investment risks?

Al Furjan investment risks typically include JVC competition on apartment yield, construction noise in expanding phases, Nakheel service charge resets near AED 14 to 20 per sq ft, and incomplete retail until eastern phases mature.

- JVC yield competition on comparable one-bedroom stock
- Construction noise while eastern phases complete
- Service charge resets near AED 14 to 20 per sq ft
- Incomplete retail until amenity timelines catch up

Red flag checklist: missing Mollak filings, unclear parking for commuter tenants, and gross yield marketed above 10% without verified rent and cost stack. Our underwriting walks away when escrow or developer NOC stays undocumented past the diligence window.

## What financing and service charges should you model?

Al Furjan financing in 2026 typically quotes resident mortgages near 4.5 to 6.5% variable with terms up to 25 years, while non-resident LTV often lands near 65 to 70%, and apartment service charges run about AED 14 to 20 per sq ft annually.

| Charge band | Typical AED per sq ft |
|---|---|
| Apartments | 14-20 |
| Townhouses | 16-22 |
| Villas | 18-25 |

Cash purchases protect net yield from interest; financed buys need stress tests of a +2% rate move against Ejari rent. Our checklists rebuild net after charges, management, and void before SPA.

## How should you stress-test Al Furjan numbers before offer?

Stress-testing Al Furjan typically means rebuilding net yield on Mollak service charges by phase, verifying Ejari on the exact building, and comparing ready stock against [Mudon](/areas/mudon-property-investment/) and [JVC](/uae/dubai/jvc/) before paying a metro premium.

**Off-plan:** Verify escrow on the regulator portal and model handover delay up to 12 months.

**Ready resale:** Stack agency commission, DLD transfer, and trustee fees on top of price; obtain developer NOC if a mortgage is outstanding.

**Buy-to-let:** Underwrite with real service charge filings, not brochure estimates, and use conservative void assumptions for apartment turnover.

**Insider tip:** Measure walking minutes from the unit door to Ibn Battuta or Route 2020, not community marketing, and demand two years of Mollak filings before you accept any 8%+ gross claim on Al Furjan apartments.
