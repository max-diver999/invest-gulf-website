Aldar Properties built it as a family-oriented alternative to dense tower living on Al Reem Island: low-rise gardens, marina access, beach clubs, and integrated retail without the CBD commute pressure of Al Maryah.

Investors buy Al Raha for three reasons: waterfront positioning at a discount to Saadiyat, stable family tenancy anchored in government and aviation employment, and Aldar delivery credibility on an ADX-listed balance sheet.
