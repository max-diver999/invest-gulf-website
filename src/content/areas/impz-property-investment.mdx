International Media Production Zone, IMPZ, is Dubai's quiet yield corridor between JLT and Dubai Sports City. Most investors who focus on Marina, Downtown, or branded master communities never visit Production City. That oversight creates opportunity: gross rental returns of 7.6-9.1% on studios and one-bedrooms, entry prices from AED 400,000, and a tenant base anchored to JLT and Media City employment within a 10-15 minute drive.

The zone was originally conceived as a media production hub. Residential towers followed, and today IMPZ functions as a mid-market apartment community with a mix of end-user families and buy-to-let investors. The media-industry branding is largely historical, the real investment case is location, yield, and price.

For comparable mid-market yield communities, see [JVC Property Investment](/areas/jvc-property-investment/) and [Discovery Gardens Property Investment](/areas/discovery-gardens-property-investment/).**

IMPZ studios and one-bedrooms in Dubai Production City typically deliver 7.6-9.1% gross yield on Q1 2026 Ejari rents, with net yield landing near 5.5-7.0% after service charges, management, vacancy, and maintenance. Entry prices start around AED 400,000 for studios and AED 520,000-780,000 for one-beds, which is why foreign buyers screen the area as a cash-flow corridor rather than a Marina lifestyle ticket. Service charges average AED 14-18 per sq ft, higher than Discovery Gardens in some towers but still below Marina bands of AED 20-28. Invest Gulf underwriting prefers ready towers with two-plus years of operating history unless an off-plan discount exceeds 12% versus comps. Commute times of 8-15 minutes to JLT and Media City support tenant demand from workers earning AED 10,000-20,000 monthly who need rents below AED 70,000 per year.

A worked IMPZ one-bedroom at AED 580,000 with AED 50,500 gross rent prints about 8.71% gross before costs. After AED 10,500 service charges on 700 sq ft at AED 15 psf, 6% management, 7% vacancy, and maintenance, net income near AED 31,035 implies roughly 5.35% net for foreign buyers who model the full stack. That cash-on-cash profile often beats premium-address communities once mortgage rates of 4.5-5.5% are compared to net yield. Invest Gulf still flags Empower district cooling of AED 800-1,500 per month as a tenant all-in cost that can force rent discounts if ignored. Resale liquidity is thinner than JVC, so hold periods of 5+ years fit the thesis better than flip timelines.

Insider tip: Pull three years of actual JOPD service-charge statements before you trust an SPA estimate, because IMPZ towers routinely bill 20-30% above brochure service-charge lines once Empower and common-area reserves are live.

## What rental yields does IMPZ deliver in 2026?

IMPZ rental yields typically land at 7.6-9.1% gross on studios and one-beds in Q1 2026 Ejari data, with net yields near 5.5-7.0% after the full cost stack for foreign buyers. The area is a long-let market, not an STR beach play, so underwrite 12-month Ejari contracts rather than Marina nightly rates.

Invest Gulf screens IMPZ as a yield community first and only then checks tower-level service charges.

The tenant base is driven by proximity to JLT, Dubai Media City, and Internet City, three of Dubai's largest employment clusters for media, technology, and professional services. Workers on AED 10,000-20,000 monthly salaries need apartments below AED 70,000 per year. IMPZ delivers that price point with a commute under 15 minutes to JLT, versus 25-35 minutes from JVC or Discovery Gardens.

## How do IMPZ prices compare to JLT and Marina?

IMPZ entry prices typically sit 40-55% below Dubai Marina and 30-45% below JLT for comparable one-beds. A unit at AED 600,000 renting for AED 50,000-55,000 often beats JLT stock at AED 900,000-1.1M with AED 65,000-75,000 rent on yield for foreign buyers.

Invest Gulf price comps always pair Ejari rents with asking prices in the same tower, not community averages alone.

- Studio ask bands: AED 400,000-620,000
- One-bed ask bands: AED 520,000-780,000
- Yield edge appears when rent discount is smaller than price discount

## What service charges and net yield should you model?

IMPZ service charges typically average AED 14-18 per sq ft, below Marina bands of AED 20-28 and above many Discovery Gardens buildings at AED 11-16. A worked AED 580,000 one-bed with AED 50,500 gross rent can finish near 5.35% net for foreign buyers who load management, vacancy, and maintenance.

Foreign buyers should assume car-owning professionals earning AED 10,000-20,000 monthly, because no Metro station serves the area directly.

## Is off-plan or ready stock better in Production City?

Ready IMPZ stock typically suits yield-first foreign buyers because rent starts immediately, while off-plan 1BRs at AED 450,000-700,000 with 40-60% plans over 2-3 years carry 18-30 months without income. Off-plan clears only when the discount exceeds 12% versus ready comps and on-time delivery is 85%+.

Invest Gulf off-plan screens refuse SPA service-charge estimates without a ready-tower JOPD cross-check.

Considerations:
- No rental income during construction (typically 18-30 months)
- SPA service charge estimates often understate actual costs by 20-30%
- Handover-year listing pressure when multiple investors list simultaneously
- Developer delivery track record matters, verify via DLD Trakheesi and on-time delivery history

For off-plan mechanics, see [Off-Plan Property Dubai Guide](/guides/off-plan-property-dubai-guide/) and [How to Evaluate Dubai Developer](/guides/how-to-evaluate-dubai-developer/).

Empower district cooling adds AED 800-1,500 per month to tenant utility bills. That affects rent competitiveness, tenants compare all-in monthly cost, not just Ejari rent. Model the total occupancy cost when setting rent expectations.

## Who rents in IMPZ and how does that affect liquidity?

IMPZ tenant demand typically concentrates in media, technology, and professional services roles paying AED 10,000-20,000 monthly, with retention often spanning 15-36 months by sector. Foreign buyers should underwrite long-let stability rather than tourist STR, and accept thinner resale liquidity than JVC on 5-year holds.

| Media/Creative | 25% | 25-30% of salary | 18-24 months |

| Banking/Finance | 15% | 20-25% of salary | 20-30 months |
| Healthcare | 12% | 22-27% of salary | 24-36 months |

Invest Gulf liquidity notes: IMPZ wins on yield and JLT proximity at mid-market prices, and loses on Metro access and capital appreciation versus waterfront stock.

Nationality preferences and requirements:
IMPZ tenants typically prefer:
- Western expats: Ground floor or low-floor units, premium finishes, gym access
- Indian professionals: Mid-floor units, value for money, parking included
- Filipino families: Family-friendly buildings, school bus access, community feel
- Arab nationals: Cultural amenities, mosque proximity, halal dining options nearby

Non-resident buyers can access 75% LTV on ready IMPZ stock from select banks. Factor mortgage pre-approval before offer, some towers have developer restrictions on investor concentration that affect NOC timing. ## What checklist should run before you sign?

Pre-SPA diligence in IMPZ typically starts with budgeting 4% DLD transfer on ready purchases, plus agent commission, trustee fees, and 0.25% mortgage registration if you finance. Those acquisition lines sit outside the net yield spreadsheet but decide whether leveraged stock cash-flows from month one for foreign buyers in the area.

Invest Gulf checklists refuse SPA estimates as the sole service-charge source.

1. **Request three years of JOPD service charge statements**: not the SPA estimate.
2. **Pull Ejari transacted rents** for the same tower on Dubai REST: compare floor and view premiums.
3. **Count active listings** in the building on Property Finder: over 15 simultaneous listings signals investor-heavy stock.
4. **Confirm cooling type**: Empower vs individual AC affects tenant utility bills and rent competitiveness.
5. **Verify parking allocation**: some older towers sell without dedicated bays; this caps rent by 8-12%.
6. **Check handover year**: towers delivered 2015-2017 may need facade or lift maintenance reserves in JOPD budgets.
7. **Walk the lobby and common areas**: tenant quality correlates with building management standards.
