Tilal Al Ghaf is a Majid Al Futtaim master-planned community of about 3 million sqft in the Dubai South corridor, structured around a central lagoon. Apartments, townhouses, and villas sit beside retail still completing through 2026-2027. Our research treats the area as family freehold stock with Al Maktoum Airport drives often at 15-20 minutes.

Tilal Al Ghaf property investment for foreign buyers typically means buying freehold apartments from about AED 850K, townhouses from AED 2.2M, or villas from AED 3.5M inside a Majid Al Futtaim lagoon community of roughly 3 million sqft.
