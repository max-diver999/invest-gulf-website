Red flags that should pause an Al Barari purchase typically include verbal service-charge schedules, botanical maintenance excluded from yield decks, and missing DLD Unit Profiles for freehold status. Gross yield marketing without a net model after **AED 8 to 12/sq ft** charges and **6%** management is another hard stop. Resale promises under **6 months** are usually fantasy.
