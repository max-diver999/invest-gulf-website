# Dubai Production City (IMPZ) Property Investment 2026

Dubai Production City, still called IMPZ on older title deeds, is where Dubai parks media warehouses and stacks residential towers beside them. Investors buy yield on studios and one-bedrooms that rent to production staff and west-Dubai commuters who priced out of JVC, not marina lifestyle.

## What is Dubai Production City for investors in 2026?

IMPZ sits beside the media and logistics free zone, so tenant demand tracks production hiring more than tourism. Gross yields on compact units land in the top quartile for Dubai apartments because ticket sizes stay low while Ejari rents hold for furnished studios. Capital growth is slower than family districts, so underwrite hold period and exit liquidity separately from headline yield.

## Who rents studios and one-beds in IMPZ?

Typical tenants include media freelancers, warehouse supervisors, and commuters working toward Ibn Battuta and Tecom. Do not market units as family communities, mismatched expectations drive void. Furnished premiums of 10 to 15% are common on studios where employers relocate staff for 12-month contracts.

| Tenant type | Lease length | Furnishing note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Media / production staff | 12 months | Often furnished |
| Logistics coordinators | 12 months | Mixed |
| Budget west-Dubai commuters | 12 months | Price sensitive |

## What do IMPZ studios and one-beds cost and rent for?

June 2026 portal bands cluster as below. Pull three Ejari comps from the same tower before you rely on gross yield math, interior blocks and Tecom-adjacent towers diverge.

| Unit | Purchase band (AED) | Annual rent band (AED) | Gross yield band |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Studio | 400,000 to 550,000 | 34,000 to 46,000 | 8.0 to 9.5% |
| 1 bedroom | 550,000 to 750,000 | 44,000 to 60,000 | 7.5 to 9.0% |

Vacancy allowance of 8 to 10% is conservative on studio stock with heavy turnover, price to re-let in 30 days not 60.

## How do you model net yield on an IMPZ studio?

Take AED 480,000 studio at AED 42,000/year rent (8.75% gross). Deduct AED 7,200 service charge at AED 12/sq ft on a 600 sq ft unit, AED 3,360 management at 8%, and one month void (AED 3,500). Net rent near AED 27,940 on AED 480,000 is about 5.8% before finance. Stress at -10% rent and you can compress toward 5.0%.

| Line | AED/year |
| --- | --- |
| Gross rent | 42,000 |
| Service charge | 7,200 |
| Management (8%) | 3,360 |
| Void (1 month) | 3,500 |
| Net before mortgage | ~27,940 |

## What location and building risks matter in IMPZ?

Metro access is usually bus to Ibn Battuta then Red Line, so advertise commute honestly in listings. Older towers may carry rising sinking funds, request five-year service charge payment history from the owners association. Holiday-home restrictions vary by tower, confirm in writing from the OA before you assume short-term lets are allowed.

Before buying, check elevator downtime and parking allocation in tenant surveys, both drive churn in mid-rise stock. Production City demand correlates with advertising and production hiring cycles, track Tecom and Dubai Media City occupancy quarterly if you hold multiple units.

## How does IMPZ compare to JVC for investors?

JVC offers deeper family demand and resale liquidity. IMPZ offers higher gross on smaller tickets with rougher tenant profiles and longer average days-on-market on exit.

| Factor | IMPZ | JVC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gross yield (2026 band) | 8.0 to 9.5% studios | 7.5 to 9.2% |
| Tenant profile | Media / budget commuter | Families + singles |
| Resale liquidity | Thinner | Stronger |
| Best fit | Pure yield hold | Yield + exit option |

## What should you verify before signing on IMPZ?

Stack 2% agency, 4% DLD transfer, and trustee fees on ready resale, and obtain developer NOC if a mortgage is outstanding. Underwrite with real service charge filings, not brochure estimates, and use void assumptions that match turnover-heavy studio markets.

Request holiday-home restriction confirmation in writing. Pair this checklist with [Due diligence Dubai property](/guides/due-diligence-dubai-property/) for SPA, Form F, and snagging steps beyond IMPZ-specific items.

Invest Gulf underwrites IMPZ deals with Ejari-backed rent, not listing-agent gross slides. If a tower shows 15+ identical listings at the same rent, treat renewal pricing power as weak until supply clears.

## Yield, tenant mix, and exit notes for DPC

Creative and media tenant narratives can support rent, but you still need signed Ejari evidence over brochure job stories. Stress vacancy if your block is heavy with investor stock handing over together.

Compare service charge load against JVC and similar fringe freehold before you accept a gross yield screenshot. Exit buyers may be end-users seeking value space or investors recycling capital; marketing copy should match the real buyer pool. ## How do tenants actually reach metro and jobs from IMPZ?

Most residents bus to Ibn Battuta station then ride the Red Line toward Marina and JLT. Market that honestly in listings, tenants who expected a five-minute walk churn at renewal.

| Commute leg | Typical time (peak) |
| --- | --- |
| Tower to Ibn Battuta bus | 15-25 min |
| Bus to metro | 10-20 min |
| Metro to Marina | 15-25 min |

Production hiring cycles matter more than tourism seasons for studio void. When media agencies trim headcount, IMPZ feels it before JVC family stock.

## What furnishing cycle should studio landlords budget?

Furnished studios command 10-15% rent premia but need mattress, linen, and appliance refresh every 24-36 months on heavy turnover. Model AED 8,000-15,000 refresh every two years against gross rent before you chase furnished Ejari peaks.

Offer checklist: title or Oqood status, service charge statement, snagging, and a hold period that survives a soft year. Confirm current official DLD rules on your transaction type.

## Who should buy in Dubai Production City

End-users who work in nearby media or logistics belts and want newer freehold stock at a discount to marina-adjacent towers.

Investors who accept thinner liquidity and will hold through a full lease cycle rather than flip at handover.

Not ideal for buyers who need same-week resale depth or who underwrite only on brochure creative-industry job growth.

Run the numbers against JVC and similar fringe options on entry, service charge, and signed rent evidence before you stretch for a DPC view.
