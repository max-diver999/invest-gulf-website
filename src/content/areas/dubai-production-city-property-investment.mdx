## What is Dubai Production City for investors in 2026?

Dubai Production City is a freehold media and logistics free-zone pocket in the area (still called IMPZ on older deeds) where studios and one-beds print top-quartile Dubai gross yields of 8.0 to 9.5% because tickets start near AED 400,000. Tenant demand tracks production hiring more than tourism, so Invest Gulf underwrites hold period and exit liquidity separately from brochure yield.

- Ticket band: about AED 400,000 to 750,000 on studios and one-beds
- Gross yield band: 8.0 to 9.5% with Ejari proof
- Trade-off: thinner resale than JVC, higher turnover
