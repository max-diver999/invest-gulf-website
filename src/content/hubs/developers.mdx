## How do you verify a developer delivery track record?

Ask for the Trakheesi project ID, then cross-check completion status on Dubai REST before foreign buyers commit to a 3 to 5 years plan. Walk completed towers by the same developer and speak to owners about snagging delays over the last 24 months. Our checklist work treats marketing delivery percentages as non-evidence until project-level proof exists.

## How does RERA escrow protect off-plan buyers?

Off-plan payments must flow into a DLD-regulated escrow account tied to the project, with funds released only when an approved engineer certifies milestones. Match the escrow IBAN on the SPA to Trakheesi and Dubai REST before transferring beyond the booking fee. Red flag policy is simple: no verified escrow, no deal, even when a 20/80 plan looks cheap.

1. Confirm escrow IBAN on SPA
2. Match IBAN to Trakheesi / REST within 7 days of booking
3. Refuse payments into non-escrow accounts
4. Re-check milestones before each 10% construction tranche

## How do Dubai developer payment plans and incentives work?

Payment plans commonly split as 30/70, 40/60, 20/80, or post-handover schedules over 2 to 3 years after keys, which changes cash-flow risk for foreign buyers even when the headline looks affordable. A DLD waiver on an AED 2 million unit equals AED 80,000 only if written into the SPA. Pre-signature checklist models net promotions against ready-market pricing before treating them as discounts.

| Structure | Typical split | Buyer implication |
|---|---|---|
| 30/70 | 30% during build, 70% at handover | Standard; heavy handover lump sum |
| 40/60 | 40% during build, 60% at handover | More construction-phase cash commitment |
| 20/80 or 10/90 | Low entry, large handover balance | Popular with Danube; leverage-like exposure |
| Post-handover | e.g. 50% build + 50% over 2 to 3 years after keys | Lower upfront; developer debt after handover |

Developer incentives to parse carefully:

- DLD fee waiver, real if in SPA; compare net price to ready stock
- Guaranteed rental returns, often priced in; model post-guarantee year
- Furniture packages, low replacement value
- Golden Visa language still requires Oqood/title at AED 2M registered value

## How do developer service charge estimates compare to reality?

Launch brochures often understate stabilised charges, and after handover the owners association sets the Mollak-filed rate that foreign buyers actually pay. A 1 AED per sqft underestimate on a 1,000 sqft apartment is AED 1,000 per year, every year. Our checklist work compares written estimates to completed towers via Dubai REST before SPA.

| Source | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Developer estimate | Launch planning only |
| Mollak / REST comps | Stabilised reality check |
| Sister-tower owners | Snagging and charge surprises |

## What developer red flags should pause your purchase?

Pause if escrow cannot be verified, Trakheesi status is unclear, the developer has repeated delays on similar towers, or the SPA lacks defined handover dates and penalty clauses. Delivery below 75% on recent comparable towers is a hard red flag. We also scrutinise unsold concentration near claimed completion percentage.

### Tier 2 additional scrutiny

- No completed project in the same community to inspect build quality
- Delivery rate below 75% on recent comparable towers
- High concentration of units still unsold near claimed completion percentage
- Post-handover payment plan with no clear registration of outstanding balance

## Should you pick the developer or the location first?

Location and tenant demand should anchor the investment case for foreign buyers; developer quality determines whether you receive the asset on time and at the quality promised over 3 to 5 years. Use both filters before you sign. Our checklist sequencing always defines thesis before brand loyalty.

1. Define investment thesis: yield, Golden Visa, end-user, or flip
2. Shortlist communities with tenant demand evidence (Ejari rents)
3. Filter projects by developer tier appropriate to risk tolerance
4. Verify escrow, Oqood, and delivery record on the specific project
5. Model net yield including service charges and vacancy
6. Legal review of SPA before any payment beyond reservation

## How does Abu Dhabi differ, and what happens after handover?

Dubai has thousands of registered developers and heavy launch volume, while Abu Dhabi is dominated by Aldar on major master plans with ADX-listed reporting. Do not assume Dubai tier labels transfer across emirates after 12 months of Dubai research alone.
