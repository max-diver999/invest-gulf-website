## How long from snagging to move-in in Dubai?

Snagging-to-move-in in Dubai typically takes 4 to 8 weeks after key collection when utilities, critical defects, and furniture logistics are sequenced correctly, and Invest Gulf blocks furniture delivery for foreign buyers until DEWA is live because uncooled storage in Dubai heat warps goods within days on the project.

Days 1 to 5, DEWA connection:
DEWA must be connected before furniture delivery.
- Apply online at dewa.gov.ae with premise number, Emirates ID, and contacts
- Pay security deposit: AED 2,000 (apartment) or AED 4,000 (villa), refundable
- Connection typically live within 1 to 2 business days

Days 1 to 7, Cooling account setup:
District cooling (EMPOWER in Marina, JLT, Business Bay) needs a separate account with deposits often AED 2,000 to 5,000. Split AC buildings run cooling through DEWA alone.

Days 3 to 7, Internet and telecoms:
Du or e& installation usually takes 5 to 7 business days once building infrastructure is confirmed.

### Week 2 to 4: Defect Resolution

Critical defects (must fix before occupancy):
- Active water leaks or damp
- Non-functional AC
- Electrical faults
- Security gaps on doors or windows
- Broken fixtures that pose safety risk

Important defects (fix within first month): tile cracks, grouting, cabinet alignment, paint issues, developer appliances.

Minor defects (fix within 6 months): paint touch-ups, silicone joints, small fixture adjustments.

Document everything in writing with photographs and snag list numbers.

### Week 3 to 5: Furniture and Fit-Out

Book freight lifts 48 to 72 hours ahead. Custom carpentry typically takes 3 to 6 weeks from order to install.

Insider tip: Measure freight-lift dimensions with building management before ordering sectional sofas or king mattresses. Oversized pieces stuck in the lobby waste both delivery slots and defect-free move-in days.

Dubai post-snag move-in typically sequences DEWA deposits of AED 2,000 for apartments or AED 4,000 for villas, cooling deposits of AED 2,000 to 5,000 where EMPOWER applies, and internet installs of 5 to 7 business days before furniture arrives. Invest Gulf relocation files for foreign buyers treat 4 to 8 weeks as the realistic settle window when critical snags clear in 2 to 6 weeks and custom millwork needs 3 to 6 weeks. Service charges on a 1,000 sq ft unit at AED 18/sq ft equal AED 18,000 per year and should be budgeted beside OA registration in week one. Developer portals (Emaar, DAMAC, Nakheel, Meraas) should receive written defect tickets with photos inside the 1-year finishes warranty and the 10-year structural cover. Freight lifts booked 48 to 72 hours ahead prevent delivery day collisions with unfinished AC work.

## What does the Owners Association do after handover?

The Owners Association typically bills service charges, maintains common areas, and approves fit-out works after handover, and Invest Gulf registers foreign buyers in week one because OA access codes, parking fobs, and AED 12 to 55/sq ft charge invoices start as soon as keys leave the developer desk.

RERA regulates OAs and appoints management companies to operate them.

| Building Type | Typical Service Charge Range |
| --- | --- |
| Standard apartment building | AED 12 to 20 per sq ft |
| Premium tower (Marina, Downtown) | AED 20 to 35 per sq ft |
| Luxury development (ultra-prime) | AED 35 to 55+ per sq ft |
| Villa communities | AED 5 to 15 per sq ft |

OA responsibilities include lobby and amenity maintenance, security, common-area insurance, service charge collection, fit-out approvals, and community rules.

### Registering as a New Owner

1. Contact the OA manager (welcome pack contacts)
2. Submit title deed, Emirates ID, registration form
3. Receive OA resident ID or access code
4. Register vehicles and regular contractors

RERA publishes project rates on Dubai REST. Dispute charges above regulated maximums.

## How long is the developer defect liability period?

Developer defect liability typically covers general finishes for about 1 year, mechanical and electrical systems for about 2 years, and structural elements for up to 10 years under UAE law, and our diaries month-10 revisits for foreign buyers so unfinished snags do not expire silently on the project.

| Defect class | Typical cover from handover |
| --- | --- |
| Structural (foundations, load-bearing) | 10 years |
| MEP / AC / plumbing / elevators | About 2 years |
| General finishes (tiles, paint, joinery) | About 1 year |

Action items:
1. Report defects in writing inside the applicable window
2. Keep dated correspondence and photos
3. Escalate via RERA Trakheesi, DLD, or courts if ignored

Register on developer portals immediately: Emaar Service Portal, DAMAC Customer Care, Nakheel and Meraas care desks, or email for smaller developers.

## How do you settle daily living in a new Dubai tower?

Daily living setup typically starts with security introductions, parking bay confirmation, storage access, and freight-lift rules booked 48 to 72 hours ahead, and Invest Gulf spends the first 10 minutes of week one on those relationships because foreign buyers who skip them wait longer on every contractor and emergency call.

- Confirm allocated parking bays on title and handover papers
- Register pool, gym, and visitor parking credentials within the first 7 days
- Learn waste room locations and book bulk packing disposal via Dubai Municipality when needed
- Keep OA and security numbers beside DEWA and cooling account IDs (AED 2,000 to 5,000 deposits already paid)

## What should you do in the first year after handover?

First-year ownership typically means tracking the 1-year finishes warranty, attending the OA AGM, and filing a consolidated snag list at months 10 to 11, and Invest Gulf blocks structural renovations for foreign buyers until written OA fit-out approval lands on the project.

| Month | Action | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 3 | Chase critical snags on developer portal | Protects 1-year finishes cover |
| 6 | Mid-year AC and silicone check | Dubai summer reveals movement |
| 10 to 11 | Consolidated snag revisit | Captures items before warranty closes |
| 12 | OA AGM attendance | Votes on AED 12 to 55/sq ft budgets |

Before warranty expiry, recheck joinery movement after the first summer, silicone shrinkage, and prior repairs that were only patched. Cosmetic painting usually needs no approval; structural, AC, or services work does. Remember MEP cover near 2 years and structural cover up to 10 years sit on separate clocks.

## What move-in checklist runs week by week?

A week-by-week move-in checklist typically runs DEWA and cooling in week 1, critical snag chase and internet in weeks 2 to 3, furniture and insurance in weeks 4 to 6, then a month-11 warranty revisit, and Invest Gulf times foreign buyers to 6 to 8 weeks fully settled when freight lifts and custom pieces stay on schedule.

**Week 1:**
- DEWA and cooling accounts live
- OA registration and parking fobs
- Developer portal defect tickets opened

**Week 2 to 3:**
- Chase critical snags
- Order Du or e&
- Order furniture; note 3 to 6 week custom lead times
- Collect OA fit-out guide

**Week 4 to 6:**
- Pre-booked freight-lift delivery
- Test all utilities
- Set home insurance
- Note OA AGM schedule

**Month 11:**
- Full defect revisit before 1-year finishes warranty closes
