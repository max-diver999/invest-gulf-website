## How long from snagging to move-in in Dubai?

Plan four to eight weeks after key collection: utilities first, critical defects fixed, then furniture.

Days 1 to 5, DEWA connection:
DEWA (Dubai Electricity and Water Authority) must be connected before furniture delivery because:
- You need electricity for tools and lighting
- Air conditioning must be operational, furniture stored in unventilated Dubai heat can warp and mould
- Refrigerators, washing machines, and other appliances need power for testing

Apply online at dewa.gov.ae:
- Search for premise number (from title deed or developer letter)
- Submit Emirates ID and contact details
- Pay security deposit: AED 2,000 (apartment) or AED 4,000 (villa), refundable when you cancel the account
- DEWA connection is typically live within 1 to 2 business days of successful application

Days 1 to 7, Cooling account setup:
If your building uses district cooling (EMPOWER in Dubai Marina, JLT, Business Bay, or similar), you also need a separate cooling account:
- Apply to EMPOWER (or relevant district cooling provider) online or at their service centre
- Security deposit required: AED 2,000 to 5,000 typically
- Account linked to your apartment's chiller allocation

Alternatively, if your building uses individual split AC units, DEWA covers your cooling.

Days 3 to 7, Internet and telecoms:
Du and Etisalat (e&) are the two main UAE telecoms providers. For new developments:
- Check which provider has building infrastructure (some buildings are Du-only or e&-only)
- Apply online, installation engineer appointment usually within 5 to 7 business days
- Fibre broadband is standard in newer Dubai buildings

### Week 2 to 4: Defect Resolution

The developer's customer service team should be working through your snag list. Follow up on each item:

Critical defects (must fix before occupancy):
- Active water leaks or evidence of damp
- Non-functional air conditioning or split AC units
- Electrical faults (circuit breakers tripping, outlets not working)
- Non-closing doors or windows creating security gaps
- Broken fixtures or fittings that pose safety risk

Important defects (fix within first month):
- Tile cracks or lippage
- Grouting issues
- Cabinet door alignment
- Incomplete paintwork or significant paint issues
- Malfunctioning kitchen appliances (if developer-supplied)

Minor defects (fix within 6 months):
- Paint touch-ups
- Silicone joint finishing
- Minor fixture adjustments

Document everything in writing: Email the developer's handover/customer service team for every item. Include photographs, apartment number, and reference to the snag list item number. Keep a record of all correspondence, this protects your rights during the defect liability period.

### Week 3 to 5: Furniture and Fit-Out

Dubai has a well-developed furniture market. Main options:
- IKEA (Festival City, Jebel Ali), most popular for accessible price-to-quality
- Pan Emirates, Marina Home, THE ONE, mid-range lifestyle furniture
- Pottery Barn, West Elm (City Walk, Dubai Mall), premium
- Restoration Hardware (RH), luxury
- Custom carpentry and fit-out companies (kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, built-ins)
- Online: Amazon.ae, Noon.com

Delivery logistics in Dubai buildings:
- Most buildings require advance booking of freight lift for furniture delivery
- Book through building management or security at least 48 to 72 hours in advance
- Some buildings restrict delivery hours (not after 22:00, not during Fajr hours)
- Large furniture pieces (sectional sofas, king mattresses) need measurements against freight lift dimensions, ask management for lift dimensions before ordering

Custom carpentry lead times: Built-in wardrobes, kitchen upgrades, and custom millwork in Dubai typically take 3 to 6 weeks from order to installation. Order these early, they are often the longest-lead item.

### Week 4 to 8: Building Community Setup

## What does the Owners Association do after handover?

The OA bills service charges, maintains common areas, and approves fit-out works. Register as an owner in week one.

RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Agency) regulates OAs and appoints management companies to operate them.

OA responsibilities:
- Maintenance of common areas (lobby, gyms, pools, car parks, elevators)
- Building security and access control
- Building insurance (common areas)
- Annual service charge collection and management
- Fit-out approvals for owner renovation works
- Enforcement of community rules

### Registering as a New Owner

When you collect your keys from the developer, the OA should initiate your registration. If not:
1. Contact the OA manager directly (contact details in the developer's welcome pack)
2. Submit copy of title deed, Emirates ID, and completed registration form
3. Receive your OA resident ID or access code
4. Register your vehicles with security (if basement parking requires fob or card)
5. Register domestic workers and regular contractors

### Service Charges

Service charges are annual fees every unit owner pays for building maintenance. In Dubai, they are quoted as AED per square foot per year:

| Building Type | Typical Service Charge Range |
| --- | --- |
| Standard apartment building | AED 12 to 20 per sq ft |
| Premium tower (Marina, Downtown) | AED 20 to 35 per sq ft |
| Luxury development (ultra-prime) | AED 35 to 55+ per sq ft |
| Villa communities | AED 5 to 15 per sq ft |

A 1,000 sq ft apartment at AED 18/sq ft = AED 18,000 per year in service charges. This is billed annually or quarterly.

RERA service charge regulation: RERA publishes annual service charge rates per project on its Dubai REST platform. If your OA is charging more than RERA-regulated maximums, you can dispute this.

### Your Rights as an OA Member

- Vote at Annual General Meetings (AGM), OAs are legally required to hold AGMs
- Request audited financial statements from OA management
- Challenge excessive service charges through RERA
- Propose agenda items for AGM
- Stand for election to the Owners Committee (a board that oversees the management company)

Being an active OA member protects your investment, OAs that are poorly managed (unpaid maintenance contracts, deferred major works) create building deterioration and property value risk.
## How long is the developer defect liability period?

General finishes carry about one year, systems about two years, and structural coverage up to ten years under UAE law.

Under UAE law (Civil Transactions Law and Real Estate sector regulations), developers have mandatory warranty obligations:

Structural defects: 10 years from handover. Covers foundations, load-bearing elements, structural frame. This is a constitutional guarantee.

Mechanical and electrical systems: 2 years from handover in most cases. Covers plumbing systems, electrical distribution, AC systems, elevators.

General finishing defects: 1 year from handover. Covers everything else, tiles, paint, joinery, kitchen fittings.

Your action items during the defect period:

1. Report all defects to the developer in writing (email or portal) within the applicable warranty period
2. Keep all correspondence with dates and reference numbers
3. Photograph defects at discovery: a timestamp is valuable
4. If developer refuses to repair: file a complaint with RERA's Trakheesi system or Dubai Courts

### Developer Customer Service Access

Most Dubai developers have dedicated handover/customer service portals:

- Emaar: Emaar Service Portal (myeservices.emaar.com)
- DAMAC: DAMAC Customer Care (customer.damacproperties.com)
- Nakheel: Nakheel customer portal
- Meraas: Meraas customer care
- Smaller developers: email-based systems

Register on the developer's portal immediately after handover. This is where defect reports are tracked and responded to.

### Escalating Unresolved Defects

If the developer is unresponsive or disputes a genuine defect:

1. RERA Dispute Resolution: File a formal complaint via the RERA portal
2. **Dubai Land Department:** Major disputes can be escalated to DLD
3. **Dubai Courts:** Civil case for breach of warranty: applicable for significant defects
4. **Dubai Consumer Protection:** DED can mediate consumer disputes

Keep records for all escalation, a documented trail of attempts to resolve matters directly with the developer is essential before escalating.

## How do you settle daily living in a new Dubai tower?

Introduce yourself to security, confirm parking and storage, and book freight lifts before large deliveries.

The relationship with your building security manager, facilities manager, and OA team pays dividends:
- You get faster response to maintenance requests
- Your contractor access requests are handled promptly
- Emergency incidents are responded to with context
- You hear about building issues (upcoming major works, lift servicing windows) in advance

A brief introduction and the exchange of a business card or contact number is worth the 10 minutes.

### Parking, Storage, and Common Areas

- Confirm your allocated parking bays, check the title deed and handover paperwork for bay numbers
- Additional storage (if applicable), confirm storage unit allocation and access
- Pool, gym, and community centre, register your pass or access credentials
- Visitor parking, understand your building's visitor parking policy (many Dubai buildings have limited visitor bays)

### Organising Waste Collection

Dubai Municipality waste management is standard for residential buildings. Your building will have waste disposal areas in service corridors or dedicated waste rooms. DEWA recycling bins (blue, yellow, green) are increasingly available in building basements.

For large disposal of packing materials after move-in: DM allows booking a bulk waste collection online at dubaimunicipalityservice.gov.ae.

## What should you do in the first year after handover?

Track warranty deadlines, attend the OA AGM, and revisit snags before the one-year finish warranty expires.

### The 11-Month Snag Revisit

Before your 1-year general warranty expires, do a thorough walkthrough:
- Check all joinery (cabinets, wardrobes, doors) for movement or warping in the first summer
- Check all silicone joints for shrinkage
- Recheck any defects you reported, confirm they were properly repaired, not just patched
- Submit a consolidated list to the developer at month 10 to 11 to capture all remaining items before the 1-year warranty closes on general finishes

### Building Upgrades and Renovations

After the first year, many owners personalise their spaces:
- Kitchen upgrades (new appliances, backsplash, countertops)
- Bathroom reseal and refresh
- Flooring changes (polished concrete, herringbone parquet)
- Built-in storage additions

**Always check OA fit-out guide first.** Most Dubai buildings restrict: drilling into structural elements, changes to external glazing, modifications to balcony railings, and any work that affects building services.

Get OA approval in writing before starting non-cosmetic works.

## What move-in checklist runs week by week?

**Week 2 to 3:**
- Chase developer on critical snag defect resolutions
- Order internet connection (Du or e&)
- Order furniture, note lead times for custom pieces
- Register vehicles with building security
- Get OA fit-out guide if planning any renovation works

**Week 4 to 6:**
- Furniture delivery (pre-booked freight lift)
- Confirm all utilities tested and working
- Set up home insurance (contents and/or building)
- Note OA AGM schedule for upcoming year

**Month 11:**
- Comprehensive defect revisit before 1-year warranty closes
- Submit outstanding snag items in writing to developer

Moving into a new Dubai property is a genuinely exciting milestone. The administrative steps can feel overwhelming alongside the aesthetic decisions, but handled systematically, most residents are fully settled within 6 to 8 weeks of key collection.

If you will lease the unit, photograph handover condition before the first tenant arrives. Those images separate developer defect liability from tenant damage later. Open Ejari only once DEWA, cooling, and critical snags are resolved so the legal tenancy start matches a habitable home. Keep a shared folder of utility account numbers and OA contacts for your property manager.
