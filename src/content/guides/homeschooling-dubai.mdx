## Is homeschooling legal in Dubai?

Homeschooling in the traditional sense is typically not permitted: parents cannot act as sole educators without licensed enrollment from age 6, so Invest Gulf steers families to KHDA schools or recognized online providers Written evidence beats broker summaries at every gate Keep SPA, REST screenshots, and fee quotes in one folder Allow 7 to 14 days for verification.

When families ask about homeschooling, they usually mean one of three needs:

1. Flexibility and curriculum control
2. Short-term or transitional gaps while waiting for a seat
3. Medical or SEN-related needs that limit campus attendance

Each path still requires enrollment somewhere recognized by KHDA or the Ministry of Education. Sustained unexplained absences trigger welfare follow-up.

Dubai compulsory education planning starts at age 6 and runs through school years aligned roughly to age 18, so unregulated parent-only teaching is not a lawful substitute for KHDA enrollment. Families arriving mid-year often face a short seat gap of a few weeks while applications clear, which is normal, but months without a licensed school or recognized online provider raise welfare flags. Accredited online options at AED 15,000 to 35,000 per year keep the file compliant while campus waitlists of 6 to 12 months continue. Hybrid attendance with Principal approval can reduce on-site hours, yet the child stays on a school roll. Tutors at AED 150 to 450 per hour support exams; they do not replace enrollment. Keep certificates and invoices ready before anyone claims the household is "just homeschooling" without institutional registration.

## When does KHDA grant exceptions?

KHDA exceptions are typically narrow and document-driven for severe SEN or medical cases lasting one term or less, so Invest Gulf requires specialist reports before families assume open-ended home schooling Model the full cash timeline, not only the headline instalment Treat marketing claims as unverified until DLD records match Allow 7 to 14 days for verification.

### Special Educational Needs (SEN)

KHDA's Inclusive Education framework expects:

- Psychoeducational assessment from a licensed UAE psychologist
- Statement of need and recommended provision
- Evidence that licensed SEN schools were explored first

### Medical exceptions

Serious illness or recovery may allow time-limited home tuition while the child stays on a school's roll, typically one term or less, with UAE physician certification. See [nursery and early years](/guides/dubai-nursery-early-years/) for early SEN context.
