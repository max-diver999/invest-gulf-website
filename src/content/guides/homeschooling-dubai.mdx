### Special Educational Needs (SEN)

Children whose disabilities or learning needs make mainstream school attendance impossible may be considered for alternative provision. KHDA's Inclusive Education framework (published guidance updated annually) requires:

- A comprehensive psychoeducational assessment from a licensed UAE psychologist or specialist centre
- A statement of need and recommended provision
- Evidence that the family has genuinely explored KHDA-licensed SEN specialist schools before seeking a home-based exception

Dubai has a growing number of specialist SEN schools, KHDA's directory lists providers rated for autism, physical disabilities, hearing impairment, and learning differences. The default KHDA position is to find an appropriate licensed school, not to authorize home provision. True home exceptions are rare and typically involve severe physical illness preventing any travel.

### Medical Exceptions

Children recovering from serious illness or surgery may receive KHDA authorization for a period of home tuition while remaining on a school's enrollment roll. This is typically time-limited (a school term at most) and requires medical certification from a UAE-licensed physician. The school remains the enrolled institution; home tuition is a temporary supplement.

If your child has complex medical needs and you are considering Dubai relocation, building school selection around specialist medical infrastructure, proximity to clinics, school nurse capacity, emergency protocol quality, is as important as KHDA rating.
