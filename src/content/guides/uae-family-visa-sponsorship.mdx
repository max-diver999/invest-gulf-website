## Who can sponsor family in the UAE?

Only UAE residence visa holders with qualifying employment, or Golden Visa holders under separate rules, can typically sponsor spouse, children, and in limited cases parents, and Invest Gulf waits until the sponsor visa is stamped and Emirates ID issued before any dependant filing for foreign buyers across a 2 to 3 month document window.

If your own residency is still processing, finish [UAE employment visa process](/guides/uae-employment-visa-process/) first. Entry-permit stage sponsorship is a common rejection path.

## What is the AED 4,000 salary rule for family visa?

The AED 4,000 salary rule typically means AED 4,000 monthly basic on the MOHRE contract, or AED 3,000 basic plus employer-provided accommodation written into that contract, and Invest Gulf ignores cash housing allowances when testing Path A versus Path B for foreign buyers sponsoring spouse and children.

| Path | Monthly basic salary required | Accommodation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A, salary only | AED 4,000 minimum | Not required on contract |
| B, salary + housing | AED 3,000 minimum | Employer-provided and written into employment contract |
| Parents only | AED 20,000 minimum | Family-sized housing required (verify current figure) |

What counts: the salary line on your ICP/MOHRE-registered employment contract. Housing, transport, education allowances, bonus, and overtime typically do not count.

Example checklist: AED 3,500 basic + AED 2,500 cash housing allowance = AED 6,000 total still fails Path A and Path B. Restructure with HR to raise basic to AED 4,000 before applying.

UAE family sponsorship for spouse and children should be modelled as a 2 to 3 month project from attestation start to Emirates ID collection. Invest Gulf files show the standard ICP threshold remains AED 4,000 monthly basic salary, or AED 3,000 basic when employer-provided accommodation is written into the contract, while parent sponsorship typically needs about AED 20,000 monthly plus family-sized housing. Government and typing fees often land near AED 1,500 to 3,500 per dependant before health insurance of AED 3,000 to 8,000 yearly for a spouse and two children on a basic Dubai plan. Attestation of marriage and birth certificates commonly takes 4 to 8 weeks and is the main bottleneck. Golden Visa holders skip the salary test but still need DHA or DOH-approved insurance. Confirm current official ICP or GDRFA figures before you book flights.

## Which family members can you sponsor?

Spouse and minor children are the core sponsorship path, while adult sons typically need their own visa after age 18 unless studying, and our underwriting treats parent sponsorship at about AED 20,000 monthly salary as a separate capital and housing checklist.

- Adult sons: some PRO offices extend family cover to age 25 for full-time UAE students (verify with ICP)
- Adult daughters: often remain on parent sponsorship until marriage (confirm if over 21)
- Parents: AED 20,000 threshold, family housing, and comprehensive insurance; many households use long-stay visit visas instead

## What documents are required for UAE family visa?

Document packs typically require the sponsor's stamped residency and Emirates ID, attested marriage and birth certificates, Ejari housing proof, salary certificate, and DHA or DOH health insurance, and we reject photocopies without the full attestation chain across a 4 to 8 week prep window.

Sponsor checklist:
- Sponsor's valid UAE residency visa (stamped, not entry permit stage)
- Sponsor's Emirates ID
- Attested and legalised marriage certificate
- Spouse's valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
- Valid Ejari-registered tenancy contract showing family-appropriate housing
- Health insurance policy covering the spouse
- Salary certificate from employer (current, HR stamp)
- Employment contract copy (MOHRE-attested or authorised equivalent)
- Recent utility bill or DEWA proof when officers request it

Children need attested birth certificates, passports, and insurance each. Parents add AED 20,000+ salary proof, bedroom evidence on Ejari, and sometimes 3 to 6 months bank statements.

## How does marriage and birth certificate attestation work?

Attestation typically runs home-country foreign ministry then UAE embassy legalisation before UAE MOFA, because missing steps cause immediate rejection, and we budget 4 to 8 weeks for the full chain ahead of ICP filing so foreign buyers do not miss school terms by 2 months.

Common mistakes checklist:
- Photocopies instead of originals
- Local notarisation without MOFA
- Assuming an Apostille alone always works (confirm per country)
- Starting attestation only after you decide to apply

Start before or immediately after UAE arrival, not when school term is 2 weeks away.

## What is the step-by-step family visa process?

The family visa process typically runs attestation, insurance, entry permit, arrival, medical, Emirates ID biometrics, then stamping, because we expect 5 to 10 working days for ICP entry-permit approval and 2 to 6 weeks after arrival excluding the 4 to 8 week attestation lag.

1. Gather and attest documents; confirm Ejari is valid
2. Obtain DHA-approved (Dubai) or DoH-approved (Abu Dhabi) dependant insurance before submission → [health insurance rules](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/)
3. Submit entry permit via typing centre, employer PRO, or ICP/GDRFA portal
4. Dependant travels on entry permit (or change status in-country if rules allow)
5. Medical fitness for ages 18+ at MOHAP-approved centres; under-18s usually exempt
6. Emirates ID biometrics → [Emirates ID guide](/guides/emirates-id-application-guide/)
7. Visa stamping once medicals clear and ID application is in

**Insider tip:** Start marriage and birth certificate attestation 8 to 10 weeks before your preferred arrival week; a 4 to 8 week attestation delay is the most common reason we see families miss school enrolment windows even when salary already clears AED 4,000.

## What do UAE family visa fees cost in 2026?

Family visa fees typically total AED 1,500 to 3,500 per dependant in government and typing charges before insurance, because entry permit, stamping, Emirates ID, and medicals stack, and we add AED 3,000 to 8,000 yearly for a basic spouse-plus-two Dubai health plan.

| Fee type | Indicative range (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Entry permit (per dependant) | 350-500 |
| Visa stamping | 500-700 |
| Emirates ID | 350-400 |
| Medical fitness (18+) | 250-350 |
| Typing centre service | 200-500 |

Abu Dhabi Thiqa-equivalent DoH plans often cost more than basic Dubai cover.

## Is health insurance mandatory for dependants?

Health insurance is typically mandatory before ICP or GDRFA will issue dependant visas, because travel insurance is not accepted as a substitute, and we budget AED 3,000 to 8,000 yearly for a basic spouse-plus-two Dubai plan before entry-permit submission for foreign buyers across every sponsored person.

Budget this line before flights. Wrong insurer is a hard rejection, not a soft delay. Abu Dhabi DoH-approved plans often cost more than basic Dubai cover across the same 12 months.

## How does Golden Visa family sponsorship differ?

Golden Visa holders typically sponsor family without the AED 4,000 salary test, because the 10-year framework uses a separate ICP path, and we still require mandatory health insurance and Emirates ID for every dependant within the same 2 to 6 week post-arrival window.

Turnaround is similar to employment sponsorship, without salary-certificate gatekeeping. → [Golden Visa family sponsorship](/guides/golden-visa-family-sponsorship/) · [UAE residency visa types](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/)

## What should you do after the visa is stamped?

After stamping you typically have about 30 days to enrol schools, open bank files, and register clinics, because Emirates ID and residence visa unlock KHDA and hospital registration, and our underwriting treats unregistered Ejari as a renewal red flag.

Within first 30 days checklist:
- Register children for school (KHDA needs Emirates ID and residence visa)
- Open dependant bank accounts if needed
- Register with a GP clinic or hospital
- Update Ejari if you moved or extended the lease

Ejari must show the current address with the sponsor listed. Informal subleases create renewal complications.

## What mistakes delay UAE family visa applications?

Unattested documents, travel insurance instead of resident plans, and skipped change-of-status fees typically cause the longest delays, because ICP rejects incomplete chains without exception, and we see 4 to 8 week attestation gaps plus overstay fines when visit visas lapse during conversion.

Delay checklist:
- Certificates without home MOFA → UAE embassy → UAE MOFA
- Travel insurance presented instead of DHA/DoH resident cover
- In-country change of status assumed to be automatic
- Overstaying a visit visa while waiting for residence stamping

## Who should use employment sponsorship versus Golden Visa paths?

Employment sponsorship typically suits salary-qualified residents at AED 4,000 basic or AED 3,000 with housing on contract, while Golden Visa paths suit AED 2 million property or other qualifying holders who skip salary tests, and we map both to the same insurance and Emirates ID checklist for foreign buyers over 2 to 3 months.

Decision checklist:
- Employment path: confirm basic salary line and Ejari bedrooms before attestation spend
- Golden Visa path: confirm holder category still allows the dependants you need
- Parents: model AED 20,000 salary and housing before promising tickets
- School term: reverse-plan 8 to 10 weeks from attestation start
- Budget buffer: keep AED 1,000 to 2,000 spare per dependant for courier, photos, and repeat typing visits

Practical sequencing still matters after salary clears. Families relocating into Dubai or Abu Dhabi should align Ejari bedrooms, school waitlists, and insurance quotes before paying for attestation couriers. A household that only restructures the employment contract in week one of term often discovers the 4 to 8 week attestation lag too late. Golden Visa holders should still diary Emirates ID biometrics within the same post-arrival window as employment sponsors. Parents on the higher income gate need family-sized housing evidence that matches the Ejari record officers will read.
