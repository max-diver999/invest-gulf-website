## How does the 183-day physical presence test work?
The physical presence test means spending 183 days or more in the UAE in a calendar year as a primary basis for individual tax residency under Ministry of Finance guidance. Days must be provable, and all emirates count toward one federal tally. Invest Gulf models a weekly day-count checklist from January for foreign buyers.

- Count 183 days with stamps

- Weekly log checklist

### What counts as a day

Best practice: count full days where you are present in the UAE at midnight local time. Arrival and departure days are contentious; some advisers count arrival and exclude departure, others use hours-based rules. Do not treat marketing copy as tax advice without adviser sign-off.

Transit: airside transfers without immigration entry typically do not count. Remote work from a Dubai apartment counts the same as tourism days if you are physically present.

### What does not substitute for days

- Title deed or Oqood without presence
- Golden Visa stamp without day count
- DEWA account alone
- Tenant occupancy in your empty unit

| Airside transit | No | Boarding pass, no entry stamp |
| Short Golden Visa trip | Yes per day present | Stamps + flight records |
| Property viewing weekend | Yes if overnight | Hotel, stamps |
