## How much does a dental consultation cost in Dubai in 2026?

A routine check-up with X-rays at a private clinic typically runs **AED 150-400** in 2026. Scaling and polishing add **AED 200-450**. Emergency pain visits without insurance often cost **AED 250-600** before any treatment starts. Always confirm whether your insurer uses direct billing or expects you to pay and claim later.

| Clinic tier | Consultation (typical) | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget (Deira, Karama, International City) | AED 100-250 | Basic care, simple extractions |
| Mid-range independent | AED 200-400 | Routine family dentistry |
| Hospital chains (Mediclinic, Aster, NMC) | AED 250-450 | Insurance direct billing |
| Premium multi-specialty centres | AED 350-600 | Implants, cosmetic, complex cases |

Budget clinics often sit in older residential districts. Mid-range practices dominate volume and balance price with equipment quality. Network chains standardise pricing and paperwork for employer plans. Premium centres bundle specialists under one roof and charge accordingly. Every clinic must hold a **DHA licence**; verify it on the Dubai Health Authority portal before booking.

## What do fillings, crowns, and root canals cost?

Restorative work drives most out-of-pocket dental spend in Dubai. A single composite filling averages **AED 350-600** at a mid-range clinic. Molars needing root canal therapy often reach **AED 1,800-3,500** plus a crown. Crowns in zirconia or porcelain typically add **AED 2,500-4,000** per tooth on top of any root canal fee.

## How much do dental implants cost in Dubai?

A single implant (fixture, abutment, and crown) usually totals **AED 4,500-9,500** in Dubai in 2026. Premium centres quote toward the top of that band; mid-range clinics often land near **AED 5,500-7,000** per tooth. Treatment spans **3-6 months** because the implant must integrate with bone before the final crown is fitted.

| Component | Typical range |
| --- | --- |
| Implant fixture | AED 2,500-5,000 |
| Abutment | AED 500-1,500 |
| Crown | AED 1,500-3,000 |

Additional costs may include bone grafts (**AED 1,500-4,000**), sinus lifts (**AED 3,000-8,000**), and CBCT scans (**AED 400-800**). Full-arch solutions such as All-on-4 commonly run **AED 35,000-70,000** per arch. Implants are excluded from most basic dental insurance; executive plans sometimes cap lifetime implant benefit at **AED 10,000-20,000**.

## How much do braces and Invisalign cost in Dubai?

Metal braces for a full course (12-24 months) typically cost **AED 7,000-15,000**. Ceramic braces run **AED 10,000-18,000**. Invisalign or other clear aligners range from **AED 8,000** for mild cases to **AED 25,000** for complex bite corrections. Lingual (behind-the-teeth) systems can exceed **AED 18,000-30,000**.

Children's interceptive orthodontics (ages 7-10) often costs **AED 4,000-8,000** for phase 1, with full braces in the teen years adding **AED 6,000-12,000**. Orthodontics is rarely covered by standard dental allowances; standalone riders exist but many families self-fund.

## Does health insurance cover dental treatment in Dubai?

Most **Essential Benefits Plan (EBP)** and basic employer packages **exclude routine dental**. Enhanced plans may include **AED 500-3,000** per year for check-ups, scaling, and simple fillings. Premium executive plans can offer **AED 5,000-10,000+** for major restorative work but still often exclude implants, veneers, and braces.

| Plan tier | Typical annual dental allowance | Usually covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic / EBP | None | Emergencies only via hospital ER |
| Enhanced employer | AED 500-3,000 | Check-ups, fillings, basic extractions |
| Premium / executive | AED 5,000-10,000+ | Root canals, crowns, some specialist visits |
| Optional dental rider | AED 600-2,500/year premium | Varies; read schedule before buying |

Standalone dental riders are available from major insurers. Compare the rider premium against expected treatment cost; for implants or orthodontics, paying cash or using a payment plan is often cheaper than buying coverage you cannot use.

## How do you find a licensed dentist in Dubai?

Search the **DHA facility list** for licensed dental clinics, then shortlist by location, insurer panel, and specialist need. Dubai Healthcare City (DHCC), Jumeirah, Marina, and Business Bay cluster premium practices; Deira and Bur Dubai host lower-cost options. For emergencies after hours, major hospitals (American Hospital, Mediclinic, Rashid Hospital) operate dental ER services.

Ask for a written treatment plan with itemised fees before accepting major work. Direct billing clinics reduce upfront cash flow; reimbursement plans require you to keep receipts and diagnosis codes for claims. Word-of-mouth in expat communities helps, but licence status and written quotes matter more than social media reviews alone.

## How can expats reduce dental bills in Dubai?

Prevention is the cheapest strategy: six-monthly check-ups (**AED 200-350**) catch decay before root canals (**AED 2,000+**). DHA polyclinic dental units and teaching clinics (including MBRU dental school) offer supervised care at reduced rates with longer waits. Some residents use licensed clinics in Sharjah for **30-50%** savings on equivalent procedures.

Compare at least two written quotes for implants or full-mouth work. Payment plans are common at premium centres. Dental tourism for complex cases can save money but multi-visit treatments (implants, orthodontics) fit poorly with overseas travel unless you have a trusted overseas clinician and follow-up plan.

## What does children's dental care cost in Dubai?

Paediatric dentists recommend a first visit by **age 1** or when the first tooth erupts. Paediatric specialist consultations run **20-40%** above general dentist fees but help anxious children. Fissure sealants cost **AED 200-400** per tooth and reduce molar decay risk. Many international schools run screening days; these supplement but do not replace clinic care.

## How do dental emergencies work in Dubai after hours?

Severe pain, facial swelling, or trauma needs same-day care. Major private hospitals (American Hospital, Mediclinic City Hospital, Saudi German) maintain on-call dental officers for true emergencies. Rashid Hospital (DHA) accepts walk-in dental trauma. For pain that is not life-threatening, many clinics open until **9-10pm**; pharmacies sell ibuprofen or diclofenac without prescription for short-term relief until you see a dentist.

Expect emergency consultation fees of **AED 300-800** plus treatment. Keep your insurer emergency hotline number saved; some plans cover accident-related dental even when routine care is excluded.

## How do insurance claims and payment plans work?

Direct-billing clinics swipe your insurance card at reception and collect only co-pays. Reimbursement plans require paid invoices, tooth numbers, and ICD-style diagnosis codes on the receipt. Claims through apps (Daman, Oman Insurance, ADNIC, etc.) usually need upload within **30-90 days**.

| Payment route | When it fits | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct billing | Employer plan with dental benefit | Annual cap exhaustion mid-year |
| Pay and claim | Small clinics, expired network | Missing codes on invoice |
| Credit / instalment plans | Implants, orthodontics, smile makeovers | 0% promos may still carry admin fees |
| Cash / card | Tourists, basic EBP holders | No VAT on most healthcare services |

Ask for a **pre-authorisation** letter before crowns, implants, or surgical extractions if your plan requires it. Without pre-auth, claims above **AED 2,000** are sometimes rejected even when the treatment is otherwise covered.

Dental is rarely bundled into basic relocation packages: budget **AED 1,500-4,000/year** per adult for maintenance even with insurance, and **AED 8,000+** in the year you start orthodontics or implant work. Pair dental planning with [Gulf healthcare comparison](/guides/gulf-healthcare-comparison/) and employer benefits review before you sign a lease in a premium district. If you split time between Dubai and another GCC city, carry recent X-rays on USB; Dubai clinics often repeat imaging unless films are less than **12 months** old and readable. Weekend appointments fill fast in January and September when new school terms start, so book hygiene visits **2-3 weeks** ahead in those months. Fluoride treatments for adults are optional but cost about **AED 150-300** if you want extra decay protection.
