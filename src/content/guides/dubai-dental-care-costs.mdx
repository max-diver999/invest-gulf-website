## How much does a dental consultation cost in Dubai in 2026?

A routine check-up with X-rays at a private Dubai clinic typically costs **AED 150 to 400** in 2026. Scaling and polishing add **AED 200 to 450**, and uninsured emergency pain visits often run **AED 250 to 600** before treatment. Invest Gulf data treats clinic-tier spreads as the first filter for foreign buyers relocating to this market.

| Clinic tier | Consultation (typical) | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget (Deira, Karama, International City) | AED 100-250 | Basic care, simple extractions |
| Mid-range independent | AED 200-400 | Routine family dentistry |
| Hospital chains (Mediclinic, Aster, NMC) | AED 250-450 | Insurance direct billing |
| Premium multi-specialty centres | AED 350-600 | Implants, cosmetic, complex cases |

Budget clinics often sit in older residential districts. Mid-range practices dominate volume and balance price with equipment quality. Network chains standardise pricing and paperwork for employer plans. Premium centres bundle specialists under one roof and charge accordingly. Every clinic must hold a **DHA licence**; verify it on the Dubai Health Authority portal before booking.

## What do fillings, crowns, and root canals cost?

Restorative dental work typically drives most out-of-pocket spend in Dubai. A mid-range composite filling averages **AED 350 to 600**, molar root canals often reach **AED 1,800 to 3,500**, and zirconia crowns add **AED 2,500 to 4,000** per tooth. Our analysis treats stacked root-canal-plus-crown bills as the real budget line.

## How much do dental implants cost in Dubai?

A single dental implant in Dubai typically totals **AED 4,500 to 9,500** for fixture, abutment, and crown in 2026. Mid-range clinics often land near **AED 5,500 to 7,000** per tooth, and treatment spans **3 to 6 months** while the implant integrates. Our underwriting flags bone grafts of **AED 1,500 to 4,000** as the common surprise add-on.

| Component | Typical range |
| --- | --- |
| Implant fixture | AED 2,500-5,000 |
| Abutment | AED 500-1,500 |
| Crown | AED 1,500-3,000 |

Additional costs may include bone grafts (**AED 1,500-4,000**), sinus lifts (**AED 3,000-8,000**), and CBCT scans (**AED 400-800**). Full-arch solutions such as All-on-4 commonly run **AED 35,000-70,000** per arch. Implants are excluded from most basic dental insurance; executive plans sometimes cap lifetime implant benefit at **AED 10,000-20,000**.

## How much do braces and Invisalign cost in Dubai?

Metal braces in Dubai typically cost **AED 7,000 to 15,000** for a full **12 to 24 month** course, ceramic braces **AED 10,000 to 18,000**, and clear aligners **AED 8,000 to 25,000** by case complexity. Lingual systems can exceed **AED 18,000 to 30,000**. Our research treats orthodontics as largely self-funded.

Children's interceptive orthodontics (ages 7-10) often costs **AED 4,000-8,000** for phase 1, with full braces in the teen years adding **AED 6,000-12,000**. Orthodontics is rarely covered by standard dental allowances; standalone riders exist but many families self-fund.

## Does health insurance cover dental treatment in Dubai?

Health insurance typically excludes routine dental on Essential Benefits Plan and basic employer packages in Dubai, while enhanced plans may include **AED 500 to 3,000** per year for check-ups and fillings and premium executive plans can offer **AED 5,000 to 10,000** yet still exclude implants and braces. Invest Gulf data treats rider premiums as worth comparing against cash quotes before foreign buyers buy cover they cannot use.

| Plan tier | Typical annual dental allowance | Usually covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic / EBP | None | Emergencies only via hospital ER |
| Enhanced employer | AED 500-3,000 | Check-ups, fillings, basic extractions |
| Premium / executive | AED 5,000-10,000+ | Root canals, crowns, some specialist visits |
| Optional dental rider | AED 600-2,500/year premium | Varies; read schedule before buying |

Standalone dental riders are available from major insurers. Compare the rider premium against expected treatment cost; for implants or orthodontics, paying cash or using a payment plan is often cheaper than buying coverage you cannot use.

## How do you find a licensed dentist in Dubai?

Finding a licensed Dubai dentist typically starts on the DHA facility list, then filters by insurer panel and district. Premium practices cluster in Dubai Healthcare City, Jumeirah, Marina, and Business Bay at consultation fees near **AED 350 to 600**, while Deira and Bur Dubai often quote **AED 100 to 250**. Our underwriting requires a written itemised plan before major work.

Ask for a written treatment plan with itemised fees before accepting major work. Direct billing clinics reduce upfront cash flow; reimbursement plans require you to keep receipts and diagnosis codes for claims. Word-of-mouth in expat communities helps, but licence status and written quotes matter more than social media reviews alone.

## How can expats reduce dental bills in Dubai?

Expats typically cut Dubai dental bills with six-monthly check-ups at **AED 200 to 350**, which catch decay before root canals above **AED 2,000**. DHA polyclinics and teaching clinics offer lower supervised rates, and licensed Sharjah clinics often save **30% to 50%** on equivalent work. Our clients still insist on DHA or MOHAP licences before crossing the border.

Compare at least two written quotes for implants or full-mouth work. Payment plans are common at premium centres. Dental tourism for complex cases can save money but multi-visit treatments (implants, orthodontics) fit poorly with overseas travel unless you have a trusted overseas clinician and follow-up plan.

## What does children's dental care cost in Dubai?

Children's dental care in Dubai typically starts with a first visit by age 1, or when the first tooth erupts. Paediatric specialist consultations run **20% to 40%** above general dentist fees, and fissure sealants cost **AED 200 to 400** per tooth. Invest Gulf data treats school screening days as supplements, not replacements, for clinic care.

## How do dental emergencies work in Dubai after hours?

Dental emergencies in Dubai typically need same-day care for severe pain, facial swelling, or trauma, with hospital dental officers on call at major private groups and Rashid Hospital for walk-in trauma. Many clinics stay open until **9pm to 10pm**, and emergency consultations often cost **AED 300 to 800** before treatment. Our underwriting tells foreign buyers to save the insurer hotline even when routine dental is excluded.

## How do insurance claims and payment plans work?

Dental insurance claims in Dubai typically follow either direct billing at reception or reimbursement with tooth numbers and diagnosis codes on the paid invoice. App claims usually need upload within **30 to 90 days**, and treatments above **AED 2,000** often require pre-authorisation. Our research treats missing codes as the main red flag for rejected claims.

| Payment route | When it fits | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct billing | Employer plan with dental benefit | Annual cap exhaustion mid-year |
| Pay and claim | Small clinics, expired network | Missing codes on invoice |
| Credit / instalment plans | Implants, orthodontics, smile makeovers | 0% promos may still carry admin fees |
| Cash / card | Tourists, basic EBP holders | No VAT on most healthcare services |

Ask for a **pre-authorisation** letter before crowns, implants, or surgical extractions if your plan requires it. Without pre-auth, claims above **AED 2,000** are sometimes rejected even when the treatment is otherwise covered.

## What should relocating expats budget for dental care in Dubai?

Relocating expats typically budget **AED 1,500 to 4,000** per adult yearly for dental maintenance in Dubai even with insurance, because basic relocation packages rarely bundle routine dentistry and orthodontics or implant years jump above **AED 8,000**. We track those living-cost lines alongside property holds for foreign buyers moving for work or asset management.

Our relocation files show the year you start orthodontics or implant work often jumps above **AED 8,000** per adult after insurance caps. Pair dental planning with the [Gulf healthcare comparison](/guides/gulf-healthcare-comparison/) and an employer benefits review before signing a lease in a premium district. Carry recent X-rays on USB if you split time across GCC cities, because Dubai clinics often repeat imaging unless films are under **12 months** old. Weekend hygiene slots fill **2 to 3 weeks** ahead in January and September school peaks, and optional adult fluoride runs about **AED 150 to 300** when you want extra decay protection on top of the annual maintenance band.
