UAE founders choose between **mainland** licences (full domestic market access) and **free zone** licences (international trade, sector clusters, packaged setup). Since Federal Decree-Law No. 32 of 2021, **100% foreign ownership** is available for most activities in both tracks, so the decision is no longer "local sponsor vs free zone" but **market access, cost, tax, banking, and visa quota**.

Disclaimer: fees and FTA rules change. Confirm figures with a UAE-licensed setup adviser before you sign.

## What is the core free zone vs mainland difference?

| Track | Regulator | UAE domestic sales | Typical first-year cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mainland LLC | DED / emirate ADDED | Direct | Higher (office + licence) |
| Free zone | Zone authority | Via agent or separate licence | Lower on flexi-desk |
| DIFC / ADGM | Financial centre | Sector-specific | Premium |

Mainland wins when you need retail footprint, onshore contracts, or government tenders. Free zone wins when clients and revenue are mostly offshore or free zone to free zone.

## How does the UAE setup landscape break down?

Over **40 free zones** plus mainland in each emirate create real choice and real complexity. Three buckets matter:

1. **Mainland:** DED Dubai, ADDED Abu Dhabi, or peer departments; governed by UAE civil law for most commercial activities.
2. **Free zones:** JAFZA, DMCC, IFZA, RAKEZ, SHAMS, and others with sector or geography focus.
3. **Financial centres:** DIFC and ADGM under English common law for regulated finance and institutional structures.

**2021 ownership reform** removed mandatory 51% local shareholding for most mainland activities. Ownership parity shifted the debate to **tax, banking, and market access**.

Property note: a standard free zone company **cannot** hold Dubai mainland property in the company name without approved structures. Many investors use **personal freehold** or a mainland vehicle for real estate while keeping operations in a zone. See [DMCC company setup](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/) for corporate tax and substance context.

Free zone savings are largest on **flexi-desk** packages. Dedicated offices and visa scaling erase much of the gap because rent dominates both tracks.

## Can free zone companies sell to UAE mainland customers?

Free zone entities sell freely **internationally** and to **other free zone** companies. Selling to mainland UAE consumers or unlicensed mainland B2C usually requires a **mainland agent, distributor, or separate mainland licence**.

Grey area **B2B services to mainland companies** (IT, consulting) happen often but corporate tax treatment under Qualifying Free Zone Person rules may classify that income as **non-qualifying** at 9%. Get written tax advice before you rely on 0%.

Activities that fit free zone well:

- Global SaaS or digital services
- International commodities flow
- Holding international assets
- Serving other free zone entities

Activities that push mainland:

- UAE retail shops
- On-site UAE consumer services
- Government contract bidding without branch structures

## How does corporate tax treat each structure?

**Mainland:** 9% on UAE-taxable profits above AED 375,000 (Small Business Relief may ease compliance for revenue under AED 3 million but does not erase tax on profits above thresholds).

**Free zone QFZP:** Potential **0% on Qualifying Income** if you meet:

1. **Economic substance** in the zone (people, expenses, management).
2. **Qualifying Income** tests (often free zone to free zone or foreign sources).
3. **De minimis** cap on non-qualifying revenue (commonly 5% of revenue or AED 5 million threshold per FTA rules, confirm current guidance).
4. No disqualifying activities.

Mixed mainland B2B from a free zone desk can trigger 9% on non-qualifying slices or jeopardise QFZP status entirely. Full analysis: [DMCC company setup](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/) corporate tax section.

## Which structure is easier to bank?

| Category | Banking experience |
| --- | --- |
| Mainland LLC | Familiar to all major UAE banks |
| Established free zones (DMCC, IFZA, JAFZA) | Widely accepted |
| DIFC / ADGM | Preferred by international banks |
| New or niche zones | More compliance questions |

Enhanced due diligence hits free zone entities with **only offshore income** and no UAE invoices. Keep contracts, invoices, and substance evidence ready for KYC refresh.

## What special cases push DIFC or dual structures?

Regulated financial services, fund managers, and certain holding plans often belong in **DIFC** or **ADGM**, not a generic trading free zone. Real estate agencies, healthcare clinics, and schools need **sector mainland approvals** regardless of zone marketing.

Dual structure pattern: free zone entity for international revenue + mainland LLC for UAE retail or government work. Costly but flexible.

## How should founders choose in 2026?

| Choose free zone if | Choose mainland if |
| --- | --- |
| Clients are mostly abroad | You need UAE retail or local contracts |
| Lean team, flexi-desk OK | Physical shop or office frontage required |
| Commodities or global SaaS | Government tenders are core revenue |
| QFZP 0% plausible with substance | You expect material mainland UAE sales |

Financial services: start with [DIFC company setup](/guides/difc-company-setup/) or [ADGM setup](/guides/abu-dhabi-adgm-setup/) rather than a general trading zone.

## What checklist runs before you sign a licence?

1. Activity list on licence matches how you invoice clients.
2. Market access test: name three UAE customers you must serve directly.
3. Tax memo on QFZP vs 9% mainland exposure.
4. Visa count vs office or package quota.
5. Bank pre-approval or referral from setup partner.
6. Property plan if you also buy UAE real estate (personal vs corporate title).

See [Dubai business setup guide](/guides/dubai-business-setup-guide/) and [Dubai mainland LLC setup](/guides/dubai-mainland-llc-setup/) for next steps.

## When does a freelancer permit beat a company?

Solo consultants with one visa and no UAE invoicing may start on a [UAE freelance permit](/guides/uae-freelance-permit-dubai/) while testing demand. Once UAE B2B contracts, employees, or warehouse activity appear, upgrade to free zone or mainland rather than stacking penalties for trading without the right licence.

## How do property investors combine setup with freehold purchase?

Many buyers establish UAE residence through employment or property-linked visa routes while keeping operating companies in DMCC or IFZA. Separate **operating licence** from **property title**: personal freehold is common for Golden Visa property thresholds; corporate ownership needs activity approval and bank sign-off.
