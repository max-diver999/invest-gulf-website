AED 2,000,000+ → 10-Year Golden Visa (strongly recommended)

Properties of AED 2M or more in your personal name qualify for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa. Critical distinction from 2-year visa:
- Must be fully paid (not mortgaged for this purpose), the AED 2M value must be equity, not total purchase price with outstanding mortgage
- For mortgaged properties: your paid equity must reach AED 2M (e.g., property worth AED 3.5M with AED 1.5M mortgage = AED 2M equity → qualifies)
- Off-plan: if total price is AED 2M+ and paid in full or 50%+ paid depending on rules, verify current GDRFA/ICA policy
- Property valuation used is the purchase price or RERA valuation (whichever is applicable, often a RERA-registered valuer's certificate is required)

**The Golden Visa is unambiguously the right choice if your budget allows it:**

| Feature | 2-Year Investor Visa | 10-Year Golden Visa |

The Golden Visa's "no minimum stay requirement" is significant for families with international careers, you can maintain UAE as a tax-efficient residence base while spending substantial time in other countries.

### Off-Plan vs Ready Property for Visa

Ready property: Fastest path to visa. Once purchased, title deed is issued immediately and you can proceed with investor visa application within days.

Off-plan property: Visa is possible once 50%+ of purchase price is paid (verify current GDRFA rules) but the property is not physically available for occupancy. Families relocating want to live in the property, off-plan requires temporary rental during the construction period, adding cost and complexity.

**For the relocation bundle, ready (secondary market) property is generally preferable** unless:
- You are buying specifically as an investment (not to live in yet)
- Construction timeline is under 12 months and you can plan temporary rental during that period
- Launch price advantage is significant enough to justify the temporary rental cost

Immediately after the title deed is issued by DLD:

Step 1: Property valuation
GDRFA requires a RERA-registered property valuer to confirm the property value for visa purposes. Cost: AED 2,500–5,000. This takes 3–5 business days.

Step 2: Apply for Golden Visa or Investor Visa
Applications go through GDRFA (General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs) in Dubai.

- Online via GDRFA Smart App or gdrfad.gov.ae
- Or through authorised typing centres (Amer)
- Or via your PRO/immigration consultant

Required documents:
- Title deed (certified copy)
- RERA property valuation certificate
- Passport (valid, minimum 6 months remaining)
- 6 passport photographs
- Medical fitness test results (see Step 3)

Step 3: Medical fitness test
Required for all UAE residents. Done at approved medical centres (al-Safar, Aster, etc.). Includes:
- Blood test (HIV, Hepatitis B and C, TB)
- Chest X-ray
- Cost: AED 350–500 per person
- Results: 1–3 business days

Step 4: Emirates ID application
Applied for simultaneously with visa. Biometrics (fingerprints, photo) taken at GDRFA/Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship centres.

Step 5: Visa stamping
Once visa is approved, it is stamped in your passport (or a UAE ID card serves as the residency document if you entered legally).

**Total visa timeline after title deed: 4–8 weeks for investor visa; 6–10 weeks for Golden Visa.**

### Finding Your Home

Option A: Rent first, buy later

most families rent for 6–12 months to experience neighbourhoods before committing to a purchase. The risk is the 4% DLD fee on any purchase, even renting for 12 months first adds only the rent cost, while a wrong property purchase costs 4% DLD plus 2% agent to exit.

**Option B: Buy immediately (the bundle approach)**

If you are committed to the Golden Visa route, buying before or immediately on arrival aligns property ownership, visa, and lifestyle in one transaction. The risk is buying under time pressure, rushing a property decision in a city you do not know yet.

**Mitigated bundle approach:** Research and shortlist properties extensively online before arriving (Dubai has excellent property portals, Property Finder, Bayut, Dubizzle). Arrange viewings in the first week. Make an offer in week 2–3 with a longer MOU-to-transfer period (60–90 days) giving you time to settle in and confirm the choice before the DLD transfer commits fully.

### What Not to Do

**Do not move family on tourist visas expecting to sort it out later.** Tourist visas allow 30–90 days in UAE and cannot convert to investor visa in-country without exiting and re-entering in most cases. The stress of a family living in Dubai without confirmed visa status while processing is real.

**Do not choose property based on price alone.** A AED 2M apartment in a location that does not suit your school commute or lifestyle will be frustrating within months. The area selection matters as much as the property itself.

**Do not defer school applications.** The waiting list problem in Dubai's top schools can delay a child's school start by a full academic year if applications are not submitted in advance. Submit school applications before your own arrival if at all possible.
