## Why bundle property, visa, and relocation together?

Buying for foreign buyers who will relocate differs from pure investment: visa track, the area, school timing, and banking must align before flights. A Dubai property-visa bundle typically picks freehold stock near AED 2,000,000 Golden Visa equity, matches school commute, and keeps 6.5 to 7.5% cash for DLD fees.

Dubai property-visa relocation for a family of four typically means buying freehold stock that can support Golden Visa equity near AED 2,000,000, clearing DLD transfer near 4% of price, then filing GDRFA medical and biometrics after the title deed issues. Invest Gulf clients who sequence valuation in the first 5 business days after transfer usually finish principal stamping in 6 to 10 weeks for Golden Visa tracks, versus 4 to 8 weeks on shorter investor visas. Dependent sponsorship then adds 2 to 4 weeks per person once the principal visa is live. School letters and Ejari address proof should match the same compound before week 8, because late applications can slip a full term. Cash beyond purchase price commonly lands near AED 260,000 to 310,000 when shipping, four weeks of temporary housing, and first-quarter living costs are included.

Insider tip: book RERA valuation within 5 business days of title deed issue, because GDRFA files that wait for a late valuer often lose 2 to 3 weeks before medical slots open.

| Pillar | Decision it drives |
| --- | --- |
| Property | Freehold area, price vs visa track, ready vs off-plan |
| Visa | Golden Visa vs 2-year investor, mortgage equity |
| Relocation | Schools, Ejari tenancy if renting first, bank account |

Done in sequence, many families compress ad-hoc steps from 6 to 8 months toward 90 to 120 days. Done out of order (family arrives on visit visas, school applications late), costs spike.

## Which property price fits which visa track in 2026?

Golden Visa typically targets AED 2,000,000+ equity for foreign buyers with strict registered-value and mortgage rules, while shorter investor visas use lower thresholds if ICA tables are met. Joint buyers may qualify with a minimum share when total registered value clears. Invest Gulf verifies live GDRFA policy before deposit.

**Golden Visa (10-year, property route):**

- Typically AED 2,000,000+ qualifying equity in personal name
- Mortgaged purchases: equity (value minus loan) must meet threshold, not headline price alone
- Off-plan: paid amount and registration rules vary; confirm with PRO
- RERA valuation certificate often required at application

| Feature | 2-year investor visa | 10-year Golden Visa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duration | 2 years, renewable | 10 years, renewable |
| Employer-tied | No (property-based) | No (property-based) |
| UAE stay rules | Stricter than Golden Visa | Often more absence-friendly |
| Family sponsorship | Yes | Yes, expanded in some cases |
| Property threshold | Lower registered value | AED 2M equity typical |

## What does the purchase cost beyond the property price?

Secondary Dubai purchase costs typically add 6.5 to 7.5% above the negotiated price for DLD transfer, agency, and trustee fees, so an AED 2,000,000 unit needs roughly AED 128,000 to 150,000 liquid beside the deposit. We treat the 4% DLD fee as non-negotiable cash before trustee booking.

Acquisition costs on a secondary Dubai freehold purchase typically add 6.5 to 7.5% above the negotiated price once DLD transfer, agency commission, and trustee fees clear. On an AED 2,000,000 unit that stack is about AED 128,000 to 150,000 before mortgage arrangement costs. RERA valuation for visa filing usually costs AED 2,500 to 5,000 and takes 3 to 5 business days, while medical fitness runs AED 350 to 500 with results in 1 to 3 days. Families who keep these cash lines separate from the deposit avoid DLD delays when trustee booking is already set. Our underwriting treats the 4% transfer fee as non-negotiable liquidity, not a soft contingency that can wait until after flights are booked for the household. Sequence the purchase ahead of the visa application rather than alongside it, because the registered title is the input the visa file needs and reversing the order adds weeks for no benefit.

| Cost | Rate | On AED 2M property |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DLD transfer fee | 4.0% | AED 80,000 |
| DLD admin | Fixed | AED 4,500 |
| Agent commission | 2.0% | AED 40,000 |
| Conveyancing / trustee | Fixed | AED 4,000 to 6,000 |
| Mortgage costs (if used) | Varies | Arrangement plus registration |
| **Total acquisition (indicative)** | ~6.5 to 7.5% | ~AED 128,000 to 150,000 |

Keep this liquid alongside down payment; DLD will not transfer without cleared fees.

### Ready vs off-plan for relocation

Ready property: fastest path to title deed and visa filing.

Off-plan: possible with partial payment rules, but you cannot occupy until handover. Families relocating usually rent temporarily unless completion is under 12 months and budget allows.

## What happens after DLD issues the title deed?

Visa processing after Dubai title deed issue typically follows RERA valuation, GDRFA application, medical fitness, biometrics, and Emirates ID before family sponsorship can start. Invest Gulf timelines usually show 4 to 8 weeks for shorter investor visas and 6 to 10 weeks for Golden Visa principal stamping after ownership registers.

1. **Property valuation:** RERA-registered valuer, AED 2,500 to 5,000, 3 to 5 business days
2. **Visa application:** GDRFA Smart App, Amer centres, or PRO
3. **Medical fitness:** Approved clinic, AED 350 to 500, 1 to 3 days for results
4. **Emirates ID:** Biometrics with visa filing
5. **Visa stamping / ID card:** SMS when ready for collection

Documents typically include certified title deed, valuation certificate, passport copies, photos, and medical results.

| Fee item | Investor visa (2yr) | Golden Visa (10yr) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visa application | AED 1,800 to 2,500 | AED 2,800 to 4,500 |
| Emirates ID | AED 400 to 600 | AED 400 to 600 |
| Valuation | AED 2,500 to 5,000 | AED 2,500 to 5,000 |
| Medical | AED 350 to 500 | AED 350 to 500 |
| PRO / typing | AED 500 to 2,000 | AED 500 to 2,000 |

Total visa-side costs are small versus property price but timing-sensitive.

## How do you sponsor spouse and children?

Spouse and child sponsorship after a Dubai property visa typically costs AED 3,000 to 6,000 per dependent and takes 2 to 4 weeks each for entry permit, medical where required, biometrics, and stamping once the principal visa is approved. Invest Gulf files keep income or asset proofs ready for foreign buyers before Amer centre bookings.

Golden Visa routes may sponsor adult daughters in situations where standard visas do not. Parents of Golden Visa holders may qualify in some cases; confirm with GDRFA.

Income tests historically near AED 3,000 to 4,000 monthly salary for standard dependent visas; property holders often satisfy means via asset value instead. Your PRO should confirm the current table.

School place pressure is separate: submit applications before arrival when possible. Compare curricula in the [Gulf schools guide](/guides/gulf-schools-comparison/).

## What should the first 90 days after arrival look like?

The first 90 days after a Dubai property move typically cover medical completion, temporary housing, school tours in week one, then Emirates ID collection, bank onboarding, and DEWA setup across weeks two to four. Our clients who delay school emails past week two often miss a full term seat.

**Rent first vs buy immediately:**

- Rent 6 to 12 months to learn neighbourhoods; cost is rent only
- Buy immediately for Golden Visa alignment; risk is rushing the wrong community
- Mitigated approach: shortlist online, view week 1, offer week 2 to 3 with 60 to 90 day MOU to transfer at DLD

Do not sign a 12-month lease until visa category is confirmed if your PRO advises waiting.

## What sequence mistakes cost the most?

Sequence mistakes on Dubai property moves typically cost the most when families arrive on tourist visas for 30 to 60 days, buy on price alone without school commute checks, skip RERA valuation before GDRFA filing, or leave school applications until after flights. Our checklists put title deed and valuation ahead of dependent entry stamps.

- Tourist-status gaps create re-entry friction
- Price-only buys ignore school commute and Ejari timing
- Late school files can cost a full term fee
- Missing valuation before GDRFA often adds weeks

**Property chosen on price alone** ignores school commute and Ejari timing for dependent proofs.

**Late school applications** can cost a full term fee or delay start by a year.

**Skipping valuation before visa file** when GDRFA requires it adds weeks.

## Why does Ejari matter before family visas and schooling?

Ejari matters before Dubai family visas and schooling because foreign buyers sponsoring dependents and many schools expect consistent address proof, even when you already own the home. Our research treats early Ejari on a temporary lease as mandatory when DEWA and school letters must match within the first 30 to 60 days.

| Situation | Ejari role |
| --- | --- |
| Renting first year | Mandatory for DEWA and many school seats |
| Owner-occupier after purchase | Utilities in your name; keep title deed copy with ID file |
| Sponsoring dependents | Address consistency across GDRFA forms |
| Renewals | Lapsed Ejari can delay Emirates ID updates |

See [Ejari tenancy basics](/guides/dubai-ejari-tenancy-contract/) if you split time between hotel and apartment in month one.

## How do banking and salary proof interact with property visas?

Banking and salary proof on Dubai property visas typically still require source-of-funds checks on large transfers even when Golden Visa eligibility rests on AED 2,000,000 equity rather than local salary. Our clients open account talks in week one with passport and entry stamp, then add Emirates ID after SMS collection.

- Bring home-country bank statements for six months if you wire purchase funds
- Ask whether non-resident account is needed if you travel frequently
- Set up online banking before sponsor family cards for school fee payments
- Confirm credit card policies; some issuers close cards on visa type change

## What 90-day checklist should a family print?

A printable 90-day Dubai move checklist typically sequences shortlist and school enquiry in weeks 0 to 2, MOU and valuation in weeks 3 to 6, trustee and 4% DLD clearance in weeks 7 to 10, then title deed, principal stamping, and family sponsorship from week 11. Invest Gulf adjusts the grid for off-plan handovers.

| Week | Property / DLD | Visa / ID | Relocation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 2 | Shortlist, RERA agent check | Confirm visa track vs budget | School enquiry emails |
| 3 to 6 | MOU, deposit, SPA | Valuation ordered | Shipping quotes |
| 7 to 10 | Trustee, 4% DLD fee cleared | Medical, biometrics | Temporary lease if needed |
| 11 to 13 | Title deed received | Principal visa stamped | DEWA, furniture |
| 14+ | Handover snagging | Family sponsorship filed | School registration |

Adjust for off-plan purchases: visa may proceed with partial payment rules, but handover and Ejari for owner occupancy wait until completion.
