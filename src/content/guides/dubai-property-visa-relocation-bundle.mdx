## Why the Bundle Makes Sense

most expats approach Dubai property, visas, and relocation as separate decisions. They buy property as an investment first, then think about residency later. Or they get a work visa first, then consider buying. Or they arrive on a tourist visa planning to relocate and discover the visa-first-residence-first complexity.

The bundle approach treats all three as a coordinated plan:

Property selection → chosen to qualify for investor visa + suit your family's lifestyle
Visa → structured from the start around Golden Visa or investor visa depending on purchase price
Relocation → planned around the visa timeline so the family arrives with status, not on tourist entry

Done in sequence, the timeline shrinks from 6–8 months of ad-hoc steps to roughly 90 days, and there are no awkward gaps where family members sit in Dubai on tourist visas while residency paperwork crawls through approvals.

## Step 1: Property Selection Aligned with Visa Goal

### The Two Visa-Qualifying Tiers

AED 750,000+ → 2-Year Property Investor Visa

Properties from AED 750,000 in any Dubai freehold area qualify for the 2-year investor visa. Key rules:
- Property in your personal name (not company)
- Must be a freehold property (not leasehold)
- Can be off-plan if AED 750,000+ already paid to developer
- Must be residential (some commercial may qualify, verify)
- Multiple properties can be combined to reach the AED 750,000 threshold

AED 2,000,000+ → 10-Year Golden Visa (strongly recommended)

Properties of AED 2M or more in your personal name qualify for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa. Critical distinction from 2-year visa:
- Must be fully paid (not mortgaged for this purpose), the AED 2M value must be equity, not total purchase price with outstanding mortgage
- For mortgaged properties: your paid equity must reach AED 2M (e.g., property worth AED 3.5M with AED 1.5M mortgage = AED 2M equity → qualifies)
- Off-plan: if total price is AED 2M+ and paid in full or 50%+ paid depending on rules, verify current GDRFA/ICA policy
- Property valuation used is the purchase price or RERA valuation (whichever is applicable, often a RERA-registered valuer's certificate is required)

**The Golden Visa is unambiguously the right choice if your budget allows it:**

| Feature | 2-Year Investor Visa | 10-Year Golden Visa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visa duration | 2 years (renewable) | 10 years (renewable) |
| Employer-tied? | No, property-based | No, property-based |
| Minimum UAE stay | 6 months rule for renewal | Not required (can stay abroad) |
| Family sponsorship | Yes | Yes + daughters over 18 |
| Domestic worker sponsorship | Limited | Easier |
| Path to UAE residency | 2-year cycles | Stable 10-year platform |
| Property required | AED 750K+ | AED 2M+ (fully paid) |

The Golden Visa's "no minimum stay requirement" is significant for families with international careers, you can maintain UAE as a tax-efficient residence base while spending substantial time in other countries.

### Freehold Areas: Where Foreigners Can Buy

Not all Dubai land allows foreign ownership. Freehold designated zones, established by Decree No. 3 of 2006, are the only areas where non-UAE nationals can own property outright.

**Key freehold areas for families (property + livability + visa eligibility):**

| Area | Property Price Range | Lifestyle Fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dubai Marina | AED 800K–5M (apartments) | Young professionals, waterfront |
| Downtown Dubai | AED 1.2M–20M (apartments) | City centre, Burj Khalifa proximity |
| Dubai Hills Estate | AED 1.5M–12M (villas/apts) | Families, golf, schools nearby |
| Arabian Ranches | AED 2M–8M (villas) | Families, quiet, GEMS school |
| Jumeirah Village Circle | AED 550K–2.5M | Value, central location |
| Palm Jumeirah | AED 2M–50M+ | Luxury, beach, prestige |
| MBR City / Sobha Hartland | AED 1.2M–6M | Families, parks |
| Emaar Beachfront | AED 1.5M–4M | Beachfront, newer |
| Tilal Al Ghaf | AED 2M–10M (villas) | Family-focused masterplan |

For family relocation, Dubai Hills Estate and Arabian Ranches consistently top the livability ratings, golf, schools, parks, quiet streets, and community feel. Price entry for villas starts at AED 2.5M–3M, conveniently aligned with the Golden Visa threshold.

## Step 2: The Property Purchase Process

### Buying Timeline

| Stage | Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Search and offer | 1–4 weeks |
| MOU signing + deposit (5–10% of purchase price) | Week 2–3 |
| Finance (if mortgaged) | 3–6 weeks |
| DLD registration / title deed | 1–2 days (after finance cleared) |
| Total: ready to proceed property purchase | 6–10 weeks |

### Key Costs on Purchase

| Cost | Rate | On AED 2M Property |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DLD Transfer Fee | 4.0% | AED 80,000 |
| DLD Admin Fees | Fixed | AED 4,500 |
| Agent Commission | 2.0% | AED 40,000 |
| Mortgage Arrangement (if applicable) | 0.5–1.5% | AED 10,000–30,000 |
| Mortgage Registration | 0.25% of loan | Varies |
| Conveyancing / Trustee | Fixed | AED 4,000–6,000 |
| Total acquisition costs | ~6.5–7.5% | ~AED 128,000–150,000 |

**Budget for acquisition costs when planning the property purchase**, a AED 2M property requires approximately AED 130,000–150,000 in transaction costs, money that needs to be liquid before the DLD transfer.

### Off-Plan vs Ready Property for Visa

Ready property: Fastest path to visa. Once purchased, title deed is issued immediately and you can proceed with investor visa application within days.

Off-plan property: Visa is possible once 50%+ of purchase price is paid (verify current GDRFA rules) but the property is not physically available for occupancy. Families relocating want to live in the property, off-plan requires temporary rental during the construction period, adding cost and complexity.

**For the relocation bundle, ready (secondary market) property is generally preferable** unless:
- You are buying specifically as an investment (not to live in yet)
- Construction timeline is under 12 months and you can plan temporary rental during that period
- Launch price advantage is significant enough to justify the temporary rental cost

## Step 3: Investor Visa or Golden Visa Application

### After Title Deed: The Visa Application

Immediately after the title deed is issued by DLD:

Step 1: Property valuation
GDRFA requires a RERA-registered property valuer to confirm the property value for visa purposes. Cost: AED 2,500–5,000. This takes 3–5 business days.

Step 2: Apply for Golden Visa or Investor Visa
Applications go through GDRFA (General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs) in Dubai.

- Online via GDRFA Smart App or gdrfad.gov.ae
- Or through authorised typing centres (Amer)
- Or via your PRO/immigration consultant

Required documents:
- Title deed (certified copy)
- RERA property valuation certificate
- Passport (valid, minimum 6 months remaining)
- 6 passport photographs
- Medical fitness test results (see Step 3)

Step 3: Medical fitness test
Required for all UAE residents. Done at approved medical centres (al-Safar, Aster, etc.). Includes:
- Blood test (HIV, Hepatitis B and C, TB)
- Chest X-ray
- Cost: AED 350–500 per person
- Results: 1–3 business days

Step 4: Emirates ID application
Applied for simultaneously with visa. Biometrics (fingerprints, photo) taken at GDRFA/Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship centres.

Step 5: Visa stamping
Once visa is approved, it is stamped in your passport (or a UAE ID card serves as the residency document if you entered legally).

**Total visa timeline after title deed: 4–8 weeks for investor visa; 6–10 weeks for Golden Visa.**

### Cost of Investor Visa / Golden Visa

| Fee Item | Investor Visa (2yr) | Golden Visa (10yr) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visa application fee | AED 1,800–2,500 | AED 2,800–4,500 |
| Emirates ID | AED 400–600 | AED 400–600 |
| Property valuation | AED 2,500–5,000 | AED 2,500–5,000 |
| Medical test | AED 350–500 | AED 350–500 |
| Typing/PRO service | AED 500–2,000 | AED 500–2,000 |
| Total | AED 5,500–10,500 | AED 6,500–12,600 |

These are materially small costs relative to the property purchase, a rounding error in the total investment, but a significant one for timeline management.

## Step 4: Sponsoring Your Family

Once your own UAE residence visa is issued, you can sponsor:

Spouse: Regardless of nationality. Same-sex partners are not recognised under UAE law, unmarried couples do not have a formal sponsorship route.

Children under 18: Standard family sponsorship.

Sons 18–25: Can be sponsored as dependents if in full-time education (university student visa) or if not yet employed.

Daughters over 18: Can be sponsored on a Golden Visa regardless of marital status, a specific benefit of the Golden Visa.

Parents: Possible under Golden Visa, parents of Golden Visa holders can be sponsored as dependents.

### Family Sponsorship Process

For each family member:
1. Apply for a UAE entry permit (if joining from outside UAE)
2. Family member enters UAE on the entry permit
3. Medical fitness test (spouse and children over 11 typically)
4. Emirates ID biometrics
5. Residence visa stamping

**Timeline per family member:** 2–4 weeks after your own visa is issued.

**Cost per family member:** AED 3,000–6,000 per person (visa fees, medical, Emirates ID).

**Sponsor salary/income requirement:** You must demonstrate ability to support dependents. Income minimums historically around AED 3,000–4,000 monthly salary for standard dependent visas. Golden Visa property holders often satisfy this via property asset value rather than salary, verify current requirements with GDRFA.

## Step 5: The Practical Relocation

### Before Arrival

**3 months before move:**
- Identify target school(s) and submit applications immediately, waiting lists are real
- Open a UAE bank account (some banks allow account opening pre-arrival for certain nationalities; more commonly requires in-person on arrival)
- Research residential areas aligned with school location and lifestyle preferences
- Begin packing/shipping logistics, sea freight takes 3–6 weeks

**1 month before:**
- Book temporary accommodation (serviced apartment or hotel) for first 2–4 weeks
- Sort out health insurance, may need UAE health insurance for Emirates ID processing even before employer-provided cover
- Cancel or redirect home-country subscriptions, banking

### First Week in Dubai

**Days 1–3:**
- Medical fitness tests (GDRFA-approved medical centres), earliest possible
- SIM card, Du or Etisalat/e&; available at airport kiosks immediately on arrival
- Temporary driving (international permit valid for tourists; convert to UAE licence once resident)
- Open UAE bank account with Emirates ID once received (or use bank that opens on passport pre-Emirates ID, some banks do this)

**Days 3–7:**
- School visit appointments
- Begin apartment/villa viewing (if not already selected remotely)
- DEWA account for property if owner-occupying
- Grocery store orientation, Carrefour, Spinneys, Waitrose, Lulu, Viva are main chains

**Week 2–4:**
- Emirates ID collection (you will receive an SMS)
- Driving licence conversion (if from qualifying country)
- Children's school enrollment paperwork
- Shipping delivery coordination if sea freight arriving

### Finding Your Home

**Option A: Rent first, buy later**

most families rent for 6–12 months to experience neighbourhoods before committing to a purchase. The risk is the 4% DLD fee on any purchase, even renting for 12 months first adds only the rent cost, while a wrong property purchase costs 4% DLD plus 2% agent to exit.

**Option B: Buy immediately (the bundle approach)**

If you are committed to the Golden Visa route, buying before or immediately on arrival aligns property ownership, visa, and lifestyle in one transaction. The risk is buying under time pressure, rushing a property decision in a city you do not know yet.

**Mitigated bundle approach:** Research and shortlist properties extensively online before arriving (Dubai has excellent property portals, Property Finder, Bayut, Dubizzle). Arrange viewings in the first week. Make an offer in week 2–3 with a longer MOU-to-transfer period (60–90 days) giving you time to settle in and confirm the choice before the DLD transfer commits fully.

## The Right Sequence

### Optimal Sequence for the Bundle

1. **Research property remotely**: shortlist 5–10 properties matching criteria and Golden Visa qualification
2. **Visit Dubai on a property trip** (if pre-relocation): 4–7 days viewing and neighbourhood assessment
3. **Purchase property**: MOU, finance if needed, DLD transfer (6–10 weeks)
4. **Apply for Golden Visa**: simultaneously with DLD transfer if possible; visa processing during property completion
5. **Apply for family entry permits**: immediately after your own visa issued
6. **Family arrives**: ideally after your visa is issued and entry permits granted
7. **School enrollment**: should be in progress from Step 1 (applications submitted before arrival)
8. **DEWA, utilities, bank account**: first week of arrival

### What Not to Do

**Do not move family on tourist visas expecting to sort it out later.** Tourist visas allow 30–90 days in UAE and cannot convert to investor visa in-country without exiting and re-entering in most cases. The stress of a family living in Dubai without confirmed visa status while processing is real.

**Do not choose property based on price alone.** A AED 2M apartment in a location that does not suit your school commute or lifestyle will be frustrating within months. The area selection matters as much as the property itself.

**Do not defer school applications.** The waiting list problem in Dubai's top schools can delay a child's school start by a full academic year if applications are not submitted in advance. Submit school applications before your own arrival if at all possible.

## Dubai Property Visa Relocation Bundle — applicant scenarios

**Scenario A — property-linked residence bundle first filing:** Start medical tests and document attestation 3–4 weeks before travel for property-linked residence bundle. Do not sign a 12-month lease until property-linked residence bundle category is confirmed with your PRO.

**Scenario B — property-linked residence bundle family joining:** Sequence sponsor salary proof, housing fit-out, and school admissions for property-linked residence bundle. Dependent visas bottleneck on Ejari or municipality tenancy evidence tied to Dubai Property Visa Relocation Bundle.

**Scenario C — property-linked residence bundle renewal:** File at least 30 days before expiry on property-linked residence bundle status. Overstay fines and re-entry bans compound when you delay property-linked residence bundle paperwork.
