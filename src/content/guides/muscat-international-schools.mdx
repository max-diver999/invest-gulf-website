## Why school choice drives your address in Muscat

Muscat is car-dependent — no Dubai metro. School-run traffic between 06:45 and 07:45 drives family stress more than marina views.

Get the triangle right:

- Home: ITC (Al Mouj), Qurum, MQ, Al Khoud value belt
- School: often Qurum, Madinat Sultan Qaboos or Seeb corridors
- Office: Ruwi, CBD, airport corridor, port logistics

A 25-minute run at 7:15 can become 45 minutes at 7:40. Test both before you pay a deposit.

## School system overview: no KHDA dashboard

Unlike Dubai’s KHDA portal, Muscat has no single government dashboard with unified ratings and fee QR codes for all internationals.

What you use instead:

- Ministry of Education framework for private schools  (confirm current official rules)**
- School-published fee schedules (PDF, request annually)
- Parent WhatsApp groups (verify gossip against registrar letters)
- Accreditation bodies (e.g. BSO, CIS, NEASC, per school (confirm current official rules))

Academic year: typically **September–June, confirm per school; some offer January entry  (confirm current official rules).

Language: English instruction in most internationals; Arabic mandatory hours vary by curriculum and grade  (confirm current official rules)**.

## Age-band advice (2–18): condensed

### Early years (2–5)

Priority: proximity, shaded play, toilet training, feeder agreement to primary  (confirm current official rules).

- Fees OMR 2,500–4,500/year even inside “primary” schools  (confirm current official rules)
- Cut-off birthday often 31 August, school-specific
- Visit at **pick-up to see heat management

### Primary (6–11)

Priority: literacy/numeracy, homework load, Arabic hours, bullying policy.

- British KS2 sets IGCSE path, weak Year 6 limits options
- Class size, ask actual, not brochure cap

### Lower secondary (12–14)

Priority: subject breadth before IGCSE/MYP lock-in.

- Moving here before **IGCSE start is disruptive, avoid if possible

### IGCSE (15–16)

Priority: exam board (Cambridge vs Edexcel), predicted grades culture.

- Budget **OMR 200–600/year exam fees per child

### Sixth form: A-Level / IB DP (17–18)

Priority: university counsellor ratio; HL subjects for IB.

- Fees peak OMR 7,500–9,500/year tier
- US counsellor availability thinner than Dubai, ask university list, not logos

Children leaving Dubai often mourn:

## FAQ

**Are international schools in Muscat good quality?**  
Top tiers are solid for British and Indian tracks; breadth is smaller than Dubai, visit campuses.

**Are Muscat school fees lower than Dubai?**  
Typically yes at mid-tier, §18 and COL hub confirm; premium IB gap narrows.

**How many international schools are in Muscat?**  
Dozens of private options but far fewer premium British/IB campuses than Dubai, plan earlier.

**Which curriculum is most popular?**  
British largest share; Indian CBSE strongest value; American smaller pool.

**When should I apply?**  
6–12 months before September for competitive years; Indian schools sometimes shorter ** (confirm current official rules)**.

**Can I live in Al Mouj and school British in Qurum?**  
Yes, bus standard; test Sunday morning commute before buying ITC.

**Is there a KHDA equivalent in Oman?**  
No single consumer portal, rely on accreditation + visits + fee PDFs.

**Can I switch from Dubai British to Muscat mid-year?**  
Possible but hard in Years 10–11, align with registrar before resigning Dubai seat.

**Do schools accept children on investor residency visa?**  
Usually yes with valid residence documentation, ** (confirm current official rules)**.

**What budget for two children British mid?**  
OMR 900–1,100/month amortised tuition + bus; see [Muscat cost of living](/guides/muscat-cost-of-living/).

**Are IB Diploma programmes available?**  
Limited seats, confirm HL subjects and counsellor before move.

**Special needs support?**  
Variable and thinner than UAE, get written SEN policy; do not rely on tours alone.
