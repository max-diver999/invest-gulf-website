# UAE double tax treaties: how they work for property owners

The UAE has one of the world's largest double tax treaty networks, yet **zero personal income tax** at home. Property owners often misread that combination: treaties still matter for **home-country tax** on UAE rent, for **withholding on dividends and interest** when you become UAE tax resident, and for **Tax Residency Certificate (TRC)** claims. This guide explains mechanics in plain language; it is **not** personal tax advice. Confirm positions with qualified advisers in the UAE and your home jurisdiction.

## How do double tax treaties actually work?

Double tax treaties typically allocate which country may tax wages, dividends, interest, royalties, property income, and capital gains, then use exemption or credit rules so income is not taxed twice. A simple example is Country A at 25% and Country B at 30%, with the treaty capping combined burden. Invest Gulf underwriting separates zero local rent tax from foreign withholding relief.

| Income type | Common treaty outcome for UAE landlords |
| --- | --- |
| UAE rent | UAE taxing rights; local personal tax is zero |
| Foreign dividends with TRC | Possible reduced withholding abroad |
| US dividends for US persons | 30% default US withholding; no US-UAE DTA |

**UAE nuance:** personal income tax on UAE rent is **zero**. When a treaty gives the UAE primary taxing rights on UAE property income, the UAE charge is nil. A UK tax resident landlord may still owe UK tax on worldwide income because there is no UAE tax to credit away. Conversely, a **genuine UAE tax resident** receiving UK dividends may use the UAE-UK treaty and a **TRC** to access reduced UK withholding rates on those dividends. UAE-resident Indians generally outside Indian tax on UAE salary and rent but still use treaty rates on Indian-source dividends and interest with substance and anti-abuse rules in mind.

**France, Germany, Canada:** Exit taxes and extended limited tax liability (especially Germany) can follow you after departure. Treaties do not automatically erase those domestic rules. French and Canadian exit regimes deserve specialist review before moving.

**Russia:** UAE-Russia DTA relevant for Russian capital in UAE real estate; genuine UAE tax residence changes which domestic rules apply to UAE income.
