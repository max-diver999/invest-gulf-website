## Who qualifies as a freelancer under the Green Visa?

The UAE Green Visa freelancer track is a self-sponsored 5 years residency route that typically requires a recognised freelance permit plus annual income of at least AED 360,000. Foreign buyers of mobility (not property) use this path when they want residency without an employer sponsor, with work evidence via contracts and invoices.

Invest Gulf eligibility screens start with permit issuer recognition by ICP before income maths.

Freelancer Green Visa qualification in the UAE is a three-part test: hold an ICP-recognised freelance permit or professional licence, evidence annual income of at least AED 360,000, and show active work through contracts or invoices. Free zone permits are the usual route because they are faster than mainland licences and often need no physical office, with indicative annual fees from about AED 5,500 in Umm Al Quwain up to AED 14,000 to 18,000 at DMCC. Bank evidence commonly covers the last 6 months at roughly AED 30,000 per month, or a consistent AED 360,000 self-sufficiency balance when income is lumpy. Invest Gulf mobility reviews for freelancers treat the permit activity code as a hard match to client work before any ICP upload, because mismatched media-versus-tech descriptions are a frequent rejection trigger.

Applicants who already have UAE residency through an employer-sponsored work visa can switch to the Green Visa without leaving the country, provided they obtain a freelance permit before submitting the ICP application. Those applying from abroad must enter on a visit visa, complete the medical fitness test and biometrics in the UAE, then process the visa from within the country.

Core requirements checklist:

- Valid UAE freelance permit or DED professional licence
- AED 360,000 annual income evidence (or self-sufficiency)
- Contracts, invoices, or equivalent work proof

See also [Golden Visa vs Dubai residence visa](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/) for neighbouring tracks.

## What is the AED 360,000 income threshold and how does ICP check it?

The AED 360,000 income threshold is the standard freelancer Green Visa bar, usually shown as about AED 30,000 per month across the last 6 months of bank statements. Financial self-sufficiency at AED 360,000 held consistently can substitute when invoice income is uneven, and renewal every 5 years requires re-qualification.

Invest Gulf file reviews for foreign buyers overweight UAE-domiciled balances versus overseas accounts when both are available.

| Evidence type | Required format | Period covered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank statements | Stamped, signed by bank | Last 6 months |
| Client contracts | Signed originals or notarised copies | Active or last 12 months |
| Freelance invoices | Itemised, showing client and amount | Last 6 to 12 months |
| Free zone income certificate | Official letterhead | Issued within 3 months of application |

## Which free zones issue qualifying freelance permits?

Qualifying freelance permits are issued by ICP-recognised free zones with annual fees that typically range from AED 5,500 in lower-cost northern zones to AED 18,000 for premium Dubai options such as DMCC. Dubai Media City and Dubai Internet City often sit near AED 12,000 to 14,000 for media and tech freelancers among foreign buyers.

We shortlist for foreign buyers usually start with Meydan or Creative City when cost matters more than TECOM branding.

### Dubai free zones

Dubai Media City (DMC) issues freelance permits in media, journalism, publishing, broadcasting, and content creation. Annual fee runs approximately AED 12,000 to 14,000 including the permit and e-channel registration. One of the longest-established freelance ecosystems in the UAE; work sample or portfolio evidence typically required.

Dubai Internet City (DIC) covers technology, software development, IT consulting, and digital services. Same fee range as DMC. Both DMC and DIC are managed under TECOM Group, so companies operating in either zone are the natural client base.

Dubai Production City (formerly IMPZ) caters to media production, publishing, and printing. Annual permit from approximately AED 10,500. Less common than DMC but accepted for Green Visa purposes.

Meydan Free Zone is among the more accessible Dubai options, with freelance permits starting around AED 7,500 to 9,000 per year for consulting, marketing, design, and technology activities. No physical office required. Meydan has grown significantly in popularity among digital nomads specifically because of the lower cost.

Dubai Multi Commodities Centre (DMCC) issues individual trader and freelance permits for commodities, gold, tea, and certain professional services. Costs are higher, typically AED 14,000 to 18,000, but the DMCC branding carries weight for finance and commodities professionals. Scaling to a full company? See [DMCC company setup](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/).

### Abu Dhabi free zones

twofour54 is Abu Dhabi's media free zone and issues freelance permits for content creators, filmmakers, journalists, and digital media professionals. Annual fees are comparable to DMC at approximately AED 12,000 to 14,000. The zone has a physical creative campus in Abu Dhabi.

Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) is primarily a financial centre on Al Maryah Island. Individual professional licences are available but fees are substantially higher, from AED 25,000 per year, making it less cost-efficient for freelancers unless ADGM's financial services branding is specifically needed.

### Northern Emirates

Fujairah Creative City is consistently one of the lowest-cost free zones in the UAE for freelancers. Permits cover media, consulting, education, and professional services, starting around AED 6,000 to 8,000 per year. Creative City has a long track record and is accepted by ICP. Popular with freelancers who want to minimise licence cost while still qualifying for the Green Visa.

Sharjah Media City (Shams) offers a broad range of media and professional activities at competitive rates, typically AED 6,500 to 8,500 per year. Shams has grown rapidly and is commonly used by social media professionals, influencers, and digital marketing consultants.

Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ) covers manufacturing, education, services, and consulting. Freelance and individual licences start around AED 6,000 to 7,500. More relevant for consultants outside the media/tech categories.

UAQ Free Trade Zone (UAQ FTZ) in Umm Al Quwain is one of the lowest-cost options available, with freelance permits from AED 5,500 per year. The zone is less prominent than Dubai or Sharjah options but is accepted for ICP applications. Suitable for freelancers prioritising cost over prestige.

## What documents does the permit and Green Visa file require?

Document packs for the freelancer Green Visa typically include a valid freelance permit, 6 months of bank statements evidencing AED 360,000 annual income, contracts or invoices, and UAE medical plus Emirates ID steps. Foreign buyers of residency should allow attestation time for overseas bank statements before the ICP upload.

Invest Gulf document checklists separate free-zone permit items from ICP residency items so nothing is uploaded twice with mismatched dates.

For the free zone freelance permit:

- Passport copy (valid for at least 6 months)
- Passport-size photo
- CV or professional profile
- Portfolio or work samples (for creative/media zones)
- Application form and fee payment

For the ICP Green Visa application (freelancer track):

- Valid passport and UAE-spec photo
- Valid UAE freelance permit or professional licence
- Bank statements covering the last 6 months
- Client contracts and/or freelance invoices
- Medical fitness certificate and Emirates ID biometrics

Notarisation requirements vary. Documents issued by UAE authorities do not require notarisation. Foreign contracts and bank statements issued outside the UAE may need to be apostilled or attested depending on the country of issue.

## How does the Green Visa process run step by step?

The Green Visa process is a sequenced ICP filing that typically completes in 3 to 5 weeks when documents are clean, with entry permit decisions often in 3 to 5 working days after upload. Medical results usually return in 2 to 3 working days, and Emirates ID issuance commonly takes 5 to 7 working days.

End-to-end freelancer Green Visa timing typically runs 3 to 5 weeks when the ICP file is complete, after an entry permit decision in about 3 to 5 working days, medical results in 2 to 3 working days, and Emirates ID issuance in 5 to 7 working days. Government charges for medical, Emirates ID, and ICP fees usually total AED 1,500 to 1,800 every 5 years, while health insurance adds AED 600 to 2,000 per year and the freelance permit remains the main annual outlay. Tax residency still requires about 183 days of UAE presence for a Ministry of Finance certificate, so the visa stamp alone is not a home-country exit plan. Family sponsorship can start after residency if a suitable home, often a 2-bedroom when children under 18 are included, and income proof are ready.

Invest Gulf sequencing for foreign buyers puts income evidence assembly before the ICP portal upload.

1. Gather 6 months of statements showing about AED 30,000+ per month plus contracts
2. Apply for the Green Visa entry permit via ICP smart services or a typing centre
3. Complete medical fitness testing inside the UAE
4. Book Emirates ID biometrics (often 15 to 20 minutes at the centre)
5. Complete visa stamping or e-visa linkage to Emirates ID

Total Green Visa government fees for medical, Emirates ID, and ICP charges typically land around AED 1,500 to 1,800 every 5 years, while the freelance permit is the larger annual cost at about AED 5,500 to 18,000 depending on free zone. Health insurance usually adds AED 600 to 2,000 per year.

Invest Gulf cost models for foreign buyers treat permit fees as the line that decides whether a 3 years stay beats staying on an employer visa.

## Can freelancers sponsor family on a Green Visa?

Family sponsorship on a freelancer Green Visa is allowed from day one for a spouse and dependent children when accommodation, insurance, and income evidence meet standard UAE rules. A minimum 2-bedroom home is commonly expected when sponsoring children under 18, and income proof is assessed again at sponsorship for foreign buyers relocating as a household.

Invest Gulf family files flag housing size before ICP sponsorship upload, especially when the main applicant still sits near the AED 360,000 income floor.

Sponsorship checklist:

- Suitable home (often 2-bedroom with children under 18)
- Valid health insurance for each dependant
- Income evidence refreshed at sponsorship time

For a broader overview of UAE residency options for families and dependants, see [Golden Visa vs Dubai residence visa](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/).

## Is tax residency separate from the Green Visa?

UAE tax residency is separate from holding a Green Visa and typically requires 183 days of physical presence in the UAE in a calendar year under Ministry of Finance rules. A Tax Residency Certificate usually follows after presence and banking substance are established, not from the visa stamp alone for foreign buyers leaving high-tax homes.

Invest Gulf tax notes keep visa stamps and 183 days presence on different checklist rows.

Relevant rules:

- UAE Ministry of Finance Resolution No. 27 of 2023 sets individual tax residency criteria
- Primary test: 183 days of physical presence per calendar year
- Secondary ties test can matter when days sit slightly under 183 days

Sequence for freelancers: Green Visa and UAE bank account first, then 183 days presence, then TRC application. Corporate substance questions also appear in [DMCC company setup](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/).

## Why do freelancer Green Visa files stall?

Freelancer Green Visa files typically stall when permit activity codes do not match client contracts, when permit validity falls below about 60 days at filing, or when foreign bank statements lack translation and attestation. Missing DHA or DoH-approved health insurance also blocks activation at the final step.

Invest Gulf rejection reviews still see activity-code mismatches more often than income maths errors.

Common stalls:

- Permit lists media consulting while contracts show software work
- Permit renews annually and lapses mid-ICP review
- Overseas statements need translation or apostille
- Insurance lacks DHA or DoH approval

Insider tip: Renew the freelance permit so at least 60 days remain before you hit submit on ICP, because a permit that expires between upload and approval is a clean administrative rejection even when income evidence is perfect.

## How does the Green Visa compare with an employer-sponsored visa?

The Green Visa is a self-sponsored alternative to employer visas, with 5 years validity and unrestricted client mobility once issued. Over a 5 years hold, permit fees of AED 5,500 to 18,000 per year plus roughly AED 1,500 to 1,800 in government charges replace sponsor dependency for freelancers staying 3 years or more.

Invest Gulf comparisons for foreign buyers favour Green Visa once the stay plan exceeds about 3 years.

| Item | Employer-sponsored visa | Green Visa (freelancer) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visa fees | Typically paid by sponsor | AED 1,500 to 1,800 self-paid |
| Freelance permit | Not required | AED 5,500 to 18,000 per year |
| Dependence on sponsor | Yes | None |
| Job mobility | Restricted | Unrestricted |
| Validity | Tied to employment | 5 years, renewable |

For a full comparison of UAE visa types, see [Golden Visa vs Dubai residence visa](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/).

## What should you prepare for renewal at year 5?

Green Visa renewal at year 5 typically costs about AED 1,500 to 1,800 again for medical, Emirates ID, and ICP fees, while the freelance permit still renews every 12 months. Start renewal 60 to 90 days before expiry, because overstay fines are AED 25 per day after short grace limits for foreign buyers who leave renewal late.

Invest Gulf renewal calendars put permit anniversary dates beside the 5 years visa stamp date.

Renewal checklist:

- Fresh income evidence at AED 360,000 track
- Valid permit with months of runway
- Updated insurance and Emirates ID appointment

The freelancer Green Visa is best suited to self-employed professionals earning above AED 360,000 annually who want 5 years of residency without an employer sponsor. We compare this path with Golden Visa property tracks when foreign buyers need both mobility and a longer hold.

Choose Green Visa if:

- Annual freelance income clears AED 360,000
- You want 5 years without an employer sponsor
- Client mobility matters more than property-linked residency
