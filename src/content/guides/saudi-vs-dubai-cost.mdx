## Currency math: do this first or break the spreadsheet

SAR and AED both peg to USD. They look interchangeable on a napkin. They are not.

| Conversion | Rate (indicative) |
|---|---|
| SAR → AED | × ~0.98 |
| SAR → USD | ÷ ~3.75 |
| AED → USD | ÷ ~3.67 |
| SAR 10,000/mo | ~AED 9,800/mo |
| SAR 30,000/mo | ~AED 29,400/mo |
| AED 40,000/mo | ~SAR 40,800/mo |

Fatal error: assuming SAR 15,000 rent "equals" AED 15,000 rent, SAR is slightly stronger in AED terms; always multiply by ~0.98.

## Transport and cars

Both markets are car-first for families.

| Cost line | Riyadh SAR/mo | Dubai AED/mo |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel (one sedan) | 600–1,000 | 500–900 |
| Second car | +600–1,000 | +500–900 |
| Salik / tolls | 0 | 200–600 |
| Parking (tower) | Often included compound | 200–500 |
| Insurance (sedan) | 2,500–4,500/yr amortised | 3,000–5,500/yr amortised |
| RTA registration amortised | N/A | 1,200–2,000/yr |

SUV culture: Toyota/Ford popular in Saudi compounds, resale deep; budget SAR 120,000–180,000 mid SUV purchase or employer car allowance.

Ride-hail: Careem/Uber viable in Riyadh/Jeddah for singles; families still buy cars for school runs.

## When Saudi is genuinely cheaper

- Rent-only comparison at non-premium compound tier
- Indian curriculum schools vs Dubai British premium
- No alcohol spend (for households that would spend in UAE)
- Employer-provided compound villa + schooling in package
- Single professional in city apartment without schools

## When Dubai wins value

- Sharjah/RAK commuter saves 30–40% rent, no Saudi equivalent at the same job market
- School waitlist failure, second-choice options abundant in UAE
- Spouse income offsets COL, UAE job market deeper
- Liquidity, selling lease or property faster on exit

## Vision 2030 packages: read the fine print

Saudi offers often headline tax-free + housing + schooling. Model:

| Package line | Question |
|---|---|
| Housing cap | SAR ceiling vs actual compound quote |
| School allowance | Per child per year, top-up from pocket? |
| Transport | Car allowance vs company car |
| Tickets | Annual class, economy vs business |
| End of service | Saudi Labour Law calc in writing |

A SAR 45,000 package with uncapped British schools beats AED 50,000 with AED 40,000/school cap.

## FAQ

Is Saudi Arabia cheap for expats?  
Not at premium tier. Mid rent can save vs Dubai; schools often match UAE fees.

SAR or AED, which peg is better for savings?  
Nearly identical USD pegs, salary currency and package inclusions matter more.

Riyadh or Jeddah for COL?  
Similar mid totals; Jeddah humidity and Corniche premium vary lines; see R72.

Does 0% tax make Saudi automatically ahead?  
UAE also 0% employment income tax typically, compare net package after schools.

## Entertainment and travel: Saudi vs Dubai spend

Saudi has no legal alcohol retail, household entertainment budget reshapes:

| Spend type | Riyadh SAR/mo | Dubai AED/mo |
|---|---|---|
| Cinema / events | 400–1,200 | 400–1,200 |
| Licensed dining/nightlife | N/A alcohol line | 800–3,000+ |
| Gym / padel | 300–800 | 400–1,000 |
| Weekend UAE/Bahrain trip | 1,500–4,000 (quarterly amortised) | 800–2,000 domestic |

Riyadh Season and Formula 1 tickets spike months, model as annual ÷ 12, not every month.

**Dubai** brunch and licensed venue culture is a **recurring COL line** Saudi replaces with **compound events + travel**.

**Net spend** after savings: Saudi packages sometimes **higher gross** with **housing included**, compare **pocket money** after rent/schools, not headline.

Gross **SAR 42,000**, villa provided, schooling capped **SAR 70,000/child** → effective COL **SAR 24,000–28,000** pocket after schools vs Dubai **AED 38,000** all-in, Saudi **wins pocket** if lifestyle accepted.

### Archetype B: Dubai Marina British school family
