## How do SAR and AED compare when both peg to USD?

SAR and AED both peg to USD, so SAR 1 is roughly AED 0.98 and SAR 10,000 per month maps to about AED 9,800, which we treat as the first conversion step before any Riyadh versus Dubai rent story for foreign buyers comparing a 12 month assignment.

| Conversion | Approx value |
| --- | --- |
| SAR 1 | AED 0.98 |
| AED 1 | SAR 1.02 |
| SAR 10,000/month | AED 9,800/month |
| SAR 30,000/month | AED 29,400/month |

Always convert before comparing headline numbers from recruiter slides.

## How do compound and apartment trade-offs compare?

Compounds typically bundle security and maintenance into one SAR rent line, while Dubai apartments often split DEWA, district cooling, and chiller accounts that can add AED 400 to 900 in summer, so we add those lines before foreign buyers call Saudi cheaper here. Confirm live listings and school invoices before you sign.

| Profile | Saudi lean | Dubai lean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Young single | Apartment near office | Metro-linked tower |
| Family with toddlers | Gated compound | JVC or Mirdif villa |
| Senior exec | Premium compound | Downtown or Palm fringe |

Decision rule: families with young children often pay the compound premium in both markets for 12 months or more.

## How do school fees compare for two-child British mid-tier families?

Two-child British mid-tier fees typically amortise near SAR 10,500 to 13,500 per month in Riyadh versus AED 8,700 to 12,000 in Dubai once transport is included, so our research treats schools as the line that erases rent savings.

| Line | Riyadh SAR/mo | Dubai AED/mo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuition amortised | 9,000-11,000 | 7,500-10,000 |
| Transport/uniforms | 1,500-2,500 | 1,200-2,000 |
| School subtotal | ~10,500-13,500 | ~8,700-12,000 |

Package trap: employer quotes SAR 40,000 gross but caps schooling at SAR 60,000 per year per child, leaving SAR 3,000-plus monthly from pocket. → [Riyadh international schools](/guides/riyadh-international-schools/)

**Insider tip:** Match curriculum tier before you celebrate a SAR rent discount; comparing Riyadh compound plus CBSE with Dubai British premium is not a 15 to 30% like-for-like save on Invest Gulf family spreadsheets.

Ride-hail works for singles; families still buy cars for school gates in both cities.

## When is Saudi genuinely cheaper, and when does Dubai win?

Saudi is typically cheaper when compound rent plus CBSE or Indian mid-tier schools stack together, while Dubai wins when spouse job depth, Sharjah or RAK commute savings, or widest school choice matter more than a 10 to 25% rent gap that Invest Gulf stress-tests for foreign buyers. ## What hidden costs appear in both markets?

Hidden costs typically include school capital levies, furniture import or setup, summer camps of SAR 2,000 to 8,000 or AED 2,000 to 10,000 per child, and cooling deposits that hit month two, so we keep a six week rent buffer for foreign buyers here. Confirm live listings and school invoices before you sign.

| Cost | Saudi note | Dubai note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School capital levy | Some premium schools | Some KHDA schools |
| Summer camp | SAR 2,000-8,000/child | AED 2,000-10,000/child |
| Cooling / humidity | Jeddah SAR 200-500 extra AC | AED 400-900 cooling common |
| Domestic help | SAR 1,500-3,500 full-time | AED 2,500-4,500 live-out nanny band |

## How should you read Vision 2030 and salary packages?

Vision 2030 packages typically inflate housing allowances that can expire after 2 years, and Dubai education caps create the same year-two cliff in AED, so we ask for twelve months of net local payslips before foreign buyers accept a gross USD narrative. Confirm live listings and school invoices before you sign.

| Trap | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Housing allowance expiry | Does year three still cover compound premium? |
| Education cap | SAR or AED per child versus real invoice |
| Gross USD quote | Net SAR/AED sample payslip |

End-of-service gratuity is not monthly COL but still affects exit liquidity on a 3 year assignment.
