## Riyadh compound versus Dubai apartment economics

Compounds bundle security, maintenance, and sometimes utilities allowances into one rent line. Dubai apartments often split DEWA, district cooling, and chiller accounts. When comparing SAR 12,000 compound rent with AED 15,000 Dubai rent, add AED 400-900 cooling in Dubai summers before calling Saudi cheaper.

## Jeddah and Red Sea humidity line items

Jeddah corniche towers fight humidity with heavier AC load. Budget SAR 200-500/month extra electricity versus comparable Riyadh square meters in peak summer. School runs along the corniche still require cars; there is no Dubai-style metro shortcut.

## Domestic help and driver costs

Live-in help packages in Saudi often include visa sponsorship and housing on compound. Dubai may use part-time nanny shares. Model SAR 1,500-3,500/month for full-time help in Riyadh versus AED 2,500-4,500 for live-out nanny five days in Dubai, verify with your compound rules.

## Groceries price bands (mid tier)

Tamimi and Danube in Riyadh track close to Carrefour and Spinneys in Dubai once currency converted. Imported US and EU goods carry premium in both. Local produce and halal meat remain affordable anchors.

## Healthcare top-ups

Employer cover is standard in both. Dental and optical riders cost SAR 200-600/month family equivalent in Saudi versus AED 250-700 in Dubai at mid insurers. Maternity riders need explicit check before pregnancy planning.

## Planning note for June 2026 relocations

Employers still mix USD-quoted packages with BHD or SAR payroll. Ask finance to show twelve months of net payslips in local currency, not only gross expat narrative. School registrars and landlords both prefer local currency cheques; FX gains on paper do not pay EWA bills. Keep six weeks rent liquid after deposits clear. Review [Gulf expat living comparison](/guides/gulf-expat-living-comparison/) if your shortlist still includes Qatar or Oman alongside this destination. Trailing spouses should visit preferred grocery, school gate, and clinic locations once before shipment; photos online miss heat, humidity, and parking friction that change monthly spend.

Saudi weekend traffic to malls peaks Thursday nights; Dubai peaks Friday brunch hours. Entertainment timing affects fuel and dining lines in ways spreadsheets hide.

Compound maintenance fees sometimes appear only in Arabic lease annexes; translate before sign. Dubai service charge disclosures are more familiar to British expats but still vary by tower.

Insurance renewals on two cars hit the same month as school re-enrollment in both countries; smooth cash flow with a sinking fund line in your model.

When recruiters quote thirty percent Saudi savings, ask which Dubai comparator they used. Marina to Olaya is not Sharjah to Riffa. Match tier, match school curriculum, match compound versus tower cooling lines, then convert SAR to AED at peg. Only then compare totals for your household, not for a generic expat poster.

## Salary packaging traps in both markets

Gross USD quotes hide local deductions. Ask for net SAR or AED payslip sample. Education caps, housing caps, and one-off relocation allowances change year-two COL dramatically if you ignore expiry dates.

End-of-service gratuity is not monthly COL but affects exit liquidity; still note it when comparing three-year assignments.

If your employer offers company school, model bus and activity fees anyway; they rarely cover full British premium stack.
