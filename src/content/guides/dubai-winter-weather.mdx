# Dubai Winter Weather: Month-by-Month Guide for Expats and Visitors

> Planning note: Temperature figures are long-term averages based on NCMS (UAE National Centre of Meteorology) historical data. Year-to-year variation is real, a warm winter can push January highs to 28°C, while a cold spell can keep February nights near 10°C.

## Why Dubai Winter Is the Reason Expats Stay

From November through March, daytime highs usually sit between 22°C and 28°C, humidity drops, and the city moves outdoors. This is when beach clubs fill on weekday mornings, desert safari season peaks, farmers markets run every weekend, and new residents see why long-term expats plan social life around October to April.

Winter is also peak tourist season and the busiest window for outdoor events, which affects hotel rates, flight prices, and short-term rental demand if you own property in Dubai.

## What are Dubai winter temperatures by month?

Long-term NCMS averages for Dubai city (coastal). Actual days can run 3°C to 5°C above or below these bands.

| Month | Typical high (°C) | Typical low (°C) | Rain days (avg) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| November | 31 | 20 | 1 to 2 |
| December | 26 | 16 | 2 to 3 |
| January | 24 | 14 | 3 to 5 |
| February | 25 | 15 | 3 to 4 |
| March | 28 | 17 | 2 to 3 |

Evenings feel cooler than the afternoon number suggests because desert radiates heat quickly after sunset. January and February nights often need a light jacket outdoors after 9 PM.

## How warm is the sea in Dubai each winter month?

Arabian Gulf water lags air temperature by several weeks. Shallow beach zones feel warmer than open water.

| Month | Sea surface (°C) | Swim comfort (typical) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November | 28 to 29 | Warm, summer-like |
| December | 25 to 27 | Comfortable for most |
| January | 22 to 24 | Cool but swimmable |
| February | 22 to 23 | Coolest swim month |
| March | 24 to 26 | Warming quickly |

Unheated apartment pools track sea temperatures closely. Premium hotels with heated pools stay busy in January and February.

## When does it rain in Dubai in winter?

The practical implication for new residents: do not attempt to drive through flooded underpasses. Depth is impossible to judge visually and vehicles regularly become stranded. The Dubai Roads and Transport Authority (RTA) typically issues alerts via the Dubai Now app and traffic radio during and after significant rainfall.

Following the unusual April 2024 rainfall event (approximately 250 mm in 24 hours, two and a half years of average annual rainfall in a single day), the government accelerated infrastructure investment in drainage capacity. The city handles typical winter rain events well; exceptional events remain a wildcard.

Practical planning: Check the UAE weather app or NCM app forecast the night before any significant outdoor event from November through March.

| Month | Typical rain (mm, long-term avg) | What to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November | 3 to 8 | Isolated showers, rare all-day rain |
| December | 8 to 15 | Short bursts, cooler air after storms |
| January | 15 to 25 | Wettest month, flash-flood risk spots |
| February | 10 to 20 | Similar to January, fewer events |
| March | 5 to 12 | Tail end of season, clearing skies |

## How does winter humidity feel in Dubai?

Relative humidity in winter is often 50% to 65% in the afternoon versus 70% to 90% in August. Lower moisture makes 26°C feel pleasant rather than sticky.

| Month | Typical afternoon humidity | How it feels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November | 55% to 65% | Dry warmth |
| December | 55% to 60% | Clear, crisp evenings |
| January | 50% to 60% | Best outdoor comfort |
| February | 50% to 58% | Similar to January |
| March | 52% to 62% | Warming, still dry |

Many residents notice better skin and hair condition in winter compared with constant air-conditioned dryness in summer offices.

## What to Pack for Dubai Winter

**Indoor/mall:** Air conditioning in Dubai malls, hotels, and offices runs year-round regardless of outdoor temperature. Even on a 15°C January day, malls are often chilled to 20 to 21°C. Carry a light layer if you are cold-sensitive.

**Footwear:** Sandals, trainers, and smart casual shoes all work. Wellington boots or waterproof shoes are worth packing for rain-prone January visits.

Sun protection: Winter sun in Dubai remains strong enough to burn. UV index of 5 to 7 in December and January is still significant. Pack and use SPF, particularly for beach days.

### Packing Checklist: 2-Week Winter Dubai Visit

- 7 to 10 light T-shirts or tops
- 2 to 3 light trousers or smart casual bottoms
- 1 to 2 light dresses or shorts for beach/pool
- 1 light jacket or blazer (evenings)
- 1 medium-weight coat or warm layer (January/February)
- 1 lightweight rain jacket or packable mac
- Swimwear (multiple sets, beach clubs are a feature)
- Sun hat and sunglasses
- SPF 30 to 50 sunscreen
- Comfortable walking shoes and sandals
- Smart shoes if attending higher-end venues

## Which outdoor activities work each winter month?

Beach and watersports: Consistently excellent from November through March. Paddleboarding, kayaking, and jet skiing all operate peak-season from November. Kite surfing season runs November through April along the Jumeirah coast.

Desert excursions: Morning desert safaris, overnight camping, dune biking, and camel trekking all operate comfortably in winter. Morning temperatures in the desert can drop to 8 to 12°C in January, making early departures cold, pack a warm layer.

**Cycling:** The Al Qudra cycle track and Mushrif Park cycle routes become usable from October onwards. Competitive cycling events like the Dubai Ride typically fall in November.

**Running:** Dubai Marathon runs in January, drawing international participants. The cooler months see numerous 5K and 10K events across Dubai parks and the waterfront.

**Golf:** Dubai's golf courses peak in winter. Major tournaments including the DP World Tour Championship fall in November. Course conditions are at their best and early morning rounds become genuinely enjoyable.

Hiking and Wadi walks: Day trips to Hatta and the Hajar Mountains are best undertaken from October to March. Summer makes these impossible due to heat. Winter mornings in Hatta deliver temperatures of 15 to 20°C, a world away from the coast.

## Why winter matters for families relocating to Dubai

For families with children evaluating Dubai as a relocation destination, winter is when the lifestyle proposition reaches its peak. School-age children can play outdoors, families can spend weekends at the beach without heat stress, and the city's enormous park and outdoor infrastructure gets used.

## Which weather apps and alerts should you use?

Download the official UAE NCM app for rain, fog, and sandstorm warnings. Dubai Now aggregates RTA road alerts during heavy rain. Windy or Windfinder help kite surfers track shamal wind windows along Jumeirah.

Sandstorm awareness: winter can bring occasional shamal winds and sandstorms, though less frequently than in spring. A haboob (dust storm) can reduce visibility dramatically and make outdoor activity inadvisable. The NCM app provides sandstorm warnings with reasonable lead time.

| App / source | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| NCM (official) | Rain, fog, sandstorm alerts |
| Dubai Now | RTA flooding and road closures |
| Windy / Windfinder | Coastal wind for watersports |
| Apple / Google Weather | Day-ahead planning (cross-check NCM) |

## Extra winter weather questions answered

December and November are most popular among visitors who want reliable warmth (24 to 28°C) with minimal rain risk and peak city energy. January and February offer slightly cooler conditions that some prefer, with lower hotel rates outside the Christmas to New Year premium period.

### Can you use the outdoor pool in winter?

Most apartment complex and hotel pools are unheated. Water temperatures typically drop to around 22 to 25°C in January and February. Heated pools exist at premium hotels. For residents in unheated pools, January swimming is possible but cool, beach swimming in shallower water is often warmer.

### Is the desert cold in winter?

Yes, significantly cooler than the coast. Desert overnight temperatures in January can drop to 8 to 12°C, and pre-dawn is genuinely cold. Overnight camping requires proper sleeping bags rated to at least 5°C, warm clothing for mornings, and a plan for warmth at sunrise before temperatures climb.

### How does winter weather affect driving in Dubai?

Rain is the main winter driving risk. Dubai's road drainage has improved but heavy downpours can still cause flooding on underpasses. Reduced visibility in rain, combined with road surfaces that become slippery after prolonged dry periods, makes caution essential. Sandstorms require pulling off the road and waiting. Fog in the early morning is an occasional hazard from November through February, particularly on elevated highways.

## Key takeaways for Dubai winter

Plan outdoor life for November to March: beaches, desert camps, golf, and school playtime all peak in this window. Pack layers for malls and evenings, rain gear for January, and SPF year-round.

If you are scouting Dubai as a relocation base, visit at least once in winter and once in summer so heat and humidity expectations stay realistic. Winter shows the lifestyle sell; summer shows why air-conditioned commute and villa pool matter. Most expat families schedule school tours and neighbourhood walks in January or February when walking outdoors is comfortable.
