## Why is Dubai winter the season expats stay for?

Dubai winter from November through March typically means daytime highs between 22°C and 28°C, lower humidity, and about 5 months when the city moves outdoors. Beach clubs fill on weekday mornings, desert safari season peaks, and farmers markets run every weekend for Invest Gulf relocatees.

- Season length: about 5 months (November-March)
- Daytime highs: roughly 22-28°C
- Peak use: beaches, desert camps, outdoor dining

Winter is also peak tourist season and the busiest window for outdoor events, which affects hotel rates, flight prices, and short-term rental demand if you own property in Dubai.

Dubai winter weather from November through March is the city's premium outdoor window: long-term coastal averages put daytime highs near 22-28°C, January nights often near 12-15°C, and sea surfaces near 22-24°C in the coolest swim months. Most of the annual 94 mm of rainfall falls in this season, with January typically the wettest month at roughly 15-25 mm and about 3 to 5 heavier shower days rather than weeks of drizzle. Humidity often sits near 50-65% in the afternoon versus 70-90% in August, which is why 26°C feels pleasant rather than sticky. Invest Gulf relocation planning treats at least one winter visit as mandatory before a family commits to a school-year move, because beach clubs, desert camps, park play, and rooftop dining all concentrate in these 5 months while summer forces most of that life indoors.

## What are Dubai winter temperatures by month?

Dubai winter temperatures by month typically mean November highs near 31°C cooling to January highs near 24°C across about 5 months, with night lows around 14-16°C in the coolest weeks. Long-term NCMS coastal averages can run warmer or cooler by a few degrees for Invest Gulf planners.

| Month | Typical high (°C) | Typical low (°C) | Rain days (avg) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| November | 31 | 20 | 1 to 2 |
| December | 26 | 16 | 2 to 3 |
| January | 24 | 14 | 3 to 5 |
| February | 25 | 15 | 3 to 4 |
| March | 28 | 17 | 2 to 3 |

Rain-day averages run about 1 to 5 days per month across the cool season. Evenings feel cooler than the afternoon number suggests because desert radiates heat quickly after sunset. January and February nights often need a light jacket outdoors after 9 PM.

## How warm is the sea in Dubai each winter month?

Arabian Gulf sea temperatures in winter typically mean 28-29°C in November, cooling to 22-24°C in January and February for about 2 months of coolest swimming, then warming again toward 24-26°C in March. Shallow beach zones feel warmer than open water for Invest Gulf residents.

| Month | Sea surface (°C) | Swim comfort (typical) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November | 28 to 29 | Warm, summer-like |
| December | 25 to 27 | Comfortable for most |
| January | 22 to 24 | Cool but swimmable |
| February | 22 to 23 | Coolest swim month |
| March | 24 to 26 | Warming quickly |

Coolest swim conditions last about 2 months in January and February. Premium hotels with heated pools stay busy across those weeks.

## When does it rain in Dubai in winter?

Dubai winter rain typically means most rainfall falls across about 5 months from November through March, with January the wettest month and roughly 3 to 5 heavier shower days rather than weeks of drizzle. Do not drive through flooded underpasses; RTA alerts via Dubai Now matter after significant rainfall for Invest Gulf drivers.

| Month | Typical rain (mm, long-term avg) | What to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November | 3 to 8 | Isolated showers, rare all-day rain |
| December | 8 to 15 | Short bursts, cooler air after storms |
| January | 15 to 25 | Wettest month, flash-flood risk spots |
| February | 10 to 20 | Similar to January, fewer events |
| March | 5 to 12 | Tail end of season, clearing skies |

Following the unusual April 2024 rainfall event (approximately 250 mm in 24 hours, about 2.5 years of average annual rainfall in a single day), the government accelerated drainage investment. The city handles typical winter rain events well; exceptional events remain a wildcard. Check the UAE weather app or NCM app the night before outdoor plans from November through March.

## How does winter humidity feel in Dubai?

Winter humidity in Dubai typically means afternoon relative humidity near 50-65% versus 70-90% in August, so mid-20s daytime weather feels dry and pleasant rather than sticky. January and February often sit near 50-60% for about 2 months, which is the best outdoor comfort band for Invest Gulf families.

| Month | Typical afternoon humidity | How it feels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November | 55% to 65% | Dry warmth |
| December | 55% to 60% | Clear, crisp evenings |
| January | 50% to 60% | Best outdoor comfort |
| February | 50% to 58% | Similar to January |
| March | 52% to 62% | Warming, still dry |

Many residents notice better skin and hair condition in winter compared with constant air-conditioned dryness in summer offices.

## What should you pack for Dubai winter?

Dubai winter packing typically means light daytime clothes for cool-season highs, plus a jacket for evenings and malls chilled year-round, across a stay of 1 to 2 weeks or a full season. Add a warmer layer for January or February nights, light rain gear for 3 to 5 shower days, SPF, and swimwear for Invest Gulf visitors.

- 7 to 10 light T-shirts or tops for a 1 to 2 week trip
- 2 to 3 light trousers or smart casual bottoms
- 1 light jacket or blazer for evenings
- 1 medium-weight layer for January/February
- 1 lightweight rain jacket for 3 to 5 shower days
- Swimwear, sun hat, sunglasses, SPF

Insider tip: Pack the rain layer even if the 10-day forecast looks dry; January can deliver 3 to 5 heavy shower days, and mall AC feels colder than the outdoor afternoon high after sunset.

## Which weather apps and alerts should you use?

Weather apps for Dubai winter typically mean the official UAE NCM app for rain, fog, and sandstorm warnings across 5 months, plus Dubai Now for RTA flooding alerts during heavy rain. Windy or Windfinder help kite surfers track shamal windows along Jumeirah for Invest Gulf watersports users.

| App / source | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| NCM (official) | Rain, fog, sandstorm alerts |
| Dubai Now | RTA flooding and road closures |
| Windy / Windfinder | Coastal wind for watersports |
| Apple / Google Weather | Day-ahead planning (cross-check NCM) |

Sandstorms are less frequent than in spring but can still cut visibility across 5 months; pull off the road and wait when NCM warns.
