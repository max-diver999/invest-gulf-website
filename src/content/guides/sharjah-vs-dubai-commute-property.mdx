## What should buyers verify on maintenance and property management for commuter r?

Parking management:
Commuter tenants require guaranteed parking spots. Buildings with insufficient parking or expensive parking fees lose rental competitiveness. Monthly parking charges above AED 200 significantly impact tenant retention in price-sensitive commuter segments.

Tenant communication preferences:
Dubai commuter tenants often prefer:
- Digital communication (WhatsApp, email) over phone calls
- Evening/weekend availability for maintenance appointments

Property management fees:
Sharjah property management companies typically charge:
- Long-term rental management: 5-8% of annual rent

Preventive maintenance strategies:
Buildings with high commuter tenant percentages should prioritize:
- AC maintenance programs (units run continuously during work hours)
- Elevator reliability (essential for daily schedules)
