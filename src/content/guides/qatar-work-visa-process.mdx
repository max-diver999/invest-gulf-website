# Qatar Work Visa Process 2026: Employer Steps, QID & Timeline

**TL;DR:** Qatar's work visa is **100% employer-driven** — you cannot self-sponsor. Normal path: **entry permit → land → medical → biometrics → QID**, usually **4–8 weeks**. Government fees run about **QAR 800–1,800** and should be **employer-paid**. If HR asks you to reimburse MOI fees, treat that as a **red flag**.

**Hub:** [Qatar relocation guide](/guides/qatar-relocation-guide/)

---

## Who sponsors your work visa

Only a **registered Qatari employer** with an active labour quota can sponsor your work residence permit. Your company — not you — owns the application.

**You cannot:**
- Apply on your own like some freelance visas elsewhere
- Convert a tourist visa to work without the employer starting the file
- Work on a business visit visa — that is illegal employment

**Free zones / QFC:** still employer-sponsored. Different authority, same rule: no employer, no QID.

---

## Pre-arrival — employer side

Before you fly, HR and the PRO handle the paperwork. Your job is to supply clean documents and not book flights until the entry permit PDF is in hand.

| Step | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Job offer signed | You + HR | Week 0 |
| Labour contract registered | Employer | 1–2 weeks |
| Entry permit / work visa issued | Employer PRO | 1–3 weeks |
| Flight booked | You/employer | After permit PDF |

**Your documents before travel:**
- Passport 6–9 months validity
- Attested degree (if required for role classification)
- Passport photos
- Medical clearance from home country (sometimes)

---

## Step 1: Entry permit

Employer submits to Ministry of Labour / immigration systems.

You get a PDF entry permit — print a colour copy. Airlines check at departure and arrival.

**Cost:** ~QAR 200 reference. Employer pays.

---

## Step 2: Arrival in Qatar

Immigration stamps entry at Hamad International Airport.

**Do not work** until residence permit process complete — though you are legally entering for employment setup.

Corporate hires: PRO meets with signboard or WhatsApp coordination.

---

## Step 3: Medical fitness test

Within days of arrival, appointment at **approved medical centre**:

- Blood tests
- Chest X-ray
- General fitness screen

**Fail scenarios:** communicable disease flags — retest or deportation in severe cases (rare for standard hires).

**Cost:** QAR 100–200 — employer pays.

**Tip:** fasting not usually required; bring passport copy.

---

## Step 4: Biometrics

MOI or designated centre — fingerprints and facial capture for QID database.

Appointment often booked by PRO.

---

## Step 5: Work permit & residence permit (QID)

**Work permit:** ties you to employer; annual renewal ~QAR 100 reference fee.

**Residence permit (QID card):** your physical ID for daily life.

**QID fee:** ~QAR 500 reference.

**Timeline:** card issued 1–3 weeks after biometrics if no holds.

---

## Step 6: Metrash2 activation

Download **Metrash2** (MOI official app):

- Track visa expiry
- Renew some services
- Pay fines
- Family sponsorship modules (when eligible)

Link QID number after card received.

## Total timeline diagram

```
Offer signed ──► Entry permit (1-3 w) ──► Fly in ──► Medical (week 1)
     ──► Biometrics (week 2-3) ──► QID issued (week 4-8)
```

**12+ weeks** usually means document mismatch, PRO backlog, or Ramadan slowdown.

Qatar Labour Law: employer bears visa costs for recruitment — push back if contract clause says otherwise.

## Employer PRO — your lifeline

**Public Relations Officer** (company immigration clerk) submits everything.

**How to work with PRO well:**
- WhatsApp group with HR + PRO
- Weekly status ping if no update 10 days
- Never surrender original passport except for official processing with receipt

**If PRO ghosting:** escalate to HR director in writing.

## NOC and employer transfer — 2026 overview

Changing jobs without exiting Qatar hinges on NOC policy and Ministry of Labour classification. Rules tighten and loosen in waves — treat below as an operational framework and verify PRO wording at signing.

### Who typically needs an NOC

| Situation | NOC from old employer? |
|---|---|
| Completed contract term + notice served | Often streamlined transfer |
| Resignation mid-contract | Usually required unless exempt category |
| Termination for cause | Harder; may be exit-only |
| Skill shortage / government-listed roles | Sometimes exempt per circular |

### Transfer sequence (happy path)

1. Signed offer from **new** licensed employer with quota
2. New employer PRO requests **transfer / NOC clearance** in labour system
3. Old employer approves or system auto-clears after waiting period (category dependent)
4. New entry/work permit amendment without full exit stamp (case dependent)
5. Medical repeat sometimes skipped if recent; biometrics update
6. QID reissued with new employer name

**Timeline:** **2–6 weeks** when both PROs cooperate; **8+ weeks** if old employer delays NOC.

### 2026 practical notes

- Labour Law amendments emphasise **employee mobility** in some tiers but **not universal** — do not assume Dubai-style free transfer
- **Non-compete** clauses in contract are separate from NOC — civil dispute risk remains
- **Debt and loans:** clearing bank liabilities before transfer reduces hold flags
- **Children in school:** mid-year transfer with invalid QID risks seat — plan summer window

**Gap risk:** do not let residence expire — see grace periods section.

## Visa cancellation — step-by-step (resignation, termination, exit)

Cancellation is **employer-initiated** in the system even when you resign. You cooperate or exit becomes messy.

### Step 1 — HR notice and settlement discussion

- Written resignation or termination letter
- Ask HR date they will file **cancellation request**
- Clarify **final settlement**: gratuity, leave balance, ticket home

### Step 2 — Dependants first

| Order | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cancel or transfer children's residence |
| 2 | Cancel or transfer spouse residence |
| 3 | Cancel domestic worker if any |
| 4 | Cancel sponsor work permit |

**Why:** primary cancellation with active dependants blocks clean exit.

### Step 3 — Work permit cancellation

- Employer PRO submits cancellation in MoL/MOI systems
- You receive status in Metrash2 — screenshot
- Hand back company assets; get **clearance letter** if policy exists

### Step 4 — Final exit visa / exit permit

- Book flight within permitted window
- Pay outstanding **traffic fines**, **Kahramaa**, cheque cases if any
- Close bank accounts or freeze per bank policy

### Step 5 — Airport final exit

- Immigration verifies no ban flags
- Employer may require **exit interview** or passport return receipt history

### Fired for cause — extra risks

- Immediate cancellation possible — shorter grace
- Challenge via Labour dispute channel if unfair termination claimed
- Do not sign Arabic documents you cannot read — translator

### Voluntary exit to new country job

- Often still need old employer cancellation unless transfer already completed
- **Absconding** label if you leave without cancellation — future Gulf bans

Gratuity payout timing is **separate** from cancellation — negotiate written payment date.

## Grace periods after job loss or cancellation

Grace rules change; use Metrash2 countdown as source of truth.

| Status | Typical expectation | Your actions |
|---|---|---|
| Cancellation filed, final exit booked | Days to weeks window | Do not overstay |
| Seeking new employer transfer | Limited extension if transfer in progress | Daily PRO proof |
| Dependants on your visa | Same clock — cancel or transfer fast | School notified |
| Visit visa after cancel | Different rules — do not confuse | |

**Lease:** landlord may demand proof of valid QID renewal or exit — see [Doha rent prices by area](/guides/doha-rent-prices-by-area/) for early termination clauses.

**Bank loans:** car loans and personal loans accelerate if salary stops — settle before cancellation if possible.

## Industry-specific notes — oil and gas, QFC, government

### Oil, gas, and major energy employers

- **PRO maturity:** large HR immigration teams; timelines often **4–6 weeks** predictable
- **Camp housing:** visa files list company accommodation — family letter from employer not landlord
- **Rotation workforce:** some roles are FIFO — confirm if family sponsorship allowed on grade
- **Medical:** same centres; TB holds rare but retest adds weeks
- **Security clearance:** extra background for some nationals — add 2–4 weeks pre-entry

### Qatar Financial Centre (QFC) and regulated firms

- **Sponsor entity:** QFC licensed company still employer of record for visa
- **Contract law:** English-law contracts common — labour jurisdiction nuances — read sponsor entity name on QID
- **Salary certificates:** HR issues for family visa with precise basic line — finance roles often meet QAR 10,000 basic
- **Transfer:** NOC culture similar but HR legal cautious — notice periods longer

### Construction and subcontractor tiers

- **Quota risk:** smaller subs hit quota caps — verify permit before quitting old job
- **Wage dispute:** visa cancellation used as leverage — Labour complaint channel
- **Camp vs family:** many grades bachelor-only housing

### Healthcare and education

- **Credential attestation:** degrees heavily checked pre-permit
- **Shift workers:** PRO batches medical appointments nights

### Tech and startups

- **Immature PRO:** one admin doing everything — you chase weekly
- **Cash visa fee requests:** red flag — see expanded red flags

## PRO red flags — expanded list for candidates and hires

Beyond "pay your own government fees," watch for these before you fly.

| Red flag | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| PRO is external "agent" not on payroll | No accountability when stuck |
| Offer letter PDF only, no MOI-ready contract | Salary basic hidden |
| "Start on visit visa, we fix later" | Illegal work; deport risk |
| WhatsApp-only communication, no ticket reference | Scam pattern |
| Passport taken without signed receipt | Illegal hold |
| Medical booked after 30+ days in country | Overstay grey zone |
| Company name on permit differs from interview brand | Sponsorship bait-and-switch |
| Deduction clause for "visa recovery" on resignation | Labour Law conflict |
| No Metrash2 update 21 days after arrival | PRO not filing |
| Requests for blank signed cheques | Walk away |
| "Family visa included" but basic QAR 8,000 | Impossible — [family visa guide](/guides/qatar-family-visa-sponsorship/) |
| Housing allowance only, no accommodation, grade bachelor | Family lease blocked |

**Escalation ladder:** PRO → HR director → CEO office → Ministry of Labour hotline → embassy if passport withheld.

Document every promise in email; Qatar disputes favour paper trail.

## Pre-arrival document attestation for work visa

Degree attestation chains mirror family documents but focus on **education and experience**.

| Document | Typical use |
|---|---|
| University degree | Skill classification on labour file |
| Transcripts | Sometimes requested for regulated roles |
| Professional licence | Medical, engineering councils |
| Police clearance | Energy and government contractors |

**UK / US / India / Philippines** paths mirror family guide — allow **6–10 weeks** before notice period ends.

Employer should give **exact list** — do not attest documents they will not use (saves cost).

## Opening bank account and payroll after QID

Banks require:

- Valid QID (not just entry stamp)
- Salary certificate
- Employer letter
- Minimum salary for some premium accounts

Payroll may start only after **WPS registration** active — first salary delay common week 6–8.

**Cheque book:** order immediately if planning [Doha rent](/guides/doha-rent-prices-by-area/) with post-dated cheques.

## Ramadan, Eid, and national holiday delays

Government PRO throughput drops:

- **Ramadan:** offices shorter; medical centres reduced hours
- **Eid Al Fitr / Adha:** full stop 3–7 days
- **National Day:** partial closures

Add **2 weeks** to employer-promised timeline if landing in these windows.

## Dual employment and side work — compliance

Work permit ties you to **one sponsor**. Freelance work for second client without second permit is illegal.

**Consulting on visit** while resident — grey and risky.

QFC and some free-zone structures still use employer-of-record — read contract entity.

## Property purchase while on work visa

You may buy property in approved zones while employed — mortgage and developer KYC still need QID and salary proof.

Investor residency is **separate application** — do not stop work permit renewal because you bought a flat.

## FAQ

**Can I enter on visit visa and convert?** Employer must initiate the proper work permit. Do not work during the gap.

**How long is work visa valid?** Tied to contract. Renew annually, sometimes with a repeat medical.

**Do I need Arabic translation?** PRO handles labour files. You sign where directed.

**Can I drive before QID?** Rules vary. Assume you need QID for licence conversion.

**Does work visa allow buying a car?** Yes, once QID and bank account are active.

**Ramadan impact?** Government offices run shorter hours. Add 1–2 weeks buffer.
