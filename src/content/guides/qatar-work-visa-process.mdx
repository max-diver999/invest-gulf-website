faq:
  - question: "How long does Qatar work visa processing take?"
    answer: "Typical timeline is 4-8 weeks total. Entry permit approval takes 1-2 weeks, then you travel to Qatar for medical tests and biometrics (1-2 days), followed by QID processing (2-4 weeks). Some nationalities may have expedited processing or additional security clearances."
  - question: "Can I apply for Qatar work visa myself?"
    answer: "No, Qatar work visas are 100% employer-driven. Only your sponsoring employer can initiate and process your work visa through their PRO (Public Relations Officer) and MOI systems. You cannot self-sponsor or apply independently."
  - question: "Who pays the Qatar work visa fees?"
    answer: "Employer should pay all government fees (approximately QAR 800-1,800 total for visa, medical, QID processing). If HR asks you to reimburse MOI fees, this is a red flag potentially indicating illegal fee pass-through, which violates Qatar labor law."
  - question: "What documents do I need for Qatar work visa?"
    answer: "You need a clean criminal background check from home country (attested), educational certificates (attested), passport with 6+ months validity, and medical fitness certificate. Your employer handles most documentation through their systems."
  - question: "Can I change employers while on Qatar work visa?"
    answer: "Yes, but requires NOC (No Objection Certificate) from current employer or completing minimum contract period. New employer must sponsor transfer of your residence. Process can take 2-4 weeks and you typically cannot work during transfer."
  - question: "What's the difference between entry permit and QID?"
    answer: "Entry permit allows you to enter Qatar and work temporarily. QID (Qatar ID) is your permanent residence card issued after medical tests and biometrics in Qatar. You need QID for banking, driving license, family sponsorship, and most official transactions."

### Transfer sequence (happy path)

1. Signed offer from new licensed employer with quota
2. New employer PRO requests transfer / NOC clearance in labour system
3. Old employer approves or system auto-clears after waiting period (category dependent)
4. New entry/work permit amendment without full exit stamp (case dependent)
5. Medical repeat sometimes skipped if recent; biometrics update
6. QID reissued with new employer name

Timeline: 2–6 weeks when both PROs cooperate; 8+ weeks if old employer delays NOC.

### 2026 practical notes

- Labour Law amendments emphasise employee mobility in some tiers but not universal, do not assume Dubai-style free transfer
- Non-compete clauses in contract are separate from NOC, civil dispute risk remains
- Debt and loans: clearing bank liabilities before transfer reduces hold flags
- Children in school: mid-year transfer with invalid QID risks seat, plan summer window

Gap risk: do not let residence expire; see grace periods section.
