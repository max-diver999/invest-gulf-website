## Who sponsors your work visa

Your Qatari employer is the only entity that can sponsor a work residence permit; you cannot self-file a work visa like a UAE Green Visa freelancer route. The company's PRO submits labour and immigration requests in MOI systems under the employer's commercial registration and quota.

## Pre-arrival: documents your employer should request

Expect attested education certificates, a police clearance from your home country, passport copies, and role-specific licences before the entry permit stage. Ask HR for a written checklist so you do not pay for attestations the company will not use.

## Changing employers: NOC and transfer sequence

### Transfer sequence (happy path)

1. Signed offer from new licensed employer with quota
2. New employer PRO requests transfer / NOC clearance in labour system
3. Old employer approves or system auto-clears after waiting period (category dependent)
4. New entry/work permit amendment without full exit stamp (case dependent)
5. Medical repeat sometimes skipped if recent; biometrics update
6. QID reissued with new employer name

Timeline: 2 to 6 weeks when both PROs cooperate; 8+ weeks if old employer delays NOC.

### 2026 practical notes

- Labour Law amendments emphasise employee mobility in some tiers but not universal, do not assume Dubai-style free transfer
- Non-compete clauses in contract are separate from NOC, civil dispute risk remains
- Debt and loans: clearing bank liabilities before transfer reduces hold flags
- Children in school: mid-year transfer with invalid QID risks seat, plan summer window

Gap risk: do not let residence expire; see grace periods section.

## Visa cancellation and final exit

### Step 1: HR notice and settlement discussion

- Written resignation or termination letter
- Ask HR date they will file cancellation request
- Clarify final settlement: gratuity, leave balance, ticket home

### Step 2: Dependants first

| Order | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cancel or transfer children's residence |
| 2 | Cancel or transfer spouse residence |
| 3 | Cancel domestic worker if any |
| 4 | Cancel sponsor work permit |

Why: primary cancellation with active dependants blocks clean exit.

### Step 3: Work permit cancellation

- Employer PRO submits cancellation in MoL/MOI systems
- You receive status in Metrash2, screenshot
- Hand back company assets; get clearance letter if policy exists

### Step 4: Final exit visa / exit permit

- Book flight within permitted window
- Pay outstanding traffic fines, Kahramaa, cheque cases if any
- Close bank accounts or freeze per bank policy

### Step 5: Airport final exit

- Immigration verifies no ban flags
- Employer may require exit interview or passport return receipt history

### Fired for cause: extra risks

- Immediate cancellation possible, shorter grace
- Challenge via Labour dispute channel if unfair termination claimed
- Do not sign Arabic documents you cannot read, translator

### Voluntary exit to new country job

- Often still need old employer cancellation unless transfer already completed
- Absconding label if you leave without cancellation, future Gulf bans

Gratuity payout timing is separate from cancellation, negotiate written payment date.

## Industry-specific visa quirks (QFC, construction, healthcare)

### Qatar Financial Centre (QFC) and regulated firms

- Sponsor entity: QFC licensed company still employer of record for visa
- Contract law: English-law contracts common, labour jurisdiction nuances, read sponsor entity name on QID
- Salary certificates: HR issues for family visa with precise basic line, finance roles often meet QAR 10,000 basic
- Transfer: NOC culture similar but HR legal cautious, notice periods longer

### Construction and subcontractor tiers

- Quota risk: smaller subs hit quota caps, verify permit before quitting old job
- Wage dispute: visa cancellation used as leverage, Labour complaint channel
- Camp vs family: many grades bachelor-only housing

### Healthcare and education

- Credential attestation: degrees heavily checked pre-permit
- Shift workers: PRO batches medical appointments nights

### Tech and startups

- Immature PRO: one admin doing everything, you chase weekly
- Cash visa fee requests: red flag; see expanded red flags
## Employer red flags before you resign abroad

Escalation ladder: PRO → HR director → CEO office → Ministry of Labour hotline → embassy if passport withheld.

Document every promise in email; Qatar disputes favour paper trail.

## FAQ

**Can I enter on visit visa and convert?** Employer must initiate the proper work permit. Do not work during the gap.

**How long is work visa valid?** Tied to contract. Renew annually, sometimes with a repeat medical.

**Do I need Arabic translation?** PRO handles labour files. You sign where directed.

**Can I drive before QID?** Rules vary. Assume you need QID for licence conversion.

**Does work visa allow buying a car?** Yes, once QID and bank account are active.

**Ramadan impact?** Government offices run shorter hours. Add 1 to 2 weeks buffer.
