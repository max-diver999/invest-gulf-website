**Insider tip:** Do not resign abroad until you hold a colour PDF entry permit and a written fee clause that the employer pays MOI costs. Invest Gulf clients who fly on visit-then-convert promises often lose 2 to 4 weeks and risk fines.

Qatar work residence for employed expats is an employer-sponsored path that typically takes 4 to 8 weeks from entry-permit filing to physical QID collection. You cannot self-sponsor a standard work visa the way some UAE freelance routes allow. Government fees commonly total about AED 800 to 1,800 equivalent across entry permit, medical, and QID steps, and Qatar Labour Law expects the employer to bear recruitment visa costs. Medical and biometrics usually complete in 1 to 2 days after landing when the PRO books slots correctly. Foreign buyers planning Doha housing should not sign a 12 month lease until the entry permit PDF exists and HR confirms the QID timeline in writing. Keep passport validity above 6 months and store colour permit copies for airline checks on both departure and arrival days.

Changing employers in Qatar typically requires an NOC or system clearance and often takes 2 to 6 weeks when both PROs cooperate, stretching past 8 weeks if the old employer delays. Dependants must be cancelled or transferred before the primary work permit ends, or exit clearance stalls for additional weeks. Bank accounts, driving licence conversion, and family sponsorship modules in Metrash2 generally need an active QID, not only an entry stamp. Ramadan and Eid windows can add about 2 weeks to employer-promised dates across government counters and medical clinics. Pair this process guide with [Qatar residency by investment](/guides/qatar-residency-by-investment/) only if your path is investment residency, not employment sponsorship, and keep transfer reference numbers for at least 12 months of audits after the new QID issues for banking and school files.

## Who sponsors a Qatar work visa?

A Qatar work visa is sponsored only by a licensed Qatari employer through its PRO and MOI labour systems, and candidates cannot self-file the residence. The company commercial registration and quota control the file from day one. Typical end-to-end timing still lands in the 4 to 8 week band for clean corporate hires.

| Party | Role | Timing signal |
|---|---|---|
| Employer / PRO | Files labour and immigration requests | Owns 100% of filing |
| Candidate | Attests docs, travels on permit | 6+ months passport validity |
| Medical centre | Fitness tests after landing | 1-2 days |
| MOI / QID | Residence card issuance | 2-4 weeks after biometrics |

- Confirm employer legal name matches the labour listing
- Get PRO email before you resign
- Keep a sponsorship checklist with quota confirmation

## What documents should you prepare before arrival?

Pre-arrival documents for Qatar work residence typically include attested degrees, police clearance, passport copies, and role licences the employer actually uses. Attestation paths from the UK, US, India, or Philippines often need 6 to 10 weeks before your notice period ends. Ask HR for a written list so you do not pay for unused stamps.

| Document | Typical use | Planning buffer |
|---|---|---|
| University degree | Skill classification | 6-10 weeks |
| Police clearance | Energy and government contractors | 4-8 weeks |
| Professional licence | Medical, engineering councils | Case dependent |
| Passport bio page | Entry permit | 6+ months validity |

Our clients treat a missing written attestation checklist as a PRO maturity red flag.

## How does the entry permit stage work?

The entry permit stage is the employer-issued PDF that airlines check at departure and arrival, and it typically costs about AED 200 equivalent in government fees the employer should pay. Print a colour copy and keep a phone backup for the first 7 days of travel. Do not book flights until the PDF window is confirmed.

- Colour print in hand before airport check-in
- Align flight dates to the permit validity window
- Screenshot PRO WhatsApp instructions for the first 48 hours
- Add permit dates to the same relocation checklist as housing

## What happens after you land in Qatar?

Travel on a Qatar work entry permit typically means you enter for employment setup, then complete medical and biometrics before the residence is fully active. Corporate PROs often meet arrivals within the first 1 to 2 days. Avoid client-site work until HR confirms permit activation rules for your grade.

| Day band | Expected admin | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0-2 | Entry stamp, PRO pickup | PRO + employee |
| Day 1-3 | Medical fitness | PRO booking |
| Day 2-5 | Biometrics | PRO booking |
| Week 2-8 | QID card | MOI process |

## What does the medical fitness test include?

The Qatar work medical is a fitness screen that typically includes blood tests, chest X-ray, and a general exam, usually completed in 1 to 2 days when slots are booked. Employer-paid government fees often sit near AED 100 to 200 equivalent per person. Fasting is not usually required; bring passport copies for the clinic checklist.

- Blood tests and chest X-ray
- General fitness screen
- Retest path if communicable-disease flags appear

## How do biometrics and QID issuance work?

Biometrics for Qatar ID typically follow the medical within the first week, and the physical QID card often arrives 2 to 4 weeks later. The PRO usually books the appointment; you attend with passport and entry papers. Banking and licence conversion in the area generally wait for the QID number, not the entry stamp alone.

| Step | Typical timing | Dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Biometrics appointment | First 7 days | PRO booking |
| Residence activation | System update after biometrics | MOI |
| Physical QID | 2-4 weeks | Collection instructions |
| Metrash2 link | After QID number | Employee setup |

Invest Gulf research flags any HR plan that promises a bank account in week 1 without QID.

## What end-to-end timeline should you expect?

The end-to-end Qatar work visa timeline typically runs 4 to 8 weeks from entry-permit approval to QID in hand for standard corporate hires. Entry permit approval alone often needs 1 to 2 weeks before travel. Add about 2 weeks if you land across Ramadan or Eid closures.

| Phase | Indicative duration |
|---|---|
| Entry permit approval | 1-2 weeks |
| Medical + biometrics | 1-2 days |
| QID processing | 2-4 weeks |
| Total common band | 4-8 weeks |

- Week 1 checklist: stamp, medical, biometrics booked
- Week 4-8 checklist: QID, bank, licence conversion start
- Family sponsorship clock starts only after QID

## Who should pay government visa fees?

Government fees on Qatar work residence typically total about AED 800 to 1,800 equivalent across visa, medical, and QID steps, and the employer should pay them. Labour Law expects the sponsor to bear recruitment visa costs, so reimbursement demands are a red flag. Put the fee clause on your offer checklist before resignation day.

| Cost item | Indicative band | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| Entry permit | ~AED 200 equiv. | Employer |
| Medical | ~AED 100-200 equiv. | Employer |
| QID processing | Balance to ~AED 800-1,800 total | Employer |

Our clients escalate fee pass-through from PRO to HR director within 5 business days of the request.

## How do employer transfers and NOCs work?

Employer transfer in Qatar typically requires a new licensed sponsor, system clearance or NOC, and 2 to 6 weeks when both PROs cooperate, or 8+ weeks if the old employer delays. You often cannot work during the gap. Clear bank loans before transfer where possible, and plan school seats around summer windows for children.

1. Signed offer from new licensed employer with quota
2. New PRO requests transfer or NOC clearance
3. Old employer approves or auto-clear waiting period runs
4. Medical repeat sometimes skipped if recent
5. QID reissued with new employer name

Keep the transfer reference number for at least 12 months of audits.

## What cancellation and exit steps protect your record?

Visa cancellation and final exit in Qatar typically require dependant cancellations first, then the primary work permit, then exit within the permitted window. Skipping dependants blocks clean exit and can add 2 to 4 weeks of delay. Settle fines before airport day and keep the exit checklist paper-based.

| Order | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cancel or transfer children | Primary cancel blocks otherwise |
| 2 | Cancel or transfer spouse | Same |
| 3 | Cancel domestic worker if any | Clean sponsor file |
| 4 | Cancel sponsor work permit | Enables exit |

- Written resignation or termination letter
- Screenshot Metrash2 cancellation status
- Book flights inside the grace window of about 30 days when applicable (confirm current official rules)

## What red flags should candidates refuse?

Candidate red flags before resigning abroad typically include cash MOI fee requests, missing PRO contacts, and visit-visa conversion plans without a work entry permit. Document every promise by email for the first 90 days. Escalation runs PRO to HR to Ministry channels if passports are withheld.

- Employer legal name mismatch on labour listing
- Contract silent on who pays visa fees
- No entry permit PDF before day-one office work
- Pressure to attest unused documents at your cost

Foreign buyers comparing Doha life should still finish the visa checklist before any 12 month tenancy deposit.

## How does Qatar employment visa differ from UAE routes?

Qatar employment residence typically remains 100 percent employer-driven, while the UAE offers more self-sponsored and freelance-style paths alongside company visas. Qatar timelines of 4 to 8 weeks are comparable to many UAE corporate hires, but transfer culture is less flexible than Dubai assumptions. Model both paths for a 2 to 3 year assignment.

| Factor | Qatar work visa | UAE employment visa |
|---|---|---|
| Self-sponsor option | No for standard work | Some Green/freelance routes |
| Typical timeline | 4-8 weeks | Often 2-6 weeks corporate |
| Fee bearer | Employer expected | Employer common, varies |
| Transfer friction | NOC culture stronger | More mobility in some tiers |
