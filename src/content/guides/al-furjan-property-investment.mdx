Al Furjan is Nakheel's **west Dubai family and commuter hybrid** — townhouses with gardens, mid-rise apartments, and a metro corridor that finally gives Jebel Ali fringe tenants a credible DIFC commute without pretending they live in Downtown.

**Quick answer:** Gross yield 6.0–8.5% by product. Entry AED 700K–2.5M. Metro upside. Nakheel freehold.

## Summary decision framework

Before signing any lease or purchase contract in Dubai, consolidate **rent, utilities, schools, transport, and one-off relocation** into one spreadsheet tab. Compare against **net household income**, not gross headline salary. Dubai rewards families who model honestly and punishes those who trust brochure economics.

If total fixed costs exceed **70% of net income**, you are one school fee invoice away from financial stress — downgrade area, curriculum, or negotiate package before move, not after.

## Al Furjan investor hold period and exit strategy

Minimum sensible hold **5 years** — captures metro maturation and Nakheel retail completion. Shorter flips fight transaction costs (**~6%** acquisition stack) and thin buyer pool vs Marina.

**Exit buyers:** end-users from Jebel Ali employment and Dubai South airport corridor; investors rotating from DSO yield to townhouse stability. Price exit using **RERA transaction sub-index** for Al Furjan — not Marina comps.

**Tenant profile stability:** families on 2-year Ejari with children in Ibn Battuta corridor schools renew at **70%+** rate when rent increases stay within RERA index — model void at 5% not 10% for established west phases.
