## Opening documents for a personal account

Bahrain retail banks typically ask for passport, CPR once residence is stamped, employer salary certificate and a municipality-registered lease or EWA proof that matches your visa address. Start collecting attested family documents early if you plan joint accounts for a spouse.

Dependants: spouse accounts need sponsor CPR + marriage certificate; children need birth certificates. Align with family visa timeline  (confirm current official rules).

Address chain: lease registration → EWA → bank KYC → school admin, mismatch delays everything.

## Salary accounts and payroll transfer

Employers route payroll to a declared IBAN; switching banks requires HR to issue a fresh salary transfer letter before you close the old account.

Salary transfer letter: switching banks requires new assignment form signed, do not close old account until first payroll hits new IBAN.

Multi-currency salary: rare; most contracts BHD. Offshore pay needs home-country tax advice, out of scope.

## FAQ

Which bank is best for expats in Bahrain?  
No single best, HSBC for international, NBB for local depth, BBK for remittance promos. Open where HR mandates, then optimise FX.

Can I open before residence visa?  
Limited on visit, full salary account needs CPR / LMRA residence (confirm current official rules). Plan 2-4 weeks after stamping.

HSBC vs NBB vs BBK?  
HSBC: global; NBB: widest local; BBK: competitive retail/remittance. Compare **your home corridor FX**.

**How do remittances work?**  
Bank app or **CBB-licensed exchange**. Compare **spread**, not fee. Test small before large.

**What is CBB?**  
**Central Bank of Bahrain**, regulator for licensed banks and AML rules.

**Salary account mandatory?**  
**Yes**, payroll to registered IBAN; **salary transfer letter** to switch.

**Documents for KYC?**  
Passport, **CPR**, salary certificate, **municipality lease / EWA**, mobile number.

**Credit card salary needed?**  
Typically **BHD 800-1,500+** monthly for classic tier ** (confirm current official rules)**.

**Source of funds for property?**  
Salary savings, asset sale, inheritance, document above **BHD 10,000-20,000 cumulative (confirm current official rules)**. See [Bahrain property foreigner living](/guides/bahrain-property-foreigner-living/).

**Islamic or conventional?**  
Compare **all-in cost**, **Al Baraka** vs conventional windows at NBB/BBK/HSBC.

**Golden Residence banking?**  
**Separate LMRA track ~BHD 200K (confirm current official rules)**, do not mix with employment salary compliance.
