Which bank is best for expats in Bahrain?  
No single best, HSBC for international, NBB for local depth, BBK for remittance promos. Open where HR mandates, then optimise FX.

Can I open before residence visa?  
Limited on visit, full salary account needs CPR / LMRA residence (confirm current official rules). Plan 2–4 weeks after stamping.

HSBC vs NBB vs BBK?  
HSBC: global; NBB: widest local; BBK: competitive retail/remittance. Compare **your home corridor FX**.

**How do remittances work?**  
Bank app or **CBB-licensed exchange**. Compare **spread**, not fee. Test small before large.

**What is CBB?**  
**Central Bank of Bahrain**, regulator for licensed banks and AML rules.

**Salary account mandatory?**  
**Yes**, payroll to registered IBAN; **salary transfer letter** to switch.

**Documents for KYC?**  
Passport, **CPR**, salary certificate, **municipality lease / EWA**, mobile number.

**Islamic or conventional?**

**Golden Residence banking?**  
**Separate LMRA track ~BHD 200K (confirm current official rules)**, do not mix with employment salary compliance.
