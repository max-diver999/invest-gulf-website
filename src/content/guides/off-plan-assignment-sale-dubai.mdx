## What is an off-plan assignment sale in Dubai?

An assignment transfers your rights under an off-plan sale and purchase agreement (SPA) to a new buyer **before** handover. The assignee steps into your contractual position, assumes remaining construction-linked instalments, and becomes the registered Oqood holder after DLD processing. You are selling **contractual position**, not a completed unit with title deed.

Assignment is secondary market for off-plan, distinct from developer primary sales covered in [off-plan vs secondary market](/guides/off-plan-vs-secondary-market-dubai/).

## When are you legally allowed to assign?

Most developers permit assignment only after minimum paid-in capital and construction milestones.

| Requirement | Typical rule |
|  |  |
| Minimum paid | 20-40% of SPA price |
| Construction milestone | 30-50% building completion |
| Payment record | No default on instalments |
| SPA clause | Assignment not prohibited |
| Holding period | Some developers impose 12-month lockout |

Pull the **assignment article** in your SPA, not the marketing brochure. If flip flexibility matters, verify NOC policy **before** launch purchase.

## What is the step-by-step assignment process?

**Phase 1: Confirm eligibility.** Developer sales confirms assignment window, fees, and document list.

**Phase 2: Market the position.** Use a RERA-licensed broker experienced in assignments. Price against the developer's **current launch** for equivalent specification. Disclose remaining instalment schedule and any post-handover payment tail.

**Phase 3: Negotiate assignee terms.** Common structures: assignee pays premium and assumes instalments; assignee pays full exit while you settle developer separately; or hybrid partial premium plus instalment takeover.

Assignment price formula:

```
Assignee pays = Premium to assignor + (may assume instalments to developer)
Assignor profit = Premium - (instalments paid + DLD + NOC + broker + penalties)
```

**Phase 4: Developer NOC.** Submit passports, Oqood certificate, SPA, proposed price, and assignee KYC. NOC fees are indicative:

| Developer tier | NOC fee range |
|  |  |
| Tier 1 (Emaar, Nakheel) | AED 500-5,000 |
| Tier 2 volume | AED 3,000-15,000 or 1-2% of price |

Timeline often **1-4 weeks**; peak launch seasons extend queues.

**Phase 5: Tripartite agreement.** Assignor, assignee, and developer sign. Covers price, instalment assumption, penalties, handover obligations, and defect liability flow-through. Budget **AED 5,000-15,000** legal review per side.

**Phase 6: DLD Oqood transfer.** At a Registration Trustee: NOC, tripartite agreement, IDs, DLD transfer fee (commonly **4%** of assignment value, verify current schedule), trustee admin ~**AED 4,000**. Seller liability ends on **DLD confirmation**, not on handshake.

**Phase 7: Settlement.** Assignee wires premium per agreement; developer updates payment plan records. Retain DLD transfer proof for tax and disputes.

## How much does an assignment cost end to end?

| Cost line | Who pays | Indicative |
|  |  |  |
| Developer NOC | Usually assignor or split | AED 500-15,000+ |
| DLD Oqood transfer | Commonly assignee | ~4% of assignment value |
| Trustee admin | Assignee | ~AED 4,000 |
| Broker | Negotiated | 2% + VAT typical |
| Legal | Both sides | AED 5,000-15,000 each |
| Outstanding instalments | Per SPA | May need settlement |

**Worked example:** Original SPA AED 1,200,000; instalments paid AED 480,000 (40%); assignment price AED 1,350,000; gross premium AED 150,000. Less NOC AED 5,000, broker AED 28,350 (2%+VAT on 1.35M), legal AED 8,000: net to seller about **AED 108,650** before home-country tax. Thin margin if the market will not pay premium.

## How do developer assignment policies differ?

Volume developers (Danube, Samana, Binghatti, Azizi) often treat assignment as core to investor buyers, but NOC fees and lockouts vary by launch. Some SPAs prohibit assignment for the first **12-24 months** or until **50%+** construction. Violation attempts waste broker time.

Always cross-check [due diligence](/guides/due-diligence-dubai-property/) if assignment is your exit strategy.

## What should assignees verify before they buy?

Assignees gain early entry to sold-out phases or discounted positions versus current launch, but inherit SPA risk.

**Assignee advantages**

- Shorter handover wait if building is 70%+ complete
- Entry below current launch if assignor discounts

**Assignee risks**

- Inherits delay penalties and SPA remedies
- Post-handover payment plan tail if present
- Service charge estimates may not be final
- Mortgage rare until handover
- Developer may restrict future assignment

Run the same diligence as a primary off-plan buyer. See [how to flip off-plan Dubai](/guides/how-to-flip-off-plan-dubai/) for exit sequencing.

## Assignment vs waiting for handover: which exit wins?

| Path | Best when |
|  |  |
| Assignment | Capital redeployment needed; project risk rising; premium still available |
| Handover sale | Building near completion; rental strategy ready; mortgage buyers needed for exit price |

Handover mechanics: [Dubai property handover checklist](/guides/dubai-property-handover-checklist/).

## What does the 2025-2026 assignment market look like?

Normalised conditions mean:

- Assignments often trade at **par or small discount** to cost
- Premiums selective: prime locations or sold-out phases only
- NOC queues can slow when developer fee revenue drops
- **Cash assignees** preferred over complex payment-plan takeovers

Strategic timing: market the assignment **6-12 months before handover** when construction progress is visible but before completed-unit competition floods the submarket.

## What tax and home-country issues apply?

UAE has no personal income tax on assignment gain for most individuals, but **home-country capital gains** may apply. Document cost basis: SPA price, instalments, DLD, NOC, broker, and penalties. Consult a cross-border tax adviser before pricing premium.

## What are common seller and assignee mistakes?

**Sellers:** marketing before NOC eligibility; ignoring post-handover tails; staying liable after handshake without DLD proof; pricing off brochure not current launch.

**Assignees:** skipping SPA penalty clauses; assuming mortgage during construction; not verifying assignor payment record with developer.

## What red flags should pause an assignment deal?

Pause when developer refuses written NOC policy; assignor payment record shows defaults; post-handover tail exceeds 24 months; DLD transfer fee payer unclear in MOU; or assignee KYC fails developer AML without refund path.

## What checklist should you run before signing?

| Step | Pass criteria |
|  |  |
| SPA assignment clause | Not prohibited; thresholds met |
| Developer NOC fee | Written quote |
| Tripartite draft | Lawyer reviewed |
| Instalment schedule | Assignee accepts assumption |
| DLD fee payer | Documented in agreement |
| Liability end date | Tied to DLD registration |

**Buyer scenarios:** **Scenario A (early exit):** assign at 40% paid if launch premium exists. **Scenario B (ready resale):** stack 2% agency, 4% DLD, trustee on assignee budget. **Scenario C (buy-to-let at handover):** model net yield with filed service charges, not brochure gross.

## How do you manage NOC delays and document gaps?

NOC queues stretch when developers batch launch sales or audit KYC during peak seasons. Build **14-21 day** contingency into MOU expiry dates. If assignee financing depends on home-country sale, avoid non-refundable deposits until NOC is issued in writing.

| Document | Assignor | Assignee | Developer |
|  |  |  |  |
| Passport copies | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Oqood certificate | Yes | Copy on file | Yes |
| SPA and payment receipts | Yes | Review | Yes |
| AML / source of funds | Summary | Full KYC | Yes |
| MOU / assignment term sheet | Sign | Sign | N/A |
| NOC application form | Sign | Sign | Processes |

Incomplete packs restart the clock. Use the same checklist for [off-plan property Dubai guide](/guides/off-plan-property-dubai-guide/) purchases if assignment is your planned exit.

Assignors who market too early often pay double broker fees when the first buyer fails KYC. Stage marketing after developer pre-clearance when possible. Keep assignee deposits in escrow tied to NOC issuance, not merely MOU signature, so you are not forced to refund from personal account if the developer rejects the buyer profile.

If your SPA names a specific registration trustee, confirm they handle **Oqood assignment** transfers, not only ready title deeds. Some trustees redirect off-plan assignments to specialist counters, adding half a day to scheduling.

## How does assignment interact with escrow and developer payment plans?

Instalments remain payable to the developer on schedule until DLD registers the assignee. If the assignor misses a milestone payment, NOC can freeze even after buyer deposit. Request developer ledger confirmation showing **zero arrears** before tripartite signing.

Post-handover tails (12-24 month payment plans after completion) shrink the assignee pool. Disclose tails in listing copy; otherwise deals die at legal review.

*Verify current DLD assignment fee schedule and developer policy. Not legal advice.*

Before you list, screenshot Dubai REST Oqood status and payment plan balance. Assignees increasingly ask for developer ledger proof in 2026 after several stalled deals where marketing premium did not match instalments paid. Transparency speeds NOC.
