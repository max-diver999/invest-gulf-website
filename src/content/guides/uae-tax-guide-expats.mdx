UAE imposes no personal income tax on:

- Employment salaries and bonuses
- Business profits earned by individuals  
- Investment returns (dividends, capital gains)
- Rental income from property
- Freelance and consultancy income
- Inheritance and gifts

Exceptions: UAE nationals and long-term residents working for National Oil Companies may face specific tax obligations, but this rarely affects expats.

- Commercial property: 5% VAT applicable
