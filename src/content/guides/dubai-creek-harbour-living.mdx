## What is Dubai Creek Harbour today in 2026?

Dubai Creek Harbour in 2026 is a liveable Emaar waterfront district with completed towers, partial retail, and major phases still under construction, and Invest Gulf treats cranes as normal background noise before foreign buyers expect Downtown maturity over a 5 year hold with written fee schedules before reservation funds move.

Delivered and occupied residential phases include:

- Creekside 18 (Towers A and B)
- Creek Rise (Towers 1 and 2)
- Harbour Gate
- Cedar
- Cove (Emaar Beachfront adjacent)

Active construction continues on multiple additional phases. The Creek Tower's timeline has been revised multiple times and a specific delivery date has not been formally confirmed. Remaining masterplan phases will likely deliver across the next 5-8 years into the early 2030s.

## What is living next to Ras Al Khor and the Creek like?

Living beside Ras Al Khor typically means flamingo season from October to March with about 2,000-3,000 lesser flamingos visible from many apartments, plus Creek-facing historic waterway views across a 5-10 year hold, and Invest Gulf ranks sanctuary sightlines as a durable asset.

Ras Al Khor Wildlife Sanctuary, Arabic for "Cape of the Creek", is a protected wetland reserve adjacent to Dubai Creek Harbour, designated a wildlife sanctuary by the UAE government. From Creek Harbour apartments and the waterfront promenade, the flamingos are directly visible across the Creek. The sanctuary also hosts herons, cormorants, spoonbills, and various wading bird species.

Buildings facing the Creek offer views of Dubai Creek, one of Dubai's original commercial waterways. The Creek view is more characterful and contextually interesting than a generic Gulf sea view, though opinions on this are personal.

## Why is the Ras Al Khor sanctuary view a long-term asset?

Sanctuary protection is typically statutory under UAE law, so flamingo-facing sightlines cannot be built out like a marina view might across a 5-10 year hold, and our research treats that rarity as Creek Harbour's main differentiator for foreign buyers over a 5 year hold with written fee schedules.

The protected status:

- UAE law protects Ras Al Khor as a wildlife sanctuary permanently
- The sanctuary boundary directly adjacent to Creek Harbour is protected urban open space
- About 2,000-3,000 lesser flamingos winter from October through March

This is Creek Harbour's most differentiating asset versus any other Emaar development, and it has no equivalent in Downtown, Marina, or Dubai Hills.
