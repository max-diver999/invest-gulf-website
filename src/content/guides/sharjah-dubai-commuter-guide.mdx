# Sharjah to Dubai Commute 2026: Peak Times, Routes, Salik & Survival Guide

TL;DR: The Sharjah–Dubai commute is possible and rarely pleasant. Peak runs from Al Khan, Muwailih or central Sharjah to major job hubs routinely hit 45–90 minutes each way, not the 25-minute whisper from your agent. Budget Salik AED 250–500 plus fuel AED 800–1,400 per car monthly. Rent savings need AED 3,500–6,000 net after those lines or the maths fails by month six.

> Disclaimer: Times are June 2026 indicative from resident reports, not SLAs. Test your origin at 07:15 Sunday twice before lease.

## Route anatomy: E311, E11, Al Khan corridor

### Typical chains

Al Khan → Deira / Airport corridor:
1. Al Khan Rd / Corniche connectors
2. Al Ittihad Rd (E11) or E311 depending on app
3. Al Khan Bridge / Mamzar adjacency
4. Deira surface grid: last 15 min often worst

Muwailih → Business Bay / DIFC:
1. Muwailih → E311 merge
2. E311 south through Sharjah–Dubai border
3. Al Khail / Hessa / Sheikh Zayed Rd last mile
4. Business Bay ramp queue: unpredictable

Al Nahda → anywhere:
- Shortest km, border adjacency
- Trade-off: noise, density, older stock

| Origin | Best for Dubai hub | Weak for |

| Al Khan | Deira, Healthcare City, Airport | Marina without penalty |
| Muwailih | E311 north Dubai, Sharjah-side offices | DIFC without E311 pain |
