  - question: "Should I buy property immediately when moving to Qatar?"
    answer: "No, rent for 12-18 months first to understand Doha neighborhoods, school catchments, commute patterns, and your lifestyle preferences. This prevents expensive location mistakes and gives time to research off-plan projects properly."
  - question: "What areas in Qatar can foreigners buy property for residency?"
    answer: "Foreigners can buy freehold property in designated areas like The Pearl-Qatar, Lusail City, and West Bay Lagoon. Property investment of QAR 730,000+ in these zones can qualify for 5-year renewable residence visa through MOI application."
  - question: "How much do I need to invest for Qatar property residency?"
    answer: "Minimum QAR 730,000 for 5-year residence, or QAR 3.65 million for enhanced benefits. Investment must be in eligible freehold areas and meet MOI criteria. This is separate from Qatar's permanent residency program which has different requirements."
  - question: "Can I get a mortgage as a foreigner buying in Qatar?"
    answer: "Yes, both local and international banks offer mortgages to qualifying expats. Typical requirements include salary assignment, down payment of 20-25%, and debt-to-income ratios. Islamic and conventional financing options available with different structures."
  - question: "What are the ongoing costs of owning property in Qatar?"
    answer: "Budget for service charges (QAR 15-25 per sq ft annually), utilities (Kahramaa), municipality fees, insurance, and maintenance reserves. The Pearl and Lusail have homeowners association fees. Factor these into your total ownership cost."
  - question: "Is Qatar property residency the same as permanent residency?"
    answer: "No, property residency is a 5-year renewable residence visa tied to property ownership. Qatar's permanent residency (Law 10/2018) is a separate program requiring ~20 years residence with strict criteria and annual caps around 100 approvals."
