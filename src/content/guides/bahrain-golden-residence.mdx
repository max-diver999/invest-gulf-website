## What does Golden Residence require beyond buying a freehold apartment?

Golden Residence is an NPRA investor residency track that requires a qualifying investment plus approval separate from employment visas, usually planned over 2 to 6 months after registration for foreign buyers. LMRA still runs work permits on their own track. Invest Gulf underwriting rejects broker claims that any freehold apartment unlocks residency in this market.

Employed expats who buy a weekend flat do not automatically receive Golden Residence. Property title and investor residency are linked only when the asset appears on the eligible list and the total qualifying investment meets the cited threshold.

| Golden Residence | NPRA investor file | 2 to 6 months after investment |
| Employment visa | LMRA work permit | Sponsor-driven, separate |
| Dubai Golden Visa (property) | AED 2M registered value | GDRFA / DLD process |
