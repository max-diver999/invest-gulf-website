## Mistake 1: Do you model gross yield instead of net cash flow?

- Use Ejari transacted rents, not Bayut listings
- Pull Mollak charges for the exact building via Dubai REST
- Apply 7% vacancy unless you have contrary evidence
- Calculate on total acquisition cost (price + 4% DLD + fees)

Tools: [How to Calculate Rental Yield in Dubai](/guides/how-to-calculate-rental-yield-dubai/) | [Gross vs Net Yield Dubai](/guides/gross-vs-net-yield-dubai/)

## Mistake 2: Do you pay off-plan outside RERA escrow?

Rule: If it is off-plan and not in escrow, it is not a standard protected purchase.

Verify the escrow account on Dubai REST before any SWIFT. Trakheesi permits should match the project name on the SPA.

## Mistake 3: Do you confuse Golden Visa with citizenship?

Golden Visa is long-term residency, not Emirati nationality. Qualifying property value may support an application, but GDRFA approval is separate and discretionary.

## Mistake 4: Do you skip independent SPA legal review?

Developer-drafted SPAs favour delay penalties and service charge estimates. Budget AED 5,000-15,000 for independent review, higher for complex off-plan payment plans.

## Mistake 5: Do you ignore developer delivery track record?

- Verify specific project on Trakheesi
- Inspect developer's last completed building
- See [How to Evaluate a Dubai Developer](/guides/how-to-evaluate-dubai-developer/)

## Mistake 6: Do you assume freehold without zone verification?

- Confirm freehold zone on DLD portal before deposit
- Distinguish freehold vs usufruct
- Dubai-specific zones: [Can Foreigners Buy Property in Dubai](/guides/can-foreigners-buy-property-uae/)
- UAE-wide context: [Can Foreigners Buy Property in the UAE](/guides/can-foreigners-buy-property-uae/)

## Mistake 8: Do you trust guaranteed ROI programmes?

If marketing promises fixed rental returns, ask who funds shortfalls when vacancy hits. Sustainable yield comes from Ejari rents minus service charges, not brochure tables.

## Mistake 9: Do you pick the wrong letting strategy for the building?

Short-let bans, holiday home rules, and RERA registration differ by tower. Match strategy to bylaws before you furnish for Airbnb-style income.

## Mistake 10: Do you ignore home-country tax and reporting?

Many buyers focus on UAE zero income tax and miss capital gains or worldwide income rules at home. Document purchase price, fees, and rent for your accountant before closing.

## Honourable mentions: rushed remote closings

Remote buyers who skip video snagging and independent escrow checks remain the fastest-growing fraud category in Dubai resale and off-plan.

## What checklist should you run before you sign?

- Title deed or Oqood status verified on Dubai REST
- Escrow account matches Trakheesi project
- Mollak service charge schedule pulled for ready stock
- Net yield model includes 4% DLD and 7% vacancy unless proven otherwise
- SPA reviewed by UAE property counsel

## When should you walk away?

Walk away when sellers refuse escrow, service charge history, or Form B cost basis past day ten of due diligence.

## What Dubai gets right (context, not hype)

DLD registration, RERA escrow, and Ejari tenancy data give foreign buyers more transparency than many emerging markets. Use the portals; do not bypass them for speed.

Errors cluster at three gates: initial yield math before viewing, escrow verification before transfer, and visa assumptions at SPA signing. Slowing 48 hours at each gate prevents most capital loss.

## Buyer scenarios: who avoids most mistakes?

Investor-buyers who hold five-plus years should stress-test vacancy at 8% and model two exit years. End-user buyers should prioritise school radius and commute over headline yield. Remote buyers should never skip escrow verification because a gallery agent pressures a launch deadline.

Scenario D: European buyer trusts developer net yield slide on Business Bay tower, ignores Mollak history showing AED 28/sqft charges versus AED 18 launch estimate. Net yield falls from marketed 6.8% to 4.9%. Fix would have been one Dubai REST building pull before deposit.

Scenario E: CIS buyer wires reservation fee to marketing LLC, not escrow. Project registers later but deposit sits outside protected account for eleven months. Recovery required RERA complaint plus counsel fees exceeding AED 40,000. Fix is Trakheesi escrow screenshot before any transfer.

Scenario F: US buyer assumes LLC-owned Dubai unit qualifies for Golden Visa. Registered value on title below AED 2M threshold after developer discount accounting. Visa denied; asset still fine as rental but immigration plan failed. Fix is GDRFA pre-check with title draft before SPA.

## How do Ejari comparables stop resale overpay?

Pull three Ejari transacted rents in the same tower before you sign a secondary-market SPA. Portal asking rents often sit above registered contracts in soft submarkets. If your net yield model uses inflated rent, you overpay on day one and discover the gap only when the first lease renews at market.

## What mistakes appear with Power of Attorney closings?

Remote buyers often grant POA to a friend or gallery agent, then skip independent SPA review because they are not in the room. Delay penalties, service charge caps, and termination rights still bind you. Budget UAE property counsel even when you never visit before handover.

## How should you sequence mortgage, trustee, and DLD transfer?

Mortgage pre-approval, trustee appointment, and a DLD transfer slot should be lined up before you waive SPA conditions. Most delays come from missing NOC or uncleared service charge arrears on resale units, not from DLD mechanics themselves. Foreign buyers who treat transfer as a same-week formality often lose deposits when the seller's bank release slips.

## What off-plan payment-plan mistakes repeat?

Buyers focus on the headline price and miss post-handover cheques that stack after occupancy. Model cash flow through handover plus twelve months of service charges and fit-out. A cheap launch payment plan can still fail if post-handover instalments collide with vacancy during snagging.

## Which Dubai portals should foreign buyers use first?

Start with Dubai REST for title and escrow, Trakheesi for project permits, Mollak for service charges, and Ejari for rent evidence. One hour on these portals before a site visit prevents most YMYL errors in this guide. Cross-check visa questions on official GDRFA/ICP guidance rather than sales brochure footnotes.

Remote closings work when POA is notarised correctly, but video walk-through snagging is not optional on ready units. Capture meter readings, DEWA account numbers, and community access cards on film the day you receive keys.

Compare financing early: non-resident LTV caps near 50-60% change total acquisition cost and can flip a yield-positive unit into a negative carry if you rely on leverage.

Keep a single due-diligence folder with Trakheesi screenshots, escrow IBAN proof, Mollak charge PDFs, and lawyer mark-ups. Most preventable losses come from scattered WhatsApp threads rather than missing laws.
