## What is the AED 15,000 Green Visa skilled-worker threshold?

The skilled employee Green Visa salary floor requires AED 15,000 per month from a UAE-registered employer payroll at the April 2022 ICP launch, not offshore income. Mid-2026 that equals about USD 4,085 monthly before government fees near AED 1,500-1,800. Verify the current threshold on ICP Smart Services before filing.

| Threshold element | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skilled employee salary floor | AED 15,000/month | Payslip + certificate |
| Freelancer alternative | AED 360,000/year | Separate track |
| Typical government fees | AED 1,500-1,800 | When file complete |

Invest Gulf residency files treat the salary certificate total, not basic salary alone, as the primary AED 15,000 evidence line.

Important caveat: Visa rules in the UAE are periodically revised by ministerial decree. Confirm the figure on the ICP Smart Services portal or with a registered immigration consultant.

## Which ISCO levels 1 to 3 qualify for the skilled track?

ISCO skill levels 1, 2, and 3 qualify for the UAE Green Visa skilled employee track, while level 4 clerical roles do not qualify even at AED 20,000 monthly. Level 1 covers managers; level 2 covers professionals; level 3 covers technicians. Your MOL occupation code on the work permit is the primary reference at AED 15,000 salary floor.

| ISCO level | Example roles | Salary context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | CFO, operations director | Often well above AED 15,000 |
| Level 2 | Engineer, doctor, lawyer | Common at AED 15,000-35,000 |
| Level 3 | Technician, associate professional | Must still meet AED 15,000 |

Immigration briefs flag hybrid job titles for ICP review before filing fees of AED 1,500-1,800.

### ISCO level 1: Managers and executives

Level 1 includes chief executives, general managers, functional directors, and senior administrators. These leadership roles typically command compensation well above AED 15,000.

Examples: Chief Financial Officer, Operations Director, Regional General Manager, Head of Department in a listed company, Senior Government Official.

### ISCO level 2: Professionals

Level 2 is the largest qualifying group and covers roles requiring specialist knowledge, typically via tertiary education. The UAE does not mandate a degree certificate in statute for Green Visa eligibility; ISCO classification of the role is what matters.

Examples include:
- **Engineering:** Civil, mechanical, electrical, structural, and software engineers; project engineers; systems architects
- **Finance:** Accountants, financial analysts, auditors, actuaries, investment managers
- **Healthcare:** Physicians, dentists, pharmacists, specialist nurses, physiotherapists
- **Legal:** Licensed lawyers, legal counsel, compliance officers
- **Technology:** Software developers, data scientists, cybersecurity specialists, network architects
- **Education:** University lecturers, research professionals
- Architecture and design: Registered architects, urban planners

### ISCO level 3: Technicians and associate professionals

Level 3 covers roles requiring technical knowledge and practical skills supporting level 2 professionals.

Examples include:
- Engineering technicians, draftspersons, quality control inspectors
- IT support supervisors, network administrators, database administrators
- Healthcare associates: radiographers, laboratory technicians, operating theatre technicians
- Finance associates: loan officers, securities and finance clerks in supervisory roles
- Legal associates: paralegal supervisors, senior case managers

What does not qualify: ISCO skill level 4 and below. Consulting an immigration lawyer for borderline hybrid titles is worth the cost before you apply.

## What counts as salary: basic vs total package?

ICP's standard reference is the monthly total on the employer-issued salary certificate, and applicants with total package at or above AED 15,000 are generally accepted even when basic salary alone is lower. Bonuses, commissions, overtime, and in-kind benefits usually do not count unless converted to documented cash in the contract. Payslips must match the certificate for three to six months.

Our analysis tells applicants within AED 500 of the line to print total gross monthly on letterhead, not basic salary only.

- Discretionary bonuses and commissions are not counted
- In-kind benefits count only if converted to monthly cash in the contract
- Overtime and variable pay are not counted for threshold purposes

For applicants close to AED 15,000, ask HR to itemise components and show the monthly total explicitly on letterhead.

## UAE green visa skilled worker
Applicants should assemble a salary certificate, three to six months of payslips, an employment contract, work permit, bank statements, passport with 6+ months validity, photo, health insurance, and proof of accommodation before filing. Documents outside English or Arabic need UAE-recognised translation. Processing typically takes 14 to 28 days when the file is complete at AED 15,000 salary proof.

Invest Gulf checklists require payslip totals to match the salary certificate within AED 100 before portal upload.

- Work permit job title must align with ISCO levels 1 to 3 on file
- Bank credits should match payslip net within AED 100 for three to six months

## What happens when your salary is just below the threshold?

Salaries below AED 15,000 do not qualify on the skilled employee Green Visa track, but a standard UAE employment visa still has no salary floor. Alternatives include the freelancer track at AED 360,000 per year (AED 30,000 monthly) or the investor track at AED 500,000 capital. Do not file on a thin certificate.

| Route | Income threshold | Typical timeline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skilled employee Green Visa | AED 15,000/month | 2-4 weeks |
| Freelancer Green Visa | AED 360,000/year | 3-6 weeks |
| Standard work visa | No salary floor | Employer-led |

Our underwriting routes sub-AED 15,000 earners to standard employment visa first, then Green Visa upgrade after payroll rises.

- Confirm whether allowances push total package over AED 15,000
- Ask HR whether a revised salary certificate can reflect contractual total
- Compare freelancer documentation cost before quitting employment

## Does free zone vs mainland employment matter?

ICP processes Green Visa applications for free zone and mainland employees through the same Smart Services portal, and DIFC or ADGM salary documents are accepted when they reflect the legal employing entity paying at least AED 15,000 monthly. Your work permit and salary certificate must name the UAE entity that pays you. Parent-company letters from another jurisdiction are not a substitute for UAE payroll proof.

| Employment base | Salary proof | Typical processing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mainland LLC | MOL-stamped contract | 14-28 days |
| Free zone | FZ salary certificate | 14-28 days |
| DIFC / ADGM | Authority-compliant contract | 14-28 days |

Relocation files treat DIFC and ADGM employees as eligible when stamped salary totals meet AED 15,000, same as mainland.

DIFC and ADGM employees follow a slightly different labour authority path, but their employment records remain valid for Green Visa filing when stamped correctly.

## How does the salary threshold compare to freelancer and investor tracks?

The skilled employee route requires AED 15,000 per month, while freelancers need AED 360,000 per year (AED 30,000 monthly) and investors need AED 500,000 in UAE business capital for their respective Green Visa tracks. Employed professionals face the lowest income-based floor because a UAE company relationship already documents stability.

| Track | Monthly equivalent | Evidence pack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skilled employee | AED 15,000 | Salary certificate + payslips |
| Freelancer | AED 30,000 | Licence + bank inflows |
| Investor | Varies | AED 500,000 capital proof |

Our analysis tells freelancers to show six months of clean inflows before they resign from employment paying AED 15,000 or more.

For professionals debating a freelance conversion, consistent invoicing, a UAE freelance permit, and bank statements showing AED 30,000+ monthly inflows over six months are the practical standard ICP applies.

## Can a Green Visa remain valid when you change jobs?

A Green Visa can remain valid when you change jobs because it is self-sponsored, with ICP policy providing a grace period of up to 180 days to secure new qualifying employment. You update the employment record rather than restarting residency from scratch. The new role must still meet ISCO levels 1 to 3 and the AED 15,000 salary floor.

- Interview and negotiate a new role without immediate visa cancellation pressure
- Complete a notice period while onboarding a new employer
- Establish a freelance licence only if you can meet the AED 360,000 annual track

This continuity matters in technology, finance, and consulting where professionals move firms often. A company reorganisation does not automatically end residency during the grace window.

Insider tip: If your basic salary sits under AED 15,000 but contractual allowances push the total over the line, insist HR prints the full monthly total on the salary certificate before you file; payslip mismatches are the fastest ICP delay we see on skilled-worker files.

## What comes after approval: Emirates ID and health insurance?

After approval, residents complete Emirates ID biometrics and arrange UAE-compliant health insurance, with the ID typically produced in about five working days for the five-year Green Visa term. Government fees for the residency bundle often total AED 1,500 to 1,800 when the file is complete. The Emirates ID is mandatory for banking, utilities, and many property steps above AED 500,000 tickets.

Practical requirements are a valid UAE work permit in an ISCO 1 to 3 role, a salary certificate at AED 15,000 or above, three to six months of payslips and bank statements, and the standard residency document set. Processing typically takes 14 to 28 days. Government fees total about AED 1,500 to 1,800.

| Requirement | Threshold | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skilled employee salary | AED 15,000/month | Certificate + payslips |

Invest Gulf summary files treat ISCO level 4 roles as ineligible regardless of salary above AED 15,000.

- Work permit and salary certificate must name the same UAE employer
- Bank statements must show three to six months of matching credits
- Emirates ID and insurance follow within about five working days after approval

For those currently below the threshold, the standard UAE employment visa remains available with no salary floor. The skilled employee Green Visa becomes an upgrade once compensation and role align.
