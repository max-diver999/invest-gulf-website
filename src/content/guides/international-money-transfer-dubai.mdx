## How international transfers work from the UAE

The core advantage for Dubai residents: AED is pegged to USD at 3.6725. Dollar-denominated transfers carry zero currency risk. GBP, EUR, INR, and all non-USD currencies require spread analysis.

CBUAE (Central Bank UAE) licenses all operators. Exchange houses, banks, and fintech remittance providers fall under Money Service Businesses (MSB) regulation. Transfers above AED 55,000 require formal source-of-funds documentation regardless of provider.

Where Wise underperforms in Dubai:

- INR corridor, exchange houses often beat Wise total cost due to corridor volume
- PKR, NPR, PHP, BDT, Al Ansari, Lulu Exchange frequently offer better net rates
- Property-scale transfers (AED 500K+), banks provide SWIFT MT103 with source-of-funds trail; some developers and DLD trustees require formal bank-to-bank documentation
- Business-to-business: large commercial transfers may require bank SWIFT

## When are UAE exchange houses cheaper than banks?

Major licensed exchange houses in Dubai:

- Al Ansari Exchange, largest network by branches; strong on INR, PKR, NPR, LKR
- Lulu Exchange, competitive South Asian and Philippine corridors; mall presence
- Al Fardan Exchange, competitive on GBP, EUR; also strong on Gulf corridors
- GCC Exchange, good for Gulf-to-UK; branches across Dubai
- UAE Exchange / Unimoni, rebrand of UAE Exchange after stake changes; regional coverage

All are CBUAE-licensed. Compare rates online at provider websites or aggregators before visiting a branch, advertised rates are real-time and bindingly displayed.

## When do you need a UAE bank SWIFT transfer?

However, bank SWIFT is essential in three situations:

1. Property purchase transfers
DLD trustees and developer escrow agents typically require funds to arrive via UAE bank account, not a Wise wallet or exchange house. The MT103 SWIFT confirmation creates the documented trail needed for:
- AML source-of-funds compliance at the developer
- DLD trustee verification
- Home-country capital movement records (India RBI, UK HMRC, etc.)

2. Large one-off transfers requiring audit trail
Business proceeds, inheritance, sale of overseas property, any source requiring a documented chain prefers SWIFT, which produces an MT103 with reference fields.

3. Correspondent bank requirements
Receiving banks in some jurisdictions (US, Germany, Switzerland) only accept SWIFT from licensed banks, not from MSB intermediaries.

Major UAE banks for international SWIFT transfers:

- Emirates NBD, broad correspondent network; competitive FX desk for large transfers
- First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB), strong on major currencies; private banking desk available
- Mashreq, historically competitive rates for individual customers; strong USD and GBP
- ADCB, good for salary-linked accounts; competitive when relationship established
- HSBC UAE, direct connection to HSBC network reduces correspondent fees in 40+ countries
- Standard Chartered UAE, efficient for UK, India, Singapore corridors via group accounts

Opening a UAE bank account is the prerequisite step; see the [Dubai bank account opening guide](/guides/open-bank-account-dubai/).

## Which remittance corridors are cheapest from Dubai?

The Dubai-to-India corridor is the world's busiest remittance lane by volume. Exchange houses dominate. Top options in 2026:

- Al Ansari Exchange, consistently competitive on INR; real-time rates posted
- Lulu Exchange, strong across INR and smaller Indian cities via NEFT/IMPS
- Wise, competitive but typically 0.2 to 0.4% behind exchange houses on INR
- PhonePe, GPay NRE corridor, growing for NRI accounts but AED on-ramp still limited

India's LRS (Liberalisation Remittance Scheme) caps outward remittance at USD 250,000 per financial year per person. This applies to transfers sent *from* India, not inbound transfers. Indian residents receiving money from Dubai face no cap, declare under FEMA correctly.

### UK (GBP)

Exchange houses offer variable GBP rates. For this corridor, Wise, Revolut, and Currencies Direct typically deliver better net rates than exchange houses and UAE banks.

- Wise, 0.5 to 0.8% total cost on AED → GBP
- Currencies Direct, available in UAE; negotiated rates on GBP 10,000+
- Al Fardan Exchange, competitive GBP branches across Dubai
- UAE bank SWIFT, 1.5 to 2.5% spread; use only when bank documentation needed

### Europe (EUR)

Similar to GBP. Fintech corridors (Wise, Paysend) beat exchange houses on EUR. Banks slowest and costliest except on private banking large amounts.

### Philippines (PHP)

Exchange houses dominate alongside dedicated Filipino remittance services:

- LBC UAE, Filipino-focused; strong on PHP with door delivery options
- Lulu Exchange, competitive PHP with Palawan and rural network access
- Western Union / MoneyGram branches, cash pickup options; competitive PHP
- Wise, works but exchange houses beat on PHP total cost in most comparisons

### Russia / CIS (RUB, KZT, UZS)

SWIFT transfers to Russia are severely restricted post-2022 sanctions. Options in 2026:

- RAKBANK UAE → licensed Russian payment systems, limited pathways via SWIFT-alternative rails
- Cash withdrawal + physical transfer, legal within CBUAE cash declaration limits
- Crypto-fiat corridors, legal in UAE with VARA licence; peer compliance required
- KZT/UZS, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan corridors less restricted; exchange houses have bilateral agreements

Always verify current sanctions status before attempting Russian transfers, this corridor changes with geopolitical developments.
## How should you wire funds for a Dubai property purchase?

Step 3: Book FX before wiring
If transferring non-USD: lock in your rate when SPA is signed, not on trustee day. USD buyers: peg removes rate risk; wire any time before trustee.

Step 4: SWIFT transfer with SPA reference
Use SWIFT from your home bank. In the payment reference field: include your name, SPA reference number, and property address. This threads through the MT103 and satisfies DLD trustee AML inquiry.

Step 5: Receive MT103 confirmation
The sending bank issues an MT103 slip (SWIFT confirmation). Print and retain. DLD trustees and developer finance departments regularly request this as proof of international transfer. Wise and exchange house transactions do not generate MT103, another reason bank SWIFT is standard for property transfers.

Step 6: Allow 5 to 7 business days before trustee appointment
Correspondent bank routing can add 2 to 3 days. CBUAE AML hold on large first inbound can add 1 to 2 more. Book trustee appointment after confirming cleared funds in UAE account.

**Step 7: Manager's cheque or local transfer for DLD**
Once funds clear in UAE, convert to manager's cheque (cashier's cheque) for developer payment and DLD. Personal transfers work for some developers; others require manager's cheque as final instrument.

## What AML and source-of-funds rules apply to large transfers?

**CRS/FATCA reporting:**
UAE participates in the Common Reporting Standard (CRS). Banks report account information on non-UAE-national residents to their home tax authorities annually. This covers balances and interest, not remittance amounts, but home country tax authorities see account existence. FATCA reporting applies to US person accounts specifically.

This is not a reason to avoid transfers, it is the compliance reality for all UAE-resident expats. Structure transfers cleanly with documentation and the process is routine.

| Property deposit or trustee day | UAE bank SWIFT | MT103 and local account clearance |

## How can you reduce FX and transfer fees from Dubai?

Compare **total landed cost** (send amount minus receive amount), not the advertised fee. On AED 100,000 to GBP, a 0.3% spread difference is AED 300 every transfer.

- Batch less urgent payments to hit better FX tiers at exchange houses.
- Avoid double conversion (AED to USD to INR) unless the receiver requires it.
- Use limit orders or rate alerts during SPA negotiations for property.
- Keep one UAE salary account for SWIFT property wires and a separate wallet for daily remittance.

## What checklist should run before you sign?

1. Open UAE resident bank account within first two weeks of residency (requires Emirates ID: typically available 2 to 3 weeks post-visa)
2. Notify home-country bank of UAE address; update international transfer limits if needed
3. Register with one exchange house account online (Al Ansari app or Lulu Exchange) for routine remittance
4. Download Wise and compare on first non-routine transfer
5. For property purchase: align bank SWIFT capability before making an offer

Exchange rate volatility affects buyers from GBP, EUR, and INR backgrounds most. Set a rate alert in Wise or your bank app when you enter SPA negotiations, a 1% move on AED 2M is AED 20,000.

## Key takeaways for Dubai international transfers

Wise and exchange houses win on everyday remittance; UAE bank SWIFT wins when you need MT103 proof for property or heavy AML review. AED is pegged to USD, so dollar earners avoid FX spread on the Dubai leg. Document source of funds above AED 55,000 and keep SPA references on property wires. Start property transfers at least five to seven business days before trustee day.
