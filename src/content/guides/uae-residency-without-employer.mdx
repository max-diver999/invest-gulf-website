In 2026, more than a third of high-net-worth individuals looking at UAE residency come without a job offer in hand. They are freelancers, remote workers, retirees, property investors, or entrepreneurs who want long-term legal presence in the UAE without tying their right to live there to a single employer's HR department.

The UAE now accommodates all of them. The routes are different, the thresholds are different, and the rights that come with each visa differ significantly. Here is what you actually need to know.

What happens if the property value drops below AED 2M after I get the Golden Visa?
The Golden Visa is tied to the registered property value at the time of application. A subsequent drop in market value does not automatically cancel your visa. The threshold matters most at the point of renewal; if the registered value falls below AED 2M by renewal time, you would need to acquire additional qualifying assets or switch to another eligible category.

Can I hold two UAE residency visas simultaneously?
No. UAE residents hold one active residency visa at a time. If you hold a Remote Work Visa and subsequently apply for a Golden Visa through a property purchase, the Remote Work Visa must be cancelled before the new visa is activated.

Is there a UAE residency route based purely on business ownership without active income?
The Property Investor Visa and the Golden Visa (investor route) are the closest equivalents for pure capital deployment. Operating a UAE company through a free zone or mainland licence with minimal personal income typically falls under the Green Visa framework, which requires demonstrating business activity and income.

How long can I stay outside the UAE without losing residency?
Standard employment and family visas are cancelled if the holder remains outside the UAE for more than 6 continuous months. The Golden Visa is commonly cited as allowing longer absence periods, though the exact rules are applied at authority discretion and have been subject to updates. Remote Work, Green, and Retirement Visa re-entry rules generally follow standard residency obligations unless specific exceptions apply. Check current ICP and GDRFA guidance for your visa category before extended travel.
