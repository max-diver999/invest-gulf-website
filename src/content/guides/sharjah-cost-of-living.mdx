## How should you use this Sharjah cost guide?

Sharjah cost planning typically starts by copying the budget tables into a spreadsheet and replacing indicative rents with three live listings per area. Two families on the same **AED 35,000** gross can land at **AED 24,000** or **AED 38,000** monthly spend depending on Dubai school choice alone.

- Swap indicative rents for three live listings
- Amortise school fees monthly before comparing households
- Stress-test summer SEWA and a second car before signing

Our research treats portal asks as opening bids, not closed budgets.

Sharjah rent in the area typically ranges from about **AED 5,500 to 8,000** for mid 2BR stock in Muwailih and Al Majaz, while similar Dubai fringe towers run **AED 10,000 to 14,000**. Budget one month deposit plus **5%** agency on most leases, and expect **4 to 6** post-dated cheques unless an employer negotiates quarterly transfer.

## What do SEWA utilities cost in Sharjah summers?

SEWA utilities in the area typically cost **AED 350 to 900** per month in winter and **AED 900 to 1,400** in peak summer on older split-AC apartments. District-cooled towers can look cheaper until chiller bills arrive, and large 3BR units can exceed **AED 1,800** per month in summer. Invest Gulf asks for July and August statements before foreign buyers sign.

| Utility line | Typical apartment (AED/month) |
| --- | --- |
| Winter baseline | 350 to 700 |
| Summer peak (split AC) | 900 to 1,400 |
| District-cooled tower | 500 to 1,100 (verify provider) |

## How much does Dubai commuting cost from Sharjah?

Dubai commuting from Sharjah typically costs **AED 1,400 to 2,800** per month for one office car, rising to **AED 2,400 to 5,000** for a two-car household with school runs. Fuel, Salik, and parking are the main lines, and a second school car often erases a large share of the rent save.

| Item | AED/month |
|---|---|
| Car 1, office commute | 1,400 to 2,800 |
| Car 2, school / reverse | 1,000 to 2,200 |
| Household transport | 2,400 to 5,000 |

See [Sharjah Dubai commuter guide](/guides/sharjah-dubai-commuter-guide/) and [Sharjah schools commute Dubai](/guides/sharjah-schools-commute-dubai/). We model commute before celebrating a 40% rent gap.

## Are groceries and dining cheaper in Sharjah?

Groceries in Sharjah typically run about **5% to 10%** below Dubai premium mall baskets on many staples, but the real swing is rent and schools, not weekly food shops. Dining stays affordable in Al Majaz and Muwaileh, while alcohol runs often mean a Dubai or Ajman store trip of about **AED 300 to 800** per month.

- Staples: often 5% to 10% below Dubai premium mall baskets
- Real savings drivers: rent and school path, not groceries
- Alcohol budget line if relevant: AED 300 to 800 per month

Our research keeps grocery gaps as a footnote behind commute math.

## Are Sharjah schools cheaper than Dubai KHDA schools?

SPEA-regulated schools in the area typically sit **15% to 30%** below equivalent KHDA tiers on tuition, yet many families still choose Dubai KHDA campuses and pay **AED 8,000 to 15,000** per year per child in bus fees from Sharjah. We model both paths before foreign buyers sign a **12-month** lease tied to a school commute.

| Path | Typical monthly school + transport load |
| --- | --- |
| Two children, SPEA mid tier in Sharjah | AED 6,000 to 8,500 amortised |
| Two children, KHDA British mid from Sharjah home | AED 11,000 to 14,000 amortised + dual peak drives |

## What should you budget for healthcare in Sharjah?

Healthcare top-ups in Sharjah typically cost **AED 200 to 500** per month when employer cover handles basics for Dubai-employed residents. Specialists still pull families to Dubai hospitals, so keep **AED 200 to 400** per trip in the model when paediatrics or orthopaedics sit outside Sharjah panels.

- Employer cover: basics for many Dubai-employed residents
- Outpatient top-up band: AED 200 to 500 per month
- Dubai specialist trip allowance: AED 200 to 400

Our analysis treats healthcare as a small line unless a chronic specialist path is known.

## When does Sharjah rent arbitrage stop working?

Sharjah rent arbitrage typically stops working when a **AED 6,500** rent save is erased by two cars at about **AED 4,200** plus a KHDA school delta near **AED 3,000** versus SPEA. Net monthly spend then turns negative versus a Dubai base, and the household should relocate or change schools.

Failure example:
- Same rent save AED 6,500
- Two cars AED 4,200
- KHDA school delta +AED 3,000 vs SPEA
- Net negative, relocate to Dubai or change schools

We flag dual Dubai school runs as the most common arbitrage killer.

## What salary do singles and families need in Sharjah?

Salary needs in Sharjah typically start near **AED 12,000+** net for a comfortable mid-tier single with one car commute, keeping rent under **35%** of net where possible. Families on SPEA schools frequently land **AED 22,000 to 28,000** monthly all-in, while Dubai KHDA schooling from Sharjah pushes many households toward **AED 32,000 to 38,000**.

- Single mid tier with one car: often AED 12,000+ net
- Family on SPEA schools: AED 22,000 to 28,000 all-in
- Family on Dubai KHDA from Sharjah: AED 32,000 to 38,000
- Relocation liquidity: about AED 20,000 for cheques in weeks 5 to 6

Invest Gulf carries the liquidity buffer before first payroll clears.

## Who should choose Sharjah over Dubai?

Sharjah is a fit for Dubai-employed families who accept commute time for about **AED 2,500 to 6,000** monthly net savings after fuel and Salik. The area fits poorly when both spouses need daily Dubai school runs at opposite ends of the city, or when off-plan resale needs a **6 to 12 month** exit without stress testing.

- Good fit: Dubai salary plus apartment rent arbitrage
- Weak fit: dual opposite-end Dubai school runs every day
- Weak fit: off-plan exit assumed inside 6 to 12 months without stress test

We prefer SPEA or employer-covered schooling when underwriting the savings band.

## How should you budget a Sharjah household month?

A Sharjah household month typically requires one sheet covering rent, utilities, school, Dubai commute, and groceries, because a lower rent can still lose to fuel and two hours of daily driving. Build the model before signing a **12-month** lease, and stress-test a second car at **AED 1,000 to 2,200** for foreign buyers.

| Bucket | What to include |
| --- | --- |
| Housing | Rent plus any chiller or service fees you pay |
| Transport | Fuel, parking, Salik if used |
| Schools | Fees, bus, activities |
| Health | Insurance top-ups |
| Lifestyle | Malls, dining, weekend Dubai trips |

**Commute and school stress test:** Sharjah rent arbitrage only holds after commute and school lines are fully loaded. A classic family pattern saves AED 4,000 to 8,000 per month on a 2BR versus Dubai, yet net savings often shrink to AED 2,500 to 5,000 once fuel, Salik, parking, and a second school car are included. SPEA mid-tier schooling amortised near AED 6,000 to 8,500 can preserve the edge, while KHDA British mid pathways at AED 11,000 to 14,000 plus dual peak drives frequently erase it. Summer SEWA spikes of AED 900 to 1,400 on older apartments are another line that brochure rent comparisons omit. Invest Gulf's practical rule is to reject any Sharjah-versus-Dubai decision that has not modelled two cars, school transport, and a July utility bill on the same spreadsheet.

**Insider tip:** Before you sign a Sharjah 2BR lease to "save versus Dubai," price the second school-run car and one July SEWA bill; those two lines erase more rent arbitrage than grocery price gaps of 5% to 10%.
