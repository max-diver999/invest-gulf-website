## How should you use this Sharjah cost guide?

Copy the budget tables into a spreadsheet and swap indicative rents for three live listings per area. Two families on the same AED 35,000 gross can land at AED 24,000 or AED 38,000 monthly spend depending on Dubai school choice alone. Amortise school fees monthly, add bus or second-car costs, and stress-test summer SEWA before you treat the agent's first quote as your plan.

## How much is rent by area in Sharjah?

Sharjah rent savings versus Dubai usually show up first on 2BR stock in Muwailih and Al Majaz, where AED 5,500 to 8,000 bands still exist while similar Dubai fringe towers run AED 10,000 to 14,000. Budget one month deposit plus 5% agency on most leases, and expect 4 to 6 post-dated cheques unless your employer negotiates quarterly transfer.

## What do SEWA utilities cost in Sharjah summers?

Apartments on split AC often run AED 350 to 900/month in winter and AED 900 to 1,400 in peak summer on older stock. District-cooled towers can look cheaper on paper until chiller bills arrive, so ask the landlord for last July and August SEWA statements before you sign. Villas and large 3BR units with poor insulation can exceed AED 1,800/month in summer without pool pumps.

| Utility line | Typical apartment (AED/month) |
| --- | --- |
| Winter baseline | 350 to 700 |
| Summer peak (split AC) | 900 to 1,400 |
| District-cooled tower | 500 to 1,100 (verify provider) |

## Are groceries and dining cheaper in Sharjah?

Groceries run roughly 5 to 10% below Dubai premium mall baskets on many staples, but the real swing is rent and schools, not weekly food shops. Dining out stays affordable in Al Majaz and Muwaileh, while alcohol purchases usually mean a Dubai or Ajman licensed store run, budget AED 300 to 800/month if that applies to your household.

## Are Sharjah schools cheaper than Dubai KHDA schools?

SPEA-regulated schools often sit 15 to 30% below equivalent KHDA tiers on tuition, yet many families still choose Dubai KHDA campuses and pay AED 8,000 to 15,000/year per child in bus fees from Sharjah. Model both paths in one sheet before you sign a 12-month lease tied to a school commute.

| Path | Typical monthly school + transport load |
| --- | --- |
| Two children, SPEA mid tier in Sharjah | AED 6,000 to 8,500 amortised |
| Two children, KHDA British mid from Sharjah home | AED 11,000 to 14,000 amortised + dual peak drives |

## What should you budget for healthcare in Sharjah?

Employer cover handles basics for many Dubai-employed Sharjah residents, but outpatient top-ups of AED 200 to 500/month are common. Specialists for complex cases still pull families to Dubai hospitals, so keep AED 200 to 400 per trip in the model when paediatrics or orthopaedics sit outside Sharjah panels.

## When does Sharjah rent arbitrage stop working?

Failure example:
- Same rent save AED 6,500
- Two cars AED 4,200
- KHDA school delta +AED 3,000 vs SPEA
- Net negative, relocate to Dubai or change schools

## How does Sharjah compare to RAK and Ajman on cost?

Sharjah wins on dense apartment rent if you work in Dubai or Sharjah city. RAK wins on beach villa and townhouse product at different price points, while Ajman can undercut Sharjah slightly on older stock with fewer amenities. Cross-read [RAK vs Sharjah living](/guides/rak-vs-sharjah-living/) and [RAK cost of living detailed](/guides/rak-cost-of-living-detailed/) before you assume Sharjah is always the cheapest UAE commuter emirate.

| Ajman | Lowest apartment ticket | Often 5 to 10% below Sharjah peers |
| RAK | Coastal villa lifestyle | Different product, not like-for-like tower |

## What salary do singles and families need in Sharjah?

Keep rent under 35% of net where possible, easier in Sharjah than Dubai at the same salary. Singles often need AED 12,000+ net for a comfortable mid tier with one car commute. Families on local SPEA schools frequently land AED 22,000 to 28,000/month all-in, while Dubai KHDA schooling from Sharjah pushes many households toward AED 32,000 to 38,000 before holidays.

First pay often arrives week 5 to 6 after relocation, so carry AED 20,000 liquidity for cheques, deposits, and furniture while payroll catches up.

## Who should choose Sharjah over Dubai?

Sharjah fits Dubai-employed families who accept commute time in exchange for AED 2,500 to 6,000/month net savings after fuel and Salik. It fits poorly when both spouses need daily Dubai school runs at opposite ends of the city, or when resale liquidity on Sharjah off-plan is part of your investment thesis without a 6 to 12 month exit stress test.

## How to budget a Sharjah household month

Build rent, DEWA or Sharjah utilities, school, commute to Dubai, and groceries on one sheet. A lower rent can still lose to fuel and two hours of daily driving.

| Bucket | What to include |
| --- | --- |
| Housing | Rent plus any chiller or service fees you pay |
| Transport | Fuel, parking, Salik if used |
| Schools | Fees, bus, activities |
| Health | Insurance top-ups |
| Lifestyle | Malls, dining, weekend Dubai trips |
