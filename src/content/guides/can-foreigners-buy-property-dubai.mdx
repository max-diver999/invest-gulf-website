## Legal basis: Dubai Property Law

Law No. 7 of 2006 (Dubai Property Law) grants non-GCC nationals:

- Freehold ownership in designated zones
- Usufruct and musataha (long-term leasehold up to 99 years) in a wider zone set

Registration = ownership. A reservation cheque, marketing agreement, or unsigned SPA draft is not legal title. Only DLD registration, title deed (ready) or **Oqood** (off-plan), creates enforceable ownership.

**Freehold** means indefinite ownership of the unit plus a defined share of common areas. **Leasehold** means time-limited use rights, verify which you are buying if the price looks unusually low.

## Ownership types: what foreigners actually receive

| Type | Duration | Resale | Typical product |
|---|---|---|---|
| Freehold | Indefinite | Full | Most apartments/villas in designated zones |
| Usufruct | Up to 99 years | With restrictions | Select developments |
| Musataha | Up to 99 years | Land development rights | Rare for residential investors |

Most international buyers purchase freehold apartments in high-rise towers. Villa communities (Arabian Ranches, Dubai Hills) are also predominantly freehold for foreigners in designated sub-zones.

## No visa required: but residency is available separately

**Purchase:** no UAE residence visa needed. Tourist entry or remote POA purchase is standard.

After purchase, optional residency routes:

| Route | Typical threshold | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Property investor visa | AED ~750,000+ (confirm current official rules) | 2-year renewable |
| Golden Visa | AED 2M registered value | 10-year renewable |
| Employment visa | Job offer | 2–3 years |

Property ownership does not automatically grant any visa. Application to **GDRFA/ICP** follows separately.

## Purchase process: Dubai-specific steps

### Ready (secondary) property

1. Zone and building verification: Trakheesi / Dubai REST
2. Offer and **Form F** (RERA MOU)
3. **NOC** from developer (if applicable)
4. DLD transfer at Registration Trustee Center
5. **Title deed** issued in buyer name

Timeline: 2–5 business days for cash deals after NOC.

### Off-plan property

1. Trakheesi project verification
2. SPA signing
3. Payments to RERA escrow only
4. Oqood registration at DLD (4% + admin)
5. Handover → title deed conversion

Full step-by-step: [How to Buy Property in Dubai](/guides/how-to-buy-property-dubai-step-by-step/).

## RERA escrow: non-negotiable for off-plan

Dubai's off-plan market runs on forward sales, 60–70% of volume. RERA escrow is the buyer protection layer.

Verify before any off-plan deposit:

- Escrow account number and approved bank
- Trakheesi project registration status
- Payment schedule tied to construction milestones
- Oqood registration commitment in SPA

**Never pay** into a developer general account for off-plan "discount."

See [Off-Plan Property Dubai Guide](/guides/off-plan-property-dubai-guide/).

## Remote purchase: Power of Attorney

Foreign buyers in UK, India, Russia/CIS, China, Pakistan commonly complete purchases without visiting Dubai.

POA requirements:

- Notarised and attested (apostille where applicable)

## Leasehold traps: when "ownership" is not freehold

Foreigners encounter **usufruct** and **musataha** structures in developments marketed with ownership language.

| Signal | Likely structure |
|---|---|
| Price 30–40% below freehold comparables | Leasehold |
| "99-year ownership" wording | Usufruct, verify renewal terms |
| Developer is landowner government entity | May be leasehold grant |
| Resale restricted to GCC only | Leasehold condition |

Always read the title deed preview or SPA schedule describing ownership type. DLD registration documents state freehold explicitly when it is freehold.

## Golden Visa and foreign buyers: Dubai registration path

Dubai foreign purchases register with **DLD**. Golden Visa property route references **registered value** on title or Oqood.

Foreign buyer sequence:

1. Purchase in freehold zone (no visa needed)
2. Complete Oqood or title registration
3. Gather immigration documents
4. Apply via GDRFA/ICP
5. Receive 10-year residence if approved

Mortgage note: 2026 commentary widely reports mortgaged purchases may qualify with bank NOC, earlier 50% equity guidance is widely reported as revised. Confirm on your case.

## Foreign buyer FAQ: practical scenarios

Indian investor, first Dubai purchase: JVC freehold studio, cash, remote POA, legal and common. Verify RERA broker and escrow if off-plan.

UK buyer, Golden Visa target: AED 2M+ in Marina or Hills, confirm registered value on title, not marketing "from" price.

Russian buyer, existing funds offshore: UAE banks apply source-of-funds rules, plan transfers before handover cheque dates.

Chinese buyer, company purchase: Possible via corporate structure, corporate tax and home-country reporting apply.

Saudi GCC national: Different ownership rules apply to GCC citizens, not identical to non-GCC foreign framework.

## Post-purchase foreign landlord obligations

Foreign landlords without UAE residency can still own and rent, but need:

- Ejari-registered tenancy for legal enforcement
- Licensed property manager if not self-managing from abroad
- **DEWA** account management between tenants
- Service charge payments to OA, non-payment blocks resale NOC

Non-resident landlords sometimes forget OA communications, special levies arrive by email to outdated addresses.
