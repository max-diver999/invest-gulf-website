## What law allows foreigners to own freehold in Dubai?

**Law No. 7 of 2006** (as amended) grants non-GCC nationals freehold ownership in **designated zones** listed by the Ruler. Ownership types include:

- **Freehold** in designated zones (indefinite title plus common-area share)
- **Usufruct and musataha** (long-term leasehold, commonly up to 99 years) in a wider area set

Registration equals ownership. A reservation cheque, marketing brochure, or unsigned SPA is not legal title. Only **DLD registration** on a title deed (ready) or **Oqood** (off-plan) creates enforceable ownership you can sell, mortgage, or use for Golden Visa evidence.

## Which Dubai communities are freehold for foreign buyers?

Over **60 designated freehold zones** cover most investor-facing stock. Core clusters include Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai, Palm Jumeirah, Business Bay, JVC, Dubai Hills Estate, Arabian Ranches, Dubai Creek Harbour, and Dubai South. Emerging pipelines worth mapping on the DLD portal include:

| Zone | Note |
|  |  |
| Dubai South / Expo City corridor | Logistics + residential pipeline |
| Tilal Al Ghaf | Villa-led master plan |
| Dubai Islands (Deira Islands) | Waterfront redevelopment |
| Sobha Hartland II | Premium mid-rise |
| MBR City | Mixed luxury inventory |

Outside designated zones, agents may still say "ownership" when they mean **leasehold**. If the price looks unusually low for Marina or Hills pricing, verify ownership type on the DLD unit profile before you wire a deposit.

## Do foreigners need a UAE visa before they can buy?

**No.** You may buy on a tourist visa, during a short business trip, or entirely remotely through a notarised **Power of Attorney**. Property ownership does **not** automatically grant residency. Golden Visa or other routes are separate applications through GDRFA/ICP after you hold qualifying registered value. See [UAE Golden Visa through property](/guides/uae-golden-visa-property/) and [UAE residency vs citizenship](/guides/uae-residency-vs-citizenship/).

## What is the DLD process for ready (secondary) property?

For completed units, the sequence is predictable once NOC and funds are ready:

1. Zone and building verification via Trakheesi / Dubai REST
2. Agreed price and **Form F** (RERA MOU) with a licensed broker
3. Developer **NOC** where required (service charges cleared)
4. Transfer at a **Registration Trustee** centre linked to DLD
5. **Title deed** issued in the buyer name

Cash deals often close in **2 to 5 business days** after NOC. Full mechanics: [How to buy property in Dubai step by step](/guides/how-to-buy-property-dubai-step-by-step/).

## How does RERA escrow protect off-plan foreign buyers?

Off-plan buyers must pay into a **RERA-regulated escrow account**, not a developer operating account. Funds release against certified construction milestones. The registration path is:

1. Trakheesi project verification
2. SPA signing with a RERA-registered developer
3. Instalments to escrow only
4. **Oqood** registration at DLD (4% transfer fee plus admin on qualifying events)
5. Handover and conversion to full title deed

Never accept a "bank transfer discount" to a general corporate account. Deep dive: [Off-plan property Dubai guide](/guides/off-plan-property-dubai-guide/).

## What fees should foreign buyers budget at DLD?

Dubai transaction costs are transparent relative to many Gulf markets, but they sit **on top of** the purchase price:

| Cost item | Typical range |
|  |  |
| DLD transfer fee | 4% of purchase price |
| DLD admin (title deed) | AED 250 to 580 |
| Trustee office fee | AED 2,000 to 4,000 |
| NOC (developer) | AED 500 to 5,000 |
| Broker commission | Often 2% (confirm side in Form F) |
| Mortgage registration | 0.25% of loan amount if financed |

Budget roughly **5 to 6% above price** for a cash buyer. Detail tables: [Cost of buying property in Dubai](/guides/cost-of-buying-property-dubai/).

## Can foreigners buy Dubai property remotely or through a company?

**Remote purchase:** A UAE-notarised or home-country POA (apostilled where required) can sign Form F, SPA, and attend trustee transfer. Plan courier timelines for manager's cheques and bank compliance on source of funds.

**Corporate ownership:** UAE mainland or free-zone entities can hold title, but trigger **UAE corporate tax**, substance, and home-country reporting rules. Most first-time investors buy in personal name unless a tax adviser recommends an SPV.

**Mortgages:** Non-residents often receive up to **50% LTV** on properties under AED 5 million; residents may access higher LTV on a first home. Pre-approval before Form F reduces walk-aways.

## How does Golden Visa link to foreign-owned Dubai property?

Golden Visa through property generally requires **AED 2 million registered value** on title deed or Oqood, excluding the 4% DLD fee from the threshold calculation. Mortgaged units may qualify when the bank issues a **Golden Visa NOC** on registered full value (2026 practice widely reports revision of older 50% equity guidance; confirm with your lender and GDRFA). Aggregation across multiple DLD-registered units in one name can reach AED 2M+. Application steps: [Golden Visa application step by step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/).

## How does Dubai foreign ownership compare to Abu Dhabi and RAK?

Dubai offers the deepest foreign-buyer infrastructure: DLD data, trustee network, RERA brokers, and escrow discipline. Abu Dhabi uses **DMT** with a shorter list of investment zones under Law 13/2019. Ras Al Khaimah markets freehold to foreigners in specific master plans with its own fee schedule. Do not assume a Dubai checklist copies to another emirate. Portfolio context: [Dubai property investment guide](/guides/dubai-property-investment-guide/).

## Who is actually buying Dubai property in 2026?

Dubai logged **205,000+ transactions in 2024**, with foreign participation near **68%** in recent quarters. Indian, British, Russian, Chinese, and GCC buyers dominate volume, but each cohort faces different bank KYC, POA, and tax reporting paths at home. Deep comparables help valuation; they do not replace zone and escrow checks.

### Practical buyer scenarios

**Indian investor, first studio in JVC:** Cash purchase with remote POA is routine; verify RERA broker and escrow if off-plan.

**UK buyer targeting Golden Visa:** Confirm **registered** value on title or Oqood, not marketing "from" pricing.

**Corporate buyer from China:** Possible via UAE entity; model corporate tax and repatriation before SPA.

**Saudi GCC national:** GCC ownership rules differ from non-GCC foreign frameworks; confirm category with DLD before assuming identical treatment.

## What landlord duties apply after a foreign buyer completes purchase?

Non-resident landlords still need **Ejari** for enforceable tenancy, timely **service charge** payments to the owners association (arrears block NOC on resale), **DEWA** handovers between tenants, and a licensed manager if you are not self-managing from abroad. Special levies often arrive by email; keep OA contacts current.
