## What is the UAE Golden Visa through property?

The UAE Golden Visa through property is a 10-year renewable residency route that typically requires AED 2 million in DLD or DMT registered freehold value, and Invest Gulf treats it as the clearest path for foreign buyers who want family sponsorship without employer sponsorship in this market.

The programme was introduced in 2019 and expanded several times since. Applications run through ICP or GDRFA Dubai for Dubai-based files.

Other pathways include fund or company investors at AED 2M+, entrepreneurs, scientists, outstanding professionals, and high-performing students. For real estate buyers, the property route remains the most straightforward.

- 10-year renewable residency
- Family sponsorship rights
- Not tied to a UAE employer

## What counts toward the AED 2 million threshold?

The AED 2 million threshold typically means the SPA or Title Deed registered purchase price shown on Oqood or the Title Deed, and Invest Gulf excludes the 4% DLD transfer fee, agent commissions, and trustee fees from qualifying value for foreign buyers targeting this market.

| Included | Excluded |
| --- | --- |
| Purchase price on Oqood or Title Deed | 4% DLD transfer fee |
| Aggregated units in the same personal name | Agent commissions |
| Freehold registrations in eligible zones | Trustee and admin fees |

For off-plan: if the SPA price is AED 2.2 million and Oqood shows AED 2.2M, that qualifies even if you have not paid AED 2.2M yet. Buying a AED 1.95M unit and hoping to round up via fees does not work.

## Can mortgaged property qualify for the Golden Visa?

Mortgaged property can typically qualify when the DLD-registered purchase value is at least AED 2 million, because Invest Gulf now models the registered price rather than equity paid as the headline metric for foreign buyers, subject to a bank NOC and current GDRFA or ICP practice.

A property purchased for AED 2.5 million with an active UAE bank mortgage of AED 1.2 million can still qualify on registered value. Pre-2024 practice often required 50% or more paid; mid-2025 onward reporting shifted to registered value.

Before you rely on a mortgaged unit:

1. Call GDRFA Dubai to confirm current practice
2. Obtain a bank letter confirming outstanding mortgage and registered price
3. Engage a licensed immigration consultant with recent mortgaged-property filings

## Can you combine several properties for AED 2 million?

Multiple freehold properties registered in the same personal name can typically be aggregated to reach AED 2 million, and Invest Gulf examples include AED 800,000 plus AED 1,300,000 or AED 550,000 plus AED 1,600,000 as qualifying pairs for foreign buyers.

| Scenario | Property 1 | Property 2 | Total | Qualifies? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Two JVC apartments | AED 800,000 | AED 1,300,000 | AED 2,100,000 | Yes |
| JVC studio + Marina 1-bed | AED 550,000 | AED 1,600,000 | AED 2,150,000 | Yes |
| Three studio apartments | AED 500,000 | AED 550,000 + AED 600,000 | AED 1,650,000 | No |

Aggregation allows buyers who cannot stretch to a single AED 2M unit to assemble a qualifying portfolio. Two mid-market apartments at AED 2.1M combined often deliver stronger net yield than a single premium unit at AED 2.1M.

## What benefits does the Golden Visa provide?

Golden Visa benefits typically include a 10-year renewable residency, Emirates ID, family sponsorship, and re-entry flexibility without the standard 6-month absence lapse, and Invest Gulf treats independence from employer sponsorship as the core gain for foreign buyers after the AED 2 million property route.

| Benefit | Practical detail |
| --- | --- |
| Residency term | 10 years, renewable |
| Absence rule | Ordinary visas lapse after 6 months abroad; Golden Visa holders are not subject to that trap |
| Family | Spouse and children under 18; parents in some cases |
| Cost context | Main applicant fees AED 4,000 to 5,500 after property qualifies |

UAE resident identity card. The Emirates ID issued to Golden Visa holders is the same as any UAE resident card, accepted for UAE banking, healthcare, government services, and identity verification.

Freedom from employment dependency. Unlike a standard UAE work visa, the Golden Visa is not tied to an employer. If you leave a job, change business, or stop working, your visa is not immediately at risk.

- Emirates ID for banking and government services
- Family sponsorship for spouse and eligible children
- Re-entry without the ordinary 6-month absence trap

## What does the Golden Visa not include?

The Golden Visa does not include UAE citizenship, automatic employment authorisation, or automatic tax residency, and Invest Gulf still requires a separate work permit for most UAE jobs and a Tax Residency Certificate after about 183 days for foreign buyers.

| Not included | What still applies |
| --- | --- |
| Citizenship | Residency only, 10-year renewable |
| Automatic work rights | Work permit still needed for most jobs |
| Automatic tax residency | Typically 183 days or centre-of-life proof |

Automatic right to work. Self-employment and freelance work may be possible through a freelance permit from relevant free zones or DED, but employment by a UAE company still needs a work permit.

Access to all UAE public services at resident rate varies by category. Verify specific service eligibility as needed.

## Does the Golden Visa make you a UAE tax resident?

UAE tax residency typically requires about 183 days in the UAE per year or a clear centre-of-life case certified by the Ministry of Finance, and Invest Gulf warns that holding a Golden Visa on an AED 2M buy-to-let while living abroad for 11 months does not create tax residency for foreign buyers.

Simply holding a Golden Visa does not satisfy either requirement. Home-country tax rules can continue to apply under CRS and local law.

If tax efficiency is a primary motivation, engage a tax adviser in both the UAE and your home country before you buy. Exit tax, deemed disposal, and tie-breaker clauses need professional advice.

## How long does the application take and what does it cost?

Golden Visa processing typically takes 5 to 15 working days after documents are complete, with total fees near AED 4,000 to 5,500 per main applicant and AED 2,000 to 3,500 per dependent, and Invest Gulf requires physical presence in the UAE for the 2 to 3 week medical and Emirates ID window for foreign buyers.

### Process:

1. Prepare documentation: gather all property and identity documents; obtain health insurance if not already held
2. Submit application: via GDRFA Dubai (for Dubai properties) or ICP online portal
3. **Medical test**: must be completed in the UAE; includes blood test and chest X-ray; takes 1-3 days
4. Emirates ID application: submitted simultaneously or immediately after visa approval
5. Visa and EID issuance: 5-15 working days from submission; both documents arrive together

| Item | Approximate cost |
|---|---|
| Golden Visa fee | AED 2,800-3,500 |
| Medical test | AED 500-700 |
| Emirates ID | AED 370 |
| Service fees (typing centre / PRO) | AED 300-500 |
| Health insurance (annual) | AED 800-3,000 (depending on coverage) |
| Total per main applicant | **AED 4,000-5,500** |

## How does the property route compare to other visa paths?

The property route typically remains the most accessible for buyers who want UAE residency without UAE employment, while the 2-year investment visa near AED 750K property provides a shorter option below AED 2M, and Invest Gulf compares both paths before foreign buyers lock a freehold unit.

| Path | Typical property threshold | Term |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Golden Visa property | AED 2 million registered | 10 years |
| Shorter investment visa | Near AED 750K property band | About 2 years |
| Employment visa | No property minimum | Tied to employer |
