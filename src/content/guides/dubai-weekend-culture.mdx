## What days count as the weekend in Dubai now?

The UAE federal weekend is Saturday-Sunday for government entities since January 2022, and most private employers aligned within 12 months. Friday remains the Islamic holy day with midday prayers, so some offices run shorter hours while malls, restaurants, and hotels operate seven days a week across the area for Invest Gulf readers.

| Calendar item | Typical timing |
| --- | --- |
| Federal weekend | Saturday-Sunday since January 2022 |
| Friday prayers | Midday window roughly 11:30-13:30 |
| UAE National Day | 2-3 December |
| Retail / hotels | Open 7 days |

Public holidays follow the Islamic calendar (Eid Al Fitr, Eid Al Adha, Islamic New Year, Prophet's Birthday) plus UAE National Day and Commemoration Day. Dates shift each year; check official UAE channels before you book travel.

## What happens on Friday morning versus Friday afternoon?

Friday morning until roughly 13:00 means quieter roads, many residents at prayer, and a good window for beach walks, Kite Beach swims, or Ripe Market visits before brunch crowds. Afternoon onward, brunches from about AED 200, beach clubs, and mall energy ramp up across the area for Invest Gulf newcomers.

- Morning: beaches, markets, lighter traffic until about 13:00
- Afternoon: brunch seatings typically 12:30-16:00, soft packages from AED 200
- Evening: dining and social plans after 19:00 on Friday

## What is Friday brunch and how much does it cost?

Friday brunch is a long social meal, typically 12:30-16:00, with buffet or station service and optional drinks packages from about AED 200 soft to AED 700-1,500 luxury. Booking 1-3 weeks ahead is normal at popular venues; same-day walk-ins are rare on peak Fridays.

| Package type | Cost per person AED | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft (non-alcoholic) | 200-350 | Food, soft drinks, juices |
| House package | 350-550 | Food, house wine, beer, spirits |
| Premium package | 500-750 | Food, premium labels |
| Luxury brunch | 700-1,500 | Fine dining, Champagne, premium spirits |

Popular venues include Atlantis The Palm, JW Marriott Marquis (Graze), Palazzo Versace, The Ivy DIFC, Jumeirah Beach Hotel family options, and Address Boulevard. Family brunches often discount children (free under 5, AED 75-200 for ages 5-12). Search "family brunch" when booking.

Dubai Friday brunch culture means a 3.5 to 4 hour social meal that anchors expat calendars from soft packages near AED 200-350 per person through house packages at AED 350-550 and premium or luxury stacks at AED 500-1,500. Established venues on the Palm, DIFC, and Downtown fill 1-3 weeks ahead on peak Fridays, so walk-in strategies fail for groups larger than two. Family menus often waive children under 5 and charge AED 75-200 for ages 5-12, which changes the maths for a household of four versus two professionals. Our clients relocating with school-age children usually book one soft or house brunch in month one, then decide whether a second monthly brunch fits a leisure budget of AED 3,000-5,000. Treat brunch as a recurring cost line, not a one-off tourist treat, when you model annual lifestyle spend next to rent and school fees.

## How does Saturday differ from Friday?

Saturday is the shared Gulf weekend day with Sunday: theme parks, malls, beach clubs, and sports leagues peak for 8-12 crowded hours. If you skip Friday brunch, Saturday is when Global Village (seasonal, about AED 18 entry), Desert Safari (AED 250-450 per person), and Al Qudra cycling fill up across the area for Invest Gulf readers.

- Global Village entry: about AED 18 (seasonal)
- Desert safari: AED 250-450 per person
- Paid parking: often free Friday mornings until about noon

## What do beach clubs cost on weekends?

Beach clubs charge day passes or minimum spend, often AED 150-400+ per person on Friday-Saturday, and popular clubs require online booking 3-7 days ahead. Walk-ins are often turned away on peak days; swimwear stays at the pool or beach, with cover-ups required in restaurant zones that our clients favour.

- Day pass / minimum spend: AED 150-400+ per person
- Booking lead time: 3-7 days on peak weekends
- Dress: swimwear at water; cover-up in restaurants

Family weekend options in Dubai typically include IMG Worlds, Legoland, Motiongate, Dubai Frame, Creek walks, Al Fahidi, museums, Al Qudra cycling, and desert safaris. Global Village (October-April) entry is about AED 18; budget AED 1,500-3,000 for a moderate family weekend and AED 3,000-6,000+ for premium stacks.

UAE National Day weekend around 2-3 December means fireworks, light shows, Zabeel Park events, and road congestion that can add 30-90 minutes to cross-city trips. Ramadan typically means daytime eating stays private and evening iftar gatherings replace some brunch culture after sunset for about 29-30 days. Licensed venues adjust hours; malls stay busy after sunset; respect reduced daytime energy in offices and schools if weekend plans include family visits across the area for Invest Gulf relocatees.

- Daytime: private eating; public respect rules
- Evening: iftar social peak after sunset
- Duration: about 29-30 days each year
