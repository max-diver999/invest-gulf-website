## Which commute corridors make Sharjah rent work?

| Area | 2BR AED/mo | Peak commute |

## Negotiation and contract details that change net cost

Ask who pays chiller, what the deposit return process is, and whether cheques are multiple post-dated instruments. These lines move annual cost as much as a small rent cut.

Ejari registration timing affects utility setup and school address letters. Put the registration obligation in writing.

If you need early exit flexibility, negotiate a break clause before you celebrate a low headline rent. Cheap rigid leases are expensive when jobs move.
