## What comparison framework should expats use first?

Comparison framework for Sharjah versus Dubai rent starts with commute corridor and school location, not a single discount percentage on asking rent. Our research treats a 40% to 50% mid-market headline gap as a hypothesis until Salik and bus fees are added. Foreign buyers should compare three live listings per emirate before any lease.

Sharjah often wins the rent line. Dubai often wins walk-to-school options, denser weekend retail, and shorter office access when the job is local.

## How does the headline rent table compare in 2026?

Headline rent in 2026 typically shows Sharjah 2BR apartments near AED 4,500 to AED 7,500 against Dubai fringe stock near AED 8,500 to AED 14,000. Invest Gulf uses that 40% to 50% band as spreadsheet input, not an agent quote. Foreign buyers should expect narrower gaps on new-build Dubai stock.

| Unit type | Sharjah (mid) | Dubai fringe (mid) | Dubai premium (mid) | Sharjah discount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Studio | AED 2,400 to 4,200 | AED 4,500 to 7,000 | AED 6,500 to 10,000 | ~35 to 45% |
| 1BR | AED 3,200 to 5,500 | AED 6,000 to 9,500 | AED 9,000 to 14,000 | ~40 to 45% |
| 2BR | AED 4,500 to 7,500 | AED 8,500 to 14,000 | AED 12,000 to 22,000 | ~40 to 50% |
| 3BR apt | AED 6,500 to 10,500 | AED 11,000 to 18,000 | AED 16,000 to 28,000 | ~35 to 45% |
| 3BR villa | AED 8,000 to 14,000 (limited) | AED 15,000 to 25,000 | AED 25,000 to 45,000+ | ~35 to 50% |

Fringe Dubai = JVC, Sports City, Discovery Gardens, International City, Dubai Silicon Oasis. Premium Dubai = Marina, Downtown, JBR, Palm fringe, newer Dubai Hills tier.

Move-in costs for Gulf renters typically include 5% agency, 5% deposit, and the first rent cheque at signing, on top of Ejari and utility deposits.
