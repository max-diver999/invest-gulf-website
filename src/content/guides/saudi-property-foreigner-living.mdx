## What does Law M/14 allow foreigners to buy?

Law M/14 (2021) allows non-Saudi real estate ownership in designated areas approved by authorities. It did not open the entire Kingdom to foreign freehold.

| Statement | Accurate? |
|---|---|
| “Foreigners can buy villas in Jeddah anywhere” | False, zone and project limited |
| “Riyadh apartments are all freehold for expats” | False, check designation |
| “Off-plan on Red Sea giga-project = automatic title” | Risky, escrow and licence  (confirm current official rules) |
| “Buying gives me permanent visa” | False by default; see Premium Residency separately |
| “Renting is wasting money, must buy” | False, rent is valid expat strategy |

Saudi Arabia is opening fast on zones, Premium Residency ~SAR 4M (confirm current official rules) and entertainment, but conservative public norms remain and foreign buyer resale liquidity is immature vs the UAE. Treat ownership as a 10-year+ horizon, not a flip.

Registration and transfer costs in Saudi follow MOJ and REGA processes for the Kingdom, not Dubai Land Department fee tables. Agents who paste UAE DLD language onto Saudi SPAs are a documentation red flag; insist on Saudi-specific fee schedules in writing.

## Three planning scenarios for foreign buyers

**Scenario A (zone purchase):** Confirm REGA eligibility for the exact unit, escrow for any off-plan tranche, and MOJ registration steps before paying more than a refundable holding deposit.

**Scenario B (family relocation):** Model all-in monthly cost for housing, schooling, insurance, and transport in your target city, not headline rent alone.

**Scenario C (cross-border investor):** Separate lifestyle goals from ROI. Keep 6-12 months liquidity in SAR while you validate occupancy, rental rules, and resale channels on the ground.
