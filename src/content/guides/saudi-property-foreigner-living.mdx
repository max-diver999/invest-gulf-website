## What does Law M/14 allow foreigners to buy?

| “Buying gives me permanent visa” | False by default; see Premium Residency separately |
| “Renting is wasting money, must buy” | False, rent is valid expat strategy |

Registration and transfer costs follow MOJ and REGA processes for the Kingdom, not Dubai Land Department fee tables.
