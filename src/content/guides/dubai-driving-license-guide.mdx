## FAQ

How do I get a driving licence in Dubai as an expat?
Convert your foreign licence at an RTA Customer Happiness Centre if your country is on the approved list (AED 220–550 plus eye test). Otherwise enrol at an RTA-approved driving school for AED 3,500–8,000.

**Is the UK driving licence valid in Dubai?**
Yes, UK licence holders can directly convert at RTA without a practical test, subject to current list status. Always verify on rta.ae before booking.

**How much is car insurance in Dubai?**
Comprehensive insurance for a standard sedan with clean history runs AED 2,500–4,200 per year. New arrivals with no UAE driving history pay more. Compare at least three insurers.

Can I drive in Abu Dhabi with a Dubai licence?
Yes, UAE licences are federal documents valid across all emirates. Update your traffic file address when you change emirate of residence.

What is the Salik fine for negative balance?
AED 50 administrative charge per incident of driving through a gate with insufficient balance, plus the AED 4 toll. Monitor balance via the Salik app.

**How do I pay Dubai traffic fines?**
Dubai Police app, police.gov.ae, or RTA Customer Happiness Centres. Early payment discounts sometimes apply. Never ignore a fine, it blocks vehicle registration renewal.

**Do I need a car in Dubai?**
Depends on location. Central areas (Downtown, Marina, JLT, DIFC) are metro-accessible. Suburban family communities require a car, often two per household.

**How long is a Dubai driving licence valid?**
Linked to your UAE residency visa validity. Upon visa renewal, renew the licence through RTA.

**Can a woman drive in Dubai?**
Yes, no restrictions. Same licence process and rules apply regardless of gender.

**What is the eye test cost in Dubai?**
AED 100–200 at RTA-approved optical centres. Certificate valid for 30 days from issue date.
