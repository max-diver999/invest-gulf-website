**TL;DR:** Saudi moves work when iqama sponsorship, school seats, and compound or apartment choice line up before you fly. We help families and professionals avoid three expensive mistakes: signing a lease before dependent visa salary checks, picking a corniche tower before school bus routes exist, and confusing Law M/14 zone property with automatic residency.

## Why Saudi Arabia — and why plan before you land

KB §19: Saudi in 2026 is a Vision 2030 career bet — large domestic market, rapid entertainment opening, 0% personal income tax on typical employment — with conservative public norms and infrastructure still maturing outside Riyadh/Jeddah cores.

The move works when:
- Your employer sponsor is licensed with a real iqama track record
- You apply to schools 6–12 months early (Riyadh/Jeddah premium tiers)
- You rent compound or apartment 6–12 months before any zone property purchase
- You accept compound vs public social rhythm — not Dubai nightlife parity

## The three mistakes we fix most often

### 1. Package looks generous — basic salary blocks family visa

Housing SAR 8,000 plus basic SAR 6,000 reads as SAR 14,000 on paper. Ministry thresholds may still reject dependants. The basic salary line is what counts — not the headline package.

→ [Saudi family visa](/guides/saudi-family-visa/)

### 2. Lease signed — school waitlists your district

Premium school seats are non-refundable. Apply before you pick a neighbourhood.

→ [Riyadh international schools](/guides/riyadh-international-schools/) · [Jeddah international schools](/guides/jeddah-international-schools/)

### 3. Zone property marketed — title not eligible for foreigners

Law M/14 covers designated zones only — implementing rules still evolving **[VERIFY REGA]**. Premium Residency is a separate application from any property deposit.

→ [Saudi property foreigner living](/guides/saudi-property-foreigner-living/) · [Saudi Premium Residency living](/guides/saudi-premium-residency-living/)
