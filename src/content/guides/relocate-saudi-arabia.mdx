**Citable process note:** Saudi relocation for a sponsored employee is an employer-led immigration process, not a self-service visitor conversion. The employer and its PRO normally coordinate the work-visa file, while the employee supplies a passport, medical documentation, attested qualifications where the role requires them, and other documents requested by the relevant Saudi authorities. The passport should generally have at least 6 months of validity because visa and entry processing can fail on a short passport term. After entry, the sponsor completes the residence-permit steps and issues the iqama through the official channels available to it. Family members should be planned as a separate evidence set, typically including attested marriage or birth certificates. Premium Residency is not a substitute document for a routine employer iqama, and any property purchase must be assessed under the rules that apply to the property location.

## How does iqama eligibility depend on employer sponsorship?

Iqama eligibility typically requires a licensed Saudi sponsor with quota, attested certificates, medical fitness, police clearance, and a passport with at least 6 months validity. Work-visa filing often takes 4 to 6 weeks, with dependants 4 to 8 weeks after the primary card. Invest Gulf timelines treat attestation chains as the main variance driver.

Employee requirements:
- Job offer from licensed Saudi employer
- Educational certificates attested by home country and Saudi embassy
- Medical fitness certificate from approved clinic
- Police clearance certificate from home country (recent)
- Passport valid for minimum 6 months

Processing timeline:
- Initial work visa: 4-6 weeks after document submission
- Entry to Saudi Arabia: Must enter within 3 months of visa issuance
- Iqama registration: Within 90 days of arrival in Saudi Arabia
- Dependent visas: 4-8 weeks after primary iqama is issued

Iqama validity and renewal:
- Initial iqama: Usually 1-2 years depending on contract
- Renewal: Annually, tied to employment contract
- Exit/re-entry permits: Required for travel outside Saudi Arabia
- Final exit visa: Required if leaving Saudi permanently

### Premium Residency program

Premium Residency is a separate residence status from the employer-sponsored iqama, so applicants should not use it as a shortcut assumption when negotiating an employment move. It may suit an investor or self-funded household that can document the category-specific capital, property, or business evidence requested by the official programme. Confirm the currently available category and fee directly with the Premium Residency Center before placing a non-refundable property deposit or restructuring employment.

Investment thresholds (confirm current official rules):
- Real estate investment: SAR 4 million+ cited in program materials
- Bank deposit: SAR 7 million+ for certain periods
- Investment in Saudi company: Various thresholds depending on sector

Program benefits:
- 5-year renewable residence independent of employment
- Multiple entry/exit without sponsor approval
- Family sponsorship rights (spouse and unmarried children under 25)
- Property ownership rights in designated areas
- Business establishment rights in certain sectors

Application process:
- Investment must be completed before application
- Legal and financial due diligence required
- Processing time: 2-4 months after investment verification
- Annual renewal fees apply

**Citable residency note:** Saudi Premium Residency is an official residence programme with its own application, eligibility review, and documented evidence requirements. It is separate from the iqama issued to an employee through an employer sponsor. This guide cites SAR 4 million for a real-estate investment route and SAR 7 million for a deposit-related route only as figures previously published in programme materials, not as a promise that a given applicant or property qualifies. Categories, fees, designated property areas, and implementing requirements can change. A defensible application starts by matching the applicant's actual route to the current Premium Residency Center criteria, then obtaining legal review of the target property or business. The practical distinction matters for relocation planning: employer sponsorship controls the routine work move, while Premium Residency requires its own approval and does not turn an ordinary lease or a property reservation into residence status.
