Sponsor requirements:
- Saudi employer must be registered with Ministry of Labour and Social Development
- Company must have available visa quotas for foreign workers

Employee requirements:

Processing timeline:

Iqama validity and renewal:
- Initial iqama: Usually 1-2 years depending on contract

- Exit/re-entry permits: Required for travel outside Saudi Arabia
