# Dubai Police Clearance Certificate: Complete Application Guide 2026

**TL;DR:** A Dubai Police Clearance Certificate, officially called a **Good Conduct Certificate**, confirms you have no criminal record in Dubai during your period of residency. It is required for immigration applications in most countries, some employment background checks, professional licensing, and Golden Visa processing. The **online application** via dubaipolice.gov.ae takes 10 minutes and issues within **3–5 business days** for AED 200. This guide covers every step from application to attestation.

> **Note:** Document requirements and processing times may change. Verify current requirements on the Dubai Police official website before applying.

## What Is a Dubai Police Clearance Certificate?

The Dubai Police Clearance Certificate is an official document issued by **Dubai Police** confirming that the named individual has no criminal record in Dubai during their period of residency. It is also referred to as:

- Good Conduct Certificate (الشهادة الحسن السيرة والسلوك)
- Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)
- Certificate of No Criminal Record

The document is issued on official Dubai Police letterhead with an official seal and signature. It can be apostilled and attested for international use.

---

## When You Need a Dubai Police Clearance

### Immigration and Visa Applications

The most common reason Dubai expats need a Police Clearance Certificate:

- **UK skilled worker visa / ILR applications**, UK Visas and Immigration requires certificates for countries you have lived in for 12+ months
- **Canadian Permanent Residency / Citizenship**, IRCC requires PCCs from all countries of residence over 6 months in the past 10 years
- **Australian PR and citizenship**, all countries where you lived for 12+ months required
- **Schengen long-stay visas**, some EU countries require PCC from all countries of residence
- **US Green Card**, USCIS may require foreign PCCs from embassies
- **New Zealand residence**, all countries of past residence required

### UAE Visa Applications and Golden Visa

Some UAE residency transitions require a Good Conduct Certificate:
- Long-term UAE Golden Visa applications (certain categories)
- Sponsor-to-self-sponsor residency changes
- Professional licensing applications (healthcare, legal, financial services)

### Employment Background Checks

Many multinational employers, regulated industries, and professional bodies require police clearances:
- Financial services (DFSA, SCA-regulated entities)
- Healthcare professionals (DHA, HAAD licensing)
- Legal professionals (DIFC Courts, UAE legal bar)
- Education sector (teaching positions)
- Government and semi-government employment

### Property Transactions and Licensing

Some property management companies, real estate brokerages (RERA), and business licensing applications require a Good Conduct Certificate as part of character verification.

## Who Can Apply

**Current UAE residents:**
- Anyone with a valid UAE residence visa and Emirates ID can apply
- Sponsored employees, self-employed, investors, Golden Visa holders
- Application can be made online or in person

**Former UAE residents:**
- Individuals who have previously lived in UAE can apply based on their former residency period
- Required documents include passport details from the residency period
- Online application available; some former residents prefer to use UAE-based PRO services

**UAE nationals:**
- Full application available through Dubai Police systems
- Different document requirements (National ID instead of Emirates ID)

**Note:** The certificate covers your record in **Dubai emirate only**. If you also lived in Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, or other emirates, you may need separate clearance certificates from those emirate police forces (Abu Dhabi Police, Sharjah Police, etc.) depending on the requesting country's requirements.

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Method 1: Online Application (Recommended)

**Step 1: Access Dubai Police e-Services**

Visit [dubaipolice.gov.ae](https://www.dubaipolice.gov.ae/) and navigate to:
- Services → Individuals → Good Conduct Certificate

Or use the Dubai Police app (iOS/Android) → Services → Good Conduct Certificate.

**Step 2: Log In or Register**

You will need a UAE PASS account (UAE digital identity platform) if you are a current resident with Emirates ID. Former residents may use passport details to access the system, the portal guides you through the relevant verification.

**Step 3: Complete the Application Form**

Provide:
- Full name (exactly as on passport)
- Passport number
- Emirates ID number (current residents)
- Date of birth
- Nationality
- UAE residency period (if former resident, provide approximate dates)
- Reason for application (select from dropdown: immigration, employment, personal, etc.)
- Country the certificate is intended for

**Step 4: Upload Documents**

Required documents:
- Digital passport-size photograph (white background, recent, meeting Dubai Police photo specifications, typically JPG, under 2MB)
- Copy of passport (bio data page)
- Copy of Emirates ID (current residents) or previous UAE residency details (former residents)

**Step 5: Pay the Fee**

AED 200 payable by credit/debit card or UAE digital payment. Fees are non-refundable regardless of outcome.

**Step 6: Receive Certificate**

Processing time: 3–5 business days for standard applications. The certificate is issued as a PDF document that can be downloaded from your Dubai Police account. For official use, you will need to either print the official version or request a physical copy.

---

### Method 2: In-Person Application (Walk-In)

**Dubai Police General HQ (Al Twar)**
- Location: Al Twar, Deira, Dubai (near Al Twar Centre)
- Hours: Sunday–Thursday, 07:30–14:00
- Bring: Original passport, Emirates ID, passport photographs, and payment

**Dubai Police Customer Happiness Centres**
Dubai Police operates customer service centres across the emirate. Check dubaipolice.gov.ae for current locations, these change periodically.

**Typing Centres (Tasheel and Amer)**
Authorised government typing centres across Dubai can assist with filling forms and submitting applications on your behalf. Fee: AED 30–60 typing fee plus the AED 200 government fee. Useful if you are unfamiliar with the online process or need help with translation.

**Processing time for walk-in:** 1–3 business days, occasionally same-day for urgent requests with justification.

---

### Method 3: Through a PRO Service

If you are a former resident applying from outside the UAE, or if you have a complex situation (name change, multiple passports, expired Emirates ID), a UAE-based PRO (Public Relations Officer) service can apply on your behalf.

Cost: AED 400–800 all-inclusive including government fees and service charges. Turnaround: 5–10 business days.

Recommended for: former residents who cannot access the online portal easily, individuals with Arabic name variations, and cases where the certificate needs simultaneous apostille/attestation.

## Certificate Format and Validity

### What the Certificate Contains

- Full name of the individual
- Passport/ID number
- Nationality
- Date of birth
- Period of residence in Dubai (dates)
- Statement that no criminal record found OR details of any records
- Issue date and Dubai Police official seal
- Certificate reference number

**Language:** Issued in Arabic. An official English translation is included on standard international-use certificates. If the requesting country requires a certified translation into another language, this is arranged separately.

### Validity Period

Most countries accept Dubai Police Clearance Certificates issued within the **6 months** prior to the application submission. Check the specific requirement of the requesting country or authority, some accept 12-month-old certificates, others require certificates issued within 3 months.

If your certificate expires before your immigration application is submitted, you will need to apply for a new one.

## Apostille and Attestation

For use in foreign countries, the Dubai Police certificate typically requires authentication:

### Level 1: UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) Attestation

Required for countries that accept MOFA attestation (most Arab countries, India, Pakistan, and others):
- Submit the original certificate to MOFA attestation desk (Dubai or Abu Dhabi)
- Or use Tasheel/Amer centres for MOFA attestation services
- Fee: approximately AED 150–200 per document
- Processing time: 1–5 business days

**Online MOFA attestation:** MOFA offers online attestation services for eligible documents via mofa.gov.ae.

### Level 2: Apostille (Hague Convention Countries)

The UAE joined the Hague Apostille Convention in 2021. For countries that accept apostilles (UK, EU members, Australia, Canada, USA, etc.), an apostille certificate from the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs replaces the full embassy legalisation chain.

**Process:**
1. Obtain Dubai Police certificate
2. MOFA attestation first (required before apostille in UAE)
3. Apply for apostille via MOFA (same office, additional step)

**Fee for apostille:** AED 150–300 per document
**Timeframe:** 3–7 business days

**Key benefit of apostille:** Once apostilled, the document is accepted in all 124+ Hague Convention member countries without further embassy verification.

### Level 3: Embassy Legalisation (Non-Hague Countries)

For countries not party to the Hague Convention (some Asian and African nations, Saudi Arabia):
1. MOFA attestation
2. Certificate attested at the relevant country's embassy in Abu Dhabi or Dubai
3. Possibly further attestation in the home country

Embassy attestation fees and timelines vary by embassy. Some embassies accept by post; others require in-person submission.

## Common Issues and How to Handle Them

### Name Discrepancy

Your name on the Police Clearance must match your passport exactly. If your name appears differently across documents (Arabic transliteration variations, middle name omitted), clarify with Dubai Police before applying. Bring all relevant documents and a cover letter explaining the discrepancy.

### Previous Criminal Record

If you have a UAE criminal record (including previous traffic-related criminal matters, bounced cheque cases under old UAE law, labour disputes escalated to criminal court), the certificate will reflect this. Contact a UAE-licensed lawyer before applying if you have any prior UAE legal issues, you need to understand whether records are still active and whether clearance is possible.

### Lost or Expired Emirates ID

Former residents who cannot provide their former Emirates ID number can apply with passport details. Provide your full name, date of birth, nationality, and approximate residency dates. Processing may take longer while records are searched manually.

### Multiple Residency Periods

If you lived in Dubai, left, and returned (common for expats), both periods are typically captured in a single certificate. Specify all periods of UAE residency when completing the application.

## Dubai Police Clearance vs Abu Dhabi Police Clearance

Many countries require separate clearances from each emirate where you lived. The Dubai Police certificate covers **Dubai emirate only**.

**If you also lived in Abu Dhabi:**
- Apply to Abu Dhabi Police via adpolice.gov.ae
- Process is similar, online application, AED 200 fee, 3–5 business day processing
- Abu Dhabi also joined the Hague Apostille framework

**If you lived in Sharjah:**
- Sharjah Police certificate obtained via sharjahpolice.gov.ae

**Checking what is required:** Your immigration adviser or the requesting immigration authority will specify which emirate certificates they need based on your residence history.
