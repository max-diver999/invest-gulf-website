Step 1: Access Dubai Police e-Services

Visit [dubaipolice.gov.ae](https://www.dubaipolice.gov.ae/) and navigate to:
- Services → Individuals → Good Conduct Certificate

Or use the Dubai Police app (iOS/Android) → Services → Good Conduct Certificate.

Step 2: Log In or Register

You will need a UAE PASS account (UAE digital identity platform) if you are a current resident with Emirates ID. Former residents may use passport details to access the system, the portal guides you through the relevant verification.

Step 3: Complete the Application Form

Provide:
- Full name (exactly as on passport)
- Passport number
- Emirates ID number (current residents)
- Date of birth
- Nationality
- UAE residency period (if former resident, provide approximate dates)
- Reason for application (select from dropdown: immigration, employment, personal, etc.)
- Country the certificate is intended for

Step 4: Upload Documents

Required documents:
- Digital passport-size photograph (white background, recent, meeting Dubai Police photo specifications, typically JPG, under 2MB)
- Copy of passport (bio data page)
- Copy of Emirates ID (current residents) or previous UAE residency details (former residents)

Step 5: Pay the Fee

AED 200 payable by credit/debit card or UAE digital payment. Fees are non-refundable regardless of outcome.

Step 6: Receive Certificate

Processing time: 3-5 business days for standard applications. The certificate is issued as a PDF document that can be downloaded from your Dubai Police account. For official use, you will need to either print the official version or request a physical copy.

### Method 2: In-Person Application (Walk-In)

Dubai Police General HQ (Al Twar)
- Location: Al Twar, Deira, Dubai (near Al Twar Centre)
- Hours: Sunday-Thursday, 07:30-14:00
- Bring: Original passport, Emirates ID, passport photographs, and payment

Dubai Police Customer Happiness Centres
Dubai Police operates customer service centres across the emirate. Check dubaipolice.gov.ae for current locations, these change periodically.

Typing Centres (Tasheel and Amer)
Authorised government typing centres across Dubai can assist with filling forms and submitting applications on your behalf. Fee: AED 30-60 typing fee plus the AED 200 government fee. Useful if you are unfamiliar with the online process or need help with translation.

Processing time for walk-in: 1-3 business days, occasionally same-day for urgent requests with justification.

### Method 3: Through a PRO Service

If you are a former resident applying from outside the UAE, or if you have a complex situation (name change, multiple passports, expired Emirates ID), a UAE-based PRO (Public Relations Officer) service can apply on your behalf.

Cost: AED 400-800 all-inclusive including government fees and service charges. Turnaround: 5-10 business days.

Recommended for: former residents who cannot access the online portal easily, individuals with Arabic name variations, and cases where the certificate needs simultaneous apostille/attestation.

### Level 1: UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) Attestation

Required for countries that accept MOFA attestation (most Arab countries, India, Pakistan, and others):
- Submit the original certificate to MOFA attestation desk (Dubai or Abu Dhabi)
- Or use Tasheel/Amer centres for MOFA attestation services
- Fee: approximately AED 150-200 per document
- Processing time: 1-5 business days

Online MOFA attestation: MOFA offers online attestation services for eligible documents via mofa.gov.ae.

### Level 2: Apostille (Hague Convention Countries)

The UAE joined the Hague Apostille Convention in 2021. For countries that accept apostilles (UK, EU members, Australia, Canada, USA, etc.), an apostille certificate from the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs replaces the full embassy legalisation chain.

Process:
1. Obtain Dubai Police certificate
2. MOFA attestation first (required before apostille in UAE)
3. Apply for apostille via MOFA (same office, additional step)

Fee for apostille: AED 150-300 per document
Timeframe: 3-7 business days

Key benefit of apostille: Once apostilled, the document is accepted in all 124+ Hague Convention member countries without further embassy verification.

### Level 3: Embassy Legalisation (Non-Hague Countries)

For countries not party to the Hague Convention (some Asian and African nations, Saudi Arabia):
1. MOFA attestation
2. Certificate attested at the relevant country's embassy in Abu Dhabi or Dubai
3. Possibly further attestation in the home country

Embassy attestation fees and timelines vary by embassy. Some embassies accept by post; others require in-person submission.
