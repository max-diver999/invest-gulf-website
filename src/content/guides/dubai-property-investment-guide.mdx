Dubai remains the default entry point for Gulf property investors: deep liquidity, mature regulation, and a large stock of apartments built for international buyers. That does not mean every tower or payment plan is a good deal — it means you can compare options transparently if you know where to look.

This guide frames **how to evaluate Dubai property in 2026** — yields, fees, product types, and red flags — without developer marketing language.

## Who Dubai suits

| Buyer profile | Why Dubai | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| Yield investor | Large rental pool, short-let rules in many buildings | Service charges eat net yield |
| Golden Visa buyer | Clear property-linked residency thresholds | Must maintain qualifying investment |
| Remote worker / end-user | Tax-free income, lifestyle infrastructure | Premium locations = lower yield |
| First-time Gulf buyer | English contracts, escrow on off-plan | Do not skip SPA legal review |

## Yields: gross vs net

Brochures quote **gross** yields (annual rent ÷ price). Your bank account cares about **net**:

- Service charges (AED 12–25+ per sq ft/year in many towers)
- Property management (often 5–8% of rent)
- Vacancy and turnover on short-let units
- DLD and renewal fees amortised over hold period

Mid-market communities (JVC, Sports City, parts of Business Bay) often show stronger gross yields than Palm or Downtown — with different tenant profiles and liquidity.

## Fee stack (ready resale)

| Item | Typical range |
|---|---|
| DLD transfer fee | 4% of purchase price |
| Broker commission | 2% (often paid by buyer on resale) |
| Trustee / registration | AED 4,000+ |
| Mortgage costs | Bank arrangement + valuation if financed |

Off-plan: developer payment plan + Oqood registration — review late-payment clauses and handover snagging rights in the SPA.

## Freehold vs leasehold

Most investor focus is **freehold** in designated zones (Marina, JVC, Downtown, Creek Harbour, etc.). Verify the title type on the **Unit Profile** from DLD or your broker — not only the marketing map.

## Off-plan due diligence checklist

1. Developer track record and escrow account (RERA registration)
2. Payment schedule vs your cash flow — penalties for delay
3. Service charge estimate post-handover
4. Comparable ready-unit prices (would you overpay for future delivery?)
5. Exit liquidity: who buys this unit type on resale?

## 2026 market context

Transaction volumes have normalised after the 2022–2024 surge. Buyers who underwrote 15%+ annual appreciation have less margin for error. Underwrite **conservative rent and flat capital growth** unless you have a specific catalyst (infrastructure, undersupply in a micro-location).
