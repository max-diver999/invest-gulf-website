## How do spouse work rights interact with dependent iqama?

Answer first: dependent iqama **does not automatically grant local employment**. Separate work visas, Premium Residency categories, or evolving MOI policies may apply (confirm current official rules). Remote work for a foreign employer remains a **grey zone**; get written legal clarity before spouses count on overseas payroll while resident in KSA.

## How should you sequence school seats with dependent iqama?

Apply to schools **in parallel** with MOI filing if conditional enrolment is offered. British and IB seats in Riyadh and Jeddah fill early; iqama delays should not be a surprise if you read [Riyadh international schools](/guides/riyadh-international-schools/) timelines. Budget **SAR 10,000+ per month** all-in for two children in mid-tier British tracks as a planning band, excluding housing.

## What happens when family arrives on visit visa first?

Visit entry can keep families together early but **overstay risk** rises if PRO slips. Monitor visit expiry daily and keep exit tickets flexible. Pattern choice should be explicit in your offer letter: visit-first versus home-country wait versus iqama-first travel.

## How do newborn and parent sponsorship edge cases work?

Newborns need **passport and birth certificate** workflows parallel to hospital discharge; plan embassy appointments early. Parent sponsorship sits in a **separate MOI category** with higher salary thresholds (confirm current official rules). Divorce and custody orders require **extra attestation**; stop and use an immigration lawyer rather than copying UAE or UK habits.

## How does banking for spouse and teens work after iqama?

Joint accounts depend on bank policy and sponsor letter; start with [Saudi banking expats](/guides/saudi-banking-expats/) checklist once dependent cards show in Absher. Teens may need their own iqama-linked accounts for school trips; plan branch visits in the same week as card collection to avoid cash-only gaps.

Final reminder: every salary, levy, and spouse-work rule here needs **current MOI/MHRSD confirmation** at filing. Policies shift; your PRO letter beats this article when they disagree.

Salary thresholds, document attestation, and dependent iqama medicals decide whether your family arrives with you or months later. Build a calendar that starts from contract signature, not from flight bookings.
