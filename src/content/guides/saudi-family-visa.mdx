## Who can sponsor family: eligibility sketch

A standard employed expat sponsor usually needs:
- Valid iqama (not a visit visa)
- Clean sponsor record, no unpaid fines
- Salary at or above MOI/MHRSD minimum  (confirm current official rules)
- Accommodation proof, Ejar lease or employer housing letter
- Attested relationship documents

Premium Residency holders follow different dependent rules,  (confirm current official rules).

Family on a visit visa can enter the country. That is not the same as dependent iqama for a full school year.

## Document attestation chain

Same discipline as [relocation hub](/guides/saudi-arabia-relocation-guide/) attestation section.

### Marriage certificate

1. Home country notary/registry copy
2. Foreign ministry attestation
3. Saudi embassy in issuing country
4. Saudi MOFA translation if non-Arabic  (confirm current official rules)

### Children's birth certificates

- Each child separate attestation chain
- Name spelling matches passport, iqama errors painful to fix

### Timeline

8–12 weeks from home country if starting cold, start before job acceptance if family follows in 90 days.

Tip: scan colour PDFs to PRO WhatsApp while originals courier.

## Step-by-step dependent iqama process

### Step 1: Primary iqama active

Employee medical, biometrics, card collected, [iqama process](/guides/saudi-iqama-process/).

### Step 2: PRO eligibility check

Employer submits salary certificate + Qiwa data, system may auto-reject below threshold.

### Step 3: MOI application

Application for visit visa conversion or direct dependent iqama depending on whether family enters on visit or stays home until issued  (confirm current official rules).

| Strategy | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Family enters on visit | Together early | School iqama wait |
| Family waits abroad | Cleaner status | Separation weeks |
| Single entry timing | PRO coordinates | Flight change fees |

### Step 4: Medical (dependents)

Spouse and children above age threshold complete approved medical, similar communicable disease screen.

### Step 5: Biometrics

MOI appointment, children included per age rules.

### Step 6: Dependent iqama cards

Collect plastic cards, verify sponsor ID matches employee iqama.

### Step 7: Absher linkage

Dependents added to employee Absher for exit/re-entry requests.

Timeline: 4–10 weeks after eligibility confirmed.

## Entry strategy: visit first vs iqama abroad

Families use three patterns, each has COL and stress trade-offs:

### Pattern 1: Employee alone first 90 days

Lowest risk for salary threshold proof and lease registration before dependents arrive.

| Phase | Who travels |
|---|---|
| Month 1–2 | Employee only |
| Month 3 | Family after Ejar + eligibility letter |

Childcare cost during separation, budget emotional + travel tickets.

### Pattern 2: Family on visit visa immediately

Family together early, school may wait for dependent iqama.

Risk: visit overstays if PRO slow, monitor expiry daily.

### Pattern 3: Family stays home until dependent iqama stamped

Longest separation, clearest immigration status.

Best for summer school sync, arrive July/August with cards in hand  (confirm current official rules).

## Who this suits — decision framework

Sponsorship complexity depends on the applicant's Iqama category. **Standard employment Iqama** with salary above SAR 4,000: straightforward dependant visa for spouse and children under 18, processing 2–4 weeks via Muqeem. **Premium residency holder:** sponsor unlimited dependants with no salary-floor requirement and faster processing (1–2 weeks). **Self-employed or freelance Iqama:** family sponsorship requires additional proof of income (bank statements for 6 months showing SAR 8,000+ average).

Total per-dependant cost including medical, visa stamping, and Iqama: SAR 3,500–6,000. Children under 18: SAR 2,000–4,000. Processing via Muqeem typically takes 15–30 working days from submission to stamp.

Saudi family visa reference numbers: the Muqeem platform processes 90% of applications within 15–30 days. Medical screening results are valid for 3 months from the test date. The 2024 fee schedule sets dependent visa stamping at $265 per person. Iqama issuance for each family member takes 5–10 days after visa entry. School registration requires attested certificates — processing takes 2–4 weeks through the Saudi embassy. Housing allowance for family-visa holders averages 25% of base salary in most corporate packages. The minimum salary threshold of $1,070 per month applies from 2025 onwards.

## Single-parent and split-custody edge cases (confirm current official rules)

Families with **shared custody** or **single-parent sponsorship** face extra MOI scrutiny:

- **Court orders** may need attestation chain beyond standard marriage cert  
- **Ex-spouse consent** for child exit/re-entry sometimes requested  
- **Sponsor salary** still must meet threshold on one income

Stop and engage **immigration lawyer**, do not fly children on assumptions from UAE or UK practice.

## Pets and family relocation

Pet import rules run **parallel** to dependent iqama, rabies titre, MOA approval, summer airline embargoes.

**Compound pet policy**, breed bans and weight limits before signing **Ejar**.

Budget **SAR 3,000–8,000** vet/import first pet, not in family visa fee but same arrival window.

## Repatriation: family exit sequence

When contract ends:

1. School **transcripts** and **TC** 60 days early  
2. **Final exit** for dependents before employee or coordinated same day  
3. **Vehicle sale**: both spouses on registration if co-owned  
4. **Bank closure** after liabilities  
5. **Compound deposit** inspection with children moved out

Poor sequencing strands **spouse on invalid iqama**, PRO written timeline mandatory.

## Saudi family visa — sponsor paths

**Path A — iqama sponsor:** Verify profession code and salary against Jawazat dependant rules the week you apply. Family visit visas are not interchangeable with long-term dependant iqama without status change.

**Path B — housing and schooling:** Compound leases in Riyadh or Jeddah must match declared family size on visa forms. International school seats should be held in writing before shipping household goods.

**Path C — renewal before expiry:** File dependant renewal at least 45 days early; fines accumulate quickly on overstayed iqama. Exit/re-entry visa timing affects summer home leave—plan GOSI and medical insurance continuity.
