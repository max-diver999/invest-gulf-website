## What does the IB continuum (PYP, MYP, DP) mean?

The IB continuum typically means three programmes: PYP (ages 3-12), MYP (ages 11-16), and DP for 2 years (ages 16-19) ending in exams scored out of 45 with a 24-point pass mark. Leading Dubai schools often post 90-98% Diploma pass rates for Invest Gulf families.

- PYP: inquiry-based, portfolio assessment, Year 6 Exhibition
- MYP: seven subject groups, personal project in MYP Year 5
- DP: six subjects over 2 years, TOK, Extended Essay, CAS

A full-continuum school offers all three, which matters for mobile families who may leave mid-cycle with transferable IB credentials.

## Which Tier 1 IB schools lead on outcomes and fees?

Tier 1 IB schools in Dubai typically mean KHDA Outstanding or Very Good ratings, full PYP/MYP/DP continuity, DP averages above 34 points, and annual fees near AED 75,000-120,000+ before extras. GEMS World Academy, Swiss International, Dwight, and Hartland lead for Invest Gulf shortlists.

| School | Area | Annual fees (AED) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GEMS World Academy | Sheikh Zayed Road | 95,000-120,000+ | Large cohort, strong US/UK placements |
| Swiss International Scientific School | Healthcare City | 90,000-115,000 | Trilingual IB continuum |
| Dwight School Dubai | MBZ City | 85,000-110,000 | Global Dwight network |
| Hartland International | Meydan | 75,000-95,000 | Newer campus, growing IB results |

Swiss International Scientific School is the only UAE school with trilingual instruction (English, French, German) across all three IB programmes. Hartland suits families in MBR City, Dubai Creek Harbour, or Business Bay who want to avoid Al Barsha or Sheikh Zayed Road commutes.

IB school fees in Dubai for full-continuum World Schools typically span about AED 65,000-80,000 at mid-tier campuses and AED 95,000-120,000+ at premium names such as GEMS World Academy and Swiss International Scientific School, before year-one extras. Registration, uniforms, and activities often add AED 8,000-12,000 in year one, with bus transport another AED 6,000-9,000 if you need it, so true all-in cost can sit AED 15,000-25,000 above the published tuition sheet. Leading Dubai IB Diploma cohorts commonly report pass rates near 90-98% versus a global average around 80%, with school averages often 34-38 points out of 45 against a 24-point pass mark. Invest Gulf family budgeting treats school fees as a housing constraint: a AED 100,000 tuition line plus extras changes whether a Marina lease or a Dubai Hills purchase still fits after service charges and weekend leisure.

## Is IB or British A-Levels the better pathway for your child?

IB typically suits internationally mobile families and students who want six subjects plus TOK, CAS, and a 4,000-word Extended Essay, while British A-Levels suit clear UK specialisation from Year 12 over 2 years. Both are well regarded; choose by learning style and university geography for Invest Gulf parents.

- IB: breadth, multi-country university optionality
- A-Levels: deep subject focus, confirmed UK path

## Where do Dubai IB graduates go to university?

Dubai IB graduates typically place into UK Russell Group offers at about 32-38 points, with Oxford and Imperial medicine often needing 38-40 with high HL scores. US colleges grant credit for HL scores of 5+, and Tier 1-2 schools report over 80% of DP graduates in global top-200 universities for Invest Gulf outcome checks.

- UK: 32-38 common conditionals; 38-40 for top medicine
- US/Canada: HL 5+ credit pathways at many universities
- NL/AU/SG: direct IB recognition tables published by universities

## How do KHDA ratings affect IB school choice?

KHDA ratings typically mean Outstanding and Very Good schools can raise fees within caps and attract wait lists of 6-12 months at popular entry years, while Good schools need direct IB pass-rate checks near 90-98% at top campuses. Download the latest KHDA report for we shortlist.

| KHDA rating | What it signals for IB families |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding | Stable leadership, strong DP outcomes, longest waits |
| Very Good | Solid IB delivery, often better seat availability |
| Good | Acceptable; verify recent IB pass rates directly |
| Acceptable / Weak | Higher risk on staff turnover and facilities |

## How should parents shortlist IB schools in five questions?

A five-question IB shortlist typically means confirming full continuum versus DP-only, last year's average DP points and pass rate in writing, a commute under 35 minutes at school-run hours, and total fees near AED 65,000-120,000+ versus your housing model. Confirm mid-year entry for Invest Gulf parents.

1. Full continuum or DP in senior years only?
2. Last year's average DP points and pass rate (written)?
3. Commute under 35 minutes at school-run hours?
4. Total fee fit vs [school and housing budget](/guides/school-fees-vs-property-budget-dubai/)?
5. Mid-year entry confirmed for your year group?

Insider tip: Ask for the external DP entry policy in writing; many Year 12 seats are filled from internal MYP cohorts, so a January application for September can still fail at premium schools even when PYP wait lists looked open.
