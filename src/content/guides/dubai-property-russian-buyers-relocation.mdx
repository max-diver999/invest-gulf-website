### Golden Visa Application Process

**Application body:** GDRFA (General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs, Dubai) or ICP (Federal Identity Authority) for federal processing.

**Typical timeline from title deed to stamped visa:** 60-90 days

**Required documents (current standard, verify before applying):**
- Valid passport with sufficient remaining validity
- Passport-size photograph
- DLD title deed or GDRFA-approved property valuation certificate
- Proof of property value meeting AED 2M threshold (valuation report if below asking)
- Health insurance proof
- Medical fitness test result (taken at approved UAE clinic)

**Fees:** Government fees are approximately AED 3,000-5,000 for the Golden Visa processing, plus AED 1,000-1,500 for medical test and Emirates ID. Agent-assisted service costs AED 3,000-8,000 additionally. See the [Golden Visa application step-by-step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/) for the complete fee schedule.

## What should buyers verify on common mistakes russian buyers make during relocat?

**Starting the Golden Visa too late.** The 60-90 day processing window is often underestimated. Buyers who want a September school start and begin the visa process in July will miss the intake. Start as soon as the title deed is in hand.

**Underestimating setup costs.** Transaction costs (DLD, agent) are well-understood. Setup costs, Golden Visa fees, Emirates ID, school enrolment deposits, DEWA deposit, initial rental overlap, and furnishing, easily total AED 50,000-120,000 additional to the purchase price. Budget this explicitly.

**Poor area choice relative to lifestyle needs.** Buying in a lower-priced outer zone for yield reasons, then renting in Marina, is a valid strategy but should be planned. Buyers who assume they will live in JVC or DAMAC Hills and then find daily life inconvenient create unnecessary friction.

**Not using Russian-speaking advisors.** The Dubai service ecosystem for Russian speakers is sophisticated. Using generic English-only advisors is not necessary and can create miscommunication in legal and banking processes.

**Ignoring maintenance obligations.** Service charges are annual and compulsory. High-end buildings in Marina and Downtown charge AED 15-25 per sq ft annually. A 1,000 sq ft apartment carries AED 15,000–25,000 in annual service charges. Budget these before year one ends.
