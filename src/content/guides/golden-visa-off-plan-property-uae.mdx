Roughly 60 to 65% of Dubai transactions are off-plan. A large share of Golden Visa applicants never touch a ready Title Deed at application time; they apply on Oqood while the tower is still under construction.

That works when the paperwork is right. It fails when a buyer trusts a launch brochure, pays into the wrong account, and discovers six months later that DLD has no record of their AED 2.2M investment.

## What is the AED 2 million threshold on off-plan?

Federal threshold is AED 2 million registered value on Oqood, not emirate or product type alone. See the full framework in [UAE Golden Visa Through Property](/guides/golden-visa-mortgage-property-uae/). Confirm current ICP and GDRFA categories at application date with licensed immigration counsel.

## What is the Oqood registration sequence?

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Buyer selects RERA-listed project |
| 2 | SPA signed; reservation deposit paid |
| 3 | 4% DLD fee triggered on Oqood registration (not at handover) |
| 4 | Payments flow to DLD-regulated escrow |
| 5 | Oqood certificate issued in buyer's name |
| 6 | Golden Visa application submitted with Oqood extract |

Until step 5 completes, you have a sales promise, not a qualifying immigration asset. For escrow and SPA detail, read [Off-Plan Property Dubai Guide](/guides/off-plan-property-dubai-guide/).

## Does full payment matter before Golden Visa?

Payment plan illusion: you can qualify at AED 2.1M Oqood while having paid only 30% in cash. Immigration looks at registered value, not cumulative bank outflow to date.

April 2026 mortgage note: reported removal of the old 50% equity minimum means financed off-plan may qualify on registered price with UAE bank NOC. Sources still conflict; confirm at application via [Golden Visa Mortgage Guide](/guides/golden-visa-mortgage-property-uae/).

## When should you apply after Oqood?

| Strategy | When it fits |
|---|---|
| Apply immediately after Oqood | Visa urgency, documents ready, tier-1 developer |
| Wait until first major instalment | Bank or developer admin delays Oqood |
| Wait until handover | Usually unnecessary for initial qualification if Oqood meets AED 2M |

Physical presence: you generally must stay in the UAE during processing (5 to 15 working days typical, up to 2 to 4 weeks in peaks). Do not book exit flights before visa stamp.

Cost band: ~AED 4,000 to 5,500 government and medical per principal applicant, plus consultant fees if used.

## What developer and project checks matter?

| Check | Tool / action |
|---|---|
| RERA registration | Trakheesi / Dubai REST |
| Escrow account active | Dubai REST |
| Project % complete | Developer disclosures + site visit |
| Delivery track record | Historical handover data |
| Service charge estimate | Mollak filings if available |
| Assignment rules | SPA, resale before handover |

A Golden Visa on a delayed project is still a visa, but your AED 2M may be illiquid paper for years.

### Tier-1 developers (indicative)

**Emaar:** High delivery track record; Oqood often 2 to 4 weeks from SPA on standard towers.

**Aldar (Abu Dhabi):** DMT-regulated escrow; registration timing 3 to 6 weeks from SPA.

**Nakheel:** Full DLD compliance; Oqood timing 2 to 5 weeks depending on project complexity.

### Tier-2: moderate visa risk

**DAMAC:** Complex payment plans can delay Oqood; verify registration SLA in SPA.

**Sobha:** Quality focus; Oqood generally 2 to 6 weeks.

**Omniyat:** Bespoke structures; Oqood can run 4 to 10 weeks.

### Red flag developers for visa buyers

- No completed UAE projects or single UAE project only
- Oqood delays beyond 12 weeks from SPA
- Escrow not verified on Dubai REST within 30 days of launch
- Payments required before Oqood registration
- Marketing visa eligibility without sample Oqood certificates

## What red flags should pause the purchase?

- Visa needed in 30 days but Oqood "coming soon"
- Project not on Trakheesi
- Primary unit under AED 2M with vague "add parking" math
- You would not buy the unit without visa marketing
- Wire instructions outside RERA escrow account

## How does off-plan differ in Dubai vs Abu Dhabi?

Dubai routes through DLD Oqood and GDRFA Dubai in many cases. Abu Dhabi registers with DMT; federal AED 2M threshold still applies but filing may route through ICP. Use emirate-specific counsel for SPA and escrow wording.

## How should you plan payment plans and cash flow?

Model instalments separately from visa timing. Golden Visa qualification does not pause developer payment demands. Keep 6 to 12 months liquidity for construction-linked cheques plus visa medical and insurance.

## What happens at handover?

Title Deed replaces Oqood as ownership proof for renewal and banking. Plan Ejari or self-use before handover if you intend to rent; visa renewal may ask for continued qualifying ownership evidence.

## Can multiple units aggregate for Golden Visa?

See [Golden Visa Multiple Properties UAE](/guides/golden-visa-multiple-properties-uae/). Off-plan units in different phases may misalign Oqood dates; same developer and phase reduces timing risk.

## What is the tax and residency distinction?

Golden Visa is UAE immigration status, not automatic home-country tax non-residence. UK, US, and EU buyers still need cross-border tax advice. UAE personal income tax remains 0% on employment for most residents, but global reporting rules may still apply abroad.

## How do foreign buyers complete the purchase legally?

| Step | Foreign buyer note |
|---|---|
| Zone verification | Designated freehold only |
| SPA signing | Passport ID; POA if remote |
| Oqood | 4% DLD at registration |
| Escrow | Never wire outside RERA account |
| Visa filing | Enter UAE for medical and biometrics |
| Bank account | Easier post-Emirates ID |

Allow 6 to 10 weeks from SPA to stamped visa if Oqood issues promptly; longer if developer delays registration. Step-by-step buy-side: [How to Buy Property in Dubai](/guides/how-to-buy-property-dubai-step-by-step/).

## What decision framework fits visa-motivated buyers?

Choose off-plan Golden Visa when:

- Oqood value already at or above AED 2M on registered SPA
- Developer tier and escrow verified on REST
- You would hold the asset after visa approval

Walk away when:

- Only reservation form exists
- Qualification depends on unregistered parking or storage add-ons
- Payment plan prevents escrow compliance

## What timeline should you expect from Oqood to visa?

**Week 1 to 2:** SPA signed, escrow funded, developer submits Oqood.

**Week 3 to 4:** Oqood certificate issued; assemble medical, insurance, passport copies.

**Week 4 to 5:** Golden Visa submission to GDRFA/ICP.

**Week 5 to 6:** Visa stamping and Emirates ID collection.

Standard total 6 to 10 weeks. Extended 10+ weeks when DLD backlogs, nationality-specific reviews, or document corrections occur. Mitigation: tier-1 developer, pre-prepared medical, experienced immigration consultant.

## What about joint ownership and corporate structures?

**Joint spouses:** Both names can appear on one Oqood; either spouse may be primary applicant; marriage certificate required.

**UAE company ownership:** Individual shareholders may qualify based on company-owned property value with extra corporate documents and 2 to 4 weeks additional verification.

## How does UAE compare to other Golden Visa programs?

| Factor | UAE off-plan route |
|---|---|
| Processing | Often faster than EU property routes |
| Residency minimum | No continuous stay requirement for many categories |
| Citizenship path | None automatic |
| Property liquidity | Dubai deep; off-plan illiquid until handover |

## What belongs in renewal planning?

Keep valid health insurance, clean criminal record certificates, and proof of continued ownership. Property market downturns can affect perceived asset backing even if visa is not auto-cancelled for construction delay alone.

## What documents should sit in your visa folder?

Keep notarised SPA, Oqood certificate, escrow payment receipts, title deed predecessor documents, passport copies, medical results, insurance policy, and GDRFA submission receipt in one PDF bundle. Banks and renewal agents request the same pack years later; missing Oqood history delays mortgage releases at handover.

## Can dependents apply on the same Oqood investment?

Dependent visas typically attach to the primary holder's approved Golden Visa. Plan school admission timelines if children need Emirates ID before KHDA term starts. Spouse employment visas may still require separate labour approvals even after property-based residency is granted.
