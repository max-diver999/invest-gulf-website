## How should you convert OMR and AED before comparing?

OMR 1 equals roughly AED 9.54 under the USD peg family. Never compare OMR 500 to AED 500; multiply riyal amounts before you judge Dubai offers against Muscat packages.

## What hidden costs surprise movers?

Visa medical tests, shipping, school deposits, and Salik tags in Dubai often sit outside rent quotes. In Muscat, second-car insurance and long school bus routes stack quietly.

## Who gains most from the Muscat discount?

Families on mid-tier British schools with one Oman-based earner gain the most. Dual Dubai salaries with employer-paid tuition often erase Muscat savings.

## How does Salalah change the cost map?

Salalah khareef season offers a cooler second-home escape; it does not replace Muscat school and job logic for most families but affects travel budgets.

## What decision rules simplify the spreadsheet?

If rent plus schools in Muscat convert to more than 85% of Dubai equivalent, rerun the model with spouse income included before you move.

## How should recruiters present Muscat packages without peg confusion?

Recruiters often quote Muscat salaries in OMR while candidates mentally compare to AED headline figures. Multiply OMR by about 9.54 before you negotiate. Presenting OMR 3,000 as "similar to AED 3,000" understates Muscat purchasing power by roughly AED 25,000 per month at mid-tier rent bands.

## Scenario planning for recruiters and HR teams

When relocating staff, present Muscat offers with AED-equivalent rent and school lines so candidates see real purchasing power. Add Dubai weekend flight budgets if the role is socially Dubai-linked.

Car depreciation and insurance differ: Dubai comprehensive policies on two sedans often exceed OMR 120/month equivalent in Muscat, but Salik and paid parking in Dubai can close that gap for cross-city commuters. Model all-in mobility, not fuel alone.

Furnishing and shipping: first-year expats often spend OMR 2,000-4,000 or AED 20,000-35,000 setting up villas, a one-off that makes year-one Muscat savings look smaller. Amortise setup over 24 months when you compare offers.

## When does Dubai still win on total wallet?

Dual-income Dubai households with employer school fees often beat Muscat on surplus even when Muscat rent is lower. Run three-column budgets: Muscat cost, Dubai cost, and household income including spouse work.
