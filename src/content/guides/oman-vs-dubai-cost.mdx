# Oman vs Dubai Cost of Living 2026: OMR/AED Budget Tables & Family Scenarios

TL;DR: At mid-tier family lifestyle, Muscat typically runs 25–40% below Dubai on rent and schools, the two lines that actually matter. An OMR 2,400/month Muscat family budget often compares to AED 22,000–28,000 in Dubai, not AED 2,400. Premium Al Mouj plus IB versus Dubai Marina plus IB narrows savings to 10–20% or less. Always convert: OMR 1 ≈ AED 9.54. Parent hub: [Muscat cost of living](/guides/muscat-cost-of-living/) (R53). ## Currency math: do this first or break the spreadsheet

The OMR looks small on paper. It is not. One wrong cell in your spreadsheet, treating OMR 500 like AED 500, and every downstream comparison is fiction.

| Conversion | Rate (indicative) |
|---|---|
| OMR → AED | × ~9.54 |
| OMR → USD | × ~2.597 (OMR peg) |
| AED → USD | ÷ ~3.67 (AED peg) |
| OMR 1,000 | ~AED 9,540 |
| OMR 3,000/mo | ~AED 28,620/mo |

Fatal error: treating OMR 500 rent as comparable to AED 500 rent, it is closer to AED 4,770.

## Sensitivity analysis: when Muscat saves nothing

Model these break-even cases before celebrating OMR rent:

| Scenario | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Dubai employer pays school 100% | Muscat fee advantage irrelevant |
| Dubai company car + fuel | Transport saving disappears |
| Muscat premium ITC + IB vs Dubai mid-tier | Gap under 15% |
| Dual Muscat cars + long school bus | OMR transport stacks |
| Imported organic grocery habit | Both cities punish |
| Trailing spouse lost AED salary | Oman cheaper but household income crash |

Spreadsheet rule: build Muscat column, Dubai column, third column net household income including spouse work, sort by surplus, not rent alone.
