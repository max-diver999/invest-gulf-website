## Which UAE residency visa fits your situation in 2026?

UAE residency choice in 2026 typically starts with a side-by-side view of Golden, Green, 2 year investor, employment, retirement, remote work, and student routes. Government fees alone often run from about AED 500 to AED 5,500 by category. Invest Gulf uses validity, self-sponsorship, and family rules before any property MOU.

| Visa Type | Validity | Self-Sponsored | Family Sponsorship | Approx. Gov. Fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Golden Visa (investor / talent) | 10 years | Yes | Yes | AED 4,000-5,500 |
| Green Visa | 5 years | Yes | Yes (limited) | AED 2,500-4,000 |
| Property Investor Visa (AED 750K+) | 2 years | Yes | Limited | AED 1,500-2,500 |
| Employment Visa | 2-3 years | No (employer-tied) | Yes (with salary threshold) | AED 1,000-2,000 |
| Retirement Visa | 5 years | Yes | Yes | AED 2,500-4,000 |
| Remote Work Visa | 1 year (renewable) | Yes | Limited | AED 1,000-1,500 |
| Student Visa | Duration of study | No (institution-tied) | No | AED 500-1,200 |

Fees above are approximate government charges only as of mid-2026 and exclude typing centre, medical test, Emirates ID, and consultant costs. Verify current schedules with ICP or GDRFA before applying.

## How does the Golden Visa work for property investors?

The Golden Visa typically grants renewable 10 year UAE residency without an employer sponsor when you meet property, investment, or talent tests. Property paths commonly need AED 2 million registered value, including some Oqood off-plan units. Invest Gulf budgets AED 4,000-5,500 in government and medical fees per principal applicant.

UAE Golden Visa property residency typically requires a registered asset of AED 2 million or more for a renewable 10 year stamp without an employer sponsor, with government and medical fees often totaling AED 4,000-5,500 per principal applicant. Certain Oqood-registered off-plan units can qualify, and mortgage-financed purchases may work when the required bank NOC is available under current 2026 practice across Dubai filings. Processing through GDRFA or ICP for complete files commonly takes 5 to 15 business days from a clean submission package. We still verify live ICP thresholds before any MOU because fee schedules and qualifying values can shift within a quarter, and family member fees sit on top of the principal applicant budget each filing cycle for the household. Attestation from abroad is the step that slips most often, and paying more does not accelerate an embassy queue, so start the document track alongside the application.

Who it suits: property investors, entrepreneurs, and talent-category applicants who want 10 year validity, lower job-change visa risk, and spouse or child sponsorship. - Qualifying property: commonly AED 2 million+
- Processing: often 5 to 15 business days when complete
- Absence rules: usually more flexible than standard visas
- Family fees: budget beyond the principal applicant

## How does the Green Visa work for freelancers and skilled workers?

The Green Visa typically offers 5 year self-sponsored residency for freelancers, skilled employees, and some investors. Freelancers often need about AED 360,000/year income proof, while skilled employees commonly face an AED 15,000 monthly salary floor. We use it as the exit ramp from employer-tied stamps.

- Freelancer track: UAE freelance permit + income proof near AED 360,000/year
- Skilled employee track: degree plus about AED 15,000/month
- Investor track: licenced business meeting ICA tests
- Parents: generally not sponsorable on standard Green files

Practical note: employment-visa holders going independent can often transition without an exit-reentry if status change is filed correctly.

## What is the 2-year property investor visa at AED 750,000?

The 2 year property investor visa typically fits ready residential assets registered from AED 750,000 upward that are fully paid in most interpretations. Validity and family rights stay thinner than the AED 2 million Golden path. Our underwriting treats it as a bridge, not a permanent substitute for 10 year status.

- Ready residential property, usually fully paid
- 2 year renewals versus Golden 10 year cycles
- Stricter absence tolerance than Golden in many cases
- Useful bridge while saving toward AED 2 million

## How does a standard UAE employment visa work?

A standard UAE employment visa typically lasts 2 to 3 years, ties stay to one employer sponsor, and resets on resignation or termination with a grace window. Family sponsorship still follows salary floors. Invest Gulf moves independence seekers toward Green Visa once AED 15,000 salary and skill tier qualify.

| Item | Employment visa norm |
| --- | --- |
| Validity | 2 to 3 years |
| Sponsor | UAE employer |
| Family | Salary-threshold dependent |
| Exit risk | High on job loss |

Professionals who want independence often migrate to the [Green Visa](/guides/uae-green-visa-guide/) once income and skill tier qualify.

## Who qualifies for the UAE Retirement Visa?

The UAE Retirement Visa typically renews in 5 year cycles without an employer when savings, pension, or property thresholds are met. Spouse sponsorship is common; adult children and parents are more restricted. Invest Gulf re-checks the financial proof at each renewal because sold property can kill eligibility.

- 5 year renewable cycles
- No active employer required
- Spouse sponsorship common
- Financial proof rechecked at renewal

Popular among British, European, and Indian retirees who already own Dubai or Abu Dhabi property.

## How does the Dubai remote work visa work?

The Dubai remote work visa typically allows 1 year renewable stay for foreign-sourced income at about USD 3,500+/month without UAE-client work. Banking and tax-certificate treatment can lag longer residencies. We expect many banks to want about 6 months of presence before premium accounts.

- Foreign income only; no UAE-client work
- About USD 3,500+/month documented
- 1 year renewable via GDRFA frameworks
- Banking KYC can lag longer residencies

Compare mechanics in the dedicated [remote work visa guide](/guides/uae-remote-work-visa/) when that is the primary intent.

## How does the UAE student visa work?

UAE student visas typically track the study programme length and cost about AED 500-1,200/year in government fees, separate from tuition. Dependents cannot be sponsored on this route. Invest Gulf plans the usual 60 day post-graduation grace window for employment, Green, or Golden conversion.

- No dependent sponsorship
- About AED 500-1,200/year government fees
- 60 day grace common after graduation
- Convert to employment, Green, or Golden when eligible

## How does family sponsorship work across visa types?

Family sponsorship typically works most cleanly on Golden and employment visas once salary or asset tests clear. Green and remote routes can sponsor dependents with shifting income proofs, while student visas cannot. We verify current ICP or GDRFA circulars before assuming Golden-style parent rights within 1 year.

| Visa | Typical family scope |
| --- | --- |
| Golden | Spouse, children; parents in some cases |
| Employment | Spouse and children with salary tests |
| Green / remote | Dependents with shifting proof rules |
| Student | None |
| Retirement | Spouse; children more restricted |

## What is the difference between UAE residency and citizenship?

UAE residency is typically a renewable permit, not citizenship, and most expats never receive a passport pathway from these visa classes. Golden 10 year and Green 5 year stamps remain immigration statuses, not nationality. We separate residency planning from rare citizenship talks so buyers do not confuse the two.

- Residency: renewable permit tied to a category
- Citizenship: rare, separate, not sold via these visas
- Golden or Green status is still residency
- Tax residency is another test again (often 183 days)

## How do you choose between UAE visa types?

Choosing a UAE visa typically follows investment level, employer dependency, and family needs in the area: below AED 750,000 there is no property investor route, AED 750,000-1.99 million maps to the 2 year visa, and AED 2 million+ opens Golden. Invest Gulf then asks whether you can tolerate employer sponsorship.

Side-by-side UAE residency planning typically contrasts Golden 10 year self-sponsorship at AED 2 million property, Green 5 year paths with freelance income near AED 360,000/year or skilled salary near AED 15,000/month, the older 2 year investor visa from AED 750,000, employment stamps of 2 to 3 years, retirement cycles of 5 years, and remote work at about USD 3,500+/month for 1 year. Government fee bands alone often run from roughly AED 500 on student files to AED 4,000-5,500 on Golden principals before typing and insurance. Invest Gulf picks the route from sponsor dependency and family needs first, then overlays property size, because buying below the Golden floor without an upgrade plan is the usual long-term residency trap. Budget the dependant filings in the same cycle rather than treating them as a later problem. Attestation from abroad is the step that most often slips, and it cannot be accelerated by paying more.

1. Map family sponsorship needs first
2. Decide employer dependency tolerance
3. Size property capital: under AED 750K, AED 750K-1.99M, or AED 2M+
4. Check freelance or skilled Green eligibility if independent
5. Confirm remote-only income rules if you will not take UAE work

## How does Emirates ID link to your visa type?

Emirates ID typically links to whichever residency stamp you hold and is required for banking, SIM, and many tenancy steps in the area within days of biometrics. Card fees often sit near AED 270-370 by validity. We will not treat a visa as operationally complete until the ID number is live.

- Biometrics after medical in most chains
- Card fees often AED 270-370
- Needed for bank, SIM, and many Ejari flows
- Number should match the active residency file

## What does Invest Gulf recommend for visa routing in 2026?

Invest Gulf routing in Q2 2026 typically sends AED 2 million+ buyers to Golden first, AED 750,000-1.99 million buyers to a 2 year investor bridge with a written upgrade plan, and AED 15,000+ skilled employees toward Green when independence matters. Remote applicants should expect about 6 months before smoother banking.

Insider tip: Write the upgrade path from a AED 750,000 2 year investor stamp to AED 2 million Golden before you buy the cheaper asset; renewing the lower tier forever is how residency plans stall.
