## How can Indians remit funds for Dubai property under LRS?

Resident Indians can remit up to USD 250,000 per financial year under RBI LRS for overseas property, so a married couple can combine USD 500,000 in one year. A JVC unit near AED 1.1 million can fit one year plus fees. Invest Gulf maps the funding path before the MOU.

### NRIs: NRE, NRO, and foreign earnings

Non-Resident Indians have broader funding paths outside LRS caps:

| Account type | Use for Dubai property |
|---|---|
| NRE account | Repatriable funds, straightforward for overseas property |
| NRO account | Non-repatriable rupee funds, remittance subject to USD 1M annual cap with documentation |
| Foreign salary/savings | Direct SWIFT from overseas bank to UAE escrow or trustee |
| UAE resident account | Indian nationals employed in UAE fund from local salary |

NRIs must comply with FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act), property must be for personal use or investment, not for prohibited activities. Retain all SWIFT confirmations, SPA copies, and bank declarations.

### What not to do

- Hawala or unlicensed channels, UAE banks and DLD trustees reject unclear source-of-funds
- Third-party transfers without documented relationship, triggers compliance holds
- Structuring remittances to evade TCS or LRS reporting, RBI audit risk
- Corporate routing without substance, FEMA scrutiny on shell structures

Consult an Indian chartered accountant or FEMA specialist before large outbound transfers.

## How does Indian tax apply to Dubai rental income?

Indian tax residents must declare Dubai rental income on a worldwide basis even when UAE personal tax is 0% and rent never leaves a UAE account. An AED 60,000 annual rent example is still reportable in the ITR. Our analysis treats skipped Schedule FA disclosure as a compliance red flag.

### Capital gains on disposal

When an Indian tax resident sells Dubai property:

| Element | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Acquisition cost | Purchase price + DLD 4% + agent + legal fees |
| Sale proceeds | Net of selling costs |
| Gain | Taxable as capital gains in India |
| Holding period | Long-term vs short-term per Indian IT rules on foreign assets |
| Reporting | Schedule FA in ITR for foreign assets; capital gains in relevant schedule |

No UAE capital gains tax applies, but Indian CGT may take a significant share for Indian residents.

### Schedule FA and asset disclosure

Indian tax residents must disclose foreign assets in Schedule FA of the Income Tax Return, including Dubai property, bank accounts, and rental income. Non-disclosure carries penalties under the Black Money Act. This is not optional for Indian tax residents, regardless of whether income is remitted to India.

LRS interaction: Mortgage reduces the LRS amount needed for purchase, only the down payment and fees require outbound remittance from India.

## What is the Dubai purchase process for Indian buyers?

The Dubai purchase process for Indian buyers uses the same DLD trustee path as other foreign buyers, typically from MOU through 10% deposit, NOC, 4% transfer fee and title or Oqood issue. Keep LRS Form A2 and SWIFT names aligned. Our research treats mismatched remitter names as the top red flag.

1. Confirm funding path: LRS, NRI account, or UAE salary
2. Select property in a designated freehold zone
3. **Verify** via Dubai REST app (Unit Profile, ownership, encumbrances)
4. **Agree terms**: MOU (Form F) on secondary; SPA on off-plan
5. **Pay deposit**: typically 10% on secondary market
6. **Mortgage** (if applicable): 3 to 6 weeks processing
7. **Transfer funds**: SWIFT to escrow (off-plan) or trustee (ready) with full documentation
8. **NOC** from developer (secondary market)
9. DLD registration: 4% transfer fee at trustee centre
10. **Title deed** or Oqood certificate issued
11. Golden Visa application (if AED 2M+)
12. Ejari registration (if renting out)

Indian buyers purchasing remotely use notarised Power of Attorney, attested at the UAE Embassy in New Delhi, Mumbai, or other Indian consulates, or through a UAE-registered solicitor.

## What mistakes do Indian Dubai buyers make?

Seven red flags dominate Indian buyer mistakes: assuming UAE 0% tax ends Indian reporting, funding via hawala, buying on gross yield only, skipping Schedule FA, ignoring LRS timing, omitting Ejari, and paying unlicensed brokers. Marina headline yields near 7% can net closer to 4%. Our research requires a RERA BRN before any deposit.

1. **Assuming UAE 0% tax ends Indian reporting.** Worldwide income rules apply to Indian tax residents.
2. **Funding via informal channels.** Hawala or unclear third-party transfers trigger trustee holds.
3. **Buying on gross yield only.** Marina headline 7% can net near 4% after charges and vacancy.
4. **Skipping Schedule FA.** Foreign assets must appear in ITR for Indian residents.
5. **Ignoring LRS timing.** Large purchases need a multi-year remittance plan or NRI funding path.
6. **No Ejari on tenants.** Without Ejari, RERA dispute routes are weaker.
7. **Unlicensed brokers.** Confirm RERA BRN before paying a deposit.

## Does Dubai property qualify Indians for the Golden Visa?

Dubai property qualifies Indians for a 10-year UAE Golden Visa application when registered freehold value reaches AED 2 million or above after DLD registration. Off-plan buyers may qualify once Oqood reflects the threshold. Invest Gulf notes that Golden Visa status does not change Indian tax residency.

## Why do Indian investors still choose Dubai over domestic markets?

Indian investors still choose Dubai when freehold title, USD-pegged pricing, and net yields in JVC or Sports City beat post-tax returns of roughly 2 to 3% on many domestic apartments. Deep South Asian expat rental demand completes the case. Invest Gulf buyer scenarios keep Golden Visa access as a secondary underwriting input.

## What checklist should Indian buyers run before signing?

Indian buyers should run a seven-point checklist before signing: confirm the FEMA funding path, model net yield after charges and Indian tax, verify the unit on Dubai REST, budget 6 to 9% acquisition costs, register Ejari, file Schedule FA if required, and apply for Golden Visa only after AED 2M registration.

1. Confirm FEMA path: LRS, NRE/NRO, or UAE salary with full documentation
2. Model net yield with service charges, vacancy, and Indian tax on declared rent
3. Verify unit via Dubai REST and agent RERA licence
4. Budget 6 to 9% above price for DLD, trustee, broker, and legal fees
5. Register Ejari on every tenancy and retain Mollak payment receipts
6. File Schedule FA and rental income in ITR if Indian tax resident
7. Apply for Golden Visa after registration if value meets AED 2M threshold
