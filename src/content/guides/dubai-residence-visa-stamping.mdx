# Dubai Residence Visa Stamping: Process, Timelines & PRO Guide

> Disclaimer: June 2026 procedural guide. MOHRE/ICP workflows update, follow your PRO instructions.


<TldrBlock text={"Medical clear, then EID biometrics, then PRO submits passport; stamping usually takes 3 to 7 working days. Without the stamp you face limited banking, illegal work risk, and no licence conversion. Do not travel while the passport is with PRO."} />

## What is visa stamping and why do expats confuse it with approval SMS?

Visa stamping is the physical residency sticker placed in your passport after medical fitness and Emirates ID biometrics clear. An "approved" SMS is not finished residency until the stamp appears and your passport is returned. New arrivals often book travel before this step and then miss payroll or lease signing windows.

## How does the employment visa stamping chain work?

Typical employment flow: entry permit or in-country status change, medical test, Emirates ID biometrics, MOHRE labour card linkage, PRO passport submission, then GDRFA/ICP stamping. End-to-end timelines often run 2 to 4 weeks from arrival, though corporate PRO quality varies. Escalate HR if the PRO is silent more than seven days after medical clearance.

## How does Golden Visa stamping differ?

Golden Visa routes use ICP/AMER centres and property or investor evidence rather than employer MOHRE files. Budget 4 to 8 weeks total when title and insurance proofs chain together. Compare categories in [Golden Visa vs Dubai residence visa](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/) and the step guide at [Golden Visa application step by step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/).

## What unlocks after the stamp is in your passport?

Stamped residency unlocks full salary account opening, Ejari leases in your name, driving licence conversion, and dependent sponsorship filings. Temporary EID receipts help some banks interim, but landlords and HR usually want the stamped page. Photocopy the visa page the day you collect the passport; typing centres rarely rescan for HR later.

## What medical steps must finish before stamping?

Fitness tests at approved centres are mandatory for most adult applicants. Failures require re-test or appeal paths depending on cause. Schedule early morning slots during summer heat. Insurance active before PRO submission blocks approval even when medical and biometrics are complete.

## What happens at Emirates ID biometrics?

Book ICA appointments early; bring passport, photos per centre rules, and dependants if applicable. Glasses and dress code rules apply. Children must attend for their own files. Biometrics completion triggers the PRO handoff window for passport stamping.

| Tip | Detail |
|---|---|
| Appointment | Book early morning |
| Dress code | Modest |
| Glasses | Follow ICA guidelines |
| Children | All dependants attend |

## How does passport handover with PRO work?

Corporate PROs collect passports for stamping unless you pay for premium same-day services at authorised centres. Save the PRO receipt number when surrendering the passport; tracing queues without it is painful in September peak season. Plan zero international travel until return.

## How are dependant visas stamped relative to the primary holder?

Dependants usually stamp after the primary holder is approved, often 1 to 2 weeks later with separate medical and biometrics. School admissions may proceed with conditional letters, but final enrollment needs stamped dependant pages. Family files bottleneck on tenancy evidence tied to Dubai housing; see [first 30 days in Dubai](/guides/first-30-days-dubai-expat/).

## What visa duration appears on the stamp?

Employment stamps commonly show 2 to 3 year validity linked to contract and sponsor. Golden Visa stamps may show 10-year validity when category rules match. Align passport expiry buffers; renewal requires re-medical in many cases.

## What delays stamping in practice?

Delays cluster around incomplete insurance, MOHRE labour card mismatches, security clearance for certain nationalities, and PRO backlog during peak relocation months. Overstay fines accrue if status is not resolved before prior visa expiry; treat grace periods as narrow.

## What should you do after collection?

Connect DEWA where applicable, finalize bank KYC with stamped visa, apply for driving licence conversion, and upload visa copies to HR portals. Property buyers can proceed to [Emirates ID after property purchase](/guides/emirates-id-after-property-purchase/) and banking at [opening bank account for Dubai property buyers](/guides/opening-bank-account-dubai-property-buyers/).

## What are indicative government and service fees?

End-to-end processing in Dubai often runs 10 to 25 working days from application to stamp; Golden tracks can add days for verification. Government fees commonly fall in bands such as 3,000 to 7,000 AED for standard employment packages and higher for investor categories, plus medical 300 to 500 AED per person and Emirates ID around 370 AED. Typing centres charge 150 to 350 AED per form; expedited services add 500 to 1,500 AED when available. Verify live fee screens at submission; rules change.

## How do AMER centres and digital visa display fit in?

AMER centres handle many Golden and ICP workflows with mandatory online booking trending emirate-wide. UAE Pass may show digital visa status for some categories, but employment sponsors still often rely on physical stamps during transition. Carry passport backup until HR confirms digital acceptance.

## What checklist should you run before PRO submits?

Confirm medical clearance uploaded, biometrics complete, insurance active, labour card matched to MOHRE for employment, passport validity sufficient, and no pending travel. For property-linked Golden files, confirm title and NOC chain before passport surrender.

## What if stamping fails or passport is lost after stamp?

Medical or security failures need category-specific appeals. Lost passports require embassy reissue plus MOI visa recovery; scanned stamps and PRO receipts accelerate recovery. Cancellation and exit stamps follow separate MOHRE/ICP flows if employment ends.

## How do insurance and employer HR portals fit the stamping window?

HR systems often block payroll until visa copies upload. Medical insurance must stay continuous; a one-day lapse can reject submission even when biometrics are done. Keep PDF scans of every receipt from typing centres; PROs rarely resend them during audits.

## What grace periods apply after employment cancellation?

Grace rules vary by category and overstay history; treat any gap between cancellation and new stamp as a legal question for PRO, not a forum post. Exit stamps and labour bans interact with MOHRE files; plan new employer transfers before passport cancellation if HR advises sequential flow.

## How do premium same-day stamping services work?

Authorised centres sometimes offer expedited passport return for an extra fee when slots exist. Employment sponsors still route through corporate PRO policies; ask HR before paying personally. Same-day does not skip medical or biometrics prerequisites.

## What nationality-specific delays appear in practice?

Some nationalities face extra security clearance steps that extend stamping beyond the usual 3 to 7 working days after PRO submission. Submit clean scans, consistent name spellings across passport, degree, and employment contract to reduce rework.

## Employment vs Golden Visa stamping at a glance

| Step | Employment visa | Golden Visa (typical) |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsor | Employer MOHRE file | Self via ICP/AMER |
| Medical | Mandatory | Mandatory |
| Biometrics | ICA centre | ICA centre |
| Passport hold | Corporate PRO | Typing centre or self |
| Timeline | Often 2 to 4 weeks total | Often 4 to 8 weeks |

Property investors should read [Golden Visa application step by step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/) before surrendering passport during developer payment windows.

## Post-stamp month one checklist

Apply for driving licence conversion if eligible, upload visa copies to HR and insurance portals, finalize DEWA or housing utility accounts, and register dependants for school once stamped pages are available. Photograph every stamp and EID receipt the day you collect documents; recovery after loss is slower than prevention.

## Digital visa display and UAE Pass (2026 transition notes)

Some categories already show visa status inside UAE Pass while employment files still use physical stamps. Employers may accept digital proof for internal HR, but banks and landlords often want passport stickers during transition. Carry physical passports to appointments until your PRO and bank confirm digital acceptance in writing.

## Remote work and property investor stamping notes

Remote workers on freelance or free-zone visas follow sponsor-specific stamping paths similar in medical/biometrics order but different in document sets. Property investors on Golden tracks should align [opening bank account for Dubai property buyers](/guides/opening-bank-account-dubai-property-buyers/) only after stamp and EID numbers exist; tourist status blocks most mortgage workflows.

## Family stamping sequence checklist

Primary holder stamped first, then spouse, then children usually one to two weeks apart. Book ICA slots in sequence rather than assuming parallel processing. Keep colour scans in cloud storage shared with spouse before passport surrender.

## MOHRE labour card and stamp alignment

Employment stamping fails when labour card job title, salary, or employer trade license mismatches MOHRE records. Ask HR for a pre-submission screenshot of the MOHRE file before PRO takes your passport. Golden Visa files skip MOHRE but require investor evidence chains instead; mixing document sets between routes causes week-long rejections.

## Overstay fines and status changes during processing

Entry permits and in-country status changes carry narrow validity windows. Track expiry dates in a shared calendar with PRO. Overstay fines near AED 100 per day after grace periods in many 2026 cases, verify live ICP screens. Pay fines before stamping submission when PRO advises settlement to avoid rejection loops.

## How residence visa stamping usually sequences in Dubai

Stamping sits after entry permissions, medical fitness, and biometrics in most employment or investor pathways. Missing one document resets appointments more often than the stamping fee itself. Keep passport validity, photos, and insurance proofs in one folder.

| Checkpoint | Failure mode |
| --- | --- |
| Entry status | Wrong visa class for the application track |
| Medical | Clinic report not linked to your file |
| Emirates ID biometrics | Missed slot after medical |
| Insurance | Policy not accepted for the residence type |
| Stamping window | Travel planned inside a blocked period |
