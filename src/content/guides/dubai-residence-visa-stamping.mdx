# Dubai Residence Visa Stamping: Process, Timelines & PRO Guide

> Disclaimer: June 2026 procedural guide. MOHRE/ICP workflows update, follow your PRO instructions.


<TldrBlock text={"Medical clear, then EID biometrics, then PRO submits passport; stamping usually takes 3 to 7 working days. Without the stamp you face limited banking, illegal work risk, and no licence conversion. Do not travel while the passport is with PRO."} />

Dubai residence visa stamping typically means a physical residency sticker placed after medical fitness and Emirates ID biometrics clear, with PRO submission often taking 3 to 7 working days once those steps finish. Invest Gulf treats an "approved" SMS as incomplete residency because foreign buyers and new hires still lack full banking, Ejari leases in their own name, and driving licence conversion until the passport returns. Employment chains commonly run 2 to 4 weeks from arrival; Golden Visa property routes often need 4 to 8 weeks when title and insurance proofs chain together. Plan zero international travel while the passport sits with the PRO or typing centre, and keep colour scans of every receipt for HR and bank KYC later.

## What is visa stamping and why do expats confuse it with approval SMS?

Visa stamping is the physical residency sticker placed in your passport after medical fitness and Emirates ID biometrics clear, and an approved SMS is not finished residency until the stamp appears. Invest Gulf sees foreign buyers book travel before this step and then miss payroll or lease windows of 1 to 2 weeks.

- Approved SMS ≠ stamped passport
- Banking and licence conversion usually wait for the stamp
- Keep photocopies the day you collect the passport

## How does the employment visa stamping chain work?

Typical employment flow requires entry permit or in-country status change, medical test, Emirates ID biometrics, MOHRE labour card linkage, PRO passport submission, then GDRFA/ICP stamping over 2 to 4 weeks. Invest Gulf escalates HR if the PRO is silent more than 7 days after medical clearance for foreign buyers.

| Step | Typical owner | Planning note |
|---|---|---|
| Entry permit / status change | Employer PRO | Start clock |
| Medical fitness | Applicant | Early morning slots |
| Emirates ID biometrics | Applicant | Book ICA early |
| Labour card match | MOHRE / HR | Title and salary must match |
| Passport stamping | Corporate PRO | 3 to 7 working days after handoff |

## How does Golden Visa stamping differ?

Golden Visa stamping typically uses ICP/AMER centres and property or investor evidence rather than employer MOHRE files, with total timelines often 4 to 8 weeks when title chains are clean. Invest Gulf aligns title, insurance, and NOC proofs before passport surrender so foreign buyers avoid week-long rejections that restart the 10 to 25 working day clock.

Compare categories in [Golden Visa vs Dubai residence visa](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/) and the step guide at [Golden Visa application step by step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/).

| Step | Employment visa | Golden Visa (typical) |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsor | Employer MOHRE file | Self via ICP/AMER |
| Medical | Mandatory | Mandatory |
| Biometrics | ICA centre | ICA centre |
| Passport hold | Corporate PRO | Typing centre or self |
| Timeline | Often 2 to 4 weeks total | Often 4 to 8 weeks |

## What unlocks after the stamp is in your passport?

Stamped residency typically unlocks full salary accounts, Ejari leases in your name, driving licence conversion, and dependent sponsorship filings within 7 to 14 days of passport return. Invest Gulf still treats temporary EID receipts as interim only because foreign buyers face landlord and HR requests for the stamped page on 2 to 3 year employment visas.

## What medical steps must finish before stamping?

Fitness tests at approved centres are mandatory for most adult applicants and typically cost AED 300 to 500 per person before PRO submission can start. Invest Gulf schedules early morning slots in summer heat because foreign buyers who skip active insurance still face rejection even when medical and biometrics are complete for 2 to 3 year employment files.

## What happens at Emirates ID biometrics?

Emirates ID biometrics typically require ICA appointments with passport, photos per centre rules, and dependants attending their own files before the PRO handoff begins. Invest Gulf books early slots for foreign buyers because biometrics completion triggers the 3 to 7 working day stamping window and Emirates ID fees near AED 370.

| Tip | Detail |
|---|---|
| Appointment | Book early morning |
| Dress code | Modest |
| Glasses | Follow ICA guidelines |
| Children | All dependants attend |
| Emirates ID fee | Around AED 370 (verify live) |

## How does passport handover with PRO work?

Corporate PROs typically collect passports for stamping unless you pay AED 500 to 1,500 for premium same-day services at authorised centres when slots exist in peak months. Invest Gulf saves the PRO receipt number for foreign buyers because tracing September queues without it can add 3 to 7 working days of delay.

**Insider tip:** Ask HR for a pre-submission screenshot of the MOHRE labour card before PRO takes your passport; a job title or salary mismatch is a common week-long rejection that looks like a "stamping delay."

## How are dependant visas stamped relative to the primary holder?

Dependants typically stamp 1 to 2 weeks after the primary holder with separate medical and biometrics, not on the same day as the sponsor. Invest Gulf sequences ICA slots for foreign buyer families because school enrollment needs stamped dependant pages even when conditional letters cover the first 2 to 4 weeks.

Family files often bottleneck on tenancy evidence tied to Dubai housing; see [first 30 days in Dubai](/guides/first-30-days-dubai-expat/).

## What visa duration appears on the stamp?

Employment stamps commonly show 2 to 3 year validity linked to contract and sponsor, while Golden Visa stamps may show 10-year validity when category rules match the file. Invest Gulf aligns passport expiry buffers of at least 6 months for foreign buyers because renewal often requires a fresh medical.

## What delays stamping in practice?

Stamping delays typically cluster around incomplete insurance, MOHRE mismatches, security clearance for certain nationalities, and PRO backlog in peak months from August to October. Invest Gulf tracks overstay risk for foreign buyers because fines near AED 100 per day can accrue if status is not resolved before prior visa expiry.

Checklist before PRO submits:

1. Medical clearance uploaded
2. Biometrics complete
3. Insurance active without gaps
4. Labour card matched to MOHRE (employment)
5. Passport validity sufficient
6. No travel booked during passport hold
7. Title and NOC chain ready (Golden / property)

## What are indicative government and service fees?

End-to-end Dubai processing typically runs 10 to 25 working days from application to stamp, with government packages often AED 3,000 to 7,000 for standard employment and higher for investor categories. Invest Gulf adds medical AED 300 to 500, Emirates ID around AED 370, and typing fees AED 150 to 350 per form for foreign buyers.

| Fee line | Indicative band |
|---|---|
| Employment package (gov) | AED 3,000 to 7,000 |
| Medical per person | AED 300 to 500 |
| Emirates ID | Around AED 370 |
| Typing centre per form | AED 150 to 350 |
| Expedited passport return | AED 500 to 1,500 |

Verify live fee screens at submission; rules change.

## How do AMER centres and digital visa display fit in?

AMER centres typically handle many Golden and ICP workflows with mandatory online booking, while UAE Pass may show digital status for some 2026 categories during a multi-month transition. Invest Gulf still advises foreign buyers to carry passport backup for 2 to 4 weeks after stamping until HR and banks confirm digital acceptance in writing.

- Book AMER slots online before peak September relocation weeks
- Keep physical stamps for bank KYC even if UAE Pass shows status
- Ask PRO whether your employer accepts digital proof for payroll only

## What if stamping fails or passport is lost after stamp?

Medical or security failures typically need category-specific appeals lasting 2 to 6 weeks, and lost passports require embassy reissue plus MOI visa recovery. Invest Gulf keeps scanned stamps and PRO receipts for foreign buyers because recovery after loss is slower than a same-day photocopy habit by 10 to 20 working days.

Appeal checklist for failed stamps:

- Confirm medical re-test vs security hold with PRO in writing
- Keep embassy police report numbers for lost-passport recovery
- Budget 2 to 6 weeks before assuming a new employment start date
- Do not travel on an incomplete status change

## How do insurance and employer HR portals fit the stamping window?

HR systems typically block payroll until visa copies upload within 7 days of collection, and a one-day insurance lapse can reject submission even when biometrics are done. Invest Gulf archives PDF receipts from typing centres for foreign buyers because PROs rarely resend them during audits 30 to 60 days later.

## What grace periods apply after employment cancellation?

Grace rules typically vary by category and overstay history, so any gap between cancellation and a new stamp is a PRO legal question lasting days, not a forum guess. Invest Gulf plans employer transfers before passport cancellation when HR advises sequential flow for foreign buyers facing MOHRE labour-ban interactions and AED 100/day overstay risk.

Cancellation checklist:

- Ask PRO for the written grace end date in calendar days
- Align new employer transfer before passport cancellation when possible
- Settle overstay fines before the next stamping submission
- Keep exit-stamp rules separate from labour-ban questions

## How do premium same-day stamping services work?

Authorised centres sometimes offer expedited passport return for AED 500 to 1,500 when slots exist, but medical and biometrics still cannot be skipped in any route. Invest Gulf asks HR before foreign buyers pay personally because corporate PRO policies may reject private same-day routes during 2 to 4 week employment chains.

## What nationality-specific delays appear in practice?

Some nationalities typically face extra security clearance that extends stamping beyond the usual 3 to 7 working days after PRO submission, sometimes adding 1 to 3 weeks. Invest Gulf submits clean scans with consistent name spellings across passport, degree, and contract so foreign buyers reduce rework loops that restart the 10 to 25 working day clock.

## What should you do after collection?

Post-stamp month one typically means DEWA setup where applicable, bank KYC with stamped visa, driving licence conversion, and HR portal uploads within the first 7 to 14 days after passport return. Invest Gulf points property investors to [Emirates ID after property purchase](/guides/emirates-id-after-property-purchase/) and [opening bank account for Dubai property buyers](/guides/opening-bank-account-dubai-property-buyers/) once EID numbers exist.

Remote workers on freelance or free-zone visas follow sponsor-specific stamping paths similar in medical/biometrics order but different in document sets. Property investors on Golden tracks should align banking only after stamp and EID numbers exist; tourist status blocks most mortgage workflows.

Entry permits and in-country status changes carry narrow validity windows. Track expiry dates in a shared calendar with PRO. Pay fines before stamping submission when PRO advises settlement to avoid rejection loops.

## How residence visa stamping usually sequences in Dubai

Stamping typically sits after entry permissions, medical fitness, and biometrics, and missing one document resets appointments more often than the AED 150 to 350 typing fee itself. Invest Gulf keeps passport validity, photos, and insurance proofs in one folder for foreign buyers running 2 to 8 week pathways from arrival to stamp.

| Checkpoint | Failure mode |
| --- | --- |
| Entry status | Wrong visa class for the application track |
| Medical | Clinic report not linked to your file |
| Emirates ID biometrics | Missed slot after medical |
| Insurance | Policy not accepted for the residence type |
| Stamping window | Travel planned inside a blocked period |

Invest Gulf budgets government and typing fees in the AED 3,000 to 7,000 band for standard employment packages, plus medical AED 300 to 500 per person and Emirates ID near AED 370, before foreign buyers schedule bank KYC or school admissions. Dependants usually stamp 1 to 2 weeks after the primary holder with separate appointments, and overstay risk rises if prior status expires during PRO backlog. Keep receipt numbers, colour scans of the stamped page, and continuous insurance active through submission day so a one-day lapse does not restart the queue.

Passport handover for Dubai stamping typically means surrendering the booklet to a corporate PRO or typing centre for 3 to 7 working days, unless an authorised centre offers same-day return for about AED 500 to 1,500 when slots exist. Invest Gulf plans zero international travel in that window because foreign buyers who keep a flight booked often miss the collection day and restart biometrics or labour-card matches.
