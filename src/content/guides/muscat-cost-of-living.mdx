## What does rent cost by Muscat area?

Deposits of one month are common, and unfurnished setups often need AED 19,000-57,000 in the first 60 days.

## Do you need a car in Muscat?

A car is typically required for Muscat families because there is no metro, and we budget AED 760-1,150 equivalent monthly for one car or AED 1,400-1,900 for two-car households including fuel. Licence swaps from some GCC states exist, yet ROP lists change and should be verified before the first 30 days.

## What checklist validates COL before you move?

A Muscat COL checklist typically requires three rent comps, written school fee PDFs, landlord utility estimates, a two-car insurance quote, and mapped employer benefits before we will call a package safe. Keep total spend under 75 percent of net salary or renegotiate before the flight, not after the first invoice.

1. Three rent comps per target area, furnished status noted.
2. School fee PDF plus bus for each child, seat confirmation in writing.
3. Utility estimate from landlord or neighbour for same building type.
4. Car plan (purchase, lease, second car) with insurance quote.
5. Employer benefits mapped (housing, schooling, flights, health).
6. Total spend under **75% of net**; if not, renegotiate before the flight.
