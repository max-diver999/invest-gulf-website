# Muscat Cost of Living 2026: Rent, Schools, Utilities and Monthly Budgets

Parent hub: [Oman relocation guide](/guides/oman-property-foreigner-living/)

> Disclaimer: Figures are June 2026 planning ranges, not quotes. ROP visa, property ITC rules and school fees change. Verify with employer PRO, school and licensed advisor before relocating.

<TldrBlock text={"Muscat families spend OMR 2,200-3,400 monthly for mid-tier lifestyle, significantly below Dubai equivalents due to lower rent and school fees. Singles need OMR 700-1,100 monthly. Key savings are housing and education; imported goods remain expensive."} />

## How should you use this Muscat COL guide?

Copy the budget tables into a spreadsheet. Replace indicative rents with three live listings per area. Amortise school fees: annual tuition divided by 12, plus bus, uniforms and registration. Schools break budgets more often than groceries.

Two families on the same OMR 3,500 gross can land OMR 2,400 or OMR 3,800 monthly spend. Al Mouj marina three-bedroom plus premium British is not the same universe as Al Khoud villa plus Indian mid-tier. Model both before you negotiate your package.

Oman trades slower pace and lower density for smaller luxury stock and stronger local culture integration than UAE or Qatar.

## Why does Muscat feel cheaper than Dubai for many expats?

Muscat savings concentrate in **rent and school fees**, not in imported electronics or premium dining. The **OMR peg to USD** removes FX noise for dollar earners. Personal income tax is not part of the usual expat COL stack in the same way as many Western countries, but **visa, schooling, and car ownership** still dominate cash flow.

| Line item | Muscat (mid family) | Dubai (comparable) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rent 3BR | OMR 750-1,100 | AED 12,000-18,000+ |
| British mid schools (2 kids, amortised) | OMR 900-1,100/mo | AED 7,500-12,500/mo |
| Utilities summer | OMR 80-140 villa | AED 600-1,200+ with DC |

## What does rent cost by Muscat area?

| Area | Profile | 1BR OMR/mo | 2BR OMR/mo | 3BR / villa OMR/mo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Al Mouj | Marina ITC, expat families, golf | 450-650 | 550-900 | 900-1,400 |
| Qurum / Shati Al Qurum | Established, cafes, clinics | 400-600 | 450-750 | 700-1,100 |
| Madinat Sultan Qaboos | Central, diplomatic belt | 450-700 | 500-800 | 750-1,050 |
| Al Hail / Al Khoud | Value, commute | 280-450 | 350-550 | 500-800 |
| Al Amerat | Villa belt, space | rare 1BR | 400-650 | 600-1,000 |
| Muscat Hills / premium ITC | Waterfront, newer | 500-750 | 650-950 | 1,000-1,600 |
| Salalah (second city) | Monsoon tourism, port | 250-400 | 300-500 | 450-750 |

Deposits: one month common; agency fees vary, negotiate. Lease registration with municipality compliance is required for utilities and some visa steps; follow landlord or PRO guidance.

Furnished vs unfurnished: corporate arrivals often get furnished units; DIY relocators budget **OMR 2,000-6,000** for basics if unfurnished.

## How much do utilities cost in Muscat?

Apartments typically run **OMR 25-65/month**; villas **OMR 80-140+** in peak summer when AC load spikes. Unlike Dubai's split between DEWA and district cooling, most Muscat rentals use **split AC on one Kahramaa bill**. ITC master developments may add community chiller or OA pass-through lines, so read the lease.

Activation: put the utility account in your name before the first summer bill; it belongs on the landlord handover checklist.

## How do school costs fit the monthly budget?

British mid-tier about **OMR 5,000/year** per child amortises to roughly **OMR 420/month** plus bus **OMR 60-100** and uniforms or exams **OMR 30+**. Two children in British mid often create an **OMR 900-1,100/month** school line alone.

| Action | Timeline |
| --- | --- |
| Shortlist 3 schools | 12 weeks pre-arrival |
| Apply with reports | 8 weeks |
| Placement test | Arrival weeks 2-3 |
| Confirm seat before 12-month lease | Critical in Muscat |

Full fee tiers: [Muscat international schools](/guides/muscat-international-schools/). Special needs support is thinner than UAE; verify written SEN policy before you sign a lease tied to a campus.

## What do groceries, dining, and household help cost?

Groceries for a family of four often land **OMR 280-380/month** at mixed Lulu/Carrefour baskets; imported specialty items cost more. Dining in Qurum or Al Mouj runs **OMR 8-15** per person at mid restaurants; hotel brunch **OMR 25-45** per couple.

Alcohol is sold in licensed venues, not supermarket retail like some UAE emirates; budget it as dining-out, not grocery.

Part-time cleaner **OMR 4-6/hour**; live-in help requires visa sponsorship, often **OMR 250-450** all-in monthly once visa and medical are amortised.

## Do you need a car in Muscat?

Yes for almost all families and most professionals. Budget **OMR 80-120/month** fuel and maintenance for one car, **OMR 150-200+** for two-car households. Driving licence swaps from UAE/GCC exist for some nationalities; verify ROP current list via [Oman driving licence](/guides/oman-driving-license/).

Weekend wadi and diving trips are discretionary COL lines many expats actually spend on.

## What about healthcare and insurance?

Employer packages usually cover GP and emergency care; family top-up dental or maternity runs **OMR 15-40/month**. Confirm hospital network before pregnancy; Muscat facilities are solid but network gaps hurt.

See [Oman healthcare guide](/guides/oman-healthcare-guide/).

## How does banking and remittance fit COL?

Opening a local salary account is standard for employed expats. Exchange houses can beat bank wires for India and Philippines corridors; compare fees monthly if you remit large shares of salary.

[Oman banking for expats](/guides/oman-banking-expats/) covers account setup.

## What one-off costs hit in the first year?

Plan **OMR 3,000-5,000** out-of-pocket in the first **60 days** for deposits, car purchase or lease, school registration, and furniture if unreimbursed. Visa and medical for dependents add spikes aligned with [Oman family visa](/guides/oman-family-visa/) timing.

## How does Muscat COL compare to Qatar and Dubai?

Muscat generally runs **lower than Doha** on rent and schools at mid-tier; Qatar still wins on nightlife depth and some residency-by-property products. Versus Dubai, mid-family COL savings often reach **25-40%** until you choose Al Mouj plus IB, which compresses the gap.

## Should you rent first or buy ITC property?

Rent-first is common for new assignees: prove school seats and employer stability for **6-12 months** before SPA on ITC freehold. Buying shifts COL from rent to **mortgage or cash**, plus **service charges** and maintenance.

Investor residency near **OMR 250,000** (confirm current official rules) is a separate decision from monthly COL; do not merge the spreadsheets.

## What do three household case studies show?

**Case 1:** Single professional, Qurum fringe 1BR, net **OMR 1,800**, spend **~OMR 780**, savings **~OMR 1,020**. Comfortable without schools; Al Mouj upgrade tightens margin.

**Case 2:** Family of four, Al Khoud villa, British mid schools, net **OMR 3,200**, spend **~OMR 2,650**, margin **OMR 550**. School tier jump or Al Mouj rent needs allowance renegotiation.

**Case 3:** Al Mouj premium, IB plus marina villa, net **OMR 5,500**, spend **~OMR 4,100**, surplus **OMR 1,400**. Premium ITC narrows COL gap with Dubai; lifestyle choice as much as savings.

## What checklist validates COL before you move?

1. Three rent comps per target area, furnished status noted.
2. School fee PDF plus bus for each child, seat confirmation in writing.
3. Utility estimate from landlord or neighbour for same building type.
4. Car plan (purchase, lease, second car) with insurance quote.
5. Employer benefits mapped (housing, schooling, flights, health).
6. Total spend under **75% of net**; if not, renegotiate before the flight.

## What about Salalah or second-city COL?

Only model Salalah if the **job is local**. Muscat commute fantasies fail on time and fuel. Salalah rent bands run lower but job pools are narrower.

## What should remote workers and trailing spouses budget?

Serviced apartments in Qurum **OMR 600-900/month** short-term beat hotels but lose to 12-month leases. Self-purchased insurance **OMR 800-2,000/year** family if not employed. No schooling allowance means full Muscat fees from net income. Trailing spouse lost income plus club and transport costs still hit cash flow even when labeled "free" on paper.

## What COL pros and cons should you expect?

**Pros:** Lower rent and school fees than Dubai/Doha at mid-tier; pegged currency; quieter family pace; outdoor lifestyle (wadi, diving).

**Cons:** Car mandatory; thinner luxury and tutoring market; premium ITC closes savings gap; imported goods pricey; school seat timing risk.

## How do rent negotiations work in Muscat?

Unlike some Gulf markets with bidding wars, Muscat often softens when corporate rotations slow. You may still negotiate **one free month**, **chiller cap**, or **white goods** on 12-month leases, especially on villas in Al Khoud and Al Amerat. Always confirm **school seat** before you leverage vacancy; a cheap villa with no British Year 7 place is not a saving.

| Tactic | When it works |
| --- | --- |
| 12-month vs 6-month | Landlords prefer 12-month stability |
| Furnished premium | Ask what furniture is included |
| Summer start | May reduce friction on villa stock |
| Corporate lease | Employer PRO may beat DIY pricing |

## What does a monthly cash flow calendar look like?

| Timing | Typical cash hit |
| --- | --- |
| Month 1 | Deposit, agency, car, furniture |
| Month 2 | School registration, insurance top-up |
| Month 3-5 | Lower spend after setup |
| June-August | Higher Kahramaa bills |
| September | School term fees spike |
| December | Flights and holiday travel |

If total spend exceeds **75% of net** after you map this calendar, renegotiate the package before you fly, not after the first school invoice.

## How should retirees or long-stay visitors budget?

Long-stay visitors without employer benefits must self-insure, self-fund school if applicable, and own transport outright. Budget **OMR 1,200-1,800/month** for a couple in a mid 1BR excluding major travel. ITC marina rentals push higher without employer housing support.
