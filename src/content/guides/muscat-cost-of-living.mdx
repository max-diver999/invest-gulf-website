# Muscat Cost of Living 2026: Rent, Schools, Utilities & Monthly Budgets

**TL;DR:** A family of four on a normal Muscat expat lifestyle spends **OMR 2,200–3,400/month — below an equivalent Dubai tier on rent and schools, not on imported groceries or cars. Singles in Al Hail or Al Khoud can run **OMR 700–1,100 without premium schools or a second car. Schools still break spreadsheets: two children in British mid-tier land **OMR 800–1,200/month once you amortise tuition and add buses. Before you sign a lease, confirm **school seat and bus route — Muscat’s pool is smaller than the UAE, and a cheap villa in the wrong district can cost more in petrol than rent saved.

**Parent hub:** [Oman relocation guide](/guides/oman-relocation-guide/)

> **Disclaimer:** Figures are June 2026 planning ranges, not quotes. ROP visa, property ITC rules and school fees change — verify with employer PRO, school and licensed advisor before relocating.

## How to use this guide

This hub answers **muscat cost of living in spend terms — what leaves your account after rent, not what Al Mouj marina marketing implies.

Copy the budget tables into a spreadsheet. Replace indicative rents with three live listings per area. Amortise school fees: annual tuition ÷ 12, plus bus, uniforms and registration. Schools break budgets more often than groceries.

Two families on the same OMR 3,500 gross can land **OMR 2,400 or **OMR 3,800 monthly spend. Al Mouj marina 3BR plus premium British is not the same universe as Al Khoud villa plus Indian mid-tier. Model both before you negotiate your package.

Oman trades **slower pace and lower density for **smaller luxury stock and stronger local culture integration than UAE or Qatar.

## Currency, tax and why Muscat feels cheaper than Dubai

- **OMR pegged to USD ~**2.597 since 1986 — one of the world’s highest nominal unit values. **OMR 500 is not AED 500.**
- **0% Omani personal income tax on typical employment — UK/US/German/Indian home-country obligations may still apply under CRS/FATCA.
- **Foreign property:** freehold only in **ITC zones (Al Mouj, Muscat Bay, designated tourism complexes; Usfan/Khazaen expansions — verify at publish).
- **Investor residency:** knowledge base cites **~OMR 250,000 threshold — **must verify with ROP before property purchase.
- **School fees:** British/American/Indian tiers typically **below Dubai equivalent — breadth and IB depth are smaller markets.

## Rent by area — Al Mouj, Qurum, MQ, Al Khoud and beyond

Muscat rent is not one market — ITC marina premiums, Qurum convenience, and Al Khoud value villas sit in different universes. Match district to school bus and office commute before you chase the lowest OMR figure.

| Area | Profile | 1BR OMR/mo | 2BR OMR/mo | 3BR / villa OMR/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Al Mouj | Marina ITC, expat families, golf | 450–650 | 550–900 | 900–1,400 |
| **Qurum / Shati Al Qurum | Established, cafes, clinics | 400–600 | 450–750 | 700–1,100 |
| **Madinat Sultan Qaboos (MQ)** | Central, diplomatic belt | 450–700 | 500–800 | 750–1,050 |
| **Al Hail / Al Khoud | Value, commute | 280–450 | 350–550 | 500–800 |
| **Al Amerat | Villa belt, space | rare 1BR | 400–650 | 600–1,000 |
| **Muscat Hills / premium ITC | Waterfront, newer | 500–750 | 650–950 | 1,000–1,600 |
| **Salalah (second city) | Monsoon tourism, port | 250–400 | 300–500 | 450–750 |

Deposits: 1 month common; agency fees vary — negotiate.

Lease registration: municipality compliance required for utilities and some visa steps — follow landlord/PRO guidance.

Furnished vs unfurnished: corporate arrivals often furnished; DIY relocators budget OMR 2,000–6,000 for basics.

## Rent negotiation — Muscat market reality

Oman negotiation is relationship-based, not Dubai auction style.

| Tactic | When it works |

Do not expect Pearl-style bidding wars — Muscat softens when oil-sector rotations slow; still verify school seat before leveraging vacancy.

## FAQ

**Is Muscat cheaper than Dubai for expats?**  
Yes at mid-tier family COL — rent and schools drive the gap. Premium Al Mouj plus IB narrows it fast.

**What is a comfortable salary in Muscat?**  
Single professionals: OMR 1,500+ net for a mid lifestyle. Family of four: OMR 2,800–3,500+ net with British mid schools and Khoud or Qurum rent.

**How much are utilities in Muscat?**  
Apartments OMR 25–65; villas OMR 80–140+ in summer. Verify the meter before you sign the lease.

**Can foreigners buy property in Oman?**  
Generally ITC zones only — verify title and ROP rules before any deposit.

**What is the Oman investor residency amount?**  
Cited around OMR 250,000 — **[VERIFY ROP]** at application time.

**Are Muscat schools cheaper than Dubai?**  
Typically yes at comparable curriculum tier — but breadth and IB depth are smaller markets.

**Do I need a car in Muscat?**  
Yes for almost all families and most professionals.

**Best area for value families?**  
Al Khoud, Al Amerat, and Al Hail for villa space; Qurum for central mid-tier.

**How does COL compare to Qatar?**  
Muscat generally runs lower than Doha on rent and schools. Qatar still wins on nightlife and residency-by-property product depth.

**Where to start relocation planning?**  
[Oman relocation guide](/guides/oman-relocation-guide/) — this COL hub is the spend model; that guide is the visa and first-90-days map.

**Does Muscat have district cooling like Dubai?**  
Rare. Most rentals use split AC on one electricity bill. ITC master developments may add separate community chiller charges.
