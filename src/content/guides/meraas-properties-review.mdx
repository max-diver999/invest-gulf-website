### Bluewaters Island

Location: Off JBR coast, connected by bridge; home to Ain Dubai.

- Beachfront and marina-view apartments
- Limited townhouse stock
- Hospitality and retail at podium level

Bluewaters is waterfront scarcity product. Entry pricing reflects views and island positioning. Short-term rental demand exists, but DET Holiday Home permits and building STR rules require verification before underwriting Airbnb-style revenue.

Yield note: Gross yields commonly sit below Business Bay mid-towers on a pure rent-to-price basis. Occupancy and tenant willingness to pay for waterfront can partially offset, model with Ejari data, not peak-season STR screenshots.

### Practical cost reference

Meraas properties attract the standard 4 % DLD transfer fee plus a trustee charge of 4,000 AED + VAT. Meraas NOC fees sit at 1,000-5,000 AED; City Walk and Bluewaters units are at the higher end due to premium community classification. Service charges in Meraas communities run 20-30 AED per square foot   among the highest in Dubai. Buyers should verify the exact annual charge with Meraas property management before signing the MOU. From listing to title deed, resale timelines in Meraas projects average 5-7 weeks.

## Who is Meraas and why does Dubai Holding matter?

Meraas operates under Dubai Holding, a government-linked group with hospitality, retail, and infrastructure assets. For buyers that backing supports long-horizon master plans (City Walk, Bluewaters, La Mer) with lower insolvency tail risk than small private developers. It does not remove SPA delay clauses or market-cycle pricing risk.

## What buyer profiles fit Meraas stock?

End-users who want walkable boulevards, beach adjacency, and tenant quality over maximum gross yield. Golden Visa applicants counting registered value on premium units. Investors who accept 5-7% gross and higher service charges in exchange for location scarcity.

## What fees apply on Meraas resale (brief)?

Budget standard Dubai buyer costs on resale: about 4% DLD transfer fee on purchase price, trustee fee near AED 4,000 + VAT, agency commission if applicable, and Meraas NOC in the AED 1,000-5,000 band for premium communities. Service charges often run AED 20-30 per sqft in City Walk and Bluewaters, verify with building management before MOU.

## Off-plan SPA points to negotiate

Confirm RERA escrow account, Oqood registration timeline, snagging window, and service charge cap language where available. Delivery databases cite ~91% on-time for Tier 1 peers, still inspect the last handover in the same community.

## When should you skip Meraas?

Skip if you need 8%+ gross yield, if STR is core thesis but the building bans holiday homes, or if you cannot carry premium service charges through a void year. Compare [JVC property investment](/areas/jvc-property-investment/) for yield-first math.

## Invest Gulf notes on Meraas underwriting

Invest Gulf underwrites Meraas with Ejari rents and Mollak service charge history, not launch brochures. Net yield often lands 2-3 points below gross once vacancy and management are included. Request three live comps in the same phase before you waive SPA conditions.

## City Walk and Bluewaters: quick community contrast

City Walk suits buyers who want low-rise urban dining and walkability near Al Wasl. Bluewaters suits waterfront and Ain Dubai adjacency with island scarcity pricing. La Mer adds public beach activation with similar premium charges. Pick community first, tower second: service charge and tenant profile shift block by block even inside the same Meraas masterplan.
