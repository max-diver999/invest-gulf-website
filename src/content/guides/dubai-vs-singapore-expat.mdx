## Tax comparison: the real take-home difference

### Dubai tax structure

Personal income: 0% across all brackets
Additional costs:
- 5% VAT on most goods/services
- No wealth tax, no inheritance tax
- Real estate transfer fees 4% (buyers)

Net effect: Your gross salary IS your net salary (minus voluntary savings)

### Singapore tax structure 2026

Income tax brackets:

| Taxable income (SGD) | Rate | Effective rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First 20,000 | 0% | 0% |
| Next 10,000 | 2% | 0.67% |
| Next 10,000 | 3.5% | 1.83% |
| Next 40,000 | 7% | 4.33% |
| Next 40,000 | 11.5% | 7.42% |
| Next 40,000 | 15% | 9.75% |
| Next 40,000 | 18% | 11.88% |
| Above 200,000 | 22% | Varies |

Additional mandatory:
- CPF contributions: 20% employee + 17% employer
- GST 9% (scheduled increase)

Example: SGD 150,000 salary = ~SGD 18,000 income tax + SGD 30,000 CPF = SGD 102,000 take-home

Dubai equivalent: AED 202,500 gross = AED 202,500 take-home (same purchasing power, 0% deduction)

## Healthcare and insurance

### Dubai healthcare system

Structure: Mix of public (heavily subsidized for nationals) and private (expats)

Insurance: Employer-sponsored mandatory for visa holders
- Typical coverage: AED 150,000-500,000 annually
- Co-pays common: AED 150-300 per visit
- Major hospitals: American Hospital, Mediclinic, NMC

Quality: Excellent private facilities, international standards
Cost: Can be expensive without comprehensive insurance

### Singapore healthcare system

Structure: Subsidized public system (Medisave/Medishield) + private options

For expats:
- Employer insurance typically required
- Access to public hospitals at higher foreigner rates  
- Private healthcare expensive but top-tier 
Quality: Consistently ranks in global top 5 healthcare systems
Public transport: Easy access to medical facilities

Advantage: Singapore system more affordable long-term if PR/citizen path succeeds

## Decision framework: Dubai vs Singapore

## FAQ

**Which has better food, Dubai or Singapore?**  
Singapore wins on authentic Asian cuisine diversity and hawker culture. Dubai offers excellent international dining but at higher price points.

**Are domestic helper costs different?**  
Both cities allow domestic helpers. Singapore has established agency system with regulated contracts. Dubai costs vary more widely based on nationality and experience.

**Which is better for retirees?**  
Dubai Golden Visa offers long-term residency; Singapore PR pathway can lead to citizenship. Both have excellent healthcare. Climate preference matters significantly.

**Can I run a business in both?**  
Dubai free zones offer 100% foreign ownership with easier setup. Singapore has stronger regulatory environment but more compliance requirements.

**Which has better shopping?**  
Dubai Mall and Mall of Emirates vs Orchard Road, both top-tier. Dubai has more luxury brands and gold trading. Singapore better for electronics and regional goods.

**Are both cities family-friendly?**  
Yes, both design significant infrastructure around families. Dubai offers more space; Singapore offers better public amenities.

**Which is better for single professionals?**  
Dubai Marina/Downtown vs Singapore CBD, both offer lively single expat communities. Singapore edges on urban walkability; Dubai on lifestyle variety.
