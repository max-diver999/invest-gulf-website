Dubai's mortgage fee structure is layered, and many buyers are surprised by the total cash required at DLD registration beyond just their down payment. The 0.25% DLD mortgage registration fee is the headline number, but the complete picture includes bank valuation, processing charges, insurance requirements, and the simultaneous 4% property transfer fee. Budgeting correctly prevents last-minute shortfalls at the Registration Trustee Center.

## What is the DLD mortgage registration fee in Dubai?

The Dubai Land Department charges **0.25% of the registered loan amount** when a bank registers its mortgage against your title. On a AED 1,600,000 loan that is **AED 4,000**, plus small ancillary DLD lines (map fee AED 250, knowledge and innovation fees AED 10 each, admin about AED 290). The borrower pays this at the **Registration Trustee Center** on transfer day. It is separate from the **4% transfer fee** on the purchase price.

| Fee item | Amount | Payer | When paid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DLD property transfer fee | 4% of purchase price | Buyer | Trustee Center (transfer day) |
| DLD mortgage registration | 0.25% of loan | Buyer | Trustee Center (transfer day) |
| DLD map fee | AED 250 | Buyer | Trustee Center |
| Knowledge/innovation fee | AED 20 | Buyer | Trustee Center |
| Registration Trustee fee | AED 4,000 + VAT | Buyer | Trustee Center |
| Bank valuation fee | AED 2,500 to 5,000 | Buyer | Before approval |
| Bank processing fee | AED 1,000 to 3,000 | Buyer | At approval |
| Life insurance (annual) | 0.3 to 0.5% of loan | Buyer | Ongoing annually |

## How does the 0.25% mortgage fee differ from the 4% transfer fee?

The **4% fee** is calculated on **property value**. The **0.25% fee** is calculated on **loan principal** only. On a AED 2,000,000 purchase with AED 1,600,000 financing, expect roughly AED 80,000 transfer plus AED 4,000 mortgage registration before trustee and bank lines. Neither fee can be added to the mortgage principal under standard UAE Central Bank LTV rules. See [UAE Central Bank mortgage rules](/guides/uae-central-bank-mortgage-rules/) for current LTV caps.

## What does a full mortgaged purchase cost in cash?

Use a **two-bucket budget**: pre-trustee bank costs, then trustee-day DLD and registration.

**Before transfer day (bank and professional fees):**

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Bank valuation fee | AED 3,500 |
| Bank processing fee | AED 2,500 |
| Broker commission (2% + VAT) | AED 42,000 |
| Independent legal review (optional) | AED 7,500 |
| Pre-transfer subtotal | ~AED 55,500 |

**Example: AED 2,000,000 property, 80% LTV (AED 1,600,000 loan), ready unit**

| Stage | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Down payment (20%) | AED 400,000 |
| DLD transfer (4%) | AED 80,000 |
| DLD mortgage registration (0.25%) | AED 4,000 |
| DLD ancillary + trustee (typical) | ~AED 4,520 |
| Bank valuation + processing | ~AED 6,000 |
| Broker (2% + VAT) | AED 42,000 |
| **Total cash above loan** | **~AED 536,520** |

Total cash above property price in this illustration: about **7.2%** of purchase price, before life insurance and annual building cover.

## How do Dubai mortgage registration costs compare across the Gulf?

| Emirate / market | Transfer fee | Mortgage registration | Combined acquisition extra (indicative) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dubai | 4% DLD | 0.25% on loan | ~6.5 to 7.5% total |
| Abu Dhabi | 2% DMT | 0.25% on loan (DMT) | ~4.5 to 5.5% total |
| RAK | Varies 2 to 4% | Typically 0.25% | ~4.5 to 6.5% total |
| Qatar | 0% (foreign lease) | N/A | Lower barrier |
| Bahrain | 2% | Small registration | ~4% total |
| Saudi (new law) | 5% stamp duty | N/A currently | 5%+ |

Abu Dhabi's **2% DMT** versus Dubai's **4% DLD** is the most meaningful UAE comparison for investors. On AED 2,000,000, transfer fee savings alone can exceed the entire mortgage registration fee on a typical loan.

## What does mortgage discharge cost on sale or refinance?

| Discharge fee item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Early settlement fee (current bank) | 1% of outstanding balance (min AED 10,000 at some banks) |
| DLD mortgage discharge | AED 1,000 to 1,500 |
| Liability letter (from bank) | AED 500 to 1,500 |
| Bank processing | AED 500 to 1,000 |

Discharge at DLD must complete before a new buyer's name or a new lender's charge registers. Allow **1 to 3 business days**. Refinancing often adds **AED 5,000 to 20,000** all-in depending on lock-in and loan size. Full walkthrough: [Refinancing Dubai property](/guides/refinance-property-dubai-guide/).

**Refinancing illustration:** AED 1,320,000 outstanding, exit costs ~AED 22,300, new registration and bank fees ~AED 9,300, combined ~AED 31,600. Monthly saving AED 687 implies breakeven near **46 months** if the rate gap holds. Refinancing only makes sense if you expect to hold the loan **4+ years** beyond the refinance date.

## What bank fees and insurance apply beyond DLD?

Life insurance on the loan is typically **0.3 to 0.5% of outstanding balance per year** on reducing-balance policies. On AED 1,600,000 at 0.4%, that is **AED 6,400/year**. Building insurance is separate, roughly **AED 1,500 to 3,000/year** for a standard apartment, and most lenders require it.

Year 1 mandatory ongoing costs to model for an investment unit:

- Life insurance on AED 1,600,000 loan (~0.4%): AED 6,400/year
- Property insurance (building): approximately AED 1,500 to 3,000/year

Add these to **net yield**, not gross rent marketing.

## When is the 0.25% fee paid on off-plan handover?

On **Oqood** registration at booking, DLD charges **4%** on the contract value plus admin. At **handover**, when the bank registers the mortgage on the Title Deed, the **0.25%** applies to the loan amount again with admin lines. Confirm with your bank whether valuation and processing repeat at handover.

| Stage | Fee | Typical amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oqood registration | DLD transfer | 4% |
| Oqood admin | DLD | AED 580 |
| Mortgage at handover | 0.25% + admin | Per loan |
| Title Deed conversion | DLD | AED 250 |

## What DLD fees apply when refinancing only?

| Fee | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Discharge of existing mortgage | 0.25% of discharged amount |
| Discharge admin fee | AED 290 |
| New mortgage registration | 0.25% of new mortgage |
| New mortgage admin fee | AED 290 |
| Bank valuation (new bank) | AED 2,500 to 3,500 |

Compare against [Cash vs mortgage for Dubai property](/guides/cash-vs-mortgage-dubai-property/) if you are deciding whether leverage is worth the fee stack at current rates.

## Can you negotiate bank fees?

Bank fees are more flexible than DLD statutory fees.

| Bank type | Typical negotiation |
| --- | --- |
| ADCB / FAB / ENBD | Processing waivers for salary transfer or deposits above AED 50K |
| HSBC / Citi / Standard Chartered | Rate focus more than fee waivers |
| Mashreq / RAKBank | Often flexible on processing for high LTV files |

Submit to **3 to 4 banks** and use competing offers. Q4 and Q1 promotions often include reduced processing. Mortgage brokers with volume sometimes beat direct branch quotes.

## What should you verify before trustee day?

**Pre-approval stage**

- Request complete fee schedule in writing
- Confirm life insurance provider and premium calculation
- Check early settlement penalty structure
- Understand variable rate adjustment mechanics

**Transaction stage**

- Reconfirm fees have not changed since pre-approval
- Verify DLD calculation bases (price vs loan)
- Confirm certified funds format (manager's cheques, drafts)
- Understand post-closing payment account setup

**Post-closing**

- Mortgage charge appears correctly on Title Deed
- Life insurance policy issued
- Annual statement and payment procedures documented

DLD fee rates reflect **Q1 2026** schedules; verify on the DLD portal at transaction date. Bank fees vary by institution and promotion. This guide is information only, not financial advice.

## Invest Gulf: what we see on mortgaged closings

Invest Gulf tracks Gulf buyer files where **trustee-day cash shortfalls** (missing 4% + 0.25% + trustee lines) cause delayed registration more often than rate negotiation failures. The fix is a **single closing spreadsheet** built from the bank's cost illustration plus DLD's published fee list, reviewed **5 business days** before the trustee appointment. Buyers who wire only the down payment and forget statutory fees lose the slot and sometimes the rate lock.
