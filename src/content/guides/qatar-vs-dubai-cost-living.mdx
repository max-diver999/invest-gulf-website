# Qatar vs Dubai Cost of Living 2026: Rent, Schools, Cars & Groceries Compared

**TL;DR:** At **expat mid-tier** — comparable 2BR, two kids in British mid-tier schools, one car — **Qatar and Dubai land in the same band**: about **QAR/AED 22,000–30,000/month** all-in (treat peg as **1:1**). **Dubai looks cheaper on rent** if you accept **Sharjah/Ajman commutes** (AED 4,500–7,000 2BR vs Al Wakrah QAR 6,000–9,000 without cross-emirate school logistics). **Qatar wins on tolls** (no Salik) and **district cooling** (rarer). **School fees** match at premium tiers. ## Currency pegs — why QAR and AED move together

Both currencies ride the USD peg — for household budgeting you can treat **1 AED ≈ 1 QAR** unless you are modelling FX home-country exposure.

| Currency | USD peg (official) | Practical impact |
|---|---|---|
| **QAR** | ~3.64 per USD | Salary quoted in QAR = stable vs USD earners |
| **AED** | ~3.6725 per USD | Same for AED packages |
| **Cross rate** | 1 AED ≈ 0.991 QAR | Treat as **1:1 for budgeting** |

**EUR, GBP, RUB earners:** both cities bill in local pegged currency. Home-currency volatility affects **real purchasing power** equally — neither city "protects" you from EUR weakness.

**Remittance:** both cities charge 0.5–2% FX spread on transfers home. Budget identically.

## Utilities — summer AC spike comparison

| Utility | Doha (Kahramaa) | Dubai (DEWA + cooling) |
|---|---|---|
| 1BR apartment monthly | QAR 200–450 summer | AED 250–500 + cooling AED 200–400 |
| 2BR apartment monthly | QAR 350–700 summer | AED 400–800 + cooling AED 300–600 |
| 3BR / villa monthly | QAR 600–1,500 summer | AED 800–2,000 + cooling AED 500–1,200 |
| Water | Included in Kahramaa | Included in DEWA |
| District cooling | Rare separate | **Common** — can exceed DEWA in summer |
| Connection deposit | QAR 2,000 ref. | AED 2,000 ref. |

**Dubai district cooling trap:** many Marina/Downtown towers bill **DEWA + Empower/Tabreed separately**. Summer **AED 800–1,500** combined is normal for 2BR — always request **12-month utility history** before signing a lease.

**Qatar Kahramaa:** single bill, simpler — but **villa AC** still spikes. Budget **QAR 1,000+** July–September for large glazed units.

## FAQ

**Is Qatar cheaper than Dubai overall?** At **mid-tier expat standard**, **no meaningful difference** — typically within **5%**. Dubai can be cheaper on **rent only** via Sharjah; Qatar can be cheaper on **tolls and cooling**.

**Which city is cheaper for a family of four?** **Tie** at QAR/AED 28,000–30,000/month for British mid schools + 2BR in comparable districts. **School tier** and **rent district** dominate.

**Is The Pearl cheaper than Dubai Marina?** **Similar** — QAR/AED 12,000–18,000 for 2BR both. Pearl **service charges** on purchase are separate from rent comparison.

**Are groceries cheaper in Dubai?** **Slightly** — wider competition and more discount retail. Difference **~5–8%** on equivalent basket, not 30%.

**Is car ownership cheaper in Qatar?** **Marginally** — no Salik saves **QAR 150–350/month** per car. Insurance and fuel **similar**.

**Do Dubai schools cost more?** **Premium IB DP** can run **AED 5,000–10,000/year higher** per child at top tier. **British mid** and **CBSE** track closely.

**Should I compare gross salary or net?** **Net in local currency** after any deductions. Then model **identical lifestyle tier** — not Pearl vs Sharjah.

**Where does Dubai get expensive fast?** **District cooling**, **Salik**, **leisure/restaurants**, **premium school top tier**.

**Where does Qatar get expensive fast?** **Pearl rent + IB schools + second car** — same stack as Dubai premium.
