## FAQ

Is Qatar cheaper than Dubai overall? At mid-tier expat standard, no meaningful difference, typically within **5%**. Dubai can be cheaper on **rent only** via Sharjah; Qatar can be cheaper on tolls and cooling.

Which city is cheaper for a family of four? **Tie** at QAR/AED 28,000–30,000/month for British mid schools + 2BR in comparable districts. School tier and rent district dominate.

Is The Pearl cheaper than Dubai Marina? **Similar**, QAR/AED 12,000–18,000 for 2BR both. Pearl service charges on purchase are separate from rent comparison.

Are groceries cheaper in Dubai? **Slightly**, wider competition and more discount retail. Difference **~5–8%** on equivalent basket, not 30%.

Is car ownership cheaper in Qatar? Marginally, no Salik saves QAR 150–350/month per car. Insurance and fuel **similar**.

Do Dubai schools cost more? Premium IB DP can run AED 5,000–10,000/year higher per child at top tier. British mid and **CBSE** track closely.

Should I compare gross salary or net? Net in local currency after any deductions. Then model identical lifestyle tier, not Pearl vs Sharjah.

Where does Dubai get expensive fast? District cooling, **Salik**, leisure/restaurants, premium school top tier.

Where does Qatar get expensive fast? Pearl rent + IB schools + second car, same stack as Dubai premium.
