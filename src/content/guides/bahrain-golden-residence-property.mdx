Bahrain Golden Residence is an **NPRA investor residency track** — not a job visa, not automatic with any apartment purchase, and not interchangeable with **LMRA work permits**. Public sources cite **BHD 200,000** qualifying investment [VERIFY NPRA], with November 2025 press reports mentioning a possible reduction toward **BHD 130,000** [VERIFY goldenresidency.gov.bh at application time].

Property in **Bahrain freehold zones** — Seef, Amwaj Islands, Diyar Al Muharraq, Juffair — may form part of qualifying investment **only if** NPRA asset rules explicitly include it [VERIFY]. This guide covers the property pathway, zone map, costs, yields, and comparison to Dubai Golden Visa.

**Quick answer:** BHD 200K [VERIFY NPRA] — not auto on purchase. Freehold zones only. Gross yields 5–7%. Acquisition 3–5%. Thinner liquidity than Dubai.

## Golden Residence vs property purchase: two processes

| Process | Authority | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Property purchase | Notary + LMRA property register | Title deed (freehold if eligible) |
| Golden Residence | NPRA [VERIFY] | Long-stay residency permit |
| Work permit | LMRA | Employment authorisation |

**Purchase does not equal Golden Residence.** Qualifying investment threshold, asset category, and NPRA approval are separate gates.

Bahrain Golden Residence property suits buyers who:

- Want **GCC residency** at **~USD 530K capital** [VERIFY NPRA]
- Value **lower cost of living** than Dubai or Qatar
- Target **5–7% gross yields** in a compact market
- Work in or near **Bahrain financial sector**
- Can **hold 3–5 years** through moderate liquidity
