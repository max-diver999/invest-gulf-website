Bahrain Golden Residence is an NPRA investor residency track, not a job visa, not automatic with any apartment purchase, and not interchangeable with LMRA work permits. Public sources cite BHD 200,000 qualifying investment (confirm current official rules), with November 2025 press reports mentioning a possible reduction toward BHD 130,000 (confirm current official rules).

Property in Bahrain freehold zones, Seef, Amwaj Islands, Diyar Al Muharraq, Juffair, may form part of qualifying investment **only if** NPRA asset rules explicitly include it (confirm current official rules). This guide covers the property pathway, zone map, costs, yields, and comparison to Dubai Golden Visa.
