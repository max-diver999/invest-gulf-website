Bahrain Golden Residence is an NPRA investor residency track, not a job visa, not automatic with any apartment purchase, and not interchangeable with LMRA work permits. Public sources cite **BHD 200,000** qualifying investment (confirm current official rules), with November 2025 press reports mentioning a possible reduction toward **BHD 130,000** (confirm current official rules).

Property in Bahrain freehold zones, Seef, Amwaj Islands, Diyar Al Muharraq, Juffair, may form part of qualifying investment **only if** NPRA asset rules explicitly include it (confirm current official rules). This guide covers the property pathway, zone map, costs, yields, and comparison to Dubai Golden Visa.

**Quick answer:** BHD 200K (confirm current official rules), not auto on purchase. Freehold zones only. Gross yields 5–7%. Acquisition 3–5%. Thinner liquidity than Dubai.

## Bahrain property legal framework for foreign investors

Freehold Act 2006 established foreign ownership rights in designated areas, not blanket permission across all Bahrain real estate. Ministry of Justice maintains the official freehold register, while **LMRA** handles property ownership permits separate from residency applications.

| Legal aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Ownership structure | Individual name or company, verify NPRA rules on corporate ownership |
| Title registration | Notary Public + Ministry of Justice, Arabic language deed |
| **Property tax** | None on residential, verify commercial rates if applicable |
| Transfer restrictions | None on subsequent sale to qualifying buyers |
| **Inheritance** | Follows home country law if registered properly |

Due diligence essentials: Verify seller's clear title, check for mortgages or liens, confirm freehold zone status with Ministry of Justice, and ensure no pending litigation against the property through Bahrain court records.
