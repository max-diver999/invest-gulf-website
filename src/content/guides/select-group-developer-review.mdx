## Risk Assessment: Select Group-Specific Considerations

### Building-level risks in Marina towers

**Aging infrastructure** in older Select Group buildings:

**Marina Gate towers** (2008-2012 handover):
- **Facade maintenance**: Glass curtain wall systems requiring 7-10 year maintenance cycles
- **MEP updates**: Air conditioning systems, fire safety upgrades, elevator modernization
- **Waterproofing**: Balcony and exterior waterproofing renewal needed every 5-8 years

**Peninsula towers** (2013-2016 handover):
- **Service charge volatility**: Newer buildings face higher-than-expected operational costs as buildings age
- **Special assessments**: Owners' Association may levy additional charges for major repairs

### Market cycle risk factors

**Marina oversupply risk**: Adjacent new developments adding 3,000+ units 2026-2027 include:
- Dubai Harbour Phase I completions
- Marina Gate extensions
- JBR-adjacent towers from other developers

**Impact on Select Group stock**: Established buildings with proven Ejari history generally weather new supply better than newer handovers, but rental growth may stagnate.

**Mitigation strategies**:
- Buy in buildings with under 3-year average tenancy length (lower turnover)
- Focus on unique unit features (corner units, upgraded views, larger layouts)
- Budget for building improvement capex to compete with new handovers

## Due Diligence: Select Group Building Assessment

### Pre-purchase building health evaluation

**Financial due diligence checklist** for Select Group completed buildings:

1. **Owners' Association financials**: Request 24-month P&L and balance sheet
2. **Service charge history**: Analyze annual increases and special assessment frequency
3. **Sinking fund adequacy**: Confirm reserve funds for planned maintenance (10-year outlook)
4. **Outstanding maintenance**: Review OA meeting minutes for deferred maintenance items
5. **Building occupancy**: Confirm owner-occupied vs. investor-owned ratio
6. **STR compliance**: Verify building STR policy and DET permit allowance

### Physical inspection priorities

**Unit-level inspection** for Select Group resale purchases:

| Inspection area | What to check | Red flags |
|---|---|---|
| MEP systems | AC performance, electrical panel condition, plumbing flow | Unusual noises, poor cooling, water pressure issues |
| Interior finishes | Tile condition, paint quality, fixture operation | Cracks, stains, non-functional fixtures |
| Views and exposure | Actual vs. marketed view, future obstruction risk | Permanent construction blocking views |
| Balcony/exterior | Railing safety, waterproofing, structural condition | Rust, cracks, water damage |

**Building common area inspection**:
- **Lobby and corridors**: Maintenance level, cleanliness, security systems
- **Amenities**: Pool condition, gym equipment functionality, landscaping
- **Parking**: Space allocation, covered parking availability, security
- **MEP infrastructure**: Generator backup, fire safety systems, elevator condition
