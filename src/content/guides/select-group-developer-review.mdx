Select Group is a Dubai Marina institution, among the developers who built the waterfront skyline when Marina was still a construction site, not the world's most photographed rental corridor.

Unlike Azizi or Danube pushing volume in JVC, Select Group's identity is established waterfront: Marina Gate, Peninsula, and JBR-adjacent towers with daily secondary-market trading and Ejari rental depth. That legacy matters because you underwrite completed Select Group stock against years of transacted data, not launch brochure projections.

## How does Select Group rank among Dubai developers in 2026?

Select Group sits in upper Tier 2 with roughly 84% on-time delivery in industry databases, credible for off-plan pipeline but strongest on completed Marina and JBR towers where Ejari history is deep. The brand is not Emaar-scale master-plan volume and not Omniyat-scale ultra-luxury landmarks. It is a boutique-premium waterfront specialist with proven secondary liquidity on flagship stock.

Gulf and foreign buyers typically choose Select Group when they want a walkable marina address with bankable resale comparables, not when they need maximum gross yield or a peripheral growth corridor bet.

| Emaar | Tier 1 master-plans | ~95% industry reference | Liquidity + master-plan depth |

| Omniyat | Ultra-luxury | Project-specific | Trophy landmarks |

## What yield should you expect on Marina Select Group stock?

Waterfront pricing compresses yield percentages even when occupancy is strong. Model net income after service charges, management, vacancy, and maintenance, not gross marketing alone.

Illustrative Marina one-bedroom Select Group unit:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Purchase | AED 1,100,000 |
| Annual rent | AED 72,000 |
| Gross yield | 6.5% |
| Service charges | AED 22,000 |
| Net before vacancy | ~4.5% |

4.5% net in Marina with strong occupancy and liquidity is a fair outcome for a prestige address, not a failure. Compare against JVC net yields above 6% with a different tenant pool and thinner trophy resale story.

## What should you verify before an off-plan Select Group purchase?

Off-plan is workable with standard RERA discipline, but Select Group's reputation rests on delivered Marina stock. Treat any new pipeline phase as a standalone underwriting file.

Verify before you sign an SPA:
1. Last Select Group handover on a comparable tower: SPA date vs key collection
2. Construction site visit: facade, MEP, amenity completion
3. Broker months-on-market for completed Select Group units in the target building
4. Escrow account letter and Trakheesi permit in writing

Typical payment plan shape:

| Phase | Typical structure |
|---|---|
| Booking | 10-20% |
| Construction | 50-60% |
| Handover | 20-30% |

SPA scrutiny points: delay penalty caps, service charge estimates (Marina towers often AED 18-28/sqft), assignment and NOC fees if you plan a pre-handover exit, short-let bylaws, and snagging timelines versus prior Select Group handovers. ## Where is secondary-market liquidity strongest?

Liquidity ranking within the Select Group portfolio:
1. Marina Gate completed phases (dual-tower, deep Ejari history)
2. Marina Promenade-era buildings (established vintage)
3. JBR corridor completed stock (walk lifestyle premium)
4. Peninsula completed phases (Business Bay adjacency)
5. Off-plan pipeline (thinner assignment market)
6. Branded ultra-premium pipeline (event-driven sales)

Practical signals: Marina Select Group stock often sells in 45-90 days when priced to Ejari; UAE banks actively lend on established Select Group buildings; short-let demand exists but building bylaws vary. Investors needing exit within three to five years should favour completed Marina Gate or Promenade stock over launch-premium off-plan.

## Who should buy Select Group property?

**Strong fit:** investors wanting established Marina/JBR waterfront with secondary-market comparables; end-users prioritising walkable marina lifestyle; capital-preservation buyers accepting compressed yield for location liquidity.

**Consider alternatives if:** maximum gross yield (JVC volume developers); deepest master-plan liquidity (Emaar Dubai Hills); ultra-luxury landmark (Omniyat); Palm scarcity (Nakheel); aggressive off-plan payment-plan marketing (Danube or DAMAC).

## What are the main risks?

- Premium Marina pricing compresses gross yield to roughly 5-7%
- High service charges on waterfront towers (AED 18-28/sqft on many buildings)
- Older Promenade-era stock needs maintenance reserves and OA health checks
- Thinner off-plan pipeline than volume developers
- Marina cycle risk when adjacent new handovers pressure rents
- Short-let complexity: DTCM permits and building bylaws vary by tower

If STR is the thesis, confirm **DET, OA, and DTCM pathway** before purchase; see [short-term rental Dubai license](/guides/short-term-rental-dubai-license/). Long-let Ejari remains the default underwriting for Marina Select Group stock in 2026.

## What does a pre-sign checklist look like?

1. Trakheesi registration on any off-plan phase
2. Escrow account verified on Dubai REST for off-plan
3. Ejari rental history: 24 months on identical layout in the target building
4. Mollak service charges: OA budget actuals, not developer estimates
5. Building bylaws if income strategy includes STR
6. OA financial health: special assessments and reserve fund status
7. Mortgage valuation if financing completed stock
8. SPA delay clause benchmarked to prior Select Group handovers
9. Comparable pricing against adjacent Marina towers, not brand premium alone
10. DTCM permit pathway if short-let planned

### Practical cost reference (Dubai purchases)

Ready and secondary buyers typically budget **4% DLD transfer** plus trustee fees (often around AED 4,000 + VAT), agent commission on resale, and developer NOC fees commonly AED 1,000-3,000 on resale. Off-plan registration runs through **Oqood at 2%** (minimum AED 5,000). Select Group has been operational since 2002 with 12+ completed towers; verify individual handover dates against the SPA because some older phases slipped 6-18 months. Average resale timeline on priced-to-market Marina stock is often 4-6 weeks.

## How should you underwrite Promenade-era building vintage?

Older Marina Promenade stock can deliver strong Ejari rents but needs reserve budgeting for facade, lifts, and chiller replacements. Request the last three years of Mollak statements, not only the current year budget. Compare special assessment history against peer towers on the same promenade. A well-maintained 2008 vintage tower with refreshed MEP often outperforms a 2018 handover with an under-funded owners association.

Walk the parking and podium levels on inspection day. Water ingress, poorly maintained landscaping, and elevator downtime show up in tenant renewal rates before they show up in broker marketing. If the OA cannot produce audited accounts within five business days, treat that as a pricing discount or walk away.

## Can you finance completed Select Group stock as a foreign buyer?

UAE banks lend on established Select Group towers when Ejari comparables support valuation. Non-residents typically see 60-75% LTV on ready units; residents often reach 80% with salary transfer. Budget 4% DLD transfer, trustee fees, agent commission on resale, and 0.25% mortgage registration on the loan amount. If net yield after debt service is the decision metric, model vacancy and service charges before you compare against unlevered JVC plays.

Emirates NBD, ADCB, and Mashreq actively finance Marina stock with deep comparables on Marina Gate and Promenade-era buildings. Processing often runs three to five weeks when valuation and NOC timelines align. Islamic finance structures (Murabaha/Ijara) are available at similar pricing bands for buyers who prefer Sharia-compliant documentation.

## What does Marina Gate teach about Select Group resale?

Marina Gate is the liquidity anchor of the portfolio because dual-tower density creates continuous Ejari sampling. When underwriting any Select Group resale, pull Marina Gate transacted rents for the same bedroom count and floor band, then adjust for view, parking, and OA health on your target building. Investors who price 8-12% above that band without a verifiable upgrade story often sit 120+ days on market even in active cycles.

Branded ultra-premium phases trade more like trophy assets: fewer buyers, longer diligence, higher service charges. Treat them as capital-preservation or end-user purchases unless you have a specific STR or corporate lease channel already contracted.
