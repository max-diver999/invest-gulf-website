    answer: "International school fees in Doha range from QAR 25,000-40,000/year for Indian CBSE schools to QAR 95,000+/year for premium IB diploma programmes. British schools typically cost QAR 45,000-85,000/year, with additional costs for registration (QAR 2,000-10,000), transport (QAR 8,000-14,000), and exam fees."
  - question: "Can expat families choose any international school in Doha?"
    answer: "Yes, but most schools have waiting lists and require applications 6-12 months before the September start. Top British and IB schools fill fast, so families typically apply to multiple schools across different tiers for backup options."
  - question: "Which curriculum is best for international families in Doha?"
    answer: "It depends on your university destination. British IGCSE/A-Level works for UK universities, IB is best for global mobility, American suits US college tracks, and Indian CBSE offers value at QAR 25,000-40,000/year. Choose based on where your child will apply to university at 18."
  - question: "Do I need to live near my child's school in Doha?"
    answer: "Highly recommended. School-run traffic between 6:45-7:45 AM is severe, and a 25-minute Sunday morning commute becomes 45+ minutes during term time. Many families choose housing within 25-30 minutes of their selected school."
  - question: "Are Arabic classes mandatory in Doha international schools?"
    answer: "Yes, Arabic instruction is required by Qatar's Ministry of Education, typically 3-5 periods per week. Most schools offer beginner streams for non-Arabic speakers, but plan for additional homework and consider private tutoring at QAR 150-300/hour if needed."
  - question: "Can children switch between curricula mid-stream in Doha?"
    answer: "Possible but challenging, especially after Year 8. Switching from CBSE to British is manageable until Year 7, but moving from any curriculum to IB DP at 17 is academically difficult. Plan your curriculum pathway from the start."
