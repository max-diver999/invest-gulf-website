German tax residency does not end the day your flight to Dubai departs. Unlimited liability (unbeschränkte Steuerpflicht) follows Wohnsitz and habitual abode tests, exit tax can crystallise on company shares, and the Außensteuergesetz can extend German taxing rights for years if domestic income or ties remain. Dubai's zero personal income tax makes the UAE attractive, but it also makes German anti-abuse rules salient for auditors.

This guide outlines the main departure steps for German residents relocating to Dubai. It is not Steuerberatung; engage a qualified adviser before Abmeldung, share sales, or property purchases.

## Why is Germany's tax exit harder than a simple move abroad?

Germany's tax exit is harder than a simple move abroad because residents face worldwide taxation plus exit rules designed for low-tax destinations such as the UAE's 0% personal income tax. Our analysis flags Wohnsitz availability, Wegzugsbesteuerung on shares, and up to 10 years of extended liability when German-source income continues after Dubai relocation.

German residents relocating to Dubai typically need a sequenced exit plan rather than a one-way flight and UAE visa stamp. Unlimited tax liability ends only after Wohnsitz and habitual abode tests clear, while Wegzugsbesteuerung under Section 6 Außensteuergesetz can crystallise a deemed disposal of substantial shareholdings in the departure year. Moves to Dubai as a non-EU destination generally make the German exit-tax bill due immediately, unlike some EU or EEA instalment paths. Extended unlimited liability may still reach German-source rents, business profits, dividends, and certain pensions for up to 10 years if domestic income continues. Invest Gulf underwriting therefore models Abmeldung timing, German dwelling availability, and liquidity for exit tax across a full 12-month pre-move window before any Dubai freehold SPA is signed.

Insider tip: model Wegzugsbesteuerung cash needs before you book Dubai viewings if you hold GmbH or AG stakes above participation thresholds.

## When does German unlimited tax liability actually end?

German unlimited tax liability typically ends only when Wohnsitz and gewöhnlicher Aufenthalt tests are cleared under the Income Tax Act, not on the departure flight day. Invest Gulf client files show Abmeldung plus removing personal use of German homes, then 6 months-plus UAE presence evidence, as the practical sequence foreign buyers still skip.

**Wohnsitz:** A dwelling available to you (owned or rented) where you maintain meaningful presence. You need not live there full time; availability can suffice.

**Gewöhnlicher Aufenthalt:** More than six months continuous stay in Germany. Genuine relocation to Dubai breaks this once absence is real.

Practical exit steps:

1. File Abmeldung with the Einwohnermeldebehörde (necessary but not sufficient alone).
2. Remove personal use of German homes: sell, or lease to third parties without retained use rights.
3. Build UAE residence evidence: visa, Emirates ID, lease or title, utility and banking footprint.
4. Update German banks and your Steuerberater on declared residence.

The Finanzamt may request proof that departure is genuine; keeping a spare room with family can recreate Wohnsitz risk.

## What is Wegzugsbesteuerung and when does it hit Dubai relocations?

Wegzugsbesteuerung means a deemed disposal of qualifying shareholdings at fair market value under Section 6 Außensteuergesetz when unlimited liability ends. Dubai as a non-EU destination typically requires the German bill immediately rather than EU instalment relief, so our research insists on liquidity planning 12 months before Abmeldung.

- Confirm current 1% or 10% style participation thresholds with a Steuerberater
- Model cash for an immediate non-EU exit-tax bill in the departure year
- Avoid restructuring only after the UAE visa is already stamped

## How long can Germany tax you after you leave?

Germany can tax former residents for up to 10 years under extended unlimited liability if prior residency tests are met, the move is to a low-tax country, and domestic German income remains. Invest Gulf scenarios often see rental profits, GmbH dividends, and pensions keeping Finanzamt reach even after years in Dubai.

- Domestic rents and business profits stay in scope during the window
- GmbH dividends and certain pensions often extend Finanzamt reach
- Mitigation usually needs a 6 to 12 month pre-exit restructure plan

Limited liability (beschränkte Steuerpflicht) may still apply to other German-source items under separate rules.

## Do German CFC rules reach UAE free zone companies?

German CFC rules can reach UAE free zone companies when Hinzurechnungsbesteuerung attributes passive rents, interest, royalties, or dividends taxed below German benchmarks. UAE corporate tax at 9% from June 2023 and Qualifying Free Zone Person rules change numbers but do not remove scrutiny in our analysis for foreign buyers holding shells.

## What if you keep German real estate after moving to Dubai?

Keeping German real estate after moving to Dubai typically leaves rental income taxable in Germany under limited or extended liability, while retained personal use can recreate Wohnsitz risk. Invest Gulf clean-exit playbooks prefer unrelated tenants, zero owner access, and documented Abmeldung within the first 30 to 90 days abroad.

- Let to unrelated tenants and remove owner access rights
- File Abmeldung within the first 30 to 90 days abroad
- Keep German return reporting if domestic rents continue

## How should UAE residency fit the German exit timeline?

UAE residency should fit the German exit timeline because a visa, Emirates ID, lease or title, and consistent CRS certifications typically prove centre of life after Abmeldung. Golden Visa via roughly AED 2 million registered property can support long-term evidence, yet our research stresses it does not alone end German liability.

- Visa + Emirates ID + lease or title as centre-of-life evidence
- CRS self-certifications aligned to the UAE filing position
- Golden Visa near AED 2 million is evidence, not a German exit

Obtain ICA/GDRFA registration and a credible UAE footprint via lease or [property purchase](/guides/dubai-property-for-german-buyers/), then see [Golden Visa and mortgage property](/guides/golden-visa-mortgage-property-uae/).

## Does buying Dubai property create German tax issues?

Buying Dubai property does not create automatic German tax relief while you remain a German resident under worldwide income rules. Once non-resident, UAE-source rent is generally allocated by treaty, and we still route foreign buyers to local cost guides for DLD-style fees around 4% of purchase price.

- Worldwide income rules apply until Wohnsitz exit is complete
- Treaty allocation matters only after genuine non-residence

## What timeline should you use for a coordinated exit?

A coordinated German-Dubai exit timeline typically runs 12-plus months for exit-tax modelling, 6 months for Abmeldung and visa prep, then a departure month for Wohnsitz closure and first-year German return filing. Our analysis warns foreign buyers that rushing a Dubai SPA before liquidity for Wegzugsbesteuerung can strain cash in the same tax year.

| Phase | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| 12+ months before | Exit tax modelling, share restructuring, German property plan |
| 6 months before | Abmeldung preparation, UAE visa strategy, bank updates |
| Departure month | File Abmeldung, close Wohnsitz, document UAE arrival |
| First UAE year | German return for split year, CRS consistency, Steuerberater follow-up |

Practical German-Dubai coordination usually spans four distinct phases with hard calendar dates attached. Twelve-plus months before departure cover exit-tax modelling and share restructuring; six months before focus Abmeldung prep, UAE visa strategy, and bank residency updates; the departure month files Abmeldung and documents UAE arrival; the first UAE year completes a split-year German return and CRS consistency checks. Golden Visa evidence commonly references about AED 2 million registered property value, yet that visa alone does not terminate German unlimited liability. UAE corporate tax at 9% from June 2023 may alter CFC arithmetic for free zone entities without removing German attribution risk. Our analysis treats mismatched CRS self-certifications within the first 12 months as a common audit trigger for foreign buyers who keep German accounts open.

## How do CRS and bank reporting interact?

CRS and bank reporting interact because UAE banks report to Germany when you certify German residence, and German banks report UAE-linked accounts when you certify UAE residence. Invest Gulf compliance notes treat mismatched self-certifications as enquiry triggers within the first 12 months after relocation for foreign buyers who keep dual banking footprints.

- Align UAE and German bank residency certifications in the same month
- Expect CRS mismatches to surface within about 12 months
- Keep centre-of-life facts consistent with both self-certifications

## What checklist should you run before committing to Dubai?

A pre-commitment checklist for Dubai should include a Steuerberater letter on Wohnsitz and exit tax, a written German property plan, a map of surviving German income, UAE visa and housing evidence, and liquidity for any immediate non-EU exit tax. Our research ranks this among the most complex 6 to 12 month cross-border exercises.

1. Steuerberater letter on Wohnsitz exit and exit tax exposure.
2. Written plan for German property (sell, let without use, or accept continued liability).
3. Map German income that survives departure (pensions, GmbH profits, portfolios).
4. UAE visa and housing plan that matches your stated tax residence.
5. Liquidity for any immediate German exit tax due on non-EU moves.

## Should you sell German shares before or after the UAE move?

Share disposal timing relative to Abmeldung can change whether gain is taxed under exit rules versus standard capital rules over the departure year. We never assume a Dubai holding company alone avoids German attribution without a written opinion covering substance, treaty, and CFC tests across 1% to 10% participation thresholds for foreign buyers.

- Get a written Steuerberater opinion before any pre-move share sale
- Test 1% and 10% style participation thresholds against current law
- Do not treat a Dubai holding company as automatic CFC protection
