German tax residency does not end the day your flight to Dubai departs. Unlimited liability (unbeschränkte Steuerpflicht) follows Wohnsitz and habitual abode tests, exit tax can crystallise on company shares, and the Außensteuergesetz can extend German taxing rights for years if domestic income or ties remain. Dubai's zero personal income tax makes the UAE attractive, but it also makes German anti-abuse rules salient for auditors.

This guide outlines the main departure steps for German residents relocating to Dubai. It is not Steuerberatung; engage a qualified adviser before Abmeldung, share sales, or property purchases.

## Why is Germany's tax exit harder than a simple move abroad?

Germany taxes worldwide income for residents and applies exit rules designed to prevent value shifts to low-tax countries. The UAE's 0% personal income tax creates a wide rate gap, so Finanzämter scrutinise Abmeldung timing, available dwellings, and ongoing German income.

| Factor | Why it matters for Dubai movers |
| --- | --- |
| Wohnsitz | Available German home can block exit |
| Exit tax | Deemed sale of qualifying shares on departure |
| Extended liability | Up to 10 years on German-source income |
| CFC-style rules | Passive income in UAE entities may be attributed |
| CRS | Cross-border account reporting continues |

## When does German unlimited tax liability actually end?

Unlimited liability ends when you no longer have a Wohnsitz or gewöhnlicher Aufenthalt in Germany under the Income Tax Act tests.

**Wohnsitz:** A dwelling available to you (owned or rented) where you maintain meaningful presence. You need not live there full time; availability can suffice.

**Gewöhnlicher Aufenthalt:** More than six months continuous stay in Germany. Genuine relocation to Dubai breaks this once absence is real.

Practical exit steps:

1. File Abmeldung with the Einwohnermeldebehörde (necessary but not sufficient alone).
2. Remove personal use of German homes: sell, or lease to third parties without retained use rights.
3. Build UAE residence evidence: visa, Emirates ID, lease or title, utility and banking footprint.
4. Update German banks and your Steuerberater on declared residence.

The Finanzamt may request proof that departure is genuine; keeping a spare room with family can recreate Wohnsitz risk.

## What is Wegzugsbesteuerung and when does it hit Dubai relocations?

Section 6 Außensteuergesetz triggers a deemed disposal of qualifying shareholdings at fair market value when unlimited tax liability ends. Thresholds for "substantial participation" were tightened in recent reforms; confirm the current percentage with your adviser (historically 1% or 10% tests applied in different contexts).

Example pattern: a resident holds a GmbH stake worth far above cost. On exit, embedded gain can be taxed in Germany in the departure year even if no sale occurred. EU or EEA destinations may allow instalments; **Dubai (non-EU) generally does not**, so liquidity for the German bill must be planned in advance.

Restructuring, timing departures, or treaty analysis belongs in pre-move planning, not after the UAE visa is stamped.

## How long can Germany tax you after you leave?

Extended unlimited liability can apply for up to **10 years** if you were resident for sufficient prior years, move to a low-tax jurisdiction, and retain **domestic German income** (rental property, business profits, dividends from German companies, certain pensions).

During that window Germany taxes that domestic income at resident-like rates even though you live in Dubai. Mitigation often means eliminating or restructuring German income sources before exit, which requires coordinated German and UAE advice.

Limited liability (beschränkte Steuerpflicht) may still apply to other German-source items under separate rules.

## Do German CFC rules reach UAE free zone companies?

Germany's Hinzurechnungsbesteuerung can attribute passive income of low-taxed foreign companies to German shareholders or persons under extended liability. UAE free zone entities with passive rents, interest, royalties, or dividends may trigger analysis, especially when effective tax is below German benchmarks.

UAE corporate tax at 9% (from June 2023) and Qualifying Free Zone Person rules change the calculus but do not remove scrutiny. See [DMCC company setup](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/) for the UAE corporate side.

## What if you keep German real estate after moving to Dubai?

Rental income from German property remains taxable in Germany (limited or extended liability depending on status). Personal use rights on a German flat can undermine Wohnsitz exit. Many clean exits involve letting the property to unrelated tenants, removing owner access, and documenting Abmeldung.

German real estate also keeps reporting obligations in German returns even when you are non-resident.

## How should UAE residency fit the German exit timeline?

Obtain a UAE residence visa and Emirates ID, register with ICA/GDRFA as applicable, and maintain a credible centre of life in the UAE: lease or [property purchase](/guides/dubai-property-for-german-buyers/), local bank account, and consistent CRS self-certifications.

Golden Visa via qualifying property (commonly AED 2 million registered value, confirm with ICP/GDRFA) can support long-term UAE residence evidence but does not by itself terminate German liability. See [Golden Visa and mortgage property](/guides/golden-visa-mortgage-property-uae/).

## Does buying Dubai property create German tax issues?

UAE property ownership does not replace German exit steps. Once non-resident, Dubai rental income is generally UAE-source; Germany may tax it only under treaty allocation and any remaining German reporting duties. While still German-resident, worldwide income rules apply regardless of where the asset sits.

For UAE transaction costs and local levies, see [Dubai property taxes explained](/guides/dubai-property-taxes-explained/).

## What timeline should you use for a coordinated exit?

| Phase | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| 12+ months before | Exit tax modelling, share restructuring, German property plan |
| 6 months before | Abmeldung preparation, UAE visa strategy, bank updates |
| Departure month | File Abmeldung, close Wohnsitz, document UAE arrival |
| First UAE year | German return for split year, CRS consistency, Steuerberater follow-up |

Rushing property purchase in Dubai before German exit clarity can create cash flow strain if a large Wegzugsbesteuerung bill lands the same year.

## How do CRS and bank reporting interact?

UAE banks report to Germany under CRS when you certify German residence; German banks report UAE-linked accounts when you certify UAE residence. Mismatches trigger enquiries. Align certifications with your actual centre of life and filing position.

## What checklist should you run before committing to Dubai?

1. Steuerberater letter on Wohnsitz exit and exit tax exposure.
2. Written plan for German property (sell, let without use, or accept continued liability).
3. Map German income that survives departure (pensions, GmbH profits, portfolios).
4. UAE visa and housing plan that matches your stated tax residence.
5. Liquidity for any immediate German exit tax due on non-EU moves.

German tax exit planning is among the most complex cross-border exercises for individuals. Coordinate Steuerberater advice with UAE legal counsel before signing employment, company, or property contracts.

## Should you sell German shares before or after the UAE move?

Timing share disposals relative to Abmeldung can change whether gain is taxed under exit rules versus standard capital rules. Never assume a Dubai holding company alone avoids German attribution without a written opinion on substance, treaty, and CFC tests.
