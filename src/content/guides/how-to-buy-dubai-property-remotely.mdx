Most foreign buyers in Dubai never set foot in the city until handover day. Power of Attorney, RERA escrow, DLD online verification, video tours, and international wires make remote purchase routine from London, Moscow, Mumbai, or Singapore.

This guide is the 2026 workflow: order of steps, documents, common failures, and when you must visit in person.

## What can you complete remotely versus in person?

Most of the transaction chain runs through a POA holder in Dubai. Golden Visa biometrics and most UAE bank KYC still need your physical presence.

| Action | Remote? | Mechanism |
|  |  |  |
| Unit search and shortlisting | Yes | Broker video tours, DLD data |
| SPA signing | Yes | Power of Attorney |
| Escrow payments | Yes | International wire to escrow account |
| Oqood registration | Yes | POA holder at DLD |
| DLD title transfer (secondary) | Yes | POA at trustee centre |
| Independent SPA review | Yes | UAE solicitor reviews remotely |
| Professional snagging | Yes | POA holder + snagging company |
| Ejari registration | Yes | POA holder |
| Golden Visa application | No | Personal attendance required |
| UAE bank account opening | Usually no | Personal KYC attendance |
| Mortgage application | Partially | Bank may require personal visit |

Never wire to a personal account without written escrow confirmation on a RERA-registered project account.

## Step 1: What budget and fees should you model first?

Plan **6% to 9% above purchase price** for closing costs before you reserve a unit.

Example ready apartment at AED 1.5M:

| Item | AED |
|  |  |
| Purchase price | 1,500,000 |
| DLD fee (4%) | 60,000 |
| Trustee fee | 4,000 |
| Agent commission (2%) | 30,000 |
| Legal review | 5,000 |
| **Total** | **~1,599,000** |

Cross-check [escrow and Oqood rules](/guides/escrow-oqood-dubai-explained/) and the [step-by-step Dubai buy guide](/guides/how-to-buy-property-dubai-step-by-step/) before you sign.

## Step 2: How do you shortlist and verify a unit remotely?

Request a **live** video walkthrough (not a pre-recorded marketing clip). Pull comparable sales on Dubai REST or broker DLD data for the same tower. For off-plan, confirm RERA project registration, escrow account name, and developer handover history. Read [buying as a foreigner](/guides/can-foreigners-buy-property-uae/) for freehold zone checks.

## Step 3: How do you set up Power of Attorney?

Allow **2 to 4 weeks** for draft, notarisation, home-country legalisation, UAE embassy attestation, and MOFA UAE. Use a **property-specific POA**, not a general mandate. Typical split: UAE property lawyer for SPA signing; licensed broker or lawyer for DLD trustee steps and handover. Detail: [Power of Attorney for Dubai property](/guides/power-of-attorney-property-dubai/).

## Step 4: Who reviews the SPA before you sign?

Budget **AED 3,000 to 10,000** for independent solicitor review of payment plan penalties, handover dates, service charge caps, and cancellation clauses. Your POA holder signs only after written legal clearance.

## Step 5: How do you pay from abroad safely?

**Off-plan:** wire only to the **RERA-registered escrow account** listed on Dubai REST. **Secondary:** payment through the DLD trustee centre at transfer (POA holder attends with manager's cheque or confirmed transfer). Prepare **source-of-funds** bank statements; AML checks are standard.

## Step 6: When is Oqood or title registration complete?

At registration you pay the **4% DLD fee** (off-plan Oqood or ready title). You receive Oqood or title deed proof; verify on Dubai REST within 30 days. Registered value at **AED 2M+** can support Golden Visa eligibility once other criteria are met.

## Step 7: What happens at handover and snagging?

POA holder attends handover with a snagging company, logs defects, and withholds final payments until developer rectification where the SPA allows. Accepting keys without snagging makes remote defect claims harder.

## Step 8: How do you lease the unit remotely?

Engage RERA-licensed management (often 5% to 8% of rent), register Ejari via POA, and connect DEWA plus [district cooling](/guides/dubai-district-cooling-charges/) accounts. Gross yield marketing of 8% to 9% often becomes **5% to 6% net** after vacancy, management, and service charges.

## Step 9: When must you visit the UAE?

Plan **5 to 10 working days** for Golden Visa medicals, biometrics, and Emirates ID after title registration. Some banks require in-person KYC for non-resident accounts.

## Step 10: What document pack should you prepare upfront?

| Document | Purpose |
|  |  |
| Passport (6+ months validity) | ID across all steps |
| Proof of address | Bank and developer KYC |
| Proof of funds (3 to 6 months statements) | AML |
| Notarised POA (attested chain) | Remote signing |
| POA holder Emirates ID copy | DLD appointments |
| Solicitor engagement letter | SPA review trail |
| RERA Form B | Broker commission clarity |

Having the pack ready before unit selection avoids losing a reservation during POA attestation.

## What red flags should pause the deal?

- Deposit requested outside RERA escrow
- Developer refuses Dubai REST escrow verification
- General POA instead of property-specific powers
- No independent SPA legal review
- Service charge history hidden on ready stock
- Handover pressure without snagging report

## Ready versus off-plan: which suits remote buyers?

**Ready stock** fits rental income within 12 months and faster trustee transfer. **Off-plan** spreads payments but needs longer escrow discipline. **Golden Visa within six months** usually favours ready AED 2M+ or off-plan with immediate Oqood at AED 2M+ registered value. See [Gulf residency by investment](/guides/gulf-residency-by-investment-guide/) for visa timing.

Total active decision time from abroad is often **10 to 15 hours** across the purchase phase; POA holder, solicitor, and broker execute the rest.

## How do international wires and home-country tax interact?

Most buyers use SWIFT through their home bank or an FX specialist. For wires above AED 500K, compare bank SWIFT fees (often 0.5% to 2.5%) against specialist FX (often 0.2% to 1%). Keep a paper trail: contract, invoice from escrow, and bank confirmation for AML reviews.

| Origin | Typical extra paperwork | Timing note |
|  |  |  |
| UK / EU | Bank statements, employment or tax return | 3 to 10 business days |
| US | FATCA forms, wire documentation | 5 to 7 business days |
| India | RBI LRS declaration, bank NOC | 7 to 14 business days |
| Russia / CIS | Source-of-funds affidavits | Enhanced due diligence |

Remote purchase does not remove home-country reporting. Consult a cross-border tax adviser before you close; many jurisdictions tax foreign rental income or require annual property disclosures even when the UAE asset is held via POA.

## What remote verification tools help beyond video tours?

| Tool | What it confirms |
|  |  |
| Dubai REST | Escrow account, Oqood status, service charges |
| DLD transaction data | Achieved prices in the building |
| RERA project registration | Off-plan legitimacy |
| Live snagging stream | Defects at handover |
| Mollak portal | Service charge history (via broker) |

Some buyers commission a local due diligence visit (often AED 3,000 to 8,000) for high-value units: physical inspection, comparable rent checks, and escrow verification in one report.

## What timeline should you expect end to end?

| Phase | Typical duration |
|  |  |
| POA preparation and attestation | 2 to 4 weeks |
| Unit reservation to SPA | 1 to 2 weeks |
| Off-plan Oqood after first payment | 2 to 6 weeks |
| Ready secondary trustee transfer | 2 to 4 weeks after NOC |
| Handover (off-plan) | Per SPA (often 2 to 4 years from launch) |
| Golden Visa after title | 5 to 10 working days in UAE |

Off-plan remote buying is common because the long build window makes flying for every payment impractical. Ready stock compresses the calendar but demands stronger trust in live video inspection and comparable sales data. Align your POA expiry date with the SPA handover window so you are not re-notarising mid-project.

## Can non-residents get a mortgage without visiting Dubai?

Some UAE banks allow mortgage pre-approval remotely, but most require at least one KYC visit before disbursement. Non-resident LTV caps are often **50% to 60%**. Model arrangement fees (0.5% to 1% of loan), life insurance, and stress rates **1.5 to 2 points** above the quoted margin. Your POA holder can submit documents, yet the borrower usually must sign final loan agreements in person.

## What contingency reserve should remote owners hold?

Keep **10 to 15% of property value** liquid for void periods, special assessments, snagging rectification, and emergency travel if handover disputes arise. Remote ownership fails when every AED 15,000 repair requires a last-minute flight because no local manager is appointed.
