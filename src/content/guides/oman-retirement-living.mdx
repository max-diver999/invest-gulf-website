## Who retires to Oman: and who should not

### Strong fit profiles

- Couples 55+ selling UAE/Qatar apartment for quieter Muscat ITC life
- **Nature-forward retirees, diving, hiking, dolphin tours, wadi weekends
- **Budget-conscious former Gulf employees leveraging OMR purchasing power
- **Investor retirees with liquidity for ITC freehold + verified residence track
- **Second-home retirees splitting EU winters and Muscat summers, visa cadence discipline

### Weak fit profiles

- Nightlife-heavy singles expecting Dubai Marina rhythm
- Complex chronic care needing US-tier specialist density daily
- **Ultra-premium IB grandchildren schooling, Muscat pool is smaller
- Foreign freehold anywhere hunters, ITC zones only
- Zero-capital retirees without employment or verified investor path

## Healthcare in retirement: plan before you sell home country cover

| Repatriation | Evacuation rider for serious illness |
| Dental / optical | Often separate policies |
| **Public MOH | Subsidised paths limited for non-nationals |

Muscat private hospitals: Starcare, Apollo, Muscat Private, NMC, adequate for most retiree needs; complex oncology / neuro may need regional referral.

## FAQ

Can foreigners retire in Oman?  
Via verified investment residence or other ROP tracks, not a simple age-based pension visa.

How much to retire in Oman monthly?  
OMR 1,400–2,200 couple mid-tier indicative.

Is healthcare free for retirees?  
No broad expat public subsidy, private insurance essential.

Best area for retirees?  
Al Mouj / Qurum for services; Salalah for climate niche.

Can I work part-time in retirement?  
May need separate work permit,  (confirm current official rules) even if investor resident.

Does Oman have retirement communities?  
ITC developments approximate, not US-style CCRC model.

Is Oman safe for retirees?  
Low violent crime; driving and heat are main practical risks.

## Healthcare abroad vs Muscat: when to keep home country cover

Some retirees maintain EU private cover for specialist second opinions while using Muscat GP for routine care. Model:

- Annual EU premium vs Muscat top-up
- Flight cost for annual oncology screening if family history
- Repatriation if **incapacity, insurance rider vs cash reserve

Do not cancel home cover day one without adviser math, pre-existing windows reset painfully.
