## ROP vs Dubai RTA vs Abu Dhabi Muroor: who does what

In the UAE you learn RTA versus Muroor fast. In Oman, Royal Oman Police Traffic handles almost everything licence-related, conversion, tests, fines, and renewals.

## Who this suits — decision framework

Oman licensing timelines differ by applicant category. **GCC licence holder:** direct swap at ROP within 1 day, fee approximately 5–10 OMR. **International licence holder from a recognised country:** practical test only, budget 30–60 OMR and 1–2 weeks. **First-time applicant:** full training programme at an authorised driving school, 20–40 lessons at 3–5 OMR each, total timeline 2–4 months.

Full licensing programme in Oman costs 80–200 OMR over 2–4 months. Theory test fee: 3 OMR. Practical test: 5 OMR per attempt. Licence issuance: 5 OMR. Annual insurance premium for new drivers: 150–350 OMR. International licence conversion from approved countries: 10–20 OMR, completed in 1–3 days.

Oman driving facts to budget around: the full process takes 3–4 months for first-timers, with 20–40 practical sessions of 45 minutes each. The theory exam covers 35 questions in 30 minutes; the practical road test runs 15–20 minutes. Annual vehicle registration renewal costs $130–$260 depending on engine capacity. Insurance for drivers under 25 years runs 25–40% above standard rates. Speed cameras trigger fines starting at $130 for 20 km over the limit. The 2024–2025 fee schedule introduced a 10% surcharge for late renewals.
