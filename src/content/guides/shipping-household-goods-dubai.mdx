## Who ships household goods to Dubai: and why

Dubai attracts 205,000+ new residents annually, many relocating with families and established homes. The decision to ship versus buy new depends on your profile and timeline.

| Profile | Shipping priority | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate family transfer | Employer allowance, sentimental items | Full container, professional packing |
| Golden Visa buyer | Establishing permanent base | Ship quality items, buy basics locally |
| Young professional | Cost-conscious, minimal belongings | Airfreight essentials, buy furniture in Dubai |
| Executive relocation | High-value collection, antiques | White-glove service, marine insurance |
| Trailing spouse | Family continuity | Ship children's items, familiar home goods |
| Returning expat | Previously lived in Gulf | Ship adapted items, replace Gulf-unsuitable |

Consider alternatives: Dubai has comprehensive retail (IKEA, Home Centre, West Elm, local suppliers) with same-week delivery on most furniture. Many relocators ship personal effects only and buy big items locally.

|---|---|---|
| IKEA furniture | Same global pricing, 48hr delivery | 20ft container = AED 15K+ shipping cost |

## Sea freight vs air freight comparison

### Sea freight: the standard choice

| Container size | Cubic capacity | Typical cost from Europe | Cost from US | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20ft FCL | 33 cubic meters | AED 15,000–25,000 | AED 18,000–30,000 | 2-3 bedroom household |
| 40ft FCL | 67 cubic meters | AED 25,000–40,000 | AED 30,000–45,000 | 4+ bedroom, or shared load |
| LCL (shared) | Charge per cubic meter | AED 800–1,200/cbm | AED 900–1,500/cbm | Studio or minimal load |

FCL (Full Container Load) gives you exclusive use and control. LCL (Less than Container Load) shares space but adds consolidation delays.

Timeline expectations:
- Europe to Dubai: 4–6 weeks door-to-door
- US East Coast: 6–8 weeks
- US West Coast: 5–7 weeks via Suez
- Australia/New Zealand: 8–12 weeks
- Southeast Asia: 3–4 weeks

### Air freight: premium speed

| Weight range | Typical cost per kg | Dubai airport to door | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 100kg | AED 30–50/kg | 5–7 days | Documents, essentials, rush items |
| 100–500kg | AED 25–35/kg | 7–10 days | Electronics, urgent household |
| 500kg+ | AED 20–30/kg | 10–14 days | Smaller household, no furniture |

Volume limits: Air freight charges by volumetric weight, large/light items like mattresses become prohibitively expensive. Formula: length × width × height ÷ 6000 = volumetric kg.

When air makes sense:
- Urgent relocation (visa issued, start work immediately)
- High-value, low-volume items (electronics, documents, clothes)
- Temporary move with return planned under 2 years

## UAE customs and duties: resident advantages

### Household goods duty exemption

UAE residents with valid visas get duty-free import on genuine household goods within limits:

| Status | Duty exemption | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| First-time import | Full household goods | Within 6 months of first residence visa |
| Returning resident | Full household goods | After 1+ year abroad |
| Tourist/visit visa | None | Pay standard 5% duty + 5% VAT |
| Family member | Partial | Under sponsor's allowance |

Required documents for exemption:
- Valid residence visa (Emirates ID preferred)
- Passport with entry stamp
- Detailed inventory list with estimated values
- Original purchase receipts for items over AED 5,000
- Marriage certificate (for spouse's belongings)
- Employment contract or visa sponsor letter

### Prohibited and restricted items

Cannot ship to UAE:
- Alcohol (any quantity)
- Pork products, non-halal meat
- Narcotics and non-prescription drugs
- Weapons, ammunition, military items
- Gambling equipment
- Religious materials for distribution
- Radioactive materials

Require permits or inspection:
- Pets (separate import process)
- Plants and seeds
- Medications (prescription letter needed)
- Professional equipment (trade licence may apply)
- Wooden items (fumigation certificate)
- Used mattresses (hygiene inspection)

Electronics note: Personal electronics under normal household use qualify for exemption. Commercial quantities or for-sale intent trigger standard duties.

5. Confirm Dubai delivery contact: agent who will handle UAE customs

## Dubai port and customs clearance

### Jebel Ali Port arrival

Dubai is served by Jebel Ali Port, one of the world's largest container hubs. Most household goods arrive here for customs inspection.

Port timeline:
- Container discharge: 1–2 days after vessel arrival
- Customs documentation: 2–3 days for processing
- Physical inspection: 24–48 hours if selected (20–30% of shipments)
- Release for delivery: 1–2 days after clearance

Tracking your shipment:
1. Dubai Customs app: track with customs declaration number
2. Freight forwarder updates: your shipping agent provides port status
3. SMS notifications: UAE customs sends arrival and clearance updates
4. Online payment: pay any duties through Dubai Trade portal

### Customs clearance costs

| Service | Typical cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Customs clearance | AED 500–800 | Standard processing fee |
| Agent handling fee | AED 1,500–2,500 | Local customs broker |
| Port storage | AED 150/day after 5 days free | Avoid delays |
| Physical inspection | AED 800–1,200 | If randomly selected |
| Documentation | AED 300–500 | Translation, attestation |
| Delivery to home | AED 1,500–3,000 | Depends on emirate and volume |

Payment: UAE customs accepts online payment through Dubai Trade portal. Keep receipts for employer reimbursement if applicable.

### Red flags that cause delays

| Issue | Result | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete inventory | Inspection order, delays | Professional packing with detailed lists |
| High-value items without receipts | Duty assessment at market rate | Keep original purchase documentation |
| Tourist visa during clearance | Full duty applies | Time shipping after residence visa stamped |
| Prohibited items found | Confiscation, penalties | Review restricted list carefully pre-packing |
| Damaged packaging | Security inspection | Use professional packing service |

- Warehouse pickup, you collect from agent's facility (cheapest)

Dubai delivery zones:
- Dubai mainland, standard rates
- Sharjah, add AED 500–800
- Abu Dhabi, add AED 1,000–1,500

## Insurance and risk management

### Marine insurance essentials

Coverage types:
- All risks, comprehensive protection against loss, damage, theft (recommended)
- Named perils, covers specified risks only (fire, sinking, collision)
- Total loss only, cheapest but limited protection

Typical premiums:
- All risks coverage: 1.5–2.5% of declared value
- Named perils: 0.8–1.5% of declared value
- Additional UAE delivery coverage: +0.5%

Claim process:
1. Document damage immediately: photos before unpacking
2. Notify insurance within 24–48 hours: most policies have strict timelines
3. Get repair estimates: UAE-based contractors for settlement
4. Keep damaged items: insurers may want inspection before disposal

### Common exclusions

Items typically not covered by standard marine insurance:
- Normal wear and tear, pre-existing damage, age deterioration  
- Improper packing; if you pack yourself without professional standards
- Inherent vice, items that decay naturally (food, plants, liquids)
- War and strikes, force majeure events
- Customs confiscation, prohibited items seized by UAE authorities

Additional coverage available:
- Temporary storage; if delivery delayed beyond planned date
- Currency fluctuation, protects against exchange rate changes during claims
- Pair and sets, full replacement if part of matching set damaged

## Troubleshooting common problems

### Delayed shipments

Typical causes and solutions:

| Delay cause | Additional time | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Weather/port congestion | 3–7 days | Built into good shipping quotes |
| Documentation issues | 1–2 weeks | Professional customs broker |
| Random inspection | 2–5 days | Proper packing and documentation |
| Customs queries | 1–3 weeks | Respond quickly with clarifications |
| UAE public holidays | 2–7 days | Plan around Ramadan, National Day |

Mitigation: Ship 2–3 weeks earlier than your ideal arrival date to buffer delays.

### Damaged items claims

Dubai-specific considerations:
- Heat damage during summer months, ensure climate-controlled containers
- Salt air corrosion, use proper protective wrapping for metals
- Rough handling at Jebel Ali, professional crating for fragile items

Local repair network: Dubai has skilled furniture repair, electronics service, and restoration services, often cheaper than replacement for quality items.

### Customs disputes

Common issues:
- Value disputes, customs assesses higher value than declared
- Use determination, personal vs commercial use questions  
- Quantity challenges, multiple identical items trigger scrutiny
- Prohibited item discovery, unaware packing of restricted goods

**Resolution process:**
1. **Work through registered customs broker**: local expertise crucial
2. **Provide additional documentation**: purchase receipts, personal use certificates
3. **Consider duty payment**: sometimes faster than appeals process
4. **Escalate through shipping company**: they have established customs relationships

## FAQ

**Is it cheaper to ship or buy new furniture in Dubai?**  
Depends on total value and replacement cost. Ship if furniture collection worth over AED 15,000 and has sentimental value. Dubai has competitive furniture market, IKEA, Home Centre, local suppliers with same-week delivery. Many relocators ship personal effects only and buy mass furniture locally.

**Can I ship my car with household goods?**  
Cars typically ship separately with specialized auto freight. UAE car import requires different documentation and inspections. most expats buy cars locally due to emissions standards and right-hand drive requirements. Check UAE car import regulations if considering.

**What if my employer relocates me mid-contract?**  
Corporate relocations often include shipping allowances. Standard packages: AED 15K–25K middle management, AED 25K–50K senior roles. Negotiate allowance in AED not cubic meters, include marine insurance, request temporary accommodation if shipping delayed.

**How do I handle prescription medications during shipping?**  
Ship personal medications with prescription letters and original packaging. Large quantities may require UAE health ministry permits. Critical medications should be carried personally rather than shipped, avoid gaps during transit period.

**Can I ship alcohol or pork to Dubai?**  
No, UAE prohibits alcohol and pork imports in any quantity. Remove all alcoholic beverages, cooking wines, vanilla extract containing alcohol, pork products, non-halal meat, and items containing pork-derived ingredients before packing.

**What if shipping container is delayed at port?**  
Jebel Ali port charges storage fees after 5 free days, typically AED 150/day. Monitor tracking closely, respond quickly to customs requests, use registered Dubai customs broker to minimize delays. Budget 1–2 week buffer in your accommodation planning.

**Should I buy marine insurance?**  
Yes, strongly recommended. Costs 1.5–2.5% of declared value but covers loss, damage, theft during transit. Standard coverage requires professional packing to be valid. Dubai has good repair services but replacement costs can exceed shipping value.

**How long can I store goods in Dubai before collecting?**  
Port storage allows 5 free days then charges daily fees. Customs cleared goods can be stored at freight forwarder warehouses, typically AED 15–25 per cubic meter per month. Some relocators use this option if permanent accommodation not ready.
