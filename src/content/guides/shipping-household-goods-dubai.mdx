## Who ships household goods to Dubai, and why?

Corporate transfers, Golden Visa buyers, and cost-conscious professionals typically ship different mixes of household goods, and Invest Gulf budgets sea freight at AED 15,000 to 45,000 for 20ft to 40ft containers over 4 to 12 weeks door-to-door.

| Profile | Shipping priority | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate family transfer | Employer allowance, sentimental items | Full container, professional packing |
| Golden Visa buyer | Establishing permanent base | Ship quality items, buy basics locally |
| Young professional | Cost-conscious, minimal belongings | Airfreight essentials, buy furniture in Dubai |
| Executive relocation | High-value collection, antiques | White-glove service, marine insurance |

Dubai retail (IKEA, Home Centre, West Elm) often delivers furniture the same week, so many relocators ship personal effects only.

**Insider tip:** Stamp the residence visa before the container clears Jebel Ali, because tourist status typically triggers full 5% duty plus 5% VAT on the same household inventory.

## Should you buy bulky basics locally and ship high-value items?

Bulky basics under AED 10,000 replacement cost typically belong in Dubai retail rather than a 20ft container priced from AED 15,000 upward, and Invest Gulf ships only sentimental or custom pieces above that line.

### Items to buy new in Dubai

| Category | Dubai advantage | Local cost signal |
|---|---|---|
| IKEA furniture | Same global pricing, 48hr delivery | 20ft container = AED 15K+ shipping cost |
| Appliances | UAE voltage, local warranty, installation | Washing machine AED 1,500-3,000 |
| Mattresses | Hygiene restrictions, local preferences | King size AED 2,000-5,000 |
| Garden furniture | Heat-resistant materials | Shipping weight penalty high |

Property context: furnished rentals change the maths; see [rent vs buy Dubai expat](/guides/rent-vs-buy-dubai-expat/).

## When should you choose sea freight versus air freight?

Sea freight typically wins on volume at AED 15,000 to 45,000 for 20ft to 40ft containers over 4 to 12 weeks, while air freight costs AED 20 to 50 per kg for 5 to 14 day door delivery, and we reserve air only for urgent low-volume essentials for foreign buyers.

Timeline expectations:

- Europe to Dubai: 4-6 weeks door-to-door
- US East Coast: 6-8 weeks
- Australia/New Zealand: 8-12 weeks
- Southeast Asia: 3-4 weeks

### Air freight: premium speed

| Weight range | Typical cost per kg | Dubai airport to door | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 100kg | AED 30-50/kg | 5-7 days | Documents, essentials, rush items |
| 100-500kg | AED 25-35/kg | 7-10 days | Electronics, urgent household |
| 500kg+ | AED 20-30/kg | 10-14 days | Smaller household, no furniture |

Sea freight for a full household from Europe typically costs AED 15,000 to 30,000 for a 20ft container and AED 25,000 to 45,000 for a 40ft load, with door-to-door timing of 4 to 8 weeks before UAE clearance. Air freight at AED 25 to 40 per kg can finish in 5 to 10 days but rarely makes sense for bulky furniture once volumetric weight applies. Residence visa holders can claim household-goods duty relief inside the first 6 months after stamping, while tourist status usually pays 5% duty plus 5% VAT. Clearance, broker, and home delivery commonly add AED 2,000 to 4,000 plus AED 1,500 to 3,000. Book marine insurance at about 1.5 to 2.5% of declared value and keep receipts for items over AED 5,000.

## How does duty exemption depend on residency status?

UAE residents with a valid visa typically receive duty-free import on genuine household goods within 6 months of first residence stamping, and we treat tourist clearance as a red flag that triggers 5% duty plus 5% VAT on the same inventory for foreign buyers.

| Status | Duty exemption | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| First-time import | Full household goods | Within 6 months of first residence visa |
| Returning resident | Full household goods | After 1+ year abroad |
| Tourist/visit visa | None | Pay standard 5% duty + 5% VAT |
| Family member | Partial | Under sponsor's allowance |

Required documents usually include residence visa, Emirates ID, inventory with AED values, and receipts for items over AED 5,000.

### Prohibited and restricted items

Cannot ship to UAE:

- Alcohol (any quantity)
- Pork products, non-halal meat
- Narcotics and non-prescription drugs
- Weapons, ammunition, military items

Require permits: pets, plants, medications with prescription letters, wooden items with fumigation certificates.

## How should you book shipping around visa stamping?

Shipping bookings typically start 4 to 6 weeks before collection with a survey, marine insurance at 1.5 to 2.5% of declared value, and a Dubai delivery address, and Invest Gulf only confirms sail dates after residence visa timing is locked for foreign buyers.

### 4-6 weeks before shipping

1. Finalize Dubai address for warehouse or home delivery
2. Arrange pre-shipping survey and written quote
3. Purchase marine insurance at 1.5-2.5% of declared value
4. Gather receipts for items over AED 5,000
5. Book professional packing dates

### 2-3 weeks before shipping

1. Confirm UAE residence visa status for duty exemption
2. Prepare customs inventory with AED values
3. Pack personal documents to travel with you
4. Confirm the Dubai customs broker contact

## How do you track the shipment and clear customs?

Dubai Customs apps and freight-forwarder updates typically track Jebel Ali arrival, while clearance fees often land at AED 500 to 800 plus broker fees of AED 1,500 to 2,500, and we budget home delivery at AED 1,500 to 3,000 all-in for foreign buyers.

| Service | Typical cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Customs clearance | AED 500-800 | Standard processing fee |
| Agent handling fee | AED 1,500-2,500 | Local customs broker |
| Port storage | AED 150/day after 5 days free | Avoid delays |
| Physical inspection | AED 800-1,200 | If randomly selected |
| Delivery to home | AED 1,500-3,000 | Depends on emirate and volume |

### Red flags that cause delays

| Issue | Result | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete inventory | Inspection order, delays | Professional packing with detailed lists |
| High-value items without receipts | Duty assessment at market rate | Keep original purchase documentation |
| Tourist visa during clearance | Full duty applies | Time shipping after residence visa stamped |
| Prohibited items found | Confiscation, penalties | Review restricted list carefully pre-packing |

Port storage after 5 free days typically costs AED 150 per day, while clearance and broker fees often total AED 2,000 to 3,300, and random inspections add AED 800 to 1,200 when inventories lack values. Tourist visa status at clearance usually means 5% duty plus 5% VAT even on genuine household goods that would otherwise qualify. Residence-visa files inside the first 6 months avoid that stack when Emirates ID, passport, and AED inventory lines match the bill of lading exactly. Keep original receipts for pieces over AED 5,000 and photograph high-value items on packing day for marine insurance. Ship 2 to 3 weeks earlier than the ideal home-delivery date so weather, public holidays, or document queries do not force temporary storage at Jebel Ali.

Pets, art, and corporate moves typically need separate quotes, with pet shipping alone at AED 8,000 to 15,000 and C-level allowances often AED 50,000 to 100,000 or more, and we never mix alcohol into UAE household containers for foreign buyers.

| Seniority level | Typical allowance | Covers |

Wine collections cannot enter the UAE. Books and personal libraries usually qualify as household goods when values are documented.

## What does marine insurance actually cover?

Marine insurance typically costs 1.5 to 2.5% of declared value and require professional packing, while claims need photos inside 24 to 48 hours of delivery, and we exclude normal wear, DIY packing, and customs seizures from standard cover for foreign buyers.

Common exclusions:

- Normal wear, pre-existing damage, age deterioration
- DIY packing outside professional standards
- Inherent vice (food, plants, liquids)
- Customs confiscation of prohibited items

## Why do most shipping delays happen?

Most delays typically come from incomplete documents, random inspections of 2 to 5 days, or UAE holiday windows of 2 to 7 days, and Invest Gulf ships 2 to 3 weeks early so Jebel Ali storage at AED 150 per day never starts.

| Delay cause | Additional time | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Weather/port congestion | 3-7 days | Built into good shipping quotes |
| Documentation issues | 1-2 weeks | Professional customs broker |
| Random inspection | 2-5 days | Proper packing and documentation |
| UAE public holidays | 2-7 days | Plan around Ramadan, National Day |
