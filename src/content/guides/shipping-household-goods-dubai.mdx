## FAQ

**Is it cheaper to ship or buy new furniture in Dubai?**  
Depends on total value and replacement cost. Ship if furniture collection worth over AED 15,000 and has sentimental value. Dubai has competitive furniture market, IKEA, Home Centre, local suppliers with same-week delivery. Many relocators ship personal effects only and buy mass furniture locally.

**Can I ship my car with household goods?**  
Cars typically ship separately with specialized auto freight. UAE car import requires different documentation and inspections. most expats buy cars locally due to emissions standards and right-hand drive requirements. Check UAE car import regulations if considering.

**What if my employer relocates me mid-contract?**  
Corporate relocations often include shipping allowances. Standard packages: AED 15K–25K middle management, AED 25K–50K senior roles. Negotiate allowance in AED not cubic meters, include marine insurance, request temporary accommodation if shipping delayed.

**How do I handle prescription medications during shipping?**  
Ship personal medications with prescription letters and original packaging. Large quantities may require UAE health ministry permits. Critical medications should be carried personally rather than shipped, avoid gaps during transit period.

**Can I ship alcohol or pork to Dubai?**  
No, UAE prohibits alcohol and pork imports in any quantity. Remove all alcoholic beverages, cooking wines, vanilla extract containing alcohol, pork products, non-halal meat, and items containing pork-derived ingredients before packing.

**What if shipping container is delayed at port?**  
Jebel Ali port charges storage fees after 5 free days, typically AED 150/day. Monitor tracking closely, respond quickly to customs requests, use registered Dubai customs broker to minimize delays. Budget 1–2 week buffer in your accommodation planning.

**Should I buy marine insurance?**  
Yes, strongly recommended. Costs 1.5–2.5% of declared value but covers loss, damage, theft during transit. Standard coverage requires professional packing to be valid. Dubai has good repair services but replacement costs can exceed shipping value.

**How long can I store goods in Dubai before collecting?**  
Port storage allows 5 free days then charges daily fees. Customs cleared goods can be stored at freight forwarder warehouses, typically AED 15–25 per cubic meter per month. Some relocators use this option if permanent accommodation not ready.
