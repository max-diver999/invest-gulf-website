## Why are Gulf residency and property separate decisions in 2026?

No Gulf country grants immigration residence automatically on property purchase. You apply after a qualifying registered purchase in a designated zone.

The three questions to answer before any deposit:

- Is this property in a designated zone for foreign ownership?
- Does the registered value meet the residency threshold?
- Have I verified the current rules with the government portal, not the broker?

## How does UAE Golden Visa property residency work?

UAE Golden Visa at AED 2 million registered property is the most liquid Gulf route, with processing often two to eight weeks after title or Oqood.

Liquidity advantage: UAE resale market depth (205,000+ annual transactions) helps exit if residency goals change.

## How does Saudi Arabia fit Gulf residency by investment?

Law M/14 zone ownership and Premium Residency at about SAR 4 million are separate tracks; property purchase alone does not bundle Premium Residency.

Stability and liquidity point to UAE. Lowest capital entry often points to Qatar. Lifestyle and quiet markets point to Oman ITC zones.
