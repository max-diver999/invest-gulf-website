## Why Qatar — and why plan before you land

Qatar has **tax-free employment**, strong airport connectivity, and a stable currency (QAR pegged to USD). Doha is smaller than Dubai — quieter socially, with solid hiring in energy, finance, aviation, and healthcare.

**The move works when:**
- Your **basic salary** meets family sponsorship rules (QAR 10,000+ for dependants)
- You apply to **schools 6–12 months early**
- You **rent first** 6–12 months before buying in The Pearl or Lusail

## The three mistakes we fix most often

### 1. Total package looks great — basic salary blocks family visa

Housing allowance QAR 8,000 + basic QAR 9,500 = headline QAR 18,000 — but MOI may reject family sponsorship. The basic line is what counts.

→ [Qatar family visa sponsorship](/guides/qatar-family-visa-sponsorship/)

### 2. Lease signed — then school says no place

School deposits are non-refundable. Apply before you pick a neighbourhood.

→ [Doha international schools guide](/guides/doha-international-schools-guide/)

### 3. Bought Pearl apartment — residency track misunderstood

Property ownership and work visa are **separate**. Get legal review before any deposit.

→ [Qatar property buyer relocation](/guides/qatar-property-buyer-relocation/)
