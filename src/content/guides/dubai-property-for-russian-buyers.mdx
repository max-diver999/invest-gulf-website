## Can Russian citizens legally buy Dubai property?

Russian nationals can legally buy Dubai freehold in DLD-designated zones on the same title basis as other foreign buyers, and Invest Gulf typically models banking KYC as the real friction rather than DLD registration, with average Russian cheques near AED 2.8 to 2.9 million.

- Freehold only in DLD-designated zones
- Banking KYC runs parallel to title
- Average CIS cheque near AED 2.8 to 2.9M

Russian nationals buying Dubai freehold in 2026 typically face the same DLD registration path as other foreign buyers, with friction concentrated in banking KYC and RUB to AED corridors rather than at the title counter. Invest Gulf underwriting for CIS files models average purchase cheques near AED 2.8 to 2.9 million, a ready-purchase fee stack of about 6% to 7% including roughly 4% DLD and 2% secondary brokerage, and Golden Visa planning only when registered freehold value clears about AED 2 million with eligible title. Source-of-funds packs should include employment or sale evidence, SPA purpose letters, and SWIFT confirmations per milestone, because trustees reject unlicensed channels and crypto narratives. UAE personal income tax on rent remains 0%, yet Russian tax residency can still create reporting duties for worldwide income depending on days spent in Russia. Practical sequencing keeps bank pre-checks 2 to 4 weeks ahead of MOU, independent English SPA review before deposit, and Ejari plus management contracts before remote owners leave the country.

## How do RUB to AED transfers work in practice?

RUB to AED transfers typically require modelling the full purchase budget in dirhams first, because Invest Gulf files show corridor availability changes by bank and by week, and foreign buyers should keep SWIFT trails tied to SPA milestones when wiring AED 2 million class deposits into trustee escrow into trustee escrow accounts.

| Channel | Notes |
|---|---|
| SWIFT from RF or foreign bank | Corridor availability changes; verify each milestone |
| Pre-existing UAE account | Fastest if already open |
| Exchange house | Useful for smaller tranches with documentation |

Documentation pack banks expect:

- Passport and visa copies if resident
- Source-of-funds evidence (employment, business sale, savings history)
- SPA or MOU showing property purpose
- SWIFT confirmations per milestone
- Tax returns or corporate docs if business-derived

If one bank declines, another may accept with fuller documentation (confirm current official rules). Never use unlicensed transfer channels; trustees reject unclear source of funds.

- Start bank talks 2 to 4 weeks before MOU

- Try a second bank if the first declines

## What mistakes do Russian buyers make most often?

The most common Russian buyer mistakes are unlicensed fund channels, assuming UAE 0% tax means Russia 0% tax, paying with crypto, modelling gross yield only, skipping Ejari, and relying on a single bank, and we see each path burn capital or 30 to 90 days for foreign buyers on CIS purchase files.

1. Unlicensed fund transfer channels: trustees reject unclear source of funds
2. Assuming UAE 0% tax means Russia 0% tax: RF reporting may apply
3. Crypto for property payment: banks and trustees reject
4. Buying on gross yield: model net after service charges
5. No Ejari registration: cannot enforce tenancy at RDC
6. Skipping independent SPA review: translation errors in contracts
7. Single-bank strategy: if one bank declines, try another with fuller docs
