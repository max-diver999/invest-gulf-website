## Can Russian citizens legally buy Dubai property?

Yes. DLD registers Russian passport holders in freehold zones on the same basis as other foreign nationals. Friction appears at bank compliance desks, not at the title counter.

## How do RUB to AED transfers work in practice?

Model budget in AED; the dirham peg removes FX risk after conversion.

| Channel | Notes |
|  |  |
| SWIFT from RF or foreign bank | Corridor availability changes; verify each milestone |
| Pre-existing UAE account | Fastest if already open |
| Exchange house | Useful for smaller tranches with documentation |

Documentation pack banks expect:

- Passport and visa copies if resident
- Source-of-funds evidence (employment, business sale, savings history)
- SPA or MOU showing property purpose
- SWIFT confirmations per milestone
- Tax returns or corporate docs if business-derived

If one bank declines, another may accept with fuller documentation (confirm current official rules). Never use unlicensed transfer channels; trustees reject unclear source of funds.

## Are Russian buyers blocked from UAE banks?

Not categorically. Risk-based KYC, sanctions screening, and source-of-funds depth vary by institution. Open [UAE bank account](/guides/opening-bank-account-dubai-property-buyers/) early if you plan to finance or receive rent locally.

## Does Golden Visa work for Russian property buyers?

Golden Visa via property generally requires about AED 2 million registered freehold value and eligible title (confirm with GDRFA/ICP). Mortgaged units may not qualify until equity clears threshold. See [Golden Visa mortgage](/guides/golden-visa-mortgage-property-uae/).

## What mistakes do Russian buyers make most often?

1. Unlicensed fund transfer channels: trustees reject unclear source of funds
2. Assuming UAE 0% tax means Russia 0% tax: RF reporting may apply
3. Crypto for property payment: banks and trustees reject
4. Buying on gross yield: model net after service charges
5. No Ejari registration: cannot enforce tenancy at RDC
6. Skipping independent SPA review: translation errors in contracts
7. Single-bank strategy: if one bank declines, try another with fuller docs

## What does sanctions-aware compliance mean operationally?

Use licensed banks and trustees only. Keep SWIFT trails and SPA purpose letters. Screen counterparty names against your bank policy. This is institution process, not a public ban list.

## What should family buyers budget for schools?

Parallel school search with property search if relocating children. Dubai KHDA fees sit above many CIS expectations; Sharjah SPEA may save fees if commute works.

## How should remote owners operate from Russia or CIS?

**Power of attorney:** UAE solicitors can structure POA for a trusted manager to sign Ejari and handle emergencies if you remain abroad full-time.

Property management is non-negotiable for absentee landlords. Budget 5 to 8% of rent for full service.

## Worked personas

### Persona 1: Marina lifestyle plus Golden Visa

Budget AED 1.8M to 2.5M one to two bed Marina or JLT. Gross yield 5 to 6% acceptable. Golden Visa if registered value clears AED 2M. Russian-speaking agent helpful; independent solicitor mandatory.

### Persona 2: JVC yield landlord (remote)

AED 600K to 900K one-bed ready stock. Target net 5 to 6% after management from abroad. Agency management required.

### Persona 3: Golden Visa family

AED 2.0 to 2.3M cluster. School research parallel to property. Ready or near-handover only. Confirm mortgage rules with ICP if not full equity.

## How do Russian-speaking services help the process?

Agents and lawyers who speak Russian speed daily logistics but do not replace independent legal review of the SPA in English. Title, trustee, and bank compliance documents remain in English and Arabic. Budget for bilingual review if needed.

## What estate planning should remote owners consider?

UAE freehold passes via local will or succession rules. If you hold multiple units, draft a UAE will with a qualified solicitor and keep trustee contact details with your manager under POA.

## How do short-let rules affect Marina and Palm buyers?

Communities differ on holiday-home permits and RERA short-let rules. Russian buyers often buy Marina or Palm for own use plus seasonal rent. Verify building bylaws and permit path before you model yield.

## How do trustees view repeated transfers from CIS banks?

Trustees want a clear paper trail from named accounts to escrow. Split large transfers into documented tranches with matching SPA payment schedule. Keep employment and sale contracts aligned with amounts wired.

## Should you hold Dubai property in personal or company name?

Corporate holding adds setup cost and annual compliance but can help succession planning for some families. Personal name remains most common for first apartment. Discuss structure with UAE solicitor before MOU, not after.
