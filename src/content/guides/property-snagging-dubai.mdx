Every off-plan buyer in Dubai reaches the same moment: the developer calls to schedule handover. The apartment looks finished. The sales team is encouraging you to sign the acceptance form, collect keys, and start the DEWA connection. This is the most important 48 hours in your purchase, and the moment most buyers rush.

Snagging is the systematic inspection that happens before you accept. It is the difference between a developer fixing 40 defects at their cost and you discovering those defects six months later when the defect liability period has expired.

## Why Does Snagging Matter in Dubai?

Snagging matters because it is the last clean window to force developer repairs before the defect liability period clock starts, typically 12 months from handover acceptance. Invest Gulf tells foreign buyers that signing without a written snag list often shifts AED 15,000 to 25,000 of remediable defects onto the owner. The SPA covers what you document; silence after keys is expensive.

| Without snagging | With snagging |
| --- | --- |
| Accept handover, discover leaks at month 3 | Document leaks at handover, developer fixes under DLP |
| Pay DEWA deposit on a unit with no AC | Refuse handover until AC commissioned |
| Cosmetic issues become your cost | Cosmetic issues logged in developer snag list |
| No leverage after acceptance form signed | Formal snag list creates contractual obligation |

The SPA defect liability period (DLP) typically runs 12 months from handover. Some developers offer 24 months on structural elements. The clock starts when you sign acceptance, not when you discover the problem.

## What Is the Dubai Handover Snagging Process?

Dubai handover snagging is a same-day to 24 hour inspection process that should finish before you sign acceptance: book a snagger (AED 1,000 to 3,500 for apartments), walk the unit for 2 to 4 hours, then file a photographed list inside the SPA window of 7 to 30 days. Invest Gulf sequences foreign buyers so DEWA and the DLP clock start only after critical snags clear. Villas typically cost AED 3,000 to 7,000 to inspect.

1. Confirm handover appointment and bring SPA, passport, and payment cheques if final tranche is due.
2. Book a professional snagger for the same slot (budget AED 1,000 to 3,500 for apartments).
3. Walk every room for 2 to 4 hours with the inspector.
4. Receive a photographed snag report (critical, major, minor).
5. Submit the snag list in writing within the SPA inspection window (often 7 to 30 days).
6. Re-inspect after the developer claims fixes are complete.
7. Sign acceptance, collect keys, activate DEWA, and start the DLP clock.

| Unit type | Typical snagging fee | Typical snag count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio to 1-bed | AED 1,000 to 2,000 | 15 to 40 items |
| 2-bed apartment | AED 1,500 to 3,500 | 25 to 60 items |
| Villa / townhouse | AED 3,000 to 7,000 | 40 to 100+ items |

## What Should Your Snagging Checklist Include?

A Dubai snagging checklist means live-system checks before cosmetics: AC in every room, water pressure in all bathrooms, DEWA activation, and parking allocation ahead of paint notes. We budget AED 1,000 to 3,500 for apartment walks that usually log 15 to 60 defects, or AED 3,000 to 7,000 for villas.

### Plumbing
- Water pressure at all taps (hot and cold)
- Drainage speed in all sinks, showers, baths
- Toilet flush mechanism and seal
- Water heater function and temperature
- Visible pipe leaks under sinks

### Electrical
- All outlets functional (test with device)
- Light switches and dimmers
- Circuit breaker panel labelling
- Intercom and doorbell
- TV/data outlet locations match floor plan

### HVAC
- AC cooling in every room (run for 30 minutes minimum)
- Thermostat function
- Vent positioning and airflow
- Noise levels from AC units

### Finishes
- Tile alignment and grout consistency
- Paint finish (runs, patches, colour match)
- Door alignment and lock function
- Kitchen cabinet alignment and soft-close
- Wardrobe internals and shelving

### External and common areas
- Parking bay number and access
- Storage unit allocation (if applicable)
- Gym, pool, lobby condition (for service charge justification)
- Fire exit access from your floor

## When Does Professional Snagging Pay Off?

Professional snagging pays off when the fee is a fraction of remediable defects: on an AED 2 million apartment, an AED 2,500 report often documents AED 15,000 to 25,000 of developer work and saves 8 to 12 hours of DIY time. Invest Gulf strongly prefers pros for foreign buyers above AED 1 million, and for any POA handover. Re-inspection should be included, not sold as a surprise add-on.

### What Professional Snaggers Catch That DIY Misses

Technical defects requiring expertise:
- HVAC commissioning issues, incorrect refrigerant levels, ductwork leaks
- Waterproofing failures, invisible until next rainy season
- Electrical code violations, improper grounding, overloaded circuits
- Structural movement signs, early indicators of settling problems
- Building envelope defects, window frame sealing, balcony drainage slopes

Documentation standards:
- Categorised severity levels developers recognise in their workflows
- Technical terminology that matches construction industry standards
- Photographic evidence protocols accepted in DLD dispute proceedings
- Measurement-based defect description rather than subjective observations

For remote buyers using POA, professional snagging is essential. Your POA holder should attend handover with the snagging company, not on behalf of the developer.

### Choosing the Right Snagging Company

Evaluation criteria:
- RICS or equivalent certification for inspectors
- Insurance coverage for errors and omissions
- Portfolio of completed reports across different developers
- Response timeline for rush handovers
- Re-inspection policy included vs additional cost

Red flags in snagging companies:
- Guaranteed snag-free reports (impossible)
- Verbal reports only (no written documentation)
- Same-day report delivery (insufficient review time)
- No re-inspection option (incomplete service)
- Developers recommending specific companies (potential conflict of interest)

## How Long Do Developers Have to Fix Snags?

Developer remedy timelines typically mean 30 to 90 days to fix snags by severity, while critical safety defects such as failed AC in summer or major leaks should be fixed before acceptance when possible. We tell foreign buyers to timestamp every portal ticket so DLD escalation has a clean 30 to 90 day delay trail. Cosmetic items can wait inside the 12 month DLP; safety items should not.

| Severity | Typical SPA window | Buyer stance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical safety | Fix before acceptance if possible | Pause keys |
| Major functional | 30 to 90 days | Written snag list |
| Minor cosmetic | Inside 12 month DLP | Photo + follow up |

## How Do You Escalate Snagging Disputes With DLD?

DLD escalation is the formal path when a developer misses a written 30 to 90 day remedy window, and it requires a photographed snag report plus proof of notice. We tell foreign buyers to add an independent engineer letter for structural claims, because severity codes from a professional report travel better than WhatsApp complaints. Keep the acceptance form and portal tickets in one file for the full 12 month DLP.

| Evidence pack | Why DLD cares |
| --- | --- |
| Snag report + photos | Proves defects at handover |
| Portal timestamps | Proves 30 to 90 day delay |
| Engineer letter | Supports structural claims |

## How Does Snagging Differ for Villas and Apartments?

Villa snagging means a wider structural scope than apartment punch lists: foundation, roof waterproofing and external drainage, with fees that typically run AED 3,000 to 7,000 versus AED 1,000 to 3,500 for flats. Invest Gulf wants foreign buyers on villa product to use inspectors with structural experience, not a one-hour cosmetics walk. Apartments still need full HVAC and wet-area checks for 2 to 4 hours and often log 25 to 60 items.

| Unit type | Fee band | Typical snag count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apartment | AED 1,000 to 3,500 | 15 to 60 items |
| Villa / townhouse | AED 3,000 to 7,000 | 40 to 100+ items |

## How Should Remote Buyers Handle Snagging?

Remote snagging means a POA holder attends with an independent snagging company, not with developer sales, and runs live AC and water tests on site for 2 to 4 hours. We tell foreign buyers that video tours help but do not replace pressure and cooling checks. Pre-authorize withholding the final 5 to 10 percent where the SPA allows until critical snags clear inside the 7 to 30 day inspection window.

| Remote control | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Independent snagger + POA | Avoids sales-desk bias |
| Live AC / water tests | Video cannot replace |
| 5 to 10 percent retain | Leverage until critical fixes |

## Do Snagging Delays Affect Service Charges?

Service charges typically start at handover even when snags remain open, so delayed fixes do not pause your bill. We tell foreign buyers to negotiate acceptance wording that keeps major defects on the developer punch list, and to photograph common-area gaps that undermine the first 12 months of service-charge value. Track the DLP clock separately from the service-charge start date across the same 30 to 90 day remedy window.

| Clock | Starts when | Buyer action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service charges | Often at handover | Budget from key day |
| SPA remedy | Snag submission | Chase 30 to 90 days |
| DLP | Acceptance signed | 12 month claim window |

## Which Snagging Red Flags Should Pause Handover?

Handover red flags typically mean you should pause keys: AC fails commissioning, main plumbing leaks, parking or storage is missing, DEWA is inactive, or the developer refuses written snag-list acknowledgment. Our research treats those five as deal-stoppers, because signing starts the typical 12 month DLP clock and weakens leverage. Temporary delay beats absorbing AED 15,000-plus of preventable fixes.

| Red flag | Action |
| --- | --- |
| AC / major leak / grounding fail | Pause acceptance |
| Missing parking / storage / DEWA | Pause acceptance |
| No written snag acknowledgment | Do not sign |

## What Is the Defect Liability Period in Dubai?

The defect liability period in Dubai typically runs 12 months from handover for workmanship, with structural protection often cited around 10 years and some developers extending select covers to 24 months. We tell foreign buyers to file the first snag list within 7 days and a final sweep 45 to 60 days before DLP end. Signing acceptance without a list can forfeit later claims.

| DLP layer | Typical window | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workmanship | About 12 months | Tiles, paint, HVAC, doors |
| Structural | About 10 years | Foundation, load-bearing, envelope |
| Enhanced developer covers | Up to 24 months on select items | Premium project warranties |

| DLP month band | Buyer action |
| --- | --- |
| Month 1 to 3 | Submit initial list within 7 days; watch first AC/rain failures |
| Month 4 to 9 | Track seasonal defects with dated photos |
| Month 10 to 12 | Final list 45 to 60 days before expiry |

## How Do You Choose a Snagging Company in Dubai?

Choosing a snagging company means judging sample reports, not price alone: a 10-page file with 50 photographed items beats a one-page checklist, and re-inspection should be included. Invest Gulf wants foreign buyers to confirm indemnity cover and independence from the developer sales office.

| Check | Pass signal | Fail signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Report depth | 10+ pages, 50 items | One-page tick box |
| Re-inspection | Included | Extra surprise fee |
| Independence | No developer referral kickback | Sales-desk recommendation only |

Insider tip: on units above AED 2 million, pay for the snagger who will re-inspect after the developer claims "all fixed," because the first visit finds defects and the second visit proves closure before you release leverage.
