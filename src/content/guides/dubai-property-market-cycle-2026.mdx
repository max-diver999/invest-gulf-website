- 2010 to 2014: post-GFC recovery with mid-market yields near 9% to 11% gross
- 2014 to 2019: oversupply correction of about 20% to 35% in many communities
- 2020 to 2025: bull run from pandemic trough to record volumes
- 2026: mature expansion where easy momentum returns fade

## Does population growth still support Dubai housing demand?

Yes. Roughly 5% annual population growth typically adds end-user and rental demand that absorbs units when jobs keep expanding, lifting Dubai from about 2.5 million residents in 2010 toward 4 million-plus by 2025 on Invest Gulf demand models before you lock dates.

- Employment in finance, tech, aviation, and tourism remains the leading indicator
- 2040 population targets near 5.8 million imply hundreds of thousands of extra homes
- Work-permit or major employer shocks would be the negative leading signals

- Still elevated off-plan sales and record prime prices at cycle peaks
