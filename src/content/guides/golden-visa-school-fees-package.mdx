  - question: "Does the Golden Visa cover children's school enrolment priority?"
    answer: "The Golden Visa itself does not grant school priority. However, residency status is a prerequisite for school enrolment in most KHDA-regulated schools. Golden Visa holders have the same residency status as standard visa holders for school enrolment purposes, the advantage is long-term certainty that eliminates mid-year enrolment disruption if a job changes."
  - question: "What is the timing sequence for a family doing property purchase and school setup?"
    answer: "Typical sequence: SPA signing and DLD registration (Month 1-2) → Golden Visa application (Month 2-3, using DLD certificate) → Emirates ID for all family members (Month 3-4) → School application with Emirates ID (Month 4-5) → Enrolment for September intake. Start at least 8 months before target school year."
  - question: "Which Dubai areas combine good schools and Golden Visa-eligible property?"
    answer: "Dubai Hills Estate, Arabian Ranches, Jumeirah, and Mirdif offer proximity to high-rated KHDA schools and properties meeting the AED 2M threshold. Dubai Marina and Downtown have the property price points but require longer school commutes. Families typically prioritise community and school access over central location."
  - question: "Are school fees stable or do they rise annually?"
    answer: "KHDA regulates school fee increases through a fee cap formula linked to school rating and performance score. Outstanding-rated schools can raise fees up to around 6-7% annually; Good-rated schools 3-5%; Acceptable 0-2%. Budget for 4-5% annual increase on average. Fee caps are subject to KHDA review, verify current permitted increase for specific schools."
  - question: "Can the AED 2M Golden Visa property be off-plan?"
    answer: "The current UAE Golden Visa regulations for property require a completed, registered freehold title. Off-plan properties registered under Oqood do not qualify until handover and title deed issuance. Some families time an off-plan purchase so handover aligns with their intended relocation date. Verify current eligibility criteria with DLD and GDRFA before exchange."

**School deposits and registration fees.** Most KHDA-regulated schools charge a non-refundable registration fee of AED 3,000-10,000 per child on acceptance. Some schools also take a security deposit equivalent to one term's fees, held against damage or non-payment.

**School bus transport.** A round-trip school bus contract in Dubai typically costs AED 5,000-8,000 per child annually. Families with two children pay AED 10,000-16,000 on top of tuition, this is routinely omitted from headline fee comparisons.

**Golden Visa renewal and maintenance.** The 10-year visa requires renewal at year 10, with associated government and medical fees. Family members also require individual visa renewals. Budget approximately AED 5,000-10,000 per family member per renewal cycle, spread across the 10-year horizon this is modest, but it is a real line item.

**School activity and enrichment costs.** Schools with strong extra-curricular programmes charge AED 5,000-15,000 per child annually for sport teams, music lessons, drama, and school trips. Outstanding schools in Dubai often have rich activities programmes that are genuinely compelling but add meaningfully to annual costs.

**Annual service charge on the property.** A AED 2.2M apartment in a premium Dubai building carries service charges of AED 15,000-30,000 annually. This is a compulsory payment to the building's Owners Association and is separate from mortgage or rent.

Accounting for these items produces a more accurate 5-year total that informs whether the combined package is financially sustainable at your household income level.
