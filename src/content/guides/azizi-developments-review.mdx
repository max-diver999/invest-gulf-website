Azizi Developments built market share by selling mid-market apartments at competitive launch PSF with flexible payment plans across Dubai South, Al Furjan, and Meydan, corridors where Tier 1 developers price higher and Tier 2 competitors fight for volume.

That positioning creates a clear trade-off. Azizi's ~82% on-time delivery beats the worst Tier 2 players (Samana ~65%, Binghatti ~78%) but trails Emaar (~95%) and DAMAC (~88%). You get entry pricing and payment accessibility in exchange for more SPA scrutiny, thinner resale liquidity, and higher investor concentration at many handovers.

Quick answer: ~82% delivery. Volume mid-market developer. Competitive launch PSF. Flagship corridors: Dubai South, Al Furjan, Meydan. Best for yield buyers who lawyer-review SPAs. Not best for maximum liquidity or delivery certainty.

## Delivery track record: what 82% means

Azizi's ~82% on-time delivery places it in the upper Tier 2 band, not Tier 1, but not a speculative unknown.

Practical meaning:
- Roughly 1 in 5 Azizi projects experiences meaningful delay outside RERA tolerance
- High launch volume means handover clusters, multiple towers completing in the same corridor simultaneously
- Completed Azizi stock generally delivers acceptable build quality for mid-market segment, expect snagging, not structural failure
- Delay risk matters more if you are on post-handover payment plans, you may pay before keys

Verify before buying:
1. Last three Azizi handovers in the same community: SPA date vs DLD completion
2. Construction site visit: crane activity, facade completion, MEP progress
3. Broker resale stock: months-on-market for completed Azizi units in target building
4. RERA project status on Trakheesi portal

## Due diligence checklist: Azizi-specific

Before signing an Azizi SPA:

1. **Trakheesi registration**: project number on DLD portal
2. **Escrow account**: verify on Dubai REST app (bank, account, balance)
3. **Phase-specific delivery**: ask for handover dates on your building, not corporate average
4. **Mollak service charges**: on last handed-over Azizi building in same corridor
5. **SPA delay clause**: read penalty cap and termination rights aloud
6. **Comparable ready pricing**: net promotional PSF against completed Azizi stock
7. **Rental data**: Ejari transacted rents on handed-over phase (not launch brochure)
8. **NOC policy**: if planning off-plan assignment, confirm fees and timing
9. **Handover supply map**: count competing towers in same sub-cluster for 2026–2027
10. **Oqood registration**: confirm process and timeline in SPA

### Practical cost reference

Buyers in an Azizi project should budget for DLD transfer (4 % of the purchase price), a trustee fee of 4,000 AED plus VAT, Oqood registration for off-plan (2 % or minimum 5,000 AED), and Azizi's own NOC charge (typically 1,000–5,000 AED depending on the community). If financing, add a bank valuation fee of 2,500–3,500 AED and an arrangement fee of 0.25–1 %. From booking to title deed, most Azizi resale deals close in 4–6 weeks; off-plan transfers can take longer when DLD approval queues spike.
