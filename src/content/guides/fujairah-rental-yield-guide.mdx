Fujairah is the UAE's only east coast emirate: Indian Ocean coastline, Hajar Mountains, and employment anchored in the Port of Fujairah, logistics free zones, and local government rather than Dubai commuter flows. For yield investors, low AED per sqft against achievable local rents creates headline gross returns of 7-10% on apartments, with the trade-off that secondary liquidity is among the thinnest in the country.

## What rental yields does Fujairah offer in 2026?

Mid-market apartments in Sakamkam, Merashid, and older town-centre stock often deliver 7 to 10% gross on low entry prices. Waterfront and newer towers run 6 to 8% gross. Net yields after fees typically land at 5.5 to 8% when service charges stay moderate and void stays near one month.

Insight: Fujairah often matches or beats Dubai gross percentages because prices lag rents, not because absolute rents exceed Dubai. A one-bed that rents for AED 32K to 42K/year against AED 400K to 520K ticket produces the 7 to 10% band investors quote in broker decks.

## Which Fujairah areas deliver the best yields?

### Sakamkam

Core yield corridor for studios and one-beds. Port staff, government employees, and retail workers dominate tenancy. Gross 7.5 to 10% on AED 250K to 550K tickets when fit-out is functional.

### Merashid corridor

Similar yield math with more industrial adjacency. Logistics firms, quarry contractors, and free-zone back-office staff rent here for affordability versus beach premiums. Yields of 7 to 9% gross are common on 2010-era mid-rises.

| Factor | Sakamkam | Merashid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gross yield | 7.5-10% | 7-9% |
| Tenant mix | Port, gov, retail | Logistics, industrial |
| Building age | Mixed | Often 2005-2015 |
| Resale depth | Slightly better | Similar thin |
| Noise / truck routes | Lower | Verify location |

Choose Merashid when price per sqft undercuts Sakamkam by 8% or more on comparable size; choose Sakamkam when resale to end-users matters because name recognition is stronger among local agents.

### Dibba border districts

Cross-border trade, fishing support, and industrial suppliers drive tenancy. Gross yields can touch 7.5 to 9% on units priced AED 280K to 450K. Underwrite conservatively: model 5 to 6 weeks void, inspect truck traffic, confirm freehold status project by project.

### Beach and waterfront stock

Gross yields typically compress to 6 to 7.5% because entry prices carry a lifestyle premium. Pure yield investors often prefer inland Sakamkam unless they buy at a discount to portal asking prices.

Net example: AED 480,000 purchase, AED 38,000 rent, AED 9,200 costs (service charge, management, one month void) yields AED 28,800 net, about 6.0% net. Push void to two months in a poorly managed tower and net can fall toward 5.2%.

## Who are the port and logistics tenants?

Port of Fujairah staff, bunkering firms, stevedoring contractors, and free-zone logistics operators within a 20-minute drive anchor demand. Lease norms: predominantly 12 months, cheques 1 to 4 per year, one-month deposit common. Unlike Dubai, Ejari depth is shallow. Pull rent evidence from closed broker deals in the same building, not portal asking prices alone.

Citability block (June 2026): Fujairah buy-to-let underwriting should assume a locally employed tenant, not a Dubai commuter saving on rent. Median one-bedroom rent in Sakamkam runs roughly AED 30,000 to 42,000 per year against purchase prices of AED 350,000 to 520,000, producing 7 to 9% gross before fees. Vacancy of four to six weeks per year is a prudent base case; two months is appropriate for border industrial stock or towers with deferred maintenance. Net yield after 5 to 8% management, AED 5 to 12/sqft service charges, and void typically lands at 5.5 to 7.5% on mid-market apartments.

## How do Fujairah yields compare to Dubai and Ajman?

| Market | Gross headline | Liquidity | Tenant depth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fujairah Sakamkam | 7 to 10% | Thin (90 to 180 days) | Local employment |
| Dubai JVC | 6 to 8% | Deep | Ejari-backed |
| Ajman border | 7 to 9% | Moderate | Dubai commuters |

Choose Fujairah when you want east coast diversification and can hold through thin exits. Choose Ajman when Dubai commuter depth matters most. Choose Dubai when net AED income at scale and fast resale dominate your return model.

## What costs turn gross yield into net?

| Cost line | Typical range |
| --- | --- |
| Property management | 5 to 8% of rent |
| Service charge | AED 5 to 12/sqft |
| Void | 4 to 6 weeks base case |
| Agent renewal | 5% common on new tenant |
| Maintenance reserve | 2 to 3% of annual rent |

Use [how to calculate rental yield Dubai](/guides/how-to-calculate-rental-yield-dubai/) formulas with Fujairah inputs. Rule: gross above 8% often nets 6 to 7% when charges stay moderate. Gross above 10% without inspection usually signals deferred capex, illegal splits, or inflated rent comps.

## What freehold checks are mandatory for foreign buyers?

Verify freehold title, SPA clauses, and Fujairah Municipality registration before purchase. Do not assume every tower in Sakamkam is foreign-eligible. Confirm project name on title matches marketing name on portals.

## How thin is Fujairah resale?

| Hold horizon | Realistic exit |
| --- | --- |
| under 24 months | High discount risk |
| 3-5 years | Moderate discount |
| 5-7 years | Yield carried return |
| 7+ years | Appreciation optional |

## What risks should you model?

- Older towers without documented maintenance reserves
- Illegal partitioning triggering municipality action
- FEWA summer spikes on poorly insulated units
- Single-employer tenant concentration in border stock

## When should you pick Fujairah over Ajman or Dubai?

Choose Fujairah if:

- East coast exposure fits your portfolio
- Tenants are locally employed, not Dubai commuters
- You accept 5+ year hold and thin resale

Choose Dubai instead if:

- Resale within 12 months is possible
- You need Ejari-backed rent comps
- Golden Visa qualifying stock matters

## What does a three-year Sakamkam cash flow look like?

Model purchase AED 450,000, rent AED 36,000/year, costs AED 8,800 (management, service charge, one month void). Net about AED 27,200 (6.0% net). IRR improves with hold, not flip. Treat capital appreciation as optional; Fujairah price growth has lagged Dubai hotspots in most recent cycles.

Stress-test at minus 10% rent and plus one month void: net can compress toward 5.0%.

## What registration and compliance steps apply?

Fujairah Municipality tenancy registration and FEWA utility rules differ from Dubai Ejari. Confirm landlord registration requirements with your property manager before first tenant move-in. STR and holiday-home rules are emirate-specific; do not copy Dubai DET assumptions.

## How should you stress-test yield assumptions?

Run three scenarios: (1) base case with current asking rents and 5-week void, (2) downside with rents 10% below asking and 8-week void, (3) upside with 5% rent growth and 3-week void. Factor 5% agency commission on each new tenancy, landlord insurance AED 500 to 1,500 per year, and maintenance reserve 2 to 3% of annual rent. If downside net falls below 5%, renegotiate price or walk.

Track FEWA tariff changes and cooling arrangements: chiller-free or included cooling lets you charge slightly higher rent versus units where tenants pay heavy summer bills.

## What furnishing level maximises Fujairah yield?

Port and logistics tenants often accept functional furnishing (beds, sofas, appliances) without Marina-grade finishes. Cap fit-out spend at 8 to 12 months of gross rent unless you target short corporate lets. Over-furnishing raises ticket price without moving achievable rent in Sakamkam studios.

## How do school and family demand affect yields?

Families employed locally may pay modest premia for 2BR units near SPEA schools, but school-driven demand is thinner than Dubai KHDA corridors. Investors chasing family rent should verify school bus routes and employer housing allowances in the tenant's contract before underwriting premium rent.

## What monitoring should you run after purchase?

Review achieved rent versus underwrite every 12 months, track void days, and compare portal listings only as upper bound, not market mid. If two consecutive renewals require rent cuts, reassess exit timing early because Fujairah discounts on resale can exceed one year of lost yield.

## Who should not buy Fujairah yield stock?

Skip Fujairah if you need Dubai Ejari depth for bank leverage models, if your tenant strategy assumes Sharjah-Dubai commuters, or if you must exit within 24 months. Skip border industrial micro-locations if you cannot inspect truck routes and dust exposure in person.
