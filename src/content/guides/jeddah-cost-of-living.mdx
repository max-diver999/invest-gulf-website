## How to use this guide

This spoke answers jeddah cost of living in spend terms, what households pay on the Red Sea coast, not what tourism campaigns imply about giga-projects north of the city.

Copy the budget tables into a spreadsheet. Replace indicative rents with three live listings per compound or Corniche tower. Amortise school fees: annual tuition ÷ 12, plus transport, uniforms and registration.

Two families on the same SAR 40,000 gross can land SAR 28,000 or SAR 44,000 monthly spend. North Obhur compound plus American premium is not the same universe as south Jeddah apartment plus Indian mid-tier. Model both before you negotiate your package.

Saudi Arabia is opening fast on Law M/14 zones, Premium Residency ~SAR 4M (confirm current official rules), entertainment and women driving, but conservative public norms remain. Jeddah feels relatively more coastal-open in daily rhythm; **public law and dress expectations still apply everywhere in the Kingdom**.

## Jeddah vs Riyadh: why COL is not a simple “cheaper coast” story

Humidity and Corniche premiums can erase a “coastal discount.” Compare on your actual commute and school shortlist.

| Factor | Jeddah | Riyadh |
|---|---|---|
| Climate | Humid coastal heat; AC load higher | Hot dry summers; cooler winters |
| Rent, premium | Corniche / Obhur premiums | DQ / north compound premiums |
| Rent, mid | Older compound stock, negotiable | Legacy compounds, Vision 2030 influx |
| Schools | Established private pool | Fast-growing international tier |
| Transport | Corniche traffic; Hajj season spikes | Ring-road commutes; metro rollout |
| Leisure spend | Sea-facing dining, Red Sea trips | Entertainment city, Diriyah |
| Job market | Logistics, healthcare, tourism | Government, finance, HQ moves |
| “Liberal” feel (public) | Relatively more coastal, still KSA | Conservative; rapid social opening |

Rule: Job location decides city, not rent headlines. A Yanbu or Red Sea Project rotation with Jeddah base rent is a commute and per-diem problem, not a COL spreadsheet line.

## Currency, tax and coastal cost drivers

- SAR pegged to USD ~3.75 since 1986, stable for USD earners.
- 0% personal income tax on typical employment, corporate, withholding and zakat differ for business owners; specialist advice if not pure employee.
- Premium Residency: ~SAR 4 million investment track cited,  (confirm current official rules); separate from standard iqama.
- Foreign property: Law M/14 designated zones only, regulations evolving through 2026  (confirm current official rules); never assume Dubai-wide freehold.
- Social costs: no legal alcohol retail, entertainment spend shifts to ticketed events, compound life, family travel, Umrah/Hajj season logistics.
- Humidity premium: electricity for dehumidification and AC often 10–20% above Riyadh for same apartment size, model utilities separately.

## Rent by area: Corniche, compounds and Obhur

## Compound vs Corniche apartment vs city flat: COL trade-offs

Jeddah splits three ways: Obhur compound villa life, Corniche tower views, inland city value. Pick geography before you compare SAR numbers.

| Choice | Monthly cost | Lifestyle | Family fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legacy compound | Mid-high | Pools, buses, expat density | Strong for new arrivals |
| Corniche tower | High | Views, dining walkable | Couples; traffic at peak |
| City apartment | Mid | Shorter hospital/HQ commute | Teens may want compound social |
| Obhur coastal | Mid-premium | Beach proximity | Verify school bus routes |

Cultural note: Compounds offer private social space within conservative public norms, many families accept higher rent to reduce daily friction. That is a values and safety choice, not only a luxury line item.

## Red Sea lifestyle: what actually hits your budget

“Red Sea lifestyle” in Jeddah is not a daily Maldives substitute. It is:

- Corniche walks and cafés, modest recurring dining spend
- Private beach clubs / hotel day passes, episodic SAR 200–800 per visit  (confirm current official rules)
- Diving and boat trips, seasonal SAR 300–1,500 per outing
- Weekend travel, Riyadh flights, Bahrain causeway, UAE trips, budget SAR 2,000–8,000/quarter for mobile families
- Umrah / Hajj season, traffic, hotel inflation, leave from work; not optional for many Muslim households
- Humidity wardrobe / AC maintenance, hidden SAR 200–500/month

Compare entertainment assumptions with UAE only if you accept no alcohol retail and modest public dress everywhere outside compound gates.

## Transport: cars, fuel and Hajj-season friction

Jeddah spreads along the coast, Uber-only rarely survives school runs. Hajj and Ramadan reshape traffic; test your commute in peak season, not January.

| Item | SAR/month (indicative) |
|---|---|
| Car lease (mid sedan) | 1,800–2,800 |
| Fuel (one car, commute) | 600–1,000 |
| Second car | double fuel + insurance slice |
| Parking (Corniche towers) | 200–500 |

Driver vs self-drive: many families use compound buses for schools; private driver SAR 2,500–4,500/month still common for dual-income households  (confirm current official rules).

Hajj / Umrah peaks: road closures and surge pricing, add stress buffer, not just calendar glamour.

## Seasonal and religious calendar: hidden COL

| Period | Budget impact |
|---|---|
| Ramadan | Shorter work hours; dining shifts; grocery patterns change |
| Hajj | Traffic, hotel inflation if family visits; leave from work |
| Umrah weekends | Road congestion to holy sites, fuel and time cost |
| Summer (Jun–Sep) | Peak AC, add 15–25% to utility line vs winter |
| School August start | Registration + bus + uniform lump sums |

Families who underestimate religious calendar friction overspend on last-minute flights and temporary housing, model SAR 3,000–8,000/year buffer.

## Single vs family: sensitivity notes for planners

Singles: city apartments near hospitals (KFSH, Dr Soliman Fakeeh belt) reduce commute; social life inside compounds or vetted cafés, public nightlife is not Dubai.

Families: compound pools and school buses justify rent premium; teens need honest conversation about dress, dating norms and online behaviour, deportation risk is YMYL.

Women professionals: driving and workplace access improved; budget SAR 2,000–4,000 extra for driver if household chooses not to self-drive all school runs, personal choice, not legal mandate for all ** (confirm current official rules)**.
