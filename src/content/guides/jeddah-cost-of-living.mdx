## How to use this guide


Model grocery and domestic help in SAR using your actual compound quote, not Dubai habit costs.

Copy the budget tables into a spreadsheet. Replace indicative rents with three live listings per compound or Corniche tower. Amortise school fees: annual tuition ÷ 12, plus transport, uniforms and registration.

Two families on the same SAR 40,000 gross can land SAR 28,000 or SAR 44,000 monthly spend. North Obhur compound plus American premium is not the same universe as south Jeddah apartment plus Indian mid-tier. Model both before you negotiate your package.

Saudi Arabia is opening fast on Law M/14 zones, Premium Residency ~SAR 4M (confirm current official rules), entertainment and women driving, but conservative public norms remain. Jeddah feels relatively more coastal-open in daily rhythm; public law and dress expectations still apply everywhere in the Kingdom.

## What should you check on currency, tax and coastal cost drivers?

Saudi riyal is pegged to USD, so USD earners see stable rent in dollar terms. There is no personal income tax on employment income for most expats, but VAT applies on goods and services. Coastal humidity adds 10-20% to electricity versus inland Riyadh for similar housing size.

## How does rent by area work in Jeddah in 2026?

North Obhur and legacy compounds dominate expat family stock. Corniche towers suit couples who accept peak traffic. South Jeddah offers budget apartments with longer school bus routes.

## How does red sea lifestyle work in Jeddah in 2026?

Leisure spend tilts to Corniche walks, dive weekends, and regional flights rather than Dubai-style beach clubs. Budget dining and Red Sea activities separately from compound rent.

Compare entertainment assumptions with UAE only if you accept no alcohol retail and modest public dress everywhere outside compound gates.

## How does international schools work in Jeddah in 2026?

British and IB premium schools run SAR 70,000-120,000 per year per child. Employer caps often lag secondary years, model self-pay from Year 6 onward.

Add-ons: registration SAR 5,000-25,000 · bus SAR 8,000-15,000/year · uniforms · exam fees.

Monthly amortisation example: two children, British mid, SAR 50,000/year each → ~SAR 8,300/month before buses.

## What should you check on utilities, internet and coastal ac?

Budget higher AC load than Riyadh: dehumidification and coastal heat push electricity 10-20% above inland equivalents for the same villa size. Fibre internet is widely available in compounds and Corniche towers.

Absher-linked mobile simplifies government fines and visa messages, register number early  (confirm current official rules).

## What should you check on groceries, dining and household help?

Carrefour, Danube, and local markets cover most expat baskets. Live-in help is common in compound villas; budget SAR 2,500-4,500/month for full-time domestic help plus iqama costs via sponsor.

## How does transport work in Jeddah in 2026?

Two-car families are normal for compound life. Uber and Careem work in city belts; Obhur traffic spikes at school hours. Plan fuel and parking inside compounds separately from city commuting.

Hajj / Umrah peaks: road closures and surge pricing, add stress buffer, not just calendar glamour.

## What should you check on banking, iqama and first-60-days float?

Opening a Saudi bank account typically requires iqama, employer letter, and address proof from Ejar. Budget SAR 5,000-15,000 cash float for deposits, car registration, and furniture before first payroll lands. Premium Residency holders follow a separate onboarding path; confirm with your advisor.

## How does foreign property ownership affect COL in Jeddah?

Buyers in Law M/14 zones face mortgage, service, and maintenance lines renters skip. Verify zone eligibility with REGA before you model ownership COL against compound rent. Renters should still budget Ejar deposits and annual renewal fees separately from monthly rent.

## How does vision 2030 packages work in Jeddah in 2026?

Entertainment and tourism investment increases dining and leisure options but does not remove conservative public norms. Packages that include housing plus transport still need explicit school fee mapping for families.

## How does women and family col work in Jeddah in 2026?

Women drive, work, and access healthcare independently under current Saudi rules, but public dress and behaviour expectations remain conservative outside compounds. Family COL includes school bus timing and compound social infrastructure many Riyadh assignments omit.

Public life remains conservative despite coastal relative openness, YMYL accuracy matters for readers making life decisions.

## Jeddah vs Riyadh: quick COL verdict

Jeddah trades inland Riyadh savings for coastal humidity cost and Red Sea lifestyle. Corporate packages in Riyadh often include higher housing allowances; Jeddah packages may assume compound rent is "enough" without school top-up.

## What should you check on shopping, furniture and relocation shipment?

Allow SAR 12,000-35,000 fit-out for unfurnished city apartments. Shipping a full container from Europe or Asia adds SAR 15,000-40,000 depending on volume and customs clearance timing.

## What should you check on insurance, pets and misc monthly lines?

Pet import requires vaccination records and Saudi approval; budget vet and quarantine fees. Home contents insurance is optional but recommended for Corniche towers with sea exposure.

## How does rent negotiation on the jeddah coast work in Jeddah in 2026?

Legacy compound renewals negotiate best in August rotation season. Corniche premium stock moves faster; offer 12-month Ejar with clear maintenance clauses rather than aggressive discount asks only.

## How does seasonal and religious calendar work in Jeddah in 2026?

Ramadan and Hajj periods shift traffic, school hours, and flight prices. Families who underestimate religious calendar friction overspend on last-minute flights and temporary housing, model SAR 3,000-8,000/year buffer.

## Single vs family: sensitivity notes for planners

Singles can live mid-tier at SAR 8,500-15,000/month outside compounds if they skip premium schools. Families need compound or north belt housing plus school amortisation; do not compare single SAR 9,400 table to family premium Corniche life.

**Scenario A - single on corporate package:** compound optional, focus on Ejar, iqama, and car within first 60 days.

**Scenario B - family with two school-age children:** compound plus British mid schools drives SAR 30,000+ monthly all-in before help.
