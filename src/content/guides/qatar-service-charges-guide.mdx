Service charges are the number most Qatar property investors underestimate. Every pitch deck and every developer brochure quotes gross yield. None of them lead with the fact that service charges are the landlord's cost, apply 12 months a year whether the unit is occupied or vacant, and in premium Qatar buildings can absorb two to three months of rental income annually.

This guide gives you actual rates by area, worked yield calculations, and the specific questions to ask before any Qatar property purchase.

## How is the Qatar service charge structured?

Qatar freehold service charges are landlord-paid community fees funding shared facilities for 12 months a year, occupied or vacant. Standard inclusions cover security, lifts, pools, and building insurance, while Kahramaa stays outside the invoice. Hotel-managed stock often costs QAR 30-50/sqm/month. Foreign buyers with Invest Gulf start from invoices.

| Service category | Included | Not included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common area maintenance | Yes | In-unit repairs |
| Building security and CCTV | Yes | Private guard services |
| Shared pool, gym, landscaping | Yes | In-unit AC maintenance |
| Building insurance (common areas) | Yes | Contents insurance |
| Building management fees | Yes | Individual Kahramaa utility |
| Elevator maintenance | Yes | Private parking (some buildings) |
| Pest control (common areas) | Yes | Balcony repairs (owner) |

Hotel-managed branded residences in Lusail and The Pearl carry higher SC (often **QAR 30-50/sqm/month**) because hospitality operations sit inside the budget.

### Billing and who sets the rate

Unlike Dubai's Mollak registry (published SC per building), **Qatar has no central government SC portal**. Rates are set by:

1. **Developer** during handover and early years.
2. **Owners' association** once formed and voting on budgets.
3. **Property management company** where the PMC collects and enforces payment.

Request the **latest invoice**, not a verbal estimate, before you sign a purchase agreement.

## What are service charge rates by Qatar investment area?

Qatar service charge rates typically span QAR 15-30 per sqm per month across freehold areas, with The Pearl at 18-28, Lusail at 15-22, and West Bay at 20-30. On a 100 sqm apartment that means about QAR 18,000-36,000 yearly, often 1.5 to 2.5 percentage points of yield drag. Foreign buyers with Invest Gulf convert every quote before price talks.

| Area | Typical SC (QAR/sqm/month) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Pearl | 18-28 | Mature community, rising budgets |
| Lusail City | 15-22 | Introductory rates on newer phases |
| West Bay towers | 20-30 | Corporate tenant profile, premium amenities |

SC trend: The Pearl rates have increased roughly **10-15%** over three years as introductory budgets moved to full-cost recovery. Plan for gradual increases as infrastructure ages.

Insider tip: Our underwriting treats introductory Lusail SC as provisional. Request written year-3 and year-5 budgets; phases that look cheap at handover often rise 25 to 40% once pools, gyms, and full staffing hit the OA vote.

The Pearl SC is high relative to rental income. That is why Pearl net yields often lag Lusail despite strong tenant demand.

## How do you model gross-to-net yield in Qatar?

Gross-to-net yield modelling in Qatar typically starts with annual rent, then subtracts service charges, management near 6%, insurance near 0.2% of price, vacancy around 7%, and a maintenance reserve. A Lusail 110 sqm case can fall from 6.55% gross to 3.00% net. Foreign buyers with Invest Gulf assume that compression.

### Worked example: 2BR Lusail Marina, 110 sqm

| Item | Annual (QAR) | % of gross rent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gross rent | 72,000 | 100% |
| Service charges (QAR 20/sqm) | 26,400 | 36.7% |
| Property management (6%) | 4,320 | 6.0% |
| Insurance (0.2% of QAR 1.1M) | 2,200 | 3.1% |
| Vacancy (7%) | 5,040 | 7.0% |
| Maintenance reserve (1.5%) | 1,080 | 1.5% |
| **Total costs** | **39,040** | **54.2%** |
| **Net rental income** | **32,960** | - |
| Purchase price | 1,100,000 | - |
| **Net yield** | **3.00%** | - |
| Gross yield | 6.55% | - |
| Gross-to-net compression | 3.55 pp | - |

Gross-to-net compression of **3-4 percentage points** is normal for non-resident investors using a property manager.

## How do Qatar service charges compare with Dubai?

Qatar and Dubai premium service charges are broadly equivalent on absolute cost, but Qatar quotes monthly per sqm while Dubai Mollak publishes annual per sqft. Yield drag still typically clusters around 1.5 to 2.5 percentage points after unit conversion. Foreign buyers with we never compare brochure SC without converting units first.

| Market | How SC is quoted | Transparency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qatar freehold | QAR/sqm/month from developer or OA | No public registry; verify invoices |
| Dubai (Mollak) | AED/sqft/year on register | Published building-level SC |

The real operational difference is **transparency**: Dubai's [Mollak-linked data](/guides/dubai-property-taxes-explained/) lets buyers see registered SC before purchase. In Qatar you must request invoices, clearance certificates, and projected budgets directly.

## How can investors minimise service charge drag?

Investors minimise Qatar service charge drag by selecting smaller efficient units, avoiding unverified introductory rates, and rejecting branded residences unless rent covers SC that typically costs QAR 30-50/sqm/month. Demand the latest invoice, arrears history, and year-3 projections for the area. Foreign buyers with Invest Gulf put clearance certificates before transfer funds.

| Selection criterion | Effect on SC | Effect on rent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smaller sqm at same SC/sqm | Lower absolute SC | Lower rent |
| Older building, mature budget | More predictable | Slightly lower rent/sqm |
| New Lusail phase | Introductory SC, rise risk | Market rent from handover |
| Branded / hotel-managed | SC 30-50/sqm/month | 10-20% rent premium possible |
| Villa vs apartment | SC sometimes on land sqm | Different tenant pool |

### Due diligence documents to request

1. **Current SC invoice** (most recent quarter or year).
2. **Payment history** confirming no arrears that transfer with title.
3. **SC clearance certificate** at sale (zero balance).
4. **Projected SC budget** for Lusail off-plan (years 1, 3, 5).
5. **OA minutes** if formed, noting voted increases.

See [Qatar property due diligence](/guides/qatar-property-due-diligence/) for the wider purchase checklist.

## What red flags should pause a Qatar purchase?

Qatar purchase red flags on service charges typically include missing invoices, no clearance certificate at transfer, and gross yield marketing that ignores SC as a percentage of achievable rent. Pause when introductory rates lack a written year-3 projection, or when SC already exceeds 40% of rent for the area. Foreign buyers with Invest Gulf treat unauditable branded F&B budgets as a hard stop.

Other stops:

- Introductory SC quoted without written year-3 projection.
- Arrears disclosed only after MOU signature.
- Branded residence SC bundled with F&B you cannot audit.

The Pearl often sits at QAR 18-28/sqm/month, Lusail at QAR 15-22, and West Bay at QAR 20-30. On a Pearl 102 sqm two-bed bought near QAR 1.35 million with QAR 65,000 rent, SC near QAR 24/sqm/month costs about QAR 29,376 and alone removes roughly 2.17 percentage points from a 4.81% gross yield. Lusail can look better on paper, but introductory phases commonly rise 25 to 40% by year 3 once facilities are fully staffed. Convert every quote to an annual total, divide by realistic rent, and treat SC above 40% of rent as a negotiation problem, not a rounding error.

## How do service charges affect residency and hold planning?

Service charges remain QAR-denominated operating costs independent of Qatar residency rules, yet a vacant unit during visa processing still pays the full annual SC bill. Convert SC to an annual total and subtract from gross yield before you negotiate in this market. Foreign buyers with Invest Gulf model 6 to 12 months of hold cash explicitly for the area.

Pair operating cost models with [Qatar property investment guide](/guides/qatar-property-investment-guide/) and [Lusail City property investment](/areas/lusail-city-property-investment/) when you compare communities.

Request **Kahramaa** consumption history where the seller will share it: high AC load in summer can push tenant churn even when headline rent looks stable. That operating detail sits outside the service charge invoice but still affects net cash flow for landlords who pay turnover costs between leases.

Non-resident investors modelling Qatar net yield should assume service charges are the largest single recurring deduction after vacancy. A Lusail Marina 110 sqm example with QAR 72,000 gross rent, SC at QAR 20/sqm/month (QAR 26,400), 6% management, 0.2% insurance on a QAR 1.1 million price, 7% vacancy, and a 1.5% maintenance reserve leaves about QAR 32,960 net, or 3.00% on cost, versus 6.55% gross. Gross-to-net compression of 3 to 4 percentage points is normal when you are abroad and use a manager. Dubai comparisons need unit conversion because Mollak quotes AED per sqft per year while Qatar quotes QAR per sqm per month; after conversion, mid-market yield drag still clusters near 1.5 to 2.5 percentage points in both cities. Always demand the latest invoice and a clearance certificate dated within 30 days of transfer.
