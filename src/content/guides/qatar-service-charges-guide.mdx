Quick answer: Qatar freehold service charges run QAR 15–30 per sqm per month - QAR 18,000–36,000/year on a 100 sqm apartment. The Pearl: QAR 18–28/sqm/month, Lusail City: QAR 15–22/sqm/month, West Bay towers: QAR 20–30/sqm/month. Expect service charges alone to cut 1.5–2.5 percentage points from gross rental yield before any other deductions.

Service charges are the number most Qatar property investors underestimate. Every pitch deck and every developer brochure quotes gross yield. None of them lead with the fact that service charges are the landlord's cost, apply 12 months a year whether the unit is occupied or vacant, and in premium Qatar buildings can absorb two to three months of rental income annually.

This guide gives you actual rates by area, worked yield calculations, and the specific questions to ask before any Qatar property purchase.

---

## Qatar Service Charge Structure: How It Works

### What Service Charges Cover

Qatar freehold community service charges fund the operation of shared facilities and common areas in the development. Standard inclusions:

| Service category | Included | Not included |
|---|---|---|
| Common area maintenance | Yes | In-unit repairs |
| Building security and CCTV | Yes | Private guard services |
| Shared pool, gym, landscaping | Yes | In-unit AC maintenance |
| Building insurance (common areas) | Yes | Contents insurance |
| Building management fees | Yes | Individual Kahramaa utility |
| Elevator maintenance | Yes | Private parking (some buildings) |
| Pest control (common areas) | Yes | Balcony repairs (owner) |

For hotel-managed branded residences - increasingly common in Lusail and The Pearl - service charges also incorporate hospitality operations: concierge, pool-side service, and front desk. These carry materially higher SC rates: QAR 30–50/sqm/month versus standard residential QAR 15–28.

### Service Charge Billing Structure in Qatar

Unlike Dubai's Mollak system (which registers SC rates in a government database), Qatar does not have a centralised service charge registry. Rates are set by:

1. **Developer (off-plan and newly handed-over)**: Developer sets the initial budget during handover period.
2. **Owners' Association (established communities)**: Once formed, OA sets annual budgets and votes on SC increases.
3. **Property Management Company**: In some developments, a contracted PMC sets and collects charges directly.

This means SC data must be verified directly with the developer or managing agent - you cannot look it up in a government portal as you can with Dubai's Mollak.

## Service Charge Rates by Qatar Investment Area

### The Pearl-Qatar: Established Premium Community

The Pearl is Qatar's most mature freehold community, with SC structures that reflect 10+ years of operational history. Rates vary significantly by precinct and tower quality.

| The Pearl precinct | Building type | SC range (QAR/sqm/month) | Annual SC on 100 sqm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Porto Arabia | High-rise, marina front | 22–28 | QAR 26,400–33,600 |
| Viva Bahriya | High-rise, sea view | 20–26 | QAR 24,000–31,200 |
| Qanat Quartier | Low-rise, canal facing | 18–24 | QAR 21,600–28,800 |
| Pérle, Al Mutahidah | Mid-rise, community | 16–22 | QAR 19,200–26,400 |
| Giardino Villas | Villa complex | 12–18 (per sqm land) | QAR varies by plot |

SC trend: The Pearl SC rates have increased approximately 10–15% over the past three years as original introductory rates moved to full-cost recovery budgets. Expect further gradual increases as infrastructure ages.

**Yield impact on The Pearl 2BR (1,100 sqft / 102 sqm):**

| Metric | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Purchase price | QAR 1,350,000 |
| Annual rent | QAR 65,000 |
| Gross yield | 4.81% |
| Service charges (QAR 24/sqm/month) | QAR 29,376 |
| SC as % of rental income | 45.2% |
| SC yield drag | 2.17 pp |
| Gross yield after SC only | 2.64% |

The Pearl SC is high relative to rental income - this is the structural reason Pearl net yields lag behind Lusail despite strong tenant demand.

### Lusail City: Lower SC, Higher Gross Yield

Lusail City offers Qatar's best current yield-to-service-charge ratio because: (a) the area is newer, with facilities not yet at full operational cost, and (b) property prices per sqm are lower than The Pearl for comparable construction quality.

| Lusail district | Building type | SC range (QAR/sqm/month) | Annual SC on 100 sqm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fox Hills | Mixed residential | 15–20 | QAR 18,000–24,000 |
| Marina District | High-rise, waterfront | 18–25 | QAR 21,600–30,000 |
| Energy City North | Corporate residential | 14–19 | QAR 16,800–22,800 |
| Al Maha Island | Premium residential | 22–30 | QAR 26,400–36,000 |
| Lusail Boulevard | Mixed-use towers | 16–22 | QAR 19,200–26,400 |

Caveat: Several Lusail phases remain under construction or in early operation. SC rates for many buildings are still developer-introductory. Request the projected year-3 and year-5 SC budget from any Lusail developer before committing - introductory rates can increase 25–40% within three years as facilities reach full operation.

**Yield impact on Lusail 1BR (75 sqm):**

| Metric | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Purchase price | QAR 750,000 |
| Annual rent | QAR 46,000 |
| Gross yield | 6.13% |
| Service charges (QAR 18/sqm/month) | QAR 16,200 |
| SC as % of rental income | 35.2% |
| SC yield drag | 2.16 pp |
| Gross yield after SC only | 3.97% |

Lusail delivers better absolute gross yield than The Pearl, but SC drag is similar in percentage-of-income terms. The higher gross yield is what creates the better net outcome.

### West Bay: Premium Office District, Higher SC

West Bay towers cater primarily to corporate tenants - regional HQ employees, embassy staff, and international finance professionals. Building quality is high, amenities are premium, and SC reflects this.

| West Bay characteristic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Typical SC range | QAR 20–30/sqm/month |
| Average unit size | 120–180 sqm (larger corporate units) |
| Annual SC on 140 sqm unit | QAR 33,600–50,400 |
| Typical annual rent (2BR) | QAR 80,000–110,000 |
| SC as % of rental income | 30–50% |

West Bay makes sense for investors prioritising tenant quality and lease term stability over net yield maximisation. Corporate tenants pay on time, maintain units well, and sign multi-year leases. The yield tradeoff is real but the management intensity is significantly lower than The Pearl short-let market.

## Gross-to-Net Yield: Full Qatar Cost Stack

Service charges are one deduction. The full Qatar ownership cost structure:

| Cost item | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Service charges | QAR 15–30/sqm/month | Landlord cost, applies when vacant |
| Property management | 5–8% of annual rent | Required for non-resident investors |
| Municipality tax | 5% of annual rent | Typically tenant-paid in Qatar leases |
| Insurance (contents + liability) | 0.1–0.3% of property value | Owner's cost |
| Vacancy allowance | 5–10% of potential rent | Conservative Gulf estimate |
| Maintenance reserve | 1–2% of annual rent | Budget for in-unit wear |
| Kahramaa connection (one-off) | QAR 1,000–3,000 | Set-up only |

### Full Worked Example: 2BR in Lusail Marina, 110 sqm

| Item | Annual (QAR) | % of gross rent |
|---|---|---|
| Gross rent | 72,000 | 100% |
| Service charges (QAR 20/sqm) | 26,400 | 36.7% |
| Property management (6%) | 4,320 | 6.0% |
| Insurance (0.2% of QAR 1.1M) | 2,200 | 3.1% |
| Vacancy (7% of gross) | 5,040 | 7.0% |
| Maintenance reserve (1.5%) | 1,080 | 1.5% |
| **Total costs** | **39,040** | **54.2%** |
| **Net rental income** | **32,960** | - |
| Purchase price | 1,100,000 | - |
| **Net yield** | **3.00%** | - |
| Gross yield | 6.55% | - |
| Gross-to-net compression | 3.55 pp | - |

This example is not pessimistic - it reflects realistic Lusail numbers. Gross-to-net yield compression of 3–4 percentage points is the Qatar norm for non-resident investors using a property manager.

## Service Charges vs Dubai: Meaningful Comparison

| Market | SC measurement | Typical residential SC | Equivalent annual cost (100 sqm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qatar (The Pearl) | QAR/sqm/month | 18–28 | QAR 21,600–33,600 |
| Qatar (Lusail) | QAR/sqm/month | 15–22 | QAR 18,000–26,400 |
| Dubai (Mollak mid-market) | AED/sqft/year | 12–20 | AED 11,148–18,580 (≈ QAR 11,300–18,800) |
| Dubai (Mollak premium/Marina) | AED/sqft/year | 20–35 | AED 18,580–32,515 (≈ QAR 18,800–32,900) |

On an absolute basis, Qatar and Dubai premium SC are broadly equivalent. Qatar SC looks higher when quoted monthly; Dubai SC looks lower because it is quoted annually and in AED (which has a fixed 3.64 QAR parity).

The real difference is how each market handles transparency: Dubai's [Mollak system](/guides/dubai-property-taxes-explained/) gives buyers access to actual registered SC before purchase. Qatar requires you to ask the developer directly and verify with a term sheet, creating information asymmetry that favours buyers who know to ask.

For a detailed UAE comparison, see [Gross vs Net Yield Dubai](/guides/gross-vs-net-yield-dubai/).

## Minimising Service Charge Drag: Practical Approaches

### Unit Selection Strategy

SC drag varies more by building than by area. Within The Pearl, a Qanat Quartier unit at QAR 18/sqm/month performs 25–35% better on SC than a Porto Arabia tower at QAR 26/sqm/month - for what may be only a 10–15% difference in achievable rent.

| Selection criterion | Effect on SC | Effect on rent |
|---|---|---|
| Smaller sqm, same SC/sqm rate | Lower absolute SC | Proportionally lower rent |
| Older building, mature SC | More predictable, often stable | Slightly lower rent per sqm |
| Newer Lusail building | Introductory SC - risk of increase | Market rent from day one |
| Hotel-managed branded residence | SC 30–50/sqm/month | 10–20% premium rent |
| Villa vs apartment (same area) | SC often per land sqm (lower) | Larger tenancy, corporate demand |

### Due Diligence on SC Before Purchase

Request these documents from the seller or developer:

1. **Current SC invoice**: the most recent quarterly or annual bill, not a verbal estimate.
2. **SC payment history**: confirm there are no arrears that transfer with the property.
3. **SC clearance certificate**: confirm zero balance at transfer. Without this, you inherit the debt.
4. **Projected SC budget (Lusail)**: ask for year 1, 2, and 5 projections from developer.
5. **Owners' association minutes (if formed)**: any voted SC increases on the agenda?

For more on Qatar property purchase process, see [Qatar property due diligence](/guides/qatar-property-due-diligence/).

## Red Flags in Qatar Service Charge Data

- **No SC invoice shown on request**: Developer or agent quoting a number without a document. Get the bill.
- **Lusail SC quoted "as-is" without growth projection**: Many Lusail buildings are still at introductory rates. Phase 2 and 3 of facilities will push costs up.
- **Branded residence SC quoted without breakdown**: Some managed residences include hotel F&B subsidies in SC budgets. Verify what is included.
- **SC arrears from previous owner**: Qatar's clearance process is less standardised than UAE's. Include an SC zero-balance certificate as a purchase condition.
- **Annual SC below QAR 10,000 on a QAR 1M+ property**: Implausibly low - either an error, an introductory pre-operational rate, or the quote excludes some charge categories.

## Qatar Service Charges and Residency Calculations

For investors using Qatar property for residency qualification, note that the QAR 730,000 minimum refers to property value at MOJ registration - not equity after deducting service charge liabilities. However, unpaid SC arrears can create title encumbrances that complicate the residency application process.

Keep SC payments current if you are using the property to underpin Qatar residency status. For residency pathway details, see [Qatar residency by property](/guides/qatar-residency-by-property/).

## Summary: Qatar Service Charge Decision Framework

| Investor priority | Best SC choice | Areas to consider |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum net yield | Lowest SC/sqm, moderate rent | Lusail Fox Hills, Lusail Energy City North |
| Net yield + liquidity | Balanced SC, established market | The Pearl Qanat Quartier, Pérle |
| Tenant quality over yield | Higher SC, premium facilities | West Bay, The Pearl Porto Arabia |
| Long-term appreciation play | Newer, lower SC now (risk: increases later) | Lusail Marina District, Al Maha |
| Hands-off, corporate tenant | Hotel-managed SC fully included | Branded Lusail residences |

The key discipline: always convert the SC quote to an annual absolute figure, calculate it as a percentage of the actual achievable rent (not listed yield), and factor it into your net yield before negotiating purchase price.
