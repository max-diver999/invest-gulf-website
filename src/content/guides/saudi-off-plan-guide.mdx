Saudi Arabia's off-plan market launched with Law M/14 in January 2026, allowing foreign ownership in Real Estate General Authority (REGA) designated zones. Unlike Dubai's mature off-plan ecosystem or Qatar's established Pearl/Lusail developments, Saudi represents a first-generation market with significant Vision 2030 infrastructure backing but limited transaction history.

The off-plan landscape splits between ROSHN communities (residential-focused, nearer-term delivery) and giga-projects (NEOM, Red Sea Project, Qiddiya) with transformational scale but decade-long timelines. All non-Saudi purchases require REGA zone verification and escrow protection, but implementing regulations continue evolving through 2026-2027.

This guide covers the Saudi off-plan purchase process, REGA requirements, payment structures, major project types, delivery expectations, and risk-return positioning for early-market investors.

## How does Saudi off-plan work under Law M/14?

Saudi off-plan for foreign buyers is lawful only in REGA-designated zones under Law M/14 from January 2026, and we require project-level registration plus escrow before any SAR deposit. Early-market diligence standards apply because transaction history is still thin versus Dubai's 2 to 5 year average delivery book.

- Confirm designated zone status for the exact phase
- Confirm developer REGA licence and escrow bank
- Model 0% rent until handover

## What REGA checks run before you pay a deposit?

REGA checks before deposit are a hard gate, and Invest Gulf walks away when foreign buyers cannot obtain current zone verification, developer licence proof, and escrow bank details within 14 days. SPA language must cite registration numbers, escrow IBANs, and milestone definitions before any SAR wire of 10% or more.

Red flag: Any developer unable to provide current REGA zone verification should be avoided.

### Step 2: Developer Due Diligence

### Step 3: Sales and Purchase Agreement

- REGA registration numbers and zone designation

Legal review essential: engage Saudi property counsel given evolving rules.

## How do Saudi off-plan payment plans and escrow work?

Saudi off-plan payment plans typically start with 10% to 20% booking and end with 30% to 50% at handover, and we model ROSHN-style 2 to 4 year schedules separately from giga plans that stretch 5 to 15 years. REGA escrow release follows independent milestone certification.

| Payment stage | Percentage | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Booking and SPA | 20% | Month 0 |
| Foundation complete | 15% | Months 6-12 |
| Structure to 50% | 15% | Months 12-18 |
| Structure complete | 20% | Months 18-30 |
| Handover | 30% | Months 30-48 |

### Giga-Project Payment Plans (Extended Structures)

| Payment stage | Percentage | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Booking | 10% | Month 0 |
| Construction phases | 40% | Years 1-5 |
| Handover | 20% | Years 5-10 |
| Post-handover | 30% | Years 10-15 |

Mandatory escrow protection:

1. Independent consultant certifies construction milestone
2. REGA reviews certification and approves release
3. Escrow bank transfers funds to developer
4. Buyer notification of payment release and progress

## How long do ROSHN vs giga-project deliveries take?

ROSHN-style residential delivery typically runs 2 to 4 years, while giga-project residential phases often need 5 to 15 years, and we add a 12 to 24 month buffer. Historical ROSHN-era phases show roughly 70% on-time delivery with 25% delayed under 12 months in completed samples.

Current project expectations:

- 2-bedroom apartments: 30 to 40 months
- 3-4 bedroom units: 36 to 48 months
- Villa communities: 42 to 60 months

NEOM major districts can extend 8 to 15 years; Red Sea Phase 1 hotels and residences often model 3 to 5 years.

## What due diligence checklist runs before SPA signature?

Pre-SPA diligence should cover developer backing, REGA escrow, conservative delivery buffers of 12 to 24 months, and 0% rent during construction, and we will not green-light a SAR deposit for foreign buyers on verbal zone claims. Plan cash capability and a 5 to 10 year hold minimum.

**Developer assessment:**

- Review PIF vs private backing
- Check completed Saudi residential track record
- Verify REGA escrow account and bank details

**Project analysis:**

- Add 12 to 24 month buffer to brochure delivery
- Review infrastructure dependencies
- Plan zero rental income during construction

**Financial planning:**

- Budget full cash payment capability
- Model SAR FX exposure
- Consider Premium Residency only if the SAR 4 million track is separately confirmed

## What buyer scenarios should you stress-test before you commit?

Buyer scenarios should separate ROSHN 2 to 4 year holds from giga-project 10 year bets, and Invest Gulf stress-tests Premium Residency overlap before foreign buyers size deposits toward SAR 4 million. Liquidity on resale remains limited in all three paths over the first 36 months of ownership.

**Scenario B (giga-project token exposure):** Lower upfront, longer post-handover tail, higher execution risk across 10+ years.
