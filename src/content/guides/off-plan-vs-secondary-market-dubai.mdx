## What should buyers verify on three transaction lanes in dubai property?

Dubai has three distinct purchase lanes in 2026: primary off-plan from a developer (Oqood registration, instalments, construction risk), ready secondary resale (title deed transfer at DLD, immediate possession), and off-plan assignment (resale of an Oqood contract before handover). Gulf investors lose money when agents blur these labels because fees, liquidity, and yield proof differ on each lane.


Brokers often say "secondary" when they mean assignment, clarify which registration type changes hands. Assignment mechanics: [Off-Plan Assignment Sale Dubai](/guides/off-plan-assignment-sale-dubai/).
## What should buyers verify on 2026 market structure?

Off-plan still dominates transaction volume, often cited at 60-70% of 205,000+ annual DLD deals, but secondary liquidity in mature towers remains the benchmark for net yield. Launch marketing pushes primary stock; disciplined buyers anchor decisions to Ejari and Mollak on completed buildings before accepting developer pro formas.


## How does this comparison stack up for Gulf investors?

Off-plan can win on instalment cash flow and launch pricing when the developer tier and escrow are verified. Secondary wins when you need Ejari income within 90 days, bank financing on existing title, or a verified service charge history from Mollak. Assignment sits between: faster than waiting for handover, thinner than Marina resale.


- Post-handover payment tails embed financing cost
- Service charge estimates understate 30-50% vs Mollak reality
- Premium for branded residences (DAMAC, Address) compresses net yield

### Secondary market pricing

Resale prices reflect:

- DLD transacted data (not listing ask)
- Building-specific service charges from Mollak
- Tenant quality and vacancy history
- Seller motivation (distress, relocation, portfolio trim)

In supply-heavy 2026 pockets, certain Business Bay towers, older JLT stock, secondary can undercut new off-plan launches in the same district when sellers accept 5-12% below 2022 peaks.

### Assignment market pricing

Assignments trade on sentiment + instalments paid:

- Hot Tier 1 launch: premium to original SPA
- Oversupplied mid-market: discount to cost basis
- Liquidity thinner than ready secondary, fewer bid participants

## What should buyers verify on liquidity comparison?

Secondary stock in Marina, JLT, and Downtown trades daily with transparent DLD comparables. Off-plan liquidity before handover depends on developer NOC policy and assignment demand, which can disappear in oversupplied launches. If you need exit within 24 months, verify assignment premiums or buy secondary in a liquid tower.


Investor rule: if exit within 24 months matters, secondary in liquid buildings beats off-plan unless assignment demand is verified pre-purchase.

## What should Gulf buyers budget for cash flow and yield timing?

### Secondary cash flow

- Immediate Ejari registration possible
- Gross yield 5-9% by area (JVC high, Palm lower)
- Net yield 3-6% after service charges (10-25% of gross drag), vacancy (~7% citywide), management (5-10%)

| Community | Gross yield | Net yield (typical) |
|---|---|---|
| JVC | 7.5-9.2% | 5.4-7.1% |
| Dubai Sports City | 7.8-9.5% | 5.7-7.4% |
| Dubai Marina | 5.5-7.2% | 4.0-5.5% |
| Downtown | 5.0-6.5% | 4.8-5.5% |

Secondary buyers model net on day one using Mollak. Off-plan buyers guess until handover.

## How does fee stack compare for Gulf buyers in 2026?

Both lanes pay 4% DLD, but timing differs: off-plan often registers Oqood at SPA signing while secondary pays at trustee transfer. Secondary buyers commonly pay 2% broker commission plus VAT; off-plan launches sometimes absorb broker fees inside unit price. Model total cash to keys, not brochure net.


Off-plan appears cheaper when developer absorbs broker, but embedded in unit price.
## What should buyers verify on risk matrix?

Off-plan risk concentrates in construction delay, specification drift, and SPA penalty clauses. Secondary risk shifts to building condition, undisclosed service charges, special assessments, and seller misrepresentation. Tier 1 escrow off-plan is structurally protected but not risk-free; secondary removes builder risk but not building governance risk.


## What should buyers verify on mortgage and financing?

UAE banks lend on secondary title with known completion dates. Off-plan financing follows milestone schedules and handover timelines, with EIBOR lock risk if handover slips. Non-residents face lower LTV caps on both lanes. Confirm bank approved building list before you assume leverage on either path.


## What should Gulf buyers budget for golden visa and residency timing?

Golden Visa property routes reference registered value at DLD, which may attach to Oqood on off-plan or title on secondary. Mortgaged units below equity thresholds may not qualify until loan balance drops. Plan visa filing separately from SPA signing and confirm ICP rules on your exact registration type.


## What should buyers verify on buyer profile matching?

Choose off-plan if you can absorb a 2-5 year income gap, want newer specification, and trust the developer escrow track record. Choose secondary if you need immediate rent, verified net yield, or mortgage on existing title. Assignments suit experienced traders who understand NOC fees and thin bid pools.


## What should buyers verify on due diligence split?

Off-plan diligence focuses on RERA escrow account, developer delivery history, SPA penalties, and milestone schedule. Secondary diligence focuses on DLD encumbrances, snagging, Mollak charge trends, Ejari comps on identical floor plates, and owners association minutes. Both need independent legal review above AED 2M exposure.


### Secondary checklist

1. DLD Unit Profile: encumbrances, ownership match
2. Mollak service charge: 3-year trend
3. Physical inspection + snagging report
4. Ejari rent comps: same floor plate
5. OA minutes: special assessments, litigation
6. Seller motivation: how long listed, prior reductions

## What should buyers verify on 2026 relative value verdict?


- Payment plan cash-flow for buyers without full cash
- New specification, smart home, amenities arms race
- Launch discounts in genuine undersupplied micro-locations

Secondary wins on:

- Certainty, what you see is what you own
- Net yield clarity, Mollak + Ejari = real numbers
- Mortgage access, immediate leverage
- Distressed pricing in correcting submarkets

The 2021-2023 "always buy off-plan" mantra is obsolete. Building-level analysis beats market-level generalisation.

## What should buyers verify on supply pipeline impact?

Heavy 2026 handover waves in Business Bay and JLT can compress rents and resale prices for both new off-plan completions and older secondary stock in the same district. Underwrite vacancy and competing supply from the same developer phase before you buy launch pricing as a discount.


## When Both Markets Fail

Both lanes fail when you buy the wrong building, not the wrong market label. Off-plan fails on weak escrow or fantasy handover dates. Secondary fails on towers with spiking service charges or special assessments. Walk away when RERA registration, escrow, or Mollak history cannot be verified in writing within 48 hours.


## What should buyers verify on decision framework (10 questions)?

Ask: hold period, need for Ejari income this year, developer tier, assignment NOC cost, total cash deployed including void years, mortgage LTV, Golden Visa goal, exit liquidity, service charge certainty, and SPA penalty exposure. If six or more answers point to income now, default to secondary for 12 months before revisiting off-plan.


## Transaction cost stack: off-plan vs secondary

| Cost line | Off-plan (typical) | Secondary (typical) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| DLD transfer fee | 4% at handover (or on Oqood for visa) | 4% at trustee appointment | Same rate, different timing |
| Agent commission | 2% on SPA value (often split) | 2% buyer-side common | Negotiate before viewing |
| Mortgage registration | At handover if financing | Immediate if financing | EIBOR lock risk on off-plan |
| Service charge deposit | First year estimate at handover | Pro-rata from completion date | Check Mollak history on secondary |
| Snagging / fit-out | Developer warranty period | Immediate if tenant-ready needed | Budget AED 15-40K for secondary refresh |
| Assignment fee (exit pre-handover) | 1-4% to developer + DLD | N/A | Kills flip margin if not modeled |

**Off-plan cash-flow trap:** paying 20-40% during construction with **zero rent** for 24-36 months means your true yield denominator is **total cash deployed including void**, not headline launch price.

**Secondary advantage:** Ejari rent and Mollak service charges are observable before offer, you can model net yield on day one instead of trusting developer pro formas.

## What should buyers verify on 2026 route picker (quick reference)?

Income now plus verified net yield points to secondary. Instalment deployment plus launch discount plus Tier 1 developer points to off-plan. Pre-handover flip only if assignment demand and NOC fees are modeled on paper. Hybrid portfolios often hold secondary for cash flow and off-plan for long-dated spec in different districts.


**Hard rule:** if RERA registration or escrow account cannot be verified in writing within 48 hours, a secondary purchase with independent survey beats any off-plan brochure.

**Data sources before you sign:** DLD transaction portal for comps, Mollak for service charges, Ejari for achieved rent on secondary; for off-plan, RERA project page + developer milestone schedule, not renderings.

## What checklist should run before you sign?

Confirm registration type (Oqood vs title), escrow or encumbrance clearance, fee stack in writing, broker commission payer, handover or possession date, snagging window, and exit plan if employment changes. Do not wire SPA deposits until RERA Form F and payment schedule match your model.


## What should buyers verify on key numbers to model (june 2026 planning)?

Model 4% DLD, trustee near AED 4,000, service charges from Mollak not marketing, vacancy near 7% citywide, management 5-10%, and 24-36 months zero rent on off-plan instalment paths. Secondary buyers can rebuild net yield with three Ejari comps in the same building before offer.

## Which lane fits your 2026 hold period and visa goal?

| Hold period | Primary goal | Favoured lane | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Under 3 years | Flexibility or trial | Secondary or rent | 6-9% acquisition stack needs time to amortise |
| 3-5 years | Ejari income plus exit option | Secondary in liquid tower | Mollak and DLD comps support resale pricing |
| 5+ years | Instalments plus new spec | Tier 1 off-plan with escrow | Launch discount if developer delivery track record is clean |
| Pre-handover exit | Trading only | Assignment with NOC modeled | Thin bids if project is oversupplied |

Golden Visa planners should confirm whether Oqood or title value counts toward AED 2 million before choosing lane. Mortgaged secondary units may qualify only after equity crosses the threshold, which pushes some buyers toward smaller freehold secondary tickets rather than large off-plan payment plans.

Portfolio builders often mix one secondary unit for cash flow with one off-plan ticket in a different district to avoid competing handover supply in the same cluster. That diversification does not remove building-level risk, but it avoids concentrating void years and assignment liquidity in a single registration type.

Before you wire any deposit, save DLD transaction screenshots, Mollak service charge statements, and RERA escrow confirmation in one folder. Gulf investors who document these three sources up front close trustee appointments faster and avoid renegotiating after SPA signing.
