## Schools — ADEK vs KHDA is not a quality score

Parents google **"ADEK vs KHDA"** expecting a winner. There isn't one — just **separate regulators** and overlapping curricula.

### What ADEK and KHDA actually do

| Regulator | Emirate | Parent-facing effect |
|---|---|---|
| **ADEK** | Abu Dhabi | Approves fees, inspects, publishes school guidance |
| **KHDA** | Dubai | Same function + highly visible **DSIB ratings** |

**Curricula exist in both:** British, American, IB, Indian CBSE, French, etc.

**Fee lever (KB §14):** ADEK mid-tier often **10–20% below** KHDA equivalent — on **two children**, that's **AED 15,000–40,000/year** saved at British mid, not pocket change.

### School choice breadth

| Need | Dubai (KHDA) | Abu Dhabi (ADEK) |
|---|---|---|
| **Compare 5 British Outstanding tiers** | Easy | Harder — fewer campuses |
| **Indian CBSE budget** | Many | Several |
| **IB continuum K-12** | Many | Several strong |
| **American AP track** | Many | Several |
| **Special needs depth** | Wider | Growing |
| **New school openings** | Frequent | Slower |

**Dubai wins** when your shortlist is **"visit ten schools, pick three"**. **Abu Dhabi wins** when your shortlist is **"Raha + Al Yasmina + one backup"** and **fee cap** matters.

### Waiting lists — both cities hurt

| City | Reality |
|---|---|
| **Dubai** | Outstanding KHDA — apply **12 months** ahead |
| **Abu Dhabi** | Top ADEK British/IB — **6–12 months** |

**Do not sign 12-month lease** anywhere without **acceptance letter** or **realistic backup**.

## FAQ

**Is Abu Dhabi boring for kids compared to Dubai?** 
**Quieter**, not boring — Yas theme parks, Louvre, sports academies exist. Dubai has **more volume** — teens feel difference.

**Can my child switch ADEK to KHDA mid-year?** 
Possible but **disruptive** — curriculum and term dates differ. Plan **emirate before Year 7** if possible.

**Which emirate has better beaches for families?** 
**Saadiyat and Corniche (AD)** vs **JBR, Kite, La Mer (DXB)** — both strong; Dubai **more crowded** public beaches.

**Is childcare/nanny cost different?** 
**Similar UAE market** — AED 2,500–4,500+ live-out depending on hours and experience.

**Pet-friendly — Abu Dhabi or Dubai?** 
**Villa belts both** — Khalifa, Springs; towers restrict. Verify lease.

**Which has better special needs support?** 
**Dubai wider**; Abu Dhabi **growing** — verify specific diagnosis support at named school.

**Do families need two cars in both?** 
**Usually yes** with two working parents and activities.

**Ramadan — emirate difference for kids' activities?** 
**UAE-wide daytime eating law**; public respect **stronger in quieter Abu Dhabi suburbs** — plan indoor schedules both.
