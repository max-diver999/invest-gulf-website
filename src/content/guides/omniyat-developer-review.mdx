Omniyat does not compete with Emaar on volume. It competes with **global luxury real estate** — architectural landmarks, Dorchester Collection hospitality branding, and buildings designed to be photographed from helicopter tours.

**The Opus** by Zaha Hadid Architects. **One at Palm Jumeirah**. **The Sterling** with interiors by Gilles & Boissier. These are not JVC yield plays. They are **trophy assets** where rental yield is a rounding error and capital preservation, end-user exit, and landmark scarcity drive the thesis.

Omniyat's **~93% on-time delivery** places it in Tier 1 territory — remarkable for ultra-luxury complexity where MEP, bespoke facades, and branded fit-out extend timelines. The buyer pool is small, liquidity events are infrequent, and **service charges run AED 25–40/sqft** on flagship stock.

**Quick answer:** ~93% delivery. Ultra-luxury boutique. Architectural landmarks. Flagships: The Opus, One Palm, Sterling. Best for trophy capital preservation. Not for yield investors.

## Payment plans and SPA structure

Omniyat typically offers **milestone-linked plans** appropriate for ultra-luxury ticket sizes:

| Phase | Typical Omniyat structure |
|---|---|
| Booking | 10–20% |
| Construction milestones | 50–60% |
| Handover | 20–30% |

Omniyat does **not** compete on 1% monthly volume marketing — counterparty exposure is shorter but absolute AED exposure per buyer is high.

**SPA scrutiny points specific to ultra-luxury:**
- Completion specification schedule — bespoke finishes, fixtures, and branded elements
- Delay penalty asymmetry — often limited to refund not consequential damages
- Hospitality pass-through costs — Dorchester Collection operating items in service charge
- Short-let bylaws — building rules and DTCM permit requirements
- Assignment restrictions — NOC fees and buyer qualification on off-plan resale

## Secondary-market liquidity

Omniyat liquidity is **event-driven and thin** — not daily trading like Emaar Downtown.

**Liquidity ranking within Omniyat portfolio:**
1. The Sterling handed-over phases (lower ticket, more buyer pool)
2. The Opus completed stock (landmark but high ticket)
3. One at Palm Jumeirah (scarcity — infrequent transactions)
4. Newest off-plan phases (assignment market minimal)
5. Dorchester Collection branded (buyer qualification filters)

**Practical signals:**
- Months-on-market for resale Omniyat units — often 6–18 months for priced-right trophy stock
- Transaction frequency — count DLD sales in target building over 24 months
- Mortgage LTV — limited lender appetite on ultra-luxury; cash buyers dominate

Investors needing exit within 3–5 years should **not** assume Omniyat liquidity matches Emaar — plan for 7–10 year holds or end-user exit.

## Due diligence checklist: Omniyat-specific

Before signing an Omniyat SPA:

1. **Trakheesi registration** — project number on DLD portal
2. **Escrow account** — verify on Dubai REST app
3. **Independent luxury property solicitor** — SPA review mandatory at this ticket size
4. **Completion specification schedule** — attached to SPA, not marketing brochure
5. **Service charge breakdown** — hospitality pass-through items itemised
6. **Mollak actuals** — on handed-over Omniyat building in same tier
7. **Comparable resale** — DLD transacted PSF on handed-over stock, not launch price
8. **Short-let bylaws** — building rules and DTCM requirements if income planned
9. **SPA delay clause** — penalty cap, refund timeline, specification remedies
10. **Assignment/NOC policy** — buyer qualification and fees if pre-handover exit planned

## Who should buy from Omniyat

Omniyat suits buyers who:

- Seek **architectural trophy assets** and capital preservation over yield
- Are **ultra-high-net-worth** with 7–10+ year hold horizons
- Accept **3–5% gross yield** (often lower net) as fair for landmark scarcity
- Plan **end-user exit** or infrequent resale — not 3-year flip strategies
- Value **Dorchester Collection and design pedigree** over payment-plan accessibility

**Consider alternatives if:**
- Rental income dependency — JVC volume or Emaar established communities
- Maximum resale liquidity — Emaar Downtown or Dubai Hills
- Branded mid-premium without ultra-luxury ticket — DAMAC Cavalli
- Build quality premium at lower PSF — Sobha Realty
