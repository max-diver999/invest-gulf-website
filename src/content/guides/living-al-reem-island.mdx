## FAQ

**Is Al Reem Island good for families?** 
Good for **small families** (1–2 children) who accept **school drive** and tower life. Large villa families usually prefer [Khalifa City](/guides/abu-dhabi-cost-of-living/).

**How much is rent on Al Reem Island?** 
**AED 6,500–9,000** for 1BR; **AED 9,500–14,000** for 2BR (2026 indicative). See [rent guide](/guides/abu-dhabi-rent-prices-by-area/).

**Al Reem vs Yas, which is cheaper?** 
**Reem** is typically **AED 1,000–2,500/month below** Yas for equivalent apartment tier.

**Al Reem vs Saadiyat, worth the savings?** 
If you do not need **walk-to-Cranleigh** or daily Saadiyat Beach, Reem saves **AED 30,000–60,000/year** rent for similar tower lifestyle.

**Do you need a car on Reem?** 
**Yes** for most families and many couples, school runs and mall trips are car-first.

**Are there schools on Al Reem Island?** 
Limited on-island, most families **drive 15–35 min** to Raha, Saadiyat, or Khalifa belts.

**Is Al Reem good for ADGM workers?** 
**Strong fit**, **10–15 minute** off-peak commute to Al Maryah.

**Can foreigners buy property on Al Reem?** 
Yes in **designated freehold** buildings, verify zone list. [Property guide](/guides/abu-dhabi-property-investment-guide/).

**What utilities cost on Reem?** 
ADD **AED 400–900** apartments typical; **chiller** may add **AED 300–1,500** summer in older towers. [ADD guide](/guides/abu-dhabi-cost-of-living/).

**Does living on Reem qualify for Golden Visa?** 
**Ownership** of AED 2M+ qualifying unit can, not renting alone. [Golden Visa living](/guides/abu-dhabi-golden-visa-property/).

**Is Reem quieter than Dubai Marina?** 
**yes**, Abu Dhabi pace ; fewer late-night venues.

**Best towers on Al Reem Island?** 
Subjective, prioritise **chiller transparency, parking, and maintenance** over lobby marble.
