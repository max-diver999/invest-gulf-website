## How should you use these fee tables?

Pick a curriculum tier, apply the hidden-cost multiplier for child count and bus distance, then subtract employer allowance caps. Cross-check monthly burn against [Doha cost of living](/guides/doha-cost-of-living/) and [Qatar family visa sponsorship](/guides/qatar-family-visa-sponsorship/) before you commit to a lease.

Workflow:
1. Pick your tier from the master band table
2. Add the hidden-cost multiplier for child count and commute distance
3. Compare net cost to the employer allowance table
4. Cross-check monthly burn in [Doha cost of living](/guides/doha-cost-of-living/)
5. Lock family visa and housing before you commit: [Qatar family visa sponsorship](/guides/qatar-family-visa-sponsorship/)

Skip step 4 and Pearl or West Bay rent can absorb the gap between allowance and tuition, then bus fees add QAR 12,000 per year per child when the villa sits far from the campus.

## How are school fees treated for VAT and home-country tax?

Most international school invoices in Qatar are zero-rated or exempt for VAT purposes on tuition lines, but always read the invoice wording for transport, uniforms, and activity levies. UK, US, and EU tax deductions for foreign school fees are generally limited; get cross-border advice rather than assuming employer reimbursement is tax-free at home.

## How do schools schedule fee payments through the year?

Most campuses bill on a three-term calendar with August, January, and April peaks. Post-dated cheques and bank transfers remain common; size your Qatar account for the largest single debit, not the monthly average.

### Calendar cash peaks (typical British calendar)

| Month | Typical charges |
|---|---|
| April to May | Re-registration for next year |
| August | Term 1 tuition + bus + uniforms |
| January | Term 2 tuition |
| April | Term 3 + exam fees (secondary) |
| June | Trips, activity settlements |

## What red flags suggest a school fee quote is incomplete?

Quotes without bus, registration, or exam-year lines, or pressure for a non-refundable deposit before employer allowance confirmation, need a written fee schedule follow-up.
