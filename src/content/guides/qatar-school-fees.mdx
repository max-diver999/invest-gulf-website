Employer schooling allowances in Qatar typically leave a material gap because caps cover only part of a mid-tier bill, often leaving parents to top up 10 to 25% or more. Family pools fail quickly with a second child. Foreign buyers should demand written caps; Invest Gulf blocks housing commits until the gap is modelled for this market.

## How are school fees treated for VAT and home-country tax?

Most international school invoices in Qatar are zero-rated or exempt for VAT on tuition lines, while transport and activity levies may differ on the same bill. Home-country deductions are generally limited for foreign buyers. Invest Gulf treats employer reimbursement as cash-flow support, not automatic 100% tax relief abroad in this market.
