  - question: "Can I open a bank account in Ras Al Khaimah?"
    answer: "Yes with valid UAE residence visa, Emirates ID, salary certificate or proof of funds, and RAK tenancy (Tawtheeq-style registration) or employer letter. Visit RAK Bank, ENBD, FAB, ADCB, Mashreq or RAK branches of national banks. Processing 3–10 business days typical."
  - question: "Which banks have branches in RAK?"
    answer: "RAK Bank (headquartered in emirate), Emirates NBD, FAB, ADCB, Mashreq, ADIB, CBD and others maintain RAK City and mall branches. Digital onboarding varies, most still want Emirates ID scan in branch for first account."
  - question: "If I work in Dubai but live in RAK, where should my salary account be?"
    answer: "Usually where HR mandates WPS, often Dubai employer's preferred bank (ENBD, FAB, etc.). You can add a second RAK account for local cheques and FEWA. Do not assume RAK address forces RAK Bank unless employer agrees."
  - question: "Is RAK Bank only for RAK residents?"
    answer: "RAK Bank serves UAE-wide customers but is strong for RAK municipality payments, local mortgages and community familiarity. Dubai commuters still use it for second accounts and some property loans in RAK freehold."
  - question: "How do I pay FEWA bills from banking apps?"
    answer: "Register FEWA customer number in bank bill-pay (RAK Bank, ENBD, etc.). Auto-debit after first manual payment. Keep AED 2,000+ buffer in summer for villa spikes, see rak-cost-of-living-detailed utilities section."

  - question: "Are Islamic banking options available in RAK?"

  - question: "What is proof of funds for RAK property purchase?"

many households keep Dubai job + RAK home on E611. Banking mirrors that split:

## WPS and salary transfer

Wage Protection System requires employers to pay through registered channels. Dubai company files WPS under Dubai trade licence, salary still legal for RAK visa employee living in Hamra.

| Scenario | Pitfall |
|---|---|
| Cash salary | Illegal for covered employers |
| Delayed WPS | MOHRE complaint route |
| Split accounts HR refuses | Negotiate before move |

Salary certificate for account opening must match WPS deposit amount within tolerance.

## Cheques, rent and UAE tenancy culture

RAK landlords often want 1–4 post-dated cheques:

| Cheque rule | Practice |
|---|---|
| Bounce penalty | Criminal + civil risk UAE-wide |
| Cancelled cheque for Ejari | Sometimes Dubai only, RAK uses municipality registration |
| Security deposit | Transfer separate from cheques |

**Bank requirement:** Cheque facility needs salary history, open account **before** signing lease.

## AECB credit score: why commuters should care

**Al Etihad Credit Bureau (AECB)** records loans, cards, telco and some utilities. RAK residents with **Dubai salary** still build UAE credit on that file.

| Action | Score impact |
|---|---|
| 6 months clean WPS | Positive |
| Missed card minimum | Negative |
| Bounced cheque | Severe |
| Early loan settlement | Mixed, read terms |

Mortgage in RAK at year two needs score plus salary multiple, typically **8–15×** basic for expat employers on approved list.

## CRS / FATCA: RAK address does not exempt reporting

| Scenario | Reporting |
|---|---|
| US citizen | FATCA continues |
| UK tax resident | HMRC may still care if UK resident |
| UAE tax resident 183+ days | UAE certificate, separate process |

Bank asks **tax residency self-certification** at onboarding, answer accurately.

## Property purchase payment choreography

| Payment | Rail |
|---|---|
| Booking fee | Manager cheque or transfer to developer escrow |
| Instalment plan | SWIFT from abroad, SOF docs |
| Handover | Trustee office, mortgage bank cheque |

**Never** wire to individual broker for "reservation" without SPA escrow clause, UAE-wide scam pattern.
