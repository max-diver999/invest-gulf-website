# Saudi Tax & Zakat for Expats 2026: 0% Income Tax, Business Rules & CRS

**TL;DR:** Typical expat employees on Saudi iqama pay 0% Saudi personal income tax on salary — the recruiter headline is mostly true for employment income. Zakat hits Saudi/GCC owners and certain business structures, not something most employees calculate on a payslip. Corporate tax, withholding, VAT, and home-country CRS/FATCA still matter. Premium Residency and side businesses change the picture — **[VERIFY with ZATCA-licensed advisor]**.

## The recruiter headline — what is actually true

For most readers: salary from a KSA employer into a Saudi bank bears no Saudi PAYE-style deduction. That part checks out.

What does not follow automatically:
- You owe zero tax anywhere in the world
- Zakat is irrelevant to you
- Stock options are clean
- Rental income from your home country stops needing a return
- Premium Residency has no tax substance effect

Never write "tax-free" without a home-country qualifier. Families make decisions on that word.

## Saudi personal income tax — employment

Saudi Arabia does not run a broad personal income tax on employment income for typical expat iqama holders.

| Income type | Typical expat employee |
|---|---|
| Base salary | 0% Saudi personal income tax |
| Housing allowance (cash) | Usually bundled in employment — still verify contract |
| Bonus | Employer reporting — specialist if equity |
| Overseas rental while in KSA | Home-country taxation may apply |

Compare UAE — also **0% employment income tax** for typical expats; UAE added **9% corporate tax** on business profits above threshold — Saudi corporate rules **differ**.

## Zakat — who pays, who does not

Zakat (الزكاة) is an Islamic wealth obligation administered in Saudi context primarily for Saudi nationals, GCC nationals in certain cases, and Saudi companies subject to zakat base rules vs income tax.

### Typical expat employee

Salary earner on iqama without Saudi ownership interest:
- Does not usually pay personal zakat like a Saudi national
- Payslip should not show zakat deduction unless unusual structure **[VERIFY]**

### Expat business owner

If you hold Saudi LLC shares, a branch, or a professional company:
- Entity may face zakat (GCC/Saudi ownership %) vs income tax (foreign ownership %)
- Mixed ownership blends rules — ZATCA calculation mandatory

"No income tax" does not mean "no zakat on business assets."

→ Licensed Sharia + tax advisor for entity setup.

## Corporate income tax — ZATCA snapshot

**ZATCA** (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority) administers:

| Tax type | Indicative | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **CIT** on foreign-owned share | **20%** commonly cited on profits | Entity type matters |
| **Zakat base** | GCC/Saudi share | Different base calculation |
| **Withholding tax** | On remittances to non-residents | Service fees, royalties |
| **VAT** | **15%** on many goods/services | Consumer sees at checkout |

**Expat side hustle** invoicing foreign clients from Saudi apartment — **entity and PE risk** — not "0% because salary."

## CRS, FATCA and Saudi banking

Saudi banks participate in CRS (Common Reporting Standard):

| Event | Action |
|---|---|
| Account opening | Tax residency self-certification |
| W-9 (US persons) | FATCA parallel |
| Annual reporting | Bank reports to ZATCA → exchange partners |

CRS does not replace your filing duty — it informs other countries.

UK: statutory residence test still applies — Saudi 0% does not auto-clear HMRC if UK ties remain.

US: FATCA + citizenship filing — Foreign Earned Income Exclusion requires qualifying facts.

## Religious vs tax zakat — clarity for non-Muslim expats

**Zakat** is religious obligation on **eligible wealth** for Muslims meeting **nisab** — administered in Saudi corporate/national context.

## Scenario notes — three common expat tax profiles

### Profile A — UK employee, family remains in London

**Statutory Residence Test** may keep UK tax residency — Saudi salary still on **UK Self Assessment** if resident. **CRS** reports Saudi account to HMRC. **0% Saudi** does not silence UK if resident.

### Profile B — US citizen, family in compound Riyadh

**FEIE** may exclude earned income if **bona fide residence** or **physical presence** tests met — still file **FBAR/FATCA**. **State tax** if US domicile retained. **GOSI** does not replace **Social Security** strategy.

### Profile C — Indian professional, RNOR window

**RNOR** year may shield foreign income briefly — **India residential status** after 2–3 years abroad shifts. **DTAA** article review with Indian CA before Premium Residency purchase.

These are **illustrative** only — **[VERIFY]** with licensed advisors.
