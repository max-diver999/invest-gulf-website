## What bank transfer method is actually required?

UAE ready-property settlement requires cleared AED funds and manager's cheques to the trustee and DLD payees named on appointment day, while off-plan instalments must hit the RERA escrow in the SPA. Invest Gulf guidance for foreign buyers rejects chat-only payee details, cash, crypto, and foreign drafts. Use a 7 to 10 business day SWIFT buffer.

- Ready property: UAE account → manager's cheques → trustee appointment
- Off-plan: SPA escrow IBAN per milestone only
- Never pay from chat-only instructions

Cash payments, cryptocurrency, and foreign-issued bank drafts are not accepted. The UAE's Financial Intelligence Unit and Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA) require a full audit trail from source to settlement.

For buyers wiring funds from outside the UAE, the typical flow is: **international wire → UAE bank account → manager's cheque at branch → trustee appointment**. Each step has lead times, and skipping the middle step creates problems at closing.

UAE property bank transfers succeed when sequencing is correct: open a non-resident UAE account (typically 2 to 6 weeks), clear international SWIFT funds with a 7 to 10 business day buffer, then collect same-day manager's cheques at AED 10-30 each for the purchase price, 4% DLD fee, trustee fee of roughly AED 4,000-6,300 plus 5% VAT, and 2% agency commission. On an AED 2,000,000 example, buyers often walk in with about AED 2,127,000 across four instruments. Off-plan instalments follow the same banking trail but use the developer escrow IBAN from the SPA, not a seller personal account. Source-of-funds packs (6 to 12 months of statements plus income evidence) are reviewed at the receiving bank and again at the trustee desk, so incomplete paperwork delays manager's cheque issuance even when the wire has already landed.

## Why do manager's cheques dominate UAE trustee appointments?

A manager's cheque is the settlement instrument Dubai Land Department trustees require at ready-property appointments because the UAE bank has already debited cleared AED and guaranteed the paper. Direct seller wires create bounce risk trustees reject. Invest Gulf deals for foreign buyers typically need four cheques covering price, 4% DLD, trustee fee, and 2% agency commission.

A manager's cheque (also called a banker's draft) is a guaranteed instrument: the bank debits your account immediately and issues a cheque that cannot bounce. Trustees require guaranteed instruments because they need to verify cleared funds before handing over the title deed, a personal or corporate cheque creates settlement risk.

**Standard cheques required at trustee appointment for a ready property:**

| Payment | Payee | Typical amount (AED 2M example) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase price | DLD-approved trustee | AED 2,000,000 |
| DLD registration fee (4%) | Dubai Land Department | AED 80,000 |
| Trustee service fee | Trustee office | AED 4,200 + VAT (approx.) |
| Agency commission (2%) | Real estate agency | AED 40,000 |

On a standard AED 2M purchase, you walk into the trustee office with 4 manager's cheques totalling approximately AED 2,127,000+. Some trustees accept the agency commission directly from the seller's proceeds, confirm arrangements with your broker before issuing cheques.

Manager's cheques are available at any UAE bank branch where you hold an account with sufficient cleared funds. Fee: typically AED 10-30 per cheque. Processing: same-day if branch is not busy. You cannot obtain manager's cheques without a UAE bank account, which is why [opening an account as a non-resident](/guides/open-bank-account-dubai/) is a prerequisite, not an afterthought.

## How do off-plan escrow transfers work for UAE buyers?

Off-plan buyers must pay into the developer escrow account named in the SPA, not a seller personal IBAN, and each milestone requires its own UAEFTS transfer or manager's cheque with the unit reference. RERA Law No. 8 of 2007 ring-fences the project. Late instalments typically cost 1% per month for Invest Gulf foreign buyers.

- Confirm escrow IBAN matches the SPA before every instalment
- Put unit number and milestone ID on the payment reference
- Keep receipts as proof of contribution if the developer defaults

What you need for each off-plan payment:

- Manager's cheque or UAEFTS transfer payable to the specific escrow account number provided in the SPA
- Payment reference including unit number, development name, and installment number
- Retain all receipts, they form your proof of contribution in case of developer default

For the full picture on escrow accounts and developer risk, see the [step-by-step buying process guide](/guides/how-to-buy-property-dubai-step-by-step/).

## How long do international bank transfers into the UAE take?

International SWIFT transfers into a UAE property account typically take 1 to 5 business days, yet our underwriting plans a 7 to 10 business day buffer before trustee day. Correspondent hops, AML holds above AED 500,000, and FX queues delay foreign buyers. First wires above AED 1,000,000 to a new account need the longer buffer.

- Send a AED 1,000 test transfer about 2 weeks early on new routes
- Keep MT103 copies for the receiving bank and trustee file
- Avoid booking trustee on the same day an inbound SWIFT is expected

SWIFT international transfers to UAE typically take 1-5 business days from initiation to credited balance, but the real-world range is wider. Delays come from:

- Correspondent bank hops, a wire from a smaller regional bank may route through 2-3 intermediary banks, each deducting USD 10-25 and adding 24-48 hours
- AML holds, large first-time inbound transfers above AED 500,000 often trigger a manual review by the UAE receiving bank, adding 1-2 days
- Currency conversion queues; if your wire arrives in USD or EUR and the account is AED, the bank's treasury converts same-day only during business hours
- Documentation requests, the UAE bank may ask for source of funds documentation mid-transfer, freezing funds until you respond

**Rule: initiate the international wire at least 7 business days before your trustee appointment.** For your first transfer above AED 1M to a new account, plan 10 business days.

Always send a small test amount, AED 1,000, 2 weeks before the main transfer if you are using a new route. Confirm it arrives and your bank can see the incoming SWIFT MT103. This eliminates technical routing errors before they block your transaction.

## What source of funds documentation is required at each transfer stage?

Source of funds documentation is required at the UAE receiving bank and again at the trustee desk, typically a signed SOF form, SPA or MOU, 6-month statements, and income evidence. Deals above AED 5,000,000 often trigger enhanced wealth review. Invest Gulf foreign buyers assemble the pack before SWIFT leaves home.

At the UAE bank when receiving an international wire:

| Bank check | Document required |
| --- | --- |
| Source of funds declaration | Signed SOF form, provided by the bank |
| Evidence of purchase intent | Signed SPA or MOU with unit reference |
| Fund origin documentation | 6-month bank statements showing accumulation |
| Employment or business income | Pay slips, tax returns, company financials |

At the trustee office on transaction day:

| Trustee check | Document required |
| --- | --- |
| Passport / ID | Original + copy |
| Manager's cheques | Drawn on UAE account only |
| NOC from developer | For off-plan or mortgaged properties |
| Original SPA or MOU | Signed by both parties |

High-value transactions, generally above AED 5M, trigger enhanced due diligence at both the bank and trustee level. Prepare source-of-wealth documentation (investment statements, business ownership records, inheritance probate) in advance. See the full requirements breakdown in the [proof of funds guide](/guides/proof-of-funds-property-uae/).

## What are the payment stages on a ready UAE resale?

Ready resale payment stages typically start with a 10% MOU deposit held by the agency or seller, then purchase balance, 4% DLD fee, trustee fee, and 2% agency commission once NOC and cleared AED are confirmed. Our checklists treat the MOU cheque as non-refundable without cause, so foreign buyers verify escrow wording before the first manager's cheque.

- MOU deposit: about 10% by manager's cheque or wire
- Trustee day: price, 4% DLD, trustee fee + 5% VAT, 2% agency
- Release: seller paid only after NOC and title registration path is clear

## How does an off-plan payment plan timeline work?

Off-plan payment plans typically require a separate bank transfer or manager's cheque for each construction milestone, not one wire at booking. Invest Gulf foreign buyers often automate later instalments with UAEFTS standing orders once AED sits in-country. Late arrivals still risk the standard 1% per month penalty on the developer SPA calendar.

- One transfer per milestone with unit and instalment reference
- Standing orders only after the UAE account is funded
- Keep every receipt against developer default risk

## How do mortgage buyers fund the trustee appointment?

Mortgage funding requires the equity down payment cleared in a UAE bank account before the lender schedules disbursement, then a liability letter plus equity manager's cheque at the trustee. Non-resident LTV often tops out near 50%, so an AED 3,000,000 purchase typically needs about AED 1,500,000 equity plus AED 210,000 fees for Invest Gulf foreign buyers.

- Equity manager's cheque from the UAE account only
- Bank wires its loan portion in AED to the seller path
- Confirm mortgage validity dates cover any SWIFT delay

## What Are the Most Common Mistakes That Delay UAE Property Transfers?

The most common mistakes that delay UAE property transfers are booking the trustee before SWIFT clears, using foreign bank drafts, underfunding the 5% VAT on trustee fees, and arriving without the seller NOC. Invest Gulf reviews treat same-day international wires as failures for foreign buyers: keep 7 business days international and 2 business days domestic buffer before manager's cheques.

- Keep 7 business days for international SWIFT clearance
- Keep 2 business days domestic before drawing manager's cheques
- Confirm seller NOC at least 2 weeks before trustee day

**2. Transferring funds the day before the trustee appointment**
UAEFTS domestic transfers clear same-day, but if your receiving bank applies a hold or a manual AML review triggers on the same day, funds will not be available for manager's cheques. Build in at least 2 business days domestic buffer, 7 days international.

**3. Using a foreign-issued banker's draft**
A bank draft from Barclays, HDFC, or Sberbank is not accepted by UAE trustees. Funds must be in a UAE-licensed bank account with a UAE-issued instrument.

**4. Forgetting VAT on trustee fee**
The trustee service fee is subject to 5% UAE VAT. Budget approximately **AED 4,200-6,300 + VAT** (5%) depending on property value bracket. An under-prepared cheque cannot simply be topped up in the room.

**5. Paying without an NOC**
For properties with an existing mortgage or developer services charge arrears, the seller must obtain a No Objection Certificate (NOC) before the trustee can transfer title. Without the NOC, the appointment cancels. Confirm NOC status 2 weeks before trustee date.

## Which transfer scenarios should foreign buyers plan for?

Transfer scenarios for Invest Gulf buyers typically diverge by corridor: UK and EU wires need a UAE account opened 3 to 4 weeks before MOU plus a 7 to 10 business day SWIFT buffer, CIS corridors need early SOF checks, and off-plan milestones need the developer escrow payee from the SPA for foreign buyers.

- Scenario A (UK/EU): account first, then SWIFT with 7 to 10 business days
- Scenario B (CIS): confirm SOF checklist before large tranches leave
- Scenario C (off-plan): never reuse a resale trustee cheque template

**Scenario A: first-time buyer wiring from Europe or UK:** Open the UAE account 3-4 weeks before MOU signing. Initiate SWIFT 7-10 business days before trustee. Keep 5% buffer above SPA total for FX drift and VAT on trustee fees.

**Scenario B: Russian or CIS buyer using AED corridor:** Confirm your UAE bank's current source-of-funds checklist before you transfer. Split large amounts across documented tranches if your bank requests trade invoices or sale contracts. Manager's cheques still must come from a UAE-licensed bank.

**Scenario C: off-plan milestone payment:** Developer escrow accounts have different payee names than DLD trustees. Never reuse a manager's cheque template from a resale deal. Request the developer's registered escrow account details in writing for each milestone.

**Red flags:** trustee appointment booked before wire clears; payee name mismatch on cheques; seller NOC not issued; funds sitting in exchange house instead of UAE bank account.

## What belongs on a UAE property transfer checklist?

A pre-submission checklist for UAE property bank transfers requires an active UAE account, SWIFT started at least 7 business days before trustee, and cleared AED covering purchase price, 4% DLD, trustee fee plus 5% VAT, and 2% agency commission. We also require SOF packs, cheques ordered 1 to 2 days ahead, and seller NOC for foreign buyers.

Insider tip: book the trustee only after your UAE bank confirms the inbound MT103 is fully cleared and available for manager's cheques, not when the home bank says the wire was "sent."

- UAE bank account open, active, and able to receive international wires ✓
- International wire initiated at least 7 business days before trustee ✓
- Cleared AED balance covers: purchase price + 4% DLD + trustee fee + VAT + agency commission ✓
- Source of funds documentation submitted to UAE bank ✓
- Proof of funds letter obtained for trustee ✓
- Manager's cheques ordered 1-2 days before appointment with correct payee names ✓
- Seller's NOC from developer confirmed ✓
- SPA and MOU copies available on appointment day ✓

International wires into Dubai property accounts fail less from rate choice than from sequencing errors: the UAE account must exist before large SWIFT amounts leave the home bank, the receiving bank must finish AML and source-of-funds review before manager's cheques can be printed, and the trustee appointment must sit after a 7 to 10 business day buffer on first transfers above AED 1,000,000. Domestic UAEFTS moves can clear within hours, yet same-day cheque requests still fail when a manual hold lands after 2 pm. Buyers who send a AED 1,000 test transfer two weeks early, keep 6 to 12 months of statements ready, and separate the 4% DLD cheque from the purchase-price cheque rarely reschedule. MOU penalties of 0.5-1% per delayed week make that discipline cheaper than optimism.
