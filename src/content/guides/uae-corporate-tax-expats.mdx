UAE corporate tax at **9% on taxable profits above AED 375,000** applies to licensed businesses, including many expat freelancers on sole establishments or free zone companies. Employment salary remains outside corporate tax, but invoice income through your licence usually counts as business income. Zero UAE personal income tax does not remove FTA registration and filing when thresholds are met.

This guide explains rates, Small Business Relief, free zone qualifying income, deductions, registration, and how home-country tax may still apply. It is general information, not advice for your entity.

## Why does UAE corporate tax matter for expat freelancers now?

From June 2023 the UAE federal corporate tax regime applies to UAE businesses. Consultants, creators, IT contractors, and coaches with a freelance permit or company licence must know whether they must **register**, **file**, and **pay**, even when the calculated tax is zero after relief.

Misunderstanding is costly: late registration penalties apply without tax due, and free zone marketing does not automatically mean 0% on all invoices.

## What are the core rates and who is a Taxable Person?

Corporate tax uses a **0% band on the first AED 375,000** of taxable profit and **9%** on the remainder for standard businesses. A **Taxable Person** includes UAE companies and natural persons conducting business under a UAE trade licence, including sole establishments tied to freelance permits.

| Income type | Usually in scope? |
| --- | --- |
| Employment salary (WPS) | No (employment rules separate) |
| Freelance / consulting invoices | Yes, via licensed establishment |
| Dividends from UAE subsidiaries (many cases) | Often excluded; verify |
| Personal bank interest as individual | Outside business scope |
| Rent in personal name | Generally not corporate tax (individual practice) |
| Rent in company name | May be taxable to the company |

Personal versus business accounts must be separable. Mixed spending triggers audit friction.

## How are freelance permits and sole establishments taxed?

A freelance permit registers you as a sole establishment. All net profit from permitted activities rolls into your corporate tax calculation after allowable deductions. Revenue above **AED 1 million** generally triggers **mandatory FTA registration**, even if tax due is nil after relief.

Keep contracts, invoices, and bank statements aligned to the licence activity list. Activities outside the permit scope create both licensing and tax risk.

## What is Small Business Relief and who qualifies?

Small Business Relief lets eligible entities elect **nil taxable income** for a period if **revenue is below AED 3 million** in that period and was below AED 3 million in prior periods since June 2023, subject to FTA conditions (including exclusion of certain MNE groups). Relief is available for tax periods **ending on or before 31 December 2026**.

Example: AED 2 million revenue and AED 900,000 net profit would exceed the AED 375,000 band and owe tax without relief. With a valid election and revenue test, liability may be **zero**, but filing still required.

| Revenue / election | Typical outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Profit under AED 375,000 | 0% on that profit |
| Under AED 3M revenue + relief elected | Nil tax; return still filed |
| Over AED 3M revenue | 9% on profit above AED 375,000 |
| Free zone qualifying income only | 0% on qualifying slice; 9% on rest |

Confirm each test on EmaraTax guidance before electing.

## Do free zone companies pay 0% corporate tax?

Only **Qualifying Free Zone Persons** with **Qualifying Income** and adequate **substance** may access 0% on that income. Mainland UAE client fees, many domestic professional services, and non-qualifying passive streams often fall at **9%**.

Requirements include audited financials, real operations in the zone, and de minimis limits on non-qualifying revenue. A DMCC entity serving mostly Dubai mainland clients should not assume full exemption.

## Which expenses can freelancers deduct?

Allowable deductions generally follow ordinary business expense principles: licence fees, rent for office, software, insurance, subcontractor costs, and bank charges tied to the business. Capital assets may be subject to depreciation rules per FTA publications.

| Category | Documentation habit |
| --- | --- |
| Home office | Allocate rent and utilities with a consistent method |
| Travel | Client purpose on invoice and itinerary |
| Equipment | Asset register and purchase proof |
| Mixed cards | Stop using personal cards for client income |

Open a dedicated business account; see [open a bank account in Dubai](/guides/open-bank-account-dubai/) and [UAE CRS/FATCA banking](/guides/uae-crs-fatca-banking/).

## When must you register and file with the FTA?

Registration timing depends on incorporation date and commencement of business. Many entities must register within **three months** of incorporation if formed after early 2024 windows; older entities faced earlier deadlines. Use the EmaraTax portal for current forms.

| Obligation | Risk if missed |
| --- | --- |
| Late registration | Fixed penalties |
| Late filing | Percentage penalties on tax due |
| Late payment | Additional penalties and interest |

Even **nil tax** after Small Business Relief usually still requires a return documenting the election.

## How does corporate tax interact with personal tax residency?

UAE corporate tax does not replace UK, US, German, or other home-country rules on worldwide income. UK residents may face CFC or remittance issues; US citizens remain on US returns for ownership of foreign companies; EU residents may have foreign company attribution rules.

Coordinate with advisers in both jurisdictions. Personal UAE residency tests are explained in [UAE tax residency and property](/guides/uae-tax-residency-property/) and the broader [UAE tax guide for expats](/guides/uae-tax-guide-expats/).

## What international reporting applies?

UAE participates in global transparency standards. Banks report under CRS; US persons face FATCA. Corporate tax registration increases the importance of consistent books because FTA data may cross-check with bank inflows.

If you hold foreign subsidiaries or receive foreign dividends, transfer pricing and master file rules may apply at higher revenue tiers. Treat this as a trigger to engage a qualified tax firm, not a blog-level guess.

## What practical steps should freelancers take in 2026?

1. Confirm licence type and whether revenue crossed AED 1 million.  
2. Register on EmaraTax if required, even when expecting nil tax.  
3. Separate business banking and accounting from personal spending.  
4. Evaluate Small Business Relief before the 2026 period end if revenue under AED 3 million.  
5. Revisit free zone qualifying income if mainland clients grew.  
6. Book a home-country consult if you are UK/US/EU resident or citizen.  

## How should you prepare records before the first FTA return?

Start a simple monthly close: reconcile bank feeds to invoices, tag personal transfers as drawings, and store PDF contracts for every client above AED 5,000 per month. VAT-registered businesses follow separate VAT rules; corporate tax taxable income is not identical to VAT turnover.

| Record | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Signed client contracts | Proves business versus personal gifts |
| Invoice numbering series | Shows continuity if audited |
| Expense receipts | Supports deductions |
| Lease for office or desk | Substance for free zone claims |
| Payroll if you hire | Withholding and employment compliance separate |

Cloud accounting tools used by UAE firms (Xero, QuickBooks, Zoho) are acceptable if configured for AED and exportable to your tax agent. Many freelancers engage an FTA-registered tax agency for the first return even when they expect nil liability, because election wording for Small Business Relief must be precise.

## What mistakes trigger penalties even when no tax is due?

Common errors include missing registration after crossing AED 1 million revenue, filing late while waiting for "clarity," mixing personal rent payments through the business account without allocation, and assuming a free zone licence banner equals automatic 0% on Dubai client work.

Treat the first EmaraTax login as a deadline event, not an optional admin task. Penalties stack independently of whether you ultimately owe 9% or zero.

For property-specific levies (municipality fees, VAT on commercial rent), see [Dubai property taxes explained](/guides/dubai-property-taxes-explained/). Residency via property is covered in [UAE Golden Visa property](/guides/uae-golden-visa-property/).

## How should expats model tax before taking a Dubai client contract?

Build a simple three-column forecast: gross revenue, deductible business costs, and home-country top-up tax if you remain tax resident abroad. UAE corporate tax applies only after AED 375,000 of **taxable** profit, not gross billing, so retain invoices for software, marketing, co-working, and professional fees.

| Scenario (illustrative) | Taxable profit | UAE CT at 9% (no relief) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AED 500,000 profit | AED 500,000 | AED 11,250 on slice above AED 375,000 |
| AED 900,000 profit | AED 900,000 | AED 47,250 on slice above AED 375,000 |
| Under AED 3M revenue with valid Small Business Relief election | Treated as nil | Often nil if election accepted |

These figures are planning math only. Relief elections, free zone splits, and foreign tax credits change outcomes. Re-run the model each quarter if client mix shifts between mainland UAE, free zone, and overseas payers.
