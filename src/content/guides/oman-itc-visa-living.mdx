## FAQ

What does ITC mean in Oman?  
Integrated Tourism Complex, designated zone allowing foreign freehold with tourism-residential regulation.

Can foreigners live in ITC zones without buying?  
Yes, renting ITC apartments on employment visa is common; purchase is optional.

Is Al Mouj the main ITC for expats?  
Most established marina ITC for families, not the only option; compare Muscat Bay and emerging zones  (confirm current official rules).

Does buying ITC property give automatic residency?  
No, may support investor residency if you meet ~OMR 250K (confirm current official rules) and other rules.

Are ITC service charges higher than normal apartments?  
Often yes, budget OMR 40–120/month plus sinking fund; ask 5-year history.

Can I work in Oman while living in ITC on investor visa?  
Local work may still need employer permit (confirm current official rules), do not assume investor card = work card.

Are schools inside ITC?
