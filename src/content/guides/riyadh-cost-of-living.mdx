# Riyadh Cost of Living 2026: Rent, Schools, Compounds & Monthly Budgets

TL;DR: A family of four on a normal Riyadh expat lifestyle spends SAR 28,000–38,000/month, compound villa plus premium British schools can push SAR 45,000+ before help and entertainment. Singles in city apartments outside legacy compounds can run SAR 8,000–14,000 without premium schools or a second car. Schools break spreadsheets: two children in top-tier international curricula land SAR 8,000–14,000/month amortised, often UAE-level. Before you sign, read Ejar terms and confirm iqama-dependent bank and school steps. Vision 2030 packages look generous until Premium Residency, Law M/14 property and trailing-spouse income sit in grey zones.

## How to use this guide

This hub answers riyadh cost of living in spend terms, what leaves your account inside compounds and city apartments, not what giga-project renderings imply.

Copy the budget tables into a spreadsheet. Replace indicative rents with three live listings per compound shortlist. Amortise school fees: annual tuition ÷ 12, plus transport, uniforms and registration.

Two families on the same SAR 45,000 gross can land SAR 30,000 or SAR 48,000 monthly spend. Diplomatic Quarter apartment plus American premium is not the same universe as north Riyadh compound plus Indian mid-tier. Model both before you negotiate your package.

Saudi Arabia is opening fast on Law M/14 zones, Premium Residency ~SAR 4M, entertainment and women driving, but conservative public norms remain and lifestyle infrastructure matures unevenly outside Riyadh and Jeddah.

## Currency, tax and why Riyadh COL is not “cheap Saudi”

- SAR pegged to USD ~3.75 since 1986, stable for USD earners; headline “Saudi discount” rarely shows in premium compounds or top schools.
- 0% personal income tax on typical employment, corporate, withholding and zakat differ for business owners; get specialist advice if not pure employee.
- Premium Residency: ~SAR 4 million investment track cited, verify with Premium Residency Center; separate from standard iqama.
- Foreign property: Law M/14 designated zones only, regulations evolving through 2026; never assume Dubai-wide freehold.
- Social costs: no legal alcohol retail, entertainment spend shifts to ticketed events, compound life, travel abroad.

## Compound vs city apartment: COL trade-offs

Most first-year expats underestimate how much compound rent buys quiet weekends, and how much a city apartment saves if you do not need a pool or school bus.

| Factor | Compound villa | City apartment |
|---|---|---|
| Rent per sqm | Higher headline | Lower entry |
| School bus | Often included route | May need private driver |
| Pool / gym | Bundled | Gym membership extra |
| Security / friction | Lower for new families | Higher outside gate |
| Commute to Vision 2030 HQs | Variable | Sometimes shorter |
| Social life | In-compound calendar | More city exploration |

Decision rule: families with young children often pay compound premium; single executives near Olaya/KAFD sometimes optimize city tower + ride-hail.

Market context: schools growing but premium tier expensive, compound choice is often school-bus geography first, sqm second.

## Utilities: electricity, water and summer spikes

Riyadh dry heat still punishes AC at scale. Villa and compound bills spike June–September, ask for last summer's meter history, not a landlord guess.

| Unit type | SAR/month (indicative) |
|---|---|
| 1BR apartment | 200–450 |
| 2BR apartment | 350–700 |
| 3BR compound villa (summer) | 600–1,200 |
| Large villa + pool | 1,000–2,000+ |
| Internet fibre (STC / Mobily / Zain) | 250–400 |

Summer planning: Riyadh dry heat drives AC load May–September, model 1.5–2× winter.

Water: subsidised feel varies by meter, still line-item in villa budgets.

Unlike Dubai, no Salik tolls, but fuel and distance in sprawling Riyadh add transport COL.

## FAQ

**Rent can be similar** in compound tier; **schools at premium often match UAE**, total COL is not automatically lower.

**What is a comfortable salary in Riyadh?**  
Single **SAR 18,000+ net** mid lifestyle; family of four **SAR 35,000–42,000+ net** with British mid schools and compound rent.

**3BR villa SAR 10,000–16,000** mid legacy stock; **SAR 14,000–22,000** premium new compounds.

**What is iqama?**  
Your **residence permit ID**, required for bank, Ejar, school and daily compliance checks.

**How much is Saudi Premium Residency?**
