  - question: "Is Riyadh cheaper than Dubai for expat families?"
    answer: "Not necessarily. Compound rent often matches Dubai levels, and premium international schools cost SAR 55,000-120,000+ per year, similar to UAE. The main savings come from 0% personal income tax and slightly lower utility costs in some cases."
  - question: "How much should I budget for school fees in Riyadh?"

  - question: "What are the hidden costs of living in Riyadh?"

  - question: "Should I choose a compound or city apartment in Riyadh?"
    answer: "Compounds offer pools, security, and school bus routes but cost more per square meter. City apartments near Olaya/KAFD save money but may need private drivers for school runs. Families with young children usually prefer compounds."

    answer: "Iqama is your standard work-based residence permit required for daily life. Premium Residency costs around SAR 4 million investment and provides different benefits, verify current rules with the Premium Residency Center as they're separate systems."
