## How should you use this Riyadh cost of living guide?

Copy the budget tables into a spreadsheet. Replace indicative rents with **three live listings** per compound shortlist. Amortise school fees: annual tuition divided by 12, plus transport, uniforms, and registration.

Two families on the same SAR 45,000 gross can land SAR 30,000 or SAR 48,000 monthly spend. Diplomatic Quarter apartment plus American premium is not the same universe as north Riyadh compound plus Indian mid-tier. Model both before you negotiate your package.

## Why is Riyadh not automatically cheaper than Dubai?

**0% personal income tax** helps net pay, but **compound rent** and **premium international schools** often match UAE levels. Savings show up in utilities on some villa types and in lifestyle choices (no alcohol spend), not in a blanket "Saudi is cheap" narrative.

| Line item | Riyadh note | Dubai comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Income tax | 0% on employment income | 0% |
| Compound 3BR rent | SAR 10,000-16,000 mid | Similar tier in Dubai suburbs |
| British mid school / child | SAR 55,000-90,000/year | Comparable band |
| Utilities summer | Spike 1.5-2x winter | AC-heavy both cities |
| Alcohol | Not available | Licensed emirates add spend |

## How much do international schools cost?

| Curriculum | SAR/year (indicative) | Monthly amortised (1 child) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Indian CBSE mid | 18,000-35,000 | 1,500-2,900 |
| British mid | 55,000-90,000 | 4,600-7,500 |
| American mid | 55,000-85,000 | 4,600-7,100 |

Add bus **SAR 800-1,200**/month, uniforms, and registration. Two children in British mid often creates a **SAR 10,000-12,000** school line alone.

Apply to three schools **10-12 weeks** before arrival; confirm seat before Ejar lease if compound-linked schools matter.

## What utilities, transport, and hidden costs surprise newcomers?

**Utilities:** villa AC drives summer bills; budget **1.5-2x** winter spend June-September.

**Transport:** no Salik tolls, but Riyadh sprawl adds fuel and second-car costs. Women driving is normal; still budget **SAR 1,200-1,500**/month per car all-in for many families.

**Hidden first-year hits:** school debentures on premium campuses, compound service fees separate from rent, deposits, furniture, **SAR 15,000-25,000** setup float.

**Help:** live-in maid sponsorship common in compounds, **SAR 2,500-4,500** all-in monthly plus housing room.

## How do iqama, Premium Residency, and property zones affect COL planning?

**Iqama** is your work-based residence ID for bank, Ejar, school, and compliance. **Premium Residency** is a separate track (commonly cited ~**SAR 4 million** investment, verify with Premium Residency Center).

Foreign **property purchase** in Riyadh is limited to **Law M/14 designated zones** (REGA). Ownership does not replace iqama for daily life budgeting unless your visa category changes.

## What checklist should you run before you sign a lease?

1. School seat confirmation or waitlist in writing
2. Ejar contract reviewed for break clauses
3. Compound service fee schedule
4. Summer utility estimate from current tenant
5. Employer allowance mapped to **net** not gross
6. SAR 20,000 float for first WPS salary lag (week 5-7)

## What red flags break a Riyadh COL budget?

Red flags: accepting gross salary talk without school amortisation; signing compound lease before school assessment slots; ignoring compound service fee separate from rent; assuming Premium Residency from a standard employment package.

## What relocation scenarios should you model?

**Scenario A (single, 2-3 years):** City apartment, one car, no schools: target under SAR 11,000 spend on SAR 20,000+ net.

**Scenario B (family, 3-5 years):** Lock school shortlist before lease; commute triangle matters more than brochure rent savings.

**Scenario C (trial before buying):** Rent 12 months locally before REGA zone purchase; tenancy evidence and tax reporting differ from other GCC hubs.

## How do Ejar leases and iqama paperwork affect cash flow?

Ejar digital contracts standardise rent payment schedules and renewal notices. Read break clauses carefully: some compounds require **90-day** notice even when employer relocation ends. Iqama renewal fees and dependent cards sit outside rent but hit the same monthly budget.

Link iqama, Ejar, and bank account setup in week one. Delays block school registration and car insurance. See [Saudi iqama context](/guides/saudi-arabia-property-foreigners-guide/) for process timing.

## How should employers structure allowances?

Negotiate **school cap** separately from **housing cap**. Secondary years and IB diploma stages exceed primary tuition. Clarify whether utilities, furniture, and relocation shipment are grossed up or net. A SAR 5,000 housing uplift rarely covers a jump from city apartment to premium compound.

First-year cash flow often spikes in August when school invoices, furniture trucks, and car purchases overlap. Model **SAR 20,000-30,000** buffer beyond employer relocation grant. Compare packages using **net** salary after GOSI and any home-country pension lines you still pay.

Summer electricity on compound villas can exceed SAR 1,200/month; ask current tenants for July and August bills, not agent winter estimates. Compound maintenance requests may bill separately from rent; capture those lines in your spreadsheet under housing, not misc.

Drivers and school nannies are common in premium packages; if you self-fund help, add SAR 3,000-5,000/month all-in to the model. Gym and club memberships inside compounds sometimes carry join fees not included in quoted rent.

Compare your offer to Dubai using the same school tier; many families find total cost within 5-10% once tax and housing are netted, which surprises teams expecting a large Saudi discount.

Entertainment spend rises with Vision 2030 events; families who previously left the Kingdom for holidays now mix local concerts and domestic Red Sea trips. Budget SAR 500-1,500/month if you participate regularly, or keep a separate travel line item.

Car insurance and periodic registration (Istimara) add roughly SAR 200-400/month averaged across the year; include them under transport, not one-off surprises.

Riyadh COL planning should align with your [Saudi property foreigner living](/guides/saudi-property-foreigner-living/) timeline if you later buy in a REGA zone: rent-first remains the norm because compound leases and school contracts are easier to unwind than off-plan deposits.

Medical insurance top-ups for dental and optical care often cost SAR 150-300/month per adult; employer plans vary on outpatient limits, read the table of benefits before you skip private cover.
