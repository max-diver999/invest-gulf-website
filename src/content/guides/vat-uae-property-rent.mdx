UAE VAT on real estate splits into three practical buckets for investors: standard-rated at 5%, zero-rated at 0% with input recovery for suppliers, and exempt supplies where no VAT is charged and input recovery is blocked. Residential long-term rent is exempt. Commercial rent and most short-term holiday lets are standard-rated. Property sales are either zero-rated (first supply of new residential within three years of completion) or exempt on resale.

This guide explains how Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 and Executive Regulations apply to landlords, developers, and buyers. It is general information only; confirm treatment with an FTA-registered tax adviser for your entity structure.

UAE residential landlords collecting only long-term Ejari rent typically face no VAT registration duty because exempt residential leases do not count toward the AED 375,000 taxable turnover threshold under Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017. Commercial landlords and holiday-home operators must register once taxable rents exceed that figure in any rolling 12-month period, then charge 5% VAT, issue tax invoices, and file quarterly EmaraTax returns with payment due by the 28th of the month after quarter end. Zero-rated first supplies of new residential buildings within 3 years of completion allow developers to recover input VAT on construction, while exempt residential resales and long-term rents block that recovery. Investors comparing a furnished tourist let against a 12-month habitation lease should model the registration flip: the same apartment can move from exempt to standard-rated under DET or DTCM short-stay rules, and Tourism Dirham of AED 10 to 15 per room night remains a separate levy outside the FTA VAT return.

Invest Gulf underwriting for UAE rental portfolios separates exempt residential cash flows from standard-rated commercial and short-term streams before any SPA or management agreement is signed. A mixed building with AED 280,000 residential rent and AED 420,000 commercial rent triggers mandatory VAT registration on the taxable portion alone, requires documented apportionment of shared lobby and utility costs, and needs 15 years of auditable records once registered. Buyers should confirm whether quoted commercial rent is VAT-exclusive, whether the landlord already holds a TRN, and whether holiday-home licence class sits with the owner or the operator. Off-plan residential first supplies inside the 3-year completion window are often zero-rated for the developer, so unit prices rarely show a 5% VAT line, yet fit-out vendors can still invoice VAT separately. Treat Dubai transfer fees as acquisition costs, not VAT, when building total cost of ownership models for foreign buyers.

Insider tip: Model VAT registration on taxable commercial and short-term rent alone, never on total portfolio rent including exempt Ejari residential leases, before you sign a mixed-use SPA.

## How does UAE VAT apply to property at a glance?

UAE VAT on property means three buckets for foreign buyers and landlords: standard-rated commercial and short-term lets at 5%, zero-rated first supply of new residential within 3 years of completion, and exempt long-term residential rent plus most resales under Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017.

| Category | VAT rate | Who it affects | Input VAT recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard-rated | 5% | Commercial rent, STR, hotels | Yes, if registered |
| Zero-rated | 0% | First supply of new residential (3-year window) | Yes for developer |
| Exempt | None charged | Long-term residential rent, resale residential | No |

- Standard-rated supplies charge tenants or buyers 5% VAT when the landlord is registered.
- Zero-rated first supplies keep the buyer price free of VAT while allowing developer input recovery.
- Exempt residential rent and resales block input VAT recovery for the supplier.

## Is long-term residential rent exempt from VAT?

Long-term residential rent is an exempt UAE VAT supply for foreign buyers and local landlords: Ejari habitation leases do not add 5%, and that rent does not count toward the AED 375,000 registration threshold. Short tourist stays on the same unit flip to standard-rated serviced accommodation under holiday-home rules.

| Property use | Typical VAT treatment |
| --- | --- |
| 12-month Ejari residential lease | Exempt |
| Hotel or serviced apartment (short stay) | Standard 5% |
| DTCM-licensed holiday home (short stay) | Standard 5% |
| Furnished daily or weekly tourist let | Standard 5% |

- Confirm the lease is registered as residential habitation, not tourist inventory.
- Recheck classification if average stay drops below one month.
- Keep a red-flag note when a unit switches between LTR and STR mid-year.

## How are property sales treated for VAT?

Residential property sales are either zero-rated or exempt for foreign buyers. The first supply of a newly completed residential building within 3 years of completion can be zero-rated, while subsequent resales are exempt. Buyers pay no 5% VAT either way; the developer cares because zero-rating allows input recovery.

| Sale type | VAT label | Buyer 5% charge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First supply within 3 years | Zero-rated | No |
| Residential resale | Exempt | No |
| Stock held past 3-year window | Often exempt | No |

- Ask the developer for the completion certificate date before assuming zero-rating.
- Treat off-plan first supplies as typically zero-rated when structured correctly.
- Keep the transfer fee receipt separate from any VAT invoice trail.

## Does commercial property rent attract 5% VAT?

Commercial property rent attracts standard 5% UAE VAT on offices, retail, warehouses, industrial units, and hotels that foreign buyers often hold through companies. Landlords must register when taxable turnover exceeds AED 375,000 in any 12-month period, issue tax invoices, and file quarterly EmaraTax returns with input recovery on attributable costs.

| Landlord status | Registration | Invoice duty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Taxable turnover under AED 375,000 | Usually optional | No mandatory VAT charge |
| Taxable turnover at or above AED 375,000 | Mandatory | 5% VAT tax invoices |
| Mixed residential + commercial | Taxable portion counts | Apportion shared costs |

- Recover input VAT only on costs tied to taxable rental activity.
- Apportion lobby and utility costs with a documented method.
- Use a checklist before SPA: TRN status, exclusive vs inclusive rent, OA invoice samples.

## Are holiday homes and short-term rentals taxable?

Holiday homes and short-term furnished tourist lets are treated as serviced accommodation at the standard 5% VAT rate once turnover crosses registration thresholds that foreign buyers often underestimate. Many operators stay below AED 375,000 and avoid mandatory registration, though voluntary registration can unlock input VAT on fit-out.

| Stay type | Typical VAT | Local levy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-term Ejari habitation | Exempt | None as VAT |
| DET/DTCM holiday home | Standard 5% | Tourism Dirham AED 10-15/night |
| Hotel / serviced apartment | Standard 5% | Tourism Dirham may apply |

- Confirm DET licence class before marketing nightly stays.
- Separate Tourism Dirham remittances from FTA VAT returns.
- Re-run the AED 375,000 test after each peak tourist season.

## How is VAT registration decided for property owners?

VAT registration for property owners is decided by taxable turnover, not unit count, which matters for foreign buyers holding mixed portfolios. Long-term residential rent is exempt and generally does not trigger registration, while commercial or short-term income above AED 375,000 in 12 months makes registration mandatory through emaratax.gov.ae.

| Your activity | VAT category | Registration trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-term residential only | Exempt | Generally no registration |
| Commercial or STR income | Taxable 5% | Mandatory above AED 375,000 turnover |
| Mixed residential + commercial | Split | Taxable portion counts to threshold |

- File quarterly returns and pay by the 28th of the month after quarter end.
- Keep records for 15 years once registered.
- Align the TRN entity with the title-holding company on the checklist.

## What practical steps should landlords take before leasing?

Practical landlord prep requires classifying each income stream as exempt residential or taxable commercial or short-term before foreign buyers sign management agreements. Model whether taxable turnover will exceed AED 375,000 over 12 months, register early if commercial or short-term rental applies, and confirm DET licensing for holiday homes.

| Step | Action | Number to watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Classify unit use | Exempt vs 5% taxable |
| 2 | Model taxable turnover | AED 375,000 / 12 months |
| 3 | Register if required | EmaraTax TRN issuance |

1. Classify each unit and income stream.
2. Model taxable turnover for the past and next 12 months.
3. Register before crossing the mandatory threshold.
4. Separate books for mixed portfolios.
5. Confirm DET licensing for STR.

## How does VAT differ from property transfer fees?

VAT differs from emirate transfer fees and means a separate federal charge: the Federal Tax Authority administers VAT on qualifying supplies, while Dubai Land Department charges a separate 4% transfer fee on many purchases by foreign buyers. Treat the transfer fee as an acquisition cost, never as a VAT invoice line.

| Cost line | Administrator | Typical figure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VAT on taxable rent/sale | FTA | 5% or 0% / exempt |
| Dubai transfer fee | DLD | Often 4% of price |
| Trustee / admin | Trustee office | Fixed AED fees |

- Ask whether commercial rent quotes are VAT-exclusive.
- Keep DLD receipts out of EmaraTax input claims.
- Use a completion checklist that lists VAT and transfer fees on separate rows.

Recent FTA property guidance typically updates mixed-use apportionment, holiday-home treatment, and invoice formats on fta.gov.ae, which foreign buyers should re-read before restructuring portfolios. Pull the latest clarification or a written adviser opinion tied to your TRN codes whenever 5% taxable streams are added to an exempt residential block.

| Topic | Why it matters | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mixed-use apportionment | Shared cost recovery | Document split method |
| Holiday-home rules | 5% vs exempt flip | Confirm DET class |
| Invoice formats | Audit defence | Match TRN on every tax invoice |

- Recheck guidance when expanding across emirates.
- Misclassification after crossing AED 375,000 is a common audit finding.
- Keep a red-flag file for units that changed use in the last 12 months.

## What mistakes do investors make on UAE property VAT?

Common UAE property VAT mistakes typically include charging 5% on exempt residential rent, ignoring short-term VAT after crossing AED 375,000 turnover, blocking input recovery while unregistered, and treating Dubai transfer fees as VAT. Foreign buyers who skip a compliance checklist create tenant disputes and penalty exposure.

| Mistake | Why it hurts |
| --- | --- |
| Charging VAT on residential rent | Overcharges tenant; compliance failure |
| Ignoring STR VAT after crossing turnover | Penalties and back-dated assessments |
| Blocking input VAT on commercial costs while unregistered | Lost recovery on legitimate expenses |
| Treating DLD fees as VAT | Wrong tax line; distorts DD budgets |
| Using listing rent for VAT turnover tests | Understates or overstates registration duty |

- Never use listing rent alone for the AED 375,000 test.
- Register before the first taxable quarter closes.
- Separate transfer fees from VAT in every underwriting model.

## What should you verify before signing a lease or SPA?

Before signing a lease or SPA, foreign buyers should request written VAT treatment from the counterparty, including whether commercial rent is quoted exclusive or inclusive of the 5% charge. Holiday-home setups need DET licence class clarity and a named filer; unsigned VAT-inclusive quotes without a TRN are a red flag.

| Document | Confirm | Fail signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial lease | Exclusive vs inclusive of 5% | No TRN on draft invoice |
| Holiday-home agreement | DET class + filer | Licence missing |
| Developer MOA | Zero-rated vs exempt sale | Vague "no tax" wording |

- Align UAE registration with home-country GST reporting.
- See [UAE double tax treaty guide](/guides/double-tax-treaty-uae/) and [tax on UAE property as UAE resident](/guides/tax-uae-property-uae-resident/).
- Keep counsel notes with the SPA pack for 15 years if registered.

## How should developers treat input VAT on construction?

Developers on zero-rated first supplies can recover VAT paid to contractors where invoices show the 5% charge inside the 3-year residential first-supply window that foreign buyers rely on for clean pricing. Exempt residential rental landlords cannot recover input VAT on refurbishment, so capex on exempt portfolios costs more on a cash basis.

| Supplier role | VAT on invoice | Recovery if zero-rated first supply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main contractor | Typically 5% | Recoverable |
| Design consultant | Typically 5% | Recoverable |
| Exempt residential landlord refurb | Typically 5% | Blocked |

- Split mixed-portfolio invoices year to year.
- Track the 3-year completion clock on a checklist.
- Do not assume recovery after the first-supply window closes.

## What records should a VAT-registered landlord keep?

VAT-registered landlords should keep Ejari contracts, rent invoices, supplier tax invoices, bank statements matching VAT payments, and apportionment worksheets for the statutory 15 years that foreign buyers rarely budget into holding costs. Digital copies are acceptable if auditable, and the TRN must match the title-holding entity.

| Record | Retention | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ejari + rent invoices | 15 years | Output VAT support |
| Supplier tax invoices | 15 years | Input VAT claims |
| EmaraTax filings | 15 years | Payment by 28th proof |

- Match TRN on every outbound invoice to the title entity.
- Store apportionment worksheets with quarterly returns.
- Use a records checklist before each EmaraTax filing cycle.

## How do VAT and service charges interact on invoices?

Service charges billed by owners associations often sit outside ordinary residential rent VAT treatment and require a sample OA invoice plus TRN status before foreign buyers budget net operating costs. Registered agents usually charge 5% VAT on management fees even when long-term residential rent itself remains exempt from VAT.

| Invoice line | Typical VAT | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-term residential rent | Exempt | No 5% on habitation lease |
| Agent management fee | 5% if registered | Hits net yield |
| STR guest invoice | 5% when taxable | Recheck AED 375,000 |

- Ask the OA for a sample invoice before SPA.
- Budget fit-out VAT separately on off-plan units.
- Flip LTR to STR only after re-running registration maths.
