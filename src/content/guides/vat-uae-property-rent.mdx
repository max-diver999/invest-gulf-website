UAE VAT on real estate splits into three practical buckets for investors: standard-rated at 5%, zero-rated at 0% with input recovery for suppliers, and exempt supplies where no VAT is charged and input recovery is blocked. Residential long-term rent is exempt. Commercial rent and most short-term holiday lets are standard-rated. Property sales are either zero-rated (first supply of new residential within three years of completion) or exempt on resale.

This guide explains how Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 and Executive Regulations apply to landlords, developers, and buyers. It is general information only; confirm treatment with an FTA-registered tax adviser for your entity structure.

## How does UAE VAT apply to property at a glance?

| Category | VAT rate | Who it affects | Input VAT recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard-rated | 5% | Commercial rent, STR, hotels | Yes, if registered |
| Zero-rated | 0% | First supply of new residential (3-year window) | Yes for developer |
| Exempt | None charged | Long-term residential rent, resale residential | No |

The category drives both what you charge tenants or buyers and whether you can reclaim VAT on costs. Developers and commercial operators feel the distinction daily; a private landlord with only Ejari-registered residential units usually has no VAT filing obligation.

## Is long-term residential rent exempt from VAT?

Yes. Leasing a residential unit for habitation under a long-term agreement registered in the usual way (typically annual or multi-year Ejari leases) is an exempt supply. You do not add 5% to the rent invoice, and residential rent does not count toward the AED 375,000 mandatory registration threshold.

"Long-term" means the unit is let as a home, not as nightly tourist inventory. The same physical apartment flips to standard-rated if operated under a holiday home licence with short stays.

| Property use | Typical VAT treatment |
| --- | --- |
| 12-month Ejari residential lease | Exempt |
| Hotel or serviced apartment (short stay) | Standard 5% |
| DTCM-licensed holiday home (short stay) | Standard 5% |
| Furnished daily or weekly tourist let | Standard 5% |

## How are property sales treated for VAT?

Subsequent sales of residential property are exempt: buyers pay no VAT on the purchase price. The first supply of a newly completed residential building within three years of its completion certificate can be zero-rated. Off-plan purchases from developers are often structured as that first supply.

For buyers, zero-rated and exempt sales both mean no 5% VAT on the price. The developer cares about the label because zero-rating allows recovery of input VAT on construction costs, which can affect pricing.

If a developer holds stock beyond the three-year window before first sale, zero-rating may no longer apply and the supply becomes exempt. Timing of first sales is therefore a developer structuring question, not a buyer VAT line item.

## Does commercial property rent attract 5% VAT?

Yes. Offices, retail, warehouses, industrial units, and hotel operations are standard-rated. Landlords must register when taxable turnover exceeds AED 375,000 in any 12-month period, issue tax invoices, and file quarterly returns through EmaraTax.

Registered commercial landlords can recover input VAT on attributable costs: maintenance where the supplier charged VAT, agent commissions with VAT, and professional fees tied to the rental business.

Mixed-use buildings require apportionment: residential rent stays exempt, commercial rent is taxable, and shared costs (lobby maintenance, common utilities) must be split on a documented, consistent basis per FTA guidance.

## Are holiday homes and short-term rentals taxable?

Yes. Furnished units let for short tourist stays under DET or DTCM rules are serviced accommodation at 5% VAT when turnover crosses registration thresholds. Many small operators stay below AED 375,000 and are not forced to register, but voluntary registration can make sense if input VAT is material.

Tourism Dirham (AED 10 to 15 per room per night in typical setups) is separate from VAT: it is a local tourism levy collected from guests and remitted under DET rules, not part of the FTA VAT return.

## How is VAT registration decided for property owners?

| Your activity | VAT category | Registration trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-term residential only | Exempt | Generally no registration |
| Commercial or STR income | Taxable 5% | Mandatory above AED 375,000 turnover |
| Mixed residential + commercial | Split | Taxable portion counts to threshold |

Registration runs through emaratax.gov.ae with Emirates ID, trade licence if applicable, bank details, and activity description. Approval often takes a few working days; you receive a TRN and must issue compliant tax invoices for taxable supplies.

Quarterly returns net output VAT collected against input VAT paid; payment is due by the 28th of the month following the quarter end. Records must be kept for 15 years.

## What practical steps should landlords take before leasing?

1. Classify each unit and income stream (residential exempt vs commercial or STR taxable).
2. Model whether taxable turnover will exceed AED 375,000 in the past or next 12 months.
3. Register before crossing the mandatory threshold if commercial or STR applies.
4. Separate accounting for exempt residential rent vs taxable streams in mixed portfolios.
5. For STR, confirm DET licensing and whether VAT invoices to guests are required.

See [rental income tax UAE](/guides/rental-income-tax-uae/) for personal income tax angles (UAE has no personal income tax, but home-country rules may still apply).

## How does VAT differ from property transfer fees?

VAT is administered by the Federal Tax Authority on qualifying supplies. Emirate-level property registration fees (for example Dubai's transfer fee on purchase) are not VAT. They are separate acquisition costs and belong in total cost of ownership models, not in VAT returns.

For a full stack of purchase and holding costs, see [Dubai property taxes explained](/guides/dubai-property-taxes-explained/).

## What changed recently in FTA property guidance?

FTA publishes updates on mixed-use apportionment, holiday home treatment, and invoice formats on fta.gov.ae. Before you restructure a portfolio or add STR to a residential block, pull the latest public clarification or ask your tax adviser for a written opinion tied to your TRN activity codes.

Corporate landlords expanding from one emirate to another should confirm whether each lease is still "residential" under local registration practice; misclassification is a common audit finding.

## What mistakes do investors make on UAE property VAT?

| Mistake | Why it hurts |
| --- | --- |
| Charging VAT on residential rent | Overcharges tenant; compliance failure |
| Ignoring STR VAT after crossing turnover | Penalties and back-dated assessments |
| Blocking input VAT on commercial costs while unregistered | Lost recovery on legitimate expenses |
| Treating DLD fees as VAT | Wrong tax line; distorts DD budgets |
| Using listing rent for VAT turnover tests | Understates or overstates registration duty |

## What should you verify before signing a lease or SPA?

Request written confirmation of VAT treatment from the counterparty (developer MOA, landlord invoice template, or management agreement). For commercial leases, check whether rent is quoted VAT-exclusive or inclusive. For holiday home setups, confirm DET licence class and who files VAT.

Cross-border owners should align UAE VAT registration with home-country GST or VAT reporting; see [UAE double tax treaty guide](/guides/double-tax-treaty-uae/) and [tax on UAE property as UAE resident](/guides/tax-uae-property-uae-resident/).

## How should developers treat input VAT on construction?

Developers on zero-rated first supplies can recover VAT paid to contractors, consultants, and suppliers where invoices show VAT. Exempt residential rental landlords cannot recover input VAT on refurbishment, which makes capex on exempt portfolios more expensive on a cash basis. Mixed portfolios should split invoices and apportion shared costs consistently year to year to survive FTA review.

## What records should a VAT-registered landlord keep?

Keep Ejari contracts, rent invoices, tax invoices from suppliers, bank statements matching VAT payments, and apportionment worksheets for mixed buildings. Digital copies are acceptable if auditable. Align TRN on all outbound invoices with the entity that owns the lease on the title or sublease chain.

## How do VAT and service charges interact on invoices?

Service charges billed by owners associations are often exempt or outside standard VAT treatment depending on how the OA invoices; commercial service charge lines may differ from residential. Ask the OA for a sample invoice and TRN status before you budget net operating costs. Property management fees charged by agents usually carry 5% VAT when the agent is registered, which affects your net rent stack even when rent itself is exempt.

Off-plan buyers rarely see VAT on the unit price when the supply is exempt or zero-rated, but fit-out, furnishing, and STR setup vendors may invoice VAT separately. Budget those lines in total cost of ownership alongside OA fees. If you flip from LTR to STR, re-run registration tests because taxable turnover can cross AED 375,000 quickly in tourist seasons.
