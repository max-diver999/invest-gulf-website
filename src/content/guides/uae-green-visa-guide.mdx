The UAE Green Visa is a 5-year self-sponsored residence permit for skilled employees, freelancers, qualifying investors, and outstanding students who do not want residency tied to a single UAE employer. Processing commonly runs 2 to 4 weeks with government out-of-pocket costs around AED 1,500 to 1,800. This guide covers routes, documents, family sponsorship, and renewal timing for 2026 applicants.

Freelance Green Visa files typically fail when bank statements show one large transfer instead of six months of consistent inflows near AED 30,000 per month toward the AED 360,000 annual test, even when the TECOM or free-zone permit itself is valid at AED 7,500 to 10,000 per year. ## What is the UAE Green Visa in 2026?

The UAE Green Visa typically means 5-year renewable self-sponsored residency that stays with the holder when they switch employers, as long as skilled, freelance, investor, or student qualification remains active. There is no property purchase minimum on this route, unlike Golden Visa property at AED 2 million. We treat Green Visa as the default professional track for foreign buyers who are not yet buying property.

| Feature | Green Visa |
|---|---|
| Duration | 5 years, renewable |
| Sponsor | Self-sponsored |
| Minimum property requirement | None |
| Main qualification routes | Skilled employee / freelancer / investor / student |
| Family sponsorship | Yes |
| Emirates ID | Issued for 5 years |
| Can switch jobs | Yes, visa is not tied to employer |
| Path to citizenship | No (neither does Golden Visa automatically) |

The visa is processed through the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP), with Dubai and Abu Dhabi portals largely aligned on the Green Visa framework.

## Who qualifies for the UAE Green Visa?

Four Green Visa tracks typically cover skilled employees on AED 15,000+ monthly salary in ISCO levels 1 to 3, freelancers proving AED 360,000 annual income, business investors with active UAE capital from AED 500,000, and outstanding students at GPA 3.75. Mismatch between track and paperwork is the most common ICP return reason. We confirm the route checklist before any typing-centre fee.

- Skilled: ISCO 1 to 3 and AED 15,000+ salary
- Freelance: permit plus AED 360,000/year proof
- Investor: active capital from AED 500,000
- Student/graduate: GPA 3.75 or top-100 foreign degree

### Route 1: Skilled employee

Requirements typically include UAE employment with a valid work permit, an ISCO skill level 1 to 3 role, and a minimum monthly salary of AED 15,000 as stated in current ICP guidance (verify at application because thresholds are reviewed). Unlike a standard employment visa cancelled on resignation, the Green Visa belongs to the holder for a grace period to find new work. We apply once the work permit is already live.

### Route 2: Freelancer and self-employed professional

Freelancer qualification typically requires a valid UAE freelance permit or business licence plus annual income of at least AED 360,000 (AED 30,000 per month) or accepted financial self-sufficiency evidence across about 6 months of statements. TECOM permits often cost AED 7,500 to 10,000 per year depending on activity. We reject one-off large payments without consistent flow.

### Route 3: Business investors

Business investors typically need an active UAE mainland or free-zone company with registered share capital of at least AED 500,000 or equivalent qualifying investment, not a dormant shell. Golden Visa investor tiers sit higher (including AED 2 million property for 10 years). We separate Green Visa business capital from Golden Visa property math.

### Route 4: Outstanding students and fresh graduates

Student and graduate tracks typically need UAE university standing in the top 5% with GPA 3.75 on a 4.0 scale, graduation within the past 2 years at that GPA, or a foreign university ranked in the top 100 globally. The route buys career-establishment time without immediate ISCO employment. We use it as a bridge visa for high-achieving graduates.

## How does the Green Visa compare to the Golden Visa?

Green Visa typically delivers 5-year self-sponsored residency without a property floor, while Golden Visa typically delivers 10-year residency via AED 2 million UAE real estate, exceptional talent, or larger investment. High-earning freelancers and skilled professionals usually finish Green Visa faster and cheaper than property-linked Golden Visa. Invest Gulf upgrades to Golden Visa only after the AED 2 million property threshold is real.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Duration | 5 years | 10 years |
| Property minimum | None | Often AED 2M property route |
| Typical user | Skilled / freelance / small investor | Property / large investor / talent |
| Family sponsorship | Yes | Broader options in many cases |

## How do you apply for the UAE Green Visa step by step?

Green Visa applications typically run confirm route, gather documents, complete medical fitness for AED 320 to 370, submit via ICP, GDRFA, typing centre (AED 200 to 500), or consultant (AED 1,500 to 4,000+), then Emirates ID for AED 370 over a 2 to 4 week core timeline. Peak months can stretch to 6 to 8 weeks. We start only after the category checklist is complete.

### Step 1: Confirm your qualification route

Pick the track that matches your documents before you pay fees: skilled employee (ISCO tier 1-3 and AED 15,000+ salary), freelancer or self-employed (permit plus AED 360,000/year income proof), investor (active UAE company with qualifying capital), or student or graduate (GPA and ranking evidence).

### Step 2: Gather your documents

Standard document set typically includes passport with at least 6 months' validity, entry stamp or existing visa copy, UAE photo specs, Emirates ID if resident, health insurance, tenancy or title proof, and category-specific evidence. Attestation or Arabic translation may be required for non-English or non-Arabic originals.

### Step 3: Medical fitness test

Every residency applicant must complete a medical fitness test at an ICP-approved clinic. Results are typically available within 24 to 48 hours at AED 320 to 370. Book ahead in busy periods.

### Step 4: Submit the application

Submission can run through ICP Smart Services, GDRFA Dubai, typing centres, or immigration consultants. Typical processing time is **2 to 4 weeks** for straightforward files.

### Step 5: Emirates ID issuance

Emirates ID for Green Visa holders is issued for **5 years**, matching visa duration, at AED 370. Full walkthrough: [Emirates ID application guide](/guides/emirates-id-application-guide/).

### Step 6: Visa stamping

Outside-UAE applicants enter on an entry permit then complete stamping in-country. In-country status adjustment usually needs no exit-re-entry trip.

## How much does the UAE Green Visa cost in government fees?

Green Visa government costs typically total AED 1,500 to 1,800 out of pocket when you add visa fees of AED 700 to 1,000, medical fitness of AED 320 to 370, Emirates ID of AED 370, and biometrics or service charges of AED 150 to 300. Typing centres and consultants sit on top of that band. Invest Gulf quotes government fees separately from advisory packages.

| Cost item | Typical AED range |
| --- | --- |
| Visa / government fees | 700 to 1,000 |
| Medical fitness | 320 to 370 |
| Emirates ID | 370 |
| Biometrics / service | 150 to 300 |
| Common total (government) | 1,500 to 1,800 |
| Typing centre (optional) | 200 to 500 |
| Consultant package (optional) | 1,500 to 4,000+ |

Fees are updated periodically. Confirm current rates on the ICP portal before submitting.

## Can Green Visa holders sponsor family members?

Green Visa holders typically can sponsor a spouse and children under standard UAE family visa rules once accommodation size, health insurance, and financial means evidence are ready for ICP review. Budget family medical tests and Emirates ID cards beyond the principal's AED 1,500 to 1,800 government band. We sequence family files after the principal Emirates ID is live.

- Principal Emirates ID first
- Family medical and insurance next
- Accommodation proof ready for ICP

## What changes when you live in the UAE on a Green Visa?

Living on a Green Visa typically does not auto-confer UAE tax residency; the Ministry of Finance still uses physical presence of 183 days or a centre-of-life test across the tax year. Freehold remains available without Golden Visa unless AED 2 million property criteria are met. We model healthcare of AED 600 to 1,200 basic or AED 3,000 to 8,000+ comprehensive.

- Tax residency still needs 183 days or centre-of-life proof
- Golden Visa property floor stays AED 2 million
- Driving licence conversion often 1 to 2 weeks after Emirates ID

See also the [UAE tax residency 183-day rule guide](/guides/uae-tax-residency-property/).

## How do you renew the UAE Green Visa?

Green Visa renewal typically mirrors the initial application under the same category, with government fees again around AED 1,500 to 1,800 and a recommended start window of 60 to 90 days before expiry. Dubai overstay fines commonly run AED 200 per day and renewals are not automatic. Our calendars a renewal checklist at the 4-year, 9-month mark.

- Re-qualify under the same category
- Budget AED 1,500 to 1,800 government fees again
- Start 60 to 90 days before expiry

## What mistakes delay Green Visa approval?

Approval delays typically come from income proof that fails the AED 360,000 freelance test, salary certificates that do not match the ISCO tier, passports under 6 months' validity, missing medical results, or submitting before the freelance permit is active. Peak September and January queues can add 2 to 4 weeks on top of the normal 2 to 4 week core. Invest Gulf blocks portal submission until the full pack is export-ready.

| Mistake | Typical impact |
| --- | --- |
| One-off income dump | Rework; 2 to 4 weeks |
| ISCO / salary mismatch | ICP return |
| Permit not yet active | Rejection or hold |
| Late renewal | AED 200/day overstay risk |
