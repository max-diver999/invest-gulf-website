## What should you verify for Handover in the off-plan lifecycle?

Handover is where construction risk ends and operating cost reality begins, service charges, vacancy, and DEWA bills replace instalment schedules.
## What should you verify for Pre-handover preparation (30 to 60 days before notice?

### Document pack

- Oqood certificate
- SPA copy with payment receipts
- Passport and Emirates ID
- Golden Visa / residence visa copy
- Power of Attorney (if remote handover via representative)

### Remote handover

Non-resident buyers use POA attested for representative to:

- Attend snagging
- Sign handover acceptance (carefully; see snagging section)
- Activate DEWA
- Collect keys

Never give unrestricted POA without trusted representative.

## What should you verify for Handover notice and appointment?

Do not assume handover date equals SPA date, delays of 6 to 18 months occurred even with Tier 1 developers in 2023 to 2025 cycle.

## How does snagging work for Gulf buyers in 2026?

Professional snagging company (AED 1,500 to 4,000): 2 to 4 hour systematic report with photos, standard for investors.

DIY: Acceptable for experienced owners; risk missing MEP defects.

### Snagging report delivery

Submit report to developer within handover window, often 7 to 14 days. Developer schedules remediation batches.

### Conditional acceptance

Best practice: sign handover with reservation of rights for listed defects, not blank acceptance. Some developers pressure clean sign-off, resist until critical MEP resolved or formally logged.

### Defect liability period

12 months standard from handover for workmanship. Structural longer per SPA. Calendar reminders at month 10 for final defect sweep.

## What should you verify for Final payment and settlement?

### Post-handover plans

If 40/60 post-handover applies, handover may require only 40% paid, but monthly developer instalments begin immediately after keys. Budget DEWA + service charge + developer instalment simultaneously.

### Payment before snagging?

Avoid paying final 100% before snagging report filed unless SPA requires it, negotiate holdback for critical defects where possible.

## What should you verify for Dewa activation?

### Steps

1. Apply online (DEWA website/app) or service centre
2. Provide title deed or developer handover letter + Oqood
3. Emirates ID and contact details
4. Pay security deposit (~AED 2,000 apartment; higher villas)
5. Receive account number: activation 1 to 3 days

### Housing fee

5% of DEWA bill as Dubai Municipality housing fee, appears on bill automatically.

### Smart meter

Verify meter reading at activation, baseline for future tenant billing disputes.

## What should you verify for Title deed conversion (oqood → title deed)?

| Developer tier | Typical issuance |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 2 weeks  to  2 months post-handover |
| Tier 2 | 1 to 4 months |

### Why title deed matters

- Mortgage registration requires title deed
- Clean resale, buyers prefer title over late Oqood
- Golden Visa already satisfied by Oqood, title strengthens banking

4% DLD was paid at Oqood, no second 4% at title conversion for standard off-plan.
## What should you verify for Ejari registration (if renting)?

### Long-term rental setup

1. Fit-out unit (if unfurnished: 2 to 6 weeks)
2. Market on Property Finder / Bayut
3. Sign tenancy contract
4. Register Ejari (~AED 220 via typing centre or app)
5. Connect DEWA tenant account or landlord billing arrangement

### Rent benchmarking

Use Ejari transacted rents in building, not listing asks. Gross yield = annual rent / total acquisition cost including 6 to 9% transaction stack.

### STR / holiday homes

Separate DET holiday home permit, AED 1,520/year apartment. OA may prohibit. See cluster STR guides.
## What checklist should run before you sign?

At unit:
- Snagging inspection complete (2 to 4 hours)
- Meter readings photographed
- Keys, fobs, parking cards counted
- Warranty documents received
- Developer handover form signed with defect list attached
- Final payment released per SPA

Within 7 days:
- Snagging report submitted to developer
- DEWA activated
- District cooling account opened
- Service charge deposit paid
- OA management introduced
- Title deed status tracked with developer

Within 30 days:
- Critical defects remediation scheduled
- Ejari prep if renting (photos, listing)
- Insurance quote (contents/landlord)
- Property manager engaged (if overseas owner)

Within 12 months:
- Defect liability items closed out
- Final snagging sweep before liability expires

## Who should attend Dubai handover with you?

Bring the registered buyer (passport and Emirates ID if available), your snagging inspector, and a trusted friend who can photograph defects while you walk the unit. If you use a mortgage, notify the bank before handover so the final drawdown aligns with the developer's completion certificate. Power of Attorney holders must carry the notarised POA and their own ID; trustees will not release keys without matching SPA names.

## How does life work immediately after handover in 2026?

After keys, sequence DEWA and district cooling first, then furniture delivery and Ejari if you plan to rent. Owners Association move-in permits often require a refundable deposit and a booked lift slot, so schedule movers only after the OA confirms rules. Keep every handover email, snagging PDF, and payment receipt in one folder: you will need them for warranty callbacks, DLD title conversion, and the first resale file.

## What should you verify for Post-handover revenue generation timeline?

**Renovation scenario (8 to 16 weeks):**
- Major defect rectification: 3 to 6 weeks
- Kitchen/bathroom upgrades: 6 to 10 weeks
- Flooring replacement: 2 to 3 weeks
- Final staging and marketing: 2 weeks

Model lost rental income from handover to first lease when comparing off-plan total returns versus secondary market immediate cash flow.

## What should you verify for Warranty and defect management?

Unresolved defects become owner costs after warranty expiry and reduce effective net yield through ongoing maintenance expense. Log every item in the snagging report with photos and dates; developers respond faster when defects are grouped by room and tied to the SPA defect liability clause.

## Dubai Property Handover Checklist: planning checklist

Use this closing block as a single reference for move-in cash need and inspection scope, not as a substitute for your SPA handover letter or developer snagging appointment.

### Transaction specifics

At handover, inspect all 12 categories: flooring (level check, chip/scratch), walls (paint finish, alignment), kitchen (cabinet alignment, countertop seams, appliance function), bathrooms (waterproofing, tile grout, drainage speed), windows (seal integrity, opening mechanism), doors (alignment, lock function), electrical (all sockets, switches, light fittings), AC (airflow, thermostat response), water pressure, intercom, car park allocation, and common-area access.

### Transaction timeline and hidden fees

Handover-day cost stack: final payment tranche (5 to 10 % of the purchase price held until completion), snagging-company fee (1,500 to 4,000 AED per unit), DEWA connection deposit (2,000 to 4,000 AED), chiller deposit if applicable (500 to 2,000 AED), community move-in fee (500 to 1,000 AED), and first-quarter service charges (calculated from handover date, not calendar quarter). Off-plan buyers should budget an additional 100 to 250 AED per square foot for basic fit-out if the unit is delivered as a shell. Total move-in costs beyond the sale price: typically 15,000 to 40,000 AED for an apartment and 30,000 to 80,000 AED for a villa. Cross-check service charge notices against the [Dubai Service Charge Index Explained](/guides/dubai-service-charge-index-explained/) guide before you set your first rent.
