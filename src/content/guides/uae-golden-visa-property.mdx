**TL;DR:** UAE Golden Visa requires AED 2M minimum property ownership for 10-year renewable residency. Property can be cash or mortgaged (with bank NOC), single unit or multiple properties, completed or off-plan. Must be in designated foreign-ownership zones with proper Title Deed or Oqood registration.

The **UAE Golden Visa property pathway** provides long-term residency through real estate investment. Understanding the **AED 2M qualification mechanics** ensures proper investment structure and smooth visa processing.

This guide covers the **property qualification requirements** — for latest rule changes and application updates, see our [2026 Golden Visa update guide](/guides/uae-golden-visa-property-2026/).

## AED 2M property requirement explained

### What counts toward the AED 2M threshold

The **AED 2 million minimum** is based on:

**Registered property value:** The amount recorded on your Title Deed (completed property) or Oqood certificate (off-plan property)

**Multiple property aggregation:** You can combine several properties if each is registered in your name

**Mortgage scenarios accepted:** Full registered value counts even with outstanding mortgage, provided:
- Property financed through UAE-licensed bank
- Bank provides No Objection Certificate (NOC) for Golden Visa application  
- You maintain required ownership documentation

### What does NOT count

**DLD transfer fees:** The 4% DLD registration fee is not included in the AED 2M calculation

**Agent commissions:** Broker fees and transaction costs separate from property value

**Commercial property:** Golden Visa typically requires residential property (verify current ICA guidance)

**Joint ownership:** Property must be registered in the applicant's name (spousal ownership rules may vary)

For mortgage scenarios and financing options, see our detailed [Golden Visa mortgage guide](/guides/golden-visa-mortgage-property-uae/).

## Property financing and mortgage scenarios

### Cash purchase pathway

**Straightforward qualification:**
- Purchase property worth AED 2M+ with cash
- Receive Title Deed showing full ownership
- Apply for Golden Visa with clear ownership documentation
- No additional bank requirements or NOCs needed

### Mortgage financing pathway (updated April 2026)

**Key rule change:** The previous 50% down payment requirement has been removed

**Current requirements:**
- Property registered value ≥ AED 2M (full purchase price)
- Mortgage through UAE-licensed bank (local or international branch)
- Bank-issued NOC confirming Golden Visa support  
- Maintain mortgage payments and property ownership

**Practical considerations:**
- UAE banks typically require 20-25% down payment for expat mortgages
- Golden Visa eligibility based on property value, not equity percentage
- Bank NOC processing may add 1-2 weeks to application timeline

## Off-plan property qualification

### Oqood registration system

**Off-plan properties qualify** through Dubai's Oqood system:

- Sales Purchase Agreement (SPA) registered with DLD
- Oqood certificate issued showing property details and value
- Payment schedule and escrow account (RERA-regulated)
- Golden Visa application possible before property completion

Learn more about off-plan timing and strategies in our [Golden Visa off-plan guide](/guides/golden-visa-off-plan-property-uae/).

### Developer requirements

**Approved developers:** Property must be from RERA-registered developer with:
- Valid development license and approvals
- DLD-regulated escrow account for buyer protection
- Construction timeline and handover commitments

**Due diligence essentials:**
- Verify developer track record and delivery history
- Confirm project approvals and construction status
- Check escrow account setup and payment protection

## Application process and documentation

### Required documentation

**Property ownership proof:**
- Title Deed (completed property) or Oqood certificate (off-plan)  
- Property valuation if requested by authorities
- NOC from bank (if mortgaged property)

**Personal documentation:**
- Valid passport with UAE entry stamp
- Emirates ID (if already UAE resident)
- Recent passport photos (as per ICA specifications)
- Health insurance certificate

**Financial documentation:**
- Bank statements showing financial capacity
- Salary certificate or income proof (if employed)
- Investment documentation if applicable

### Application timeline and process

**Inside UAE application:**
- Submit documents through ICA channels or approved centers
- Processing time: 5-15 working days typically
- Cannot leave UAE during processing period
- Fees: Approximately AED 4,000-5,500 per applicant

**Outside UAE application:**
- Apply through UAE consulates or approved visa centers
- Processing time: 2-4 weeks typically
- May require additional documentation verification
- Entry to UAE required for final processing steps

## Property valuation and verification

### Official valuation requirements

**DLD registered value:** Primary qualification criteria based on official registration

**Independent valuation:** May be requested by authorities, especially for:
- Older properties with significant appreciation
- Properties in emerging areas without comparable sales
- Off-plan properties with complex payment structures

### Market value vs registered value

**Qualification based on registration:** Golden Visa uses official DLD/ADREC recorded value

**Market fluctuations:** Property market value changes don't affect visa eligibility if registration value ≥ AED 2M

**Renewal considerations:** Property value rechecked at 10-year renewal — maintain qualifying investment level

## Property valuation scenarios

| Property Scenario | Registered Value | Market Value | Golden Visa Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| New purchase AED 2M | AED 2M | AED 2M | ✅ Qualifies |
| Property appreciation | AED 2M | AED 2.5M | ✅ Qualifies (registration basis) |
| Market decline | AED 2M | AED 1.7M | ✅ Qualifies (registration basis) |
| Multiple properties | AED 1.2M + 0.9M | Market varies | ✅ Qualifies (AED 2.1M total) |

## Multiple property aggregation strategies

### Combining properties for AED 2M threshold

**Aggregation rules:**
- Each property must be registered in applicant's name
- Properties can be across different emirates (if in designated zones)
- Mix of completed and off-plan properties acceptable
- Combined registered values must equal or exceed AED 2M

**Strategic approaches:**
- Diversify across districts to reduce concentration risk
- Balance completed properties (immediate rental) with off-plan (potential appreciation)
- Consider different property types (apartments, villas, townhouses)

### Portfolio management considerations

**Liquidity planning:** Multiple smaller properties may offer easier exit options than single large unit

**Rental optimization:** Different property types and locations can optimize tenant diversity

**Market risk distribution:** Spread investment across various development stages and areas

## Renewal and property ownership obligations

### 10-year renewal requirements

**Property ownership verification:** Authorities check continued ownership of qualifying property

**Minimum value maintenance:** Property must maintain AED 2M registration value or be replaced with qualifying investment

**Documentation updates:** Provide current Title Deeds and ownership proof at renewal

### Exit strategies and property changes

**Selling qualifying property:** May impact Golden Visa renewal eligibility

**Replacement property:** Can substitute with different property meeting AED 2M threshold

**Alternative qualification:** May qualify through other Golden Visa categories (investment, income, etc.) if property sold
