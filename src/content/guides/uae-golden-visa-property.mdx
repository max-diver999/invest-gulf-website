## What should Gulf buyers budget for Golden Visa tiers linked to property in 2026?

The standard property route typically requires a registered value of at least AED 2 million for a 10-year renewable UAE residence permit, and Invest Gulf budgets government and medical fees of AED 4,000 to 5,500 per adult outside the property price.

What the 10-year route generally includes:

- Long-term UAE residence without a local employer sponsor
- Ability to sponsor eligible family members subject to GDRFA rules
- Multiple entry and exit flexibility relative to tourist status
- Access to resident banking, telecom, and utility contracts

What it does not include:

- UAE citizenship
- Automatic tax residency certificate
- Guaranteed approval
- Protection against a bad property investment

## How does the AED 2 million threshold use registered property value?

The AED 2 million test typically uses the Title Deed or Oqood registered value, not the 4% DLD transfer fee or furnishing packages, and Invest Gulf treats brochure-only values as a red flag before any reservation deposit.

Usually does not count:

- DLD 4% transfer fee and trustee charges
- Unregistered reservation deposits before Oqood or title
- Property outside freehold or investor-eligible zones
- Marketing brochure values without land-department registration

The property route starts with a registered value of at least AED 2 million, not a developer headline price. In Dubai, buyers should retain the Title Deed or Oqood record, then confirm the current submission route with GDRFA Dubai or ICP for the relevant emirate. The 4% DLD transfer fee is a transaction cost and does not add to the qualifying property value. For a mortgaged purchase, secondary reporting in 2026 indicates that an outstanding UAE bank mortgage may not by itself prevent eligibility, but a bank no-objection letter can be required and practice can differ by file. Multiple units may sometimes be aggregated if they are correctly registered, yet this should be confirmed in writing before a split purchase. This is a qualified process summary, not an approval guarantee. Only GDRFA, ICP, DLD, and the relevant land department can confirm a current application.

## Can off-plan property qualify after Oqood registration?

Off-plan property typically supports an application only after Oqood or equivalent registration and a recorded value of AED 2 million or more, and we treat launch brochures without RERA registration as a red flag.

| Red flag | Why visa risk rises |
|---|---|
| Visa eligible launch but Oqood delayed months | No registered value until DLD registration completes |
| Combined units across emirates without aggregation policy | Documentation mismatch at GDRFA |
| Mortgage promised pre-handover without bank NOC pathway | Bank may block visa until title conditions met |
| Developer not RERA-registered | Oqood may never issue; funds at risk |
| Handover pushed 24+ months | Long wait before residency plan works |

Before relying on off-plan for visa timing, read the [Off-Plan Property Dubai Guide](/guides/off-plan-property-dubai-guide/).

An off-plan buyer should separate the sale timeline from the visa timeline because marketing can claim visa eligibility at launch while the application still needs a registered Oqood or Title Deed at AED 2 million. Practical red flags include an Oqood delayed by months, units spread across emirates without written aggregation guidance, a lender without a bank-NOC pathway, and a developer that is not RERA-registered. A handover delayed by 24 months or more can leave residency plans waiting even when the SPA looks complete on paper. Before a non-refundable payment, obtain the registration schedule, developer details, and written confirmation of the current GDRFA or ICP process for your emirate. These checks are process controls, not proof that every off-plan unit qualifies for residency approval in writing.

## Is Golden Visa the same as UAE tax residency?

Golden Visa is typically an immigration status only, while UAE tax residency often links to 183 days of presence or centre-of-interests tests, and we separate visa stamping from home-country tax advice across a full year.

- UK: non-dom changes from April 2025 affect remittance planning
- US citizens: worldwide taxation continues regardless of UAE visa
- EU/EEA: residency tests vary by country

## Will Golden Visa rules tighten in 2026?

Rules typically evolve on mortgage evidence and document channels rather than deleting the AED 2 million property route, and Invest Gulf rechecks GDRFA and ICP bulletins every 3 to 6 months before SPA signing.

None of these updates invalidate the programme for many buyers; they change timing and paperwork.

## Is a property Golden Visa the right decision for you?

A property Golden Visa typically fits buyers who want 10-year UAE residence and already like the AED 2 million asset on yield and exit tests, and our underwriting treats passport-seeking or sub-90-day residency needs as a poor fit.

**Weak fit:**

- Primary goal is passport or instant citizenship
- You need residency in under 90 days but chose 24-month handover off-plan
- Property is in an illiquid emirate or project with no resale depth
- You have not modelled home-country tax on rental income or eventual sale
