## What is Tawtheeq and what does it cost?

Tawtheeq is Abu Dhabi's mandatory tenancy registration, similar to Dubai Ejari, and typically costs about AED 220 plus admin for visa renewals, ADD utility setup, and many ADEK steps. Invest Gulf treats an agent who says "we register later" as a red flag in the area before any deposit leaves your account.

Budget about AED 220 plus admin, and confirm current DMT tariff at signing.

Tawtheeq checklist:
- Landlord is Tawtheeq-ready at signing
- Confirm current DMT tariff
- Keep registration receipt for visa and utilities

## How does cheque culture work?

Cheque culture in Abu Dhabi typically means 4-6 post-dated cheques per year, with four cheques plus a slightly higher annual total versus six cheques a common negotiation trade on AED 10,000-18,000 family leases.
