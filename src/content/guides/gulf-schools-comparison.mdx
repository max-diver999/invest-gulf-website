American AP tracks are strongest in Saudi compound schools and select UAE campuses. Match curriculum to repatriation plan first, then filter by regulator ratings and commute radius.

Where Saudi packages win: gross salary, compound housing, lower rent outside villa belts, not headline SAR tuition.

## How do Manama and Dubai compare for finance-sector school value?

Manama offers compact British mid tiers at lower FX-adjusted burn than Dubai Marina corridors, with shorter commutes inside the island. Dubai wins on backup school choice if a seat falls through. Finance-sector packages in Bahrain often include housing near Seef or Diplomatic Area, model school bus time before you accept.

## How does school-stage fee shape compare for Gulf buyers in 2026?

IB DP years: budget 100 to 115% of school average, peak burn years 11 to 12.

## How does decision framework compare for Gulf buyers in 2026?

Shortlist three schools per city before you compare country flags. Weight seat certainty 40%, all-in fee 35%, and commute or housing fit 25%. If two cities tie on salary, pick the market with a confirmed school offer in writing, not the cheaper rent alone.
