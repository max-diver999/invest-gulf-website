# Leaving Dubai: Complete Exit Checklist for Expats 2026

## Why does leaving Dubai take longer than most expats expect?

Leaving Dubai typically takes 8 weeks of parallel work because visa, lease, school, car, and bank tracks each need 2 to 6 weeks and block each other if run in sequence. Invest Gulf treats sequential exits as the main reason deposits and school fees get stuck after flights are booked.

Leaving Dubai on a family employment visa typically needs an 8-week runway because school notice alone runs 10 to 12 weeks at many international campuses, while Ejari exit needs 2 to 4 weeks and visa cancellation needs 5 to 10 working days after employment ends. Invest Gulf exit files show the expensive failures are late school notice at AED 15,000 to 30,000 per missed term, DEWA refunds delayed 10 to 21 working days when bank details are wrong, and resident bank accounts freezing 30 to 90 days after ICA flags cancellation. Golden Visa holders follow a different cancellation path than MOHRE employment visas, so do not copy a colleague's checklist without checking your visa type. Run resignation, school notice, and landlord notice in week 0 rather than waiting for a confirmed flight, and keep a cash buffer of AED 3,000 to 15,000 for typing fees, final utilities, and car sale slippage before shipping quotes of AED 8,000 to 25,000.

Exit costs for a family typically stack visa typing of AED 500 to 1,500, DEWA final bills of AED 500 to 2,000 in summer, and car sale discounts of AED 3,000 to 8,000 versus retail, before optional shipping of AED 8,000 to 25,000. Invest Gulf underwriting adds a school-penalty contingency of AED 15,000 to 30,000 when term notice is late on campuses charging AED 60,000 to 120,000 per year, because that single miss can exceed every other exit line combined. Non-resident bank conversion should finish 4 to 5 weeks before the cancellation stamp so rent or deposit refunds of AED 2,000 to 4,000 are not trapped for 30 to 90 days after ICA updates residency status for foreign buyers leaving this market.

| Track | Typical duration | Starts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School notice | 10 to 12 weeks | Week 0 |
| Resignation / MOHRE | 30 to 90 days notice | Week 0 |
| Ejari exit and deposit | 2 to 4 weeks | Week 4 to 6 |
| Visa cancellation | 5 to 10 working days | After employment ends |
| DEWA refund | 10 to 21 working days | After final meter read |

## What should you do 8 weeks before departure?

Eight-week actions typically mean written school notice, written resignation with a confirmed last working day, and landlord notice so Ejari cancellation can be booked within 2 to 4 weeks of the target move-out date. Invest Gulf also flags Golden Visa holders to confirm GDRFA rules before any cancellation request is filed, because a late school notice alone can cost AED 15,000 to 30,000 per missed term on campuses charging AED 60,000 to 120,000 per year.

### Employment resignation and visa timeline

| Step | Who | Typical wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submit resignation (written) | You to HR | Day 0 |
| MOHRE contract end filing | Employer | 3 to 5 days |
| Visa cancellation stamp | GDRFA / ICA | 5 to 7 working days |
| 30-day grace period starts | Automatic | After stamp |
| Emirates ID deactivated | Automatic | On cancellation |

Freelancers and freezone licence holders cancel via their authority; timelines vary from 2 to 6 weeks. Investment-linked Golden Visa holders should review [Golden Visa property rules](/guides/golden-visa-mortgage-property-uae/) before submitting anything.

### School notice: give it immediately

Dubai schools typically penalise late notice with one full term's fees. On an international school at AED 60,000 to 120,000 per year, one missed term's notice costs AED 15,000 to 30,000.

| Notice type | Typical requirement | Penalty for late |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard departure | One full term written notice | One term's tuition |
| Documented relocation | 4 weeks written plus proof | Often waived or halved |
| Mid-term departure | Immediate with documentation | Case by case |

## What should you handle 6 weeks before move-out?

Six-week tasks typically include booking Ejari cancellation with the landlord, listing the car if selling, and starting furniture sales if you are not shipping. Invest Gulf coordinates lease end dates with the DEWA final meter read so deposits are not trapped for 30 days or more.

Ejari and DEWA checklist:

1. Joint walkthrough with photographs before handover
2. Signed handover form for deposit recovery (often 30 days)
3. DEWA disconnection by the account holder only
4. Confirm refund bank details before the final read

| Account type | Security deposit | Refund timeline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio / 1BR apartment | AED 2,000 | 10 to 21 working days |
| 2BR+ apartment | AED 2,000 | 10 to 21 working days |
| Villa | AED 4,000 | 10 to 21 working days |

See the [Ejari tenancy guide](/guides/dubai-ejari-tenancy-contract/) for contract context. RDC disputes often take 4 to 8 weeks when deductions feel excessive.

## What belongs on the 5-week and 4-week checklist?

Five-week and four-week work typically covers car sale or bank settlement letters, furniture disposal, non-resident bank conversion with minimums of AED 3,000 to 25,000, and clearing traffic fines before visa cancellation. Invest Gulf treats unpaid RTA fines as an exit blocker that can delay the cancellation stamp by days, while furniture returns of 30% to 50% of original price often need 2 to 4 weeks of Marketplace listing time for foreign buyers leaving this market.

| Action | Timeline | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get car valuation | Week 8 | Dubizzle and dealers |
| List car on Dubizzle | Week 6 | Leave room to negotiate |
| Convert or close bank | Week 4 to 5 | Before ICA flags residency |
| Clear traffic fines | Week 4 | RTA or Dubai Police app |
| Telecom final bill | Week 4 | 30 days notice on many postpaid plans |

Furniture returns of 30 to 50% of original price are common on Marketplace or Dubizzle over 2 to 4 weeks. Non-resident bank minimums often run AED 3,000 to 25,000 depending on tier. Shipping containers need 6 to 12 weeks; storage runs AED 200 to 800 per month if you may return.

## What should you finish in the final two weeks?

Final two weeks typically mean Emirates ID outcomes, cloud backups of contracts, building access returns, and written gratuity confirmation due within 14 days of the last working day. Invest Gulf also archives Ejari, DEWA, and school transfer certificates before the grace period clock starts.

Document checklist:

- Emirates ID front and back
- Passport visa pages
- Ejari final copy and handover form
- DEWA final bill and refund confirmation
- Bank conversion or closure letter
- School transfer certificates
- Car transfer paperwork

Return access cards, parking tags, gym fobs, and keys with a written receipt from landlord or building management.

## What does it typically cost to leave Dubai?

Exit costs typically run AED 3,000 to 15,000 beyond shipping for a family, excluding school penalties or lost deposits. Invest Gulf keeps a buffer because missed school notice alone can add AED 15,000 to 30,000 on top of a rushed car sale loss of AED 3,000 to 8,000.

| Item | Typical range (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Visa cancellation / typing | 500 to 1,500 |
| DEWA final bill (summer move) | 500 to 2,000 |
| Car sale loss vs retail | 3,000 to 8,000 |
| Shipping (optional) | 8,000 to 25,000 |
| School penalty (if late notice) | 15,000 to 30,000 |
| Temporary storage | 600 to 2,400 |

Before listing owned property, weigh [rent vs buy](/guides/rent-vs-buy-dubai-expat/) in reverse. Sellers follow the [leaving Dubai property checklist](/guides/leaving-dubai-selling-property-checklist/).

## What master timeline ties the exit together?

The master timeline typically starts at week 8 with school and resignation notices, then stacks Ejari, banking, and visa cancellation so the 30-day grace period matches the flight, with DEWA refunds of 10 to 21 working days booked before the final meter read. Invest Gulf rejects plans that leave DEWA or RTA fines for the final 48 hours before departure, because ICA feeds can freeze resident accounts for 30 to 90 days once the cancellation stamp posts.

| Week | Priority actions |
| --- | --- |
| 8 | School notice, resignation, landlord notice |
| 6 | Ejari date set, car listed, DEWA plan |
| 4 | Bank conversion, fine clearance, furniture sales |
| 2 | Visa cancellation window, document scans |
| 1 | Handover, disconnect utilities, fly |

Insider tip: Convert the bank account to non-resident status before the visa cancellation stamp hits ICA feeds; waiting until after cancellation is how resident accounts freeze for 30 to 90 days with rent still arriving.

## MORE Group note for departing owners

Departing owners who keep a Dubai investment unit typically need property management, a new Ejari for any tenant, and a non-resident bank route for rent before residency ends. Invest Gulf helps owners who exit personally but retain Gulf rentals, and that conversation should start at least 4 weeks before visa cancellation.

Owner checklist:

- Appoint management before keys leave Dubai
- Open or convert a non-resident account for rent
- Align Golden Visa or title status with the exit plan
- Avoid cancelling residency while a mortgage NOC is still open
