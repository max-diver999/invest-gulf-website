# Leaving Dubai: Complete Exit Checklist for Expats 2026

**TL;DR:** Start your exit plan **8 weeks out minimum**. Run visa, DEWA, Ejari, school notice, and car sale **in parallel**, not sequentially. Budget **AED 5,000 to 15,000** for clearance gaps, deposits-in-limbo, and moving costs. If you own property, see the [property seller exit checklist](/guides/leaving-dubai-selling-property-checklist/), it runs on a separate 8 to 12-week track. **Hubs:** [relocation guide](/guides/dubai-relocation-guide/) · [Ejari deep-dive](/guides/dubai-ejari-tenancy-contract/) · [first-month mirror](/guides/first-30-days-dubai-expat/)

> **Disclaimer:** June 2026 exit guide. Immigration rules, fee schedules, and bank policies change, verify current details with relevant authorities before acting.

## Why leaving Dubai takes longer than you expect

Most expats plan their departure the same week they accept a job offer back home. That is too late. Dubai's bureaucracy runs on parallel tracks, visa, utilities, tenancy, schools, and finances each have their own office, their own timing, and their own way of blocking the others if you get the sequence wrong.

The result for rushed exits: deposit money frozen in accounts you cannot access, children enrolled in schools you owe a term's fees to, landlords holding keys because Ejari never closed, and a final DEWA bill arriving three weeks after you landed in Frankfurt.

This checklist runs the tracks in parallel, with realistic timings and cost estimates for each.

## 8 weeks out: start these immediately

**Open these tracks on the same day.** Each one has a hidden waiting period that only appears after you submit.

### Employment resignation and visa timeline

Resign and notify HR in writing immediately. Your visa cancellation window is 30 days from your last working day by default, employers sometimes start the MOHRE process later, which compresses your timeline.

| Step | Who | Typical wait |
|---|---|---|
| Submit resignation (written) | You to HR | Day 0 |
| MOHRE contract end filing | Employer | 3 to 5 days |
| Visa cancellation stamp | GDRFA / ICA | 5 to 7 working days |
| 30-day grace period starts | Automatic | After stamp |
| Emirates ID deactivated | Automatic | On cancellation |

Freelancers and freezone licence holders cancel via their respective authority (DMCC, DIFC, IFZA, etc.), timelines vary from 2 to 6 weeks.

**Golden Visa holders:** cancellation is not automatic on job change. If your visa is investment-linked, review the [UAE Golden Visa property rules](/guides/uae-golden-visa-property/) before submitting anything, cancelling incorrectly resets your residency status.

### School notice: give it immediately

Dubai schools penalise late notice with one full term's fees. On an international school at AED 60,000 to 120,000 per year, one missed term's notice costs AED 15,000 to 30,000.

| Notice type | Typical requirement | Penalty for late |
|---|---|---|
| Standard departure | One full term written notice | One term's tuition |
| Documented relocation | 4 weeks written + proof | Often waived or halved |
| Mid-term departure | Immediate with documentation | Case by case |

Submit notice in writing to the principal's office and request written acknowledgement by return. Keep the email chain. Schools are not obliged to waive fees without formal documentation of your relocation (offer letter, flight booking, new school enrolment).

Request Transfer Certificates (TCs) and all academic records in hard copy and digital format, some destination schools require original documents, not PDFs.

## 6 weeks out: rental and utilities

### Ejari cancellation and deposit recovery

Ejari cancellation requires both tenant and landlord signatures at an authorised typing centre or DLD-approved trustee. You cannot cancel unilaterally as a tenant.

Notify your landlord in writing at least 60 days before lease end if your contract requires it, check your specific clause. Standard Dubai tenancy law does not cap early termination fees, so what you owe depends entirely on your contract wording.

**Deposit recovery timeline:**

Most landlords return deposits within 30 days of handover, this is convention, not law. Disputes go to RERA's Rental Disputes Centre. Protect yourself:

1. Do a joint walkthrough with landlord present before handover
2. Photograph every room, every wall, every appliance
3. Get a written handover form signed by both parties
4. Keep a copy of every communication about deposit

Common deduction disputes: painting (landlord liable for reasonable wear after 12 months), AC cleaning (landlord liable if annual maintenance not provided), deep cleaning. If deductions feel excessive, the RDC process typically resolves in 4 to 8 weeks.

For a deeper look at your contract rights, see the [Ejari and tenancy guide](/guides/dubai-ejari-tenancy-contract/).

### DEWA disconnection and deposit refund

DEWA disconnection is tenant-only, landlords cannot disconnect on your behalf. Request final disconnection via the DEWA app, website, or walk-in service centre.

| Account type | Security deposit | Refund method | Refund timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio / 1BR apartment | AED 2,000 | Bank transfer or cheque | 10 to 21 working days |
| 2BR+ apartment | AED 2,000 | Bank transfer or cheque | 10 to 21 working days |
| Villa | AED 4,000 | Bank transfer or cheque | 10 to 21 working days |

Confirm your bank account details are updated in DEWA before disconnecting, refunds go to the account on file. A meter reading appointment is usually scheduled within 3 to 5 working days of your request.

**Tip:** If you are moving out before lease end (landlord finding new tenant), coordinate DEWA timing carefully, the new tenant cannot connect until your disconnection clears.

## 5 weeks out: vehicles and furniture

### Selling your car

UAE-spec vehicles can be difficult to re-import to many home countries due to specification differences (lighting, emissions, cooling systems). Shipping costs typically run AED 8,000 to 20,000 by sea, which erases the value advantage for mid-range cars.

**Sale preparation:**

| Action | Timeline | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Get valuation (Dubizzle, Motors.ae, dealer) | Week 8 | Know realistic market price |
| Service records and tyres | Week 7 | Buyers will ask |
| List on Dubizzle + WhatsApp groups | Week 6 | Over-price by 5 to 8% for negotiation |
| Transfer at RTA centre | At sale | Buyer pays AED 350 transfer fee |
| Cancel Salik account | At/after sale | Refund any remaining balance |
| Cancel car insurance | After transfer | Pro-rata refund |

If your car is under finance, contact the bank early, settlement letters and NOC from the lender add 2 to 3 weeks to the sale process. Buyer's bank will not finance without clear title.

**Dealer or private?** Dealers offer AED 3,000 to 8,000 less than private buyers typically but complete same-day. If your departure date is under 4 weeks, dealer trade-in avoids timing risk.

### Furniture and household goods

Dubai's expat resale market moves fast. Facebook Marketplace groups ("Dubai Expat Furniture", "Leaving Dubai, Everything Must Go") turn full apartments around in a weekend for motivated sellers.

| Option | Realistic return | Timeline | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook Marketplace / Dubizzle | 30 to 50% of original | 2 to 4 weeks | High, coordinate viewings |
| Charity donation (Habitat for Humanity UAE, Goodwill) | Zero cash, tax receipt | 1 to 3 days pickup | Low |
| Shipping container (Europe/UK) | Depends on value | 6 to 12 weeks door to door | High planning |
| Storage in UAE | AED 200 to 800/month | Ongoing | Low; if returning |

For electronics, buyer scarcity is real, UAE-spec appliances run on 220V/50Hz and handle Gulf heat well, but specific features (region locks, warranty) deter some buyers. Price 20% below online retail and they move quickly.

## 4 weeks out: finances and clearances

### Bank accounts: convert, don't close

Closing your UAE bank account immediately on departure cuts off access to any pending refunds: DEWA deposit, rental deposit, Salik balance, credit card cashback, and any final salary components. Keep it open.

Most major UAE banks (Emirates NBD, FAB, Mashreq, ADCB) offer non-resident account status, call or visit a branch and ask them to reclassify your account before visa cancellation. This typically requires:

- Passport copy
- Proof of home-country address (utility bill or bank statement)
- Minimum balance maintenance, AED 3,000 to 25,000 depending on bank and tier

If you do not convert the account, ICA flags visa cancellation to the banking system, accounts can be frozen within 30 to 90 days, which makes recovering deposits a bureaucratic headache.

**Credit cards:** Clear balances before cancellation, outstanding UAE debt stays on Al Etihad Credit Bureau (AECB) and can create complications on return visits or future UAE residency applications.

### NOC letters and clearance certificates

Several entities need formal clearance before you leave:

| Entity | Document needed | How to get | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employer | NOC / end-of-service certificate | HR / PRO | Free |
| Bank (if mortgage) | Liability letter | Bank branch or app | AED 50 to 200 |
| Traffic Police | No outstanding fines | RTA app check | Clear fines first |
| RERA (if landlord) | No dispute status | RDC online | Free |
| Courts | No pending cases | Courts.gov.ae | Free |

Traffic fines must be cleared before visa cancellation, unpaid fines create a travel block. Check the RTA app or Dubai Police app weekly in the final month.

### Etisalat / du: telecoms

Postpaid contracts typically require 30 days written notice to cancel. Early termination on 24-month contracts incurs a remaining-months penalty fee. Calculate whether prepaid for final weeks is cheaper than the ETF.

Request final bill on the last day, some providers issue it within 24 hours, others take 7 working days. Settle immediately to avoid debt collection letters following you home.

## 2 weeks out: final sweep

Run this sweep 2 weeks before your departure flight:

**Documents and digital:**
- Scan and email yourself: Emirates ID (front and back), all residency and visa pages of passport, Ejari final copy, DEWA final bill, bank account closure/conversion confirmation, school TCs, car transfer documents
- Export WhatsApp chat histories if used for business or property correspondence
- Download your UAE PASS verified documents, access may change after visa cancellation

**Financial close-out:**
- Salik account balance refund request
- Municipality fees (DEWA bills include these, confirm zero balance)
- Dubai Health Authority health insurance, cancel or check home-country continuation options
- Gratuity payment confirmed in writing from employer (legally required within 14 days of last working day)

**Physical handover:**
- Return access cards, parking tags, gym fobs, and building keys to landlord or building management
- Get a written receipt for everything returned
- Confirm building management has updated their records, some buildings continue charging maintenance fees on old tenant names

## Total cost to leave: realistic budget

Most expats underestimate exit costs. Typical ranges:

| Category | Low (apartment, no car) | High (villa, car, children) |
|---|---|---|
| Ejari / move-out cleaning | AED 500 | AED 3,000 |
| DEWA final bills + wait | AED 0 gap | AED 500 gap |
| School fees (if notice missed) | AED 0 | AED 30,000 |
| Car shipping or sale discount | AED 0 | AED 5,000 |
| Furniture removal / shipping | AED 1,000 | AED 20,000 |
| Bank account maintenance | AED 0 | AED 500/year ongoing |
| Telecoms ETF | AED 0 | AED 1,500 |
| Traffic fines clearance | AED 0 | AED 2,000+ |
| Storage (if needed) | AED 0 | AED 2,400/year |
| **Total realistic range** | **AED 2,000** | **AED 50,000+** |

The high-end scenario, missed school notice, late car sale, shipping costs, unforeseen Ejari dispute, is genuinely common. Build a buffer.

Dubai's exit machinery is designed for people who stay, not people who leave. Every refund, every cancellation, and every clearance letter assumes you have time to follow up in person. Build that time into your plan, and you leave cleanly, with your money intact and your next chapter starting on your terms.
