# Leaving Dubai: Complete Exit Checklist for Expats 2026

> Disclaimer: June 2026 exit guide. Immigration rules, fee schedules, and bank policies change; verify current details with relevant authorities before acting.

<TldrBlock text={"Start 8 weeks before departure: give school notice, resign in writing, begin Ejari exit and DEWA disconnection in parallel, and schedule visa cancellation so your 30-day grace period matches your flight. Most expensive mistakes are late school notice, tourist visas for family, and bank accounts left on resident status after cancellation."} />

## Why does leaving Dubai take longer than most expats expect?

Parallel tracks (visa, lease, school, car, bank) each need 2 to 6 weeks, and they block each other if you run them in sequence. An 8-week runway is realistic for a employed family; Golden Visa holders need a different cancellation path than employment visas.

| Track | Typical duration | Starts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School notice | 10 to 12 weeks | Week 0 |
| Resignation / MOHRE | 30 to 90 days notice | Week 0 |
| Ejari exit and deposit | 2 to 4 weeks | Week 4 to 6 |
| Visa cancellation | 5 to 10 working days | After employment ends |
| DEWA refund | 10 to 21 working days | After final meter read |

Rushed exits leave deposits frozen, school penalties unpaid, landlords holding keys because Ejari never closed, and DEWA bills arriving after you land abroad. This checklist runs the tracks in parallel with timings and costs for each phase.

## What should you do 8 weeks before departure?

Submit school notice in writing, resign with HR confirmation of last working day, and tell your landlord your move-out date so Ejari cancellation can be booked. Golden Visa holders should confirm GDRFA rules before any cancellation request.

### Employment resignation and visa timeline

Resign and notify HR in writing immediately. Your visa cancellation window is 30 days from your last working day by default; employers sometimes start the MOHRE process later, which compresses your timeline.

| Step | Who | Typical wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submit resignation (written) | You to HR | Day 0 |
| MOHRE contract end filing | Employer | 3 to 5 days |
| Visa cancellation stamp | GDRFA / ICA | 5 to 7 working days |
| 30-day grace period starts | Automatic | After stamp |
| Emirates ID deactivated | Automatic | On cancellation |

Freelancers and freezone licence holders cancel via their authority (DMCC, DIFC, IFZA, etc.); timelines vary from 2 to 6 weeks.

**Golden Visa holders:** cancellation is not automatic on job change. If your visa is investment-linked, review the [Golden Visa property rules](/guides/golden-visa-mortgage-property-uae/) before submitting anything; cancelling incorrectly resets residency status.

### School notice: give it immediately

Dubai schools penalise late notice with one full term's fees. On an international school at AED 60,000 to 120,000 per year, one missed term's notice costs AED 15,000 to 30,000.

| Notice type | Typical requirement | Penalty for late |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard departure | One full term written notice | One term's tuition |
| Documented relocation | 4 weeks written plus proof | Often waived or halved |
| Mid-term departure | Immediate with documentation | Case by case |

Submit notice to the principal's office and request written acknowledgement. Keep the email chain. Request Transfer Certificates and academic records in hard copy and digital format; destination schools sometimes require originals, not PDFs.

## What should you handle 6 weeks before move-out?

Book Ejari cancellation with your landlord, list your car if you plan to sell, and start furniture sales if you are not shipping. Coordinate lease end date with DEWA final read.

### Ejari cancellation and deposit recovery

Most landlords return deposits within 30 days of handover by convention, not fixed law. Disputes go to RERA's Rental Disputes Centre.

1. Do a joint walkthrough with landlord present before handover
2. Photograph every room, wall, and appliance
3. Get a written handover form signed by both parties
4. Keep a copy of every communication about deposit

Common deduction disputes: painting (reasonable wear after 12 months is often landlord cost), AC cleaning, deep cleaning. If deductions feel excessive, RDC processes often resolve in 4 to 8 weeks. See the [Ejari tenancy guide](/guides/dubai-ejari-tenancy-contract/) for contract context.

### DEWA disconnection and deposit refund

Request final disconnection via the DEWA app, website, or service centre. Only the account holder can disconnect.

| Account type | Security deposit | Refund timeline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio / 1BR apartment | AED 2,000 | 10 to 21 working days |
| 2BR+ apartment | AED 2,000 | 10 to 21 working days |
| Villa | AED 4,000 | 10 to 21 working days |

Confirm bank details on file before disconnecting. Meter reading is usually scheduled within 3 to 5 working days of your request. If you leave before lease end, coordinate timing so the incoming tenant can connect after your disconnection clears.

## What belongs on the 5-week and 4-week checklist?

Sell or ship household goods, convert or close banking, and clear traffic fines before visa cancellation. Unpaid fines can block exit.

### Car sale preparation

| Action | Timeline | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get valuation (Dubizzle, dealers) | Week 8 | Know realistic price |
| Service records and tyres | Week 7 | Buyers will ask |
| List on Dubizzle | Week 6 | Leave room to negotiate |
| Transfer at RTA centre | At sale | Buyer pays transfer fee |
| Cancel Salik | At or after sale | Refund remaining balance |

Financed cars need bank settlement letters early; buyer finance waits on clear title. Dealers pay less but close same day if departure is under 4 weeks.

### Furniture and household goods

| Option | Realistic return | Timeline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook Marketplace / Dubizzle | 30 to 50% of original | 2 to 4 weeks |
| Charity donation | Receipt only | 1 to 3 days pickup |
| Shipping container | Varies by destination | 6 to 12 weeks |
| UAE storage | AED 200 to 800/month | If you may return |

### Banking and NOC letters

Convert to a non-resident account or close before cancellation. ICA flags visa cancellation to banks; resident accounts can freeze within 30 to 90 days.

Typical non-resident requirements: passport copy, home-country address proof, minimum balance AED 3,000 to 25,000 depending on bank tier.

Clear credit cards; UAE debt stays on Al Etihad Credit Bureau and can complicate future UAE residency.

| Entity | Document | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employer | End-of-service certificate | From HR |
| Bank (if mortgage) | Liability letter | Branch or app |
| Traffic | No outstanding fines | RTA or Dubai Police app |
| Telecom | Final bill | Etisalat or du |

Traffic fines must be cleared before visa cancellation. Postpaid mobile contracts often need 30 days written notice; early termination on 24-month plans may carry a penalty.

## What should you finish in the final two weeks?

Collect Emirates ID outcomes, scan critical documents to cloud storage, return building access cards, and confirm gratuity payment in writing from your employer (legally due within 14 days of last working day).

**Documents to archive:**

- Emirates ID (front and back)
- Passport visa pages
- Ejari final copy and handover form
- DEWA final bill and refund confirmation
- Bank conversion or closure letter
- School transfer certificates
- Car transfer paperwork

**Physical handover:**

- Return access cards, parking tags, gym fobs, and keys
- Get written receipt from landlord or building management
- Confirm building management updated tenant records

Export WhatsApp threads used for landlord or employer correspondence if disputes are possible.

## What does it typically cost to leave Dubai?

Budget AED 3,000 to 15,000 beyond shipping for a family, excluding school penalties or lost deposits. The high-end scenario (missed school notice, Ejari dispute, rushed car fire sale) is common enough to keep a buffer.

| Item | Typical range (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Visa cancellation / typing | 500 to 1,500 |
| DEWA final bill (summer move) | 500 to 2,000 |
| Car sale loss vs retail | 3,000 to 8,000 |
| Shipping (optional) | 8,000 to 25,000 |
| School penalty (if late notice) | 15,000 to 30,000 |
| Temporary storage | 600 to 2,400 |

Before listing owned property, weigh [rent vs buy](/guides/rent-vs-buy-dubai-expat/) in reverse: letting through an agent sometimes beats a rushed sale. Sellers follow the [leaving Dubai property checklist](/guides/leaving-dubai-selling-property-checklist/).
