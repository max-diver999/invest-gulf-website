Every UAE brochure quotes gross yield. Your bank account receives net yield. The gap is usually 1.5 to 3 percentage points on long-term lets, wider on short-term rental. An investor who underwrites on gross alone misallocates capital.

| Input | Use this source |
| --- | --- |
| Annual rent | Ejari / RERA Rental Index (not listings) |
| Service charges | Building Mollak schedule |
| Management fee | 5% to 8% LTR; 15% to 20% STR |
| Vacancy | 4% to 5% prime; 7% to 8% citywide |
| Capital deployed | Purchase plus acquisition costs |
| Rent growth | RERA calculator bands at renewal |

This guide gives the net yield formula, a UAE cost stack, worked Dubai and Abu Dhabi examples, spreadsheet logic, and common modelling errors.

For gross-to-net theory see [Gross vs net yield Dubai](/guides/gross-vs-net-yield-dubai/). For renewal caps see [Dubai rent increase calculator (RERA)](/guides/dubai-rent-increase-calculator-rera/).

## What is the net yield formula for UAE property?

Gross yield:

```
Gross Yield (%) = (Annual Rent / Purchase Price) x 100
```

Net yield:

```
Net Yield (%) = (Annual Rent Collected - Operating Costs) / Total Capital Deployed x 100
```

Where collected rent is potential rent minus vacancy, operating costs are service charges, management, maintenance, licensing, and utilities between tenancies, and total capital deployed is purchase price plus DLD or AD transfer fees, trustee, agent, legal, and finance arrangement costs if any.

## Who should model net yield before buying?

**Short hold (12 to 24 months):** prioritise exit friction, service charge arrears risk, and achievable Ejari rent, not developer gross.

**Family relocation (3 to 5 years):** total monthly spend including schools and insurance may matter more than yield, but net still informs buy vs rent.

**Pure investors:** net yield vs home-country tax and mortgage cost of capital drives the decision. Keep 6 to 12 months liquidity for voids and fee spikes.

## What belongs in the UAE operating cost stack?

### Annual operating costs (subtract from rent)

| Cost bucket | Typical range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service charges | AED 12 to 25/sq ft/year (mid-market) | Building-specific; check Mollak |
| Property management (LTR) | 5% to 8% of collected rent | Full-service agent |
| Property management (STR) | 15% to 20% of revenue | Holiday home operator |
| Vacancy allowance | 4% to 8% of potential rent | See assumptions below |
| Ejari registration | AED 220/tenancy | Annual renewal |
| DEWA / ADDC reconnection | AED 2,000 to 4,000 between tenancies | Landlord cost |
| Maintenance provision | 0.5% to 1% of property value/year | AC, appliances, wear |
| DET Holiday Home permit (STR) | AED 1,520/year (apartment) | Mandatory for Airbnb class lets |
| Tourism Dirham (STR) | AED 10 to 15/room/night | Remitted to DET |
| Building insurance | AED 1,000 to 3,000/year | Often in service charge |

Abu Dhabi's lower transfer fee (2% vs Dubai's 4% on many purchases) improves net yield on identical gross rents, one reason affordable Abu Dhabi stock can look stronger on paper despite lower resale liquidity.

## How do you calculate net yield step by step?

```
Total Capital = Purchase Price + DLD Fee + Trustee Fee + Agent Commission + Legal Fees
```

Example (Dubai): AED 800,000 purchase + AED 32,000 DLD (4%) + AED 4,200 trustee + AED 16,800 agent + AED 8,000 legal = **AED 861,000** deployed.

**Step 2:** Ejari annual rent **AED 58,000** (not a listing price).

**Step 3:** Collected rent with 7% vacancy: 58,000 x 0.93 = **AED 53,940**.

**Step 4:** Operating costs (illustrative JVC 1BR, 750 sq ft):

| Cost | Calculation | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service charges | 750 x AED 14 | AED 10,500 |
| Management (6%) | 53,940 x 0.06 | AED 3,236 |
| Ejari + DEWA + maintenance | Lump sum | AED 5,000 |
| **Total operating** | | **AED 18,736** |

**Step 5:** Net income 53,940 - 18,736 = **AED 35,204**. Net yield = 35,204 / 861,000 x 100 = **4.09%**.

Gross on purchase price alone: 58,000 / 800,000 = **7.25%**. That 3+ point gap is typical for JVC and should drive underwriting, not the brochure gross.

## What does a Dubai Marina example look like?

Marina 1BR might show **6.5% gross** on AED 1.1M with AED 72,000 Ejari rent but **AED 22 to 28/sq ft** service charges on 800 sq ft (AED 17,600 to 22,400) plus 6% management and 5% vacancy. Net on fully loaded capital often lands near **4% to 5%**. Marina's case is often liquidity and appreciation, not yield leadership.

## What about Abu Dhabi Al Reef?

Lower transfer fee and moderate service charges can push **net toward 5.5% to 6%** on well-priced 2BR stock when Ejari rents hold. Compare [Abu Dhabi property investment guide](/guides/abu-dhabi-property-investment-guide/) for community context. Always pull building-specific service charge budgets before you trust gross marketing.

## Is short-term rental net yield higher?

STR gross can look 30% to 50% above LTR, but net often closes the gap.

| Input (Marina studio example) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Total capital (incl. fit-out) | AED 950,000 |
| Annual STR gross revenue | AED 120,000 |
| DET permit + Tourism Dirham | AED 8,000 |
| Management (18%) | AED 21,600 |
| Cleaning + consumables | AED 12,000 |
| Service charges | AED 14,000 |
| Vacancy (15% STR) | AED 18,000 lost |
| **Net income** | **AED 46,400** |
| **Net yield** | **4.88%** |
| Equivalent LTR net | ~4.0% |

STR premium here is under 1 point net, not the gross spread agents quote. Confirm building STR rules and DET licence before underwriting.

## How should you build a spreadsheet model?

| Cell | Field | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B2 | Purchase price (AED) | 800,000 |
| B3 | DLD fee rate | 4% |
| B4 | Other acquisition costs | 29,000 |
| B5 | Property size (sq ft) | 750 |
| B6 | Annual rent (Ejari) | 58,000 |
| B7 | Vacancy rate | 7% |
| B8 | Service charge (AED/sq ft) | 14 |
| B9 | Management fee % | 6% |
| B10 | Other annual costs | 5,000 |

Formulas: total capital `=B2*(1+B3)+B4`; collected rent `=B6*(1-B7)`; service `=B5*B8`; management `=B13*B9`; net yield `=(collected-operating)/capital*100`.

Run sensitivity on rent (+/- 15%) and service charge (10 to 28 AED/sq ft). A range beats a single-point broker number.

## How do multi-year models differ from year one?

A sitting tenant far below market renews under RERA caps; do not assume instant jump to listing rent.

## What mistakes inflate net yield?

| Mistake | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Listing rent instead of Ejari | Overstates income 5% to 15% |
| Zero vacancy | Unrealistic void model |
| Ignoring acquisition costs in denominator | Overstates yield ~0.5 to 0.7 pts |
| Developer gross only | Misses 1.5 to 3 pt gap |
| STR without licence and PM fees | Collapses on audit |

## Do foreign buyers need a different net yield line?

UAE net yield is pre home-country tax. UK, US, Indian, and German residents may owe reporting or tax on foreign rental income. Nationality guides cover angles; process sits in [How foreigners buy property in the UAE](/guides/can-foreigners-buy-property-uae/).

## What checklist should run before you sign?

1. Capital deployed = price + all acquisition costs.
2. Rent = Ejari or RERA index, not Property Finder ask.
3. Service charges = Mollak or REST building schedule.
4. Vacancy = 7% to 8% default; 4% to 5% only with evidence.
5. Management = 6% LTR or 18% STR.
6. Rent growth = RERA bands, not unlimited escalation.
7. Compare **net to net** across options.
