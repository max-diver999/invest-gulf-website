## Is Dubai cheaper than London after tax?

After tax, Dubai is often cheaper for employed expats because UAE personal income tax is 0% on employment. Before tax, headline lifestyle costs are similar at equivalent tiers: premium Dubai Marina living can match Zone 1 London spend. UK tax trap: non-domiciled rules changed; many UK nationals in Dubai remain UK tax exposed on worldwide income if the statutory residence test fails. "Tax-free" marketing ignores HMRC obligations.

| Factor | London (indicative) | Dubai (indicative) |
|---|---|---|
| Income tax on GBP 120K gross | 35 to 45% effective | 0% UAE employment tax |
| Council tax / housing fees | GBP 100 to 250/month | Service charge + chiller on towers |
| VAT on purchases | 20% | 5% |

## How does rent compare between Dubai and London?

FX at GBP 1 = AED 4.6 (approximate 2026): London Zone 2 GBP 2,800 ≈ AED 12,880, comparable to Marina 2BR pricing, not JVC. British expats who expect Kensington living on a Hammersmith budget arrive shocked. Map zones honestly.

| London zone (2BR indicative) | GBP/month | Dubai equivalent (space) | AED/month (indicative) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zone 1 premium | 3,500 to 5,000+ | Marina / Downtown | 14,000 to 22,000 |
| Zone 2 family | 2,400 to 3,200 | JVC, Mirdif, Sports City | 10,000 to 14,500 |
| Zone 3 value | 1,800 to 2,500 | Older Sharjah commuter (not Dubai core) | 7,000 to 10,000 |

| London gross | Approx net after HMRC | Dubai package (indicative net) | Notes |

Dubai wins on take-home pay; London wins when state schools, NHS, and tenant protections anchor your model.

## How do schools compare?

Premium British schools in Dubai (AED 85,000 to 110,000 per year per child) match UK independent tiers when FX-adjusted. State school advantage in London disappears if you would have paid private anyway.

## Healthcare: NHS vs DHA insurance

Employer enhanced Dubai plans approximate private London BUPA-tier speed, not NHS emotionally. Self-pay comprehensive Dubai insurance often lands similar to London private annual premiums (AED 5,000 to 15,000 per person depending on age and cover).

## Transport: Tube vs two cars

London Zone 1 to 2 travelcard runs GBP 180+/month. Dubai Metro + occasional Careem often AED 800 to 1,200 for a single commuter, but family life frequently needs two cars at AED 2,500+/month all-in (lease, Salik, parking). Dubai wins on pure transit cash; car culture pushes many households higher.

## What hidden costs differ?

Dubai: service charge, chiller, school bus, summer camp, alcohol licensed venue premiums. London: council tax, commuter rail, nursery waiting lists, seasonal heating. → [Hidden costs living Dubai](/guides/hidden-costs-living-dubai/)

## Who saves moving London to Dubai?

- Dual income with employer school allowance
- Willing to live JVC/Mirdif not Marina
- UK tax non-resident with clean advice
- Remote worker with Dubai salary, modest housing

## Who should stay in London?

- State school + NHS as core value
- Career tied to UK-regulated roles
- UK tax resident despite Dubai presence
- Needs strong tenant rights and predictable lease law

## What salary parity should you model?

Worked parity GBP 80K London vs AED package: Dubai wins on tax and service access; London wins on school predictability and tenant rights. GBP earners should model FX on AED savings; dirham peg helps stability but repatriation timing matters.

## Childcare and nursery gap

Dubai summer 3-month heat pushes indoor camp spend; budget AED 6,000 to 12,000 per child per summer unless home leave covers it. London nursery fees in inner zones often exceed AED-equivalent costs but availability differs by borough.

## Relocation scenarios

**Scenario A (family, British schools Dubai):** Model total monthly spend, not headline rent. Stress 4 to 6 weeks vacancy if you buy to let later.

**Scenario B (single, Zone 2 equivalent rent):** JVC 1BR + one car; target AED 12,000 to 16,000/month all-in mid tier.

**Scenario C (investor):** Separate lifestyle from ROI; see [Dubai property for British buyers](/guides/dubai-property-for-british-buyers/).

## What checklist runs before you relocate?

- UK tax residence opinion in writing
- Three live rent quotes in target Dubai zone
- School acceptance or wait-list status
- Employer insurance tier documented
- 6 to 12 months liquidity in GBP and AED
- Compare [Dubai cost of living](/guides/dubai-cost-of-living-guide/) line by line with your London spreadsheet

Dubai looks cheaper on tax but dental, optical, and physio often come out of pocket unless you carry an executive plan. Budget AED 3,000 to 8,000/year family top-up when employer cover is basic.

## What do utilities and council tax equivalents cost?

London council tax bands add GBP 100 to 250/month on top of rent. Dubai replaces that stack with service charge, chiller or district cooling, and DEWA. Older split-AC towers can spike in August; ask for last summer bills before lease signing.

| Cost line | London (indicative) | Dubai (indicative) |
|---|---|---|
| Property tax / service | Council tax GBP 100 to 250 | Service charge AED 8 to 18/sqft/year on towers |
| Electricity / gas | GBP 120 to 220/month | DEWA AED 400 to 1,400/month by season |
| Water | Often included in council bands | Included in DEWA bundle |

## How much do dining, entertainment, and alcohol add?

Licensed dining in Dubai costs similar or higher than central London for comparable venues. Alcohol purchases require licensed stores or hotel venues with markup. Budget GBP 200 to 400/month equivalent if social spend matches a London pub-and-restaurant pattern.

## What is the property ownership angle for British relocators?

Buying in Dubai adds transfer costs and service charges but removes rent inflation on owned stock. British buyers often compare mortgage serviceability on [Dubai property for British buyers](/guides/dubai-property-for-british-buyers/) against continuing to rent in Zone 2. Ownership does not automatically fix school or commute costs; run rent-vs-buy on the same school and office pins.

## What is the state school vs Dubai private trade-off?

London state places can zero out tuition for families in catchment. Dubai private British curriculum is the default for many relocating families, which can add AED 80,000 to 110,000 per child per year. A Dubai salary uplift that looks generous on tax alone can disappear when two children move from UK state to KHDA private.

## How does Brexit-era diaspora affect 2026 moves?

UK nationals still face statutory residence tests and worldwide income reporting regardless of UAE employment tax. Remote work for UK employers from Dubai does not automatically break UK ties. Document days in country and contract location before you resign a London lease.

## What monthly single-professional budget is realistic?

Studio or 1BR in JVC or Business Bay fringe at AED 5,500 to 8,000, DEWA AED 400 to 700, groceries AED 1,200 to 1,800, one car AED 1,400 to 2,000, dining AED 800 to 1,500 lands near AED 10,000 to 14,000/month all-in mid tier. That can beat London Zone 2 single rent-plus-tax on GBP 45K to 55K gross when UAE salary is quoted net.

## How do childcare and after-school costs compare?

London wraparound care and holiday clubs vary by borough; Dubai after-school activities and camps are priced in AED with summer indoor programmes often AED 6,000 to 12,000 per child unless you travel. Dual-income London households sometimes rely on grandparents; Dubai assignments rarely have that network in year one, so budget paid care explicitly.

## What insurance and pension gaps should UK movers model?

Employer pension matching on UK payroll often disappears on Dubai contract restructures. Build voluntary savings to replace employer NI and pension uplift you lose. Life and critical illness cover may need repatriating policies if UAE group cover is basic.

## What one-page spreadsheet should you build before moving?

Column A: London net pay after HMRC. Column B: Dubai net package. Row each of rent, schools, cars, utilities, insurance, holidays, and savings rate. Only compare row totals after tax, not headline gross job offers. Update FX monthly; peg stability helps planning but repatriation timing still matters for GBP savers. Re-run the sheet after any school offer or rent quote change before you resign.
