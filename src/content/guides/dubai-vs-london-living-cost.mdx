# Dubai vs London Living Cost 2026: Rent, Tax, Schools & Real Comparison

**TL;DR:** Dubai is **not automatically cheaper** than London, it is **tax-different**. A GBP 120,000 London salary loses **35–45%** to HMRC; the same role in Dubai at AED 45,000/month net has **0% UAE income tax** but **AED 35,000–55,000/month** family spend on rent, schools and cars. Zone 2 London rent maps to **JVC or Mirdif**, not Marina vs Kensington. British families save most when **school fees are employer-paid** and they resist Marina/Palm lifestyle inflation.

**Dubai hub:** [Dubai cost of living](/guides/dubai-cost-of-living-guide/) · **British buyers:** [Dubai property for British buyers](/guides/dubai-property-for-british-buyers/)

> **Disclaimer:** Tax depends on UK residency status, remittance basis, and individual circumstances, consult qualified tax adviser. Figures June 2026 planning ranges.

## The comparison framework: gross vs net

London comparisons fail when people match **gross salaries** without tax and **net lifestyle tiers**.

| Factor | London | Dubai |
|---|---|---|
| Income tax (typical professional) | 20–45% + NI | 0% UAE employment tax |
| Council tax | GBP 150–300/month | 0 (included in community/service context) |
| TV licence | GBP 169/year | N/A |
| Healthcare | NHS (tax-funded) | Employer insurance or private |
| Schools (international) | GBP 15,000–25,000/year private | AED 55,000–110,000/year |
| Public transport | Strong Tube/bus | Car-centric |

**UK tax trap:** non-domiciled rules changed, many UK nationals in Dubai remain UK tax exposed on worldwide income if statutory resident test fails. "Tax-free" marketing ignores **HMRC obligations**.

## Where Dubai wins: tax-adjusted

Example: **GBP 100,000 gross London** → ~**GBP 60,000 net** take-home (simplified).

Same role **AED 45,000/month gross** Dubai → **AED 45,000 net** (no UAE employment tax) = **~GBP 117,000 equivalent gross** spending power if no UK tax leakage.

**Winner:** Dubai on **pure net cash** for same nominal package.

**Loser:** Dubai if employer offers **20% Dubai premium** over London gross without full school and housing allowance, premium erodes fast.

## Transport: Tube vs two cars

London **Zone 1–3 annual travelcard** ~**GBP 2,400**, excellent value.

Dubai family with school run realistically **AED 3,000–4,500/month** all-in two cars, **loss vs London transport cost**, win on time and heat comfort.

Singles near Metro Red Line: Dubai transport cost drops toward London parity.

## Childcare and nursery: London vs Dubai gap

| Service | London (monthly) | Dubai (monthly) |
|---|---|---|
| Nursery full-day | GBP 1,200–2,000 | AED 3,500–6,500 |
| After-school club | GBP 200–400 | AED 800–1,500 |
| Nanny live-out | GBP 2,000+ | AED 3,500–5,500 |

Dubai **does not replace** state school with free nursery, expat families pay from age 1–4 before FS1. Budget **AED 4,000–7,000/month** per child in nursery years on top of rent.

UK assignees often underestimate this gap because London childcare may have been partially offset by grandparents or tax-free childcare vouchers, neither transfers to Dubai.

Property investors: buildings near **Outstanding nursery clusters** (Dubai Hills, Marina, JLT) see faster lease-up, not just school catchment.

**Council tax equivalent:** London council tax bands disappear in Dubai, but **service charge + chiller + DEWA** often replace that line item entirely on towers.
