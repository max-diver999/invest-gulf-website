## Why is "best country for families" the wrong question?


Only the third question decides year one.

Families who pick a country from a lifestyle blog, then discover QAR 10,000 basic is not met, or Riyadh British Year 12 has no seat, pay the relocation tax twice: sunk shipping plus broken school year.

This is meta-layer content: it sits above country hubs and below individual school tours. Use it after [Gulf expat living comparison](/guides/gulf-expat-living-comparison/) (R105) when you know the Gulf is the region, but not which passport stamp your kids will grow up beside.

Read this if:
- Comparing UAE vs Qatar vs Saudi on one spreadsheet
- Two kids, British curriculum, spouse not working, need visa + tuition truth
- Employer offers Abu Dhabi HQ but you've only holidayed in Dubai
- Considering Sharjah rent + Dubai schools or Bahrain + causeway weekends

Skip this if: you've already locked one city and one school offer, go to area guides and COL hubs instead.

## How do Gulf school costs compare in 2026?

Headline tuition is half the story; buses, uniforms, and exams add 15-35%. Model two-child amortised monthly cost before you pick a country on rent alone.

Deep dives: [Doha international schools](/guides/doha-international-schools-guide/) (R38) · [Riyadh international schools](/guides/riyadh-international-schools/) (R75) · [Gulf schools comparison](/guides/gulf-schools-comparison/) (R108)

Hidden cost rule: Add 15-35% to headline tuition for registration, bus, uniforms, exams, trips.

Two-child amortisation examples (monthly, tuition only):
- Dubai British mid ×2: ~AED 9,000-11,000/mo
- Doha British mid ×2: ~QAR 9,000-11,000/mo
- Riyadh British mid ×2: ~SAR 10,000-14,000/mo

If employer allowance caps at one child or AED 40K/year, country choice may not matter; gap is the same everywhere.

## How do family visa rules differ by country?

Visa eligibility on basic salary and housing type often beats lifestyle marketing. Verify official rules before school deposits.

Federal immigration, GDRFA/ICP. Employment visa family sponsorship common; Golden Visa AED 2M property enables self-sponsorship. Thresholds well-documented relative to Gulf peers.

See [Abu Dhabi Golden Visa living](/guides/abu-dhabi-golden-visa-living/)

### Qatar

Critical: Family residence requires QAR 10,000 basic salary OR QAR 6,000 basic + employer-provided accommodation (2023 MOI circular). "Total package" on offer letter is irrelevant if basic line fails.

Housing suitability: two-bedroom minimum typical for child sponsorship, Pearl studio fails.

See [Qatar family visa sponsorship](/guides/qatar-family-visa-sponsorship/) · [Qatar relocation guide](/guides/qatar-relocation-guide/)

### Saudi Arabia

Iqama via employer dominates. Salary and profession categories affect dependant rules, verify HR against current Ministry guidelines. Premium Residency (~SAR 4M investment track, verify) is separate product for investors.

See [Saudi family visa](/guides/saudi-family-visa/) · [Saudi iqama process](/guides/saudi-iqama-process/)

### Oman

Employer sponsorship standard. Investor residence ~OMR 250,000 (verify ROP) for property-linked paths, not casual family move.

See [Oman family visa](/guides/oman-family-visa/)

### Bahrain

LMRA employment track straightforward for finance hires. Golden Residence ~BHD 200,000 (verify NPRA; Nov 2025 press cited BHD 130K reduction, verify) for investor families.

See [Bahrain family visa](/guides/bahrain-family-visa/) · [Bahrain Golden Residence](/guides/bahrain-golden-residence/)

## How does family safety compare across the Gulf?

Violent street crime against expats is low across GCC capitals; planning focuses on road traffic, heat, and cultural fit rather than mugging risk.

## What healthcare should families verify for children?

Confirm employer insurance covers paediatrics, maternity, and network hospitals near your school commute before you relocate.

See [Qatar healthcare guide](/guides/qatar-healthcare-guide/) · [Oman healthcare guide](/guides/oman-healthcare-guide/) · [Bahrain healthcare guide](/guides/bahrain-healthcare-guide/)

## What is a step-by-step relocation sequence?

Sequence: school seat offer, visa eligibility check, housing suitability, shipping window, then resignation. Reversing the order triggers the relocation tax twice.

## What is the country verdict summary?

UAE wins breadth, Qatar wins fixed Doha packages when salary rules clear, Saudi wins compound packages, Oman and Bahrain win calm and value with smaller pools.
