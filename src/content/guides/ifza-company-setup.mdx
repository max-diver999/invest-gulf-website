## What is IFZA and who is it for?
IFZA is a Fujairah Creative City free zone for Dubai founders who want lean setup from about AED 11,900 to 14,900 in year-one flexi packages. Registration typically completes in 5 to 10 business days with clean KYC and 100% foreign ownership. Our underwriting treats IFZA as a budget e-commerce path.

- Low entry cost: promotional flexi packages from roughly AED 11,900-14,900 all-in for year one
- E-commerce and trading friendly: general trading and commercial brokerage activities widely available
- Fast turnaround: 5-10 business days for standard applications with clean KYC
- 100% foreign ownership: no local sponsor required inside the free zone
- Remote-first onboarding: digital application path suited to overseas founders
- Visa options on upgraded tiers: from zero-visa holding packages to multi-visa office bundles

IFZA is not the right zone for commodities vaulting, DFSA-regulated finance, or Jebel Ali warehouse logistics. For those intents, compare [DMCC company setup](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/) and [JAFZA company setup](/guides/jafza-company-setup/).

## How does IFZA compare to DMCC and JAFZA?
IFZA typically costs AED 12,000 to 22,000 in year one versus AED 35,000 to 55,000 for a comparable DMCC flexi-desk, while JAFZA suits port logistics. Banking friction is higher on IFZA trading files than on DMCC brand recognition. Invest Gulf chooses zones by 12 month deal flow.

Choose DMCC when: banks and counterparties expect a recognised JLT free zone, you trade commodities, or you want three visas on a standard flexi-desk without warehouse space.

Choose JAFZA when: you need Jebel Ali Port access, bonded warehouse, or large visa counts tied to industrial space. See the dedicated [JAFZA guide](/guides/jafza-company-setup/).

For mainland market access rules that apply to all three zones, read [UAE free zone vs mainland](/guides/uae-free-zone-vs-mainland/).

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Year-one lean cost | AED 12,000 to 22,000 | AED 35,000 to 55,000 | AED 20,000 to 35,000 |
| Best for | E-commerce, remote services | JLT prestige, commodities | Port logistics, warehouse |
| Typical registration | 5 to 10 business days | 5 to 10 business days | 7 to 15 business days |
| Banking friction | Higher for trading | Lower brand risk | Trade-finance friendly |

## Which IFZA company structure should you use?
IFZA structure typically means a Free Zone Company for two or more shareholders, or a foreign branch when the parent remains liable abroad. Standard activities allow 100% foreign ownership with nominal share capital on many trading codes. We confirm activity codes before any AED 12,000 package fee clears.

Branch of a Foreign Company: extension of an existing overseas entity. Parent remains liable. Useful when you already have brand and contracts abroad.

All structures allow 100% foreign ownership. Minimum share capital is typically nominal for standard trading and service activities, confirm the current IFZA schedule for your activity code before filing.

## How long does IFZA registration take?
IFZA registration typically means 5 to 10 business days from complete documents to licence issuance, with name approval often same-day. Bank accounts add 2 to 8 weeks and first visas add 2 to 4 weeks after the establishment card. We model a 4 to 8 week calendar.

## What are the IFZA setup steps?
IFZA setup steps typically start with package selection, then name reservation, KYC, payment, licence issuance, banking, and visas. Visa quotas follow workspace tier, not assumptions on promotional flyers. We lock visa count in the package contract before any AED 11,900 payment.

- Zero-visa flexi: licence + registered address only
- One-visa bundle: common entry promotion for founders who need UAE residence
- Multi-visa upgrade: higher workspace tier for teams

Confirm visa count, renewal price, and whether the package includes establishment card fees before you pay.

### Step 2: Reserve Company Name

Names must comply with UAE conventions: no prohibited words (Bank, Insurance, Royal without approval), no offensive or religious misuse, and no confusing similarity to existing entities. IFZA typically approves names within one business day if the list is clean.

### Step 3: Submit Shareholder and Director KYC

Standard requirements for individual shareholders:

- Passport copy (valid at least six months)
- Proof of residential address (utility bill or bank statement under three months)
- CV or short professional profile (some activities)
- Source of funds summary for trading and e-commerce

Corporate shareholders add certificate of incorporation, good standing, board resolution, and UBO chain documentation for anyone above 25% ownership.

### Step 4: Pay Fees and Pass Compliance Review

IFZA conducts AML and beneficial ownership checks aligned with UAE federal requirements. Once approved in principle, pay the package fee. Rejections usually trace to incomplete UBO disclosure or activity mismatch with business description.

### Step 5: Receive Corporate Documents

After payment clearance, IFZA issues:

- Trade Licence
- Certificate of Registration / Incorporation
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Share certificates (if applicable)

These documents are mandatory for bank account opening and visa processing.

### Step 6: Open Corporate Bank Account

Proceed immediately after licence issuance. IFZA entities face more questions on e-commerce and trading than DMCC entities. Prepare:

- Business plan (one to two pages)
- Expected countries you pay and receive from
- Sample invoices or website URL
- Personal bank statements for shareholders

Allow **2-8 weeks** for account opening. Full guidance is in [opening a bank account in Dubai](/guides/open-bank-account-dubai/).

### Step 7: Apply for Visas (If Included in Package)

Activate establishment card, then process employment or investor visas. Medical test and Emirates ID biometrics require UAE presence. Budget **2-4 weeks** per visa after establishment card activation.

## When should you upgrade from IFZA to DMCC?
Upgrading from IFZA to DMCC typically makes sense when banks or anchor clients expect a JLT licence and lost deals exceed the AED 20,000 to 35,000 cost gap. Lean Stripe contracts often stay viable on IFZA. We run a 12 month deal-loss tally before paying the DMCC premium.

## How do e-commerce founders stay compliant on IFZA?
E-commerce compliance on IFZA typically means matching activity codes to general trading or e-commerce, keeping supplier invoices for bank KYC, and separating personal flows from day one. Website footers should show the licence number before volume scales. Invest Gulf blocks payment gateways until the corporate account is active 30 days.

- Match activity code to general trading or **e-commerce** explicitly
- Keep supplier invoices and shipping records for bank KYC
- Separate personal and corporate flows from day one
- Use a UAE payment gateway only after corporate account is active
- Display registered licence number on your website footer

## Do IFZA companies pay UAE corporate tax?
IFZA companies typically face UAE corporate tax at 9% on profits above AED 375,000 from June 2023, with Qualifying Free Zone Person 0% only on Qualifying Income under strict tests. Mainland UAE client revenue often fails QFZP. We consistently model tax before the first invoice.

## How do IFZA founders open a UAE bank account?
IFZA banking typically takes 2 to 8 weeks and faces tighter KYC on e-commerce and trading than DMCC files. Founders need a one-page business model, corridors, and clean source-of-funds before branch meetings. We apply only to banks already holding IFZA accounts at volume.

- Apply with a bank that already holds IFZA corporate accounts at volume
- Avoid listing high-risk jurisdictions in your payment flow without explanation
- Bring a one-page business model diagram to the bank meeting
- If e-commerce, show marketplace seller dashboards or supplier agreements
- Do not commingle personal crypto flows with corporate account applications

If banking blocks progress, some founders temporarily use payment service providers while a second bank review runs. Treat that as a stopgap, not a long-term substitute for a UAE corporate account.

## What are common IFZA setup mistakes?
Common IFZA mistakes typically include buying the cheapest package without confirming visa count, misclassifying e-commerce codes, and mixing personal crypto with corporate applications. Assuming automatic 0% corporate tax without QFZP assessment is another miss. Our research treats each as a hard stop before AED 12,000-plus fees.

- Choosing the cheapest package without confirming visa count
- Misclassifying activity codes for e-commerce or trading
- Mixing personal and corporate bank flows before account approval
- Assuming 0% corporate tax without a QFZP assessment

## Is IFZA the right free zone for your business?
IFZA is typically wrong when you need DFSA-regulated finance, Jebel Ali warehouse logistics, or an investor who mandates DMCC or DIFC on the licence. Three-visa needs on the cheapest package often fit DMCC flexi better. We start from the Dubai business setup hub before any 5 to 10 day IFZA filing.

- You need DFSA-regulated financial services (use DIFC)
- You require Jebel Ali warehouse and port logistics (use JAFZA)
- Your bank or anchor investor mandates DMCC or DIFC specifically
- You need three visas on the cheapest package (DMCC flexi-desk may be better value)
- Most revenue will come from mainland UAE retail consumers
