Al Marjan Island is a man-made archipelago extending into the Arabian Gulf off the coast of Ras Al Khaimah (RAK). Built in the 2000s, it consists of four coral-shaped fronds covering approximately 2.7 million square metres. It sits about 10 km from RAK's main urban area and 70–80 km from central Dubai.

The island is a designated freehold zone for non-GCC nationals, allowing full property ownership. It hosts residential towers, beach hotels (including DoubleTree by Hilton and Hampton Inn), restaurants, beach clubs, and the under-construction Wynn Al Marjan Island resort.

The transformation from a quiet investment zone into a high-profile destination accelerated after the Wynn casino announcement in 2022. Since then, property prices and developer activity have increased substantially.

## FAQ

**Is Al Marjan a good place to live for expats?**
Yes for lifestyle-first residents comfortable with island pace and RAK-Dubai commute. Not ideal for daily Dubai office workers.

**How far is Al Marjan from Dubai?**
70–80 km, 75–90 minutes off-peak, up to 120 minutes in morning rush heading toward Dubai.

**What is the rental market like?**
Substantially cheaper than Dubai Marina equivalents. Studio from AED 30,000–40,000/year; 1BR AED 45,000–70,000/year. Wynn effect is pushing prices up.

**Are there international schools on the island?**
No. Schools are in RAK city, 15–25 minutes away. School bus availability matters.

**What is the Wynn casino project?**
Major integrated resort with UAE's first legal casino, scheduled to open 2027. Already driving significant property price uplift.

**Can foreigners buy property on Al Marjan?**
Yes, designated RAK freehold zone. Freehold ownership for non-GCC nationals permitted.

**What healthcare is available nearby?**
Clinics on the island; Burjeel RAK and Sheikh Khalifa Hospital in RAK city (20 min). Dubai hospitals 75–90 min.

**Is banking convenient from Al Marjan?**
RAKBANK, Emirates NBD, and ADCB branches in RAK city. Online and mobile banking handles most daily needs.
