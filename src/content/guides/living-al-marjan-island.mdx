## What is Al Marjan Island?

Al Marjan Island is a man-made archipelago in the area covering about 2.7 million square metres across four fronds, roughly 10 km from RAK city and 70 to 80 km from central Dubai. The project is a designated freehold zone for non-GCC nationals. Wynn’s 2027 resort timeline is reshaping rents and buyer interest.

Invest Gulf living briefs treat Al Marjan as a coastal RAK base, not a Dubai suburb substitute.

Island facts checklist:
- Built in the 2000s as coral-shaped fronds
- Freehold for foreign buyers in approved stock
- Hotels already operating; Wynn under construction
- No major international school on the island itself

| Fact | Figure |
|---|---|
| Distance to Dubai | 70 to 80 km |
| Off-peak drive | 75 to 90 minutes |
| Island scale | ~2.7 million sq m |

## How long is the commute from Al Marjan to Dubai and RAK?

Commute time from Al Marjan typically means a car-only trip on E311, with no public transport link to Dubai. Off-peak Downtown runs take about 75 to 90 minutes over roughly 80 km; peak windows often stretch to 90 to 120 minutes and can add AED 1,500 to 2,500 monthly in fuel.

Invest Gulf commute tests always use a weekday peak drive, not a Sunday morning sample.

Commute reality defines whether Al Marjan Island works as a primary residence. Off-peak drives to Downtown Dubai take about 75 to 90 minutes over roughly 80 km on E311, while peak windows often stretch to 90 to 120 minutes and can add AED 1,500 to 2,500 per month in fuel for daily commuters. RAK city centre is nearer at 15 to 20 minutes off-peak, and RAK Airport about 25 to 30 minutes, which suits island-based work far better than daily DIFC attendance. Wynn Al Marjan Island, targeting a 2027 opening with roughly 1,000 hotel rooms and the UAE's first legal casino facility, is already lifting rental interest, but post-opening traffic will raise noise versus today's quieter island pace. Families must budget school runs of 15 to 25 minutes into RAK city because no major international campus sits on the island itself, and beachfront rents still carry a 20% to 30% premium.

## What are rent ranges and neighbourhoods on Al Marjan?

Al Marjan rents typically run well below Dubai Marina for similar bedroom counts. Studios often lease at AED 30,000 to 55,000 yearly, one-beds AED 45,000 to 80,000, and two-beds AED 65,000 to 120,000, with beachfront premiums of 20% to 30%.

Invest Gulf rent comps always separate interior-facing stock from sea-view premiums.

Al Marjan Island rent bands in 2026 still sit well below Dubai Marina for similar bedroom counts. Studio leases commonly run AED 30,000 to 55,000 per year depending on sector, one-bedroom units AED 45,000 to 80,000, and two-bedroom units AED 65,000 to 120,000, with beachfront and sea-view stock commanding a 20% to 30% premium over interior-facing apartments. A comparable Dubai Marina one-bedroom often leases at AED 90,000 to 130,000 annually, so Al Marjan can save a single professional roughly AED 4,000 to 8,000 per month when groceries, dining, and gym spend are included. Landlords typically request two to four post-dated cheques; some newer towers allow monthly payments for a slight premium. Car ownership is essential because the island has no public transport link to Dubai, and a daily E311 commute can add about AED 1,500 to 2,500 per month in fuel for round trips near 140 km.

## Where do Al Marjan families send children to school?

School access for Al Marjan families typically means a 15 to 25 minute drive into RAK city because the island has no major international campus. GEMS Westminster, RAK Academy, and Delhi Private School are common options, and families should budget school transport on top of rents already AED 45,000 to 80,000 for many one-beds.

Invest Gulf family moves lock the school shortlist before the tenancy cheque book.

## What healthcare and banking access do residents have?

Healthcare on Al Marjan typically means island clinics for minor care and RAK city hospitals about 20 minutes away for A&E. Burjeel RAK and Aster cover private needs; Dubai hospitals remain 75 to 90 minutes for complex cases. Banking is mostly a 15 to 20 minute drive into RAK city or online, which still beats a daily Dubai office commute costing AED 1,500 to 2,500 in fuel.

**Insider tip:** If your employer insurance network is Dubai-centric, confirm Burjeel RAK and Sheikh Khalifa Hospital coverage in writing before you sign a lease; emergency number 998 still routes to RAK city A&E first.

Our relocation packs treat insurance network maps as a hard gate, not a footnote.

Access checklist:
- Clinics on island for minor care
- Burjeel / Aster / Sheikh Khalifa in RAK city
- Banks: RAKBANK, ENBD, ADCB, RAKIslamic
- ATMs in hotel lobbies on island
- Confirm network cover before signing a 12-month lease

Rents and investor interest already rose after the 2022 announcement, with beachfront premiums still near 20% to 30%. Quieter island life will get busier post-opening.

Invest Gulf living notes separate “pre-Wynn quiet” from “post-Wynn traffic” when clients value silence.

- Noise and footfall will increase versus 2024 to 2026

## Can foreigners buy freehold property on Al Marjan?

Foreign buyers can purchase freehold on Al Marjan because the island is a designated RAK freehold zone for non-GCC nationals. Indicative studios run AED 450,000 to 700,000 with gross yields often cited near 6% to 9% depending on rental strategy. Verify with RERA RAK-registered agents.

Invest Gulf purchase files still separate SPA freehold confirmation from any residency marketing claims.

Buy checklist:
- Studio AED 450,000 to 700,000
- 1BR AED 700,000 to 1,200,000
- 2BR AED 1,100,000 to 2,000,000
- Yields: short-term often higher; annual more stable

| Type | Price (AED) | Yield (indicative) |
|---|---|---|
| Studio | 450k-700k | 6% to 9% |
| 1BR | 700k-1.2M | 7% to 8% |
