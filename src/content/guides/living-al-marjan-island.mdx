## What is Al Marjan Island?

Al Marjan Island is a man-made archipelago extending into the Arabian Gulf off the coast of Ras Al Khaimah (RAK). Built in the 2000s, it consists of four coral-shaped fronds covering approximately 2.7 million square metres. It sits about 10 km from RAK's main urban area and 70-80 km from central Dubai.

The island is a **designated freehold zone** for non-GCC nationals, allowing full property ownership. It hosts residential towers, beach hotels (including DoubleTree by Hilton and Hampton Inn), restaurants, beach clubs, and the under-construction Wynn Al Marjan Island resort.

The transformation from a quiet investment zone into a high-profile destination accelerated after the Wynn casino announcement in 2022. Since then, property prices and developer activity have increased substantially.

## How long is the commute from Al Marjan to Dubai and RAK?

Al Marjan Island has **no public transport links** to Dubai. Car is essential.

| Destination | Distance | Off-peak time | Peak time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RAK city centre | ~15 km | 15-20 min | 20-30 min |
| RAK Airport | ~25 km | 25-30 min | 30-40 min |
| Umm Al Quwain | ~25 km | 25-30 min | 30-40 min |
| Dubai (Downtown) | ~80 km | 75-90 min | 90-120 min |
| Dubai Airport (T3) | ~70 km | 70-80 min | 80-110 min |
| Abu Dhabi | ~180 km | 150-180 min | 160-200 min |

Route to Dubai: E311 (Emirates Road) via Al Ghubaiba intersection is the primary route. Some drivers use E11 (Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road), compare travel apps at commute time.

Fuel costs: round trip to Dubai ~70 km each way = ~140 km per day. At an average sedan fuel efficiency, this adds approximately AED 1,500-2,500 per month to transport costs for a daily Dubai commuter.

## What are rent ranges and neighbourhoods on Al Marjan?

Al Marjan Island's residential stock consists primarily of low-to-mid-rise towers clustered in named sectors:

| Sector | Character | Studio AED/yr | 1BR AED/yr | 2BR AED/yr |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bora Bora | Beach-adjacent, older stock | 30,000-40,000 | 45,000-60,000 | 65,000-85,000 |
| Mediterranean | Mid-island, mixed use | 32,000-45,000 | 48,000-65,000 | 68,000-90,000 |
| Pacific | Newer towers, sea view premium | 38,000-50,000 | 55,000-75,000 | 80,000-110,000 |
| Bermuda | Beachfront, premium end | 40,000-55,000 | 60,000-80,000 | 85,000-120,000 |

Beachfront and sea-view units carry a 20-30% premium above interior-facing units.

For comparison: a similar 1BR in Dubai Marina runs AED 90,000-130,000 annually. Al Marjan's 1BR at AED 45,000-75,000 represents a significant saving.

**Payment terms:** Most Al Marjan landlords accept post-dated cheques (2-4 cheques). Some newer buildings accept monthly for a slight premium.

**Furnished vs unfurnished:** both available. Holiday rentals are common on the island, some buildings have restrictions on short-term rental by residents.

## What healthcare access do Al Marjan residents have?

Healthcare on Al Marjan Island is limited to clinics. For significant medical care, RAK city or Dubai is required.

| Facility | Location | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Al Marjan Island clinics | On island | Minor consultations |
| Burjeel RAK | RAK city | Private, good standards |
| Aster RAK Clinic | RAK city | GP and specialist |
| Sheikh Khalifa Hospital RAK | RAK city | Government, emergency |
| Rashid Hospital / Dubai private network | Dubai | Major procedures |

**Insurance tip:** If your employer insurance network is Dubai-based, confirm it covers RAK clinics and Burjeel RAK. Some policies require pre-authorisation for treatment outside the policy emirate.

Emergency number: **998** (ambulance). Nearest A&E is in RAK city, approximately 20 minutes from island.

## How does banking work for Al Marjan residents in RAK?

Major banks operate in RAK with branches in RAK city:

- **RAKBANK (National Bank of Ras Al Khaimah)**, local champion, competitive products
- **Emirates NBD**, UAE-wide network, convenient for cross-emirate banking
- **Abu Dhabi Commercial Bank (ADCB)**, UAE-wide
- **RAKIslamic**, Islamic banking window

For Al Marjan residents, most banking is done in RAK city (15-20 min drive) or online. Cash ATMs are available on the island at hotel lobbies.

**Impact on the living market:**

- Short-term rental yields projected higher post-opening
- New F&B and retail on island improving lifestyle infrastructure
- Traffic and footfall on the island are expected to rise post-opening

**Living consideration:** If you value quiet island life, understand that the post-Wynn environment will be significantly more active than current conditions. Those drawn to the resort atmosphere will find it improving significantly.

## What is community life like on Al Marjan Island?

Al Marjan Island's lifestyle offering has improved steadily:

**Beach access:** Direct beach access from most residential towers. Public beach stretch available. Hotel beach clubs (DoubleTree, Hampton) have paid day passes.

**Dining:** Several restaurants and cafés on the island. Growing F&B cluster near the hotels. Limited variety compared to Dubai, most residents drive to RAK city or keep a home kitchen culture.

**Water sports:** Jet ski, paddleboard, kayak rentals on beach.

**Running and cycling:** Island perimeter walk/run path (approximately 5 km loop). Cycling popular in mornings, flat terrain.

**Weekend excursions from Al Marjan:**
- Jebel Jais mountain (highest peak in UAE, 40 min), via ferrata, zip line
- RAK old town and Corniche (15-20 min)
- Dhayah Fort (20 min)
- Dubai beach clubs and city (75-90 min)

## What should you check before moving to Al Marjan?

Before committing to Al Marjan:

- Test commute to Dubai at your actual commute time (not off-peak Sunday morning)
- Confirm school bus route from your building to chosen school
- Verify employer insurance covers Burjeel RAK and RAK city clinics
- Check building short-term rental policy if you plan holiday use or Airbnb
- Clarify post-dated cheque payment requirement for rent
- Visit during a weekday evening to assess real community activity level
- Check planned construction near unit, Wynn area generates noise and traffic
