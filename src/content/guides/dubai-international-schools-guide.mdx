# Dubai International Schools Guide 2026: KHDA Ratings, Curricula & Admissions

**TL;DR:** Dubai has **200+ KHDA-regulated** international schools — the densest school market in the Gulf. British and IB premium tiers run **AED 85,000–120,000/year**; Indian CBSE offers value at **AED 25,000–40,000**. Top Outstanding schools want applications **6–12 months** before September start. Pick your school shortlist first, then hunt rent — a 25-minute school run at 7:15 AM is fine; the same route at 7:45 is gridlock.

## Why school choice drives your Dubai address

Dubai is geographically compact but **traffic-isolated** at school run hours. Families who get this right map the **home → school → office triangle** on a Sunday morning before they touch a lease.

Get it wrong and you pay twice: AED 90,000 in tuition plus 90 minutes daily in the car, five days a week, for three years. That is not a commute — it is a second unpaid job.

## KHDA — the regulator that matters

Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) oversees **private schools in Dubai** (not Abu Dhabi ADEK, not Sharjah SPEA).

| KHDA tool | Use |
|---|---|
| School ratings | Outstanding / Very Good / Good / Acceptable |
| Fee index | Caps annual increase by rating |
| Inspection reports | Public PDF — read before tour |
| School directory | Official list — avoid unlicensed operators |

**Rating ≠ fit:** Outstanding British school across town may lose to Very Good school 12 minutes from home on stress-adjusted family metrics.

## Fee tiers (2026 indicative)

### Budget (AED 25,000–45,000/year)
- Indian CBSE/ICSE mid-tier
- Some newer British Acceptable-rated schools

### Mid (AED 50,000–75,000/year)
- Good and Very Good British schools
- Established American programmes

### Premium (AED 80,000–120,000+/year)
- Outstanding British (JESS, Repton, Kings)
- IB diploma years at tier-1 schools
- Smaller class sizes, heavy extracurricular

**Hidden costs:** registration AED 2,000–10,000 · bus AED 8,000–14,000/year · uniforms · trips · exam fees

| Month | Action |

| October–January | Apply — premium schools fill early |
| January–March | Assessments and interviews |
| April–May | Accept place, pay deposit |

## British schools — landscape

Dubai's largest expat cohort chooses British curriculum.

| Tier | Examples (illustrative) | Fee range |
|---|---|---|
| Outstanding premium | JESS, Repton, Kings College | AED 85K–110K |
| Very Good mid | GEMS cluster, Horizon English | AED 55K–75K |
| Good value | Newer British openings | AED 45K–60K |

**A-Level vs IB:** British schools offer IGCSE then A-Level or IB diploma in sixth form — confirm pathway before Year 10.

## IB schools

Full IB continuum (PYP-MYP-DP) schools charge premium — diploma years most expensive.

**Best for:** families expecting **3+ country moves** before university — credit transfer consistency.

**Trade-off:** heavier workload than A-Level for some students — visit student support department.

## American schools

AP track schools serve US State Department, defence contractors, and American corporate families.

| Consideration | Detail |

| College counselling | Stronger in established American schools |
| Sports culture | Often stronger than British mid-tier |

## Indian schools — value and volume

CBSE schools dominate **labour-cost-sensitive** expat segment and Indian business community.

| Strength | Limitation |
|---|---|
| Strong STEM | Heavy homework culture |
| Lower fees | Less Western extracurricular breadth |
| Large diaspora peer group | Class sizes can be 25–35 |

Fee band **AED 25,000–40,000** — critical for family COL under AED 40,000/month total.

## SEN and inclusion

If your child needs support:

1. Ask **SENCO** directly — not admissions marketing
2. Request **Individual Education Plan** process outline
3. Confirm **shadow teacher** costs — often **AED 50,000–120,000/year** extra
4. Read KHDA inclusion section in inspection report

Outstanding rating does not guarantee SEN capacity.

## Outstanding vs Very Good — when rating misleads

| Scenario | Better choice |
|---|---|
| Outstanding 35 min drive vs Very Good 12 min | Very Good for young children |
| Outstanding with wait list vs Good immediate | Good if assignment under 3 years |
| Outstanding fees +20% vs Very Good | Very Good if university outcomes similar |

KHDA inspection **sub-scores** matter: **Teaching and assessment** vs **Leadership** — read PDF page 3–8.

## Waiting list management — practical tactics

1. Apply **all siblings same day** — sibling priority real at many schools
2. Pay **assessment fees** promptly — queue position lost on delay
3. Accept **backup school deposit** — forfeitable but secures seat
4. Join **Facebook parent groups** — vacancy rumours verified via admissions only
5. Mid-year **January intake** — ask explicitly; some grades have churn

Dubai's expat cost structure in 2026 reflects **visa liberalisation**, **remote work normalisation**, and **record school enrolment growth**. ## FAQ

**How many international schools in Dubai?**
**200+** KHDA-regulated — verify active list on KHDA portal.

**Cheapest good school?**
Indian CBSE **AED 25,000–35,000** — British mid starts **AED 45,000+**.

**When to apply?**
**6–12 months** before September for premium British/IB.

**Does KHDA rating matter?**
Yes for fee increase trajectory and resale narrative — not sole fit factor.
