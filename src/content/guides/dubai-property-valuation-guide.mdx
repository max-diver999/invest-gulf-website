Overpaying usually happens when buyers trust a broker PDF instead of those public records. This guide shows how to triangulate transaction comps, income value, and operating drag before you sign an MOU.

| Yield floor | Ejari rent data | Income property |
| Service charge drag | Mollak filing | Net yield accuracy |
| Off-plan benchmark | DLD per sqft vs community | Pre-handover pricing |
| Formal appraisal | RERA-licensed valuer | Mortgage, inheritance, dispute |

Dubai property valuation typically rests on three public floors: DLD transaction medians in AED per sqft over 6 to 12 months, Ejari registered rents for an income floor, and Mollak service charges that cut net yield. Portal asking prices often run 8 to 15% above recent registered sales, so buyers who rely on a broker PDF alone commonly overpay by 5 to 15%. A Marina unit at AED 1,800,000 with AED 28 per sqft service charges can lose AED 9,600 per year versus an identically priced JVC unit at AED 16 per sqft on 800 sqft, or AED 96,000 over ten years. Foreign buyers should triangulate all three sources before an MOU deposit. Invest Gulf reconciles REST, Ejari, and Mollak into one net yield band on each underwriting file.

Income valuation for a JVC 1-bedroom typically starts from an Ejari median near AED 62,000 rent, then subtracts service charges around AED 10,400, management near 8%, vacancy near 7%, and a maintenance reserve near AED 2,500 to leave about AED 39,800 net. At a 6.0% target net yield that income supports roughly AED 663,000 of price; at 5.5% the same income implies about AED 723,600, and at 6.5% about AED 612,300. An asking price of AED 850,000 on that cash flow is closer to 4.7% net unless rents rise. Off-plan that prices 15 to 20% above ready secondary stock in the same master community needs an explicit delivery and liquidity premium. We treat missing Mollak or Ejari data as a pause point, not a guess.

## Why is Dubai property valuation more transparent than most Gulf markets?

Dubai valuation is typically more transparent because every completed sale registers with DLD, so buyers can read median AED per sqft by building over 12 months instead of guessing from portals. Ejari rents and Mollak service charges add income and cost layers most Gulf markets still hide from casual buyers. Invest Gulf checklist:

The practical stack:

- Dubai REST: ownership, transfers, escrow, service charge hints
- TruEstimate (Bayut): automated range from DLD comps
- Ejari (RERA): registered rents by building
- Mollak (RERA): approved service charge budgets
- DLD transaction index: community-level price history

Buyers who use all four sources usually land in a narrow value band. Buyers who rely on a single comparison PDF often miss 5 to 15% versus registered transactions.

## How do you pull DLD transaction comparables step by step?

DLD comparables typically start on Dubai REST by filtering the same building and unit type, then reading median AED per sqft over 6 to 12 months. Floor premiums often add 1 to 3% per five floors, and asks more than 10% above median need a verified upgrade story. Our checklist:

1. Open Dubai REST or DLD online services
2. Search by building or area
3. Filter studio, 1BR, 2BR, 3BR, or villa as relevant
4. Note median AED/sqft over the last 6 to 12 months
5. Compare asking price to that median

Community price benchmarks (Q1 2026, indicative):

| Community | AED/sqft range | Transaction volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| JVC | AED 900 to 1,400 | Very high |
| Business Bay | AED 1,600 to 2,200 | High |
| Dubai Marina | AED 1,900 to 2,600 | High |
| Downtown Dubai | AED 2,200 to 3,500 | Medium-high |
| Palm Jumeirah (apartments) | AED 2,800 to 4,000 | Medium |
| Dubai Hills (villas) | AED 1,600 to 2,400 | Medium |
| JLT | AED 1,100 to 1,600 | Medium |

If asking price exceeds the building median by more than 10%, ask for a specific justification: upgraded finish, parking, view, or recent comparable you can verify on REST.

## How do Ejari rents set an income-based value floor?

Ejari rents typically set the income floor because registered contracts run 5 to 10% below portal asking rents in many towers. Dividing net operating income by a 5.5 to 7.0% target net yield in JVC-style stock caps a sensible AED purchase price before you negotiate. Our checklist:

Example, JVC 1-bedroom:

- Ejari median rent: AED 62,000/year
- Target net yield: 6.0%
- Service charge (AED 16/sqft x 650 sqft): AED 10,400
- Management (8%): AED 4,960
- Vacancy (7%): AED 4,340
- Maintenance reserve: AED 2,500
- Net income: AED 39,800
- Income value at 6% net: AED 663,000

If asking price is AED 850,000, expect roughly 4.7% net unless rents rise or costs fall. That may be acceptable for growth bets, but it should be a conscious choice.

| Community tier | Target net yield band |
| --- | --- |
| JVC, Sports City, Discovery Gardens | 5.5 to 7.0% |
| Business Bay, JLT, Dubai South | 5.0 to 6.5% |
| Dubai Marina, Downtown | 4.0 to 5.5% |
| Palm Jumeirah, DIFC | 3.5 to 4.5% |

Sensitivity: on AED 39,800 net income, a 5.5% target implies AED 723,600; at 6.5% target AED 612,300. A 1 point yield assumption moves price by six figures.

Model rents on 12-month Ejari averages, not peak-season listings. See [capital appreciation vs yield](/guides/dubai-capital-appreciation-vs-yield/) for how growth and income trade off.

## How do Mollak service charges change fair value?

Mollak service charges typically range from about AED 11 to 16 per sqft in Discovery Gardens to AED 22 to 35 in Downtown, and on 800 sqft that is AED 8,800 to 28,000 yearly. High charges must cap fair value even when headline AED per sqft looks cheap. Our checklist:

| Community | AED/sqft/year (typical) | On 800 sqft unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery Gardens | AED 11 to 16 | AED 8,800 to 12,800 |
| JVC mid-tier | AED 13 to 18 | AED 10,400 to 14,400 |
| Business Bay | AED 16 to 22 | AED 12,800 to 17,600 |
| Dubai Marina | AED 20 to 28 | AED 16,000 to 22,400 |
| Downtown | AED 22 to 35 | AED 17,600 to 28,000 |

Efficiency matters: a building with gym, pool, and district cooling may justify higher charges if tenants pay comparable rent. Red flags include arrears above 10% of budget, repeated special assessments, and deferred lift or facade work.

Request three years of service charge statements before offer. Mollak via RERA or Dubai REST confirms filed rates; seller invoices show payment behaviour.

## How should you value off-plan when DLD comps are thin?

Off-plan pricing typically needs a ready-stock AED per sqft benchmark in the same master community, then discounts for handover wait and delivery risk. Paying 15 to 20% above secondary sales needs a clear appreciation or payment-plan thesis, and Tier 1 developers often command 5 to 15% premiums. Our checklist:

**Entry price vs ready stock:** Off-plan should usually discount ready prices for waiting and construction risk. Paying 15 to 20% above ready stock needs a clear view on appreciation or payment plan economics.

**Developer premium:** Tier 1 developers with strong on-time records often command 5 to 15% over comparable Tier 2 product. Discount weaker track records explicitly.

**Assignment market:** Sold-out launches trade on assignments at premiums to original price; model yield on what you pay today, not the launch price from 2022.

## When do you need a formal RERA valuation?

Formal RERA valuations are typically required by banks, courts, and some visa filings, and turnaround is usually 5 to 14 days once access is arranged. REST prints and TruEstimate support negotiation but do not replace a signed licensed report when a lender prices an AED mortgage facility. Checklist:

Typical methods:

- Sales comparison (primary for residential)
- Income approach (capitalised Ejari income)
- Cost approach (unique assets)

Valuation fees are modest relative to purchase price; timeline is usually days to two weeks once access is arranged.

## What registered DLD value matters for Golden Visa thresholds?

Golden Visa property routes typically use registered DLD transaction value, not side letters, and the 4% transfer fee is calculated on that same registered figure. Registering AED 1,900,000 while paying AED 2,100,000 can fail eligibility thresholds and creates clear compliance risk for buyers. Our checklist:

- DLD registers the agreed price you declare at transfer
- Paying AED 2,100,000 but registering AED 1,900,000 can fail eligibility and creates compliance risk
- Verify current ICA/GDRFA rules with your PRO; thresholds and mortgage treatment change

For broader scam patterns around "discounted registration," see [Dubai property red flags](/guides/dubai-property-scams-red-flags/).

## What should you request from a seller before offering?

Sellers should typically provide three years of service charge invoices, Mollak printouts, 12 months of same-type DLD comps, and Ejari history if tenanted before any cash deposit. Missing packs should delay the MOU deposit, not trigger painful renegotiation after a survey booking date. Pre-signature checklist:

- Three years of service charge invoices and Mollak printout
- Ejari history if tenanted
- DLD transaction list for same unit type (12 months)
- Title deed and any mortgage charge
- OA bylaws: STR rules, pets, parking
- Seller clearance on service charge arrears
- Developer NOC cost estimate for secondary sales

Verify broker credentials via [RERA broker verification](/guides/rera-broker-verification-dubai/) before you rely on their comp sheet.

## What valuation mistakes show up most in 2026?

The three repeat 2026 mistakes typically are using portal rents 5 to 10% above Ejari, ignoring Mollak service charge trajectories, and pricing off-plan off peak secondary highs. Full-year Ejari medians beat September to February spikes when you set a durable net yield floor for offers. Pre-signature checklist:

Market timing: Q1 and Q3 often give buyers more negotiation room than Q4 when new arrivals compete. That is a tactical discount, not a substitute for DLD comps.

## How should you negotiate once comps disagree with the asking price?

Negotiation typically works best when DLD median printouts and Ejari net yield at the ask are attached to the written offer pack. Mid-market sellers who cannot reconcile those figures often accept 3 to 8% reductions where REST inventory is visible on screen today. Our checklist:

| Negotiation lever | When it works | Typical discount band |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stale listing (90+ days) | Marina, JLT, older JVC stock | 3 to 10% |
| High service charge vs peers | Same community, older tower | 2 to 7% |
| Vacant unit, motivated seller | Secondary market | 5 to 12% |
| Off-plan assignment premium | Sold-out hype | Case by case |

Put your offer range in writing with comp attachments. Trustees and agents respond faster when MOU drafts reference registered transactions, not portal screenshots alone.
