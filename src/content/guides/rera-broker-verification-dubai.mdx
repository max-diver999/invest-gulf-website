## Why does RERA license brokers in Dubai?

RERA licensing is the accountability layer for individual brokers and brokerage offices, and Invest Gulf requires an active BRN plus a valid office licence before foreign buyers tour stock. High transaction volumes above 205,000 deals in recent years attract WhatsApp entrants without registration or Form B. Treat unverified BRNs as a 0% trust starting point.

- Individual brokers pass DREI exams and renew annually
- The office licence, not only the agent, carries escrow routing accountability
- Money sent to personal accounts sits outside reliable DLD recovery paths
- Re-check BRN within 24 hours of any AED reservation

## How do you run the five-minute Trakheesi check?

The Trakheesi check is a five-step verification that typically takes under 5 minutes, and we require Active BRN status plus a listing permit before foreign buyers pay even AED 1 in reservation money. Repeat the search within 1 day of reservation and again before each cheque tranche on a 30 to 90 day deal.

1. Open trakheesi.ae or Dubai REST and choose broker search
2. Enter the BRN from the card, not only a mobile number
3. Confirm status Active, matching name, brokerage name, and future expiry
4. Search the office licence and confirm the BRN sits under that firm
5. Locate the Trakheesi permit number on Bayut, Property Finder, or Dubai REST

## What is Form B and how does it differ from Form A?

Form A is the seller-broker listing agreement, while Form B is the buyer-broker mandate, and we require Form B with written commission before foreign buyers attend the first viewing. Market norm on secondary sales is 2% plus 5% VAT, but only the signed Form B binds the fee.

| Form | Who signs | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form A | Seller + listing broker | Registers listing, exclusivity often 3 to 6 months, commission split |
| Form B | Buyer + buyer broker | Search mandate, commission, engagement term |

On off-plan primary sales, developer-paid commission should leave the buyer line at 0% on genuine developer inventory.

## Where do commission disputes come from?

Commission disputes typically start from verbal fee promises and missing Form B, and we have seen brokers claim introduction fees after private completions even when foreign buyers thought no mandate existed. Fix the number in Form B before viewing and keep WhatsApp subordinate to the signed contract across a 30 to 90 day search window.

- Negotiate 2% plus 5% VAT or your agreed figure in Form B first
- Refuse blank commission boxes
- Keep listing trail screenshots if co-broking appears later

## What changes for off-plan purchases?

Off-plan broker engagement requires a developer authorisation letter for the named project, and we match unit price to the official schedule so foreign buyers avoid risky assignment stock. Deposits must hit the Dubai REST escrow IBAN within the SPA window, with Oqood timed after the first AED payment.

- Demand project-specific authorisation, not a logo on a card
- Keep buyer broker fees at 0% on genuine developer-paid inventory
- Match escrow IBAN to the SPA and Dubai REST
- Treat deep "discounts" as a diligence trigger, not a win
- Match escrow IBAN within 24 hours of any AED booking

## How should remote buyers use brokers safely?

Remote foreign buyers should add an independent UAE property lawyer at roughly AED 5,000 to 15,000 who reviews the SPA separately from the broker, and we reject selling-broker power of attorney as a default path. Verify title on Dubai REST by video before authorising transfer within the same 7 to 14 day reservation window.

- Do not grant POA to the selling broker
- Share dated Trakheesi screenshots with your lawyer
- Confirm trustee appointment slots yourself on transfer day

Hard stops include missing BRN, personal-account deposits, Form B refusal, and same-day pressure, and we tell foreign buyers to exit within 24 hours when any appear. Dual agency still needs independent negotiation even when BRN is Active and commission is quoted at 2% plus 5% VAT on secondary stock.

**Hard stops:**

- BRN missing or expired (0% continuation)
- Deposit to a personal account for any AED amount (0% continuation)

## How do you report an unlicensed broker?

Reporting starts on dubailand.gov.ae with WhatsApp, receipts, and marketing evidence, and we add Dubai Police cybercrime filing immediately when foreign buyers have already moved funds to a personal account. Recovery inside UAE escrow is far stronger than chasing overseas transfers over 3 to 6 months. File the same day if AED already left your account.

- File via DLD / RERA services with dated screenshots
- Add bank fraud recall the same day if money moved
- Keep Trakheesi "Not Found" or "Expired" exports

## How does a licensed broker fit your wider due diligence?

RERA registration covers the intermediary, not the asset, and we still require title, Mollak service charges, OA rules, and snagging checks for foreign buyers even when BRN is Active. Brokers coordinate logistics; lawyers sign off on SPA and transfer mechanics across a 30 to 90 day close window. Run asset checks across the same 30 to 90 day close.

| Layer | Broker role | Your independent check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Must show active BRN | Trakheesi + office licence |
| Listing | Marketing with permit | Permit number on portal ad |
| Contract | Form B commission | Lawyer on SPA and transfer |
| Payment | Routes to escrow | IBAN match on REST |

## What mistakes do international buyers repeat?

Common mistakes are skipping Trakheesi for brand names, paying personal-account holds, and signing Form B with blank commission boxes, and we see each erase audit trails for foreign buyers within days. Friendly agents can still be unlicensed at 0% regulatory cover on a 2% fee pitch.

- Verify the individual BRN, not only the office brand, within 24 hours
- Refuse personal-account holds above AED 0
- Refuse personal-account "hold" fees on off-plan
- Re-run Trakheesi before every new cheque tranche

## How do brokers interact with mortgages and transfers?

Mortgage brokers and RERA sales brokers are different licences, and we require conflict disclosure when one person plays both roles for foreign buyers. Form B should not auto-trigger full 2% commission if financing fails outside your control unless you explicitly agreed that clause over a 60 to 90 day mortgage window first.

- Separate mortgage fee entities from sales BRN
- Verify cheque amounts and seller NOC yourself on transfer day
- Read the final transfer summary before signing

## How often should you re-check Trakheesi?

Licences renew annually, so Invest Gulf re-runs BRN search on reservation day and before each MOU or cheque for foreign buyers. An Active BRN under Firm A yesterday can sit inactive under Firm B today after a job change within 30 days. Keep screenshots for 12 months. Keep dated PDFs for at least 12 months after transfer.

- Screenshot Trakheesi results dated the same day as any deposit
- Keep PDF exports in a folder shared with your lawyer
- Reconcile Arabic permit text with English listing IDs when numbers disagree

## What quick reference applies before you pay any broker fee?

Minimum actions before fees are Trakheesi BRN and office checks, Form B with filled commission at 2% plus 5% VAT or your figure, escrow path confirmation, and listing permit verification, and we apply this checklist to every new agent for foreign buyers within the first 2 days. Refuse fees until all four rows show written proof.
