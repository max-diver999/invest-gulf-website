  - question: "Is Salalah cheaper than Muscat for expat living?"
    answer: "Yes, Salalah rent typically runs 20-35% below comparable Muscat levels. However, international school options are more limited and complex medical cases often require flights to Muscat, which can offset housing savings for families with specific needs."
  - question: "Should I choose Salalah or Muscat for expat life in Oman?"

  - question: "What are the main challenges of living in Salalah?"

  - question: "How is healthcare in Salalah compared to Muscat?"
    answer: "Salalah handles routine medical care adequately with Sultan Qaboos Hospital and private clinics. However, complex procedures, specialists, or serious emergencies often require medical evacuation to Muscat's larger facilities, which expats should factor into planning."
