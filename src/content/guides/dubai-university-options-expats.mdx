1. Conditional offer accepted. Pay the registration deposit and receive your enrolment letter.
2. University submits visa application. The institution acts as the visa sponsor through the ICA (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security) system.
3. Entry permit issued. Typically within 1–3 weeks of submission; valid for 60 days to enter the UAE or change status.
4. Medical and Emirates ID. Complete the health test at an approved centre, submit biometrics, and receive your Emirates ID.
5. Residence visa stamped. One-year residence visa; renewable annually upon continued enrolment with satisfactory academic standing.

Dependent sponsorship: Students on a student visa cannot sponsor family members. If a student's parent is the UAE visa holder, the student can remain on the parent's dependent visa while enrolled, this is common for expat families where the child is already a dependent.

After graduation: The student visa lapses within 30 days of completion. Students must either secure employment (and transfer to an employment visa) or depart. Given Dubai's active graduate job market, most students who want to stay find sponsorship within the grace period, but have a plan before finals month.

## Who this suits — decision framework

Not every expat profile benefits equally from this route in Dubai. Consider three common scenarios:

- **Scenario A — employed professional relocating with family:** salary above AED 15,000/month, employer covers most setup costs, priority is speed and compliance. This route offers the clearest paperwork trail and the fastest timeline (typically 10–20 working days).
- **Scenario B — freelancer or remote worker on a flexible visa:** income from multiple clients, lower tolerance for bureaucratic friction, and a preference for digital processes. Budget 20–30 % more time for documentation and plan around the renewal cycle.
- **Scenario C — investor or retiree planning long-term residency:** capital deployed in property or business, lower urgency but higher stakes. Focus on the 10-year horizon — cost differences compound over a decade, so optimise for total lifecycle expense rather than upfront convenience.

Each path converges at the same destination; the difference is timeline, cost, and hassle level. Pick the scenario closest to yours and skip sections that don't apply.
