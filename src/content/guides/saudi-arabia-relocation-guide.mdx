## Who relocates to Saudi Arabia: and why

Saudi’s expat workforce powers **energy, construction, healthcare, finance, entertainment, tourism and giga-projects**. Unlike the UAE’s decades-old expat layer, much of what you experience in 2026 is still being built.

Who we see most often:
- Senior professionals on Vision 2030 packages (Riyadh HQ moves)
- Healthcare and education specialists in compound-friendly roles
- Engineers and PMOs on NEOM, Red Sea, Qiddiya, with eyes open on maturity timelines
- Investors testing Premium Residency or zone property, high diligence burden
- Jeddah coastal families preferring humidity and a relatively more open coastal rhythm vs inland Riyadh

Weak fit: Dubai-identical beach-club daily life everywhere; nationwide foreign freehold without zone verification; anyone uncomfortable with conservative public norms regardless of recent reforms.

## Pre-arrival checklist (10–14 weeks before)

### 1. Contract and sponsorship audit

| Line | Verify |
|---|---|
| Sponsor entity | Licensed KSA employer with prior iqama history |
| Job title on visa | Matches actual role, mismatch risks inspection |
| Salary structure | Basic vs housing vs transport, affects banking and family visa |
| Medical insurance | Class C minimum or employer enhanced, hospital list |
| Schooling | Allowance cap, currency, direct billing |
| End of service | Saudi Labour Law calculation |
| Repatriation | Notice, ticket class, shipping |

Red flag: “visa run” promises without employer attestation, illegal work risks deportation and bans.

### 2. Attestation chain

Degrees, marriage and birth certificates typically require:
- Home country notary/ministry
- Saudi embassy attestation
- Saudi Cultural Mission steps for education documents (employer-dependent)

Plan 8–12 weeks; US/UK/Indian chains differ.

### 3. School research (families)

Riyadh and Jeddah international schools are growing but premium seats fill early.

→ [Riyadh international schools](/guides/riyadh-international-schools/) · [Jeddah international schools](/guides/jeddah-international-schools/)

British, American and IB available; fees high at top tier, model separately from rent.

### 4. Compound vs apartment decision

Many families choose secured compounds (pools, community, school buses) vs city apartments near work. Compounds cost more but reduce cultural friction for new arrivals.

### 5. Property and Premium Residency: do not prepay on rumour

If relocation bundles with property purchase or Premium Residency:
- Obtain written programme rules from official channel
- Lawyer review in designated zone under Law M/14
- Separate job iqama from investor status, they are not interchangeable by default

## Visa and iqama pathways

### Standard employment iqama (majority path)

1. Employer obtains visa authorization (block visa or individual)
2. You enter on work visit / employment visa
3. Medical test and biometrics in KSA
4. Iqama (residence permit) issued: your daily legal ID
5. Absher account setup: government portal for fines, visas, dependents

Timeline: 3–8 weeks on-ground for straightforward cases; employer PRO quality dominates variance.

→ [Saudi iqama process](/guides/saudi-iqama-process/)

### Family visit / dependent iqama

Sponsor must meet salary and accommodation rules (verify current Ministry of Human Resources thresholds). Attested marriage/birth certificates required.

→ [Saudi family visa](/guides/saudi-family-visa/)

→ [Saudi Premium Residency living](/guides/saudi-premium-residency-living/)

Do not conflate marketing brochures with iqama until government confirmation in hand.

### Visitor / remote tracks

## Law M/14: foreign property ownership (verify at publish)

## First 90 days: operational timeline

### Weeks 1–2

- Hotel or corporate temporary housing
- Medical for iqama
- Biometrics
- SIM (STC, Mobily, Zain), passport + visa
- Absher activation with PRO help
- Do not drive until Saudi licence process clear, fines are automated

### Weeks 3–5

- Iqama collected, laminate copy in wallet
- Bank account opening (see banking)
- Compound or apartment lease, Ejar registration where applicable
- School assessments for children
- Car purchase or lease, SUV popular for compound↔school routes

### Weeks 6–12

- Dependent iqama filed
- Paediatrician within network
- Explore entertainment calendar (seasonal events, sports)
- Understand Prayer/Friday rhythm for scheduling
- If buying property, lawyer sign-off only after zone verification

## Culture, religion and social change: YMYL tone

Saudi Arabia has changed fast since 2016, cinemas, concerts, women driving, tourism visas, but public conservatism remains the baseline for content accuracy and personal safety.

### Practical respect norms (2026)

- Modest dress in malls and government buildings, abaya not legally required for all foreign women in every context but conservative dress reduces friction
- Ramadan: no eating, drinking or smoking in public during daylight; shorter hours
- Alcohol: prohibited, do not expect UAE-style licensed home supply
- Photography: restricted near government/military sites; ask people for consent
- Relationships: public displays of affection discouraged
- Friday prayer: traffic and shop closures around midday

### Social life: what improved

- Ticketed events, sports leagues, compound social calendars
- Riyadh Season and Jeddah events, quality varies year to year
- Expat clubs (running, rugby) inside compounds and licensed venues

→ [Social life Riyadh expats](/guides/saudi-entertainment-social-life/)

## Household goods, pets and compound logistics

Shipping: Riyadh receives air and sea freight; compound addresses simplify last-mile. From UAE, road freight 2–4 days; from Europe 6–12 weeks port-to-door with customs broker.

| Origin | Tip |
|---|---|
| UAE | Sell furniture in Dubai, re-buy in KSA, often cheaper than VAT/duty friction |
| UK/EU | Winter shipping avoids summer port heat delays |
| US | Long lead, temp furnished compound common |

Pets: import rules exist, rabies titre timelines, MOA approval; summer embargo periods on some airlines. Compound pet policies vary, confirm breed and weight before lease.

Domestic help: sponsorship similar to UAE, visa + medical for live-in maid; budget SAR 2,500–4,500 all-in monthly plus housing room.

Cars: many expats buy SUV new or nearly new, resale market deep for Toyota/Ford; Salik not applicable in KSA but fuel subsidised feel still adds SAR 800–1,500/month per car.

## Leaving Saudi Arabia: repatriation checklist

When Vision 2030 project ends or family relocates:

1. Final exit visa via sponsor: do not overstay iqama
2. Absher fines cleared: traffic cameras strict
3. Bank account closure after liabilities
4. School transcripts requested 60 days early (June crunch)
5. Compound deposit: inspection and utility clearance
6. Vehicle: export or sell with proper title transfer
7. End-of-service benefit calculation in writing: dispute window short
8. Premium Residency holders: separate cancellation rules, verify

Poor exit discipline affects re-entry bans, treat PRO instructions seriously.

## Dual-career households: Riyadh reality

Second trailing spouse employment is harder than Dubai:

- Own iqama tied to own sponsor historically, Premium track may change this, verify
- Volunteering and remote work for foreign employer sit in **grey zones**, get written clarity
- Compound social networks become primary career support for trailing spouses, plan mental health and community, not only salary spreadsheet

## FAQ

**Is Saudi Arabia good for expats in 2026?**  
Strong for **career and packages** tied to Vision 2030; harder for **lifestyle-only** relocators expecting Dubai parity everywhere.

**What is iqama?**  
Your **residence permit ID**, required for bank, lease, school and domestic travel checks.

**How much is Saudi Premium Residency?**  
Commonly cited **~SAR 4 million** investment track, **verify with Premium Residency Center**; rules evolve.

**Can foreigners buy property in Saudi Arabia?**  
Only in **designated zones under Law M/14**, **confirm current zone list and lawyer title opinion** before any deposit.

**Riyadh or Jeddah for families?**  
**Riyadh** for HQ jobs and new compounds; **Jeddah** for Red Sea climate and older expat coastal life, job location decides.

**Are schools expensive?**  
Premium tiers rival **UAE fees**, read allowance caps carefully.

**Is alcohol available?**  
**No** legal retail alcohol for residents, unlike some UAE emirates.

**How conservative is daily life?**  
**Public life remains conservative**; compounds and licensed venues are more international, honesty matters for YMYL readers.

**Can women drive and work?**  
**Yes**, driving licences and workforce participation expanded; still plan transport and compound norms.

**Saudi or UAE for tax-free family life?**  
Both **0% employment income tax** for typical expats; choose on **schools, visa independence, lifestyle and job**, not tax alone.
