## Pathway map: every route remote workers ask about

### 1. Tourist eVisa (short stay)

Online visa for 50+ nationalities. Multiple-entry options cited. Stays often run up to 90 days per visit within a one-year visa window  (confirm current official rules).

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cost | ~SAR 535 including insurance cited |
| Processing | Often 24–72 hours online |
| Purpose | Tourism, sightseeing, family visit, exploration |
| Work rights | Not a remote-work permit |
| Banking | Difficult without iqama, tourist limitations |
| Schools | Not viable for dependant schooling |

Grey zone warning: some remote workers physically work from a hotel or apartment on tourist status. Saudi enforcement and labour-law classification differ from UAE's structured remote programme. Treat this as risk until counsel confirms, we do not recommend relying on tourist status for 90+ day work stays.

### 2. Employer-sponsored iqama (standard expat path)

Residence permit tied to a licensed Saudi employer.

| Remote relevance | Reality |
|---|---|
| Foreign employer only | Does not qualify, no Saudi sponsor |
| Saudi branch hires you | Qualifies, you work for KSA entity |
| “Remote” inside KSA for foreign HQ | Needs explicit legal structure, often not standard iqama |

→ [Saudi iqama process](/guides/saudi-iqama-process/)

Best for professionals taking Riyadh/Jeddah packages, not laptop nomads.

### 3. Premium Residency (self-sponsored long stay)

Long-stay programme expanded to multiple categories (2024–2025 reforms): investment, talent, real estate, direct payment tracks.

Commonly cited tiers (confirm current official rules):

| Category | Indicative threshold | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Direct payment (annual) | SAR 100,000/year | 1–5 years renewable |
| Direct payment (permanent) | SAR 800,000 one-time | Permanent |
| Investor track | SAR 7M+ investment + jobs cited | Permanent |
| Real estate owner | Approved zone property  (confirm current official rules) | Varies |
| Special talent | Licensed profession / expertise | Varies |

Remote worker angle: only high-income, capital-rich profiles, say, six-figure remote earners planning a multi-year Gulf base with 0% personal income tax, may model Premium Residency against repeated UAE renewals. Not an entry-level nomad path.

→ [Saudi Premium Residency living](/guides/saudi-premium-residency-living/)

### 4. Business / MISA investment licence

Establish a Saudi company via Ministry of Investment, then residency through your entity.

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Legal work in KSA market | Setup cost, compliance, corporate tax exposure |
| Potential government alignment | Not “quiet laptop by pool” nomad life |
| Scales to Premium tracks | Accountant and local partner diligence |

Freelance permit (Saudi nationals/residents): HRSD freelance system targets Saudi citizens and existing residents, not foreign tourist nomads  (confirm current official rules).
