# Saudi Remote Work Visa 2026: Programme Status, Premium Residency & UAE Comparison

**TL;DR:** As of Draft v1 2026-06-04, Saudi Arabia does not offer a confirmed, active dedicated remote work / digital nomad visa comparable to the UAE Virtual Working Programme. **[VERIFY]** official status before any relocation plan. Practical long-stay paths remain employer iqama, Premium Residency (high capital), or short tourist eVisa (not structured work permission). Vision 2030 commentary references a future nomad programme — not reliably live. This guide maps legal options, grey zones, and UAE comparison so remote earners do not confuse tourism marketing with residency rights.

## Pathway map — every route remote workers ask about

### 1. Tourist eVisa (short stay)

Online visa for 50+ nationalities. Multiple-entry options cited. Stays often run up to 90 days per visit within a one-year visa window **[VERIFY MOFA current terms]**.

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cost | ~SAR 535 including insurance cited |
| Processing | Often 24–72 hours online |
| Purpose | **Tourism** — sightseeing, family visit, exploration |
| Work rights | **Not a remote-work permit** |
| Banking | Difficult without iqama — tourist limitations |
| Schools | **Not viable** for dependant schooling |

Grey zone warning: some remote workers physically work from a hotel or apartment on tourist status. Saudi enforcement and labour-law classification differ from UAE's structured remote programme. Treat this as risk until counsel confirms — we do not recommend relying on tourist status for 90+ day work stays.

### 2. Employer-sponsored iqama (standard expat path)

Residence permit tied to a licensed Saudi employer.

| Remote relevance | Reality |
|---|---|
| Foreign employer only | **Does not qualify** — no Saudi sponsor |
| Saudi branch hires you | **Qualifies** — you work for KSA entity |
| “Remote” inside KSA for foreign HQ | **Needs explicit legal structure** — often **not** standard iqama |

→ [Saudi iqama process](/guides/saudi-iqama-process/)

Best for professionals taking Riyadh/Jeddah packages — not laptop nomads.

### 3. Premium Residency (self-sponsored long stay)

Long-stay programme expanded to multiple categories (2024–2025 reforms): investment, talent, real estate, direct payment tracks.

Commonly cited tiers [VERIFY all at publish]:

| Category | Indicative threshold | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Direct payment (annual) | SAR 100,000/year | 1–5 years renewable |
| Direct payment (permanent) | SAR 800,000 one-time | Permanent |
| Investor track | SAR 7M+ investment + jobs cited | Permanent |
| Real estate owner | Approved zone property **[VERIFY]** | Varies |
| Special talent | Licensed profession / expertise | Varies |

Remote worker angle: only high-income, capital-rich profiles — say, six-figure remote earners planning a multi-year Gulf base with 0% personal income tax — may model Premium Residency against repeated UAE renewals. Not an entry-level nomad path.

→ [Saudi Premium Residency living](/guides/saudi-premium-residency-living/)

### 4. Business / MISA investment licence

Establish a Saudi company via Ministry of Investment — then residency through your entity.

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Legal work in KSA market | Setup cost, compliance, corporate tax exposure |
| Potential government alignment | Not “quiet laptop by pool” nomad life |
| Scales to Premium tracks | Accountant and local partner diligence |

Freelance permit (Saudi nationals/residents): HRSD freelance system targets Saudi citizens and existing residents — not foreign tourist nomads **[VERIFY MHRSD scope]**.

## Banking, tax and compliance for remote earners

### Banking

Saudi banks (SNB, Al Rajhi, Riyad Bank, SABB) typically require iqama or Premium Residency ID. Tourist cards get rejections or limited products.

Workaround rumours — fintech, foreign accounts — do not replace local SAR rails for long stays.

→ [Saudi banking expats](/guides/saudi-banking-expats/)

### Tax

0% Saudi personal income tax on employment-style income — but home-country tax residency may still tax worldwide income. US, UK, Germany, Australia, and Russia reporting rules all differ.

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Assume 0% KSA = 0% global | Home audit risk |
| Premium Residency without substance review | Treaty and certificate complexity |
| Invoice Saudi clients on tourist visa | Commercial law breach |

→ [Saudi tax expats zakat](/guides/saudi-tax-zakat-expats/)

### Zakat and business

Remote founders with a Saudi entity trigger corporate tax and zakat questions — nomad framing does not cover this.

### Step 1: Classify your income source

- **Saudi entity revenue** → iqama/MISA track
- **Capital-rich passive + remote** → Premium Residency modelling

### Step 2: Confirm programme status [VERIFY]
