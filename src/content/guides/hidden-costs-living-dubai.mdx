## What does the Dubai move-in month really cost?

Budget AED 45,000 to 60,000 all-in before move-in on a AED 180,000 per year lease in this market. First rent cheque, 5% agency, deposits, Ejari, DEWA near AED 2,000, and cooling registration typically stack in week one. Invest Gulf planning treats cash gate, not monthly rent, as the constraint.

Move-in cash for a Dubai lease at AED 180,000 per year typically requires AED 45,000 to 60,000 beyond the monthly rent headline before the first night in the apartment. Agency fees at 5% of annual rent equal AED 9,000 at signing, security deposits commonly take one to two months of rent, Ejari registration starts around AED 220, and the DEWA connection deposit sits near AED 2,000 with district cooling deposits of AED 500 to 2,000 in many towers. Unfurnished two-bedroom furniture budgets of AED 15,000 to 30,000 and school registration of AED 2,000 to 10,000 per child often hit the same calendar week as the first rent cheque. Invest Gulf relocation planning treats a 60-day liquid runway as the minimum buffer once cheques, deposits, and school lines stack, because refund timelines on DEWA deposits can stretch 30 to 90 days after move-out and do not fund week-one obligations.

Families who model only monthly rent arrive undercapitalised, the number one relocation failure mode we reviewed across Invest Gulf move files.

**Insider tip:** Ask the landlord for twelve months of DEWA plus chiller PDFs before you counter on rent. A AED 2,000 lower annual lease that hides AED 700 summer chiller months is not a saving.

## How much are Dubai agency and brokerage fees?

Residential rent agency fees for foreign buyers typically cost 5% of annual contract value, or AED 9,000 on a AED 180,000 lease, due at signing. Soft markets sometimes allow 2% to 3%, and invoices may add 5% VAT. Invest Gulf files confirm who pays before the first viewing.

- Clarify sole agent status in writing before the viewing
- Keep the invoice separate from the security deposit ledger
- Reject verbal "we'll split later" arrangements

## Why does Ejari block utilities and school enrollment?

Ejari registration in this market typically costs AED 220 or more, yet without it DEWA transfers stall, visa address proof weakens, and most KHDA schools block enrollment. Hotel slips of AED 800 to 1,500 per night follow delays. Our checklists treat Ejari as a hard dependency.

- DEWA will not transfer to tenant name cleanly
- Visa renewal may stall on address proof
- Most KHDA schools require Ejari certificate for enrollment
- Some banks update KYC only with registered tenancy

## Why is district cooling a silent Dubai budget killer?

District cooling in the area around Marina, JLT, Business Bay, and Downtown typically means chiller bills separate from DEWA, often AED 400 to 900 per month in summer. Winter bills understate August by about 100%. Invest Gulf reviews require twelve months of chiller PDFs before signing.

District cooling and school extras are the two Dubai cost lines that most often break a rent-only budget in summer and at term start. A two-bedroom Marina tower can show DEWA near AED 400 plus chiller near AED 700 in August, about AED 1,100 for utilities alone, while winter bills used for budgeting understate summer by 100 percent. School registration and assessment fees of AED 2,000 to 10,000 per child sit beside bus costs of AED 8,000 to 14,000 per year, uniforms of AED 1,500 to 3,000, and books or devices of AED 2,000 to 5,000 before exam-year spikes of AED 3,000 to 8,000. Our underwriting for family moves adds these lines to the lease model before signing, then stress-tests Salik school runs at AED 4 per gate across 22 weekdays, because a two-gate return trip already approaches AED 352 per month before parking of AED 200 to 800 in Marina or Downtown.

| Mistake | Cost impact |
| | |
| Using winter bill to budget summer | +100% underestimate |
| Ignoring chiller in "all-inclusive" marketing | AED 400-900/month surprise |
| Not asking previous tenant bill | Repeat their error |

## How do Salik and transport costs leak from a Dubai budget?

Salik in this market typically costs AED 4 per gate, so two gates and two daily trips across 22 weekdays already mean about AED 352 per month. Dual-parent corridors often reach AED 500 to 800 before parking. Invest Gulf commute sheets count gates on a Sunday trial.

- School run: 2 gates × 2 trips × 22 days = AED 352/month minimum
- Dual parents commuting different corridors: AED 500-800/month
- Serious camera fines: AED 600-3,000 per violation

| Option | 20ft container from UK | Buy in Dubai (IKEA + mid) |
| | | |
| Upfront | AED 25,000-45,000 | AED 25,000-40,000 |
| Timeline | 6-10 weeks | 1-2 weeks |
| Exit loss | Shipping back or sell | 30-50% resale loss |
| Best for | 5+ year assignment | 2-3 year assignment |

Add AED 3,000 to 8,000 for curtains, lamps, and kitchen basics, and villa gardens can need another AED 5,000 to 15,000. Sequence cash as visa, lease, DEWA, school deposit, car, then furniture so school debentures do not collide with the first rent cheque.
