## Who can sponsor a domestic worker visa?

Sponsoring a domestic worker visa in Dubai typically requires valid UAE residency, demonstrated income around AED 6,000 per month or higher, and adequate private accommodation for the worker. Tourists and visit-visa holders cannot sponsor. Invest Gulf clients in this market confirm salary certificates before starting MOHRE paperwork in the first 30 days after housing is settled.

**Residency status:** Valid UAE residency visa. Tourists, visit visa holders, and individuals without residency cannot sponsor domestic workers.

**Income threshold:** GDRFA expects financial capacity to support a domestic worker. The commonly applied informal minimum is AED 6,000/month in confirmed salary, shown via employment contract, salary certificate, or bank statements. Self-employed sponsors use trade licence documents and bank statements.

**Accommodation:** Adequate accommodation for the worker, typically a private room within the sponsor's residence. Inadequate housing can trigger MOHRE complaints if reported.

**Labour standing:** Employers with outstanding MOHRE violations or blacklisting may not sponsor new workers until cleared.

**Household ratio:** Guidelines limit domestic workers relative to household size. Typical families rarely hit the cap; large households requesting multiple workers may face review.

Property ownership is not required. Renters with valid residency and income evidence can sponsor.

Domestic worker sponsorship in Dubai for expat households in 2026 means proving UAE residency, income capacity near AED 6,000 per month or above, and a private room before MOHRE will register a contract that GDRFA can attach to an entry permit. First-issue government and medical costs typically land between AED 5,000 and AED 8,500 when Emirates ID at AED 370, medical tests at AED 300-700, and residency stamping at AED 500-1,200 are bundled with permit and contract fees. Invest Gulf relocation files show renters sponsor successfully when Ejari and salary certificates are current within 30 days. Overseas recruitment adds 8 to 16 weeks; an in-UAE transfer often finishes in 1 to 3 weeks. Property ownership is not required, but building rules on household size still matter before you sign a second live-in contract.

## How do overseas recruitment and UAE transfers compare?

Overseas recruitment versus UAE transfer typically means an 8 to 16 weeks timeline from abroad versus 1 to 3 weeks for an eligible in-country move. Entry permits often allow about 60 days to arrive. Our clients in this market prefer transfers when a NOC exists, because agency and source-country steps drop out of the critical path.

### Route 1: Hire from abroad

1. Contract a licensed recruitment agency in the source country (Philippines, Sri Lanka, India, Indonesia, Ethiopia, Kenya, and others).
2. Worker vetting and pre-screening in the source country.
3. Employment contract drafted and attested by the source country labour ministry or POLO equivalent.
4. MOHRE contract registration in UAE through smart services.
5. GDRFA entry permit application.
6. Medical screening (source country for some nationalities, UAE upon arrival for all).
7. Worker travels on entry permit (usually 60-day validity).
8. UAE medical fitness test at approved centre.
9. Emirates ID biometrics at ICA.
10. Residency visa stamping through GDRFA.
11. MOHRE domestic worker card issued.

Timeline: 8-16 weeks including source country processing.

### Route 2: Transfer from another UAE employer

Eligibility:

- Valid, unexpired domestic worker visa.
- Current employer NOC, or minimum service period met under MOHRE transfer rules.
- No active labour dispute on the worker file.

Steps: confirm GDRFA status, MOHRE transfer approval, new contract registration, sponsorship update, new Emirates ID if required, card update.

Timeline: 1-3 weeks, significantly faster than overseas recruitment.

## How do MOHRE contracts and GDRFA entry permits work?

MOHRE contract registration is required before GDRFA will issue an overseas entry permit, and oral agreements are not recognised for domestic workers in this market. Standard processing runs about 3 to 7 working days, with entry windows often near 60 days. Our checklists treat the contract ID as the document every later step references.

**MOHRE contract:** Registration generates a contract ID referenced in all GDRFA steps. Standard fields include salary (must meet UAE minimums for domestic workers), rest day, annual leave, and return flight entitlement.

**GDRFA entry permit (overseas hires):** Applied through GDRFA smart services or an authorised typing centre.

- Processing: 3-7 working days standard, express available for extra fee.
- Validity: typically 60 days; worker must enter UAE within the window.
- Delivery: electronic permit to employer and worker.

## What do medical fitness tests and Emirates ID cost?

Medical fitness testing for domestic workers typically costs AED 300-700 at a MOHRE-approved centre, with results in about 2 to 5 working days before Emirates ID biometrics. Emirates ID itself is about AED 370 for a standard 2-year card. Our clients in this market book medical only after the entry permit category is correct.

All domestic workers pass a UAE medical fitness test at a MOHRE-approved centre:

- Tuberculosis screening (chest X-ray).
- Communicable disease panel including HIV.
- Fitness for employment in UAE conditions.

Negative result issues the medical certificate required for Emirates ID and visa stamping. Positive results for certain conditions block residency; this is non-negotiable under current health policy.

Emirates ID biometrics are captured in person at ICA. Processing often 3-7 working days.

## How does residency visa stamping finish the file?

Residency visa stamping is the final passport step after medical and Emirates ID, and typically costs AED 500-1,200 depending on category and service charges in this market. Without the stamp, the worker cannot complete legal stay. Invest Gulf files treat stamping as a same-week task once biometrics clear, not a flexible 30 days buffer.

Stamping checklist:

- Medical certificate cleared
- Emirates ID biometrics completed
- GDRFA fee of about AED 500-1,200 paid
- Passport returned with residency stamp within about 7 days in routine files

Using a PRO typically costs AED 500-1,500 per domestic worker application and covers portal submissions, document checks, and service-centre attendance. First-time sponsors in this market often save 2 to 4 weeks versus self-filing errors. Our clients use PROs when parallel family visas already consume household bandwidth.

PRO fit checklist:

## What ongoing employer obligations apply after stamping?

Ongoing employer obligations typically include on-time WPS salary payment, continuous medical insurance, 30 calendar days annual leave after each completed year, and one full rest day per week. Lapsed insurance is a labour violation in this market. Invest Gulf reminders put renewal medical booking 30 to 60 days before visa expiry.

Monthly compliance list:

- WPS salary on the contract date
- Active medical insurance every day of the visa
- One rest day each week
- 30 calendar days leave after each completed year

**Monthly salary:** Pay on time through UAE Wage Protection System (WPS). Late payment triggers MOHRE alerts and fines.

**Medical insurance:** Valid insurance for the worker throughout the visa period.

**Annual leave and flights:** 30 calendar days annual leave after each completed year of service, plus return flight home when entitled under contract.

**Rest day:** One full rest day per week mandatory. Day is contractual (often Friday, but negotiable).

**Accommodation:** Safe private room with ventilation and privacy. Workers may file MOHRE complaints over substandard housing.

## How do domestic worker rights and disputes work?

Domestic worker rights in Dubai typically mean MOHRE mediates disputes, and either party may terminate with notice of about 30 to 60 days unless a material breach allows faster exit. End-of-service rules can apply on shorter service. Our clients in this market confirm the MOHRE calculator before any termination letter goes out.

## How do you cancel a domestic worker visa when leaving?

Visa cancellation is required before your own UAE residency ends, and failing to cancel leaves sponsor liability for overstay fines that can accrue for days or weeks. Cancel MOHRE first, then GDRFA, then settle salary. Invest Gulf exit checklists in this market put worker cancellation ahead of your own file by 7 days.

1. Cancel MOHRE employment contract.
2. Cancel GDRFA visa for the worker.
3. Settle final salary, leave, and ticket entitlements.

## What changes when you hire two workers or switch mid-contract?

Hiring two domestic workers typically requires higher demonstrated income, often AED 12,000-15,000+ per month, plus separate contracts and visas for each worker in this market. Early exits still follow notice periods unless MOHRE finds employer breach. Our underwriting flags studio leases plus two live-in workers as a building-rule risk before immigration filing.

## What common mistakes delay domestic worker files?

Common mistakes that delay domestic worker files typically include unattested civil documents, salary letters older than 30 days, entry permits that expire before travel, and name mismatches between passport MRZ and MOHRE contracts. Each error can add 1 to 3 weeks. Invest Gulf PRO packs in this market refresh salary certificates before every renewal cycle.

- Marriage or birth certificates not attested through the full chain before travel.
- Salary certificate showing total package but not basic pay where MOHRE asks for basic.
- Entry permit expiring before worker travels.
- Medical booked before entry permit category is correct.
- Name spelling mismatch between passport MRZ and MOHRE contract.

## How do nationality and bilateral labour agreements affect hiring?

Bilateral labour frameworks with the Philippines, Sri Lanka, India, Indonesia, Ethiopia, and Kenya typically shape which nationalities dominate Dubai domestic hiring in this market. Temporary restrictions can appear with little notice, so verify MOHRE nationality guidance before paying agency deposits. Our clients reconfirm corridor rules within 14 days of signing a recruitment invoice.

## Which practical scenarios should sponsors plan for?

Practical scenarios for Dubai domestic worker visas typically split into overseas hires of 8 to 16 weeks, in-UAE transfers of 1 to 3 weeks, and exit cancellations before the sponsor leaves. New families should secure housing first. We sequence in this market start the maid file only after primary residency is live.

**New expat family arriving:** Secure your employment visa and housing first, then start MOHRE contract while recruitment runs abroad. Do not book worker travel until entry permit PDF is issued.

**Transfer from another Dubai household:** Confirm NOC or eligibility, then run transfer route to save 6-10 weeks versus overseas hire.

**Leaving UAE:** Cancel worker visa before cancelling your own sponsorship to avoid fines.

## How do WPS salary rules and minimum pay work?

WPS salary rules require domestic worker pay through approved channels on the registered contract date, and underpaying blocks renewal while exposing sponsors to fines in this market. Minimum floors vary by nationality schedule each year. Invest Gulf points families to AED monthly salary bands before locking offers for a full 12 months contract cycle.

## How should renewals and absconding risk be handled?

Renewals typically start 30 to 60 days before visa expiry, because summer medical and Emirates ID queues in Dubai can add about 2 weeks. Absconding cases require MOHRE procedure, not passport retention, which is prohibited. Our clients in this market document communication attempts before assuming a file is closed.

## How do Ejari, tenancy, and household size affect sponsorship?

Ejari-registered tenancy is not always requested for domestic visa files, yet building rules on household size can still block two live-in workers in a studio even when GDRFA accepts papers in this market. Renters sponsor successfully with adequate rooms. Invest Gulf reviews lease capacity before a second AED contract is filed.

Tenancy checks before filing:

- Confirm bedroom count matches live-in headcount
- Ask building management about domestic worker rules
- Keep Ejari address aligned with GDRFA submissions
- Avoid studio-plus-two-worker plans for the first 12 months

## How should domestic worker visas coordinate with family visas?

Domestic worker sponsorship is separate from [spouse and children residence](/guides/moving-to-dubai-with-family/) and typically runs smoother after primary visas clear so PRO bandwidth is not split across too many parallel files in the same 4 to 8 weeks. We sequence in this market put the maid file after Emirates IDs for the core family.

## What document pack should sponsors prepare once?

A reusable sponsor document pack typically includes passport and visa copies, Emirates ID, a salary certificate fresher than 30 days, Ejari or tenancy, worker passport copy, and the signed MOHRE contract PDF. Refreshing the salary letter before renewal avoids the common six-month-old rejection. Invest Gulf PRO folders in this market keep digital and paper twins ready.

## What language and orientation expectations matter after arrival?

MOHRE contracts specify rest days, leave, and ticket entitlements in Arabic and English on standard templates, and disputes are judged against that registered text, not informal WhatsApp house rules. Walk through guest policies and child pickup permissions in the first 7 days. Our clients in this market separate legal contract clauses from daily house orientation on purpose.
