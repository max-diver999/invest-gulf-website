Dubai's off-plan market runs on two mechanisms that most buyers have heard of but few understand: escrow and Oqood. Together they form the regulatory backbone that separates Dubai's off-plan system from less regulated markets. When they work, buyer funds are protected and legal ownership is registered before a single brick is laid. When buyers skip verification, they discover the difference at the worst possible moment.

This guide explains how escrow and Oqood work in 2026, what each step costs, how to verify compliance, and what protection you actually have if something goes wrong.

## Why does off-plan need escrow and Oqood instead of a title deed?

Off-plan typically means you pay a developer for a unit that does not exist yet over a 2 to 4 year build cycle, so we treat RERA escrow and Oqood registration as the buyer-protection layer before title deed issuance Treat marketing claims as unverified until DLD records match.

In a secondary market purchase, you pay the seller, receive a Title Deed, and own the property. In off-plan, the gap between payment and delivery is where protection must live. Dubai's answer is mandatory escrow for funds and Oqood for DLD registration before construction completes.

## What does Oqood registration create for an off-plan buyer?

Oqood typically records your off-plan purchase with DLD before title deed issuance, triggers the one-time 4% transfer fee, and blocks double-sale of the same unit, so Invest Gulf verifies Dubai REST listing within 60 days Keep SPA, REST screenshots, and fee quotes in one folder Model the full cash timeline, not only the headline instalment.

Why Oqood matters:

- Creates an official DLD record of your purchase
- Prevents developer from selling the same unit twice
- Qualifies off-plan property for Golden Visa when registered value meets the threshold
- Required for any resale before handover (with developer NOC)
- Triggers the one-time DLD fee, not repeated at handover

How to verify: log into Dubai REST with Emirates ID or passport. Registered off-plan units appear under "My Properties." If your unit is not listed within 60 days of SPA signing, escalate with broker and developer immediately.

## How does RERA escrow protect off-plan buyer funds?

RERA escrow typically holds buyer deposits of 10% to 20% in a DLD-approved bank account and releases funds only against verified construction milestones, so Invest Gulf rejects any SWIFT to a corporate account outside escrow Confirm figures on official portals before you commit capital Written evidence beats broker summaries at every gate.

1. Sign SPA
2. Pay deposit (typically 10% to 20%) into escrow
3. Escrow agent confirms receipt
4. Developer requests milestone release after progress verification
5. Funds release only after confirmation
6. Repeat for each instalment on the payment plan

Escrow stops a developer from using your deposit on a different project or unrelated debts. The escrow agent must verify construction progress before any release.

RERA escrow planning for Dubai off-plan should treat every instalment as a verified release, not a developer invoice you pay on trust. A typical SPA deposit of 10% to 20% lands in the registered escrow IBAN within 7 days of signing, then later releases at months 6, 12, 18 and 24 only after surveyor confirmation. On an AED 1.5 million unit that means AED 150,000 to 300,000 protected on day one, with further percentages of 15% to 25% moving only when foundation, structure or fit-out milestones clear. Buyers who accept a corporate account "for speed" leave DLD protection even if Oqood later shows their name. Keep the escrow letter, SWIFT confirmations and Dubai REST screenshots aligned before each transfer, because mismatch between SPA price and escrow receipt is a common Oqood delay of 7 to 14 days.

## When is the 4% DLD fee paid on Oqood registration?

The 4% DLD transfer fee is typically due at Oqood registration on the SPA purchase price, not at handover, so we budget AED 60,000 on an AED 1.5 million unit plus about AED 1,020 admin Re-check thresholds if your assignment starts more than 90 days out.

Some developers offer a "DLD fee waiver" as a sales incentive. That means the developer pays the 4% on your behalf; it does not mean Oqood registration is skipped. Always confirm Oqood is registered regardless of who pays the fee.
