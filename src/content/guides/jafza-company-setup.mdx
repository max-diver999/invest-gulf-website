## JAFZA at a Glance

Jebel Ali Free Zone Authority (JAFZA) is one of the largest and oldest free zones in the Middle East, built around Jebel Ali Port and the industrial corridor southwest of Dubai. Where DMCC offers JLT desk prestige and IFZA offers digital low-cost packages, JAFZA offers **port-adjacent logistics**, warehouse leases, and visa quotas scaled to real estate footprint.

JAFZA hosts thousands of trading, manufacturing, and logistics companies that import, store, re-export, or distribute physical goods across the GCC, Africa, and Asia. If your business model includes containers, bonded storage, or fleet operations, JAFZA is usually the first Dubai free zone on the shortlist.

Why trading and logistics founders choose JAFZA:

- **Jebel Ali Port access:** customs, re-export, and maritime logistics cluster
- **Warehouse and industrial plots:** scalable space for inventory and assembly
- **Visa quota tied to facility size:** plan headcount alongside square metres
- **100% foreign ownership:** standard for JAFZA establishments and companies
- **Established trade finance ecosystem:** banks understand import/export KYC
- **Long operating history:** counterparties recognise JAFZA on invoices and LC documents

JAFZA is rarely the best fit for solo consultants, DFSA-regulated finance, or e-commerce sellers with no UAE stock. Compare [IFZA company setup](/guides/ifza-company-setup/) for low-cost digital trading and [DMCC company setup](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/) for JLT services and commodities.

## JAFZA Activity Categories: What You Can Register

| Category | Example Activities | Facility Need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Trading | Import/export of approved goods | Office or warehouse |
| Logistics | Freight forwarding, 3PL coordination | Office + warehouse common |
| Industrial / Manufacturing | Assembly, packaging, light industry | Industrial plot or warehouse |
| Automotive | Spare parts trading and distribution | Warehouse |
| FMCG Distribution | Food and consumer goods (compliance-heavy) | Temperature-controlled warehouse |
| E-Commerce with UAE stock | Fulfilment from Jebel Ali | Warehouse |
| Professional Services | Consulting supporting trade operations | Flexi-office |

Regulated activities (financial services, certain healthcare, hazardous materials) require additional approvals. Financial services requiring DFSA regulation belong in [DIFC](/guides/difc-company-setup/) or ADGM, not a standard JAFZA trading licence.

The DMCC guide already notes JAFZA's warehouse advantage. This table expands the decision framework for 2026 founders.

| Feature | JAFZA | DMCC |

**Rule of thumb:** if goods move in containers through Jebel Ali, start with JAFZA. If goods are precious metals, tea, coffee, or energy paper traded on DMCC infrastructure, start with DMCC. If goods never touch UAE soil, consider IFZA instead.

## JAFZA Company Types

**Free Zone Establishment (FZE):** single shareholder. Common for owner-operated trading houses.

**Free Zone Company (FZCo):** multiple shareholders. Used for joint ventures and investor groups.

**Branch of a Local or Foreign Company:** extension of an existing entity. Parent retains liability.

Branch and company structures both require a registered facility address inside JAFZA unless using an approved flexi-office tier for service activities.

## How JAFZA Visa Quotas Work (Office and Warehouse)

JAFZA does **not** typically sell flat visa bundles like DMCC flexi-desks. Visa entitlement derives from your **lease area** and **activity category**.

Planning framework founders use with JAFZA consultants:

| Facility type | Indicative sqm | Typical visa range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flexi-office / business desk | Shared desk | 1-3 | Service activities only |
| Small office | 50-100 sqm | 5-10 | Confirm with lease |
| Standard office | 100-200 sqm | 10-20 | Quota scales with sqm |
| Warehouse | 200-500 sqm | 15-40 | Activity-dependent |
| Large industrial | 500+ sqm | 40+ | Custom quota calculation |

JAFZA immigration calculates allowed visas when your lease is registered. You cannot sponsor employees beyond the approved quota without expanding facility space or upgrading lease category. Always obtain **written visa quota** on the lease summary before payment.

Contrast with DMCC: a standard flexi-desk often includes **three visas** without dedicated square metres. JAFZA trades lower land cost per visa for industrial users but offers fewer visas on entry-level service packages.

## Step-by-Step JAFZA Registration Process

### Step 1: Define Activity and Facility Requirements

Map your supply chain: Do you need bonded warehouse, cold storage, or office-only? Pick the JAFZA activity code that matches customs declarations you will file. Trading without storage uses office tiers; inventory in UAE requires warehouse lease quotes early.

### Step 2: Secure Office or Warehouse Lease

JAFZA licence issuance ties to a registered facility. Options include:

- **Flexi-office / business centre:** AED 15,000-25,000 per year, limited visas, suited to service and light trading
- **Serviced office:** AED 40,000-80,000 per year, moderate visa quota
- **Warehouse:** AED 80,000-250,000+ per year depending on sqm, racking, and power
- **Industrial land / built unit:** custom pricing for manufacturing

Sign the lease or LOA and obtain landlord documents for JAFZA filing.

### Step 3: Reserve Company Name

Follow UAE naming rules. Avoid restricted words and conflicts with existing JAFZA or mainland entities. Approval typically within 1-2 business days.

### Step 4: Submit Shareholder KYC and Application

Individual shareholders provide:

- Passport copy (six months validity)
- Proof of address under three months
- Bank reference or source of funds (trading activities)
- CV or trade experience summary

Corporate shareholders add incorporation certificates, good standing, board resolution, and full UBO disclosure.

### Step 5: Pay JAFZA Fees and Pass Compliance

JAFZA reviews AML, UBO, and lease alignment. Mismatch between stated trading volume and tiny flexi-office footprint triggers questions. Pay licence, registration, and facility-related fees upon approval in principle.

### Step 6: Collect Licence and Corporate Documents

You receive trade licence, incorporation certificate, MOA, and share certificates. Register for customs codes if you will import/export physically through Jebel Ali.

### Step 7: Open Bank Account and Sponsor Visas

Use licence and lease pack for corporate banking. Trade-finance banks ask for supplier and buyer contracts, expected shipment volumes, and HS codes. Allow **2-6 weeks** for accounts.

Activate establishment card, then process visas up to leased quota. See [opening a bank account in Dubai](/guides/open-bank-account-dubai/) for general banking steps.

## Port, Customs, and Logistics Advantages

JAFZA sits adjacent to Jebel Ali Port, the largest container port in the Middle East. Licensed traders benefit from:

- Shorter drayage from port to bonded warehouse
- Established customs broker ecosystem inside the free zone
- Re-export workflows without mainland duty for goods leaving UAE
- Cluster of freight forwarders, packers, and inspection services

A self-contained planning block for logistics founders:

JAFZA trading companies that import for re-export should register appropriate customs codes and align licence activity with bill of lading descriptions. Mismatch between licence, warehouse type, and declared HS codes delays clearance and bank trade finance approval. Budget for a licensed customs broker in year one even if you self-file later. DMCC cannot offer equivalent port-proximate warehouse scale, which is why the DMCC FAQ directs physical traders to JAFZA.

## Tax Position for JAFZA Companies

UAE corporate tax applies from June 2023. JAFZA entities may pursue **Qualifying Free Zone Person** status for 0% on Qualifying Income when substance and de minimis tests are met. Non-qualifying mainland revenue remains exposed to 9% tax.

Trading companies often mix:

- Qualifying free zone to free zone sales
- Qualifying foreign export revenue
- Non-qualifying mainland B2B or retail income

Model each stream before year-end. Transfer pricing applies to related-party imports between your JAFZA entity and mainland affiliates.

Full planning context: [UAE corporate tax for expats](/guides/uae-corporate-tax-expats/).

## Banking and Trade Finance for JAFZA Entities

JAFZA companies open standard corporate accounts and, with track record, letter-of-credit and trade finance lines. Banks expect:

- Trade licence matching import/export activity
- Warehouse or office lease proof
- Three to six months of projected shipment plan
- Counterparty contracts or purchase orders
- Clean shareholder source-of-funds narrative

Emirates NBD, Mashreq, ADCB, and RAK Bank actively serve JAFZA traders. Allow **2-6 weeks** for first account; trade finance facilities follow after operating history.

## JAFZA Setup Risks and Pre-Commit Checklist

- **Lease before quota:** Signing licence expectations before lease confirmation locks wrong visa count
- **Undersized warehouse:** Cheap small unit that blocks inventory growth mid-year
- **Activity mismatch:** General trading licence with manufacturing equipment on site
- **Mainland sales tax:** UAE client revenue may break QFZP assumptions
- **Customs compliance:** Missing importer code delays first shipment
- **DMCC comparison:** Paying JAFZA warehouse rates without containers is wasted capex

## Is JAFZA Right for Your Business?

**JAFZA is the right choice when:**

- Goods physically enter and leave through Jebel Ali
- You need warehouse, industrial, or large visa counts tied to space
- You run 3PL, distribution, or import/export trading
- Trade finance and customs documentation are core operations
- DMCC flexi-desk visa bundles are too small for your warehouse headcount plan

**JAFZA is likely not the right choice when:**

- You sell digital services or remote consulting only
- You need JLT address for client meetings (consider DMCC)
- Budget is under AED 20,000 year one (consider IFZA)
- You trade commodities on DMCC infrastructure (consider DMCC)
- You need DFSA-regulated financial services (consider DIFC)

## Summary

JAFZA remains the default Dubai free zone for **port-linked trading, warehousing, and industrial operations**. Lean service setups start around **AED 20,000-35,000** year one; warehouse operations scale into six figures when lease and visa quota grow. Visas follow **office and warehouse square metres**, unlike DMCC flexi-desk bundles. Registration typically takes **7-15 business days** once lease and KYC are complete.

Pair JAFZA logistics with DMCC or IFZA only if you deliberately split operating and holding entities. For most founders, one zone should match the primary revenue stream. Compare [DMCC company setup](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/) when JLT presence or commodities matter more than containers.
