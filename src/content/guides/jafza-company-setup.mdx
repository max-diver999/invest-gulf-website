## What is JAFZA and who should use it?

JAFZA hosts thousands of trading, manufacturing, and logistics companies that import, store, re-export, or distribute physical goods across the GCC, Africa, and Asia. If your business model includes containers, bonded storage, or fleet operations, JAFZA is usually the first Dubai free zone on the shortlist.

Why trading and logistics founders choose JAFZA:

- Jebel Ali Port access: customs, re-export, and maritime logistics cluster
- Warehouse and industrial plots: scalable space for inventory and assembly
- Visa quota tied to facility size: plan headcount alongside square metres
- 100% foreign ownership: standard for JAFZA establishments and companies
- Established trade finance ecosystem: banks understand import/export KYC
- Long operating history: counterparties recognise JAFZA on invoices and LC documents

JAFZA is rarely the best fit for solo consultants, DFSA-regulated finance, or e-commerce sellers with no UAE stock. Compare [IFZA company setup](/guides/ifza-company-setup/) for low-cost digital trading and [DMCC company setup](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/) for JLT services and commodities.

## Which business activities fit JAFZA best?

- **Timeline:** 100% typical trustee turnaround when docs are pre-certified.

Regulated activities (financial services, certain healthcare, hazardous materials) require additional approvals. Financial services requiring DFSA regulation belong in [DIFC](/guides/difc-company-setup/) or ADGM, not a standard JAFZA trading licence.

## When should you choose JAFZA over DMCC or IFZA?

Rule of thumb: if goods move in containers through Jebel Ali, start with JAFZA. If goods are precious metals, tea, coffee, or energy paper traded on DMCC infrastructure, start with DMCC. If goods never touch UAE soil, consider IFZA instead.

For mainland access rules shared by both zones, see [UAE free zone vs mainland](/guides/uae-free-zone-vs-mainland/).

## How do JAFZA visa quotas link to office and warehouse space?

JAFZA immigration calculates allowed visas when your lease is registered. You cannot sponsor employees beyond the approved quota without expanding facility space or upgrading lease category. Always obtain written visa quota on the lease summary before payment.

Contrast with DMCC: a standard flexi-desk often includes **three visas** without dedicated square metres. JAFZA trades lower land cost per visa for industrial users but offers fewer visas on entry-level service packages.
## How long does JAFZA registration take?

Standard service and trading licences with existing flexi-office contracts often complete in 7-15 business days after JAFZA receives full documentation. Warehouse and industrial setups depend on landlord NOCs and fit-out inspections, add 2-4 weeks.

## What are the JAFZA setup steps?

JAFZA licence issuance ties to a registered facility. Options include:

- Flexi-office / business centre: AED 15,000-25,000 per year, limited visas, suited to service and light trading
- Serviced office: AED 40,000-80,000 per year, moderate visa quota
- **Warehouse:** AED 80,000-250,000+ per year depending on sqm, racking, and power
- Industrial land / built unit: custom pricing for manufacturing

Sign the lease or LOA and obtain landlord documents for JAFZA filing.

### Step 3: Reserve Company Name

Follow UAE naming rules. Avoid restricted words and conflicts with existing JAFZA or mainland entities. Approval typically within 1-2 business days.

### Step 4: Submit Shareholder KYC and Application

Individual shareholders provide:

- Passport copy (six months validity)
- Proof of address under three months
- Bank reference or source of funds (trading activities)
- CV or trade experience summary

Corporate shareholders add incorporation certificates, good standing, board resolution, and full UBO disclosure.

### Step 5: Pay JAFZA Fees and Pass Compliance

JAFZA reviews AML, UBO, and lease alignment. Mismatch between stated trading volume and tiny flexi-office footprint triggers questions. Pay licence, registration, and facility-related fees upon approval in principle.

### Step 6: Collect Licence and Corporate Documents

You receive trade licence, incorporation certificate, MOA, and share certificates. Register for customs codes if you will import/export physically through Jebel Ali.

### Step 7: Open Bank Account and Sponsor Visas

Use licence and lease pack for corporate banking. Trade-finance banks ask for supplier and buyer contracts, expected shipment volumes, and HS codes. Allow **2-6 weeks** for accounts.

Activate establishment card, then process visas up to leased quota. See [opening a bank account in Dubai](/guides/open-bank-account-dubai/) for general banking steps.

## What port and customs advantages does JAFZA offer?

JAFZA trading companies that import for re-export should register appropriate customs codes and align licence activity with bill of lading descriptions. Mismatch between licence, warehouse type, and declared HS codes delays clearance and bank trade finance approval. Budget for a licensed customs broker in year one even if you self-file later. DMCC cannot offer equivalent port-proximate warehouse scale, which is why the DMCC FAQ directs physical traders to JAFZA.

## Do JAFZA companies pay UAE corporate tax?

Model each stream before year-end. Transfer pricing applies to related-party imports between your JAFZA entity and mainland affiliates.
