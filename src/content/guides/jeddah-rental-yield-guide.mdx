## What gross yields is Jeddah printing in 2026?

Answer first: mid-market apartments in REGA-designated districts most often land **5% to 6.5% gross** on asking rent, while premium Corniche towers trade **4% to 5.5% gross** for stronger tenant credit. Net yields after management, vacancy, and community charges typically sit **1.5 to 2 points below gross** unless the building is fully stabilised.

| District type | Gross (apt) | Tenant driver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Obhur / north coast | 5.5 to 6.5% | Tourism, corporate housing |
| Al Salamah (mid) | 5 to 6.5% | Mixed expat/local |
| Corniche premium | 4 to 5.5% | Senior exec, stability |
| New master plans | 4.5 to 6% | Price rises faster than rent |

Confirm zone classification on the title bundle before you trust a broker yield sheet. Non-designated marketing is the fastest way to model rent you cannot legally collect.

## Which districts fit a yield-first budget?

**Al Salamah** refurbished mid-rise stock can be yield-friendly when acquisition price avoids over-renovated premiums. **Corniche towers** print lower gross but attract longer corporate leases. **Obhur** mixes villa and apartment demand tied to Red Sea corporate and leisure traffic.

Worked net example (illustrative): SAR 700,000 purchase, SAR 44,000 annual rent, SAR 11,000 combined management, vacancy reserve, and community charges → **SAR 33,000 net (about 4.7%)**. Stress-test the same unit at **10% lower rent** and **one extra void month** before you compare against [Dubai vs Saudi rental yield](/compare/dubai-vs-saudi-rental-yield/).

See [Jeddah property investment](/areas/jeddah-property-investment/) for zone context and [Riyadh property investment](/guides/riyadh-property-investment/) for the government-tenant contrast inside Saudi.

## How does REGA foreign ownership affect rent collection?

Foreign investors may collect rent only where **Law M/14 designated zones** and REGA registration allow foreign ownership. Your checklist should include: title eligibility letter, Ejar-ready lease template, bank repatriation path for rent, and property management contract licensed for your building type.

| Step | Why it matters for yield |
| --- | --- |
| Zone confirmation | Blocks illegal foreign marketing |
| Ejar registration | Supports enforceable lease terms |
| PM contract | Converts gross marketing into collected rent |
| Tax and Zakat advice | Home-country reporting varies by structure |

This is a Saudi **REGA and Ejar** path, not a UAE trustee workflow. Keep Dubai fee tables on [Dubai rental yield](/guides/dubai-rental-yield-guide/) when you compare emirates, not when you close Jeddah.

## How do you stack gross to net in SAR?

Answer first: budget **8% to 12%** of gross rent for professional management, **one month** of vacancy in year one on new stock, and **community or HOA charges** that vary by master developer. Agency lease-up fees often run **2% to 5%** of the first year rent on new tenancies.

| Cost line | Typical Jeddah range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property management | 8 to 12% of rent | Verify VAT and renewal fees |
| Vacancy reserve | 4 to 8% of rent | Higher in new districts |
| Community charges | Project-specific | Ask for last 12 months invoices |
| Maintenance reserve | 2 to 3% of rent | Older coastal stock needs more |

Model exit separately: resale marketing often runs **3 to 9 months**, longer than Dubai Marina for comparable price points. Cross-read [Saudi rental yield](/guides/saudi-rental-yield-guide/) when you size a multi-city GCC portfolio.

## Who are the tenants and how long do they stay?

Corporate families and Red Sea project staff often take **12 to 24 month** leases. Tourism-linked pockets can show seasonality around school calendars and religious travel peaks; unless short-term use is explicitly legal for your unit, underwrite a **long-let baseline**.

Furnished units can command premium rent but add capex and churn. Unfurnished stock suits corporate tenants bringing their own shipment. Villa segments in Obhur may need separate pool and garden maintenance lines in the net model.

## What Vision 2030 demand is real versus brochure?

Logistics, hospitality, and Red Sea giga-project hiring support **corporate lease depth** in Jeddah more than greenfield cities with immature schools. Yield rises when **rent growth lags price growth** in new master plans; entry cap rate looks high until stabilised occupancy proves out over **18 to 36 months**.

## How liquid is Jeddah resale versus Dubai?

Jeddah is **more liquid than frontier Saudi cities** but **slower than Dubai** on average. Price your hold period honestly: a **0.5% net yield edge** rarely compensates for a **6 month** longer exit if you need capital back for another deal.

## What risks should you stress-test before you commit?

Run three scenarios on the same spreadsheet: base case at asking rent with four-week void, downside at **10% lower rent** and eight-week void, upside with modest rent growth and two-week void. If Jeddah net beats Dubai by less than **0.5%** after those cuts, default to the market with faster resale unless you have a multi-year Saudi operating reason to stay local.

Archive broker rent comps in writing. Walk away when the seller cannot produce **Ejar history**, **community charge invoices**, or **title eligibility** for foreign buyers in the zone marketed.

## What checklist runs before you sign?

1. REGA zone and foreign eligibility confirmed with counsel.
2. Gross-to-net model includes PM, vacancy, community charges, and lease-up fees.
3. Tenant profile matches lease length you modeled.
4. Bank repatriation path confirmed for rent flows.
5. Exit timeline priced at 3 to 9 months unless you have a local broker track record on the exact building.
6. Sensitivity rows for lower rent and longer void included.
7. Comparison to [Dubai vs Saudi rental yield](/compare/dubai-vs-saudi-rental-yield/) completed on net, not gross alone.

## How does this fit a wider Gulf portfolio?

Jeddah suits investors who want **Saudi Red Sea exposure** with documented REGA ownership and can hold through stabilisation. Dubai still wins many **pure cash-flow** mandates on depth of tenants and resale. Mixed portfolios often keep Dubai core liquidity and add Jeddah size only when the net spread survives stress tests.

## How do corporate leases differ from retail tenants in Jeddah?

Answer first: **corporate-backed leases** often run **24 months** with fewer void weeks, while small retail tenants may negotiate harder on fit-out contributions. Underwrite **two tenant types** on the same unit: a corporate family at slightly lower gross but faster collection, versus a self-employed tenant at higher gross but more payment follow-up.

| Lease feature | Corporate | Individual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Term | 12 to 24 months | 6 to 12 months common |
| Fit-out ask | Lower | Higher |
| Payment track | Payroll-linked | Bank transfer discipline varies |

Ask the broker for **Ejar history** on the last two tenants before you trust a gross yield quote.

## How do Hajj and Umrah seasons affect coastal voids?

Religious travel peaks can increase **short-stay demand** in some coastal pockets while reducing **family occupancy** in others. Unless your unit is legally cleared for the stay type you model, keep **long-let assumptions** in the base case and treat seasonal upside as optional. Discuss peak-week noise with building management before you buy a "tourism" narrative.

## What broker questions should you ask before a Jeddah offer?

Request **title eligibility for foreign buyers**, **12 months of community charge invoices**, **three independent rent comps**, and **property management quotes in writing**. If the seller will not share Ejar or REGA documentation within ten business days, pause the deal. Cross-check the same questions on [Jeddah property investment](/areas/jeddah-property-investment/) area pages and [Saudi rental yield](/guides/saudi-rental-yield-guide/) hub guidance.

## How should you track assumptions over a multi-year hold?

Keep a **dated spreadsheet footer** with rent, void, PM rate, and community charges. Refresh after every **material rent change** in comparable buildings. Re-run net yield when any input moves more than **half a percentage point**. Archive email trails with brokers; they become lender and partner evidence if you refinance or sell.

## What water and utility costs change coastal net yield?

Coastal towers may bundle **chiller or water** differently than inland stock. Ask for **last 12 months utility bills** from the seller or tenant before you underwrite net. Master communities sometimes shift **HOA lines** after handover; model a **5% HOA step-up** in year three as a conservative stress row. Pair utility diligence with [Jeddah cost of living](/guides/jeddah-cost-of-living/) if you relocate while managing the asset.

## What due diligence timeline should you run before transfer?

Week one: zone and title eligibility. Week two: rent comps and PM quotes. Week three: gross-to-net model with stress rows. Week four: offer only if Ejar history and community charges check out. Add five business days for **Shariah-compliant finance** approvals if you leverage, because bank panels re-price net yield after debt service.

Hold assumptions for at least **five years** when you buy in master plans where supply is still ramping; early resale often meets thin buyer pools even when gross yield looked attractive in year one.

If two buildings show the same gross yield, pick the one with **shorter void history on Ejar** even when the prettier tower asks for a premium. Collected rent beats brochure yield every time.

## How to underwrite Jeddah rents without brochure math

Use REGA-zone eligibility, tenant employer mix, and humidity capex before you accept a gross yield screenshot. Port, tourism, and Hajj seasonality can move short-stay demand; long-stay corporate leases need different vacancy assumptions.

| Diligence item | Why it moves net yield |
| --- | --- |
| Zone and deed type | Wrong zone kills the thesis (confirm current official rules) |
| Service charge and facade reserve | Salt air raises opex vs inland stock |
| Lease length and deposit norms | Affects cash flow timing |
| Comparable rents on same street | Towers two blocks apart are not comps |
| Exit liquidity | Thin resale needs longer hold |

## Corniche vs inland yield framing

Waterfront asking rents can look higher while net falls after maintenance and longer void risk. Inland family compounds may post steadier annual occupancy with lower headline rent. Compare both with [Jeddah property investment](/areas/jeddah-property-investment/) and lifestyle notes in [living on Jeddah Corniche](/guides/living-jeddah-corniche/).

## Offer checklist

Verify developer or landlord registration trails, ask for three years of rent history where available, and separate Premium Residency marketing from the SPA. Hedge all legal thresholds with current official rules before deposit.
