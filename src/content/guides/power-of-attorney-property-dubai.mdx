Quick answer: Dubai Power of Attorney enables remote property purchase with 68% of transactions involving foreign nationals. POA must be notarized in home country, attested by UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and translated to Arabic. Costs range AED 2,000-5,000 for preparation. POA holders can sign SPAs, register Oqood, and complete DLD transfers, but Golden Visa applications still require personal UAE presence.

Buying Dubai property from abroad is standard practice. Over 68% of transactions involve foreign nationals, and a significant share complete the purchase without ever visiting Dubai until handover. The mechanism that makes this possible is the Power of Attorney (POA), a notarised legal document authorising a named agent to act on your behalf at DLD, the developer's office, and the registration trustee centre.

This guide explains how POA works for Dubai property in 2026: what it covers, how to prepare it, what it costs, and the risks that remote buyers underestimate.

## When You Need a Power of Attorney

| Scenario | POA needed? | What POA covers |
|---|---|---|
| Remote off-plan purchase | Yes | SPA signing, escrow payments, Oqood registration |
| Remote secondary market purchase | Yes | SPA, NOC collection, DLD transfer at trustee |
| In-person purchase | No | You sign directly |
| Golden Visa application | No, personal attendance required | POA does not substitute for biometrics |
| Mortgage application | Usually yes for remote | Bank may require personal attendance (confirm current official rules) |
| Handover and snagging | Optional | POA holder can attend on your behalf |
| Ejari registration | Optional | POA holder can register lease |

## Preparing a Dubai-Compliant POA

Step 1, Draft a property-specific POA

A general POA ("my agent can do anything") is often rejected by DLD and developers. Your POA should specify:

- Your full legal name and passport details
- The agent's full legal name and Emirates ID or passport
- The specific property (project name, unit number, or "any property in Dubai freehold zones")
- Enumerated powers: sign SPA, pay into escrow, register Oqood, attend DLD transfer, collect keys
- Expiry date (typically 1 to 2 years)
- Revocation clause

Have the POA drafted by a UAE-licensed property lawyer or notary, not a generic template from the internet.

Step 2, Notarise in your home country

The POA must be notarised by a notary public in your country of residence. If you are a UK resident, a UK notary. If US-based, a US notary.

Step 3, Attest at UAE Embassy

Take the notarised POA to the UAE Embassy or Consulate in your country for attestation. This confirms the notary's authority.

Step 4, UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs attestation

Upon arrival in UAE (or via courier to a UAE-based agent), the POA requires MOFA attestation. Budget AED 500 to 1,500.

Step 5, Arabic translation

If the POA is in English, a certified Arabic translation is required for DLD and developer acceptance. Budget AED 300 to 800.

Step 6, DLD registration (optional but recommended)

Registering the POA with DLD provides additional verification. Your agent can do this at a trustee centre.

## Who Should Hold Your POA

| POA holder type | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Licensed UAE broker | Knows DLD process, developer relationships | Commission incentive, review SPA independently |
| UAE property lawyer | Independent, protects your legal interests | Higher cost (AED 5,000 to 15,000) |
| Trusted family member in UAE | Personal trust | May lack DLD process knowledge |
| Property management company | Handles handover and Ejari | Conflict if they also manage competing units |

Best practice: Use a licensed lawyer as POA holder for SPA signing. Use your broker as POA holder for DLD transfer and handover only. Never give unrestricted financial POA to a party with a sales commission.

## What POA Cannot Do

- Apply for UAE Golden Visa (personal attendance required)
- Open a UAE bank account in your name (bank KYC requires personal presence in most cases)
- Guarantee rental income or property performance
- Override DLD regulations or developer escrow requirements
- Sign a mortgage agreement without bank-specific POA provisions

## POA for Off-Plan vs Secondary Market

| Transaction type | POA powers needed | Additional requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Off-plan SPA signing | Sign SPA, pay escrow, register Oqood | Developer may require original passport copy |
| Off-plan instalment payments | Authorise escrow wire transfers | Payment schedule per SPA |
| Secondary market SPA | Sign SPA, collect NOC | Seller NOC from developer |
| DLD transfer (ready) | Attend trustee centre, sign transfer | Banker's draft or confirmed wire |
| Handover | Accept keys, sign snag list | Snagging company attendance recommended |
| Ejari registration | Sign lease on owner's behalf | Tenant KYC documents |

Off-plan POA is more common because the timeline spans years. Secondary market POA is typically a single trustee centre visit.

## Timeline: POA Preparation from Abroad

| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Engage UAE lawyer to draft property-specific POA |
| 1 to 2 | Review and approve POA text |
| 2 | Notarise in home country |
| 2 to 3 | UAE Embassy attestation |
| 3 to 4 | Courier to UAE agent for MOFA attestation |
| 4 | Arabic translation and DLD registration |
| 4+ | POA active, SPA signing can proceed |

Allow 4 weeks minimum. Rush attestation services exist but cost more. Do not sign SPA before POA is fully attested and accepted by your target developer.

## Revoking a Power of Attorney

If your relationship with the POA holder breaks down mid-transaction:

1. Draft a revocation deed (UAE notary or home country notary)
2. Attest revocation at UAE Embassy and MOFA
3. Notify DLD, developer, and escrow bank in writing
4. Appoint a new POA holder if transaction must continue

Revocation during an active SPA can delay the transaction. Choose your POA holder carefully at the outset.

## Red Flags

1. General POA without property-specific powers: DLD or developer may reject
2. **POA holder who is also the selling broker without independent legal review: conflict of interest
3. Payments directed outside RERA-registered escrow: POA does not legitimise non-escrow payments
4. No revocation mechanism**: if the relationship breaks down, you need a clear revocation path
5. Skipping MOFA attestation: unattested POAs are not accepted at DLD

## Document Checklist for POA Holder

Your POA holder should maintain:

- Original attested POA (plus copies)
- Your passport copy (certified)
- SPA copies (signed and unsigned versions)
- Escrow account details verified on Dubai REST
- Snagging reports and correspondence
- All payment receipts and wire confirmations
- Developer and broker contact details
- Your written instructions for each action (email trail)

A organised POA holder prevents delays at DLD and developer offices. Disorganised POA holders are the primary cause of missed payment deadlines and expired reservation periods.

## POA and Golden Visa: What Requires Personal Attendance

| Action | POA sufficient? |
|---|---|
| SPA signing | Yes |
| Oqood registration | Yes |
| DLD title transfer | Yes |
| Handover and snagging | Yes |
| Ejari registration | Yes |
| Golden Visa biometrics | No, personal attendance |
| Emirates ID issuance | No, personal attendance |
| Medical fitness test | No, personal attendance |
| UAE bank account opening | Usually no |

Plan one UAE trip of 5 to 10 working days for Golden Visa after property registration is complete. POA covers everything before that trip.

Some developers reject POAs from certain jurisdictions or require additional legalisation steps. A 30-minute confirmation call prevents a 4-week delay.

## Advanced POA structures for complex transactions

Sophisticated property transactions may require specialized POA arrangements:

### Multiple property transactions
When purchasing multiple units or developing a property portfolio:

Master POA approach:
- Single POA covering "any freehold property in Dubai"
- Broader authority for portfolio decisions
- 2-year validity recommended for multi-transaction timeline
- Higher scrutiny from DLD, ensure very precise legal drafting

Property-specific POAs:
- Individual POA for each transaction
- Lower risk of authority disputes
- Easier revocation if one transaction fails
- Higher preparation cost but cleaner legal structure

### Corporate vs individual POA holders
UAE Company as POA holder:
- Advantages: Continuity if individual employees change, institutional accountability
- Requirements: UAE trade license, authorized signatory documentation
- Costs: AED 3,000-8,000 additional for corporate verification
- Best for: Large portfolio investors, institutional buyers

Individual lawyer/broker as POA holder:
- Advantages: Personal accountability, direct communication
- Requirements: UAE practicing license verification
- Costs: Lower setup cost, but potential for conflict of interest
- Best for: Single property purchases, hands-on investors

## Country-specific POA preparation requirements

Different home countries have varying notarization and attestation requirements:

### United Kingdom
Notarization requirements:
- Must use FCO-approved notary public (not just any UK solicitor)
- Apostille under Hague Convention (replaces UAE embassy attestation for UK)
- Arabic translation still required in UAE
- Timeline: 2-3 weeks typical
- Cost: £150-400 for notarization + £30 apostille

Common UK-specific issues:
- Scottish law differences for POA validity
- Northern Ireland separate apostille process
- Brexit-related documentation changes (post-2020)

### United States
Notarization requirements:
- State-level notary public (requirements vary by state)
- State authentication (Secretary of State)
- UAE Embassy attestation in Washington D.C.
- Timeline: 3-4 weeks with mail processing
- Cost: $50-200 notarization + $50-150 authentication + $200-300 embassy

State-specific considerations:
- California, New York, Texas have streamlined processes
- Some states require additional county-level authentication
- Remote online notarization (RON) acceptance varies

### Canada
Notarization requirements:
- Provincial notary public or lawyer
- Provincial authentication (varies by province)
- UAE Embassy attestation in Ottawa
- Timeline: 3-4 weeks
- Cost: CAD 100-300 notarization + CAD 75 authentication + CAD 200 embassy

### India
Notarization requirements:
- Notary public appointment by state government
- State Home Department attestation
- Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) attestation
- UAE Embassy attestation in New Delhi/Mumbai/Chennai
- **Timeline:** 4-6 weeks (longer in smaller cities)
- **Cost:** INR 2,000-8,000 depending on location and expedited service

Different purchase scenarios require tailored POA authority:

**SPA signing authority:**
- Specific project and unit number identification
- Authority to agree to developer amendments
- Payment schedule modification authority (within defined limits)
- Authority to accept handover delays within reasonable parameters

**Escrow and payment authority:**
- Bank account designation for escrow payments
- Authority to make installment payments per agreed schedule
- Wire transfer authorization (specific banking procedures)
- Authority to request escrow account statements

**Construction monitoring authority:**
- Site visit and progress inspection rights
- Authority to approve minor specification changes
- Snagging list review and approval
- Handover acceptance authority

### Secondary market purchase POA requirements
**Due diligence authority:**
- Property inspection and valuation coordination
- RERA and DLD record verification
- Service charge and utility clearance verification
- Existing tenant agreement review (if applicable)

**Negotiation parameters:**
- Price negotiation range (typically 5-10% below asking)
- Completion date flexibility
- Condition precedent authority
- Authority to walk away if conditions not met

### Mortgage-related POA authority
**Bank interaction authority:**
- Mortgage application completion and submission
- Property valuation coordination
- Document submission and verification
- Mortgage offer acceptance (with defined parameters)

**Settlement coordination:**
- Bank draft collection authority
- Mortgage fund release coordination
- Lender settlement attendance
- Mortgage registration completion

## POA fraud prevention and security measures

Protecting against POA abuse requires systematic security measures:

### Authentication and verification procedures
**Principal verification:**
- Multiple identity documents (passport, driver's license)
- Video conference during POA signing
- Independent witness requirements
- Biometric verification where available

**POA holder verification:**
- UAE Emirates ID or passport verification
- Professional license validation (for brokers/lawyers)
- Reference checks from previous clients
- Financial background verification for large transactions

### Ongoing monitoring and control measures
**Transaction authorization protocols:**
- Written approval required for all major decisions
- Spending limits without additional authorization
- Regular reporting requirements (weekly/monthly)
- Photo documentation of all property-related activities

**Emergency revocation procedures:**
- 24-hour emergency revocation notification system
- Multiple communication channels (email, SMS, phone)
- Backup POA holder designation
- Legal support for urgent revocation

### Insurance and liability protection
**Professional indemnity insurance:**
- Verify POA holder carries adequate insurance
- Minimum coverage recommendations based on transaction value
- Exclusions and limitations understanding
- Claims procedure familiarity

**Fidelity bonds:**
- Protection against POA holder dishonesty
- Recommended for transactions over AED 2M
- UAE insurance market options limited but improving
- International insurance company alternatives

## Troubleshooting common POA problems

Experienced investors encounter predictable POA challenges:

### Documentation rejection scenarios
**DLD rejection reasons:**
- Insufficient MOFA attestation
- Arabic translation errors or omissions
- POA scope too broad or vague
- Expired or outdated identity documents

**Resolution strategies:**
- Maintain relationship with reliable UAE legal counsel
- Keep backup POA documents prepared
- Establish expedited attestation procedures
- Use experienced translation services with DLD track record

### Authority disputes and limitations
**Scope interpretation conflicts:**
- Developer interprets POA authority differently than intended
- Bank requires additional authorization for mortgage procedures
- Trustee center questions signature authority
- **Prevention:** Ultra-specific POA drafting with explicit authority lists

**Commercial disputes:**
- POA holder disagrees with principal's instructions
- Market conditions change, requiring strategy modification
- Timeline pressures conflict with proper authorization procedures
- **Resolution:** Clear communication protocols and decision-making hierarchies

### Emergency situations and crisis management
**Principal incapacitation:**
- Medical emergency preventing communication
- Natural disaster or political instability in home country
- Family emergency requiring immediate POA decisions
- **Preparation:** Emergency contact systems and backup authorization procedures

**POA holder unavailability:**
- Illness, travel, or business conflicts
- Professional license suspension or legal problems
- Death or incapacitation of POA holder
- **Preparation:** Backup POA holder designation and cross-training
