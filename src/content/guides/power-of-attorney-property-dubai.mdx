Buying Dubai property from abroad is standard practice. A large share of transactions involve foreign nationals, and many buyers complete the purchase without visiting Dubai until handover. The mechanism is the Power of Attorney (POA): a notarised document authorising a named agent to act at DLD, the developer's office, and the registration trustee centre.

This guide explains how POA works for Dubai property in 2026: scope, preparation, costs, DLD acceptance, and risks remote buyers underestimate.

Golden Visa biometrics and most mortgage final signatures still need personal UAE presence even when purchase steps are remote. We treat POA as a transaction tool, not a substitute for escrow discipline or independent SPA review.

**Citable factual block.** Timeline planning for a Dubai-compliant POA usually allows 2-6 weeks for home notary and embassy work, then 3-10 working days for MOFA and translation in the UAE, plus 3-15 working days for developer acceptance before a trustee appointment of 1-5 working days. Special property-specific POAs listing unit scope and enumerated acts are accepted more reliably than general authority wording. Fraud controls remain non-negotiable: escrow-only wires, written SPA approval, and license checks on the POA holder.

## When do you need a Power of Attorney for Dubai property?

You need a Power of Attorney for Dubai property when you cannot attend SPA signing, escrow funding, Oqood registration, or trustee transfer in person. Invest Gulf sees foreign buyers use it most across a 2-6 weeks attestation window, with document prep often budgeted at AED 2,000-8,000.

## What POA types does Dubai accept for property?

Dubai recognises a narrow set of powers rather than one general instrument, and the type you need depends on whether the attorney will buy, sell, mortgage or simply administer. Naming the wrong type is the most common reason a file is rejected at the trustee office.

| POA style | Typical outcome at DLD / developer |
| --- | --- |
| Special, property-specific | Accepted when attestation chain is complete |
| General | Often rejected or sent back for redraft |
| Dual holders (two agents) | Accepted only if SPA and trustee rules allow joint signing |

## What notary pitfalls delay remote Dubai purchases?

Most POA delays are formatting problems rather than legal ones. An instrument that names the wrong emirate, omits a specific authority such as mortgage registration, or carries an attestation chain with a missing step will be rejected at the trustee office, and the correction cycle typically runs 2 to 4 weeks from abroad.

## How do you prepare a Dubai-compliant POA?

Preparation runs in a fixed order: draft in the required bilingual form, notarise in the home country, then legalise through the chain that ends at UAE MOFA. Budget AED 2,000 to 8,000 and allow 2 to 6 weeks, because the attestation chain is the step that cannot be accelerated by paying more.

Have the POA drafted by a UAE-licensed property lawyer or notary, not a generic internet template.

## Who should hold your POA?

The attorney should be someone with no financial interest in the transaction and enough local presence to attend appointments at short notice. Appointing the selling agent or a developer representative creates a conflict that a Dubai notary will not resolve for you.

| Holder type | Best use | Risk note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UAE property lawyer | SPA signing, legal review | Professional indemnity, clear scope |
| Licensed broker | DLD transfer, handover | Conflict if same party earns commission |
| Family member | Only with legal training | Emotional pressure, scope creep |

Best practice: lawyer as POA holder for SPA; broker only for registration steps with written limits. Never grant unrestricted financial POA to a commission-earning salesperson.

## What can POA not do in Dubai?

A power of attorney transfers authority, not judgement, and several acts stay outside it entirely. Assuming an instrument covers something it does not is what forces a second trip abroad in the middle of a transaction.

Clarify with developer, trustee centre, and bank **before** preparing documents. A 30-minute confirmation call prevents a 4-week delay.

## How long does the POA workflow take?

Two to six weeks is the realistic window from drafting to a usable instrument, and the variance sits almost entirely in the legalisation chain rather than in the drafting. Start it alongside the property search rather than after an offer is accepted.

| Stage | Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Home notary + embassy | 2 to 6 weeks |
| MOFA + translation in UAE | 3 to 10 working days |
| Developer POA acceptance review | 3 to 15 working days |
| DLD transfer appointment | 1 to 5 working days after docs ready |

Start POA preparation **before** you sign reservation forms with tight expiry dates.

## How do you revoke a POA safely?

Revocation has to be registered, not merely signed. A revocation executed at a Dubai notary and lodged against the original instrument is what stops the attorney acting; telling them privately does not. Notify the trustee office and any bank holding the file on the same day the revocation is registered.

Run the checks before the attorney needs the authority rather than on the day. A power that names the wrong emirate, omits a specific act, or carries a broken attestation chain fails at the counter, and the correction cycle from abroad is measured in weeks.

## How do country-specific POA rules differ?

The Dubai side of the process is consistent; the home-country side is not. Whether your jurisdiction uses an apostille or a full consular chain changes both the timeline and the cost, and it is the variable worth confirming first.

### United Kingdom

Use UK notary public, then UAE Embassy attestation. Scottish and Northern Ireland processes differ; confirm apostille path with your lawyer.

### United States

State notary, Secretary of State authentication, UAE Embassy in Washington D.C. Remote online notarisation acceptance varies by developer.

### India

Notary, state Home Department, MEA attestation, then UAE Embassy. Allow 4 to 6 weeks from tier-2 cities.

### Canada

Provincial notary or lawyer, provincial authentication, UAE Embassy Ottawa.

Confirm jurisdiction acceptance with developer **before** notarising.

## How do you reduce POA fraud risk?

Most POA fraud is enabled by scope rather than by forgery. A power drafted to cover everything, with no expiry and no instruction letter, is the instrument that gets misused, so narrow the authority and date-limit it from the start.

## What happens after handover with POA?

A purchase POA usually stops being useful the moment the title deed issues, which is exactly when owners discover they still need authority for DEWA, Ejari and owners' association matters. Decide before drafting whether the instrument should cover post-handover administration or expire at transfer.

## How does POA cost compare to flying to Dubai?

A notarised and attested POA prepared abroad typically runs USD 300 to 900 including legalisation, against a return flight, hotel and several working days for an in-person transfer. The POA usually wins on cost and loses on control, which matters most where the transaction still has unresolved conditions.

## What post-handover authority should POA include?

If the attorney will manage the property after transfer, the instrument needs explicit authority for utility connections, Ejari registration, tenancy signature and owners' association correspondence. Each of these is treated as a separate power in Dubai practice, so an omission means drafting a fresh POA rather than amending one.

## What should your POA instruction letter include?

The instruction letter sits alongside the POA and records what the attorney may and may not agree: a price ceiling, an acceptable completion window, and any condition they cannot waive without written consent. It is not a registry document, but it is what protects you when a decision has to be made in a day.
