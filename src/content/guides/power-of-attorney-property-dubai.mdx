Buying Dubai property from abroad is standard practice. A large share of transactions involve foreign nationals, and many buyers complete the purchase without visiting Dubai until handover. The mechanism is the Power of Attorney (POA): a notarised document authorising a named agent to act at DLD, the developer's office, and the registration trustee centre.

This guide explains how POA works for Dubai property in 2026: scope, preparation, costs, DLD acceptance, and risks remote buyers underestimate.

## When do you need a Power of Attorney for Dubai property?

Use POA when you cannot attend in person for SPA signing, escrow payments, Oqood registration, or trustee-centre transfer. Common cases: expats in Europe or Asia, buyers managing multiple time zones, or investors who want legal counsel in Dubai to sign while they review remotely.

POA is optional if you plan to fly to Dubai for each milestone. Many buyers hybridise: POA for transfer and handover, personal visit for snagging only.

## What POA types does Dubai accept for property?

Dubai developers and registration trustee centres prefer a **special POA**: one named agent, one property or defined project, and a numbered list of permitted acts (sign SPA, pay escrow, register Oqood, attend transfer). A **general POA** that grants broad authority is commonly rejected because it creates unlimited signing risk. Some buyers use a **transaction-limited POA** valid only between fixed dates matching a reservation expiry. Irrevocable wording is rare and should be reviewed by UAE counsel because it complicates mid-deal exit.

| POA style | Typical outcome at DLD / developer |
| --- | --- |
| Special, property-specific | Accepted when attestation chain is complete |
| General | Often rejected or sent back for redraft |
| Dual holders (two agents) | Accepted only if SPA and trustee rules allow joint signing |

## What notary pitfalls delay remote Dubai purchases?

The attestation chain fails more often on paperwork than on law. Common delays: using a notary class your home country does not recognise for international deeds; omitting passport bio pages from the POA bundle; Arabic translation by a translator outside MOJ-approved lists; POA expiry before the trustee appointment; agent legal name spelled differently from Emirates ID or passport; missing embassy leg on a multi-page POA. Fix these before you pay a reservation fee with a 7-day expiry.

## How do you prepare a Dubai-compliant POA?

A general POA ("my agent can do anything") is often rejected by DLD and developers. Your POA should specify:

- Your full legal name and passport details
- The agent's full legal name and Emirates ID or passport
- The specific property (project name, unit number, or defined freehold scope)
- Enumerated powers: sign SPA, pay into escrow, register Oqood, attend DLD transfer, collect keys
- Expiry date (typically 1 to 2 years)
- Revocation clause

Have the POA drafted by a UAE-licensed property lawyer or notary, not a generic internet template.

### Step-by-step attestation chain

| Step | Action | Typical cost / time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Draft property-specific POA | AED 1,000 to 3,000 legal fees |
| 2 | Notarise in home country | Varies by country |
| 3 | UAE Embassy attestation | Country-specific fees |
| 4 | MOFA attestation in UAE | AED 500 to 1,500 |
| 5 | Certified Arabic translation | AED 300 to 800 |
| 6 | Optional DLD POA registration | Trustee centre visit |

Step 4 must complete before DLD or developer will accept the document. Courier original to your Dubai agent if you are not flying in.

## Who should hold your POA?

| Holder type | Best use | Risk note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UAE property lawyer | SPA signing, legal review | Professional indemnity, clear scope |
| Licensed broker | DLD transfer, handover | Conflict if same party earns commission |
| Family member | Only with legal training | Emotional pressure, scope creep |

Best practice: lawyer as POA holder for SPA; broker only for registration steps with written limits. Never grant unrestricted financial POA to a commission-earning salesperson.

## What can POA not do in Dubai?

POA typically **cannot** replace you for:

- UAE Golden Visa biometrics and medical (personal presence required)
- Most mortgage final signatures at UAE banks
- Opening personal bank accounts without your KYC presence (bank policy varies)
- Unlimited resale negotiation without price caps in the POA text

Clarify with developer, trustee centre, and bank **before** preparing documents. A 30-minute confirmation call prevents a 4-week delay.

## What should Gulf buyers budget for POA and transfer costs?

| Item | Typical range |
| --- | --- |
| POA prep + attestation | AED 2,000 to 8,000 |
| DLD transfer fee | 4% of property value |
| Trustee centre fee | ~AED 4,000 to 6,000 (varies) |
| NOC / developer fees | AED 500 to 5,000 |
| Mortgage registration (if applicable) | Bank schedule |

POA document cost is small against AED 1M+ purchase exposure. Model DLD 4% and trustee fees in your transfer spreadsheet separately from POA legal fees.

## How long does the POA workflow take?

| Stage | Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Home notary + embassy | 2 to 6 weeks |
| MOFA + translation in UAE | 3 to 10 working days |
| Developer POA acceptance review | 3 to 15 working days |
| DLD transfer appointment | 1 to 5 working days after docs ready |

Start POA preparation **before** you sign reservation forms with tight expiry dates.

## How do you revoke a POA safely?

Revocation requires a notarised revocation document, MOFA attestation, and notification to DLD, developer, and bank. Mid-transaction revocation can freeze deals if escrow milestones are pending. Build revocation rights into the original POA and keep email approval trails for major decisions.

## What red flags should pause a remote purchase?

- Agent asks for wire to personal account, not RERA escrow
- POA holder refuses to share SPA draft before signing
- Developer rejects POA from your jurisdiction without written alternative path
- Missing Oqood or title deed references on off-plan resale
- Pressure to skip independent legal review

See [Escrow and Oqood in Dubai explained](/guides/escrow-oqood-dubai-explained/).

## What checklist should run before activating POA?

- Original attested POA plus copies
- Certified passport copy
- SPA drafts (signed and unsigned)
- Escrow details verified on Dubai REST / developer letter
- Payment receipts and SWIFT confirmations
- Written instructions per milestone (email trail)
- Confirmation developer and trustee accept POA from your country

Disorganised document packs are the main cause of missed reservation deadlines.

## How does POA interact with Golden Visa planning?

POA can cover property purchase and DLD registration. Golden Visa processing typically needs your physical presence for medical and biometrics. Plan one UAE trip of 5 to 10 working days after title registration if visa is the next step.

## How do country-specific POA rules differ?

### United Kingdom

Use UK notary public, then UAE Embassy attestation. Scottish and Northern Ireland processes differ; confirm apostille path with your lawyer.

### United States

State notary, Secretary of State authentication, UAE Embassy in Washington D.C. Remote online notarisation acceptance varies by developer.

### India

Notary, state Home Department, MEA attestation, then UAE Embassy. Allow 4 to 6 weeks from tier-2 cities.

### Canada

Provincial notary or lawyer, provincial authentication, UAE Embassy Ottawa.

Confirm jurisdiction acceptance with developer **before** notarising.

## How does POA work for off-plan vs secondary purchases?

**Off-plan:** POA signs SPA, pays escrow instalments, registers Oqood, attends snagging and handover.

**Secondary:** POA holder coordinates inspection, verifies Form B / title, attends transfer at trustee centre, and respects NOC timing from seller's developer.

For secondary, embed negotiation limits and walk-away conditions in the POA or side instruction letter.

## How do you reduce POA fraud risk?

- Limit POA to one property and enumerated acts
- Require written approval before SPA execution
- Use escrow-only payment instructions
- Verify POA holder license (lawyer or RERA broker)
- Consider fidelity bond for transactions above AED 2M

## What advanced structures exist for portfolio buyers?

**Master POA** covering any freehold in Dubai speeds multi-unit buys but draws heavier DLD scrutiny. **Property-specific POAs** cost more to prepare but reduce authority disputes. Corporate POA holders (UAE company) suit institutional buyers with authorised signatory packs.

## What happens after handover with POA?

POA can sign Ejari, connect utilities, and appoint property management. Renew or revoke POA after handover if you no longer need an agent acting on your behalf.

## What troubleshooting fixes common POA delays?

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Embassy rejects notary | Re-notarise with approved notary list |
| Developer rejects POA country | Alternate legalisation path or personal visit |
| MOFA backlog | Use authorised typing centre expedite |
| Missing Arabic translation | Certified translator on MOJ approved list |
| Expired POA mid-deal | Renew before reservation expiry |

## How does POA cost compare to flying to Dubai?

A European round trip for two transfer visits can exceed AED 15,000 to 25,000 with hotels and time off work. POA preparation often costs AED 2,000 to 8,000 one time. Remote purchase is economically rational for most non-GCC buyers if document chain is clean.

## What post-handover authority should POA include?

If you plan to rent the unit, POA can cover Ejari registration and property management appointment. Scope these powers explicitly. Revoke or renew POA after handover if you no longer need an agent signing on your behalf.

## What should your POA instruction letter include?

Send your agent a separate email instruction schedule: maximum SPA price, escrow-only wires, snagging standards, and when to escalate to you. POA grants legal authority; instruction letters reduce day-to-day disputes. Update instructions if mortgage terms or completion dates shift.

## How do mortgages interact with POA?

Most UAE banks require buyer presence for final loan documents and KYC refresh. POA rarely covers full mortgage execution. Plan a short Dubai trip for finance even if purchase steps are remote.

Keep digital copies of every attested POA page, translation, and MOFA sticker on cloud storage your POA holder can access if originals are delayed in courier.

*POA requirements may vary by developer and trustee centre. Verify current DLD acceptance criteria before preparing documents. Not legal advice.*
