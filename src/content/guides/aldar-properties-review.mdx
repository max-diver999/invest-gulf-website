When budgeting for this process in the Gulf, account for government processing fees (typically 500–5,000 AED depending on service type), document attestation costs (200–800 AED per document), typing-centre charges (100–350 AED per application), and potential agent or PRO service fees (1,500–5,000 AED for end-to-end handling). Timeline varies from 3 working days for straightforward applications to 4–8 weeks for complex cases requiring inter-departmental coordination.
