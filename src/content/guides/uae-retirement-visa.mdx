Retire in Dubai at 57, draw your pension, own a studio in Jumeirah Village Circle, and hold valid UAE residency doing all of it. That is the practical pitch of the UAE retirement visa, a 5-year permit introduced as part of the UAE's effort to retain high-net-worth older residents who are neither employees nor large-scale investors.

The visa gets less attention than the Golden Visa. It should not. For anyone aged 55+ whose wealth sits in savings or pension income rather than AED 2M property portfolios, it is often the simpler, cheaper route to long-term UAE residency.

This guide covers every qualifying route, exact thresholds, what happens at renewal, how to handle dependents, and where retirement visa holders sit relative to other UAE residency pathways.

## What is the UAE Retirement Visa in 2026?

The UAE Retirement Visa is a 5-year renewable residency permit for applicants aged 55+ who prove financial independence through AED 2M property, AED 1M savings, or AED 20,000 monthly passive income, and we treat the route as the practical live-and-stay path when foreign buyers lack a Golden Visa property ticket.

Unlike an employment visa, no UAE-based job or employer is required. Unlike the Golden Visa, no AED 2M property investment is mandatory. The retirement visa was designed to allow economically independent older expats to remain in the UAE after ending employment.

Key characteristics:

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Duration | 5 years, renewable |
| Age requirement | 55+ at time of application |
| Qualifying routes | Property, savings, or income (one is enough) |
| Dependents | Spouse + children sponsorable |
| Work permitted | Not automatically; employment requires separate work permit |
| Path to permanent residency | Not a direct path, renewable on continued eligibility |

## Who can apply for the UAE Retirement Visa?

Applicants typically must be aged 55 or older at filing and meet one published financial route: AED 2M registered property, AED 1M UAE bank savings, or AED 20,000 monthly passive income, and Invest Gulf sees both in-country transitions and overseas applicants use the same thresholds for foreign buyers.

Applicants who previously lived in the UAE on employment or investor visas can transition once they reach 55 if the financial evidence still holds. Overseas applicants usually file through an approved typing center and GDRFA or ICP portals, then complete medical fitness inside the UAE.

Eligibility checklist:

- Passport validity covering the full 5-year cycle
- Age evidence for the principal applicant only
- One complete financial route with bank or title proof
- UAE-compliant health insurance before stamping

## What are the three Retirement Visa qualification routes?

Three qualification routes typically allow UAE Retirement Visa filing: AED 2 million registered freehold property, AED 1 million held in a UAE bank, or AED 20,000 monthly passive income, and Invest Gulf recommends picking the route with the cleanest paper trail for foreign buyers before booking medical appointments this quarter.

### Route 1: Property Investment: AED 2 Million

Own real estate registered in your name in the UAE with a minimum combined market value of AED 2 million.

Key rules:

- Property must be in a freehold zone or designated area open to foreign ownership.
- The AED 2M threshold is assessed against registered market value, not original purchase price.
- A single property or combination of multiple units can reach the threshold.
- Off-plan units registered with Oqood are generally acceptable; verify with GDRFA at application.
- Jointly owned property may count proportionally; 50% of a AED 4M unit equals AED 2M qualifying.
- Mortgaged property can qualify when registered value meets the threshold and the bank issues an NOC.

### Route 2: Financial Savings: AED 1 Million

Hold a minimum of AED 1 million (or foreign-currency equivalent) in a UAE-licensed bank as a fixed deposit or maintained balance.

- Savings must sit in a UAE bank, not an offshore account.
- Fixed deposits are the clearest proof because they generate a bank certificate.
- Current accounts may qualify if GDRFA or ICP accepts the bank letter format.
- Joint accounts typically need a declaration of your individual share.
- Foreign currency equivalent to AED 1M needs conversion documentation.

The savings route suits retirees who liquidated property abroad and want residency before buying UAE real estate. Timeline is often faster than a property search plus title registration.

### Route 3: Monthly Income: AED 20,000

Demonstrate regular monthly income of at least AED 20,000 from pension, dividends, rental income, annuity, or a documented combination.

Accepted income sources typically include:

- Pension payments from a government or corporate scheme
- Dividends from investment portfolios with consistent history
- Rental income evidenced by contracts and bank deposits
- Annuity or trust distributions
- Combinations totalling AED 20,000 per month

Provide 3 to 6 months of bank statements showing recurring credits plus source letters. AED 20,000 per month equals about AED 240,000 per year, or roughly USD 65,500 at 2026 rates.

UAE Retirement Visa qualification in 2026 typically rests on one of three published financial routes: registered freehold property at AED 2 million market value, AED 1 million held in a UAE bank, or AED 20,000 monthly passive income evidenced over 3 to 6 months of statements. Property filings should use land-department registered value rather than original purchase price, and jointly owned units usually count only the applicant share toward the AED 2M floor. Savings filings work cleanest with a UAE fixed-deposit certificate; offshore balances rarely satisfy GDRFA or ICP reviewers. Income filings succeed when pension, dividends, and rental credits arrive on a predictable monthly cadence rather than as sporadic consultancy fees. Invest Gulf intake files that mix incomplete routes waste 2 to 4 weeks at typing centers, so complete one route fully before medical booking.

## What belongs on a UAE retirement visa checklist?
Pre-filing checklists typically cover passport validity, age evidence for the 55+ principal, one complete financial route with AED thresholds met, UAE health insurance, and attested family documents if dependents will be sponsored, and we flag incomplete bank letters as the top delay for foreign buyers in 2026.

Core documents:

- Passport copies and photos for each applicant
- Property title at AED 2M+, UAE bank certificate at AED 1M, or income letters at AED 20,000 monthly
- 3 to 6 months of bank statements for the income route
- Medical fitness booking confirmation (AED 300 to 500 clinic band)
- Marriage and birth certificates (attested) when sponsoring dependents

If sponsoring dependents, add spouse passport pages and proof of accommodation sized for the household.

## How do you apply for the UAE Retirement Visa step by step?

Application steps typically run through eligibility confirmation, document typing, medical fitness, Emirates ID biometrics, and residency stamping over roughly 2 to 6 weeks once files are complete, and we sequence insurance purchase before the medical clinic day for foreign buyers aged 55+.

1. Confirm age 55+ and choose one financial route with AED evidence ready.
2. Book a licensed typing center or online GDRFA/ICP submission channel.
3. Complete medical fitness inside the UAE and keep the clinic receipt.
4. Enrol Emirates ID biometrics and submit insurance proof.
5. Collect residency stamp and Emirates ID; diarise renewal 3 to 4 months early.

Budget typing fees of AED 50 to 200 per transaction on top of government stamps.

## Can Retirement Visa holders sponsor dependents?

Retirement Visa holders typically can sponsor a spouse and dependent children for the same 5-year cycle when accommodation and insurance cover the household, and Invest Gulf reminds families that children turning 18 need their own residency path before dependent status ends for foreign buyers in the UAE.

Dependent checklist:

- Attested marriage certificate for a spouse
- Attested birth certificates for children under 18
- Proof of housing suitable for the family size
- Continuous UAE health insurance of AED 3,000 to 7,000 yearly per adult plan band
- Budget AED 1,000 to 3,000 in government fees per dependent stamp

If a dependent child reaches 18 and wants to stay, transition to employment, student, or independent investment residency before the dependent visa expires.

Choosing between the visas typically means comparing a 5-year retirement path at age 55+ with savings or income routes against a 10-year Golden Visa that needs AED 2M qualifying property and has no age floor, and we map both paths before foreign buyers lock a purchase decision.

## How do you renew the UAE Retirement Visa?

Renewal typically requires proving the same AED thresholds still hold: property still registered at AED 2M+, AED 1M still in a UAE bank, or income still above AED 20,000 monthly, plus fresh medical, insurance, and Emirates ID, and we start files 3 to 4 months before expiry for foreign buyers.

Property holders who sell without a replacement plan create the same compliance gap as Golden Visa owners; see [Golden Visa Renewal Requirements](/guides/golden-visa-renewal-requirements-uae/).

Savings route at renewal: replenish any drawdown before the appointment. Income route: refresh pension letters and 3 to 6 months of statements. Also renew medical fitness, health insurance, Emirates ID, and confirm passport validity through the next cycle.

## How long can you stay away on re-entry rules?

Re-entry rules typically treat absences beyond 6 months without a return entry as a cancellation risk in GDRFA or ICP systems, so retirement visa holders planning long summers abroad should confirm absence notifications early, and we treat the Golden Visa as more flexible on extended travel for foreign buyers.

Practical travel checklist:

- Log exit and re-entry dates across the 5-year cycle
- Keep insurance continuous while outside the UAE
- Ask your typing center about approved absence notifications before a 6-month trip
- Avoid assuming Golden Visa absence flexibility applies automatically

## What mistakes delay Retirement Visa approval?

Approval delays typically come from mixing Golden Visa rumours with retirement savings rules, filing off-plan property without emirate acceptance checks, letting insurance lapse, or ignoring children who turn 18 during the 5-year cycle, and we see each error add 2 to 6 weeks for foreign buyers filing in Dubai.

Common mistakes:

1. Mixing retirement visa and Golden Visa property thresholds. Both use AED 2M on property, but AED 1M savings and AED 20,000 income are retirement-only.
2. Buying off-plan without checking acceptance. Dubai Oqood is often acceptable; confirm Abu Dhabi or Ras Al Khaimah projects locally.
3. Letting insurance lapse between renewal stages even if the stamp still shows validity.
4. Waiting until a dependent turns 18 before planning independent residency.

For the property route, the AED 2M floor aligns with the Golden Visa property threshold. If you are buying in that range anyway, assess whether 10-year Golden Visa validity better matches your plan.
