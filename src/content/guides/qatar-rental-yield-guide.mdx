- Annual rent: QAR 70,000

## Which five mistakes cost Qatar landlords the most yield?

The recurring Qatar landlord mistakes typically include Pearl-only liquidity bias, ignoring service charge spreads that can cost more than 1% net yield, and planning Dubai-speed exits inside 90 days, and we flag each error as avoidable with documents before cash moves with documents on file.

West Bay is a clear example: foreign ownership applies to selected towers, not the district, so a listing described as freehold West Bay tells you nothing until the register confirms it. We treat an unverified freehold claim, a missing service charge schedule, or a refusal to share lease comparables as reasons to pause rather than points to negotiate.
