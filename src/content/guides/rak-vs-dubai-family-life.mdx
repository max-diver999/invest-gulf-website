## FAQ

**Is RAK or Dubai better for expat families?**
Dubai for school breadth, entertainment, and Dubai employer proximity. RAK for cost savings (40–60% lower rent), outdoor lifestyle, and quiet community. Employment location drives the answer.

**How much cheaper is RAK vs Dubai?**
3BR villa in RAK: AED 75,000–110,000/year vs AED 180,000–280,000 in Dubai. Plus 20–30% lower school fees. Total annual saving of AED 100,000–180,000 is achievable.

**What international schools are in RAK?**
GEMS Westminster, RAK Academy British, Delhi Private School CBSE, Choueifat SABIS. Good for British and Indian curriculum; IB Diploma options are limited.

**What is the daily commute from RAK to Dubai?**
60–75 minutes off-peak; 80–100 minutes in morning rush. Round trip daily commute: 2.5–3.5 hours driving.

**Is RAK safe for families?**
Very safe. Low crime, calm traffic compared to Dubai, established family expat communities at Al Hamra and Mina Al Arab.

**What are the best family areas in RAK?**
Al Hamra Village (established, gated, golf and beach), Mina Al Arab (newer waterfront), Al Marjan Island (apartments, Wynn future), and RAK city for affordability.

**Does RAK have good healthcare?**
Adequate for most family needs. Complex specialist care may require Dubai trip (60–90 min). Emergency response good in RAK city areas.

**Should I choose RAK if my children need IB Diploma?**
Plan carefully. IB Diploma options in RAK are limited. Consider whether a RAK school with transfer to Dubai at year 10 or 11 is workable.
