## FAQ

**Can I set up a Dubai company remotely from outside the UAE?**
Yes, the initial incorporation and document submission can often be handled remotely or through a power of attorney. However, your UAE visa and Emirates ID require physical presence in the UAE for biometrics. Most owners visit for 3–5 days to complete the visa process.

**What is the minimum share capital for a Dubai LLC?**
There is no minimum share capital requirement for most LLC activities since the 2021 law amendment. Previously, AED 300,000 was nominal, this no longer applies for most commercial activities. Regulated activities (banking, insurance) still have minimum capital requirements.

**Do I need a local Emirati partner for a mainland company?**
No, for the vast majority of commercial and professional activities since 2021. Certain strategic sectors retain restrictions, check your specific activity code against the current restricted list.

**What is an Ejari and why does my business need it?**
Ejari is Dubai's tenancy registration system. All commercial tenancies in Dubai must be registered through Ejari. DED requires an Ejari-registered office address to issue a mainland licence. The landlord typically provides this through their Ejari account.

**How long does business bank account opening take in Dubai?**
Typically 4–8 weeks for a clean application at UAE banks. Complex ownership, high-risk activity, or certain nationalities may extend to 10–12 weeks. Begin the process immediately after licence issuance, do not wait until your visa is finalised.

**Can my freezone company hire staff who work in Dubai mainland offices?**
Freezone companies can have employees on freezone visas who work within the freezone zone. Working regularly at a Dubai mainland client office raises immigration compliance questions, technically, freezone visa holders need to work within the freezone or remotely. For substantial mainland operations, a mainland entity or dual structure is the cleaner approach. Get legal advice for your specific situation.
