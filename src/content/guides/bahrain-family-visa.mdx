# Bahrain Family Visa 2026: LMRA Sponsorship, Salary Rules & Documents

> Disclaimer: LMRA rules change. Employer PRO is primary source, confirm thresholds at application.

## Who is this Bahrain family visa guide for?

This guide covers employment-sponsored family joining through LMRA: spouse and children on your work permit, housing proof, and PRO timelines. It is not for tourist extensions, domestic worker visas, or Golden Residence dependants (separate track; verify current official rules via [Bahrain Golden Residence](/guides/bahrain-golden-residence/)).

## What must the sponsor have before LMRA accepts dependants?

The sponsor needs a valid work permit, active CPR (Central Population Registry), qualifying salary certificate, and family-suitable housing registered with the municipality where possible. Without active CPR on the primary holder, dependant files stall at the first PRO submission.

## What salary rules does LMRA apply to family sponsorship?

LMRA links family joining to profession category and salary, with commonly cited totals around BHD 400+ for many employment tiers. Allowances may not count toward the threshold; only the employer PRO can confirm the live category table. Golden Residence investors near BHD 200,000 follow separate dependant rules; verify current official rules before planning school and housing spend.

## Which dependants can you sponsor?

Typical categories include spouse and children; parent sponsorship requires proof of dependency and category approval. Male spouse sponsorship by a female sponsor is restricted in many cases; verify current official rules early if your household does not match the default nuclear family template.

| Dependent | Extra document |
|---|---|
| Spouse | Attested marriage certificate |
| Child | Attested birth certificate and passport |
| Parent | Proof of dependency (verify category) |

## What housing proof does LMRA expect?

LMRA expects a lease or ownership evidence for a unit that fits declared family size. Studio leases in Seef rarely pass when two children are listed. Municipality-registered tenancy helps align EWA utilities and visa address. Model rent bands in [Manama cost of living](/guides/manama-cost-of-living/) before promising a district to your spouse.

## How long does the family visa process take?

Plan 6 to 10 weeks from active sponsor CPR and work permit to dependant CPR, plus attestation and medical time abroad if dependants are not in Bahrain. August PRO slowdowns can push school start dates; escalate HR in week one if deadlines slip.

| Phase | Action | Risk if skipped |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-arrival | Attest marriage and birth certificates | LMRA rejection |
| Week 1 to 2 | CPR primary active | Cannot sponsor |
| Week 3 | Municipality lease in family name | Address mismatch |
| Week 4 | School conditional offer if needed | Lease signed too early |
| Week 5 to 8 | Family visa stamping abroad | Kids miss term start |

## What fees should you budget per dependant?

Indicative government and PRO fees often land BHD 150 to 400 per dependant excluding attestation abroad. Medical screening runs roughly BHD 30 to 50 per applicant. Full processing commonly takes 15 to 30 working days after file submission. School deposits BHD 1,000 to 3,000 may sit alongside visa fees; stack both in your [Manama cost of living](/guides/manama-cost-of-living/) model.

## Can dependants arrive on a visit visa first?

Visit status is short-term only. Dependants need joining status linked to the sponsor work permit before employment or schooling assumes legal residency. Overstay harms LMRA records and complicates future transfers.

## How do schools interact with LMRA timelines?

A school offer letter is not immigration status, but many families secure conditional admission before signing a 12-month lease. LMRA rejects overcrowded housing claims when bedroom counts do not match declared dependants. See [Bahrain international schools](/guides/bahrain-international-schools/) for seat strategy.

## What healthcare do dependants need on arrival?

Private insurance or employer family cover should be active before dependants land. Clinics copy visa pages repeatedly; renew passport stickers before expiry buffers shrink. Overview: [Bahrain healthcare guide](/guides/bahrain-healthcare-guide/).

## What if you lose your job while family is on visa?

Dependant status usually ties to sponsor employment. Grace periods are limited; coordinate transfer to a new employer PRO or exit before overstays accumulate. Banking and school contracts may pause if CPR updates lag; treat job loss as a same-week PRO conversation, not a month-end task.

## What documents belong in your PRO folder?

Keep attested marriage and birth certificates, passport copies, salary certificate, lease, EWA account proof, and school letters in physical and scanned form. PRO email templates to HR should request written salary category confirmation, not verbal thresholds.

## What mistakes cause LMRA rejections?

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Spouse arrives on visit visa | Convert before overstays |
| Child born abroad | Register and attest before travel |
| Ex-spouse on visa | Cancel dependant before final exit |
| Wrong bedroom count on lease | Resize unit or adjust dependant list with PRO |

## When is an external PRO worth paying?

If employer PRO misses two deadlines, external PRO support (often BHD 100 to 300) can cost less than flight changes and forfeited school deposits. Still route filings through LMRA-compliant channels; unofficial cash shortcuts backfire on renewal.

## How do banking and CPR updates interact for new dependants?

Once dependants receive CPR, open or link accounts for school fee payments and EWA deposits. Banks copy visa pages on every product upgrade; renew stickers before they fade. See [Bahrain banking for expats](/guides/bahrain-banking-expats/) for payroll alignment if spouse later seeks local employment with separate work permit rules.

## What visit and re-entry rules should families respect?

Repeated visit entries while the sponsor already works on a permit can trigger LMRA scrutiny. Align arrival dates with PRO submission windows so dependants are not stuck abroad while housing sits empty. Causeway commuters to Saudi Arabia should keep passport pages legible; worn stamps fail automated readers.

## How do attestation and translation timelines affect planning?

Home-country marriage and birth certificates need apostille or embassy attestation chains before LMRA accepts them. Budget two to six weeks abroad for paperwork if dependants remain in origin country. Scan certified copies the day they return; PRO folders get lost when only paper exists.

## Can a nanny or domestic worker ride on the family visa?

Domestic worker visas use a separate LMRA category with housing inspection. Do not list helpers on the family bundle without category approval. Compound referrals beat social media hires for compliant filings.

## How does Golden Residence differ from employment family join?

Golden Residence investors follow capital and ROP-style rules separate from LMRA employment sponsorship. Do not assume one Golden approval automatically bundles spouse and children; verify dependant policy on the exact investor category before property transfer.

## Final checklist before dependants travel

Confirm salary category in writing, lease registered, medical appointments booked, school seat conditional, and CPR active on sponsor. If your PRO is silent seven days after submission, escalate HR with dates in writing. Related hub: [Bahrain relocation guide](/guides/bahrain-relocation-guide/).

## Extended Q&A: LMRA edge cases families ask

Can spouse work on a dependant visa? Generally a separate work permit is required; verify current official rules before informal cash work. LMRA inspections happen.

Does a school visa letter replace immigration status? No. Enrollment letters help housing timing but children still need stamped dependant visas.

How many renewals lead to permanent residency? Bahrain does not grant citizenship through tenure; Golden Residence remains a separate capital track.

Should you hire an external PRO? If internal PRO misses two deadlines, external support often costs less than flight changes and lost school deposits.

What if children hold dual nationality? Carry both passports but align LMRA filings with the passport used on the sponsor CPR record to avoid mismatch at border control.

## Sample PRO email to HR (adapt before sending)

Request written confirmation of LMRA salary category, estimated dependant processing weeks, and list of attested documents the PRO will submit. Ask whether housing inspection is required before lease signing and whether school letters must precede LMRA filing. Copy the PRO manager if front-line staff only give verbal dates.

## Case study: Indian family joining from abroad (illustrative)

A Manama-based IT sponsor with active CPR and BHD 450 total salary attestation started attestation in India six weeks before travel. Marriage and two birth certificates moved through embassy channels while the sponsor secured a two-bedroom lease in Juffair registered with the municipality. Week 1 in Bahrain: PRO submitted salary certificate and lease scan. Week 3: LMRA requested clearer bedroom photos; inspection passed on second visit. Week 6: spouse and children completed medical in Bahrain after entry on proper joining status, not visit visas. Week 8: CPR issued for dependants. Total cash outlay near BHD 900 including fees, medical, and school deposit held separately. Timeline slips when visit visas precede joining status or when Seef studio leases fail bedroom counts.

## Employer change mid-process: what changes

If you switch employers while dependants are in flight, LMRA may require a new sponsor file and refreshed salary certificate before stamps finalize. Treat job changes as freeze-travel events until PRO confirms dependant status transferred or re-submitted. Banking and school invoices should pause auto-debits until new CPR copies circulate.

## School waitlist strategy in Manama before visa completes

Apply to two schools with conditional acceptance letters that do not require full year tuition until visas stamp. Ask admissions whether they accept LMRA-in-progress letters for seat holds. August intake fills fast; January mid-year entry may ease housing and visa overlap if your employer allows delayed family travel.

## Dual-nationality children and passport choice

When children hold two passports, LMRA filings should consistently use the passport number registered on the sponsor CPR household record. Switching passports mid-process triggers border questions. Scan both passports but follow PRO instruction on which to stamp first.

## How Bahrain family sponsorship fits a real move calendar

Dependent visas follow salary, housing evidence, and document attestation more than flight dates. Build a week-by-week plan from contract signature: which certificates need attestation, which medicals are required, and when school seats must be paid.

| Stage | Common blocker |
| --- | --- |
| Salary threshold proof | HR letters missing required fields |
| Marriage or birth docs | Attestation gaps |
| Medical and biometrics | Peak-season appointment waits |
| School deposits | Seats will not wait for card printing |
| Housing | Landlords asking for CPR before long leases |
