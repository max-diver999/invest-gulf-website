## Who is this Bahrain family visa guide for?

This guide is for employment-sponsored family joining through LMRA: spouse and children on your work permit, housing proof, and PRO timelines across a 6 to 10 week window after sponsor CPR is active, and our research treats tourist extensions, domestic worker visas, and Golden Residence dependants as separate tracks.

- Employment work-permit sponsors filing through employer PRO
- Families needing housing and school timing before flights
- Households comparing Golden Residence versus LMRA joining

## What must the sponsor have before LMRA accepts dependants?

The sponsor typically requires a valid work permit, active CPR, qualifying salary certificate, and family-suitable housing registered with the municipality where possible, because Invest Gulf sees dependant files stall within the first 7 to 14 days when primary CPR is missing before any school deposit leaves the account.

- Valid work permit plus active CPR on the primary holder
- Salary certificate matching the live LMRA category table
- Family-suitable lease or ownership evidence on file

## What salary rules does LMRA apply to family sponsorship?

LMRA typically links family joining to profession category and salary, with commonly cited totals around BHD 400+ for many employment tiers over a 12 month contract year, and Invest Gulf requires written PRO confirmation that allowances count before school deposits are paid before you wire school deposits or rent.

- Confirm whether basic salary or total package is the qualifying figure
- Golden Residence near BHD 200,000 follows separate dependant rules
- Verify current official rules before promising school start dates

## Which dependants can you sponsor?

Typical categories include spouse and children under LMRA family joining, while parent sponsorship requires dependency proof and category approval that can add 4 to 8 weeks, and we flag male spouse sponsorship by a female sponsor as a restricted path needing early counsel before the household books one-way tickets.

| Dependent | Extra document |
|---|---|
| Spouse | Attested marriage certificate |
| Child | Attested birth certificate and passport |
| Parent | Proof of dependency (verify category) |

## What housing proof does LMRA expect?

LMRA typically expects a lease or ownership evidence for a unit that fits declared family size across a full 12 month tenancy, and we see Seef studio leases fail when two children are listed because bedroom counts must match the dependant list before the spouse books flights.

- Municipality-registered tenancy helps align EWA and visa address
- Model rent in [Manama cost of living](/guides/manama-cost-of-living/) before promising a district
- Two-bedroom minimum when two children appear on the file

## How long does the family visa process take?

Family visa processing typically means 6 to 10 weeks from active sponsor CPR and work permit to dependant CPR, plus attestation and medical time abroad if dependants are not in Bahrain, and our research treats August PRO slowdowns as a school-start risk needing week-one HR escalation.

| Phase | Action | Risk if skipped |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-arrival | Attest marriage and birth certificates | LMRA rejection |
| Week 1 to 2 | CPR primary active | Cannot sponsor |
| Week 3 | Municipality lease in family name | Address mismatch |
| Week 4 | School conditional offer if needed | Lease signed too early |
| Week 5 to 8 | Family visa stamping abroad | Kids miss term start |

## Can dependants arrive on a visit visa first?

Visit status is short-term only and typically fails as a family joining strategy, because dependants need joining status linked to the sponsor work permit before schooling assumes residency, and we treat overstay as a 12 month LMRA record risk before school enrollment paperwork starts.

- Convert to joining status before school enrollment assumes residency
- Avoid repeated visit entries once the sponsor work permit is active
- Treat overstay as a transfer and renewal risk, not a soft buffer

## How do schools interact with LMRA timelines?

A school offer letter is not immigration status, but many families secure conditional admission 4 to 8 weeks before signing a 12 month lease, and we reject overcrowded housing claims when bedroom counts do not match declared dependants before the 12 month lease is signed. - Conditional offers help seat holds without full-year tuition

## What if you lose your job while family is on visa?

Dependant status typically ties to sponsor employment, so grace periods are limited to a short transfer window of about 7 to 30 days, and we treat job loss as a same-week PRO conversation rather than a month-end task before overstay risk compounds.

- Coordinate transfer to a new employer PRO before overstays accumulate
- Pause school auto-debits until new CPR copies circulate
- Escalate banking freezes inside 7 days, not at month end

## What documents belong in your PRO folder?

A complete PRO folder typically includes attested marriage and birth certificates, passport copies, salary certificate, lease, EWA proof, and school letters in physical and scanned form for the full 6 to 10 week cycle, and we require written salary category confirmation instead of verbal thresholds before the first LMRA submission.

- Keep physical and scanned copies in the same folder structure
- Request written category confirmation from HR, not chat summaries
- Refresh scans the day attestation papers return from abroad

## What mistakes cause LMRA rejections?

LMRA rejections typically follow visit-visa arrivals, unattested birth certificates, ex-spouse names still on file, or bedroom counts that fail inspection inside the first 30 days, and we use the table below as the fix checklist before dependants leave the origin country. Confirm current official rules.

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Spouse arrives on visit visa | Convert before overstays |
| Child born abroad | Register and attest before travel |
| Ex-spouse on visa | Cancel dependant before final exit |
| Wrong bedroom count on lease | Resize unit or adjust dependant list with PRO |

- Keep employer PRO copied even when an external agent helps

## What visit and re-entry rules should families respect?

Repeated visit entries while the sponsor already works on a permit typically trigger LMRA scrutiny within a 6 to 12 month window, and Invest Gulf aligns arrival dates with PRO submission so dependants are not stuck abroad while a 12 month lease sits empty before empty housing burns rent.

- Align arrivals with PRO submission windows, not airline sales
- Keep passport pages legible for Causeway and airport readers
- Avoid visit hopping once joining status is available

## How do attestation and translation timelines affect planning?

## Can a nanny or domestic worker ride on the family visa?

Domestic worker visas typically use a separate LMRA category with housing inspection and cannot ride on the family bundle without category approval that adds 2 to 6 weeks of delay, and we prefer compound referrals over social media hires for compliant filings before helpers are listed on any form.

- Do not list helpers on the family joining file without approval
- Expect a housing inspection before domestic worker stamping
- Keep family and domestic worker visas on separate PRO tracks

## How does Golden Residence differ from employment family join?

Golden Residence typically follows capital and ROP-style rules separate from LMRA employment sponsorship, so one investor approval near BHD 200,000 does not automatically bundle spouse and children within the first 90 days, and we require category-specific dependant checks before property transfer before any property transfer completes.

- Do not assume title deed equals family residency
- Verify dependant policy on the exact investor category
- Keep employment joining and Golden tracks on separate calendars

## What changes if you switch employers mid-process?

An employer change mid-process typically freezes travel until a new sponsor file and refreshed salary certificate clear LMRA, often adding 2 to 4 weeks, and we pause school auto-debits until new CPR copies circulate before stamps are treated as final. Confirm current official rules.

- Treat job changes as freeze-travel events until PRO confirms
- Refresh salary certificates before stamps finalize
- Pause banking and school invoices until new CPR copies circulate

## How should you handle school waitlists before visas complete?

School waitlist strategy typically means applying to two campuses with conditional acceptance that does not require full-year tuition until visas stamp inside a 6 to 10 week window, and we prefer January mid-year entry when August intake is full before full-year tuition is paid.

- Ask admissions whether LMRA-in-progress letters hold seats
- Avoid paying full-year tuition before stamping dates are real
- Use January entry when August seats are already gone

## How should dual-nationality children pick a passport?

Dual-nationality filings typically require consistent use of the passport number registered on the sponsor CPR household record for the full 6 to 10 week process, and we treat mid-process passport switches as a border mismatch risk before border control questions the file.

- Scan both passports but stamp the PRO-instructed booklet first
- Keep the CPR household passport number consistent end to end
- Avoid switching booklets after LMRA submission
