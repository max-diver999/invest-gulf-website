# Bahrain Family Visa 2026: LMRA Sponsorship, Salary Rules & Documents

**TL;DR:** Family joining runs through **LMRA** on your **employment work permit** — not visit visas. **Salary, profession, housing** must align before dependants fly. Budget **BHD 150–400 per dependant** in fees plus **6–10 weeks** after sponsor CPR is active. ** (confirm current official rules)** every time — HR verbal promises fail. **Hubs:** [Bahrain relocation guide](/guides/bahrain-relocation-guide/) (R91) · [Manama cost of living](/guides/manama-cost-of-living/) (R92)

> **Disclaimer:** LMRA rules change. Employer PRO is primary source — confirm thresholds at application.

## LMRA — who sponsors

Your employer **PRO** submits. You need:

- Valid **work permit**
- Active **CPR** (Central Population Registry)
- Qualifying **salary certificate**
- **Family-suitable housing**

---

## Salary rules ** (confirm current official rules)**

There is no stable public single number — LMRA ties salary to profession and basic pay. Planning ranges from current practice:

| Sponsor profile | Often cited threshold |
|---|---|
| Skilled private sector | BHD 400+ total or basic line ** (confirm current official rules)** |
| Manager / professional | Higher ease |
| Labour categories | Family joining restricted |

**Allowances:** housing and transport **may not count** — read **basic salary line** in contract.

**Golden Residence:** ~**BHD 200,000** investor path — **separate family rules (confirm current official rules)**

## Extended planning — employer and PRO coordination

| Stakeholder | What to ask in writing |
|---|---|
| **HR** | Medical insurance tier PDF, family inclusion, basic vs total salary breakdown |
| **PRO** | LMRA family eligibility letter before dependants book flights |
| **Landlord** | Municipality registration timeline and EWA account transfer |
| **School** | Seat offer letter with start date and deposit refund policy |
| **Bank** | Salary transfer account opening docs for CPR linkage |

 Bahrain: **finance-sector** hires often assume smooth relocation — **PRO bandwidth** at mid-size employers can still bottleneck at **August school peak**. Start PRO email thread **week 1**, not month 3.

Golden Residence investors: parallel legal track — ** (confirm current official rules) BHD 200,000 minimum investment, eligible assets, annual maintenance fees, and whether dependants require separate sponsorship.**

| **Mall grocery Saturday** | Parking stress acceptable? |
| **Hospital network** | Nearest in-network private ER 15 min? |

## FAQ depth — operational answers

**Can spouse work?** Separate work permit — not automatic on family visa. ** (confirm current official rules)**

**Pets?** Import rules apply — airline and veterinary certificates.

**CPR for children?** PRO workflow after arrival — school may ask early.

**Alcohol in home?** Licensed purchase rules — hotel/club culture; respect local norms.

**Friday prayer traffic?** Short congestion near mosques — plan appointments.

**Summer power cuts?** Rare in towers; verify generator in older villas.

**F1 week?** Hotel and road spikes — visitors plan ahead.

**Exit visa?** Employment termination LMRA process — coordinate notice period.
