## How do you get a personal alcohol licence in Dubai?

A personal alcohol licence is a Dubai home permit that typically allows non-Muslim residents aged 21 and above with Emirates ID to buy from MMI under a monthly spend limit. Light drinkers often cap spend near AED 400-800. Invest Gulf client checklist notes treat the card as cheap legal hygiene.

| Complete application | Non-Muslim declaration, ID details |
| Set monthly spend limit | You specify; can be adjusted |
| Receive licence | Digital and/or physical card |
| Use at MMI/A+E | Swipe or show at purchase |

## What is the drunk driving rule in Dubai?

Dubai drunk driving rules mean zero measurable blood alcohol while operating a vehicle, not a 0.05 or 0.08 limit, with fines from AED 20,000, confiscation up to 60 days, licence loss, and possible deportation. Morning-after residual alcohol still counts. Invest Gulf transport checklist default is Careem or Uber after any drink.

## Is public intoxication a separate offence in Dubai?

Public intoxication is a separate criminal offence from driving and typically covers loud or stumbling behaviour on streets, beaches outside licensed clubs, and airport departures. The pavement outside a bar counts as public space after a AED 250-700 brunch package. Invest Gulf nightlife notes say finish inside the venue and taxi home.

| Situation | Risk |
| --- | --- |
| Stumbling or loud behaviour on a public street | Police arrest |
| Drunk behaviour at beach (non-licensed area) | Criminal offence |
| Drunk at airport (departures) | Boarding denial or arrest |
