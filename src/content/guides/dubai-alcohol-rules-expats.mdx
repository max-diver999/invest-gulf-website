What is legal:

- Drinking at a licensed hotel bar or restaurant
- Drinking at a licensed beach club or brunch venue
- Purchasing from MMI or African+Eastern with valid licence (or as a resident in practice)
- Drinking in a private residence
- Receiving alcohol as a gift

What is illegal:

- Public drinking or visible intoxication in any public space
- Drinking on beaches outside licensed beach clubs
- Drinking in parks, streets, car parks, or non-licensed premises
- Drinking in a vehicle (even as a passenger)
- Driving with any detectable blood alcohol
- Purchasing without a licence (technically, enforcement varies)
- Minors (under 21) consuming alcohol
- Muslims purchasing or consuming alcohol

## FAQ

Can I have alcohol delivered to my home in Dubai?
Yes, MMI and African+Eastern both offer home delivery. Some third-party apps also partner with licensed retailers. Confirm the delivery service requires licence or residency proof.

What should I do if I am stopped by police after drinking at a hotel?
If walking (not driving): remain calm, be cooperative, and explain clearly that you were at a licensed hotel. If any doubt, contact your embassy. Do not be combative. The most common resolution for non-driving incidents is a warning.

Can I have alcohol at a beach barbecue?
Only if the beach is within the perimeter of a licensed beach club. Public beaches in Dubai do not permit alcohol consumption. Kite Beach, Jumeirah Open Beach, and JBR Beach are public, no alcohol.

Is wine at a licensed restaurant different from wine at a hotel?
No legal difference for the drinker. Both are licensed venues. The licence is held by the venue. Your experience is the same.

Can I bring wine from France in my checked luggage?
Non-Muslim arrivals aged 18+ may bring up to 4 litres of alcohol (wine, spirits) duty-free. Declare above this limit at customs. Bring as checked baggage, not hand luggage, same rules as most countries.

What if I am pulled over and have had one drink three hours ago?
Any measurable blood alcohol is illegal. There is no safe amount. Use taxis if you have consumed any alcohol.

Does a personal liquor licence limit what I can buy?
The licence sets a monthly spending limit which you specify and can adjust. There is no restriction on type of product (beer, wine, spirits).

Can I find alcohol-free alternatives in Dubai supermarkets?
Yes, non-alcoholic beers (Heineken 0.0, Bavaria 0.0, etc.) and non-alcoholic wines are widely available in supermarkets. These are popular options during Ramadan and for Muslim colleagues at social events. See [Dubai relocation guide](/guides/dubai-relocation-guide/) for grocery shopping context.
