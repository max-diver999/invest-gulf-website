## Can Pakistanis legally buy in Dubai?

Yes. Restrictions are geographic (designated freehold zones), not nationality-based. A Pakistani passport holder buys the same way as any other foreign national:

1. Select unit in freehold zone
2. Sign MOU / SPA
3. Pay via documented transfer
4. Register Oqood (off-plan) or Title Deed (ready) at DLD
5. Apply for Golden Visa separately if eligible

Not allowed: buying outside freehold zones as foreign freehold owner. Your agent must confirm zone status, verify on Dubai REST app.

## Money transfer: PKR to AED

Pakistani buyers typically fund purchases through:

| Channel | Notes |
|  |  |
| SWIFT wire (Pakistani bank → UAE bank) | Standard for large amounts; SBP documentation |
| Exchange houses (licensed) | Common for staged payments; keep receipts |
| Existing UAE account | If already resident, simpler trustee settlement |
| Off-plan milestones | Match transfer timing to SPA schedule |

Practical steps:

1. Confirm SBP remittance policy and annual limits for your account category
2. Open or use UAE bank account before trustee appointment (recommended)
3. Retain FTT / SWIFT confirmations: DLD and banks may request source of funds
4. Never use unlicensed hawala for property: trustee and compliance risk

For off-plan, developers accept transfers to RERA escrow only, verify escrow account on Dubai REST before first dirham.

## UAE banking for Pakistani buyers

| Buyer type | Banking path |
|  |  |
| UAE resident (employment visa) | Salary account → straightforward large transfers |
| Non-resident | Some banks offer non-resident accounts; higher minimums (confirm locally before purchase) |

Banks apply KYC, source-of-funds, and PEP screening regardless of passport. Pakistani nationals are not blocked as a category, but incomplete documentation delays transfers.

Prepare: tax returns, business ownership proof, sale contracts if funding from asset disposal, and salary certificates if employed in UAE/GCC.

## Mortgage option

UAE banks (conventional and Islamic finance) lend to Pakistani nationals with:

- Proof of income (UAE or overseas)
- Bank statements (typically 6-12 months)
- Property in approved building list
- LTV up to 50-80% depending on profile

Non-residents face lower LTV and higher rates. Islamic products use Ijara/Murabaha structures, compare total cost vs conventional.

Golden Visa note: mortgage may affect AED 2M eligibility, verify whether net equity must hit threshold.

## Tax: UAE and Pakistan

| Jurisdiction | Rule |
|  |  |
| UAE | 0% personal income tax on rental income locally |
| Pakistan (FBR) | Tax residents may report foreign income and assets, rules depend on days in Pakistan and residential status |
| Wealth statement | Pakistani tax residents declare foreign assets in annual wealth statement |

Practical example: A Pakistani tax resident owns a JVC apartment generating AED 50,000 annual rent (approximately PKR 3.8 million). The rent stays in a UAE bank account.

- UAE tax: AED 0
- Pakistan tax: Potentially reportable as foreign income depending on residential status under FBR rules
- Wealth statement: Dubai property value and rental income may require declaration

Dubai's zero tax is not automatic zero tax worldwide. Pakistani tax residents should consult FBR-qualified advisers before assuming rental income is invisible at home.

## Pakistani buyer mistakes to avoid

1. Unlicensed hawala transfers: compliance failure at trustee
2. Assuming UAE 0% tax means Pakistan 0% tax: FBR reporting may apply
3. Buying on gross yield: model net after service charges and vacancy
4. No Ejari registration: cannot enforce tenancy at RDC
5. Exceeding SBP limits without planning: stage transfers with bank documentation
6. Golden Visa marketing without registered value: verify DLD registration
7. Using unlicensed agents: verify RERA BRN before engaging

## Rental operations tips

- Use Ejari transacted rents, not Property Finder asking prices
- Standard lease: 1-4 cheques, Pakistani tenants often prefer structured cheques
- Register tenancy on Ejari, mandatory
- Budget DEWA transfer between tenants
- Consider agent management if living in Pakistan, 5-8% of rent

## Red flags for Pakistani buyers

- Project without RERA escrow (off-plan)
- Broker promising "guaranteed 12% yield", usually inflated price
- Transfer via unlicensed channels, compliance failure
- Buying non-freehold stock marketed as ownership
- Golden Visa purchase without registered value confirmation
- Skipping SPA legal review to save AED 5,000

## Family relocation and schooling

Pakistani families buying Dubai property often anchor decisions to **school catchment**, not yield alone:

| School corridor | Communities to shortlist |
|  |  |
| Jumeirah / Umm Suqeim | Arabian Ranches, Dubai Hills, JVC fringe |
| Dubai Marina / JLT | JLT, Marina, Sports City (bus routes) |
| New Dubai south | Dubai South, Town Square, parts of Al Furjan |

**Practical sequence for relocating families:**

1. Shortlist schools and confirm **seat availability** before locking community
2. Buy or rent **ready** property within commute tolerance: off-plan delays clash with school year
3. Register tenancy on **Ejari** if renting first while buying
4. File Golden Visa after title: children sponsorship typically follows main applicant (confirm with ICP)

Property near good schools trades at **yield discount**, budget 5-6% gross in family zones vs 7-9% in pure yield pockets.

## Power of attorney and remote purchase

Pakistani buyers abroad frequently use **POA** for trustee appointments:

| Step | Detail |
|  |  |
| Draft POA | UAE-format or Pakistan notarised, solicitor must approve |
| Attestation | MOFA UAE + Pakistan consulate chain if signed in Pakistan |
| Scope | Limit POA to **specific property**, not blanket authority |
| Trustee day | Attorney attends with your passport copy and attested POA |
| Funds | Must still come from **your documented account** |

POA does not bypass **source-of-funds** rules. Banks and trustees match wire sender to buyer name.

## Currency risk: PKR vs AED

The AED is **pegged to USD** (AED 3.67). Pakistani buyers face **PKR conversion risk**, not AED volatility:

| Strategy | When it helps |
|  |  |
| Stage transfers on PKR strength | Off-plan milestones align naturally |
| Keep UAE account balance | Reduces conversion timing pressure at trustee |
| Model worst-case PKR move | Stress-test total AED capital needed +15% |
| Dollar earners in Pakistan | IT exports, remittances, smoother than pure PKR savers |

Do not assume today's PKR rate holds through a **3-year off-plan** payment plan.

## Currency and PKR planning

PKR volatility against AED matters for **overseas earners**. Practical approach:

1. Budget purchase in **AED**: peg removes USD noise
2. Convert PKR in **tranches** aligned with SPA: not one heroic transfer if limits apply
3. Keep **6-month fee reserve** in AED account post-purchase (service charges, vacancy)
4. Model **worst-case PKR move**: does deal still work at 10% worse FX?

1. Confirm SBP remittance path and document every SWIFT transfer
2. Open UAE bank account before trustee appointment
3. Model net yield with service charges, management, and FBR tax exposure
4. Select area by goal: yield (JVC/Sports City) or premium (Marina)
5. Verify agent RERA licence and property via Dubai REST
6. Engage independent solicitor for SPA review
7. Register Ejari on every tenancy
8. Apply for Golden Visa if purchase exceeds AED 2M
