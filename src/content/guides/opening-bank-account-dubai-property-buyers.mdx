## What documents do employed expats bring?
Employed expats typically bring passport, visa, Emirates ID or receipt, salary certificate, and Ejari or title deed for address proof. ## What extra KYC do property investors face?
Property investors typically face enhanced KYC: a one-page source-of-funds story, title deed or Oqood, and 3 to 6 months of home-country statements. Golden Visa files often take 1 to 3 weeks rather than the employed 3 to 7 business day path. Invest Gulf finishes the SOF pack before any SPA deposit window.
