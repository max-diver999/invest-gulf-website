## What are the foreign freehold rules in Abu Dhabi?

Foreign freehold ownership in Abu Dhabi is limited to designated Investment Zones under Law 19/2005, with title registered at DMT rather than Dubai's DLD. Outside those zones, foreigners typically hold 99-year musataha or usufruct rights. Transfer fees are 2% versus Dubai's 4%, so total acquisition costs usually land near 3 to 4% instead of 6 to 7%.

Abu Dhabi freehold for foreigners means title registered with DMT inside nine designated Investment Zones under Law 19/2005, not the open 60-plus community model used in Dubai. Transfer fees sit at 2% versus Dubai's 4% DLD charge, and total acquisition costs typically land near 3 to 4% instead of 6 to 7%. Prices for comparable apartments run roughly 30% below Dubai mid-market levels, with city benchmarks near AED 1,900 per sqft against Dubai mid-market bands of AED 1,400 to 2,200. Foreign buyers already account for about 88% of recent Aldar residential sales, so liquidity exists inside the main zones even though the freehold map is tighter. Before any reservation deposit, confirm the specific unit's freehold status on DMT systems rather than trusting a brochure community name alone, then compare fee stacks across emirates before you reserve.

Law 19/2005 and amendments (including Law 13/2019 designated zone updates) govern foreign ownership. All transactions register with DMT (Department of Municipalities and Transport), Abu Dhabi's equivalent of Dubai's DLD.

Insider tip: On Al Reem and Yas Island, ask the conveyancer to pull the DMT zone designation for the exact building plot, not the master-plan brochure map, because a few towers sit on the edge of designated freehold boundaries.

The legal evolution is significant. Before 2005, foreign property ownership in Abu Dhabi was effectively prohibited outside diplomatic or corporate structures. The gradual opening mirrors Dubai's timeline but with deliberately controlled supply zones rather than Dubai's liberal expansion approach.

Practical rule: If you want clean freehold title as a foreign national, buy inside a designated Investment Zone below.

### Due Diligence Requirements

Every foreign buyer must verify freehold status through DMT's Property Registration System. Unlike Dubai's DLD transparency, Abu Dhabi requires formal legal verification for each unit. The process includes:

1. Title verification through DMT or approved conveyancing firm
2. Master developer confirmation of zone designation for specific buildings
3. Municipality clearance ensuring no outstanding service dues
4. ADDC utility transfer coordination with DMT registration

## Does Abu Dhabi freehold qualify for Golden Visa?

Golden Visa eligibility for Abu Dhabi freehold requires DMT-registered value of AED 2 million or more in a qualifying zone, matching Dubai's federal threshold. Count registered value, not transfer fees or soft-furnishing packages. Our underwriting for foreign buyers treats off-plan DMT registration as mandatory before Golden Visa maths.
