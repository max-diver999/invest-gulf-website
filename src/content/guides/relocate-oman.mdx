## The three mistakes we fix most often

### 1. Total package looks great — basic salary blocks family visa

Housing OMR 200 + basic OMR 280 = headline OMR 480 — but ROP may reject dependants. The basic line is what counts.

→ [Oman family visa](/guides/oman-family-visa/)

### 2. Lease signed — then school says no place

School deposits are non-refundable. Apply before you pick a neighbourhood.

→ [Muscat international schools](/guides/muscat-international-schools/)

### 3. Bought ITC apartment — residency track misunderstood

Property ownership and work visa are separate. Get ROP legal review before any deposit.
