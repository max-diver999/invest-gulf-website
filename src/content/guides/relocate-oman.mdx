- Default path: employer-sponsored residence, not brochure retirement marketing

## Which Muscat areas match daily routines?

Muscat area choice typically means matching commute and school runs before touring listings: Al Mouj waterfront for many 2-3BR flats, Qurum central access, MQ quieter value, and Al Khoud budget space with 30 to 45 minute CBD drives on we shortlist across a 12 month lease for foreign arrivals.

### Al Mouj (Waterfront master community)

- Rent range: OMR 600-1,500/month for 2-3BR apartments, OMR 1,200-3,000/month for villas
- On-site school, marina, golf; 25-35 minutes to central business areas
- Freehold available for foreigners in designated stock

### Qurum (Established expat favorite)

- Rent range: OMR 400-900/month apartments, OMR 800-1,800/month villas
- Multiple international schools within 10-15 minutes
- Higher peak-hour traffic than MQ

### Madinat Qaboos (MQ)

- Rent range: OMR 350-700/month apartments, OMR 650-1,200/month villas
- Quieter planned streets; 15-20 minutes to most international schools

### Al Khoud and Azaiba

- Al Khoud: OMR 280-550/month apartments, 30-45 minute CBD commute
- Azaiba: OMR 250-500/month apartments, more local character, 25-35 minutes to major schools

## What should buyers understand about ITC ownership before purchasing?

ITC ownership typically means freehold only in designated zones such as Al Mouj, Muscat Bay, and Saraya Bandar Jissah, with registration near 3% of property value and investor residence rules that we separate from SPA signing across a 30 to 90 day diligence window.

| Cost line | Indicative band |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | About 3% of property value |
| Legal fees | OMR 500-2,000 |
| Inspection | OMR 200-500 |
| Service charges | OMR 50-200 monthly |

Investor residence thresholds are commonly cited near OMR 250,000; confirm current official ROP rules before transfer. Ownership does not automatically grant work permission.
