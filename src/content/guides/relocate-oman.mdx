### Family visa sponsorship process

Eligibility requirements:
- Basic salary meeting family sponsorship threshold (varies by family size)
- Suitable accommodation verified by authorities
- Health insurance coverage for all family members
- Clean criminal record for sponsor

Required documents:
- Attested marriage certificate (embassy and foreign ministry attestation)
- Birth certificates for children (similarly attested)
- Medical fitness certificates for all family members
- Passport-size photographs meeting Oman specifications
- No Objection Certificate from employer

Processing timeline:
- Initial sponsor visa: 4-6 weeks after job start
- Family joining application: 6-8 weeks after sponsor visa approval
- Total timeline from job offer to family arrival: 3-4 months typically

Common complications:
- Document attestation delays from home country
- Medical fitness issues requiring additional testing
- Accommodation inspection failures
- Salary documentation not meeting ROP requirements
