### UK statutory residency test

The UK SRT assesses days present, ties (property, family, work), and previous residence history. A UK citizen who:

- Retains a UK home
- Has a spouse/partner in the UK
- Works for a UK employer (even remotely from Dubai)

…may remain UK resident under the "sufficient ties" test despite spending 250 days in the UAE.

**Action required:** obtain a formal exit date determination, ideally from a UK-qualified adviser, before you leave. Submit form P85. UK property rental income remains taxable in the UK even after non-residence is confirmed.

### German residency criteria

Germany applies **"habitual abode"** and **"domicile"** tests. Keeping a flat in Hamburg while based in Dubai may be enough to maintain German unlimited tax liability. Double taxation treaty (UAE–Germany) helps but does not eliminate all German obligations.

### Australian residency

Australia uses a facts-and-circumstances test with the **domicile test** as a fallback. The ATO has actively challenged expats claiming foreign tax residency who kept bank accounts, property and family in Australia.

## FAQ

**Does Dubai have income tax for remote workers?**
No UAE personal income tax exists. Salary, freelance, dividends, zero UAE tax for individuals. Home-country obligations may remain.

**How many days in Dubai for UAE tax residency?**
183 days in a calendar year is the primary threshold. Secondary 90-day rule applies with specific ties and no other tax domicile.

**Do I still owe home-country tax?**
Possibly. UK, Germany, Australia and others assess residency on ties, not just days. Formal exit analysis required before moving.

**What is the UAE corporate tax rate?**
9% on taxable business profits above AED 375,000. Does not apply to individual employees. Small business relief available under AED 3M revenue.

**Which visa is best for remote workers?**
Remote Work Visa (1-year) for those with foreign employer; Green Visa (5-year) for freelancers and skilled professionals. See [remote-work-visa-vs-green-visa](/guides/remote-work-visa-vs-green-visa/).

**Can I open a UAE bank account as a remote worker?**
Yes, with valid UAE residency visa. Emirates NBD, ADCB, Mashreq all accept remote workers.

**Is crypto taxed in Dubai?**
No UAE capital gains or income tax on individual crypto gains. Corporate entities follow corporate tax rules.

**What about rental income from my UK property?**
UK retains taxing rights on UK-source rental income even after you become UAE tax resident. UAE-UK DTA does not eliminate this.

**Do I need an FTA tax domicile certificate?**
Strongly recommended if you plan to claim treaty relief with your home country. FTA issues the certificate once 183-day threshold is met.
