# Remote Work Dubai: Full Tax Guide for Expats 2026

TL;DR: Dubai charges zero personal income tax, no salary tax, no dividend tax, no capital gains tax for individuals. To benefit fully you need to establish UAE tax residency (183+ days), formally sever ties with your home country, and understand the new 9% UAE corporate tax which affects business structures but not employed individuals. Get a cross-border adviser before you book the one-way flight.

> Disclaimer: Tax rules change. This guide is informational, not legal or tax advice. Confirm all thresholds, treaty positions and residency criteria with a qualified tax adviser before acting.

## Why Dubai attracts remote workers: the tax picture

Dubai's core appeal is structural: the UAE levies no personal income tax. Salary, freelance income, dividends, and most investment returns for individuals are not taxed at the UAE level. This applies whether your employer is a UAE entity or a foreign company paying you from London, Berlin, or Singapore.

That is not the whole story. Three complications matter:

1. Home-country residency rules: your departure may not end your obligations there.
2. UAE corporate tax (2023): affects business structures, not employees.
3. UAE tax residency criteria: you must actually qualify, not just assume.

Understanding all three is the difference between a clean tax position and an unexpected liability letter from HMRC or Finanzamt two years after you moved.

## The UAE personal income tax zero rate: what it covers

The UAE has no:

- Personal income tax on salary
- Capital gains tax on individual investment returns
- Dividend tax for individuals
- Wealth tax
- Inheritance tax

What does exist at the individual level:

| Tax type | Rate | Who pays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal income tax | 0% | Nobody |
| VAT (purchases) | 5% | Consumers |
| Corporate tax on business profits | 9% above AED 375K | Business entities, not employees |
| Excise tax (tobacco, energy drinks) | 50–100% | Manufacturers/importers |
| Property transfer (DLD) | 4% on transaction | Buyer (one-off) |

For a remote worker collecting salary from a foreign employer while living in Dubai, the UAE-side tax bill is: zero.

## UAE tax residency: the 183-day rule

Having a UAE residence visa does not automatically make you a UAE tax resident for international purposes. The **Federal Tax Authority (FTA)** issues tax domicile certificates under two conditions:

**Primary path, 183 days:**
Spend 183+ days in the UAE in any consecutive 12-month period. This creates UAE tax residency from the FTA's perspective and is the evidence most home countries accept to accept a treaty exit.

**Secondary path, 90 days:**
If you have no other country recognising you as a tax resident, 90 days of presence plus certain UAE ties (residence visa, property, business activity) may qualify.

Practical implications:

| Scenario | UAE tax residency status |
| --- | --- |
| In Dubai 200 days, visa active | Likely qualifies under 183-day rule |
| In Dubai 100 days, UK domicile maintained | Likely not UAE tax resident; UK likely still primary |
| Full-time Dubai, no home-country property or family | May qualify under 90-day secondary rule |
| Splitting time 90 UAE / 130 India | UAE secondary rule possibly; India residency still active |

## Home-country exit: where remote workers trip up

The most common mistake: **assuming that arriving in Dubai ends home-country obligations**. Most high-tax jurisdictions have residency tests that look beyond physical presence.

### UK statutory residency test

The UK SRT assesses days present, ties (property, family, work), and previous residence history. A UK citizen who:

- Retains a UK home
- Has a spouse/partner in the UK
- Works for a UK employer (even remotely from Dubai)

…may remain UK resident under the "sufficient ties" test despite spending 250 days in the UAE.

**Action required:** obtain a formal exit date determination, ideally from a UK-qualified adviser, before you leave. Submit form P85. UK property rental income remains taxable in the UK even after non-residence is confirmed.

### German residency criteria

Germany applies **"habitual abode"** and **"domicile"** tests. Keeping a flat in Hamburg while based in Dubai may be enough to maintain German unlimited tax liability. Double taxation treaty (UAE–Germany) helps but does not eliminate all German obligations.

### Australian residency

Australia uses a facts-and-circumstances test with the **domicile test** as a fallback. The ATO has actively challenged expats claiming foreign tax residency who kept bank accounts, property and family in Australia.

### General checklist before exit

| Step | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Obtain UAE tax domicile certificate | FTA issues for 183-day qualifiers; needed for treaty relief |
| File exit tax return in home country | Crystallises departure date and capital positions |
| Close or restructure home accounts | Some countries trigger residency from bank ties |
| Review property rental obligations | Most countries retain right to tax domestic rents |
| Get dual-tax treaty advice | UAE has treaties with 100+ countries, not all same terms |

## UAE corporate tax: what remote workers need to know

From **June 2023**, the UAE introduced a **9% corporate tax** on taxable business profits above AED 375,000. This does not affect employees. It does affect:

- Freelancers operating under a UAE commercial licence
- Sole establishments (sole proprietorships)
- Free zone companies (qualifying vs non-qualifying income rules apply)
- Individuals who run a business in addition to remote employment

### Small business relief

Entities with revenue under **AED 3 million** can elect for **small business relief**, effectively reducing their taxable income to zero for eligible periods. Verify current MoF guidance, the threshold and conditions have administrative nuance.

### Employed vs freelance comparison

| Status | Corporate tax applies? | Personal income tax? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salaried employee (UAE or foreign employer) | No | No |
| Freelancer under UAE licence | Potentially, above AED 375K profit | No |
| Consultant via UAE free zone company | Qualifying income rules apply | No |
| PJSC shareholder (individual) | No (corporate level only) | No |

## Property taxes and investment income in Dubai

Dubai has **no annual property tax** in the ongoing sense used in the UK or US. What exists:

- **DLD transfer fee:** 4% paid at purchase (one-off)
- **Service charges:** building/community maintenance, not a government tax
- **VAT:** applies to some commercial transactions, not standard residential sales

Rental income earned from Dubai property by a UAE resident: **zero UAE tax**.

Rental income from a property in your home country while you are UAE resident: **zero UAE tax**, but **home-country tax almost certainly applies**.

Capital gains from selling Dubai property: **zero UAE capital gains tax** for individuals.

Capital gains from selling home-country assets while UAE resident: treaty-dependent, often still taxable in home country.

## Visa structure for remote workers: the legal foundation

Working from Dubai without a valid residency visa creates immigration risk regardless of tax position. Three main paths for remote workers:

### UAE Remote Work Visa

- Duration: 1 year, renewable
- Requirement: employed outside UAE, minimum income threshold (verify current MOHRE level)
- Allows: legal residence, UAE bank account, driving licence
- Tax note: presence on this visa counts toward 183-day UAE tax residency

→ [UAE Remote Work Visa full guide](/guides/uae-remote-work-visa/)

### UAE Green Visa: freelancer track

- Duration: 5 years, self-sponsored
- Requirement: UAE freelance permit or skilled professional criteria
- Requirement: minimum annual income threshold and educational qualifications (verify)
- Tax note: strongest long-term residency position, counts toward 183-day rule

→ [Green Visa vs Remote Work Visa comparison](/guides/remote-work-visa-vs-green-visa/)

### UAE Freelance Permit

- Issued by TECOM, RAKEZ, Sharjah Media City, and other free zones
- Enables legal UAE business activity under individual name
- Subject to UAE corporate tax rules if structured as business entity

## Banking and financial setup for tax efficiency

Establishing clean UAE banking is both a practical and compliance step:

**Why UAE banking matters for tax position:**
- Demonstrates economic ties to UAE
- Required for FTA tax domicile certificate application
- Needed for payroll if switching to UAE-incorporated employer
- Enables separation of UAE-sourced and foreign-sourced income streams

**Opening a UAE account as a remote worker:**
Most major banks require active UAE residency visa plus income proof. Emirates NBD, ADCB, and Mashreq accept remote work visa holders. FAB and HSBC UAE offer international banking products that simplify multi-currency transfers.

**Transfer services:**
Wise, Western Union, and local exchange houses are widely used for salary remittance to home-country accounts. UAE has no capital controls.

## Double taxation treaties: how they help

The UAE has double taxation avoidance agreements (DTAAs) with over 100 countries, including UK, Germany, France, India, Russia, Canada, and many others. These treaties:

- Prevent the same income being taxed fully in both countries
- Typically assign taxing rights to the country of residence or source depending on income type
- Require proof of UAE tax residency (FTA certificate) to invoke treaty relief

**Treaty does NOT mean:**
- Zero obligation in all cases to home country
- Automatic recognition without documentation
- Protection if you never formally established UAE residency

UAE-specific treaty notes:

| Country | Key treaty provision |
| --- | --- |
| UK | DTA exists; UK SRT still determines UK residency independently |
| Germany | DTA exists; German domicile test applies regardless |
| Russia | DTA exists; UAE residency certificate useful for Russian-source income |
| Australia | No DTA as of mid-2026, higher complexity, verify current status |
| USA | No DTA, US citizens taxed on worldwide income regardless of UAE residency |

**US citizens:** US taxes on worldwide income regardless of where you live. UAE residency helps with some investment structures but does not eliminate US filing obligations. FBAR and FATCA apply to UAE accounts above thresholds.

## Side income streams: platform work, dividends, crypto

### Platform work (Upwork, Fiverr, freelance platforms)

Income received by a UAE-resident individual from non-UAE platforms: zero UAE tax. If you operate under a UAE freelance licence, corporate tax considerations apply above AED 375K profit.

### Dividends from foreign company shares

Received by a UAE-resident individual: zero UAE tax. Home country may withhold at source, treaty claim possible with FTA certificate.

### Crypto trading gains

UAE has no capital gains tax for individuals. Crypto is treated as a commodity for regulatory purposes. Corporate entities trading crypto fall under corporate tax rules. Home-country treatment varies dramatically, verify specifically.

### Rental income from home-country property

Home country retains taxing rights in almost all UAE DTAAs. UAE tax certificate confirms your residency but does not eliminate UK or German source-country tax on UK or German property.

## FAQ

**Does Dubai have income tax for remote workers?**
No UAE personal income tax exists. Salary, freelance, dividends, zero UAE tax for individuals. Home-country obligations may remain.

**How many days in Dubai for UAE tax residency?**
183 days in a calendar year is the primary threshold. Secondary 90-day rule applies with specific ties and no other tax domicile.

**Do I still owe home-country tax?**
Possibly. UK, Germany, Australia and others assess residency on ties, not just days. Formal exit analysis required before moving.

**What is the UAE corporate tax rate?**
9% on taxable business profits above AED 375,000. Does not apply to individual employees. Small business relief available under AED 3M revenue.

**Which visa is best for remote workers?**
Remote Work Visa (1-year) for those with foreign employer; Green Visa (5-year) for freelancers and skilled professionals. See [remote-work-visa-vs-green-visa](/guides/remote-work-visa-vs-green-visa/).

**Can I open a UAE bank account as a remote worker?**
Yes, with valid UAE residency visa. Emirates NBD, ADCB, Mashreq all accept remote workers.

**Is crypto taxed in Dubai?**
No UAE capital gains or income tax on individual crypto gains. Corporate entities follow corporate tax rules.

**What about rental income from my UK property?**
UK retains taxing rights on UK-source rental income even after you become UAE tax resident. UAE-UK DTA does not eliminate this.

**Do I need an FTA tax domicile certificate?**
Strongly recommended if you plan to claim treaty relief with your home country. FTA issues the certificate once 183-day threshold is met.
