Owner action: Call GDRFA Dubai or ICP with your exact Title Deed / Oqood before you structure finance.

## Why does the 2026 Golden Visa mortgage rule change matter?

Dubai's policy incentive: keep foreign capital entering even with bank leverage, as long as registered value hits AED 2M and loans stay onshore UAE banks.

## How does registered value differ from equity for Golden Visa?

Equity alone no longer drives the headline test under updated guidance, registered price does.

## What should buyers verify on application document pack (mortgaged)?

Plan a mortgaged Golden Visa file as three stacks: property, bank, and immigration. Property: Title Deed or Oqood showing registered value at least AED 2 million, SPA if Oqood is recent, and DLD receipt for the 4% fee if already paid. Bank: mortgage offer or account letter, outstanding balance statement dated within 30 days, and the NOC on bank letterhead. Immigration: passport copies, medical fitness (if required on your route), Emirates ID if resident, and attested documents for dependants. Missing one bank page is the most common reason files bounce back from typing centres.

## How does cash purchase compare with a mortgaged Golden Visa route?

Cash buyers skip NOC friction and often close immigration faster, but they tie up AED 2 million or more in illiquid property. Mortgaged buyers keep liquidity for school fees, business capital, or a second UAE asset, yet they depend on bank turnaround and EIBOR moves. Model 12 months of mortgage service plus visa renewal rules before you choose leverage purely for visa speed.

## How does step-by-step compare for Gulf buyers in 2026?

A practical sequence: select qualifying stock, complete DLD registration, draw the bank NOC, book medicals if required, file Golden Visa through ICP or GDRFA, then register Emirates ID for dependants. Allow 4 to 8 weeks end to end when mortgage and visa run in parallel, longer if Oqood on off-plan is still pending.

## When is mortgage plus Golden Visa a bad idea?

Skip leverage if the registered price barely clears AED 2 million, if your bank will not issue an NOC on off-plan, or if EIBOR plus fees push debt service above 35% of stable income. Golden Visa is a residency tool, not a substitute for affordable housing math.

## What should Gulf buyers budget for EIBOR and rate risk for visa holders?

UAE mortgages reprice with EIBOR. A 1 percentage point rise on a AED 1.5 million loan adds roughly AED 15,000 per year in interest, which does not affect visa eligibility directly but can stress cash flow if you counted on rental income to cover the note. Keep 6 to 12 months of payments liquid after you file immigration.

## What should buyers verify on bank-by-bank noc practical notes?

Bring: Title Deed or Oqood, Emirates ID if already resident, passport, mortgage account number. Non-resident buyers without Emirates ID yet can still request NOC after DLD registration in some banks, call ahead.

Mortgage interest deduction considerations:
While UAE has no personal income tax, international tax treatment of mortgage interest varies:
- UK landlords: Mortgage interest treated as allowable expense against rental income
- US persons: Limited deductions on foreign rental property, potential Section 988 currency issues
- Canadian tax residents: Rental property mortgage interest fully deductible against rental income
- Indian NRIs: Interest deduction available under standard deduction or actual expenses method

Currency and foreign exchange implications:
- UAE mortgage payments in AED while rental income may be earned in multiple currencies
- Foreign exchange gains/losses on mortgage principal may create tax implications in some jurisdictions
- Hedging strategies using UAE bank products or international currency forwards
- Regular mortgage payments create ongoing currency exposure management requirements
