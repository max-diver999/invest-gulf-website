## Is Al Marjan Island an appreciation or yield play?

At 2026 launch pricing Al Marjan models 3-5% gross long-term with appreciation tied to Wynn Al Marjan opening in 2027.


Risk: Wynn delay, regulatory change, or lower-than-expected regional visitor volumes compress appreciation thesis.

### Al Marjan off-plan developer landscape

- Soil testing and foundation engineering reports
- Architectural and engineering firm credentials
- Environmental impact assessments completed
- Utility connection agreements in place

- Exit strategy options given secondary market liquidity

- Compare service charge estimates to completed comparable projects

## How do banks finance RAK off-plan?

LTV is often lower than Dubai with 5-10% valuation haircuts; submit to multiple lenders.


International and private banks:
- HSBC UAE: 60% LTV, premium pricing, extensive due diligence requirements
- Standard Chartered: 65% LTV, focus on branded residence projects
- Citibank UAE: Limited appetite, ultra-HNW clients only

Financing challenges specific to RAK:
- Limited comparable sales data for property valuations
- Bank valuers often apply 5-10% haircut to purchase prices
- Longer processing times than Dubai equivalents
- Some lenders require higher down payments for Northern Emirates

### Mortgage process optimization for RAK off-plan

Pre-approval strategy:
- Obtain pre-approval before SPA signing to confirm financing terms
- Submit applications to multiple banks given limited RAK appetite
- Prepare for potentially lower valuations than purchase price
- Consider UAE national bank preference over international banks

Documentation requirements:
- Standard UAE mortgage documents plus RAK-specific forms
- Property consultant report on RAK market and project viability
- Developer financial strength assessment
- Detailed project timeline and delivery milestone documentation
