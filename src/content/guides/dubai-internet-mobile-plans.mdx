Dubai home fibre in 2026 typically costs AED 299 for 100 Mbps, AED 349 for 250 Mbps, AED 399 for 500 Mbps, and AED 449 for 1 Gbps on representative du tiers, with e& pricing in a near-identical band. Installation and the router are usually free on a 12-month contract, while a refundable security deposit of AED 300 to 500 is standard. Five-G home broadband from AED 249 to 369 per month delivers 200 to 600 Mbps and suits villas awaiting fibre or renters who need activation in two to three days. Latency on 5G home commonly runs 15 to 30 ms versus 5 to 10 ms on fibre, which is the main reason gamers still prefer a wired line. Expats should check DEWA premise availability on du.ae or etisalat.ae before comparing headline speeds, because coverage beats brand preference in older villa communities and newer JVC towers alike.

A family-of-four telecom budget in Dubai usually combines one home connection with four SIMs rather than four separate retail contracts. Four mid-tier 60 GB postpaid lines on a household plan often total AED 558 to 575 per month after 20 to 30% family discounts, while buying the same lines a la carte costs materially more. Adding 500 Mbps fibre at AED 399 without a converged offer can push the stack above AED 1,100 monthly, whereas a matched home-plus-mobile bundle near AED 879 saves roughly AED 240 per month or about AED 2,880 per year. Prepaid remains useful for the first 30 days at roughly AED 150 to 200 total until Emirates ID enables postpaid. Invest Gulf relocation checklists treat AED 650 to 900 as the planning band for a connected family of four once fibre or 5G home is live.

Insider tip: Apply for fibre the same day you sign the tenancy contract in busy areas such as Marina, JLT, or Dubai Hills, because technician queues of 1 to 2 weeks are common and DEWA name matching delays activation when EJARI details are incomplete.

## What do du and e& fibre plans cost in 2026?
Home fibre plans from du and e& in 2026 typically cost AED 299 to 499 per month for unlimited 100 Mbps to 2 Gbps tiers on a 12-month contract, usually with a free router and installation. Security deposits of AED 300 to 500 are common and refund when the account closes in the area.

| Speed | Monthly price | Best for |

| 100 Mbps | AED 299 | Couple, light WFH |
| 250 Mbps | AED 349 | Family of four, streaming |

Setup fee: typically AED 0 with 12-month contract. Security deposit: AED 300 (refunded on cancellation). Early termination: one month's fee or remaining months, whichever is less.

### E& Home Fibre Plans 2026

e& also offers eLife triple-play bundles (internet + TV + phone landline) starting at AED 449/month at 250 Mbps, which is worth evaluating if your household watches Arabic-language sports channels included in the package.

### Which Speed Tier Do You Actually Need?

- 100 Mbps, sufficient for a couple streaming 4K on two devices and two people working from home on video calls simultaneously.
- 250 Mbps, comfortable for a family of four with heavy simultaneous use: gaming, 4K streaming, and WFH without any throttling.
- 500 Mbps to 1 Gbps, relevant if you have a home office uploading large files, run a NAS/home server, or have 5+ heavy users. Future-proof but not necessary for most families.
- 2 Gbps, overkill for household use; useful if you run a home business or need dedicated bandwidth for multiple workstations.

For a typical [expat family in Dubai](/guides/dubai-monthly-budget-expat-family/), the 250 Mbps du plan at AED 349/month represents the best value sweet spot.

## When does a prepaid SIM make sense?
Prepaid SIMs typically make sense for the first weeks in Dubai and for visitors because activation costs AED 55 to 75 with starter credit and needs no Emirates ID at many kiosks. Residents usually switch to postpaid after ID issue for better value on 30 to 100 GB packs in the area.

**How to get one:**
- Available at any du or e& store, most supermarkets (Carrefour, Lulu), airport kiosks, and petrol stations
- Cost: AED 55 to 75 including the SIM and AED 20 to 30 starter credit
- No ID required at some kiosks; passport or visit visa accepted at official stores

Prepaid data packs (representative rates):

| Weekly pack | 10 GB | 7 days | 35 to 45 |
| Monthly pack | 30 GB | 30 days | 80 to 100 |
| Monthly pack | 100 GB | 30 days | 150 to 180 |

For your [first 30 days in Dubai](/guides/first-30-days-dubai-expat/), budget around AED 150 to 200 on prepaid until your Emirates ID arrives and you can switch to postpaid. Most expats keep a prepaid SIM as a second line for data backup or family members on visit visas.

## How long does it take to get connected in Dubai?
Getting connected in Dubai typically follows a fixed sequence airport prepaid SIM on day one for AED 55 to 75 postpaid application after Emirates ID in week two to three fibre technician booking in week two to four or 5G home self install within two to three days in the area Confirm the AED day or percent.

Typical setup timeline:

| Step | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Airport prepaid SIM | Day 1 | AED 55 to 75 at arrivals; immediate activation |
| Postpaid SIM application | Week 2 to 3 | Requires Emirates ID (issued after medical + biometrics) |
| Home fibre activation | Week 2 to 4 | Technician visit required; schedule 2 to 3 days in advance |
| 5G home broadband | Days 2 to 3 | Ships to your address; self-install via app |
| Port existing number | Any time after Emirates ID | Takes 24 hours; free; keep your original number |

**Practical tips:**

- Apply for fibre on the day you sign your tenancy contract, popular areas have 1 to 2 week technician queues.
- Both du and e& require the tenant's name to match the DEWA connection; have your EJARI and Emirates ID ready.
- Number portability is free, you can switch operators and keep your UAE number within 24 hours by visiting any store.
- Roaming is expensive; if you travel frequently, activate an international roaming package (AED 25 to 50/day, or AED 150 to 300/month unlimited roaming) rather than paying per-use rates.
- ESIM is available on both networks for iPhone XS and later, Google Pixel 4 and later, and most Samsung Galaxy S series. You can activate immediately via app without visiting a store.
