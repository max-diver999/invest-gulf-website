## Who Provides Internet in Dubai?

Dubai has two licensed telecom operators: du (Emirates Integrated Telecommunications Company) and e& (formerly Etisalat). There is no third option, the market is a regulated duopoly. Both operators provide:

- Home fibre broadband via FTTH (fibre-to-the-home)
- 5G home broadband via fixed wireless access (FWA)
- Postpaid mobile plans (monthly contracts)
- Prepaid mobile SIMs (no contract, recharge as needed)
- Converged home + mobile bundles

Both networks cover Dubai comprehensively. In practice, your choice often comes down to which operator has active fibre infrastructure in your specific building or villa, not every tower is connected to both. Most apartments in JVC, Dubai Marina, Business Bay and Downtown are covered by both. Villa communities (Jumeirah, Springs, Arabian Ranches) vary by street.

Quick check: Enter your building name or DEWA premise number on du.ae or etisalat.ae before comparing plans, availability beats brand preference every time.

## Home Fibre Broadband: du vs e& Plan Comparison

Fibre is the gold standard for Dubai home internet: symmetric speeds, low latency, and no congestion during peak hours. Both operators price their tiers competitively, and introductory offers frequently include free installation, a free router, and three months at a reduced rate.

|---|---|---|---|
| 100 Mbps | AED 299 | Unlimited | 12 months |
| 250 Mbps | AED 349 | Unlimited | 12 months |
| 500 Mbps | AED 399 | Unlimited | 12 months |
| 1 Gbps | AED 449 | Unlimited | 12 months |
| 2 Gbps | AED 499 | Unlimited | 12 months |

Setup fee: typically AED 0 with 12-month contract. Security deposit: AED 300 (refunded on cancellation). Early termination: one month's fee or remaining months, whichever is less.

### e& Home Fibre Plans 2026

| Speed | Monthly Price | Included Data | Contract |

| 100 Mbps | AED 329 | Unlimited | 12 months |

e& also offers eLife triple-play bundles (internet + TV + phone landline) starting at AED 449/month at 250 Mbps, which is worth evaluating if your household watches Arabic-language sports channels included in the package.

### Which Speed Tier Do You Actually Need?

- 100 Mbps, sufficient for a couple streaming 4K on two devices and two people working from home on video calls simultaneously.
- 250 Mbps, comfortable for a family of four with heavy simultaneous use: gaming, 4K streaming, and WFH without any throttling.
- 500 Mbps–1 Gbps, relevant if you have a home office uploading large files, run a NAS/home server, or have 5+ heavy users. Future-proof but not necessary for most families.
- 2 Gbps, overkill for household use; useful if you run a home business or need dedicated bandwidth for multiple workstations.

For a typical [expat family in Dubai](/guides/dubai-monthly-budget-expat-family/), the 250 Mbps du plan at AED 349/month represents the best value sweet spot.

## 5G Home Broadband: A Cable-Free Alternative

Both du and e& offer 5G fixed wireless access routers, a plug-and-play indoor or outdoor device that connects to the 5G network and broadcasts Wi-Fi throughout your home. No technician visit required; the router ships within 24–48 hours, and you activate it via app.

5G home broadband is particularly useful in Dubai for:

- **Villas not yet fibred**, some older villa communities have 5G coverage before FTTH rollout completes
- **Short-term residents** (under 12 months) who prefer month-to-month without a long contract
- **New apartments** where the building fibre hasn't been activated yet
- **Secondary homes or holiday lets** where you need occasional connectivity

### 5G Home Broadband Pricing

| Provider | Speed (typical) | Monthly Price | Contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| du 5G Home | 200–500 Mbps | AED 249–349 | Month-to-month or 12 months |
| e& 5G Home | 200–600 Mbps | AED 269–369 | Month-to-month or 12 months |

5G home plans are around AED 50–100/month cheaper than equivalent fibre tiers and offer genuine flexibility. The tradeoff is slightly higher latency (15–30 ms vs 5–10 ms for fibre) and occasional peak-hour variation in congested areas.

## Prepaid SIMs: Best for New Arrivals and Short-Term Stays

If you arrive in Dubai before your Emirates ID is processed, typically a 2–3 week window, a prepaid SIM is your practical starting point.

**How to get one:**
- Available at any du or e& store, most supermarkets (Carrefour, Lulu), airport kiosks, and petrol stations
- Cost: AED 55–75 including the SIM and AED 20–30 starter credit
- No ID required at some kiosks; passport or visit visa accepted at official stores

**Prepaid data packs (representative rates):**

| Pack | Data | Validity | Price (AED) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily pack | 1–2 GB | 24 hours | 5–8 |
| Weekly pack | 10 GB | 7 days | 35–45 |
| Monthly pack | 30 GB | 30 days | 80–100 |
| Monthly pack | 100 GB | 30 days | 150–180 |

For your [first 30 days in Dubai](/guides/first-30-days-dubai-expat/), budget around AED 150–200 on prepaid until your Emirates ID arrives and you can switch to postpaid. most expats keep a prepaid SIM as a second line for data backup or family members on visit visas.

## Setting Up Connectivity: Timeline and Practicalities

Understanding the activation timeline helps you plan your [Dubai relocation](/guides/dubai-relocation-guide/) without gaps in connectivity.

**Typical setup timeline:**

| Step | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Airport prepaid SIM | Day 1 | AED 55–75 at arrivals; immediate activation |
| Postpaid SIM application | Week 2–3 | Requires Emirates ID (issued after medical + biometrics) |
| Home fibre activation | Week 2–4 | Technician visit required; schedule 2–3 days in advance |
| 5G home broadband | Days 2–3 | Ships to your address; self-install via app |
| Port existing number | Any time after Emirates ID | Takes 24 hours; free; keep your original number |

**Practical tips:**

- **Apply for fibre on the day you sign your tenancy contract**, popular areas have 1–2 week technician queues.
- **Both du and e& require the tenant's name** to match the DEWA connection; have your EJARI and Emirates ID ready.
- **Number portability is free**, you can switch operators and keep your UAE number within 24 hours by visiting any store.
- **Roaming is expensive**; if you travel frequently, activate an international roaming package (AED 25–50/day, or AED 150–300/month unlimited roaming) rather than paying per-use rates.
- **eSIM is available** on both networks for iPhone XS and later, Google Pixel 4 and later, and most Samsung Galaxy S series. You can activate immediately via app without visiting a store.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does home internet cost in Dubai per month?**
Home fibre broadband runs AED 299–499/month for 100 Mbps to 2 Gbps on unlimited data. Both du and e& include free installation and a router on a 12-month contract. A one-time security deposit of AED 300–500 is refunded when you cancel.

**Which is better in Dubai, du or e&?**
Both have comparable coverage and pricing. e& tends to have stronger fibre penetration in older villa communities; du often prices bundles slightly more aggressively and has competitive 5G home broadband in newer developments. Check coverage at your address first, that narrows the choice for most expats.

**Can I use a prepaid SIM in Dubai as an expat?**
Yes. Prepaid SIMs are available from airport kiosks on arrival for AED 55–75. Monthly 30 GB data packs cost around AED 85–100. Once your Emirates ID is ready, switch to a postpaid contract for better data value.

**How much does a family mobile bundle cost in Dubai?**
Four postpaid lines at 60 GB each on a family plan run AED 558–575/month combined after household discounts. Adding home fibre in a converged bundle brings the total to around AED 879/month for fibre plus four 60 GB mobile lines.

**Is 5G home broadband as good as fibre in Dubai?**
For streaming, browsing, and working from home, yes. You get 200–600 Mbps with no installation wait. Fibre remains superior for latency-sensitive tasks (video calls, gaming) and for households with 5+ heavy simultaneous users. If your villa isn't fibred yet, 5G home broadband is a practical and fast alternative.

**Can I bundle home internet and mobile on one account?**
Yes, both du and e& offer converged accounts that save AED 150–300/month versus buying services separately. You manage everything from one app and receive a single bill.
