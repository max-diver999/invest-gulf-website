### Which Speed Tier Do You Actually Need?

- 100 Mbps, sufficient for a couple streaming 4K on two devices and two people working from home on video calls simultaneously.
- 250 Mbps, comfortable for a family of four with heavy simultaneous use: gaming, 4K streaming, and WFH without any throttling.
- 500 Mbps–1 Gbps, relevant if you have a home office uploading large files, run a NAS/home server, or have 5+ heavy users. Future-proof but not necessary for most families.
- 2 Gbps, overkill for household use; useful if you run a home business or need dedicated bandwidth for multiple workstations.

For a typical [expat family in Dubai](/guides/dubai-monthly-budget-expat-family/), the 250 Mbps du plan at AED 349/month represents the best value sweet spot.

How much does home internet cost in Dubai per month?
Home fibre broadband runs AED 299–499/month for 100 Mbps to 2 Gbps on unlimited data. Both du and e& include free installation and a router on a 12-month contract. A one-time security deposit of AED 300–500 is refunded when you cancel.

Which is better in Dubai, du or e&?
Both have comparable coverage and pricing. e& tends to have stronger fibre penetration in older villa communities; du often prices bundles slightly more aggressively and has competitive 5G home broadband in newer developments. Check coverage at your address first, that narrows the choice for most expats.

Can I use a prepaid SIM in Dubai as an expat?
Yes. Prepaid SIMs are available from airport kiosks on arrival for AED 55–75. Monthly 30 GB data packs cost around AED 85–100. Once your Emirates ID is ready, switch to a postpaid contract for better data value.

How much does a family mobile bundle cost in Dubai?
Four postpaid lines at 60 GB each on a family plan run AED 558–575/month combined after household discounts. Adding home fibre in a converged bundle brings the total to around AED 879/month for fibre plus four 60 GB mobile lines.

Is 5G home broadband as good as fibre in Dubai?
For streaming, browsing, and working from home, yes. You get 200–600 Mbps with no installation wait. Fibre remains superior for latency-sensitive tasks (video calls, gaming) and for households with 5+ heavy simultaneous users. If your villa isn't fibred yet, 5G home broadband is a practical and fast alternative.

Can I bundle home internet and mobile on one account?
Yes, both du and e& offer converged accounts that save AED 150–300/month versus buying services separately. You manage everything from one app and receive a single bill.
