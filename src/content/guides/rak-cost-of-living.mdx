# Ras Al Khaimah Cost of Living 2026: Budget Tables, Commute Costs and RAK vs Dubai

> Disclaimer: All figures are July 2026 planning ranges, not quotes or guarantees. Rents, tariffs and school fees change; verify with live listings, FEWA, and licensed advisers before relocating.

## Is Ras Al Khaimah cheaper than Dubai for rent?

Yes on headline rent. Indicative 2026 annual rents run **35-50% below** comparable Dubai fringe communities for villas and beach apartments. Groceries at Carrefour, Lulu, and Geant track within **5-10%** of Dubai prices because supply chains overlap.

A three-bed villa at Al Hamra near **AED 120,000/year** against a similar Dubai Hills villa near **AED 220,000/year** saves about **AED 8,333/month** before you drive anywhere. That number is the one to stress-test against commute and school choices.

| Property type | Indicative annual rent (RAK) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio | AED 22,000-35,000 | RAK City inland lower |
| 1-bed apartment | AED 35,000-55,000 | Beach premium 15-25% |
| 2-bed apartment | AED 50,000-80,000 | Al Hamra / Mina Al Arab upper band |
| 3-bed villa | AED 80,000-150,000 | Garden stock plentiful |
| Beachfront villa | AED 130,000-220,000+ | Resort-style communities |

## Who actually saves money living in RAK?

**Works well:** hybrid Dubai workers (2-3 office days), remote earners wanting villa space, families prioritising beach and gardens, owners living in their RAK home on a Golden Visa.

**Works poorly:** five-day commuters to Business Bay or DIFC (often 2+ hours each way), families keeping kids in Dubai KHDA schools while living in RAK, two-car households with both drivers commuting daily, heavy weekend spend in Dubai restaurants.

## What monthly budget should a single professional plan?

| Category | AED/month (local life) | With daily Dubai commute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rent (1-bed) | 3,000-4,500 | Same |
| FEWA + internet | 350-700 | Same |
| Groceries / household | 1,200-2,000 | Same |
| Car (loan, fuel local) | 1,500-2,500 | 2,500-4,000 |
| Salik + extra fuel | 0-100 | 650-2,600 |
| Dining / leisure | 800-2,000 | 1,200-2,500 |
| **Total indicative** | **8,000-13,000** | **9,250-16,300** |

Commute adds roughly **AED 1,500-2,500/month** in direct costs plus faster tyre and service wear.

### Dubai schools plus commute

Add **AED 8,000-14,000/month** for two children in Dubai KHDA schools and **AED 1,500-2,500/month** commute transport. Totals can reach **AED 34,000-58,000+**, at which point living in Dubai closer to school and office may match all-in cost.

## How much does the Dubai commute cost from RAK?

E611 is the main artery. Planning times assume live navigation; sand, accidents, or holiday traffic can add 20-40 minutes.

| Item (5 days/week) | Low | Mid | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fuel (mid SUV) | AED 900 | AED 1,400 | AED 2,000 |
| Salik (2 gates each way) | AED 300 | AED 440 | AED 600 |
| Extra servicing / tyres | AED 500 | AED 800 | AED 1,200 |
| **Monthly total** | **AED 1,700** | **AED 2,640** | **AED 3,800** |

If rent saves AED 7,000/month but commute costs AED 2,600, net saving is near **AED 4,400/month**. Still positive for many, but not the full rent gap.

## FEWA vs DEWA: what utilities cost in RAK

RAK uses **FEWA**, not DEWA. Summer AC load drives bills; model **peak summer**, not annual average.

| Property | Winter (Oct-Apr) | Summer peak (Jun-Sep) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio / 1-bed | AED 180-320 | AED 400-650 |
| 2-bed apartment | AED 280-500 | AED 550-900 |
| 3-bed villa | AED 500-900 | AED 900-1,800 |
| 4-bed villa + pool | AED 800-1,400 | AED 1,500-2,800 |

Select Mina Al Arab towers include district cooling in service charges; ask the landlord which bills you pay directly.

## How do RAK schools compare with commuting to Dubai?

Local RAK schools (British, Indian, and American curricula) run **AED 22,000-45,000 per year per child** depending on tier, plus uniforms and activities. Dubai schools add tuition and **bus or parent commute** cost.

| Choice | Budget effect |
| --- | --- |
| Both children in RAK mid-tier school | Keeps RAK savings intact |
| One child in Dubai school | Often AED 4,000-7,000/month extra |
| Two in Dubai + commute | Can erase rent advantage |

See [Gulf schools comparison](/guides/gulf-schools-comparison/) for curriculum notes.

## How does RAK compare with Dubai and Sharjah on spend?

| Category | RAK vs Dubai | Comment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rent | 35-50% lower (like-for-like villa) | Biggest lever |
| Groceries | ~5-10% lower | Chain parity |
| Dining out | Fewer premium options | Dubai weekends add cost |
| Schools | Local cheaper; Dubai schools costly | Often decisive |
| Transport | Car essential; no metro | Commute-sensitive |

Sharjah offers cheaper older apartments and shorter trips to Deira but heavy E311 congestion. RAK trades commute length for beach lifestyle. Compare scenarios in [RAK vs Sharjah living](/guides/rak-vs-sharjah-living/) if your office sits in Deira or Marina.

## What local costs do new RAK residents overlook?

Chiller or AC maintenance on older villas, garden water for large plots, second car insurance, and resort-style community service charges in Al Hamra or Mina Al Arab. RAK has **no Salik internally**; tolls hit when you enter Dubai.

Healthcare: employer insurance is standard; self-employed should budget **AED 1,500-2,500/month** family cover on mid plans.

## What should you check before signing a RAK lease?

1. Drive your Dubai commute at **7:30 a.m.** on a weekday, not only on Sunday evening.  
2. Confirm FEWA account setup and whether cooling is in service charge.  
3. Map school runs if children stay in Dubai.  
4. Compare three live listings; beach premiums vary 15-25% inside RAK.  
5. Read Ejari and deposit rules; use registered brokers for clarity.

## How do groceries, dining, and entertainment prices compare?

Supermarket baskets track Dubai within a few percent because chains share distribution. Local RAK restaurants run **20-40% below** Dubai Marina equivalents for family dining, but many residents still drive to Dubai malls on weekends.

| Spend pattern | Typical monthly add-on |
| --- | --- |
| Mostly cook at home | AED 1,200-2,000 per adult |
| Mixed local + Dubai dining | AED 2,000-4,000 household |
| Frequent Dubai brunches | AED 1,500-3,000 extra travel spend |

Budget honestly for leisure if you moved for beach calm but still socialise in Dubai every Friday.

## Can you live in RAK without a car?

A car is practical for almost all families and for professionals outside the RAK City core. Taxis and ride-hail exist but cost adds up without a fixed commute schedule. Remote workers in Al Hamra or Mina Al Arab often run one car per adult once school runs begin.

## What relocation timeline keeps costs predictable?

Month 1: short-term furnished lease while you test commute. Month 2: Ejari on annual lease after rush-hour trial. Month 3: FEWA activation and school registration if staying. Avoid signing a 12-month beach premium lease before one full week of Dubai office commute at peak hours.

## How does buying versus renting in RAK affect your budget?

Purchase can lower monthly housing cost if you accept illiquidity. Indicative service charges in master communities run **AED 12-22 per sq ft** on apartments and higher on villas with shared facilities. Compare total carry (mortgage or cash opportunity cost + service charge + FEWA) against rent before you assume ownership saves money.

| Tenure | Cash flow focus |
| --- | --- |
| Renting | Flexibility; no DLD fees; easier exit if commute fails |
| Buying | Build equity; pay service charge + maintenance |
| Off-plan | Payment plan spreads cost; delivery risk |

Investors should read [Ras Al Khaimah property investment guide](/guides/ras-al-khaimah-property-investment-guide/) for yield bands separate from this living-cost view.

## What insurance and visa costs sit outside rent tables?

Employer medical cover is standard for sponsored employees. Self-sponsored Golden Visa holders budget **AED 1,500-3,000 per month** for family medical depending on network. Visa renewal every two to ten years adds PRO fees and medical tests, usually low four figures annually amortised.

Children on dependent visas require sponsored insurance compliant with UAE rules; add this line if you moved for school quality rather than job transfer package.

## What seasonal costs spike in RAK?

Summer FEWA and outdoor maintenance peak June-September. Ramadan and school start (August/September) can tighten cash flow when fees and deposits land together. Build a **three-month buffer** in AED before you leave employment in Dubai to absorb those lumps. Track FEWA bills monthly in summer so winter averages do not lull you into under-budgeting air conditioning.

Track school fee invoices in August and December separately from rent cheques so you do not double-count one salary against both obligations in the same week.
