UAE Golden Residence applications through the property route typically depend on the land-department record and the immigration file handled through GDRFA Dubai or ICP. A ready property is evidenced by a title deed, while an off-plan property is evidenced by active Oqood registration. The AED 2 million threshold refers to registered property value, not the 4% Dubai Land Department registration fee or a broker appraisal. Where the property is financed, the lender's written no-objection confirmation connects the land record to the immigration application. Treat the title deed or Oqood, payment evidence, passport validity of at least 6 months, insurance, and any mortgage letter as one document set. GDRFA, ICP, DLD, and the financing bank can each request clarification, so consistent applicant names and property references matter more than upload order.

A mortgage does not remove the need to prove the property route for foreign buyers. The practical evidence chain is the land department record, the financing bank's current liability or no-objection document, and the applicant's passport and immigration paperwork. In Dubai, applicants can use Dubai REST and land department records to verify the property reference before filing; off-plan buyers should also verify that Oqood registration is active. The AED 2 million figure is the property-category threshold used in this guide, and the Dubai Land Department's 4% registration fee is separate because it is a transaction cost, not the property value recorded for the visa. The official service channel can ask for payment receipts or a sale agreement where the record needs clarification. Request the bank letter early because the bank, not the typing centre, controls its issue date and wording for this market.
