Tyre pressure and blowouts: Road surface temperatures regularly exceed 60°C on peak days. Underinflated tyres blow out with little warning on high-speed sections. Check pressure early morning before temperatures rise, the reading changes significantly through the day.

**Vehicle:** Standard 4x4 SUV minimum, not all 4x4s are equal in sand. Proper off-road tyres (not road tyres) and a working low-ratio gearbox are necessary for dune terrain.

**Insurance clause:** Standard comprehensive car insurance policies often exclude off-road use or "sand driving." Read the exclusion clauses before your first desert trip. Adding an off-road rider is possible with most UAE insurers, cost varies AED 500–1,500 per year.

**Recovery kit:** Carry sand tracks (ladders), a tow rope, and a quality 12V compressor at minimum. Tyre deflation to 16–20 PSI for soft sand driving is standard, you need equipment to re-inflate before returning to tarmac.

**Convoy rule:** Never drive desert dunes alone. Abu Dhabi Police and community groups strongly advise convoys of 3+ vehicles; if one gets stuck, others can assist or get help.

**Liwa and Al Dhafra:** The main off-road destination for Abu Dhabi residents. Route south on E11/E30 toward Liwa, approximately 2.5 hours from the city. The Moreeb Dune (Tal Moreeb) is popular but challenging for casual drivers.

## FAQ

What route should I take from Yas Island to Abu Dhabi city centre?
Take E20 north toward Abu Dhabi, then follow signs to Sheikh Khalifa bin Zayed Street or Corniche depending on your destination. Journey 25–40 minutes depending on time of day.

Are there speed cameras on every road in Abu Dhabi?
Not every road, but camera density on main highways and arterials is high. Fixed gantry cameras on E10, E11 and E20 are spaced at intervals that make sustained speeding virtually certain to generate a fine. Treat every posted limit as the ceiling.

Can I use Waze or Google Maps reliably in Abu Dhabi?
Both apps work well but may lag on new infrastructure. Download offline maps for Abu Dhabi emirate as a backup. Waze has an active local community that updates construction and speed trap alerts in near-real-time.

How do I pay Abu Dhabi parking fees?
Use the Mawaqif app for paid zones, pre-load credit and register your plate. Street meters and SMS payment methods also exist in some zones. Fines for non-payment arrive by SMS and escalate if unpaid.

Is it safe to drive at night in Abu Dhabi?
City roads are safe and well-lit. Desert roads (E22, E30) require caution at night due to animals on the road and limited ambient light. Avoid desert off-road at night entirely as a new resident.

What should I do differently during Ramadan?
Avoid driving in the 30-minute window around Iftar (sunset). Roads become significantly more dangerous as fasting drivers rush home. Roads are quieter in the mornings, use this window for longer commutes.
