## How does Abu Dhabi residency work in the UAE federal system?

ICP processes visas nationally. Your local service centre (Abu Dhabi or Dubai) handles typing and file submission, but the residency rights apply across all seven emirates once stamped.

Summary of available routes in Abu Dhabi (2026):

| Visa Category | Duration | Self-Sponsored | Minimum Investment / Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Golden Visa, Property | 10 years | Yes | AED 2M registered property in freehold zone |
| Golden Visa, Talent/Professional | 10 years | Yes | Exceptional talent or federal approval |
| Green Visa, Skilled Employee | 5 years | Yes | ISCO Level 1 to 3 job + AED 15,000/month salary |
| Green Visa, Freelancer | 5 years | Yes | UAE freelance permit + AED 360K/year income |
| Investor Visa | 2 to 3 years | Yes | AED 500K+ in UAE business/property |
| Employment Visa | 2 years | No | UAE employer sponsor |
| Family/Dependent Visa | Tied to sponsor | No | Qualifying sponsor |
| Retirement Visa | 5 years | Yes | Age 55+ and AED 1M savings / AED 20K/mo income / AED 1M property |
| Remote Work Visa | 1 year | Yes | Employment abroad + USD 3,500/month income |

## Can you get a Golden Visa through Abu Dhabi property?

The property route requires AED 2 million in registered freehold value inside Abu Dhabi designated zones under Law 19/2005 and Executive Council updates.

- Yas Island
- Saadiyat Island
- Al Reem Island
- Al Maryah Island
- Masdar City
- Khalifa City (select plots)
- Al Shamkha
- Al Reef
- Hydra Village
- Al Jurf (Ghantoot)

Outside these zones, non-GCC foreigners cannot hold freehold title. This is a material constraint compared to Dubai, where freehold areas are more numerous and geographically spread. If you are buying primarily to secure Golden Visa residency, confirm the specific tower or plot is within a designated zone before contracting.

For a deep dive into Abu Dhabi property investment zones, see our [Abu Dhabi property investment guide](/guides/abu-dhabi-property-investment-guide/).

Registration process: Property is registered with the Abu Dhabi Department of Municipalities and Transport (DMT) via the Tawtheeq system, not the Dubai Land Department (DLD). Once the property is registered, the DMT issues a title deed and confirmation letter. This DMT title deed is submitted to ICP for the Golden Visa application, just as a DLD title deed would be in Dubai.

Mortgaged properties: A property under mortgage can qualify if the registered value (not just equity) meets AED 2 million. The bank (ADCB, ADIB, FAB, or others) must issue a No Objection Certificate. Confirm with your conveyancer whether the specific lending bank will issue the NOC for residency purposes.

## What is the UAE Green Visa in Abu Dhabi?

The Green Visa is a 5-year self-sponsored federal category. Abu Dhabi applicants use the same ICP rules as Dubai; local difference is how you prove income or freelance status.

1. Skilled employee (ISCO 1 to 3): Managers, professionals, and technicians in the first three tiers of the International Standard Classification of Occupations qualify if they earn at least AED 15,000 per month from a UAE employer. Abu Dhabi's employment structure, heavy in government, semi-government, energy (ADNOC group), and financial services (ADCB, FAB), means most expats in the emirate are in ISCO-qualifying roles.

2. Freelancer / self-employed: You need a valid UAE freelance permit (Abu Dhabi's Hub71 and twofour54 both issue permits) and provable annual income of at least AED 360,000. Bank statements covering 12 months are the standard evidence.

3. Investor in a UAE company: Investment of at least AED 500,000 in a licensed UAE business (not property) qualifies. The business must be profit-generating and not a dormant entity.

4. Outstanding student: Students graduating with a GPA of 3.75 or above from UAE universities, or top international universities on the approved list, can apply within two years of graduation.

The Green Visa does **not** require you to live in Abu Dhabi specifically, you can relocate within the UAE after receiving it. Many holders obtain the Green Visa through an Abu Dhabi freelance permit but later reside in Dubai.

## How does employment sponsorship work in Abu Dhabi?

Employment visas remain the default for ADNOC group companies, Mubadala portfolio firms, SEHA, ADGM entities, and Abu Dhabi government departments. HR teams usually manage medical, Emirates ID, and stamping through ICP with predictable 10 to 20 business day cycles once documents are complete.

## Who qualifies for the UAE retirement visa from Abu Dhabi?

The federal retirement visa suits residents aged 55+ who meet one financial test:
- AED 1,000,000 or more in UAE bank savings
- AED 20,000 or more per month in proven income (pension, investment income, or salary)
- AED 1,000,000 or more in UAE real estate (property doesn't need to be in Abu Dhabi, any UAE freehold counts)

**Duration:** 5 years, renewable provided you continue to meet qualifying criteria.

Age requirement: 55 years old at time of application.

Abu Dhabi advantage: Abu Dhabi's healthcare infrastructure, Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi, Yas Clinic Group, and SEHA network, is a genuine draw for retirees who prioritise medical access. The lower density compared to Dubai and a slower pace of city life also appeal to retirees.

## What is the remote work visa and how long does it last?

The one-year remote work visa suits employees of overseas companies who earn at least USD 3,500 per month and hold valid UAE health insurance.
- Employed abroad by a company registered outside the UAE
- Monthly income of at least USD 3,500 (provable via bank statements and employment contract)
- Valid health insurance covering the UAE

The visa is non-renewable as-is, after 12 months, you must reapply or transition to a different category (Green Visa, employer sponsorship, or Golden Visa). Many holders use the 12-month period to establish UAE income or qualify for the Green Visa.
## Abu Dhabi vs Dubai: which emirate should process your visa?

Process where your qualifying asset or employer sits. The visa grants UAE-wide residency; DMT vs DLD only matters for property-based Golden Visa evidence. Property investors often buy on Yas or Al Reem to hit the AED 2 million Golden Visa threshold at a lower entry price per square metre than comparable Dubai zones.

## How do families and dependants attach to Abu Dhabi sponsors?

Dependants receive visas tied to the primary sponsor's category. Golden Visa holders can sponsor spouse and children subject to ICP income and accommodation rules. Employment sponsors follow company HR policies; government employers typically process family visas in the same batch as the employee file.

School registration uses the sponsor's visa and tenancy proof. ADEK regulates private schools in Abu Dhabi; confirm seat availability before signing a lease in a new district. See [Gulf schools comparison](/guides/gulf-schools-comparison/) for curriculum and fee bands.

## What is the step-by-step ICP application flow?

1. Secure qualifying basis (property title, employment contract, freelance permit, or savings proof)
2. Complete medical fitness test at an approved centre
3. Submit application through ICP typing centre with passport, photos, and supporting documents
4. Pay government fees (primary applicant roughly AED 4,000 to 5,500; dependants about AED 2,500 to 3,500 each)
5. Complete biometrics and collect Emirates ID

Processing time: typically 10 to 20 business days from medical test to Emirates ID for straightforward files.

## Which residency mistakes cost time or renewal risk?

1. Buying off-plan without registered title when you need a Golden Visa immediately. DMT registration must complete before ICP accepts the property route.

2. Using marketing brochures instead of DMT zone confirmation. Only designated freehold zones count toward foreign ownership and Golden Visa property tests.

3. Assuming mortgage equity equals Golden Visa qualification. DMT registered value governs, not net equity after loan.

4. Selling the qualifying property before renewal without a replacement route. Plan an alternate visa category before disposal.

5. Staying on an employer visa when you already qualify for Green or Golden self-sponsorship. Job loss can cancel employer-tied visas within the standard grace window.

## What should you prepare before visiting ICP?

Gather passport copies, entry stamps, medical result, tenancy contract or title deed, salary certificates or bank statements, and passport photos. Property investors should carry the DMT title deed and mortgage NOC if applicable. Compare your file against the [Golden Visa vs Dubai residence visa](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/) checklist if you are choosing between emirates. Allow one extra week if dependants apply in the same filing batch.
