In August 2023, Russia issued a decree suspending application of its double tax treaties with 38 countries it classified as "unfriendly states", primarily EU member states, the UK, the US, Canada, Australia, and others that imposed sanctions. **The UAE is not on Russia's unfriendly-states list.** As of mid-2026, the Russia-UAE DTT continues to operate.

What the treaty covers:
- **Income from immovable property**: may be taxed in the country where the property is situated (the UAE), which has zero personal income tax
- **Capital gains** from sale of immovable property: taxable in the country of location (UAE)
- **Dividends** from UAE companies
- **Residence tiebreaker**: for individuals simultaneously tax resident in both countries

The treaty's allocation of taxing rights to the UAE for property income and capital gains, combined with the UAE's zero-tax environment, can significantly reduce, or potentially eliminate, Russian tax on Dubai-source property income and gains for qualifying Russian nationals. **Russian tax resident** = individual spending **183 or more days** in Russia during a 12-month calendar year.

Russian tax residents are subject to Russian personal income tax (NDFL, Nalog na Dokhody Fizicheskikh Lits) on **worldwide income**, including:
- Rental income from Dubai property
- Dividends from UAE or other foreign companies
- Interest income
- Capital gains from property sales

Russian **non-residents** (who spent fewer than 183 days in Russia) are taxed only on Russian-source income at flat 30% rate. A Russian national living full-time in Dubai and spending fewer than 183 days per year in Russia may become a Russian non-resident, eliminating Russian personal income tax obligations on UAE-source income, while also becoming potentially eligible for UAE tax residency via the 183-day rule.

The year you relocate and the year following are particularly important for day-counting. Russian passports and border crossing records are the primary documentation the Russian tax authority (Федеральная налоговая служба, FNS) uses.
