## How does the Russia-UAE double tax treaty affect Dubai property income?

The Russia-UAE double tax treaty typically allocates taxing rights on Dubai property income and capital gains in ways that can reduce Russian tax when UAE residence and income characterisation are documented correctly, while the UAE side remains 0% personal income tax. Invest Gulf treats treaty relief as a filing project for foreign buyers, not an automatic outcome, and requires specialist Russian advice before modelling savings.

Treaty relief is not automatic: you must usually prove UAE source, character of income, and residency under the tie-breaker articles. Keep rental contracts, bank statements, and any Russian filing receipts in one folder so a future FNS review does not rely on memory alone.

Treaty checklist:
- Confirm UAE is still outside Russia's unfriendly-states list for your filing year
- Keep DLD title, leases, and bank statements together
- Obtain FTA Tax Residency Certificate before claiming treaty positions

## When are you still a Russian tax resident while living in Dubai?

Russian tax residency typically means spending 183 or more days in Russia during a calendar year, which keeps worldwide income including Dubai rent inside Russian NDFL even if you hold UAE residency papers. Invest Gulf starts every relocation file for foreign buyers by modelling day counts for the purchase year and the next 12 months in this market.

Russian tax residents are subject to Russian personal income tax (NDFL, Nalog na Dokhody Fizicheskikh Lits) on worldwide income, including:
- Rental income from Dubai property
- Dividends from UAE or other foreign companies
- Interest income
- Capital gains from property sales

Russian non-residents (who spent fewer than 183 days in Russia) are taxed only on Russian-source income at flat 30% rate. A Russian national living full-time in Dubai and spending fewer than 183 days per year in Russia may become a Russian non-resident, eliminating Russian personal income tax obligations on UAE-source income, while also becoming potentially eligible for UAE tax residency via the 183-day rule.

The year you relocate and the year following are particularly important for day-counting. Russian passports and border crossing records are the primary documentation the Russian tax authority (Федеральная налоговая служба, FNS) uses.

## How do Russian CFC rules apply to UAE property companies?

Russian CFC rules typically apply when a Russian tax resident owns 25% or more of a UAE company (or 10%+ where combined Russian ownership exceeds 50%), bringing undistributed profits into Russian personal income tax unless an exemption fits. Invest Gulf flags company-held Dubai flats as a CFC checklist item for foreign buyers before SPV structuring in this market.

CFC exemptions available:
- Active foreign company exemption: If over 80% of revenue comes from active business operations (not passive rental/investment income), the CFC may qualify for exemption, difficult to meet for a pure property-holding vehicle
- Low-profit exemption: CFC profits below RUB 10 million per year are exempt from CFC taxation in Russia

For many Russian investors holding a single Dubai apartment personally (not through a company), CFC rules do not directly apply. The CFC framework becomes critical when structuring portfolios through UAE holding companies, free zone SPVs, or multi-property vehicles. Russian legal advice specific to your structure is essential.

## How do Russian currency controls affect Dubai purchases and repatriation?

Russian currency controls since February 2022 typically restrict how residents receive foreign-currency rent or sale proceeds and how capital leaves Russian banks for Dubai purchases, with fines often cited at 20% to 100% of the transaction value when authorisations are missing. Invest Gulf prefers funding from lawfully held non-Russian accounts for foreign buyers in this market.

Currency control checklist:
- Document lawful source of funds before reservation
- Confirm UAE bank onboarding policy for Russian nationals
- Do not assume sale proceeds can land freely in a Russian account

Receiving rental income abroad: Russian residents may have restrictions on receiving foreign currency rental income into foreign bank accounts without appropriate authorisation or notification. Permitted transactions and required procedures have changed multiple times since 2022 and vary by counter-party country. The UAE is not a "friendly country" for all Russian currency control purposes, precise rules depend on the specific transaction structure.

Receiving sale proceeds abroad: Proceeds from foreign property sales denominated in foreign currency face reporting requirements and, in some cases, mandatory repatriation rules or special permissions. Practical enforcement has been uneven, but the regulatory risk is real.

Transferring funds from Russia to buy Dubai property: Capital outflows from Russian bank accounts for foreign property purchases are subject to restrictions. Many Russian buyers use funds already held outside Russia (e.g. savings accumulated pre-February 2022, or income earned while abroad) to fund UAE property purchases.

**UAE bank account for Russians**: Some UAE banks impose heightened compliance screening for Russian national clients following FATF and international guidance. Emirate-to-emirate variation exists, consult [Gulf Banking Comparison for Expats](/guides/open-bank-account-dubai/) for practical banking options. FAB, ENBD, Mashreq, and various digital banks have different Russian-client policies that have shifted over 2022 to 2026.

Currency control violations in Russia carry fines of 20 to 100% of the transaction value. Obtaining proper authorisations and structuring transactions correctly from the outset is critical. This requires a Russian lawyer specialising in currency regulation.
