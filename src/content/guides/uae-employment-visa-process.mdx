The UAE employment visa is the residency backbone for most expatriates in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and the other emirates. It is not one document but a chain: MOHRE work permit approval, entry permit, medical fitness, Emirates ID biometrics, and residence visa stamping. Skipping or reordering steps is the most common cause of overstay fines and travel disruptions.

## What is a UAE employment visa in 2026?

A UAE employment visa typically pairs a MOHRE work permit with a GDRFA or ICP residence stamp that authorises stay while you work for one employer in the area. Standard validity is 2 to 3 years by contract format. Our research treats the entry permit alone as insufficient for Ejari, banking, or family sponsorship once grace rules expire.

Both the labour card and residence stamp must stay active together. Renewal repeats medical, Emirates ID, and stamping unless you qualify for a longer Golden or Green route.

## What documents should you prepare before MOHRE submission?

Document prep before MOHRE submission typically means a passport with 6+ months validity, a reviewed MOHRE-format contract, and attested degrees for regulated roles in the area. Missing attestation can add 4-8 weeks. Invest Gulf starts home-country MOFA steps during offer negotiation, not after the portal filing begins.

| Document | Who prepares | Typical delay if missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passport (6+ months validity) | Employee | Immediate block |
| MOHRE employment contract | Employer + employee | Amendment cycle if wrong |
| Attested degree (regulated roles) | Employee | 4-8 weeks |
| Professional licence (health/engineering) | Employee | 4-8 weeks parallel track |
| Passport photos (white background) | Employee | Same-day fix |

Degree attestation is the silent timeline killer for regulated titles.

## How does MOHRE work permit approval work?

MOHRE work permit approval typically clears in 3 to 7 business days when the employer file is clean in the area. Manual review triggers for company violations or nationality-job quota limits. Invest Gulf will not treat a visit visa already held inside the UAE as a reason to skip this labour step.

- Employer or PRO submits electronic contract and quota category
- Clean files: often 3-7 working days
- Quota or violation files: manual review
- Visit visa holders still need this MOHRE step

Once approved, the work permit reference unlocks the immigration entry permit.

## How do you obtain the entry permit and enter the UAE?

Entry permits typically allow 60 days from issuance to enter the UAE, not 60 days of stay after landing, in the area. Overseas entry, in-country status change, and some GCC routes all still lead to medical, Emirates ID, and stamping. We track issue date and entry date separately to avoid fines from AED 50 per day.

| Route | When it fits | Watch-out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overseas entry | You are outside the UAE | Miss the 60-day entry window |
| Status change inside UAE | Valid visit/tourist visa in country | Must file before current visa expires |
| GCC passport holders | Eligible nationalities | Fewer steps but same medical/ID chain |

After landing or status change approval, the clock starts for medical, Emirates ID, and stamping. See [First 30 days in Dubai as an expat](/guides/first-30-days-dubai-expat/).

## What happens at the UAE medical fitness test?

UAE medical fitness tests typically cost AED 300-700 at DHA, DOH, or MOHAP authorised clinics and must return a fit result before residency stamping in the area. Results often post within 24 to 72 hours. We expect employers to pay under Federal Decree Law No. 33 of 2021.

- Book only authorised clinics
- Keep the fit reference for stamping
- Verify chronic-condition rules by emirate before you fly
- Employers should pay clinic fees

## How do you apply for Emirates ID?

Emirates ID applications typically use the ICP UAE app or the employer PRO with work permit and entry references in the area. Government card fees are about AED 270 for 2 years or AED 370 for 3 years, plus typing admin of AED 50-150. We budget 7 to 14 business days for card delivery.

Biometrics happen at an authorised centre. Emirates ID unlocks bank accounts, SIM registration, Ejari, and most employer payroll checks.

## How does passport visa stamping finish the process?

Passport visa stamping typically returns a 2 to 3 year residence visa in 3 to 7 business days after fit medical and Emirates ID receipt are filed in the area. Long absences can trigger cancellation under the 6 month rule for standard visas. Invest Gulf insists on formal cancellation papers when changing employers.

Submit passport (original), fit medical reference, Emirates ID receipt, and photos through the employer's PRO. Golden Visa holders follow different absence rules than standard employment visas.

## What timeline and costs should you expect in 2026?

End-to-end UAE employment visa timelines typically run 3 to 6 weeks once documents are ready in the area, with MOHRE in 3 to 7 business days and entry permits in 5 to 10 business days. Government visa charges sit with the employer by law. Our underwriting treats salary deductions for visa fees as a compliance red flag.

UAE employment visa processing typically takes 3-6 weeks from a signed contract to passport stamping when attestation is already done, with MOHRE work permits often clearing in 3-7 working days and entry permits in 5-10 working days in Dubai or other emirates. After arrival or status change, medical fitness, Emirates ID biometrics, and stamping each commonly add several working days, and Ramadan or summer peaks can extend the chain by 1-2 weeks. Government visa charges, medical fees of about AED 300-700, and Emirates ID fees near AED 270-370 are the employer's cost under Federal Decree Law No. 33 of 2021. We treat any request to prepay those lines or deduct them from salary as a compliance red flag worth escalating to MOHRE before you travel.

| Stage | Typical duration | Who pays (by law) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Document prep / attestation | 1-8 weeks | Employee for attestation travel; employer for visa fees |
| MOHRE work permit | 3-7 working days | Employer |
| Entry permit | 5-10 working days | Employer |
| Medical + Emirates ID + stamp | 10-20 working days | Employer |
| **Total (documents ready)** | **3-6 weeks** | Employer for government visa charges |

For line-item fee tables, see [UAE visa fees 2026](/guides/uae-visa-fees-2026/).

Insider tip: Photograph every medical, Emirates ID, and entry-permit receipt the day you receive it; PRO handoffs mid-file are the usual reason a clean 3-6 week case stretches past 8 weeks.

## Can you sponsor family after stamping?

Family sponsorship after stamping typically needs salary near AED 4,000, or AED 3,000 with provided accommodation, though landlords and schools often expect AED 6,000-8,000 in practice in the area. Each dependent repeats entry, medical, Emirates ID, and stamping. We ty dependent grace periods to the principal visa status.

- Spouse and unmarried children are the common first file
- Sons often face age caps at 18, or 21 if in university
- Dependent status falls if the principal visa cancels

## How do renewals and employer transfers work?

Employment visa renewals typically start 30-60 days before expiry with fresh medical and Emirates ID steps in the area. After 1 year of service, many transfers proceed without an NOC under 2021 reforms. We still collect cancellation papers and settlement proof before any bank or landlord update.

| Situation | Typical MOHRE/ICP action | Employee prep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard renewal | Employer files renewal bundle | Fresh medical, Emirates ID fee |
| Transfer after 1 year | New employer submits amendment | Cancel old permit cleanly |
| Transfer before 1 year | NOC + new work permit | Negotiate NOC early |
| Resignation with notice | Employer cancels visa | Plan exit or new sponsor within grace |
| Termination | Immediate cancellation risk | Clarify grace days in contract |

## How does employment visa differ from Golden or Green visa?

Employment visas typically tie legal stay to one UAE employer and MOHRE contract for 2 to 3 years in the area. Golden Visa property routes often need AED 2 million+, while Green Visa tracks target freelancers and skilled self-sponsored workers. We compare those routes before anyone resigns solely for investor status.

| Route | Sponsor | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employment visa | UAE company | Job offer in hand |
| Golden Visa | Self via property/talent | AED 2M+ property or elite categories |
| Green Visa | Self via skills/income | Freelancers and skilled workers |

If you plan to buy Dubai property for residency, compare [Golden Visa application step by step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/) before resigning.

## What mistakes cause the longest delays?

The longest employment visa delays typically come from late degree attestation, misread entry-permit validity windows of 60 days, and unsigned MOHRE contracts that force salary or title amendments in the area. Our underwriting treats those three as the primary timeline killers across Dubai and Abu Dhabi filings.

1. Late attestation for regulated roles, blocking MOHRE for weeks
2. Misreading entry permit validity versus in-country stay counters
3. Skipping MOHRE contract review before registration

Entry permit validity for UAE employment visas typically runs 60 days from issuance to enter, which is a different clock from post-arrival stay counters, and missing that window is a common fine trigger from AED 50 per day after grace rules. Overseas applicants, in-country status changes from visit visas, and eligible GCC routes still converge on the same medical, Emirates ID, and stamping sequence once inside the country. Standard residence stamps then run 2 to 3 years while the MOHRE labour card stays tied to one employer in Dubai or another emirate. We track issue date, entry date, medical booking, and PRO stamping slots on one checklist so a single missed appointment does not cascade into overstay exposure for the employee and any planned dependents on the file.

## What should you do in the first 30 days after stamping?

The first 30 days after stamping typically sequence a salary account and SIM in week 1, Ejari in week 2, insurance in week 3, and family files in week 4 when proofs are ready in the area. Invest Gulf aligns school admissions only after Emirates ID and tenancy documents exist.

1. Week 1: Salary account + mobile SIM (Emirates ID required)
2. Week 2: Ejari registration or hotel contract for address proof
3. Week 3: Health insurance activation aligned to employer policy
4. Week 4: Family visa file if salary proofs and tenancy are ready

Property investors switching routes should compare [Golden Visa vs employment](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/) before buying for visa purposes alone.

## Who handles each step: employer vs employee?

Clear ownership typically means the employer PRO owns MOHRE registration, work permit, entry permit, and stamping, while you own UAE entry timing, medical, and Emirates ID biometrics in the area. We keep PDF receipts for every AED 50-150 typing fee line because PRO teams change mid-process.

| Step | Primary owner | Your action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MOHRE contract registration | Employer | Review salary and title before sign |
| Work permit + entry permit | Employer PRO | Track validity dates |
| UAE entry / status change | Employee | Do not overstay visit visa |
| Medical test | Employee | Book authorised clinic |
| Emirates ID biometrics | Employee | Keep appointment slip |
| Passport stamping | Employer PRO | Hand passport only via PRO |

Store MOHRE contract copies alongside passport scans for bank and landlord requests.
