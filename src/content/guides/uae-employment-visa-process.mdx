The UAE employment visa is the residency backbone for most expatriates in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and the other emirates. It is not one document but a chain: MOHRE work permit approval, entry permit, medical fitness, Emirates ID biometrics, and residence visa stamping. Skipping or reordering steps is the most common cause of overstay fines and travel disruptions.

## What is a UAE employment visa in 2026?

A UAE employment visa pairs two records: the **MOHRE work permit** (labour card) that authorises your job with a specific employer, and the **GDRFA/ICP residence stamp** that gives you legal stay while employed. Both must stay active. You cannot bank, lease with Ejari, or sponsor family on an entry permit alone once the grace rules expire.

Standard employment visas run **2-3 years** depending on contract format and emirate. Renewal repeats medical, Emirates ID, and stamping steps unless you qualify for a longer Golden or Green route.

## What documents should you prepare before MOHRE submission?

Answer in the first week after signing: attested qualifications, passport validity, and a MOHRE-format contract reviewed **before** the employer registers it in the portal.

| Document | Who prepares | Typical delay if missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passport (6+ months validity) | Employee | Immediate block |
| MOHRE employment contract | Employer + employee | Amendment cycle if wrong |
| Attested degree (regulated roles) | Employee | 4-8 weeks |
| Professional licence (health/engineering) | Employee | 4-8 weeks parallel track |
| Passport photos (white background) | Employee | Same-day fix |

Degree attestation is the silent timeline killer. Start home-country MOFA attestation and UAE embassy steps **during** offer negotiation if your role requires verified qualifications.

## How does MOHRE work permit approval work?

The employer (or PRO) submits the electronic contract and quota category to MOHRE. Clean files usually clear in **3-7 working days**. Manual review triggers if the company has violations or the nationality/job pairing hits quota limits.

Once approved, MOHRE issues a work permit reference that unlocks the immigration entry permit. You cannot skip this step even if you already hold a visit visa inside the UAE.

## How do you obtain the entry permit and enter the UAE?

The employer applies for an **entry permit** through GDRFA (Dubai) or ICP (other emirates). Validity is often **60 days from issuance** to enter, not 60 days of stay after landing. Track issue date and entry date separately to avoid overstay fines (from AED 50 per day after grace).

| Route | When it fits | Watch-out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overseas entry | You are outside the UAE | Miss the 60-day entry window |
| Status change inside UAE | Valid visit/tourist visa in country | Must file before current visa expires |
| GCC passport holders | Eligible nationalities | Fewer steps but same medical/ID chain |

After landing (or status change approval), the clock starts for medical, Emirates ID, and stamping. See [First 30 days in Dubai as an expat](/guides/first-30-days-dubai-expat/) for housing and banking sequencing.

## What happens at the UAE medical fitness test?

You must obtain a **fit** result before residency stamping. Clinics authorised by DHA, DOH, or MOHAP charge roughly **AED 300-700**; employers should pay under Federal Decree Law No. 33 of 2021.

Results usually post to the health authority portal within **24-72 hours**. Rules on specific conditions vary by emirate and job category, verify current screening policy if you have chronic conditions before you fly.

## How do you apply for Emirates ID?

Apply via the **ICP UAE** app or through the employer's PRO using work permit and entry references. Government card fees are about **AED 270 (2-year)** or **AED 370 (3-year)**, plus typing centre admin (AED 50-150).

Biometrics happen at an authorised centre. Physical cards typically arrive in **7-14 working days** by courier. Emirates ID unlocks bank accounts, SIM registration, Ejari, and most employer payroll checks.

## How does passport visa stamping finish the process?

Submit passport (original), fit medical reference, Emirates ID receipt, and photos through the employer's PRO. Stamping usually returns the passport in **3-7 working days** with a **2-3 year** residence visa.

Travel abroad does not auto-cancel a valid employment visa, but long absences can trigger cancellation under the **6-month** absence rule for standard visas (Golden Visa holders follow different rules). Cancel formally when changing employers or exiting the UAE permanently.

## What timeline and costs should you expect in 2026?

| Stage | Typical duration | Who pays (by law) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Document prep / attestation | 1-8 weeks | Employee for attestation travel; employer for visa fees |
| MOHRE work permit | 3-7 working days | Employer |
| Entry permit | 5-10 working days | Employer |
| Medical + Emirates ID + stamp | 10-20 working days | Employer |
| **Total (documents ready)** | **3-6 weeks** | Employer for government visa charges |

If an employer asks you to prepay visa fees or deduct them from salary, treat it as a compliance red flag and escalate to MOHRE. Article 14 of Federal Decree Law No. 33 of 2021 places visa costs on the sponsor.

For line-item fee tables, see [UAE visa fees 2026](/guides/uae-visa-fees-2026/).

## Can you sponsor family after stamping?

Yes, if salary meets sponsorship thresholds (often **AED 4,000**, or **AED 3,000** with provided accommodation). Schools and landlords may still expect **AED 6,000-8,000** net for practical approvals.

Each dependent repeats entry permit, medical (adults), Emirates ID, and stamping. Dependent visas cascade from yours: if your employment visa cancels, dependents enter a grace period to exit or change status.

## How do renewals and employer transfers work?

Renewals start **30-60 days** before expiry with updated medical and Emirates ID. After **one year** of service, many employees can transfer employers without an NOC under 2021 reforms; before one year, NOC is usually required. Transfers amend the MOHRE record without mandatory exit/re-entry when filed correctly.

| Situation | Typical MOHRE/ICP action | Employee prep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard renewal | Employer files renewal bundle | Fresh medical, Emirates ID fee |
| Transfer after 1 year | New employer submits amendment | Cancel old permit cleanly |
| Transfer before 1 year | NOC + new work permit | Negotiate NOC early |
| Resignation with notice | Employer cancels visa | Plan exit or new sponsor within grace |
| Termination | Immediate cancellation risk | Clarify grace days in contract |

Always collect **cancellation papers** and final settlement proof. Banks and landlords ask for them when you switch employers or leave the UAE.

## How does employment visa differ from Golden or Green visa?

Employment visas tie legal stay to a **specific employer** and MOHRE contract. Golden and Green routes decouple residency from a single sponsor when you meet investment, salary, or freelancer thresholds.

| Route | Sponsor | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employment visa | UAE company | Job offer in hand |
| Golden Visa | Self via property/talent | AED 2M+ property or elite categories |
| Green Visa | Self via skills/income | Freelancers and skilled workers |

If you plan to buy Dubai property for residency, compare timelines in [Golden Visa application step by step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/) before you resign from an employment sponsor.

## What mistakes cause the longest delays?

1. **Late attestation** for regulated roles, blocking MOHRE submission for weeks.
2. **Misreading entry permit validity** versus in-country stay counters, triggering fines.
3. **Unsigned MOHRE contract review** before registration, forcing amendment cycles on salary or title.

## What should you do in the first 30 days after stamping?

Open a salary account, register Ejari if leasing, activate health insurance, and align school admissions if sponsoring children. The [Dubai relocation guide](/guides/dubai-relocation-guide/) covers neighbourhood choice and commute math by family size.

Practical sequence many PRO teams follow:

1. **Week 1:** Salary account + mobile SIM (Emirates ID required).
2. **Week 2:** Ejari registration or hotel contract for address proof.
3. **Week 3:** Health insurance activation aligned to employer policy.
4. **Week 4:** Family visa file if salary proofs and tenancy are ready.

Property investors switching from employment to investor residency should compare [Golden Visa vs employment routes](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/) before buying for visa purposes alone. A property MOU does not replace an active employment visa until ICP approves a status change.

## Who handles each step: employer vs employee?

| Step | Primary owner | Your action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MOHRE contract registration | Employer | Review salary and title before sign |
| Work permit + entry permit | Employer PRO | Track validity dates |
| UAE entry / status change | Employee | Do not overstay visit visa |
| Medical test | Employee | Book authorised clinic |
| Emirates ID biometrics | Employee | Keep appointment slip |
| Passport stamping | Employer PRO | Hand passport only via PRO |

Clear ownership prevents the common delay where employees wait on employers who assume you already finished medical or ID steps.

Keep PDF copies of every receipt (medical, Emirates ID, entry permit). PRO teams change; your document folder is the audit trail if a renewal or transfer stalls mid-process. Store MOHRE contract copies alongside passport scans for bank and landlord requests.
