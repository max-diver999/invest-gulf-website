UAE visa fees are one of the most searched, and most misunderstood, costs in UAE immigration planning. Government fee tables look deceptively simple, but the total you actually pay includes Emirates ID, medical test, typing centre charges, and sometimes attestation costs that can push the real total 40-80% above the headline figure. This guide maps every major fee category for 2026, breaks down each component, and shows you how to build a realistic budget before you apply.

> **TL;DR:** Golden Visa costs AED 4,000-5,500 per applicant in government fees (plus ~AED 600-900 for Emirates ID and medical). Green Visa and Retirement Visa run AED 2,500-4,000. Employment visas cost AED 1,000-2,500 with employers often covering part of the total. Tourist visas range from AED 100-1,000 depending on duration and entry type. Always add Emirates ID, medical test, and typing centre fees to any estimate.

## What are UAE visa government fees in 2026?

UAE visa government fees in 2026 are the ICP and GDRFA charges for tourist, employment, Green, Golden, retirement, and family permits. Headline ranges start at AED 100 for a 30-day tourist visa and reach AED 4,000-5,500 for a 10-year Golden Visa main applicant. Always add Emirates ID and medical before you lock a budget.

| Visa Category | Validity | Government Fee Range (AED) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tourist, 30-day single entry | 30 days | 100-200 |
| Tourist, 60-day single entry | 60 days | 200-350 |
| Tourist, 90-day single entry | 90 days | 350-500 |
| Tourist, 5-year multiple entry | 5 years | 1,000 |
| Transit visa | 48-96 hours | 50-100 |
| Employment visa (2-year residency) | 2 years | 1,000-2,500 |
| Family/dependent visa | 1-2 years | 1,000-2,000 |
| Property investor visa (2-year) | 2 years | 1,500-2,500 |
| Retirement visa (5-year) | 5 years | 2,500-4,000 |
| Green Visa (5-year) | 5 years | 2,500-4,000 |
| Golden Visa (10-year) | 10 years | 4,000-5,500 |
| Remote work / Digital nomad visa | 1 year | 1,000-1,500 |
| Student visa | Study duration | 500-1,200 |
| Mission / business visa | 30-90 days | 300-700 |

*Figures are approximate mid-2026 government fee ranges. Fees vary by emirate (GDRFA Dubai vs ICP), entry route (new entry vs status change), and quota category for employment visas. Verify current schedules at icp.gov.ae before applying.*

Invest Gulf budgeting note for relocation planners: treat the table as a floor, not a quote. Families who model only the Golden Visa stamp often understate Emirates ID, medical, and typing by AED 600-1,200 per person within the first 30 days.

Airlines and agencies often add surcharges on top of ICP rates. Invest Gulf planning treats a 90-day visa plus one extension as about 180 days before residency is required.

## What fees make up a UAE residency visa bill?

A UAE residency visa bill in this market typically includes entry permit or status change (AED 500-600 when applicable), residency stamping (AED 500-1,200), Emirates ID (AED 270-370), medical fitness (AED 300-500), plus knowledge and innovation dirham surcharges. Our checklists show status-change routes often cut AED 500-600 versus a fresh entry permit.

Checklist for a complete residency total:

- Entry permit or status-change fee
- Residence stamping
- Emirates ID card years
- Medical fitness clinic fee
- Typing centre service charge (AED 50-200)

## How much does a UAE Golden Visa cost in 2026?

A UAE Golden Visa for a main applicant typically costs AED 4,000-5,500 in government fees for the 10-year track. That band covers entry or status change, stamping near AED 1,000-1,200, Emirates ID around AED 370 for 10 years, and medical near AED 300-500. Dependents add roughly AED 1,000-3,000 each.

For a complete application walkthrough and document checklist, see [Golden Visa Application Step by Step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/).

| Stage | Component | Approximate Fee (AED) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Entry permit (or status change) | 500-600 |
| 2 | ICP/GDRFA service fee | 100-150 |
| 3 | Knowledge dirham | 10 per transaction |
| 4 | Innovation dirham | 10 per transaction |
| 5 | Medical fitness test (clinic) | 300-500 |
| 6 | Residency stamping | 1,000-1,200 |
| 7 | Emirates ID (10-year card) | 370 |
| 8 | Emirates ID service fee | 30-50 |
| Total, main applicant | | **~4,000-5,500** |

**Insider tip:** Ask your PRO to itemise Emirates ID years and medical clinic separately on the invoice. Our clients who accepted a single “all-in Golden” line often discovered AED 800-1,500 of clinic and ID charges billed later, after the 10-year stamp was already approved.

Dependent fees (spouse, children) run approximately AED 1,000-3,000 each depending on age and dependent category. The Golden Visa does not include the 4% DLD transfer fee paid at property purchase; that is a separate one-off registration cost. Optional PRO fees of AED 2,000-8,000 sit outside the government total.

Golden Visa government fees for a main applicant in mid-2026 typically land between AED 4,000 and AED 5,500 when you add entry or status change, residency stamping near AED 1,000-1,200, a 10-year Emirates ID near AED 370, medical fitness near AED 300-500, and the small knowledge and innovation dirham surcharges on each ICP transaction. Dependents are billed separately at roughly AED 1,000-3,000 each, so a couple with two school-age children can see family government totals near AED 8,000-14,000 before any PRO package. Property buyers should also keep the 4% DLD transfer fee outside this visa line; it is a purchase cost, not an immigration stamp. Build a 30-day cash buffer of at least AED 1,000 per person for typing centres, reprints, and clinic retests if biometrics fail the first pass.

## Do Dubai and Abu Dhabi charge the same visa fees?

Dubai and Abu Dhabi visa fees in this market are largely aligned through ICP, but GDRFA Dubai still adds small service differences of roughly AED 10-150 per transaction. We budget the same AED bands nationwide, then confirms exact totals in-app. Knowledge and innovation dirham charges apply on most filings within 30 days.

| Channel | Where used | Budget tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GDRFA Dubai | Dubai residency files | Confirm in GDRFA app |
| ICP | Abu Dhabi and other emirates | Confirm on icp.gov.ae |

1. Use one channel per application file.
2. Budget AED 10-150 variance for service lines.
3. Re-quote fees the week you pay, not the month you research.

### Does the Golden Visa fee include the Emirates ID?

No. The AED 4,000-5,500 government fee range quoted for the Golden Visa does not always include the Emirates ID, which is a separate ICP transaction. Some PRO packages bundle everything; always ask what is included before paying.

### Are visa fees refundable if my application is rejected?

Entry permit fees and most residency application fees are non-refundable once the application has been submitted and processing has begun. Medical test fees paid to clinics are similarly non-refundable. If rejection is due to a documentation error rather than eligibility failure, you can reapply, but you pay fees again from the entry permit stage.

### Can I pay UAE visa fees online?

ICP Smart Services allows online payment for most applications via credit or debit card. GDRFA Dubai has its own smart app which also accepts online payments. Typing centres additionally accept cash and card at their physical counters. For employer-sponsored employment visas, payment is made through the employer's MOHRE account.

### Are there discounts for applying multiple visas at once?

Batch corporate applications processed through an employer's MOHRE and ICP accounts may attract slightly reduced per-unit service fees, particularly for high-volume employers. There is no standard public discount scheme for individual applicants applying for multiple family members simultaneously; each application is processed and billed separately.

Visa fee budgeting for common UAE scenarios in this market typically starts with the government band, then adds AED 570-870 per person for Emirates ID and medical. We tell a single Golden applicant to hold AED 6,000-8,000 liquid. A family of four on employment dependents often needs AED 6,000-12,000 before PRO fees.

| Scenario | Cash to hold (AED) | Notes |

| Solo employment | 1,500-3,500 | Confirm HR coverage |
| Couple Green Visa | 6,000-10,000 | Two medicals + IDs |
| Golden + 2 children | 10,000-18,000 | Dependents separate |

1. List every person who needs medical and ID.
2. Separate DLD 4% property costs from visa cash.
3. Verify icp.gov.ae or GDRFA the week you pay.

All-in budgeting for UAE visas in 2026 works best when you treat government fee tables as the floor and then layer Emirates ID, medical fitness, typing centres, and optional PRO support as separate lines. For a main Golden Visa applicant, that usually means AED 4,000-5,500 in government charges plus roughly AED 600-900 for ID and clinic work, with dependents adding AED 1,000-3,000 each before any consultant package of AED 2,000-8,000. Employment visas look cheaper at AED 1,000-2,500 government-side, yet families still underestimate four medical appointments and four Emirates ID cards within the first month. Tourist stays remain the lightest line at AED 100-1,000 depending on duration, but frequent visitors who bounce on 90-day caps often spend more annually than a single Green Visa filing. Keep a written checklist, confirm channels (GDRFA Dubai versus ICP), and leave a 15-25% contingency above your spreadsheet total for reprints and attestation.
