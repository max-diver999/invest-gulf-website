UAE visa fees are one of the most searched, and most misunderstood, costs in UAE immigration planning. Government fee tables look deceptively simple, but the total you actually pay includes Emirates ID, medical test, typing centre charges, and sometimes attestation costs that can push the real total 40–80% above the headline figure. This guide maps every major fee category for 2026, breaks down each component, and shows you how to build a realistic budget before you apply.

> **TL;DR:** Golden Visa costs AED 4,000–5,500 per applicant in government fees (plus ~AED 600–900 for Emirates ID and medical). Green Visa and Retirement Visa run AED 2,500–4,000. Employment visas cost AED 1,000–2,500 with employers often covering part of the total. Tourist visas range from AED 100–1,000 depending on duration and entry type. Always add Emirates ID, medical test, and typing centre fees to any estimate.

## Tourist and Visit Visa Fees

Tourist visas cover leisure travel, short-term business visits, and family visits. They are typically issued through airlines, online visa platforms, travel agencies, or sponsoring UAE residents.

### Single-entry tourist visas

| Duration | Approximate Government Fee (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| 30-day single entry | 100–200 |
| 60-day single entry | 200–350 |
| 90-day single entry | 350–500 |

Single-entry visas can usually be extended once inside the UAE for a comparable fee. The 90-day visa with one extension gives a practical maximum stay of 180 days before residency is required for long-term presence.

### Multiple-entry tourist visas

| Duration | Approximate Government Fee (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| 1-year multiple entry | 500–700 |
| 5-year multiple entry | 1,000 |

The 5-year multiple-entry visa has become popular with frequent business travellers and property investors who visit the UAE regularly but do not yet hold residency. Each permitted stay is capped at 90 consecutive days.

### Transit visas

| Duration | Approximate Government Fee (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| 48-hour transit | 50 |
| 96-hour transit | 100 |

Many nationalities receive free 48-hour or 96-hour transit access through Dubai airport without a formal visa depending on passport. Check ICP or Emirates/flydubai transit policy for your nationality.

## Residency Visa Fee Components

Every UAE residency visa is built from multiple fee components. The "government fee" quoted for a visa type is rarely a single transaction, it is the sum of several separate charges processed at different stages.

The five standard components for any residency visa:

| Component | What it covers | Approximate fee (AED) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry permit | Right to enter UAE for visa stamping | 500–600 |
| Medical fitness test | Health clearance for residency | 300–500 |
| Residency stamping | Stamps the visa in your passport | 500–1,200 |
| Emirates ID | Biometric identity card | 270–370 |
| Typing/service charge | Centre that processes forms | 50–200 per visit |

The entry permit stage is skipped if you are changing status inside the UAE (i.e., you are already in the country on a valid permit). Status-change routes often reduce the total fee by AED 500–600.

The 10-year Golden Visa carries the highest government fee of any UAE residency route. The table below breaks down each billable stage for the property investor route.

| 2 | ICP/GDRFA service fee | 100–150 |
| 3 | Knowledge dirham | 10 per transaction |
| 4 | Innovation dirham | 10 per transaction |
| 5 | Medical fitness test (clinic) | 300–500 |
| 6 | Residency stamping | 1,000–1,200 |
| 7 | Emirates ID (10-year card) | 370 |

Dependent fees (spouse, children) run approximately AED 1,000–3,000 each depending on age and dependent category. The Golden Visa does not include the 4% DLD transfer fee paid at property purchase, that is a separate one-off registration cost.

Optional PRO or immigration consultant fees range from AED 2,000–8,000 and are not reflected in the government total above.

## Green Visa Fees

The 5-year Green Visa targets freelancers, self-employed professionals, and skilled employees at certain salary and qualification thresholds. It shares many structural similarities with the Golden Visa but at a lower cost and shorter validity period.

For full eligibility criteria and sub-categories, see [UAE Green Visa Guide](/guides/uae-green-visa-guide/).

| Component | Approximate Fee (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Entry permit or status change | 500–600 |
| Residency stamping | 700–1,000 |
| ICP/GDRFA service charges | 100–150 |
| Medical fitness test | 300–500 |
| Emirates ID (5-year card) | 270–300 |
| Typing centre service charge | 100–200 |
| **Estimated total** | **~2,500–4,000** |

Green Visa dependent sponsorship is available but more limited than the Golden Visa, verify current GDRFA rules on spouse and child inclusion before budgeting dependents.

## Employment Visa Fees

Employment visas are the most common route to UAE residency. The employer is legally responsible for the work permit (issued by the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation, MOHRE) and is typically required to cover most or all immigration costs. However, some employees pay these fees and are reimbursed later, so understanding the full cost structure matters.

For a complete process timeline and document guide, see [UAE Employment Visa Process](/guides/uae-employment-visa-process/).

| Component | Approximate Fee (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Work permit, MOHRE (quota cat. A/B) | 250–750 |
| Work permit, MOHRE (quota cat. C/D) | 750–1,500 |
| Entry permit | 500–600 |
| Medical fitness test | 300–500 |
| Residency stamping (2-year) | 500–800 |
| Emirates ID (2-year card) | 270 |
| Typing centre service charge | 50–200 |
| **Estimated total (cat. A, employer-paid)** | **~1,000–2,500** |

MOHRE quota category (A through D) depends on the company's Emiratisation performance rating. Category A companies pay the lowest work permit fee; category D companies face the highest. This affects what the employer pays, not the individual employee's personal fees.

## Family and Dependent Visa Fees

Sponsors holding UAE residency can sponsor spouses, children (under 18 for sons, under certain age thresholds for daughters depending on status), and in some cases parents.

| Visa type | Approximate Government Fee (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Spouse dependent | 1,000–2,000 |
| Child dependent (per child) | 500–1,500 |
| Parent dependent | 1,500–2,500 |

Each dependent also requires a medical fitness test (AED 300–500) and Emirates ID (AED 270). The minimum salary threshold for family sponsorship from employment visa holders is currently AED 4,000 per month for spouse and children (or AED 3,000 if accommodation is included). Golden Visa holders face no salary threshold for family sponsorship.

## Property Investor Visa Fees (2-Year)

The 2-year property investor visa is available for UAE property owners with a minimum ownership stake (the exact minimum threshold has evolved, verify the current qualifying amount with GDRFA or ICP before applying). It costs significantly less than the Golden Visa and is renewed every two years as long as ownership is maintained.

| Component | Approximate Fee (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Entry permit or status change | 500–600 |
| Residency stamping | 600–900 |
| Medical fitness test | 300–500 |
| Emirates ID (2-year card) | 270 |
| Typing/service charges | 100–200 |
| **Estimated total** | **~1,500–2,500** |

Investors who plan to hold UAE property long term typically graduate to the Golden Visa once their property reaches the AED 2 million registered value threshold. See [UAE Residency Visa Types](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/) for a comparison of both routes.

| Medical fitness test | 300–500 |
| Emirates ID (5-year card) | 270–300 |
| Service charges | 100–150 |
| **Estimated total** | **~2,500–4,000** |

### Remote Work Visa (1-year)

The Remote Work Visa requires proof of employment with a non-UAE company and a minimum monthly income threshold. It is renewable but does not automatically lead to permanent residency.

## Emirates ID: Fee Breakdown

Every UAE resident must hold a valid Emirates ID, issued by ICP. The card is tied to your residency visa validity, when your visa is renewed, so is your ID. See the full registration process in [Emirates ID Application Guide](/guides/emirates-id-application-guide/).

| Card validity | ICP Application Fee (AED) | Service fee | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-year card | 100 | 30–50 | ~130–150 |
| 2-year card | 200 | 30–50 | ~230–250 |
| 3-year card | 300 | 30–50 | ~330–350 |
| 5-year card | 300 | 30–50 | ~330–350 |
| 10-year card | 370 | 30–50 | ~400–420 |

Late renewal fines are charged per day after the card expires. The current fine schedule is published at icp.gov.ae and is subject to periodic revision, do not rely on historical figures.

## Medical Fitness Test Fees

All new residency applicants must pass a medical fitness test at an ICP-approved clinic. The test checks for tuberculosis and certain communicable diseases.

| Test component | Approximate fee (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Blood test | 100–150 |
| Chest X-ray | 100–150 |
| Clinic processing fee | 50–200 |
| **Total** | **~300–500** |

Results are uploaded directly to the ICP system, you do not receive a paper certificate to carry to your typing centre appointment. Processing typically takes 1–3 working days.

## Typing Centre and Service Charges

Typing centres (also called Amer centres in Dubai, Tasheel for business applications, or ICP service centres in other emirates) are the physical points where most immigration applications are processed. Their service charges are separate from government fees.

| Service | Typical charge (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Application typing (per form) | 50–150 |
| Document scanning | 20–50 per document |
| Residency stamping admin | 100–200 |
| Entry permit printing | 30–60 |
| Smart services transaction | 30–50 per submit |

Typing centre charges vary by centre and emirate. Budget AED 200–500 in total typing costs for a standard residency application.

## PRO and Immigration Consultant Fees

For complex applications, Golden Visa through multiple ownership structures, employer-sponsored batches, or cases with document attestation requirements, many applicants use a licensed PRO or immigration consultancy. These fees are entirely separate from government charges.

| Service scope | Market rate (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Employment visa processing (full service) | 1,500–3,500 |
| Golden Visa full-service (1 applicant) | 3,000–8,000 |
| Green Visa full-service | 2,000–5,000 |
| Document attestation (per document) | 200–500 |
| Translation (per page) | 80–200 |

If your employer has an in-house PRO team, these costs are typically covered. Self-sponsored applicants (Golden Visa, Green Visa) should factor PRO fees into their budget unless they are confident processing ICP Smart Services applications independently.

## Frequently Asked Questions on UAE Visa Fees

### Do visa fees differ between Dubai and other emirates?

The fee schedule is largely unified through ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security) across the country. However, GDRFA Dubai retains some independent fee components, and service/knowledge surcharges can vary slightly. As a practical rule, budget the same ranges across emirates and confirm exact totals when you initiate the application.

### Does the Golden Visa fee include the Emirates ID?

No. The AED 4,000–5,500 government fee range quoted for the Golden Visa does not always include the Emirates ID, which is a separate ICP transaction. Some PRO packages bundle everything, always ask what is included before paying.

### Are visa fees refundable if my application is rejected?

Entry permit fees and most residency application fees are non-refundable once the application has been submitted and processing has begun. Medical test fees paid to clinics are similarly non-refundable. If rejection is due to a documentation error rather than eligibility failure, you can reapply, but you pay fees again from the entry permit stage.

### Can I pay UAE visa fees online?

ICP Smart Services allows online payment for most applications via credit or debit card. GDRFA Dubai has its own smart app (GDRFA Dubai) which also accepts online payments. Typing centres additionally accept cash and card at their physical counters. For employer-sponsored employment visas, payment is made through the employer's MOHRE account.

### Are there discounts for applying multiple visas at once?

Batch corporate applications processed through an employer's MOHRE and ICP accounts may attract slightly reduced per-unit service fees, particularly for high-volume employers. There is no standard public discount scheme for individual applicants applying for multiple family members simultaneously, each application is processed and billed separately.
