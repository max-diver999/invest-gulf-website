Residents with a valid QID usually need a Qatar licence issued through the Ministry of Interior (MOI) Traffic Department. Many nationalities convert a foreign licence without a practical test if their country appears on MOI golden or approved lists; everyone else enrolls at an approved driving school. Budget QAR 500-1,500 for a straightforward conversion or QAR 2,500-5,000 for the full school route. Qatar has **no Salik-style toll gates**; running costs are fuel, insurance, Mawgif parking, and fines paid via Metrash2.

> Disclaimer: MOI Traffic Department rules and country lists change. This is operational guidance, not legal advice. Always verify eligibility on Metrash2 or at MOI licensing before booking tests.

## Do you need a car in Doha?

Car need depends on where you live and work, not on property ownership elsewhere.

| Lifestyle | Vehicle need |
|---|---|
| West Bay office + Pearl home | Car or daily ride-hail budget |
| Lusail + Metro line | Car still common for families |
| Industrial area worksite | 4x4 or employer transport |
| Single IT worker on metro | Delay purchase 3-6 months |

Factor **QAR 2,500-6,000/month** all-in for a mid-size sedan (lease, fuel, insurance, parking) using the [Doha cost of living](/guides/doha-cost-of-living/) guide before you commit.

## Who can drive on which permit?

**Visit visa holders** may drive short-term on a valid home licence plus International Driving Permit (IDP) where required. **Residents with QID** must obtain a Qatar licence within the MOI grace period. Driving on expired foreign paper stacks fines and can void insurance after an accident.

Each family member needs an **individual conversion**. A spouse cannot rely indefinitely on a sponsor's Qatar licence once she holds her own QID. Align timing with your [work visa](/guides/qatar-work-visa-process/) and [family visa](/guides/qatar-family-visa-sponsorship/) so licensing starts as soon as QID prints.

## Licence categories and light vehicles

Light vehicle licences must match the category on your foreign licence (car to car). Holders are usually expected to be **21+** for light vehicles. Automatic-only foreign licences typically convert to **automatic-restricted** Qatar licences; you cannot drive a manual car until you pass a manual test.

## Golden countries and direct MOI conversion

Direct swap (no practical test) is available when MOI lists your issuing country or US state as eligible **on the day you apply**. Lists change; always check Metrash2 **Driving Licence** services before assuming UK, US, EU, or GCC eligibility.

Requirements commonly checked:

- Foreign licence valid on arrival (not expired)
- Category matches (car to car)
- Minimum age (usually 21 for light vehicles)
- Eye test pass
- QID valid; some counters ask for 6+ months remaining

| Region (historically common on lists) | Typical treatment |
|---|---|
| GCC countries | Direct swap frequent |
| UK, EU select states | Often direct, confirm live |
| USA (some states) | Variable, check state |
| Canada, Australia | Often direct |
| India, Pakistan, Philippines | Frequently require school + test |

**Non-golden route:** register at an approved driving school, complete theory if required, minimum lesson hours, then practical test at MOI.

## Step-by-step: direct conversion via Metrash2

1. Book and pass an **MOI-approved eye test** (bring glasses or contacts you actually drive with; wrong prescription can hurt insurance claims).
2. Confirm golden-list eligibility in Metrash2.
3. Upload or present foreign licence, QID, photos, and fees (typically **QAR 500-1,500** all-in for conversion path including eye test and issuance).
4. Collect Qatar licence or arrange delivery per MOI process.

Some licences need **Ministry-approved translation**. Driving school offices often bundle translation when you are already on the school route.

## Step-by-step: full driving school route (non-golden)

Allow **6-10 weeks** in peak season when tests are busy.

| Item | QAR range |
|---|---|
| School registration | 1,000-2,500 |
| Extra lessons (10 h) | 800-1,500 |
| Test fees | 200-500 each |
| Eye test | 100-150 |
| Licence issuance | 300-500 |

Total commonly lands **QAR 2,500-5,000** with one pass; retests add fees.

**Path B (school route detail):** non-eligible licences require Qatari driving institute enrollment. Peak-season waits hit Lusail and Al Wakrah family compounds hardest even when Lusail Tram covers part of the commute.

**Path C (employer fleet):** some employers arrange bulk conversions for new hires. Confirm whether fleet insurance covers **personal weekend use** before skipping individual comprehensive quotes.

## Metrash2 for traffic and licensing services

Metrash2 is MOI's primary digital channel for licence services, fine payment, and black-point balance. Fund payments via debit card linked through your [Qatar banking setup](/guides/qatar-banking-expats/). Metrash2 is **not** a Salik account; Qatar has no gate toll product like Dubai.

Pay traffic fines in-app. Some fines show a **25% early-payment discount** within the published window; confirm active promotion rules before you pay.

## Salik vs Qatar: toll and running costs

Dubai commuters budget Salik tags and top-ups. **Qatar expressways do not use Salik or equivalent gate tolls.** Compare total mobility cost on fuel, Mawgif parking zones, compound parking, and insurance instead.

## Renting vs buying a car

Many newcomers **rent for six months** while licence and credit history mature, then buy once commute patterns are clear.

**Lease providers:** majors at Hamad International Airport (HIA), West Bay agencies, app-based monthly subscriptions.

**Buy channels:** certified pre-owned dealers, expat resale groups (extra caution on history), showroom zero-km with bank murabaha tied to salary assignment at your bank.

| Segment | Monthly rent (illustrative QAR) | Buy price band (QAR) |
|---|---|---|
| Economy sedan | 1,800-2,800 | 45,000-70,000 used |
| SUV family | 3,500-5,500 | 120,000-200,000 |
| Luxury | 6,000+ | 300,000+ |

Renting with a **brand-new Qatar licence** is possible but rental counters often want a credit card hold; debit-only deposits are frequently rejected.

## Vehicle registration (Istimara) and insurance

Purchasing requires valid **comprehensive or third-party insurance** (third party is legal minimum; comprehensive is recommended), then **Istimara** vehicle registration through MOI channels. Plan compound or villa **parking** before you buy; some employers issue NOC-style salary certificates for auto finance similar to consumer loans at [Qatar banks](/guides/qatar-banking-expats/) even when HR NOC is not required for outright purchase.

After accidents, exchange details, file police report as required, and notify insurer promptly. Dashcams are widely used; keep placement within windshield rules so footage helps claim disputes.

## Traffic fines, black points, and speed discipline

Black points accumulate toward suspension; check Metrash2 after trips abroad if you drove rental cars elsewhere. Radar enforcement is active on Doha corridors:

| Corridor | Typical limit (km/h) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Urban Doha inner | 60-80 | School zones lower |
| Expressways | 100-120 | Variable signage |
| Residential compounds | 30-40 | Private security fines separate |

Stopping in **school bus yellow zones** triggers heavy fines; watch flashing signs near [Qatar school fees](/guides/qatar-school-fees/) catchment areas.

## Parking, fuel, and daily operations

Paid **Mawgif** zones cover much of central Doha. Compound parking is often included but villa street parking may need passes. Fuel pricing is state-regulated; budget alongside insurance renewals and periodic maintenance in summer heat.

## Women, family drivers, and IDP visitors

Women follow the same conversion rules as men once on a residence QID. Visitors may use home licence plus IDP briefly; residents must convert. **International Driving Permit** alone does not replace a Qatar licence after residency starts.

## Qatar vs UAE driving setup (relocation comparison)

| Topic | Qatar | UAE (Dubai example) |
|---|---|---|
| Toll gates | None like Salik | Salik on major corridors |
| Licensing authority | MOI Traffic | RTA |
| Digital services | Metrash2 | RTA/Dubai Drive apps |
| Typical new-resident friction | Golden list vs school route | Emirates ID + RTA conversion rules |

If you split time between countries, treat licences and insurance as **separate legal systems**.

## Child seats and MOI passenger rules

| Age band | Restraint |
|---|---|
| 0-15 months | Rear-facing infant seat |
| 15 months-4 years | Forward-facing with harness |
| 4-10 years | Booster |
| Under 10 front seat | Prohibited in many guidance materials |

## Ride-hail during the first 90 days

Karwa metered taxis and app bookings work citywide, including HIA. Ride-hail can substitute for car ownership while conversion queues clear, especially for single workers on the Metro.

## Electric vehicles, motorcycles, and accessibility

EV charging infrastructure is growing but plan home or mall charging if you buy electric. Motorcycles and scooters require appropriate licence classes through the same MOI school or conversion framework. Disabled driver parking and plate benefits exist; carry medical documentation MOI requests at application time.

## Fleet, multi-car households, and housing search

Multi-car households need separate insurance policies or fleet quotes per vehicle. When choosing a villa or apartment, confirm **parking bays, guest parking, and school run distance** alongside rent; see the [Qatar property investment guide](/guides/qatar-property-investment-guide/) for area selection.

## Winter vs summer vehicle preparation

Summer heat stresses batteries, tyres, and AC systems. Service AC before May, check tyre pressure weekly in peak heat, and keep emergency water in the car for breakdown waits on expressways.

## Checklist before you rent, buy, or convert

- QID valid and visa status stable
- Golden-list status confirmed in Metrash2
- Eye test appointment booked
- SOF and salary docs ready if financing
- Insurance quote collected
- Parking confirmed at home and office
- Metrash2 installed and debit card linked
- Family members' licences planned individually

## Common mistakes

- Driving past MOI grace period on foreign licence
- Assuming spouse drives legally on your Qatar licence after she has QID
- Renting without credit card when debit hold fails
- Ignoring black points until suspension notice
- Buying manual car on automatic-only licence
- Treating Qatar like Dubai and budgeting Salik top-ups

## Exporting or selling when you leave Qatar

Selling locally is usually easier than export. Clear outstanding fines on Metrash2, cancel or transfer insurance, and complete Istimara transfer when a buyer is found. Export remains possible but adds shipping and customs cost that rarely makes sense for mass-market sedans.

## Renewing your Qatar licence

Renew through Metrash2 before expiry. Renewal tracks **QID validity** in most cases, so renew residence ID first if it expires sooner than your licence. Driving on an expired Qatar licence triggers fines and can complicate insurance claims.

## Corporate fleet and chauffeur use

If your employer provides a fleet car, confirm in writing whether you may drive outside work hours and whether you still need a personal Qatar licence for non-fleet vehicles. Chauffeur-only roles do not remove MOI licensing rules for family drivers at home.

## Who this guide suits

**Strong fit:** new residents with QID ready to convert, families planning two cars in Al Wakrah or Lusail, employers sending staff who need a clear MOI checklist.

**Weak fit:** visitors staying under two weeks on tourist rules only (home licence + IDP may suffice), or anyone needing legal advice on court cases or serious traffic prosecutions (hire local counsel).
