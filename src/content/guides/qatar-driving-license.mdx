# Qatar Driving Licence 2026: Conversion, MOI Process, Insurance & Fines

TL;DR: With a valid QID, many nationalities swap a foreign licence without a full test if their country sits on MOI golden / approved lists, everyone else takes lessons and a practical exam. Budget QAR 500–1,500 for conversion, eye test, and photos. Renting needs licence plus credit card; buying needs insurance, Istimara, and a parking plan at your compound. Pay fines on Metrash2. No Salik in Qatar, unlike Dubai there are no gate toll tags; fuel and parking drive running costs.

Parent hub: [Qatar relocation guide](/guides/qatar-relocation-guide/)

> Disclaimer: MOI Traffic Department rules and country lists change. This is operational guidance, not legal advice. Always verify eligibility on Metrash2 or at MOI licensing before booking tests.

## Driving in Doha: reality check for new expats

Qatar is car-centric. Pedestrian networks outside Pearl and West Bay are thin; April–September heat kills walk-to-school plans; school runs assume a car or bus; see [international schools](/guides/doha-international-schools-guide/).

| Lifestyle | Vehicle need |
|---|---|
| West Bay office + Pearl home | Car or daily ride-hail budget |
| Lusail + Metro line | Car still common for families |
| Industrial area worksite | 4x4 or employer transport |
| Single IT worker on metro | Delay purchase 3–6 months |

Factor QAR 2,500–6,000/month all-in for mid-size sedan (lease/fuel/insurance/parking) in [cost of living](/guides/doha-cost-of-living/) before committing.

## Licence types and categories

Most expats only need the light-vehicle category. Commercial and heavy classes sit on separate employer-sponsored tracks.

| Category | Vehicle |
|---|---|
| Light motor vehicle | Cars, SUVs for personal use |
| Motorcycle | Separate test |
| Heavy / commercial | Truck, bus, employer sponsored |
| Trailer | Add-on endorsement |

Most expats need light vehicle only.

## Who can drive on what permit

| Status | Driving right |
|---|---|
| Visit visa | Home country licence + IDP sometimes, short term only; not for residency lifestyle |
| Work residence QID | Must obtain Qatar licence within MOI grace period |
| Family dependant QID | Own licence pathway, you can't drive on sponsor licence |
| No licence | Heavy fines; insurance void if unlicensed driver |

Got your [work visa](/guides/qatar-work-visa-process/) and [family visa](/guides/qatar-family-visa-sponsorship/)? Schedule conversion immediately, driving on expired foreign paper stacks fines and voids insurance if you're caught.

## Golden countries and direct conversion

MOI maintains lists of countries whose licences convert without a practical test, subject to:

- Licence valid on arrival (not expired)
- Category matches (car licence → car)
- Holder age minimum (usually 21 for light vehicle)
- Eye test pass
- QID valid 6+ months sometimes requested

Lists change. Verify on Metrash2 "Driving Licence" service before assuming UK/US/EU/GCC automatic conversion.

| Region (historically common on lists) | Typical treatment |
|---|---|
| GCC countries | Direct swap frequent |
| UK, EU select states | Often direct, confirm |
| USA (some states) | Variable, check state |
| Canada, Australia | Often direct |
| India, Pakistan, Philippines | Frequently require school + test |

Non-golden: register at approved driving school, attend theory (if required), minimum hours, practical test at MOI.

## Step-by-step: licence conversion (golden path)

| Step | Action | Est. time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Book eye test at approved clinic | 30 min |
| 2 | Gather passport, QID, foreign licence, Arabic translation if required | 1 day prep |
| 3 | Apply via Metrash2 or MOI Traffic Department | 30–60 min queue |
| 4 | Pay fees (conversion + card) | QAR 300–600 band |
| 5 | Biometrics and photo | On site |
| 6 | Collect Qatar plastic licence | Same day to 2 weeks |

Translation: some licences need Ministry-approved translation, driving school offices often bundle service.

Automatic only: Qatar issues manual and automatic categories; if your foreign licence is automatic, Qatar licence may be restricted to automatic vehicles.

## Step-by-step: full school route (non-golden)

| Phase | Detail |
|---|---|

| Fail retry | Extra fees per attempt |

Timeline: 4–10 weeks depending on slot demand and Ramadan hours.

## Salik: not applicable in Qatar

Salik is Dubai/UAE electronic tolling. Qatar doesn't operate Salik or an equivalent nationwide gate system on Doha expressways as of 2026. Don't waste time setting up a toll tag you don't need.

| Cost type | Qatar | Dubai (contrast) |
|---|---|---|
| Road toll tags | None (Salik N/A) | Salik AED 4+ per gate |
| Fuel | Subsidised retail | Market pricing |
| Parking | Mawgif zones, mall fees | RTA parking + Salik |

Running cost focus: fuel, insurance, depreciation, parking, fines, not toll top-ups.

## Vehicle registration (Istimara) and ownership

When you buy, seller transfers Istimara at MOI Traffic:

| Document | Role |
|---|---|
| Istimara | Registration card |
| Fahs | Inspection certificate periodic |
| Insurance policy | Mandatory before transfer |
| Buyer QID | Must match |
| Sale agreement | Dealer or private |

Company car: employer retains ownership, you only drive; insurance employer problem.

## Insurance: mandatory third party and comprehensive

| Type | Covers | Typical annual (QAR) |
|---|---|---|
| Third party liability | Damage to others | 800–1,400 |
| Comprehensive | Own damage + third party | 1,800–4,500+ |
| Agency repair add-on | Dealer parts | +15–25% premium |

Factors: driver age, claims history, vehicle value, agency vs garage repair.

No insurance driving: fines + impound + personal liability unlimited.

Health link: accident injuries bill to [healthcare insurance](/guides/qatar-healthcare-guide/), motor third party does not cover your medical fully.

## Radar hotspots and speed discipline (Doha corridors)

Speed cameras cluster on **Salwa Road**, **Al Shamal Road**, **Lusail Expressway**, and **Industrial Area** connectors. GPS apps with community-reported camera pins help but **don't replace** Metrash2 fine notifications, register your mobile on Metrash for alerts within 24 hours of a violation.

| Corridor | Typical limit (km/h) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Urban Doha inner | 60–80 | School zones lower |
| Expressways | 100–120 | Variable signage |
| Residential compounds | 30–40 | Private security fines separate |

**School bus stops:** stopping in yellow bus zone triggers heavy fines, watch flashing signs near [school zones](/guides/qatar-school-fees/).

## Who this suits — decision framework

Three paths depending on your current licence status. Existing licence from an approved country (33 nations): transfer at Qatar traffic department, 1–3 days, approximately 250 QAR total. **Non-approved-country licence holder:** mandatory lessons at an authorised school (15–25 sessions at 100–200 QAR each), plus practical and theory tests. **No prior licence:** full programme at Karwa or another licensed school, 3–5 months, budget 5,000–8,000 QAR.


## FAQ

**How long to convert UK licence?** Often 1–2 visits if on golden list, verify live MOI list first.

**Do Indians need a driving test?** Historically yes, full school route; confirm Metrash2 eligibility.

**Can I drive rental on airport arrival before QID?** Short visit rules only, not for new residents.

**Is international licence enough for 6 months?** No, residents must obtain Qatar licence within legal grace.

**What is golden country list?** MOI-approved states for direct licence swap without practical test.

**How much are driving school fees total?** QAR 2,500–5,000 all-in typical with one pass.

**Does Qatar use Salik?** **No**, Salik is UAE; Qatar has no equivalent gate toll system.

**Where pay traffic fines?** Metrash2 app primarily.

**Can spouse drive my car on her foreign licence?** No after residency, she needs Qatar licence.

**Insurance minimum?** Third party liability at minimum, comprehensive recommended.

**Automatic licence to manual car?** Not allowed if licence shows automatic only.

**How renew Qatar licence?** Metrash2 before expiry, linked to QID validity.

**Rent car without credit card?** Rare, debit holds sometimes rejected.

**Parking fines?** Mawgif or municipality, pay via app or Metrash depending on issuer.

**Export car when leaving?** Possible but costly, selling locally is usually easier.

**Can I drive in Saudi on Qatar licence?** GCC reciprocal arrangements often allow short visits, verify current MOI/GCC circular before a border drive.

**Is Karwa only at airport?** Karwa operates citywide metered taxis plus app booking.

**Do I need NOC from employer to buy a car?** Not for purchase, finance may need salary certificate from HR like [banking](/guides/qatar-banking-expats/) auto loan process.

**Are dashcams legal?** Widely used; don't obstruct windshield beyond permitted zone, footage helps insurance claim disputes.
