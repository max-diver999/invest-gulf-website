## How does school map compare for Gulf buyers in 2026?

Riyadh's pool is smaller than Dubai's 200+ KHDA-regulated options but deeper than it was five years ago. Names change after mergers; always confirm legal entity on the invoice matches the campus you toured.

Research method (repeat for each shortlist school):

1. Request 2026 to 27 fee PDF and refund policy in writing
2. Ask for accreditation certificates (BSO/CIS/etc.) (confirm current official rules)
3. Confirm exam boards (Cambridge vs Edexcel vs College Board)
4. Request waiting-list status by year group: not generic "limited places"
5. Tour at pick-up time (heat, traffic, bus chaos)
6. Ask for university destinations list (last three cohorts), not logo wall

## How does waiting lists compare for Gulf buyers in 2026?

Typical timeline:

| Action | When |
|---|---|

## How does admissions process compare for Gulf buyers in 2026?

1. Online enquiry: child passport, previous reports, iqama copy (if available)
2. Assessment fee: SAR 500 to 2,500 non-refundable (confirm current official rules)
3. Placement test: English, maths; sometimes CAT4 or writing sample

Documents employers should prep early:

- Attested transcripts and withdrawal letter from previous school
- Vaccination record (Arabic/English)
- Medical insurance meeting school minimum network

## Who this suits decision framework

Not every expat profile benefits equally from this route in Saudi Arabia. Consider three common scenarios:

- **Scenario A employed professional relocating with family:** salary above AED 15,000/month, employer covers most setup costs, priority is speed and compliance. This route offers the clearest paperwork trail and the fastest timeline (typically 10 to 20 working days).
- **Scenario B freelancer or remote worker on a flexible visa:** income from multiple clients, lower tolerance for bureaucratic friction, and a preference for digital processes. Budget 20 to 30 % more time for documentation and plan around the renewal cycle.
- **Scenario C investor or retiree planning long-term residency:** capital deployed in property or business, lower urgency but higher stakes. Focus on the 10-year horizon cost differences compound over a decade, so optimise for total lifecycle expense rather than upfront convenience.

Each path converges at the same destination; the difference is timeline, cost, and hassle level. Pick the scenario closest to yours and skip sections that do not apply.
