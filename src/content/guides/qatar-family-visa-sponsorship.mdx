## Who can sponsor family in Qatar?

Sponsors typically need a valid Qatar work residence permit with qualifying basic salary and occupation category before MOI accepts a family file, and Invest Gulf rejects visit-visa-only or bachelor-studio filings when children will reside for a 12 month school year. Confirm current official MOI rules before you book flights.

Do not assume you can sponsor if:

- Basic salary sits under the threshold even when total package tops QAR 25,000
- HR will not issue a salary certificate for MOI
- You hold a visit visa only
- Your lease is a bachelor studio and you are bringing children

Property investors use separate tracks; employment family sponsorship is not investor residency and can take a separate 2 to 4 month path. See [Qatar residency by property](/guides/qatar-residency-by-investment/).

## What salary and housing rules apply?

Private-sector sponsors typically require QAR 10,000 basic salary on the e-contract without employer-provided family housing, or QAR 6,000 basic plus housing written into the contract, and Invest Gulf stresses that MOI reads the basic salary line across a 12 month contract.

| Path | Basic salary | Housing on e-contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path A | QAR 10,000+ | Not required as employer housing |
| Path B | QAR 6,000+ | Employer-provided family housing in writing |

Cash housing, transport, and schooling allowances typically do not count toward Path A's QAR 10,000 basic line. Profession filters still apply; confirm current official rules with your PRO.

**Insider tip:** Ask HR to print the basic salary field exactly as MOI will see it before you pay for attestation; total-package letters that leave basic under QAR 10,000 are the most common 4 to 8 week restart we see after families have already given notice at home.
