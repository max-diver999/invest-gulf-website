# Qatar Family Visa Sponsorship 2026: QAR 10,000 Salary Rule & Documents

**TL;DR:** To sponsor **spouse and children**, you need **QAR 10,000/month basic salary** (private sector, no family housing on contract) **or QAR 6,000/month plus employer-provided family housing** written into your e-contract. Add attested marriage and birth certificates, plus housing MOI will accept — not a bachelor studio with kids. Budget **QAR 2,000–4,000 per dependant** and **2–4 months** from submission. Read your **electronic labour contract** before you fly: housing, transport, and schooling allowances usually **don't** count toward the MOI salary line.

**Parent hub:** [Qatar relocation guide](/guides/qatar-relocation-guide/)

> **Disclaimer:** MOI rules change. Employer PRO is primary source. This is operational guidance, not immigration legal advice.

---

## Who can sponsor family in Qatar

You need to be a **resident employee** on a valid work QID, with an employer and salary structure that qualify.

**Don't assume you can sponsor if:**
- Basic salary sits under the threshold even when your "total package" tops QAR 25,000
- HR won't issue a salary certificate for MOI
- You're on a visit visa only
- Your lease is a bachelor studio and you're bringing children

**Property investors:** different tracks — employment family sponsorship is not the same as investor residency. → [Qatar residency by property](/guides/qatar-residency-by-property/)

## The salary and housing rule — two paths (MOI 2023+)

Since December 2023, family residence comes down to what MOI can read on your **electronic work contract** — salary and accommodation lines, not verbal HR promises.

| Path | Private sector (technical/specialised roles) | Government / semi-government |
|---|---|---|
| **A — salary only** | QAR **10,000**/month minimum | QAR **10,000**/month minimum |
| **B — salary + housing** | QAR **6,000**/month **plus** employer-provided **family housing** on contract | Employer **family housing** **or** QAR 10,000 |

**Source:** MOI announcement via QNA, December 2023 — confirm current text with your PRO before applying.

Cash housing, transport, and schooling allowances typically **do not** count toward Path A's QAR 10,000 basic line on contract.

**Profession filter:** private-sector sponsors usually need **non-labour / technical or specialised** occupations — not every job category qualifies. **[VERIFY job title classification with PRO]**

## The QAR 10,000 rule — basic vs total package (Path A)

MOI family sponsorship keys off **basic salary** on the labour contract — not housing, transport, or schooling allowances.

| Component | Counts for family visa? |
|---|---|
| Basic salary | **Yes** — primary |
| Housing allowance (cash) | Usually **no** |
| Transport allowance | Usually **no** |
| Schooling allowance | **no** |
| Performance bonus | **no** |

**Example trap:** Basic QAR 9,500 + housing QAR 8,000 = QAR 17,500 headline — **family visa denied**.

**Fix before move:** restructure contract to raise basic above QAR 10,000 even if total unchanged (employer HR + legal must agree).

## Document checklist

### For sponsor (employee)

- Valid QID copy
- Passport copy
- Salary certificate from employer stating **basic salary** (English + Arabic)
- Employment contract copy
- Lease agreement + **landlord NOC** for family occupancy
- Kahramaa bill or utility proof (sometimes)
- Bank statements (sometimes requested)

### For spouse

- Passport (6+ months validity)
- Marriage certificate — attested chain (home country → Qatar embassy → MOFA Qatar)
- Photos (MOI spec)
- Medical fitness (after entry or in-country per current rule)

### For each child

- Passport
- Birth certificate — attested
- Photos
- School letter (if school-age and enrolling)

**Attestation timeline:** start 8–12 weeks before planned family arrival if documents not ready.

## Accommodation — "suitable housing" explained

MOI wants proof your unit can legally house a family:

- **Landlord letter** confirming spouse and X children permitted
- **Two-bedroom minimum** commonly expected when sponsoring children
- **Studio / bachelor flat** with two kids — frequent rejection

Rent 2BR+ before submitting, or stay in a corporate family compound with an HR letter.

**Pearl/Lusail towers:** check building rules — some cap occupants per sqm.

## Step-by-step process

### Phase 1: Pre-arrival (optional)

Spouse/children can remain home until your QID stable — reduces visit visa overstay stress.

### Phase 2: Entry permit for dependants

Sponsor applies via employer PRO or Metrash2 (eligibility varies). Entry permit issued — dependants fly in.

### Phase 3: Medical fitness

Each dependant over required age — blood test, X-ray at approved clinic.

### Phase 4: Biometrics

Fingerprints and photos at MOI centre.

### Phase 5: Residence permit sticker / QID card

Fees ~QAR 500 per adult reference; children similar tiers.

**Total timeline:** 2–4 months from document submission if clean first time.

## Newborn registration in Qatar — from hospital to QID

A baby born in Qatar triggers **two parallel tracks**: civil registration and immigration add-on to sponsor permit.

### Within first days (hospital)

- Hospital issues **birth notification** — get multiple Arabic/English copies
- Confirm father's QID and marriage attestation on file with hospital admin
- Add baby to **employer health insurance** within policy window (often 30 days)

### MOI and passport steps (typical order)

| Step | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Birth certificate from civil authority | Parents | May need MOFA attestation if embassy involved |
| Baby passport from home country embassy | Parents | Book early — some embassies batch weekly |
| Add dependant under sponsor file | PRO | Requires updated salary certificate still valid |
| Medical for infant | Clinic | Rules vary by age — PRO books |
| Baby QID / residence sticker | MOI | Fees similar tier to child reference |

**Timeline target:** complete within **60–90 days** of birth; overstaying informal status risks fines.

**If mother on visit visa during birth:** legal status is sensitive — employer PRO and embassy **before** delivery due date if possible.

**Naming:** ensure passport name matches birth certificate exactly — mismatch delays Metrash2 linking.

## Divorce, separation, and custody edge cases

Qatar family visa logic assumes an intact marriage certificate chain. Separation scenarios need a lawyer early.

### Still married but separated

- Sponsor cannot usually transfer spouse to independent status without spouse finding own employer sponsor or exit
- Children often remain on father's sponsorship in practice — court orders from home country are **not automatically** recognised; local lawyer if dispute

### Divorce initiated in Qatar or abroad

- **Residence cancellation** for spouse may be required before new marriage sponsorship elsewhere
- Custody orders: school may require both parents' consent letters until MOI status clear
- Housing: landlord family letter may list spouse until lease amended

### Single father or single mother sponsorship

- **Single mother** sponsoring child: case-by-case MOI discretion; employer support letter critical; some employers only structure male sponsor files
- **Single father** with custody docs attested: more common but attestation chain heavy
- Do not rely on forum advice — PRO plus immigration lawyer for your nationality

### Death of sponsor

- Dependants enter grace / cancellation pathway — embassy and employer HR emergency protocol
- Life insurance and repatriation clause in contract matter more than visa article reading

## Document attestation — country examples

Attestation is where timelines die. Start in **home country** before shipping originals to Doha.

### United Kingdom

1. UK marriage/birth cert → **FCDO apostille** (or solicitor notarisation then apostille)
2. Qatar Embassy London legalisation (check appointment slots)
3. MOFA Qatar upon arrival if second leg required

**Planning time:** 4–8 weeks if mail service.

### India

1. Local notary → state Home Department attestation
2. MEA apostille (new Hague route for some docs — verify category)
3. Qatar Embassy India
4. MOFA Qatar

**Planning time:** 3–6 weeks; state variation huge.

### Philippines

1. PSA copies (marriage, birth)
2. DFA authentication
3. Qatar Embassy Manila
4. MOFA Qatar

**Planning time:** 2–5 weeks; PSA courier adds days.

### United States

1. County clerk copy → state Secretary of State
2. US Dept of State apostille (Washington)
3. Qatar Embassy DC or consulate jurisdiction
4. MOFA Qatar

**Planning time:** 6–10 weeks if state and federal both needed.

### Pakistan, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon

- Often **four-step** chains with religious court docs for marriage
- Arabic translation quality matters — use MOI-approved translators in Doha for final leg

**Rule:** one broken link = full restart. Photograph every stamp page.

## PRO timeline — Gantt-style planning (weeks)

Use this as a programme plan when HR promises "family in six weeks."

```
Week 0-2   HR restructures basic salary to >= QAR 10,000 (if needed)
Week 2-4   Attestation completed in home country; scans to PRO
Week 4-5   Landlord family letter + 2BR lease signed
Week 5-6   Entry permit application submitted (Metrash2 / PRO)
Week 6-7   Dependants fly in (visit vs entry permit per status)
Week 7-8   Medical fitness appointments all dependants
Week 8-9   Biometrics MOI centre
Week 9-12  QID printing; health card; Metrash2 activation
Week 12+   Buffer for resubmission (+2-6 weeks)
```

| Milestone | Risk if late |
|---|---|
| Salary certificate | Rejection day one |
| Landlord letter | Rejection after flight booked |
| Medical fail | Retest; tuberculosis hold rare |
| Passport validity under 6 months | Airlines deny boarding |

**Ramadan / Eid:** add **2 weeks** to every MOI-facing milestone.

Coordinate with [Qatar work visa process](/guides/qatar-work-visa-process/) so sponsor QID has **12+ months validity** remaining at submission.

| Factor | Qatar | UAE (typical employment) | Saudi Arabia |

## Trailing spouse on visit visa — risks and limits

Some families do **short visits** while sponsor settles. Rules tighten; overstaying visit harms later residence.

- Visit visa duration stamped at border — track in Metrash2
- Spouse cannot work on visit
- Driving on visit conversion rules change — assume sponsor drives
- Medical for residence must happen after correct entry permit type
- Children out of school too long may lose placement — coordinate with admissions office

Better: spouse waits until **entry permit for residence** issued, then flies once.

## FAQ

**Does housing allowance count toward QAR 10,000?** No — basic salary line only.

**Can I sponsor family on visit visa without job?** No. You need valid work residence as sponsor.

**Single mother sponsoring child?** Case-specific. Get legal counsel.

**Can family stay if I lose job?** Grace period is limited. Transfer sponsorship or plan exit immediately.

**Is newborn automatically on visa?** No. Register and add through MOI process.

**Company villa — still need landlord letter?** HR letter from company housing often substitutes.
