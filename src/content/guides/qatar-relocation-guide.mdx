## Who relocates to Qatar and why does salary structure matter?

Relocating to Qatar typically suits energy, finance, and project professionals who can clear MOI family rules in the area within 2 to 4 months after arrival. Foreign buyers must separate basic salary from allowances early. We treat a below-threshold basic as a blocked family move even when total pay looks 20% higher on paper.

## What checklist should you run before you sign an offer?

An offer checklist for Qatar typically starts with basic salary on its own line, because MOI family sponsorship in the area uses that figure, not the full package. Foreign buyers should confirm housing, schooling caps, insurance, flights, and gratuity for the first 5 years. Our analysis rejects verbal HR summaries without numbers.

- Basic salary clear for family visa math
- Housing: company villa/compound written in, or cash only?
- Schooling allowance: cap per child, paid direct or reimbursed?
- Health insurance: principal only, or spouse and children?
- Annual flights and end-of-service formula in writing

Family visa math (MOI 2023 guidance): sponsor at QAR 10,000 basic, or QAR 6,000 basic plus employer-provided accommodation. A QAR 8,000 basic plus cash housing usually fails. Government visa fee clawbacks in contracts are a red flag.

**Insider tip:** Open a one-page salary audit before flights. If basic sits under the MOI line, negotiate that number first. Housing upgrades will not rescue family sponsorship later.

## How does document attestation work for Qatar moves?

Document attestation for Qatar typically takes 2 to 6 weeks from zero once degree, marriage, birth, and police papers enter embassy queues. Foreign buyers in the area should budget translation where MOI wants Arabic versions. Invest Gulf uses PRO queue relationships rather than DIY reprints that get rejected.

- Start attestation before resignation week if possible
- Carry English prescription letters for chronic medication
- Keep immunisation records for school enrolment
- Avoid unlicensed translation printouts at counters

## How does the Qatar visa pathway work step by step?

The Qatar visa pathway typically takes 4 to 8 weeks from document submission through medicals, stamping, and QID collection in the area. Foreign buyers receive a PDF entry permit and should keep passport validity at 6 months minimum, with 9 months safer. Our analysis books medical appointments inside 48 hours of arrival.

1. Employer submits; you print the entry permit
2. Arrive, stamp in, meet PRO or driver if arranged
3. Complete medical fitness without skipping slots
4. Biometrics and QID collection before banking depth

Employer package negotiation in the area typically means protecting basic salary for MOI family rules and confirming end-of-service at about 21 days basic per year for the first 5 years, then 30 days thereafter. Foreign buyers need family processing of 2 to 4 months after landing. We consistently model dependant fees as a cash line.

Foreign buyers should not sign Pearl or Lusail leases until school buses and a 07:30 office commute are tested. Our analysis keeps a cash buffer for deposits across the first 60 days.

- Map West Bay, Pearl, Lusail, and Al Sadd against office pins
- Reject listings that only work on weekend traffic samples

## What salary and cash rules matter before you sign a lease?

MOI basic-salary math matters more than tower views for most families. A household targeting monthly burn in the mid band still fails if basic salary sits under the sponsorship line for 2 to 4 months of dependant processing after arrival. Bring first-60-days cash because medicals, deposits, and temporary housing hit before payroll, often equal to 30 to 45 days of planned burn across Doha neighbourhoods. Singles can live leaner, yet the same 4 to 6 cheque culture applies across West Bay and Pearl stock for new contracts. If HR cannot put family housing or a qualifying basic in writing, delay school applications rather than forcing a Pearl lease you may need to exit within 12 months. Qatar's process is employer-led to a greater degree than the UAE's, which puts the timeline largely outside the employee's control. Build the plan around the employer's file rather than around your preferred dates.
