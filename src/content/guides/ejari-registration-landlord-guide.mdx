## What Ejari is and how it fits Dubai's rental framework

**Ejari** (Arabic: "my rent") is RERA's electronic tenancy registration platform under Dubai Land Department. It standardises lease contracts, stores transacted rent levels, and feeds the **RERA Rental Index** that caps renewal increases.

### Ejari vs other systems

| System | Purpose | User |
|---|---|---|
| Ejari | Long-term lease registration (6+ months) | Landlords, tenants |
| Dubai REST | Inquiry — title, Ejari bands, transacts | Investors, landlords |
| DET Holiday Home | STR permit (under 12 months) | STR operators |
| Mollak | Service charge schedules | Owners, landlords |
| RDC | Rental disputes | Landlords, tenants |

## Legal requirements for landlords

### Mandatory registration

- All **residential** tenancy contracts **longer than 6 months** must be registered
- Commercial leases use separate registration pathways
- Standard form incorporates RERA Law No. 26 of 2007 (as amended) and Law No. 33 of 2008

### Landlord obligations

| Obligation | Detail |
|---|---|
| Register lease | Within required period of contract start |
| Accurate rent recording | Registered rent = actual contracted rent |
| Maintain property | Structural and major systems per law |
| RERA increase rules | At renewal only — not mid-term |
| Security deposit handling | Documented in contract — typically 5–10% |

### Tenant rights tied to Ejari

- DEWA account in tenant name (requires Ejari)
- Visa and Emirates ID address proof
- School admission documentation
- RDC access for disputes

## Step-by-step: registering a new Ejari contract

### Method 1: Dubai REST app (recommended 2026)

1. Log in with UAE Pass (landlord or authorised manager)
2. Select **Ejari Registration**
3. Enter property details matching title deed (community, building, unit)
4. Enter tenant Emirates ID / passport and contact
5. Input contract dates, rent amount, payment cheques schedule
6. Upload signed tenancy contract (RERA standard template)
7. Pay registration fee (~AED 220)
8. Receive Ejari certificate with unique contract number

### Method 2: Approved typing centres

1. Visit RERA-approved typing centre with title deed copy, IDs, signed lease
2. Centre enters data and processes fee
3. Certificate issued same day typically

### Method 3: Property management company

Most Dubai agencies include Ejari in management fee (5–8% of rent) — confirm in management agreement.

### Documents required

| Document | Landlord | Tenant |
|---|---|---|
| Title deed copy | Yes | No |
| Passport / Emirates ID | Yes | Yes |
| Signed tenancy contract | Yes | Yes |
| Previous Ejari (if renewal) | Optional | Optional |
| POA (if agent registers) | If applicable | No |

## Renewal workflow for landlords

### Timeline

Begin renewal **90 days before expiry** — Dubai market standard notice period is often 90 days for rent review discussions.

### Renewal steps

1. Pull **RERA Rental Increase Calculator** result
2. Compare current Ejari rent to index band
3. Propose lawful increase (0–20% per calculator)
4. Negotiate with tenant or accept index outcome
5. Sign renewal contract
6. Register renewal on Ejari (new certificate)
7. Update cheques / payment method

### RERA rent increase table (simplified)

| Current rent vs RERA index | Allowed increase |
|---|---|
| More than 10% below index | Up to 20% |
| 6–10% below index | Up to 15% |
| 1–5% below index | Up to 10% |
| At index (within band) | Up to 5% |
| Above index | 0% |

Exact percentages follow RERA calculator output — do not rely on this table alone for legal decisions.

**Investor note:** You cannot raise rent 15% annually because "market feels hot." Ejari + index govern renewals.

## Ejari and DEWA: landlord practicalities

Tenants require Ejari to transfer **DEWA** (utilities) to their name. Landlord workflow:

| Event | Landlord action |
|---|---|
| New tenant | Register Ejari before handover keys |
| Tenant exit | Confirm DEWA final bill cleared |
| Vacancy gap | Landlord pays DEWA minimum + AC district cooling |
| Between tenancies | Budget AED 2,000–4,000 connection costs |

District cooling (Empower, Tabreed, etc.) is separate from DEWA — clarify in contract who pays.

## Security deposits and Ejari

Standard practice:

- **5%** deposit most apartments
- **10%** some villas and premium units
- Held in landlord account — not RERA escrow
- Return within agreed period after exit inspection

Ejari contract should document deposit amount and conditions. Disputes → RDC.

## Eviction and dispute pathways

Ejari registration strengthens landlord position at **Rental Dispute Centre (RDC)**.

### Common grounds (simplified — legal advice for specific cases)

- Non-payment of rent after formal notice process
- Contract expiry with lawful non-renewal notice
- Property sale (specific notice rules apply)
- Breach of contract terms

Unregistered leases weaken enforcement. Always register.

### RDC process outline

1. File case via RERA portal
2. Mediation attempt
3. Hearing and judgment
4. Execution via courts if needed

## Advanced Ejari strategies for portfolio landlords

### Multi-property registration workflow

Landlords managing multiple units benefit from systematised Ejari processes:

| Portfolio size | Recommended approach | Annual time saving |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2–3 units | Self-managed via REST app | 5–8 hours |
| 4–8 units | Property manager with bulk processing | 15–20 hours |
| 9+ units | Dedicated admin or agency relationship | 25+ hours |

**Bulk renewal coordination:** Align renewal dates across portfolio to batch Ejari renewals, RERA calculator runs, and rent increase negotiations. Some landlords target January–February renewals to avoid Ramadan and summer vacation disruptions.

### RERA index optimisation tactics

Smart landlords use Ejari data strategically to maximise lawful increases:

| Scenario | Tactic | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit renting below market | Register actual rent (not lower) | Preserve increase headroom |
| Market softening | Accept smaller increase | Maintain tenant, avoid vacancy |
| Strong demand area | Push full RERA allowance | Maximise income within legal limits |
| Long-term tenant | Negotiate mid-band increase | Balance retention vs income |

**Legal note:** Always use RERA's official calculator. "Rules of thumb" about increase percentages are unreliable and may violate tenancy law.

### Cross-border tax implications

Foreign landlords should understand Ejari's role in tax compliance:

| Jurisdiction | Ejari relevance | Tax implications |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UK tax residents | Rental income evidence | Declare UAE rental on UK return |
| US citizens | IRS foreign income reporting | FBAR if accounts exceed USD 10K |
| German residents | KAP-VERF requirements | Document foreign rental income |
| UAE Golden Visa | Tax residence establishment | Support 90-day presence claims |

**Documentation:** Maintain Ejari certificates, RERA calculator results, and bank transfer records for cross-border tax compliance.

## Economic cycle management

### Market downturn strategies

During economic stress, Ejari becomes even more critical for landlord protection:

| Economic scenario | Tenant behaviour | Ejari-enabled response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recession/job losses | Payment delays increase | RDC enforcement stronger with registration |
| Market oversupply | Tenants demand rent reductions | RERA calculator prevents illegal decreases |

**Counter-cyclical advantage:** Properly registered landlords can maintain legal rent levels while unregistered competitors face tenant pressure without regulatory backing.

### Inflation and rent escalation

UAE's inflation targeting affects rental markets:

| Inflation scenario | RERA index response | Landlord strategy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low inflation (1–2%) | Index increases modestly | Focus on tenant retention |

| High inflation (5%+) | Index may lag inflation | Negotiate allowable maximum increases |
| Deflation (negative) | Index may decrease | Accept market reality, avoid vacancies |

**Long-term perspective:** Ejari's systematic approach smooths rental income volatility compared to unregulated markets.
