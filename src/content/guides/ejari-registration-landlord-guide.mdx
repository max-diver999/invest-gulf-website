Ejari is not administrative paperwork, it is the legal and data backbone of Dubai's long-term rental market. For landlords, registration determines whether rent increases follow RERA rules, whether DEWA transfers cleanly to tenants, and whether the Rental Dispute Centre enforces your contract. For investors, Ejari transacted rents are the income input behind every serious yield model in the [Dubai Rental Yield Guide](/guides/dubai-rental-yield-guide/). Registration also matters at exit. A landlord selling a tenanted unit needs a current Ejari to evidence the income the buyer is underwriting, and an unregistered tenancy adds weeks to due diligence while both sides reconstruct what was actually agreed. Registering each contract inside the first 30 days keeps the rent history clean across a 5 to 10 year hold and removes an argument the rental committee would otherwise have to settle.

## What is Ejari and how does it fit Dubai's rental framework?

Ejari typically registers your Dubai tenancy contract with RERA under the Dubai Land Department so registered rent feeds renewals, DEWA transfers, and REST yield research, and our research treats unregistered leases as weak RDC evidence before foreign buyers underwrite portal rents over a 12 month term on apartment stock.

Without Ejari, landlords lose clean DEWA transfers and weaken RDC enforceability on the same 12 month contract they thought was already binding.

## What are the legal requirements for landlords?

Landlord legal duties typically include registering the lease, recording actual rent, maintaining major systems, applying RERA increases only at renewal, and documenting deposits of 5 to 10%, and Invest Gulf checks each obligation before foreign buyers rely on handshake terms over a 12 month tenancy for apartment and villa residents.

| Obligation | Detail |
|---|---|
| Register lease | Within required period of contract start |
| Accurate rent recording | Registered rent = actual contracted rent |
| Maintain property | Structural and major systems per law |
| RERA increase rules | At renewal only, not mid-term |
| Security deposit handling | Documented in contract, typically 5 to 10% |

### Tenant rights tied to Ejari

- DEWA account in tenant name (requires Ejari)
- Visa and Emirates ID address proof
- School admission documentation
- RDC access for disputes

## How do you register Ejari step by step?

Ejari registration typically completes through Dubai REST, approved typing centres, or a manager charging 5 to 8% of rent, with official fees near AED 220 plus centre add-ons, and Invest Gulf prefers landlord-controlled data entry before foreign buyers leave unit numbers mismatched for 1 to 3 working days.

### Method 1: Dubai REST app

1. Log in with UAE Pass or landlord credentials
2. Upload signed tenancy contract, title deed, IDs
3. Pay Ejari fee online
4. Download Ejari certificate

Detail: [Dubai REST App Due Diligence](/guides/dubai-rest-app-property-due-diligence/)

### Method 2: Approved typing centres

1. Visit RERA-approved typing centre with title deed copy, IDs, signed lease
2. Centre enters data and processes fee
3. Certificate issued same day typically

### Method 3: Property management company

Most Dubai agencies include Ejari in management fee (5 to 8% of rent). Confirm in the management agreement. See [Property Management Dubai Cost](/guides/property-management-dubai-cost/).

### Documents required

| Document | Landlord | Tenant |
|---|---|---|
| Title deed copy | Yes | No |
| Passport / Emirates ID | Yes | Yes |
| Signed tenancy contract | Yes | Yes |
| Previous Ejari (if renewal) | Optional | Optional |
| POA (if agent registers) | If applicable | No |

## What do Ejari fees cost and who pays?

Ejari fees typically cost about AED 220 officially, AED 100 to 250 at typing centres, and AED 200 to 500 for attested translations, with renewals priced similarly, and our research treats the fee as minor versus vacancy before foreign buyers skip registration to save cash over a 12 month lease.

Negotiate at lease signing who pays, but cost is minor versus vacancy or dispute cost.

## What is the landlord renewal workflow?

Renewal workflow typically starts 90 days out with the RERA calculator, then lawful notices for 0 to 20% increases, signed renewal, and fresh Ejari registration, and we reject agent "market feel" hikes before foreign buyers attempt mid-term raises outside index bands for apartment and villa residents.

1. Pull RERA Rental Increase Calculator result
2. Compare current Ejari rent to index band
3. Propose lawful increase (0 to 20% per calculator)
4. Negotiate with tenant or accept index outcome
5. Sign renewal contract
6. Register renewal on Ejari (new certificate)
7. Update cheques / payment method

### RERA rent increase table (simplified)

| Current rent vs RERA index | Allowed increase |
|---|---|
| More than 10% below index | Up to 20% |
| 6 to 10% below index | Up to 15% |
| 1 to 5% below index | Up to 10% |
| At index (within band) | Up to 5% |
| Above index | 0% |

Exact percentages follow RERA calculator output. Use the live [Dubai rent increase calculator RERA](/guides/dubai-rent-increase-calculator-rera/) at renewal, not agent "market rent" quotes.

Investor note: You cannot raise rent 15% annually because "market feels hot." Ejari plus index govern renewals.

**Insider tip:** Never under-register rent to "help" a tenant on day one; Invest Gulf renewals stall when year-two calculator inputs sit 10% below true contracted rent and lawful increases are permanently capped.

## How does Ejari link to DEWA?

DEWA linkage typically requires a valid Ejari certificate before tenants open or transfer accounts into their name, and a delay of 14 to 30 days can leave landlords exposed to utility disputes, and we keep PDF packs together before foreign buyers inherit void periods over a 12 month lease.

Keep the Ejari PDF, tenancy contract, RERA calculator output, and DEWA transfer confirmation in one folder per unit; missing any piece slows reconnection when tenants change mid-year and bills of AED 400 to 1,100 sit in the wrong name.

## What should you verify on security deposits?

Security deposits typically run 5% on most apartments and 10% on some villas, held in the landlord account rather than RERA escrow, and Invest Gulf documents amounts on Ejari before foreign buyers face exit disputes without a 12 month condition trail for apartment and villa residents across ready stock and new leases.

- 5% deposit most apartments; 10% some villas and premium units
- Held in landlord account, not RERA escrow
- Return within agreed period after exit inspection
- Ejari contract documents deposit amount and conditions; disputes go to RDC

## What are eviction and dispute pathways?

Eviction and dispute paths typically weaken without Ejari, so landlords should register before relying on RDC mediation, hearings, and court execution that can run for several months, and Invest Gulf files registered contracts first before foreign buyers attempt rent recovery on a 12 month arrears case.

Unregistered leases complicate DEWA transfers and rent increase math, and enforcement cases for late registration have reported fines from AED 500 to 5,000 within the first 14 to 30 days after signing.

1. File case via RERA portal
2. Mediation attempt
3. Hearing and judgment
4. Execution via courts if needed

## How does Ejari differ from short-term licensing?

Ejari typically covers long-term leases, while stays under 6 months require a DET Holiday Home Permit rather than a fake 12 month Ejari wrapped around nightly Airbnb, and we separate periods before foreign buyers mix STR and long-term rules on one unit.

Landlords cannot avoid DET requirements by signing a fake 12-month Ejari while operating nightly Airbnb. DET and building OA enforce separately. Full STR guide: [Short-Term Rental Dubai License](/guides/short-term-rental-dubai-license/).

## How should investors use Ejari data before buying?

Investors typically should pull Ejari mid-band rents on REST before offering, because listing rent of AED 85,000 against Ejari mid AED 72,000 can distort gross yield by 1.5 percentage points or more, and we anchor purchase models on registered rents for apartment and villa residents.

Why it matters for portfolio tracking: registered rent is the renewal baseline for every unit you own.

## What common landlord mistakes cost money?

Common mistakes typically include registering late within 14 to 30 days, under-recording rent, mixing STR without permits, and skipping a 3 to 5% maintenance reserve, with fines reported from AED 500 to 5,000, and we flag each error before foreign buyers inherit capped increases.

1. Failing to register within 14 to 30 days of signing (fines AED 500 to 5,000 reported in enforcement cases)
2. Under-registering rent, which caps future lawful increases
3. Mixing STR and long-term without permits and building NOC
4. Skipping maintenance reserve (benchmark 3 to 5% of annual rent for older stock)

## What makes up renewal due diligence?

Renewal checklist typically runs calculator work at 90 days, lawful notice at 60 days, and Ejari re-registration at 30 days, with screenshots kept for banks and RDC, and Invest Gulf stores cross-border tax packs before foreign buyers miss a 12 month filing trail for apartment and villa residents.

- 90 days before expiry: run calculator with current Ejari rent
- 60 days: serve lawful increase notice if applicable
- 30 days: sign renewal and re-register Ejari
- Keep RERA calculator screenshot for RDC or bank audits

**Cross-border landlords:** Keep Ejari PDF, bank credit slips, and calculator output for home-country tax filing (UK, US, DE, IN treat UAE rental as reportable even at 0% UAE personal tax).

## How should portfolio landlords manage multiple units?

Portfolio landlords typically need written Ejari SLAs with one manager or batched REST filings, because banks auditing mortgages expect registered leases on every financed unit, and we track renewals per door before foreign buyers assume one missed unit is harmless over a 5 year hold across a multi-door book.

Write SLAs that require Ejari certificates within 14 days of each new 12 month lease and renewal packs 90 days before expiry. Management fees of 5 to 8% of rent should include those filings in writing.

Register every long-term lease promptly via Dubai REST or approved centres. Use RERA's calculator at renewal, not portal comparables. Keep Ejari distinct from DET short-term licensing. For income modelling, anchor on Ejari via the [Dubai Rental Yield Guide](/guides/dubai-rental-yield-guide/). For purchase verification, use [Dubai REST App Due Diligence](/guides/dubai-rest-app-property-due-diligence/) and the [Dubai Property Investment Guide](/guides/dubai-property-investment-guide/).

## What if the tenant refuses to register Ejari?

Tenant refusal to register typically still leaves the landlord responsible, and missing Ejari can keep DEWA in your name as void risk, and Invest Gulf serves written notices and documents breaches before foreign buyers escalate to RDC after 30 days of stalled papers for apartment and villa residents.

## How do furnished leases affect Ejari rent recording?

Furnished leases typically require registering the full contracted annual rent including furniture premiums paid in the cheque total, because side letters that understate rent cap future index increases, and we reject split structures before foreign buyers audit renewals after 12 months for apartment and villa residents.

## What changes when you sell a tenanted unit?

Selling a tenanted unit typically means buyers will demand Ejari history for in-place rent and remaining term, which speeds bank valuation, and we coordinate vacant versus in-place notice before foreign buyers accept offers inside a 30 to 90 day closing window for apartment and villa residents.

## How should landlords handle mid-contract maintenance?

Mid-contract maintenance typically keeps major repairs with landlords and consumables with tenants per contract wording, with photo snag lists dated before Ejari helping deposit exits, and Invest Gulf stores contractor invoices before foreign buyers face uninhabitable claims over a 12 month lease for apartment and villa residents.

## When should landlords use professional Ejari typing centres?

Typing centres typically help when REST rejects unit-number mismatches, bilingual contracts need structured entry, or portfolios need batch filing for AED 100 to 250, and we keep centre receipts with Ejari PDFs before foreign buyers lose weeks to rejection loops for apartment and villa residents.

Invest Gulf client landlord files show the expensive mistakes cluster around late registration within 14 to 30 days, understated rent on Ejari, and mixing DET short-term stays with long-term contracts on the same unit. Typing centres add AED 100 to 250 but often save more than a week of REST rejection loops when title deed unit numbers disagree with the lease. Security deposits of 5% on apartments and 10% on some villas sit in landlord accounts, not RERA escrow, so condition photos dated before registration matter at exit. Portfolio owners should write Ejari SLAs into management mandates charging 5 to 8% of rent. Cross-border owners still report UAE rental income at home even when UAE personal tax is 0%, so keep PDFs and bank slips for each 12 month cycle. Register the contract in the first weeks rather than at renewal, because Ejari is the document DEWA, schools and the rental committee all ask for, and none of them accept a signed lease alone.
