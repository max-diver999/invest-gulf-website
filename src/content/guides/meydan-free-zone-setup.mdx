## What should buyers verify on step-by-step mfz registration process?

### Step 1: Select Activity and Package Tier

Log in through Meydan Free Zone or an authorised corporate service provider portal. Search the activity list and pick the code that matches your first 12 months of revenue, not a wish-list of future services. Misclassified activities trigger compliance rework at renewal.

Choose a package tier at the same time:

- **Zero-visa flexi:** licence plus registered address only
- **One-visa bundle:** common entry promotion for founders who need UAE residence
- **Multi-visa upgrade:** higher workspace tier for teams

Confirm visa count, renewal price, and whether the package includes establishment card fees before you pay.

### Step 2: Reserve Company Name

Names must comply with UAE conventions: no prohibited words (Bank, Insurance, Royal without approval), no offensive or religious misuse, and no confusing similarity to existing entities. MFZ typically approves names within one business day if the list is clean.

### Step 3: Submit Shareholder and Director KYC

Standard requirements for individual shareholders:

- Passport copy (valid at least six months)
- Proof of residential address (utility bill or bank statement under three months)
- CV or short professional profile (media and trading activities)
- Source of funds summary for trading and e-commerce

Corporate shareholders add certificate of incorporation, good standing, board resolution, and UBO chain documentation for anyone above 25% ownership.

### Step 4: Pay Fees and Pass Compliance Review

Meydan Free Zone conducts AML and beneficial ownership checks aligned with UAE federal requirements. Once approved in principle, pay the package fee. Rejections usually trace to incomplete UBO disclosure or activity mismatch with business description.

### Step 5: Receive Corporate Documents

After payment clearance, MFZ issues:

- Trade Licence
- Certificate of Registration / Incorporation
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Share certificates (if applicable)

These documents are mandatory for bank account opening and visa processing.

### Step 6: Open Corporate Bank Account

Proceed immediately after licence issuance. MFZ entities with e-commerce and trading activities face standard UAE KYC. Prepare:

- Business plan (one to two pages)
- Expected countries you pay and receive from
- Sample invoices or website URL
- Personal bank statements for shareholders

Allow **2-6 weeks** for account opening. Full guidance is in [opening a bank account in Dubai](/guides/open-bank-account-dubai/).

### Step 7: Apply for Visas (If Included in Package)

Activate establishment card, then process employment or investor visas. Medical test and Emirates ID biometrics require UAE presence. Budget **2-4 weeks** per visa after establishment card activation.
