## Meydan Free Zone at a Glance

Meydan Free Zone (MFZ) operates within the Meydan master development in Dubai, positioning itself as an affordable alternative to JLT free zones for e-commerce sellers, digital agencies, and media entrepreneurs. Unlike Fujairah digital zones that list a non-Dubai jurisdiction on the licence, MFZ issues trade licences under a **Dubai free zone address** in the Meydan district, which some banks and clients prefer when reviewing UAE incorporation paperwork.

MFZ bundles licence, registration, and flexi-desk workspace into promotional packages that undercut DMCC on year-one cost while staying competitive with IFZA and SHAMS on lean setups. Applications run online through authorised corporate service providers, and most shareholders never maintain a dedicated Meydan office beyond the registered flexi tier.

Why founders choose Meydan Free Zone in 2026:

- **Dubai free zone jurisdiction:** licence address in Meydan, Dubai
- **Low entry cost:** promotional flexi packages from roughly AED 12,000-15,500 all-in for year one
- **E-commerce and media friendly:** trading, commercial brokerage, marketing, and content activities widely available
- **Fast turnaround:** 5-10 business days for standard applications with clean KYC
- **100% foreign ownership:** no local sponsor required inside the free zone
- **Visa options on upgraded tiers:** from zero-visa holding packages to multi-visa office bundles

MFZ is not the right zone for commodities vaulting, DFSA-regulated finance, or Jebel Ali warehouse logistics. For those intents, compare [DMCC company setup](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/) and [JAFZA company setup](/guides/jafza-company-setup/).

**Choose Meydan when:** you want a **Dubai free zone licence** at IFZA-level pricing, you run e-commerce or media, you do not need JLT address prestige, and you want to avoid Fujairah jurisdiction on corporate documents.

**Choose DMCC when:** banks and counterparties expect a recognised JLT free zone, you trade commodities, or you want three visas on a standard flexi-desk without negotiating upgrades.

**Choose IFZA when:** absolute lowest promotional price matters more than Dubai jurisdiction, and you accept Fujairah address on the licence. See the dedicated [IFZA guide](/guides/ifza-company-setup/).

## MFZ Company Types

**Free Zone Establishment (FZE):** single shareholder company. Common for solo founders and one-owner e-commerce stores.

**Free Zone Company (FZC):** two or more shareholders. Used for partnerships and small teams with external investors.

**Branch of a Foreign Company:** extension of an existing overseas entity. Parent remains liable. Useful when you already have brand and contracts abroad.

All structures allow 100% foreign ownership. Minimum share capital is typically nominal for standard trading and service activities, confirm the current MFZ schedule for your activity code before filing.

## Step-by-Step MFZ Registration Process

### Step 1: Select Activity and Package Tier

Log in through Meydan Free Zone or an authorised corporate service provider portal. Search the activity list and pick the code that matches your first 12 months of revenue, not a wish-list of future services. Misclassified activities trigger compliance rework at renewal.

Choose a package tier at the same time:

- **Zero-visa flexi:** licence plus registered address only
- **One-visa bundle:** common entry promotion for founders who need UAE residence
- **Multi-visa upgrade:** higher workspace tier for teams

Confirm visa count, renewal price, and whether the package includes establishment card fees before you pay.

### Step 2: Reserve Company Name

Names must comply with UAE conventions: no prohibited words (Bank, Insurance, Royal without approval), no offensive or religious misuse, and no confusing similarity to existing entities. MFZ typically approves names within one business day if the list is clean.

### Step 3: Submit Shareholder and Director KYC

Standard requirements for individual shareholders:

- Passport copy (valid at least six months)
- Proof of residential address (utility bill or bank statement under three months)
- CV or short professional profile (media and trading activities)
- Source of funds summary for trading and e-commerce

Corporate shareholders add certificate of incorporation, good standing, board resolution, and UBO chain documentation for anyone above 25% ownership.

### Step 4: Pay Fees and Pass Compliance Review

Meydan Free Zone conducts AML and beneficial ownership checks aligned with UAE federal requirements. Once approved in principle, pay the package fee. Rejections usually trace to incomplete UBO disclosure or activity mismatch with business description.

### Step 5: Receive Corporate Documents

After payment clearance, MFZ issues:

- Trade Licence
- Certificate of Registration / Incorporation
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Share certificates (if applicable)

These documents are mandatory for bank account opening and visa processing.

### Step 6: Open Corporate Bank Account

Proceed immediately after licence issuance. MFZ entities with e-commerce and trading activities face standard UAE KYC. Prepare:

- Business plan (one to two pages)
- Expected countries you pay and receive from
- Sample invoices or website URL
- Personal bank statements for shareholders

Allow **2-6 weeks** for account opening. Full guidance is in [opening a bank account in Dubai](/guides/open-bank-account-dubai/).

### Step 7: Apply for Visas (If Included in Package)

Activate establishment card, then process employment or investor visas. Medical test and Emirates ID biometrics require UAE presence. Budget **2-4 weeks** per visa after establishment card activation.

## E-Commerce and Media Through Meydan Free Zone

MFZ has become a practical choice for Shopify sellers, Amazon operators, content studios, and influencer agencies who sell **outside** the UAE or to other free zone entities. A citable planning summary:

Meydan Free Zone trading and media licences cover purchase and sale of goods or provision of creative services within approved categories. You may hold inventory abroad or use third-party fulfilment without a Meydan warehouse. Mainland UAE consumer sales require a local distributor, marketplace arrangement, or mainland DED licence. Corporate tax treatment of mainland B2B revenue may fall outside Qualifying Income even inside a free zone. Model tax before you scale mainland clients.

Practical e-commerce and media checklist:

- Match activity code to **general trading**, **e-commerce**, or **media production** explicitly
- Keep supplier invoices and platform payout records for bank KYC
- Separate personal and corporate flows from day one
- Use a UAE payment gateway only after corporate account is active
- Display registered licence number on your website footer

## Tax Position for MFZ Companies

UAE corporate tax (9% on taxable profits above AED 375,000 from June 2023) applies to Meydan Free Zone entities. Planning points:

**Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP):** MFZ companies may qualify for 0% on Qualifying Income if they maintain adequate substance and meet de minimis rules on non-qualifying income. Qualifying Income broadly includes free zone to free zone transactions and foreign-source income, not unrestricted mainland UAE revenue.

**Non-qualifying income:** Mainland client fees, UAE retail sales, and some passive income can trigger 9% tax. If non-qualifying income exceeds thresholds, wider tax consequences apply.

**Transfer pricing:** Related-party transactions with mainland affiliates need arm's-length documentation.

Read the full framework in [UAE corporate tax for expats](/guides/uae-corporate-tax-expats/) before you assume MFZ equals zero tax.

## Banking Tips for MFZ Applicants

Meydan Free Zone banking is generally smoother than Fujairah digital zones because the licence shows Dubai jurisdiction. Reduce delays:

- Apply with a bank that already holds MFZ corporate accounts at volume
- Avoid listing high-risk jurisdictions in your payment flow without explanation
- Bring a one-page business model diagram to the bank meeting
- If e-commerce, show marketplace seller dashboards or supplier agreements
- Do not commingle personal crypto flows with corporate account applications

If banking blocks progress, some founders temporarily use payment service providers while a second bank review runs. Treat that as a stopgap, not a long-term substitute for a UAE corporate account.

## MFZ Setup Risks and Pre-Commit Checklist

- **Activity mismatch:** Media licence with pure goods trading invoices triggers audit risk at renewal
- **Visa quota assumptions:** Cheapest package may include **zero** visas; read the schedule line by line
- **Mainland sales:** Free zone licence does not replace DED retail permission
- **Banking timeline:** Do not sign mainland client contracts requiring AED receipts until your account is live
- **Promotion expiry:** Entry pricing may jump sharply at year-two renewal; budget renewal quotes early
- **Corporate tax:** QFZP relief is conditional; mainland revenue needs modelling at 9%

## Is Meydan Free Zone Right for Your Business?

**Meydan Free Zone is the right choice when:**

- Year-one budget is under AED 25,000 including one visa
- You want a Dubai free zone address without DMCC pricing
- You run e-commerce, media, general trading, or remote professional services
- You can tolerate standard UAE banking onboarding timelines
- Your customers and suppliers are primarily international

**MFZ is likely not the right choice when:**

- You need DFSA-regulated financial services (use DIFC)
- You require Jebel Ali warehouse and port logistics (use JAFZA)
- Your bank or anchor investor mandates DMCC or DIFC specifically
- You need three visas on the cheapest package (DMCC flexi-desk may be better value)
- Most revenue will come from mainland UAE retail consumers
