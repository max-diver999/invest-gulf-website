## Frequently asked investor questions

**Should I buy ready or off-plan?**
Ready stock provides immediate rental income and proven service charges. Off-plan may offer lower entry but carries completion risk and zero income until handover. In thin-liquidity markets, ready stock is the conservative default.

**Can I self-manage from overseas?**
Technically possible but not recommended. Local property management (6–10% of rent) protects tenancy registration, maintenance response, and tenant relations. Overseas self-management correlates with higher vacancy and tenant disputes.

**How do I exit if I need liquidity?**
Price at market from day one of listing. In Gulf secondary markets outside UAE, overpricing adds 3–6 months to exit. Budget agent commission on sale (1–2%) and transfer fees on exit.

**Is now the right time in 2026?**
Gulf property is structurally supported by pegged currencies, 0% income tax, and growing expat employment. Market-specific timing matters: UAE is mature, Saudi is frontier, Qatar is stable post-World Cup, Bahrain and Oman are compact. Match timing to your hold horizon, not headlines.
