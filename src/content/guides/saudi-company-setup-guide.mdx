  - question: "Can foreigners own 100% of a Saudi LLC?"

UAE freezones offer faster setup (days vs weeks) and mature banking. Saudi suits founders targeting Vision 2030 contracts and local market access."
  - question: "What is the difference between MISA licence and Commercial Registration?"
    answer: "MISA issues the foreign investment licence authorising non-Saudi capital. The Ministry of Commerce then issues the Commercial Registration (CR) that legalises the entity to operate. ### Post-CR Registrations

Once Commercial Registration (CR) is issued:

1. Chamber of Commerce membership (Riyadh or Jeddah chamber)
2. **ZATCA** tax registration (VAT if turnover exceeds threshold; corporate income tax rules for foreign shareholders)
3. **GOSI** social insurance when hiring employees
4. **Municipality** licence for physical premises
5. Saudi National Address registration for official correspondence

Skipping any step blocks invoicing, visa issuance, or government portal access.
