## Why are foreign founders setting up companies in Saudi Arabia now?

Vision 2030 opened most commercial activities to 100% foreign ownership via MISA while government contracts and consumer scale sit inside the kingdom. Setup takes longer than UAE freezones but unlocks onshore invoicing to Saudi clients.

For international founders, the practical question is not whether Saudi allows foreign business, it does through MISA for most sectors. The question is whether Saudi market access justifies longer setup timelines, Saudisation compliance, and higher administrative friction compared with a UAE freezone.

Property investors should treat company setup and real estate as parallel tracks. Law M/14 designated zones (effective 22 January 2026) govern foreign property ownership under REGA. MISA governs operating a business. Premium Residency is a third pathway. Cross-read the [Saudi Arabia property for foreigners guide](/guides/saudi-arabia-property-foreigners-guide/) and [Saudi rental yield guide](/guides/saudi-rental-yield-guide/) before assuming one application covers both.
## How does the MISA investment licence work for foreigners?

MISA approves foreign capital for activities off the negative list. You file activity code, business plan, UBO data, and financial proof; timelines run about 2-6 weeks when documents are complete.

- Upstream oil and gas exploration
- Some security and military manufacturing
- Certain telecom and media categories (verify current list)
- Hajj and Umrah service provision

Most commercial, professional, technology, trading, and consulting activities are open to 100% foreign ownership. Search your ISIC code on invest.sa before drafting shareholder agreements.

### MISA Application Requirements

Typical documentation:

| Document | Individual investor | Corporate investor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passport / CR | Certified passport | Parent company CR and MOA |
| Business plan | Activity description, 3-year forecast | Group structure chart |
| Financial proof | Bank statements, audited accounts | Parent financials |
| UBO disclosure | Personal details above 25% | Ultimate beneficial owners |
| Activity evidence | Contracts, LOIs (if available) | Same |

MISA may request interviews or clarifications for capital-intensive or sensitive activities.

### MISA Fee and Timeline Bands

| Stage | Indicative cost (SAR) | Indicative timeline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MISA licence fee | 2,000-50,000+ (activity-based) | 2-6 weeks |
| Legal review | 15,000-40,000 | Parallel |
| Translation / notarisation | 3,000-10,000 | 1 week |

Premium activities (banking, large industry) carry higher licence fees and capital minimums.
## How do you register a Saudi LLC after MISA approval?

Ministry of Commerce issues the Commercial Registration, then you register with chamber, ZATCA, GOSI if hiring, municipality for premises, and National Address. Missing any gate blocks visas and invoicing.

### LLC Structure Basics

| Element | Typical rule |
| --- | --- |
| Shareholders | 1-50 (verify current Companies Law) |
| Minimum capital | Activity-specific; many services have no high minimum |
| Liability | Limited to share capital |
| Management | Manager(s) named in articles |
| Name | Arabic primary name; English translation registered |

Branch offices of foreign companies are an alternative when the parent already holds contracts and brand value. Branches do not separate liability from the parent.

### Post-CR Registrations

Once Commercial Registration (CR) is issued:

1. Chamber of Commerce membership (Riyadh or Jeddah chamber)
2. **ZATCA** tax registration (VAT if turnover exceeds threshold; corporate income tax rules for foreign shareholders)
3. **GOSI** social insurance when hiring employees
4. **Municipality** licence for physical premises
5. Saudi National Address registration for official correspondence

Skipping any step blocks invoicing, visa issuance, or government portal access.

## What does a Saudi LLC cost in year one?

Lean service companies often spend SAR 75,000-150,000 all-in including licence, legal, PRO, and serviced office. Trading and industrial activities need higher capital, warehouse cost, and customs setup.

### Lean Services LLC (1-3 employees)

| Cost line | SAR range |
| --- | --- |
| MISA licence | 5,000-15,000 |
| CR and notarisation | 5,000-12,000 |
| Legal and PRO | 25,000-60,000 |
| Office (serviced, annual) | 30,000-80,000 |
| Chamber and municipal | 3,000-8,000 |
| Bank account (minimum balance) | 10,000-50,000 tied |
| Visa / iqama (per expat) | 5,000-12,000 |
| **Year-one total** | **75,000-150,000+** |

### Trading or Industrial LLC

Capital requirements, warehouse lease, and customs registration push totals to SAR 200,000-500,000+ in year one.

### Ongoing Annual Costs

- CR renewal and chamber fees
- Office lease renewal
- GOSI and payroll (Saudisation-driven)
- ZATCA filing and audit if applicable
- MISA licence renewal

## How long does Saudi company setup take end to end?

Plan 2-4 months from first MISA filing to bank account and first employee visa: 2-6 weeks for MISA, 1-3 weeks for CR, 4-8 weeks for banking KYC, plus iqama processing if you hire immediately.

| Phase | Duration (typical) | Blocker if incomplete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MISA licence | 2-6 weeks | Wrong ISIC code or weak business plan |
| CR + chamber | 1-3 weeks | Name reservation or attestation gaps |
| Bank account | 4-8 weeks | Source-of-funds documentation |
| First iqama | 2-4 weeks after hire | GOSI registration |


## Is Saudi 100% foreign ownership cheaper than a UAE freezone?

UAE freezones win on speed and banking maturity. Saudi wins when customers, regulators, or Vision 2030 sector contracts require a local CR. Many groups run a UAE holdco plus Saudi operating LLC.

Founders serving Saudi government and enterprise clients often need a Saudi CR regardless of UAE presence. Founders serving global SaaS clients often start in UAE and add Saudi later.

## Which Vision 2030 sectors attract foreign LLCs?

Technology, tourism, logistics, healthcare, and professional services see active MISA licensing. Oil upstream, Hajj services, and parts of telecom remain restricted or need Saudi partners.

Priority sectors with active foreign licensing include software and cloud services, tourism hospitality, logistics, healthcare clinics, and engineering consultancies tied to giga-projects. Restricted list items still require Saudi partnership or special approval: verify on invest.sa each quarter because negative list updates land without long notice periods.

| Sector | Foreign LLC fit | Typical friction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology / SaaS | Strong if selling to Saudi enterprises | Bank KYC, data residency clauses in contracts |
| Tourism and F&B | Strong in Riyadh and Red Sea corridors | Municipality inspections, seasonal staffing |
| Logistics | Strong near ports and bonded zones | Capital and warehouse proof |
| Healthcare | Licensed activities only | Ministry of Health approvals |
| Oil and gas upstream | Restricted | Saudi partner or special licence |

## Does a Saudi company let you buy property as a foreigner?

No. Law M/14 property zones and REGA rules are separate from MISA operating licences. Premium Residency and designated zone purchases follow property guides, not your CR activity alone.

Operating a Saudi LLC does not automatically qualify you to buy residential stock outside REGA-designated zones. Founders who need both office and home should budget MISA setup, CR costs, and property due diligence as separate projects. Premium Residency tracks (commonly quoted around SAR 4 million on investment pathways) are distinct from a standard service LLC. Cross-read [Saudi Arabia property for foreigners](/guides/saudi-arabia-property-foreigners-guide/) before you wire property deposits.

## What compliance risks hit new Saudi LLCs?

Nitaqat Saudisation colour, GOSI payroll, ZATCA filing, and bank KYC delays cause most post-setup friction. Model Saudi headcount before you promise delivery dates to government clients.

- **Nitaqat:** hiring expats before Saudis can push your colour band and block renewals.
- **Banking:** expect in-person KYC and conservative onboarding for new entities.
- **ZATCA:** VAT registration thresholds and corporate income tax for foreign-owned shares need adviser mapping.
- **Contract law:** government tenders often require local CR and sometimes in-kingdom delivery history.

Saudi setup suits founders who already have Saudi clients or need to bid on Vision 2030 supply chains. UAE freezones suit remote teams, e-commerce, and property investors who want Golden Visa eligibility in personal name. Dual structure (UAE holding company, Saudi operating LLC) is common for GCC groups serving both markets.

Choose Saudi when revenue must land in riyals from Saudi entities. Choose UAE for global services, faster setup, or property-linked Golden Visa at AED 2M individual investment.
