## Saudi Company Setup in Vision 2030 Context

Saudi Arabia is restructuring its economy away from oil dependency toward tourism, entertainment, logistics, fintech, healthcare, and advanced manufacturing. Vision 2030 created giga-projects (NEOM, Red Sea Global, Qiddiya, ROSHN) and opened foreign investment pathways that did not exist a decade ago.

For international founders, the practical question is not whether Saudi allows foreign business, it does through MISA for most sectors. The question is whether Saudi market access justifies longer setup timelines, Saudisation compliance, and higher administrative friction compared with a UAE freezone.

Property investors should treat company setup and real estate as parallel tracks. Law M/14 designated zones (effective 22 January 2026) govern foreign property ownership under REGA. MISA governs operating a business. Premium Residency is a third pathway. Cross-read the [Saudi Arabia property for foreigners guide](/guides/saudi-arabia-property-foreigners-guide/) and [Saudi rental yield guide](/guides/saudi-rental-yield-guide/) before assuming one application covers both.

## MISA Licensing: First Gate for Foreign Investors

The Ministry of Investment (MISA) issues investment licences to non-Saudi nationals and entities. The licence confirms your activity is permitted and foreign capital is welcome.

### Activities and the Negative List

MISA publishes a negative list of activities restricted to Saudi or GCC nationals, or requiring minimum Saudi shareholding. As of 2026, restricted areas commonly include:

- Upstream oil and gas exploration
- Some security and military manufacturing
- Certain telecom and media categories (verify current list)
- Hajj and Umrah service provision

Most commercial, professional, technology, trading, and consulting activities are open to 100% foreign ownership. Search your ISIC code on invest.sa before drafting shareholder agreements.

### MISA Application Requirements

Typical documentation:

| Document | Individual investor | Corporate investor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passport / CR | Certified passport | Parent company CR and MOA |
| Business plan | Activity description, 3-year forecast | Group structure chart |
| Financial proof | Bank statements, audited accounts | Parent financials |
| UBO disclosure | Personal details above 25% | Ultimate beneficial owners |
| Activity evidence | Contracts, LOIs (if available) | Same |

MISA may request interviews or clarifications for capital-intensive or sensitive activities.

### MISA Fee and Timeline Bands

| Stage | Indicative cost (SAR) | Indicative timeline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MISA licence fee | 2,000-50,000+ (activity-based) | 2-6 weeks |
| Legal review | 15,000-40,000 | Parallel |
| Translation / notarisation | 3,000-10,000 | 1 week |

Premium activities (banking, large industry) carry higher licence fees and capital minimums.

## LLC Formation: Ministry of Commerce Registration

After MISA approval, register a Limited Liability Company (LLC) with the Ministry of Commerce. The LLC is the standard vehicle for foreign-backed SMEs in Riyadh and Jeddah.

### LLC Structure Basics

| Element | Typical rule |
| --- | --- |
| Shareholders | 1-50 (verify current Companies Law) |
| Minimum capital | Activity-specific; many services have no high minimum |
| Liability | Limited to share capital |
| Management | Manager(s) named in articles |
| Name | Arabic primary name; English translation registered |

Branch offices of foreign companies are an alternative when the parent already holds contracts and brand value. Branches do not separate liability from the parent.

### Post-CR Registrations

Once Commercial Registration (CR) is issued:

1. **Chamber of Commerce** membership (Riyadh or Jeddah chamber)
2. **ZATCA** tax registration (VAT if turnover exceeds threshold; corporate income tax rules for foreign shareholders)
3. **GOSI** social insurance when hiring employees
4. **Municipality** licence for physical premises
5. **Saudi National Address** registration for official correspondence

Skipping any step blocks invoicing, visa issuance, or government portal access.

## Riyadh vs Jeddah: Where to Register

| Factor | Riyadh | Jeddah |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market | Government, finance, corporate HQs | Red Sea trade, logistics, tourism |
| Vision 2030 angle | PIF ecosystem, ROSHN, Diriyah | Red Sea Project, Jeddah Central |
| Office cost | Moderate to high (OLAYA, KAFD) | Moderate (Corniche, north Jeddah) |
| Talent pool | Deep finance and tech | Logistics and hospitality |
| Property link | Designated zone apartments | Coastal designated zones |

Most foreign services firms choose Riyadh for client proximity to ministries and SOEs. Trading and hospitality-linked businesses often anchor in Jeddah.

## Saudi Company Setup Costs: 2026 Bands

Indicative year-one totals for planning. Confirm with a Saudi PRO or law firm.

### Lean Services LLC (1-3 employees)

| Cost line | SAR range |
| --- | --- |
| MISA licence | 5,000-15,000 |
| CR and notarisation | 5,000-12,000 |
| Legal and PRO | 25,000-60,000 |
| Office (serviced, annual) | 30,000-80,000 |
| Chamber and municipal | 3,000-8,000 |
| Bank account (minimum balance) | 10,000-50,000 tied |
| Visa / iqama (per expat) | 5,000-12,000 |
| **Year-one total** | **75,000-150,000+** |

### Trading or Industrial LLC

Capital requirements, warehouse lease, and customs registration push totals to SAR 200,000-500,000+ in year one.

### Ongoing Annual Costs

- CR renewal and chamber fees
- Office lease renewal
- GOSI and payroll (Saudisation-driven)
- ZATCA filing and audit if applicable
- MISA licence renewal

## Timeline: MISA to First Invoice

| Week | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| 1-2 | Activity confirmation, business plan, legal engagement |
| 2-6 | MISA licence approval |
| 6-8 | LLC articles, CR issuance |
| 8-10 | Chamber, ZATCA, national address |
| 8-12 | Bank account opening (variable) |
| 10-14 | Office fit-out, municipality licence |
| 12-16 | First employee iqama and payroll live |

Fastest realistic path to operational billing: 8 weeks. Conservative plan with banking delays: 16 weeks.

## Foreign Ownership Rules vs UAE Freezone

| Feature | Saudi LLC (MISA) | UAE freezone (e.g. IFZA, DMCC) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foreign ownership | 100% on most activities | 100% |
| Setup speed | 2-4 months | 1-4 weeks |
| Year-one cost (lean) | SAR 75,000-150,000 | AED 12,000-55,000 |
| Local market sales | Direct to Saudi clients | UAE mainland restrictions apply |
| Saudisation / Emiratisation | Nitaqat quotas | Freezone exempt from Emirati quota in zone |
| Corporate tax | 20% on foreign shareholder share (verify ZATCA) | 9% UAE CT; QFZP 0% possible |
| Property link | Separate Law M/14 zones | Golden Visa AED 2M common pathway |
| Banking | Improving; due diligence heavy | Mature international banking |

Founders serving Saudi government and enterprise clients often need a Saudi CR regardless of UAE presence. Founders serving global SaaS clients often start in UAE and add Saudi later.

## Vision 2030 Sectors: Where MISA Pushes Investment

High-activity sectors attracting foreign company registrations:

- **Tourism and entertainment:** Red Sea, Qiddiya, Diriyah hospitality supply chain
- **Healthcare:** Private hospitals, medtech distribution, telehealth
- **Technology:** Cloud, cybersecurity, fintech (subject to SAMA/CMA where regulated)
- **Logistics:** Bonded zones, last-mile, e-commerce fulfilment
- **Professional services:** Engineering, consulting supporting giga-projects

NEOM and giga-project suppliers should verify contract eligibility: some tenders require prior Saudi CR and local substance.

## Property Investment Linkage (Separate from Company Setup)

Foreign property ownership under Law M/14 does not substitute for MISA licensing. Key distinctions:

| Track | Authority | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MISA + LLC | Ministry of Investment / Commerce | Operate business, hire, invoice |
| Law M/14 property | REGA designated zones | Own residential/commercial unit |
| Premium Residency | Premium Residency Center | Long stay; SAR 4M investment track commonly cited |

Rental yields in designated zones run 4% to 6% gross per the [Saudi rental yield guide](/guides/saudi-rental-yield-guide/). Company profits and property rent are taxed and regulated separately.

## Saudi Setup Risks and Pre-Commit Checklist

- **Activity misclassification:** Wrong ISIC code blocks CR renewal or MISA upgrade fees.
- **Saudisation surprise:** Hiring plan must match Nitaqat colour before scaling expat headcount.
- **Banking delays:** Saudi banks apply strict AML. Source-of-funds documentation must be complete.
- **Regulatory overlap:** Fintech, healthcare, and education need sector regulators beyond MISA.
- **Property confusion:** Depositing on a Jeddah apartment does not grant CR or work rights.

Saudi company setup in 2026 is viable for foreign founders targeting Vision 2030 market access, government-linked contracts, and on-ground presence in Riyadh or Jeddah. The path runs MISA licence first, then LLC registration, then tax and labour registrations. Budget SAR 75,000 to 150,000 all-in for a lean year one and plan 2 to 4 months to operational status.
