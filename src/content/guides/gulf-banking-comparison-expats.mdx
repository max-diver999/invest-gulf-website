## How long does account opening actually take?

Account opening timelines are the most underestimated item in a Gulf relocation. A UAE resident holding a stamped visa and Emirates ID typically clears a current account in 3 to 10 working days, while a non-resident applying for a property-purchase account should plan 2 to 4 weeks and expect source-of-funds documentation covering 6 to 12 months. Qatar and Saudi Arabia generally require the residence permit first, which pushes the effective start date out by several weeks, and Bahrain and Oman sit between the two. The sequence that avoids trouble is residence status, then bank account, then any property deposit, because a trustee appointment booked before an active IBAN exists is a date you will move at least once. Where a spouse also needs an account, open both files together, because a second application started later restarts most of the same checks.
