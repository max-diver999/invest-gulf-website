## What tax checklist should run before you relocate?
### Monthly budget benchmarks

The living seef bahrain landscape in Bahrain comes with these concrete numbers. A single professional in Bahrain can expect to spend 4,500 to 7,000 AED on a studio or one-bedroom apartment, 1,500 to 3,000 AED on groceries and dining, 400 to 800 AED on utilities (water, electricity, cooling), 500 to 1,200 AED on transport (car loan, fuel, parking, or metro pass), and 300 to 800 AED on telecom and subscriptions. A family of four typically needs 15,000 to 28,000 AED per month before school fees - add 3,000 to 10,000 AED per child depending on curriculum.

### Monthly spending by lifestyle tier

Budget tier in Bahrain (single professional): studio rental 3,000 to 5,500 AED, groceries 1,200 to 1,800 AED, transport 400 to 800 AED, total 6,000 to 9,000 AED/month. Mid-range (couple): 2-bed apartment 7,000 to 13,000 AED, groceries 2,500 to 3,500 AED, car payment 1,500 to 2,500 AED, total 14,000 to 22,000 AED/month. Premium (family of four, villa): villa rent 15,000 to 35,000 AED, amortised school fees 3,000 to 10,000 AED/month, total 25,000 to 50,000 AED/month.
