## British versus American versus Indian: practical picks

Choose British if your child already sits IGCSE tracks and you may return to UK or European universities. American AP fits US university paths but seat count is smaller in Bahrain. Indian CBSE delivers lowest fee stack and strong STEM outcomes for families comfortable with larger class sizes.

IB diploma years need confirmed HL seat availability. Waitlists hit hardest in small markets when DP cohorts are capped at forty students.

## Special educational needs

Verify learning support explicitly. Bahrain has SEN units in select British schools but pool is thinner than Dubai. Ask for written support plan and therapist availability before paying registration deposit.

## Extracurricular depth

Football academies, swimming, and rugby clubs cluster around Isa Town and Saar corridors. Premium schools run international trips that add BHD 200-600/year per child. Budget activities outside tuition if your child joins competitive squads.

## Final seat and lease sequencing

Secure provisional school seat email before you sign twelve-month lease in Amwaj or Riffa. Registrars can revoke holds if visa delay exceeds their window. Keep PDF fee schedule with annual escalation clause; some schools cap increases, others do not.

Finance employers often assume August start; mid-year joiners should ask about trimester entry and bus seat availability. Indian tier schools accept more mid-year than British senior years.

For aviation and airport-shift families, test school run against night-shift sleep schedules; bus times are rarely flexible. Hybrid work parents sometimes relocate pin closer to Isa Town even if waterfront appeal of Amwaj is strong.

Employers still mix USD-quoted packages with BHD or SAR payroll. Ask finance to show twelve months of net payslips in local currency, not only gross expat narrative. School registrars and landlords both prefer local currency cheques; FX gains on paper do not pay EWA bills. Keep six weeks rent liquid after deposits clear. Trailing spouses should visit preferred grocery, school gate, and clinic locations once before shipment; photos online miss heat, humidity, and parking friction that change monthly spend.

School bus GPS apps vary by operator; ask whether live tracking exists before you rely on bus for dual-working-parent schedules.

Sibling priority policies differ: some campuses guarantee second seat, others treat each child as new application. Ask registrar in writing.

Visit registrar office in person once; email tone differs from onsite seat availability. Bring printed previous report cards even if PDF already uploaded.

## Open day questions to ask

Ask registrar about average class size, teacher turnover, and last three years IGCSE or CBSE outcome bands. Ask whether capital levy is refundable on withdrawal. Ask bus waitlist length for your pin code.

Photograph campus security and pick-up queue at 14:30 on a normal Sunday, not on staged open day.

Keep scanned fee PDFs in the same folder as lease and LMRA documents for renewal season.
