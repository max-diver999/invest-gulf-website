| Fire Brigade | 997 | 24/7 |
| Coast Guard | 996 | 24/7 |

Save these as labelled contacts on your phone right now. The numbers are the same in every UAE emirate, 999, 998, and 997 work whether you are in Dubai Marina, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, or Ras Al Khaimah.

### When to Call Which Number

999, Dubai Police Emergency:
- Crime in progress or just occurred
- Serious accidents involving injuries
- Threats to personal safety
- Suspicious activity
- Any situation requiring immediate police presence

998, Ambulance:
- Any medical emergency: chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, unconsciousness, stroke symptoms, serious injury
- Cardiac arrest, describe situation immediately; dispatcher will guide CPR
- Road accident with injured persons
- Suspected overdose or poisoning

997, Fire Brigade / Civil Defence:
- Fire of any kind
- Gas leak or explosion
- Hazardous materials incident
- Building structural emergency
- Entrapment requiring rescue (vehicle, elevator, collapsed building)

## Dubai Police: Emergency vs Non-Emergency

### Emergency: 999

Call 999 for situations requiring immediate police response. The operator will ask for your location, provide the most specific address or landmark you can. Dubai's postal addressing uses building and apartment numbers, or well-known landmarks if you are outdoors.

For non-Dubai residents or visitors: 999 covers the entire UAE. If you are in Sharjah and call 999, you will reach Sharjah Police. If in Abu Dhabi, Abu Dhabi Police. The same 999 number routes to the correct emirate automatically.

### Non-Emergency: Dubai Police 901

For non-urgent matters:
- Report a minor theft (wallet, phone) after the fact
- Traffic violations or minor accidents without injury
- Neighbour disputes
- Noise complaints
- Lost property enquiries

Online Report: [dubaipolice.gov.ae](https://www.dubaipolice.gov.ae/), many non-urgent incidents can be reported online with case number issued immediately. Useful for insurance claims where a police report reference is required.

Dubai Police App: Available on iOS and Android. Features: emergency call, non-emergency report, lost and found, vehicle information, fines, and permit applications.

### Tourist Police: 800-4673 (800-HOPE)

A dedicated multilingual tourist assistance line for visitors who need help but do not require emergency response. English and multiple other languages available.

## Medical Emergency Services

These emergency numbers dubai figures for Dubai were last cross-checked against official sources in Q1 2026. Government processing fees typically range from 500-5,000 AED; document attestation adds 200-800 AED per document.

### 998: Ambulance (DCAS)

Dubai Corporation for Ambulance Services (DCAS) operates the ambulance network with approximately 180 ambulances across 40 stations. Key facts:
- 120 Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulances with trained paramedics
- 60 Basic Life Support (BLS) ambulances for lower-acuity transfers
- Response time target: under 6 minutes in 90% of urban areas (current average 5.2 minutes)
- Annual call volume: approximately 350,000 emergency calls
- Dispatcher will provide pre-arrival instructions (CPR guidance, first aid coaching)
- Emergency ambulance costs AED 150-200 for UAE residents with insurance; AED 500-800 without insurance

When you call 998:
1. State the nature of the emergency ("unconscious person", "chest pains", "road accident with injuries")
2. Give your precise location (building name, floor, apartment, street, area)
3. Stay on the line: dispatcher will guide you
4. Send someone to meet the ambulance at building entrance if in a complex

## Fire and Building Emergencies

### 997: Civil Defence (Fire Brigade)

Dubai Civil Defence operates the fire and rescue service. Call 997 for:
- Any fire, do not assess size before calling, call immediately
- Gas leak, leave building, then call
- Elevator entrapment, call 997 and building management simultaneously
- Flooding from external source threatening structural safety
- Any collapse or structural emergency

**Before evacuating a fire:**
1. Alert others in immediate area
2. Do not use elevators: use fire stairs
3. Close doors behind you to slow fire spread
4. Meet at designated assembly point (posted in lobby of most buildings)
5. Do not re-enter the building for belongings

**Building Fire Wardens:** Most large Dubai residential buildings have trained fire wardens on each floor or zone. The names and contact numbers should be posted in fire safety notices in the stairwells. Introduce yourself to building security who can direct you in an emergency.

| Water leak or burst main | **991** |
| General enquiries / non-urgent | 04 601 9999 |

**991** is the DEWA 24/7 emergency line for dangerous electrical faults and major water mains issues. For routine faults (streetlight out, minor water pressure issue), use the DEWA app or website.

### EMPOWER (District Cooling: Dubai Marina, JLT, Business Bay)

Many apartments in Dubai Marina, JLT, and Business Bay use EMPOWER for district cooling. Cooling faults are not fire/police emergencies but feel significant in summer heat.

- EMPOWER 24/7: **800 EMPOWER (800 367 6937)**

### Gas Emergencies

- Natural gas distribution by DUGAS (parts of Dubai): DEWA 991 for gas-related emergencies
- LPG cylinder emergencies: Call 997 (Fire Brigade), exit building immediately first

## Road Accidents

### Procedure for a Dubai Road Accident

**Minor accident (no injuries, vehicles still drivable):**
1. Move vehicles to the hard shoulder or side road if safe to do so
2. Exchange details with other driver: driving licence, registration card, insurance
3. Call Dubai Police 901 or use the Accident reporting app/website to report and obtain a case number
4. Both drivers use the **"Accident Report" (Tamm portal or Dubai Police app)**: required by insurance companies (fine for not reporting: AED 1,000)
5. Dubai Police handle approximately 140,000 traffic incidents annually with response times averaging 4-6 minutes

**Serious accident (injuries, major damage, road blockage):**
1. Call 999 (police) and 998 (ambulance) simultaneously if injuries
2. Do not move injured persons unless there is immediate danger (fire, approaching traffic)
3. Activate hazard lights and use warning triangles if you have them
4. Wait for police: do not leave the scene of an injury accident

**Bystander first aid:** If you witness an accident, call 998 first. UAE law provides Good Samaritan protections for bystanders providing first aid in good faith.

**Recovery and towing:** Dubai has authorised towing companies. Your insurance may provide roadside assistance, call your insurer's 24-hour hotline. Unauthorised recovery trucks exist, ensure any tow goes to an authorised workshop.

## Specific Situation Guides

### If You Witness a Drowning or Water Emergency

Call 998 (ambulance) and 996 (coast guard) simultaneously. Most Dubai beaches have lifeguards during staffed hours (typically 07:00-sunset). JBR, Jumeirah Beach Park, and Kite Beach have trained lifeguards on duty, report to the nearest lifeguard post directly for fastest response.

### If Your Child Goes Missing in a Mall or Public Place

All major Dubai malls (Dubai Mall, Mall of the Emirates, etc.) have **Security Operations Centres** that activate child-missing protocols immediately. Approach any security guard, state "missing child," and they will initiate a mall-wide announcement and lock-entry protocol. Simultaneously call 999 to file a report.

### If You Experience a Cardiac Event

**CHAIN OF SURVIVAL (international standard):**
1. Call 998 immediately
2. Start CPR if trained (30 compressions to 2 breaths or hands-only CPR)
3. Use AED (Automated External Defibrillator) if available: AEDs are located in malls, airports, gyms, and many buildings; they provide verbal instructions
4. DCAS paramedics will take over on arrival

Dubai Mall, Dubai International Airport, major hotels, and many gyms have registered AED locations. The UAE Heart Association maintains an AED registry.

### If You Are in a Sandstorm (Shamal)

Not typically a calling-999 situation, but:
- Pull off the road completely (not on the hard shoulder) if visibility drops to zero
- Turn off all lights (other drivers may follow your tail lights and hit you)
- Leave hazard lights on
- Wait for the storm to pass
- If stuck in desert: call 999 for police assistance
