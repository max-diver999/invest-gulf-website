## What are the core emergency numbers in Dubai?

Dubai core emergency numbers are 999 for police, 998 for ambulance, and 997 for fire and civil defence, all operating 24/7 with English-speaking dispatch that covers about 90% of urban medical calls within target times. Coast Guard is 996. Invest Gulf relocation checklists require these three saved before the first night in the city.

Dubai core emergency numbers are 999 for police, 998 for ambulance through Dubai Corporation for Ambulance Services, and 997 for fire and civil defence, all operating 24/7 with English-speaking dispatch across the emirate. DCAS runs roughly 180 ambulances across about 40 stations, including around 120 advanced life support units, with urban response targets under 6 minutes in 90% of cases and recent averages near 5.2 minutes. Emergency ambulance charges commonly run AED 150-200 for insured UAE residents and AED 500-800 without cover. Non-urgent police matters go to 901 or the Dubai Police app, while tourist assistance uses 800-4673. DEWA electricity and water emergencies use 991, and Empower district cooling faults use 800 367 6937 in Marina, JLT, and Business Bay towers for residents.

### When to Call Which Number

999, Dubai Police Emergency:
- Crime in progress or just occurred
- Serious accidents involving injuries
- Threats to personal safety
- Suspicious activity
- Any situation requiring immediate police presence

998, Ambulance:
- Any medical emergency: chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, unconsciousness, stroke symptoms, serious injury
- Cardiac arrest, describe situation immediately; dispatcher will guide CPR
- Road accident with injured persons
- Suspected overdose or poisoning

997, Fire Brigade / Civil Defence:
- Fire of any kind
- Gas leak or explosion
- Hazardous materials incident
- Building structural emergency
- Entrapment requiring rescue (vehicle, elevator, collapsed building)

## When should you call 999 versus Dubai Police 901?

Call 999 when immediate police presence is required for crime in progress, injury accidents, or threats to safety; use 901 for minor theft, noise, and non-injury reports that can wait. Online filing typically issues a case number the same day for insurance claims worth AED 1,000 and up. Invest Gulf client checklist packs save Tourist Police 800-4673.

### Emergency: 999

Give the operator the most specific building, apartment, or landmark you can. The same 999 number routes to the correct emirate automatically if you call from Sharjah or Abu Dhabi.

### Non-Emergency: Dubai Police 901

- Report a minor theft (wallet, phone) after the fact
- Traffic violations or minor accidents without injury
- Neighbour disputes and noise complaints
- Lost property enquiries

### Tourist Police: 800-4673 (800-HOPE)

A dedicated multilingual tourist assistance line for visitors who need help but do not require emergency response.

## How do medical emergencies and ambulance costs work in Dubai?

Medical emergencies in Dubai require 998 for DCAS ambulance dispatch, not a hospital switchboard, and insured residents typically pay AED 150-200 versus AED 500-800 without cover. Urban response targets sit under 6 minutes in 90% of areas. Invest Gulf relocation packs list Rashid Hospital and two private ER numbers beside 998.

### 998: Ambulance (DCAS)

Dubai Corporation for Ambulance Services (DCAS) operates the ambulance network with approximately 180 ambulances across 40 stations. Key facts:
- 120 Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulances with trained paramedics
- 60 Basic Life Support (BLS) ambulances for lower-acuity transfers
- Response time target: under 6 minutes in 90% of urban areas (current average 5.2 minutes)
- Annual call volume: approximately 350,000 emergency calls
- Dispatcher will provide pre-arrival instructions (CPR guidance, first aid coaching)
- Emergency ambulance costs AED 150-200 for UAE residents with insurance; AED 500-800 without insurance

When you call 998:
1. State the nature of the emergency ("unconscious person", "chest pains", "road accident with injuries")
2. Give your precise location (building name, floor, apartment, street, area)
3. Stay on the line: dispatcher will guide you
4. Send someone to meet the ambulance at building entrance if in a complex

## What should you do for fire and building emergencies?

Fire, gas leaks, elevator entrapment, and structural emergencies require 997 for Dubai Civil Defence, and you should evacuate by stairs rather than elevators as soon as the alarm sounds. Large towers typically post fire-warden contacts on each floor. Invest Gulf building walkthroughs confirm assembly points on day one of a 12 months lease.

### 997: Civil Defence (Fire Brigade)

Dubai Civil Defence operates the fire and rescue service. Call 997 for:
- Any fire, do not assess size before calling, call immediately
- Gas leak, leave building, then call
- Elevator entrapment, call 997 and building management simultaneously
- Flooding from external source threatening structural safety
- Any collapse or structural emergency

Drill the stair route once in your first 30 days so night evacuations take under 5 minutes from a mid-floor apartment.

**Before evacuating a fire:**
1. Alert others in immediate area
2. Do not use elevators: use fire stairs
3. Close doors behind you to slow fire spread
4. Meet at designated assembly point (posted in lobby of most buildings)
5. Do not re-enter the building for belongings

**Building Fire Wardens:** Most large Dubai residential buildings have trained fire wardens on each floor or zone. The names and contact numbers should be posted in fire safety notices in the stairwells. Introduce yourself to building security who can direct you in an emergency.

## What is the procedure after a Dubai road accident?

Minor no-injury accidents typically require a police report via 901 or the Dubai Police app because failing to report can draw an AED 1,000 fine, while injury scenes need 999 and 998 immediately. Response times near 4-6 minutes are common on urban corridors. Invest Gulf driving briefings stress hazard lights and authorised towing only.

### Procedure for a Dubai Road Accident

**Minor accident (no injuries, vehicles still drivable):**
1. Move vehicles to the hard shoulder or side road if safe to do so
2. Exchange details with other driver: driving licence, registration card, insurance
3. Call Dubai Police 901 or use the Accident reporting app/website to report and obtain a case number
4. Both drivers use the **"Accident Report" (Tamm portal or Dubai Police app)**: required by insurance companies (fine for not reporting: AED 1,000)

**Serious accident (injuries, major damage, road blockage):**
1. Call 999 (police) and 998 (ambulance) simultaneously if injuries
2. Do not move injured persons unless there is immediate danger (fire, approaching traffic)
3. Activate hazard lights and use warning triangles if you have them
4. Wait for police: do not leave the scene of an injury accident

Road accidents without injury should be moved to a safe shoulder when possible, details exchanged, and reported through Dubai Police 901 or the official accident reporting tools because failing to report can draw an AED 1,000 fine. Dubai Police handle roughly 140,000 traffic incidents annually with typical response times of 4-6 minutes. Injury accidents require 999 and 998 immediately, and injured persons should not be moved unless fire or traffic creates immediate danger. Domestic violence support includes the Dubai Foundation for Women and Children on 800-988, child protection on 116111, and 999 when danger is active. Utility, consumer, and labour lines such as DEWA 991, Municipality 800 900, RTA 800 9090, and MOHRE 800 60 cover non-life-threatening but time-sensitive resident needs after the core three numbers are saved.

## What should you do in specific Dubai emergency situations?

Specific Dubai situations have clear first calls: drowning needs 998 and 996 together, a missing child in a mall needs security plus 999, and cardiac events require 998 then CPR and AED. Beach posts at JBR and Kite Beach typically staff about 365 days a year. Invest Gulf situation checklist cards keep these steps short.

### If Your Child Goes Missing in a Mall or Public Place

All major Dubai malls (Dubai Mall, Mall of the Emirates, etc.) have **Security Operations Centres** that activate child-missing protocols immediately. Approach any security guard, state "missing child," and they will initiate a mall-wide announcement and lock-entry protocol. Simultaneously call 999 to file a report within the first 5 minutes.

### If You Experience a Cardiac Event

**CHAIN OF SURVIVAL (international standard):**
1. Call 998 immediately
2. Start CPR if trained (30 compressions to 2 breaths or hands-only CPR)
3. Use AED (Automated External Defibrillator) if available: AEDs are located in malls, airports, gyms, and many buildings; they provide verbal instructions
4. DCAS paramedics will take over on arrival

Dubai Mall, Dubai International Airport, major hotels, and many gyms have registered AED locations. Ambulance charges still follow the AED 150-800 band noted above once DCAS transports.

### If You Are in a Sandstorm (Shamal)

Not typically a calling-999 situation, but:
- Pull off the road completely (not on the hard shoulder) if visibility drops to zero
- Turn off all lights (other drivers may follow your tail lights and hit you)
- Leave hazard lights on
- Wait for the storm to pass
- If stuck in desert: call 999 for police assistance
