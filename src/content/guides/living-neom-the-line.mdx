## Who actually lives near NEOM projects: profiles

### Profiles that sometimes work (with employer clarity)

- Single senior engineers on rotation with compound/camp housing defined
- Child-free couples both employed on same sponsor site package
- Consultants on 4-week-on / 4-week-off with family in Jeddah
- Specialists with explicit medevac and clinic SLAs in contract

### Profiles that usually fail without planning

- Two school-age children expecting British IB walk-to-school
- Trailing spouse needing local employment immediately
- Chronic healthcare requiring monthly tertiary visits
- Property investors from abroad without site visit and REGA counsel
- Influencer relocators documenting "day one city life" without sponsor

## Employer contract checklist: before you say yes

- Exact work location pin on map
- Housing type photos (not renders)
- Rotation schedule in writing
- School plan for each child
- Spouse visa and work rights
- Medevac and hospital list
- Leave/flight allowance
- End-of-assignment repatriation
- Summer heat work protections
- Sponsor entity named on iqama

## Spouse employment and boredom risk

- Remote job for foreign employer, legal review
- Riyadh weekly commute, expensive but sanity-saving

Discuss honestly in pre-move counselling.

- Screen time escalation
