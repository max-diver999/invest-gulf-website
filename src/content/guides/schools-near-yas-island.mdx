## Why does Yas Island work for school-age families?

Yas Island typically works for school-age families because West Yas Academy sits on-island under 5 minutes away, Raha International is a 10-15 minute drive, and family tenancies often run 4-6 years once a place is secured. We treat school catchment as the primary lease filter for foreign buyers before entertainment amenities.

For investors: void periods on school-anchored 2-3BR Yas apartments are typically 4-8 weeks versus 10-16 weeks in entertainment-only or studio-heavy buildings.
## How does West Yas Academy compare for Yas families in 2026?

West Yas Academy is the only on-island school for Yas residents: British EYFS through secondary, ADEK Good rated, with fees roughly AED 38,000-62,000 per year and school runs under 5 minutes from most apartments and villas. Invest Gulf defaults foreign buyers with younger children to West Yas when commute time beats an Outstanding mainland rating.

| Detail | West Yas Academy |
| --- | --- |
| Curriculum | British EYFS, KS1-KS4 |
| ADEK rating | Good |
| Approximate fee band | AED 38,000-62,000/year |
| Campus | On Yas Island, under 5 min from all residential zones |
| Language of instruction | English |
| University pathway | UK/international via A-Level preparation |

The ADEK "Good" rating sits one tier below "Outstanding", a meaningful distinction for families applying to selective UK universities. West Yas Academy is consistently cited by Yas residents for its convenience: school runs that take under 5 minutes by car (or even bicycle for villa residents) eliminate morning stress and childcare cost. For families with younger children or those prioritising commute time, it is the default choice.

Families with older children targeting Russell Group UK universities or US Ivy League pathways sometimes look beyond West Yas to the Outstanding-rated or IB-offering schools on the mainland. The standard move is: **West Yas for primary, Raha International or Nord Anglia for secondary**, a two-phase school plan that many Yas families adopt.

## How does Raha International compare for Yas Island families?

Raha International School typically sits 10-15 minutes from Yas via Sheikh Khalifa Road, carries an ADEK Outstanding rating, and charges about AED 52,000-78,000 per year with bus options near AED 5,500-8,000 per child. Invest Gulf tells foreign buyers to apply 9-12 months ahead for FS1 and Year 1 because waiting lists can add a full academic year.

| Detail | Raha International School |
| --- | --- |
| Curriculum | British and IB |
| ADEK rating | Outstanding |
| Approximate fee band | AED 52,000-78,000/year |
| Drive from Yas Island | 10-15 min via Sheikh Khalifa Road |
| Bus service from Yas | Available, AED 5,500-8,000/year per child |

Raha International is the school most often cited in Yas Island property marketing, because it is close enough to be genuinely convenient and carries enough academic reputation to justify Yas Island rents. The 10-15 minute school run off-peak extends to 18-25 minutes during Sunday-Thursday morning rush (07:00-08:15), workable but worth testing before signing a villa lease.

Waiting list pressure at Raha is real: apply 9-12 months ahead for FS1 and Year 1 places. Families who move to Yas without a confirmed Raha place often discover the waiting list adds a full academic year before their child can transfer.

## What does Nord Anglia cost from Yas Island?

Nord Anglia International School Abu Dhabi fees typically sit in the AED 65,000-85,000 per year band depending on year group, with a 15-20 minute drive from Yas and bus pricing similar to Raha International. We map Nord Anglia for foreign buyers who want Juilliard or MIT enrichment and can absorb premium fees.

Nord Anglia's partnership programmes (Juilliard for music, MIT for STEM) appeal to families with specific enrichment priorities. The school draws significant corporate-housing tenant demand from ADNOC-affiliated families, a tenant profile that often overlaps with Yas Island's newer Aldar towers.

For Yas residents commuting to Abu Dhabi City for work (common given Abu Dhabi's employment base), school drop-off at Nord Anglia on the way reduces the school run from a separate trip to a zero-cost detour.
## How far is GEMS American Academy from Yas?

GEMS American Academy Abu Dhabi typically sits a 20-25 minute drive from Yas residential zones, with American curriculum and AP pathways and fee bands that overlap Nord Anglia more than West Yas Academy's AED 38,000-62,000 range. Invest Gulf recommends the longer commute only when foreign buyers specifically need a US university track.

## How do ADEK ratings differ from KHDA in Dubai?

ADEK ratings in Abu Dhabi use bands such as Outstanding and Good on a separate inspection cycle from Dubai's KHDA framework, so a Yas family cannot treat the labels as interchangeable when comparing West Yas Academy Good with Dubai Outstanding schools. We require foreign buyers to download the current adek.gov.ae PDF each cycle because section scores can move within 12 months.

## When should you apply to Abu Dhabi schools near Yas?

Apply to Raha International and Nord Anglia 9-12 months ahead for FS1 entry, and never sign a Yas villa lease before at least one confirmed school place, because a waitlisted FS1 can force a mainland nursery year while rent still runs. We treat the acceptance letter as a lease checklist item.

We prefer family stock over studio churn for foreign buyers underwriting Yas Island.

Two-bedroom and larger units attract school-anchored families who accept slightly lower gross rent in exchange for certainty and location. Net yield gap between studio and 2BR narrows significantly when void periods are factored in.

## What after-school options exist on Yas Island?

After-school life on Yas Island typically includes Yas Waterworld, Ferrari World, SeaWorld, and Yas Mall within a short drive, which can cut weekend outing spend by about 20-30% versus Al Reem families who must leave the island for comparable entertainment. Our research treats that lifestyle stack as a tenancy retention signal for foreign buyers holding 2-3BR stock.
## What school checklist should you complete before leasing?

Complete a school checklist before any Yas lease: confirm West Yas FS1 availability, formal Raha waitlist status, current ADEK PDF, a Sunday 07:30 drive test, bus pickup at your building, and a backup place, because break-lease fees often run AED 15,000-25,000. Invest Gulf blocks villa reservations for foreign buyers until at least one seat is confirmed.

| Pre-lease school checklist | Action |
| --- | --- |
| West Yas Academy FS1 availability | Call Aldar Academies, confirm open places, not waitlist |
| Raha International waitlist status | Apply formally before property decision |
| ADEK report download | Read full inspection PDF for current cycle |
| Morning school run test | Drive Yas → school on a Sunday at 07:30, not Saturday 19:00 |
| Bus route confirmation | Verify Raha bus picks up at your specific Yas building |
| Second-school backup | Accept one place at backup before signing 12-month lease |

Break lease fees on Yas villas typically run **AED 15,000-25,000** plus agent re-let costs, more expensive than a failed school plan is worth avoiding.

**Insider tip:** Drive Yas to Raha International at 07:30 on a Sunday before you sign; the 10-15 minute off-peak run often stretches to 18-25 minutes in term-time traffic, and that gap decides whether a villa lease still feels workable.
