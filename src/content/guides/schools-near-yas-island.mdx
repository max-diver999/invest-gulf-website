## FAQ

**Best school on Yas Island?**
West Yas Academy for convenience, Raha International (10–15 min) for ADEK Outstanding rating and stronger UK/US university track.

**Are Abu Dhabi school fees lower than Dubai?**
Yes, typically **15–20% lower** for equivalent curriculum and ADEK/KHDA tier. Abu Dhabi also has fee increase controls that Dubai does not.

**Can I walk or cycle to West Yas Academy from Yas residences?**
From Yas Acres villas, yes, cycling is practical in cooler months. From Ansam or Mayan apartments, **5–8 minute drive**; walking is possible but uncommon due to heat.

**Is there a school bus from Yas Island to Raha International?**
Yes. Raha International operates buses with Yas Island pickup. Budget **AED 5,500–8,000/year per child**.

**How does ADEK Good compare to ADEK Outstanding?**
Good is the third-highest tier. For most primary-age families it is entirely adequate, Outstanding becomes relevant for selective sixth-form and university preparation decisions.

**Do Yas Island schools have Arabic language requirements?**
Yes, Arabic as a subject is compulsory for all students in Abu Dhabi regardless of curriculum. Most international schools provide Arabic as an Additional Language (AAL) for non-native speakers. Check individual school provision for native Arabic speaker vs AAL stream.

**Does school proximity affect Yas Island property prices?**
Directly. Aldar markets Yas Acres villas explicitly on West Yas Academy proximity. 3BR Yas Acres villas command a **10–15% rent premium** over equivalent 3BR stock elsewhere on Yas precisely because of the school-run advantage. Investors buying Yas Acres target this family-demand premium.
