West Bay is Doha's **central business district** — a corridor of diplomatic missions, government ministries, QNB and QatarEnergy headquarters, and luxury hotel towers. For property investors, West Bay is a **corporate tenancy play**, not a yield maximisation zone. Foreign freehold is **building-limited** — far fewer qualifying units than The Pearl or Lusail.

**Quick answer:** Gross 4.5–6%. Corporate tenants. Limited freehold — MOJ verify. CBD proximity premium. Capital preservation over yield.

## Freehold verification: critical step

West Bay has **two markets**:

1. **Foreign-freehold qualifying towers** — MOJ register confirmed
2. **Leasehold / restricted stock** — not available to foreign investors

**Never assume CBD address = freehold.** Pull MOJ register entry before SPA.

West Bay Lagoon (adjacent) has **more foreign-freehold inventory** — villas and apartments with established family tenancy.

## Office oversupply spillover risk

West Bay CBD has experienced **commercial office supply growth**. Towers with **empty commercial podiums** may face:

- Reduced building prestige
- Higher service charge pressure
- Tenant preference for occupied mixed-use buildings

Inspect **commercial occupancy** at ground level before buying residential floors above.

## Frequently asked investor questions

**Should I buy ready or off-plan?**
Ready stock provides immediate rental income and proven service charges. Off-plan may offer lower entry but carries completion risk and zero income until handover. In thin-liquidity markets, ready stock is the conservative default.

**Can I self-manage from overseas?**
Technically possible but not recommended. Local property management (6–10% of rent) protects tenancy registration, maintenance response, and tenant relations. Overseas self-management correlates with higher vacancy and tenant disputes.

**How do I exit if I need liquidity?**
Price at market from day one of listing. In Gulf secondary markets outside UAE, overpricing adds 3–6 months to exit. Budget agent commission on sale (1–2%) and transfer fees on exit.

**Is now the right time in 2026?**
Gulf property is structurally supported by pegged currencies, 0% income tax, and growing expat employment. Market-specific timing matters: UAE is mature, Saudi is frontier, Qatar is stable post-World Cup, Bahrain and Oman are compact. Match timing to your hold horizon, not headlines.
