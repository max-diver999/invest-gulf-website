**TL;DR:** Dubai Hills Estate is built around **school-tied family demand** — GEMS World Academy, Repton, and Kings College sit inside or adjacent to the master plan. Fees run **AED 60,000–110,000/year** depending on curriculum and grade. School runs **under 15 minutes** from most Hills addresses — the core reason families pay **AED 22,000–45,000/month** villa rent and investors accept **lower gross yield** for tenancy stability.

## Summary decision framework

Before signing any lease or purchase contract in Dubai, consolidate **rent, utilities, schools, transport, and one-off relocation** into one spreadsheet tab. Compare against **net household income**, not gross headline salary. Dubai rewards families who model honestly and punishes those who trust brochure economics.

If total fixed costs exceed **70% of net income**, you are one school fee invoice away from financial stress — downgrade area, curriculum, or negotiate package before move, not after.
