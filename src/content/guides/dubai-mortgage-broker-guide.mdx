## Should you use a mortgage broker or go direct to the bank?

Use a broker when you are non-resident, self-employed, have had a prior bank rejection, or are time-poor during relocation. Brokers shop FAB, ENBD, Mashreq, ADCB, and Islamic products in one paperwork pack.

Go direct when your salary already sits at your employer's preferred bank with a pre-negotiated staff mortgage rate. Direct applications can be faster if your relationship manager knows your employer.

## Who qualifies for a Dubai mortgage in 2026?

UAE Central Bank caps set the floor. Individual banks can be stricter on nationality, building list, or minimum loan size.

| Profile | Typical max LTV (first home under AED 5M) | Notes |
|  |  |  |
| UAE resident salaried | 80% | Salary transfer often required for best rate |
| UAE resident self-employed | 70 to 75% | 2 years audited accounts |
| Non-resident | 50 to 75% | Bank-dependent; see [non-resident guide](/guides/non-resident-mortgage-dubai/) |
| Second property | 60 to 70% | Disclose existing UAE loans |
| Off-plan | About 50% | Drawdown tied to construction milestones |

Minimum salary benchmarks: AED 15,000/month for many expat products; joint applications can combine incomes. Age at loan maturity often capped at 65 to 70.

## How do fixed and variable rates compare?

Fixed introductory periods (1 to 5 years) give payment certainty. Variable EIBOR-linked products reprice when EIBOR moves.

| Product | Indicative expat rate (2026) | Risk |
|  |  |  |
| Fixed 1 to 3 years | 4.5 to 5.5% | Break cost if you exit early |
| Fixed 5 years | 5.0 to 5.75% | Higher starting rate |
| Variable EIBOR + margin | From about 4% | Stress test at +2% before you sign |

Stress test yourself at +2% above quoted rate. If fixed expires into higher EIBOR, can you still service the loan from salary or rent?

## What fees sit on top of the interest rate?

Mortgage fees are separate from the 4% DLD property transfer on purchase price. Model both stacks before MOU.

| Fee | Typical amount | When paid |
|  |  |  |
| Bank arrangement | 0.5 to 1% of loan | At drawdown |
| DLD mortgage registration | 0.25% of loan | Trustee appointment |
| Valuation | AED 2,500 to 3,500 | After property chosen |
| Property insurance | Bank mandated | Annual |
| Life insurance | Optional or required | Annual |

On an AED 800,000 loan: arrangement AED 4,000 to 8,000 + DLD reg about AED 2,000 + valuation about AED 3,000 = roughly AED 10,000 before interest accrues. Full purchase stack: [cost of buying property Dubai](/guides/cost-of-buying-property-dubai/).

## What documents do banks expect?

Pack these before pre-approval to avoid losing a unit during a hot week.

| Document | Resident | Non-resident |
|  |  |  |
| Passport + visa | Yes | Passport minimum 6 months |
| Salary certificate / payslips | 3 to 6 months | Same from overseas employer |
| Bank statements | 3 to 6 months | Home country + UAE if any |
| Employment contract | Yes | Notarized where required |
| Self-employed | 2 years audited accounts | Same |
| Property | MOU or draft SPA | MOU or draft SPA |

Open a [UAE bank account](/guides/opening-bank-account-dubai-property-buyers/) before pre-approval if you plan to draw down locally. [Currency transfer](/guides/currency-transfer-buy-property-uae/) timing should align with trustee milestones.

## How long does approval and drawdown take?

| Stage | Typical duration |
|  |  |
| Pre-approval (in principle) | 3 to 7 business days |
| Property valuation | 3 to 5 days after inspection |
| Final offer letter | 2 to 4 weeks total |
| DLD registration + drawdown | Aligns with [buying step by step](/guides/how-to-buy-property-dubai-step-by-step/) |

Do not sign SPA without pre-approval unless your MOU includes finance contingency.

## Can you combine a mortgage with Golden Visa at AED 2M?

Fully mortgaged stock generally does not qualify until net equity on the title meets the Golden Visa property threshold. Plan either cash purchase for visa timing or mortgage first and visa later when loan balance drops.

Details: [Golden Visa mortgage property UAE](/guides/golden-visa-mortgage-property-uae/). Confirm registered value on title, not marketing price.

## Is salary transfer mandatory for the best rate?

Many banks discount 0.25 to 0.75% if salary moves to their account. Switching mid-process adds HR paperwork (2 to 4 weeks). Negotiate before you accept the offer letter, not after final offer.

## Ready property vs off-plan: what changes?

| Type | Mortgage behaviour |
|  |  |
| Ready secondary | Standard LTV, single drawdown |
| Off-plan | Lower LTV, staged drawdown vs payment plan |
| Handover mortgage | Common product post-completion |

Some banks refuse off-plan entirely. Match lender to developer payment plan before you pay booking.

## What red flags should pause your application?

- Valuation below purchase price (bank lends on lower figure)
- Building not on lender approved list
- Service charges above 15% of expected rent on investment units
- Seller cannot produce title or NOC for mortgage
- Employer probation period not cleared

## Rent vs buy when you need a mortgage

Mortgage can beat rent monthly on a spreadsheet while upfront costs (25% down + 6 to 7% transaction) kill liquidity. Run [rent vs buy Dubai expat](/guides/rent-vs-buy-dubai-expat/) with your actual rate and tenure.

## How do joint applications and non-working spouses work?

Primary earner drives LTV. Non-working spouse as co-borrower may help age spread but not income unless they show assets. Age limit at maturity often 65 to 70, shorter tenor if older.

## What happens when EIBOR moves?

Variable rates reset on EIBOR plus bank margin. A 1% EIBOR rise on AED 1M loan adds about AED 10,000/year interest. Keep 6 months payments liquid after drawdown.

## Refinance and exit before you leave Dubai

Refinance when another bank offers lower margin and break cost is smaller than savings. On exit: rental conversion, early settlement (often 1 to 3% penalty on fixed), or sale. See [leaving Dubai selling checklist](/guides/leaving-dubai-selling-property-checklist/).

## Islamic vs conventional products

Murabaha structures quote profit rate instead of interest but cash flow looks similar. DIB and other Islamic windows compete on LTV and salary transfer rules. Compare total cost, not label.

Joint mortgage FAQ: portfolio landlords face reduced LTV on property two; off-plan post-handover mortgage is a different product from developer payment plan; broker commission is often paid by lender, confirm in writing.

## How do brokers get paid in Dubai?

Many brokers earn lender commission, not a separate client fee. Ask who pays arrangement and whether you owe anything if the deal does not complete. Get the fee disclosure in email before you share salary documents.

## What buildings fail bank panels?

Older towers with litigation history, short-let heavy buildings with RERA restrictions, or non-standard title can fail valuation or panel approval. Match lender list to building before MOU.

## How does insurance fit the mortgage stack?

Property insurance is mandatory for drawdown. Life insurance may be optional or bundled. Add both to your first-year cash plan beside arrangement and DLD mortgage registration.

## How should investors treat mortgage on yield units?

If you finance a rental unit, stress-test debt service when void for one month and when service charges rise. Net yield after loan often sits 2 to 3 points below cash buyers. Compare [rent vs buy](/guides/rent-vs-buy-dubai-expat/) with your actual down payment locked in escrow.

## What happens if valuation comes in low?

The bank lends on the lower of price or valuation. You must top up cash or renegotiate price. This is common in off-plan handover when market moved. Keep contingency cash beyond minimum down payment.

## Quick reference: mortgage fees on AED 1M loan

| Item | Indicative |
|  |  |
| Arrangement 1% | AED 10,000 |
| DLD mortgage reg 0.25% | AED 2,500 |
| Valuation | AED 3,000 |
| First-year insurance | AED 2,000 to 4,000 |

Add your down payment and 4% DLD property transfer separately from this loan fee stack.

Keep three months of loan payments liquid after drawdown for rate shock or void periods on investment units. Brokers can reorder bank submissions after a decline, but they cannot override Central Bank LTV floors.

Pre-approval letters expire. Refresh if your property search runs longer than 60 to 90 days so MOU deadlines still match bank validity dates.

Ask the broker to confirm the building is on the lender approved list before you pay a booking deposit on off-plan handover stock.

Fixed-rate expiry dates belong in your calendar with a reminder sixty days ahead.
