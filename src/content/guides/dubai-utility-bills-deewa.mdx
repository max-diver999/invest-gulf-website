## What DEWA covers

| Utility | DEWA bill | Separate provider |
|---|---|---|
| Electricity (lights, appliances, sockets) | Yes |, |
| Water | Yes |, |
| Sewage / drainage fee | Often bundled |, |
| Split AC compressor | Yes (DEWA meter) |, |
| District cooling FCU | Partial meter | Empower / Tabreed chiller bill |
| Gas (cooking) | Sometimes central gas invoice | Community gas in some villas |

Critical: Marina 2BR total "utilities" = DEWA + chiller, never DEWA alone.

## Tariff structure: housing vs commercial

Residential leases use housing tariff, lower than commercial. Verify meter classification at move-in; misclassified commercial meters inflate bills until corrected.

Slab system (conceptual): consumption increases → higher AED/kWh marginal rate for upper slabs.

| Consumption band | Effect |
|---|---|
| Low (winter baseline) | Lower slab rates |
| Medium | Standard expat apartment |
| High (summer AC load) | Upper slabs trigger |

Exact fils/kWh values change with fuel surcharge, download current PDF from DEWA rather than relying on year-old blog posts.

## Connection, deposit and Ejari link

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Ejari registered tenancy | Prerequisite |
| DEWA application | Online or Happiness Centre |
| Deposit | ~AED 2,000 (refundable) |

## Summer AC: why bills explode

Outdoor temps 40–45°C May–September mean:

- AC runs 10–16 hours/day
- Upper tariff slabs engaged
- Poor insulation amplifies load
- Villa multi-split runs multiple compressors

Villa tip: set all zones to 24°C not 19°C, bill difference 20–35%.

Apartment tip: district cooling consumption separate, DEWA spike smaller but total utilities still jump.

## Water charges: often overlooked

Dubai water is desalinated, not free despite desert imagery.

| Usage | Signal |
|---|---|
| Normal couple | Low water line |
| Family + garden irrigation | High water line |
| Pool villa | Significant pump and top-up cost |
| Leak | Sudden 2–3× water line |

Irrigation on villa communities may be sub-metered, check if included in service charge or DEWA.

## DEWA vs district cooling: payment calendar

Both due monthly, different providers:

| Provider | Pay via |
|---|---|

| Empower | Empower portal / app |

Set two calendar reminders, missing one while paying the other is common expat mistake.

### Practical cost reference

Setting up DEWA (Dubai Electricity and Water Authority) requires a refundable security deposit (2,000 AED for a flat, 4,000 AED for a villa), a connection fee of 130 AED, plus knowledge/innovation fees totalling roughly 10 AED. Activation typically takes 1–2 working days online or same-day at a DEWA customer happiness centre. For disconnection when vacating, submit a move-out request at least 3 days before your Ejari end date to avoid overlapping billing.

### Utility and rental specifics

DEWA's slab structure means the first 2,000 kWh per month are cheapest (AED 0.23/kWh), with the rate climbing to AED 0.38/kWh above 6,000 kWh. Average monthly DEWA bills: studio AED 250–450, 1-bed AED 400–700, 2-bed AED 600–1,100, villa AED 1,500–3,500. The housing-fee surcharge (5 % of annual rent) is billed monthly through DEWA.

### Reducing your utility bill — practical tactics

DEWA-specific savings: (1) enrol in DEWA's Shams Dubai net-metering programme if you own a villa — solar panels offset 30–60 % of electricity in peak months. (2) Use DEWA's smart-home app to set consumption alerts at 80 % of your average bill. (3) Report leaks promptly — a dripping tap wastes 20–40 litres per day, adding 50–100 AED per quarter. DEWA slab tariff: the first 2,000 kWh costs 0.23 AED/kWh; consumption above 6,000 kWh jumps to 0.38 AED/kWh — heavy villa users should monitor the slab carefully. Water tariff: first 6,000 IG at 0.03 AED/IG, next tier at 0.04 AED/IG.
## FAQ

**How much is DEWA per month in Dubai?**
**AED 400–750** average apartment annualised; **AED 900–1,600** villa with split AC.

**Is DEWA deposit refundable?**
Yes on proper account closure.

**Does DEWA include AC in towers?**
Includes FCU fan partial electricity, **cooling energy often on chiller bill**.

**Why did my bill double?**
Summer AC, slab tier, leak, or commercial meter misclassification.

**Can I transfer DEWA without Ejari?**
No, Ejari is required for tenant account.

**How long to connect new DEWA?**
Typically **1–3 working days** after Ejari submission.

## How to read DEWA bills before signing

DEWA is not a citywide average, it shifts with insulation, AC age, villa irrigation, and whether cooling is on a separate chiller invoice.

| Check before Ejari | Why |
|---|---|
| Request Jul–Sep bills from landlord | Summer slab tier is the real test |
| Housing fee line on invoice | Small daily charge adds up annually |
| Chiller provider name | Tabreed/Empower/DEWA cooling split |
| Meter reading at handover photo | Dispute protection on move-out |
| Final bill + deposit refund process | Avoid losing AED 2,000 deposit |

## DEWA slab tiers: why your neighbour pays half

DEWA residential billing uses **increasing block tariffs**. A villa running four split units at 22°C all day crosses higher slabs by July:

| Usage pattern | Annual DEWA (indicative 3BR villa) |
|---|---|
| Conservative AC (26°C day, 24°C night) | AED 9,000–12,000 |
| Family default (24°C all rooms) | AED 14,000–18,000 |
| Always-cold + pool pump | AED 20,000–28,000 |

**Housing fee** (municipality line) adds **5% of rent** pro-rated daily, appears on every bill, easy to miss in budgeting.

Tenants on inclusive rent: confirm whether **housing fee** sits inside cap or stacks on top. Landlords on void: minimum consumption keeps DEWA active, budget AED 150–300/month baseline even empty.

Smart meter apps show hourly usage, run **one week in May** before signing a villa lease; May bill extrapolated ×3 approximates August pain.

**Landlord void tip:** switch AC to **28°C** during showings-only voids, cuts DEWA 40–60% vs tenant-style cooling while keeping unit viewable.

## Tenant vs landlord account setup

| Step | Tenant | Landlord (void) |
|---|---|---|
| Ejari required | Yes | No for owner account |
| Deposit | AED 2,000 typical | Same |
| Housing fee | 5% of rent share | N/A if owner-occupied billing |
| Final bill | On move-out | Keep active min 1 month after tenant exit |

Disputes often arise when **tenant closes DEWA** before chiller NOC, landlord loses deposit on both accounts. Lease should specify **order of utility closures** at handover.
