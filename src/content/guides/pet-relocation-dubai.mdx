## FAQ

**Can I bring my pet snake or bird to Dubai?**  
Exotic pets require separate CITES permits and special facilities. Birds face quarantine requirements. Reptiles generally restricted. Contact Dubai Municipality exotic animal department.

**What happens if my pet fails the airport inspection?**  
Pet may be held at airport facility until documentation corrected or quarantine completed. Owner responsible for daily boarding fees. Serious violations may require return to origin country.

**Are there pet-friendly beaches in Dubai?**  
Kite Beach allows leashed dogs early morning and evening. JBR and most public beaches restrict pets. Private beach clubs increasingly pet-friendly during off-peak hours.

**Do I need pet insurance in Dubai?**  
Not mandatory but recommended. Limited options compared to Western countries. Self-insurance (emergency fund) common approach. International pet insurance may provide partial coverage.

**Can my pet travel on Dubai Metro?**  
Small pets in carriers allowed during off-peak hours with additional ticket. Large dogs prohibited. Most expats use private transport for pet mobility.

**What if I rent a furnished apartment with pets?**  
Pet damage beyond normal wear may result in security deposit forfeit. Document pre-existing condition on move-in. Professional cleaning expected on move-out.

**Are there pet relocation services for departure?**  
Yes, same companies handling entry often manage exit. Some destination countries require longer preparation than Dubai entry. Plan 3-4 months ahead for complex destinations.
