## What Are Dubai Pet Import Rules in 2026?

You need an import permit, ISO microchip, valid rabies vaccination (21+ days before travel), and a health certificate issued within 10 days of departure. Banned breeds are refused at Dubai Municipality inspection even if airlines accept the booking.

Prohibited breeds (Dubai Municipality 2026):
- Pitbull terriers and mixes
- Staffordshire terriers  
- American bulldogs
- Japanese Tosa (restricted)
- Wolf-dog hybrids
- Any mixed breed showing banned characteristics

Breed verification: Dubai Municipality inspectors assess pets visually. Mixed breeds can be refused entry if they appear to contain banned genetics.

## What Is the Step-by-Step Pet Import Process?

Week 1-2: Vet consultation and microchip
- ISO 11784/11785 compliant microchip installation
- Register microchip number in home country database
- Begin vaccination schedule if pet not current

Week 3-4: Rabies vaccination
- Primary rabies shot (if first time) or annual booster
- Record in EU pet passport or equivalent official document
- Vaccination must be minimum 21 days old at travel

Week 8-12: Rabies antibody titer test
- Blood sample sent to approved laboratory
- Confirms adequate immunity levels
- Results take 2-4 weeks
- Required from certain countries (check current MOHRE list)

Week 12-16: Additional vaccinations

Dogs required:
- DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza)
- Kennel cough (Bordetella)
- Leptospirosis in endemic areas

Cats required:
- FVRCP (Feline Viral Rhinotracheitis, Calicivirus, Panleukopenia)
- Feline Leukemia (FeLV) test

Both species:
- Heartworm treatment (if coming from endemic areas)
- Flea and tick treatment within 48 hours of travel

### Phase 2: Official documentation (4-8 weeks before)

MOHRE import permit application:
- Submit application via UAE embassy/consulate in home country
- Required documents: passport copy, Emirates ID (if available), pet photos, health records
- Processing time: 1-3 weeks depending on country
- Fee: AED 200 per pet

Endorsement requirements:
- USDA endorsement (for pets from USA)
- EU veterinary certificate (for pets from EU)
- Country-specific requirements vary, check with UAE embassy

### Phase 3: Final preparation (10 days before travel)

Health certificate timing critical:
- Official veterinary health certificate valid only 10 days
- Must certify: vaccinations current, pet healthy, no infectious diseases
- Vet must be licensed/approved by home country agricultural authority

Flight booking considerations:
- Pet space limited, book early
- Summer restrictions May-September (heat embargo on some routes)
- Direct flights preferred to minimize stress

## What Happens at Dubai Airport With Your Pet?

Cargo pets procedure:
1. Flight arrives, pets unloaded to climate-controlled facility
2. Emirates SkyCargo notifies owner when ready for collection
3. Owner presents: passport, MOHRE permit, health certificates
4. Dubai Municipality inspector examines pet and documents
5. Inspection fee paid (AED 300-600)
6. Pet released for transport home

Cabin pets procedure:
1. Pet clears immigration with owner
2. Proceed to customs for declaration
3. Present MOHRE permit and health certificates
4. Quick inspection if requested
5. Exit to arrivals with pet

Required documents for collection:
- Original MOHRE import permit  
- Health certificate and vaccination records
- Flight booking confirmation
- Owner passport and Emirates ID (if available)

### Transport from airport

Recommended:
- Pre-arrange pet-friendly taxi or car rental
- Many Dubai taxis refuse pets, book specialty service
- Uber/Careem policies vary by driver

**Pet relocation agents often include airport pickup and home transport in service packages.**

## Which Dog Breeds Are Banned in Dubai?

Documentation strategies:
- Breed certificate from home country kennel club
- Veterinary breed assessment letter
- Photos showing clear breed characteristics
- Mixed breed documentation emphasizing permitted parentage

### Size and accommodation matching

Large dogs (over 25kg):
- Villa communities strongly recommended
- Apartment living challenging due to space and exercise needs
- Higher transport costs due to airline size restrictions
- Consider breed exercise requirements in Dubai heat

Small dogs and cats:
- Adapt better to apartment living
- Lower transport costs (cabin vs cargo)
- Easier to manage in Dubai's urban environment
- Still need heat considerations during summer months

Age considerations:
- Senior pets (over 8 years) may face additional health scrutiny
- Puppies/kittens under 4 months restricted on some airlines
- Adult pets (2-6 years) typically easiest to relocate
## How Do Dubai Summers Affect Pets?

### Adaptation strategies

First few weeks:
- Gradual exposure to heat and environment
- Monitor for stress signs: excessive panting, lethargy
- Ensure constant water access and shade
- Air conditioning essential for pet comfort
- Avoid middle-day outdoor activities

Long-term heat management:
- Professional grooming to reduce coat thickness
- Cooling mats and raised beds for comfort  
- Adequate indoor climate control
- Learn to recognize heat stress symptoms

## What Pet Import Mistakes Should You Avoid?

### Flight booking problems

Peak season issues:
- Summer embargo limits pet cargo space
- Holiday periods book up months ahead
- Last-minute bookings face significant surcharges

Solution: Book pet space immediately after confirming travel dates

### Housing preparation gaps

Common oversights:
- Lease signed before confirming pet policy
- No research on nearby vet clinics
- Unprepared for pet registration requirements

Solution: Coordinate pet housing requirements during [Dubai relocation checklist](/guides/dubai-property-investment-guide/) process

### Quarantine assumptions

False assumptions:
- "All pets go to quarantine" (false, only if documentation incomplete)
- "Quarantine is automatic and brief" (false, can be expensive and lengthy)

Reality: Perfect documentation = direct home. Any gaps = potential quarantine costs

## Can Pets Travel Within the GCC From Dubai?

**By road (to other emirates):**
- No additional permits within UAE
- Vaccination records recommended for vet visits
- Abu Dhabi and other emirates same requirements

**By air within GCC:**
- Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain have different requirements  
- Oman generally easier for UAE residents
- Qatar Airways good option for regional pet travel

**Vacation planning:**
- Pet boarding services available during travel
- Some hotels in regional Emirates accept pets
- Mountain areas (Hatta, Fujairah) more comfortable for pets

## What Does Long-Term Pet Ownership in Dubai Involve?

**Ongoing obligations:**
- Annual vaccination updates (especially rabies)
- Pet registration renewal with community management
- Health check requirements for travel outside UAE

**Cost planning:**
- Annual vet costs: AED 1,000-2,500 per pet
- Pet insurance options limited but emerging
- Emergency fund recommended (AED 5,000+) for unexpected procedures

### Exit planning when leaving Dubai

**Documentation for departure:**
- Health certificate for destination country
- Vaccination records up to date
- Some countries require quarantine on return
- Exit permits not typically required

**Property damage considerations:**
- Security deposit implications for pet damage
- Professional cleaning costs if pet hair/odors present
- Documentation of pre-existing wear vs pet damage

### Building long-term pet community

**Social opportunities:**
- Dubai dog walking groups on social media
- Pet-friendly cafes and restaurants growing
- Veterinary clinics often host community events
- Villa communities organize pet meet-ups

**Integration benefits:**
- Pets provide social connections with neighbors
- Walking routines help learn local community
- Pet ownership bridges cultural gaps among expats
