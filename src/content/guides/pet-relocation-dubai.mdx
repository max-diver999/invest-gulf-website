# Moving Pets to Dubai 2026: Import Rules, Costs & Quarantine Guide

**TL;DR:** Dubai pet import requires **MOHRE permit** (AED 200), **microchip**, **rabies vaccination 21+ days old**, and **health certificate within 10 days** of travel. **No mandatory quarantine** if requirements met. ## Dubai pet import overview — 2026 requirements

Dubai welcomes pets under strict import controls managed by **Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE)** and **Dubai Municipality**. No blanket quarantine, but documentation must be perfect.

**Key authorities:**

| Authority | Responsibility | Contact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MOHRE** | Import permits, entry authorization | uae.embassy websites, Dubai airports |
| **Dubai Municipality** | Airport inspection, breed restrictions | dubaimunicipality.ae pet section |
| **Airlines** | Pet transport, carrier requirements | Emirates, Qatar, Lufthansa, etc. |
| **Home country vet** | Health certificates, vaccinations | USDA/EU/country-specific |

**Prohibited breeds (Dubai Municipality 2026):**
- Pitbull terriers and mixes
- Staffordshire terriers  
- American bulldogs
- Japanese Tosa (restricted)
- Wolf-dog hybrids
- Any mixed breed showing banned characteristics

**Breed verification:** Dubai Municipality inspectors assess pets visually. Mixed breeds can be refused entry if they appear to contain banned genetics.

## Step-by-step import process — timeline critical

### Phase 1: Health preparation (16-20 weeks before travel)

**Week 1-2: Vet consultation and microchip**
- ISO 11784/11785 compliant microchip installation
- Register microchip number in home country database
- Begin vaccination schedule if pet not current

**Week 3-4: Rabies vaccination**
- Primary rabies shot (if first time) or annual booster
- Record in EU pet passport or equivalent official document
- Vaccination must be minimum 21 days old at travel

**Week 8-12: Rabies antibody titer test**
- Blood sample sent to approved laboratory
- Confirms adequate immunity levels
- Results take 2-4 weeks
- Required from certain countries (check current MOHRE list)

**Week 12-16: Additional vaccinations**

**Dogs required:**
- DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza)
- Kennel cough (Bordetella)
- Leptospirosis in endemic areas

**Cats required:**
- FVRCP (Feline Viral Rhinotracheitis, Calicivirus, Panleukopenia)
- Feline Leukemia (FeLV) test

**Both species:**
- Heartworm treatment (if coming from endemic areas)
- Flea and tick treatment within 48 hours of travel

### Phase 2: Official documentation (4-8 weeks before)

**MOHRE import permit application:**
- Submit application via UAE embassy/consulate in home country
- Required documents: passport copy, Emirates ID (if available), pet photos, health records
- Processing time: 1-3 weeks depending on country
- Fee: AED 200 per pet

**Endorsement requirements:**
- USDA endorsement (for pets from USA)
- EU veterinary certificate (for pets from EU)
- Country-specific requirements vary — check with UAE embassy

### Phase 3: Final preparation (10 days before travel)

**Health certificate timing critical:**
- Official veterinary health certificate valid only 10 days
- Must certify: vaccinations current, pet healthy, no infectious diseases
- Vet must be licensed/approved by home country agricultural authority

**Flight booking considerations:**
- Pet space limited — book early
- Summer restrictions May-September (heat embargo on some routes)
- Direct flights preferred to minimize stress

## Airline options and pet policies — 2026 update

### Emirates pet policy

**Cabin pets (in-cabin with passenger):**
- Combined weight under 7kg (pet + carrier)
- Soft-sided carrier fitting under seat
- Fee: approximately AED 1,000-1,500 each way
- Book minimum 48 hours advance

**Cargo pets (Emirates SkyCargo):**
- No weight limit but size restrictions apply
- Hard-shell IATA-approved carrier required
- Climate-controlled cargo holds
- Fee: AED 2,000-4,000+ depending on size/route

**Blackout dates:** Summer embargo June-September on some routes due to heat

### Alternative carriers

| Airline | Cabin limit | Cargo service | Notable policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Qatar Airways** | 8kg total | Available | Good ME connections |
| **Lufthansa** | 8kg total | Excellent cargo network | Strong EU routing |
| **KLM** | 8kg total | Climate cargo service | Amsterdam hub |
| **Turkish Airlines** | 8kg total | Available | Istanbul connections |

**Route considerations:**
- Direct flights minimize stress and connection risks
- Hub connections (Istanbul, Doha, Amsterdam) can work but add complexity
- Summer routing may require overnight stops in cooler climates

Pet travel agents can coordinate complex routing and handle cargo documentation.

## Dubai arrival process — airport to home

### Dubai International Airport (DXB) pet clearance

**Cargo pets procedure:**
1. Flight arrives, pets unloaded to climate-controlled facility
2. Emirates SkyCargo notifies owner when ready for collection
3. Owner presents: passport, MOHRE permit, health certificates
4. Dubai Municipality inspector examines pet and documents
5. Inspection fee paid (AED 300-600)
6. Pet released for transport home

**Cabin pets procedure:**
1. Pet clears immigration with owner
2. Proceed to customs for declaration
3. Present MOHRE permit and health certificates
4. Quick inspection if requested
5. Exit to arrivals with pet

**Required documents for collection:**
- Original MOHRE import permit  
- Health certificate and vaccination records
- Flight booking confirmation
- Owner passport and Emirates ID (if available)

### Transport from airport

**Recommended:**
- Pre-arrange pet-friendly taxi or car rental
- Many Dubai taxis refuse pets — book specialty service
- Uber/Careem policies vary by driver

**Pet relocation agents often include airport pickup and home transport in service packages.**

## Breed-specific considerations and restrictions

### Banned breeds enforcement

Dubai Municipality actively enforces breed restrictions at entry and during residency:

**Inspection criteria:**
- Visual assessment of breed characteristics
- Comparison to banned breed photos and descriptions
- Mixed breeds showing banned traits may be refused
- No genetic testing required, but visual similarity triggers rejection

**Documentation strategies:**
- Breed certificate from home country kennel club
- Veterinary breed assessment letter
- Photos showing clear breed characteristics
- Mixed breed documentation emphasizing permitted parentage

### Size and accommodation matching

**Large dogs (over 25kg):**
- Villa communities strongly recommended
- Apartment living challenging due to space and exercise needs
- Higher transport costs due to airline size restrictions
- Consider breed exercise requirements in Dubai heat

**Small dogs and cats:**
- Adapt better to apartment living
- Lower transport costs (cabin vs cargo)
- Easier to manage in Dubai's urban environment
- Still need heat considerations during summer months

**Age considerations:**
- Senior pets (over 8 years) may face additional health scrutiny
- Puppies/kittens under 4 months restricted on some airlines
- Adult pets (2-6 years) typically easiest to relocate

## Seasonal considerations and climate adaptation

### Dubai climate challenges for pets

**Summer months (May-September):**
- Outdoor temperatures 40-48°C
- Pavement temperatures burn paws — check before walks
- Heat stroke risk high, especially for flat-faced breeds
- Morning/evening exercise only
- Pool access beneficial for dogs in villa communities

**Winter months (November-March):**
- Ideal pet weather 20-30°C
- Excellent outdoor activity season
- Beach activities possible for dogs
- Optimal time for arrival if flexibility exists

### Adaptation strategies

**First few weeks:**
- Gradual exposure to heat and environment
- Monitor for stress signs: excessive panting, lethargy
- Ensure constant water access and shade
- Air conditioning essential for pet comfort
- Avoid middle-day outdoor activities

**Long-term heat management:**
- Professional grooming to reduce coat thickness
- Cooling mats and raised beds for comfort  
- Adequate indoor climate control
- Learn to recognize heat stress symptoms

## Common mistakes and how to avoid them

### Documentation errors that delay entry

**Timing mistakes:**
- Health certificate issued over 10 days before travel
- Rabies vaccination under 21 days old at entry
- Missing endorsements from home country agricultural authority

**Solution:** Create detailed timeline working backwards from travel date

### Flight booking problems

**Peak season issues:**
- Summer embargo limits pet cargo space
- Holiday periods book up months ahead
- Last-minute bookings face significant surcharges

**Solution:** Book pet space immediately after confirming travel dates

### Housing preparation gaps  

**Common oversights:**
- Lease signed before confirming pet policy
- No research on nearby vet clinics
- Unprepared for pet registration requirements

**Solution:** Coordinate pet housing requirements during [Dubai relocation checklist](/guides/dubai-relocation-checklist/) process

### Quarantine assumptions

**False assumptions:**
- "All pets go to quarantine" (false — only if documentation incomplete)
- "Quarantine is automatic and brief" (false — can be expensive and lengthy)  

**Reality:** Perfect documentation = direct home. Any gaps = potential quarantine costs

## FAQ

**Can I bring my pet snake or bird to Dubai?**  
Exotic pets require separate CITES permits and special facilities. Birds face quarantine requirements. Reptiles generally restricted. Contact Dubai Municipality exotic animal department.

**What happens if my pet fails the airport inspection?**  
Pet may be held at airport facility until documentation corrected or quarantine completed. Owner responsible for daily boarding fees. Serious violations may require return to origin country.

**Are there pet-friendly beaches in Dubai?**  
Kite Beach allows leashed dogs early morning and evening. JBR and most public beaches restrict pets. Private beach clubs increasingly pet-friendly during off-peak hours.

**Do I need pet insurance in Dubai?**  
Not mandatory but recommended. Limited options compared to Western countries. Self-insurance (emergency fund) common approach. International pet insurance may provide partial coverage.

**Can my pet travel on Dubai Metro?**  
Small pets in carriers allowed during off-peak hours with additional ticket. Large dogs prohibited. Most expats use private transport for pet mobility.

**What if I rent a furnished apartment with pets?**  
Pet damage beyond normal wear may result in security deposit forfeit. Document pre-existing condition on move-in. Professional cleaning expected on move-out.

**Are there pet relocation services for departure?**  
Yes — same companies handling entry often manage exit. Some destination countries require longer preparation than Dubai entry. Plan 3-4 months ahead for complex destinations.
