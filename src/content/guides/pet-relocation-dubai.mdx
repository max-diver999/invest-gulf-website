## What Are Dubai Pet Import Rules in 2026?

Dubai pet import rules require a MOHRE permit (AED 200), an ISO microchip, rabies vaccination at least 21 days old, and a health certificate issued within 10 days of departure. Invest Gulf tells foreign buyers relocating into this market to verify breed status before booking, because banned dogs are refused at Dubai Municipality even when airlines sold the seat. Total budgets commonly land AED 5,000 to 12,000 per pet when docs are clean.

Prohibited breeds (Dubai Municipality 2026):
- Pitbull terriers and mixes
- Staffordshire terriers
- American bulldogs
- Japanese Tosa (restricted)
- Wolf-dog hybrids
- Any mixed breed showing banned characteristics

Breed verification: Dubai Municipality inspectors assess pets visually. Mixed breeds can be refused entry if they appear to contain banned genetics.

## What Is the Step-by-Step Pet Import Process?

The Dubai pet import process typically takes 4 to 6 months for complex origin countries: microchip and vaccines first, rabies shot at least 21 days before travel, titer results in 2 to 4 weeks where required, then MOHRE permit (AED 200) and a health certificate valid only 10 days. Invest Gulf sequences foreign buyers so flight booking never precedes the titer window. Summer heat embargos from May to September can erase last-minute cargo plans.

| Phase | Timing | Key requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microchip + vaccines | Week 1 to 4 | ISO chip; rabies 21+ days old at travel |
| Titer (if required) | Week 8 to 12 | Lab results in 2 to 4 weeks |
| MOHRE permit | 4 to 8 weeks before | AED 200; 1 to 3 weeks processing |
| Health certificate | Final 10 days | Must match travel date |

Dogs usually need DHPP and kennel cough; cats need FVRCP; both need flea/tick treatment within 48 hours of travel where required. Family context: [Moving to Dubai with family](/guides/moving-to-dubai-with-family/).

## Which Airlines Allow Pets Flying to Dubai?

Airlines flying pets into Dubai typically allow cabin carriage under about 7kg to 8kg total with carrier, or cargo for larger animals, with summer heat embargos common from June to September. We tell foreign buyers to book pet space as soon as travel dates firm, because cargo slots disappear weeks before seats. Cabin tickets often land AED 1,000 to 1,500; cargo commonly AED 2,000 to 4,000+.

| Qatar Airways | About 8kg total | Strong ME connections |

## What Happens at Dubai Airport With Your Pet?

Dubai airport pet clearance means Municipality inspection against your MOHRE permit and health pack, with inspection fees commonly AED 300 to 600 before release. We tell foreign buyers to pre-book pet-friendly transport, because many standard taxis refuse animals. Cargo pets route through a climate facility; cabin pets clear with the owner after immigration.

| Path | Steps | Fee signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cargo | Unload → notify → docs → inspect → release | AED 300 to 600 |
| Cabin | Immigrate → declare → inspect if asked → exit | Same inspection band |
| Agent assist | Pickup + paperwork buffer | Often inside AED 2,000 to 4,000 package |

Bring original permit, health certificate, flight confirmation, passport and Emirates ID if available.

## Where Can You Rent With a Pet in Dubai?

Pet-friendly rentals in Dubai are far more reliable in villa communities than in Marina or JBR towers, where most buildings restrict or ban animals. We tell foreign buyers to hard-code a pet clause before signing, because deposits of AED 1,000 to 3,000 are common and verbal promises fail at move-in. Downtown and Business Bay policies vary building by building.

| Building type | Pet policy | Watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dubai Marina / JBR | Mostly restrict or ban | Bylaws over listing text |
| Downtown / Business Bay | Mixed | Ask for written approval |
| Villa communities | Usually welcome with registration | Better walking access |

We budget foreign buyers AED 1,000 to 2,500 per pet per year for ordinary care, plus an emergency buffer nearer AED 5,000+. Boarding often runs AED 150 to 400 per day when travel clashes with summer heat.

## Which Dog Breeds Are Banned in Dubai?

Dubai bans pitbull-type dogs, Staffordshire terriers, American bulldogs, wolf-dog hybrids and similar mixes, with Japanese Tosa restricted; Municipality inspectors can refuse mixed breeds on appearance alone. We tell foreign buyers to gather kennel papers and vet breed letters 4 to 6 months before travel, because a refused breed can waste AED 2,000 to 4,000 in flights. Dogs over about 25kg usually need villa living and cargo rather than cabin under the 7kg to 8kg airline band.

| Topic | Rule of thumb | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Banned breeds | Refuse at Municipality | Check list before booking |
| Large dogs (25kg+) | Villa + cargo typical | Book space early |
| Seniors (8+ years) | Extra health scrutiny | Vet letter helpful |
| Adults (2 to 6 years) | Easiest relocate band | Still need 21+ day rabies |

## How Do Dubai Summers Affect Pets?

Dubai summers from about June to September force indoor living for pets: outdoor walks belong to early morning or late evening, and mid-day heat is a medical risk rather than discomfort. We tell foreign buyers to budget grooming (AED 100 to 300) and boarding (AED 150 to 400 per day) when travel coincides with heat embargos. Air conditioning is non-negotiable for the first 2 to 4 weeks of adaptation.

| Heat tactic | Why it matters | Cost signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dawn / dusk walks only | Avoid heat stress | Free, calendar discipline |
| Grooming / coat management | Cooling | AED 100 to 300 |
| Boarding during travel | Cargo embargos | AED 150 to 400 per day |

## What Pet Import Mistakes Should You Avoid?

The costly pet-import mistakes are timing errors: booking cargo after summer embargos fill, signing a Marina lease before confirming pet bylaws, and assuming quarantine is automatic. We see foreign buyers lose AED 500 to 2,000 on rebooking and AED 150 to 300 per day if paperwork gaps trigger quarantine. Perfect docs mean home the same day; gaps mean facility fees.

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Late cargo booking | Embargo / surcharge | Book with travel dates |
| Lease before pet clause | Forced rematch | Written approval first |
| Incomplete titer / certificate | Quarantine risk | 4 to 6 month runway |

## Should You Hire a Pet Relocation Agent?

A pet relocation agent is optional but often worth AED 2,000 to 4,000 for first-time foreign buyers who need MOHRE filing, titer timing and Municipality clearance handled in one thread. Invest Gulf recommends IATA-aware agents with a Dubai presence and 24/7 travel contact, not pure forwarders. DIY works when your origin paperwork is simple and you can spend 4 to 6 months on the calendar.

| Agent value | DIY risk | Fee band |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permit + airport meet | Missed 10 day certificate | AED 2,000 to 4,000 |
| Cargo booking | Summer embargo surprises | Included or extra |
| Home transport | Taxi refusals | Often packaged |

## Can Pets Travel Within the GCC From Dubai?

Inside the UAE, pets generally move by road without a fresh import permit, but other GCC air routes reset rules country by country. We tell foreign buyers to re-check Saudi, Kuwait, Bahrain and Qatar requirements 30 to 60 days before regional flights, even when Oman travel feels easier for UAE residents. Boarding at AED 150 to 400 per day is the practical plan when hotels will not take animals.

| Trip type | Paperwork | Practical note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UAE road | Usually no new permit | Carry vaccine records |
| GCC air | Country-specific | Recheck 30 to 60 days out |
| Hotel travel | Mixed pet policies | Boarding backup |

## What Does Long-Term Pet Ownership in Dubai Involve?

Long-term pet ownership in Dubai means annual rabies updates, community registration renewals, and a realistic care budget of AED 1,000 to 2,500 per pet per year plus an AED 5,000+ emergency buffer. We tell foreign buyers to plan exit health certificates 4 to 6 weeks before leaving this market, because some destination countries impose quarantine on return. Villa communities remain the social default for dog owners.

| Ongoing item | Cadence | AED signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vaccines / rabies | Annual | 300 to 500 |
| Routine vet year | Annual | 1,000 to 2,500 |
| Emergency buffer | Keep ready | 5,000+ |
| Exit health pack | 4 to 6 weeks pre-departure | Origin-dependent |

Insider tip: never sign a tower lease on a "pets negotiable" WhatsApp message; only a written pet clause plus building bylaws approval protects the AED 1,000 to 3,000 deposit conversation later.
