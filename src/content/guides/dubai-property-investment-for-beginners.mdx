## How do you model net yield (not gross)?

Net yield in Dubai means annual rent minus service charges, management near 8% of rent, vacancy near 7%, and maintenance reserves, divided by purchase price, while brokers quote gross yield that ignores operating drag. Invest Gulf buyer scenario on a JVC 1BR at AED 850,000 and 650 sqft often lands near 5.12% net versus 7.6% gross once Mollak charges apply.

| Maintenance reserve | 2,000 |
| **Net income** | **43,500** |
| **Net yield** | **5.12%** |

## What four checks run before you offer?

Four pre-offer checks mean Trakheesi broker status, Dubai REST escrow Active, Mollak service charges in AED per sqft, and three Ejari comps in the same building before any MOU. Invest Gulf red flag: agents who skip Ejari certificates. Marina at AED 28 per sqft versus JVC near AED 16 per sqft can shift net yield 1 to 2 points typically needs written evidence.

If broker cannot produce Ejari comps, use a second agent or walk away.

## What UAE taxes apply to foreign owners?

UAE tax treatment for individual foreign owners means 0% personal income tax on rental income in standard setups, while corporate tax at 9% may apply if you hold via certain UAE companies above AED 375,000 taxable profit. Home country reporting may still apply through UK Self Assessment, US FATCA, or India house property schedules. Our methodology separates UAE headline freedom from home-country obligations in every beginner file.

## Can you manage remotely?

Remote management in Dubai requires a RERA-licensed property manager charging 5% to 10% of annual rent for Ejari, tenants, maintenance, and rent collection, with rent wiring to offshore accounts subject to bank compliance. STR needs DET permit and OA approval. Our checklist for absentee owners: signed management contract, Ejari template, and DEWA landlord account before handover week 4.

Golden Visa eligibility typically requires registered freehold from AED 2,000,000 value under current ICP rules, with property registration first and visa application after DLD completion. Lower investor visa routes exist but change; verify before buying for visa only.
