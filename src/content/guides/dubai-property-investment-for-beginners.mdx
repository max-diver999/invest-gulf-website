Dubai offers **0% personal income tax**, **freehold title** in designated zones, DLD-registered ownership, and yields that often beat Western markets on a **gross** basis. First-time investors underestimate **acquisition costs (6 to 9%)**, the **gross vs net** gap, and verification steps that separate safe deals from expensive mistakes.

| Key number | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum practical entry | AED 700,000 to 900,000 | JVC studio / 1BR |
| Total acquisition costs | 6 to 9% above price | DLD + broker + legal |
| Golden Visa threshold | AED 2,000,000 | Registered value |
| Typical gross yield (JVC) | 7.5 to 9.2% | Before costs |
| Typical net yield (JVC) | 5 to 7% | After operating costs |
| Property management fee | 5 to 10% of rent | Remote owner standard |

## How much money do you need to start?

Cash buyers should plan **purchase price plus 6 to 9%** for DLD transfer (4%), broker (typically 2% + VAT), trustee, and optional legal review. With **80% LTV**, minimum cash is roughly **25% down plus all statutory fees**; DLD fees cannot roll into the loan. Example on AED 900,000: down AED 180,000, DLD and friction ~AED 66,000, total ~AED 246,000 before furniture and DEWA deposits. Detail: [Buy property Dubai as foreigner](/guides/buy-property-dubai-foreigner/).

## What is freehold and where can foreigners buy?

Foreigners buy **freehold in designated zones** only (60+ Dubai communities including JVC, Marina, Business Bay, Downtown, Palm, Dubai Hills). Title appears on **DLD** register via Title Deed or Oqood for off-plan. Always pull **Dubai REST** before MOU. Non-freehold stock is not a beginner play.

## What is the six-step buying process?

| Step | Action | Tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Define budget and net yield target | Spreadsheet |
| 2 | Shortlist communities by liquidity | DLD transaction data |
| 3 | Verify broker | Trakheesi |
| 4 | Confirm escrow (off-plan) | Dubai REST |
| 5 | SPA review | UAE property lawyer |
| 6 | Trustee transfer + Ejari if renting | Registration Trustee |

**Title deed** issued at trustee on ready resale; **Oqood** for off-plan until handover.

## How do you model net yield (not gross)?

Formula: **(Rent minus service charges minus management minus vacancy minus maintenance) divided by price**.

JVC 1BR example, AED 850,000, 650 sqft:

| Line | AED |
| --- | --- |
| Gross annual rent | 65,000 |
| Service charges (AED 15/sqft) | 9,750 |
| Management (8%) | 5,200 |
| Vacancy (7%) | 4,550 |
| Maintenance reserve | 2,000 |
| **Net income** | **43,500** |
| **Net yield** | **5.12%** |

Broker quoted **7.6% gross**. Decision should use **5.12% net**. Inputs: **Ejari** rents (not listings), **Mollak** service charges, three management quotes. Deep dive: [Gross vs net yield Dubai](/guides/gross-vs-net-yield-dubai/).

## What acquisition costs sit on top of price?

| Cost | Typical rate |
| --- | --- |
| DLD transfer | 4% of price |
| Broker | 2% + VAT |
| Trustee + DLD mortgage (if financed) | 0.25% of loan + fixed lines |
| Legal (optional but recommended) | AED 5,000 to 10,000 |

Mortgage adds bank valuation and processing. See [DLD mortgage registration fees](/guides/dld-mortgage-registration-fees/).

## What four checks run before you offer?

1. **Trakheesi**: broker active, company matches card.
2. **Dubai REST**: seller name matches; off-plan escrow **Active**.
3. **Mollak**: service charge AED/sqft matches broker claim.
4. **Ejari**: three comparable rents in same building.

If broker cannot produce Ejari comps, use a second agent or walk away.

## What are the six costliest beginner mistakes?

| Mistake | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Buying on gross yield only | Negative cash flow surprise |
| Unverified broker | Deposit to personal account, fraud risk |
| Off-plan without REST escrow | No DLD protection if developer fails |
| Low-liquidity community | 6 to 12 month resale |
| Ignoring service charges | Marina AED 28/sqft vs JVC AED 16/sqft erodes net |
| No lawyer on SPA | Missed penalties and SC obligations |

Red flags: [Dubai property scams](/guides/dubai-property-scams-red-flags/) and [RERA broker verification](/guides/rera-broker-verification-dubai/).

## What UAE taxes apply to foreign owners?

UAE charges **0% personal income tax** on rental income for individuals in standard setups. **Corporate tax (9%)** may apply if you hold via certain UAE companies: model structure before buying. **Home country** reporting may still apply (UK Self Assessment, US FATCA, India house property schedule). UAE tax freedom does not erase home-country obligations.

## Can you manage remotely?

Yes. Hire **RERA-licensed** manager (5 to 10% of rent). They handle Ejari, tenants, and maintenance. Rent can wire to offshore account per bank compliance. STR needs **DET** permit and OA approval.

## Does property purchase lead to residency?

**Golden Visa**: eligible **freehold** from **AED 2M registered value** (confirm current ICP rules). Property registration first, visa application after. Lower-value investor visa routes exist but change; verify before buying **for visa only**. Compare [Golden Visa property 2026](/guides/uae-golden-visa-property/) paths.

## What year-one checklist runs after handover?

| Month | Task |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Ejari + DEWA tenant setup |
| 0 | Management contract signed |
| 1 | Snagging list if off-plan |
| 3 | DEWA reconciliation |
| 6 | Service charge review |
| 12 | Rent benchmark vs market |

Keep one folder: deed, Oqood, Ejari, DEWA, service charge receipts, management statements.

## When should you buy a second unit?

After **12 months** when actual net yield is within **0.5 point** of model and Ejari history exists. Then read [Dubai property portfolio strategy](/guides/dubai-property-portfolio-strategy/). Do not scale on one brochure yield.

## How does off-plan differ for beginners?

Off-plan can offer **payment plans** but adds **handover risk**, **service charge unknowns until OA forms**, and **no Ejari rent history**. Beginners should prefer **ready units with 200+ annual transactions** in the building unless you have lawyer review and developer escrow confirmed on Dubai REST. Never pay booking fees to a personal account.

| Ready resale | Off-plan |
| --- | --- |
| Immediate Ejari comps | No rent history until handover |
| Trustee transfer in weeks | Handover date risk |
| Full DLD 4% at transfer | Staged DLD on Oqood + handover |
| Lower developer default risk | Escrow must be Active on REST |

## What should your SPA lawyer check?

| Clause | Risk if missed |
| --- | --- |
| Service charge cap or spike history | Net yield collapse |
| Penalty for buyer cancellation | Lost deposit |
| Snagging and defect liability window | Costly repairs |
| Short-term rental restrictions | STR plan blocked |
| Handover delay remedies (off-plan) | No compensation |

Budget **AED 5,000 to 10,000** for review; it is cheaper than one missed clause.

## What happens in the first 90 days after handover?

| Week | Owner action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Snagging list to developer (if off-plan) |
| 2 | List unit with RERA-licensed agent; sign Form A |
| 3 to 4 | First tenant viewing cycle |
| 4 to 8 | Ejari signed; DEWA landlord account |
| 12 | Compare actual rent to model |

If net yield after 90 days is **more than 1 point below** model, revisit service charge invoices and agent marketing price before renewing the management contract.

Join one **investor community** (RERA-licensed manager referral or vetted peer group) for Ejari benchmark sharing. Avoid paid "mentorship" groups until you have twelve months of actual rent and service charge receipts to compare against marketing claims.

Record every **viewing and offer** in a spreadsheet with date, agent Trakheesi number, and asking rent. That log becomes your evidence if a seller renegotiates after you waste surveyor or legal fees.

## How do Golden Visa and beginner budget interact?

If residency is a goal, target **AED 2M registered value** in **freehold** with clean title. A AED 900,000 JVC unit is a strong **first investment** but usually **not** a Golden Visa ticket alone. Some buyers combine two units or upgrade later; model visa rules separately from yield math. See [Buy property Dubai foreigner](/guides/buy-property-dubai-foreigner/) for ownership type checks.

## What tools matter for due diligence?

**Dubai REST** (ownership, escrow, Ejari), **Mollak** (service charges), **Trakheesi** (listings), not generic PropTech dashboards. Start with [Due diligence Dubai property](/guides/due-diligence-dubai-property/) and [Dubai REST app due diligence](/guides/dubai-rest-app-property-due-diligence/).

## Invest Gulf: beginner file standards

Invest Gulf rejects beginner files missing **Mollak service charge PDF** or **Ejari rent comps** at MOU stage. The most common fix is rebuilding net yield with **building-specific** charges, which often drops marketed gross by **2 to 3 points**. Treat refusal to share Mollak as a walk-away signal before any deposit.
