## What is the step-by-step Qatar off-plan purchase process?

The Qatar off-plan purchase process typically runs MOI approval in 2 to 4 weeks, SPA signing, escrow milestones, then title at handover, and we budget legal and registration costs near 1% to 2% of price.

1. MOI approval: passport, statements, income proof, property details; often 2 to 4 weeks
2. Developer selection: registration, permits, escrow details, sister-phase delivery history
3. SPA: milestones, delay remedies, finishes, cancellation penalties, escrow bank
4. Milestone payments through escrow only after certification
5. Handover: snagging, final payment, utilities, MOI title, 1% to 2% legal and registration budget
