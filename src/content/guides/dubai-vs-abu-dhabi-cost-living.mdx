**Dubai vs Abu Dhabi rent, villas and townhouses**

The saving on a family villa is typically AED 20,000–60,000 per year, real money, but only if you can absorb the car costs that Abu Dhabi's car-dependent layout requires.

Both cities require tenancy registration: Dubai uses **Ejari** (DLD), Abu Dhabi uses **Tawtheeq** (ADREC). Registration fees run AED 160–220 in Dubai and AED 100–300 in Abu Dhabi depending on the emirate fee structure. Factor these into your move-in budget.
