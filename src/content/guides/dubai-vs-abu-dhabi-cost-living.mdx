# Dubai vs Abu Dhabi Cost of Living: Full 2026 Comparison

> **Disclaimer:** All figures are June 2026 planning ranges based on publicly available listings and government-published utility tariffs. They are not quotes. Verify current school fees with ADEK and KHDA, and utility tariffs with ADD and DEWA before making relocation decisions.

## Is Dubai more expensive than Abu Dhabi overall?

For a typical expat family, Dubai runs about 15 to 25 percent higher, driven mainly by rent and mid-tier school fees.

The key variables are rent tier, school choice, and whether you are replacing a Dubai Metro commute with a car-dependent Abu Dhabi routine. Abu Dhabi has no metro. Every adult member of the household who needs to travel independently will require a car or a taxi budget that erodes the rent saving.

| Cost category | Dubai monthly (AED) | Abu Dhabi monthly (AED) | City cheaper |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Family 3BR rent | 17,000 to 25,000 | 13,000 to 20,000 | Abu Dhabi |
| Single 1BR rent | 7,000 to 15,000 | 6,000 to 12,000 | Abu Dhabi |
| DEWA/ADD utilities (family villa) | 1,200 to 2,200 | 1,000 to 2,500 | Roughly equal |
| International school (mid-tier) | 4,500 to 7,000 | 3,500 to 5,500 | Abu Dhabi |
| Groceries (family of four) | 3,000 to 5,000 | 2,800 to 4,800 | Roughly equal |
| Car ownership (one vehicle) | 2,500 to 4,500 | 2,500 to 4,500 | Equal |
| Dining out (monthly, family) | 2,000 to 4,000 | 1,800 to 3,500 | Roughly equal |
## How do Dubai and Abu Dhabi rent levels compare in 2026?

Abu Dhabi apartments and villas usually rent 15 to 25 percent below comparable Dubai stock.

The saving on a family villa is typically AED 20,000 to 60,000 per year, real money, but only if you can absorb the car costs that Abu Dhabi's car-dependent layout requires.

Both cities require tenancy registration: Dubai uses **Ejari** through the rental regulator, Abu Dhabi uses **Tawtheeq** through ADREC. Registration fees run AED 160 to 220 in Dubai and AED 100 to 300 in Abu Dhabi depending on the emirate fee structure. Factor these into your move-in budget.

| Unit type | Dubai annual rent (AED) | Abu Dhabi annual rent (AED) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marina or Downtown 2BR | 140,000 to 180,000 | N/A comparable |
| Reem Island 2BR | N/A comparable | 100,000 to 130,000 |
| Family 3BR villa (mid-tier) | 200,000 to 300,000 | 180,000 to 250,000 |
| JVC or Khalifa City 1BR | 84,000 to 120,000 | 72,000 to 110,000 |

## Are groceries and dining cheaper in Dubai or Abu Dhabi?

Supermarket chains price similarly; Dubai offers more premium dining choice, not always higher weekly grocery spend.

| Category | Dubai AED/month | Abu Dhabi AED/month | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Family grocery basket (4 pax) | 3,000 to 5,000 | 2,800 to 4,800 | Identical chains, similar pricing |
| Single grocery basket | 800 to 1,500 | 750 to 1,400 | Mid-range supermarket |
| Casual dining (couple, 1x/week) | 400 to 800 | 350 to 750 | Similar menu pricing |
| Premium restaurant (couple, 1x/week) | 800 to 2,000 | 700 to 1,800 | Fewer top-tier venues in AD |
| Coffee and café monthly spend | 400 to 900 | 350 to 800 | Slightly fewer venues in AD |
| Takeaway deliveries (monthly) | 600 to 1,500 | 500 to 1,200 | Talabat dominates both |

Dubai has a broader selection of premium restaurants, rooftop bars, and international cuisine chains. Abu Dhabi's dining scene is strong but more concentrated in Yas, Saadiyat, and the Corniche corridor. Families who eat out frequently may find Dubai more convenient rather than meaningfully more expensive.

## Is transport cheaper in Dubai or Abu Dhabi?

Dubai wins for Metro commuters; Abu Dhabi usually requires a car, which can erase rent savings for two-worker households.

| Transport item | Dubai AED/month | Abu Dhabi AED/month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metro monthly card (commuter) | 300 to 450 | N/A (no metro) |
| Bus pass (monthly) | 100 to 200 | 50 to 100 |
| Car loan repayment (midrange sedan, 60-mo) | 1,800 to 2,800 | 1,800 to 2,800 |
| Car insurance (annual, comprehensive) | 4,000 to 9,000 | 3,500 to 8,000 |
| Petrol (family usage, monthly) | 500 to 900 | 450 to 850 |
| Parking (work + home, monthly) | 400 to 1,000 | 100 to 500 |
| Salik toll (Dubai, monthly) | 200 to 600 | N/A |
| Taxi / ride-hail (no car, monthly) | 1,500 to 3,500 | 2,000 to 4,000 |

Dubai families with work inside the Metro corridor can function on one car, which saves the AED 3,000 to 5,000/month a second vehicle costs. Abu Dhabi families with two adults who both work typically need two cars, this single factor can eliminate the rent saving entirely.

Salik tolls in Dubai average AED 200 to 600/month for a regular commuter using the Sheikh Zayed Road gates. Abu Dhabi introduced its Darb toll system on city exits but daily urban Darb costs are typically AED 50 to 150/month lower than equivalent Salik exposure.

## Which city has cheaper international schools?

Abu Dhabi mid-tier British and American fees often land 10 to 20 percent below KHDA peers in Dubai.

| School tier | Dubai annual AED (KHDA) | Abu Dhabi annual AED (ADEK) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget / Indian subcontinent curriculum | 15,000 to 25,000 | 12,000 to 22,000 |
| Mid-tier British or American curriculum | 50,000 to 80,000 | 40,000 to 65,000 |
| Premium British (Years 7 to 13) | 80,000 to 100,000 | 70,000 to 95,000 |
| IB Diploma Programme schools | 85,000 to 110,000 | 75,000 to 100,000 |
| Registration fee (one-time) | 5,000 to 15,000 | 4,000 to 12,000 |
| School bus annual cost | 5,000 to 10,000 | 6,000 to 11,000 |

For two children in mid-tier British schools, the Abu Dhabi saving can be AED 20,000 to 30,000 annually on tuition alone. Over a three-year posting, that is AED 60,000 to 90,000, equivalent to more than a year of rent savings.

Both cities have strong school supply in British, American, Indian CBSE, and IB formats. Waiting lists at top schools are real in both cities; apply before you arrive. Most international schools require proof of residence, Emirates ID, and passport copies at admission.

Note: some Abu Dhabi employers, particularly government-linked entities, provide school-fee allowances that change the net calculation entirely. Negotiate the allowance alongside salary, not separately.

## How do DEWA and ADD utility bills compare?

Tariffs are in the same band; district cooling in Dubai towers and large Abu Dhabi villas drive the swings.

Summer (June to September) is the high-utility season in both cities. Villa owners should model July and August separately, AC running 24/7 on a large villa can push electricity to AED 2,000 to 2,500/month regardless of emirate.

## What monthly budgets should singles and families plan?

Singles often need AED 9K to 15K; families with two school-age children commonly land AED 33K to 66K depending on city and school tier.

The Metro effect is significant for Dubai singles. Without a car, a Dubai single in JVC or Business Bay can come in below Abu Dhabi equivalent despite paying more rent, because the Abu Dhabi car requirement absorbs the rent saving entirely.

**Family of four (2 adults, 2 school-age children)**

| Budget line | Dubai AED/month | Abu Dhabi AED/month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3BR villa / townhouse rent (annual ÷ 12) | 17,000 to 27,000 | 14,000 to 22,000 |
| Two cars (loan + insurance + petrol + parking) | 6,000 to 11,000 | 5,500 to 10,000 |
| School fees, 2 children, mid-tier British (÷ 12) | 9,000 to 14,000 | 7,000 to 11,000 |
| Groceries, family of four | 3,000 to 5,000 | 2,800 to 4,800 |
| Dining and activities | 2,500 to 5,000 | 2,000 to 4,500 |
| DEWA / ADD + internet + cooling | 1,500 to 2,500 | 1,200 to 2,500 |
| Health insurance (family top-up) | 600 to 1,500 | 600 to 1,500 |
| Total | 39,600 to 66,000 | 33,100 to 56,300 |

The gap at the midpoint is roughly AED 6,000 to 10,000/month, meaningful over a three-year posting. Families where both adults have Metro-accessible Dubai jobs and only one school-age child will see a narrower gap.

## Which city costs less for your household profile?

Abu Dhabi wins on pure cost for most families; Dubai wins for Metro singles and certain school niches.

**Dubai is cheaper for (or equal):** singles in Metro corridors who avoid a car, households with school-age children in Indian CBSE schools (Dubai supply is larger and equally priced), and those whose social spend in restaurants and venues would be higher in Dubai but more efficient per outing.

**The crossover point** for a typical family is around AED 40,000/month total spend, below that, Abu Dhabi almost always wins on pure cost; above that, lifestyle factors (dining variety, beach access, visa flexibility) often make Dubai the preference even at a premium.

## What are the main pros and cons at a glance?

| Factor | Dubai | Abu Dhabi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rent for space | Higher, more choice | Lower, more square footage per dirham |
| Schools | Widest KHDA supply | Often 10 to 20% cheaper mid-tier ADEK fees |
| Transport | Metro plus Salik car option | Car-first layout |
| Lifestyle pace | Faster, more venues | Quieter, more suburban |

## What should a single expat budget monthly in each city?

Singles who commute on the Dubai Metro can keep transport closer to AED 300 to 450/month for the rail pass plus occasional taxis. The same lifestyle in Abu Dhabi usually assumes a car loan or lease, insurance, and petrol near AED 2,500 to 4,000/month before rent.

| Budget line | Dubai AED/month | Abu Dhabi AED/month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1BR rent (annual ÷ 12) | 7,000 to 15,000 | 6,000 to 12,000 |
| Transport | 300 to 4,500 | 2,500 to 4,500 |
| Groceries and dining | 1,800 to 3,500 | 1,700 to 3,200 |
| Utilities and internet | 600 to 1,200 | 600 to 1,200 |
| Total (typical band) | 10,000 to 15,000 | 9,000 to 13,000 |

*Updated July 2026. Figures are planning ranges, not quotes.*

Healthcare and visa costs sit outside these tables but rarely flip the city choice. Employer-provided insurance covers most expats; self-paid family top-ups run AED 600 to 1,500/month in either emirate. Neither city is materially cheaper on basic medical access when insurance is already bundled with employment.
