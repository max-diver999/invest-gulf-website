## How Expensive Is Dubai vs Abu Dhabi? The Bottom Line

Abu Dhabi is consistently cheaper than Dubai, but the margin depends heavily on lifestyle. For a single professional choosing between a Marina studio and a Reem Island studio, the difference is roughly AED 1,500–3,000/month, meaningful but not significant. For a family combining a premium villa, two cars, and two children in British schools, the gap can reach AED 7,000–12,000/month in Abu Dhabi's favour.

The key variables are rent tier, school choice, and whether you are replacing a Dubai Metro commute with a car-dependent Abu Dhabi routine. Abu Dhabi has no metro. Every adult member of the household who needs to travel independently will require a car or a taxi budget that erodes the rent saving.

### Does Abu Dhabi have cheaper supermarkets than Dubai?

No meaningful difference. The same Carrefour, Spinneys, and LuLu stores operate in both cities with similar shelf pricing. A comparable weekly shop for a family of four will cost AED 700–1,200 in either city. Premium specialty stores like Jones the Grocer and Waitrose are present in both; organic and imported items are priced at a similar premium.

### Can you live in Abu Dhabi and work in Dubai?

Technically yes, but practically difficult without a very early commute. The Abu Dhabi–Dubai highway is AED 50–70 one-way via Darb toll, takes 90–120 minutes in normal traffic, and around two hours during morning peak. Some expats base families in Abu Dhabi and commute weekly or use employer accommodation in Dubai midweek. Factor AED 2,500–4,000/month in fuel, tolls, and vehicle wear if commuting daily.

### Are school waiting lists long in both cities?

Yes, particularly for British and IB schools rated Outstanding by KHDA or ADEK. Popular schools in both cities, GEMS Wellington, Repton, BISAD, Cranleigh Abu Dhabi, report waiting lists of six months to two years for certain year groups. Apply to three to five schools before you arrive, and have documents ready (passport, visa, previous school reports).

### Do utility bills include all household services?

No. DEWA and ADD cover electricity and water. District cooling is a separate bill from the chiller operator. Internet and mobile are separate Etisalat (e&) or du contracts. Waste collection is typically included in the annual municipality fee paid via DEWA (Dubai) or ADD (Abu Dhabi) billing. Budget all line items separately for an accurate monthly total.
