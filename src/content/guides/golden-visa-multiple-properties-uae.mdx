Each unit must be in designated freehold or investor-eligible zones. Documentation must show consistent ownership across all titles or Oqood certificates."
  - question: "Can I mix Dubai and Abu Dhabi properties for Golden Visa aggregation?"
    answer: "Federal Golden Visa rules apply across the UAE. Dubai (DLD) and Abu Dhabi (DMT) registrations can potentially be combined toward the AED 2 million threshold if both are registered in your personal name and meet freehold requirements. Confirm aggregation practice with GDRFA or ICP at application, emirate processing paths differ."
  - question: "Can I combine off-plan Oqood with ready Title Deed properties?"
    answer: "In many reported cases, yes, one Oqood at AED 1.2 million plus one Title Deed at AED 900,000 equals AED 2.1 million qualifying portfolio. Both must be registered to the same applicant. Off-plan must be Oqood-registered, not merely reserved."
  - question: "Do mortgaged properties count in an aggregated Golden Visa application?"
    answer: "Under 2026 reported rules, registered value counts toward the AED 2 million total even if loans are outstanding, subject to bank NOCs per mortgaged unit. Equity is not the headline test. Each encumbered property may need its own bank NOC."
  - question: "Can company-owned properties be aggregated for personal Golden Visa?"
    answer: "Generally no for personal Golden Visa, aggregation applies to property registered in the individual applicant's name. Company-held assets follow corporate structures and different visa categories. Transfer to personal name before application if aggregation is the strategy."
  - question: "What is the minimum value per unit in a portfolio aggregation?"
    answer: "There is no published per-unit minimum for aggregation, only the combined registered total must reach AED 2 million. Practical floor: units must be legitimate freehold registrations, not parking or storage add-ons unless explicitly counted by authorities."
  - question: "If I sell one property in a two-unit portfolio, what happens to my visa?"
    answer: "If remaining registered holdings fall below AED 2 million combined, renewal risk rises. Replace sold unit with another qualifying registration before renewal, or downgrade visa category. Plan sales against renewal calendar."
  - question: "Is aggregation better than one AED 2 million unit?"
    answer: "Immigration outcome is similar if total qualifies. Investment outcome differs: two yield units may outperform one trophy flat; one trophy may simplify renewal documentation. Aggregation suits buyers building portfolio over time, not those forcing awkward unit combos only for visa."
