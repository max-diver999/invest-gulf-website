# Convert Foreign Driving Licence in Dubai: RTA 2026 Guide

> **Disclaimer:** RTA approved country lists and conversion procedures change without advance notice. Always verify eligibility at rta.ae before attending any appointment. This is not legal advice.

## Why does Dubai licence conversion matter for expats?

A UAE driving licence unlocks standard insurance, Salik registration, and employer fleet compliance. Most expats convert within two weeks of Emirates ID issuance. Corporate fleet policies often require a UAE licence within 30 to 60 days.

## Who can convert without a driving test (Track 1)?

Eligible nationalities file at an RTA Customer Happiness Centre after an eye test. Typical processing takes 1 to 3 working days once documents are accepted.

### Why the list exists

RTA considers the home country's driving standards, testing rigor, and traffic safety record. Countries with robust driving test systems (UK, Germany, Japan, Australia) are included. Countries where licence issuance is perceived as less rigorous require the full test.

### Full eligibility process for approved countries

Step 1, Confirm country eligibility

Check rta.ae or call the RTA contact centre (8009090). Do not rely on WhatsApp groups, Reddit threads, or this guide as primary confirmation, the list changes quarterly.

Step 2, Eye test

Visit an RTA-approved optical centre. The test takes 5 to 10 minutes. Bring your glasses or contact lenses if you normally drive with them, the test replicates driving conditions. Certificate is valid for 30 days.

Popular RTA-approved centres: Magrabi Optical, Safeco Optical, Al Jaber Optical, and most Vision Express branches.

Cost: AED 100 to 200 depending on branch.

Step 3, Gather documents

| Document | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Emirates ID | Original, must show valid residency |
| Foreign driving licence | Original, must be valid (not expired) |
| Passport | Original plus one copy |
| Eye test certificate | From approved centre, max 30 days old |
| Passport photos | White background, specific RTA size (3.5 x 4.5 cm typical) |
| Payment | Credit/debit card or cash |
| NOC letter | Some employer sponsors require, ask HR |

Step 4, RTA Customer Happiness Centre appointment

Book online via rta.ae or RTA Smart app. Walk-ins accepted at some centres but wait times are unpredictable, booking saves hours.

Major centres with traffic licensing:

| Centre | Location | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Al Barsha | Al Barsha 1 | Largest; all services |
| Umm Ramool | Near Deira | Convenient for east side |
| Al Qusais | Near Stadium | Serving north Dubai |
| Jebel Ali | Near Expo City | West Dubai and JA Beach |

Step 5, Submit and pay

Counter staff verify documents, enter details into the system, and issue a token. Fee payment at window or kiosk.

Licence validity options:

| Term | RTA fee (indicative) |
| --- | --- |
| 2 years | AED 220 to 300 |
| 10 years | AED 400 to 550 |

Most expats planning a multi-year stay choose 10 years to align with visa cycles, even if the card must be renewed at each residency visa renewal in practice.

Step 6, Collect licence

Same-day printing available at most major centres. Some online applications mail the card within 3 to 5 days. Confirm at counter whether collection or postal delivery applies.

## What if your country is not on the RTA list (Track 2)?

Non-listed nationalities enrol at an RTA-approved driving school, pass theory and practical tests, and pay roughly AED 3,500 to 8,000 over 6 to 14 weeks.

### Full programme stages

**Theory phase:**

- Traffic rules and signs (usually 8 to 16 hours of classes)
- Hazard perception tests
- Theory test at RTA, must pass before practical begins

Practical phase:

- Yard training (slow manoeuvres, parking exercises)
- On-road driving with instructor
- Minimum lesson hours vary by school, typically 20 to 40 hours

**RTA tests:**

1. Yard test (parking, manoeuvres)
2. On-road test (evaluated by RTA examiner, not school instructor)

| Stage cost breakdown | Indicative AED |
| --- | --- |
| File opening and registration | 1,200 to 2,000 |
| Theory classes | 600 to 1,200 |
| Practical lessons (20 to 40 hrs) | 2,500 to 5,500 |
| RTA theory test | 200 to 300 |
| RTA yard test | 200 to 300 |
| RTA road test | 200 to 300 |
| Licence issuance | 200 to 400 |
| **Total** | **3,500 to 8,000** |

Retests cost AED 200 to 500 each plus a mandatory minimum wait. Budget realistically, first-attempt pass rates are lower than many candidates expect.

## How do Track 1 conversion and driving school compare?

| Route | Typical cost (AED) | Timeline | Test required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct conversion (approved countries) | 320 to 750 | 1 to 3 working days | Eye test only |
| Full driving school | 3,500 to 8,000 | 6 to 14 weeks | Theory, yard, road |

Choose conversion when rta.ae confirms your issuing country on the live list. Choose driving school when the list excludes your licence or your foreign licence is expired beyond RTA tolerance.

## Why do RTA applications get rejected?

Common rejection causes include expired foreign licences, eye test certificates older than 30 days, missing Arabic translation for non-Latin licences, and Emirates ID mismatches versus passport details. Renew an expired home licence before visiting RTA when possible.

## When do you need an Arabic translation?

Licences issued in languages other than English or Arabic generally require a certified translation from an approved translator. Bring both the original and translation to the RTA appointment; counter staff retain copies of both.

## How can you track RTA application status?

Use the RTA Smart app or SMS updates tied to your traffic file number. If processing exceeds 3 working days for a conversion file, verify whether translation or employer NOC documents were flagged for resubmission.

## How do you transfer a licence from another UAE emirate?

- Update your traffic file emirate association via RTA
- Insurance re-quote for Dubai garaging address, premiums differ
- Salik tag registration if not already active

Most expats keep the same physical licence card until renewal; the UAE licence is a federal document. Update garaging address and insurance after the move.

For the Abu Dhabi process, see [Abu Dhabi driving licence guide](/guides/abu-dhabi-driving-license/).

## How do dual-driver households handle conversion?

Each driver pays separate RTA and eye test fees. Book back-to-back appointments at the same centre to reduce waiting time.

## What should you do first: priority checklist

1. Confirm country eligibility on rta.ae (do not rely on social media lists)
2. Complete eye test at an RTA-approved optical centre
3. Gather Emirates ID, passport, valid foreign licence, photos, and payment
4. Book RTA Customer Happiness Centre slot via app or web
5. Update car insurance and Salik once the UAE licence is printed

## Which planning scenarios change your timeline?

**Scenario A, UK/US/EU licence:** Usually Track 1 if licence is valid and matches RTA list at appointment date.

**Scenario B, Indian or Pakistani licence:** Expect Track 2 driving school unless RTA policy changes; budget 2 to 3 months before relying on a commute-only route.

**Scenario C, licence from another emirate:** Transfer traffic file to Dubai, re-quote insurance for Dubai garaging, register Salik tag if needed.

When budgeting ancillary costs, account for typing-centre fees (about AED 100 to 350), optional PRO services (AED 1,500 to 5,000), and attestation if RTA requests supporting letters from your employer.

## Can you drive while waiting for the UAE licence?

Some insurers allow limited driving on a valid foreign licence during the post-Emirates ID grace window. Once that window closes, driving without a UAE licence can void coverage. Confirm wording with your insurer before you rely on a grace period.

## Is partial online RTA processing available?

Eligible nationalities can start parts of the file on rta.ae or the RTA Smart app, but eye tests and document verification still require an in-person step at most centres. Book the happiness centre slot only after your eye test certificate is dated within 30 days.

## Does RTA keep your foreign licence?

Policy varies by nationality and conversion type. Some counters stamp the foreign licence cancelled and return it; others retain it. Ask at submission so you know whether to carry both licences on your next home-country visit.

## How does conversion interact with car insurance and Salik?

Update your motor policy to list the UAE licence number as soon as the card prints. Insurers use licence status when setting premiums and claims eligibility. Register or transfer a Salik tag under the same traffic file to avoid toll fines while plates are being updated on a newly purchased vehicle.
