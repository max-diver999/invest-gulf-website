# Convert Foreign Driving Licence in Dubai: RTA 2026 Guide

Quick answer: Eligible nationalities (UK, US, EU, GCC, Australia, and others) convert at an RTA Customer Happiness Centre for AED 220–550 plus eye test in 1–3 working days. Non-eligible nationalities complete a driving school route costing AED 3,500–8,000 over 6–14 weeks.

TL;DR: Dubai's RTA runs a two-track system, direct conversion for approved countries, full test for everyone else. Both result in the same UAE federal licence. The key difference is cost and time. This guide covers every document, fee, centre, and common rejection reason so you arrive prepared. For broader relocation context, see [Dubai relocation guide](/guides/dubai-relocation-guide/) and [Dubai cost of living](/guides/dubai-cost-of-living-guide/).

> **Disclaimer:** RTA approved country lists and conversion procedures change without advance notice. Always verify eligibility at rta.ae before attending any appointment. This is not legal advice.

## Why Dubai licence conversion matters for expats

Your UAE driving licence unlocks:

- **Vehicle registration**, impossible without valid UAE licence
- **Insurance**, insurers refuse comprehensive cover on foreign licences for residents
- **Rental cars**, hire desks increasingly require UAE licence after 30 days of residency
- **Daily commute legality**, driving on a foreign licence past the grace period voids insurance

Most expats target licence conversion in the first two weeks after Emirates ID issuance. If your employer provides a vehicle, check the fleet policy, most corporate fleets require a UAE licence within 30–60 days regardless.

## Track 1: Direct conversion (golden countries)

RTA publishes a list of countries whose citizens can exchange their home driving licence for a UAE licence without sitting a practical test. This is often called the "golden countries" or "approved countries" list.

### Why the list exists

RTA considers the home country's driving standards, testing rigor, and traffic safety record. Countries with robust driving test systems (UK, Germany, Japan, Australia) are included. Countries where licence issuance is perceived as less rigorous require the full test.

### Full eligibility process for approved countries

**Step 1, Confirm country eligibility**

Check rta.ae or call the RTA contact centre (8009090). Do not rely on WhatsApp groups, Reddit threads, or this guide as primary confirmation, the list changes quarterly.

**Step 2, Eye test**

Visit an RTA-approved optical centre. The test takes 5–10 minutes. Bring your glasses or contact lenses if you normally drive with them, the test replicates driving conditions. Certificate is valid for 30 days.

Popular RTA-approved centres: Magrabi Optical, Safeco Optical, Al Jaber Optical, and most Vision Express branches.

Cost: AED 100–200 depending on branch.

**Step 3, Gather documents**

| Document | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Emirates ID | Original, must show valid residency |
| Foreign driving licence | Original, must be valid (not expired) |
| Passport | Original plus one copy |
| Eye test certificate | From approved centre, max 30 days old |
| Passport photos | White background, specific RTA size (3.5 x 4.5 cm typical) |
| Payment | Credit/debit card or cash |
| NOC letter | Some employer sponsors require, ask HR |

**Step 4, RTA Customer Happiness Centre appointment**

Book online via rta.ae or RTA Smart app. Walk-ins accepted at some centres but wait times are unpredictable, booking saves hours.

Major centres with traffic licensing:

| Centre | Location | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Al Barsha | Al Barsha 1 | Largest; all services |
| Umm Ramool | Near Deira | Convenient for east side |
| Al Qusais | Near Stadium | Serving north Dubai |
| Jebel Ali | Near Expo City | West Dubai and JA Beach |

**Step 5, Submit and pay**

Counter staff verify documents, enter details into the system, and issue a token. Fee payment at window or kiosk.

**Licence validity options:**

| Term | RTA fee (indicative) |
| --- | --- |
| 2 years | AED 220–300 |
| 10 years | AED 400–550 |

Most expats planning a multi-year stay choose 10 years to align with visa cycles, even if the card must be renewed at each residency visa renewal in practice.

**Step 6, Collect licence**

Same-day printing available at most major centres. Some online applications mail the card within 3–5 days. Confirm at counter whether collection or postal delivery applies.

## Track 2: Full driving school (non-eligible countries)

If your country is not on the RTA approved list, you must complete a structured programme at an RTA-approved driving school.

### Approved driving schools in Dubai

| School | Known for |
| --- | --- |
| Emirates Driving Institute (EDI) | Largest; multiple locations |
| Dubai Driving Centre (DDC) | Al Barsha and Umm Suqeim |
| Galadari Motor Driving Centre | Established; flexible scheduling |
| Belhasa Driving Centre | Oud Metha and other locations |

### Full programme stages

**Theory phase:**

- Traffic rules and signs (usually 8–16 hours of classes)
- Hazard perception tests
- Theory test at RTA, must pass before practical begins

**Practical phase:**

- Yard training (slow manoeuvres, parking exercises)
- On-road driving with instructor
- Minimum lesson hours vary by school, typically 20–40 hours

**RTA tests:**

1. Yard test (parking, manoeuvres)
2. On-road test (evaluated by RTA examiner, not school instructor)

| Stage cost breakdown | Indicative AED |
| --- | --- |
| File opening and registration | 1,200–2,000 |
| Theory classes | 600–1,200 |
| Practical lessons (20–40 hrs) | 2,500–5,500 |
| RTA theory test | 200–300 |
| RTA yard test | 200–300 |
| RTA road test | 200–300 |
| Licence issuance | 200–400 |
| **Total** | **3,500–8,000** |

Retests cost AED 200–500 each plus a mandatory minimum wait. Budget realistically, first-attempt pass rates are lower than many candidates expect.

## Automatic vs manual transmission choice

You may choose to test on automatic only. RTA stamps the restriction on the physical card.

| Choice | Trade-off |
| --- | --- |
| Full licence (manual) | Drives any vehicle; harder test |
| Automatic restriction | Cannot drive manual legally in UAE |

For most expat lifestyles in Dubai, SUVs, sedans, automatics, the automatic restriction has zero practical impact. The restriction only matters for manual hire cars on overseas trips.

## Common rejection reasons: and how to fix them

| Rejection | Root cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Country not on approved list | Nationality not eligible | Enrol at driving school |
| Foreign licence expired | Licence not valid at submission | Renew via home embassy or postal application |
| Emirates ID not yet active | Visa stamp not yet in system | Wait 24–72 hours; retry |
| Eye test too old | Certificate over 30 days | Repeat eye test, same day result |
| Missing NOC | Employer-sponsored visa requires HR letter | Get NOC from HR department |
| Licence translation missing | Non-English/Arabic script | Approved translation centre in Dubai, same-day available |
| Traffic file block | Outstanding fines or ban | Clear via Dubai Police app then return |
| Automatic vs manual mismatch | Home licence category restriction | Accept automatic-only, or retest |
| Photo non-compliant | Wrong size or coloured background | Retake at digital photo shop near centre |

## Translation of foreign licences

If your licence is in a script other than Latin or Arabic (Chinese, Russian, Thai, Korean, etc.), RTA requires an official translation.

| Language | Where to translate |
| --- | --- |
| Russian, CIS languages | Certified legal translation offices near courts |
| Chinese, Japanese, Korean | Certified translators approved by Ministry of Justice |
| Thai, Tagalog, Hindi | Same, Ministry-approved translation offices |

Cost: AED 100–300 per licence. Same-day service at major legal translation offices in Business Bay and Deira.

Bring both the original and translation to the RTA appointment, counter staff keep a copy of both.

## Tracking your application

RTA Smart app shows application status in real time. At submission, you receive a reference number. Status stages:

1. Received
2. Under review
3. Approved: ready for collection
4. Collected / Posted

If status stalls at "under review" for more than 5 working days, call 8009090 with your reference number.

## Transferring a licence from another UAE emirate

If you already hold a UAE licence issued by Muroor (Abu Dhabi) or another traffic authority and you are transferring your visa to Dubai:

- Update your traffic file emirate association via RTA
- Insurance re-quote for Dubai garaging address, premiums differ
- Salik tag registration if not already active

Most expats keep the same physical licence card until renewal, the UAE licence is a federal document. The key change is updating your registered address and insurance garaging location.

For the Abu Dhabi process, see [Abu Dhabi driving licence guide](/guides/abu-dhabi-driving-license/).

## Dual-driver households: parallel conversion

Trailing spouses assume they can wait until the primary earner's licence is sorted. This delays their own conversion by 2–6 weeks and creates school run and daily mobility gaps.

**Recommended approach:**

| Week | Primary driver | Spouse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Eye test + RTA booking | Eye test on same day |
| 2 | Licence issued | RTA appointment |
| 3 | Car purchase or lease begins | Licence issued |
| 4 | Fully mobile | Added to insurance policy |

Conversion fees do not interact, each person pays independently. Book separate appointments, ideally back-to-back on the same morning.

## FAQ

**Can I drive in Dubai while waiting for my UAE licence?**
During the official grace period after Emirates ID issuance, some insurers cover foreign licence driving. Once grace expires, stop driving until UAE licence is in hand, insurance void risk.

**Does my home country need an agreement with UAE?**
RTA's list is based on bilateral assessments, not formal treaties. Some countries with no formal agreement still appear on the list due to driving standard equivalency.

**Can I get an RTA licence if I am on a tourist visa?**
No, UAE driving licence requires valid UAE residency (Emirates ID). Tourists use home licence plus IDP.

**What if my licence was issued in one country and I am a citizen of another?**
RTA checks the country that issued the licence, not nationality. A UK licence held by an Indian national is assessed as a UK licence.

**Are there extra fees for over-50 applicants?**
No standard age surcharge from RTA. Insurance may rate age separately.

**Can I use the Dubai licence for GCC travel?**
UAE licences are recognised across GCC for general travel. Check insurance extension terms, some policies cover GCC, others UAE only.

**How do I find an RTA-approved optical centre?**
Search "eye test" on the RTA Smart app or rta.ae approved service providers list. Most major optical chains in malls are approved.

**What happens to my foreign licence after conversion?**
RTA typically returns it or retains it depending on nationality and conversion type. Ask at the counter, some centres stamp "cancelled" and return.

**Is the 10-year validity licence worth the extra fee?**
Yes for most stays over 3 years. Even though the card is renewed at visa renewal, the traffic file history and category remain. The longer fee period is a one-time saving on repeat renewals.

**Can I do the RTA process completely online?**
Partial online submission possible for eligible nationalities. Eye test and document verification require an in-person visit at some stage. Check rta.ae for the current online eligibility.
