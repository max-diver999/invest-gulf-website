  - question: "How much does Ejari cost for tenants in Dubai?"
    answer: "Ejari registration costs approximately AED 220 per contract. If processed through typing centres, service fees add AED 50–150. Most lease agreements specify that landlords pay Ejari fees, but this is negotiable at signing. Renewal registration carries similar costs annually."
  - question: "How long does Ejari registration take for tenants?"
    answer: "Through the Dubai REST app: same day if documents are complete. Via typing centres: typically same day or next working day. Processing delays usually occur due to incomplete documents, incorrect property details, or title deed mismatches rather than system issues."
  - question: "Can tenants register Ejari themselves without landlord approval?"
    answer: "Yes, either party can initiate Ejari registration. However, you need the landlord's Emirates ID copy, title deed details, and signed lease contract.   - question: "What happens if my landlord refuses to register Ejari?"
    answer: "Ejari registration is legally mandatory for all residential leases over 6 months. If a landlord refuses, you can register yourself with proper documentation, file a complaint with RERA, or use non-registration as grounds for lease termination. Unregistered leases also weaken landlord rights in disputes."
  - question: "Do I need Ejari for DEWA account setup in Dubai?"
    answer: "Yes, DEWA requires a registered Ejari contract to transfer utilities to tenant name. Without Ejari, utilities remain in landlord name, creating billing complications and potential service interruptions. The Ejari certificate number is mandatory on DEWA transfer applications."
  - question: "How does Ejari affect family visa applications?"
    answer: "UAE family visa sponsorship requires proof of suitable accommodation through registered Ejari contract. Immigration authorities verify housing meets minimum standards for family size. Unregistered leases or hotel bookings typically do not qualify for family visa processing."
  - question: "Can I update Ejari if lease terms change during tenancy?"

Amendments cost approximately AED 220 and require both parties' agreement. However, mid-term rent increases outside renewal are restricted by RERA regulations regardless of Ejari amendments."

### Method 1: Dubai REST app (recommended for 2026)

1. Download Dubai REST app from official app stores
2. Create account using UAE Pass (required for residents)
3. Select "Ejari Registration" from main menu
4. Enter property details exactly matching title deed:
   - Community name and building
   - Unit number and property type
   - Built-up area from title deed
5. Add tenant information:
   - Emirates ID or passport number
   - Contact details and email
   - Visa type and residence status
6. Input lease details:
   - Contract start and end dates
   - Annual rent amount in AED
   - Payment schedule (cheques/bank transfer)
   - Security deposit amount
7. Upload required documents:
   - Signed tenancy contract (PDF)
   - Landlord ID copy
   - Your ID and visa copy
8. Pay registration fee (~AED 220 via card/digital wallet)
9. Receive Ejari certificate with unique contract number

Processing time: Same day if all documents are correct and complete.
