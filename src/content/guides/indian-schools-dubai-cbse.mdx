## Why CBSE is Dubai's second-largest curriculum


Indians are Dubai's largest expat nationality. The community spans recent corporate transferees and families whose Dubai roots go back two or three generations. CBSE travels with this community: children often have grandparents who studied CBSE, and parents who value continuity between the Indian schooling system and potential relocation back to India for university.

CBSE results feed directly into JEE (engineering), NEET (medicine), and central university admissions in India, high-stakes exams that shape family decisions about curriculum far more than local property or employment preferences. A family planning to return to India for their child's undergraduate education will almost always choose CBSE over British or IB.

The cost factor matters too. At AED 8,000 to 25,000 annually, CBSE schools serve families across a much wider income band than British curriculum institutions. A single-earner household on AED 18,000 to 25,000 per month can realistically manage two children's CBSE fees without restructuring their entire budget.

## How KHDA regulates CBSE schools


| KHDA Rating | Max fee increase (2025 to 26 cycle) |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding | up to 5% |
| Very Good | up to 4% |
| Good | up to 2.5% |
| Acceptable | fee freeze |

For families planning long-term in Dubai, an Outstanding-rated CBSE school compounds its cost advantage: starting fees lower than British, plus controlled annual increase bands. A child enrolled at nursery through Class 12 over thirteen years will face a substantially lower cumulative spend than equivalent British schooling.
## Which Dubai areas host the strongest CBSE campuses?


### Oud Metha

Indian High School is the anchor institution of CBSE education in Dubai. With over 17,000 students across its Oud Metha campus and satellite sites, it is one of the largest single-campus schools in the world by enrolment. KHDA has rated it Outstanding in consecutive inspections. Fees range from approximately AED 9,500 at KG level to AED 22,000 for Classes 11 to 12 (science stream). The waiting list for Classes 1 to 8 means families should apply a full academic year ahead of intended entry.

Our Own English High School   Oud Metha shares the same neighbourhood and has a long-standing Very Good to Outstanding KHDA history. The campus focuses on Classes 1 to 12 and is a common first choice when Indian High School is full. Science stream places in Classes 11 to 12 are competitive, commerce stream typically has more availability.

### Garhoud

New Indian Model School occupies a central spot in Garhoud, one of Dubai's established middle-income residential districts close to Dubai International Airport. It holds Very Good KHDA status and serves Classes KG through 12, with fees running AED 8,200 to 18,500 depending on year. Its location makes it practical for families living in Garhoud, Deira, and Al Twar without lengthy school runs.

Ambassador School (Garhoud) has served the community since 1978 and holds a Good KHDA rating. It is one of the more affordable CBSE options in the city, with fees below the district average. Families who want a smaller campus culture and do not require Outstanding-rated placement often shortlist it alongside New Indian Model School.

### Muhaisnah and Al Warqa

Our Own English High School   Al Warqa is the newer campus of the Our Own group, built to accommodate demand from families relocating to newer districts on the Dubai to Sharjah border. KHDA rating: Very Good. Fees broadly match the Oud Metha campus. Muhaisnah and Al Warqa have become natural residential choices for Indian families priced out of Oud Metha and Garhoud, and this campus reflects that demographic shift.

The Indian Academy (Muhaisnah) is a Good-rated CBSE school that attracts families in the Al Nahda to Muhaisnah corridor. Lower fee band, AED 8,000 to 14,000, makes it accessible to a wider income range than the established flagship campuses.

### Al Quoz and Umm Suqeim

Knowledge Point School (Al Quoz) and several smaller CBSE institutions serve the working-class and mid-income Indian population settled in the internal areas of Dubai. These schools typically carry Good ratings. Fees sit at the lower end of the range, AED 7,500 to 13,000, reflecting the cost profile of the surrounding residential neighbourhoods.

### Bur Dubai and Karama

The Bur Dubai to Karama corridor has historically been the densest Indian residential pocket in Dubai. Several CBSE schools serve this area at Good-rated tiers, including Springdales School and The Indian School. Families in furnished apartment clusters near Karama market often find these campuses within walking distance, which eliminates bus costs entirely.
## What do CBSE fee tiers look like in 2026?


These bands hold across primary and secondary with the higher end of each tier applying to Classes 9 to 12. Science stream (PCM or PCB) at Class 11 to 12 often carries a premium of AED 1,000 to 3,000 above the listed fee because of lab resources and smaller section sizes.

Additional costs to model:
- Registration / seat deposit: AED 1,500 to 4,000 (non-refundable, paid once per enrolment)
- School bus: AED 4,500 to 9,000 per year depending on route distance
- Uniform and books: AED 1,200 to 2,500 per year
- CBSE Board exam fees (Classes 10 and 12): AED 1,500 to 2,500

A family with two children at a mid-range CBSE school, including bus and extras, should budget roughly AED 35,000 to 45,000 per year, compared with [AED 90,000 to 130,000 for two children at a British mid-tier school](/guides/school-fees-vs-property-budget-dubai/).

## How do you match rent to CBSE school catchments?


Oud Metha / Bur Dubai: Proximity to Indian High School. Apartments are older stock but practical. Rents AED 45,000 to 70,000 for 1 to 2BR. Walk-to-school or short bus route. Healthcare City is nearby. Strong case if Indian High School is the top priority.

Garhoud: Covers New Indian Model School and Ambassador. Airport-adjacent means some families also find commuting logistics efficient. Rent slightly lower than Oud Metha. 2BR apartments typically AED 60,000 to 80,000 annually.

**Muhaisnah / Al Nahda:** Cheaper residential option with direct access to Our Own Al Warqa and Indian Academy campuses. Shared commuter corridor with Sharjah means families combining a Dubai school with Sharjah rent can make this area work, though the Sharjah to Dubai morning peak deserves analysis; see [Sharjah schools and commute guide](/guides/dubai-property-investment-guide/) for context.

**Al Quoz:** Industrial-adjacent but increasingly liveable. Lower rents, accessible CBSE options, good for families where one or both parents work in Trade Centre or Media City. School-run is typically short.

For families weighing total [Dubai cost of living](/guides/dubai-cost-of-living-guide/), rent, school fees, transport, the CBSE belt (Oud Metha, Garhoud, Muhaisnah, Bur Dubai) consistently returns lower aggregate family expenditure than the villa communities associated with British curriculum schools. A family saving AED 60,000 per year on school fees can upgrade residential quality or reduce financial stress substantially.

## CBSE versus British curriculum: a practical comparison


Many dual-income Indian families in Dubai who are UAE-permanent choose British curriculum precisely because they do not plan to return to India and want a globally mobile qualification. Others, particularly those on a 3 to 5 year corporate secondment, keep CBSE for continuity and cost. Neither is wrong; the error is choosing without modelling the destination.
## What is the CBSE admission process in Dubai?

Most CBSE schools run online registration from October to January for the following September. Expect an assessment or interview for Classes 1 and above, seat deposit on offer, then final enrolment once Emirates ID and visa copies are ready. Outstanding campuses may close lists by February even for the next academic year.

## How do you plan a Dubai move around CBSE schooling?

Sequence the move in three passes: shortlist schools and pay registration fees before you fly; sign a lease in the catchment that matches your accepted campus; then complete visa, Emirates ID, and bus route selection in the first two weeks on the ground. See [Emirates ID steps](/guides/emirates-id-application-guide/) and [monthly family budget](/guides/dubai-cost-of-living-guide/) alongside school fees.

## Three CBSE schooling scenarios for relocating families

**Scenario A   Dual-income family targeting Indian High School:** Register in Oud Metha or Garhoud rent band (AED 45,000 to 80,000 for a 2BR), pay registration at two CBSE campuses, and keep British curriculum as backup if the waitlist slips past your employment start date.

**Scenario B   CBSE campus waitlists and term fees mid-secondary transfer:** Curriculum continuity matters more than saving 10% on fees when moving under CBSE campus waitlists and term fees. Switching exam boards at Year 10 is costly for Indian Schools Dubai Cbse families.

**Scenario C   CBSE campus waitlists and term fees fee-sensitive plan:** Model bus, uniform, exam, and extracurricular levies for indian schools cbse beyond headline tuition in Indian Schools Dubai Cbse. CBSE and mid-tier British options reduce cost but narrow destinations for CBSE campus waitlists and term fees.
## What risks should CBSE families plan for?

Waiting lists at Outstanding CBSE schools can push you into a longer commute or a temporary British placement. Fee increases follow KHDA caps but still compound over a full school career. Mid-year moves need transfer certificates aligned to CBSE term dates to avoid repeating coursework.

## Sharjah border schools and Golden Visa notes


Are there CBSE schools outside Dubai worth considering for families living near the border?
Several strong CBSE schools operate in Sharjah (under SPEA regulation), including Delhi Private School, which has a strong academic track record and fees broadly comparable to Dubai CBSE. The [Sharjah schools and Dubai commute guide](/guides/dubai-property-investment-guide/) covers the trade-offs for border-zone families.

**Does CBSE schooling affect Golden Visa eligibility?**
Not directly, visa eligibility depends on property ownership, employment category, or academic achievement thresholds. However, a child who completes Class 12 in Dubai with high board marks (90%+) may qualify independently for a student Golden Visa. Confirm current thresholds with your visa consultant as UAE ICA rules are updated periodically.
