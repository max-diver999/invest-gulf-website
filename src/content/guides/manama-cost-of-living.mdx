# Manama Cost of Living 2026: Rent, Schools, Groceries & Monthly Budgets

**TL;DR:** A normal family of four spends **BHD 1,400–2,100/month** — before Amwaj waterfront or IB diploma years. Singles in Juffair or Riffa can run **BHD 650–950** without a second car. Schools break budgets: two kids on mid-tier British curricula hit **BHD 800–1,200/month** once you divide annual fees by twelve and add buses. KB §20: **smallest GCC**, **Manama hub**, **lower COL than Dubai** — model your numbers before you sign a lease.

## Currency and tax context

- **BHD** is **USD-pegged** (~**0.376** BHD/USD). Rent and schools stay fixed in dinars; euro savings do not.
- **No Bahrain personal income tax** on typical salary — home-country tax rules still apply.

## Transport

- **Petrol** subsidised vs Europe — budget BHD 40–70/month single commute.
- **New compact car** purchase BHD 4,500–7,500 used; insurance BHD 120–250/year.
- **Uber / Careem** reliable; island small — still most families run two cars.

**King Fahd Causeway:** Saudi weekend traffic — fuel + time cost if you commute to Khobar ** (confirm current official rules)**.

## Utilities — EWA

Electricity + water single bill. Summer AC spikes:

- Apartment 1BR: BHD 25–45 winter / BHD 45–70 summer
- Villa: BHD 70–150+ summer

No separate Dubai-style district cooling line item in most villas — simpler but villas still expensive to cool.

## FX planning for EUR and USD earners (BHD pegged to USD)

Salary in BHD is stable in USD terms (peg ~0.376 BHD/USD). If your wealth or mortgage sits in EUR, purchasing power swings even when rent is fixed in BHD.

| Home currency | Planning note | Impact on Manama lifestyle |
|---|---|---|
| **EUR** | EUR/USD volatility | Euro savings buy fewer BHD remittances when USD strong |
| **GBP** | moderate swing | British school fees feel different when GBP weak |
| **USD** | peg-aligned | simplest mental model for COL |
| **INR** | remittance corridor active | compare exchange house spread monthly |

**Practical rules for June 2026 planning:**

1. Quote salary in **BHD** first, then convert at a **stress rate**.
2. **School fees** rise in BHD — not linked to home inflation.
3. **Rent** negotiated in BHD; FX depreciation does not soften renewals.
4. Remittance fees (0.5–2% + spread) belong in budget if sending home.

## Municipality, EWA and tenancy — lifestyle friction points

Bahrain tenancy is **not** Dubai RERA portal familiarity for most expats.

| Step | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Municipality lease registration** | Links housing to visa and official address |
| **EWA account** | Tenant or landlord name — clarify contract |
| **Security deposit** | Often one month — document condition photos |
| **Maintenance** | Tower vs villa — who fixes AC compressor |
| **Notice period** | 1–3 months typical — negotiate before signing |

Budget EWA separately — summer AC surprises first-time Gulf renters from Europe.

## Seasonal and Ramadan budget adjustments

| Period | COL impact | Planning tip |
|---|---|---|
| **Summer (Jun–Sep)** | EWA +15–35% | Ask landlord for chiller-inclusive towers |
| **Ramadan** | Dining shifts home | Grocery basket +10% if catering iftar |
| **National Day / F1 week** | Short-term rent spike | Avoid signing new lease that week |
| **August school start** | Deposit + bus fees cluster | Keep BHD 2,000 cash buffer |
| **Year-end finance bonuses** | Rent renewals firmer | Negotiate in October not December |

Expat packages that quote "all-in BHD 2,000" rarely include **summer EWA delta** or **school transport** — add both explicitly.
