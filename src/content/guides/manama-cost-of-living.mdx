## FAQ

**Is Manama cheaper than Dubai?**  
Yes at mid-tier, rent and schools. Amwaj premium closes the gap.

**Biggest savings lever?**  
Housing location plus school tier, not skipping insurance.

**How much salary to live comfortably?**  
Single: BHD 1,000+ net. Family of four: BHD 2,000+ with mid schools.

**Are cheques mandatory?**  
Most landlords want post-dated cheques; some accept quarterly transfer at premium.

**EWA average bill?**  
1BR BHD 35–55 summer; villa BHD 90–150 with AC running.

**Can I negotiate rent?**  
Yes in Riffa and older Juffair stock; Amwaj premium less flexible.

**Hidden costs?**  
Agency fee, deposit, school transport, insurance top-up, municipality registration.
