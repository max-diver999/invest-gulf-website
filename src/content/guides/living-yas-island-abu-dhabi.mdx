## FAQ

**Is Yas Island a good place to live?**
Yes for families with young children and entertainment-oriented professionals. Commute to Abu Dhabi city is manageable. Dubai commute is longer.

**How far is Yas from Abu Dhabi city?**
25–30 km, 25–35 minutes by car. Abu Dhabi Airport is just 15 km away.

**What are rental prices?**
1BR from AED 75,000–100,000/year; 2BR from AED 100,000–140,000; villas from AED 160,000+.

**What schools are on Yas Island?**
GEMS World Academy Abu Dhabi (British/IB) and Yas School on the island. Bloom Education and ADNOC Schools nearby.

**Can foreigners buy property on Yas?**
Yes, designated investment zone. Aldar Projects are freehold for non-UAE nationals. AED 2M+ may qualify for Golden Visa.

**Is Yas cheaper than Saadiyat?**
Generally 15–25% cheaper at equivalent unit type. Saadiyat commands beach and cultural premium.

**What healthcare is on the island?**
Healthpoint Hospital (accident and emergency) and LLH clinic are on-island. Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi is 30 minutes away.

**Is Yas Island noisier than Saadiyat?**
F1 race weekend (November) generates significant noise. Event days at theme parks add traffic. Normal weekdays are quiet.
