  - question: "How far is Yas Island from Abu Dhabi city centre?"
    answer: "Approximately 25–30 km from central Abu Dhabi, typically 25–35 minutes by car on the main highway. Rush-hour traffic toward Abu Dhabi city can extend to 40–50 minutes in school-morning peak. Distance to Dubai is approximately 90–100 km, with a 60–75 minute drive off-peak via E10/E11."
  - question: "What are the rental prices on Yas Island?"
    answer: "Studio apartments on Yas Island range from AED 55,000–75,000 per year; 1BR from AED 75,000–100,000; 2BR from AED 100,000–140,000; and villas (3–4BR) from AED 160,000–280,000+. Prices vary significantly by project quality, view, and proximity to Yas Marina. The market has tightened as the island's popularity increased with families."
  - question: "What international schools are near Yas Island?"
    answer: "The main international schools within or near Yas Island are GEMS World Academy Abu Dhabi (within Yas), Yas School, and the nearby Bloom Education schools. For secondary years, ADNOC Schools and British School Al Khubairat (further into Abu Dhabi city) are also used by Yas Island families. The ADEK-approved school cluster is still developing compared to Saadiyat."
  - question: "Can foreigners buy property on Yas Island?"
    answer: "Yes. Yas Island is one of Abu Dhabi's designated investment zones where non-UAE nationals can purchase property in freehold or leasehold structures. Aldar Properties, the primary developer, has launched multiple residential phases including West Yas, Yas Acres, Mayan, and Perla. Purchase above AED 2M may qualify for UAE Golden Visa (verify current ICA rules)."
  - question: "What entertainment and lifestyle does Yas Island offer?"
    answer: "Yas Island is Abu Dhabi's purpose-built entertainment destination. Key venues: Yas Marina Circuit (F1 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix), Ferrari World Abu Dhabi (indoor theme park), Yas Waterworld, Warner Bros World Abu Dhabi, Yas Mall (largest in Abu Dhabi), Yas Marina, and multiple beach club and restaurant clusters. The island hosts major concert events, sports events, and motorsport year-round."
  - question: "Is Yas Island more affordable than Saadiyat Island?"
    answer: "Generally yes. Yas Island rents tend to run 15–25% below equivalent Saadiyat Island properties. Saadiyat commands a premium for its beach access, cultural institutions (Louvre), and address prestige. Yas Island offers entertainment and lifestyle value. Families with young children often prioritise Yas for activities; culture-oriented families lean toward Saadiyat."

## FAQ

Is Yas Island a good place to live?
Yes for families with young children and entertainment-oriented professionals. Commute to Abu Dhabi city is manageable. Dubai commute is longer.

How far is Yas from Abu Dhabi city?
25–30 km, 25–35 minutes by car. Abu Dhabi Airport is just 15 km away.

What are rental prices?
1BR from AED 75,000–100,000/year; 2BR from AED 100,000–140,000; villas from AED 160,000+.

What schools are on Yas Island?
GEMS World Academy Abu Dhabi (British/IB) and Yas School on the island. Bloom Education and ADNOC Schools nearby.

Can foreigners buy property on Yas?
Yes, designated investment zone. Aldar Projects are freehold for non-UAE nationals. AED 2M+ may qualify for Golden Visa.

Is Yas cheaper than Saadiyat?
Generally 15–25% cheaper at equivalent unit type. Saadiyat commands beach and cultural premium.

What healthcare is on the island?
Healthpoint Hospital (accident and emergency) and LLH clinic are on-island. Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi is 30 minutes away.

Is Yas Island noisier than Saadiyat?
F1 race weekend (November) generates significant noise. Event days at theme parks add traffic. Normal weekdays are quiet.
