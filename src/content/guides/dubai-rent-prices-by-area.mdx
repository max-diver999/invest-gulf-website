# Dubai Rent Prices by Area 2026: Marina, JVC, Hills and Ejari Guide

## How does Dubai rent work in 2026?

Most leases quote **annual rent** paid by post-dated cheques (1, 2, 4, or 6 cheques are common). **Ejari** registers the contract with RERA (about AED 220 for apartments, higher for villas). Renewals must follow the **RERA rental index** cap in most cases. Use the official calculator before you accept a renewal increase: [Dubai rent increase calculator RERA](/guides/dubai-rent-increase-calculator-rera/).

Upfront costs typically include security deposit (5 to 10% of annual rent), agent commission (often 5% of first year), Ejari, and DEWA connection. Model **10 to 15% of annual rent** in cash before move-in, not rent alone.

## What are typical monthly rent bands (unfurnished)?

Portal listings skew high. Treat the table as planning bands; confirm on viewing.

| Area profile | Studio AED/mo | 1BR AED/mo | 2BR AED/mo | 3BR / villa AED/mo |
|  |  |  |  |  |
| Budget (International City, Discovery) | 2,000 to 3,500 | 3,500 to 5,500 | 5,500 to 8,000 | 8,000 to 12,000 |
| Mid-market (JVC, Sports City, Mirdif) | 3,500 to 5,000 | 5,500 to 8,000 | 8,000 to 11,000 | 11,000 to 16,000 |
| Premium towers (Marina, Downtown, BB) | 5,500 to 7,500 | 8,500 to 14,000 | 12,000 to 18,000 | 18,000 to 28,000 |
| Family villas (Hills, Ranches, Palm) | N/A | N/A | 14,000 to 22,000 | 18,000 to 35,000+ |

Furnished units add **15 to 25%**. Short-term serviced stock adds **40 to 70%** over annual unfurnished and suits only the first 60 days while you hunt a long-term lease.

## What does Dubai Marina rent cost?

Marina suits finance, hospitality, and aviation professionals without school-age children. Typical unfurnished bands: **1BR AED 8,500 to 12,000/month**, **2BR AED 12,000 to 18,000/month**.

Watch-outs: [district cooling](/guides/dubai-district-cooling-charges/) can exceed AED 600/month in summer, parking is often AED 200 to 500/month extra, and older towers need chiller history checks. Schools: [Schools near Dubai Marina](/guides/schools-near-dubai-marina/).

## Is JVC good value for renters?

JVC targets young families and first-time Dubai renters. **2BR AED 7,500 to 11,000/month**, **3BR townhouse AED 11,000 to 16,000/month**. The trade-off is car dependence and school bus planning. Compare schools via [Gulf schools comparison](/guides/gulf-schools-comparison/).

## How do Dubai Hills and Arabian Ranches compare on rent?

**Dubai Hills Estate** commands a family premium: townhouses and apartments with park access, higher than JVC but below Marina on a per-bedroom basis for newer stock.

**Arabian Ranches** villa rent reflects school catchments and commute to Dubai South or Marina. Expect **3BR villa AED 15,000 to 22,000/month** depending on phase and upgrades.

Both belts favour **24-month tenancy** planning: school and Ejari friction makes renewals sticky after year two.

## What about Downtown and Business Bay?

**Downtown** 1BR often runs **AED 9,000 to 14,000/month**; **Business Bay** is similar with a slight discount on some towers. Premium floors and views add 10 to 20%. Tourist traffic noise affects select towers, visit at evening peak before you sign.

## Where are budget rent corridors?

International City, Discovery Gardens, Dubai Silicon Oasis, and Motor City offer lower tickets with longer commutes. Investors cross-read [Dubai Silicon Oasis](/areas/dubai-silicon-oasis-property-investment/) and [Motor City](/areas/motor-city-property-investment/) if rent savings tie to a buy decision.

## How much does Ejari cost and why does it matter?

Ejari is mandatory for legal tenancy, DEWA, schools, and many visa processes. Budget **AED 220+ for apartments** and **AED 1,000+ for villas**. Without Ejari, you lack RERA contract protection in disputes. Tenant rights overview: [Dubai tenant eviction rules RERA](/guides/dubai-tenant-eviction-rules-rera/) (not legal advice).

## What hidden costs sit beside headline rent?

| Cost | Typical range | Notes |
|  |  |  |
| DEWA | AED 200 to 800+/mo | Higher in villas |
| District cooling | AED 150 to 900+/mo | Marina/JLT towers |
| Parking | AED 0 to 500/mo | Often extra in Marina |
| Internet | AED 300 to 450/mo | Provider promos vary |
| Agent fee | 5% of year 1 | Negotiable on renewals |

Family relocations should model total monthly spend (rent, schools, transport, insurance) using the [Dubai cost of living guide](/guides/dubai-cost-of-living-guide/), not headline rent alone.

## How do renewals differ from new leases?

On renewal, RERA index caps often limit increases. On a **new lease** in the same building, market rates apply. Portal listings overstate achieved rent by **5 to 12%** in Marina and Downtown: pull three comparable listings in the same tower and ask agents for recent closed deals.

**Cheque tip:** offering two cheques instead of four sometimes unlocks **AED 5,000 to 10,000** annual discount on villa leases.

## What should you verify before signing?

| Chiller / DEWA history | Summer bill shock |

| Parking allocation | Often not included |
| Renewal cap reference | RERA index link in contract |
