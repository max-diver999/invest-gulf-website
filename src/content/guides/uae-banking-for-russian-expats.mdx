  - question: "What is source-of-funds documentation and why does it matter for Russian expats?"
    answer: "Source of funds (SOF) documentation is evidence of where your money came from: employment payslips, business ownership certificates, property sale contracts, inheritance documents, dividend confirmations, or pre-2022 transaction records. UAE banks applying enhanced due diligence (EDD) on Russian-passport accounts require a clear, auditable paper trail, sometimes going back 3-5 years, before approving accounts or large transfers."
  - question: "How do Russian expats transfer money to UAE in 2026?"
    answer: "The main corridors are: SWIFT via third-country banks (Armenia, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Turkey, Serbia) that maintain Russian correspondents; UAE exchange houses accepting non-SWIFT cash or third-country wires; and, in some cases, direct SWIFT from Russian banks not under blocking sanctions. Each route has documentation requirements. Confirm live corridor availability with your bank or exchange house before each major transfer."
  - question: "Does the UAE follow EU or US sanctions against Russia?"
    answer: "No. The UAE has not adopted EU or UK sanctions on Russia and is not a signatory to US executive orders on Russia. However, UAE banks that maintain USD clearing accounts (correspondent banking) apply OFAC compliance internally. This means accounts and transfers involving OFAC-designated individuals are blocked even in UAE. Most ordinary Russian nationals are not on OFAC lists, the risk is bank-internal risk appetite, not a legal UAE prohibition."
  - question: "Can I use a UAE account to send money back to Russia?"
    answer: "Yes, with caveats. UAE does not prohibit outbound transfers to Russia. Practical limits come from correspondent banks along the chain, many global correspondents will not route RUB or USD/EUR to Russian banks under sanctions. Workarounds include transfers to non-sanctioned Russian banks (Gazprombank remains SWIFT-connected in specific corridors as of mid-2026, verify current status), UAE exchange houses with RUB windows, and third-country routing via Armenia or Kazakhstan. Confirm live status before initiating."
  - question: "Does having a UAE bank account help with the Golden Visa?"
    answer: "Yes. For property-based Golden Visa (AED 2 million threshold), a UAE bank account is required for DLD payment, NOC deposits, and subsequent GDRFA/ICP application. For investor visa via company, a UAE corporate account is typically mandatory. Opening your account early, ideally before SPA signing, prevents delays at the critical payment stage."
  - question: "Are digital-only UAE banks an option for Russian expats?"
    answer: "Digital-first options like Mashreq Neo, YAP, and Liv by Emirates NBD exist but are generally tied to residence visa and Emirates ID, the same prerequisites as branch accounts. They offer faster onboarding UI but the same KYC gatekeeping. For Russian nationals with complex SOF, a face-to-face branch conversation and relationship manager pathway tends to produce better outcomes than app-only routes."

## What does the regulatory picture mean for Russian passport holders?

The UAE regulatory picture means no local copy of EU or UK Russia sanctions, yet banks still screen OFAC lists and apply enhanced due diligence to Russian-passport files because of USD correspondent risk. Employed expats with clean income typically open within 1 to 3 weeks. Our research treats bank choice as secondary to SOF quality.

Insider tip: Deliver one indexed PDF with a cover sheet linking every large balance to a contract, sale, or salary trail before the first branch visit.

Source-of-funds files for Russian-passport EDD typically require 6 to 12 months of overseas statements, employment contracts, and a signed narrative, sometimes covering 3 to 5 years of wealth origin before USD 100,000-plus transfers clear. Gaps in history drive most rejections. Invest Gulf prefers one indexed PDF over loose papers.
