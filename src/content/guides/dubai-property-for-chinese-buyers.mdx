## Legal access: can Chinese nationals buy?

**Yes.** Dubai restricts **where** foreigners buy (freehold zones), not **which passport**. Process:

1. Unit in **designated freehold zone**
2. MOU / SPA with seller or developer
3. Documented payment to **escrow or trustee**
4. **Oqood** (off-plan) or **Title Deed** (ready)
5. Optional **Golden Visa** after qualifying registration

Verify zone on **Dubai REST** — never trust brochure maps alone.

## Funding: mainland vs overseas Chinese paths

### Overseas Chinese / HK / Singapore banking

Often the **smoothest path**:

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Account | HK/SG/UAE account with SWIFT capability |
| Transfer | Wire to UAE escrow/trustee in AED or USD |
| Documentation | SPA, passport, source-of-funds |
| Timing | Match off-plan milestones or ready settlement date |

### Mainland China considerations

SAFE regulates **outbound capital**. Property purchases abroad require **compliant FX channels** — not grey market transfers.

| Approach | Note |
|---|---|
| **Legal annual quota** | Individual FX limits apply (confirm current official rules) |
| **Corporate outbound** | If purchasing via company — separate compliance |
| **Existing offshore holdings** | Often used for international property |
| **Mortgage in UAE** | Reduces outbound lump sum |

**Never:** undocumented third-party transfers — UAE banks and trustees will freeze or reject unclear funds.

Consult **PRC-licensed advisers** on SAFE before large outbound movement.

1. **Clarify funding path** — mainland SAFE vs offshore (with advisers)
2. **Open UAE bank** if possible
3. **Shortlist** freehold communities — yield vs visa vs schools

8. **DLD registration** — Oqood or Title Deed

11. **Management** — agency if China-based landlord

## Tax and CRS: PRC obligations for Chinese buyers

| Layer | Consideration |
|---|---|
| **UAE** | 0% personal income tax on local rent |
| **PRC tax residency** | Worldwide income reporting possible under PRC Individual Income Tax Law |
| **CRS** | UAE banks report account data to tax authorities per OECD Common Reporting Standard |
| **CBCR** | Corporate structures holding property trigger additional reporting |

**Practical example:** A PRC tax resident owns a Downtown apartment generating AED 65,000 annual rent (approximately CNY 130,000). The rent stays in a UAE bank account.

- **UAE tax:** AED 0
- **PRC tax:** Potentially reportable as overseas property income depending on residency status and domestic rules
- **CRS:** UAE bank reports account balance and interest to PRC tax authority via automatic exchange

"Dubai zero tax" ≠ "zero global obligation." Get **PRC-qualified tax advice** before assuming rental is invisible at home.

## Chinese buyer mistakes to avoid

1. **SAFE-noncompliant fund routing** — triggers bank freeze and DLD rejection
2. **Assuming UAE 0% tax means PRC 0% tax** — CRS reporting applies
3. **Buying on gross yield** — model net after service charges and management
4. **No Ejari registration** — cannot enforce tenancy at RDC without it
5. **Skipping bilingual SPA legal review** — translation errors in penalty clauses
6. **Golden Visa purchase without registered value proof** — marketing ≠ eligibility
7. **Using unlicensed agents** — verify RERA BRN before engaging

## WeChat-era due diligence

Chinese buyers often discover listings via **WeChat groups** and China-facing portals. Validate every deal through:

- DLD **Unit Profile** independent of agent chat
- **RERA escrow** number on Dubai REST (off-plan)
- **Video walkthrough** with date stamp + tower name visible
- **Solicitor in Dubai** — not only China-based intermediary

Fraud risk is low in Tier 1 developer sales; **highest in assignment/resale** with pressure to wire before MOU.
