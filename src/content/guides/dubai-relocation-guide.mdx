# Dubai Relocation Guide 2026: Visas, Housing, Schools & First 90 Days

> Disclaimer: Visa rules, school fees, and rent bands change. June 2026 operational guide, confirm thresholds with your employer PRO, DLD, and ICP before signing contracts. Not legal or tax advice.

## Who relocates to Dubai and why?

Most arrivals fit one of a few profiles: corporate hires chasing tax-free packages, families following a spouse's contract, Golden Visa buyers registering property, founders setting up in free zones, or investors who may not relocate full time. Match your visa route to your income source before you ship household goods.

| Profile | Primary driver | Relocation priority |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate hire | Tax-free package, career | Fast visa, school seats, commute-proof lease |
| Family trailing spouse | Partner's contract | School shortlist before lease pin |
| Golden Visa buyer | AED 2M+ property + 10-year residency | DLD registration, then ICP application |
| Remote worker / founder | Lifestyle + timezone | Freelance permit or company setup + insurance |
| Investor (non-resident) | Yield or capital | Property first; visa optional at start |
| GCC switcher | Package upgrade from Doha/Riyadh | Compare [Gulf expat living](/guides/gulf-expat-living-comparison/) |

## What should you prepare before moving to Dubai?

Visa routes at a glance:

| Route | Typical timeline | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Employment visa | 2 to 4 weeks | Hired expats |
| Golden Visa (property) | 4 to 8 weeks post-DLD | AED 2M+ buyers |
| Golden Visa (other) | Varies | Investors, talent, specialists |
| Family sponsorship | +1 to 2 weeks after primary | Spouse and children |
| Freelance / free zone | 2 to 3 weeks | Self-employed |

Deep dive: [Golden Visa application step-by-step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/) · [Golden Visa vs residence visa](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/)

### 2. Document attestation

Prepare attested copies where required:

- Degree certificates (MOFA attestation UAE embassy chain)
- Marriage and birth certificates (family visa)
- Previous employment references
- Passport validity 6+ months
- Passport-size photos (white background, stock up)

Delays here cause 90% of "visa stuck" complaints. Start attestation in your home country before resignation.

### 3. Budget model: realistic ranges

| Household | Monthly run-rate (mid) | Upfront relocation cash |
|---|---|---|
| Single professional | AED 12,000 to 18,000 | AED 15,000 to 25,000 |
| Couple, no children | AED 18,000 to 28,000 | AED 25,000 to 40,000 |
| Family of 4 (British mid school) | AED 35,000 to 55,000 | AED 40,000 to 70,000 |
| Family + premium school | AED 55,000 to 85,000+ | AED 70,000 to 100,000+ |

Hidden lines people forget:

| Cost | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Ejari registration | AED 220 + typing |
| DEWA deposit | AED 2,000 to 4,000 (refundable) |
| District cooling deposit | AED 2,000 to 5,000 |
| Housing agency fee | 5% of annual rent + VAT |
| Security deposit | 5% of annual rent |
| Cheque printing / manager cheques | AED 500 to 1,000 |
| Salik tag + parking | AED 500+/month commuter |
| Health insurance (if not employer) | AED 5,000 to 15,000/person/year |
| School registration + deposit | AED 3,000 to 10,000/child |

## How do you choose where to live in Dubai?

Pick commute time, school radius, and budget before aesthetics. Test the drive at 7:30 AM on a school day. Marina and Downtown suit singles; Arabian Ranches and Dubai Hills suit families; JVC and Sports City suit tighter budgets with longer commutes.

## What is the first-90-days relocation sequence?

Close visa medical and biometrics first, then housing and Ejari, then DEWA and cooling, then banking and schools. Skipping Ejari breaks utilities, many bank accounts, and most KHDA admissions.

1. Receive entry permit / visit visa as instructed by PRO
2. Enter UAE (or change status if already inside)
3. Medical fitness test at approved centre
4. Emirates ID biometrics appointment
5. Sign labour contract (employment route) if not done remotely

### Phase 2: Housing and utilities (Week 1 to 3)

1. View properties: take Tuesday 7:30 AM test drive to office
2. Sign tenancy: usually **1 cheque** (negotiate 2 to 4 if cash tight)
3. Register **Ejari**: mandatory
4. Apply **DEWA** connection
5. Activate district cooling (Empower, Tabreed, etc.): read cap clause
6. Internet (Du/Etisalat): book early; lead times vary

Ejari detail: [Ejari registration guide](/guides/ejari-registration-landlord-guide/)

### Phase 3: Visa stamping and ID (Week 2 to 4)

1. PRO submits visa stamping
2. Collect stamped passport
3. Receive **Emirates ID** card (or temporary print for urgent banking)
4. Register SIM with EID

Property buyers: [Emirates ID after property purchase](/guides/emirates-id-after-property-purchase/) · [Visa stamping](/guides/dubai-residence-visa-stamping/)

### Phase 4: Banking and schools (Week 3 to 8)

1. Open salary account: see [opening bank account](/guides/opening-bank-account-dubai-property-buyers/)
2. Set up WPS payroll with employer
3. School applications with Ejari + EID copies
4. Health insurance activation (employer or self)
5. RTA driving licence conversion or new test

## How do Dubai school places work for new arrivals?

KHDA-rated schools require Ejari, Emirates ID copies, and often previous report cards. Outstanding-rated schools fill early; mid-tier schools may offer mid-year seats if you flex on location. Budget AED 45,000 to 80,000 per child at British mid tier before you sign a 12-month lease.

Waiting list strategy:

- Apply to 3 schools minimum before lease signing
- Accept mid-tier now, transfer later if Outstanding is full
- Ask about sibling priority and corporate debentures

## Should you rent or buy when relocating to Dubai?

Rent first unless you have visited twice, mapped schools, and hold 12 or more months of runway. Buying early locks you into one community before you understand heat, traffic, and fee creep. Golden Visa buyers may buy earlier but should still model service charges and vacancy.

## What health insurance do Dubai expats need?

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Network includes your preferred hospital | American Hospital, Mediclinic, NMC, etc. |
| Maternity waiting period | 6 to 12 months common |
| Dental / optical | Often excluded on basic plans |
| Co-pay and annual cap | AED 150 visit co-pay adds up |

Self-sponsored Golden Visa holders must buy compliant cover before visa issuance.

## Do you need a car and driving licence in Dubai?

Convert home licence if eligible, RTA list varies by nationality. Plan **2 to 4 weeks** for process.
## What should be done in the first 90 days after arrival?

Close visa stamping, Ejari, DEWA, cooling, internet, banking, and insurance in that order. Delaying Ejari blocks schools and many bank accounts. Keep digital copies of every receipt for visa renewals and employer HR.

## What relocation red flags should pause a lease signing?

Walk away from landlords who refuse Ejari registration, cash-only deals without contracts, or agents who will not provide the RERA broker card. For purchases, never wire before escrow confirmation.

## When does buying property make sense during relocation?

Golden Visa: property must be **AED 2 million+** and **fully paid** (not mortgaged) for standard 10-year route, verify current ICP rules.

## What should you plan if you might leave Dubai later?

Keep exit costs in mind: lease break penalties, school notice periods, DEWA clearance, and car loan settlement. Selling property adds agency fees and service charge arrears checks at DLD.

## How do common relocation scenarios differ?

Scenario A .  single corporate hire: Optimise for commute and gym or social life near the office. Budget one month in a hotel or short let while you view flats on two weekday mornings.

Scenario B .  family on 3-year package: Fix school shortlist first, then lease within 20-minute school radius. Model AED 25,000 to 40,000/month all-in (rent, schools, car, insurance) before MOU on any villa.

Scenario C .  remote worker self-sponsored: Freelance permit or mainland consultancy licence may be cheaper than employer visa if income is overseas. Verify [freelance vs employment visa](/guides/uae-freelance-permit-dubai/) before you ship household goods.
## What setup costs should you budget for June 2026?

Singles often need AED 15,000 to 25,000 upfront; families AED 40,000 to 70,000 including deposits and school registration. Add furniture shipping if you bring containers from abroad.

## How do you know you are settled by week four?

You should have stamped visa, Ejari, DEWA, bank account, and SIM on Emirates ID. Schools should be applied or wait-listed with written confirmation. If any of those is missing, treat it as the priority before lifestyle spending.
