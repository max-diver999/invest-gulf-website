## Who relocates to Dubai and why?

Most Dubai arrivals typically fit a few profiles: corporate hires on tax-free packages, families on a spouse contract, Golden Visa buyers at AED 2M+, free-zone founders, or non-resident investors. Match your visa route to income before shipping goods, because the wrong path can add 2 to 8 weeks. Invest Gulf briefs start with that visa-to-income match.

|---|---|---|
| Corporate hire | Tax-free package, career | Fast visa, school seats, commute-proof lease |
| Family trailing spouse | Partner's contract | School shortlist before lease pin |
| Golden Visa buyer | AED 2M+ property + 10-year residency | DLD registration, then ICP application |
| Remote worker / founder | Lifestyle + timezone | Freelance permit or company setup + insurance |
| Investor (non-resident) | Yield or capital | Property first; visa optional at start |

## What should you prepare before moving to Dubai?

Preparation before a Dubai move typically means locking a visa route, attesting documents, and modelling upfront cash of AED 15,000 to 25,000 for singles or AED 40,000 to 70,000 for families. Employment visas often clear in 2 to 4 weeks. Invest Gulf checklists put attestation before any container ships.

Visa routes at a glance:

| Route | Typical timeline | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Employment visa | 2 to 4 weeks | Hired expats |
| Golden Visa (property) | 4 to 8 weeks post-DLD | AED 2M+ buyers |
| Golden Visa (other) | Varies | Investors, talent, specialists |
| Family sponsorship | +1 to 2 weeks after primary | Spouse and children |
| Freelance / free zone | 2 to 3 weeks | Self-employed |

Deep dive: [Golden Visa application step-by-step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/) · [Golden Visa vs residence visa](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/)

### 2. Document attestation

Prepare attested copies where required:

- Degree certificates (MOFA attestation UAE embassy chain)
- Marriage and birth certificates (family visa)
- Previous employment references
- Passport validity 6+ months
- Passport-size photos (white background, stock up)

Delays here cause 90% of "visa stuck" complaints. Start attestation in your home country before resignation.

| Household | Monthly run-rate (mid) | Upfront relocation cash |
|---|---|---|

| Family of 4 (British mid school) | AED 35,000 to 55,000 | AED 40,000 to 70,000 |

| School registration + deposit | AED 3,000 to 10,000/child |

## What is the first-90-days relocation sequence?

The first 90 days in Dubai typically require closing visa medical and biometrics first, then housing and Ejari, then DEWA, banking, and schools. Skipping Ejari breaks utilities, bank accounts, and most KHDA admissions within the first 1 to 3 weeks. Invest Gulf arrival plans treat Ejari as the critical path item.

1. Receive entry permit / visit visa as instructed by PRO
2. Enter UAE (or change status if already inside)
3. Medical fitness test at approved centre
4. Emirates ID biometrics appointment
5. Sign labour contract (employment route) if not done remotely

### Phase 2: Housing and utilities (Week 1 to 3)

1. View properties: take Tuesday 7:30 AM test drive to office
2. Sign tenancy: usually **1 cheque** (negotiate 2 to 4 if cash tight)
3. Register **Ejari**: mandatory
4. Apply **DEWA** connection
5. Activate district cooling (Empower, Tabreed, etc.): read cap clause
6. Internet (Du/Etisalat): book early; lead times vary

Ejari detail: [Ejari registration guide](/guides/ejari-registration-landlord-guide/)

### Phase 3: Visa stamping and ID (Week 2 to 4)

1. PRO submits visa stamping
2. Collect stamped passport
3. Receive **Emirates ID** card (or temporary print for urgent banking)
4. Register SIM with EID

Property buyers: [Emirates ID after property purchase](/guides/emirates-id-after-property-purchase/) · [Visa stamping](/guides/dubai-residence-visa-stamping/)

### Phase 4: Banking and schools (Week 3 to 8)

1. Open salary account: see [opening bank account](/guides/opening-bank-account-dubai-property-buyers/)
2. Set up WPS payroll with employer
3. School applications with Ejari + EID copies
4. Health insurance activation (employer or self)
5. RTA driving licence conversion or new test

## How do Dubai school places work for new arrivals?

Dubai school places for new arrivals typically require Ejari, Emirates ID copies, and previous report cards under KHDA rules. Outstanding schools fill early, so apply 6 to 12 months ahead, while mid-tier British seats may open mid-year. Budget AED 45,000 to 80,000 per child before a 12-month lease. Invest Gulf plans shortlist three schools first.

Waiting list strategy:

- Apply to 3 schools minimum before lease signing
- Accept mid-tier now, transfer later if Outstanding is full
- Ask about sibling priority and corporate debentures

## Should you rent or buy when relocating to Dubai?

Renting first typically means visiting twice, mapping schools, and holding 12 or more months of runway before you buy. Buying early locks you into one community before you understand heat and fee creep, while Golden Visa buyers at AED 2 million may purchase sooner. We usually recommend a lease in year one unless residency is locked.

Rent-first checklist:
- Visit at least twice across seasons
- Map school bus routes before the 12-month lease
- Model service charges if buying for Golden Visa at AED 2 million+

## What health insurance do Dubai expats need?

Dubai expat health insurance typically requires employer or self-sponsored cover before visa issuance, with visit co-pays near AED 150 and maternity waits of 6 to 12 months on many plans. Self-sponsored Golden Visa holders must buy compliant cover before ICP will proceed. We pack treat hospital networks as a day-one checklist item.

| Maternity waiting period | 6 to 12 months common |
| Dental / optical | Often excluded on basic plans |

## Do you need a car and driving licence in Dubai?

A car in Dubai typically means converting a home licence where the RTA list allows, or booking a new test, with 2 to 4 weeks for the process. Villa communities usually make car ownership essential, while Marina can lean on Metro. Invest Gulf family budgets often put AED 2,000 to 3,500 per month against car costs.

Car vs transit checklist:
- Villa or Ranches lease: plan a car in week one
- Marina or Business Bay: test Metro before buying
- Licence conversion: confirm nationality eligibility early

## What should be done in the first 90 days after arrival?

The first 90 days after arrival typically require visa stamping, Ejari, DEWA, cooling, internet, banking, and insurance in that order. Delaying Ejari blocks schools and many bank accounts within weeks, so keep digital copies of every receipt for renewals. Invest Gulf arrival audits treat missing Ejari as a stop item before lifestyle spending.

Arrival order checklist:
1. Visa stamp and Emirates ID biometrics
2. Ejari, then DEWA and cooling
3. Bank account and school applications

## What relocation red flags should pause a lease signing?

Lease red flags in Dubai typically include landlords who refuse Ejari, cash-only deals without contracts, or agents who will not show a RERA broker card. For purchases, never wire before escrow confirmation, because reclaiming funds after a bad transfer can take 2 to 6 months. Invest Gulf walks clients away from any tenancy that cannot register Ejari in week one.

Red-flag stop list:
- No Ejari path in writing within 7 days of signing
- Cash-only rent with no tenancy contract
- Agent without a visible RERA card
- Purchase wires requested before trustee escrow is open

## When does buying property make sense during relocation?

Buying during relocation typically makes sense for Golden Visa goals when property is AED 2 million or more and fully paid on the standard 10-year route, subject to current ICP rules. Casual buyers without a 3-year horizon usually rent first to test commute and schools. Invest Gulf purchase timing still models service charges before SPA.

Buy-now checklist:
- AED 2 million+ fully paid deed for standard Golden Visa route
- 3-year or longer stay horizon
- Service charges and vacancy modelled in writing

## What should you plan if you might leave Dubai later?

Exit planning for Dubai typically means lease break penalties, school notice periods, DEWA clearance, and car loan settlement before you resign. Selling property adds agency fees near 2% and service charge arrears checks at DLD, which can delay transfer by 2 to 6 weeks. Invest Gulf exit briefs list those line items beside tax residency plans.

Exit cost checklist:
- Lease break and school notice windows
- DEWA clearance and car loan settlement
- Agency near 2% plus DLD arrears checks on sale

## What setup costs should you budget for June 2026?

Setup costs for June 2026 typically mean AED 15,000 to 25,000 upfront for singles and AED 40,000 to 70,000 for families, including deposits and school registration. Add furniture shipping if containers come from abroad, plus DEWA and cooling deposits of several thousand dirhams. Invest Gulf budget sheets also reserve a 5% rent cheque buffer.

Upfront cost checklist:
- Singles: AED 15,000 to 25,000 cash floor
- Families: AED 40,000 to 70,000 including school deposits
- Buffer: 5% rent cheque plus DEWA and cooling deposits

Insider tip: Keep one manager cheque unallocated for the first Ejari or DEWA surprise fee; those two lines stall more arrivals than school interviews.

Hidden lines that break week-one plans include Ejari near AED 220, DEWA deposits of AED 2,000 to 4,000, district cooling deposits of AED 2,000 to 5,000, and housing agency fees at 5% of annual rent plus VAT. Map that cash and timeline before you resign abroad or ship a container.

Community choice in Dubai is a budget decision as much as a lifestyle one: Marina and JBR 1-beds often lease at AED 80,000 to 120,000 per year, JVC and Sports City nearer AED 55,000 to 75,000, and family villas in Arabian Ranches or Dubai Hills frequently AED 120,000 to 180,000. School fees at British mid tier commonly add AED 45,000 to 80,000 per child annually, so a family that locks a cheap villa far from the shortlisted school can erase the rent saving in car time and Salik. Our relocation plans put a 7:30 AM school-day drive test before any 12-month lease signature, and they keep Ejari, Emirates ID, and salary-account opening on the critical path in the first 30 to 60 days.
