## Who relocates to Dubai: and why

Dubai is the Gulf's default expat landing pad: 205,000+ property transactions in 2024, 68% foreign buyers, English-language contracts, and a DLD title system that actually functions. People arrive for different reasons, and the relocation playbook differs for each.

| Profile | Primary driver | Relocation priority |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate hire | Tax-free package, career | Fast visa, school seats, commute-proof lease |
| Family trailing spouse | Partner's contract | School shortlist before lease pin |
| Golden Visa buyer | AED 2M+ property + 10-year residency | DLD registration, then ICP application |
| Remote worker / founder | Lifestyle + timezone | Freelance permit or company setup + insurance |
| Investor (non-resident) | Yield or capital | Property first; visa optional at start |
| GCC switcher | Package upgrade from Doha/Riyadh | Compare [Gulf expat living](/guides/gulf-expat-living-comparison/) |

## Pre-arrival: 8–12 weeks before the move

### 1. Contract and visa pathway

Open the offer letter before you book flights. Non-negotiables:

| Line item | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Basic vs total salary | Some banks and schools reference basic for limits |
| Housing allowance | Cash vs company lease changes Ejari and budget |
| School allowance cap | Often AED 40K–60K/child, gap is out of pocket |
| Relocation lump sum | Negotiate if not included, covers first cheques |
| Notice / probation | Affects visa cancellation if role fails |
| Visa sponsor entity | Mainland vs free zone changes licensing |

Visa routes at a glance:

| Route | Typical timeline | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Employment visa | 2–4 weeks | Hired expats |
| Golden Visa (property) | 4–8 weeks post-DLD | AED 2M+ buyers |
| Golden Visa (other) | Varies | Investors, talent, specialists |
| Family sponsorship | +1–2 weeks after primary | Spouse and children |
| Freelance / free zone | 2–3 weeks | Self-employed |

Deep dive: [Golden Visa application step-by-step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/) · [Golden Visa vs residence visa](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/)

### 2. Document attestation

Prepare attested copies where required:

- Degree certificates (MOFA attestation UAE embassy chain)
- Marriage and birth certificates (family visa)
- Previous employment references
- Passport validity 6+ months
- Passport-size photos (white background, stock up)

Delays here cause 90% of "visa stuck" complaints. Start attestation in your home country before resignation.

### 3. Budget model: realistic ranges

| Household | Monthly run-rate (mid) | Upfront relocation cash |
|---|---|---|
| Single professional | AED 12,000–18,000 | AED 15,000–25,000 |
| Couple, no children | AED 18,000–28,000 | AED 25,000–40,000 |
| Family of 4 (British mid school) | AED 35,000–55,000 | AED 40,000–70,000 |
| Family + premium school | AED 55,000–85,000+ | AED 70,000–100,000+ |

Hidden lines people forget:

| Cost | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Ejari registration | AED 220 + typing |
| DEWA deposit | AED 2,000–4,000 (refundable) |
| District cooling deposit | AED 2,000–5,000 |
| Housing agency fee | 5% of annual rent + VAT |
| Security deposit | 5% of annual rent |
| Cheque printing / manager cheques | AED 500–1,000 |
| Salik tag + parking | AED 500+/month commuter |
| Health insurance (if not employer) | AED 5,000–15,000/person/year |
| School registration + deposit | AED 3,000–10,000/child |

## Choosing where to live: before you sign

Dubai is not one city, it is 30+ communities with different commute, school, and cooling economics.

| Community type | Examples | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Marina / beach towers | Dubai Marina, JBR, Emaar Beachfront | Singles, couples, short commutes to media/finance |
| Suburban villa belts | Arabian Ranches, Dubai Hills, Mirdif | Families wanting gardens and KHDA schools |
| Value apartments | JVC, Sports City, Discovery Gardens | Yield-minded renters, budget-conscious |
| Downtown / Business Bay | Downtown, BB towers | Walk-to-work, premium rent |
| **Outer / emerging** | Dubai South, Town Square | Lower rent, longer commute |

**Rule:** Pin **office location** and **school shortlist** on a map before viewing properties. A AED 3,000/month rent saving loses to **AED 2,000/month Salik + 90 minutes daily** in year one.

## The relocation chain: step by step

### Phase 1: Entry and medical (Week 1)

1. Receive entry permit / visit visa as instructed by PRO
2. Enter UAE (or change status if already inside)
3. Medical fitness test at approved centre
4. Emirates ID biometrics appointment
5. Sign labour contract (employment route) if not done remotely

### Phase 2: Housing and utilities (Week 1–3)

1. View properties: take **Tuesday 7:30 AM** test drive to office
2. Sign tenancy: usually **1 cheque** (negotiate 2–4 if cash tight)
3. Register **Ejari**: mandatory
4. Apply **DEWA** connection
5. Activate **district cooling** (Empower, Tabreed, etc.): read cap clause
6. Internet (Du/Etisalat): book early; lead times vary

Ejari detail: [Ejari registration guide](/guides/ejari-registration-landlord-guide/)

### Phase 3: Visa stamping and ID (Week 2–4)

1. PRO submits visa stamping
2. Collect stamped passport
3. Receive **Emirates ID** card (or temporary print for urgent banking)
4. Register SIM with EID

Property buyers: [Emirates ID after property purchase](/guides/emirates-id-after-property-purchase/) · [Visa stamping](/guides/dubai-residence-visa-stamping/)

### Phase 4: Banking and schools (Week 3–8)

1. Open salary account: see [opening bank account](/guides/opening-bank-account-dubai-property-buyers/)
2. Set up WPS payroll with employer
3. School applications with Ejari + EID copies
4. Health insurance activation (employer or self)
5. RTA driving licence conversion or new test

Families: [Moving to Dubai with family](/guides/moving-to-dubai-with-family/) · [School fees vs property budget](/guides/school-fees-vs-property-budget-dubai/)

## Schools: KHDA reality for new arrivals

Dubai has **200+ private schools** under KHDA. Quality varies by campus, not brochure.

| Curriculum | Annual fee band (mid tier) | Seat availability |
|---|---|---|
| British (NC) | AED 45,000–65,000 | Mid-tier often has seats |
| American | AED 50,000–70,000 | Moderate |
| IB | AED 60,000–95,000 | Competitive at premium |
| Indian CBSE | AED 12,000–25,000 | Usually available |
| French / German | AED 40,000–70,000 | Limited campuses |

**Waiting list strategy:**

- Apply to **3 schools minimum** before lease signing
- Accept **mid-tier now, transfer later** if Outstanding is full
- Ask about **sibling priority** and **corporate debentures**

## Rent vs buy on arrival

Most relocators should **rent 6–12 months** unless:

- Golden Visa purchase is pre-planned and funded
- Employer relocation includes home purchase benefit
- You have visited twice and validated commute + school

| Factor | Rent first | Buy on arrival |
|---|---|---|
| Flexibility | High, exit at lease end | Low, 4%+ transaction costs |
| Upfront cash | 5–10% of annual rent | 20–100% of property + 6–7% fees |
| Visa | Employment / sponsor | Golden Visa possible at AED 2M |
| Market timing | Neutral | Requires local price sense |

Full analysis: [Rent vs buy Dubai expat](/guides/rent-vs-buy-dubai-expat/) · [Cost of buying](/guides/cost-of-buying-property-dubai/)

## Healthcare and insurance

Employer-sponsored insurance is standard on employment visas. Verify:

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Network includes your preferred hospital | American Hospital, Mediclinic, NMC, etc. |
| Maternity waiting period | 6–12 months common |
| Dental / optical | Often excluded on basic plans |
| Co-pay and annual cap | AED 150 visit co-pay adds up |

Self-sponsored Golden Visa holders must buy compliant cover before visa issuance.

## Leaving Dubai: plan the exit early

Average Dubai assignment length is **3–5 years**. Know your exit before arrival:

- Lease break clauses and notice periods
- School transfer timing (term dates)
- [Selling property checklist](/guides/leaving-dubai-selling-property-checklist/)
- Gratuity and end-of-service entitlements
- Tax residency in home country on departure

## Dubai relocation scenarios: match your timeline

**Scenario A — 12–18 month contract:** Rent furnished in Marina or JLT with break clause. Skip Golden Visa property buys. Budget AED 8,000–12,000 setup (deposit, agency, DEWA, furniture).

**Scenario B — family on 3-year package:** Fix school shortlist first, then lease within 20-minute school radius. Model AED 25,000–40,000/month all-in (rent, schools, car, insurance) before MOU on any villa.

**Scenario C — remote worker self-sponsored:** Freelance permit or mainland consultancy licence may be cheaper than employer visa if income is overseas. Verify [freelance vs employment visa](/guides/uae-freelance-permit-dubai/) before you ship household goods.

## FAQ

**Can I move to Dubai on a tourist visa and find a job?**  
Yes, many do. You must **change status** before working. Penalties for working without proper visa are serious. Budget extra for status change fees.

**Is Dubai safe for solo female expats?**  
UAE ranks among the safest countries globally for violent crime. Standard urban precautions apply, late-night transport, road safety.

**Do I need a car in Dubai?**  
Not mandatory, Metro covers key corridors. Families with school runs usually want one car minimum.

**Can pets relocate to Dubai?**  
Yes, import permit, microchip, vaccination rules apply. Villa communities more pet-friendly than most towers.

**What about alcohol?**  
Licensed venues and home consumption with alcohol licence in permitted areas. Respect Ramadan and public behaviour norms.

**How hot is summer really?**  
June–September: 40–45°C+ outdoors. Life moves indoors. Budget cooling bills accordingly.

**Will my home country tax me?**  
Depends on residency days and domicile rules, not automatic because UAE has no income tax. See [183-day rule](/guides/uae-tax-residency-property/).

**Best month to relocate?**  
September aligns with school year. January–March avoids peak summer move stress.

## Week 4 checkpoint: are you actually settled?

By day 28 most relocators either feel on track or know something is wrong:

| Green | Red |
|---|---|
| Ejari + DEWA active | Still on hotel, no lease |
| School application submitted | Waiting to "find home first" |
| Bank account receives salary | Cash-only limbo |
| Commute tested at 07:30 | Signed lease on weekend viewing only |

If two or more **red** flags, pause discretionary spending and fix admin before furniture and school deposits stack up.
