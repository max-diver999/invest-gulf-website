## FAQ

**Can I move to Dubai on a tourist visa and find a job?**  
Yes, many do. You must **change status** before working. Penalties for working without proper visa are serious. Budget extra for status change fees.

**Is Dubai safe for solo female expats?**  
UAE ranks among the safest countries globally for violent crime. Standard urban precautions apply, late-night transport, road safety.

**Do I need a car in Dubai?**  
Not mandatory, Metro covers key corridors. Families with school runs usually want one car minimum.

**Can pets relocate to Dubai?**  
Yes, import permit, microchip, vaccination rules apply. Villa communities more pet-friendly than most towers.

**What about alcohol?**  
Licensed venues and home consumption with alcohol licence in permitted areas. Respect Ramadan and public behaviour norms.

**How hot is summer really?**  
June–September: 40–45°C+ outdoors. Life moves indoors. Budget cooling bills accordingly.

**Will my home country tax me?**  
Depends on residency days and domicile rules, not automatic because UAE has no income tax. See [183-day rule](/guides/uae-tax-residency-property/).

**Best month to relocate?**  
September aligns with school year. January–March avoids peak summer move stress.
