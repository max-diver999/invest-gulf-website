## What is Qatar permanent residency (and what is it not)?

Qatar permanent residency is a capped elite residence status under Law 10/2018 that is not citizenship, not a Qatari passport, and not the five-year property investor visa, with Invest Gulf treating PR as upside optionality after roughly 15 to 20 years rather than a base-case relocation ROI for foreign residents.

PR may widen stay rights without employer sponsorship in theory, but work, business, and family effects need MOI confirmation (confirm current official rules). Agents who blur PR with standard investor QIDs create expensive mistakes. This guide keeps the categories separate.

## How did Law 10/2018 define Qatar permanent residency?

Law 10/2018 typically defines a permanent residency permit distinct from renewable QID categories, with long-residence, high-value property, and outstanding-talent pathways under Cabinet and MOI discretion, plus Arabic requirements on some routes and an explicit non-citizenship rule that Invest Gulf maps across an 8 year implementation window from 2018 to 2026.

What the law did:

- Create a permanent residency permit separate from standard QIDs
- Open multiple eligibility pathways with discretionary approval
- Reinforce cultural integration via Arabic on certain categories
- Clarify that PR does not confer citizenship

What it did not do:

- Create a volume "golden visa" competitor to UAE's 10-year programme
- Guarantee PR at standard investor thresholds near 730K riyals
- Remove employer sponsorship for the mass expat workforce
- Publish a transparent points calculator like Canada or Australia

Practitioner consensus: PR is granted but highly selective, with annual caps treated as binding constraints (confirm current official rules).
