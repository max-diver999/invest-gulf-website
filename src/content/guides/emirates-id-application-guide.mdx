## What is Emirates ID and why do you need it from day one?

Emirates ID is the physical smart card ICP issues to every UAE resident, and Invest Gulf checklists treat it as day-one infrastructure because banks, Ejari tenancy files, mobile SIMs, RTA licensing, and UAE PASS all require it once residency is active in the first 2 to 4 weeks after medical clearance completes for newcomers.

Emirates ID application for a first-time UAE employment visa typically costs AED 150 to 450 at the typing centre once you combine the ICP government fee of AED 100 to 300 with a centre service charge of AED 40 to 150, while the medical fitness test at a government clinic adds AED 320 to 400 before biometrics. Card production usually takes 5 to 10 business days after the Emirates ID number is issued, and the full path from medical test to plastic in hand commonly spans 2 to 4 weeks when the employer PRO pipeline is clean. Expats who need Ejari registration by a fixed move-in date should land at least 3 weeks before lease start, because banks, DEWA, and SIM activation all require a valid Emirates ID number once residency is active. UAE PASS can often be created 2 to 3 business days after biometrics using the number alone, which unblocks government apps before collection.

Insider tip: ask your PRO to confirm the medical upload in ICP before you leave the clinic parking lot, then book biometrics the same afternoon so you do not lose the 5 to 7 days many newcomers waste waiting for a paper certificate that never arrives.

Practically speaking, you cannot do the following without a valid Emirates ID:

- **Open a UAE bank account**, all banks require EID as the primary ID document for resident account opening
- **Sign an Ejari tenancy registration**, Dubai Land Department's system mandates EID for both landlord and tenant fields; without it, your lease cannot be registered and DEWA utilities stay disconnected
- **Obtain a local mobile SIM**, Etisalat (e&) and du require EID plus a stamped visa for any postpaid plan
- **Register a vehicle or apply for a driving licence conversion**, RTA requires EID at every step
- **Access UAE PASS and government apps**, Emirates ID number is the universal login identity across hundreds of federal portals

Your employer's Public Relations Officer (PRO) normally manages the application on your behalf for the employment visa track. Understanding the sequence means you can track progress, prepare the right documents, and spot delays early rather than discovering them when you need the card.

## How does the Emirates ID application process work?

Emirates ID application typically means a five-step residency sequence for Dubai employment visas: entry permit arrival, medical fitness testing, typing-centre biometrics capture, ICP number generation in 2 to 5 business days, and physical card production in 5 to 10 business days on Invest Gulf relocation timelines for company-sponsored arrivals.

**Step 1, Entry permit and arrival.** Your employer applies for a work entry permit before you travel. You enter the UAE on this temporary permit, which gives you a defined window, typically 60 days, though category and employer type affect this, to complete residency formalities. Do not overstay this window; overstay fines are applied per day.

**Step 2, Medical fitness test.** Within your first week in the UAE, attend a government-approved medical fitness clinic designated by your PRO. The test includes a blood test and a chest X-ray (primarily for TB screening). Results are uploaded directly to ICP's system by the clinic, you do not receive a physical certificate to carry. Approximate cost at government medical fitness centres: AED 320-400 (verify with your PRO, as employer health contracts sometimes cover this).

**Step 3, ICP application at a typing centre.** Once your medical clearance is in the ICP system, you visit an ICP-accredited typing centre. The centre operator submits your application online, and then captures your biometrics: fingerprints (all 10 fingers), iris scan (both eyes), and a digital photograph. The entire on-site process takes roughly 15-20 minutes.

**Step 4, ICP processing and Emirates ID number generation.** ICP reviews the submitted application. Within 2-5 business days of biometrics, your Emirates ID number is generated and an SMS is sent to the registered mobile number. This number is live in ICP records and can be used for UAE PASS registration and some bank onboarding before the physical card arrives.

**Step 5, Card production and collection.** The physical card takes 5-10 business days after your EID number is issued. A second SMS confirms the card is ready. You can collect it from the typing centre, authorise your employer PRO to collect on your behalf (letter of authorisation required), or pay AED 25-35 for Emirates Post home delivery.

| Step | Typical duration | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry permit window | Up to 60 days | Employer PRO |
| Medical + upload | 3 to 7 business days | Clinic / PRO |
| Biometrics | 15 to 20 minutes on site | Applicant |
| EID number SMS | 2 to 5 business days | ICP |
| Card ready | 5 to 10 business days | ICP / collection |

## Where do you complete Emirates ID biometrics?

Typing centres are ICP-authorised biometric desks operating across all seven emirates, and our clients usually attend the PRO-preferred Dubai location where a booked appointment beats peak walk-in queues and finishes capture before the 2 to 5 business day Emirates ID number SMS arrives for UAE PASS registration ahead of plastic collection.

Typing centres across Dubai accept walk-ins, yet peak months such as September, January, and the weeks before Ramadan routinely produce long queues, while an ICP Smart Services or ICP UAE app booking typically cuts wait time under 30 minutes at busy malls. Biometrics capture itself takes roughly 15 to 20 minutes once you are at the desk: ten fingerprints, both iris scans, and a digital photograph. After submission, ICP usually generates the Emirates ID number within 2 to 5 business days and sends SMS confirmation to the registered UAE mobile. Optional Emirates Post home delivery runs AED 25 to 35 if you prefer not to return for collection. Investors using the Golden Visa property track still complete the same medical and biometrics steps, but documentation shifts to title deed or Oqood evidence and the overall timeline often stretches 4 to 8 weeks from DLD registration to card in hand at the AED 2 million paid-property threshold.

| Booking method | Appointment | Fee | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Employer PRO managed | Arranged by PRO | Included in package | Standard employment visa applicants |
| ICP Smart Services portal | Book online in advance | Typing centre fee only | Independent applicants comfortable online |
| ICP UAE mobile app | Book in-app, same day possible | Typing centre fee only | Mobile-first applicants needing speed |
| Walk-in at any centre | No booking required | Same fee, longer wait | Urgent or same-day visits |

- Book via icp.gov.ae or the ICP UAE app when your PRO does not assign a slot
- Carry originals; photocopies alone are refused
- Register an active UAE mobile for both SMS alerts

## What documents do you bring to biometrics?

Documents for Emirates ID biometrics typically mean original passport with UAE entry stamp, entry-permit or company reference, medical-upload confirmation from your PRO, recent biometric photographs, an active UAE mobile number for SMS alerts, and AED 40 to 150 ready for the typing-centre fee on Invest Gulf document checklists before the appointment.

- **Original passport** with UAE entry stamp from your current arrival
- **Entry permit** or company application reference number (PRO typically holds the digital version)
- **Medical fitness clearance confirmation**, your PRO confirms when the clinic has uploaded to the ICP system (you do not carry a paper certificate)
- **Two recent passport photographs** in biometric format (white or off-white background; many centres photograph you on-site regardless)
- **UAE mobile number** for SMS registration, use your active UAE SIM, not a home-country number
- **Payment** for typing centre fee, most centres accept card, some prefer cash for service charges

If your PRO manages the file digitally, they may only require your physical presence for the biometrics capture, meaning fingerprints, iris, and photo only. Confirm in advance whether you need to carry original documents or whether the PRO has already uploaded them.

Family visa applicants sponsored by a UAE resident follow the same biometrics step under a separate application linked to the sponsor's file. Children under 15 years old have modified biometrics requirements, verify current ICP guidance for minors at icp.gov.ae.

Emirates ID fees typically stack ICP government charges of AED 100 to 300 plus typing-centre service of AED 40 to 150 payable at the appointment desk, while medical fitness at a government clinic adds AED 320 to 400 earlier; we budget AED 500 to 700 all-in for standard employment visas excluding optional Emirates Post delivery.

| Fee component | Typical range (AED) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICP government fee, 2-year visa validity | 100-200 | Standard employment visa |
| ICP government fee, 3-year visa validity | 150-300 | Senior roles, longer-term categories |
| Typing centre service charge | 40-150 | Set by individual centre; verify in advance |
| Emirates Post card delivery (optional) | 25-35 | Alternative to centre collection |
| Medical fitness test, government clinic | 320-400 | Paid at clinic, not at typing centre |

**Total expected outlay** for most new employment visa holders: AED 500-700 including medical. Freelancer permit, Golden Visa, and long-validity investor visa categories may have different fee structures. This table reflects June 2026 ranges, verify current figures with ICP before your appointment.

UAE Labour Law requires employers to cover the costs of an employee's work visa and Emirates ID application. If your company asks you to personally pay these government fees, clarify in writing who reimburses and on what timeline. Many companies cover government fees but ask employees to personally cover the typing centre service charge and medical, confirm your company's policy before arrival.

Emirates ID timelines typically span 2 to 4 weeks from landing to plastic card in hand when medical clears in 3 to 7 business days, ICP issues the number in 2 to 5 business days, and card production then needs another 5 to 10 business days on Invest Gulf week-by-week relocation models.

| Medical fitness test | Day 2-5 | Attend clinic when PRO confirms appointment |

- Allow at least 3 weeks buffer before a hard Ejari move-in date
- Check ICP Smart Services by day 8 if no SMS movement
- Keep flexible hotel booking until the card-ready SMS arrives

If your medical result is flagged, uncommon, but it happens for blood panel abnormalities or TB screening, processing pauses for additional tests at a specialist government clinic. Your PRO handles the escalation. You can check live application status anytime via ICP Smart Services using your application reference number.

## What can UAE PASS unlock before the plastic card arrives?

UAE PASS is the federal digital identity platform that unlocks DEWA, RTA, MOHRE, and hundreds of government portals once your Emirates ID number hits ICP records, typically 2 to 3 business days after biometrics and before the plastic card arrives in 5 to 10 business days according to Invest Gulf digital-onboarding guidance for new residents.

With your EID number and the registered mobile for OTP verification, you can:

- Log into hundreds of federal and emirate-level government apps (DEWA, RTA, MOHRE, and others)
- Digitally sign government documents and applications
- Begin the Ejari tenancy registration process in the Dubai REST app
- Apply for driving licence status change at RTA
- Start account opening with some banks that accept UAE PASS as digital ID verification

| Milestone | Typical timing |
| --- | --- |
| EID number in ICP | 2 to 3 business days after biometrics |
| UAE PASS registration | Same day the number is live |
| Physical card collection | 5 to 10 business days after number |

To register: download the UAE PASS app, enter your Emirates ID number, and verify via SMS OTP. The app links to your ICP identity profile automatically. If the number is not yet in the system, wait 24 hours and retry, it is not instantaneous. Landlords can often start Ejari prep with the number before the card arrives because DLD links to the ICP record, not the plastic.

- **Bank account:** Emirates ID, passport with stamped residency visa, salary certificate, and Ejari or employer housing letter; opening often takes 3 to 10 business days
- **Ejari:** mandatory EID fields for landlord and tenant in Dubai Land Department systems
- **SIM:** Etisalat (e&) or du with EID plus visa stamp for postpaid plans
- **Driving licence conversion:** RTA eligible-country list, eye test, original home licence
- **Mortgage path:** no UAE home-loan file proceeds without Emirates ID

## Which mistakes slow down Emirates ID the most?

Common Emirates ID delays are process mistakes rather than raw ICP capacity: waiting for a paper medical certificate that never exists costs 5 to 7 days, and skipping ICP Smart Services status checks past day 8 is a red flag in Invest Gulf reviews of stalled employment-visa files before blaming centre queues.

- Waiting for a paper medical certificate that never exists (clinic uploads to ICP; lose 5 to 7 days)
- Bringing non-biometric photos and insisting centres use them
- Registering a home-country number that never receives SMS alerts
- Skipping ICP Smart Services checks past day 8
- Arriving without cash or card for typing-centre fees your employer does not cover

Typing centres photograph you on-site. Register your active UAE SIM. Clarify fee split with HR before the appointment.

## When should you renew Emirates ID and what happens?

Emirates ID renewal typically mirrors first issuance with a fresh medical fitness test, typing-centre biometrics, and 5 to 10 business days of card production for the same 2 or 3 year visa validity most residents hold, and our checklists open filings 30 days before expiry to avoid late fines on expired residency-linked cards.

| Item | Rule of thumb |
| --- | --- |
| Visa-linked validity | 2 or 3 years typical |
| Earliest renewal window | 30 days before expiry |
| Card production | 5 to 10 business days |

Your Emirates ID number stays the same through every renewal cycle. Only the expiry date and chip data update, so banks and government services keep history against the same number.

## How much Ejari buffer should you plan before move-in?

Emirates ID planning for a fixed Ejari move-in date typically means treating the card as a 3 week critical path from landing through medical upload, biometrics booking, and production on our relocation models that assume 2 to 4 weeks total when ICP processing loads remain within normal bands.

- Land at least 3 weeks before a hard lease start
- Confirm medical upload before leaving the clinic
- Book biometrics the same day upload clears
- Keep bridge housing flexible until card-ready SMS

Planning risks for Emirates ID typically mean reconfirming official ICP fee tables the week you apply, budgeting 15% to 25% above headline medical and centre costs for admin surprises, and treating any guaranteed card date as a red flag on our checklists before you lock a 12-month Ejari start date.
