**Stage 1, Pre-approval (1–10 working days by bank):** Verification of income, credit check via Al Etihad Credit Bureau, and debt burden ratio calculation. Result: a pre-approval letter confirming the maximum loan amount and approximate monthly instalment. This letter is the document sellers require to take your offer seriously.

**Stage 2, Property selection and MOU signing:** Pre-approval in hand, you negotiate with the seller and sign a memorandum of understanding or developer reservation agreement. The signed MOU is submitted to the bank to begin the formal credit process.

**Stage 3, Property valuation (3–7 working days):** A bank-appointed independent valuer assesses the market value of the property. The mortgage amount is capped at the bank's LTV applied to the lower of the purchase price or the valuation, whichever figure is smaller.

**Stage 4, Final offer and DLD registration (2–4 weeks):** Title deed verification, DLD transfer and mortgage registration. Both buyer and seller attend the DLD transfer or authorise POA holders. The bank transfers funds directly to the seller at the DLD transfer appointment.

**Stage 5, Disbursement for off-plan:** For off-plan properties, funds are disbursed in tranches aligned to developer construction milestone certificates. The mortgage registration occurs at the initial completion of foundation or the first eligible construction stage.

Total timeline from starting pre-approval to key handover: typically 6–12 weeks for completed ready properties. Off-plan transactions involve the initial registration followed by a disbursement schedule spanning the construction period.
