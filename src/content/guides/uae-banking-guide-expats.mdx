## How do CRS and FATCA affect UAE bank accounts?

CRS means UAE banks report account data to 100+ jurisdictions including the UK, EU, Australia, Canada, and India, even when UAE personal income tax is 0%. We tell US persons to plan FBAR when accounts exceed USD 10,000 at any point in the year, plus Form 8938 thresholds that can reach USD 200,000 to 600,000 for overseas residents. Corporate tax at 9% above AED 375,000 affects companies, not personal rental ownership.

| Regime | Who it hits | Practical trigger |
|---|---|---|
| CRS | Most non-US tax residents | Annual bank reporting |
| FATCA / FBAR | US persons | USD 10,000 aggregate |
| UAE corporate tax | Companies | 9% above AED 375,000 |

- Personal property rental income for individuals remains 0% on the UAE side
