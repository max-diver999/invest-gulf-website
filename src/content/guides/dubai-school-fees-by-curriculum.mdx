## British curriculum: fee anatomy

Largest Dubai cohort. Fees correlate with KHDA rating and brand.

| Rating band | Typical annual (Year 7) | Increase cap context |
|---|---|---|
| Outstanding | AED 75,000–95,000 | Higher framework headroom |
| Very Good | AED 60,000–80,000 | Moderate |
| Good | AED 50,000–70,000 | Lower |
| Acceptable | AED 45,000–60,000 | Restricted increases |

Premium brands (JESS, Repton, Kings, Wellington) exceed table top, supply constraint, not rating alone.

Exam years: IGCSE and A-Level exam fees AED 3,000–8,000 extra per year, not always in headline fee.

## IB: why diploma years cost most

IB Diploma Programme (Years 12–13) requires:

- Smaller class sizes
- Specialist subject teachers
- IB registration and exam fees
- Extended essay and TOK coordination

| Stage | Fee premium vs British same school |
|---|---|
| PYP primary | +5–10% |
| MYP | +10–15% |
| DP | +15–25% |

Value proposition: university recognition worldwide, worth premium for globally mobile families.

## American curriculum: AP layer

Base tuition plus:

| Extra | AED/year |
|---|---|
| AP exam fees (per subject) | 500–800 |
| PSAT/SAT prep optional | 3,000–8,000 |
| Sports programmes | 2,000–6,000 |

American schools with strong athletics may charge facilities levy, read fee notes.

## Indian CBSE / ICSE: value track

| Premium CBSE | 40,000–48,000 | Newer facilities, lower than British |

COL impact: two children CBSE vs two children British Outstanding = AED 100,000+/year difference, drives JVC vs Dubai Hills rent pairing.

## KHDA fee framework: how increases work

KHDA publishes fee index linking allowed increase to inspection rating.

| Concept | Practical effect |
|---|---|
| Outstanding school | Can move fees toward ceiling faster |
| New grade entry | Jump to higher band, not capped like renewal |
| Sibling discount | 5–10% at some schools, ask |
| Early payment discount | Rare, 2–3% if offered |

Protest path: if increase exceeds published framework, KHDA complaint channel exists.

## Curriculum switch cost

Switching British → IB or reverse in Year 10+:

- Repeated exam pathway risk
- Different credit recognition
- Emotional disruption
- New registration deposits

Financial planning: treat curriculum choice as 7-year lock minimum per child.

## Hidden school costs beyond tuition (2026)

| Cost line | CBSE | British mid | IB premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Registration / re-enrolment | AED 500–2,000 | AED 2,000–5,000 | AED 3,000–8,000 |
| Bus (annual) | AED 6,000–8,000 | AED 8,000–12,000 | AED 10,000–14,000 |
| Uniform + devices | AED 2,000–4,000 | AED 3,000–6,000 | AED 4,000–8,000 |
| Exam fees (IGCSE/A-Level/IB) | Lower | AED 3,000–8,000/yr | AED 5,000–12,000/yr |
| ECA / sport (termly) | AED 1,000–3,000 | AED 3,000–8,000 | AED 5,000–15,000 |

**Two-child British mid family** all-in often hits **AED 180K–220K/year**, not the AED 120K headline tuition alone.

KHDA fee increase approvals vary by rating, Outstanding schools historically received higher annual increase bands. Budget **+3–5%** fee growth even when salary flat.

Corporate debentures (refundable deposits at select schools) tie up **AED 10K–25K** per child, cash-flow hit in year one.

**Sibling planning:** second child at same school often saves **5–10%** tuition, model five-year family cost with sibling discount before choosing premium British over CBSE.
