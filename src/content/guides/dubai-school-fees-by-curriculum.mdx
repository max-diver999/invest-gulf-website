# Dubai School Fees by Curriculum 2026: British, IB, American & CBSE

**TL;DR:** Dubai school fees vary more by **curriculum and KHDA rating** than by postcode. British Outstanding schools run **AED 85,000–110,000/year** in secondary years; CBSE mid-tier **AED 25,000–40,000**. IB diploma years often top **AED 100,000**. Add **AED 10,000–20,000/year** hidden costs (bus, uniforms, exams) on every track. KHDA caps annual increases by rating — not by your salary growth.

**Quick answer:** Dubai school fees in 2026 range from AED 15,000 (budget CBSE) to AED 120,000+ (premium British/IB) per year. British Outstanding schools average AED 85,000-110,000, American schools AED 50,000-90,000, while Indian CBSE offers value at AED 25,000-40,000. Add 20-30% for hidden costs like registration, transport, uniforms, and exam fees. KHDA rating directly impacts fee levels and annual increase caps.

## British curriculum — fee anatomy

Largest Dubai cohort. Fees correlate with **KHDA rating** and brand.

| Rating band | Typical annual (Year 7) | Increase cap context |
|---|---|---|
| Outstanding | AED 75,000–95,000 | Higher framework headroom |
| Very Good | AED 60,000–80,000 | Moderate |
| Good | AED 50,000–70,000 | Lower |
| Acceptable | AED 45,000–60,000 | Restricted increases |

**Premium brands** (JESS, Repton, Kings, Wellington) exceed table top — supply constraint, not rating alone.

**Exam years:** IGCSE and A-Level exam fees **AED 3,000–8,000** extra per year — not always in headline fee.

## IB — why diploma years cost most

IB Diploma Programme (Years 12–13) requires:

- Smaller class sizes
- Specialist subject teachers
- IB registration and exam fees
- Extended essay and TOK coordination

| Stage | Fee premium vs British same school |
|---|---|
| PYP primary | +5–10% |
| MYP | +10–15% |
| DP | +15–25% |

**Value proposition:** university recognition worldwide — worth premium for globally mobile families.

## KHDA fee framework — how increases work

KHDA publishes **fee index** linking allowed increase to inspection rating.

| Concept | Practical effect |
|---|---|
| Outstanding school | Can move fees toward ceiling faster |
| New grade entry | Jump to higher band — not capped like renewal |
| Sibling discount | 5–10% at some schools — ask |
| Early payment discount | Rare — 2–3% if offered |

**Protest path:** if increase exceeds published framework — KHDA complaint channel exists.

## Summary decision framework

Before signing any lease or purchase contract in Dubai, consolidate **rent, utilities, schools, transport, and one-off relocation** into one spreadsheet tab. Compare against **net household income**, not gross headline salary. Dubai rewards families who model honestly and punishes those who trust brochure economics.

If total fixed costs exceed **70% of net income**, you are one school fee invoice away from financial stress — downgrade area, curriculum, or negotiate package before move, not after.
