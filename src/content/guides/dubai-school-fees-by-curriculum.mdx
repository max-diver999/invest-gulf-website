## What are Dubai school fees by curriculum in 2026?

British, IB, American, and Indian tracks sit in different fee bands in 2026. Outstanding British schools often charge AED 85,000-110,000 per year, IB diploma years can exceed AED 100,000, American schools typically run AED 50,000-90,000, and CBSE mid-tier schools often land AED 25,000-40,000. Always confirm the official PDF for your child's year group before you pay a registration deposit.

| Curriculum | Typical annual band (AED) | Planning note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| British (Good) | 45,000-75,000 | KHDA rating drives annual cap |
| British (Outstanding) | 85,000-120,000 | Premium brands above band top |
| IB (incl. DP years) | 70,000-120,000+ | Continuum pricing jumps in DP |
| American | 50,000-90,000 | AP and counselling levies extra |
| CBSE / ICSE | 15,000-40,000 | Strong value for rigorous track |

Request the official fee PDF for 2026-27 before you pay a registration deposit; headline web ranges often miss exam levies and bus fees.

Monthly amortisation: divide by 12 for budget, but cash flow follows term invoices (often 40/30/30 split).

## What do British curriculum schools cost in Dubai?

British schools dominate the mid-market and premium tiers. Good-rated campuses often start near AED 45,000 in primary and climb through IGCSE years; Outstanding schools cluster at AED 85,000-110,000 before exam and transport add-ons. Map [school fees vs rent](/guides/school-fees-vs-property-budget-dubai/) before you pick a community.

| Band | Primary (indicative) | Secondary (indicative) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Good British | 45,000-60,000 | 60,000-78,000 |
| Outstanding British | 70,000-90,000 | 90,000-120,000 |

Premium brands (JESS, Repton, Kings, Wellington) exceed table top, supply constraint, not rating alone.

Exam years: IGCSE and A-Level exam fees AED 3,000-8,000 extra per year, not always in headline fee.

## What do IB schools cost in Dubai?

IB fees rise sharply in the diploma programme. PYP and MYP years often sit AED 65,000-85,000; DP years frequently cross AED 100,000 once exam registration and extended essay support are included. Partial IB schools (MYP only) can be cheaper but limit university pathways.

Value proposition: university recognition worldwide, worth premium for globally mobile families.

## What do American curriculum schools cost in Dubai?

American schools typically bill AED 50,000-90,000 depending on rating and grade. High-school AP exam fees, SAT prep packages, and college counselling often sit outside headline tuition, so budget AED 8,000-15,000 in Years 11-12 for US-pathway families.

## What do Indian CBSE and ICSE schools cost in Dubai?

CBSE and ICSE schools deliver the strongest fee value in Dubai's international market. Mid-tier CBSE campuses often charge AED 25,000-40,000 per year with rigorous maths and science tracks. ICSE can run slightly higher at premium campuses but still under half a British Outstanding fee.

COL impact: two children CBSE vs two children British Outstanding = AED 100,000+/year difference, drives JVC vs Dubai Hills rent pairing.

## What hidden school costs sit beyond headline tuition?

Budget AED 10,000-20,000 per child beyond tuition for registration, uniforms, bus, textbooks, trips, and activity charges. Exam years add AED 3,000-8,000 for British IGCSE/A-Level or IB registration. Bus routes alone can run AED 8,000-12,000 annually if you live outside the school's catchment.

| Line item | Typical AED/year per child |
| --- | --- |
| Registration / re-enrolment | 500-3,000 (often non-refundable) |
| Uniform and kit | 2,000-4,000 |
| School transport | 8,000-12,000 |
| Exams (upper secondary) | 3,000-8,000 |
| Devices / lab levies | 3,000-5,000 (secondary) |

## How does KHDA cap annual fee increases?

KHDA links approved fee increases to inspection rating. Outstanding schools historically received higher annual increase bands than Acceptable-rated schools. Budget +3-5% fee growth even when salary is flat, and read the school's latest KHDA report before you assume a multi-year discount.

## How do employer school allowances map to real fees?

Many packages cap at AED 50,000 per child while British mid secondary runs AED 70,000+. Negotiate the cap before you sign the employment contract, or model self-pay from Year 6 onward. Some employers allow redirecting unused allowance to housing if HR policy permits.

## What does switching curriculum mid-stream cost?

Switching exam boards at Year 10 is costly: new uniforms, different textbook sets, catch-up tutoring, and lost registration deposits. Curriculum continuity often beats saving 10% on headline tuition when children are within three years of external exams.

## How do school fees correlate with Dubai rent areas?

Premium British clusters (Dubai Hills, Arabian Ranches, Jumeirah) pair high rent with high fees. Value families often choose JVC or Motor City rent with CBSE or Good British schools and accept a longer bus ride. Use [How to choose a school in Dubai](/guides/how-to-choose-school-dubai/) after you map commute and fee bands.

## How should families compare curriculum total cost?

Compare five-year all-in cost, not Year 1 tuition alone. A CBSE mid track plus bus can beat a single year of Outstanding British fees by AED 150,000 over five years for one child. Add a second child and sibling discounts before you upgrade curriculum tier.

## IB partial vs full continuum pricing

Schools offering IB PYP/MYP without DP often quote lower primary fees but require a later transfer to a full IB campus for diploma years. Model the DP jump explicitly rather than extrapolating primary tuition through Year 13.

## What extra costs hit CBSE exam years?

Board exam registration, lab practical fees, and intensive revision classes stack in Grades 10 and 12. Budget AED 2,000-5,000 above normal activity charges in those years.

## How do corporate fee billing and debentures work?

Some schools invoice employers directly each term; others require parent advance payment with reimbursement. Refundable debentures (AED 10,000-25,000 per child) tie up cash in year one even when tuition is reimbursed monthly.

## How real are scholarships and sibling discounts?

Sibling discounts of 5-10% are common when both children attend the same school. Academic scholarships exist but are selective; do not budget them unless confirmed in writing from admissions.

Do not budget scholarships unless offered in writing.

## What all-in budget should a two-child family plan?


Two-child British mid family all-in often hits AED 180K-220K/year, not the AED 120K headline tuition alone.

KHDA fee increase approvals vary by rating, Outstanding schools historically received higher annual increase bands. Budget +3-5% fee growth even when salary flat.

Corporate debentures (refundable deposits at select schools) tie up AED 10K-25K per child, cash-flow hit in year one.

Sibling planning: second child at same school often saves 5-10% tuition, model five-year family cost with sibling discount before choosing premium British over CBSE.

## When does the employer cap leave a self-pay gap?


Typical gap on GBP assignee packages: employer cap AED 50K, actual British mid Year 8 AED 72K, self-pay **AED 22K/child** unless renegotiated pre-offer.

Indian assignees on CBSE often **under-use cap**, surplus sometimes redirectable to housing if HR flexible.

## Which family situations change school fee planning?

**Primary entry (FS1 or Year 1):** Apply 6-12 months ahead for Outstanding-rated campuses. Registration fees are often non-refundable once a seat is offered, so hold a backup school in an acceptable commute band.

**Mid-secondary transfer:** Curriculum continuity usually matters more than saving 10% on headline tuition. Switching exam boards at Year 10 triggers new uniforms, textbooks, catch-up tutoring, and lost deposits.

**Fee-sensitive households with two children:** Model bus, uniform, exam, and activity levies beyond tuition. CBSE and mid-tier British tracks can save AED 100,000+ per year versus dual Outstanding British placements, but university pathway expectations should match the chosen track.

## How do five-year fee projections compound?


Assumes **+3% annual tuition** and stable bus route, IGCSE years 9-11 jump **15-25%** vs primary; extend table before committing to premium British over [Indian CBSE schools Dubai](/guides/indian-schools-dubai-cbse/).

**Refund policy:** KHDA rules limit term refunds after week two, mid-year corporate repatriation still leaves **one full term** payable unless employer indemnity clause covers break fee.

**Payment timing:** most schools invoice **termly in advance**, align salary transfer date or employer direct-debit to avoid **AED 25K+ single-day outflow** at September start.

Use [How to choose a school in Dubai](/guides/how-to-choose-school-dubai/) after fee modelling, curriculum fit matters once annual all-in cost is clear.

**American curriculum note:** AP exam fees and college counselling packages often sit **outside** published tuition, budget **AED 8,000-15,000** in Years 11-12 for US-pathway families.

French and German schools add **language support levies** for non-native speakers, confirm with admissions before assuming base fee covers extra French hours.

## What checklist runs before you accept a school seat?

Confirm seat offer letter lists all levies, payment schedule, refund policy after week two, and bus availability from your shortlisted home. Cross-check KHDA rating and latest inspection summary on the regulator site.

## What is the school placement checklist before move?

Apply 6-12 months ahead for Outstanding-rated campuses. Hold a backup school in acceptable commute band. Align [Dubai cost of living](/guides/dubai-cost-of-living-guide/) and property search with confirmed fee PDFs, not marketing ranges.

**Red flags:** refusal to share fee PDF, pressure to pay non-refundable registration before tour, or bus route "TBC" after deposit.
