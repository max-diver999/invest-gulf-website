## What Australian tax obligations apply to Dubai property?

Australian tax residents typically must report worldwide Dubai rental income and capital gains to the ATO even when UAE tax is 0%. Invest Gulf tax checklists flag that the main residence exemption does not cover Dubai investment stock, so a 12-month ownership year still needs full Australian reporting on rent and any disposal.

### Dubai rental income: Australian tax treatment

| Your status | Australian tax on Dubai rent |
|---|---|
| Australian tax resident | Report worldwide, full Australian income tax |
| Non-resident for tax purposes | Generally no Australian tax on foreign rent |
| Temporary resident (482/189 visa holders) | May qualify for exemptions on foreign income |
| UAE tax resident (183+ days UAE) | UAE 0%; Australia may still tax if Australian resident |
