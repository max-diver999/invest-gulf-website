## Who can buy property in Oman?

Foreign buyers can purchase in ITC zones and Ministry-approved freehold pockets, not general Muscat residential on standard expat freehold terms. Al Mouj, Muscat Bay, Jebel Sifah, and Muscat Hills are established; resale marketing often runs 60 to 90 days. New 2025 zones need per-unit registry checks before any deposit.

Invest Gulf purchase files always confirm the exact unit on the foreign ownership register before deposit.

Buyer-access checklist:
- Al Mouj Muscat: deepest resale, 60 to 90 day marketing windows
- Muscat Bay: waterfront, diplomatic adjacency, 2 to 3 year leases
- Jebel Sifah: resort distance, voids of 60 to 120 days
- Muscat Hills: golf villas
- 2025 new ITCs: verify status before off-plan wires

## Does property purchase link to investor residency?

Property purchase is not an automatic residency grant in Oman; Royal Oman Police processes investor tracks after qualifying investment commonly cited near USD 650,000. Processing takes months and stays separate from employment visas. Confirm current official rules before treating residency as a closing condition.

Invest Gulf residency checklists keep SPA and ROP tracks on different lines.

Residency checklist:
- Threshold marketing: ~USD 650,000 (confirm)
- Not automatic on SPA signing
- No citizenship path from property alone
- Months of processing, not days

|---|---|
| Pathway | Investor track via ROP |

**Insider tip:** Wait for handover plus 12 months of lease evidence before sizing large tickets in new 2025 ITC zones; brochure yields without tenancy schedules are a red flag.

Financing for foreign ITC buyers typically remains cash-heavy, even when local banks discuss non-resident products. Expect stricter documentation than UAE retail mortgages and confirm LTV before SPA. Do not assume Dubai-style 50% to 80% leverage will transfer cleanly into OMR deals.

## What ownership, tax, and management issues matter after purchase?

Ownership and tax reporting typically stay simpler inside Oman for individuals, because Oman does not charge personal income tax on rent, yet home-country rules in the UK, US, or EU may still require declarations. Absentee owners without a manager are the most common source of prolonged voids of 45 to 120 days depending on zone.

Invest Gulf post-completion packs hire local management before the first tenant, not after the first empty summer.

Post-purchase checklist:
- Individual title clarity for USD 650,000 residency math where relevant
- Service charge history of 3% to 5% annual rises on mature Al Mouj stock
- Manager references inside the same ITC
- Documented leases for any exit inside five years
