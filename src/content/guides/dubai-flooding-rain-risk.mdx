The event was extraordinary in scale, yet it fit a pattern of escalating rainfall that older drainage was not built to manage.

## Why Dubai's Drainage Infrastructure Lags

Dubai drainage typically lagged because planners assumed very low annual rainfall of under 100mm for decades, and our research treats paved surfaces plus built-over wadis as amplifiers that push more water into pipes per millimetre during a 24 hour storm for foreign buyers in older communities Plan inside a 12 month cycle.

Several factors compound this:

- Urban development speed from the 1980s onward built roads to functional dry-climate standards
- Impervious paving redirects rainfall that desert soil once dispersed
- Ancient wadi corridors, now roads or plots, can channel water again in extremes

Retrofitting drainage is more complex and expensive than designing capacity correctly from the start.

| Insurance Type | Flood Coverage | Notes |

| Building insurance | Usually covered | Verify explicitly; check deductibles |

| Comprehensive vehicle | Usually covered | Natural disaster / act of God clause |
