## Side-by-side: Golden Visa vs Green Visa

The two visas solve different problems. Golden Visa rewards capital deployment. Green Visa rewards professional status. Here is where they differ across every factor that matters to applicants.

| Factor | Golden Visa | Green Visa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duration | 10 years, renewable | 5 years, renewable |
| Self-sponsored | Yes | Yes |
| Property requirement | AED 2,000,000 registered value | None |
| Main qualification routes | Property, exceptional talent, large investment | Skilled employment, freelance, business investment (AED 500K+), outstanding student |
| Freelancer track | Not available via freelance route | Yes, AED 360,000+ annual income |
| Family sponsorship | Spouse, children, parents (some cases) | Spouse, children |
| Government fees | ~AED 4,000–5,500 per applicant | ~AED 1,500–1,800 per applicant |
| Off-plan qualifying | Yes (Oqood registered value) | N/A |
| Mortgage qualifying | Yes, subject to NOC and ICP rules | N/A |
| Emirates ID | Yes | Yes |
| Citizenship path | No | No |
| Tax residency | No, separate 183-day rule | No, separate 183-day rule |

## What the Golden Visa actually gives you

The Golden Visa is a 10-year renewable UAE residence permit introduced in 2019 and expanded significantly since. For most applicants considering it in 2026, the property route is the primary track: purchase registered UAE real estate with a DLD-recorded value of at least AED 2 million, and you qualify.

**What you get once approved:**

A 10-year renewable residency stamp. Self-sponsorship, no UAE employer needed. The ability to stay outside the UAE for extended periods without automatic cancellation (subject to re-entry rules, typically at least one entry every six months). Family sponsorship covering spouse, children, and in some documented cases parents. UAE residency status for bank account purposes, school enrolment, and long-term lease contracts.

**What you do not get:**

Citizenship. UAE passport. Automatic tax residency, that is determined separately under Ministry of Finance guidance tied to the 183-day physical presence rule. Guaranteed rental income on the qualifying property. And critically, you do not get a guarantee that the property investment is sound, the visa and the investment are two separate decisions.

**The property route in brief:**

The AED 2 million threshold is based on the registered value at DLD or the relevant emirate land department, not the price you negotiated, not the brochure figure, and not the mortgage amount outstanding. Off-plan units registered on Oqood can qualify. Mortgaged properties can qualify under 2026 rule updates, provided a bank NOC is obtained where required. Multiple units in your name can be aggregated if properly documented.

For the full process, documents list, off-plan rules, and mortgage NOC details, read the [UAE Golden Visa Through Property (2026) guide](/guides/uae-golden-visa-property/).

## What the Green Visa actually gives you

The Green Visa is a 5-year self-sponsored UAE residence permit launched in April 2022. It was designed to decouple professional residency from employer dependency, before it existed, most skilled expats lived in the UAE on company-sponsored work permits that expired within 30 days of leaving a job.

Green Visa gives you the same core residency benefits as a standard UAE visa: Emirates ID, self-sponsorship, bank account access as a resident, school enrolment, and long-term lease eligibility. What it does not require is an employer or a property investment.

**The four qualifying tracks:**

1. **Skilled employee**: working in an occupation classified at ISCO levels 1, 2, or 3 with a valid UAE employment contract and a minimum monthly salary typically cited at AED 15,000 (verify current MOHRE guidance). Your employer does not need to sponsor you, you sponsor yourself with your employment as the qualification evidence.

2. **Freelancer or self-employed professional**: holding a valid UAE freelance permit or trade licence, with documented annual income of at least AED 360,000 (AED 30,000 per month) or provable financial sufficiency through bank statements. This track is commonly used by consultants, designers, tech professionals, and media workers.

3. **Business investor**: holding a minimum AED 500,000 stake in an approved UAE company, with an annual turnover meeting the threshold set by relevant authorities. Not to be confused with the property-linked Golden Visa route.

4. **Outstanding students**: those graduating with distinction from accredited UAE universities or international institutions recognised by the UAE Ministry of Education. This track targets recent graduates, not working professionals.

**The renewal obligation:**

Every five years you must re-qualify. If you started on the freelancer track, you need a valid freelance permit and continued income evidence at renewal. If your freelance income dropped below AED 360,000 or your permit lapsed, renewal can be denied. This is the Green Visa's key structural difference from Golden Visa, the 10-year Golden Visa window reduces how often you must prove qualifying status.

For the full Green Visa eligibility breakdown, document checklist, and processing steps, read the [UAE Green Visa (2026) guide](/guides/uae-green-visa-guide/).

## Eligibility in plain terms: who can realistically get each

**You are a realistic Golden Visa candidate if:**
- You are purchasing or have purchased UAE property with a DLD-registered value of AED 2 million or more.
- You are a researcher, athlete, inventor, or creative professional designated as exceptional talent under relevant UAE authority lists.
- You are making a large-scale direct investment in a UAE company meeting the prescribed thresholds.
- You already hold a senior public sector role with the appropriate grade classification.

**You are a realistic Green Visa candidate if:**
- You work in the UAE or plan to, in management, engineering, medicine, law, finance, IT, or similar ISCO level 1–3 fields, and your salary meets the threshold.
- You are a freelancer or consultant with provable income above AED 360,000 per year and willing to maintain a UAE freelance permit.
- You are investing at least AED 500,000 in a UAE-registered business without needing the property route.
- You are a recent top-performing graduate from a recognised institution.

**Who falls between the two:**

The most common situation is a professional earning AED 30,000–50,000 per month who does not yet have AED 2 million in UAE property. Green Visa is the immediate option. Golden Visa becomes the medium-term goal if they buy property or are designated exceptional talent.

## Cost breakdown

Cost has two components: what you pay the government, and what you commit as capital.

**Green Visa fees, government only:**

| Fee item | Approximate amount (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Residency visa application | 300–500 |
| Medical fitness test | 320–370 |
| Emirates ID issuance | 370 |
| Biometrics and service charges | 150–300 |
| **Total out-of-pocket** | **~1,500–1,800** |

Typing centre or immigration consultant fees are separate and typically add AED 500–2,000 depending on the provider.

**Golden Visa fees, government only:**

| Fee item | Approximate amount (AED) |
| --- | --- |
| Residency visa application | 1,500–2,000 |
| Medical fitness test | 320–370 |
| Emirates ID issuance | 370 |
| Biometrics and service charges | 150–300 |
| Status change / entry permit (if applicable) | 500–1,200 |
| **Total out-of-pocket** | **~4,000–5,500** |

The real cost of Golden Visa via the property route is, of course, the AED 2 million minimum purchase, plus the 4% DLD transfer fee (AED 80,000), agent commission (typically 2%, AED 40,000), and ongoing service charges. Understood in that context, the ~AED 1,500–3,500 premium in government fees between Golden and Green is not the significant variable.

## Processing timeline

Both visas are processed in-country and require physical presence in the UAE during application. Remote application from abroad is generally not possible for the biometric and medical stages.

**Green Visa typical timeline:**

Approximately 2–4 weeks from document submission to Emirates ID collection, assuming documents are complete and the applicant stays in-country during processing. Some applicants report faster turnaround, as few as 10 working days, when using typed and verified applications through GDRFA-linked typing centres.

**Golden Visa typical timeline:**

Between 5 and 15 working days for the visa stamp once documents are complete, according to consistently cited immigration consultant data. Complex cases, multiple properties being aggregated, off-plan units requiring Oqood verification, or mortgaged properties requiring bank NOC, can extend to 3–4 weeks.

For a step-by-step application walkthrough for the Golden Visa, see [How to Apply for UAE Golden Visa: Step-by-Step](/guides/golden-visa-application-step-by-step/).

Both visas require similar post-approval steps: Emirates ID collection (usually 3–7 working days after visa stamp), medical insurance (mandatory), and if you plan to sponsor family members, additional dependent visa applications with their own timeline.

## Family sponsorship compared

**Golden Visa family coverage:**

Golden Visa holders can sponsor a spouse and children under standard UAE family visa requirements. In some documented cases, parents can be included as dependents, this is not a universal entitlement and depends on current GDRFA and ICP guidance at the time of application. Dependent validity aligns with the principal applicant's 10-year visa term, which means the whole family benefits from the same long renewal window.

**Green Visa family coverage:**

Green Visa holders can sponsor a spouse and children, subject to standard UAE family visa requirements including minimum accommodation size and proof of financial means. The dependent visa aligns with the holder's 5-year term and must be renewed on the same cycle. Parents cannot be sponsored under the Green Visa route in the same way they potentially can under Golden Visa.

**Practical implication:**

For families planning a long-term UAE base, enrolling children in school, buying property, integrating fully, the 10-year Golden Visa window significantly reduces administrative overhead. One 10-year cycle versus two 5-year cycles over the same period, each requiring documentation, medical tests, and fees for every family member. If you have two school-age children and a spouse, the compounded difference in renewal effort and cost over a decade is material.

## Which visa fits your situation: five buyer profiles

**Profile 1: Property investor with AED 2M+ budget**

You are purchasing a UAE apartment or villa as an investment or second home, and the unit registers at AED 2 million or more at DLD. Golden Visa via property is your natural path. The residency is a secondary benefit of the investment. Read [Dubai Property Investment Guide](/guides/dubai-property-investment-guide/) to ensure the unit works as an investment first, visa second.

**Profile 2: Employed professional earning AED 20,000–40,000/month**

You are a manager, engineer, or finance professional working in the UAE. You do not have AED 2 million available for property. Green Visa is your practical option now. You can target Golden Visa later if you accumulate qualifying property or are recognised as exceptional talent.

**Profile 3: Freelancer or consultant earning AED 30,000+/month**

Green Visa via the freelancer track is designed for you. Maintain your UAE freelance permit, document your income, and the 5-year visa gives you full residency independence from any employer. Budget for permit renewal and income verification at each 5-year renewal.

**Profile 4: Business owner with AED 500,000+ invested in a UAE company**

You may qualify for the Green Visa via the business investor track without needing real estate. If your UAE company investment grows or you add property separately above AED 2 million, upgrading to Golden Visa becomes an option.

Profile 5: Relocating family, medium-term horizon, under AED 2M property budget

Start with Green Visa for the professional breadwinner. Sponsor your family under the Green Visa. Use the 5-year window to build UAE savings and market familiarity, then target a qualifying property purchase to upgrade to Golden Visa before the first renewal cycle.

## Upgrading from Green Visa to Golden Visa

Upgrading is straightforward. You do not need to cancel your Green Visa before applying for Golden Visa, you simply apply for Golden Visa and the new status replaces the existing one on approval.

The most common upgrade path: a professional holds a Green Visa while renting in Dubai. Over 3–5 years they accumulate savings and market knowledge, purchase a unit registering at AED 2 million or more at DLD, and apply for Golden Visa. Multiple units can be aggregated toward the AED 2 million threshold.

There is no penalty for upgrading early in your Green Visa cycle. You do not get a refund of unused Green Visa fees, but there is no administrative barrier to switching before the 5-year expiry.

## Common mistakes when choosing between the two

**Assuming Green Visa is "temporary" and Golden Visa is "permanent."** Neither is permanent. Both require renewal. Golden Visa requires renewal every 10 years, Green Visa every 5. Neither grants permanent residency or citizenship.

Buying property purely for the visa without evaluating the investment.  
A unit that qualifies for Golden Visa in a tower with poor rental demand and high service charges can destroy capital over 10 years even while the visa is active. Evaluate the property on its own merits. The visa is a bonus, not the reason to overpay.

**Choosing Green Visa and letting the freelance permit lapse.**  
If you hold Green Visa on the freelancer track and your freelance permit expires without renewal, your qualifying status is compromised. Keep the permit active. Renewal typically costs AED 5,000–15,000 depending on the free zone, which is cheap compared to losing your self-sponsored residency.

**Assuming either visa replaces tax planning.**  
Both Golden and Green Visa provide UAE residency status, not automatic tax residency under your home country's rules. Many UK, EU, and US nationals must still manage home-country tax obligations on worldwide income. Speak to a cross-border tax advisor before treating UAE residency as a tax solution.

**Not checking your occupation code before applying for Green Visa.**  
The skilled employee track requires an ISCO level 1–3 occupation. Not every professional role qualifies. Verify your occupation classification against current MOHRE guidance before applying.
