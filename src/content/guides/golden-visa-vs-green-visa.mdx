## How do Golden Visa and Green Visa compare at a glance?

The UAE Golden Visa is a ten-year renewable self-sponsored permit tied to AED 2 million registered property at DLD or Oqood, while the Green Visa is a five-year renewable permit without a property minimum on skilled, freelance, or investor tracks with fees near AED 1,500 to 1,800 versus AED 4,000 to 5,500 for Golden per principal in 2026.

Invest Gulf buyers compare validity length first because a ten-year family cycle cuts repeat medical and Emirates ID rounds versus two five-year Green renewals over the same decade.

| Dimension | Golden Visa | Green Visa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Validity | 10 years, renewable | 5 years, renewable |
| Property required | Yes on property route (AED 2M registered) | No |
| Self-sponsorship | Yes | Yes |
| Typical government fees | AED 4,000 to 5,500 | AED 1,500 to 1,800 |
| Re-qualification | Less frequent | Every renewal cycle |

## What the Golden Visa actually gives you

The Golden Visa is a ten-year renewable UAE residence permit that requires registered property worth at least AED 2 million at DLD or Oqood on the property route, with government fees typically near AED 4,000 to 5,500 per adult applicant in 2026 including medical fitness and Emirates ID.

Introduced in 2019 and expanded since, the programme remains the longest self-sponsored validity window most property buyers target when holding UAE real estate ten years or more.

What you get once approved:

A 10-year renewable residency stamp. Self-sponsorship, no UAE employer needed. The ability to stay outside the UAE for extended periods without automatic cancellation (subject to re-entry rules, typically at least one entry every six months). Family sponsorship covering spouse, children, and in some documented cases parents. UAE residency status for bank account purposes, school enrolment, and long-term lease contracts.

What you do not get:

Citizenship. UAE passport. Automatic tax residency, that is determined separately under Ministry of Finance guidance tied to the 183-day physical presence rule. Guaranteed rental income on the qualifying property. And critically, you do not get a guarantee that the property investment is sound, the visa and the investment are two separate decisions.

The property route in brief:

The AED 2 million threshold is based on the registered value at DLD or the relevant emirate land department, not the price you negotiated, not the brochure figure, and not the mortgage amount outstanding. Off-plan units registered on Oqood can qualify. Mortgaged properties can qualify under 2026 rule updates, provided a bank NOC is obtained where required. Multiple units in your name can be aggregated if properly documented.

For the full process, documents list, off-plan rules, and mortgage NOC details, read the [UAE Golden Visa Through Property (2026) guide](/guides/golden-visa-mortgage-property-uae/).

Golden Visa property underwriting in 2026 starts at AED 2 million registered value on DLD or Oqood extracts, not brochure payment schedules, with mortgaged units qualifying when bank NOC rules are satisfied and multiple titles aggregating if each deed documents your share. Expect government fees near AED 4,000 to 5,500 per principal applicant plus four percent DLD transfer, about AED 80,000 on a sole AED 2 million registration, before agent commission near two percent. Approved holders receive ten-year renewable residency, self-sponsorship without a UAE employer, family sponsorship for spouse and children, and extended absence rules often cited at one entry every six months. The stamp does not confer citizenship, guaranteed rental yield, or automatic home-country tax relief. Our checklist reviews separate visa eligibility from unit investment quality because immigration approval does not validate exit liquidity or service charge exposure on the qualifying asset.

Golden Visa property route checklist:

1. DLD or Oqood extract shows at least AED 2 million registered value
2. Mortgage NOC obtained when a bank lien applies
3. Government fee reserve of AED 4,000 to 5,500 per adult applicant
4. DLD transfer reserve near four percent of registered value

## What the Green Visa actually gives you

The Green Visa is a five-year renewable UAE residence category that allows self-sponsorship without buying property, with government fees typically near AED 1,500 to 1,800 per principal applicant in 2026 bundles covering medical fitness, Emirates ID, and visa stamping on all four qualifying MOHRE-linked tracks.

Holders receive Emirates ID, bank account access, school enrolment, and long-term lease eligibility while remaining decoupled from a single employer sponsor on skilled, freelance, investor, or graduate routes.

The four qualifying tracks:

1. Skilled employee: working in an occupation classified at ISCO levels 1, 2, or 3 with a valid UAE employment contract and a minimum monthly salary typically cited at AED 15,000 (verify current MOHRE guidance). Your employer does not need to sponsor you, you sponsor yourself with your employment as the qualification evidence.

2. Freelancer or self-employed professional: holding a valid UAE freelance permit or trade licence, with documented annual income of at least AED 360,000 (AED 30,000 per month) or provable financial sufficiency through bank statements. This track is commonly used by consultants, designers, tech professionals, and media workers.

3. Business investor: holding a minimum AED 500,000 stake in an approved UAE company, with an annual turnover meeting the threshold set by relevant authorities. Not to be confused with the property-linked Golden Visa route.

4. Outstanding students: those graduating with distinction from accredited UAE universities or international institutions recognised by the UAE Ministry of Education. This track targets recent graduates, not working professionals.

The renewal obligation:

Every five years you must re-qualify. If you started on the freelancer track, you need a valid freelance permit and continued income evidence at renewal. If your freelance income dropped below AED 360,000 or your permit lapsed, renewal can be denied. This is the Green Visa's key structural difference from Golden Visa, the 10-year Golden Visa window reduces how often you must prove qualifying status.

For the full Green Visa eligibility breakdown, document checklist, and processing steps, read the [UAE Green Visa (2026) guide](/guides/uae-green-visa-freelancer/).

UAE residency planning in 2026 often pairs property due diligence with visa category choice because capital thresholds differ sharply. Golden Visa property route requires AED 2 million registered value plus roughly AED 80,000 DLD transfer at four percent on a sole AED 2 million deed, while Green Visa freelancer route requires AED 360,000 annual income evidence and permit fees that often run AED 5,000 to 15,000 per free zone renewal cycle. Skilled Green tracks cite salary floors from AED 15,000 monthly; business investor Green tracks start at AED 500,000 equity in an approved company. A buyer scenario with AED 30,000 to 50,000 monthly income but no deed yet usually files Green first, then upgrades once DLD value crosses AED 2 million. Our research treats visa validity as family admin cost across one ten-year Golden cycle versus two five-year Green cycles.

## Who qualifies for Golden vs Green Visa?

Golden Visa qualification through property means AED 2 million registered value at DLD or Oqood, while Green Visa qualification means skilled employment with salary often from AED 15,000 monthly, freelance income from AED 360,000 per year, business equity from AED 500,000, or outstanding graduate status with no deed required.

Invest Gulf maps each buyer scenario to evidence packs before typing centre submission so renewals do not fail on occupation codes or lapsed permits.

| Green track | Minimum evidence (verify current rules) |
| --- | --- |
| Skilled employee | ISCO level 1 to 3 job, salary often cited from AED 15,000/month |
| Freelancer | Valid freelance permit plus AED 360,000/year income |
| Business investor | AED 500,000+ stake in qualifying UAE company |
| Outstanding student | Accredited UAE or recognised foreign degree with distinction |

You are a realistic Green Visa candidate if:
- You work in the UAE or plan to, in management, engineering, medicine, law, finance, IT, or similar ISCO level 1 to 3 fields, and your salary meets the threshold.
- You are a freelancer or consultant with provable income above AED 360,000 per year and willing to maintain a UAE freelance permit.
- You are investing at least AED 500,000 in a UAE-registered business without needing the property route.
- You are a recent top-performing graduate from a recognised institution.

Who falls between the two:

The most common situation is a professional earning AED 30,000 to 50,000 per month who does not yet have AED 2 million in UAE property. Green Visa is the immediate option. Golden Visa becomes the medium-term goal if they buy property or are designated exceptional talent.

## How do Golden and Green Visa fees compare?

Golden Visa government bundles typically cost AED 4,000 to 5,500 per principal applicant for medical fitness, Emirates ID, and stamping, while Green Visa bundles typically cost AED 1,500 to 1,800 for the same core government steps in 2026 filings before any typing centre add-ons per adult applicant.

Invest Gulf fee models separate visa typing from property closing so clients do not confuse small government savings with the AED 2 million capital threshold on the Golden property route.

| Fee item | Golden Visa (gov.) | Green Visa (gov.) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medical fitness | Included in bundle | Included |
| Emirates ID | Included | Included |
| Visa stamping / ICP | Approx. AED 4,000 to 5,500 total | Approx. AED 1,500 to 1,800 total |
| Dependent add-on | Per person, similar medical/ID | Per person, similar medical/ID |
| Consultant / typing | AED 500 to 2,000 optional | AED 500 to 2,000 optional |

Typing centre or immigration consultant fees are separate and typically add AED 500 to 2,000 depending on the provider.

Golden Visa fees, government only:

The real cost of Golden Visa via the property route is, of course, the AED 2 million minimum purchase, plus the 4% DLD transfer fee (AED 80,000), agent commission (typically 2%, AED 40,000), and ongoing service charges. Understood in that context, the ~AED 1,500 to 3,500 premium in government fees between Golden and Green is not the significant variable.

Emirates ID pickup after either stamp usually adds three to seven working days, and dependent applications run parallel timelines with separate medical slots. Invest Gulf timeline checklists and red flag reviews assume mortgaged Golden files or Oqood off-plan aggregation can stretch toward three to four weeks.

Green Visa typical timeline:

Approximately 2 to 4 weeks from document submission to Emirates ID collection, assuming documents are complete and the applicant stays in-country during processing. Some applicants report faster turnaround, as few as 10 working days, when using typed and verified applications through GDRFA-linked typing centres. Freelancer files with income below AED 360,000 per year fail renewal even when the first issuance succeeded.

Golden Visa typical timeline:

Between 5 and 15 working days for the visa stamp once documents are complete, according to consistently cited immigration consultant data. Complex cases, multiple properties being aggregated, off-plan units requiring Oqood verification, or mortgaged properties requiring bank NOC, can extend to 3 to 4 weeks.

## How does family sponsorship differ?

Golden Visa family sponsorship allows a spouse and children on a ten-year principal stamp, while Green Visa family sponsorship requires dependent renewal on a five-year cycle tied to holder re-qualification, with parent sponsorship sometimes allowed on Golden routes but generally not on Green filings under current GDRFA guidance.

Invest Gulf family buyer scenarios model four Green renewals per decade versus one Golden cycle when counting medical tests near AED 500 to 800 per adult each round.

| Family item | Golden Visa dependents | Green Visa dependents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Validity alignment | 10-year principal stamp | 5-year principal stamp |
| Medical retest cadence | About every 10 years | About every 5 years |
| Typical medical fee | AED 500 to 800 per adult | AED 500 to 800 per adult |
| Parent sponsorship | Sometimes allowed | Generally not allowed |

Golden Visa holders can sponsor a spouse and children under standard UAE family visa requirements. In some documented cases, parents can be included as dependents, this is not a universal entitlement and depends on current GDRFA and ICP guidance at the time of application. Dependent validity aligns with the principal applicant's 10-year visa term, which means the whole family benefits from the same long renewal window.

**Green Visa family coverage:**

Green Visa holders can sponsor a spouse and children, subject to standard UAE family visa requirements including minimum accommodation size and proof of financial means. The dependent visa aligns with the holder's 5-year term and must be renewed on the same cycle. Parents cannot be sponsored under the Green Visa route in the same way they potentially can under Golden Visa.

**Practical implication:**

For families planning a long-term UAE base, enrolling children in school, buying property, integrating fully, the 10-year Golden Visa window significantly reduces administrative overhead. One 10-year cycle versus two 5-year cycles over the same period, each requiring documentation, medical tests, and fees for every family member. If you have two school-age children and a spouse, the compounded difference in renewal effort and cost over a decade is material.

## How do you upgrade from Green to Golden Visa?

Upgrading from Green Visa to Golden Visa means filing a new Golden application once registered UAE property reaches AED 2 million at DLD or Oqood, with aggregated titles allowed when deeds document combined value and no Green cancellation required before Golden approval replaces the active permit in most 2026 filings.

Our clients on freelancer Green tracks often time upgrades after remote SPA completion and first Oqood registration crossing AED 2 million registered value. Unused Green government fees are not refunded if you switch in year two of a five-year term.

| Upgrade step | Typical timing |
| --- | --- |
| DLD or Oqood shows AED 2 million | Day 0 evidence |
| Golden file submission | 1 to 3 working days |
| Golden visa stamp | 5 to 15 working days |
| Dependent Golden files | 5 to 10 working days each |

There is no penalty for upgrading early in your Green Visa cycle. You do not get a refund of unused Green Visa fees, but there is no administrative barrier to switching before the 5-year expiry. Gather DLD title or Oqood proof, passport copies, medical fitness, and dependent files in one bundle to avoid a gap between permits. Property disposal before Golden renewal can affect eligibility, so treat the deed as part of a ten-year hold plan, not a six-month flip.

## What common mistakes should you avoid when picking a visa route?

Common visa route mistakes in 2026 include treating brochure prices as AED 2 million DLD evidence, letting Green freelance permits lapse while income still exceeds AED 360,000 per year, and assuming Golden or Green stamps remove home-country tax filings without cross-border advice from a qualified adviser before MOHRE rejects the file.

Our red flag reviews catch ISCO occupation mismatches on skilled Green files before MOHRE rejection.

1. Treating brochure prices as AED 2 million DLD evidence without a title extract
2. Letting a Green freelance permit lapse while income still exceeds AED 360,000 per year
3. Skipping cross-border tax advice after either Golden or Green approval
4. Applying for skilled Green Visa without verifying ISCO level 1 to 3 occupation codes

**Choosing Green Visa and letting the freelance permit lapse.**
If you hold Green Visa on the freelancer track and your freelance permit expires without renewal, your qualifying status is compromised. Keep the permit active. Renewal typically costs AED 5,000 to 15,000 depending on the free zone, which is cheap compared to losing your self-sponsored residency.

**Assuming either visa replaces tax planning.**
Both Golden and Green Visa provide UAE residency status, not automatic tax residency under your home country's rules. Many UK, EU, and US nationals must still manage home-country tax obligations on worldwide income. Speak to a cross-border tax advisor before treating UAE residency as a tax solution.

**Not checking your occupation code before applying for Green Visa.**
The skilled employee track requires an ISCO level 1 to 3 occupation. Not every professional role qualifies. Verify your occupation classification against current MOHRE guidance before applying.
