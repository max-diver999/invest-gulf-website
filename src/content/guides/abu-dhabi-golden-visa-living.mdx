Abu Dhabi Golden Visa living through property typically means AED 2 million registered freehold value under Law 13/2019 zones, a 10-year renewable residency track, and family sponsorship once ICP or GDRFA approves the file. Daily costs still match employment-visa life: mid-tier families often spend AED 34,000 to 38,500 monthly when schools, cars, ADD utilities, and self-paid DOH insurance are included. Abu Dhabi property transfer is commonly 2 percent on registered value, not Dubai's 4 percent DLD fee, which changes year-one cash even at the same purchase ticket. Mortgage paths may qualify with a UAE bank NOC when registered value clears the threshold, but brochure starting prices do not. Selling the qualifying unit without a replacement plan remains the main renewal red flag across a 10-year horizon.

Golden Visa holders in Abu Dhabi typically still need a separate MOHRE work permit for local employment, because residence status is not an automatic labour right. Self-funded DOH insurance often runs AED 8,000 to 25,000 yearly for a family on enhanced networks when no employer package exists. Owner-occupiers on Yas or Saadiyat should model service charges of AED 2,000 to 6,000 monthly plus handover fit-out of AED 50,000 to 150,000 on completed stock. Remote workers buying Reem or Yas one-to-two bedroom units at AED 2 million-plus for qualification still face the same grocery, car, and school economics as salaried neighbours. Keep 12 months of clean bank statements for renewal even without a UAE salary trail, and align dependant visas before May to July ADEK enrollment peaks.

Golden Visa living in Abu Dhabi typically changes sponsorship and tenure, not the price of rent, ADEK schools, or ADD bills for foreign buyers in this market. Job loss no longer auto-cancels residency the way a 2 to 3 year employment visa can, provided renewal rules and qualifying property ownership stay intact.

| Family sponsorship | Employer salary thresholds | Golden sponsor rules |

The AED 2 million property threshold typically means registered freehold value in Law 13/2019 zones, not marketing starting prices, for foreign buyers seeking Golden Visa residence. Abu Dhabi transfer fees near 2 percent do not count toward the AED 2 million floor, and mortgaged cases need written ICP treatment before deposit.
