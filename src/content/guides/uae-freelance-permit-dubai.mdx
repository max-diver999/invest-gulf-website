## What is a UAE freelance permit and why does free zone choice matter?

A UAE freelance permit is a free zone licence that allows self-employed professionals to invoice clients and sponsor residency, with annual fees commonly AED 7,500 to AED 15,000. Invest Gulf treats activity match, five-year cost, and bank acceptance as three filters before zone choice. Foreign buyers linking property residency later still need a clean licence path.

Free zone choice matters for three reasons. First, activities: each free zone publishes its own approved activity list. Second, cost: annual fees range from AED 7,500 to over AED 20,000, and the five-year gap can exceed AED 60,000. Third, banking: TECOM GoFreelance, [DMCC](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/), and DIFC carry stronger account-opening credibility than newer zones.

## How does GoFreelance (TECOM) work in 2026?

GoFreelance is TECOM media and technology freelance platform, with single-activity permits that typically cost AED 9,500 to AED 11,000 per year including e-channel setup. Invest Gulf recommends it when work sits in writing, design, software, or production. Foreign buyers outside those activities should not force a TECOM filing for brand alone.

Who GoFreelance is for: journalism and writing, photography and videography, graphic design, digital marketing, software development, IT consulting, animation, music production, and film production.

Application checklist:

1. Register on the GoFreelance portal and select your primary activity
2. Upload passport (6 months validity), photo, and CV or portfolio
3. Pay the annual fee online
4. Receive permit and trade licence number within 5 to 7 days
5. Open a UAE corporate bank account
6. Apply for the residence visa through TECOM or a typing centre

A second activity often adds AED 1,000 to AED 2,000. Legal, financial advisory, healthcare, engineering, education, and real estate usually need mainland DED or specialist zones instead. See the [Emirates ID application guide](/guides/emirates-id-application-guide/) for life-admin sequencing.

## Which Dubai free zones issue freelance permits?

Dubai free zones that issue freelance permits include Meydan from about AED 7,500 to AED 9,000 and GoFreelance campuses that typically cost AED 9,500 to AED 14,000. Invest Gulf shortlists by activity first, then processing time of 5 to 15 days. Foreign buyers in general consulting usually start with Meydan packages.

Meydan accepts consulting, marketing, HR, training, e-commerce, design, and general business services. No physical office is required; flexi-desk is typically included. Processing is often 7 to 10 days. Banks may still ask for 3 to 6 months of trading history before approving accounts.

### Dubai Multi Commodities Centre (DMCC)

DMCC issues individual trader and freelance permits for commodities and professional services. Annual fees of AED 14,000 to AED 18,000 buy stronger international banking recognition, with processing up to 15 days and NOC requirements if you already hold a UAE work visa.

### Dubai Production City, Internet City, and Media City

DPC permits often sit near AED 10,500 to AED 12,000. DIC via GoFreelance is commonly AED 10,000 to AED 12,000 for technology activities. DMC via GoFreelance is commonly AED 11,000 to AED 14,000 for journalism, PR, events, and content.

| GoFreelance / DIC | 10,000 to 12,000 | Technology, IT | 5 to 7 days | Excellent |

**Citable factual block.** Dubai freelance permit pricing in 2026 clusters into clear bands that Invest Gulf uses before clients pick a free zone: Meydan single-activity packages commonly AED 7,500 to AED 9,000, GoFreelance TECOM media and technology permits roughly AED 9,500 to AED 14,000 depending on campus, and DMCC individual permits often AED 14,000 to AED 18,000 with heavier compliance review. Processing is typically 5 to 7 days on GoFreelance, 7 to 10 days at Meydan, and up to 15 days at DMCC once documents are complete, before residence visa steps add another 2 to 4 weeks. Green Visa freelancers still need AED 360,000 annual income for 5 year self-sponsorship even after the permit is issued. Foreign buyers should match activity lists first because a cheap zone that banks reject for your profession is not a saving.

## What documents do you need for a Dubai freelance permit?

Documents for a Dubai freelance permit typically include a passport with 6 months validity, a recent photo, and a 1 to 2 page CV showing relevant experience. Invest Gulf also prepares portfolio samples for TECOM filings and employer NOCs on labour visas. Foreign buyers applying from outside the UAE usually skip the NOC step.

Document checklist:

- Passport copy, minimum 6 months validity
- Passport photograph, white background, recent
- CV or professional profile, typically 1 to 2 pages
- Portfolio or work samples for TECOM zones; Meydan often does not require these
- NOC from current UAE employer if on an active employment visa
- UAE entry stamp or visa copy if applying in-country
- Health insurance before residence visa stamping, not always at permit stage

DMCC may request extra AML documents for finance-related activities. Some zones ask for brief business plans or client references for events or financial consulting.

## How to get the residence visa after your freelance permit?

Residence visa after a freelance permit requires a fixed UAE sequence: entry permit, medical fitness in 3 to 5 days, Emirates ID biometrics, then visa stamping. Invest Gulf budgets government fees near AED 1,500 to AED 1,800 on top of the free zone permit. Foreign buyers should activate health insurance before stamping day.

Visa steps checklist:

1. Entry permit from the free zone authority
2. Medical fitness test at an ICA-approved clinic (blood test and chest X-ray)
3. Emirates ID biometrics; card often issues in 5 to 10 days
4. Visa stamping or digital visa record
5. Health insurance activation (Daman or UAE-licensed insurer)

Once Emirates ID and residence visa are ready, you can open a personal bank account, register vehicles, sign tenancy contracts, and sponsor family subject to income and accommodation rules. See the [Emirates ID application guide](/guides/emirates-id-application-guide/) and [UAE Green Visa for Freelancers](/guides/uae-green-visa-freelancer/) for the AED 360,000 income path.

## Freelance permit vs mainland DED licence: which fits?

Freelance permit versus mainland DED means choosing speed against mainland access: free zone permits often cost AED 7,500 to AED 15,000 per year, while DED setup can run AED 15,000 to AED 30,000. Invest Gulf keeps freelancers on free zone licences for 1 to 3 years. Foreign buyers with mainland government revenue should price DED earlier.

Mainland makes sense when:

- Over half of revenue comes from UAE mainland government or semi-government entities
- Your activity requires mainland DED approval
- You want to hire UAE-resident employees under a mainland entity

## What you can invoice and how bank accounts work?

Invoicing on a UAE freelance permit allows VAT-compliant bills, with VAT registration voluntary below AED 375,000 turnover and mandatory above that threshold. Invest Gulf sees many freelancers stay below the mandatory band for years. Foreign buyers should compare bank minimums of AED 25,000 to AED 50,000 with digital plans near AED 99.

UAE corporate bank accounts for freelancers are available at Emirates NBD, Mashreq, FAB, RAKBANK, and Wio Bank. Stripe, Wise, and Payoneer generally accept Meydan or TECOM trade licences for business verification.

## What mistakes delay Dubai freelance permits?

Mistakes that delay Dubai freelance permits typically include filing GoFreelance without samples, applying on an expired visa, and ignoring employer NOC rules on a labour visa. Invest Gulf rejects incomplete TECOM packs before payment because resubmission can add 7 days. Foreign buyers should sort visa status first, then upload one clean batch.

Delay checklist:

- Prepare portfolio evidence before starting GoFreelance
- Exit and re-enter or cancel prior residence formally if status is broken
- Obtain employer NOC before parallel freelance filing
- Compare visa types in the [UAE Residency Visa Types Guide](/guides/golden-visa-vs-dubai-residence-visa/)

Employment and renewal paths for freelancers require employer NOC before converting from a labour visa, plus renewal sync across permit and Emirates ID. Invest Gulf typically tracks 60-day reminders because missed windows trigger bank holds. Foreign buyers should price renewal drops of about 10% to 20% after year one.

Factor health insurance of AED 2,000 to AED 5,000 per year for all visa holders.

## Who should choose a freelance permit?

Choosing a freelance permit makes sense for solo professionals who need a UAE trade licence without a full company, especially when annual fees of AED 7,500 to AED 15,000 beat mainland setup. Invest Gulf matches issuing body to profession and client geography. Foreign buyers aiming at Green Visa still need AED 360,000 income proof typically.

Reference visa costs often seen beside freelance filing: employment visa AED 3,000 to AED 7,000 in 10 to 20 days, Green Visa AED 2,800 to AED 5,500 in 10 to 15 days, medical AED 300 to AED 500, Emirates ID AED 370, and overstay fines AED 100 per day after grace periods.

**Insider tip:** Invest Gulf clients pick the free zone whose activity list a relationship manager at their target bank already recognises, even if the annual fee is AED 2,000 to AED 4,000 higher than the cheapest Meydan quote.
