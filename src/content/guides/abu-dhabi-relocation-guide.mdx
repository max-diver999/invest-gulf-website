    answer: "Abu Dhabi suits families who prefer a quieter pace, lower traffic density, and strong public-sector job market. Dubai offers more lifestyle breadth, a larger expat pool, and more school choice. Abu Dhabi rents are generally lower than Dubai for equivalent space. The right choice depends heavily on where your employer is located and your children's school needs."
  - question: "How long does a UAE work visa for Abu Dhabi take?"
    answer: "From offer letter to active Emirates ID: typically 6–12 weeks including document attestation (4–8 weeks) and in-country processing (2–4 weeks). Employer PRO processes vary, some expedite with DHA fitness medical; others have slower government department queues. Finance ministry and federal government employer sponsorship routes add compliance layers."
  - question: "What is the minimum salary to sponsor dependents in Abu Dhabi?"
    answer: "Dependent sponsorship thresholds are set by MOHRE and vary by visa category. As a general benchmark, AED 3,000–4,000 monthly basic salary has historically been cited for dependent sponsorship, verify current MOHRE rules as these change. Accommodation proof is also required. Start PRO conversation at offer stage."
  - question: "Can foreigners buy property in Abu Dhabi?"
    answer: "Yes in designated investment zones, also called freehold or leasehold areas. Saadiyat Island, Yas Island, Al Reem Island, Al Raha Beach, Masdar City, and others are designated. Outside investment zones, foreign ownership is not permitted. Purchase in a designated zone from AED 2M can qualify for Golden Visa (verify current ICA thresholds)."
  - question: "What are the best areas to live in Abu Dhabi as an expat?"
    answer: "Saadiyat Island for culture, premium lifestyle and beach proximity. Yas Island for entertainment, outlet shopping and families near ADNOC/government campuses. Al Reem Island for towers, young professionals and Metro connectivity. Khalidiyah and Al Mushrif for villa belt families. Al Reef for value. Choice depends on employer location and school route."
  - question: "How much cash do I need for the first month in Abu Dhabi?"
    answer: "Budget AED 10,000–20,000 as a float for the first month, excluding rent deposit. This covers temporary accommodation, SIM card, car deposit, Emirates ID processing fees, groceries, and initial transport. Rent deposits typically equal one to three months rent. School registration fees (if applicable) add AED 1,000–3,000 per child."
  - question: "Is healthcare free in Abu Dhabi for expats?"
    answer: "Not free but mandatory employer-provided health insurance applies to most employed residents. The DOH (Department of Health) mandates basic coverage. Quality of private hospitals in Abu Dhabi (Cleveland Clinic, Burjeel, SEHA network) is high. Maternity and dental are often rider coverage, verify your employer policy network before choosing a hospital."
  - question: "What is the difference between Abu Dhabi Golden Visa and regular work residency?"
    answer: "Regular work residency is employer-sponsored, typically 2 years renewable, and cancels if you leave the employer. Golden Visa is self-sponsored, 10 years renewable, and not tied to an employer. Golden Visa requires either AED 2M+ property investment, qualifying talent criteria, or other approved categories. Abu Dhabi has Golden Visa property qualifying zones."

### 2. Document attestation

Required documents typically:
- Degree certificates: home notary → foreign ministry → UAE Embassy in home country
- Marriage and birth certificates (for family visa)
- Police clearance (employer-dependent)
- Medical fitness (DHA-approved clinic on arrival in UAE)

Start 8–10 weeks before departure. Attestation chains vary by country, Gulf and South Asian documents often faster; European and North American add time.

### 3. School research

Abu Dhabi's international school market is regulated by ADEK. Curricula: British, American, Indian CBSE/ICSE, IB.

Apply before arrival for popular year groups. Saadiyat Island and Yas Island campuses fill quickly for September intake.

### 4. Housing strategy

Rule: rent for 6–12 months first unless you are executing a property purchase as part of a Golden Visa strategy.

Key areas to research pre-arrival:
- Saadiyat Island, beach access, cultural district, premium rent, excellent schools
- **Yas Island**, families, entertainment, outlet mall, schools nearby
- Al Reem Island, value towers, young professionals, growing amenities
- **Khalidiyah**, established, central, near American Embassy, quiet streets
- Al Mushrif / Karama, villa belt, families, slightly away from centre

## FAQ

Is Abu Dhabi or Dubai better for families?
Abu Dhabi for quieter pace, lower traffic, government jobs, strong schools. Dubai for more school choice, more lifestyle breadth, larger expat scene. Employer location is usually the deciding factor.

How long does a UAE work visa take?
6–12 weeks total including attestation. PRO processes on-ground: 2–4 weeks. Start attestation 10 weeks before expected arrival.

What salary is needed to sponsor dependents?
Approximately AED 3,000–4,000 basic monthly cited historically, verify current MOHRE rules as they evolve.

Can foreigners buy property in Abu Dhabi?
Yes in designated investment zones, Saadiyat, Yas, Al Reem, Al Raha, others. Purchase above AED 2M may qualify for Golden Visa.

Best areas for expat families?
Saadiyat for beach and culture; Yas for entertainment and family amenities; Al Reem for value towers and Metro proximity.

How much cash for the first month?
AED 10,000–20,000 float excluding rent deposit covers temp accommodation, transport, groceries, and admin fees.

Is healthcare good in Abu Dhabi?
Yes, DOH-regulated private network including Cleveland Clinic is high quality. Employer insurance mandatory for most employed residents.

What is the Golden Visa?
10-year self-sponsored residency for investors, talent, and qualifying categories. Property path from AED 2M in designated zones.
