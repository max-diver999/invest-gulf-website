# Abu Dhabi Relocation Guide 2026: Visas, Housing, Schools & First 90 Days

**TL;DR:** Abu Dhabi is the **UAE's capital and largest emirate** — government hubs, oil majors, cultural institutions, and family-oriented neighbourhoods. Your move runs on **MOHRE work visas**, **Emirates ID**, and strong employer PRO support. Housing choices span **Saadiyat Island** (beach, culture), **Yas Island** (entertainment, families), and **Al Reem** (value towers). International schools are good but the pool is smaller than Dubai. Budget **AED 10,000–20,000** liquid for the first month. Do not sign a 12-month lease before you know your school route and commute.

> **Disclaimer:** UAE immigration and property rules change. This guide is operational guidance, not legal advice. Confirm visa category, property eligibility, and residency thresholds with MOHRE and a licensed UAE adviser.

## Abu Dhabi vs Dubai — the relocation choice

Most UAE newcomers compare Abu Dhabi and Dubai. The two cities sit 130 km apart but feel meaningfully different.

| Factor | Abu Dhabi | Dubai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pace | Quieter, capital-city formality | Faster, commercial energy |
| Job market | Government, oil, federal ministries | Finance, trade, tech, tourism |
| Schools | Solid but fewer | Largest Gulf school pool |
| Rent | Generally 10–20% lower than Dubai | Higher across most categories |
| Traffic | Less intense than Dubai | Dense during peak hours |
| Nightlife / entertainment | Growing — Yas, Saadiyat events | Extensive |
| Property foreign buy | Investment zones only | Widespread freehold |
| Language | Arabic official; English works everywhere | English-dominant commercially |

The right base is usually determined by **where your employer is** — not where you want to live. ADNOC, ADNEC, Abu Dhabi government entities, the Louvre, Guggenheim project, Cleveland Clinic, and Mubadala are all Abu Dhabi anchors. If your employer is in Abu Dhabi, commuting from Dubai is possible but 90–120 minutes each way on E11 at peak times is not sustainable.

## Pre-arrival checklist — 8–12 weeks out

Start your paperwork 10–12 weeks before departure. UAE attestation chains are not fast.

### 1. Contract review

| Line item | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Basic salary vs. total package | What counts for dependent sponsorship threshold? |
| Housing allowance or accommodation | Apartment vs villa in your budget area? |
| School allowance | Per child cap? Curriculum restrictions? |
| Medical insurance | Family included? Network includes Saadiyat/Yas hospitals? |
| Annual flights | How many seats, for whom? |
| Abu Dhabi gratuity (EOSB) | UAE Labour Law formula — 21 days per year under 5 years |

### 2. Document attestation

Required documents typically:
- Degree certificates: home notary → foreign ministry → UAE Embassy in home country
- Marriage and birth certificates (for family visa)
- Police clearance (employer-dependent)
- Medical fitness (DHA-approved clinic on arrival in UAE)

Start **8–10 weeks before** departure. Attestation chains vary by country — Gulf and South Asian documents often faster; European and North American add time.

### 3. School research

Abu Dhabi's international school market is regulated by ADEK. Curricula: British, American, Indian CBSE/ICSE, IB.

Apply **before arrival** for popular year groups. Saadiyat Island and Yas Island campuses fill quickly for September intake.

→ [Abu Dhabi international schools full guide](/guides/abu-dhabi-international-schools-guide/)

### 4. Housing strategy

Rule: **rent for 6–12 months first** unless you are executing a property purchase as part of a Golden Visa strategy.

Key areas to research pre-arrival:
- **Saadiyat Island** — beach access, cultural district, premium rent, excellent schools
- **Yas Island** — families, entertainment, outlet mall, schools nearby
- **Al Reem Island** — value towers, young professionals, growing amenities
- **Khalidiyah** — established, central, near American Embassy, quiet streets
- **Al Mushrif / Karama** — villa belt, families, slightly away from centre

## Visa pathways — work residency in Abu Dhabi

### Standard employment visa (MOHRE sponsored)

The dominant path for private-sector employees:

1. Employer obtains visa approval from MOHRE
2. Employee receives entry permit (or enters on existing UAE status)
3. Medical fitness test at DHA-approved centre
4. Biometrics at GDRFA (or AMER centre for some categories)
5. Emirates ID card issued (typically 2–4 weeks)
6. Residence visa stamped in passport

Duration: **2 years** for most private sector; renewable.

**Family joining:** Sponsor dependents once your visa is activated. Salary threshold, accommodation size proof, and relationship documents required. Start PRO conversation **at offer acceptance** — not after relocation.

### Abu Dhabi government/federal employee visa

Abu Dhabi government employers (ADNOC, ADNEC, Health Authority, Education Council) often route through federal government channels. Timeline can be longer due to security and qualification checks. HR/PRO handles most steps.

### Golden Visa — 10-year self-sponsored residency

For investors, qualified talent, and property owners:

- **Property investment path:** AED 2M+ in designated investment zones (Saadiyat, Yas, Al Reem, Al Raha, others) — verify current ICA thresholds
- **Talent path:** Researchers, doctors, engineers, scientists under qualifying categories
- **Not employer-tied:** stays valid if you change jobs

## First 90 days — week-by-week operational map

### Week 1 — Survival mode

- Temporary hotel or serviced apartment (Corniche area, Al Reem Island serviced buildings)
- SIM: Etisalat (e&) or du — passport + visa copy at counter
- Cash float AED 3,000–5,000 for first week (cards widely accepted but early friction)
- Map nearest Carrefour, Lulu, Waitrose (Saadiyat), ADNOC petrol
- WhatsApp connection with employer PRO — this is your operational lifeline

### Weeks 2–4 — Administrative core

- Complete medical test and biometrics
- Emirates ID collected or tracked online (ICP website or app)
- Bank account opening → [Abu Dhabi banking for expats](/guides/abu-dhabi-banking-expats/)
- Begin long-term housing search: connect ADDC for utilities understanding
- School placement assessment if children

### Weeks 5–8 — Stabilisation

- Long-term rental signed and ADDC account activated → [Abu Dhabi utilities ADDC](/guides/abu-dhabi-utilities-addc/)
- Car: buy or short-term lease; convert licence or apply for Abu Dhabi licence → [Abu Dhabi driving guide](/guides/abu-dhabi-driving-guide/)
- Register children in school; confirm bus route
- GP registration within your medical insurance network → [Abu Dhabi healthcare guide](/guides/abu-dhabi-healthcare-guide/)

### Weeks 9–12 — Integration

- Join clubs and community groups (Abu Dhabi Rugby, Hash House Harriers, expat Facebook groups)
- Explore weekend life: Yas Marina, Corniche waterfront, Sir Bani Yas Island trips, Liwa desert
- Compare actual spend vs budget → [Abu Dhabi cost of living full data](/guides/abu-dhabi-cost-of-living/)
- If Golden Visa intended: title deed review and property lawyer engagement

## Schools — ADEK regulated, smaller pool than Dubai

Abu Dhabi's school market is supervised by ADEK (Abu Dhabi Department of Education and Knowledge). Key points:

- Inspection ratings published annually by ADEK — check before shortlisting
- British GCSE/A-Level, American AP, IB, Indian CBSE available
- International schools with strong ADEK ratings fill early — apply 6 months before intake
- Fees generally lower than equivalent Dubai campuses

Saadiyat International School, GEMS World Academy Abu Dhabi, Brighton College Abu Dhabi, ADNOC Schools, and Al Yasmina Academy are frequently named by expat families.

## Driving — licence conversion and road culture

Abu Dhabi traffic is calmer than Dubai at most hours except morning school rush and Corniche evenings on weekends.

- Most GCC and many European/North American licences convert directly — check ADPC list
- Indian/Pakistani/Asian licences: may require test regardless
- International Driving Permit: acceptable on tourist entry, not as replacement for residency licence

## Culture and lifestyle — Abu Dhabi pace

Abu Dhabi rewards expats who engage with its cultural ambitions. The city hosts:

- **Louvre Abu Dhabi** (Saadiyat Island) — world-class collection
- **Yas Marina Circuit** — F1 Grand Prix, year-round events
- **Corniche waterfront** — 8 km beachfront promenade
- **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque** — architectural landmark, open to visitors
- **Mangrove National Park** — kayaking, cycling

Alcohol: licensed hotels, clubs, and restaurants only. Not sold in supermarkets for home consumption in Abu Dhabi (unlike some other emirates).

Dress code: more conservative expectations than Dubai — modest dress in public areas, malls, and government buildings expected.

## Red flags — stop and verify

If any of these arise, pause and get written answers:

- Employer PRO processing "takes 3 months" without clear explanation — ask specific step causing delay

- School "guarantees seat" without written acceptance letter and year group confirmation
- Property agent quotes Golden Visa eligibility on a building outside designated zones

## FAQ

**Is Abu Dhabi or Dubai better for families?**
Abu Dhabi for quieter pace, lower traffic, government jobs, strong schools. Dubai for more school choice, more lifestyle breadth, larger expat scene. Employer location is usually the deciding factor.

**How long does a UAE work visa take?**
6–12 weeks total including attestation. PRO processes on-ground: 2–4 weeks. Start attestation 10 weeks before expected arrival.

**What salary is needed to sponsor dependents?**
Approximately AED 3,000–4,000 basic monthly cited historically — verify current MOHRE rules as they evolve.

**Can foreigners buy property in Abu Dhabi?**
Yes in designated investment zones — Saadiyat, Yas, Al Reem, Al Raha, others. Purchase above AED 2M may qualify for Golden Visa.

**Best areas for expat families?**
Saadiyat for beach and culture; Yas for entertainment and family amenities; Al Reem for value towers and Metro proximity.

**How much cash for the first month?**
AED 10,000–20,000 float excluding rent deposit covers temp accommodation, transport, groceries, and admin fees.

**Is healthcare good in Abu Dhabi?**
Yes — DOH-regulated private network including Cleveland Clinic is high quality. Employer insurance mandatory for most employed residents.

**What is the Golden Visa?**
10-year self-sponsored residency for investors, talent, and qualifying categories. Property path from AED 2M in designated zones.
