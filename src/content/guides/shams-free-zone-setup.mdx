## What is SHAMS at a glance?

SHAMS (Sharjah Media City) is a Sharjah free zone aimed at creatives, freelancers, and lean media agencies. Year-one packages often land **AED 5,500 to 8,500** licence-only or **AED 8,500 to 14,000** with one visa once KYC is clean. Standard media licences commonly issue in **3 to 7 business days**, but banking and first visa biometrics usually add **2 to 8 weeks** after the licence PDF arrives.

- Lowest entry cost: promotional packages from roughly AED 5,500-8,500 all-in for year one
- Media-first activity catalogue: production, marketing, design, influencer management
- Freelancer friendly: solo FZE structures widely used
- Fast turnaround: 3-7 business days for standard applications with clean KYC
- 100% foreign ownership: no local sponsor required inside the free zone
- Visa bundles on upgraded tiers: from zero-visa licence-only to multi-visa packages

SHAMS is rarely the right zone for port logistics, commodities trading, or regulated finance. Compare [IFZA company setup](/guides/ifza-company-setup/) for e-commerce breadth, [Meydan Free Zone setup](/guides/meydan-free-zone-setup/) for Dubai jurisdiction at mid-tier cost, and [RAKEZ company setup](/guides/rakez-company-setup/) for warehouse and industrial options in the northern emirates.
## How does SHAMS activity licensing compare to other UAE zones?

Activities requiring DFSA-regulated finance, regulated healthcare, or mainland-only professions may need a different free zone or mainland licence. Match your invoice activity to the SHAMS activity code at application stage so banking and corporate tax reviews stay aligned with media or consultancy revenue, not trading labels you cannot support in KYC.

## SHAMS vs IFZA vs Meydan: Which Free Zone Fits?

Choose SHAMS when: you are a creator, freelancer, or media agency optimising for lowest year-one cost, Sharjah jurisdiction is acceptable, and your revenue is services or content not heavy goods trading.

Choose IFZA when: you need general trading or e-commerce as the core activity and accept Fujairah address on the licence.

Choose Meydan when: you want a Dubai free zone address at mid-tier pricing for e-commerce or media with broader trading codes.

For mainland market access rules, read [UAE free zone vs mainland](/guides/uae-free-zone-vs-mainland/).

### Step 2: Reserve Company Name

Names must comply with UAE conventions. SHAMS often approves clean names within one business day. Avoid restricted financial and royal terms without approval.

### Step 3: Submit Shareholder KYC

Individual shareholders provide passport copy, proof of address, and short CV or portfolio for media activities. Corporate shareholders add incorporation documents and UBO disclosure.

### Step 4: Pay Fees and Pass Compliance Review

SHAMS conducts AML checks. Pay package fee once approved in principle.

### Step 5: Receive Corporate Documents

Trade licence, incorporation certificate, MOA, and share certificates support banking and visas.

### Step 6: Open Corporate Bank Account

SHAMS entities face more KYC questions than DMCC for trading-heavy models. Media founders should prepare:

- Portfolio, showreel, or client contract samples
- Expected client countries and payment channels
- Personal bank statements for shareholders

Allow **2-8 weeks** for account opening. See [opening a bank account in Dubai](/guides/open-bank-account-dubai/).

### Step 7: Apply for Visas (If Included)

Activate establishment card, then process employment or investor visas. Medical and Emirates ID require UAE presence.

## Is SHAMS Right for Your Business?

- You are a freelancer, creator, or media agency optimising cost
- Year-one budget target is under AED 15,000 including one visa
- Services and content dominate revenue, not container logistics
- Sharjah jurisdiction is acceptable to your clients and banks
- You want the fastest cheap path to a UAE corporate licence

SHAMS is likely not the right choice when:

- You need JLT or Dubai free zone address on every invoice (consider Meydan or DMCC)
- General trading or e-commerce is core (consider IFZA)
- You need warehouse or industrial space (consider RAKEZ or JAFZA)
- Your bank mandates DMCC or DIFC specifically
- Most revenue comes from mainland UAE retail consumers
