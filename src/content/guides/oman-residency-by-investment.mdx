## What is Oman investment residency (and what is it not)?

Oman investment residency is renewable residence tied to maintaining roughly OMR 250,000 in qualifying investment, often ITC freehold, and Invest Gulf stresses it is not citizenship or automatic work rights for foreign buyers. ROP rules change over multi-year renewals, so the category name on your file matters more than brochure visa slogans.

Investment residency is not:

- Omani citizenship, or a mainstream path to it for property buyers
- Unrestricted work rights for you or your spouse without a separate employer permit (confirm current official rules)
- Guaranteed approval because you bought an apartment in an ITC brochure
- A substitute for an employer visa while you work full-time for an Omani entity
- Dubai Golden Visa liquidity: Oman resale markets are thinner
- Transferable to a tenant; the next buyer must qualify independently
- Not a 30 day visa product; plan several months end-to-end
