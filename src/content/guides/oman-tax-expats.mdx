## How does Oman local tax work for expats?

Oman local tax for expats typically means 0% personal income tax on employment salary, with no payroll withholding and no annual Oman personal return for typical employees. Corporate profits can face about 15% entity-level tax. Invest Gulf clients in this market still model home-country worldwide tax before celebrating a Muscat offer.

Employer payroll: no Oman income tax withholding on payslips. Deductions may include loan or insurance co-pay, not state income tax.

## How does CRS work for expats in Oman?

CRS typically means Omani banks identify reportable persons and exchange account balances and income with partner jurisdictions, so a UK or German tax resident in Muscat should assume home authorities may see year-end balances. Self-certification must be accurate. Invest Gulf banking checklists in this market keep signed CRS forms for at least 7 years.

### What gets reported (typical)

- Name, address, tax identification number (TIN)
- Account numbers
- Year-end balance
- Interest, dividends, gross proceeds
