## DMT registration: Abu Dhabi's Oqood equivalent

Every off-plan SPA must register with DMT:

1. Sign SPA with developer
2. Pay registration fee (2% of purchase price at transfer; confirm off-plan registration charges on SPA)
3. Receive DMT registration confirmation
4. Deposits held per Abu Dhabi escrow regulations

Unregistered contract = no DMT protection. Same severity as missing Oqood in Dubai.

## Aldar SPA checklist: before you wire the deposit

Abu Dhabi off-plan risk concentrates in **contract terms**, not headline launch price. Read the SPA for:

| Clause | What to verify |
|---|---|
| DMT registration timeline | Registration within 30 days of signing |
| Handover date | Fixed date vs "target", penalty if delayed |
| Service charge estimate | AED/sqft in writing, not marketing brochure |
| Assignment rights | Permitted before handover? Fee? |
| Escrow account | Named ADCB/FAB account matching Aldar disclosure |

If the broker cannot produce **DMT registration receipt** within the SPA window, pause, unregistered deposits lack the same protection as Dubai Oqood.
