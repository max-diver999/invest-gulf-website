## How Dubai fines are issued

Dubai traffic fines are issued by Dubai Police, not RTA. RTA manages licences and vehicle registration; Dubai Police manages road violations. Both systems link to your traffic file number, which is tied to your Emirates ID and vehicle plate.

Fine notification channels:

| Channel | When |
| --- | --- |
| SMS to registered mobile | Within 24 hours of camera capture |
| Dubai Police app push notification | If notifications enabled |
| Email to registered address | If email linked to vehicle |
| Manual police stop | Immediate on-road issue |

Radar fines can arrive weeks after the incident, SMS is the fastest notification. Register your mobile number with Dubai Police and RTA immediately after Emirates ID activation.

## Speeding fines: complete table

Dubai has extensive radar coverage including average-speed cameras on major highways. The fines below apply to light vehicles in 2026.

| Speed excess | Fine (AED) | Black points | Additional penalty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–20 km/h over | 300 | 4 | None |
| 21–30 km/h over | 600 | 6 | None |
| 31–40 km/h over | 700 | 6 | None |
| 41–50 km/h over | 1,000 | 12 | None |
| 51–60 km/h over | 1,500 | 12 | None |
| Over 60 km/h | 2,000–3,000 | 24 | Vehicle confiscation 60 days |

Average-speed cameras: E11 (Sheikh Zayed Road), E311 (Emirates Road), and E66 (Al Ain Road use sections) measure average speed between two points. Driving fast for 500 metres then slowing does not help.

Heavy vehicle speeds: Lower limits apply to trucks and buses. If you drive a commercial vehicle, verify specific limits per category.

## Black points system: how it works

Black points accumulate on your UAE federal traffic file. They are not limited to Dubai, points from Abu Dhabi or other emirates count toward the same total.

| Points accumulated | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| 1–11 | Points recorded; driving continues |
| 12–23 | Mandatory awareness course may be triggered |
| 24 | Licence suspension review, 3 months minimum |
| New violation during suspension | Extended suspension period |

Point expiry: Individual violation points expire 12 months after the violation date, provided no suspension was issued in that period. If a suspension occurs, the clock resets.

**Checking points:** Dubai Police app → "Traffic File" → "Black Points". You can also check via the Ministry of Interior portal using Emirates ID.

## Salik tolls: everything expats need to know

Salik is Dubai's sole electronic toll system. Abu Dhabi has no Salik equivalents. If you are relocating from Abu Dhabi to Dubai, this is a new recurring cost.

### Active Salik gates (2026)

| Gate name | Location |
| --- | --- |
| Al Safa 1 (southbound) | SZR near Mall of the Emirates |
| Al Safa 2 (northbound) | SZR returning to Marina |
| Al Garhoud | Airport area bridge |
| Al Maktoum | Deira-Bur Dubai crossing |
| Al Barsha | SZR near Barsha Heights |
| Jebel Ali 1 and 2 | Southern entry and exit |
| Airport Tunnel | DXB T1/T3 area |

**Cost per crossing:** AED 4 regardless of vehicle type (light vehicle category).

**No charge periods:** Salik gates do not charge between 22:00 and 06:00, or on public holidays as announced by RTA.

### Setting up Salik

1. Visit salik.ae or RTA Smart app
2. Register vehicle plate number
3. Link credit or debit card for auto-top-up
4. Set low-balance alert (recommended AED 50)
5. Tag is mailed (3–5 business days) or collected from RTA centres

**Auto-top-up:** Set minimum balance top-up trigger and amount. This prevents negative balance fines and ensures smooth travel.

**Negative balance fine:** AED 50 administrative charge per gate passage with insufficient balance, in addition to the AED 4 toll. Check balance monthly if top-up is manual.

## Parking in Dubai: zone system

Dubai uses a colour-coded parking zone system managed by RTA. Zones apply in commercial and mixed-use areas; residential compounds typically have private parking managed independently.

| Zone | Hourly rate (AED) | Typical areas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silver | 2 | Deira, Bur Dubai, older districts |
| Gold | 3 | Business Bay, Media City approaches |
| Purple | 5–8 | Downtown, DIFC entrance areas |
| Blue (reserved) | Fixed permit | Business districts for permit holders |

**Free parking times:**

- Friday from midnight to 12:00 in most public zones
- Public holidays, check RTA announcements; not uniform
- 22:00 to 08:00 in most metered zones
- Some zones near mosques have extended free windows on Fridays

## How to pay Dubai fines

**Option 1, Dubai Police app (fastest)**

1. Open Dubai Police app → Traffic Services → Traffic File
2. Enter Emirates ID or licence plate
3. View all outstanding fines
4. Select and pay via saved card

**Option 2, police.gov.ae**

Same process online. Use Emirates ID number to retrieve all linked fines.

**Option 3, RTA Customer Happiness Centres**

In-person payment. Bring Emirates ID and fine notice. Queues are longer, use only if apps are not working.

**Option 4, Banks and exchange houses**

Some banks allow fine payment via internet banking or ATM services. Check your bank's bill payment section.

**Settlement deadline:** Pay within 60 days. Unpaid fines block vehicle registration renewal and can accumulate additional late fees.

## Disputing a Dubai traffic fine

If you believe a fine was issued in error:

1. **Gather evidence**: dashcam footage, parking receipt, GPS data, or witness
2. **File dispute** via Dubai Police app within 30 days of fine notification
3. **Upload supporting documents**: photo, video, or document depending on violation type
4. **Track status**: updated within 7–10 working days in most cases
5. **If approved**: fine cancelled or reduced; credited to account
6. **If rejected**: pay immediately to avoid escalation

**Common successful disputes:**

- Radar false reading (vehicle documentation proves you were elsewhere)
- Parking ticket during free period (screenshot of free period notice)
- Duplicate fine for same incident
- Salik technical error (system logs confirm gate malfunction)

**What rarely succeeds:**

- "I didn't see the sign", not a valid defence
- Speed claims in built-up areas, cameras are usually accurate
- Borrowing someone's car, the registered owner's file gets the fine; resolve privately

## Salik disputes

For Salik-specific billing errors:

- Contact Salik customer service at salik.ae (chat, email, or phone)
- Provide trip date, time, and gate name
- Request transaction log if charge seems incorrect
- Most billing disputes resolved within 5 working days

## Vehicle impound: how to retrieve

If your vehicle is impounded:

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |

| Pay outstanding fines | Online or at police station |
| Pay impound and storage fees | AED 500–1,500 release + AED 50/day storage |

Impound yards include Al Quoz, Jebel Ali, and Deira locations. Operating hours typically 08:00–20:00 weekdays; check before visiting.

## FAQ

**How do I check my Dubai traffic fines?**
Dubai Police app or police.gov.ae using your Emirates ID or vehicle plate number. Both show the full fine history linked to your traffic file.

**Can fines block my visa renewal?**
Outstanding fines do not directly block visa renewal but block vehicle registration renewal. Clear fines before registration deadline to avoid cascading issues.

**Does Salik charge at night?**
Salik gates operate 06:00–22:00. No charge outside these hours. Weekend and public holiday exceptions are announced by RTA separately.

**Are there Salik-free routes between Dubai destinations?**
Yes, parallel roads exist for some journeys. Al Khail Road avoids several SZR gates. Waze or Google Maps can route around Salik if you enable toll avoidance.

**What happens if I sell my car with outstanding fines?**
Fines stay on the traffic file linked to the vehicle's previous owner. Clear all fines before completing a vehicle transfer, buyers and RTA will check.

**How do I remove black points faster?**
Black points expire after 12 months naturally. No fast-track removal available. Completing a voluntary safe-driving course may demonstrate good faith but does not automatically clear points.

**Is there a grace period for expired parking meters?**
Theoretically no, wardens issue fines immediately. In practice, overstay fines are typically for 30+ minutes over. Do not rely on any grace period.

**Can I dispute a fine from a speed camera?**
Yes, via Dubai Police app with supporting evidence (GPS logs, vehicle tracker data). Burden of proof is on the disputing driver.

**What is the fine for using a phone at a red light?**
Same as while moving, AED 800 plus 4 black points. Holding or operating the phone while stationary in traffic is treated the same as while driving in Dubai.

**Does my home country insurance cover Dubai fines?**
No, UAE traffic fines are not covered by any insurance policy. Pay personally via Dubai Police app.
