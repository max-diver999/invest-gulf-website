## How are Dubai traffic fines issued?


Radar fines can arrive weeks after the incident, SMS is the fastest notification. Register your mobile number with Dubai Police and RTA immediately after Emirates ID activation.
## What are Dubai speeding fines in 2026?


Average-speed cameras: E11 (Sheikh Zayed Road), E311 (Emirates Road), and E66 (Al Ain Road use sections) measure average speed between two points. Driving fast for 500 metres then slowing does not help.

Heavy vehicle speeds: Lower limits apply to trucks and buses. If you drive a commercial vehicle, verify specific limits per category.

## What are other common Dubai traffic fines?


Heavy vehicle restrictions: Trucks have time-based restrictions on certain roads. Violation of peak-hour exclusions carries AED 1,000+.

## How do black points and licence suspension work?


Checking points: Dubai Police app → "Traffic File" → "Black Points". You can also check via the Ministry of Interior portal using Emirates ID.

## How much does Salik cost and how do you set it up?

Cost per crossing: AED 4 regardless of vehicle type (light vehicle category).

No charge periods: Salik gates do not charge between 22:00 and 06:00, or on public holidays as announced by RTA.

### Setting up Salik

1. Visit salik.ae or RTA Smart app
2. Register vehicle plate number
3. Link credit or debit card for auto-top-up
4. Set low-balance alert (recommended AED 50)
5. Tag is mailed (3-5 business days) or collected from RTA centres

Auto-top-up: Set minimum balance top-up trigger and amount. This prevents negative balance fines and ensures smooth travel.

Negative balance fine: AED 50 administrative charge per gate passage with insufficient balance, in addition to the AED 4 toll. Check balance monthly if top-up is manual.

## How do Dubai parking zones and fees work?

- Public holidays, check RTA announcements; not uniform
- 22:00 to 08:00 in most metered zones

## Which parking apps and payment methods work in Dubai?


Overstay fine: AED 150-500 depending on zone. Ticket placed on windscreen; pay via Dubai Police app citing the ticket number.

## What happens if you get a parking violation in Dubai?


Towing: Active in Downtown, DIFC, Marina, and Airport areas. Vehicles towed to RTA impound yards. Retrieve via Dubai Police app or in person at the impound yard with Emirates ID and fine payment receipt.

## How to pay Dubai fines


1. Open Dubai Police app → Traffic Services → Traffic File
2. Enter Emirates ID or licence plate
3. View all outstanding fines
4. Select and pay via saved card

Option 2, police.gov.ae

Same process online. Use Emirates ID number to retrieve all linked fines.

Option 3, RTA Customer Happiness Centres

In-person payment. Bring Emirates ID and fine notice. Queues are longer, use only if apps are not working.

Option 4, Banks and exchange houses

Some banks allow fine payment via internet banking or ATM services. Check your bank's bill payment section.

Settlement deadline: Pay within 60 days. Unpaid fines block vehicle registration renewal and can accumulate additional late fees.

## How do you dispute a Dubai traffic fine?


1. Gather evidence: dashcam footage, parking receipt, GPS data, or witness
2. File dispute via Dubai Police app within 30 days of fine notification
3. Upload supporting documents: photo, video, or document depending on violation type
4. Track status: updated within 7-10 working days in most cases
5. If approved: fine cancelled or reduced; credited to account
6. If rejected: pay immediately to avoid escalation

Common successful disputes:

- Radar false reading (vehicle documentation proves you were elsewhere)
- Parking ticket during free period (screenshot of free period notice)
- Duplicate fine for same incident
- Salik technical error (system logs confirm gate malfunction)

What rarely succeeds:

- "I didn't see the sign", not a valid defence
- Speed claims in built-up areas, cameras are usually accurate
- Borrowing someone's car, the registered owner's file gets the fine; resolve privately

## What are Dubai speeding fines in 2026?

Speed over the posted limit and you pay both cash and black points. Bands below are typical for light vehicles; verify on Dubai Police before you assume an amount.

| Over limit | Fine (AED) | Black points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-20 km/h | 300-400 | 4 |
| 21-30 km/h | 600 | 6 |
| 31-40 km/h | 700 | 6 |
| 41-60 km/h | 1,000+ | 12 |
| 60+ km/h | 2,000-3,000 | 12-24 |

Running a red light costs about AED 1,000, 12 points, and a 30-day impound. Mobile phone use while driving is about AED 800 plus 4 points whether you are moving or stopped at a junction.

## How do Dubai parking zones and fees work?

RTA divides street parking into zones with different hourly rates. Premium zones (Downtown, DIFC, Marina) often run AED 6-8 per hour. Standard zones are closer to AED 2-4. Multi-storey car parks add AED 10-25 per visit depending on location.

| Zone type | Typical hourly rate | Where you see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium (A) | AED 6-8 | Downtown, DIFC, Marina |
| Standard (B/C) | AED 2-4 | Many residential districts |
| Multi-storey | AED 10-25 flat | Malls, souks, hospitals |

Pay through RTA Dubai app, SMS to 7275 (plate-zone-duration), or on-street meters. Free windows still matter: many zones are free 22:00-08:00, on public holidays, and Friday 00:00-12:00 in selected areas.

## How do black points and licence suspension work?

Black points sit on your federal traffic file, not just Dubai. Reach 24 points within 12 months and you face a suspension review. Between 12 and 23 points you may need an awareness course.

| Points in 12 months | Typical consequence |
| --- | --- |
| 0-11 | Warning band; monitor file |
| 12-23 | Course or restrictions possible |
| 24+ | Suspension review (often 3 months) |

Points from a single violation usually expire after 12 months if no suspension was issued. Check the Dubai Police app under Traffic File then Black Points.

## How do you dispute Salik or parking fines?

Salik disputes go through salik.ae customer service with plate number, gate time, and tag balance screenshot. Technical double-charges and gate errors are the most common wins. Parking disputes use the Dubai Police app within 30 days with photos of signage, payment receipt, or dashcam proof of free-period rules.

## What mistakes do new Dubai drivers make?

Common errors include: driving through Salik with an unregistered plate; parking in resident-only bays without a permit; ignoring average-speed camera corridors on Sheikh Zayed Road; paying fines late and blocking registration renewal; and using a home-country licence after the grace period ends. Link your mobile to Dubai Police the week you collect Emirates ID.
