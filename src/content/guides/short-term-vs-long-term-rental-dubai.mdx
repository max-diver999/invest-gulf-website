Every Dubai investor eventually faces the same fork: register on Ejari for a 12-month tenant, or apply for a DET Holiday Home Permit and run short-term. The gross revenue gap can look enormous on a spreadsheet. The net outcome after permits, management, vacancy, and building rules often is not.

**Pros of long-term (Ejari):** predictable cheques, lower compliance, easier bank underwriting, fewer OA disputes, and passive management at 5-8% fees.

**Cons of long-term:** capped gross rent in oversupplied towers, slower repricing versus tourist peaks, and no personal-use flexibility without breaking tenancy rules.

**Pros of short-term (DET):** revenue upside in tourist micro-locations, personal stay weeks if permitted, and pricing that tracks events and seasonality.

**Cons of short-term:** OA bans, permit and Civil Defence compliance, 15-25% management, furnishing churn, and occupancy risk that can erase the spreadsheet premium in summer.

This guide compares short-term (STR) and long-term (LTR) rental in Dubai on revenue, regulation, risk, and fit, so you choose based on your property and capacity, not Airbnb averages.

## Why do long-term leases trade upside for predictable cash flow?

Ejari long-term leases typically prioritise predictable cash flow and low compliance on a **12 month** contract, while DET short-term permits can lift gross revenue **30% to 50%** in tourist towers. STR still needs OA approval, **70%+** occupancy, and **15% to 20%** management fees to beat LTR net. Invest Gulf starts with building rules, not portal asks.

- LTR: Ejari, lower fees, bank-friendly
- STR: DET permit, higher gross, higher friction
- Net test: occupancy and management decide

## What permit does STR need versus long-term renting?

STR typically requires a DET holiday-home permit for stays under **12 months**, while long-term renting needs Ejari registration under RERA tenancy law. Running STR without a DET permit while advertising on platforms exposes you to fines from **AED 5,000** upward. Our research checks OA minutes before any DET application.

### STR: DET

- Mandatory for any rental under 12 months
- Civil Defence fire inspection required
- Guest registration within 3 hours of check-in
- Permit number on all listings (Airbnb/Booking enforce)
- Fines from AED 5,000 upward for unlicensed operation
- ~91% of major platform listings now permitted (2025 data)

### LTR: Ejari

- 12-month standard tenancy; RERA rent calculator reference
- Ejari registration mandatory
- Security deposit cap (5% typical)
- Dispute resolution via Rental Dispute Centre
- Far lower compliance overhead than STR

## How do Ejari and DET differ as regimes?

Ejari and DET are different regimes: Ejari registers **12 month** tenancies with RERA, while DET licences holiday homes under tourism rules for stays under **12 months**. One does not substitute for the other. Our underwriting treats fake long-term back-to-back leases used to hide STR as a compliance red flag.

| Topic | Ejari (LTR) | DET (STR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract length | 12 months standard | Under 12 months per stay |
| Primary regulator | RERA / Dubai Land Department tenancy | DET / DTCM tourism |
| Building gate | Rarely blocks LTR | OA can ban STR outright |
| Guest registration | Tenant Ejari only | Within 3 hours of check-in |
| Typical investor fit | JVC, Sports City, Discovery Gardens | Marina, JBR, Downtown, Palm |

Running STR without DET licence while marketing on Airbnb exposes you to fines and OA enforcement, even if Ejari is registered on a fake long-term back-to-back lease. See [Short-term rental Dubai license](/guides/short-term-rental-dubai-license/) for the full licence path.

**Insurance:** Standard landlord policies often exclude commercial STR use, declare holiday-home use to your insurer before first guest check-in; claims for water damage or theft are commonly denied when STR was undeclared.

**Management choice:** Self-managing STR from overseas rarely works, guest check-in, maintenance at 22:00, and OA complaint handling need local presence or a licensed operator. Budget **20-25% management** in net models unless you live in Dubai and can handle turnovers yourself. For furnished LTR, allow **AED 8,000-15,000** fit-out refresh every 3-4 years, still far below STR furnishing cycles.
