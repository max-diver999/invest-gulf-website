Every Dubai investor eventually faces the same fork: register on Ejari for a 12-month tenant, or apply for a DET Holiday Home Permit and run short-term. The gross revenue gap can look enormous on a spreadsheet. The net outcome after permits, management, vacancy, and building rules often is not.

**Pros of long-term (Ejari):** predictable cheques, lower compliance, easier bank underwriting, fewer OA disputes, and passive management at 5-8% fees.

**Cons of long-term:** capped gross rent in oversupplied towers, slower repricing versus tourist peaks, and no personal-use flexibility without breaking tenancy rules.

**Pros of short-term (DET):** revenue upside in tourist micro-locations, personal stay weeks if permitted, and pricing that tracks events and seasonality.

**Cons of short-term:** OA bans, permit and Civil Defence compliance, 15-25% management, furnishing churn, and occupancy risk that can erase the spreadsheet premium in summer.

This guide compares short-term (STR) and long-term (LTR) rental in Dubai on revenue, regulation, risk, and fit, so you choose based on your property and capacity, not Airbnb averages.

For licensing detail, see [Short-Term Rental Dubai License](/guides/short-term-rental-dubai-license/). For community yields, see [Dubai Rental Yield Guide](/guides/dubai-rental-yield-guide/). For management fees, see [Property Management Dubai Cost](/guides/property-management-dubai-cost/).

## What should buyers verify on the core trade-off?

Ejari long-term leases prioritise predictable cash flow and low compliance overhead. DET short-term permits can lift gross revenue 30-50% in tourist towers but need OA approval, 70%+ occupancy, and 15-20% management fees to beat LTR net.

## How does revenue compare in 2026?

Marina and JBR one-beds often gross AED 120,000-180,000/year on STR versus AED 90,000-110,000 on Ejari, but STR nets converge when occupancy slips below 60%.

Occupancy breakeven: STR needs roughly 70-75% occupancy to beat LTR net of costs. At under 60%, long-term often wins even before accounting for your time.

## What should buyers verify on cost stack comparison (worked example)?

STR wins on gross. Net can tie or lose if occupancy slips or management is inefficient. Run your own unit math with Ejari transacted rents, not portal listing asks.

## What should buyers verify on regulatory landscape?

STR sits under DET holiday-home rules; LTR sits under RERA tenancy law and Ejari registration. Running STR without a DET permit while advertising on platforms exposes you to fines from AED 5,000 upward.

### STR: DET

- Mandatory for any rental under 12 months
- Civil Defence fire inspection required
- Guest registration within 3 hours of check-in
- Permit number on all listings (Airbnb/Booking enforce)
- Fines from AED 5,000 upward for unlicensed operation
- ~91% of major platform listings now permitted (2025 data)

### LTR: Ejari

- 12-month standard tenancy; RERA rent calculator reference
- Ejari registration mandatory
- Security deposit cap (5% typical)
- Dispute resolution via Rental Dispute Centre
- Far lower compliance overhead than STR
## How does building-level reality compare in 2026?

Approximately 20-30% of buildings in investment communities prohibit or restrict holiday homes. Violating OA rules triggers building fines independent of DET licensing.

LTR faces far fewer OA blockers, making mid-market yield communities viable for passive investors who cannot or will not operate STR.

## What should buyers verify on vacancy and demand context?

Dubai STR vacancy often runs 25-35% outside peak tourism months unless the tower has corporate contract demand. LTR void on mid-market stock is typically 2-4 weeks between tenants in liquid communities like JVC.

## What should buyers verify on decision framework?

Choose STR if OA and DET allow your building, you can verify occupancy above 70%, and you accept active management. Choose LTR if the community is commuter-yield stock, OA restricts holiday homes, or you invest remotely.

## What should Gulf buyers budget for impact on net yield?

Model STR with 20-25% management, AED 1,500-2,500/year permits, cleaning per turnover, and higher DEWA from guest usage. LTR usually runs 5-8% management and stable Ejari cheques.

## What should buyers verify on seasonality and event-driven str demand?

STR peaks around winter tourism, Expo-style events, and school holidays. LTR rents reprice slowly on RERA calculator references, smoothing seasonality but capping upside.

## Who should self-manage STR versus hire an operator in 2026?

Self-manage only if you live in Dubai and can handle guest check-in within 30 minutes. Remote owners should budget a licensed operator with tower-specific occupancy data, not generic Airbnb averages.

## What should buyers verify on financing and bank underwriting angle?

UAE banks underwrite LTR with Ejari contracts more easily than STR projections. If you finance, stress-test at LTR rent even when you plan STR, so mortgage service still clears in low season.

## How does furnishing capex compare in 2026?

STR-ready furnishing on a one-bed often costs AED 40,000-70,000 with hotel-grade linen and kitchen inventory. LTR unfurnished lets tenants bring their own fit-out, lowering your capex but narrowing the tenant pool.

## What should buyers verify on bottom line?

If STR net beats Ejari by less than 15% after fees, the operational risk rarely pays off unless you value personal use weeks in the unit.

| Metric | Marina STR (indicative) | JVC LTR (indicative) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual gross rent | AED 150,000 | AED 95,000 |
| Occupancy / void | 72% effective | 4% void |
| Management + cleaning | 22% of gross | 6% of gross |
| Permits and extras | AED 2,000 | Ejari AED 220 |
| Net before mortgage | Often AED 95,000-105,000 | Often AED 82,000-88,000 |

How to use this table: Replace STR gross with your operator's 12-month P&L if available. If STR net does not beat LTR Ejari by at least 15%, the operational risk rarely pays off on mid-market stock.

OA check before STR: Request written owners-association minutes on short-term policy, verbal building management assurances fail at DTCM inspection stage.

## How does str cost stack compare in 2026?

| Cost line | STR (typical) | LTR (typical) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Management | 15-25% of gross | 5-8% of rent |
| Permits / Ejari | AED 1,500-2,500/year DET stack | ~AED 220 Ejari |
| Cleaning / turnover | AED 150-300 per stay | Tenant responsibility |
| Void assumption | 25-35% effective in many towers | 2-4 weeks between tenants |

**Break-even test:** STR gross must exceed LTR Ejari by roughly **20-30%** on the same unit before risk-adjusted returns favour STR, use the [Dubai holiday home ROI calculator](/guides/dubai-holiday-home-roi-calculator-guide/) with your building's actual utility bills.

## When STR actually wins in 2026

- OA and DTCM allow STR in your **specific building**
- Micro-location with **walkable tourism or corporate demand** (Marina fringe, Palm, select Downtown towers)
- Professional operator with **verified occupancy history** in that tower
- You accept **higher capex, insurance, and vacancy volatility**

Everywhere else, JVC, Sports City, most Business Bay, IMPZ, Discovery Gardens, **Ejari LTR** remains the default. See [Dubai rental yield guide](/guides/dubai-rental-yield-guide/) for community-level gross bands.

## Ejari vs DET: two different regimes

Ejari registers 12-month tenancies with RERA; DET licences holiday homes under tourism rules. They solve different problems and one does not substitute for the other.

| Topic | Ejari (LTR) | DET (STR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract length | 12 months standard | Under 12 months per stay |
| Primary regulator | RERA / Dubai Land Department tenancy | DET / DTCM tourism |
| Building gate | Rarely blocks LTR | OA can ban STR outright |
| Guest registration | Tenant Ejari only | Within 3 hours of check-in |
| Typical investor fit | JVC, Sports City, Discovery Gardens | Marina, JBR, Downtown, Palm |

Running STR without DET licence while marketing on Airbnb exposes you to fines and OA enforcement, even if Ejari is registered on a fake long-term back-to-back lease. See [Short-term rental Dubai license](/guides/short-term-rental-dubai-license/) for the full licence path.

**Insurance:** Standard landlord policies often exclude commercial STR use, declare holiday-home use to your insurer before first guest check-in; claims for water damage or theft are commonly denied when STR was undeclared.

**Management choice:** Self-managing STR from overseas rarely works, guest check-in, maintenance at 22:00, and OA complaint handling need local presence or a licensed operator. Budget **20-25% management** in net models unless you live in Dubai and can handle turnovers yourself. For furnished LTR, allow **AED 8,000-15,000** fit-out refresh every 3-4 years, still far below STR furnishing cycles.

## What red flags should pause this Gulf purchase?

Pause if the listing markets "Airbnb ready" but OA minutes ban holiday homes, if the seller cannot produce 12 months of Ejari comparables in the same tower, or if management quotes gross yield without an occupancy audit.

Confirm **building STR policy in writing** before paying a premium for "Airbnb-ready" listings.

## What checklist should run before you sign?

Written OA minutes approving STR, DET permit quote, Ejari benchmark rent for the same floor plate, operator occupancy history for the tower, DEWA and service charge statements, and insurance endorsement for holiday-home use.

### Practical cost reference

Short-term rental licensing in Dubai requires a DTCM permit (annual fee AED 1,070 for a holiday-home operator licence), property-specific permits (AED 370 per unit), and tourism compliance checks. Long-term landlords need Ejari registration (roughly AED 220) and a DEWA account transfer. Short-term gross yields in tourist zones often land at 8-12% but carry 15-25% management fees and 25-35% effective vacancy outside peak months. Long-term gross yields on mid-market stock run 6-9% with minimal management overhead.
