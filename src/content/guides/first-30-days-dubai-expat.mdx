# First 30 Days in Dubai as an Expat: Week-by-Week Survival Guide

> Disclaimer: June 2026 timeline. PRO speed varies by employer, adjust days, keep sequence.

<TldrBlock text={"Week 1 = medical + EID + housing search. Week 2 = lease + Ejari + DEWA. Week 3 = visa stamp + bank. Week 4 = schools, car, normal life. Do not sign lease until Tuesday 7:30 AM commute test passes."} />

## Which first-month scenarios fit your move?

**Scenario A - employed professional (2-3 year contract):** run a Tuesday 7:30 AM commute from your shortlisted home to the office before you sign Ejari. That single test prevents the most common first-month lease regret.

**Scenario B - family relocation (3-5 years):** model total monthly spend (rent, schools, transport, insurance), not headline rent alone.

**Scenario C - investor or remote worker:** separate lifestyle goals from ROI, stress-test vacancy at 4-6 weeks per year, and keep 6-12 months liquidity in AED.

## What happens in week 1 (days 1 to 7)?


Cash: Keep AED 5,000 liquid for taxis, meals, SIM, medical fees.

### Days 3-5: Medical and biometrics

- Medical: blood test, chest X-ray, results in 24-48 hours typically
- EID biometrics: fingerprints, photo, card follows in 1-2 weeks
- Do not skip, blocks entire chain

Property buyers parallel: if entering on visit visa for closing, coordinate trustee same week, [Emirates ID after purchase](/guides/emirates-id-after-property-purchase/)

### Days 3-7: Housing reconnaissance

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 3-4 | View 3-5 properties PM slot |
| 5 | Tuesday 7:30 AM commute test from top choice to office |
| 6 | View 3-5 more units, different cluster if commute failed |
| 7 | Shortlist 2 finalists, request sample DEWA bills |

Families: School tour same week if term imminent, [moving with family](/guides/moving-to-dubai-with-family/)

### Week 1 mistakes to avoid

- Signing lease on day 2 because agent pressure
- Assuming Marina commute works without testing Salik gates
- Ignoring district cooling provider name in listing

## What should you check on insurance and healthcare setup strategy?


Insurance optimization: Compare employer group plan with individual comprehensive coverage. Many group plans have limited private hospital access that may not meet expat expectations.

Pre-existing conditions: Declare all conditions during first 30 days. Failure to disclose can void coverage when you need it most.

Emergency preparedness: Download insurance provider app and save emergency numbers in both English and Arabic. Test pre-authorization process with non-urgent appointment.

## What should you check on digital ecosystem integration?


| Priority Level | Apps/Services | Setup Timing | Monthly Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential (Week 1) | UAE PASS, Careem, RTA Dubai | Days 1-3 | Free |
| Important (Week 2) | Banking apps, DEWA, Du/Etisalat | After account opening | Service fees |
| Convenience (Week 3) | Talabat, Noon, Carrefour NOW | After address confirmation | Delivery fees |
| Lifestyle (Week 4) | Fitness apps, community apps | After social integration | AED 50-200/month |

UAE PASS integration: This digital identity platform accelerates government services throughout your stay. Set up during Emirates ID collection for maximum efficiency.

Payment method strategy: Apple Pay/Google Pay work immediately with international cards. Local payment apps (Beam, cbd Pay) require UAE bank account but offer merchant discounts.

## What should you check on social integration and networking framework?


Professional networking ROI: Dubai's small professional circles mean early networking pays compound returns. Attend one industry event in your first month even if you're exhausted.

## What should you check on financial optimization strategy?


International transfer optimization: Compare UAE Exchange, Al Ansari, Wise, and bank transfer rates. Differences of 1-2% on large transfers add up significantly.

Investment platform access: Some international investment platforms restrict UAE residents. Establish compliant alternatives during your first month before transferring substantial assets.

## What should you check on family-specific acceleration strategies?


School admission acceleration: If arriving mid-term, backup school acceptance within first week prevents housing mistakes. Outstanding-rated schools may require full academic year advance booking.

Dependent visa strategy: Start spouse and children visa applications immediately after primary visa stamping. Delays cascade into school start dates and spousal work permit timelines.

Children's activity pipeline: Popular activities (swimming, football, martial arts) have **waiting lists**. Register during first month for September/October start dates.
## What should you check on crisis management and contingency planning?


**Financial buffer management:** Keep **AED 10,000 liquid** throughout first month for crisis resolution. UAE banking delays can **freeze access to funds** when you need flexibility most.

## What should you check on cultural integration and legal compliance?


| Compliance Area | Key Requirements | First Month Actions | Penalties for Non-Compliance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Alcohol licensing** | Residents only, ID required | Obtain license after visa stamping | Fines + potential deportation |
| **Public behavior** | Conservative dress, no PDA | Cultural orientation | Social sanctions + legal risk |
| **Social media** | No criticism of UAE/rulers | Review posting history | Deportation + bans |
| **Business activities** | No work without proper visa | Understand employment scope | Visa cancellation + fines |

**Alcohol license advantages:** Even non-drinkers benefit from **alcohol license** for entertaining and gifts. Process takes 2-3 days after visa stamping.

**Social media audit:** Review and **clean up social media history** before arrival. Posts critical of UAE, excessive partying, or controversial political views can affect visa applications.

**Cultural sensitivity training:** Many employers provide **cultural orientation sessions**. Attend even if optional, workplace cultural mistakes have career consequences.
