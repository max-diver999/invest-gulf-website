# First 30 Days in Dubai as an Expat: Week-by-Week Survival Guide

**TL;DR:** **Week 1** = medical + EID + housing search. **Week 2** = lease + Ejari + DEWA. **Week 3** = visa stamp + bank. **Week 4** = schools, car, normal life. Do not sign lease until **Tuesday 7:30 AM commute test** passes. **Hubs:** [relocation guide](/guides/dubai-relocation-guide/) · [full checklist](/guides/dubai-relocation-checklist/)

> **Disclaimer:** June 2026 timeline. PRO speed varies by employer — adjust days, keep sequence.

---

## Before day 1 — set expectations

Your first month is **admin heavy**, not beach heavy. Successful expats treat it like a project plan with one PRO contact and one spouse lead for housing.

| Realistic | Unrealistic |
|---|---|
| Visa stamped by day 21–28 | "Sorted by weekend" |
| Functional home by day 14–21 | Fully designed interior |
| One bank account open | Investment portfolio day 1 |
| Interim school place | Dream Outstanding seat week 1 |

---

## Week 1 (Days 1–7): Land, medical, hunt

### Days 1–2 — Arrival and PRO handoff

| Priority | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Clear immigration on correct visa |
| 2 | Meet PRO — get written task list with dates |
| 3 | Book medical fitness (often PRO schedules) |
| 4 | Book Emirates ID biometrics |
| 5 | UAE SIM — data for maps and WhatsApp |
| 6 | Short-stay housing — hotel 2–3 weeks typical |

**Cash:** Keep AED 5,000 liquid for taxis, meals, SIM, medical fees.

### Days 3–5 — Medical and biometrics

- Medical: blood test, chest X-ray — results in 24–48 hours typically
- EID biometrics: fingerprints, photo — card follows in 1–2 weeks
- **Do not skip** — blocks entire chain

Property buyers parallel: if entering on visit visa for closing, coordinate trustee same week — [Emirates ID after purchase](/guides/emirates-id-after-property-purchase/)

### Days 3–7 — Housing reconnaissance

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 3–4 | View 3–5 properties PM slot |
| 5 | **Tuesday 7:30 AM** commute test from top choice to office |
| 6 | View 3–5 more units — different cluster if commute failed |
| 7 | Shortlist 2 finalists — request sample DEWA bills |

**Families:** School tour same week if term imminent — [moving with family](/guides/moving-to-dubai-with-family/)

### Week 1 mistakes to avoid

- Signing lease on day 2 because agent pressure
- Assuming Marina commute works without testing Salik gates
- Ignoring district cooling provider name in listing

## Week 2 (Days 8–14): Sign, Ejari, utilities

### Days 8–10 — Lease execution

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Negotiate | Cheques (1 vs 4), rent, maintenance, cooling cap |
| Verify | Landlord title or authorised POA |
| Sign | Tenancy contract — read penalty clause |
| Pay | Deposit 5% + agency 5% + cheques post-dated |
| Ejari | Register same week — tenant or landlord |

[Ejari registration guide](/guides/ejari-registration-landlord-guide/)

### Days 11–14 — Make it livable

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| DEWA | Apply with Ejari — deposit AED 2–4K |
| Cooling | Empower/Tabreed activation — summer critical |
| Internet | Book install — may lag 3–7 days |
| Furniture | IKEA online + next-day essentials (Mattress, kettle, router) |
| Move-in inspection | Photo every scratch — WhatsApp landlord |

**Budget this week:** AED 25–60K depending deposit and furniture level.

### Week 2 — visa status

PRO should submit visa stamping request once medical clear. Passport may be held **3–7 days** — plan travel accordingly.

Stamping detail: [Dubai residence visa stamping](/guides/dubai-residence-visa-stamping/)

## Week 3 (Days 15–21): Visa, bank, ID card

### Days 15–17 — Visa stamped

| Milestone | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| Passport stamped | Legal employment start |
| EID card received | Banking, school final enrolment |
| UAE PASS activated | Government services digital |

Without stamped visa, employer should not put you on client site requiring labour card.

### Days 17–21 — Banking

| Step | Documents |
|---|---|
| Book appointment | EID, passport, visa page |
| Salary letter | Employer on letterhead |
| Ejari | Tenancy proof |
| Minimum deposit | Varies AED 0–3K |

Timeline: **3–10 business days** for account active.

[Opening bank account guide](/guides/opening-bank-account-dubai-property-buyers/) · [Gulf banking comparison](/guides/gulf-banking-comparison-expats/)

**Tip:** Ask HR for **WPS registration** same week salary hits.

### Days 18–21 — Transport planning

| Option | Start process |
|---|---|
| Licence conversion | RTA — check eligible countries |
| New licence | Register driving school |
| No car yet | Careem + Metro Nol card |
| Car purchase | Need licence or buyer arrangement |

Budget: car lease AED 1,800–2,500/month entry; purchase needs insurance quote.

## FAQ

**Can I leave UAE during first 30 days?**  
Risky if passport with PRO for stamping — confirm before booking trip.

**Hotel length of stay visa issues?**  
Overstay fines accrue if visa not progressed — PRO accountability.

**Part-time remote for home employer?**  
Tax and visa implications — not employment visa scope without permission.

**When first salary?**  
Often month end + 30 days — confirm payroll cycle day 1.

**Grocery delivery day 1?**  
Carrefour, Talabat, Instashop — no car needed.

**Gym and beach day 1?**  
Yes — morale matters; buy hydration and SPF 50.

**Open UAE company in first 30 days?**  
Possible in free zone — separate from employment visa; use specialist.

**Cultural shocks week 1?**  
Ramadan if applicable — no public eating daytime; Friday prayer traffic.

## Insurance and Healthcare Setup Strategy

Comprehensive healthcare setup requires strategic timing in your first month:

| Week | Healthcare Priority | Timeline | Cost Factor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Week 1** | Emergency cover confirmation | Immediate | Employer group plan activation |
| **Week 2** | GP registration and health card | After EID received | AED 500-2,000 registration |
| **Week 3** | Specialist consultations if needed | Post-visa stamping | Insurance pre-authorization |
| **Week 4** | Family health assessments | After dependent visas | Preventive care baseline |

**Insurance optimization:** Compare employer group plan with **individual comprehensive coverage**. Many group plans have **limited private hospital access** that may not meet expat expectations.

**Pre-existing conditions:** Declare all conditions during first 30 days. **Failure to disclose** can void coverage when you need it most.

**Emergency preparedness:** Download insurance provider app and **save emergency numbers** in both English and Arabic. Test pre-authorization process with non-urgent appointment.

## Family-Specific Acceleration Strategies

Families face compressed timelines requiring parallel processing:

| Family Element | Single Timeline | Family Timeline | Complexity Multiplier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Visa processing** | 15-21 days | 21-35 days | 1.5x |
| **School admissions** | N/A | 7-30 days | Critical path |
| **Housing requirements** | Flexible | School zone dependent | 2x constraints |
| **Healthcare setup** | Individual | Family plan + pediatrician | 3x appointments |

**School admission acceleration:** If arriving mid-term, **backup school acceptance** within first week prevents housing mistakes. Outstanding-rated schools may require **full academic year advance booking**.

**Dependent visa strategy:** Start spouse and children visa applications **immediately after primary visa stamping**. Delays cascade into school start dates and spousal work permit timelines.

**Children's activity pipeline:** Popular activities (swimming, football, martial arts) have **waiting lists**. Register during first month for September/October start dates.

## Crisis Management and Contingency Planning

First-month problems require immediate resolution strategies:

| Crisis Scenario | Early Warning Signs | Immediate Response | Prevention Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Visa delay** | PRO communication gaps | HR escalation + direct contact | Choose employers with proven PRO |
| **Housing disaster** | Cooling failure, major defects | Hotel extension + legal review | Thorough pre-signing inspection |
| **Banking rejection** | KYC documentation issues | Alternative bank + HR support | Complete document preparation |
| **School rejection** | Waiting list only | Backup activation + consultant | Multiple applications pre-arrival |

**Support system activation:** Identify **three emergency contacts** by day 7: employer HR, real estate agent, and one expat friend/colleague. Dubai's transient nature means **formal support structures** work better than assuming help.

**Financial buffer management:** Keep **AED 10,000 liquid** throughout first month for crisis resolution. UAE banking delays can **freeze access to funds** when you need flexibility most.

## Cultural Integration and Legal Compliance

Understanding UAE cultural and legal framework prevents costly mistakes:

| Compliance Area | Key Requirements | First Month Actions | Penalties for Non-Compliance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Alcohol licensing** | Residents only, ID required | Obtain license after visa stamping | Fines + potential deportation |
| **Public behavior** | Conservative dress, no PDA | Cultural orientation | Social sanctions + legal risk |
| **Social media** | No criticism of UAE/rulers | Review posting history | Deportation + bans |
| **Business activities** | No work without proper visa | Understand employment scope | Visa cancellation + fines |

**Alcohol license advantages:** Even non-drinkers benefit from **alcohol license** for entertaining and gifts. Process takes 2-3 days after visa stamping.

**Social media audit:** Review and **clean up social media history** before arrival. Posts critical of UAE, excessive partying, or controversial political views can affect visa applications.

**Cultural sensitivity training:** Many employers provide **cultural orientation sessions**. Attend even if optional — workplace cultural mistakes have career consequences.

## Week 5 preview — what comes next

Days 31–45 typically shift from survival to optimisation: spouse work permit submission, second car purchase, gym membership, and first home leave booking. Property buyers begin snagging lists or tenant interviews. Families schedule first parent-teacher evening — the moment Dubai starts feeling like home rather than a hotel with paperwork.

If week 4 ended without stamped visa or Ejari, treat days 31–60 as **recovery mode** — escalate PRO weekly until resolved before investing in 12-month subscriptions or school extracurricular bundles you cannot use without dependant visas.
