## What is iqama and why does everything wait on it?

Iqama is the Saudi residence ID tied to your sponsor for most employment cases. Answer first: without a valid card you usually cannot open a salary account, register an Ejar lease, keep children in school long term, or sponsor dependents. Budget **3 to 8 weeks** on the ground for straightforward cases after entry, and longer when attestations or PRO backlogs slip.

Without valid iqama you typically cannot:
- Open salary bank account (SAMA KYC)
- Register Ejar lease in your name
- Enrol children in international schools long-term
- Obtain Saudi driving licence (standard path)
- Sponsor dependents (after salary rules met)
- Clear final exit cleanly on departure

Visit visa or business visit is not employment iqama. If a recruiter says "start work on visit," stop.

## How does standard employment pathway work in practice?

Straightforward cases: 3 to 8 weeks. Wrong attestations or a slow sponsor: 12+ weeks is common.

## What happens before you fly to Saudi (phase 1)?

### Document attestation (role-dependent)

Degrees, marriage and birth certificates for dependent filing later:
- Home country notary/ministry
- Saudi embassy attestation
- Saudi Cultural Mission for education docs (employer-dependent)

Plan 8 to 12 weeks pre-arrival for US/UK/Indian chains.

### Visa authorization

Employer obtains visa authorization number, you print for embassy or e-visa channel  (confirm current official rules).

Block visa vs individual, affects PRO batch timing.

## What should you do in your first week after arrival (phase 2)?

- Work visa stamp inspected
- Employer may provide airport pickup, hotel or temp housing common
- Do not drive until Saudi licence clear, fines automated

Absher: account creation may need iqama number, partial access before card physical collection  (confirm current official rules).

## How do medical and biometrics fit the timeline (phase 3)?

Women: medical conducted in approved facilities, no gender barrier post-reform for standard employment medical  (confirm current official rules).

## How does absher work in practice?

| Service | Use |
|---|---|
| Iqama expiry check | Renewal alerts |
| Traffic fines (Saher) | Pay before renewal block |
| Dependent visa | After eligibility met |
| Exit/re-entry visa | Travel while employed |
| Final exit | Leaving KSA permanently |

Activation: needs Saudi mobile, iqama, bank sometimes, PRO walks first login.

Security: enable two-factor; do not share Absher password with unofficial "fixers".

Fine payment: unpaid traffic fines block iqama renewal, treat as payroll-critical.

## How does iqama renewal work in practice?

Answer first: the **employer** initiates renewal before expiry through Qiwa/MHRSD channels; you complete medical if required, pay fees, and confirm the new expiry in Absher. Lapsed iqama triggers **daily fines** for employee and sponsor, and unpaid traffic fines can block renewal entirely.

| Renewal task | Typical owner | Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qiwa submission | Employer HR / PRO | Start 30 days before expiry |
| Medical repeat | Employee if flagged | Book approved clinic only |
| Fee payment | Employer per contract | Keep receipt PDF |
| Absher check | Employee | Screenshot new expiry date |

Multi-year validity sometimes appears on corporate batches; still set calendar alerts. Confirm current official rules on dependent renewals in parallel via [Saudi family visa](/guides/saudi-family-visa/).

## How does job transfer work in practice?

Job transfer is **not automatic**. A new employer must obtain sponsorship approval, notice periods must be respected, and exit/re-entry permissions must stay valid during the switch. Premium Residency holders follow different mobility rules (confirm current official rules). Never work for a new sponsor until transfer approval is visible in government systems.

## How does premium residency work in practice?

Do not resign a UAE job for a Premium brochure without a government confirmation letter.

## What should you check on profession codes and inspections?

## What fees and fines should you budget on iqama?

Illustrative **employee** lines employers often cover per contract: issuance, renewal, and medical. Dependent iqama fees may fall on the employee (confirm current official rules). Keep a personal log of **Absher payment receipts** even when HR pays, it speeds disputes.

| Late renewal penalty | 500 to 1,000+ | Escalates with repeat offences |
| Exit/re-entry single | ~200 for 2 months | Multiple options at higher fees |
| Lost iqama replacement | ~1,000 | Report loss in Absher immediately |

## What red flags should pause your iqama timeline?

Recruiter asks you to **work on a visit visa**, PRO refuses to share Qiwa contract Arabic/English parity, medical scheduled at a non-approved clinic, or employer delays iqama past **90 days** while you perform full duties. Each is a stop signal until HR puts fixes in writing.

## What should you do the week iqama lands?

Activate Absher with PRO help, open payroll bank per [Saudi banking expats](/guides/saudi-banking-expats/), register Ejar if housing allowance requires it, and only then start dependent applications. Parallelise school assessments if conditional enrolment is allowed, waiting for plastic alone can cost a September seat.

## How do phases four to six close the card loop?

Answer first: after medical and biometrics, the **plastic iqama** collection, **profession code verification**, and **Qiwa contract alignment** usually take **1 to 3 weeks** in straightforward files. Delays here are often **data mismatches** (passport name vs degree) rather than policy changes.

| Phase | Typical action | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Card printing | Collect plastic, verify profession | Employee + PRO |
| Qiwa parity | Match offer letter job title | HR |
| Dependent queue | Start only after primary active | Employee |

## How do dependents fit after primary iqama?

Family sponsorship requires **salary and housing thresholds** separate from employee issuance. Start attestation chains early via [Saudi family visa](/guides/saudi-family-visa/). Do not book non-refundable school seats until dependent iqama status is clear unless the school confirms conditional enrolment in writing.

## How does Absher change day-to-day life?

Treat Absher as your **MOI dashboard**: expiry alerts, traffic fines, exit/re-entry requests, and dependent services. Enable **two-factor authentication** and refuse unofficial "fixers" asking for passwords. Unpaid fines block renewal, so pay Saher tickets before HR submits renewal batches.

Premium Residency marketing is **not** a shortcut around employee iqama timelines unless you hold an approved ROP category letter (confirm current official rules). Keep employment iqama and premium tracks on separate checklists.

Document safekeeping means scanning **iqama, passport, Qiwa contract, and degree attestations** to encrypted storage the week cards arrive. Loss reporting through Absher should happen within 24 hours of discovery to avoid replacement delays before travel.

## How to keep an iqama process from stalling

Most delays come from incomplete HR letters, medical appointment gaps, or passport validity issues, not from the concept of sponsorship itself. Track each Absher or Muqeem step with dates and document versions.

| Step | Owner | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer and salary evidence | Employer HR | Wrong allowance wording |
| Entry and medical | Employee + clinic | Missed slots in peak season |
| Fingerprints and card print | PRO / employee | Passport renewal mid-stream |
| Dependent add-ons | Family track | Missing attested certificates |
| Annual renewals | HR + employee | Insurance lapses blocking renewal |

## Coordination with housing and school

Do not sign a long compound lease that requires iqama numbers until HR confirms realistic card timing, or negotiate a temporary housing clause. School admissions may accept pending paperwork for a fee deposit window; confirm in writing. Use [Saudi family visa](/guides/saudi-family-visa/) for dependent sequencing and [Saudi healthcare for expats](/guides/saudi-healthcare-expats/) for cover activation.

## Compliance notes

Exit and re-entry, job transfer, and final exit rules change in wording across years. Verify current official requirements before travel that depends on a stamp or digital permit. Keep PDF copies of every approval.
