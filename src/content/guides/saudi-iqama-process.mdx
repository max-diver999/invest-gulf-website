TL;DR: Most expats get iqama through employer sponsorship: work visa entry, medical, biometrics, plastic card, Absher activation, then bank and lease. Premium Residency (~SAR 4M cite  (confirm current official rules)) is a separate track, not a faster iqama shortcut.

## What iqama is: and why everything waits for it

Iqama is your residence ID in Saudi Arabia, plastic card, photo, sponsor name, profession code, expiry date. Everything else queues behind it.

Without valid iqama you typically cannot:
- Open salary bank account (SAMA KYC)
- Register Ejar lease in your name
- Enrol children in international schools long-term
- Obtain Saudi driving licence (standard path)
- Sponsor dependents (after salary rules met)
- Clear final exit cleanly on departure

Visit visa or business visit is not employment iqama. If a recruiter says "start work on visit," stop.

## Standard employment pathway: overview diagram

```
Employer block/individual visa authorization
        ↓
Entry to KSA on work visa (passport stamp)
        ↓
Medical examination (approved clinic)
        ↓
Biometrics (MOI enrollment)
        ↓
Iqama card issued + Absher activation
        ↓
Bank, lease, dependents, licence
```

Straightforward cases: 3–8 weeks. Wrong attestations or a slow sponsor: 12+ weeks is common.

## Phase 1: Before you fly (employer + attestation)

### Contract audit

| Line item | Verify |

### Document attestation (role-dependent)

Degrees, marriage and birth certificates for dependent filing later:
- Home country notary/ministry

Plan 8–12 weeks pre-arrival for US/UK/Indian chains.

## Phase 4: Biometrics and MOI enrollment

Biometrics capture fingerprints and facial data linked to iqama application.

- PRO schedules Jawazat/MOI appointment
- Bring passport, visa copy, employer letter
- Dress modestly, government setting

Children: dependent biometrics separate track after sponsor iqama active.

## Phase 5: Iqama card collection

Plastic card issued, verify:
- Name spelling matches passport
- Profession matches contract
- Expiry date, calendar renewal 30 days before
- Sponsor name correct employer entity

Carry laminated copy in wallet; photo on phone backup.

Digital: Absher shows iqama status, employer also sees Qiwa dashboard.

## Absher: government portal essentials

Absher (web + app) centralises:

| Service | Use |
|---|---|
| Iqama expiry check | Renewal alerts |
| Traffic fines (Saher) | Pay before renewal block |
| Dependent visa | After eligibility met |
| Exit/re-entry visa | Travel while employed |
| Final exit | Leaving KSA permanently |

Activation: needs Saudi mobile, iqama, bank sometimes, PRO walks first login.

Security: enable two-factor; do not share Absher password with unofficial "fixers".

Fine payment: unpaid traffic fines block iqama renewal, treat as payroll-critical.

## Iqama renewal: annual or multi-year
