## What is iqama and why does everything wait on it?

Iqama is typically the Saudi residence ID tied to your sponsor for employment cases, and without it you usually cannot open a salary account or register Ejar. Invest Gulf budgets 3 to 8 weeks on the ground for straightforward files after entry across the area.

Invest Gulf Saudi relocation files treat employer iqama as a 3 to 8 week on-ground project after entry for straightforward employment cases, not a two-day counter visit in Riyadh or Jeddah government offices. Medical and biometrics must run through approved clinics only, while Absher activation, payroll banking, and Ejar usually wait for a valid card number to appear in government systems. Dependent sponsorship needs separate salary and housing thresholds and should not start before primary iqama is active on Absher. Late renewal can bring daily fines and Saher unpaid tickets block renewals, so calendar alerts at 30 days matter for every household. Premium Residency cited around SAR 4 million is a separate track and must not be confused with employee issuance timelines. Changing employer resets parts of the file, so time any job move away from renewal windows.

Without valid iqama you typically cannot:
- Open salary bank account (SAMA KYC)
- Register Ejar lease in your name
- Enrol children in international schools long-term
- Obtain Saudi driving licence (standard path)
- Sponsor dependents (after salary rules met)
