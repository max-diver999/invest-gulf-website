Qatar residency by investment is **not one programme** — it is a **stack of pathways** where property ownership in **MOJ-approved freehold zones** can unlock a **renewable residence permit** from roughly **QAR 730,000**, while **Permanent Residency** under Law 10/2018 sits on a **separate elite tier** at **QAR 3.65 million** with caps and extra criteria. Developers blur these constantly. Immigration authorities do not.

This guide is the **investment-focused residency entry point** for Qatar — thresholds, zones, MOI process, comparison to **UAE Golden Visa**, family rules, and red flags. For property mechanics (yields, fees), see [Qatar property investment guide](/guides/qatar-property-investment-guide/). For step-by-step ownership residency detail, see [Qatar residency by property](/guides/qatar-residency-by-property/).

> **YMYL Disclaimer:** Immigration rules change via cabinet decisions and MOI circulars. Thresholds here are **reference figures** — **[VERIFY]** with MOI and Ministry of Justice before purchase. Not legal or immigration advice.

## Programme map: three tracks buyers confuse

| Track | Threshold (public sources) | Duration | Purchase-only? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Property investor permit** | ~QAR 730,000 [VERIFY] | Renewable [VERIFY period] | No — application required |
| **Higher property tier** | ~QAR 3.65M [VERIFY] | Longer options cited | No |
| **Permanent Residency (Law 10/2018)** | 20 years residence + criteria | Long-term elite | **No** — not buy-and-get |

**Property residency** = live in Qatar tied to qualifying ownership.  
**Permanent Residency** = capped national programme — Arabic, residence history, conduct requirements.

Never buy because a brochure said **"permanent visa included."**

## MOI application process (high-level)

**After qualifying purchase:**

1. **Complete purchase** — SPA, payment, MOJ registration
2. **Collect documents** — title deed, passport, photos, medical, insurance [VERIFY list]
3. **Submit MOI application** — property investor category
4. **Background checks** — standard GCC immigration screening
5. **QID issuance** — renewable residence ID
6. **Dependants** — separate filings if permitted [VERIFY]

**Timeline:** weeks to months — not SPA signing day.

**Property residency does not replace work visa** if you take local employment.

## Mortgage and equity rules

Qatari banks offer mortgages to expats on approved projects [VERIFY]. For residency:
