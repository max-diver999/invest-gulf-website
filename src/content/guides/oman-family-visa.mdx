# Oman Family Visa 2026: ROP Sponsorship, Salary Rules & Dependent Documents

**TL;DR:** Oman family joining runs through **Royal Oman Police (ROP)** on your **employment residence card — not on visit visas and not on verbal HR promises. Salary thresholds, **job category**, and **family-suitable housing must align before dependants fly. Budget **OMR 200–600 per dependant in government and medical fees plus **6–12 weeks processing after your card is active. Read your **labour contract basic salary line before you sign — housing and schooling allowances usually **do not count. **Hub:** [Oman relocation guide](/guides/oman-relocation-guide/) (R52)

## Who can sponsor dependants in Oman

You must hold valid employment residence sponsored by a licensed Omani employer (or authorised branch). PRO submits on your behalf — self-filing without employer cooperation is rare for standard work visas.

Typically eligible sponsors:
- Skilled private-sector employees with qualifying salary and title
- Government and semi-government employees per ROP tables **[VERIFY]**
- Some investor residency holders — **separate rules, not automatic

Usually cannot sponsor if:
- You are on visit visa only
- Residence card expired or in transfer dispute
- Basic salary below ROP minimum for your category
- Employer refuses salary certificate for ROP
- Housing is bachelor-shared and you apply for children

## Salary and job category rules — verify before you fly

Oman does not publish one consumer-friendly “OMR X family visa” page that stays stable year to year. Planning ranges from ROP practice and employer PRO experience:

| Factor | Planning assumption | Your action |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic salary | Often **OMR 300–500+** for private skilled roles sponsoring spouse + children **[VERIFY]** | Match labour contract line to PRO table |
| **Allowances | Housing, transport, schooling — **usually excluded | Restructure contract pre-arrival if borderline |
| **Job title / category | Labour classification must permit dependants | HR confirms with Ministry of Labour |
| **Gender of sponsor | Male employee sponsor is standard path | Female-led families need legal review |
| **Parents | Higher salary and case-specific **[VERIFY]** | Do not assume same as children |

Example trap: Basic OMR 280 + housing OMR 200 + transport OMR 80 = OMR 560 headline — family joining denied if threshold keys off basic only.

Fix before move: raise basic above PRO minimum even if total package unchanged — employer legal must agree.

Compare Gulf pattern: [Qatar family visa](/guides/qatar-family-visa-sponsorship/) QAR 10,000 basic — Oman thresholds are lower in nominal OMR but job category filter bites equally hard.

## Step-by-step PRO workflow

### Step 1 — Sponsor residence stabilised

Your card active, medical complete, employer payroll registered. PRO will not file dependants on pending sponsor status.

### Step 2 — Document bundle to PRO

Salary certificate, lease, marriage/birth certs, photos. PRO reviews against ROP checklist.

### Step 3 — Entry permit / visa authorisation for dependants

Dependants should not fly on visit visa if residence is goal — wait for correct permit type.

### Step 4 — Arrival medical and biometrics

Fitness test at authorised clinic; biometrics for residence card.

### Step 5 — Dependent residence cards issued

Collect cards; register for insurance extension; begin school and bank steps.

Timeline: 6–12 weeks typical after sponsor ready; attestation adds pre-arrival months.

## Newborn registration in Oman — hospital to residence card

A baby born in Muscat triggers civil registration and ROP add-on to sponsor file in parallel.

### Within first days (hospital)

- Hospital issues **birth notification — request Arabic and English copies
- Confirm father's residence card and attested marriage on hospital file
- Add newborn to **employer insurance within policy window (often 30 days)

### ROP steps (typical order)

| Step | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Birth certificate | Parents | Municipality / civil authority |
| Baby passport from embassy | Parents | Book embassy slot early |
| Add dependant to sponsor file | PRO | Updated salary certificate |
| Infant medical | Authorised clinic | PRO schedules |
| Dependent residence card | ROP | Fees per child tier |

Timeline target: 60–90 days from birth; delays risk fines.

If mother on visit during birth: status is sensitive — PRO and embassy before due date.

## Divorce, separation and custody edge cases

ROP logic assumes intact marriage chain. Separation needs Omani counsel early.

- **Still married but separated:** spouse status tied to sponsor until exit or independent permit
- **Divorce abroad:** residence cancellation for spouse may be required before remarriage sponsorship elsewhere
- **Single mother sponsoring child:** case-specific — employer letter critical
- **Death of sponsor:** dependants enter grace pathway — embassy and HR emergency protocol

Do not rely on forum advice — PRO plus licensed lawyer for your nationality.
