## Who is this Oman family visa guide for?

This guide is for expat employees in Muscat and other governorates who need to bring a spouse, children, or sometimes parents on employment-sponsored ROP residence, not tourist visits, with Invest Gulf sequencing salary proof, housing, and school seats across a 6 to 12 week filing window for foreign families.

Property buyers without employment must use separate investment residency rules if available. ## Who can sponsor dependants in Oman?

Sponsors typically eligible under ROP practice are skilled private-sector employees with qualifying basic salary and job title, government or semi-government staff in published categories, and some investor residency holders on separate NPRA tracks that our underwriting treats as non-automatic even after an ITC purchase.

Usually cannot sponsor if:

- You hold visit visa only or your residence card expired
- Basic salary falls below ROP minimum for your job category
- Employer refuses to issue a salary certificate for ROP
- Housing is bachelor-shared while you apply for children
- Active labour dispute or sponsor blacklist flags appear

Confirm current official rules with your employer PRO before booking dependant flights.

## What housing proof does ROP expect?

ROP typically expects family-suitable housing shown by a registered lease or employer housing letter with adequate bedrooms for dependant count, and bachelor studio layouts fail when sponsoring children for at least the first 12 months of family residence on Invest Gulf housing reviews. Confirm current official rules before you commit dates.

- Registered lease or employer housing letter on file
- Bedroom count matches spouse plus children
- Municipality registration aligned to utilities
- No bachelor-share layouts while sponsoring minors

## What documents belong in the PRO pack?

PRO submission typically requires sponsor passport and residence card, MOHRE contract with basic salary, recent salary certificate, registered lease or housing letter, plus spouse and child attestation packs that Invest Gulf files as an 8 to 12 week pre-travel checklist for foreign families. Confirm current official rules before you commit dates.

Sponsor pack:

- Passport and valid Oman employment residence card
- MOHRE labour contract showing basic salary and title
- Salary certificate for ROP (employer letterhead, recent date)
- Registered lease or employer housing letter
- Company CR copy and authorised signatory PRO mandate

Spouse and each child need passport (6+ months validity), fully attested civil documents, ROP-spec photos, and medical after entry permit. Name spelling must match passport MRZ exactly.
