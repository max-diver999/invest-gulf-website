> Disclaimer: ROP circulars change without wide publicity. This is operational guidance, not immigration legal advice. Your employer PRO is the primary source; confirm every threshold at application.

Oman family joining runs through Royal Oman Police (ROP) on your employment residence card, not on visit visas and not on verbal HR promises. Salary thresholds, job category, and family-suitable housing must align before dependants fly. Budget OMR 200-600 per dependant in government and medical fees plus 6-12 weeks processing after your card is active. Read your labour contract basic salary line before you sign: housing and schooling allowances usually do not count.

## Who is this guide for?

Expat employees in Muscat and other governorates who need to bring a spouse, children, or in some cases parents. The guide covers employment-sponsored family residence, not tourist visits. Property buyers without employment must use separate investment residency rules if available.

Cross-link: [Oman relocation week map](/guides/oman-property-foreigner-living/) → this guide for salary and housing → [Muscat international schools](/guides/muscat-international-schools/) for seat timing before lease signing.

## Who can sponsor dependants in Oman?

Typically eligible sponsors:

- Skilled private-sector employees with qualifying basic salary and job title per ROP tables (confirm current official rules with PRO).
- Government and semi-government employees per published categories.
- Some investor residency holders under separate NPRA tracks (not automatic with every property purchase).

Usually cannot sponsor if:

- You hold visit visa only or your residence card expired.
- Basic salary falls below ROP minimum for your job category.
- Employer refuses to issue salary certificate for ROP.
- Housing is bachelor-shared while you apply for children.
- Active labour dispute or sponsor blacklist flags appear on MOHRE or ROP systems.

## Salary and job category rules

ROP ties family joining to sponsor **basic salary** and **contract classification**, not always total package.

| Factor | Practical impact |
| --- | --- |
| Basic vs allowances | Housing, transport, and schooling allowances often excluded |
| Job title | Skilled categories qualify at lower basic than labour categories |
| Parent sponsorship | Higher basic and separate approval common |
| Salary certificate | Must match MOHRE contract line-by-line |

Fix before move: if your basic sits below PRO minimum but total package is strong, ask employer legal to restructure contract before dependants apply.

Compare Gulf pattern: [Qatar family visa sponsorship](/guides/qatar-family-visa-sponsorship/) uses QAR 10,000 basic reference; Oman thresholds are lower in nominal OMR but job category filters bite equally hard.

## Who you can sponsor

| Dependant | Typical path | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spouse | Marriage certificate attestation chain | MRZ name match strict |
| Children | Birth certificate attestation | School letter helps narrative, not visa substitute |
| Parents | Higher salary, case-by-case | Plan longer PRO timeline |
| Newborn in Oman | Birth registration then add to file | Hospital social worker often guides first steps |

Male dependant sponsorship (husband on wife's visa) is restricted; engage Omani counsel early if your family structure requires it.

## Housing and accommodation proof

ROP expects family-suitable housing:

- Registered lease or employer housing letter showing adequate bedrooms for dependant count.
- Bachelor studio layouts fail when sponsoring children.
- Municipality-registered tenancy aligns utilities and visa paperwork.

ITC property owners: buying in Al Mouj or other zones does not replace employment salary rules unless you hold a verified investor residency track that explicitly allows dependants. See [Oman ITC zones property](/guides/oman-itc-zones-property/) and [Oman residency by investment](/guides/oman-residency-by-investment/).

## Document checklist before PRO submission

### Sponsor (employee)

- Passport and valid Oman employment residence card.
- MOHRE labour contract showing basic salary and title.
- Salary certificate for ROP (employer letterhead, recent date).
- Registered lease or employer housing letter.
- Company CR copy and authorised signatory PRO mandate.

### Spouse

- Passport (6+ months validity).
- Marriage certificate fully attested (home country → embassy → Oman MFA as required).
- Photos per ROP specification.
- Medical appointment after entry permit issued.

### Each child

- Passport.
- Birth certificate attestation complete.
- Photos.
- Vaccination records for school later (not visa substitute).

Name spelling must match passport MRZ exactly; PRO rejects mismatches.

## Attestation timelines by nationality

Start attestation 8-12 weeks before dependant travel.

### India

1. Notary → state Home Department.
2. MEA apostille.
3. Oman Embassy in India (often 3-6 weeks).

### Philippines

1. PSA marriage or birth certificate.
2. DFA authentication.
3. Oman Embassy Manila (often 2-5 weeks).

### United States

1. County → state Secretary of State.
2. US Department of State apostille.
3. Oman Embassy Washington (often 6-10 weeks).

PRO may demand originals on first submission; courier loss is a real risk, use tracked shipping.

## Step-by-step PRO workflow

| Step | Owner | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Eligibility check | PRO | Salary, title, housing |
| 2. Dependent application | PRO | ROP portal or manual file |
| 3. Entry permit | ROP | Spouse/children abroad |
| 4. Travel | Family | After permit PDF issued |
| 5. Medical | Authorised clinic | Per dependant |
| 6. Residence cards | ROP | Fees per person |
| 7. Insurance activation | Sponsor | Employer or private policy |

Push PRO for application reference number; without it you cannot track queue status.

## Fees and budgets (indicative OMR)

Government fees vary by nationality and dependant type; confirm at application.

| Item | Indicative range (OMR) |
| --- | --- |
| Dependent application / card | 50-150 per person |
| Medical per adult | 15-40 |
| Medical per child | 10-30 |
| Residency stamp / renewal | 20-60 |
| PRO service bundle | 100-300 |

Total first-time dependant stack often lands OMR 150-350 per person excluding attestation courier and flights. Parent applications can run higher.

Do not rely on UAE fee tables (Emirates ID or GDRFA tariffs); Oman uses ROP and MOH pricing in rials.

## Insurance and healthcare after approval

Employer medical insurance must cover dependants once cards issue. Lapsed coverage blocks renewal. Register dependants with the same insurer network your employer uses to avoid hospital cash deposits.

See [Oman healthcare guide](/guides/oman-healthcare-guide/) and [Oman banking for expats](/guides/oman-banking-expats/) for account setup after cards.

## Schools and visa timing

International schools in Muscat fill seats by spring for September intake. Sequence:

1. Sponsor card active and salary certificate in hand.
2. Housing lease signed (schools ask for proof).
3. School application while dependant file processes.
4. Dependent entry permit before booking non-refundable flights.

File renewal at least 30 days before card expiry; overstay fines compound quickly.

## Spouse practical life after residence card

Spouse receives residence card tied to sponsor employment. Employment for spouse requires separate work visa unless policy changes; do not assume automatic work rights. Driving licence conversion follows ROP and Royal Oman Police traffic rules; see [Oman driving licence](/guides/oman-driving-license/).

## Visa cancellation and job loss

If you lose employment:

- Confirm grace days with PRO immediately.
- Transfer sponsorship to new employer or plan coordinated family exit.
- Cancel dependant cards in order ROP specifies.

Dependant cards typically lapse when sponsor employment ends unless a new sponsor file completes in time.

## Investor residency dependants

Investment residency (property or company routes) carries separate NPRA and ROP rules. Property in ITC zones supports lifestyle and may support certain residency products, but **employment family joining rules do not automatically apply**. Legal review before promising family arrival dates to schools or landlords.

## Metrash2 equivalent and PRO tracking

Oman does not mirror UAE Metrash2 self-service for every visa step. Expect employer PRO to hold paper receipts and portal screenshots.

Track weekly:

- Application reference number from ROP.
- Medical appointment slips for each dependant.
- Entry permit PDF before anyone books flights.
- Payment receipts for each dependant tranche.

If PRO is silent for 14 days, escalate through company HR with written follow-up. Absence of a digital tracker is normal; your offline folder is the system of record.

## Newborn registration in Oman

| Step | Owner | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Birth certificate | Parents | Civil authority |
| Baby passport | Embassy | Book early |
| Add to sponsor file | PRO | Updated salary certificate |
| Infant medical | Clinic | PRO schedules |
| Dependent card | ROP | Child tier fees |

Target 60-90 days from birth; delays risk fines. If mother holds visit status during birth, status is sensitive: PRO and embassy consult before due date.

## Parent sponsorship overview

Higher basic salary thresholds and separate ROP approval are common. Start 3-6 months before planned travel; medical and insurance costs rise with age.

## Divorce, separation, and custody

Custody documentation from home country courts may need attestation. ROP and embassy involvement increases; counsel familiar with Oman family immigration should review orders before applications.

## PRO mistakes that delay files

- Submitting scans when originals required first pass.
- Salary certificate total package without basic line highlighted.
- Housing letter describing bachelor layout while sponsoring two children.
- Dependants flying on visit visa before residence entry permit.

Track: application reference, medical slips, entry permit PDF, and payment receipts in one folder.

## Timing strategy by school calendar

| Calendar window | Action |
| --- | --- |
| January-March | Apply for September school seats |
| April-June | Complete housing and dependant file |
| July-August | Cards and insurance before term start |

## Printable dependant checklist

**Spouse:** passport 6+ months, attested marriage certificate, photos, medical after permit.

**Each child:** passport, attested birth certificate, photos, school reference if mid-year.

**After approval:** dependant cards collected, insurance active, school seat confirmed, spouse bank KYC booked.

## Red flags before you commit to relocation dates

- HR verbal promise without written basic salary on contract.
- Landlord unwilling to register lease municipality-required for your PRO.
- School place offered but dependant visa not started.
- Parents booked flights before entry permit PDF exists.

## Pros and cons summary

**Pros:** Lower nominal salary thresholds than Qatar on some categories, family-oriented Muscat communities, manageable government fees per dependant.

**Cons:** PRO tracking less digital than UAE apps, attestation chains slow, job loss ends sponsor chain quickly, property purchase alone rarely replaces employment sponsorship for family.
