## Dubai South Free Zone at a Glance

Dubai South free zone operates inside **Dubai World Central (DWC)**, the master-planned city surrounding Al Maktoum International Airport in south Dubai. Where JAFZA built around Jebel Ali Port and DMCC built around JLT commodities desks, Dubai South built around **aviation, air cargo, logistics parks, and the residential communities** that now surround Expo City and the airport expansion corridor.

Dubai South free zone companies range from freight forwarders and aviation support providers to e-commerce traders and professional services firms that want a south Dubai address without paying JLT premiums. The zone pairs naturally with **property investment in Dubai South** because founders can live, work, and store inventory within the same master community. For off-plan and yield context in the district, read [best off-plan Dubai South](/guides/best-off-plan-dubai-south/).

Why founders choose Dubai South in 2026:

- **Al Maktoum Airport proximity:** air cargo, aviation services, and airport-linked logistics
- **South Dubai master plan:** residential, commercial, and logistics zones in one jurisdiction
- **Mid-tier pricing:** lean setups often beat DMCC on year-one cost
- **E-commerce and trading licences:** alongside logistics activities
- **100% foreign ownership:** standard for DWC establishments and companies
- **Growing bank familiarity:** institutions increasingly recognise DWC on corporate KYC

Dubai South is not the default for Jebel Ali container warehouse scale or DFSA-regulated finance. Compare [JAFZA company setup](/guides/jafza-company-setup/) for port logistics and [DMCC company setup](/guides/dmcc-company-setup/) for JLT commodities and services prestige.

## Dubai South Company Types

**Free Zone Establishment (FZE):** single shareholder. Common for owner-operated logistics and trading houses.

**Free Zone Company (FZCo):** multiple shareholders. Used for joint ventures and investor groups.

**Branch of a Foreign Company:** extension of an existing overseas entity. Parent retains liability.

All structures allow 100% foreign ownership. Minimum share capital is typically nominal for standard trading and service activities, confirm the current Dubai South schedule for your activity code before filing.

## Step-by-Step Dubai South Registration Process

### Step 1: Define Activity and Workspace Tier

Map your operations: air cargo coordination, pure e-commerce, or warehouse-linked trading? Pick the Dubai South activity code that matches customs and invoice descriptions you will use in year one. Select flexi-desk, smart office, or warehouse tier based on visa quota and storage needs.

### Step 2: Reserve Company Name

Follow UAE naming rules. Avoid restricted words and conflicts with existing DWC, mainland, or other free zone entities. Approval typically within 1-2 business days.

### Step 3: Submit Shareholder KYC and Application

Individual shareholders provide:

- Passport copy (six months validity)
- Proof of address under three months
- Bank reference or source of funds (trading and logistics activities)
- CV or trade experience summary

Corporate shareholders add incorporation certificates, good standing, board resolution, and full UBO disclosure.

### Step 4: Pay Dubai South Fees and Pass Compliance

Dubai South reviews AML, UBO, and package alignment. Mismatch between stated trading volume and zero-visa flexi package triggers questions. Pay licence, registration, and workspace fees upon approval in principle.

### Step 5: Collect Licence and Corporate Documents

You receive trade licence, incorporation certificate, MOA, and share certificates. Register for customs codes if you will import or export physically through UAE gateways.

### Step 6: Open Bank Account and Sponsor Visas

Use licence and workspace contract for corporate banking. Logistics founders should prepare supplier and buyer contracts, expected shipment volumes, and HS code summaries. Allow **2-6 weeks** for accounts. See [opening a bank account in Dubai](/guides/open-bank-account-dubai/).

Activate establishment card, then process visas up to package quota. Medical test and Emirates ID biometrics require UAE presence.

## Tax Position for Dubai South Companies

UAE corporate tax applies from June 2023. Dubai South entities may pursue **Qualifying Free Zone Person** status for 0% on Qualifying Income when substance and de minimis tests are met. Non-qualifying mainland revenue remains exposed to 9% tax.

Trading and logistics companies often mix:

- Qualifying free zone to free zone sales
- Qualifying foreign export revenue
- Non-qualifying mainland B2B or retail income

Model each stream before year-end. Transfer pricing applies to related-party flows between your DWC entity and mainland affiliates.

Full planning context: [UAE corporate tax for expats](/guides/uae-corporate-tax-expats/).

## Banking and Trade Finance for Dubai South Entities

Dubai South companies open standard corporate accounts and, with track record, trade finance lines. Banks expect:

- Trade licence matching import/export or service activity
- Flexi-desk or office contract proof
- Projected shipment or revenue plan for logistics traders
- Counterparty contracts or purchase orders where available
- Clean shareholder source-of-funds narrative

Emirates NBD, Mashreq, ADCB, and RAK Bank serve DWC entities. Allow **2-6 weeks** for first account; trade finance facilities follow after operating history.

## Is Dubai South Free Zone Right for Your Business?

**Dubai South is the right choice when:**

- Operations tie to **Al Maktoum Airport** or south Dubai logistics parks
- You want mid-tier pricing with Dubai free zone credibility
- You plan **property in Dubai South** alongside company setup
- You run e-commerce with optional nearby fulfilment growth path
- JLT prestige matters less than airport and residential alignment

**Dubai South is likely not the right choice when:**

- Containers move primarily through **Jebel Ali Port** at warehouse scale (consider JAFZA)
- You need DMCC commodities infrastructure or JLT anchor address (consider DMCC)
- Budget is under AED 15,000 year one (consider IFZA or Meydan)
- You need DFSA-regulated financial services (consider DIFC)
- You sell digital services only with no UAE logistics angle (consider IFZA)
