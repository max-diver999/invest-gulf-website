## FAQ

**Is Dubai expensive for expats?**
Mid-tier family COL is **high but predictable**, no income tax offsets rent and schools. Premium lifestyle costs match London fringe; value areas (JVC, Mirdif) run **20–30% below** Marina equivalents.

**What is a comfortable salary in Dubai?**
Single **AED 20,000+ net** mid lifestyle; family of four **AED 38,000–45,000+ net** with British mid schools and JVC or Mirdif rent.

**How much are utilities in Dubai?**
DEWA **AED 350–900** for apartments; villas **AED 800–2,000+** in summer, add district cooling **AED 300–900** in towers.

**Is Ejari mandatory?**
Yes for visa, DEWA, most schools. Do not skip registration.

**Are Dubai schools expensive?**
British and IB premium tiers run **AED 85,000–120,000/year** per child, Indian CBSE offers value at **AED 25,000–40,000**.

**Do I need a car?**
Almost always **yes** for family life; Marina singles sometimes metro-heavy but car ownership is norm.

**Best area for value families?**
**JVC**, **Mirdif**, **Sports City** for space; **Dubai Hills** for schools plus master-plan amenities at premium.

**Does Golden Visa reduce living costs?**
No, it stabilises residency; rent, schools and DEWA remain.
