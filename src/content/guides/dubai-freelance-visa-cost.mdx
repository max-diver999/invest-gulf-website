## Why do Dubai freelance visa quotes look cheaper than the real bill?

Dubai freelance visa quotes typically mean the free zone permit only, not the full year-one bill. Real spend stacks permit (AED 7,500 to 15,500), government fees (AED 3,735 to 5,025), and DHA insurance (AED 2,400 to 7,200). We model all three.

1. Free zone permit fee: commercial permit to operate as a freelancer (annual or every 2 to 3 years)
2. UAE residence visa costs: entry permit, Emirates ID, medical fitness test
3. Health insurance: mandatory but separate from permit and visa lines

PRO service fees of AED 1,500 to 3,500 can push year one further above the headline package. Request a line-item schedule, not a single bundle number.

## How much does a GoFreelance (TECOM) permit cost in 2026?

GoFreelance (TECOM) permit cost typically ranges from AED 9,500 to 12,000 for a single activity before e-channel fees. Adding AED 2,020 e-channel and AED 1,500 to 2,500 per extra activity often lands near AED 11,530 to 14,030. Our research treats TECOM as the media and tech route.

| Component | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Single activity permit (1 year) | AED 9,500 to 12,000 |
| Additional activity (per activity) | AED 1,500 to 2,500 |
| E-channel registration fee | AED 2,020 |
| Knowledge and Innovation fee | AED 10 |
| Permit total (single activity) | AED 11,530 to 14,030 |

GoFreelance permits renew annually. Confirm your activity category is listed before applying.

### Meydan Free Zone

Meydan Free Zone typically costs AED 7,500 to 9,000 for a starter one-activity permit, making it one of the cheapest Dubai freelance routes. Standard packages with visa processing support rise to AED 12,000 to 15,000; multi-activity packs sit near AED 11,000 to 14,000. Invest Gulf uses Meydan as the low-entry benchmark for foreign buyers on a tight budget.

| Package | Annual Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Starter (1 activity, no visa included) | AED 7,500 to 9,000 |
| Standard (1 activity + visa processing support) | AED 12,000 to 15,000 |
| Multi-activity (up to 3 activities) | AED 11,000 to 14,000 |

Read bundled visa packages carefully so you do not double-count government fees.

### IFZA (International Free Zone Authority: Dubai Silicon Oasis)

IFZA freelance permits typically cost AED 12,900 to 15,500 for one activity, with AED 2,000 to 3,500 per extra activity and optional visa add-ons of AED 3,500 to 5,000. The DSO address and portal support suit consultants who want hands-on free zone ops. Invest Gulf prices IFZA above Meydan on permit alone.

| Component | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Freelance permit (1 activity, 1 year) | AED 12,900 to 15,500 |
| Additional activity | AED 2,000 to 3,500 |
| Visa package add-on (optional) | AED 3,500 to 5,000 |

### Dubai South Free Zone

Dubai South single-activity permits typically range from AED 8,500 to 11,000, with multi-activity packs near AED 12,000 to 16,000. The Al Maktoum corridor suits logistics and aviation freelancers more than TECOM media brands. We keep Dubai South as a cost-competitive alternative when TECOM prestige is unnecessary.

### Fujairah Creative City (outside Dubai, UAE)

Fujairah Creative City permits typically start around AED 7,000 to 9,500 for a single activity, comparable to Meydan on price but outside Dubai. UAE residence from Fujairah is valid, yet some Dubai banks and client contracts prefer a Dubai free zone address. We flag address friction before recommending Fujairah to foreign buyers.

## What are the UAE residence visa government fees for freelancers?

UAE residence visa government fees for freelancers typically total AED 3,735 to 5,025 once entry permit, Emirates ID, medical, and typing fees are stacked. Emirates ID is AED 370 for two years or AED 570 for five years; medical tests run AED 270 to 420. Invest Gulf subtracts e-channel AED 2,020 when already bundled.

| Fee component | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Entry permit (for overseas applicants) | AED 465 to 735 |
| Residency stamp / change of status (in-country) | AED 460 to 680 |
| Emirates ID application | AED 370 (2-year) / AED 570 (5-year) |
| Medical fitness test | AED 270 to 420 |
| E-channel registration (if not included by free zone) | AED 2,020 |
| Typing / service centre fee | AED 150 to 300 |
| Government fees subtotal | AED 3,735 to 5,025 |

## How much should you budget for mandatory health insurance?

Mandatory health insurance typically costs AED 2,400 to 7,200 per year for a single DHA-compliant plan. Essential Benefits sits near AED 2,400 to 3,600; mid-tier runs AED 4,500 to 6,500; age-45+ covers often reach AED 6,500 to 10,000+. We budget the upper band when 20 to 50% loadings apply.

| Coverage tier | Typical annual premium (single) | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DHA Essential Benefits Plan (minimum) | AED 2,400 to 3,600 | Visa compliance on a tight budget |
| Mid-tier enhanced | AED 4,500 to 6,500 | Broader hospital network, lower co-pays |
| Comprehensive / age 45+ | AED 6,500 to 10,000+ | Pre-existing loadings, maternity add-ons |

Minimum DHA coverage is enough for visa stamping, yet network limits push many freelancers to mid-tier cover.

## Is the UAE Green Visa worth the higher upfront cost for freelancers?

The UAE Green Visa for freelancers typically requires AED 360,000 per year in income and costs more upfront, yet five-year residency can save AED 3,000 to 7,000 versus 2-year cycles. Permit renewals of AED 7,500 to 15,000 still apply yearly. Invest Gulf favours Green Visa when foreign buyers clear the income gate and plan 3+ years.

| Scenario | Year 1 | Years 2 to 5 (combined) | 5-year total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meydan permit + annual 2-year visa cycle | AED 15,000 | AED 44,000 | AED 59,000 |
| Meydan permit + Green Visa (5-year) | AED 18,000 | AED 38,000 | AED 56,000 |
| GoFreelance + annual 2-year visa cycle | AED 22,000 | AED 60,000 | AED 82,000 |
| GoFreelance + Green Visa (5-year) | AED 23,000 | AED 52,000 | AED 75,000 |

Stability, bank KYC strength, and family sponsorship framing matter as much as the AED 3,000 to 7,000 saving. See the [UAE Green Visa freelancer guide](/guides/uae-green-visa-freelancer/).

## When do you pay each freelance visa cost during year one?

Payment timing typically means attestation and PRO before Month 0, permit and e-channel at registration, then medical, Emirates ID, and insurance in Month 0 to 1. Permit fees of AED 7,500 to 15,000 hit at signup; insurance of AED 2,400 to 7,200+ renews yearly. We map a three-year calendar.

- Before applying: attestation AED 0 to 2,000; PRO budget AED 1,500 to 3,500
- Month 0: permit AED 7,500 to 15,000; e-channel AED 2,020 if not bundled
- Month 0 to 1: entry/status AED 460 to 735; medical AED 270 to 420; Emirates ID AED 370 to 570; insurance AED 2,400 to 7,200+
- Renewal: permit AED 7,500 to 15,000; visa cycle AED 2,500 to 4,500; insurance again

## What compliance risks should freelancers plan for?

Compliance risk typically means insurance lapses, missed permit renewals, or tax thresholds that delay Emirates ID or bank KYC. Revenue above AED 375,000 may require corporate tax registration; VAT uses the same AED 375,000 gate. We treat one uninsured day as a renewal blocker.

- Permit renewal: expired licences invalidate visa sponsorship
- Insurance gap: blocks Emirates ID renewal
- Corporate tax / VAT: watch the AED 375,000 thresholds
- Bank KYC: keep six months of invoices aligned to licensed activities

## How do family and multi-year costs change the math?

Family and multi-year costs typically mean AED 1,800 to 3,000 per dependant in visa processing plus separate DHA insurance for each person. Year-two spend usually drops because you renew permit and insurance without full first-issue visa fees. We model school deposits and Ejari before free zone choice.

Five-year freelance residency math for Dubai should start from the same permit, visa, and insurance stack used in year one, then stretch it across renewals. A Meydan plus annual 2-year visa path can total about AED 59,000 over five years before insurance, while Meydan plus Green Visa often lands near AED 56,000 on the same permit renewals. GoFreelance with annual visa cycles can reach about AED 82,000 over five years, versus roughly AED 75,000 on a Green Visa track, saving AED 3,000 to 7,000 before counting bank KYC friction. Add dependants at AED 1,800 to 3,000 each in processing plus AED 2,400 to 7,200 insurance per person, and the lowest permit stops being the cheapest household plan. Invest Gulf recommends Green Visa only after freelance income already clears AED 360,000 per year and the stay horizon is at least three years.

Insider tip: Ask every free zone for a PDF that separates permit, e-channel, government visa, and insurance so you can delete double-counted lines before you sign; the AED 2,020 e-channel fee is the line agents most often bill twice.
