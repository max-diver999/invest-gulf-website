### Meydan Free Zone

Meydan Free Zone is one of the most cost-competitive Dubai free zones for freelancers. It accepts a broad range of activity categories and has simplified its package structure. The Meydan address (Al Meydan Road, Nad Al Sheba) is less prestigious than DMC/DIC but is fully accepted for UAE residence visa sponsorship and works for most bank account applications.

Meydan Free Zone freelance permit cost (2026):

| Package | Annual Fee |
|---|---|
| Starter (1 activity, no visa included) | AED 7,500–9,000 |
| Standard (1 activity + visa processing support) | AED 12,000–15,000 |
| Multi-activity (up to 3 activities) | AED 11,000–14,000 |

Meydan's "visa processing support" packages bundle some government visa fees into the package price, read the breakdown carefully to avoid double-counting when comparing with base permit quotes elsewhere.

### IFZA (International Free Zone Authority: Dubai Silicon Oasis)

IFZA is Dubai-based and one of the most popular overall free zones in the UAE for small businesses and solo operators. For freelancers specifically, IFZA offers competitive permit costs with Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) addresses.

IFZA freelance permit cost (2026):

| Component | Cost |
|---|---|
| Freelance permit (1 activity, 1 year) | AED 12,900–15,500 |
| Additional activity | AED 2,000–3,500 |
| Visa package add-on (optional) | AED 3,500–5,000 |

IFZA tends to price higher than Meydan on the permit itself but includes more hands-on support and has a well-developed client portal. Strong for tech, consulting, and professional services.

### Dubai South Free Zone

Dubai South (near Al Maktoum International Airport) has emerged as a cost-competitive option for logistics, aviation, and general professional services freelancers.

Dubai South freelance permit (indicative, 2026):
- Single activity permit: AED 8,500–11,000
- Multi-activity: AED 12,000–16,000

### Fujairah Creative City (outside Dubai, UAE)

For context: Fujairah Creative City is not a Dubai free zone but is frequently compared. Permit fees start around AED 7,000–9,500 for a single activity. UAE residence visa from Fujairah-based permits is fully valid, but the Fujairah address may create complications for some Dubai-based bank accounts or client contracts expecting a Dubai address.

Can I work for UAE clients with a freelance permit?

Yes. A UAE free zone freelance permit allows you to invoice UAE-based clients. Be aware that UAE corporate tax rules may apply to business income exceeding AED 375,000 annually, and VAT registration is required once taxable supplies exceed AED 375,000/year. For a solo freelancer below these thresholds, there is no corporate tax or VAT obligation.

What if my income drops below the Green Visa threshold?

The Green Visa income threshold (AED 360,000/year) is assessed at application and renewal. If your income decreases substantially, you will not be removed mid-visa. At the 5-year renewal, you will need to demonstrate you still qualify. If you do not meet the threshold at renewal, you can downgrade to a standard 2-year free zone residence visa without penalty.

Is a co-working space included in any free zone package?

Some free zone packages include hot desk access or coworking credits, GoFreelance includes access to TECOM coworking spaces as part of certain tiers. Most basic permit packages include only a registered address, not physical desk access. Confirm what physical access, if any, is included before choosing.

Does the permit let me sponsor my family?

A UAE residence visa under a free zone permit does entitle you to sponsor eligible family members (spouse, children under 18, daughters under 21 in full-time education, parents in specific circumstances). Each dependant requires their own health insurance and incurs separate processing fees of approximately AED 1,800–3,000 per person. The Green Visa carries stronger family sponsorship entitlements than a standard 2-year permit.
