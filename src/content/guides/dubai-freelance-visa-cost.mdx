## Why do Dubai freelance visa quotes look cheaper than the real bill?

Most free zone ads quote the permit only. Year-one spend stacks three layers: the freelance permit, UAE residence visa government fees, and mandatory health insurance bought separately.

1. Free zone permit fee: the commercial permit to operate as a freelancer within that zone (issued annually or every 2 to 3 years)
2. UAE residence visa costs: government visa application, entry permit, Emirates ID, medical fitness test
3. Health insurance: mandatory but separate from both of the above

Most free zone marketing quotes only the permit fee. The total cost you actually pay is the sum of all three. Add PRO (Public Relations Officer) service fees if you're using an agent to process the visa on your behalf (AED 1,500 to 3,500 is typical), and your real all-in first-year cost can differ substantially from the headline number.

This guide breaks down each layer clearly so you can model your actual cost before committing.

## How much does a GoFreelance (TECOM) permit cost in 2026?
| Component | Cost |
|---|---|
| Single activity permit (1 year) | AED 9,500 to 12,000 |
| Additional activity (per activity) | AED 1,500 to 2,500 |
| E-channel registration fee | AED 2,020 |
| Knowledge and Innovation fee | AED 10 |
| Permit total (single activity) | AED 11,530 to 14,030 |

GoFreelance permits are issued for 1 year and must be renewed annually. The e-channel fee applies once per applicant and covers the UAE residence visa system access.

Activity categories covered: Media and journalism, advertising and PR, film and production, digital marketing, software development, IT consulting, education and training, design, fashion and lifestyle. GoFreelance is activity-specific, confirm your category is listed before applying.

### Meydan Free Zone

Meydan Free Zone is one of the most cost-competitive Dubai free zones for freelancers. It accepts a broad range of activity categories and has simplified its package structure. The Meydan address (Al Meydan Road, Nad Al Sheba) is less prestigious than DMC/DIC but is fully accepted for UAE residence visa sponsorship and works for most bank account applications.

Meydan Free Zone freelance permit cost (2026):

| Package | Annual Fee |
|---|---|
| Starter (1 activity, no visa included) | AED 7,500 to 9,000 |
| Standard (1 activity + visa processing support) | AED 12,000 to 15,000 |
| Multi-activity (up to 3 activities) | AED 11,000 to 14,000 |

Meydan's "visa processing support" packages bundle some government visa fees into the package price, read the breakdown carefully to avoid double-counting when comparing with base permit quotes elsewhere.

### IFZA (International Free Zone Authority: Dubai Silicon Oasis)

IFZA is Dubai-based and one of the most popular overall free zones in the UAE for small businesses and solo operators. For freelancers specifically, IFZA offers competitive permit costs with Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) addresses.

IFZA freelance permit cost (2026):

| Component | Cost |
|---|---|
| Freelance permit (1 activity, 1 year) | AED 12,900 to 15,500 |
| Additional activity | AED 2,000 to 3,500 |
| Visa package add-on (optional) | AED 3,500 to 5,000 |

IFZA tends to price higher than Meydan on the permit itself but includes more hands-on support and has a well-developed client portal. Strong for tech, consulting, and professional services.

### Dubai South Free Zone

Dubai South (near Al Maktoum International Airport) has emerged as a cost-competitive option for logistics, aviation, and general professional services freelancers.

Dubai South freelance permit (indicative, 2026):
- Single activity permit: AED 8,500 to 11,000
- Multi-activity: AED 12,000 to 16,000

### Fujairah Creative City (outside Dubai, UAE)

For context: Fujairah Creative City is not a Dubai free zone but is frequently compared. Permit fees start around AED 7,000 to 9,500 for a single activity. UAE residence visa from Fujairah-based permits is fully valid, but the Fujairah address may create complications for some Dubai-based bank accounts or client contracts expecting a Dubai address.
## What are the UAE residence visa government fees for freelancers?
Standard UAE residence visa government fees (June 2026):

| Fee component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Entry permit (for overseas applicants) | AED 465 to 735 |
| Residency stamp / change of status (in-country) | AED 460 to 680 |
| Emirates ID application | AED 370 (2-year) / AED 570 (5-year) |
| Medical fitness test | AED 270 to 420 |
| E-channel registration (if not included by free zone) | AED 2,020 |
| Typing / service centre fee | AED 150 to 300 |
| Government fees subtotal | AED 3,735 to 5,025 |

Note: e-channel registration (AED 2,020) is typically paid once and may already be included in your free zone package. Confirm before paying again.

For the [UAE Green Visa](/guides/uae-green-visa-freelancer/) specifically, the 5-year Emirates ID costs AED 570 rather than AED 370, and there is an additional approval fee structure; see below.

## How much should you budget for mandatory health insurance?

Budget AED 2,400 to 7,200 per year for a single applicant on a DHA-compliant plan before you count dependants or upgraded hospital networks.

| Coverage tier | Typical annual premium (single) | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DHA Essential Benefits Plan (minimum) | AED 2,400 to 3,600 | Visa compliance on a tight budget |
| Mid-tier enhanced | AED 4,500 to 6,500 | Broader hospital network, lower co-pays |
| Comprehensive / age 45+ | AED 6,500 to 10,000+ | Pre-existing loadings, maternity add-ons |

Age significantly affects premiums. Under-30 applicants typically land at the lower end of each tier; 45+ applicants should budget toward the upper range. Pre-existing conditions can trigger loadings of 20 to 50% on standard premiums.

Minimum DHA-compliant coverage is sufficient for visa purposes, but the Essential Benefits Plan has significant network and annual limit restrictions. Most financially comfortable freelancers purchase mid-tier or enhanced coverage.

## Is the UAE Green Visa worth the higher upfront cost for freelancers?
Income requirement: AED 360,000/year (AED 30,000/month) from freelance activity. This is a meaningful threshold, roughly equivalent to USD 98,000 annually at current rates.

Renewal economics: After the 5-year Green Visa is issued, you do not pay separate annual permit renewals for the visa itself. You renew the free zone permit annually (AED 7,500 to 15,000 depending on zone) but the visa renewal at year 5 is a single process rather than 5 separate annual visa renewals.

5-year comparison (Green Visa vs annual renewal):

| Scenario | Year 1 | Years 2 to 5 (combined) | 5-year total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meydan permit + annual 2-year visa cycle | AED 15,000 | AED 44,000 | AED 59,000 |
| Meydan permit + Green Visa (5-year) | AED 18,000 | AED 38,000 | AED 56,000 |
| GoFreelance + annual 2-year visa cycle | AED 22,000 | AED 60,000 | AED 82,000 |
| GoFreelance + Green Visa (5-year) | AED 23,000 | AED 52,000 | AED 75,000 |

*Figures include permit renewals and visa processing for each scenario but exclude health insurance (same across all). Indicative only, permit prices change annually.*

The Green Visa saves AED 3,000 to 7,000 over 5 years, but the real advantages are stability (no annual renewal stress), stronger bank account credibility (5-year residency document), and the ability to extend sponsor status to family members under a more secure framework.

If you qualify on income and are planning to stay in Dubai for 3+ years, the Green Visa is worth the higher year-one cost.

## What hidden costs catch first-time freelance visa applicants?

Beyond permit and visa lines, plan for document attestation (AED 0 to 2,000), PRO or typing centre support (AED 1,500 to 3,500), and bank relationship fees if your free zone address triggers enhanced KYC.

| Hidden line item | Typical range | When it hits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Certificate attestation (degree, passport copies) | AED 0 to 2,000 | Before free zone approval |
| PRO / visa typing services | AED 1,500 to 3,500 | Visa stamping stage |
| Medical retest or extra biometrics | AED 270 to 600 | If first medical flagged |
| Trade licence reprint / activity add-on | AED 1,500 to 2,500 | Mid-year activity change |

## When do you pay each freelance visa cost during year one?
Before applying:
- Decide on free zone and activity categories
- Gather and attest documents if required (~AED 0 to 2,000)
- Budget for potential PRO service (~AED 1,500 to 3,500)

At free zone registration (Month 0):
- Free zone permit fee: AED 7,500 to 15,000
- E-channel registration (if not included): AED 2,020

At visa application (Month 0 to 1):
- Entry permit / change of status: AED 460 to 735
- Medical fitness test: AED 270 to 420
- Emirates ID: AED 370 to 570

At health insurance purchase (Month 0 to 1):
- Annual premium: AED 2,400 to 7,200+

Annual renewal (Month 12 for permit; Month 24/60 for visa):
- Free zone permit renewal: AED 7,500 to 15,000
- Visa renewal (at 2-year or 5-year cycle): AED 2,500 to 4,500
- Health insurance renewal: AED 2,400 to 7,200+

Modelling these outflows over a 3-year horizon before committing to a free zone makes the comparison between providers much clearer than looking at year-one permit cost alone.

## Which Dubai freelance visa route fits your profile?

Pick Meydan if lowest permit cost matters most, GoFreelance if your clients expect a TECOM media or tech address, and the Green Visa if you already invoice above AED 360,000 per year and want five-year stability.

| Profile | Usually best fit | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First-time UAE resident, tight budget | Meydan or Dubai South | Lower permit quotes, straightforward packages |
| Media, marketing, or software freelancer | GoFreelance (TECOM) | Strong sector credibility for banks and clients |
| Consultant wanting DSO address | IFZA | Mature portal, bundled visa support options |
| Established freelancer, 3+ year horizon | Green Visa + any permit | Fewer visa cycles, easier family sponsorship |

## What compliance risks should freelancers plan for?

Letting insurance lapse, missing permit renewal, or invoicing mainland clients without understanding corporate tax thresholds are the three issues that most often trigger fines or visa delays.

- Permit renewal: free zone permits expire on their anniversary; operating without a valid licence invalidates visa sponsorship.
- Insurance gap: even one uninsured day can block Emirates ID renewal.
- Corporate tax: freelance revenue above AED 375,000 may require registration; VAT applies once taxable supplies cross the same threshold.
- Bank KYC: some banks request six months of invoices; match your free zone activity list to what you actually sell.

## What is the quick cost summary for 2026?
- **Permit only:** AED 7,500 to 15,000 depending on free zone and activity count
- Government visa fees: AED 3,735 to 5,025
- Health insurance (basic): AED 2,400 to 3,600
- Year-one all-in (typical range): AED 14,000 to 24,000
- Green Visa all-in (year one): AED 15,400 to 25,100

The best-value route depends on your income level, activity category, and intended stay duration. For entry-level cost, Meydan. For credibility in media/tech, GoFreelance TECOM. For long-term stability, UAE Green Visa.

## How do family and multi-year costs change the math?

Sponsoring dependants adds roughly AED 1,800 to 3,000 per person in visa processing plus separate health insurance for each family member. Model school deposits and Ejari cheques before you choose a free zone on permit price alone.

Year-two costs drop because you usually renew the permit and insurance without paying full visa issuance again, unless you change visa type. Keep your trade licence PDF current: banks and payment processors treat licence renewal as a business event even when visa status stays active.
