# Dubai Freelance Visa Cost 2026: Full Breakdown by Free Zone and Route

Quick answer: Expect to spend AED 14,000–28,000 in year one to obtain a Dubai freelance permit plus residence visa. The specific cost depends heavily on which free zone you use, how many activity categories you register, and your health insurance premium. Meydan is cheapest at AED 7,500–9,000 for the permit alone. GoFreelance (TECOM) runs AED 9,500–15,000. The UAE Green Visa freelancer track costs more upfront but gives 5-year residency and works out cheaper per year.

TL;DR: Dubai freelance visa year-one total: AED 14,000–28,000 including permit, residence visa, Emirates ID, medical, and health insurance. Meydan entry-level starts around AED 14,000–17,000 all-in. GoFreelance runs AED 18,000–26,000 all-in. Green Visa freelancer track: AED 12,000–22,000 all-in for year one, then AED 7,000–14,000/year renewal for 5 years, best value if you're committing to 3+ years in Dubai.

> Disclaimer: Free zone fees change without notice. Always request a current fee schedule directly from the free zone before signing an agreement. Figures here are June 2026 planning ranges, not quotes.

## Why Dubai Freelance Visa Costs Confuse People

The Dubai freelance visa has three layers of cost that are often quoted separately or incompletely, which is why you see dramatically different numbers online:

1. Free zone permit fee: the commercial permit to operate as a freelancer within that zone (issued annually or every 2–3 years)
2. UAE residence visa costs: government visa application, entry permit, Emirates ID, medical fitness test
3. Health insurance: mandatory but separate from both of the above

Most free zone marketing quotes only the permit fee. The total cost you actually pay is the sum of all three. Add PRO (Public Relations Officer) service fees if you're using an agent to process the visa on your behalf (AED 1,500–3,500 is typical), and your real all-in first-year cost can differ substantially from the headline number.

This guide breaks down each layer clearly so you can model your actual cost before committing.

## Layer 1: Free Zone Permit Fees by Provider

### GoFreelance (TECOM Group: Dubai Media City / Dubai Internet City)

GoFreelance is operated by TECOM Group and grants permits under Dubai Media City (DMC) and Dubai Internet City (DIC) addresses. These are the most prestigious free zone addresses for media, tech, marketing, and creative professionals, and that prestige matters when opening UAE bank accounts and approaching enterprise clients.

GoFreelance cost (2026):

| Component | Cost |
|---|---|
| Single activity permit (1 year) | AED 9,500–12,000 |
| Additional activity (per activity) | AED 1,500–2,500 |
| E-channel registration fee | AED 2,020 |
| Knowledge and Innovation fee | AED 10 |
| Permit total (single activity) | AED 11,530–14,030 |

GoFreelance permits are issued for 1 year and must be renewed annually. The e-channel fee applies once per applicant and covers the UAE residence visa system access.

Activity categories covered: Media and journalism, advertising and PR, film and production, digital marketing, software development, IT consulting, education and training, design, fashion and lifestyle. GoFreelance is activity-specific, confirm your category is listed before applying.

### Meydan Free Zone

Meydan Free Zone is one of the most cost-competitive Dubai free zones for freelancers. It accepts a broad range of activity categories and has simplified its package structure. The Meydan address (Al Meydan Road, Nad Al Sheba) is less prestigious than DMC/DIC but is fully accepted for UAE residence visa sponsorship and works for most bank account applications.

Meydan Free Zone freelance permit cost (2026):

| Package | Annual Fee |
|---|---|
| Starter (1 activity, no visa included) | AED 7,500–9,000 |
| Standard (1 activity + visa processing support) | AED 12,000–15,000 |
| Multi-activity (up to 3 activities) | AED 11,000–14,000 |

Meydan's "visa processing support" packages bundle some government visa fees into the package price, read the breakdown carefully to avoid double-counting when comparing with base permit quotes elsewhere.

### IFZA (International Free Zone Authority: Dubai Silicon Oasis)

IFZA is Dubai-based and one of the most popular overall free zones in the UAE for small businesses and solo operators. For freelancers specifically, IFZA offers competitive permit costs with Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) addresses.

**IFZA freelance permit cost (2026):**

| Component | Cost |
|---|---|
| Freelance permit (1 activity, 1 year) | AED 12,900–15,500 |
| Additional activity | AED 2,000–3,500 |
| Visa package add-on (optional) | AED 3,500–5,000 |

IFZA tends to price higher than Meydan on the permit itself but includes more hands-on support and has a well-developed client portal. Strong for tech, consulting, and professional services.

### Dubai South Free Zone

Dubai South (near Al Maktoum International Airport) has emerged as a cost-competitive option for logistics, aviation, and general professional services freelancers.

**Dubai South freelance permit (indicative, 2026):**
- Single activity permit: AED 8,500–11,000
- Multi-activity: AED 12,000–16,000

### Fujairah Creative City (outside Dubai, UAE)

For context: Fujairah Creative City is not a Dubai free zone but is frequently compared. Permit fees start around AED 7,000–9,500 for a single activity. UAE residence visa from Fujairah-based permits is fully valid, but the Fujairah address may create complications for some Dubai-based bank accounts or client contracts expecting a Dubai address.

## Layer 2: UAE Residence Visa Government Fees

Once you have a free zone permit, you apply for your UAE residence visa. These are government-set fees applicable regardless of which free zone you use.

Standard UAE residence visa government fees (June 2026):

| Fee component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Entry permit (for overseas applicants) | AED 465–735 |
| Residency stamp / change of status (in-country) | AED 460–680 |
| Emirates ID application | AED 370 (2-year) / AED 570 (5-year) |
| Medical fitness test | AED 270–420 |
| E-channel registration (if not included by free zone) | AED 2,020 |
| Typing / service centre fee | AED 150–300 |
| **Government fees subtotal** | **AED 3,735–5,025** |

Note: e-channel registration (AED 2,020) is typically paid once and may already be included in your free zone package. Confirm before paying again.

For the [UAE Green Visa](/guides/uae-green-visa-freelancer/) specifically, the 5-year Emirates ID costs AED 570 rather than AED 370, and there is an additional approval fee structure; see below.

## Layer 3: Health Insurance

Health insurance is mandatory for all UAE residents and is not included in either the free zone permit fees or the government residence visa fees. It is purchased separately from a DHA (Dubai) or HAAD (Abu Dhabi) approved insurer.

**Dubai mandatory health insurance cost (June 2026):**

| Coverage level | Annual premium (single person) |
|---|---|
| DHA Essential Benefits Plan (EBP), basic | AED 2,400–3,600 |
| Enhanced EBP / mid-tier networks | AED 3,600–5,500 |
| Comprehensive (international networks) | AED 6,000–14,000+ |
| With dental and vision add-ons | +AED 1,200–2,500 |

Age significantly affects premiums. Under-30 applicants typically land at the lower end of each tier; 45+ applicants should budget toward the upper range. Pre-existing conditions can trigger loadings of 20–50% on standard premiums.

Minimum DHA-compliant coverage is sufficient for visa purposes, but the Essential Benefits Plan has significant network and annual limit restrictions. Most financially comfortable freelancers purchase mid-tier or enhanced coverage.

## UAE Green Visa for Freelancers: Is It Worth the Higher Cost?

The [UAE Green Visa](/guides/uae-green-visa-guide/) is a 5-year self-sponsored residence visa available to freelancers who meet the income threshold. The key financial question: does the 5-year validity justify the higher year-one cost?

**Income requirement:** AED 360,000/year (AED 30,000/month) from freelance activity. This is a meaningful threshold, roughly equivalent to USD 98,000 annually at current rates.

**Renewal economics:** After the 5-year Green Visa is issued, you do not pay separate annual permit renewals for the visa itself. You renew the free zone permit annually (AED 7,500–15,000 depending on zone) but the visa renewal at year 5 is a single process rather than 5 separate annual visa renewals.

**5-year comparison (Green Visa vs annual renewal):**

| Scenario | Year 1 | Years 2–5 (combined) | 5-year total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meydan permit + annual 2-year visa cycle | AED 15,000 | AED 44,000 | AED 59,000 |
| Meydan permit + Green Visa (5-year) | AED 18,000 | AED 38,000 | AED 56,000 |
| GoFreelance + annual 2-year visa cycle | AED 22,000 | AED 60,000 | AED 82,000 |
| GoFreelance + Green Visa (5-year) | AED 23,000 | AED 52,000 | AED 75,000 |

*Figures include permit renewals and visa processing for each scenario but exclude health insurance (same across all). Indicative only, permit prices change annually.*

The Green Visa saves AED 3,000–7,000 over 5 years, but the real advantages are stability (no annual renewal stress), stronger bank account credibility (5-year residency document), and the ability to extend sponsor status to family members under a more secure framework.

If you qualify on income and are planning to stay in Dubai for 3+ years, the Green Visa is worth the higher year-one cost.

## Hidden Costs to Budget For

Several costs regularly surprise first-time applicants:

**PRO service fees:** If you use a PRO to handle medical appointments, document processing, and visa applications, standard practice for first-timers, budget AED 1,500–3,500 for a full-service package. Some free zones include this in their setup fee; many do not.

**Document attestation:** Foreign qualifications, degree certificates, or previous employment documents may require UAE embassy or Ministry of Foreign Affairs attestation before free zone registration accepts them. Cost: AED 500–2,000 per document depending on country of origin and attestation route.

**Virtual office or physical address upgrade:** Basic Meydan and some other permits include a virtual address. If you want a dedicated postal address or hot desk access, upgrade packages typically add AED 3,000–8,000 annually.

**Bank account setup:** Opening a UAE business bank account has no government fee, but banks increasingly require deposits of AED 10,000–50,000 as a minimum balance for freelance/sole establishment accounts. Mashreq, Emirates NBD, and RAKBANK have different minimum requirements, research before choosing your free zone, as some zones have preferred banking relationships that simplify the process.

**Activity amendments:** Adding or changing activity categories mid-license year costs AED 1,000–3,000 at most free zones and may trigger a new visa cycle. Plan your activity categories carefully at the start.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I switch free zones after getting my visa?**

Yes, but it involves a full visa cancellation and re-application with the new free zone. Budget AED 2,000–3,500 for visa cancellation fees and AED 1,500–3,000 for the new application processing. If you are approaching your annual permit renewal date, switching zones at renewal rather than mid-year minimises overlap costs.

**Can I work for UAE clients with a freelance permit?**

Yes. A UAE free zone freelance permit allows you to invoice UAE-based clients. Be aware that UAE corporate tax rules may apply to business income exceeding AED 375,000 annually, and VAT registration is required once taxable supplies exceed AED 375,000/year. For a solo freelancer below these thresholds, there is no corporate tax or VAT obligation.

**What if my income drops below the Green Visa threshold?**

The Green Visa income threshold (AED 360,000/year) is assessed at application and renewal. If your income decreases substantially, you will not be removed mid-visa. At the 5-year renewal, you will need to demonstrate you still qualify. If you do not meet the threshold at renewal, you can downgrade to a standard 2-year free zone residence visa without penalty.

Is a co-working space included in any free zone package?

Some free zone packages include hot desk access or coworking credits, GoFreelance includes access to TECOM coworking spaces as part of certain tiers. Most basic permit packages include only a registered address, not physical desk access. Confirm what physical access, if any, is included before choosing.

**Does the permit let me sponsor my family?**

A UAE residence visa under a free zone permit does entitle you to sponsor eligible family members (spouse, children under 18, daughters under 21 in full-time education, parents in specific circumstances). Each dependant requires their own health insurance and incurs separate processing fees of approximately AED 1,800–3,000 per person. The Green Visa carries stronger family sponsorship entitlements than a standard 2-year permit.

## Dubai Freelance Visa Cost — visa risks

- Salary thresholds and profession lists behind Dubai Freelance Visa Cost change without wide announcement. Reconfirm with MOI/GDRFA the week you file.
- Visit-to-work status changes require in-country processing. Working on a tourist visa while pursuing Dubai Freelance Visa Cost is a serious violation.
- Medical fitness failures delay the whole family pipeline for Dubai Freelance Visa Cost. Pre-check conditions with approved clinic lists.
- Attestation mismatches on marriage and birth certificates are the most common rejection cause for Dubai Freelance Visa Cost filings.
