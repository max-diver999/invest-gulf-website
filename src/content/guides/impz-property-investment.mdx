International Media Production Zone — IMPZ — is Dubai's quiet yield corridor between JLT and Dubai Sports City. Most investors who focus on Marina, Downtown, or branded master communities never visit Production City. That oversight creates opportunity: gross rental returns of 7.6–9.1% on studios and one-bedrooms, entry prices from AED 400,000, and a tenant base anchored to JLT and Media City employment within a 10–15 minute drive.

The zone was originally conceived as a media production hub. Residential towers followed, and today IMPZ functions as a mid-market apartment community with a mix of end-user families and buy-to-let investors. The media-industry branding is largely historical — the real investment case is location, yield, and price.

**Quick answer:** Gross yield of 7.6–9.1% on studios and 1BR, net yield of 5.5–7.0% after the full cost stack. Entry from AED 400K for a studio. Best suited to buy-to-let investors who want JLT-corridor rental income at mid-market prices.

## Why IMPZ yields well

IMPZ's yield story is location arithmetic plus mid-market pricing.

The tenant base is driven by proximity to JLT, Dubai Media City, and Internet City — three of Dubai's largest employment clusters for media, technology, and professional services. Workers on AED 10,000–20,000 monthly salaries need apartments below AED 70,000 per year. IMPZ delivers that price point with a commute under 15 minutes to JLT, versus 25–35 minutes from JVC or Discovery Gardens.

Entry prices sit 40–55% below Dubai Marina and 30–45% below JLT on a price-per-square-foot basis. A one-bedroom in IMPZ at AED 600,000 might rent for AED 50,000–55,000. The same unit type in JLT costs AED 900,000–1.1M and rents for AED 65,000–75,000. The yield gap favours IMPZ because the rent discount is smaller than the price discount.

Service charges average AED 14–18 per sq ft — mid-range for Dubai. Not as low as Discovery Gardens (AED 11–16) but below Marina (AED 20–28). The service charge stack is manageable if modelled correctly from day one.

## IMPZ tenant profile: who rents here

The IMPZ tenant base includes:

- Media and creative professionals working Dubai Media City, Internet City, and Production City studios
- JLT office workers who want a shorter commute than JVC or Sports City
- Healthcare and education staff from surrounding clusters
- Young couples where one partner works in Marina or JBR and the other in JLT
- Small-business owners and freelancers in the IMPZ commercial zones

Tenancy patterns skew toward 12-month Ejari contracts with moderate renewal rates. Turnover is higher than family communities like Town Square but lower than STR-heavy Marina towers. The JLT commute advantage is the primary retention driver — tenants stay as long as their employment location remains in the western corridor.

## Off-plan vs ready in IMPZ

IMPZ has a mix of established ready towers (2015–2020) and ongoing off-plan launches from mid-tier developers.

**Ready property** at AED 520K–780K for a 1BR gives immediate rental income and verifiable service charges. You inspect the building, check Ejari transacted rents on Dubai REST, and model yield from day one. This is the lower-risk path for yield investors.

**Off-plan property** in IMPZ and adjacent Production City zones launches at AED 450K–700K for 1BR with 40–60% payment plans over 2–3 years. Considerations:

- No rental income during construction (typically 18–30 months)
- SPA service charge estimates often understate actual costs by 20–30%
- Handover-year listing pressure when multiple investors list simultaneously
- Developer delivery track record matters — verify via DLD Trakheesi and on-time delivery history

For yield-first buyers, ready stock in a tower with two-plus years of operating history is preferable. Off-plan works if the price discount exceeds 12% versus comparable ready units and the developer has an 85%+ on-time delivery rate.

## Service charges and cooling systems

| Tower type | Service charge range | Cooling |
|---|---|---|
| Basic 2015–2018 tower | AED 12–15 per sq ft | Individual AC (tenant DEWA) |
| Mid-tier with pool/gym | AED 15–17 per sq ft | Empower district cooling |
| Premium 2020+ handover | AED 16–18 per sq ft | Empower or chiller-free |

Empower district cooling adds AED 800–1,500 per month to tenant utility bills. That affects rent competitiveness — tenants compare all-in monthly cost, not just Ejari rent. Model the total occupancy cost when setting rent expectations.

## Red flags to screen for in IMPZ

- **High investor concentration:** towers where 70%+ of units are investor-owned see handover listing surges and rent compression. Check ownership mix via the selling agent.
- **Service charge escalation:** some towers have seen 15–25% service charge increases as buildings age. Request three years of JOPD statements.
- **Empower cost surprises:** district cooling bills vary significantly by floor, exposure, and usage. Ask current tenants or the building management for typical monthly Empower costs.
- **Oversupply from adjacent launches:** new towers in Production City and Dubai South add competing stock. Monitor listing volume before buying.
- **Guaranteed return marketing:** any tower marketing 8–10% guaranteed returns for two years — the guarantee is priced into the purchase. Calculate effective yield net of premium.

## Which buyer profile fits IMPZ?

IMPZ suits investors who:

- Prioritise net yield over capital appreciation
- Want JLT-corridor tenant demand without JLT acquisition prices
- Accept moderate resale liquidity in exchange for stronger cash-on-cash returns
- Can screen individual towers for service charge health and investor concentration
- Hold a 5–10 year horizon focused on rental income

IMPZ is not the right fit for STR operators, lifestyle buyers seeking beach or retail walkability, or investors buying primarily for capital gain on a branded master community.
