## What checks must Qatar buyers complete before signing?

Qatar property due diligence requires four written checks before any deposit moves: MOJ title search, freehold zone confirmation, service charge clearance, and municipality fee clearance. Off-plan deals need a fifth escrow bank letter within 5 to 15 days. Foreign buyers working with Invest Gulf treat skipped checks as a 2 to 4 week delay risk.

Before any Qatar property purchase, complete these four checks in writing, not verbal confirmation:

| Check | What you verify | Who provides it | Consequence if skipped |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOJ title search | Owner identity, zone type, encumbrances | Qatar-licensed lawyer | You may buy a leasehold or disputed title |
| Freehold zone confirmation | Foreign buyer eligibility | MOJ register / lawyer | You cannot resell to another foreign buyer |
| SC clearance certificate | Zero SC arrears | Management company/developer | You inherit seller's debt, SC block on future NOC |
| Municipality tax clearance | No outstanding municipality charges | Municipality of Qatar | Title transfer may be blocked |

For off-plan properties, add a fifth check:
| Escrow account confirmation | Payments in project-specific escrow | Developer's escrow bank | Funds at risk if developer distress |

## How do you verify MOJ title and freehold zone status?

MOJ title verification means a Qatar-licensed lawyer runs an official register search confirming the seller, encumbrances, and foreign-freehold versus leasehold status for the area. Marketing copy is never enough in Lusail, where parcel rules differ. Foreign buyers should allow 5 to 10 days; Invest Gulf builds that lag into every Doha timeline.

### What the MOJ Search Reveals

| Information | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Registered owner name and nationality | Confirms seller has legal title |
| Title deed number and date | Confirms registration is current |
| Zone classification (freehold/leasehold/usufruct) | Determines foreign resale eligibility |
| Registered encumbrances | Mortgages, liens, court orders |
| Property dimensions and unit number | Confirms you are buying the correct unit |
| Any registered disputes | Prior buyer claims or inheritance issues |

A clean MOJ search shows: owner name matches seller identity document, zone is designated foreign freehold, no encumbrances, dimensions match SPA. Any discrepancy is a deal condition - not a minor issue to move past.

### Freehold Zone Verification: Critical for Exit

Qatar's freehold zones for foreign buyers are defined by Cabinet Resolution. The designated zones as of 2026:

| Zone | Status | Foreign ownership type |
|---|---|---|
| The Pearl-Qatar | Established | 100% freehold |
| Lusail City | Designated (verify per parcel) | 100% freehold in qualifying plots |
| West Bay Lagoon | Partial | Selected freehold towers |
| Al Daayen | Partial | Designated compound areas |
| Gewan Island | New (UDC development) | 100% freehold |
| Mushayrib (Heart of Doha) | Designated | 100% freehold in approved buildings |
| Al Khor and Jabal Tharwan | Leasehold/usufruct only | 99-year usufruct - not freehold |

Lusail is the highest-risk area for zone confusion. Lusail City has multiple parcels with different ownership structures - some foreign-freehold, some Qatari-only leasehold, some government land with no private ownership. The fact that a building is in Lusail City does not guarantee foreign freehold eligibility. Verify the specific parcel number against MOJ zone classification, not just the developer's marketing materials.

## How do you confirm service charges and municipality fees are cleared?

Service charge clearance in Qatar requires a zero-balance letter dated within 30 days of transfer and naming the exact unit in the area. A balance statement is not clearance for foreign buyers. We also confirm municipality fee status, because arrears can block the NOC and delay completion by several weeks.

1. SC clearance certificate: confirms QAR 0 outstanding on the specific unit, signed by the management company.
2. Current SC rate confirmation: the monthly/annual SC amount you will pay as new owner.
3. Management company identification: name, registration number, contact for post-purchase SC queries.

**Warning:** Some sellers provide a "balance statement" instead of a clearance certificate. A balance statement shows what is owed - it is not a clearance. Require a zero-balance clearance letter dated within 30 days of planned transfer.

Insider tip: Ask for the last 12 months of SC invoices, not only the clearance letter. In Invest Gulf buyer reviews, units that looked "cleared" still showed a mid-year special assessment that lifted the next year's bill by 15 to 25%.

For full SC rate data by area: [Qatar service charges guide](/guides/qatar-service-charges-guide/).

### Municipality Fees and Utilities

Qatar municipality fees (kahramaa registration, utility connection) are primarily a tenant's obligation during tenancy, but can leave outstanding balances if previous tenant departed owing fees.

Verify at purchase:
- Kahramaa (electricity and water) balance is zero - request utility settlement statement.
- No outstanding municipality tax (5% of annual rent) balance from previous tenancy.
- Parking allocation (if applicable) is included in the title or management agreement.

## Do you need a Qatar lawyer and power of attorney?

A Qatar property purchase requires a lawyer from a Qatar-licensed firm for title search, SPA review, Arabic checks, and MOJ attendance. Foreign buyers using a power of attorney should allow 3 to 6 weeks for authentication before transfer day. Our checklists keep POA wording broad enough for SC and Kahramaa acts.

Lawyer checklist for a standard residential purchase:
- Firm must be licensed by the Qatar Ministry of Justice.
- Partner must hold a Qatar Lawyers licence (Qatari national or a foreigner with valid legal licence).
- The firm can be an international firm with Qatar office (many international firms operate in Qatar Financial Centre, but QFC firms may not handle onshore MOJ registrations - verify).

Typical lawyer scope for a Qatar purchase:
- MOJ title search
- SPA review and negotiation
- Arabic translation verification
- Power of attorney drafting (for remote buyers)
- Attendance at MOJ transfer (or as attorney)

Cost: QAR 5,000 to 20,000 for standard residential purchase, typically under 1.0% of purchase price on mid-market units.

### Power of Attorney for Non-Resident Buyers

Non-resident foreign buyers can complete a Qatar property purchase using a Power of Attorney (POA) appointing a local representative. Steps:

1. Draft POA in English with Arabic translation.
2. Notarise POA in your home country.
3. Apostille or legalise at Qatar Embassy (if your country requires).
4. Submit to Qatar Ministry of Foreign Affairs for authentication.
5. Attorney appears at MOJ on your behalf.

Processing time: 3 to 6 weeks from signing to Qatar authentication. Build this into your deal timeline. Sellers may pressure for a faster timeline - resist if your POA is not yet authenticated.

## What extra checks apply to Qatar off-plan purchases?

Qatar off-plan due diligence requires written escrow confirmation before any stage payment, plus Oqood registration at MOJ within 30 days of signing. Foreign buyers should also demand a 30 to 90 day snagging window after handover. Our underwriting treats developer brochure claims as non-binding until the project bank letter arrives.

- Escrow bank letter before reservation or stage 1
- Oqood registration within 30 days of SPA signing
- Snagging window of 30 to 90 days before accepting handover

For full off-plan analysis including financial comparison with ready property, see [Qatar off-plan vs ready property](/guides/qatar-off-plan-vs-ready-property/).

Off-plan Qatar purchases fail most often when buyers treat a developer brochure as escrow proof. Escrow confirmation means a bank letter naming the project account, the SPA parties, and the payment schedule, typically issued within 5 to 15 days of request. Oqood registration locks the buyer interest on the MOJ register and should complete within 30 days of SPA signing. Pre-handover inspection usually sits 30 to 60 days before keys, with snagging rectification lasting another 30 to 90 days depending on defect volume. Title deed issuance happens at handover once final payment clears. Investors comparing off-plan to ready stock should model both the construction timeline and the vacancy gap after handover, because service charges and municipality setup start when ownership transfers, not when marketing promised rent. Cross-check every stage payment against the SPA before you wire funds.

## How does Qatar due diligence differ from Dubai?

Qatar due diligence differs from Dubai because there is no public portal like REST or DARI, so every title and fee check needs a licensed intermediary. Foreign buyers should budget 3 to 4 weeks longer than a comparable UAE purchase in this market. We consistently model that lag into every Doha brief for the area.

| Check | Qatar | Dubai |
|---|---|---|
| Title portal | Lawyer-led MOJ search | Buyer-accessible registries |
| SC transparency | Developer or OA invoices | Mollak building data |
| Transfer tax | 0.25% of value | 4% DLD typical |
| Typical cash timeline | 3 to 6 weeks | Often faster with portals |

## What mistakes do foreign Qatar buyers make most often?

The most common Qatar buyer mistake is trusting the listing agent for freehold confirmation instead of an MOJ title search. Agents are paid by the seller and have no duty to verify parcel eligibility for foreign buyers. Invest Gulf red-flag reviews also catch verbal SC quotes that land 20 to 40% above the invoice.

- Skipping MOJ freehold confirmation for Lusail parcels
- Accepting verbal SC quotes that land 20 to 40% high
- Paying off-plan stages before a written escrow bank letter

Accepting verbal SC quotes creates another frequent loss. Actual service charges often land 20 to 40% above the figure quoted in negotiation when agents recycle outdated invoices. Require the most recent quarterly or annual SC bill as a deal condition. See [Qatar service charges guide](/guides/qatar-service-charges-guide/) for area benchmarks.

Skipping municipality fee clearance is the third red flag. Qatar municipality charges (5% of annual rent) are usually the tenant's obligation during tenancy, but a departed tenant can leave arrears registered against the address and complicate Kahramaa or new lease registration.

No escrow verification for off-plan remains the highest cash-risk error. Paying a reservation fee before a written escrow bank letter arrives leaves funds exposed. Escrow rules protect buyers only when you verify actively. See [Qatar off-plan vs ready property](/guides/qatar-off-plan-vs-ready-property/) for the full off-plan checklist.

Power of attorney gaps close the list. A POA that omits lease registration, SC clearance, and Kahramaa transfer forces extra authority paperwork mid-deal. Draft the POA broadly enough to cover all property administration acts.

Foreign buyers who close clean Qatar deals usually treat due diligence as a 3 to 6 week project with written outputs, not a weekend agent tour. MOJ title search, freehold zone confirmation, SC clearance within 30 days, municipality fee clearance, and off-plan escrow letters each need a document in the file before funds move. Transfer tax at 0.25% keeps entry costs low versus Dubai's 4% DLD, but low tax does not reduce legal risk on zone classification. Lusail parcels in particular require parcel-number verification against the MOJ register, because neighbouring towers can sit on different ownership regimes. Build POA authentication of 3 to 6 weeks into any remote purchase so sellers cannot force a rushed transfer before your attorney is authorised. We keep that written file complete before any deposit wire.

## Which Qatar property due diligence checks matter most before you sign?
A post-signing Qatar ownership checklist should cover title storage, SC setup, Kahramaa connection, rent banking, management agreements, insurance, lease templates, and residency filing when the unit supports a permit. Foreign buyers typically finish these items within 2 to 4 weeks. We keep the list active through first tenancy.

- Title deed copy stored securely (digital + physical)
- SC payment method set up with management company
- Kahramaa connected in landlord name
- Qatar bank account opened for rent receipt (if desired)
- Property management agreement signed
- Landlord insurance in place
- Lease agreement template reviewed for Qatar law compliance
- Residency application filed (if using property for permit) - see [Qatar residency by property](/guides/qatar-property-buyer-relocation/)
