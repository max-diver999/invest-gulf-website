## What hidden school costs surprise Jeddah families?

Beyond tuition, budget registration levies, exam fees for IGCSE and IB years, uniform cycles in humid coastal heat, and Ramadan-hour after-school clubs that may pause. Two-child households on mid-tier British tracks often spend SAR 8,000 to 11,000 per month once buses and activities amortise.

## How much Arabic and Islamic study do Jeddah international schools require?

Ministry rules require Arabic instruction for most pupils, with different streams for native speakers and beginners. Islamic studies intensity rises in primary years; confirm hours on the registrar PDF, not marketing brochures. Dress and campus norms remain conservative compared with Dubai beach-side schools.

## What SEN and EAL support should you verify?

Ask for written SEN policy, in-class support ratios, and whether external therapists can visit campus. EAL programmes vary widely; British schools may stream English support separately from Arabic classes. Request contact details for the learning support lead before you pay a holding deposit.

## Which school stage is hardest to enter mid-year?

Red flags: no shaded play; high teacher turnover; vague feeder priority.

### Primary (6 to 11)

Priority: homework policy, Arabic load, class size reality, pastoral lead name.

- British KS2 sets IGCSE options, weak Year 6 narrows senior subjects
- Bullying policy, smaller city, reputations travel

Switching window: Years 3 to 5 least damaging.

### Lower secondary (12 to 14)

Priority: IGCSE/MYP pathway meeting attendance even if mid-year arrival.

- Sports timetables vs Ramadan hours  (confirm current official rules)

Avoid move into IGCSE cohort start unless unavoidable.

### IGCSE (15 to 16)

Priority: exam board, subject block, predicted grade culture.

- Budget SAR 2,500 to 8,000/year exam fees  (confirm current official rules)
- Sixth form guarantee not automatic, ask criteria

### Sixth form: A-Level (17 to 18)

Priority: subject specialists, counsellor ratio, UK/US track record.

- Fees peak SAR 80,000 to 110,000/year premium  (confirm current official rules)

### IB Diploma (17 to 18)

Priority: HL subjects, TOK/EE support, CAS in heat.

- Honest schools advise A-Level if DP poor fit, ask early

## What red flags should pause a Jeddah school commitment?

Walk away when a registrar refuses a written waiting-list position by year group, when bus routes are "confirmed after term starts," or when accreditation documents are older than one inspection cycle. Coastal heat makes outdoor sport quality a real pastoral issue; ask for term-time timetables, not open-day demos in winter.

## How do relocation scenarios change school timing?

Employed professionals on standard iqama should file applications as soon as the offer letter names Jeddah, because premium seats fill before furniture arrives. Red Sea Project or Yanbu rotations need a written commute policy from HR; daily Obhur-to-Yanbu runs rarely survive term-one traffic.
