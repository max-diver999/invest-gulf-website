# Abu Dhabi Driving Licence 2026: Conversion, Muroor, Parking & vs Dubai RTA

Quick answer: Most Western/golden countries can convert foreign licences through Muroor for AED 800–2,500 plus eye test. Others need full driving school (AED 3,000–7,000). No Salik tolls in Abu Dhabi saves AED 100–300/month vs Dubai, but car ownership is essential due to limited public transport.

TL;DR: Abu Dhabi is car-first, no metro. With Emirates ID, many nationalities convert a foreign licence at Muroor without a full test if their country is on the approved list; everyone else goes through approved driving schools. Budget AED 800–2,500 for conversion plus eye test; AED 3,000–7,000 for the full school route. No Salik in Abu Dhabi emirate, a real COL saving vs Dubai. Parking on Yas/Saadiyat/Reem varies tower by tower. Model transport in [Abu Dhabi cost of living](/guides/abu-dhabi-cost-of-living/).

## Muroor vs Dubai RTA: who does what

Dubai expats often walk into the wrong office. Abu Dhabi traffic licensing runs through Muroor, not RTA.

| Function | Abu Dhabi | Dubai |
|---|---|---|
| Licence issuance / renewal | Muroor (Abu Dhabi Police) | RTA |
| Theory / practical tests | Approved AD schools | RTA + Dubai schools |
| Vehicle registration | Abu Dhabi traffic system | RTA |
| Salik toll tag | Not applicable in AD emirate | Mandatory on Dubai routes |
| Parking apps | Mawaqif (zones) + building apps | RTA / various |
| Fines payment | Abu Dhabi Police apps | RTA / Dubai Police |

Live in Abu Dhabi, work in Dubai? Clarify insurance coverage and Salik on Dubai legs before you commute daily, Salik is Dubai infrastructure, not Abu Dhabi.

## Golden countries and direct conversion

Abu Dhabi maintains an approved countries list for licence exchange without practical test (subject to age, validity, category match). Lists change, always verify on official portal at booking time.

| Region (historically common) | Typical treatment |
|---|---|
| GCC states | Direct swap frequent |
| UK, Ireland | Often direct, confirm |
| EU select countries | Often direct, per country |
| USA / Canada / Australia | Variable by state/province |
| India, Pakistan, Philippines, Egypt | Often require driving school + test |

Requirements commonly requested:

- Valid foreign licence (not expired)
- Minimum age 21 for light vehicle (verify)
- Eye test at approved centre
- Emirates ID + passport
- Arabic translation if licence not in English/Arabic (sometimes)
- Photos and fees

Timeline: golden-path conversion can be same day to 3 visits depending on queue and document gaps.

## Step-by-step conversion workflow

1. Emirates ID in hand with Abu Dhabi visa.
2. Eye test: optical chains and approved centres; AED 100–180 typical.
3. Book Muroor appointment via Abu Dhabi Police app/website.
4. Submit foreign licence, ID, photos, eye test certificate.
5. Pay fees: AED 200–700 band depending on validity period.
6. Receive UAE licence: plastic card linked to traffic file number.
7. Insurance quote immediately: registration needs insurance.

Pro tip: carry digital copies on phone, Muroor counters sometimes request reprint.

## Salik: the Dubai toll Abu Dhabi expats forget

Salik gates do not operate inside Abu Dhabi emirate, hub correctly lists AED 0 Salik for AD residents on local commutes.

| Scenario | Salik? |
|---|---|

If relocating from Dubai: cancel or repurpose Salik tag only if no Dubai commutes remain, many AD families still need AED 4–6 per gate Dubai trips.

## Muroor troubleshooting: when conversion or registration stalls

Abu Dhabi Police licensing workflows are deterministic once documents align, most expat delays come from wrong centre booking, expired foreign licence, or visa emirate mismatch. Budget two spare mornings in month one even on golden-path countries.

### Document rejection reasons (and fixes)

| Rejection message | Root cause | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Country not eligible for exchange | Home licence from non-approved state | Full driving school registration, no shortcut |
| Licence expired over 6 months | Gap since last renewal | Renew home licence abroad via embassy route or enter school file |
| Eye test certificate old | Over 30-day validity window | Retake at approved optical, AED 100–180 |
| Emirates ID emirate mismatch | Dubai visa just cancelled, AD not active | Wait for visa status update in system 24–72h |
| Automatic category mismatch | Home licence manual, applying automatic only | Retest or accept automatic restriction stamp |
| Traffic file already open Dubai | Previous RTA file active | Transfer file request at Muroor, do not open duplicate |
| Fine block on file | Unpaid Dubai fines inherited | Clear via Dubai Police app first |
| Translation missing | Non-Latin licence without certified translation | Approved translation centre same day |

### Registration and insurance deadlock

Symptom: Insurer will not quote without UAE licence; Muroor will not register without insurance.

Standard break: Purchase temporary insurance or use dealer cover note for 13 days while conversion completes, dealer on Yas Island routinely handles this for new arrivals. Keep PDF on phone before driving off lot.

Symptom: Lease company will not release car without registration card.

Fix sequence: Insurance policy number → Muroor registration appointment → plate collection → then daily commute. Do not drive uninsured even one block, accident voids claim and adds criminal traffic exposure.

## FAQ

**How do I convert my UK licence in Abu Dhabi?** 
**direct exchange** at Muroor if on approved list, eye test + documents; verify live list before flight.

**Is Abu Dhabi licence different from Dubai?** 
Both are **UAE licences** but issued via **Muroor vs RTA**, transfer file when changing emirate of visa.

**Does Abu Dhabi have Salik?** 
**No** toll gates in Abu Dhabi emirate, Dubai Salik applies only on Dubai roads.

**How much does Abu Dhabi driving school cost?** 
**AED 3,000–7,000** all-in typical if test required.

**Can I drive on Indian licence after moving?** 
Only within short grace, then convert or test per Muroor rules.

**Where is Muroor?** 
Abu Dhabi Police licensing centres, book via official app (locations update).

**How long does conversion take?** 
Golden path: **1–5 days**; school route: **4–10 weeks**.

**What is the eye test cost?** 
Roughly **AED 100–180** at approved centres.

**Do I need a car in Abu Dhabi?** 
**Almost always yes** for families; Reem singles sometimes delay.

**How much is parking in Abu Dhabi?** 
included in tower; paid zones **AED 2–5/hour** bands, verify Mawaqif maps.

**Can my wife drive on my licence?** 
**No**, she needs her own UAE licence after residency.

**Automatic licence to manual car?** 
**Not allowed** if licence shows automatic restriction.

**How pay traffic fines?** 
Abu Dhabi Police app, pay early for discounts where offered.

**Rent a car without UAE licence?** 
Short visit only, residents need UAE licence for insurance validity.
