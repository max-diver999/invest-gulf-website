## Step-by-step Muroor conversion workflow


1. Emirates ID in hand with Abu Dhabi visa.
2. Eye test: optical chains and approved centres; AED 100-180 typical.
3. Book Muroor appointment via Abu Dhabi Police app/website.
4. Submit foreign licence, ID, photos, eye test certificate.
5. Pay fees: AED 200-700 band depending on validity period.
6. Receive UAE licence: plastic card linked to traffic file number.
7. Insurance quote immediately: registration needs insurance.

Pro tip: carry digital copies on phone, Muroor counters sometimes request reprint.

## Muroor troubleshooting and deadlocks


### Registration and insurance deadlock

Symptom: Insurer will not quote without UAE licence; Muroor will not register without insurance.

Standard break: Purchase temporary insurance or use dealer cover note for 13 days while conversion completes, dealer on Yas Island routinely handles this for new arrivals. Keep PDF on phone before driving off lot.

Symptom: Lease company will not release car without registration card.

Fix sequence: Insurance policy number → Muroor registration appointment → plate collection → then daily commute. Do not drive uninsured even one block, accident voids claim and adds criminal traffic exposure.

### Traffic fine and black-point triage

| Points band | Typical trigger | Recovery path |
|---|---|---|
| 1-6 | Speed 20-30 km/h over | Pay fine, points stay 12 months |
| 8-12 | Red light camera | Mandatory awareness course may appear in app |
| 14+ | Serious speeding / reckless | Suspension risk, legal advice |
| Unknown SMS in Arabic | New radar on E11 | Translate via Abu Dhabi Police app English toggle |

Checklist after any fine SMS:

- Open Abu Dhabi Police app → traffic file number
- Confirm vehicle plate matches your registration
- Pay within 30 days for early-payment discount where offered
- Screenshot receipt, lease companies audit at return
- If fine seems wrong, dispute via app with dashcam timestamp

### Spouse and second-driver parallel track

Trailing spouses often assume UK licence + IDP covers school runs, it does not after residency grace ends.

Case, South African licence, partial conversion: Husband converted under golden list in 3 days; wife's provincial licence required 12 hours lessons, total AED 4,200. They delayed second car purchase 6 weeks, spent AED 2,100 on Careem, still cheaper than fines + impound risk.

### Licensing costs - 2025-2026 benchmarks

Readers researching abu dhabi driving license in Abu Dhabi often overlook these specifics. Total process: 6,000-9,500 AED over 3-6 months. Theory course: 800-1,200 AED, 8 sessions of 45 minutes. Practical lessons: 200-350 AED each; most learners book 20-40 sessions. Traffic test fee: 200-400 AED per attempt, pass rate around 35-50% on the first try. Eye test: 50-150 AED, valid for 12 months. Licence conversion from recognised countries: 600-1,000 AED, processed in 1-3 working days. Annual car insurance surcharge for new licence holders: 15-25% above standard. Parking fines: 150-1,000 AED depending on zone and duration.
