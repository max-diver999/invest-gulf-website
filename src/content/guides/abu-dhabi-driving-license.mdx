  - question: "How much does it cost to get Abu Dhabi driving licence?"
    answer: "Foreign licence conversion: AED 800–2,500 plus eye test and admin fees. Full driving school course for non-golden countries: AED 3,000–7,000 including theory, practical lessons, and testing."
  - question: "Is there Salik toll in Abu Dhabi?"
    answer: "No Salik tolls in Abu Dhabi emirate, significant cost saving vs Dubai where Salik adds AED 100–300/month for regular commuters. Abu Dhabi focuses on parking fees instead."
  - question: "Do I need a car in Abu Dhabi?"
    answer: "Yes for most lifestyles, no metro system like Dubai. Families typically need two cars for school runs and spouse mobility. Budget AED 2,500–4,500/month total including financing, insurance, parking, and petrol."
  - question: "Where do I apply for Abu Dhabi driving licence?"
    answer: "Muroor (Abu Dhabi Police Traffic Department) through their digital services platform or service centers. Book online first, bring Emirates ID, passport, foreign licence with translation, eye test certificate, and required photos."
  - question: "Can I drive between Abu Dhabi and Dubai with either licence?"
    answer: "Yes, UAE driving licences are federal, Abu Dhabi licence valid in Dubai and vice versa. Insurance, Salik account, and parking registrations may differ between emirates."
