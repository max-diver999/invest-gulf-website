**Quick answer:** UAE property purchases of **AED 750K+** qualify for a **2-year renewable investor visa** with family sponsorship. This is distinct from the **10-year Golden Visa** requiring **AED 2M+**. The 750K visa suits mid-market investors seeking **shorter-term UAE residency** without the higher investment threshold. **Mortgaged properties qualify**; renewal requires **continued ownership**.

The UAE's 2-year property investor visa at AED 750,000 fills a critical gap between tourist visas and the Golden Visa program. Launched as part of the UAE's investor-friendly policies, it provides legitimate residency for investors in Dubai's thriving mid-market property sectors — JVC, Business Bay, Dubai Sports City, and similar communities where AED 750K–1.5M purchases are common.

This guide examines eligibility requirements, the application process, renewal procedures, family sponsorship options, mortgage compatibility, and the strategic decision between 2-year and 10-year visa pathways based on your property investment goals.

For Golden Visa details, see [Golden Visa AED 2 Million Explained](/guides/golden-visa-2-million-aed-explained/).

## AED 750K Investor Visa: Eligibility and Requirements

### Property Investment Criteria

| Requirement | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Minimum value** | AED 750,000 registered purchase price |
| **Property type** | Freehold only (leasehold excluded) |
| **Designated zones** | Any DLD-approved freehold area |
| **Ownership structure** | Individual ownership (corporate ownership ineligible) |
| **Mortgage status** | Mortgaged or cash — both qualify |
| **Multiple properties** | Combined value counts if totaling AED 750K+ |

### Visa Specifications

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Validity period** | 2 years from issue date |
| **Renewal** | Every 2 years while maintaining property ownership |
| **Family inclusion** | Spouse and children (under 18, extendable to 25 for students) |
| **Work authorization** | Yes — can work in UAE or establish business |
| **Multi-entry** | Unlimited entry/exit during validity |
| **Processing time** | 5–15 working days |

### Application Requirements

**Primary applicant documentation:**
- Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
- Property title deed or Oqood certificate showing AED 750K+ value
- Medical fitness certificate from UAE-approved center
- Police clearance certificate from country of residence
- Passport photographs (recent, Emirates ID compliant)
- Property insurance certificate (recommended)

**Financial verification:**
Unlike Golden Visa, the 750K investor visa does not require additional bank statements or income proof beyond the property purchase. The DLD-registered property value serves as primary financial evidence.

## Application Process: Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Property Purchase and Registration
Complete your property purchase through standard DLD registration. The title deed showing purchase price of AED 750,000 or above serves as your primary visa eligibility document.

### Step 2: Gather Supporting Documentation
- Obtain UAE medical fitness certificate (valid 3 months)
- Secure police clearance from your country of residence (valid 6 months)
- Prepare passport copies and Emirates ID photographs
- Arrange health insurance (if not covered by employer)

### Step 3: Submit Application
Applications are submitted through:
- **General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA)** offices
- **Amer centers** in Dubai and other emirates
- **Online portal** (available for renewals, limited for new applications)

### Step 4: Biometric Registration and Emirates ID
After approval, complete:
- Biometric data registration at typing centers
- Emirates ID application and collection
- Medical fitness certificate validation
- Entry permit stamping in passport

### Step 5: Family Member Processing (if applicable)
Family members complete parallel applications with:
- Marriage certificates (for spouse) — attested and translated
- Birth certificates (for children) — attested and translated
- Educational certificates (for students over 18)
- Same medical and document requirements

## Renewal Process and Long-Term Considerations

### 2-Year Renewal Requirements

Renewal is contingent on maintaining qualifying property ownership:

| Renewal requirement | Verification method |
|---|---|
| **Property ownership** | Current DLD title deed or Oqood |
| **Property value maintenance** | Original purchase price (not current market value) |
| **Medical fitness** | New certificate from UAE provider |
| **Clean criminal record** | Updated police clearance certificate |
| **Passport validity** | Minimum 6 months remaining |
| **Emirates ID renewal** | Required parallel to visa renewal |

**Renewal timeline:** Begin renewal process 60–90 days before visa expiry. Processing typically takes 5–10 working days, but plan for contingencies during peak periods.

### Cost Structure

| Cost component | Initial application | Renewal |
|---|---|---|
| **Visa fees (primary)** | AED 2,500–3,500 | AED 2,500–3,500 |
| **Emirates ID** | AED 370 | AED 370 |
| **Medical certificate** | AED 320 | AED 320 |
| **Typing/processing** | AED 300–500 | AED 300–500 |
| **Family members (each)** | AED 2,200–3,000 | AED 2,200–3,000 |
| **Total (individual)** | **AED 3,500–4,500** | **AED 3,500–4,500** |
| **Total (family of 4)** | **AED 10,000–13,000** | **AED 10,000–13,000** |

### Long-Term Renewal Strategy

**Indefinite renewal possibility:** The 2-year investor visa can be renewed indefinitely provided:
- Property ownership continues
- Property value remains above AED 750K (at original purchase price)
- Standard renewal requirements are met
- No significant changes to UAE visa regulations

**Upgrade pathway:** Investors can upgrade from 2-year to 10-year Golden Visa by:
- Purchasing additional property to reach AED 2M total investment
- Meeting Golden Visa application requirements
- Applying during renewal period or mid-term

## Family Sponsorship and Dependent Coverage

### Eligible Family Members

| Relationship | Age limits | Additional requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **Spouse** | No limit | Marriage certificate (attested) |
| **Children** | Under 18 | Birth certificate (attested) |
| **Students** | 18–25 | University enrollment proof |
| **Parents** | Not eligible | (Golden Visa only) |
| **Domestic workers** | Not directly eligible | Separate visa category |

### Family Member Benefits

**Same visa validity:** Family members receive 2-year visas aligned with primary holder
**Work authorization:** Spouse can seek employment or establish business
**Education access:** Children can enroll in UAE schools and universities
**Healthcare:** Access to UAE healthcare system (insurance required)
**Banking:** Can open UAE bank accounts and access financial services

### Family Visa Costs and Processing

Processing family visas adds time and cost but follows parallel procedures:

**Additional requirements for family:**
- Relationship documentation (marriage/birth certificates)
- Attestation through UAE embassy in country of issue
- Certified translation to Arabic (if documents in other languages)
- Individual medical certificates for each family member
- Separate Emirates ID applications

**Processing timeline:** Family visas process parallel to primary application — typically 7–20 working days total.

## Mortgage Compatibility and Property Financing

### Mortgaged Property Eligibility

**Good news for leveraged investors:** Properties purchased with UAE bank mortgages fully qualify for the 750K investor visa, provided:

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Registered value** | DLD title deed shows AED 750K+ purchase price |
| **Ownership structure** | Property registered in investor's personal name |
| **Mortgage status** | Mortgage registration does not affect visa eligibility |
| **Bank approval** | Some banks verify visa intent during mortgage application |

### Financing Scenarios

**Scenario A: AED 1M property, 25% down payment**
- Purchase price: AED 1,000,000 (qualifies for visa)
- Down payment: AED 250,000
- Mortgage: AED 750,000
- **Result:** Investor visa eligible

**Scenario B: AED 900K property, 20% down payment**  
- Purchase price: AED 900,000 (qualifies for visa)
- Down payment: AED 180,000
- Mortgage: AED 720,000
- **Result:** Investor visa eligible

**Scenario C: Combined properties totaling AED 800K**
- Property 1: AED 450,000 (mortgaged)
- Property 2: AED 350,000 (cash)
- Combined value: AED 800,000
- **Result:** Investor visa eligible

### Impact on Mortgage Applications

**Visa consideration in underwriting:** UAE banks increasingly factor residency status into mortgage applications. Stating visa intent through property purchase can:
- Strengthen mortgage application (demonstrates UAE commitment)
- Simplify income verification requirements
- Access resident mortgage rates (vs non-resident premium)
- Enable longer mortgage terms in some cases

**Documentation for banks:** Include visa application intent in mortgage discussions to access potential benefits.

## Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

### Property Selection Mistakes

**1. Ignoring total acquisition costs**
Many investors budget exactly AED 750K for purchase price but forget:
- DLD fees (4% = AED 30K on 750K property)
- Agent commission (2% + VAT = ~AED 16K)
- Legal and administrative costs (AED 5–10K)
- **Total actual budget needed:** AED 800–810K for 750K property

**2. Choosing illiquid properties**
Some areas offer attractive yields but poor resale potential:
- Verify transaction volume before purchase
- Choose communities with established rental markets
- Avoid single-building developments without broader market presence

### Visa Application Errors

**1. Incomplete family documentation**
Family visa rejections due to:
- Insufficient document attestation
- Missing translations
- Expired supporting documents
- **Solution:** Complete family documentation checklist before primary application

**2. Timing of visa application**
- Apply for visa only after DLD title deed is issued
- Don't begin application with Oqood (for off-plan) — wait for completion
- Plan renewal timeline to avoid visa expiry during processing

### Renewal Planning Failures

**1. Property sale without visa consideration**
Selling qualifying property automatically terminates visa:
- **Solution:** Plan replacement property purchase before sale completion
- Consider upgrade to Golden Visa through additional AED 1.25M investment
- Build 6-month buffer for property transition

**2. Ignoring renewal deadlines**
Late renewal applications risk:
- Visa expiry and potential fines
- Exit requirement and reentry complications
- Family member status complications
- **Solution:** Begin renewal process 60–90 days before expiry

## Tax and Financial Planning Implications

### UAE Tax Residency Through Property Visa

**Tax residency requirements:**
- Physical presence in UAE for 183+ days per calendar year
- Property ownership supports but does not guarantee tax residency
- Maintain documentation of UAE presence for tax authority verification

**Benefits of UAE tax residency:**
- 0% personal income tax on rental income
- No capital gains tax on property disposal
- Potential benefits in double taxation treaties
- Enhanced banking relationships in UAE

### Home Country Tax Considerations

**UK tax residents:**
- Property visa does not automatically change UK tax residency
- UAE rental income reportable to HMRC if UK tax resident
- Capital gains on property disposal subject to UK CGT
- Consider specialist UK/UAE tax advice for planning

**US tax residents:**
- Global taxation applies regardless of UAE visa status
- FBAR reporting for UAE bank accounts
- US tax on UAE rental income (with foreign tax credit limitations)
- Consider impact on US tax obligations before property purchase

**Other jurisdictions:**
Verify home country implications with qualified advisors. UAE visa status alone does not determine tax residency in most jurisdictions.

## Upgrade Pathway: 750K to Golden Visa

### Strategic Upgrade Timing

**Optimal upgrade scenarios:**
- Portfolio growth enables AED 2M total investment
- Need for longer-term stability (fewer renewals)
- Family circumstances change (elderly parent sponsorship needed)
- Business expansion requiring longer visa validity

### Upgrade Process Options

**Option A: Additional property purchase**
- Purchase second property to reach AED 2M combined value
- Apply for Golden Visa using total portfolio value
- Transition from 2-year to 10-year renewal cycle

**Option B: Property sale and upgrade**
- Sell existing property (terminates 750K visa)
- Purchase AED 2M+ property
- Apply for Golden Visa on new property
- **Risk:** Potential gap in visa status during transition

**Option C: Portfolio expansion approach**
- Maintain 750K property and visa
- Purchase additional AED 1.25M+ property
- Apply for Golden Visa while maintaining existing visa status
- **Benefit:** No visa gap, portfolio diversification

## Summary: Is the 750K Investor Visa Right for You?

### Choose the AED 750K investor visa if:

- **Budget considerations:** AED 750K–1.5M is your comfortable investment range
- **Yield focus:** You prioritize rental return over capital appreciation
- **Flexibility needs:** You prefer lower initial commitment with upgrade optionality
- **Market entry:** You want UAE exposure without premium market investment
- **Rental income:** You need cash flow from property investment
- **Timeline flexibility:** 2-year renewal cycles fit your planning horizon

### Consider Golden Visa instead if:

- **Long-term commitment:** You plan 5+ years of UAE residency
- **Capital growth focus:** You prioritize appreciation over yield
- **Premium lifestyle:** You prefer Marina, Palm, Downtown locations
- **Family stability:** You need to sponsor elderly parents
- **Renewal simplicity:** You want to minimize administrative overhead
- **Investment capacity:** AED 2M+ investment is comfortable for your portfolio

### Hybrid approach considerations:

- Start with 750K visa to test UAE property market
- Build rental income and market knowledge
- Upgrade to Golden Visa when portfolio and commitment justify higher investment
- Use first property as proof of concept for larger investment
