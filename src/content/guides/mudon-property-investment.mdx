## Summary decision framework

Before signing any lease or purchase contract in Dubai, consolidate **rent, utilities, schools, transport, and one-off relocation** into one spreadsheet tab. Compare against **net household income**, not gross headline salary. Dubai rewards families who model honestly and punishes those who trust brochure economics.

If total fixed costs exceed **70% of net income**, you are one school fee invoice away from financial stress — downgrade area, curriculum, or negotiate package before move, not after.

## Mudon resale seasonality and tenant retention tactics

Peak Ejari renewal **June–August** aligns with school year end — refresh unit (paint touch-up, garden, AC service) before void window. Family tenants rarely move mid-academic year unless repatriating.

**Landlord playbook:** offer **2-year lease slight discount** vs 1-year — reduces void to one month every 24 months. Include **annual AC servicing** in lease as landlord cost — AED 800/year buys renewal goodwill worth AED 5,000+ in void avoidance.

**Capital gains 2021–2025:** Mudon townhouses **28–38%** cumulative — forward **3–5%** annual realistic; do not underwrite Dubai 2021 boom repeat.
