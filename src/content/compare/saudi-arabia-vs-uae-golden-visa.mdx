Choosing between Saudi Arabia and UAE residency via property is not a comparison of which passport-adjacent lifestyle sounds more exciting on a brochure. It is a comparison of AED 2 million DLD-registered freehold versus SAR 4 million Premium Residency investment track, of 205,000 annual Dubai transactions versus Law M/14 designated zones, and of how much execution risk you accept for Vision 2030 narrative exposure. The practical filter is time rather than money. UAE freehold and the AED 2 million route carry roughly 20 years of precedent, a deep secondary market and a process most lawyers have run dozens of times. The Saudi designated-zone framework has only been in force since January 2026, so pricing, resale depth and transfer mechanics are still settling. Buyers who may need liquidity inside 5 years should weight that gap heavily, while buyers holding 10 years or more can treat it as the entry cost of early exposure.

The UAE Golden Visa is the Gulf's most documented property-linked long-term residency, AED 2 million registered value in qualifying freehold zones, processed through GDRFA/ICP with established PRO infrastructure. Saudi Premium Residency operates on a separate investment framework, commonly cited at approximately SAR 4 million on an investment track, distinct from foreign property ownership under Law M/14 in designated zones. Read those scenarios against time rather than money. UAE freehold and the AED 2 million route carry roughly 20 years of precedent, a deep secondary market and a process most lawyers have run dozens of times. The Saudi framework has been in force only since January 2026, with the designated-area map approved that June, so pricing, resale depth and transfer mechanics are still settling. Buyers who may need liquidity inside 5 years should weight that gap heavily; buyers holding 10 years or more can treat it as the entry cost of early exposure.

Property purchase and residency approval are not the same transaction in either country. Buying a Dubai apartment does not automatically issue a Golden Visa. Buying in a Saudi designated zone does not automatically grant Premium Residency. Both require separate applications, documentation, and ongoing compliance.

## Due diligence differs between the UAE and Saudi re
| Item | Verification method | Red flag indicators |
|---|---|---|
| Freehold zone eligibility | DLD zone map + title deed verification | Property marketed as freehold in non-qualifying areas |
| AED 2M registered value | DLD valuation + independent appraisal | Off-plan marketed price ≠ likely registered value |
| Developer ESCROW compliance | RERA escrow verification | Developer requests payment outside RERA escrow |
| Mortgage vs cash equity | Bank pre-approval with equity calculation | Assuming mortgaged portion counts toward threshold |
| ICP eligibility requirements | Current ICP circular review | Agent citing outdated eligibility rules |

Documentation requirements:
- Source of funds, bank statements, employment letters, business ownership proof
- Health insurance, UAE-compliant coverage minimum AED 500,000
- Criminal background clearance, from residence countries past 15 years
- Educational credentials, attested copies for family members
- Marriage and birth certificates, attested and translated
