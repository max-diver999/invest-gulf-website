Choosing between Aldar and Emaar is really a choice between Abu Dhabi and Dubai, two emirates with different transaction depth, regulatory registers, tenant pools, and price discovery mechanics. Aldar is not Emaar's Abu Dhabi branch. Emaar is not Aldar's Dubai JV. They are emirate benchmarks with distinct inventory, registration authorities, and buyer ecosystems.

Emaar (~95% on-time delivery) is DFM-listed, built Downtown Dubai and Dubai Hills, and anchors the deepest Gulf secondary market. Aldar (~92% on-time delivery) is ADX-listed with Mubadala-linked institutional structure, dominating Saadiyat, Yas, Al Reem, and Al Raha in Abu Dhabi.

Both developers sell heavily to international buyers, but the market depth is different. Abu Dhabi is more concentrated around Aldar and government-linked masterplans; Dubai has broader transaction volume, more resale comparables, and more broker competition.
