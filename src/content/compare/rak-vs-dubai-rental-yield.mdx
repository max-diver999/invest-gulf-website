## Who is this RAK versus Dubai yield comparison for?

This RAK versus Dubai yield comparison is for yield-focused UAE freehold buyers who accept thinner resale liquidity for lower entry tickets and often lower service charges. Invest Gulf frames it for 5+ year holds and Golden Visa sizing near AED 2M, not for Marina walkability lifestyles.

**Scenario A (short hold):** favour Dubai or rent until you know your emirate. **Scenario B (5+ year hold):** RAK satellite positions can make sense beside a Dubai core. **Scenario C (Golden Visa sizing):** RAK can reduce dirhams per square foot toward the **AED 2M** registered property threshold (confirm current official GDRFA rules).

Buyer scenario checklist:

- Short hold under 3 years: Dubai depth first
- Satellite yield with 5+ year hold: RAK beside Dubai core
- Visa sizing to AED 2M: compare dirhams per sqft, then net yield

## Where does RAK win on headline gross yield?

Headline RAK gross yield typically means 7% to 9% on established Al Hamra and mid-market apartments, while Dubai Marina premium stock more often sits 5% to 7% and JVC still competes at 7.5% to 9%. Our analysis treats RAK entry near AED 280k to 450k for many 1-beds as the ticket advantage for foreign buyers.

| Market | Typical 1-bed entry (illustrative) | Gross band | Liquidity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RAK Al Hamra | AED 280k to 450k | 7 to 9% | 90 to 180 day exits common |
| RAK Al Marjan (new) | AED 450k to 900k | 6 to 8% early | Tourism-linked void risk |
| Dubai JVC | AED 600k to 850k | 7.5 to 9% | Faster resale depth |
| Dubai Marina | AED 1.1M to 1.8M | 5 to 7% | Strongest secondary market |

## How do Dubai DLD fees compare with RAK transfer costs?

Both emirates typically require about 4% transfer or registration on many freehold resales, plus trustee and agency lines, so all-in disposal friction often lands near 4% to 6%. Invest Gulf notes RAK mainly changes entry price and service charge load, not the existence of transfer tax.

| Fee line | Dubai (typical) | RAK (typical) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transfer / registration | ~4% of price | ~4% of price (confirm SPA) |
| Trustee / admin | AED 3k to 6k band | Similar order of magnitude |
| Agency resale | 2% common | 2% common |
| Municipality on rent | 5% of contract value | Often none on RAK contracts |

Always request **service charge schedules** and **Form F / Oqood** status in writing before deposit. Confirm current official rules with your conveyancer.

## What does a side-by-side net worked example look like?

A Dubai JVC 1-bed at AED 750,000 with AED 60,000 rent and AED 21,000 costs means about AED 39,000 net (~5.2%), while RAK Al Hamra at AED 420,000 with AED 34,000 rent and AED 10,000 costs means about AED 24,000 net (~5.7%). We still prefer Dubai for absolute cash and exit optionality.

| Pair | Purchase | Rent | All-in costs | Net |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dubai JVC 1-bed | AED 750,000 | AED 60,000 | AED 21,000 | ~5.2% |
| RAK Al Hamra 1-bed | AED 420,000 | AED 34,000 | AED 10,000 | ~5.7% |

When gross is tied, lower RAK service charges (often 8 to 14 AED/sqft versus 14 to 35 AED/sqft in Dubai) are the hidden edge. Cross-check methodology on [gross vs net yield Dubai](/guides/gross-vs-net-yield-dubai/).

RAK mid-market and Al Hamra apartments often print 7% to 9% gross on mid-2020s asking rents, while Dubai Marina premium stock more often sits 5% to 7% and JVC still competes at 7.5% to 9% on similar apartment sizes. On a worked pair, a Dubai JVC 1-bed at AED 750,000 with AED 60,000 rent and all-in costs near AED 21,000 leaves about AED 39,000 net (~5.2%), whereas an Al Hamra 1-bed at AED 420,000 with AED 34,000 rent and AED 10,000 costs leaves about AED 24,000 net (~5.7%). Our research treats RAK service charges of about AED 8 to 16/sqft versus Dubai bands of AED 14 to 35/sqft as the net edge when gross looks similar, but still prices 90 to 180 day marketing windows into IRR. Foreign buyers should not crown RAK the default UAE buy on percentage yield alone when Dubai resale depth remains deeper.

## How do financing and leverage change net yield?

Mortgage leverage typically means higher cash-on-cash return while compressing margin if void spikes, so stress rate resets and service charge escalators after handover. Our analysis keeps 6 to 12 months of AED carry even when gross yield looks attractive on leveraged spreadsheets.

| Leverage check | Planning band |
| --- | --- |
| Carry reserve | 6 to 12 months AED |
| Rate stress | Quote +2% where banks require |
| Void spike | 4 to 6 weeks on tourism stock |
| Service charges | RAK AED 8-16/sqft vs Dubai 14-35 |

Bank menus differ by emirate and employer list. Do not assume Dubai LTV caps transfer unchanged to every RAK tower.
