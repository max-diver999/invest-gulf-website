## Who is this RAK versus Dubai yield comparison for?

Answer first: **yield-focused UAE freehold buyers** who accept **thinner resale liquidity** in exchange for **lower entry tickets** and often **lower service charges**. It is not a lifestyle comparison for families who need Marina walkability or instant Ejari depth.

**Scenario A (short hold):** favour Dubai or rent until you know your emirate. **Scenario B (5+ year hold):** RAK satellite positions can make sense beside a Dubai core. **Scenario C (Golden Visa sizing):** RAK can reduce dirhams per square foot toward the **AED 2M** registered property threshold (confirm current official GDRFA rules).

## Where does RAK win on headline gross yield?

Established **Al Hamra Village** and RAK mid-market apartments often print **7% to 9% gross** on mid-2020s asking rents. **Dubai Marina** premium stock more often sits **5% to 7% gross**, while **JVC** still competes at **7.5% to 9% gross** on similar apartment sizes.

| Market | Typical 1-bed entry (illustrative) | Gross band | Liquidity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RAK Al Hamra | AED 280k to 450k | 7 to 9% | 90 to 180 day exits common |
| RAK Al Marjan (new) | AED 450k to 900k | 6 to 8% early | Tourism-linked void risk |
| Dubai JVC | AED 600k to 850k | 7.5 to 9% | Faster resale depth |
| Dubai Marina | AED 1.1M to 1.8M | 5 to 7% | Strongest secondary market |

See [Al Hamra property](/areas/al-hamra-village-property-investment/) and [Wynn Al Marjan property impact](/guides/wynn-al-marjan-island-property-impact/) for catalyst context.

## How do Dubai DLD fees compare with RAK transfer costs?

Both emirates use the **Dubai Land Department style 4% transfer fee** on many freehold resales (plus trustee and agency lines). Budget **4% to 6% all-in disposal friction** on UAE apartments unless your SPA shows a different fee schedule. RAK does not remove transfer tax; it mainly changes **entry price** and **service charge load**.

| Fee line | Dubai (typical) | RAK (typical) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transfer / registration | ~4% of price | ~4% of price (confirm SPA) |
| Trustee / admin | AED 3k to 6k band | Similar order of magnitude |
| Agency resale | 2% common | 2% common |
| Municipality on rent | 5% of contract value | Often none on RAK contracts |

Always request **service charge schedules** and **Form F / Oqood** status in writing before deposit. Confirm current official rules with your conveyancer.

## What does a side-by-side net worked example look like?

**Dubai JVC 1-bed:** AED 750,000 purchase, AED 60,000 rent, AED 21,000 all-in costs (management, void reserve, service charge) → **AED 39,000 net (~5.2%)**.

**RAK Al Hamra 1-bed:** AED 420,000 purchase, AED 34,000 rent, AED 10,000 all-in costs → **AED 24,000 net (~5.7%)**.

RAK wins **percentage net** on this pair; Dubai wins **absolute AED cash** and **exit optionality**. When gross is tied, **lower RAK service charges** (often **8 to 14 AED/sqft** versus **14 to 35 AED/sqft** in Dubai) are the hidden edge. Cross-check methodology on [gross vs net yield Dubai](/guides/gross-vs-net-yield-dubai/).

## How does Wynn Al Marjan change the story?

Resort openings boost **hospitality hiring** and **short-let narratives** near Al Marjan. Near-term yields on **new towers** can **compress** if prices rise faster than rents. **Al Hamra** already has an operating tourism ecosystem, so cash-flow investors sometimes prefer proven stock over pure catalyst bets.

Model **4 to 6 week void** on RAK units tied to seasonality; Dubai corporate tenants often smooth occupancy year-round.

## Who should pick RAK versus Dubai?

Choose **RAK** if you prioritise **yield per dirham**, can hold **5+ years**, and accept **longer marketing** on exit. Choose **Dubai** if you need **Ejari data depth**, faster resale, or Golden Visa sizing with **brand-name liquidity**. Many UAE portfolios run **70% to 85% Dubai / 15% to 30% RAK** as a yield satellite.

## What risks should you plan for before you commit?

**Liquidity discount:** underwrite **90 to 180 days** marketing unless your broker shows faster comps in the exact tower. **Seasonal voids** on tourism-linked stock. **Developer gross marketing:** rebuild net with **25% to 35%** total cost stack including void and management. **Service charge jumps** on branded Marjan towers.

Price any **liquidity discount** into IRR, not just entry yield. Read [RAK vs Dubai investment](/compare/dubai-property-investment-guide/) for capital growth framing beside yield.

## What checklist runs before you sign?

1. Building-specific service charge for 24 months.
2. Net model with void, management, and 4% disposal band.
3. Tenant demand proof (Ejari in Dubai, RAK lease history locally).
4. Compare net, not gross, against JVC alternatives at the same budget.
5. Hold period stated in writing (5+ years for RAK satellite).
6. Golden Visa math verified with current GDRFA rules if relevant.

## How does this fit a wider Gulf portfolio?

RAK pairs with Dubai when you want **UAE exposure** with **two liquidity tiers**. It does not replace **Saudi REGA** yield plays; compare those on [Dubai vs Saudi rental yield](/compare/dubai-vs-saudi-rental-yield/) separately. Keep emirate math in separate tabs so DLD fees and service charges do not leak across models.

## How should you monitor a RAK unit after handover?

Answer first: review **Ejari-equivalent lease data locally**, **service charge notices**, and **void weeks** quarterly. Tourism towers need **seasonal dashboards**; Al Hamra long-let stock needs **maintenance ticket trends**. If void exceeds your model for two consecutive quarters, revisit rent or tenant mix before adding a second RAK unit.

## How does Golden Visa per-dirham efficiency compare?

RAK's lower **dirhams per square foot** can help buyers approach the **AED 2M registered property** Golden Visa threshold with less capital than Dubai premium districts (confirm current official GDRFA rules). Visa efficiency is not yield: run **net yield** and **visa math** on separate tabs so you do not justify a weak asset with residency alone.

## What parallels should you draw with Dubai Sports City?

Both are **non-core emirate pockets** with **yield marketing** and **commuter narratives**. Sports City offers **Dubai liquidity** with slightly lower gross than JVC; RAK offers **higher gross** with **thinner resale**. Compare like-for-like net on [Dubai rental yield](/guides/dubai-rental-yield-guide/) before you treat RAK as automatic outperformance.

## How do financing and leverage change net yield?

Mortgage availability differs by bank and emirate; stress **rate resets** and **service charge escalators** after handover. Leverage can lift cash-on-cash return while compressing margin if void spikes. Keep **6 to 12 months** of carry in AED even when gross yield looks attractive on leveraged spreadsheets.

## How do Al Nakheel and older RAK city towers fit yield portfolios?

Older stock can show **8%+ gross** on low entry but carries **maintenance and elevator** risk. Inspect service charge history and reserve fund minutes before you compare against **Al Hamra** golf-community stock. Blend one older high-gross unit with one **stabilised Hamra** unit if you want RAK diversification without betting entirely on ageing buildings.

When you sell, Dubai listings often achieve **more simultaneous viewings**; price RAK exits with **fewer qualified buyers** in the same month. That liquidity gap is the main reason net yield alone does not crown RAK the default UAE buy.

Track **Marjan tourism occupancy** separately from **Hamra long-let occupancy** if you own both; blending the metrics hides seasonal pain. Export Ejari or local lease logs quarterly so your net model stays honest through year two.

## How do seasonal tourism voids on Marjan differ from Hamra?

Marjan towers tied to **hospitality seasons** can see **double void weeks** in quiet summer months unless corporate tenants anchor the building. Hamra benefits from **golf and beach residents** with more year-round presence. Underwrite Marjan with **extra void** unless your lease stack shows corporate anchors at 40%+ of units.

## How to compare RAK and Dubai yields on the same sheet

Put entry price, service charges, vacancy, and days-to-let in one table before you trust a gross percentage. Dubai mid-market apartments can show deeper tenant pools; RAK beach stock can show higher space per dirham with thinner resale.

| Line item | Dubai mid-market | RAK coastal / Al Hamra style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tenant depth | Usually deeper | Thinner, more seasonal |
| Service charges | Tower bands vary widely | Community bands; verify phase |
| Liquidity | Faster resale in known clusters | Longer marketing windows common |
| Management | More PM choice | Fewer specialist operators |
| Visa angle | Golden Visa paths more discussed | Confirm rules on your unit separately |

Choose Dubai if you need exit optionality inside three to five years. Choose RAK if lifestyle and larger units matter more than speed of resale.
