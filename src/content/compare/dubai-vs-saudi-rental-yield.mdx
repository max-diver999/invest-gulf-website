## Are Dubai rental yields higher than Saudi in 2026?

Yes on most apartment comparisons. Dubai mid-market gross yields of 7 to 9% in corridors like JVC and Sports City exceed typical Riyadh and Jeddah designated-zone gross yields of 4 to 6.5%. Net spreads narrow once you load service charges, void periods, and management fees on both sides.

| Market | Typical gross (apartments) | Net band (conservative) | Data depth |
|
| Dubai mid-market | 7-9% | 5-7% | Ejari medians, deep resale |
| Riyadh designated | 4-5.5% premium, 5-7% mid | 3-5% | Thinner, growing |
| Jeddah designated | 5-6.5% | 3.5-5% | Tourism plus corporate mix |

Detail: [Dubai rental yield guide](/guides/dubai-rental-yield-guide/) · [Saudi rental yield guide](/guides/saudi-rental-yield-guide/).

## How do Riyadh and Jeddah compare within Saudi?

Riyadh anchors government and corporate tenancy; Jeddah adds Red Sea corporate and tourism-linked demand. Jeddah mid-market apartments often print slightly higher gross than Riyadh premium towers because entry prices lag Dubai equivalents.

| District type (Jeddah) | Gross | Notes |
|
| Obhur / coastal | 5-6.5% | Tourism and corporate |
| Central refurbished | 5-6% | Mixed local and expat |
| Premium Corniche | 4-5% | Capital preservation |

## What does a net yield worked example look like?

### Dubai JVC 1-bed (illustrative)

Purchase: AED 680,000. Rent: AED 58,000. Service charges, PM, and void: AED 22,000. Net: AED 36,000, about 5.3% net on price (8.5% gross).

### Jeddah mid-zone 1-bed (illustrative)

Purchase: SAR 650,000 (about AED 637,000). Rent: SAR 42,000. HOA, PM, and void: SAR 14,000. Net: SAR 28,000, about 4.3% net (6.5% gross).

Gap: roughly one percentage point net in favour of Dubai on this pair, consistent with 2026 field data on mid-market stock.

## What REGA checks apply before you buy in Saudi?

Foreign rental income only flows from REGA-listed designated zones under the January 2026 framework. Confirm four items before any deposit:

| Check | Why it matters |
|
| Zone on current REGA foreign ownership list | Off-list purchases cannot lease legally as foreign owner |
| Developer registration and escrow | Protects against informal sales |
| Lease registration pathway | Required for compliant landlord status |
| Bank account for rent repatriation | Without it, net yield stays theoretical |

Full framework: [Saudi designated zones](/guides/saudi-arabia-property-foreigners-guide/).

## What Dubai costs affect net yield on the UAE side?

On Dubai freehold stock, budget DLD transfer at 4% on purchase plus annual service charges and RERA-compliant management. Ejari registration and renewal fees are small but mandatory for long-let compliance. Use [service charges by area](/guides/service-charges-dubai-by-area/) when converting gross marketing yields to net.

| Cost line | Typical Dubai note |
|
| DLD transfer | 4% of purchase price plus admin |
| Service charge | AED 12 to 25+ per sq ft/year by tower |
| PM fee | 5 to 8% of rent on long-let |
| Void | 2 to 6 weeks on mid-market apartments |

## Who should choose Dubai vs Saudi for rental income?

Choose Dubai if you need near-term net cash flow, liquid resale, and established PM infrastructure. Choose Saudi designated zones if you accept 5 to 10 year holds, REGA diligence, and thinner secondary markets in exchange for Vision 2030 upside. Many portfolios run 60 to 80% income core in Dubai and 10 to 25% Saudi satellite exposure.

## What risks compress net yield in each market?

Dubai risks: service charge surprises, short-let regulation changes, and supply waves in single corridors. Saudi risks: HOA schedules that arrive late, slower tenant turnover (model 6 to 10 weeks void), and resale pools limited by foreign ownership rules. Saudi gross yields compressed from 8 to 10% in 2022 toward 6 to 8% in 2026 as prices rose faster than rents in Riyadh and Jeddah.

## How does stress-testing change Dubai vs Saudi decisions?

Model Dubai with Ejari median rent minus service charge per sq ft from the building's latest statement, PM at 5 to 8%, and four weeks void. Model Saudi with HOA schedules from the master community, PM at 8 to 12% in newer districts, and six to ten weeks void because tenant turnover is slower. Financing changes the denominator: net yield must use equity deployed, not headline price, especially where Saudi LTV rules for foreigners are still evolving.

| Assumption | Dubai default | Saudi default |
|
| Void period | 4 weeks | 8 weeks |
| PM fee | 5-8% of rent | 8-12% of rent |
| Transfer tax | 4% DLD on purchase | REGA zone fees plus broker |
| Rent discovery | Ejari medians | Broker comps plus leases |

## How do employment visa trends affect tenant demand?

Dubai leans on tourism, trade, and financial services hiring cycles. Saudi leans on Vision 2030 corporate relocations to Riyadh and Jeddah. Yield investors should match product type to employer base: mid-market apartments near Dubailand or JVC for Dubai cash flow; designated-zone apartments near corporate hubs in Riyadh or Jeddah for Saudi income with longer hold.

## What does a blended portfolio look like in practice?

A common split keeps 60 to 80% of Gulf rental capital in Dubai for liquidity and net cash flow, 10 to 25% in Saudi designated zones for diversification, and reserves for REGA or DLD transaction costs in separate lines so net yield math survives diligence. Rebalance when Ejari medians or Saudi HOA schedules shift, not when a single broker quote moves.

## What currency peg risk should Gulf investors note?

Saudi riyal and UAE dirham both track USD. Yield comparisons in AED or SAR are comparable, but repatriation paths and bank fees differ. Model FX conversion at your home bank, not mid-market headlines.

Saudi white-land tax and Vision 2030 supply waves can move rents faster than Dubai mid-market corridors. Revisit Saudi HOA schedules annually; Dubai SC usually updates through Mollak with clearer history.
