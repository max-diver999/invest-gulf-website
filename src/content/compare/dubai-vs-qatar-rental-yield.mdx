## How do net yields compare after fees?

Always underwrite net. Gross-to-net compression is typically **1-2 percentage points** in both markets when you include management, vacancy, and building charges.

Qatar net compresses when purchase price on new Lusail stock runs high relative to rent. Older Pearl studios at lower entry can reach **4%+ net** with stable corporate tenants.

## Which tenant profile is more stable?

Qatar demand is narrower but stickier: energy majors, government-adjacent employers, embassy staff. Leases often run 12 months with corporate guarantors. Void rates in Pearl and West Bay commonly sit around **85-95% occupancy** versus wider variance by Dubai tower.

Dubai offers a larger expat pool, tourism-linked short-let options, and faster re-letting in mid-market stock, but also more supply shocks in oversubscribed launch corridors. Lifestyle overlap: [Dubai vs Doha property investment](/compare/dubai-vs-doha-property-investment/).

## What service charges and hidden costs compress yield?

Rule: pull **actual** service charge schedules before comparing countries. One Dubai tower at AED 40/sqft can lose to a Qatar mid-rise at QAR 18/sqm even when Dubai gross rent is higher.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Dubai | Service charge | AED 12-35/sqft/year |
| Dubai | Short-let permits / NOC | Varies by building |
| Qatar | Building / municipality | QAR 15-25/sqm/month |

## How does exit liquidity compare?

If you may need capital back within 24 months, Dubai mid-market apartments remain the Gulf's functional cash-out market. Qatar suits **5+ year** hold assumptions.

| Typical resale (mid) | 30-90 days | 6-12 months |
| Agent depth | Very high | Moderate in Pearl |
| International buyers | ~68% Dubai deals | Smaller but growing |

## How do West Bay and Dubai Marina compare on tenant quality?

West Bay targets embassy and energy-corporate tenants with lower gross but predictable renewals. Dubai Marina mixes tourism, short-let, and long-let demand, which can lift gross rent but adds permit and NOC complexity. For pure long-let yield funds, West Bay and Pearl corporate leases often behave more like Dubai Business Bay mid-towers than like Marina holiday-home stock.

## What off-plan vs ready stock caution applies in both markets?

Off-plan brochures in Dubai and Lusail often quote gross yield on launch price without service charges on the handed-over building. Ready stock lets you verify actual tenant mix and charge schedules before you wire a deposit. If you buy off-plan for yield, tie your model to **comparable handed-over units**, not renderings, and keep extra liquidity for 6-12 months of carry until the asset registers and rents.

## What Dubai acquisition costs affect net yield planning?

Yield math starts **after** you know all-in cost. Dubai freehold purchases typically include **4% DLD transfer fee** plus agency and trustee lines. That stack raises effective entry price and lowers net yield versus a gross-rent-only spreadsheet.

| Dubai cost line | Typical |
| --- | --- |
| DLD transfer | 4% of value |
| Agency | ~2% (negotiable) |
| Trustee / admin | AED 4,000-8,000 |

Qatar uses its own registration fees and lawyer workflows; compare all-in cost on each side before you rank gross percentages.

## What checklist runs before you buy yield stock?

1. Pull 2 years of achieved rent (Ejari in Dubai, local equivalents in Qatar)
2. Model net with identical void assumptions (1-2 months)
3. Compare service charges per sqft or sqm on the **same** unit size
4. Stress-test resale: 90-day Dubai exit vs 12-month Qatar exit
5. Confirm freehold / foreign ownership rules for the exact building
6. Add Dubai DLD 4% (or Qatar registration) to purchase price before dividing rent

## How should you monitor yield quarterly after purchase?

Track achieved rent versus listing ask, void days, and service charge invoices each quarter. In Dubai, pull Ejari renewal dates and compare your tower's renewals to two neighbouring buildings. In Qatar, confirm employer guarantors on new leases before you accept a discount. If net yield slips more than 0.5 points below your model for two consecutive quarters, revisit exit timing or rent strategy before the market softens further.

Currency note: both AED and QAR peg to USD, so yield differences are mostly **rent minus charges**, not FX speculation. Repatriation still needs clean bank KYC trails on both sides.

Family landlords comparing Pearl villas to Dubai villa communities should note that Qatar villa gross often looks lower but void risk can be smaller when a corporate lease backs the tenant. Apartment yield investors usually get cleaner comparables in Dubai JVC and Lusail one-beds than in villa stock on either side.
