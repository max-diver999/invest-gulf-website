Freehold vs leasehold in the UAE determines whether you own an asset permanently or rent time on it. In a market where a large share of Dubai buyers are foreign nationals, title-type mistakes are expensive and avoidable. Virtually all investor-grade stock in Dubai's major communities is freehold. The risk lives in edge cases: older sub-divisions, northern emirate listings, and broker language that says "ownership" when the register says 99-year lease.

This comparison covers all seven emirates at a practical investor level: what each title means, where leasehold still appears, and how title affects mortgage, resale, Golden Visa, and inheritance.

## What is the difference between freehold and leasehold at a glance?

| Type | What you own | Term | Foreign buyers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Freehold | Unit plus proportional land interest | Perpetual | Yes, in designated zones |
| Common Hold / JOP | Your unit; shared common areas | Perpetual (apartments) | Yes, in designated zones |
| Leasehold | Right to occupy/use | Fixed (often 99 years) | Sometimes, verify |
| Usufruct | Use rights without full ownership | Fixed term | Select projects (confirm locally) |

Freehold is the default target for foreign investors who need mortgage, Golden Visa, and clean resale. Leasehold can work for long holds if remaining term, ground rent, and transfer rules are documented before the MOU.

## What does freehold mean for foreign buyers in the UAE?

Freehold means your name (or your company) sits on the land department register with perpetual title until you sell or transfer. In Dubai, foreigners may buy freehold only in **designated zones** (60+ communities including Marina, JVC, Business Bay, Downtown, Palm, Dubai Hills, JLT, DIFC residential, Dubai South, and others).

Abu Dhabi allows foreign freehold in investment zones such as **Yas Island, Saadiyat, Al Reem**, and select Al Raha stock, but **verify each unit** on ADREC. RAK pockets (Al Marjan, Mina Al Arab) and limited Ajman stock exist; Sharjah remains the most restrictive emirate for perpetual foreign freehold.

## Where does leasehold still appear?

Leasehold grants a fixed term, commonly **99 years** from registration, after which rights revert to the land freeholder unless renewed per contract.

| Remaining term | Typical impact |
| --- | --- |
| 70+ years | Functions like freehold for many buyers |
| 50-60 years | Some banks tighten LTV |
| Under 50 years | Mortgage difficulty, resale discount |
| Under 30 years | Serious liquidity penalty |

Leasehold still shows up in older Dubai pockets, partially designated areas, Sharjah and Ajman towers marketed as "ownership," and some commercial-to-residential conversions. Always pull the **Unit Profile** before signing.

## How do the emirates compare for title type?

| Emirate | Foreign freehold depth | Leasehold risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dubai | Deepest map, most liquidity | Low in major communities |
| Abu Dhabi | Growing on Yas/Saadiyat | Verify per building |
| Sharjah | Very limited freehold | High marketing confusion |
| RAK / Ajman | Select zones | Read SPA transfer clauses |
| Fujairah / UAQ | Smaller investor pools | Case-by-case |

## What is common hold (jointly owned property)?

Apartments in Dubai often register as **common hold**: you own your unit freehold and share common areas through the owners association. For investors, common hold behaves like freehold for mortgage and Golden Visa purposes if the register says freehold on the unit. Service charge discipline still drives net yield.

## How should foreign buyers verify title before paying a deposit?

**Dubai:** DLD Unit Profile via Dubai REST or a Registration Trustee. **Abu Dhabi:** ADREC records. **Never** rely on a sales brochure alone.

Mandatory step: title search **before** MOU or SPA signature. If the broker cannot produce an official classification (Freehold, Leasehold, Common Hold), default to a Dubai freehold community with comparable capital.

See [Freehold vs leasehold in Dubai](/guides/freehold-vs-leasehold-dubai/) for Dubai-specific worked examples.

## How does title type affect UAE mortgages?

Banks prefer freehold in approved buildings. Leasehold financing often requires **50+ years remaining** and may offer **lower LTV** than an equivalent freehold unit. Some lenders decline leasehold entirely.

If you plan leverage, confirm both **bank policy on remaining term** and **building approval** before you pay a booking fee.

## Does leasehold block Golden Visa property routes?

Golden Visa property qualification generally requires **eligible freehold** at about **AED 2 million registered value** (confirm with ICP/GDRFA). Leasehold usually fails that test even if the lifestyle fit is fine.

Buy for residency only after the register confirms freehold and the registered value meets the current threshold.

## What happens to resale liquidity?

Dubai's transaction volume assumes **freehold comparables**. Leasehold resales often trade **5-15% below** equivalent freehold with fewer bids, especially as remaining term shortens.

Marketing language that hides leasehold until trustee appointment is a common source of aborted deals. Price the discount upfront if you still choose leasehold for entry price.

## What checklist should you run before you sign?

1. Official title classification PDF, not a developer factsheet.
2. Confirmation the plot sits on the **foreign ownership list** for that emirate.
3. Transfer fee to master developer or freeholder on resale.
4. **Ground rent** caps and escalation clauses.
5. Bank mortgage pre-approval on **this** unit type.

Walk away if ground rent is uncapped, Golden Visa is required but title is leasehold, or Sharjah/Ajman "ownership" lacks freehold confirmation.

## How do inheritance and family transfer differ?

| Title | Inheritance path | Consideration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Freehold | Standard transfer via UAE will or succession | Register will with Dubai Courts Notary |
| Leasehold | Assignment per SPA, may need freeholder consent | Read transfer clause before buying |
| JOP apartment | Unit passes; OA membership transfers | SC arrears block transfer |

Foreign owners should draft a **UAE will** covering real estate. Leasehold without clear assignment rights can trap capital across generations even with long remaining term.

| Scenario | Freehold | Leasehold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gift to adult child | DLD transfer | May need freeholder approval |
| Corporate to personal | Allowed with advice | Often restricted |

## How does off-plan title work?

Off-plan sales should state whether the **final title** will be freehold in a designated zone. Escrow registration (Oqood in Dubai) is not a substitute for reading the **title type on completion**. On handover, re-pull the register before you assume Golden Visa eligibility.

## What about DIFC and ADGM property?

DIFC and ADGM run parallel registration systems for some stock. Title concepts still split between **freehold-style** ownership and limited-term rights depending on the asset. Use the relevant registry and a lawyer licensed for that jurisdiction, not generic emirate advice.

## What is a worked pricing example?

Suppose a freehold JVC two-bedroom at **AED 750,000** versus a leasehold equivalent at **13%** headline discount (**AED 652,500**). The cheaper headline must cover **liquidity discount, visa ineligibility, and mortgage friction**. For most foreign investors holding **5-10 years**, freehold wins unless the discount exceeds **20%** and remaining lease term stays above **70 years**.

Budget **DLD transfer** on Dubai freehold resale at the prevailing **4%** rate plus trustee and agency lines when you compare total cost of ownership, not just purchase price.

## What is the Sharjah-specific trap?

Sharjah marketing often uses "ownership" language while the register shows **long lease** or local-partner structures. Ask five questions before you wire:

1. Can I see the official title classification?
2. Is the project on the foreign ownership list?
3. What is the master developer transfer fee on resale?
4. Does ground rent escalate?
5. Will UAE banks mortgage this unit?

If answers stay vague, **default to Dubai freehold** for comparable capital. See [Sharjah freehold areas guide](/guides/sharjah-freehold-areas-guide/).

## What about usufruct and musataha?

Usufruct and musataha are **use-right** structures more common in Abu Dhabi master developments and some commercial conversions. They are not interchangeable with freehold for Golden Visa or typical bank LTV. Treat them as specialty contracts with lawyer review.

## What red flags should pause a Gulf purchase?

Pause when brokers mix leasehold stock with freehold marketing, when **Golden Visa** is promised on non-freehold title, or when ground rent and freeholder consent fees are missing from the SPA schedules.

Corporate buyers should confirm the vehicle can hold the registered title type before structuring through offshore SPVs.

*Title rules vary by emirate and plot. Verify with the land department and qualified solicitor. Not legal advice.*
