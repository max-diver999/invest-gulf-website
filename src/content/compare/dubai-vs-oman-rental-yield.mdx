    answer: "No. Dubai's secondary market is among the world's most active for expat apartments. Oman ITC resales can take 6–12 months outside prime Muscat waterfront. Yield investors in Oman should plan longer holds."
  - question: "What fees reduce Oman net yield?"
    answer: "Property management (6–10%), maintenance, municipality charges, and 2–3% vacancy allowance. ITC service charges vary by project. Total drag is often 1.5–2.5 points off gross — similar to Dubai mid-market when charges are moderate."
  - question: "Who should choose Oman over Dubai for rental income?"
    answer: "Investors prioritising stable corporate tenants, 2–3 year leases, and lifestyle exposure in Muscat over maximum yield. Diplomatic and energy-sector demand supports occupancy. Choose Dubai if you need highest net cash flow and fast exit."
