## What is Samana Barari Heights?

Samana Barari Heights is an off-plan family apartment project in Majan Dubailand from Samana Developers, with studios from about AED 800,000 and handover targeted around Q4 2027.



Majan represents one of Dubailand's most promising residential clusters, benefiting from master-planned infrastructure while maintaining affordable price points compared to established Dubai communities. The area attracts young professionals and families seeking homeownership opportunities within reasonable commuting distance of major employment centers.

Samana Barari Heights incorporates sustainable design elements and energy-efficient systems to reduce long-term operating costs for residents while meeting Dubai Municipality's green building standards. The development's family-focused design includes dedicated children's areas, family pools, and secure play zones throughout the community.

![Samana Barari Heights Modern Interiors](https://invest-gulf.com/images/projects/samana-barari-heights/inline-2.webp)
## What unit types and payment plans are offered?

Typical Samana structures include 5% booking, 15% within 30 days, 40% during construction, and 40% on handover, with optional post-handover plans on select launches (confirm in SPA).


The pricing structure reflects Samana's commitment to affordability, with cost per square foot ranging from AED 1,780-2,000 depending on floor level and unit orientation. Corner units and higher floors command premiums of 3-8% due to enhanced natural light and privacy.

Flexible Payment Plan:
- 5% booking fee upon reservation
- 15% down payment within 30 days
- 40% during construction (quarterly installments)
- 40% upon completion and handover
- Optional post-handover payment plans available

The accessible payment structure targets first-time buyers and young families with limited initial capital while spreading major payments across the construction timeline.
