Nshama Town Square Savannah is one of Dubai's more accessible entry points into master-planned community living. Townhouses start from about AED 800,000 in a family-focused master plan with parks, retail, and schools woven into daily life.

The Savannah phase targets young families, first-time buyers, and investors who want stable tenant demand in [Dubai neighbourhoods for families](/guides/dubai-neighbourhoods-for-families/). See [Nshama developer review](/guides/nshama-developer-review/) and [Town Square area guide](/areas/town-square-property-investment/) for context.

## What is Town Square and Nshama's community model?

Nshama built Town Square as a self-contained suburban district with walkable retail, green space, and mid-rise housing rather than a single tower launch.

Savannah adds townhouse product between apartment and villa lifestyles. The master plan emphasizes pedestrian routes, community programming, and schools within the district.

For relocating families compare [Dubai property for relocating families](/guides/dubai-property-for-relocating-families/) and [first-time investment basics](/guides/dubai-property-investment-for-beginners/).

## What townhouse layouts does Savannah offer?

Configurations typically span 2-4 bedrooms with private gardens, covered parking, and modern kitchen packages.

### Standard features across units

- Private front and rear gardens
- Covered parking for one or two vehicles
- Contemporary kitchens with integrated appliances
- Master en-suite bedrooms
- Built-in storage and wardrobe lines
- Insulation and efficient climate systems

![Savannah Townhouses Interior](https://invest-gulf.com/images/projects/nshama-town-square-phase/inline-2.webp)

Townhouses suit family tenants who outgrow apartments but do not need villa-scale service charges.

## What community amenities support rental demand?

Town Square includes a central park, retail square, pools, fitness facilities, children's play areas, and cycling tracks. Schools and medical facilities continue to roll out across phases.

![Town Square Community Amenities](https://invest-gulf.com/images/projects/nshama-town-square-phase/inline-3.webp)

Amenity depth supports long-term leases from expatriate families, the core tenant pool for Savannah.

## Where is Town Square and how connected is it?

Town Square sits in central Dubai with road links to business districts and leisure hubs. Typical drive times (traffic dependent):

| Destination | Approximate drive |
| --- | --- |
| Dubai Metro (Red Line access) | ~15 minutes |
| Al Maktoum International Airport | ~25 minutes |
| Dubai International Airport | ~35 minutes |

Families accept slightly longer commutes in exchange for community space and lower entry tickets than inner-city villas.

## What rental and investment profile should you expect?

Family townhouses in comparable communities often achieve **6-8% gross** yields when priced correctly and furnished for tenant expectations. Use the [Dubai rental yield guide](/guides/dubai-rental-yield-guide/) to model net returns after service charges.

| Rental strategy | Expected yield band | Tenant profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Family long-term | 6-7% | Expat families, young professionals |
| Executive housing | 7-8% | Corporate relocations |
| Shared housing (where permitted) | 8-9% | Young professionals sharing larger units |

Compare with Arabian Ranches, The Springs, JVC, and Motor City for family-community benchmarks.

### Illustrative cost stack (AED 1.05M townhouse example)

- Down payment at 15%: AED 157,500
- Construction-linked balance: per SPA schedule
- DLD registration at 4%: budget AED 42,000 on transfer value
- Furnishing: AED 50,000-80,000 if leasing furnished

## What payment plans and ongoing costs apply?

Nshama typically offers 5-10% booking, construction-linked instalments, and limited post-handover plans for qualified buyers. Confirm current Savannah tranche terms on the sales offer.

Service charges often land around **AED 8-15 per sq ft** annually for community upkeep. DEWA is consumption-based. Budget property management if you invest from abroad.

## What legal and community rules matter?

Town Square sells freehold to international buyers with standard DLD registration. Homeowners association rules cover architecture, pets, and rental conduct.

Before purchase verify construction status, amenity delivery for your phase, school opening timelines, and utility provider contracts.

Review [due diligence for Dubai property](/guides/due-diligence-dubai-property/) before signing the SPA.

## What risks should investors weigh?

- Off-plan completion risk (track escrow and reporting)
- Competing family communities opening nearby supply
- Service charge increases as amenities mature
- Moderate capital appreciation versus prime central districts

Savannah fits portfolios seeking **income and occupancy stability** rather than trophy appreciation.

## How does Savannah compare with other family communities?

| Community | Entry ticket (indicative) | Tenant pool | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Town Square Savannah | From ~AED 800K | Expat families | Master-planned, newer stock |
| Arabian Ranches | Higher villa tickets | Established families | Mature HOA, premium SC |
| The Springs | Mid-market townhouses | Long-term leases | Limited new supply |
| JVC townhouses | Variable | Mixed | More investor turnover |

Savannah wins on price accessibility and integrated master plan. Mature communities win on proven school bus routes and resale depth. Match the product to your hold period and furnishing budget.

## What due diligence steps apply before booking?

Off-plan townhouses still require escrow discipline.

1. Confirm Nshama project registration and escrow account on the sales form.
2. Match payment milestones to reported construction percentage.
3. Read HOA rules on leasing, pets, and exterior modifications.
4. Model DEWA deposits plus annual SC per sq ft quoted in the brochure.
5. If buying for Golden Visa, verify registered value on completion, not launch price alone.

See [off-plan vs ready property in Dubai](/guides/off-plan-vs-ready-property-dubai/) for delay and refund frameworks that also apply to family communities.

## Who is Savannah best suited for?

**Strong fit:** first-time Dubai buyers, family owner-occupiers, and investors targeting 5-7 year holds with rental income.

**Weaker fit:** buyers needing immediate keys (verify phase handover), flip traders in a supply-heavy segment, or investors who cannot monitor construction updates from abroad without an agent.

Pair Savannah with professional snagging and property management if you will lease unfurnished from overseas. Community standards reward landlords who maintain gardens and AC before each tenancy.
