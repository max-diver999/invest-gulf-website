## What is Emaar South Expo Golf development?

The Expo Golf course serves as the community's centerpiece, featuring an 18-hole championship course designed to international tournament standards. The course layout incorporates natural desert landscaping with modern irrigation and maintenance systems, creating year-round playing conditions for residents and members.

The strategic timeline aligns with Al Maktoum Airport's expansion phases and Dubai South's development as an aviation and logistics hub. This positioning provides residents with unique access to employment opportunities and international connectivity while maintaining a suburban residential environment.

- Financing: UAE banks typically offer 70 to 75% LTV for villa purchases
- Service charges: Estimated AED 8 to 12 per sq ft annually

## What payment plans and closing costs apply?

Typical Emaar South structures include 10 to 15% booking, construction-linked milestones, and optional post-handover plans up to 3 to 5 years for qualified buyers.

For an AED 3.1 million villa example:

- Down payment: AED 620,000 (20% illustrative)
- Construction payments: AED 2,170,000 over 2 to 3 years
- DLD registration: AED 124,000 (4% transfer fee illustration)
- Furnishing: AED 150,000 to 250,000 for complete setup

### Legal framework and due diligence

Emaar South properties provide full freehold ownership for international buyers with inheritance and rental rights subject to community rules.

Essential due diligence before purchase:

- Building permits and master plan approvals current
- Golf course operations and access agreements documented
- Homeowners association fees and reserve fund status
- DEWA connection timeline for off-plan purchases
