## What is the DAMAC Islands master plan?

DAMAC Islands is a multi-island Dubai master plan where each cluster follows a named destination theme, Antigua, Maldives, Bora Bora, Santorini, rather than a single villa typology. The project targets foreign buyers who want resort infrastructure and lagoon access inside Dubai, with phases spanning roughly 2026 to 2030 for early cluster handovers.

Island clusters:

- **Antigua:** Caribbean-themed villas with tropical architecture and beach club amenities
- **Maldives:** Overwater-inspired designs with lagoon access and spa facilities
- **Bora Bora:** Polynesian-themed community with crystal lagoons and water sports
- **Santorini:** Mediterranean-inspired cluster with whitewashed architecture and hillside positioning
- Additional clusters: Expanding portfolio of destination-themed communities in development

Unique development features:

- Crystal lagoons with turquoise water and white sand beaches throughout communities
- Themed architecture matching each destination's authentic design characteristics
- Cultural programming including festivals, dining, and entertainment reflecting island themes
- Resort-grade amenities including spas, yacht clubs, and water sports facilities
- Year-round tropical climate recreation through landscaping and facility design

DAMAC Islands Antigua villas start from AED 2.9 million for lagoon-linked stock, while premium beachfront plots sit higher once plot size and water frontage widen. Typical off-plan plans still use a 10% booking tranche, about 60% during construction, and 20% at handover unless a post-handover schedule is offered in writing. Service charges are estimated at AED 20 to 30 per square foot annually for lagoon, landscaping, security, and resort programming, so net yield models must deduct that line before comparing Arabian Ranches or Dubai Hills. Invest Gulf underwriting treats themed resort stock as lifestyle-first: model DLD at 4% on resale, escrow milestones, and a 3 to 5 year hold before assuming liquid exit comps. Buyers who need school-walk suburbs should shortlist Dubai Hills instead of forcing a resort narrative onto daily family logistics.

Insider tip: ask for the Antigua lagoon-facing plot schedule and the written AED per-square-foot service charge before you treat brochure yields as bankable.

Service charges near AED 20 to 30 per square foot annually once lagoons are live typically sit above Arabian Ranches-style estates, and we model that line before quoting net yield.
