## What is the DAMAC Islands master plan?

Total development spans multi-island phases with integrated resort facilities and infrastructure for permanent residence and vacation use.

Island clusters:

- **Antigua:** Caribbean-themed villas with tropical architecture and beach club amenities
- **Maldives:** Overwater-inspired designs with lagoon access and spa facilities
- **Bora Bora:** Polynesian-themed community with crystal lagoons and water sports
- **Santorini:** Mediterranean-inspired cluster with whitewashed architecture and hillside positioning
- Additional clusters: Expanding portfolio of destination-themed communities in development

Unique development features:

- Crystal lagoons with turquoise water and white sand beaches throughout communities
- Themed architecture matching each destination's authentic design characteristics
- Cultural programming including festivals, dining, and entertainment reflecting island themes
- Resort-grade amenities including spas, yacht clubs, and water sports facilities
- Year-round tropical climate recreation through landscaping and facility design

The development philosophy creates destination living where residents experience authentic island cultures and lifestyle elements within Dubai's urban convenience and infrastructure framework.

## What villa types and pricing apply to the Antigua cluster?

Antigua starts from AED 2.9 million for themed villas with lagoon access. Premium waterfront plots command higher tickets. Confirm phase, handover window, and escrow milestones in the SPA before transfer.

Typical off-plan payment structure: 10% booking, 10% on agreement, 60% during construction, 20% on handover, aligned to construction progress unless the developer offers a post-handover plan.

Service charges: Estimated AED 20 to 30 per square foot annually covering lagoon upkeep, tropical landscaping, security, and resort programming. Waterfront units often sit at the top of that range.

Villa features (Antigua theme):

- Caribbean architectural styles with tropical color palettes and natural materials
- Outdoor living spaces including terraces, gazebos, and tropical gardens
- Lagoon or beach access from villa grounds with water activity facilities
- Premium finishes reflecting island luxury including teak flooring and stone accents
- Smart home integration with climate control optimized for tropical living experience
- Private pools, outdoor kitchens, and entertainment areas supporting island lifestyle

Recreation and wellness:

- Destination spas featuring treatments inspired by each island's cultural traditions
- Championship golf course with island-themed holes and tropical landscaping
- Tennis and padel courts with styling matching cluster themes
- Fitness centers with outdoor training areas and beach workout facilities
- Adventure clubs offering island exploration, fishing expeditions, and cultural experiences
- Kids' clubs with themed play areas and educational programming about island cultures

Dining and entertainment:

- Themed restaurants representing authentic cuisine from each island destination
- Beach bars and sunset lounges with live entertainment matching cultural themes
- Cultural pavilions hosting festivals, art exhibitions, and traditional performances
- Retail districts featuring artisan shops, fashion boutiques, and specialty stores
- Event spaces for weddings, corporate functions, and community celebrations

Community infrastructure:

- International schools with global culture curricula
- Healthcare facilities including wellness clinics and emergency medical services
- Business centers and co-working spaces for remote professionals
- Retail and commercial districts supporting daily living needs

Request written service charge schedules and DLD fee quotes before deposit; refusal is a due diligence red flag on off-plan stock.

## How connected is DAMAC Islands to Dubai hubs?

**Travel times:**

- Dubai International Airport: 35 minutes via major highways
- Downtown Dubai/DIFC: 30 minutes off-peak, 45 minutes peak hours
- Dubai Marina/JBR: 25 minutes via Sheikh Zayed Road
- Business Bay: 35 minutes through established route networks
- Al Maktoum International Airport: 20 minutes via direct highway access
- Emirates Hills/Arabian Ranches: 15 minutes through residential corridor connections

Infrastructure includes integrated road networks, planned shuttle and water taxi concepts, utilities for resort-grade facilities, and telecom suitable for remote work.

The location balances destination living with practical Dubai access for buyers who prioritise lifestyle differentiation over inner-city convenience.
