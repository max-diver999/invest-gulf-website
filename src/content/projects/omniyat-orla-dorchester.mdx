## About Orla by Omniyat Project

Orla is developed by Omniyat, Dubai's leading ultra-luxury developer known for architecturally significant projects including The Opus by Zaha Hadid and One at Palm Jumeirah. The partnership with Dorchester Collection brings legendary hospitality expertise to residential living, while Foster + Partners provides world-class architectural vision that has shaped landmarks including Apple Park and the British Museum Great Court.

The development occupies a unique position at Palm Jumeirah's apex, offering unobstructed 270° views across Dubai's skyline, Arabian Gulf, and the palm's iconic fronds. With only 93 residences across multiple building typologies, Orla emphasizes exclusivity and privacy over scale, targeting the same ultra-affluent demographic that acquires trophy properties in London, New York, and Hong Kong.

Omniyat's track record includes consistent delivery of premium developments with strong capital appreciation, including properties in Marasi Bay where residences have doubled in value from launch to delivery. The company's focus on architectural excellence and brand partnerships positions Orla within Dubai's established ultra-luxury segment alongside developments like Bulgari Resort and Four Seasons Private Residences.
