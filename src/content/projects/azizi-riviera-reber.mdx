## What is the crystal lagoon and design concept?

Azizi Riviera Reber markets a French Riviera themed community around a large crystal lagoon with beach-style amenity zones in MBR City (verify phased delivery in SPA).



The lagoon system incorporates advanced filtration and circulation technology that maintains crystal-clear visibility and optimal water chemistry using significantly less water than traditional systems. The technology enables white sandy beach areas, shallow recreational zones, and deeper areas suitable for kayaking, paddleboarding, and other water activities.

![Azizi Riviera Waterfront](https://invest-gulf.com/images/projects/azizi-riviera-reber/inline-2.webp)

Architectural design emphasizes French Riviera elegance through building facades, landscaping, and community layout that creates intimate neighborhoods connected by waterfront promenades and beach access points. Each residential building features Mediterranean-inspired elements including terracotta roofing, stone accents, and expansive balconies overlooking lagoon and garden areas.

The master plan integrates residential towers with comprehensive amenity zones, retail and dining districts, and recreational facilities positioned to maximize lagoon access and views for all residents. Sustainability features include energy-efficient systems, water conservation technology, and environmentally responsible construction practices.
