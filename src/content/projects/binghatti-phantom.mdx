## About Binghatti Phantom

Binghatti Phantom is a mid-rise residential development featuring contemporary apartments designed to meet the evolving needs of Dubai's diverse population. The project emphasizes functional layouts, premium finishes, and comprehensive amenities that appeal to both end-users and rental tenants in JVC's established community.

The development incorporates Binghatti's signature architectural elements while maintaining compatibility with JVC's established neighborhood character. Each unit is designed with modern living in mind, featuring open-plan layouts, floor-to-ceiling windows, and premium fixtures that maximize both space utilization and natural light penetration.

![Binghatti Phantom Interior](https://invest-gulf.com/images/projects/binghatti-phantom/inline-2.webp)

Key project specifications include energy-efficient systems, smart home pre-wiring, and premium building materials sourced from international suppliers. The development targets LEED certification standards, reflecting Binghatti's commitment to sustainable construction practices and long-term value preservation.
