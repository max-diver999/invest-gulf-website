Binghatti Phantom is an off-plan stack in Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC) from Binghatti Developers, priced from AED 1.1 million on studios. JVC remains one of Dubai's busiest mid-market rental corridors, and Binghatti has repeated delivery across similar product lines. ## What is Binghatti Phantom in JVC?

The project targets young professionals, small families, and yield-focused buyers who want Binghatti's design language without Marina entry pricing. Typical marketing includes open-plan layouts, floor-to-ceiling glazing, smart-home pre-wiring, and podium amenities (pool, gym, retail at ground level). Completion is marketed for Q4 2027 with handover into early 2028.

![Binghatti Phantom Interior](https://invest-gulf.com/images/projects/binghatti-phantom/inline-2.webp)

## What unit types and prices are offered?

Studios from AED 1.1 million sit in roughly 450 to 500 sq ft bands. One-bedroom units start near AED 1.4 million; two-bedroom from about AED 1.85 million; three-bedroom from roughly AED 2.6 million depending on view and floor.

| Unit type | Size (sq ft) | Starting price (AED) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio | 450-500 | 1,100,000 |
| 1-bedroom | 650-750 | 1,400,000 |
| 2-bedroom | 950-1,100 | 1,850,000 |
| 3-bedroom | 1,300-1,500 | 2,600,000 |

Payment plans commonly run 20% on booking, construction-linked instalments, and a handover tranche (often near 50% on completion, confirm on your SPA). All off-plan transfers must hit the escrow account named in the contract.

## Why does JVC location matter for rentals?

JVC sits between Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road and Al Khail, with quick access toward Marina, Business Bay, and DIFC by car. The community is mature: schools, clinics, supermarkets, and parks are already operating, which supports tenant stickiness compared with greenfield districts.

| Distance anchor | Typical drive (off-peak) |
| --- | --- |
| Dubai Marina | 15-20 minutes |
| Business Bay | 20-25 minutes |
| Mall of the Emirates | 15 minutes |
| DXB Airport | 25-30 minutes |

Compare yields with [Downtown Dubai investment context](/areas/downtown-dubai-property-investment/) only after you pull three Ejari-backed comps on the same unit type.

## What yields should investors model?

Marketing bands cite 7% to 9% gross on studios and high 6% to low 8% on larger units. Net returns fall after service charges, vacancy, and management fees (often 20% to 30% of rent on long-let).

| Unit type | Indicative gross yield band |
| --- | --- |
| Studio | 8-9% |
| 1-bedroom | 7-8% |
| 2-bedroom | 6.5-7.5% |
| 3-bedroom | 6-7% |

Rebuild net cash flow with a service-charge schedule in writing before you waive a reservation. DLD registration runs **4%** on the purchase plus trustee and agency lines on resale; budget those in your exit sheet, not only gross rent.

## How strong is Binghatti as a counterparty?

Binghatti has delivered multiple mid-rise towers across Dubai over the last cycle, often competing on launch PSF and milestone-heavy payment plans. Review tower-specific completion photos, escrow balance reports, and delay clauses in the SPA rather than relying on brand averages alone.

Strengths for this product: familiar JVC tenant pool, repeatable studio and 1-bed demand, accessible entry ticket under AED 1.5M on smaller lines.

Watch items: handover-year listing waves if investor mix is high, service-charge creep on premium amenity stacks, and competition from other JVC launches priced similarly.

## What risks should you stress-test?

Market cycles can compress rents even in JVC. Construction slippage affects buyers carrying rent plus instalments. Currency moves matter for non-AED earners.

Mitigation checklist:

- Independent SPA review with delay compensation spelled out
- Escrow verification on every instalment
- Three rental comparables from the same building typology
- Service charge budget signed by the developer or OA estimate
- Realistic vacancy and management fee in year one

Financing notes: UAE banks lend more easily post-handover; off-plan assignments follow Binghatti NOC rules once minimum paid thresholds clear. See [cash vs mortgage Dubai](/guides/cash-vs-mortgage-dubai-property/) and [Dubai cost of living](/guides/dubai-cost-of-living-guide/) for carry planning.

## What amenities and building specs are marketed?

Podium plans typically include a rooftop pool, gym, landscaped deck, children's play space, 24/7 security, and ground-floor retail for daily convenience. Unit specs emphasize open kitchens, balcony depth, and parking allocation per bedroom count.

Energy-efficient glazing and smart-home pre-wiring are part of Binghatti's recent launches; confirm MEP specs in the sales offer, not only brochure renders.

## Who is the typical buyer profile?

Studios and one-bedroom lines suit first-time investors and young couples working in Marina or Media City. Two and three-bedroom units attract small families who want JVC parks and schools without moving to outer Dubai corridors.

End-users should visit JVC at weekend peak hours to test parking and elevator load. Investors should speak with two property managers who already operate in JVC towers built by Binghatti to validate net rent after 25% management fee.

At handover, expect a cluster of competing listings if the tower skews investor-heavy. Price to transact within 90 days unless you can hold through a full lease cycle.

Review [Dubai developers guide](/guides/dubai-developers-guide/) for how Binghatti pricing compares with Danube, Azizi, and other JVC-active builders on launch PSF and delay history. Ask sales for the latest escrow certificate before you transfer the booking cheque.
