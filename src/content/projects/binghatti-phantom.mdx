## What is Binghatti Phantom in JVC?

Binghatti Phantom is an off-plan residential tower in Jumeirah Village Circle with studios from AED 1.1 million, completion marketed for Q4 2027, and handover into early 2028. Our research treats the project as mid-market yield product, not Marina prestige pricing. Open-plan layouts, podium amenities, and smart-home pre-wiring are the marketed differentiators.

Binghatti Phantom typically starts at AED 1.1 million for studios, about AED 1.4 million for one-bedroom lines, AED 1.85 million for two-bedroom stock, and roughly AED 2.6 million for three-bedroom plates, with payment plans often structured as 20% on booking, construction-linked instalments, and near 50% on handover across a 24 to 36 month build. Invest Gulf underwriting for the project requires escrow certificates on every instalment, three Ejari comps for the same unit type in JVC, and a written service-charge schedule before any reservation waiver. Gross yield marketing of 7% to 9% on studios compresses after 20% to 30% long-let management fees, vacancy, and DLD registration of 4% on purchase. Confirm SPA delay clauses and trustee fees before the booking cheque for the project.

- Escrow named account on every instalment
- Three same-type Ejari comps in JVC
- Service-charge schedule in writing before waiver

## What yields should investors model?

Marketing bands typically cite 7% to 9% gross on studios and high 6% to low 8% on larger Binghatti Phantom lines in JVC. Invest Gulf rebuilds net after service charges, vacancy, and 20% to 30% management before any SPA. DLD registration of 4% belongs in the exit sheet, not only the rent slide.

| --- | --- |
| Studio | 8-9% |

Insider tip: ask two JVC property managers who already operate Binghatti towers what net rent they achieve after a 25% management fee before you accept a studio yield above 8.5% gross.

Invest Gulf nets those figures after service charges often AED 12 to 16 per sq ft, 5% to 8% vacancy, and management of 20% to 30% of rent on long-let, which can pull net into the mid-5% to mid-7% range on clean towers. Rebuild cash flow with the Mollak schedule in writing, not a sales estimate, and budget 90 days to lease if handover listings cluster. ## What risks should you stress-test?

Market cycles typically compress JVC rents even on Binghatti Phantom, while construction slippage of 6 to 12 months can force buyers to carry rent plus instalments. Invest Gulf stress-tests currency moves for non-AED earners and vacancy in year one on the project. Escrow verification on every instalment remains non-negotiable.

Mitigation checklist:

- Independent SPA review with delay compensation spelled out
- Escrow verification on every instalment
- Three rental comparables from the same building typology
- Service charge budget signed by the developer or OA estimate
- Realistic vacancy and management fee in year one

Financing notes: UAE banks lend more easily post-handover; off-plan assignments follow Binghatti NOC rules once minimum paid thresholds clear. See [cash vs mortgage Dubai](/guides/cash-vs-mortgage-dubai-property/) and [Dubai cost of living](/guides/dubai-cost-of-living-guide/) for carry planning.
