DAMAC Lagoons is Dubai's largest Mediterranean-inspired villa community, a 45-million-square-foot master development featuring over 8 themed clusters built around man-made crystal lagoons in Dubailand. With prices starting from AED 1.3 million for 3-bedroom townhouses and projected rental yields of **6.2-7.8%**, this DAMAC Properties flagship represents one of Dubai's most ambitious family-focused off-plan communities.

![DAMAC Lagoons Mediterranean Villas](https://invest-gulf.com/images/projects/damac-lagoons-overview/inline-1.jpg)

DAMAC Lagoons launched in 2021 as DAMAC Properties' answer to growing demand for affordable villa communities in Dubai's expanding Dubailand district. The project targets middle-income families and international investors seeking resort-style living without premium district pricing, positioning between established communities like DAMAC Hills and emerging areas like Dubai South.

The master community spans 8 distinct clusters, each themed around iconic Mediterranean and tropical destinations including Portofino, Nice, Malta, Santorini, Venice, Maldives, Mykonos, and Amalfi Coast. Every cluster features unique architecture, landscaping, and lagoon access, creating distinct [investment and lifestyle profiles](/guides/dubai-villa-vs-apartment-investment/) within the broader development.
