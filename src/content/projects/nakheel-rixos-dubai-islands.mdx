## About Dubai Islands and Rixos Partnership

Dubai Islands is Dubai's latest ambitious waterfront destination, comprising five man-made islands spanning over 17 square kilometers. The development showcases Dubai's commitment to creating premium residential and tourism infrastructure, positioned strategically between Dubai Marina and Palm Jumeirah.

The Rixos brand brings over 40 years of Turkish hospitality expertise, known for luxury resorts across Europe, Middle East, and Central Asia. Rixos properties are renowned for their entertainment-focused approach, combining luxury accommodation with vibrant social experiences and comprehensive wellness facilities.

The development timeline aligns with Dubai Islands' broader infrastructure completion, ensuring residents benefit from the full master-planned community experience including transportation links, retail destinations, and recreational facilities.
