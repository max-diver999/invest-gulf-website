The Oasis represents Emaar Properties' flagship luxury residential master community, offering an exclusive collection of premium villas and mansions within a carefully master-planned environment that combines water-inspired design with family-friendly community living. This [off-plan development](/guides/off-plan-property-dubai-guide/) targets the ultra-luxury market segment, providing investors with access to one of Dubai's most prestigious residential addresses along Sheikh Zayed Bin Hamdan Al Nahyan Street near the Al Qudra and Dubailand corridor.

Located within a larger master community framework, The Oasis emphasizes privacy, exclusivity, and comprehensive lifestyle amenities that cater to high-net-worth individuals, expatriate executives, and [UAE Golden Visa holders](/guides/golden-visa-off-plan-property-uae/) seeking premium residential investments in Dubai's expanding luxury property market.

## What is The Oasis master community overview?

The Oasis forms part of Emaar Properties' comprehensive master development spanning multiple phases and residential product types including luxury villas and mansions. The community represents a total investment of over AED 15 billion, positioning it among Dubai's largest luxury residential projects with completion planned over the next decade along the strategic Sheikh Zayed Bin Hamdan Al Nahyan Street corridor.

The development philosophy centers on creating a water-inspired master community with lagoons, lakes, and canals comprising approximately 25% of the total area as green and water spaces. This approach provides residents with resort-style waterfront living while creating a self-contained community with all essential services, recreational facilities, and lifestyle amenities within the master plan.

Emaar Properties leverages extensive experience in master community development, applying lessons learned from successful projects like Arabian Ranches, Dubai Hills Estate, and Emirates Living to create an optimized living environment for modern families and international investors. The [Emaar properties review](/guides/emaar-properties-review/) demonstrates consistent quality and delivery standards across multiple phases.

The Oasis master plan includes multiple distinct phases and clusters such as Palmiera, Mirage, Lavita, and Address Villas Tierra, each offering different villa types and mansion configurations while maintaining the overall water-inspired design theme and premium amenities throughout the development.

## What villa configurations and specifications should you expect?

The architectural design draws inspiration from contemporary Mediterranean and modern Arabic styles, featuring clean lines, natural materials, and extensive use of glass to maximize natural light and views of the surrounding golf course and community landscapes.

Each villa includes private outdoor entertainment areas with built-in BBQ facilities, outdoor dining spaces, and direct access to community walking and cycling paths that connect to central amenities and recreational facilities.

Typical inventory spans 4 to 6 bedroom villas and larger mansions with private pools, landscaped gardens, and lagoon, lake, or golf-facing plots. Confirm built-up area, plot size, and phase-specific handover dates in the SPA before you model carry costs.

The community management approach emphasizes maintaining high standards of cleanliness, security, and service quality that preserve property values and enhance the living experience for all residents.

## How should investors underwrite The Oasis financially?

Budget DLD's 4% transfer fee on resale, trustee charges, and service charges commonly quoted at AED 25 to 35 per sqft annually on luxury villa stock before you accept brochure yield figures.

Capital appreciation factors:

- Limited supply of new luxury villa communities in Dubai
- Emaar's strong brand recognition and delivery track record
- Master community approach creates long-term value stability
- Golf course and premium amenities maintain exclusivity

**Rental market dynamics:**

- Target rental yields of 4 to 6% for luxury villa segment
- Strong demand from expatriate executives and diplomatic families
- Corporate housing arrangements with multinational companies
- Seasonal rental demand from high-net-worth tourism

**Market positioning analysis:**

- Price per square foot competitive with established luxury communities
- Payment plan flexibility attracts international investors
- Community amenities justify premium pricing over standalone villas
- Golf course access adds significant lifestyle and investment value

Investment risks to consider:

- Luxury market sensitivity to economic conditions and oil price fluctuations
- Construction timeline extending 3 to 4 years with off-plan risks
- Service charge costs of AED 25 to 35 per square foot annually
- Market competition from other luxury developments completing simultaneously

The villa market in Dubai has shown resilience during economic downturns, with luxury properties often maintaining values better than mid-market segments due to limited supply and international demand from high-net-worth individuals. ## What Golden Visa and residency benefits apply?

All listed villa types exceed the AED 2 million Golden Visa property threshold. Confirm current GDRFA rules and mortgage structure with your advisor because off-plan OQOOD stages differ from title at handover.

Residency lifestyle benefits:

- International school access within community reduces education planning concerns
- Healthcare facilities and services meet international standards
- Banking and financial services available for resident account holders
- Multiple exit and entry flexibility for international business activities

Long-term investment protection:

- UAE property ownership laws provide strong legal protections for foreign investors
- Inheritance laws allow property transfer to family members
- No annual property taxes or wealth taxes on residential holdings
- Currency stability with AED pegged to US Dollar provides inflation protection

The combination of lifestyle amenities, investment potential, and residency benefits positions The Oasis as particularly attractive for international families seeking to establish long-term roots in the UAE while maintaining global mobility.
