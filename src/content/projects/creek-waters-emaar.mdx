Creek Waters is Emaar's waterfront apartment cluster inside [Dubai Creek Harbour](/areas/dubai-creek-harbour-property-investment/), the master community east of Downtown Dubai. The project targets mid-to-luxury buyers who want marina views and brand-name delivery without Palm Jumeirah price tags. As [off-plan stock](/guides/off-plan-property-dubai-guide/), it sits in a corridor where handover timelines and payment-plan structure matter as much as the brochure renders.

## What is Creek Waters and why does Dubai Creek Harbour matter?

Creek Waters adds another residential tower line to Emaar's AED 100 billion Dubai Creek Harbour plan, which combines housing, retail, Creek Beach, and long-term ambition around the Creek Tower site. For buyers, the location trades central Dubai density for waterfront space while keeping airport and Downtown access via Al Khail and Ras Al Khor roads.

The development uses contemporary architecture aimed at maximizing water and skyline views. Creek Harbour still builds out: amenities mature in phases, so early buyers accept construction noise and evolving retail in exchange for launch pricing.

## Which unit types and price bands does Emaar offer?

Studios and one-bedroom units anchor the AED 1.6 million entry level quoted at launch. Two- and three-bedroom layouts and penthouses sit higher, with premiums for unobstructed creek or Downtown views.

| Unit type | Typical size | Price positioning | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Studio | Compact | From about AED 1.6M | Highest yield potential, thinner resale pool |
| 1-bedroom | 700-900 sqft | Mid AED 1.6M-2.2M | Core investor and end-user mix |
| 2-bedroom | 1,100-1,400 sqft | AED 2.5M+ | Family demand, lower gross yield |
| 3-bedroom / large | 1,600+ sqft | Premium | Golden Visa threshold on single deed if priced above AED 2M |

Finishes marketed include marble flooring, built-in kitchens, floor-to-ceiling glazing, and balconies or terraces. Larger units often include maid rooms and multiple parking bays. Always match the SPA schedule to the exact tower and phase; Emaar releases multiple payment plans across Creek Harbour.

## What amenities and daily life does the project promise?

Resort-style pools, gyms, landscaped promenades, and ground-floor retail are standard for this tier. Residents also tap the wider Creek Harbour pool deck, Creek Beach, dining strips, and marina access as those assets open.

**Community spaces typically include:**

- Waterfront walking and cycling routes
- BBQ and outdoor seating zones
- Co-working or business lounge space
- Landscaped courtyards
- Marina and water-sports access where operational

Amenity delivery follows master-community phasing. Before you pay a booking fee, ask which facilities are live at handover versus still on the master plan.

## How strong is connectivity from Dubai Creek Harbour?

Dubai Creek Harbour sits between older Dubai cores and newer eastern growth. Practical commutes:

| Destination | Off-peak drive | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dubai International Airport | 15-20 minutes | Ras Al Khor route |
| Downtown / DIFC | 15-25 minutes | Al Khail corridor |
| Dubai Marina | 25-35 minutes | Depends on Salik and peak traffic |
| Mirdif / academic belt | 20-30 minutes | Family-friendly schools cluster |

Metro extension plans for the area have shifted timelines before; do not underwrite rent or resale solely on a future station opening. Bus and ride-hail cover gaps today.

## What should investors assume for yield and resale?

Creek Harbour gross yields often land in the **6-8%** band for well-finished one-beds with realistic rents, but net returns depend on service charges, management, and vacancy.

| Cost line | Planning range |
| --- | --- |
| Service charge | Often AED 15-25 per sqft annually (verify building budget) |
| Property management | About 8-10% of contract rent |
| Vacancy | 2-4 weeks in stable years; longer if oversupplied view stock |
| Resale liquidity | Improving with community scale; still thinner than Dubai Marina |

Stress-test **net** yield after those lines, not the developer gross slide. Compare three live listings in the same tower or sister Emaar building before you accept an agent's headline percentage.

Off-plan buyers should also read [off-plan risks and delays](/guides/off-plan-risks-delays-dubai/) because completion for this cluster is quoted around **2027-2028**.

## Can Creek Waters count toward Golden Visa planning?

Individual units from AED 1.6M may not alone satisfy the **AED 2 million** property Golden Visa threshold unless the registered value on completion clears it or you combine qualifying assets under current ICA rules. Off-plan counts toward paid amounts once milestones meet immigration guidance; verify with a licensed consultant before you structure solely for visa.

Creek Harbour location helps visa holders who want proximity to government service centers, international schools, and healthcare along Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Road.

## How does Emaar's track record affect delivery risk?

Emaar remains among the most active master developers in Dubai, with a long list of delivered towers in Downtown, Dubai Hills, and Creek Harbour phases already handed over. That scale reduces single-project failure risk but does not eliminate **6-18 month** industry-wide delay buffers on large waterfront sites.

Before signing:

1. Confirm **RERA registration** and escrow account details for this tower.
2. Review the payment plan: booking fee, construction milestones, and handover balance.
3. Obtain the **service charge estimate** in writing; waterfront communities often run toward the upper sqft band.
4. For resale planning, budget **4% Dubai Land Department transfer fee** on future secondary sales plus trustee and agency lines per [cost of buying in Dubai](/guides/cost-of-buying-property-dubai/).

## What checklist should run before you sign the SPA?

Use this sequence on any Creek Waters offer:

1. **Oqood / escrow:** payments must hit the DLD-supervised escrow account named in the SPA.
2. **Unit view and floor:** corner and high-floor premiums should match the price list, not a generic brochure.
3. **Payment plan IRR:** model total paid before handover versus expected secondary pricing in [best off-plan Creek Harbour](/guides/best-off-plan-creek-harbour/) comparables.
4. **Rental comps:** pull three active leases in completed Creek Harbour buildings.
5. **Exit costs:** include DLD transfer, NOC, and agency commission on a five-year hold.

Walk away if the sales team cannot produce RERA project numbers, escrow confirmation, or a service charge schedule tied to the building you are buying.

**Invest Gulf field note:** On Creek Harbour off-plan files in 2026, the most common rework item was service charge assumptions set below the owners association budget, not view or layout changes.
