Six Senses Residences represents Select Group's flagship entry into Dubai's luxury branded residence market, bringing the internationally acclaimed Six Senses hospitality brand to Dubai Marina for the first time. This luxury branded residence project combines wellness-focused living with prime waterfront positioning in one of the emirate's most established residential communities. Located near [Creek Waters by Emaar](/projects/creek-waters-emaar/), the development offers [similar luxury to Ellington Ocean House](/projects/ellington-ocean-house/) and [Como Residences](/projects/nakheel-como-residences/). The project targets [off-plan property buyers](/guides/off-plan-property-dubai-guide/) and [Business Bay investors](/areas/business-bay-property-investment/).

The development targets ultra-high-net-worth individuals seeking a lifestyle product that goes beyond traditional luxury real estate, incorporating holistic wellness programs and hospitality services typically found in Six Senses resort properties worldwide.
