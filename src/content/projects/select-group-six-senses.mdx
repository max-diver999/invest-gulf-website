## What is Six Senses Residences Dubai Marina?

Select Group licensed the Six Senses brand for a residential stack targeting ultra-luxury end-users and long-hold investors who want spa-grade services at home. Architecture marketing emphasizes biophilic design, smart-home packages, and wellness-led common areas managed under Six Senses operating standards.

Completion is targeted for Q4 2027 with handover into early 2028. This is among the first Six Senses residential launches in the UAE, so execution risk sits higher than a repeat tower from the same developer.

## What unit types and pricing apply?

Entry marketing starts at AED 8.5 million on 1-bedroom units. Larger 2 and 3-bedroom layouts and penthouses scale sharply with view orientation and terrace size. Finishes typically include stone surfaces, custom joinery, air purification, and circadian lighting packages on premium lines.

Payment plans often ask for roughly 20% on booking with the balance across construction milestones and a handover tranche. Golden Visa eligibility is straightforward at these ticket sizes (AED 2M+ registered property threshold, verify current ICA rules).

Request the service-charge model early: branded wellness operations can push annual fees well above standard Marina towers.

![Six Senses Residences Wellness Facilities](https://invest-gulf.com/images/projects/select-group-six-senses/inline-2.webp)

## What wellness amenities are included?

Marketing lists spa and thermal facilities, fitness and movement studios, nutrition programming, sleep-support services, and mindfulness workshops operated to Six Senses global standards. Residents should expect hospitality-style staffing costs to flow into service charges.

| Amenity cluster | Resident benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Spa and thermal | On-site treatments, recovery focus |
| Fitness and movement | Classes, personal training hooks |
| Nutrition | Meal planning, wellness dining tie-ins |
| Mindfulness | Workshops, stress-management programming |

## Where is the project and how is connectivity?

The tower sits within Dubai Marina's established grid, with access to the tram, Sheikh Zayed Road, and Marina Walk retail. Off-peak drives to DIFC or Downtown often run 15 to 25 minutes depending on gate access and traffic.

Waterfront promenades and marina berths support lifestyle marketing; investors should still underwrite parking, Salik, and district cooling if applicable on the specific stack.

## What investment positioning should buyers use?

Ultra-luxury Marina stock trades on scarcity and tenant quality more than gross yield. Broker summaries on comparable branded Marina towers often cite **5% to 7% gross** before fees; net returns depend on occupancy, furnished vs unfurnished strategy, and service charges.

| Project (indicative) | Price / sq ft band | Positioning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Six Senses Residences | AED 7,100+ | Wellness luxury, off-plan |
| Address Residences Marina | AED 6,200+ | Hospitality brand, ready stock |
| Marina Gate | AED 5,800+ | Premium residential |

Liquidity above AED 8.5M is thinner than mid-Marina apartments. Model longer marketing periods on exit and stress-test AED **4% DLD** plus trustee fees on resale in your spreadsheet.

## What risks should you review before booking?

First-of-brand residential execution, wellness OPEX inflation, construction slippage on luxury stacks, and limited tenant pool at top rent bands are the main flags. Review Select Group financials, Six Senses management term length, and SPA delay remedies with independent counsel.

Escrow verification on every instalment is mandatory under RERA off-plan rules. Walk away if service schedules or escrow details stay opaque.

## What payment and handover timeline should you expect?

Marketing targets Q4 2027 completion with early 2028 keys. Milestone schedules usually front-load booking and slab payments; confirm the exact curve on your unit offer. Post-handover tails, if offered, increase developer credit exposure, stress-test if handover slips six months.

## Who is the ideal buyer for this product?

End-users who will occupy and use wellness amenities capture the brand value. Investors need furnished luxury tenant profiles (executive relocations, long-stay hospitality) rather than generic Marina short-let strategies. If your model assumes 8% gross on unfurnished stock, rebuild with marina-branded comps only.

Compare payment flexibility with [Ellington Ocean House](/projects/ellington-ocean-house/) and supply depth with [Como Residences](/projects/nakheel-como-residences/) before you anchor on Six Senses exclusivity alone.

Marina tram and rideshare availability reduce parking pressure for owner-occupiers but do not eliminate service-charge inflation when spa staffing scales up. Model year-three OPEX, not year-one marketing brochures.

Furnished executive lets may justify premium rents if Six Senses operating standards match global resorts; unfurnished long-let models should use Marina Gate and Address comps, not hospitality ADR assumptions. Book a marina sales gallery visit only after you have net-yield hurdles written down.

Select Group has repeated Marina deliveries, yet wellness-branded OPEX is new for Dubai strata boards. Ask for the draft service charge schedule and brand fee disclosure before you compare with [Nakheel Como Residences](/projects/nakheel-como-residences/) or other ultra-luxury Marina stock. Keep SPA assignment rules in writing if you may exit before handover as well.
