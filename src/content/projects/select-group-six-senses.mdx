## About Six Senses Residences Project

Select Group, known for delivering premium residential developments across Dubai, has partnered with Six Senses Hotels Resorts Spas to create Dubai Marina's first wellness-focused branded residence. The project occupies a prime waterfront plot with unobstructed views across Dubai Marina's canal network and the Arabian Gulf beyond.

Six Senses brand brings over two decades of experience in luxury hospitality and wellness tourism, with properties spanning from the Maldives to Bhutan. The Dubai Marina residence marks the brand's first foray into the UAE market, specifically targeting residents who prioritize health, wellness, and sustainable luxury living.

The development features contemporary architecture designed by internationally recognized architects, incorporating biophilic design principles that maximize natural light and ventilation while minimizing environmental impact. Smart home technology and sustainable building materials are integrated throughout to align with Six Senses' commitment to environmental responsibility.

## Purchase Process and Legal Considerations

Six Senses Residences follows Dubai's standard off-plan purchase procedures with additional brand approval requirements:

**Purchase Steps:**
1. Reservation with AED 100,000 deposit
2. Sales agreement signing within 14 days
3. Six Senses lifestyle assessment and approval
4. Payment plan activation according to construction milestones
5. Handover and title deed transfer upon completion

**Legal Requirements:**
- Dubai Land Department registration for all transactions

- No-objection certificate from Dubai Marina master developer
- Six Senses brand compliance agreements for resale/rental

**Foreign Ownership:**
- Freehold ownership available to all nationalities
- Golden Visa eligibility for investments AED 2M+

- Rental income fully repatriable for foreign investors

International buyers benefit from Dubai's transparent property laws and established legal framework for luxury real estate transactions.
