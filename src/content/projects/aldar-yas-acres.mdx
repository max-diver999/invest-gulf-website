# Yas Acres by Aldar

Yas Acres is Aldar Properties' golf-front community on Yas Island, combining fairway living with Abu Dhabi's entertainment cluster. Residents live minutes from Yas Links, Formula 1, theme parks, and resort hotels while holding freehold title registered with Abu Dhabi authorities. The scheme suits end-users who want golf lifestyle and investors comparing Yas Island stock with [Abu Dhabi property fundamentals](/guides/abu-dhabi-property-investment-guide/).

## What is Yas Acres and who is it for?

The community wraps Yas Links Golf Club with townhouses and villas in phases such as The Magnolias and The Dahlias (about 120 homes in the final phase). Layouts target families and golf enthusiasts who want walkable fairway views rather than high-rise Marina density. Aldar's sustainability messaging includes efficient MEP, irrigation-conscious landscaping, and integration with Yas Island utilities already live on the island.

Buyers comparing Aldar golf product should also review [Saadiyat Grove](/projects/aldar-saadiyat-grove/) for cultural district positioning versus Yas entertainment focus.

## What golf and community amenities are included?

**Golf and recreation:** Championship course access, resort-style pools, fitness studios, tennis, children's play, and walking or cycling paths through the neighborhood.

**Lifestyle services:** Security, concierge for tee times, golf cart storage, equipment rental, and guest packages for visiting players.

**Island access:** Ferrari World, Yas Waterworld, Warner Bros World, Yas Marina Circuit, beach clubs, and Yas Mall sit within a short drive, supporting both owner-occupier appeal and short-let demand in peak tourism windows.

## What should investors model on yield and costs?

Luxury golf communities rarely trade on headline gross yield alone. Budget **community service charges** per sq ft in writing, golf membership or bundled access fees, and property management if you let to corporate or tourism tenants.

| Cost line | Investor action |
| --- | --- |
| ADREC registration / transfer | Confirm emirate fees on resale, not Dubai DLD |
| Service charge | Request audited budget for your phase |
| Furnishing | Corporate lease vs short-let capex |
| Golden Visa | Registered value must meet ICA threshold on freehold |

Historical cycles saw [Yas Island residential](/guides/abu-dhabi-property-investment-guide/) outperform broader Abu Dhabi averages in strong years, driven by tourism and limited golf-front inventory. Net returns still depend on vacancy and management, not broker gross slides.

## How does Yas Acres compare with Dubai villa alternatives?

Golf-front villas in Dubai command different liquidity and tenant pools. Yas Acres offers emirate diversification, entertainment tourism, and Aldar delivery on a largely **completed** stock versus long off-plan waits in some Dubai master plans. Compare ticket size with [Nad Al Sheba Villas](/projects/meraas-nad-al-sheba-villas/) if Dubai school and commute patterns matter more than island lifestyle.

## What due diligence should buyers run before deposit?

1. ADREC title classification and phase completion certificate for resale units.
2. Service charge history and any golf access transfer rules on sale.
3. Snagging report on secondary market handovers; warranty transfer terms.
4. Golden Visa math on **registered** value, not brochure price.
5. Rental strategy: corporate long-let vs holiday home regulations.

Walk away if service charge schedules or owners association arrears status stay undocumented past your DD window.

## Payment, resale, and ownership framework

Freehold for eligible nationalities in designated Yas zones, resale to international tenants where permitted, and Golden Visa eligibility on qualifying values. Financing through UAE banks uses standard Abu Dhabi mortgage rules; golf communities may face conservative LTV on trophy villas.

## How do rental strategies differ by unit type?

Townhouses near internal greens suit **12-month family leases** tied to Yas Island employers and schools. Golf-front villas attract executives who pay premiums for views and cart access; budget furnishing to corporate standards if you target multinationals on [Saadiyat or Yas](/guides/abu-dhabi-property-investment-guide/) corridors. Short-stay use depends on building rules and DTCM licensing; many villa communities restrict nightly lets, so confirm owners association bylaws before you buy for holiday home income.

| Strategy | Typical fit | DD focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-term unfurnished | Townhouse, park-facing villa | Ejari comps, SC per sq ft |
| Corporate furnished | Golf-front 4BR+ | Noise, parking, maid's room |
| Owner-occupy + eventual let | Any completed phase | Snagging, warranty transfer |

## What financing and exit liquidity should you expect?

UAE banks lend on completed Aldar stock with standard LTV caps for non-residents. Trophy golf villas may face longer valuation cycles and smaller buyer pools at resale. Hold periods of **5+ years** align with Yas Island event calendar and tourism maturation rather than quick flips. List with realistic SC disclosure; buyers walk when golf membership transfer fees surprise them at trustee appointment.

## What events and tourism flows support short-stay demand?

Yas Island hosts Formula 1, concerts, and theme-park peaks that lift serviced-apartment demand in surrounding communities when regulations allow. Confirm short-let rules with the owners association before underwriting Airbnb-style income on a golf villa. Corporate events during race week can spike weekly rates on furnished stock if you hold inventory near the circuit.
