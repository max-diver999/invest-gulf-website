## About Yas Acres Community

Yas Acres is developed by Aldar Properties, Abu Dhabi's flagship real estate developer renowned for delivering iconic projects across the emirate including Yas Island's entertainment complex, Al Raha Beach, and numerous award-winning communities. The project represents Aldar's expertise in creating integrated lifestyle destinations that combine residential excellence with recreational amenities.

The community is strategically positioned around a championship 18-hole golf course designed by renowned golf course architects, offering residents panoramic fairway and green views from their homes. Yas Acres encompasses both the golf facilities and surrounding residential neighborhoods, creating a cohesive golf lifestyle community.

Aldar's development philosophy for Yas Acres emphasizes sustainable luxury living, incorporating energy-efficient building technologies, water conservation systems, and landscaping that complements the island's natural environment. The community design promotes active lifestyles through walkable neighborhoods, cycling paths, and seamless integration with Yas Island's broader recreational facilities.

The project benefits from Yas Island's established infrastructure including world-class utilities, high-speed internet connectivity, comprehensive security systems, and professional property management services that ensure optimal resident experiences from day one of occupancy.

Construction follows Aldar's proven delivery standards with international contractors, premium material specifications, and rigorous quality control processes that have earned the developer recognition across multiple industry awards and resident satisfaction surveys.
