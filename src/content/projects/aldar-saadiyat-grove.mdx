The community is planned as a car-free zone in residential areas, promoting walkability and cycling through dedicated pathways that connect homes to [amenities and beach access](/projects/meraas-nad-al-sheba-villas/) points.

The project timeline aligns with Saadiyat Island's broader development phases, ensuring that infrastructure, utilities, and community services are delivered in coordination with residential handovers. This integrated approach minimizes construction disruption while maximizing early resident satisfaction.
