Off-plan sales register on **Oqood** with Dubai Land Department (DLD) while the unit is under construction. Payments go to the **RERA-approved escrow** account listed in the sales agreement, not to the developer's general account. At handover you pay the **4% DLD transfer fee** on the registered value, complete trustee appointment, and receive the **title deed** before renting or reselling. Budget agency commission (often 2%), NOC fees, and service-charge deposits alongside the DLD line item. Verify the project RERA registration number and escrow IBAN on the DLD portal before your first instalment.
