## About Nikki Beach Residences Project

Nikki Beach Residences is developed through a joint venture between Aldar Properties and Nikki Beach Global, bringing together Abu Dhabi's leading developer with the internationally acclaimed lifestyle brand. The project comprises four low-rise towers containing just 22 ultra-luxury residences, emphasizing exclusivity and privacy over mass-market appeal.

Aldar Properties, known for iconic Abu Dhabi developments including Yas Island and Al Raha Beach, brings proven track record in delivering premium residential communities. The partnership with Nikki Beach Global adds significant brand value, targeting the same affluent demographic that frequents Nikki Beach clubs worldwide.

Al Marjan Island itself represents a AED 30 billion master development spanning four artificial islands, with Nikki Beach positioned strategically between established hospitality anchors including DoubleTree by Hilton and the upcoming Wynn Al Marjan Island resort, which will feature the UAE's first casino upon completion.

**Transportation Access:**

- 25 minutes to Dubai Media City and business districts
- 15 minutes to RAK International Airport for private aviation
- Direct helicopter transfer services to Dubai and Abu Dhabi

**Nearby Landmarks and Infrastructure:**
- Wynn Al Marjan Island resort: 500 meters (opening 2027)
- RAK Mall and city center: 15 minutes
- American University of Ras Al Khaimah: 20 minutes
- Al Marjan Island Resort & Spa: walking distance

The location benefits from Al Marjan Island's freehold status, allowing 100% foreign ownership without the quota restrictions found in Dubai's condominium developments. This regulatory advantage, combined with RAK's lower property prices compared to Dubai and Abu Dhabi, attracts international investors seeking UAE residency through property ownership.

## Ownership Structure and Legal Framework

Nikki Beach Residences benefits from RAK's investor-friendly legal framework:

**Ownership Rights:**
- 100% freehold ownership for foreign investors
- No quota restrictions unlike Dubai condominium developments
- Inheritance rights transferable to heirs without UAE nationality requirements
- Property can serve as collateral for UAE banking facilities

**Residency Benefits:**
- UAE Golden Visa eligibility for property investments over AED 2 million
- 5-year investor visa available for single properties over AED 1 million
- Multiple entry visa options for international investors
- Retirement visa pathways for eligible demographics

**Investment Structure Advantages:**
- Lower transfer costs compared to Dubai (4% vs 4% + additional fees)
- No annual property taxes or wealth taxes on real estate holdings
- Rental income not subject to UAE personal income tax
- Capital gains exemption for property sales by individual investors

The regulatory environment in RAK provides stability and transparency for international investors, with established legal processes for property registration, dispute resolution, and ownership transfer supported by the RAK Real Estate Regulatory Agency.
