# Nikki Beach Residences, Al Marjan Island

Nikki Beach Residences is Aldar Properties' branded beachfront launch on Al Marjan Island in Ras Al Khaimah, pairing the global Nikki Beach lifestyle brand with Dorchester Collection service positioning. The [off-plan development](/guides/off-plan-property-dubai-guide/) targets buyers who want resort-style living ahead of Wynn Al Marjan and wider island infrastructure maturing through 2028.

## What is the development and brand positioning?

Aldar brings Abu Dhabi delivery experience from Yas Island and Al Raha Beach. Nikki Beach Global adds lifestyle branding familiar to high-spend tourism demographics.

Al Marjan is a four-island, AED 30 billion master plan. This project sits between DoubleTree by Hilton and the upcoming Wynn Al Marjan Island resort. Compare with other branded stock such as [Creek Waters by Emaar](/projects/creek-waters-emaar/) and [Sobha Hartland 2](/projects/sobha-hartland-2/) when weighing developer track record vs ticket size.

## What should investors model on yield and costs?

Marketing cites **6 to 15% gross** depending on unit size and short- vs long-let strategy. Net returns need service charges (often **AED 20 to 25 per sq ft** annually on luxury island product), furnishing, management, and vacancy.

| Risk factor | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| RAK luxury liquidity | Smaller resale pool than Dubai |
| Handover 2028 | [Off-plan delay risk](/guides/off-plan-risks-delays-dubai/) across island infrastructure |
| Branded competition | Multiple launches on Al Marjan simultaneously |
| Boutique count | ~22 units: exclusivity vs resale depth |

**Transfer costs:** RAK charges about **4%** registration on disposal (not Dubai DLD). Budget trustee and agency fees separately. Golden Visa thresholds depend on registered value at title; verify current ICA rules if residency is part of the thesis.

The boutique unit count limits liquidity but supports scarcity marketing similar to [Address Residences Dubai Hills](/projects/address-residences-dubai-hills/) in emerging luxury nodes.

## How does Aldar delivery confidence compare?

Aldar's government-linked balance sheet and Yas delivery history support schedule credibility, but island-wide utilities and Wynn adjacency still create coordination risk. Track construction photos and escrow drawdowns quarterly.

## What should snagging and handover include?

Luxury beachfront finishes still show waterproofing, glazing, and MEP defects at handover. Budget snagging and minor remedial works in year one. Photograph all defects before final payment release and confirm developer warranty windows for pools and marine-facing facades.

## Who is the typical buyer profile?

End-users seeking second homes or relocation to RAK's quieter coast. Investors targeting holiday let income around Wynn and Nikki Beach events. Golden Visa planners combining this deed with other UAE assets if single-unit registered value sits below AED 2M threshold.

## What rental management options exist after handover?

Long-term Ejari leases suit corporate tenants working in RAK hospitality. Short-term luxury lets depend on building bylaws, RAK tourism rules, and Nikki Beach operating policies. Model VAT on commercial-style letting if applicable and property management fees at 5 to 8% of collected rent.

Review Aldar quarterly construction updates and Wynn opening milestones when you stress-test 2028 handover cash flow.
