# Address Residences Dubai Hills Estate

Address Residences Dubai Hills Estate sits in one of Dubai's largest master-planned communities, developed by Emaar and positioned under the Address Hotels brand for hotel-grade service expectations. The project targets buyers who want golf-front or park-facing apartments with premium finishes rather than entry-level off-plan pricing elsewhere in the emirate.

## What is the development and who is it for?

The scheme offers contemporary apartment layouts with emphasis on natural light and views toward the golf course, parklands, or skyline. End-users include golf-oriented families and professionals working along Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Road. Investors compare it with other Emaar Hills stock and [best off-plan areas in Dubai](/guides/best-off-plan-areas-dubai-2026/) when balancing brand premium against entry ticket size.

Address branding implies concierge, housekeeping options, and maintained common areas closer to hospitality standards than a typical residential tower. Confirm which services are included in service charges versus paid à la carte at sales gallery stage.

## What should investors model on yield and costs?

Marketing often cites **6 to 8% gross** on luxury Hills product. Net returns depend on service charges (commonly higher on branded/large-amenity stacks), furnishing for luxury short- or long-let, and property management fees.

| Unit profile | Indicative gross yield band | Net considerations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-bedroom | 6.5 to 7.5% | Furnishing + SC per sq ft |
| 2-bedroom | 6.0 to 7.0% | Family tenant, longer leases |
| 3-bedroom / premium view | 5.5 to 7.0% | Ticket size vs liquidity |

**Purchase costs:** Budget **4% DLD** on registered value plus trustee and Oqood fees during off-plan, per [off-plan guide](/guides/off-plan-property-dubai-guide/). **Golden Visa:** registered value at title/Oqood must meet current ICA threshold (commonly AED 2M+); a AED 1.93M entry unit may need a larger configuration or combined portfolio strategy.

**Liquidity:** Luxury Hills resales move slower than JVC studios but attract international buyers who prioritise community and school access. Hold period of 5+ years aligns with Emaar master-plan maturity.

## What payment plans and off-plan risks apply?

Typical Emaar-style plans split construction-linked instalments with a balance at handover. Read escrow account details on Trakheesi, snag inspection rights, and delay compensation clauses in the SPA.

Off-plan buyers should verify: RERA project registration, developer variation policy, and service charge cap estimates from the sales team in writing. Missing SC schedules at purchase make post-handover cash flow guesswork.

## What payment plan structure should off-plan buyers expect?

Emaar off-plan SPAs typically link instalments to construction percentage with a final payment at handover. Common patterns include 10 to 20% on booking, milestone payments during build, and 20 to 40% on completion. Post-handover plans shift part of the balance into monthly cheques for 2 to 5 years; model those cheques against expected rent if you plan to let the unit.

| Stage | Typical investor action |
| --- | --- |

| During build | Track RERA completion % vs invoice dates |
| Snagging | Independent snagging report before final tranche |
| Handover | DEWA, SC account, Ejari if letting immediately |

## Snagging and handover priorities

Address-branded finishes still show tile, MEP, and balcony door defects at handover. Budget AED 3,000 to 8,000 for snagging fixes on a 2BR unless developer remediate in warranty window. Photograph all defects before final payment clearance.

## DLD registration and investor visa at handover

At handover, 4% DLD and trustee fees apply on registered value. If your combined portfolio targets Golden Visa, confirm whether this unit alone or with other deeds meets ICA threshold. Smallest 1BR marketing at AED 1.93M may require a larger configuration for ten-year residency planning.

## Rental yield vs personal use

Address units suit owner-occupiers who value golf and mall walkability inside Hills. Pure investors should stress-test **3-month vacancy** on luxury 2BR stacks because tenant pool is narrower than JVC. Furnishing to hotel-lite standard raises capex but supports corporate lease rates.

Review Emaar quarterly construction updates on Trakheesi; payment plan milestones should match visible site progress before you release further instalments.

Ask sales for the latest service charge estimate per sq ft in writing; luxury amenity stacks in Hills often run higher than mid-market Dubai averages.
